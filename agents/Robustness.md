# [[Robustness]]

[[Robustness]] is a key benefit of [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]] and [[Agentic Systems]]. It is the system's ability to remain functional even when some of its individual components (agents) fail. This robust nature is achieved through distributed control, [[Fault Tolerance]], and [[Redundancy]], ensuring that the system can reliably achieve its goals in unpredictable conditions.
