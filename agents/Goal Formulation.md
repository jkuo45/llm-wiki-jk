# [[Goal Formulation]]

[[Goal Formulation]] is the stage in the [[Planning]] phase where an agent, based on its [[Perception]], determines its objectives. These goals are prioritized according to predefined criteria or preferences, providing the necessary direction for the subsequent [[Plan Generation]] and [[Execution]] steps.
