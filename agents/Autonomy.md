# [[Autonomy]]

[[Autonomy]] is the degree to which an agent can control its own decision-making process. In [[Agentic Systems]], high autonomy allows for decentralized and adaptive behavior, where agents can adjust their [[Planning|plans]] in response to changing circumstances without requiring constant human oversight.
