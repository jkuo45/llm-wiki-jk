# [[Agentic Systems]]

[[Agentic Systems]] are software systems that leverage agent-based architectures to handle complex and dynamic tasks. By dividing responsibilities among intelligent agents, these systems achieve high levels of [[Scalability]], [[Modularity]], and [[Flexibility]]. They often implement patterns like the [[Plan-Execute Agentic Design Pattern|Plan-Execute pattern]] to manage decision-making.
