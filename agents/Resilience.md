# [[Resilience]]

[[Resilience]] refers to the capacity of [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]] to maintain their core functions while reorganizing and adapting in the face of uncertainty and change. This attribute is a result of the system's decentralized nature and built-in [[Redundancy]], allowing the collective of agents to withstand individual failures or environmental shocks.
