# [[Reasoning]]

[[Reasoning]] is the cognitive capability of an [[Large Language Models (LLMs)|LLM]]-based agent to analyze its current state, its goals, and the possible consequences of its actions. It is a fundamental part of the [[Planning]] phase, where the agent devises strategies and makes informed choices about which [[Plan Generation|plans]] to pursue to reach its objectives.
