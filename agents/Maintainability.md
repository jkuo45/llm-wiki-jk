# [[Maintainability]]

[[Maintainability]] is a critical advantage provided by [[Agentic Design Patterns]] such as the [[Plan-Execute Agentic Design Pattern|Plan-Execute pattern]]. By separating an agent's logic into distinct planning and execution phases, developers can more easily reason about, test, and update individual behaviors within a complex system without disrupting the entire architecture.
