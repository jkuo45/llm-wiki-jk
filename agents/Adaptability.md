# [[Adaptability]]

[[Adaptability]] refers to the capacity of [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]] to effectively respond to changes in the [[Environment|environment]] or task requirements. In an adaptable system, agents can reconfigure themselves and take on new roles as needed, ensuring the system remains effective in dynamic conditions.
