# [[Robotics]]

[[Robotics]] is a major field of application for [[Agent-Based Systems (ABS)|agent-based architectures]]. Autonomous robots use these designs to [[Perception|perceive]] their surroundings, formulate [[Planning|plans]] to navigate or complete tasks, and execute actions. Swarm robotics is a specialized area that focuses on [[Multi-agent Collaboration]] between many agents to perform complex, large-scale operations.
