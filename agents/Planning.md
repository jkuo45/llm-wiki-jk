# [[Planning]]

[[Planning]] is one of the two core phases in the [[Plan-Execute Agentic Design Pattern|Plan-Execute pattern]]. It is the cognitive process by which an agent considers its current state, its [[Goal|goals]], and its [[Environment]] to determine a sequence of actions. Planning is also recognized as one of the four essential design patterns for [[Agentic Design Patterns|agentic workflows]].
