# [[Traffic Control]]

[[Traffic Control]] is an application of [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]] where agents represent individual vehicles, traffic signals, and management systems. These agents coordinate to optimize traffic flow, reduce congestion, and improve safety in [[Autonomous Systems]] and urban environments.
