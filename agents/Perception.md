# [[Perception]]

[[Perception]] is the initial step in an agent's sensing process, where it gathers data from the [[Environment]] using sensors or external inputs. This raw information is preprocessed and analyzed to build a model of the world, identify resources, constraints, and obstacles, and ultimately inform the [[Goal Formulation]] and [[Planning]] phases.
