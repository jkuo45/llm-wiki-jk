# [[Flexibility]]

[[Flexibility]] is the capacity of an [[Agentic Systems|agentic system]] to adjust its behavior and [[Planning|plans]] in response to changing circumstances or new goals. This adaptiveness allows agents to optimize their actions in dynamic environments, improving overall system [[Efficiency]] and success rates.
