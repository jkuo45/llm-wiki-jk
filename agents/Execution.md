# [[Execution]]

[[Execution]] is one of the two primary phases in the [[Plan-Execute Agentic Design Pattern|Plan-Execute pattern]]. It follows the [[Planning]] phase and involves the agent carrying out its formulated plan through interactions with other agents or the [[Environment]]. During this phase, the agent may also perform [[State Monitoring]] to ensure goal attainment.
