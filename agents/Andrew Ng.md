# [[Andrew Ng]]

[[Andrew Ng]] is a leading figure in Artificial Intelligence who identifies four key design patterns for agentic workflows:
1. [[Agent Reflection|Reflection]]
2. [[Agent Tools|Tool Use]]
3. [[Planning]]
4. [[Multi-agent Collaboration]]

His framework emphasizes how these patterns enable [[Large Language Models (LLMs)|LLM]] agents to handle complex tasks more effectively.
