# [[Inventory Management]]

[[Inventory Management]] is a practical application of [[Agentic Systems]] in business and logistics. Agents are used to track, manage, and optimize the stock levels of goods, ensuring that supplies are maintained and deliveries are executed on time within a [[Supply Chain Management]] framework.
