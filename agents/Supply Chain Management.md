# [[Supply Chain Management]]

[[Supply Chain Management]] is a major application of [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]]. Agents representing various entities (suppliers, manufacturers, retailers) coordinate their [[Planning|plans]] and actions to manage logistics and [[Inventory Management]] across a distributed network, improving system-wide [[Efficiency]] and [[Resilience]].
