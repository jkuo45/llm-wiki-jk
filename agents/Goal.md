# [[Goal]]

A [[Goal]] is a predefined objective or target that an agent aims to achieve. It serves as the primary driver for [[Goal Formulation]] and [[Plan Generation]]. Agents use their [[Perception]] of the [[Environment]] and their internal state to determine the best sequence of actions to reach their goal.
