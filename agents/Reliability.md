# [[Reliability]]

[[Reliability]] is a core attribute of [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]]. Compared to single-agent systems, the distributed control and inherent [[Redundancy]] of MAS make them more dependable. The system is less likely to experience catastrophic failure because the failure of a single agent does not cripple the entire collective.
