# [[Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)]]

[[Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)]] is an architecture for [[Large Language Models (LLMs)]] that divides models into specialized sub-networks, or "experts." Each expert handles a subset of the data, and only the relevant experts are activated for a given task, significantly reducing computation costs and improving performance. These principles can also be applied to the agent level in [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]].
