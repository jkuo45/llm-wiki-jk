# [[System Architecture]]

[[System Architecture]] in [[Agentic Systems]] refers to the structural design that manages how agents interact, coordinate their actions, and handle resources. A robust architecture is essential for maintaining the stability and performance of [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]], especially when dealing with complex, distributed tasks.
