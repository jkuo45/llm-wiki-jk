# [[Autonomous Systems]]

[[Autonomous Systems]] are systems capable of performing tasks without human intervention. Examples include [[Self-Driving Cars]], [[Robotics]], and autonomous drones. These systems often leverage [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]] and [[Agentic Design Patterns]] to [[Perception|perceive]] their [[Environment|environment]] and execute complex actions.
