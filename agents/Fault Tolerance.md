# [[Fault Tolerance]]

[[Fault Tolerance]] is a major benefit of [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]]. Because the system is decentralized, the failure of a single agent does not cause the entire system to fail. Instead, other agents can take over the responsibilities of the malfunctioning agent, providing built-in [[Resilience]] and [[Reliability]].
