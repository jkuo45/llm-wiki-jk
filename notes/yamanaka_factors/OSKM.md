# OSKM

[[OSKM]] refers to the combination of the four core transcription factors used to induce pluripotency in somatic cells: [[Oct4]], [[Sox2]], [[Klf4]], and [[c-Myc]]. These are also known as the [[Yamanaka Factors]].

## Overview
The over-expression of these four factors can reprogram [[Somatic Cells]] into [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs). This discovery, pioneered by [[Shinya Yamanaka]], revolutionized [[Stem Cell Research]] and [[Regenerative Medicine]].

## Molecular Insights
Recent studies, such as "Molecular Insights into Reprogramming-Initiation Events Mediated by the OSKM Gene Regulatory Network," have analyzed the early transcriptional changes following OSKM transduction. Key findings include:
- The immediate response is often [[Innate Immunity]], which can lead to [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] (ROS) generation, [[notes/_link/DNA Damage]], and [[notes/_link/p53]] activation.
- Early events also include the initiation of [[Mesenchymal-to-Epithelial Transition]] (MET) and the activation of pluripotency-associated surface markers like [[PODXL]].
- Interaction networks highlight paths such as [[GSK3B]] and [[Wnt Signaling]] as critical for pluripotency amplification.
