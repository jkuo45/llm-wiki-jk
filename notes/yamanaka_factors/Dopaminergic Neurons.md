# Dopaminergic Neurons

Dopaminergic neurons are the primary source of dopamine in the mammalian central nervous system. They play a critical role in the control of metabolic processes, executive functions, and motor control.

The loss of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra pars compacta is a hallmark of [[Parkinson's Disease]]. Research using [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs) and [[Induced Neural Stem Cells]] (iNSCs) aims to replace these lost neurons or provide neuroprotective support to existing ones.

## Connections
- [[Parkinson's Disease]]: Characterized by the progressive loss of dopaminergic neurons.
- [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]]: Used to generate functional dopaminergic neurons for transplantation and disease modeling.
- [[Induced Neural Stem Cells]]: Another source for generating dopaminergic neurons through direct reprogramming.
- [[Neurodegenerative Diseases]]: Dopaminergic neuron loss is a key feature of several neurodegenerative conditions.
