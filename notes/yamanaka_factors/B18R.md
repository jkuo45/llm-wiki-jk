# B18R

[[B18R]] is a recombinant protein derived from the vaccinia virus. It functions as a decoy receptor for Type I interferons (IFN-$\alpha$ and IFN-$\beta$).

## Use in Research
B18R is commonly used in molecular biology to neutralize the [[Innate Immunity|innate immune response]] triggered by the introduction of exogenous nucleic acids (such as DNA or mRNA) into cells.

## Application to Cellular Reprogramming
In the context of [[Cellular Reprogramming]], B18R supplementation is used to:
- Suppress the anti-viral response elicited by retroviral or lentiviral transduction of [[OSKM]] factors.
- Increase the survival and transfection efficiency of synthetic mRNA protocols for generating [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs).
- Potentially modulate [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] (ROS) production and [[notes/_link/p53]] activation by dampening the initial inflammatory cascade.

## Connections
