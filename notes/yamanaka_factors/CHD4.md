# CHD4

Chromodomain Helicase DNA-binding protein 4 (CHD4) is a protein that in humans is encoded by the CHD4 gene. It is a major component of the nucleosome remodeling and deacetylase (NuRD) complex.

In the context of [[Yamanaka Factors]], CHD4 has been found to suppress the expression of [[Sox2]] and regulate cancer stem cells. High CHD4 expression is often associated with worse survival prognosis in various types of [[notes/_link/Cancer]], including breast and colorectal cancers.

## Connections
- [[Sox2]]: CHD4 regulates its expression, affecting cell pluripotency and differentiation.
- [[notes/_link/Cancer]]: CHD4 is linked to tumorigenesis and drug resistance in multiple cancers.
- [[Yamanaka Factors]]: Interacts with the regulatory network of reprogramming factors.
