# Oct4

Oct4 (Octamer-binding transcription factor 4), also known as POU5F1 (POU domain, class 5, transcription factor 1), is a protein that in humans is encoded by the POU5F1 gene. It is a critical [[Transcription Factor]] involved in the self-renewal of undifferentiated [[Embryonic Stem Cells]] and is one of the four [[Yamanaka Factors]] used to induce [[Pluripotency]] in [[Somatic Cells]].

## Role in Reprogramming
Oct4 is essential for the initiation and maintenance of [[Pluripotency]]. In the context of [[Cellular Reprogramming]], it acts as a pioneer factor, binding to closed chromatin and facilitating the recruitment of other factors like [[Sox2]], [[Klf4]], and [[c-Myc]] to activate embryonic gene expression programs.

## Connections
- Part of the [[OSKM]] regulatory network.
- Interacts with [[Sox2]] to regulate the expression of [[Nanog]].
