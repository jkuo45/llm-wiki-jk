# Chain of Thought Prompting

[[Chain of Thought Prompting]] ([[CoT]]) is a technique that encourages [[Large Language Models]] to generate a series of intermediate reasoning steps before providing a final answer. This is particularly effective for complex reasoning tasks, such as arithmetic, symbolic reasoning, and commonsense tasks.

## Key Features
- **Task Decomposition**: Breaks down complex problems into smaller, manageable steps.
- **Reasoning Traces**: Provides a "window" into the model's thinking process.
- **Performance Gains**: Significantly improves accuracy on benchmarks like [[GSM8K]].

## Related Techniques
- [[Self Consistency]]: Enhances CoT by sampling multiple reasoning paths.
- [[Tree of Thoughts]]: Generalizes CoT for exploration and lookahead.
- [[ReAct Prompting]]: Combines reasoning with external actions.
