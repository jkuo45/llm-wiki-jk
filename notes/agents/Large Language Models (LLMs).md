# [[Large Language Models (LLMs)]]

[[Large Language Models (LLMs)]] are the core computational components of modern [[Agentic Systems]]. An LLM acts as the "brain" of an agent, providing the [[Reasoning]], [[Planning]], and linguistic capabilities necessary to solve complex challenges by utilizing memory and specialized [[Agent Tools|tools]].
