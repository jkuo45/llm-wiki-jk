# [[Economics]]

[[Economics]] is a field where [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]] are frequently used for modeling and simulation. In these simulations, agents represent individual market participants, households, or firms, allowing researchers to study complex economic phenomena and the [[Emergent Behavior|emergent behavior]] of financial systems.
