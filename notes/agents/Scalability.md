# [[Scalability]]

[[Scalability]] is a major advantage of [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]]. By dividing complex tasks among multiple agents, these systems can handle increased workloads and broader domains simply by adding more specialized agents. This is more efficient than redesigning a [[Single-Agent Architecture|single agent]] to handle growing complexity.
