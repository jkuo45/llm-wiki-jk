# [[Multi-Agent Planning]]

[[Multi-Agent Planning]] is a specialized form of [[Planning]] within [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]]. It involves multiple agents coordinating their individual plans to ensure compatibility and synergy. This process ensures that agents do not interfere with each other and that their collective actions effectively move the system toward common goals.
