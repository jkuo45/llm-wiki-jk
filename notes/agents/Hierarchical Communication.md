# [[Hierarchical Communication]]

[[Hierarchical Communication]] is a component of the [[Agent-to-Agent Communication Protocol (AACP)]] in [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]]. It allows for information to flow through the different levels of a system's hierarchy, enabling superior agents to coordinate, supervise, and direct the actions of subordinate agents efficiently.
