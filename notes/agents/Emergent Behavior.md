# [[Emergent Behavior]]

[[Emergent Behavior]] (or [[Emergent Behavior|Emergent intelligent behavior]]) refers to complex patterns and outcomes that arise from the collective interactions of multiple individual agents within a [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)|Multi-Agent System]]. This behavior can often solve problems that no single agent is capable of addressing alone.
