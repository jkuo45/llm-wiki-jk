# [[Markov Decision Processes (MDPs)]]

[[Markov Decision Processes (MDPs)]] are decision-making frameworks used within the [[Plan Generation]] phase of an agent. They provide a mathematical model for selecting the optimal sequence of actions based on current states, potential actions, and their probabilistic outcomes.
