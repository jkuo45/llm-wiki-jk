# [[Social Networks]]

[[Social Networks]] can be modeled as [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]] where each agent (e.g., a user) plans and executes communication strategies to achieve specific [[Goal|goals]], such as building relationships or disseminating information. These models help researchers understand social dynamics and the emergence of network-level behaviors.
