# [[Conflict Resolution]]

[[Conflict Resolution]] refers to the strategies and strategies used within [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]] to address disagreements or conflicting goals among multiple agents. These strategies are essential for maintaining system stability and ensuring that agents can continue to work towards a common objective effectively.
