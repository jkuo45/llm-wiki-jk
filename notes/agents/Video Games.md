# [[Video Games]]

[[Video Games]] often employ intelligent agents to control non-player characters (NPCs) or digital opponents. These agents use [[Agentic Design Patterns]] such as [[Agent Reflection|Reflection]] and [[Reinforcement Learning (RL)]] to evaluate their strategies, improve their performance over time, and provide more engaging experiences for players.
