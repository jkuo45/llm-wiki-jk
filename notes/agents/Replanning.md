# [[Replanning]]

[[Replanning]] is the ability of an agent to re-evaluate and modify its current course of action in response to changing circumstances or new information. In the [[Plan-Execute Agentic Design Pattern|Plan-Execute pattern]], replanning occurs when an agent's [[State Monitoring]] or [[Goal Check]] indicates that its current plan is no longer effective or that the environment has shifted significantly.
