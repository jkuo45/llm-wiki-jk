# [[Coordinated Mechanisms]]

[[Coordinated Mechanisms]] are structured protocols and system architectures in [[Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)]] that ensure agents work together harmoniously. These mechanisms are crucial for managing dependencies, avoiding redundant actions, and resolving conflicts to achieve complex goals in a distributed environment.
