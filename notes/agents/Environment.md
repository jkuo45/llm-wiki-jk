# [[Environment]]

In the context of [[Agentic Systems]], the [[Environment]] is the external world or system an agent operates within. Agents [[Perception|perceive]] the environment through sensors or data, and their actions modify its state. Understanding the environment's current state is essential for effective [[Planning]] and [[Execution]].
