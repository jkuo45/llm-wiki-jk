# [[Action Execution]]

[[Action Execution]] is the stage in the [[Execution]] phase of the [[Plan-Execute Agentic Design Pattern|Plan-Execute pattern]] where agents perform a sequence of actions derived from their [[Planning|plans]] and available resources. These actions can involve physical movement in an [[Environment|environment]] (e.g., [[Robotics|robots]] navigating) or executing computational tasks within software systems.
