# TNF-alpha
Tumor necrosis factor alpha (TNF-alpha) is a cell signaling protein (cytokine) involved in systemic [[Inflammation]] and is one of the cytokines that make up the acute phase reaction. It is a potent activator of the [[NF-kappa B]] pathway and can induce [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]] and the production of other cytokines like [[IL-1b]].
