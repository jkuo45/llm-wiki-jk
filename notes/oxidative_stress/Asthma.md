# Asthma
Asthma is a common long-term inflammatory disease of the airways of the lungs. It is characterized by variable and recurring symptoms including reversible airflow obstruction and bronchospasm. [[notes/_link/Oxidative Stress]] and [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] play significant roles in the [[notes/_link/Inflammation]] of the airways. Antioxidants like [[notes/_link/Vitamin E]] have been studied for their potential protective effects.
