# Hydrogen Peroxide

## Definition
Hydrogen peroxide ($H_2O_2$) is a non-radical reactive oxygen species (ROS). Although not a free radical itself, it is a key mediator of [[oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]] due to its ability to generate highly reactive radicals and its relative stability.

## Biological Role
- **Source:** Produced by the dismutation of [[Superoxide Radicals]] (via [[Superoxide Dismutase]]) or directly by enzymes such as [[Xanthine Oxidase]] and [[NADPH Oxidase]] (specifically NOX4).
- **Signaling:** A critical "second messenger" in cell signaling because it is relatively stable and can diffuse across biological membranes. It regulates cell growth, differentiation, and [[Apoptosis]].
- **Defense:** Used by the immune system in the killing of pathogens.

## Impact and Toxicity
- **Precursor to Damage:** In the presence of transition metals (e.g., $Fe^{2+}$, $Cu^+$), it undergoes the Fenton reaction to produce the highly destructive [[Hydroxyl Radicals]].
- **Enzyme Inactivation:** Can oxidize cysteine residues in proteins, altering their function.

## Connections
- [[Catalase]]: One of the primary enzymes responsible for decomposing $H_2O_2$ into water and oxygen.
- [[Glutathione Peroxidase]]: Neutralizes $H_2O_2$ using [[Glutathione]] as an electron donor.
- [[oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]]: Central player in the ROS cascade.
