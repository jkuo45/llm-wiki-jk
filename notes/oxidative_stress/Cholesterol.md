# Cholesterol

Cholesterol is an essential sterol lipid that maintains cell membrane structure, serves as a precursor for steroid hormones and bile acids, and modulates signaling microdomains. Its oxidation—by [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] and enzymatic pathways—yields [[Oxysterols]] and contributes centrally to [[Oxidized LDL]] formation and [[Atherosclerosis]].

## Oxysterol Formation and Signaling

Cholesterol is susceptible to non-enzymatic oxidation by [[Reactive Oxygen Species]], yielding >30 [[Oxysterols]] including 7α/7β-hydroxycholesterol, 5α,6α-epoxycholesterol, and 7-ketocholesterol. The most abundant circulating oxysterol, 27-hydroxycholesterol (27-OHC), is produced enzymatically by mitochondrial [[CYP27A1]] (sterol 27-hydroxylase). Oxysterols are potent signaling molecules: they activate [[LXRα]] and [[LXRβ]] nuclear receptors, induce cholesterol efflux via [[ABCA1]] and [[ABCG1]], and modulate inflammatory gene expression. 7-Ketocholesterol—the predominant dietary oxysterol—induces [[Apoptosis]] in vascular cells through ROS-dependent mitochondrial permeabilization and [[Caspase-3]] activation.

## LDL Oxidation and Atherogenesis

The oxidation of LDL-associated cholesterol and phospholipids is a cornerstone of the [[Lipid Peroxidation]] hypothesis of [[Atherosclerosis]]. Within the subendothelial space, [[Myeloperoxidase]]-derived [[Hypochlorous Acid]] and ROS from activated [[Macrophage]]s oxidize LDL. [[Oxidized LDL]] (oxLDL) is taken up unregulated by scavenger receptors [[CD36 Receptor]] and SR-A, driving [[Foam Cells]] formation. Cholesterol hydroperoxides (7α-OOH, 7β-OOH) accumulate in oxLDL and act as chain-propagating species in the lipid peroxidation reaction.

## HDL, PON1, and Antioxidant Defense

High-density lipoprotein ([[HDL]]) carries [[Paraoxonase 1]] (PON1), an esterase that hydrolyzes oxidized phospholipids and cholesterol ester hydroperoxides, preventing their accumulation on LDL. PON1 activity falls under [[Oxidative Stress]], converting protective HDL into dysfunctional, pro-inflammatory HDL. Polymorphisms in the [[PON1 gene]] (e.g., Q192R) influence antioxidant capacity and [[Cardiovascular Disease]] risk.

## Membrane Rafts and Redox Signaling

Cholesterol organizes [[Lipid rafts]]—ordered microdomains that compartmentalize signaling proteins. Raft cholesterol modulates [[NADPH Oxidase]] (Nox) by regulating fluidity and [[Rac GTPase]] localization; cholesterol depletion suppresses [[Superoxide Radicals]] production, enrichment enhances it. In [[Alzheimer's Disease]], membrane cholesterol influences [[Amyloid-beta]] generation by modulating [[BACE1]] activity within raft domains.

## Clinical & Therapeutic Implications

[[Statins]] reduce cardiovascular risk not only by lowering LDL but also via pleiotropic antioxidant effects: inhibiting [[Rac1]] prenylation reduces Nox-derived ROS and upregulates [[Catalase]] and [[Superoxide Dismutase]]. [[Vitamin E]] (α-tocopherol) traps lipid peroxyl radicals within LDL, though large trials showed mixed outcomes. Dietary oxysterol restriction and PON1 modulation remain active research directions.
