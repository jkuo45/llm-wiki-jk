# Rheumatoid Arthritis
Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is a long-term autoimmune disorder that primarily affects joints. It involves persistent synovial [[notes/_link/Inflammation]] and is often exacerbated by [[notes/_link/Oxidative Stress]], with pro-inflammatory cytokines like [[TNF-alpha]] playing a major role in tissue damage.
