# Multiple Sclerosis
Multiple sclerosis (MS) is a demyelinating disease in which the insulating covers of nerve cells in the brain and spinal cord are damaged. Chronic [[notes/_link/Inflammation]] and [[notes/_link/Oxidative Stress]] contribute to the process of [[Demyelination]], leading to progressive neurological impairment.
