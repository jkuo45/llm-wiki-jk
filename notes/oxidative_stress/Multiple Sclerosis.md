# Multiple Sclerosis
Multiple sclerosis (MS) is a demyelinating disease in which the insulating covers of nerve cells in the brain and spinal cord are damaged. Chronic [[Inflammation]] and [[notes/comt/Oxidative stress]] contribute to the process of [[Demyelination]], leading to progressive neurological impairment.
