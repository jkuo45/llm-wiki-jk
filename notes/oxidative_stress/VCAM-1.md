# VCAM-1
Vascular cell adhesion protein 1 (VCAM-1) is a protein that mediates the adhesion of lymphocytes, monocytes, eosinophils, and basophils to vascular endothelium. Its expression is upregulated in response to pro-inflammatory stimuli and is a marker for [[Endothelial Dysfunction]] and early [[notes/_link/Atherosclerosis]] linked to chronic [[notes/_link/Inflammation]].
