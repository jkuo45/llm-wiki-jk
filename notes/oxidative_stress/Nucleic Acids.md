# Nucleic Acids

## Definition
Nucleic acids are biopolymers, such as DNA and RNA, composed of nucleotide monomers. They are responsible for the storage and expression of genetic information in all living organisms.

## Role in Oxidative Stress
Nucleic acids are prone to damage by [[Reactive Oxygen Species]], particularly the [[Hydroxyl Radicals]]. This damage, known as **DNA oxidation**, results in base modifications, strand breaks, and DNA-protein cross-links. The most studied lesion is the formation of [[8-oxo-2'-deoxyguanosine]] (8-OHdG), which is both mutagenic and a biomarker for [[Oxidative Stress]].
