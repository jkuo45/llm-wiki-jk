# Reactive Oxygen Species

## Definition
Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS) are chemically reactive chemical species containing oxygen. Examples include [[Superoxide Radicals]], [[Hydrogen Peroxide]], and [[Hydroxyl Radicals]].

## Physiological Role
At low levels, ROS serve as important signaling molecules in processes like [[Apoptosis]] and [[Immunity]].

## Pathological Role
At high levels, they cause [[oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]] and damage cellular structures.

## Key Species
- [[Superoxide Radicals]] (O2•−)
- [[Hydrogen Peroxide]] (H2O2)
- [[Hydroxyl Radicals]] (•OH)
- [[Singlet Oxygen]] (1O2)
