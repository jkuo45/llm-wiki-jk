# Hypochlorous Acid
Hypochlorous acid (HOCl) is a weak acid that forms when chlorine dissolves in water. In biology, it is a highly reactive species generated by activated [[notes/_link/Neutrophils]] via the enzyme [[notes/_link/Myeloperoxidase]]. It is a potent component of [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] and contributes significantly to tissue damage during chronic [[notes/_link/Oxidative Stress]].
