# Hypochlorous Acid
Hypochlorous acid (HOCl) is a weak acid that forms when chlorine dissolves in water. In biology, it is a highly reactive species generated by activated [[Neutrophils]] via the enzyme [[Myeloperoxidase]]. It is a potent component of [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] and contributes significantly to tissue damage during chronic [[notes/comt/Oxidative stress]].
