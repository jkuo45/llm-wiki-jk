# IL-1b
Interleukin-1 beta (IL-1b) is a cytokine protein that is an important mediator of the inflammatory response. Its production is often upregulated during chronic [[notes/_link/Inflammation]] and is closely linked with other pro-inflammatory markers like [[TNF-alpha]] and the activation of the [[NF-kappa B]] pathway.

## Redox Regulation of IL-1β Production and Signaling

### NLRP3 Inflammasome Activation by ROS
IL-1β is produced as an inactive 31 kDa precursor (pro-IL-1β) that requires caspase-1-dependent proteolytic cleavage for maturation and secretion. Caspase-1 is activated within the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] — a multiprotein complex whose assembly is potently triggered by [[notes/oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]]. Elevated [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]], particularly [[notes/oxidative_stress/Mitochondria|mitochondrial-derived]] ROS, promote NLRP3 oligomerization and [[ASC]] (apoptosis-associated speck-like protein) speck formation. The redox-sensitive protein [[Thioredoxin-interacting protein]] ([[TXNIP]]) dissociates from [[Thioredoxin]] under oxidative conditions and directly binds NLRP3, serving as a critical molecular bridge between [[notes/oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]] and [[notes/oxidative_stress/IL-1b|IL-1β]] secretion.

### Mitochondrial DAMPs and Lysosomal Rupture
[[notes/oxidative_stress/Mitochondrial Dysfunction]] releases [[Mitochondrial DNA]] (mtDNA) and [[Cardiolipin]] into the cytosol, both of which act as damage-associated molecular patterns ([[DAMP]]s) that engage the NLRP3 inflammasome. Additionally, [[Lysosome|lysosomal]] rupture — caused by phagocytosis of [[notes/oxidative_stress/Silica]], [[Uric acid crystals]], or [[notes/oxidative_stress/Amyloid-beta]] aggregates — releases [[Cathepsin B]] and other lysosomal proteases into the cytosol, providing a second signal for NLRP3 activation. The lysosomal membrane permeabilization (LMP) step is potentiated by [[notes/oxidative_stress/Lipid Peroxidation]] of lysosomal membranes.

### IL-1β Signaling and Redox Feedback Loops
Upon binding to the [[IL-1 receptor]] ([[IL-1R1]]), IL-1β activates [[MyD88]]-dependent signaling, leading to [[NF-kappa B]] and [[notes/oxidative_stress/p38 MAPK]] activation. NF-κB drives transcription of pro-inflammatory genes including *IL1B* itself, [[TNF-alpha]], and [[COX-2]], while also upregulating antioxidant genes such as [[notes/oxidative_stress/MnSOD|MnSOD (SOD2)]]. This dual action creates a context-dependent feedback loop: acute IL-1β signaling induces protective antioxidant responses, whereas chronic IL-1β exposure overwhelms these defenses, leading to sustained [[notes/oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]] and tissue damage.

### Clinical Relevance: IL-1β Inhibition in Inflammatory Disease
The pathogenic role of IL-1β is best demonstrated by the efficacy of [[Anakinra]] (recombinant IL-1 receptor antagonist), [[Canakinumab]] (anti-IL-1β monoclonal antibody), and [[Rilonacept]] (IL-1 trap). The landmark [[CANTOS trial]] (Canakinumab Anti-inflammatory Thrombosis Outcomes Study) showed that IL-1β inhibition reduces [[notes/_link/Cardiovascular Disease]] events independent of lipid lowering — the first clinical proof that targeting [[notes/_link/Inflammation]] can prevent cardiovascular events. This underscores the central role of IL-1β as a nexus linking [[notes/oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]], [[Autoinflammation]], and [[notes/_link/Atherosclerosis]].

### IL-1β in Neuroinflammation and Neurodegeneration
In the central nervous system, IL-1β is produced primarily by [[Microglia]] and [[Astrocytes]] in response to [[notes/oxidative_stress/Amyloid-beta]] plaques, [[alpha-synuclein]] aggregates, and [[ATP]] released from damaged neurons. Elevated IL-1β in [[notes/_link/Alzheimer's Disease]] and [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]] drives synaptic dysfunction, [[Glutamate]] excitotoxicity, and [[notes/oxidative_stress/Neurodegeneration]]. Antagonizing IL-1β signaling in animal models of neurodegeneration reduces both oxidative damage and cognitive decline.

## Sirtuin Regulation of IL-1β

[[notes/sirtuins/SIRT1]] deacetylation of [[NF-κB]] downregulates pro-inflammatory cytokines including IL-1β. [[notes/sirtuins/SIRT7]] kidney-specific knockout reduces IL-1β and other inflammatory factors.
