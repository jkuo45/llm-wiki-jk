# TGF-beta
Transforming growth factor beta (TGF-beta) is a multifunctional cytokine that plays a role in cell growth, proliferation, and differentiation. It is a key mediator of [[Fibrosis]] and is influenced by the cellular level of [[notes/comt/Oxidative stress]] and chronic [[Inflammation]].
