# TGF-beta
Transforming growth factor beta (TGF-beta) is a multifunctional cytokine that plays a role in cell growth, proliferation, and differentiation. It is a key mediator of [[Fibrosis]] and is influenced by the cellular level of [[notes/_link/Oxidative Stress]] and chronic [[notes/_link/Inflammation]].
