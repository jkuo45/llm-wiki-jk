# Lipid Peroxidation
Lipid peroxidation is the process in which free radicals "steal" electrons from the lipids in [[Cell membranes]], resulting in cell damage. It leads to the formation of reactive products like [[Malondialdehyde]] and is a hallmark of [[notes/comt/Oxidative stress]]. Antioxidants like [[Vitamin E]] are essential for inhibiting this process.
