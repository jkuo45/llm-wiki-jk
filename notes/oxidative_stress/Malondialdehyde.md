# Malondialdehyde
Malondialdehyde (MDA) is a reactive dialdehyde that is a prominent marker for [[Lipid Peroxidation]] and chronic [[notes/_link/Oxidative Stress]]. It is often measured as a [[Biomarker]] to assess the level of oxidative damage in various tissues and fluids.
