# Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) represents the total amount of money a customer is expected to spend in your business, or on your products, during their lifetime. It is a key metric in [[Customer Data Analytics]] used to prioritize retention efforts and optimize pricing.
