# Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of [[Artificial Intelligence]] that gives computers the ability to understand text and spoken words in much the same way human beings can. In accounting, NLP can be used to analyze contracts, process invoices, and power chat-based [[AI Accounting Agents]].
