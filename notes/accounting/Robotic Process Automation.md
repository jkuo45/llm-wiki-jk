# Robotic Process Automation (RPA)

Robotic Process Automation (RPA) is a technology that uses software robots to automate repetitive, rules-based tasks. In accounting, RPA is often used for data entry and reconciliations, acting as a precursor or companion to more advanced [[AI Accounting Agents]].
