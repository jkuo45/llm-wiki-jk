# Microglia

[[Microglia]] are the resident macrophages and primary immune cells of the central nervous system. They play a crucial role in maintaining brain homeostasis and responding to injury or infection. In [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]], activated microglia are localized in [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]]-containing areas and are involved in [[Microgliosis]] and [[Neuronophagia]].
