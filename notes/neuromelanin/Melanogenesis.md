# Melanogenesis

[[Melanogenesis]] is the complex biosynthetic pathway by which melanin pigments are produced. The process is initiated by the enzyme [[Tyrosinase]], which oxidizes [[L-Tyrosine]] and [[L-DOPA]]. While well-studied in skin melanocytes, the exact mechanisms of neuromelanogenesis in brain neurons are still a matter of research.
