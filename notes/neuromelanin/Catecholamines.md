# Catecholamines

[[Catecholamines]] are a class of monoamine neurotransmitters and hormones that include [[notes/_link/Dopamine]], [[Norepinephrine]], and epinephrine. They are all derived from the amino acid [[L-Tyrosine]]. Neurons that produce catecholamines often accumulate [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]] and show selective vulnerability in [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]].
