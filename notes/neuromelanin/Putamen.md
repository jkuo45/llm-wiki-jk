# Putamen

The [[Putamen]] is one of the two components of the [[Sirtuins]] (along with the [[Caudate Nucleus]]), forming part of the [[Basal Ganglia]]. It receives dense dopaminergic innervation from the [[Substantia Nigra Pars Compacta]] via the [[Nigrostriatal System]] and is critically involved in motor control. Dopamine depletion in the putamen is a primary driver of [[Bradykinesia]] and [[Hypokinesia]] in [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]].
