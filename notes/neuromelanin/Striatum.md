# Striatum

The [[Striatum]] is a subcortical part of the forebrain and a major component of the basal ganglia motor circuit. It consists of the [[Caudate Nucleus]] and [[Putamen]]. The striatum receives dopaminergic input from the [[Substantia Nigra Pars Compacta]] via the [[Nigrostriatal System]]; degeneration of this pathway is the central pathological event in [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]].
