# Cytokines

[[Cytokines]] are small signaling proteins released by cells, especially immune cells such as [[Microglia]] and [[Lymphocytes]], that mediate intercellular communication during inflammation and immune responses. In the CNS, pro-inflammatory cytokines released by activated microglia contribute to [[Neuroinflammation]] and are implicated in the progression of [[Parkinson's Disease]].
