# Pons

The [[Pons]] is a region of the brainstem located between the [[Medulla Oblongata]] and the midbrain. It houses several cranial nerve nuclei, including the [[Locus Coeruleus]], which is the principal site of [[notes/_link/Norepinephrine]] synthesis and contains neuromelanin-pigmented neurons that degenerate in [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]].
