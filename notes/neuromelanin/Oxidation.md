# Oxidation

[[Oxidation]] is a chemical reaction involving the loss of electrons, often resulting in the formation of [[Quinones]] from catechol compounds. In the context of the brain, the oxidation of [[notes/_link/Dopamine]] and other catecholamines is the initiating step for [[Neuromelanin]] biosynthesis. Uncontrolled oxidation contributes to [[notes/oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]] and is implicated in the selective vulnerability of dopaminergic neurons in [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]].
