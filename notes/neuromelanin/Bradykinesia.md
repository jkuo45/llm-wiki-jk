# Bradykinesia

[[Bradykinesia]] refers to the slowness of movement and is one of the cardinal motor symptoms of [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]]. It is often associated with a decrease in the amplitude of movements (hypokinesia) and is linked to the loss of dopaminergic neurons in the [[Nigrostriatal System]].
