# Iron

[[Iron]] is an essential metal that plays a vital role in many biological processes, including oxygen transport and enzymatic reactions. In the brain, [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]] is the primary iron storage molecule in dopaminergic neurons of the [[notes/_link/Substantia Nigra]]. However, iron can also promote the formation of [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] through the Fenton reaction, contributing to [[notes/oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]] and neurodegeneration in [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]].
