# Iron

[[Iron]] is an essential metal that plays a vital role in many biological processes, including oxygen transport and enzymatic reactions. In the brain, [[Neuromelanin]] is the primary iron storage molecule in dopaminergic neurons of the [[Substantia Nigra]]. However, iron can also promote the formation of [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] through the Fenton reaction, contributing to [[Oxidative Stress]] and neurodegeneration in [[Parkinson's Disease]].
