# Neurotoxin

[[Neurotoxin]] is a substance that alters the structure or function of the nervous system, often by inducing neuronal injury or death. Key neurotoxins relevant to Parkinson's research include [[MPP+]] (the active metabolite of [[MPTP]]), [[6-hydroxydopamine]] (6-OHDA), and [[Paraquat]]. These compounds selectively destroy catecholaminergic neurons and are used to model [[Parkinson's Disease]] in experimental systems.
