# Rapamycin

[[Rapamycin]] (also known as Sirolimus) is an immunosuppressant drug that acts as a potent inhibitor of the mTOR pathway. By inhibiting mTOR, rapamycin promotes [[notes/_link/Autophagy]]. It has been studied in various models of neurodegeneration, including [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]], for its ability to enhance the clearance of protein aggregates and damaged organelles.
