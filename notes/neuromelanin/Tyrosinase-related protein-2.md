# Tyrosinase-related protein-2

[[Tyrosinase-related protein-2]] (TRP-2), also known as [[Dopachrome tautomerase]], is an enzyme that catalyzes a key step in the melanin biosynthetic pathway. Increased expression of TRP-2 has been observed in dopaminergic neurons derived from [[Parkinson's Disease]] patients, suggesting its involvement in altered neuromelanin production.
