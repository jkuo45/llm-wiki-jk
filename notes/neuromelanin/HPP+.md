# HPP+

[[HPP+]] (4-(4-chlorophenyl)-1-[4-(4-fluorophenyl)-4-oxobutyl]pyridinium) is a toxic pyridinium metabolite of [[Haloperidol]] that is structurally similar to [[MPP+]] and may induce neurotoxicity.
