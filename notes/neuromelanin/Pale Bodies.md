# Pale Bodies

[[Pale Bodies]] are intracytoplasmic inclusions that are considered to be the precursor structures to [[Lewy Bodies]] in the pathogenesis of [[Parkinson's Disease]]. They typically appear in the same intracellular areas as [[Neuromelanin]] and form a close physical association with the pigment.
