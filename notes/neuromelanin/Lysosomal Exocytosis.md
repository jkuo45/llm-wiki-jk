# Lysosomal Exocytosis

[[Lysosomal Exocytosis]] is a process in which lysosomes fuse with the plasma membrane and release their contents into the extracellular space. This mechanism is regulated by [[notes/_link/TFEB|TFEB]] and is being explored as a therapeutic pathway to reduce the intracellular accumulation of [[Neuromelanin]] and other undegradable materials.
