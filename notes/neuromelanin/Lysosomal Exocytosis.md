# Lysosomal Exocytosis

[[Lysosomal Exocytosis]] is a process in which lysosomes fuse with the plasma membrane and release their contents into the extracellular space. This mechanism is regulated by [[TFEB]] and is being explored as a therapeutic pathway to reduce the intracellular accumulation of [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]] and other undegradable materials.
