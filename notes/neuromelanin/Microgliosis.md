# Microgliosis

[[Microgliosis]] refers to the activation and proliferation of microglia, the resident macrophages and primary immune cells of the central nervous system.

## Role in Neurodegeneration
In the context of [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]], microgliosis is a key component of the [[Neuroinflammation]] observed in the [[notes/_link/Substantia Nigra]].
- **Activation by Neuromelanin:** Extracellular [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]] released from degenerating neurons is a potent activator of microglia.
- **Neuronophagia:** Activated microglia are often observed surrounding and phagocytizing extracellular neuromelanin granules, a process known as [[Neuronophagia]].
- **Progression of Disease:** While initially a protective response to clear debris, chronic microgliosis can lead to the release of neurotoxic factors, contributing to the progressive nature of PD.
