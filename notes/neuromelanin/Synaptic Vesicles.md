# Synaptic Vesicles

[[Synaptic Vesicles]] are small membrane-bound organelles within the presynaptic terminal that store [[Neurotransmitters]] such as [[Dopamine]] and [[Norepinephrine]]. They fuse with the presynaptic membrane to release their contents into the [[Synapse]]. [[Alpha-synuclein]] is thought to play a role in the clustering and recycling of synaptic vesicles, and its aggregation in [[Parkinson's Disease]] disrupts normal [[Neurotransmission]].
