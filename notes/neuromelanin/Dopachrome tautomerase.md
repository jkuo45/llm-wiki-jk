# Dopachrome tautomerase

[[Dopachrome tautomerase]] is an alternative name for [[Tyrosinase-related protein-2]] (TRP-2), an enzyme critical for the production of melanin. It plays a role in regulating the type of melanin produced and has been implicated in the pathogenic alterations of [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]] synthesis in [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]].
