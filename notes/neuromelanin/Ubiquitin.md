# Ubiquitin

[[Ubiquitin]] is a small regulatory protein found in most tissues of eukaryotic organisms. It is used to mark proteins for degradation by the [[Ubiquitin-proteasome system]] (UPS) or to direct them to other locations in the cell. The accumulation of ubiquitinated proteins is a hallmark of many neurodegenerative diseases, including [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]].
