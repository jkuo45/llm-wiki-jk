# Pheomelanin

[[Pheomelanin]] is a type of melanin pigment that contains sulfur and is characterized by its yellow or red color. In the human brain, pheomelanin is thought to constitute the core of [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]] granules, which are then covered by a surface of [[Eumelanin]].
