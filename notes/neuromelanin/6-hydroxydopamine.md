# 6-hydroxydopamine

[[6-hydroxydopamine]] (6-OHDA) is a neurotoxin used in research to model [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]] due to its ability to selectively destroy catecholaminergic neurons through the generation of reactive oxygen species.
