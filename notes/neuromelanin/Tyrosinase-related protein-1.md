# Tyrosinase-related protein-1

[[Tyrosinase-related protein-1]] (TRP-1) is an enzyme involved in the biosynthesis of melanin. While primarily known for its role in peripheral melanocytes, its expression and potential role in the brain, particularly in relation to [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]] synthesis and [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]], are subjects of ongoing research.
