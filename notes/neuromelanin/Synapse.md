# Synapse

A [[Synapse]] is the specialized junction between two neurons where signals are transmitted from a presynaptic to a postsynaptic cell. [[Neurotransmitters]] released from [[Synaptic Vesicles]] diffuse across the synaptic cleft and bind to receptors on the postsynaptic membrane. [[Synaptic plasticity]], the activity-dependent strengthening or weakening of synapses, underlies learning and memory and is impaired in [[Parkinson's Disease]].
