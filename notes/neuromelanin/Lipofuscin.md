# Lipofuscin

[[Lipofuscin]] is a brownish-yellow, electron-dense pigment composed of lipid-containing residues of lysosomal digestion. Unlike [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]], which is restricted to catecholaminergic regions, lipofuscin is widely distributed throughout the aging human brain and other tissues, serving as a marker of cellular [[notes/_link/Aging]] and lysosomal dysfunction.
