# Major histocompatibility complex class I

[[Major histocompatibility complex class I]] (MHC-I) molecules are found on the surface of nearly all nucleated cells. They present endogenous peptides to cytotoxic T cells. In [[Parkinson's Disease]], MHC-I expression is observed in [[Neuromelanin]]-containing neurons, potentially rendering them susceptible to T-cell-mediated cytotoxic attacks.
