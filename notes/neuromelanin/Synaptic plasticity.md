# Synaptic plasticity

[[Synaptic plasticity]] is the ability of synapses to strengthen or weaken over time in response to increases or decreases in their activity, a process that may be influenced by [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]]-mediated storage and release of [[notes/_link/Dopamine]].
