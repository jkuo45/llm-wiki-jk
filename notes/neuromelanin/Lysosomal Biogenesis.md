# Lysosomal Biogenesis

[[Lysosomal Biogenesis]] is the cellular process of creating new lysosomes. It is a highly regulated process, primarily controlled by the transcription factor [[TFEB]]. Proper lysosomal biogenesis is essential for maintaining cellular [[Proteostasis]] and for the degradation of materials like [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]].
