# Neuroprotection

[[Neuroprotection]] refers to the mechanisms and strategies used to protect the central nervous system from injury and neurodegeneration. In the context of [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]], neuroprotective strategies aim to preserve dopaminergic neurons, potentially by modulating [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]] levels, enhancing [[Autophagy]], or reducing [[notes/oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]].
