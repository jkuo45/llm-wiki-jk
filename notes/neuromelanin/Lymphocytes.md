# Lymphocytes

[[Lymphocytes]] are a type of white blood cell in the vertebrate immune system. They include B cells, T cells, and natural killer cells. In [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]], lymphocyte infiltration (specifically T cells) has been observed in the [[notes/_link/Substantia Nigra]], particularly in areas containing [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]].
