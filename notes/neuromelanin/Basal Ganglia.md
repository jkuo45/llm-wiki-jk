# Basal Ganglia

The [[Basal Ganglia]] are a group of interconnected subcortical nuclei involved in motor control, procedural learning, and executive functions. The core components include the [[Sirtuins]] ([[Caudate Nucleus]] and [[Putamen]]), globus pallidus, subthalamic nucleus, and [[Substantia Nigra Pars Compacta]]. The [[Nigrostriatal System]] is the dopaminergic projection that links the SNpc to the striatum; its degeneration is the defining pathology of [[Parkinson's Disease]].
