# Tree of Life

The "Tree of Life" (árbol de la vida, 生命之樹) is a recurring spiritual symbol representing the growth and fruit of one's spiritual journey. In Chapter 105, Jesus refers to the tree of life of the men of Israel, noting that while it has many leaves (words), its fruit (deeds) are often found to be full of bitterness and worms.
