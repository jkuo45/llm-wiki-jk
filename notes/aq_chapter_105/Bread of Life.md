# Bread of Life

The "Bread of Life" (pan de vida, 生命之糧) is a spiritual concept referring to the nourishment of the soul provided by divine wisdom and truth. In the context of Chapter 105, Jesus describes how his angels bring down the bread of life to him as he gives his strength to the multitudes.
