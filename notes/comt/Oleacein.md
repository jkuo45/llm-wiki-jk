# Oleacein

**[[Oleacein]]** is a phenolic compound found in extra virgin olive oil. It is recognized for its antioxidant and [[Anti-inflammatory]] properties.

## Interaction with COMT
[[Oleacein]] is a dual substrate-inhibitor of [[COMT]]. It is metabolized by the enzyme and also competes for its activity, potentially slowing the breakdown of other catechols.

## Connections
- [[COMT]]: Oleacein interacts with this enzyme as both a substrate and an inhibitor.
- [[Anti-inflammatory]]: A primary health benefit associated with oleacein.
- [[Catechols]]: The chemical class that includes oleacein.
