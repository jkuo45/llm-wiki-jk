# Hydroxocobalamin

**[[Hydroxocobalamin]]** is a naturally occurring form of [[Vitamin B12]]. It is a precursor to the two active coenzyme forms of B12 in the body.

## Use in Slow COMT
[[Hydroxocobalamin]] is often used as a supplement for individuals with slow [[COMT]] genotypes who may be sensitive to methylated vitamins. It provides a source of B12 that the body can convert into active forms at its own pace, avoiding the "methyl trap" or sudden mood swings associated with [[MethylB12]].

## Connections
- [[Vitamin B12]]: The vitamin family to which hydroxocobalamin belongs.
- [[COMT]]: Slow variants may prefer hydroxocobalamin over methylated forms.
- [[MethylB12]]: A methylated alternative that can cause sensitivity in certain genotypes.
