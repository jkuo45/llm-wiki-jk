# Methyl groups

**[[Methyl groups]]** are simple chemical groups consisting of one carbon atom bonded to three hydrogen atoms (-CH3).

## Role in Biochemistry
The transfer of [[Methyl groups]] is known as [[Methylation]]. This process is fundamental to hundreds of reactions in the body, including:
- Neutralizing [[Catechols]] (via [[COMT]]).
- Converting [[Serotonin]] to [[Melatonin]].
- Regulating gene expression.

## COMT and Methyl Groups
The [[COMT]] enzyme requires [[SAMe]] as a source of [[Methyl groups]] to metabolize neurotransmitters and estrogens.

## Connections
- [[Methylation]]: The process involving the transfer of methyl groups.
- [[COMT]]: An enzyme that utilizes methyl groups for its function.
- [[SAMe]]: The primary methyl donor in the body.
