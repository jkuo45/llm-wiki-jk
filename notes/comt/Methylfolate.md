# Methylfolate

**[[Methylfolate]]** (L-5-Methyltetrahydrofolate) is the active, methylated form of [[Folate]]. It is a critical component of the [[Methylation Cycle]].

## Interaction with COMT
Individuals with slow [[notes/_link/COMT]] variants may be sensitive to high-dose [[Methylfolate]] supplementation. A sudden influx of methyl groups can cause rapid cycling of [[Neurotransmitter]] levels, potentially leading to irritability, anger, or anxiety.

## Connections
- [[Folate]]: The base nutrient of which methylfolate is the active form.
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: The enzyme whose activity can be affected by the availability of methyl groups from methylfolate.
- [[Methylation Cycle]]: The pathway in which methylfolate serves as a primary methyl donor.
