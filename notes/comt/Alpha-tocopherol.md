# Alpha-tocopherol

**[[Alpha-tocopherol]]** is the primary form of [[notes/_link/Vitamin E]] that is preferentially used by the human body.

## Interaction with COMT
The response to [[Alpha-tocopherol]] supplementation is significantly influenced by the [[notes/_link/COMT]] [[Val158Met]] polymorphism. This interaction is particularly notable in studies regarding [[notes/_link/Cancer]] prevention and [[notes/_link/Cardiovascular Disease]].
