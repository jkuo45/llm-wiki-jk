# Green tea

**[[Green tea]]** is a type of tea made from *Camellia sinensis* leaves. It is rich in polyphenols, particularly [[notes/_link/EGCG]] (Epigallocatechin gallate).

## COMT Interactions
[[Green tea]] consumption is often discussed in the context of [[COMT]] because [[notes/_link/EGCG]] is metabolized by the [[COMT]] enzyme. While cell studies show inhibition, human studies suggest that normal dietary levels of green tea do not significantly impair [[COMT]] activity.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/EGCG]]: The primary bioactive catechin in green tea.
- [[COMT]]: Enzyme involved in the metabolism of green tea catechins.
- [[notes/_link/Quercetin]]: Can be combined with green tea to enhance EGCG levels.
