# Green tea

**[[Green tea]]** is a type of tea made from *Camellia sinensis* leaves. It is rich in polyphenols, particularly [[EGCG]] (Epigallocatechin gallate).

## COMT Interactions
[[Green tea]] consumption is often discussed in the context of [[notes/_link/COMT]] because [[EGCG]] is metabolized by the [[notes/_link/COMT]] enzyme. While cell studies show inhibition, human studies suggest that normal dietary levels of green tea do not significantly impair [[notes/_link/COMT]] activity.

## Connections
- [[EGCG]]: The primary bioactive catechin in green tea.
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: Enzyme involved in the metabolism of green tea catechins.
- [[Quercetin]]: Can be combined with green tea to enhance EGCG levels.
