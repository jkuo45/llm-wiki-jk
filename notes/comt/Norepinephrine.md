# Norepinephrine

**[[Norepinephrine]]** (also known as noradrenaline) is a chemical that functions in the brain and body as both a hormone and a [[Neurotransmitter]].

## Metabolism
As a catecholamine, [[Norepinephrine]] is degraded by:
1. [[COMT]] ([[Catechol-O-methyltransferase]])
2. [[Monoamine Oxidase]] ([[MAO]])

In the [[Prefrontal Cortex]], its clearance is also facilitated by the [[Norepinephrine Transporter]] ([[NET]]).

## Functions
It is involved in arousal, attention, and the stress response. It also affects heart rate and blood pressure.

## Connections
- [[COMT]]: Major enzyme for its metabolism.
- [[Dopamine]]: The precursor to norepinephrine.
- [[Epinephrine]]: Norepinephrine is the precursor to epinephrine.
