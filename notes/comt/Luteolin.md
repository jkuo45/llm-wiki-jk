# Luteolin

**[[Luteolin]]** is a common [[Flavonoids|flavonoid]] found in many types of plants, including celery, broccoli, and green peppers. It has [[Anti-inflammatory]] and neuroprotective properties.

## Interaction with COMT
[[Luteolin]] contains a catechol structure and is metabolized by [[COMT]]. Like other natural flavonoids with this structure, it can inhibit [[COMT]] activity when taken in supplemental doses.

## Connections
- [[COMT]]: Involved in the disposition and metabolism of luteolin.
- [[Anti-inflammatory]]: One of luteolin's primary health benefits.
- [[Flavonoids]]: The class of polyphenols to which luteolin belongs.
