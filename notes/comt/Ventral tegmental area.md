# Ventral tegmental area

The **[[Ventral tegmental area]]** (also known as **[[VTA]]**) is a group of neurons located close to the midline on the floor of the midbrain.

## Dopamine Production
The [[VTA]] is one of the two major dopamine-producing areas in the brain. It is the origin of the mesocortical and mesolimbic dopamine pathways, which project to the [[Prefrontal Cortex]] and [[Nucleus Accumbens]], respectively.

## COMT Expression
Transgenic models have shown that [[COMT]] can be expressed in the dopamine neurons of the [[VTA]]. Increased COMT activity in these neurons can influence dopamine release capacity in target regions like the [[Striatum]].

## Connections
- [[Dopamine]]: The primary neurotransmitter produced by VTA neurons.
- [[Prefrontal Cortex]]: A major projection target of the VTA.
- [[Striatum]]: Although primarily served by the substantia nigra, the VTA also influences striatal dopamine.
- [[VTA]]: The common abbreviation for the ventral tegmental area.
