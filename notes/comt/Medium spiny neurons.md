# Medium spiny neurons

**[[Medium spiny neurons]]** (also known as **[[MSNs]]**) are a special type of GABAergic inhibitory cell which represent 95% of neurons within the human [[Sirtuins]].

## Neurotransmitter Interaction
[[MSNs]] are the primary targets of [[Dopamine]] projections in the [[Sirtuins]]. They express either [[D1 receptor|D1]] or [[D2 receptor|D2]] receptors, forming the direct and indirect pathways of the basal ganglia, respectively.

## COMT Expression
[[COMT]] immunoreactivity has been detected in the cell bodies and dendritic spines of [[MSNs]] in the human striatum, where it contributes to the degradation of dopamine and other catechols.

## Connections
- [[Sirtuins]]: The primary brain region where MSNs are located.
- [[Dopamine]]: The primary neurotransmitter signaling to MSNs.
- [[D1 receptor]] / [[D2 receptor]]: The two main classes of dopamine receptors on MSNs.
- [[MSNs]]: The common abbreviation for medium spiny neurons.
