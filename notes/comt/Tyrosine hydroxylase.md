# Tyrosine hydroxylase

**[[Tyrosine hydroxylase]]** (also known as **[[TH]]**) is the enzyme responsible for catalyzing the conversion of the amino acid [[Tyrosine]] to L-DOPA.

## Role in Dopamine Synthesis
[[Tyrosine hydroxylase]] is the rate-limiting enzyme in the synthesis of [[Catecholamines]], including [[Dopamine]], [[Epinephrine]], and [[Norepinephrine]]. Its presence is often used as a marker for dopaminergic neurons in histological studies.

## Connections
- [[Tyrosine]]: The precursor amino acid for TH.
- [[Dopamine]]: One of the primary end-products of the pathway initiated by TH.
- [[Catecholamines]]: The class of neurotransmitters synthesized via TH.
- [[TH]]: The common abbreviation for tyrosine hydroxylase.
