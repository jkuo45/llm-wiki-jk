# Parkinson's Disease

**[[Parkinson's Disease]]** is a progressive nervous system disorder that affects movement. It is primarily caused by a lack of [[Dopamine]] in certain regions of the brain.

## COMT and Treatment
In the management of [[Parkinson's Disease]], [[COMT Inhibitors]] are used to increase the effectiveness of [[Levodopa]]. By slowing the degradation of dopamine, these inhibitors help maintain higher levels of the neurotransmitter in the brain.

## Connections
- [[Dopamine]]: The neurotransmitter whose deficiency causes the disease.
- [[Levodopa]]: The primary medication used to treat Parkinson's symptoms.
- [[COMT Inhibitors]]: Medications used to prevent the breakdown of dopamine and levodopa.
