# SAMe

**[[SAMe]]** (S-adenosylmethionine) is a naturally occurring compound that is a major [[Methyl Donor]] in the body. It is involved in over 100 biochemical reactions.

## Role in the Methylation Cycle
[[SAMe]] is a central part of the [[Methylation Cycle]]. It provides the methyl group required by [[notes/_link/COMT]] to metabolize [[Catechols]] like [[notes/_link/Dopamine]] and [[Estrogen]].

## Supplementation
[[SAMe]] is used as a supplement for mood support, joint health, and liver function. However, individuals with slow [[notes/_link/COMT]] genotypes may be sensitive to [[SAMe]] as it can rapidly influence [[Neurotransmitter]] levels.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: Requires SAMe as a cofactor for methylation.
- [[Methylation]]: The biochemical pathway in which SAMe is a key player.
- [[notes/_link/Dopamine]]: One of the primary neurotransmitters regulated by SAMe-dependent COMT activity.
