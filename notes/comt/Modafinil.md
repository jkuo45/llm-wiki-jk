# Modafinil

**[[Modafinil]]** is a wakefulness-promoting agent used for the treatment of disorders such as [[Narcolepsy]]. It is also widely used off-label as a [[Nootropic]].

## Interaction with COMT
The response to [[Modafinil]] is strongly influenced by the [[notes/_link/COMT]] [[Val158Met]] genotype. Individuals with the **Fast [[notes/_link/COMT]] (Val/Val)** genotype tend to have a much better response in terms of sustained attention and cognitive enhancement compared to those with the **Slow [[notes/_link/COMT]] (Met/Met)** genotype.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: The enzyme that modulates the dopaminergic response to modafinil.
- [[notes/_link/Dopamine]]: Modafinil works in part by increasing dopaminergic neurotransmission.
- [[Val158Met]]: The polymorphism that predicts the efficacy of modafinil.
