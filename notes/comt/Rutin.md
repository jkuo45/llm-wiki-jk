# Rutin

**[[Rutin]]** is a plant pigment ([[Flavonoids|flavonoid]]) that is found in certain fruits and vegetables. It is used to strengthen blood vessels and has antioxidant properties.

## Interaction with COMT
[[Rutin]] contains a catechol structure and is metabolized by the [[notes/_link/COMT]] enzyme. As a result, it can potentially inhibit [[notes/_link/COMT]] activity at high supplemental levels.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: Metabolizes rutin and can be inhibited by it.
- [[Flavonoids]]: The class of compounds to which rutin belongs.
- [[Quercetin]]: Rutin is a glycoside of quercetin.
