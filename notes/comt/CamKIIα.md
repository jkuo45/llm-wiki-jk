# CamKIIα

**[[CamKIIα]]** (Calcium/calmodulin-dependent protein kinase II alpha) is a protein kinase that is highly abundant in the brain and is essential for synaptic plasticity and memory formation.

## Use in Research
In transgenic mouse models, such as the COMT-OE mice, the **[[CamKIIα]] promoter** is often used to drive the expression of a transgene specifically in forebrain neurons. This allows researchers to study the effects of increased [[COMT]] activity in specific brain regions.

## Connections
- [[COMT]]: Transgenic models often use CamKIIα to overexpress COMT in the forebrain.
- [[Prefrontal Cortex]]: One of the primary regions where the CamKIIα promoter is active.
- [[Sirtuins]]: Another forebrain region targeted by CamKIIα-driven transgenes.
