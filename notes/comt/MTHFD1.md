# MTHFD1

**[[MTHFD1]]** (methylenetetrahydrofolate dehydrogenase 1) is a trifunctional cytosolic enzyme of folate one-carbon metabolism. It catalyzes three sequential reactions on tetrahydrofolate (THF)-bound one-carbon units: (1) NADP⁺-dependent dehydrogenation of 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate to 10-formyl-THF, (2) NADP⁺-dependent cyclohydrolase conversion of 10-formyl-THF to 5,10-methenyl-THF, and (3) NADP⁺-dependent dehydrogenase conversion back to 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate. A mitochondrial paralog, MTHFD2, performs analogous reactions during proliferation.

## Role in Folate and One-Carbon Metabolism

[[MTHFD1]] interconverts folate derivatives that feed two essential outputs:

- **Purine and thymidylate synthesis:** 10-formyl-THF supplies carbons for de novo purine ring formation; the methylene form feeds thymidylate synthase for DNA replication.
- **Methyl-group generation:** By channeling one-carbon units toward 5-methyl-THF (via [[MTHFR]]), MTHFD1 indirectly supports the [[Methylation Cycle]] and [[Methylfolate]] production.

Thus MTHFD1 links nucleotide biosynthesis with the methyl-donor economy that supplies [[SAMe]] for [[COMT]].

## Genetic Variants and Methylation Efficiency

Several MTHFD1 polymorphisms (e.g., the G1958A / R653Q missense variant) have been associated with altered enzyme kinetics and with biomarkers of impaired folate metabolism, elevated homocysteine, and modified risk for neural tube defects and certain cancers. As with [[MTHFR]], these variants can reduce the efficiency of methyl-group provision, indirectly constraining [[COMT]] throughput and catecholamine methylation.

## Interaction with COMT

Because [[MTHFD1]] contributes to the folate-derived one-carbon pool that ultimately yields [[Methylfolate]], its activity influences the availability of [[S-Adenosylmethionine]] ([[SAMe]]) — the methyl donor consumed by [[COMT]] ([[Catechol-O-methyltransferase]]) to inactivate [[Dopamine]], [[Epinephrine]], and [[Norepinephrine]]. Inefficient MTHFD1 therefore potentially compounds slow-[[COMT]] phenotypes.

## Clinical and Research Relevance

MTHFD1 is a candidate gene in folate-metabolism GWAS and a target for understanding interindividual variation in methyl-donor needs. It is relevant to prenatal supplementation strategies and to personalized approaches balancing methyl-folate intake against COMT genotype.
