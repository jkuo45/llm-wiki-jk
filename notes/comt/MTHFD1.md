# MTHFD1

**[[MTHFD1]]** (Methylenetetrahydrofolate Dehydrogenase 1) is a trifunctional enzyme involved in the metabolism of [[Folate]].

## Role in Methylation
[[MTHFD1]] is part of the [[Methylation Cycle]], contributing to the production of various folate derivatives required for DNA synthesis and methylation reactions. Mutations in this gene can impact the efficiency of the pathway, similar to [[MTHFR]].

## Connections
- [[Folate]]: The primary nutrient metabolized by MTHFD1.
- [[Methylation Cycle]]: The metabolic pathway involving MTHFD1.
- [[MTHFR]]: Another key enzyme in the same pathway.
