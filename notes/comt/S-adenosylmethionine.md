# S-adenosylmethionine

**[[S-adenosylmethionine]]**, more commonly known as **[[SAMe]]**, is a major [[Methyl Donor]] in the human body.

## Biological Role
It is a central player in the [[Methylation Cycle]], providing the [[Methyl Groups|methyl group]] required by many enzymes, including [[COMT]] and [[DNA Methyltransferase]].
