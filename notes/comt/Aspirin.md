# Aspirin

**[[Aspirin]]** (acetylsalicylic acid) is a medication used to reduce pain, fever, or inflammation and as an antithrombotic.

## Interaction with COMT
The effect of [[Aspirin]] on the risk of [[notes/_link/Cardiovascular Disease]] varies by [[notes/_link/COMT]] genotype:
- **Slow [[notes/_link/COMT]] (rs4680 A/A):** Aspirin has been shown to be effective in preventing heart disease.
- **Fast [[notes/_link/COMT]] (rs4680 G/G):** Aspirin may actually increase the risk of cardiovascular events.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: The enzyme variant determines the cardiovascular response to aspirin.
- [[notes/_link/Cardiovascular Disease]]: The condition for which aspirin is often used as a preventive measure.
- [[Val158Met]]: The specific polymorphism that interacts with aspirin treatment.
