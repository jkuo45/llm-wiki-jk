# Val158Met

**[[Val158Met]]** (rs4680) is a common functional [[SNP]] (Single Nucleotide Polymorphism) in the [[notes/_link/COMT]] gene. It involves a substitution of Valine (Val) for Methionine (Met) at position 158 of the enzyme.

## Genotypes
- **Val/Val (Fast COMT):** High enzyme activity (3-4x faster than Met/Met). Associated with lower [[notes/_link/Dopamine]] levels in the [[Prefrontal Cortex]]. Often called the "Warrior" phenotype due to better stress resilience.
- **Val/Met (Intermediate COMT):** Normal or balanced enzyme activity.
- **Met/Met (Slow COMT):** Low enzyme activity, leading to higher baseline [[notes/_link/Dopamine]] levels. Often called the "Worrier" phenotype, associated with higher anxiety but potentially better performance on complex cognitive tasks.

## Impact on Health
The [[Val158Met]] polymorphism impacts:
- [[Cognitive Performance]] and [[Working Memory]].
- Sensitivity to [[Methyl Donor]] supplements and [[COMT Inhibitors]].
- Risk for [[Psychiatric Disorders]] like [[notes/_link/Schizophrenia]].
- Response to certain medications like [[Modafinil]] and [[Aspirin]].
