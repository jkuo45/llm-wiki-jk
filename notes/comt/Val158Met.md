# Val158Met

**[[Val158Met]]** (rs4680) is a common functional [[SNP]] (Single Nucleotide Polymorphism) in the [[COMT]] gene. It involves a substitution of Valine (Val) for Methionine (Met) at position 158 of the enzyme.

## Genotypes
- **Val/Val (Fast COMT):** High enzyme activity (3-4x faster than Met/Met). Associated with lower [[Dopamine]] levels in the [[Prefrontal Cortex]]. Often called the "Warrior" phenotype due to better stress resilience.
- **Val/Met (Intermediate COMT):** Normal or balanced enzyme activity.
- **Met/Met (Slow COMT):** Low enzyme activity, leading to higher baseline [[Dopamine]] levels. Often called the "Worrier" phenotype, associated with higher anxiety but potentially better performance on complex cognitive tasks.

## Impact on Health
The [[Val158Met]] polymorphism impacts:
- [[Cognitive Performance]] and [[Working Memory]].
- Sensitivity to [[Methyl Donor]] supplements and [[COMT Inhibitors]].
- Risk for [[Psychiatric Disorders]] like [[Schizophrenia]].
- Response to certain medications like [[Modafinil]] and [[Aspirin]].

## Connections
- [[COMT]]: The gene where this polymorphism occurs.
- [[Dopamine]]: Its levels are directly regulated by this enzyme's activity.
- [[Methylation]]: Slow COMT (Met/Met) individuals are often more sensitive to changes in the methylation cycle.
