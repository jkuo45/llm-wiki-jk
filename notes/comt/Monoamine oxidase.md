# Monoamine oxidase

**[[Monoamine oxidase]]** (also known as **[[MAO]]**) is a family of enzymes that catalyze the oxidation of monoamines.

## Role in Neurotransmitter Metabolism
[[Monoamine oxidase]] is responsible for the degradation of various [[Neurotransmitter|neurotransmitters]], including:
- [[notes/_link/Dopamine]]
- [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]]
- [[notes/_link/Norepinephrine]]
- [[Serotonin]]

## Interaction with COMT
Both [[MAO]] and [[notes/_link/COMT]] are involved in the metabolism of catecholamines. While COMT performs methylation, MAO performs oxidative deamination. Together, they ensure the proper clearance and balance of neurotransmitters in the brain.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/Dopamine]]: One of the primary neurotransmitters metabolized by MAO.
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: The other major enzyme involved in catecholamine degradation.
- [[MAO]]: The common abbreviation for monoamine oxidase.
