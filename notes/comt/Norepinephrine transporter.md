# Norepinephrine transporter

The **[[Norepinephrine transporter]]** (also known as **[[NET]]**) is a protein responsible for the reuptake of [[Norepinephrine]] from the synaptic cleft.

## Role in the Prefrontal Cortex
In the [[Prefrontal Cortex]] ([[Prefrontal Cortex]]), where the [[Dopamine Transporter]] is sparse, the [[Norepinephrine transporter]] also plays a significant role in the reuptake of [[Dopamine]]. This makes it a critical component of catecholamine regulation in the frontal lobes, alongside [[COMT]].

## Connections
- [[Norepinephrine]]: The primary neurotransmitter transported by NET.
- [[Dopamine]]: Also taken up by NET in the PFC.
- [[Prefrontal Cortex]]: The region where NET activity is crucial for neurotransmitter balance.
- [[NET]]: The common abbreviation for the norepinephrine transporter.
