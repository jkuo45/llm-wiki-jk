# Hesperidin

**[[Hesperidin]]** is a plant chemical that is classified as a [[Flavonoids|flavanone]]. It is found most abundantly in citrus fruits.

## Alternative for Slow COMT
[[Hesperidin]] is considered a safe [[Anti-inflammatory]] option for individuals with slow [[COMT]] variants. Unlike quercetin, hesperidin does not contain a catechol structure and does not interact with the [[COMT]] enzyme.

## Connections
- [[Anti-inflammatory]]: One of the primary uses of hesperidin.
- [[COMT]]: Hesperidin does not inhibit or compete for this enzyme.
- [[Flavonoids]]: The class of compounds to which hesperidin belongs.
