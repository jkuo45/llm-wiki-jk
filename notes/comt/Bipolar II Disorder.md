# Bipolar II Disorder

**[[Bipolar II Disorder]]** is a type of bipolar disorder characterized by at least one episode of hypomania and at least one episode of major depression.

## Role of COMT
Research has explored the potential interaction between [[COMT]] and [[MTHFR]] genetic variants in patients with [[Bipolar II Disorder]]. Variations in dopamine regulation and methylation efficiency may influence the susceptibility to or severity of the disorder.

## Connections
- [[COMT]]: Genetic variants of this enzyme may play a role in the pathophysiology of bipolar disorder.
- [[MTHFR]]: Another genetic factor that may interact with COMT in the context of psychiatric conditions.
- [[Dopamine]]: The neurotransmitter whose dysregulation is central to many mood disorders.
