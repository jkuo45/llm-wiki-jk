# Substantia nigra

The **[[Substantia nigra]]** is a basal ganglia structure located in the midbrain that plays an important role in reward and movement.

## Dopamine Production
The [[Substantia nigra]] pars compacta is one of the major dopamine-producing areas in the brain. It provides the primary dopaminergic input to the [[Striatum]] (the nigrostriatal pathway). Loss of these neurons is the hallmark of [[Parkinson's Disease]].

## COMT Expression
Like the [[Ventral tegmental area]], neurons in the [[Substantia nigra]] can express [[COMT]], which may influence presynaptic dopamine regulation and release capacity in the striatum.

## Connections
- [[Dopamine]]: The primary neurotransmitter produced in the substantia nigra.
- [[Striatum]]: The major projection target of the substantia nigra.
- [[Parkinson's Disease]]: Characterized by the loss of substantia nigra dopamine neurons.
- [[COMT]]: Enzyme that may impact dopamine function within these midbrain neurons.
