# Adenosylcobalamin

**[[Adenosylcobalamin]]** (also known as dibencozide) is one of the two active coenzyme forms of [[Vitamin B12]].

## Use in Slow COMT
For individuals with slow [[notes/_link/COMT]] variants who are sensitive to [[MethylB12]], [[Adenosylcobalamin]] is often recommended as a non-methylated alternative. It supports mitochondrial health and energy production without providing an immediate influx of methyl groups.

## Connections
- [[Vitamin B12]]: The vitamin family to which adenosylcobalamin belongs.
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: Slow variants of this enzyme may require non-methylated forms of B12.
- [[MethylB12]]: The methylated form of B12, which can be problematic for some COMT types.
