# Betaine

**[[Betaine]]**, also known as **[[TMG]]** (Trimethylglycine), is a substance that is involved in liver function, cellular reproduction, and the [[Methylation Cycle]].

## Function
[[Betaine]] acts as a [[Methyl Donor]], particularly in the conversion of [[Homocysteine]] to [[Methionine]]. This process supports the synthesis of [[SAMe]], which is required by the [[notes/_link/COMT]] enzyme.
