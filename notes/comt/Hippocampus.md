# Hippocampus

The **[[Hippocampus]]** is a major component of the brain of humans and other vertebrates. It belongs to the limbic system and plays important roles in the consolidation of information from short-term memory to long-term memory, and in spatial navigation.

## COMT in the Hippocampus
[[COMT]] protein and enzyme activity are present in the [[Hippocampus]]. Research in COMT-OE mice shows that while COMT overexpression can occur here, it may not necessarily lead to generalized spatial memory deficits, despite the region's importance for memory.

## Connections
- [[COMT]]: Enzyme activity in the hippocampus can be affected by genetic variants or overexpression.
- [[Working Memory]]: While the PFC is the primary seat, the hippocampus supports various memory processes.
