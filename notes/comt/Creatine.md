# Creatine

**[[Creatine]]** is a naturally occurring compound that is primarily stored in the muscles and used for energy production.

## Interaction with Methylation
The synthesis of [[Creatine]] in the liver consumes approximately 40% of the body's [[Methyl Groups]]. Supplementing with low-dose creatine can "take the strain off" the [[Methylation Cycle]], potentially benefiting individuals with slow [[COMT]] or [[MTHFR]] variants by freeing up methyl groups for other essential reactions.

## Connections
- [[Methylation Cycle]]: Creatine synthesis is a major consumer of methyl groups.
- [[Methyl Groups]]: The chemical units required to produce creatine.
- [[COMT]]: Supplementing creatine can indirectly support COMT by increasing methyl group availability.
