# Cerebellum

The **[[Cerebellum]]** is a major feature of the hindbrain of all vertebrates. It is primarily involved in motor control, coordination, and precision.

## COMT in the Cerebellum
In many transgenic models, including the COMT-OE mice, [[notes/_link/COMT]] activity in the [[Cerebellum]] remains unaffected because the promoters used (like [[CamKIIα]]) are not typically active in this region. This allows the cerebellum to serve as a negative control in studies of forebrain-specific COMT overexpression.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: Often used as a control region where COMT levels remain baseline in certain models.
- [[CamKIIα]]: A promoter that is typically inactive in the cerebellum.
