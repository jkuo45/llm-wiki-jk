# Catechol-O-methyltransferase

**[[Catechol-O-methyltransferase]]** ([[COMT]]) is one of several enzymes that degrade [[Catecholamines]], such as [[Dopamine]], [[Epinephrine]], and [[Norepinephrine]].

## Biochemical Function
The enzyme performs a [[Methylation]] reaction, transferring a [[Methyl Groups|methyl group]] from [[SAMe]] to a catechol substrate. This process is essential for the inactivation of biologically active [[Catechols]] and protecting the body from [[comt/Oxidative stress]].
