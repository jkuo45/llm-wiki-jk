# mitoCPR

**mitoCPR** (mitochondrial compromised protein import response) is a quality control pathway in yeast activated when [[Mitochondrial protein import]] through the [[TOM complex|TOM]] translocon is compromised.

## Mechanism

- Activated by [[PDR3]], a transcription factor that responds to mitochondrial precursor accumulation
- [[Cis1]] (Cis1) is recruited to the TOM complex, where it engages [[Msp1]] (yeast [[ATAD1]])
- Msp1 extracts stalled precursors from the TOM complex
- Stalled precursors are then targeted for [[Ubiquitin-Proteasome System|proteasomal degradation]]
- The pathway also upregulates components of the [[Proteasome]]

## Related Pathways

- [[mitoTAD]] — Constitutive clearance of stalled precursors
- [[mPOS]] — Precursor over-accumulation stress
- [[MitoStores]] — Storage depots for precursors
- [[mitoRQC]] — Ribosome quality control
- [[UPRam]] — Mistargeting stress response
