# IκBζ

IκBζ (Inhibitor of NF-κB zeta) is a selective co-activator of [[NF-κB]] for a subset of target genes, including _IL6_ and _IL8_. It is a major regulator of SASP genes.

## Mechanism of Action

- IκBζ is a co-activator of [[NF-κB]]
- It selectively activates a subset of NF-κB target genes
- IκBζ is essential for the expression of [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]]
- IκBζ is a key regulator of SASP gene expression

## Role in SASP

- IκBζ is a master regulator of SASP interleukins
- It is required for IL-6 and IL-8 expression
- IκBζ links NF-κB signaling to SASP composition

## Clinical Relevance

- IκBζ is a therapeutic target in inflammatory diseases
- IκBζ may be a target for modulating SASP
- IκBζ is implicated in age-related inflammation
