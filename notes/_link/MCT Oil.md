# MCT Oil

**MCT oil** is a supplement of medium-chain triglycerides, typically enriched in caprylic acid (C8). Unlike long-chain fats, medium-chain fatty acids are absorbed directly into the portal vein and delivered to the liver and [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]], where they are oxidized rapidly without carnitine-dependent transport.

## Energetic and Signaling Role

MCT oil provides a direct, efficient mitochondrial fuel that increases endogenous ketone (β-hydroxybutyrate) production. Ketones serve both as an alternative energy substrate and as signaling molecules that promote [[Mitohormesis|mitohormesis]] and metabolic flexibility.
