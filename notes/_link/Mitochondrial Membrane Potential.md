# Mitochondrial Membrane Potential

The [[Mitochondrial Membrane Potential]] (ΔΨm) is the electrochemical proton gradient across the inner mitochondrial membrane generated by the [[Mitochondrial Electron Transport Chain]]. It drives ATP synthesis and maintains mitochondrial integrity; its collapse (depolarization) is an early event in oxidative neuronal injury.

## Measurement in the methylene blue study

The methylene blue derivative study quantified ΔΨm in HT-22 cells by two methods [^1]:

- **TMRE/NAO FRET:** glutamate (20 mM, 12 h) depolarized mitochondria (↑ NAO fluorescence), an effect attenuated by [[Methylene blue]], [[Toluidine Blue O]], [[Phenothiazine]], and [[2-Chlorophenothiazine]] (EC50s 17.72, 1.54, 21.18, 45.09 nM). [[Chlorpromazine]], [[Promethazine]], and [[Neutral Red]] were far weaker.
- **JC-1 dye:** glutamate shifted JC-1 from polymer (red) to monomer (green); MB co-treatment preserved the polymer signal [^1].

A strong correlation (Pearson r = 0.9456) linked ΔΨm preservation with neuroprotection across the compound series [^1].
