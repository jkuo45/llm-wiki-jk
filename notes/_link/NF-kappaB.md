# NF-kappaB

> [!note] Canonical Note
> This is an alias for [[NFKB]]. See that note for full content.

**NF-kappaB** (NF-κB) is a highly conserved pleiotropic transcription factor family that serves as the central orchestrator of inflammatory signaling, immune responses, cell survival, and cellular stress responses. The most prevalent form is the **RelA/p65** heterodimer.
