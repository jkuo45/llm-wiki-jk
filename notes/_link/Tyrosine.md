# Tyrosine

**[[Tyrosine]]** is a non-essential amino acid that is the precursor to [[Catecholamines]], including [[notes/_link/Dopamine]], [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]], and [[notes/_link/Norepinephrine]].

## Interaction with COMT
Since [[Tyrosine]] is used to synthesize neurotransmitters that are broken down by [[notes/_link/COMT]], supplementation can increase the load on the [[notes/_link/COMT]] enzyme. Individuals with slow [[notes/_link/COMT]] variants may find that tyrosine supplementation leads to excessively high levels of dopamine, potentially causing anxiety or irritability.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/Dopamine]]: One of the primary neurotransmitters synthesized from tyrosine.
- [[Catecholamines]]: The class of neurotransmitters produced from tyrosine.
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: The enzyme responsible for the degradation of tyrosine-derived neurotransmitters.
