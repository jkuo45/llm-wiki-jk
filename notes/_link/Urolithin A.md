# Urolithin A

**Urolithin A** is a postbiotic metabolite derived from ellagitannins (found in pomegranates and nuts) that has been shown to induce mitophagy and improve muscle function and longevity in various models.

## Mitohormetic Redox-Relay (MRR)
In the [[Mitohormetic Redox-Relay]] strategy, Urolithin A serves as the "cleanup" component. It ensures that the increased mitochondrial turnover triggered by the mitohormetic pulse (induced by [[Carbazochrome]]) results in the selective clearance of dysfunctional organelles through enhanced [[notes/_link/Autophagy|mitophagy]].
