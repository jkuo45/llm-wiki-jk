# RAD51

RAD51 is a recombinase enzyme essential for [[Homologous Recombination]] (HR) repair of [[Double-Strand Breaks]] and [[DNA Interstrand Crosslinks]]. It forms helical filaments on single-stranded [[DNA]], catalyzing strand exchange with homologous [[DNA Sequences]] to enable accurate repair. RAD51 is loaded onto DNA by [[BRCA2]] and interacts with [[RAD51 Paralogs]] ([[RAD51B]], [[RAD51C]], [[RAD51D]], [[XMCC3]]). RAD51 foci formation is a marker of active HR repair. [[RAD51 Overexpression]] in [[Cancer]] contributes to [[Chemoresistance]] to [[Platinum-based drugs]] and [[PARP inhibitors]]. [[RAD51 Inhibition]] is a therapeutic strategy to sensitize HR-proficient cancers to DNA-damaging agents.
