# C11-BODIPY

**C11-BODIPY** (4,4-difluoro-5-(4-phenyl-1,3-butadienyl)-4-bora-3a,4a-diaza-s-indacene-3-undecanoic acid) is a fluorescent fatty acid analogue that incorporates into cellular membranes and shifts its fluorescence emission from red (~590 nm) to green (~510 nm) upon oxidation by [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] and lipid peroxyl radicals. It is a standard probe for detecting [[Lipid Peroxidation]] in live cells via flow cytometry or fluorescence microscopy.

## Mechanism

The BODIPY fluorophore is attached to a fatty acid tail that inserts into membrane bilayers. Oxidation of the conjugated diene bridge by lipid peroxyl radicals (LOO•) or other oxidants shifts the emission from red to green. The ratio of green/red fluorescence provides a ratiometric readout of lipid peroxidation that is independent of probe loading and cell number.

## Applications

- Quantifying lipid peroxidation in [[Ferroptosis]] studies (oxidation is suppressed by [[Ferrostatin-1]], [[Liproxstatin-1]], [[Vitamin E]])
- Detecting oxidative membrane damage in response to xenobiotics, ischemia-reperfusion, or catecholamine oxidation
- Flow cytometry-based high-content screening for ferroptosis modulators
- Microscopy-based time-course imaging of lipid peroxidation dynamics
