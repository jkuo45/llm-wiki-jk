# CD8 T cells

[[CD8 T cells]] (cytotoxic T lymphocytes) recognize peptide–MHC I complexes to kill infected or malignant cells. They form long-lived memory cells that underpin durable protective immunity.

## Rapamycin and CD8 T cells

Inhibition of [[mTORC1]] by [[Rapamycin]] can improve immune responses to infection and vaccination by increasing [[CD8 T cells|CD8⁺ memory lymphocyte]] counts and [[Dendritic cells]] activity. Low-dose mTOR inhibition is immunostimulatory and potentiates vaccine efficacy, whereas higher doses are immunosuppressive ([Zerdka et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12766144/)). Human trials show improved influenza vaccine responses with everolimus.

> [!tip] Vaccine potentiation
> Brief mTORC1 inhibition raises CD8⁺ memory and antiviral proteins, translating to better vaccine responses and fewer infections in the elderly.
