# BMI1

BMI1 (B-cell-specific Moloney murine leukemia virus integration site 1) is a polycomb group protein that plays a role in epigenetic regulation and senescence. It negatively regulates [[CCL2]] through an [[IL-1α]]-mediated mechanism in senescent mesenchymal stromal cells.

## Mechanism of Action

- BMI1 is a component of the polycomb repressive complex 1 (PRC1)
- It negatively regulates [[CCL2]] expression through an [[IL-1α]]-mediated mechanism
- BMI1 is involved in epigenetic silencing of genes
- BMI1 is a known oncogene that promotes cancer stem cell self-renewal

## Role in Senescence

- BMI1 regulates SASP chemokine expression in senescent cells
- It modulates the inflammatory milieu through CCL2 regulation
- BMI1 may link epigenetic regulation to SASP composition

## Clinical Relevance

- BMI1 is a therapeutic target in cancer (cancer stem cells)
- BMI1 inhibitors are being developed for cancer therapy
- BMI1 may be a target for modulating SASP in aging
