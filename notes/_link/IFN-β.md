# IFN-β

**Interferon beta (IFN-β)** is a type I interferon that plays a central role in the innate immune response to viral infections and cellular stress.

## Role in Senescence
IFN-β is a primary output of the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS-STING]] pathway in [[Senescent Cells]]. Its production:
- Reinforces the senescent state through autocrine signaling.
- Contributes to the pro-inflammatory component of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].
- Helps coordinate the immune response to clear senescent cells.

## Clinical Relevance
Persistent IFN-β signaling, driven by cytosolic [[mtDNA]] or chromatin fragments, is a major contributor to sterile [[Inflammaging]].
