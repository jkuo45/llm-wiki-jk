# TNF Signaling

**TNF signaling** is the pathway activated by tumor necrosis factor family cytokines through TNFR1/2, converging on [[NF-κB]], MAPK, and apoptosis pathways. It is a core inflammatory signaling cascade.

## Inhibition by Apigenin in Senescence

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> KEGG pathway enrichment of apigenin-downregulated genes in senescent cells identified **TNF signaling** (alongside cytokine–cytokine receptor interaction, viral protein–cytokine receptor interaction, and [[NF-κB]] signaling) as among the most significantly inhibited pathways — consistent with apigenin's [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] suppression of the [[SASP|SASP]].
