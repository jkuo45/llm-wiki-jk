# Hypotension

[[Hypotension]] is blood pressure low enough to impair perfusion. In [[Vasoplegic Shock]] and [[Septic Shock]], excessive [[Vasodilation]] causes refractory hypotension unresponsive to standard [[Vasopressors]], prompting rescue [[Methylene blue]].
