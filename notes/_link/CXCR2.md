# CXCR2

CXCR2 (C-X-C chemokine receptor 2), also known as IL-8RB, is a G-protein-coupled receptor that binds multiple C-X-C motif chemokines including [[CXCL1]], [[CXCL2]], [[CXCL3]], [[CXCL5]], and [[IL-8]]. It mediates paracrine functions of senescent cells and reinforces senescence induction.

## Mechanism of Action

- CXCR2 binds multiple C-X-C motif chemokines (CXCL1, CXCL2, CXCL3, CXCL5, IL-8)
- It mediates paracrine signaling from senescent cells to neighboring cells
- CXCR2 reinforces senescence induction upstream of [[p38 MAPK]] and downstream of [[p53]] and the [[DNA Damage Response|DDR]]
- CXCR2 is regulated by [[CEBPβ|C/EBPβ]] and [[NF-κB]]

## Role in Senescence

- CXCR2 mediates and reinforces senescence induction
- It creates a positive feedback loop that amplifies senescence
- CXCR2 is essential for paracrine senescence and SASP propagation

## Clinical Relevance

- CXCR2 is a therapeutic target in cancer and inflammatory diseases
- CXCR2 antagonists are being developed for various conditions
- CXCR2 is elevated in age-related inflammatory conditions
