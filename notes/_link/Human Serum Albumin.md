# Human Serum Albumin

**Human serum albumin (HSA)** is the most abundant protein in blood plasma. It is used as a [[Nanoparticles|nanocarrier]] due to its biocompatibility, biodegradability, and drug-binding capacity. Fisetin-loaded HSA [[Nanoparticles|nanoparticles]] (desolvation method; 220 ± 8 nm; 84% encapsulation) showed sustained biphasic release (~75% over 72 h) and stronger cytotoxicity against MCF-7 breast cancer cells than free [[Fisetin]].
