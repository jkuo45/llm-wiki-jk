# IRF3

**Interferon regulatory factor 3 (IRF3)** is a transcription factor that plays a critical role in innate immunity, driving type I interferon and interferon-stimulated gene (ISG) expression in response to cytosolic nucleic acids.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

In resting cells, IRF3 is cytoplasmic and auto-inhibited through an intramolecular interaction. Pattern-recognition receptors such as the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS–STING]] axis and RIG-I-like receptors activate the upstream kinase [[TBK1]] (and IKKε), which phosphorylates IRF3 on multiple C-terminal serines (e.g., Ser396). This induces a conformational change, dimerization, and nuclear translocation, where IRF3 cooperates with NF-κB and AP-1 at promoter enhancers (e.g., the IFN-β enhanceosome) to induce type I interferons ([[IFN-β]], IFN-α) and ISGs. IRF3 thus constitutes the terminal effector of cytosolic DNA/RNA sensing and a key arm of sterile inflammatory responses.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

IRF3 is a key downstream effector of the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS-STING]] pathway in senescence. Senescent cells accumulate cytosolic chromatin fragments and micronuclei from genomic instability; these activate cGAS→STING→[[TBK1]], which phosphorylates IRF3. Activated IRF3 induces type I interferons and a subset of SASP factors in [[Senescent Cells]], contributing an interferon-rich branch of the secretome distinct from the NF-κB/IL-6 arm (cytoplasmic DNA–cGAS–STING–IRF3 signaling in senescence: Dou et al., 2017, PMID 28388424). IRF3 therefore helps shape both the magnitude and composition of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]], particularly its immunostimulatory and autocrine reinforcing character.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

The cGAS-STING-IRF3 axis is a major driver of sterile [[Inflammaging]] and age-related inflammatory and autoimmune disease, and emerging evidence links chronic STING/IRF3 activation to senescence-associated pathologies (e.g., fibrosis, neurodegeneration). STING/IRF3 antagonists are in development to quell maladaptive interferon responses, offering putative senomorphic strategies to limit SASP-driven inflammation.
