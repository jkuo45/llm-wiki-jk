# IRF3

**Interferon regulatory factor 3 (IRF3)** is a transcription factor that plays a critical role in the innate immune response.

## Role in SASP
IRF3 is a key downstream effector of the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS-STING]] pathway. Upon activation by [[TBK1]], IRF3:
- Translocates to the nucleus and induces the expression of type I interferons (e.g., [[IFN-β]]).
- Contributes to the production of pro-inflammatory SASP factors in [[Senescent Cells]].

## Clinical Relevance
The cGAS-STING-IRF3 axis is a major driver of sterile [[Inflammaging]] and age-related inflammatory diseases.
