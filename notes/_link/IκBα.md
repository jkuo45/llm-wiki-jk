# IκBα

**IκBα** (NFKBIA) is the cytosolic inhibitor that sequesters [[NF-κB]] (p65/p50) in the cytoplasm. Signal-induced degradation of IκBα permits NF-κB nuclear translocation and transcription of inflammatory genes, including [[SASP|SASP]] factors.

## In the Apigenin Study

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> Cellular senescence was accompanied by IκBα degradation and NF-κB (p65/p50) nuclear translocation; both apigenin and the [[HSPA8]] inhibitor [[VER155008]] reversed this, indicating a shared mechanism of curtailing NF-κB activation upstream of SASP suppression. Apigenin also inhibits [[NF-κB]] p65 via IRAK1/IκBα signaling in BJ fibroblasts[33].
