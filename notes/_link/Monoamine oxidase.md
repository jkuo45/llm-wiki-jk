# Monoamine oxidase
**[[Monoamine oxidase]]** (also known as **[[MAO]]**) is a family of enzymes that catalyze the oxidation of monoamines.
## Role in Neurotransmitter Metabolism
[[Monoamine oxidase]] is responsible for the degradation of various [[Neurotransmitters|neurotransmitters]], including:
- [[Dopamine]]
- [[Epinephrine]]
- [[Norepinephrine]]
- [[Serotonin]]
## Interaction with COMT
Both [[MAO]] and [[COMT]] are involved in the metabolism of catecholamines. While COMT performs methylation, MAO performs oxidative deamination. Together, they ensure the proper clearance and balance of neurotransmitters in the brain.
