# Vasodilation

[[Vasodilation]] is the relaxation of vascular smooth muscle that widens blood vessels and lowers systemic vascular resistance. It is physiologically mediated by [[Nitric Oxide]] → [[Soluble Guanylyl Cyclase]] → [[Cyclic Guanosine Monophosphate]]. Excessive, cytokine-driven vasodilation produces [[Hypotension]] in [[Vasoplegic Shock]] and [[Septic Shock]].
