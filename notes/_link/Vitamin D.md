# Vitamin D

**Vitamin D** is a group of fat-soluble secosteroids responsible for increasing intestinal absorption of calcium, magnesium, and phosphate. The two major forms are vitamin D₂ (ergocalciferol) and vitamin D₃ (cholecalciferol).

## Biological Functions

- Regulates calcium and phosphate homeostasis
- Modulates [[Immune System|immune function]] through the vitamin D receptor (VDR)
- Exhibits anti-inflammatory effects relevant to [[notes/_link/Inflammation]] and [[Autoimmunity]]
- Influences [[notes/_link/Mitochondria|mitochondrial function]] and [[Oxidative Stress|oxidative stress]] responses

## Clinical Relevance

Vitamin D deficiency is associated with increased risk of [[notes/_link/Cardiovascular Disease]], [[notes/_link/Diabetes Mellitus]], [[infection]], and [[notes/_link/Neurodegenerative Diseases]]. Supplementation has been studied in the context of [[Asthma]], [[notes/_link/Depression]], and [[COVID-19]].
