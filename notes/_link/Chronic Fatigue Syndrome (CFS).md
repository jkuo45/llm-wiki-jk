# Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS)

**Chronic fatigue syndrome (CFS)**, also termed [[Myalgic Encephalomyelitis (ME)|myalgic encephalomyelitis (ME)]] or [[Post-viral Fatigue Syndrome (PFS)|post-viral fatigue syndrome (PFS)]], is a debilitating disorder characterized by persistent or relapsing [[Fatigue]] lasting >6 months, [[Post-exertional Malaise|post-exertional malaise]], and accompanying [[Cognitive and Sleep Disorders|cognitive and sleep disturbances]], [[Pain]], and [[Intolerance to Physical Exertion|exercise intolerance]].

## Etiology and Hypotheses

The [[Etiology]] is unknown; infectious triggers (including post-viral), immune dysfunction, [[Autonomic Nervous System|autonomic]] and endocrine disturbances, and [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|cellular energy metabolism]] impairments have been proposed. Some evidence suggests altered [[Brain]] creatine/energy metabolism.

## Creatine and CFS

[[Creatine in Health and Disease]] reviews suggestive but preliminary findings:

- Lower [[Brain]] creatine in the left parietal cortex (and altered creatine-normalized metabolite ratios: N-acetylaspartate/creatine, choline/creatine, lactate/creatine, myo-inositol/creatine) in ME/CFS.
- [[Creatine Supplementation]] and [[Guanidinoacetate (GAA)|GAA]] supplementation improved functional capacity, pain, depression, sleep, and quality of life in small open-label/fibromyalgia studies; benefits waned after stopping.
- Conclusions remain speculative pending larger trials.

## Management

Symptomatic and graded, individualized activity; no approved disease-modifying drug.
