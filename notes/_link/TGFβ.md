# TGFβ (Transforming Growth Factor-beta)

TGFβ (Transforming Growth Factor-beta) is a multifunctional cytokine that is a SASP factor involved in tissue remodelling, [[Paracrine Senescence|paracrine senescence]], and immune suppression. It is secreted by senescent cells in aged mice to reduce mesenchymal progenitor cells and delay fracture healing.

## Mechanism of Action

- TGFβ is secreted by senescent cells
- It reduces mesenchymal progenitor cells in aged mice
- TGFβ delays fracture healing
- TGFβ is an inducer of [[Paracrine Senescence|paracrine senescence]]
- TGFβ promotes SASP upregulation and immune-suppressive conditions

## Role in SASP

- TGFβ is an early SASP factor
- It promotes paracrine senescence
- TGFβ creates immune-suppressive conditions in hypoxic tumor microenvironments

## Clinical Relevance

- TGFβ is a therapeutic target in fibrosis and cancer
- TGFβ inhibitors are being developed
- TGFβ may be a target for modulating SASP in aging
