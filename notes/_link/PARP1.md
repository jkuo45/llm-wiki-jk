# PARP1
PARP1 (Poly [ADP-ribose] polymerase 1) is a nuclear enzyme that plays a critical role in DNA repair, genome stability, and apoptosis. It responds to DNA breaks and utilizes NAD+ to produce ADP-ribose polymers, facilitating the DNA repair process.
