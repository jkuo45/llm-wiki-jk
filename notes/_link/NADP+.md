# NADP+

[[NADP+]] (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate, oxidized) is reduced to [[NADPH]] by the [[Pentose Phosphate Pathway]] and other dehydrogenases. NADPH powers fatty-acid synthesis and, critically, regenerates reduced [[Glutathione]] to neutralize [[Reactive Oxygen Species]].

## Relevance

The NADPH generated from NADP+ is required for [[Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase]]-mediated glutathione recycling and for methylene blue's reduction of [[Methemoglobin]].
