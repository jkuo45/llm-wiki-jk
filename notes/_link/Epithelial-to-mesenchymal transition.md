# Epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition

**Epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT)** is a biological process in which epithelial cells lose their apical-basal polarity and cell-cell adhesion, gaining migratory and invasive properties to become mesenchymal stem cells.

## Role in Senescence and Cancer
EMT is a critical process in [[Cancer]] progression and [[Metastasis]]. The [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] produced by [[Senescent Cells]] can promote EMT in neighboring cancer cells through factors like:
- [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]]
- [[TGF-β]]
- Matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs)

This SASP-induced EMT can enhance tumor invasiveness and therapy resistance, contributing to the detrimental effects of chronic senescence in the tumor microenvironment.
