# Topoisomerase

**Topoisomerases** are enzymes that manage [[DNA Damage|DNA]] supercoiling and topological stress during replication, transcription, and repair by transiently cleaving one (topoisomerase I) or both (topoisomerase II) DNA strands, passing another strand through the break, and religating.

[[Fisetin]], like some other [[Flavonoids|flavonoids]], is a topoisomerase inhibitor. This activity may contribute to its anti-[[Cancer|cancer]] and senotherapeutic effects, as it can induce [[Apoptosis|apoptosis]] in rapidly dividing cells by trapping topoisomerase–DNA cleavage complexes.
