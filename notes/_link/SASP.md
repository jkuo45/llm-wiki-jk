# SASP

The **senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP)** is a complex mixture of cytokines, chemokines, growth factors, matrix metalloproteinases, and bioactive lipids secreted by [[Cellular Senescence|senescent cells]]. The SASP is a hallmark of the senescence program and mediates many of the pro-inflammatory and pro-aging effects of senescent cell accumulation in vivo.

## Core Components

The SASP includes hundreds of factors, classified into major families:

- **Pro-inflammatory Cytokines**: [[IL-6]], [[IL-1α]], [[Interleukin 1β]], [[TNFα]]
- **Chemokines**: [[IL-8]]/[[CXCL8]], [[CCL2]]/[[MCP-1]], [[CCL20]], [[CCL26]], [[CXCL1]], [[CXCL2]], [[CXCL3]]
- **Growth Factors**: [[GM-CSF]], [[G-CSF]], [[HGF]], [[FGF]]-2, [[VEGF]], [[EGF]]
- **Matrix Metalloproteinases**: [[MMP1]], [[MMP-3]], [[MMP-9]], [[MMP-12]]
- **Extracellular Matrix Components**: [[Fibronectin]], [[Collagen]] fragments
- **Soluble Receptors**: [[sTNFR]], [[sIL-6R]]
- **Bioactive Lipids**: [[Prostaglandin E2]], [[Leukotrienes]]
- **Extracellular Vesicles**: exosomes and microvesicles carrying [[miRNA]], [[DNA]], and proteins

## Regulation

The SASP is transcriptionally controlled by several master regulators:

- **[[NFκB]]** — the central transcription factor driving SASP gene expression; activated by [[DNA Damage Response]] signaling and [[ATM]]-[[NEMO]] pathway
- **[[CEBPβ]]** — cooperates with NF-κB to regulate IL-6 and IL-8 transcription
- **[[GATA4]]** — activates NF-κB via [[TRAF3IP2]] engagement
- **[[p38 MAPK]]** — promotes SASP through post-transcriptional mRNA stabilization and translational control
- **[[mTORC1]]** — signals through [[4E-BP1]] to regulate translation of SASP components; [[Rapamycin]] suppresses the SASP
- **[[cGAS-STING]]** — senses cytoplasmic [[LINE-1]] and [[Alu element|Alu]] retrotransposon-derived DNA; activated in senescence to drive [[IL-6]], [[IFNβ]], and [[ISG]] expression
- **[[JAK2]]-[[STAT3]]** — autocrine amplifies SASP through positive feedback

The [[DNA Damage Response]] ([[ATM]]-[[CHK2]]-[[p53]]) is required for SASP establishment but not maintenance. Once established, the SASP is sustained by [[retrotransposon]] activation and [[cGAS-STING]] signaling.

## Biological Effects

### Paracrine Senescence
SASP factors induce [[Cellular Senescence]] in neighboring cells through paracrine signaling (bystander effect), spreading the senescence phenotype through tissues. IL-1β and IL-6 are the primary paracrine mediators.

### Immune Clearance
SASP chemokines recruit [[Natural Killer Cells]], [[Macrophages]], and [[T Cells]] to eliminate senescent cells. This immune-mediated clearance is beneficial in acute contexts (e.g., wound healing, tumor suppression) but becomes dysregulated with age.

### Chronic Inflammation
Persistent SASP drives chronic low-grade inflammation — [[Inflammaging]]. SASP components disrupt tissue architecture via MMP-mediated [[Extracellular Matrix]] degradation, promote [[Stem Cell Exhaustion]], and create a pro-tumorigenic microenvironment.

### Tumorigenesis
The SASP has dual roles in [[Cancer]]:
- **Suppressive**: The SASP reinforces senescence in premalignant cells via paracrine signaling and recruits immune cells to clear them
- **Promoting**: In established tumors, SASP factors stimulate [[Angiogenesis]] (VEGF, IL-8), invasion (MMPs), and [[Epithelial-Mesenchymal Transition]]

### Tissue Repair
Acute SASP facilitates [[Wound Healing]] by promoting [[Myofibroblast]] differentiation and ECM remodeling. Chronic SASP, however, impairs regeneration.

## Clinical Relevance

### Senolytic Combination Therapy
The SASP is the therapeutic target of [[Senolytics]] — drugs that selectively eliminate senescent cells. [[Dasatinib]] + [[Quercetin]] (D+Q) reduces SASP burden in aged mice and has shown clinical benefit in [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis]], [[Diabetic Kidney Disease]], and [[Osteoarthritis]].

### SASP Suppression (Senomorphics)
Rather than killing senescent cells, senomorphic agents suppress SASP production:
- [[Metformin]] — inhibits NF-κB
- [[Rapamycin]] — inhibits mTORC1-dependent SASP translation
- [[Ruxolitinib]] — JAK2 inhibitor; reduces SASP cytokines
- [[Glucocorticoids]] — broad NF-κB suppression
- [[Aspirin]] — inhibits prostaglandin synthesis

### Biomarker Potential
Circulating SASP factors ([[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], [[MMP-9]], [[PAI-1]]) serve as biomarkers of biological age and senescent cell burden.
