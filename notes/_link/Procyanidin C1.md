# Procyanidin C1

**Procyanidin C1 (PCC1)** is an oligomeric proanthocyanidin flavonoid shown to have senotherapeutic activity — selective senolysis of senescent cells and extension of healthspan and lifespan in mice[11, 28, 71].

## Relevance to Apigenin Study

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> PCC1 served as a positive **senolytic** control (50 μM) in the natural-product library screen that identified [[Apigenin]] as a senomorphic. Unlike apigenin, PCC1 fell in the senolytic (selective-killing) region of the screen, illustrating the distinction between senolytic and [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] classes of flavonoids.
