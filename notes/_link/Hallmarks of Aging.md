# Hallmarks of Aging

The **hallmarks of aging** are a conceptual framework, introduced by López-Otín and colleagues (2013, updated 2023), that organizes the biology of aging into a set of interconnected molecular and cellular processes. Each hallmark should (1) manifest during normal aging, (2) accelerate aging when experimentally aggravated, and (3) slow aging when therapeutically targeted.

## The Twelve Hallmarks (2023 update)

- **Primary (causes of damage):** [[Genomic Instability]], [[Telomere Attrition]], [[Epigenetic Alterations]], [[Proteostasis|loss of proteostasis]], and disabled macroautophagy.
- **Antagonistic (responses to damage):** deregulated nutrient sensing, [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]], and [[Cellular Senescence]].
- **Integrative (culprits of phenotype):** [[Stem Cell Exhaustion]], altered intercellular communication ([[Inflammaging]]), dysbiosis of the [[Gut Microbiome]], and chronic inflammation.

## Interconnections & Therapeutic Relevance

> [!important] Networked, not independent
> The hallmarks are deeply interdependent: for example, [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]] fuels [[Oxidative Stress]] and [[Genomic Instability]], while [[Cellular Senescence]] amplifies [[Inflammaging]] via the SASP. Interventions such as [[Caloric Restriction]], [[Rapamycin]] (mTOR inhibition), NAD+ precursors, and [[Senolytics]] each act on one or more hallmarks, supporting the [[Geroscience]] hypothesis that targeting aging biology can delay multiple [[Age-Related Diseases]] simultaneously.

## Connections

  - [[Genomic Instability]]: Primary hallmark — accumulation of DNA damage.
  - [[Telomere Attrition]]: Progressive telomere shortening with division.
  - [[Epigenetic Alterations]]: Drift in DNA/histone modification patterns.
  - [[Cellular Senescence]]: Growth arrest with pro-inflammatory secretome.
  - [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]]: Declining bioenergetic and redox function.
  - [[Stem Cell Exhaustion]]: Loss of regenerative capacity.
  - [[Proteostasis]]: Failure of protein quality control.
  - [[Geroscience]]: Field built on targeting these hallmarks.
  - [[Longevity]]: Outcome improved by hallmark-directed interventions.
