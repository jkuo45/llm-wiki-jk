# SUV39H1

SUV39H1 (suppressor of variegation 3-9 homolog 1), also known as KMT1A, is a histone methyltransferase that specifically trimethylates histone H3 lysine 9 ([[H3K9me3]]), a canonical heterochromatin mark. In senescence, SUV39H1 expression falls while the opposing H3K9 demethylase [[KDM4]] rises; this shift diminishes H3K9 methylation at SASP loci and supports enhanced SASP expression.

## Overview

SUV39H1 is the founding member of the SET-domain H3K9 methyltransferase family. It generates H3K9me3, which recruits heterochromatin protein 1 ([[HP1alpha]]) to nucleate facultative and constitutive heterochromatin. SUV39H1 acts in pericentric regions and at silenced loci to maintain transcriptional repression and genome stability.

## Structure & Function

The catalytic SET domain transfers methyl groups from SAM to H3K9. H3K9me3 is "read" by chromodomain proteins (HP1α/β/γ), driving chromatin compaction and the spreading of repressed domains. SUV39H1 activity is reinforced by a positive-feedback loop: H3K9me3 recruits HP1, which in turn helps recruit SUV39H1 to methylate neighboring nucleosomes.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

The review places SUV39H1 on the H3K9 methylation axis governing SASP. Loss of H3K9me2/3 around the major SASP genes [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]] correlates with their induction (ref. 67). In senescent cells, SUV39H1 protein is decreased while KDM4 is increased, yielding an overall diminished H3K9 methylation status and enhanced SASP expression (refs. 68, 69). Thus the SUV39H1↓ / KDM4↑ balance tips repressive heterochromatin toward the open, transcriptionally competent state required at SASP loci. This mirrors the H3K27 axis ([[EZH2]] vs [[JMJD3]]) and the H3K4 axis ([[MLL1]]) as a coordinated relaxation of repressive marks during senescence.

## Mechanistic Details

- H3K9me2/3 loss at IL-6/IL-8 promoters removes a brake on SASP transcription, analogous to H3K27me3 loss.
- The SUV39H1–KDM4 antagonism is part of a broader "loss of repressive chromatin" program in senescence that also includes heterochromatin decompaction ([[Senescence-Associated Distension of Satellites|SADS]]) and SAHF dynamics.
- Diminished H3K9 methylation facilitates enhancer–promoter contacts and 3D-genome reorganization that drive SASP.

## Therapeutic Relevance

Pharmacologic inhibition of H3K9 methylation (e.g., SUV39H1/SETDB1 inhibitors such as chaetocin) is explored in oncology. Conversely, restoring SUV39H1 activity could be a [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] strategy to re-impose H3K9 repression at SASP loci. The KDM4 inhibitor TACH101 is in a Phase 1 trial for advanced cancer (NCT05076552) and is noted in the review as a candidate senomorphic repurposing target.
