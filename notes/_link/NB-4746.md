# NB-4746

**NB-4746** is a brain-penetrant, clinical-stage **[[SARM1]] inhibitor** developed by Nura Bio. It is among the most advanced SARM1-directed therapeutics in clinical development.

## Mechanism

NB-4746 blocks the [[SARM1]] TIR-domain [[NAD+]] glycohydrolase, preventing the axonal NAD⁺ catastrophe that drives Wallerian degeneration. Its brain-penetrant profile distinguishes it from earlier peripherally-restricted candidates, enabling central nervous system indications.

## Clinical Status

- Completed **Phase 1** in healthy volunteers (2024) with targeted plasma and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) exposure and no serious adverse events.
- Advanced to **Phase 1b/2** in 2025.
- Primary indications: chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy (CIPN), traumatic brain injury ([[Traumatic Brain Injury]]), and chronic peripheral neuropathy.
- Therapeutic hypothesis: pharmacologically blocking SARM1 during acute injury windows halts axonal degeneration before it becomes irreversible, permitting regeneration and functional recovery.
