# CK2

**Casein kinase 2 (CK2)** is a ubiquitous and pleiotropic serine/threonine protein kinase. It is typically found as a tetramer of two catalytic (α or α') and two regulatory (β) subunits.

## Role in Senescence
Reduced levels of CK2 in [[Senescent Cells]] promote the activation of [[NFKB|NF-κB]], thereby contributing to the induction of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].

## Function
CK2 phosphorylates a wide range of substrates involved in cell cycle control, apoptosis, and signal transduction. Its regulation of the IKK complex and IκB inhibitors makes it an important modulator of inflammatory signaling.
