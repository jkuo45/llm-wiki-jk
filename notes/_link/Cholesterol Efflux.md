# Cholesterol Efflux

**Cholesterol efflux** is the process by which cells (especially [[Macrophages]]) export excess free [[Cholesterol]] to extracellular lipid acceptors, primarily [[Apolipoprotein A1|apolipoprotein A-I (apoA-1)]] and [[HDL]]. It is the first and rate-limiting step of [[Reverse Cholesterol Transport]] and a critical protective mechanism against [[Atherosclerosis]].

## Pathways

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - berrougui2009_resveratrol_as_atheroprotective_compound|Berrougui et al., Atherosclerosis 2009]]
> Free cholesterol efflux occurs by three known pathways: (1) aqueous diffusion — desorption of FC molecules from the donor lipid-water interface and diffusion through the aqueous phase to an acceptor; (2) [[SR-BI]]-mediated bidirectional FC flux depending on the cholesterol gradient; (3) [[ABCA1]]-mediated transfer of cholesterol and phospholipids to lipid-poor apoA-1.

1. **Aqueous diffusion** — Passive desorption of free cholesterol from the cell membrane through the aqueous phase to an acceptor
2. **[[SR-BI]]-mediated flux** — Bidirectional transporter; net movement depends on the cholesterol gradient
3. **[[ABCA1]]-mediated efflux** — Active transfer of cholesterol and phospholipids to lipid-poor [[Apolipoprotein A1|apoA-1]]; initiates HDL biogenesis
4. **[[ABCG1]]-mediated efflux** — Transfer of cholesterol to mature [[HDL]] particles

## Regulation

- **[[Liver X Receptor|LXR]]**: Master transcriptional activator of both ABCA1 and ABCG1
- **[[Resveratrol]]**: Upregulates ABCA1 expression; enhances apoA-1-mediated cholesterol efflux in a dose-dependent manner
- **[[Oxidative Stress]]**: Impairs cholesterol efflux by reducing ABCA1, LXR, and PPAR expression; [[Resveratrol]] restores efflux capacity under oxidative stress

## Measurement

- Cholesterol efflux is measured as the percentage of radiolabeled cholesterol released from cells to acceptors:
  - % efflux = (cpm in medium / (cpm in cell + cpm in medium)) × 100

## Clinical Relevance

- Impaired cholesterol efflux contributes to foam cell formation and [[Atherosclerosis]]
- Enhancing cholesterol efflux is a therapeutic strategy for cardiovascular disease
- [[Resveratrol]] enhances cholesterol efflux via ABCA1 upregulation and protects HDL functionality
