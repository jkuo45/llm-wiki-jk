# N-Formylkynurenine

**N-Formylkynurenine** (NFK) is the immediate enzymatic product of [[IDO1]] and [[TDO]] activity on [[Tryptophan]]. It is formed through oxidative cleavage of the indole ring of tryptophan at the 2,3-position, in an O₂-dependent reaction catalyzed by the heme iron center.

## Metabolic Fate

N-Formylkynurenine is rapidly converted to [[Kynurenine]] by **kynurenine formamidase** (also known as arylformamidase), which removes the formyl group. This step is not rate-limiting and occurs quickly in most tissues. NFK itself does not accumulate significantly under normal physiological conditions.

## Role in Oxidative Stress

N-Formylkynurenine can also be formed non-enzymatically by [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]]-mediated oxidation of tryptophan in proteins. In this context, NFK is a marker of protein [[Oxidation|oxidative damage]], particularly relevant in [[Oxidative Stress|oxidative stress]] conditions such as aging, [[Inflammation]], and [[Cataracts]].
