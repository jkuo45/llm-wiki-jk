# IL-4

**Interleukin-4 (IL-4)** is a pleiotropic cytokine produced primarily by [[Th2]] cells, mast cells, and basophils. It is the defining cytokine of type 2 immune responses and plays essential roles in allergic immunity, helminth defense, and immunoglobulin class switching. IL-4 shares significant functional overlap with [[IL-13]], as both cytokines signal through **IL-4Rα** and activate **STAT6**.

## Structure & Receptor

IL-4 is a four-helix bundle cytokine that signals through two receptor complexes:
- **Type I receptor**: IL-4Rα/γc (common gamma chain) — expressed on hematopoietic cells. Mediates T cell differentiation and proliferation.
- **Type II receptor**: IL-4Rα/IL-13Rα1 — expressed on both hematopoietic and non-hematopoietic cells. Shared with [[IL-13]]; mediates mucus production and fibrosis.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

IL-4 binding to IL-4Rα activates **JAK1** (associated with IL-4Rα) and **JAK3** (associated with γc), leading to phosphorylation of **STAT6**. STAT6 drives transcription of genes defining the type 2 immune program, including *GATA3*, *CCL17*, *CCL22*, and *MUC5AC*. IL-4 also activates **PI3K/Akt**, **IRS-1/2**, and **MAPK** pathways, promoting cell survival and proliferation.

### Key Downstream Effects

- Drives **IgE class switching** in B cells — the hallmark of allergic immune responses.
- Promotes **Th2 differentiation** by inducing **GATA3** expression and suppressing [[Th1]] development.
- Induces **alternative macrophage activation** (M2 polarization) via [[STAT6]] and [[PPARγ]].
- Inhibits classical macrophage activation (M1) and pro-inflammatory cytokine production.
- Enhances expression of vascular cell adhesion molecules (VCAM-1) promoting eosinophil recruitment.

## Biological Functions

### Humoral Immunity & IgE Production
IL-4 is indispensable for IgE class switching in B cells. IL-4-deficient mice fail to produce IgE and have impaired anti-helminth immune responses.

### Th2 Polarization
IL-4 acts in an autocrine and paracrine manner to promote and maintain the [[Th2]] phenotype by inducing GATA3 and suppressing [[T-bet]] and [[Th1]] differentiation.

### Alternative Macrophage Activation
IL-4 drives M2 macrophage polarization, which is important for wound healing, tissue repair, and anti-helminth defense, but can contribute to fibrosis and allergic pathology.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- **Asthma**: IL-4 drives IgE production, eosinophilic inflammation, and airway remodeling. **Dupilumab** (anti-IL-4Rα) blocks IL-4 and [[IL-13]] signaling and is approved for moderate-to-severe asthma, atopic dermatitis, and other type 2 inflammatory diseases.
- **Atopic Dermatitis**: IL-4 maintains the Th2-polarized immune milieu in skin lesions.
- **Allergic Rhinitis**: IL-4-mediated IgE production and eosinophil recruitment drive nasal allergy symptoms.
- **Helminth Infections**: IL-4 is critical for protective immunity against parasitic worms.
- **Cancer**: IL-4 can promote M2 tumor-associated macrophage polarization, contributing to immunosuppressive tumor microenvironments.
