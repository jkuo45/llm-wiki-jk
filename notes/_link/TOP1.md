# TOP1 (Topoisomerase 1)

TOP1 (Topoisomerase 1) is an enzyme that relieves DNA supercoiling. Its cleavage complexes (TOP1cc) have crucial roles in assisting [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS]] recognition of and binding to cytoplasmic chromatin fragments (CCFs), which is essential for downstream SASP regulation.

## Mechanism of Action

- TOP1 relieves DNA supercoiling during transcription and replication
- TOP1cc (cleavage complexes) assist cGAS recognition of cytoplasmic DNA
- TOP1cc are required for cGAS binding to CCFs
- TOP1cc link DNA damage to SASP through cGAS–STING

## Role in Senescence

- TOP1cc are essential for cGAS–STING activation
- They link DNA damage to SASP regulation
- TOP1cc may be a target for modulating SASP

## Clinical Relevance

- TOP1 is a target for cancer therapy (topotecan, irinotecan)
- TOP1 inhibitors are used in cancer treatment
- TOP1 may be a target for modulating SASP
