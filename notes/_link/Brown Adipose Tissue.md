# Brown Adipose Tissue

**Brown adipose tissue ([[Brown Adipose Tissue|BAT]], "brown fat")** is a specialized thermogenic fat distinguished from white adipose by its abundant [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]] (giving the brown color) and multilocular lipid droplets. Its defining feature is the uncoupling protein [[UCP1]], which dissipates the proton gradient of the [[Electron Transport Chain|electron transport chain]] as heat rather than ATP.

## Activation and Cold Exposure

Cold plunges or cold showers (1–3 min) activate BAT and induce [[UCP1|uncoupling protein 1 (UCP1)]], increasing mitochondrial density and non-shivering thermogenesis. This controlled cold stress is a canonical mitohormetic stimulus: a transient stressor that, followed by recovery, enhances mitochondrial quality and metabolic flexibility.

## Physiological Role

BAT is prominent in newborns and retained in adult humans at cervical, supraclavicular, and para spinal depots. Beyond thermoregulation, BAT improves glucose and lipid handling and contributes to systemic energy expenditure.
