# Promethazine

[[Promethazine]] (PMZ, Phenergan) is a phenothiazine derivative with a side chain on the position-10 nitrogen. Clinically it is used as an H1-antihistamine, antiemetic, and sedative. In the methylene blue derivative study it belonged to the "10-nitrogen side-chain substituted" group and, like [[Chlorpromazine]], lacked methylene blue-like mitochondrial protection [^1].

## Structure & Chemistry

Promethazine substitutes the 10-nitrogen with a 2-(dimethylamino)propyl side chain (structurally analogous to chlorpromazine's substitution). This blocks the free heterocyclic nitrogen required for alternative electron transfer and yields a strongly positive redox potential (E<sub>1/2</sub> ≈ 1.069 V vs NHE), so it cannot enhance oxygen consumption [^1].

## Mechanism of Action

### Antihistamine / antiemetic

Promethazine is a potent H1-receptor antagonist with antimuscarinic and sedative properties, used for allergy, nausea, and motion sickness.

### Loss of mitochondrial electron-shunt activity

In the MB-derivative study, promethazine:

- Did **not** increase [[Cytochrome c]] reduction in the [[Respiratory Chain Complex I|Complex I]]→[[Complex III]] assay.
- Did **not** increase cellular oxygen consumption rate (OCR) or affect ECAR.
- In the cell-free mitochondria-lysate oxidation assay, increased DCF fluorescence (oxidative) at 100 nM–10 µM both with and without mitochondria/[[NADH]] [^1].

## Neuroprotection (structure–activity context)

Against glutamate insult in HT-22 cells, promethazine showed the second-weakest protection after [[Neutral Red]]:

- Viability EC50 ≈ **1067 nM**
- ROS EC50 ≈ **9687 nM**
- Mitochondrial membrane potential EC50 ≈ **213.7 nM**

The ~1000-fold potency loss versus [[Methylene blue]]/[[Toluidine Blue O]] reflects the 10-nitrogen side chain. Promethazine retained protection in the [[Rotenone]] and [[Iodoacetic Acid|IAA]] models but with lower efficacy than the naked-nucleus and amine-substituted groups, and (like all study compounds) gave no protection against direct H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub> from [[Glucose Oxidase]] [^1].

> [!info] Same liability as chlorpromazine
> Promethazine confirms the study's central rule: a side chain on the phenothiazine 10-nitrogen eliminates the alternative electron-transfer phenotype and collapses neuroprotective potency, regardless of the clinical (antihistamine vs antipsychotic) indication [^1].
