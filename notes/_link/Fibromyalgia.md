**Fibromyalgia** is a chronic disorder of unknown etiology characterized by widespread musculoskeletal pain, [[Fatigue]], [[Sleep Quality|sleep disturbance]], [[Anxiety]], [[Depression]], and [[Cognitive Dysfunction|cognitive dysfunction]] ("fibro fog"). It is classified by the [[American College of Rheumatology (ACR)|American College of Rheumatology]] based on the Widespread Pain Index (WPI) and Symptom Severity (SS) scale.

## Etiology & Pathophysiology

The precise mechanisms remain incompletely understood, but converging evidence implicates:

- **Central sensitization** — aberrant [[Pain Processing|pain processing]] in the [[Central Nervous System|central nervous system]], with amplified sensory signaling and reduced descending inhibitory control.
- **Neuroendocrine dysregulation** — altered [[Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Adrenal (HPA) Axis|HPA axis]] activity, [[Growth Hormone|growth hormone]] deficiency, and autonomic nervous system imbalance.
- **Mitochondrial dysfunction & bioenergetic deficits** — reduced [[Phosphocreatine (PCr)|PCr]] and [[ATP]] levels in muscle and brain have been reported \[[^307]\], providing a mechanistic rationale for [[Creatine Supplementation|creatine supplementation]].
- **Low-grade inflammation** — elevated [[Pro-inflammatory Cytokines|pro-inflammatory cytokines]] (e.g., [[IL-6]], [[TNFα]]) and activation of [[Toll-like Receptors (TLR)|toll-like receptor]] pathways.
- **Oxidative stress** — increased [[Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS)|reactive oxygen species]] and reduced [[Antioxidant]] capacity.

## Clinical Features

- Chronic widespread pain (≥3 months) present in all four body quadrants
- Severe fatigue unrelieved by rest
- Non-restorative sleep / [[Sleep Disorders|sleep disorders]]
- Cognitive symptoms ("fibro fog"): impaired attention, memory, executive function
- Frequently comorbid with [[Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS)|chronic fatigue syndrome]], [[Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)|irritable bowel syndrome]], [[Migraine]], [[Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (TMJ)|temporomandibular joint disorder]], and mood disorders

## Creatine & Fibromyalgia

Several lines of evidence connect creatine metabolism to fibromyalgia pathophysiology:

- [[Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy|MRS]] studies have found reduced [[Creatine]] and [[Phosphocreatine (PCr)|PCr]] levels in the [[Brain]] and [[Skeletal Muscle|skeletal muscle]] of fibromyalgia patients.
- Clinical trials of [[Creatine Supplementation|creatine supplementation]] (3–20 g/day) in fibromyalgia patients have shown improvements in pain measures, [[Quality of Life|quality of life]], sleep quality, and muscle function \[[^162],[^309],[^310]\].
- Creatine's effects are hypothesized to arise from its role in [[Cellular Bioenergetics|cellular bioenergetics]] (ATP buffering via the [[Creatine Phosphate Shuttle|creatine phosphate shuttle]]), [[Antioxidant]] activity, and [[Anti-inflammatory|anti-inflammatory]] modulation of [[Cytokines|cytokines]].
