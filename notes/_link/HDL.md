# HDL

**High-density lipoprotein (HDL)** is an anti-atherogenic lipoprotein particle that plays a central role in [[Reverse Cholesterol Transport]] — the process of moving excess [[Cholesterol]] from peripheral tissues to the liver. HDL also protects [[LDL]] against oxidation and carries the antioxidant enzyme [[PON1]].

## Structure and Subfractions

- HDL is composed of lipids, [[Apolipoprotein A1|apoA-1]], and associated enzymes including [[PON1]]
- **[[HDL3]]** — smaller, denser subfraction; major mediator of cholesterol efflux
- **HDL2** — larger, lighter subfraction

## Anti-Atherogenic Functions

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - berrougui2009_resveratrol_as_atheroprotective_compound|Berrougui et al., Atherosclerosis 2009]]
> HDL is considered anti-atherogenic and plays a key role in protecting LDL against oxidation and maintaining cholesterol homeostasis by reverse cholesterol transport. HDL mediates cholesterol efflux from macrophages via ABCA1 and ABCG1. Oxidation of HDL3 alters phospholipid fluidity, modifies apoprotein structure, and decreases PON1 activity — all of which impair cholesterol efflux capacity.

- **Cholesterol efflux**: HDL removes excess cholesterol from [[Macrophages]] and other peripheral cells via [[ABCA1]] and [[ABCG1]]
- **LDL protection**: HDL protects [[LDL]] against oxidation
- **PON1 carrier**: HDL carries [[PON1]], which contributes to its antioxidant capacity

## Oxidation and Impairment

- HDL can be oxidized in vivo, causing loss of its anti-atherogenic properties
- Cu-induced oxidation of HDL3 decreases its capacity to mediate cholesterol efflux
- [[Oxidative Stress]] (Fe/ascorbate) impairs HDL-mediated cholesterol efflux from macrophages
- [[Resveratrol]] protects HDL3 from copper oxidation and preserves its cholesterol efflux capacity

## Clinical Relevance

- Elevated HDL levels are associated with reduced [[Atherosclerosis]] and [[Cardiovascular Disease]] risk
- Non-alcoholized red wine increases plasma HDL in rats
- HDL-based therapeutic strategies target [[ABCA1]], [[ABCG1]], and [[PON1]]
