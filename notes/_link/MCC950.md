## Overview

[[MCC950]] (also known as CRID3 or NLRP3-IN-1) is a potent, selective, and cell-active inhibitor of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]]. It prevents NLRP3 activation and the subsequent assembly of the inflammasome complex, suppressing cleavage of pro-IL-1β and pro-IL-18.

## Biological Role / Mechanism

MCC950 binds to the NACHT domain of NLRP3, blocking ATP hydrolysis and conformational changes required for inflammasome assembly and ASC speck formation. This specifically inhibits caspase-1 activation downstream of NLRP3, without affecting [[NLRC4]], [[AIM2]], or other inflammasomes. The result is reduced secretion of [[IL-1β]] and [[IL-18]] and pyroptosis.

## Physiological / Pathological Relevance

By dampening NLRP3-driven inflammation, MCC950 is investigated for cryopyrin-associated periodic syndromes, atherosclerosis, neurodegeneration, and metabolic inflammation. Chronic NLRP3 activation is implicated in inflammaging and the [[SASP]]. In the wiki, MCC950 is used as a pharmacological tool to probe NLRP3 contributions to [[Inflammation]] in sirtuin/adrenochrome research strategies.
