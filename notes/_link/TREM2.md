# TREM2

## Overview

[[TREM2]] (triggering receptor expressed on myeloid cells 2) is an immunoglobulin-superfamily receptor expressed on [[Microglia]] and other myeloid cells. It binds ApoE-containing [[Lipid Metabolism]] ligands and serves as a master regulator of microglial state, survival, and response to pathology. Beyond its established roles in [[Alzheimer's Disease|AD]], TREM2 has emerged as a critical modulator of the [[cGAS-STING Pathway]] and [[Type I Interferon|IFN-I]] signaling, positioning it at the intersection of genetic risk, glial activation, and [[Neuroinflammation]].

## Genetic Risk and Synergy with APOE4

The TREM2 R47H variant is a well-established AD risk allele. Strikingly, [[TREM2]] R47H synergizes with the major risk allele [[APOE4]] to amplify cGAS-STING/IFN-I signaling, driving a senescence-like microglial state characterized by mitochondrial stress, [[Cell Cycle|cell cycle]] arrest, and a [[SASP|senescence-associated secretory phenotype]] ([40](#B40)). This genetic interaction helps explain why carriers of both alleles exhibit accelerated [[Neurodegeneration]] and heightened inflammatory pathology across [[Proteinopathy|proteinopathies]].

## TREM2 Activity Independently Drives cGAS/ISG Programs

Notably, TREM2 signaling activity — independent of disease-associated variants — can itself induce [[Interferon-Stimulated Genes|ISGs]] and upregulate [[cGAS]] expression, doing so separately from the canonical [[Disease-Associated Microglia|DAM]] program ([17](#B17)). Conversely, TREM2 haploinsufficiency intensifies [[Astrocyte Senescence|astrocyte senescence]], indicating that TREM2 tone bidirectionally shapes both microglial and astrocytic responses. These findings suggest that TREM2 functions as a rheostat for cGAS-STING engagement in glial cells.

## Therapeutic Relevance

Because TREM2 both gates inflammatory signaling and supports microglial homeostasis, it is a prime target for immunomodulatory strategies. Understanding how TREM2 activity maps onto cGAS-STING activation may enable therapies that dampen maladaptive IFN-I programs without abolishing protective microglial functions, contrasting with the resilience conferred by [[APOE3 Christchurch]].
