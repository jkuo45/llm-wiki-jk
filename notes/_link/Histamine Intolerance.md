# Histamine Intolerance

[[Histamine Intolerance]] arises when histamine intake or endogenous release exceeds the body's degradative capacity, typically due to reduced [[Diamine Oxidase]] (DAO) activity. Accumulated [[Histamine]] produces allergic-like symptoms.

## Triggers and Features

- **Dietary:** aged cheeses, cured/fermented meats, alcohol, fermented foods.
- **Drug-induced:** [[Aminoguanidine]] (oral) potently inhibits gut DAO → acute histamine intolerance.
- **Symptoms:** headache, flushing, urticaria/hives, tachycardia, abdominal pain, nausea, diarrhea.

> [!warning] Oral aminoguanidine
> Oral [[Aminoguanidine]] blocks intestinal DAO; consuming normal histamine-rich foods while taking it can trigger severe flushing, hives, and rapid heart rate.
