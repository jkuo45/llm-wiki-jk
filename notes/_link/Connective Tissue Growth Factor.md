## Overview

Connective Tissue Growth Factor (CTGF, also known as CCN2) is a matricellular protein belonging to the CCN family of secreted proteins. It is a key component of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] and plays important roles in [[Fibrosis|fibrosis]], wound healing, and [[Cancer|tumor progression]].

## Structure & Domains

- **Molecular weight**: ~36-38 kDa
- **CCN family**: Contains four functional domains:
  - Insulin-like growth factor-binding domain (IGFBP)
  - Von Willebrand factor type C module (VWC)
  - Thrombospondin type-1 repeat (TSP1)
  - C-terminal cysteine knot domain (CT)
- **Secreted protein**: Released into the extracellular matrix
- **Heparin-binding**: Interacts with [[Fibronectin]] and other ECM components

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

### Downstream Signaling
- **Integrin binding**: Activates integrin αvβ3, α5β1, and α6β4 signaling
- **TGF-β synergy**: Cooperates with [[TGF-β]] to enhance fibrotic responses
- **Smad activation**: Enhances TGF-β/Smad signaling
- **MAPK/ERK**: Activates cell proliferation and migration pathways
- **PI3K/Akt**: Promotes cell survival and anti-apoptotic signaling

### Regulation
- **Transcriptional control**: Induced by [[TGF-β]], [[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], and mechanical stress
- **SASP component**: Secreted by [[Senescent Cells|senescent cells]] in response to DNA damage
- **Epigenetic regulation**: Controlled by histone modifications and DNA methylation

## Physiological Function

### Wound Healing & Tissue Repair
- Promotes [[Fibroblasts|fibroblast]] proliferation and migration
- Stimulates [[Fibronectin]] and collagen synthesis
- Regulates angiogenesis and tissue remodeling

### Development
- Essential for skeletal development and chondrogenesis
- Plays roles in cardiovascular and neural development

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

### Fibrotic Diseases
- **Pulmonary fibrosis**: Elevated CTGF drives [[Fibrosis|fibrotic]] remodeling in [[Lung Cancer|lung]] disease
- **Hepatic fibrosis**: Promotes hepatic stellate cell activation and liver fibrosis
- **Renal fibrosis**: Contributes to chronic kidney disease progression

### Cancer
- **Prostate cancer**: CTGF secreted by senescent fibroblasts promotes [[Prostate Cancer|prostate tumor]] progression
- **Breast cancer**: Enhances [[Breast Cancer|breast cancer]] cell invasion and metastasis
- **Pancreatic cancer**: Promotes [[Pancreatic Cancer|pancreatic cancer]] progression and desmoplasia
- **Tumor microenvironment**: Remodels [[Tumor Microenvironment|tumor microenvironment]] to support cancer growth

### SASP-Mediated Cancer Promotion
- CTGF is a key SASP factor that promotes [[Paracrine Senescence|paracrine]] tumor progression
- Secreted by [[Senescent Cells|senescent fibroblasts]] in the tumor microenvironment
- Stimulates cancer cell proliferation, invasion, and angiogenesis
- Promotes [[Metastasis|metastasis]] through ECM remodeling

## Key Studies

- **SASP characterization**: Identified as a major SASP factor in senescent fibroblasts (Coppé et al., 2010)
- **Prostate cancer**: CTGF from senescent stroma promotes prostate tumor progression (Fujita et al., 2010)
- **Therapeutic targeting**: CTGF-neutralizing antibodies reduce fibrosis and tumor growth in preclinical models
