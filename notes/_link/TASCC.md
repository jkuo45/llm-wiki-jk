# TASCC

The **TOR-autophagy spatial coupling compartment (TASCC)** is a specialized signaling hub that forms in [[Senescent Cells]]. It is characterized by the colocalization of [[mTORC1|mTOR]] and [[Autophagy|autophagy]] machinery on the trans-Golgi network.

## Role in SASP
The TASCC couples autophagy-derived amino acids to high secretory demand in senescent cells:
- It allows mTOR to promote the translation of key [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] factors, such as [[IL-1α]].
- This spatial organization is essential for the efficient production and secretion of the SASP.

## Significance
The TASCC represents a metabolic rewiring in senescent cells that links their high [[Autophagy|autophagic]] activity to their pro-inflammatory secretory phenotype.
