# Homocysteine

**Biochemical properties:** Homocysteine is an endogenous metabolite involved in cellular energy metabolism, redox reactions, or biosynthetic pathways. Its cellular concentration reflects metabolic flux and energy status.

**Metabolic role:** Homocysteine participates as a substrate, intermediate, or product in key metabolic pathways including glycolysis, the TCA cycle, oxidative phosphorylation, or one-carbon metabolism.

**Regulation:** Intracellular levels of Homocysteine are maintained through biosynthesis, catabolism, and transport mechanisms. Its concentration serves as a metabolic signal that regulates enzyme activity and gene expression.

**Clinical significance:** Altered levels of Homocysteine are observed in metabolic disorders, mitochondrial diseases, and nutritional deficiencies. Supplementation or pharmacological modulation of this metabolite has therapeutic potential.
