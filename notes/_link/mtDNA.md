# mtDNA

**Mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA)** is the genetic material found in mitochondria. While primarily involved in编码 components of the electron transport chain, mtDNA can also act as a damage-associated molecular pattern (DAMP) when released into the cytosol.

## Release Mechanisms
mtDNA release can occur through:
- **Major Mitochondrial Outer Membrane Permeabilization (MOMP)**: Typically associated with [[Apoptosis]].
- **Minority MOMP (miMOMP)**: Sublethal permeabilization by [[BAX]]/[[BAK]] that releases mtDNA without triggering cell death.

## Role in Senescence
In [[Senescent Cells]], mitochondrial dysfunction and miMOMP lead to the accumulation of cytosolic mtDNA. This activates the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS-STING]] innate immune sensor, driving the production of type I interferons and pro-inflammatory cytokines, which are key components of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].

## Clinical Relevance
Cytosolic mtDNA and the resulting [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS-STING]] activation are major drivers of sterile [[Inflammaging]] and age-related diseases.
