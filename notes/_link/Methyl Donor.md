# Methyl Donor

Methyl donors are compounds that provide a labile methyl group (−CH₃) for methylation reactions in [[One-Carbon Metabolism]]. The principal methyl donor is [[SAMe]] (S-adenosylmethionine), which transfers its methyl group to substrates including [[DNA]], [[RNA]], [[Histone]]s, proteins, phospholipids, and neurotransmitters. Other methyl donors include [[Methylfolate]] (5-methyltetrahydrofolate) and [[Betaine]] (trimethylglycine), which regenerate methionine from [[Homocysteine]] via the [[Methylation Cycle]]. Methyl donors are essential for [[Epigenetic]] regulation, [[Detoxification]], [[Neurotransmitter Synthesis]], and [[Phospholipid Metabolism]]. Dietary sources include [[Folate]], [[Choline]], [[Betaine]], [[Methionine]], and [[Vitamin B12]].
