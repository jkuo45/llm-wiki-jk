# Tricyclic Antidepressant

[[Tricyclic Antidepressant]]s ([[TCA]]s) are older antidepressants that block monoamine reuptake (including [[Serotonin]] and [[Norepinephrine]]). Like [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors]], they are contraindicated with [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]]s (including [[Methylene blue]]) due to [[Serotonin Syndrome]] risk.
