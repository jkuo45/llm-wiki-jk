# H3K4me1

H3K4me1 is the monomethylation of lysine 4 on histone H3, the canonical mark of enhancers (both active and "primed"). In senescence, H3K4me1 decorates the de novo activated enhancers and [[Super-enhancer|super-enhancers]] that drive [[SASP]] expression, as noted in the npj Aging review (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2).

## Definition

H3K4me1 is deposited by the MLL3/MLL4 (KMT2C/D) methyltransferase complexes at enhancers and is typically found together with H3K27ac at active enhancers, or with H3K27me3 at "poised" Polycomb enhancers. It functionally distinguishes enhancers (H3K4me1) from promoters (H3K4me3).

## Writer, Reader, Eraser

- **Writer:** MLL3/MLL4 (KMT2C/D) complexes monomethylate H3K4 at enhancers.
- **Reader:** PHD-finger and TFIID subunits recognize H3K4me1; it cooperates with H3K27ac to define active enhancers.
- **Eraser:** KDM1A (LSD1) and KDM5 family demethylases remove H3K4 methylation.

## Role in SASP and Senescence

Active enhancers that uphold an open chromatin state — and prevent repressive recruitment — are enriched in H3K4me1 and [[H3K27ac]], along with enhancer RNAs (Barral & Dejardin, 2023). In senescence, senescence-activated super-enhancers are enriched in both H3K4me1 and multiple acetyl marks (H3K27ac, [[H3K18ac]], [[H3K122ac]], [[H4K5ac]]) (Sen et al., 2019, PMID 30753809). The majority of enhancer activation in senescent cells occurs de novo from previously unmarked chromatin (Martinez-Zamudio et al., 2020, PMID 32439934). These H3K4me1-marked enhancers engage SASP promoters through [[Enhancer-Promoter Looping|enhancer-promoter looping]] within [[Topologically Associating Domain|TADs]].

## Mechanistic Details

H3K4me1 marks the enhancer landscape onto which the pioneer factor [[AP-1]] and readers such as [[BRD4]] act. Because most senescence chromatin transitions occur at enhancers rather than promoters, H3K4me1 is a primary sensor of the rewired senescence epigenome. Its co-occurrence with H3K27ac defines the active, SASP-driving enhancers.

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

Enhancer-mark readers (BRD4) and writers (p300) at H3K4me1/H3K27ac enhancers are actionable [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] targets (see [[KDM4]], [[DOT1L]]).
