# Ruxolitinib

Ruxolitinib is a JAK1/2 inhibitor that reduces SASP cytokines. It is a senomorphic therapy that targets the [[JAK]]–[[STAT3]] pathway to suppress pro-inflammatory SASP.

## Mechanism of Action

- Ruxolitinib inhibits JAK1 and JAK2 kinases
- This blocks the [[JAK]]–[[STAT3]] signaling pathway
- Ruxolitinib reduces SASP cytokine production
- It targets a key pathway for SASP regulation

## Role in SASP

- Ruxolitinib is a senomorphic therapy
- It reduces SASP cytokines through JAK-STAT inhibition
- Ruxolitinib may be beneficial in reducing age-related inflammation

## Clinical Relevance

- Ruxolitinib is FDA-approved for myelofibrosis and polycythemia vera
- Ruxolitinib is being tested for senolytic/senomorphic applications
- Ruxolitinib has anti-inflammatory properties
