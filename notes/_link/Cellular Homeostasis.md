# Cellular Homeostasis

Cellular homeostasis refers to the dynamic equilibrium maintained by cells through coordinated regulation of metabolism, signaling, gene expression, and organelle function. It encompasses the balance of energy production ([[ATP]]), redox state ([[Glutathione]]/[[NAD+]] ratio), ion gradients, proteostasis ([[Proteasome]]/[[Autophagy]]), and [[DNA Repair]]. Disruption of homeostatic mechanisms is a hallmark of [[Aging]] and contributes to [[Metabolic Syndrome]], [[Neurodegeneration]], and [[Cancer]]. Key regulatory hubs include [[AMPK]], [[mTORC1]], [[SIRT1]], and [[NRF2]], which sense nutrient/energy status and coordinate adaptive responses.
