# GAPDH

**Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase (GAPDH)** is a glycolytic enzyme that catalyzes the conversion of glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate to 1,3-bisphosphoglycerate. Beyond metabolism, it has roles in [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]], [[notes/_link/Transcription]], and [[Oxidative Stress|oxidative stress]] sensing.

## Connections

- [[Glycolysis]] — Core metabolic function
- [[Oxidative Stress]] — GAPDH is inactivated by oxidative modification
- [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]] — Nuclear translocation of GAPDH triggers cell death
