# IL-1 Receptor

The **interleukin-1 receptor type I (IL-1R1)** is a transmembrane receptor of the [[Toll-like Receptor|Toll-like receptor/IL-1 receptor (TLR/IL-1R) superfamily]] that mediates the pro-inflammatory effects of both [[IL-1α]] and [[IL-1β]]. Binding of IL-1 ligands to IL-1R1 recruits the accessory protein [[IL-1RAcP]], forming a heterodimeric signaling complex that activates [[MyD88]]-dependent downstream pathways.

## Structure

IL-1R1 is a type I transmembrane glycoprotein (~80 kDa) with three extracellular immunoglobulin-like (Ig) domains that form the ligand-binding pocket, a single transmembrane helix, and an intracellular Toll/IL-1 receptor (TIR) domain that mediates downstream signal transduction.

## Mechanism of Action

Upon binding [[IL-1α]] or [[IL-1β]], IL-1R1 undergoes conformational change and recruits the shared accessory protein [[IL-1RAcP]], forming a 1:1:1 heterotrimeric complex. The TIR domains of IL-1R1 and IL-1RAcP then recruit the adaptor protein [[MyD88]], which recruits IRAK4 and IRAK1. This triggers a phosphorylation cascade activating [[TRAF6]] and ultimately the [[IKK complex]], leading to [[NFKB|NF-κB]] nuclear translocation and transcription of pro-inflammatory genes including *IL1B*, [[TNFα]], [[IL-6]], [[COX-2]], and [[Adhesion Molecules]].

Simultaneously, [[MAPK]] cascades (ERK, JNK, p38) are activated via [[TRAF6]], leading to [[AP-1]] transcription factor activation and additional pro-inflammatory gene expression.

## Regulation

- **[[IL-1Ra]] (IL-1 Receptor Antagonist)** — Endogenous competitive antagonist that binds IL-1R1 without recruiting IL-1RAcP, blocking signaling. Recombinant form is [[Anakinra]].
- **[[IL-1R2]] (Type II Receptor)** — Decoy receptor that binds IL-1β but lacks a signaling TIR domain; acts as a sink for IL-1 ligands
- **Soluble IL-1R1 (sIL-1R1)** — Shed ectodomain that sequesters IL-1α/β in the extracellular space

## Therapeutic Targeting

IL-1R1 is the receptor through which all IL-1-targeting biologics exert their effects:
- [[Anakinra]] — Recombinant IL-1Ra that directly blocks IL-1R1
- [[Canakinumab]] — Anti-IL-1β mAb that prevents IL-1β from reaching IL-1R1
- [[Rilonacept]] — Soluble IL-1R1/IL-1RAcP fusion that intercepts IL-1 ligands before they reach IL-1R1

## Clinical Relevance

Dysregulated IL-1R1 signaling is implicated in:
- [[Inflammaging]] and age-related chronic inflammation
- [[Rheumatoid Arthritis]] and other autoimmune diseases
- [[Atherosclerosis]] and cardiovascular disease ([[CANTOS Trial]])
- Cryopyrin-associated periodic syndromes (CAPS)
- [[Gout]] — IL-1β-mediated crystal-induced inflammation
