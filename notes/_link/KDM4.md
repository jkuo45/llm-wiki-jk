# KDM4

KDM4 (Lysine demethylase 4; also JMJD2) is a family of JmjC-domain histone demethylases that remove di- and tri-methyl marks from histone H3 Lys9 (H3K9me3/me2) and H3K36. By erasing repressive H3K9me3 at lamina-associated heterochromatin, KDM4 opens chromatin at SASP loci and promotes expression of secreted factors in senescent stromal cells.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

The KDM4 family comprises KDM4A–KDM4E (JMJD2A–E). The JmjC catalytic domain uses Fe(II) and α-ketoglutarate to oxidatively demethylate H3K9me3/me2 (and H3K36me3/me2), requiring O₂ and generating succinate and formaldehyde. In senescence, KDM4A/KDM4B are induced downstream of [[p38 MAPK]] and pro-inflammatory signaling and relocalize away from constitutive heterochromatin toward SASP gene regulatory elements. Loss of H3K9me3 at these sites reduces linker-histone-mediated compaction and permits [[NF-κB]] and BRD4 access, converting a repressive mark into an active transcriptional state. KDM4 thus acts antagonistically to the Polycomb methyltransferase [[EZH2]] (which deposits H3K27me3) and to SETDB1/SUV39H (H3K9me3 writers).

## Role in Senescence / SASP

KDM4 enzymes are established epigenetic drivers of the SASP: genetic or pharmacological inhibition lowers IL-6, IL-8, and MMP secretion without necessarily abolishing growth arrest, marking KDM4 as a senomorphic target. Heterochromatin loss mediated by KDM4 also contributes to the global chromatin reorganization (SAHF formation versus decondensation) characteristic of senescent nuclei. Because KDM4 upregulation is pronounced in senescent stromal and pre-malignant cells, its demethylase activity links metabolic/oxidative state (α-KG/succinate ratio) to SASP intensity. KDM4 inhibitors (e.g., JIB-04, ML324, QC6352) are being explored both as anticancer agents and as SASP dampeners.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- KDM4 is amplified/overexpressed in several cancers (breast, lung, glioma), supporting targeting with selective inhibitors.
- Senomorphic suppression of KDM4 may reduce therapy-induced and age-related [[Inflammaging]].
- Caveat: KDM4 isoforms have partially redundant roles; isoform-selective inhibitors are still maturing.
