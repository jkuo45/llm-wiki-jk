# KDM4

KDM4 (Lysine demethylase 4) is a family of histone demethylases that reduce histone H3 Lys9 (H3K9) and H3K36 methylation, promoting the expression of SASP factors in senescent stromal cells.

## Mechanism of Action

- KDM4 proteins demethylate H3K9 and H3K36
- Reduced histone methylation promotes SASP gene expression
- KDM4 is upregulated in senescent stromal cells
- KDM4 links epigenetic regulation to SASP composition

## Role in SASP

- KDM4 is an epigenetic regulator of SASP
- It promotes SASP factor expression through histone demethylation
- KDM4 may be a target for selective SASP modulation

## Clinical Relevance

- KDM4 is a therapeutic target in cancer
- KDM4 inhibitors are being developed
- KDM4 may be a target for modulating SASP in aging
