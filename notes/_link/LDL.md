# LDL

**Low-density lipoprotein (LDL)** is a lipoprotein particle that transports cholesterol and triglycerides from the liver to peripheral tissues. Elevated LDL is a major risk factor for [[notes/_link/Atherosclerosis]] and [[notes/_link/Cardiovascular Disease]].
