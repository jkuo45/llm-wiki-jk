# PAI-1

**Plasminogen activator inhibitor-1 (PAI-1)**, also known as SERPINE1, is a serine protease inhibitor that is a key component of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].

## Role in SASP
PAI-1 is secreted by [[Senescent Cells]] and plays a role in:
- Inhibiting fibrinolysis by blocking tissue plasminogen activator (tPA) and urokinase plasminogen activator (uPA).
- Promoting [[Senescence]] in an autocrine manner.
- Contributing to extracellular matrix (ECM) remodeling and fibrosis.

## Clinical Relevance
Circulating PAI-1 levels are a biomarker of biological age and are elevated in various age-related diseases, including [[Obesity]], [[Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus]], and [[Cardiovascular Disease]].
