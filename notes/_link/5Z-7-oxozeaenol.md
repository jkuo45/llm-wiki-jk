# 5Z-7-oxozeaenol

**5Z-7-oxozeaenol** is a selective inhibitor of **TAK1** (MAP3K7), used to probe the ATM-TRAF6-TAK1 axis that orchestrates SASP development after DNA damage.

## Comparator to Apigenin

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> Apigenin's blockade of the [[Acute Stress-Associated Phenotype|ASAP]]→[[SASP|SASP]] transition is functionally reminiscent of 5Z-7-oxozeaenol, which diminishes the SASP by restraining [[TAK1]] activity in the ATM-TRAF6-TAK1 axis, subsequently suppressing [[p38 MAPK]] and [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]]/[[mTOR]] engagement that supports persistent SASP signaling[46].
