# PLA

**Poly(lactic acid) (PLA)** is a biodegradable, biocompatible thermoplastic polyester widely used in [[Nanoparticles|nanoparticle]] drug delivery. Fisetin-loaded PLA [[Nanoparticles|nanoparticles]] (spontaneous emulsification solvent diffusion; 226.85 nm; 90.35% encapsulation) increased AUC 2.32-fold and reduced tumor volume to 64.7% in a 4T1 breast cancer model (Feng et al., 2019).
