# Senescence-Associated Heterochromatin Foci (SAHF)

Senescence-Associated Heterochromatin Foci (SAHF) are distinct chromatin structures that suppress transcription and are a hallmark of senescent cells. They are formed through [[Retinoblastoma Protein|Rb]]-mediated processes and contribute to the stable proliferation arrest of senescent cells.

## Mechanism of Action

- SAHF are dense chromatin structures that silence gene expression
- They are formed through [[Retinoblastoma Protein|Rb]]-mediated processes
- SAHF suppress transcription of genes required for cell cycle progression
- SAHF are a hallmark of oncogene-induced senescence (OIS)

## Role in Senescence

- SAHF contribute to stable proliferation arrest
- They silence E2F target genes
- SAHF are a visual marker of senescent cells

## Clinical Relevance

- SAHF are used as a senescence biomarker
- SAHF formation is studied in cancer biology
- SAHF may be a target for modulating senescence
