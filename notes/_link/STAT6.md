# STAT6

**Signal Transducer and Activator of Transcription 6 (STAT6)** is a transcription factor that serves as the principal downstream mediator of [[IL-4]] and [[IL-13]] receptor signaling. It is essential for type 2 immune responses, including Th2 differentiation, alternative macrophage activation, and immunoglobulin class switching to IgE.

## Structure & Domains

STAT6 is a member of the STAT family of transcription factors. It contains an N-terminal domain, a coiled-coil domain, a DNA-binding domain, a linker domain, an SH2 domain (which recognizes phosphotyrosine motifs on activated receptors), and a transactivation domain. STAT6 is activated exclusively by **IL-4** and **IL-13** signaling — no other cytokines phosphorylate STAT6.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

IL-4 or [[IL-13]] binding to their respective receptor complexes (both containing **IL-4Rα**) activates receptor-associated kinases **JAK1** and **JAK3** (Type I) or **JAK1** and **TYK2** (Type II). These kinases phosphorylate a conserved tyrosine residue (Tyr641) in the STAT6 transactivation domain. Phosphorylated STAT6 dimerizes via reciprocal SH2–phosphotyrosine interactions, translocates to the nucleus, and binds GAS (gamma-activated sequence) elements in target gene promoters.

### Target Genes

- **GATA3** — Master transcription factor of the [[Th2]] lineage; STAT6 induces GATA3 expression to amplify and stabilize Th2 identity.
- **MUC5AC** — Mucin gene driving mucus hypersecretion in airway epithelium.
- **CCL17** (TARC) and **CCL22** (MDC) — Chemokines that recruit [[Th2]] cells and eosinophils.
- **CD23** (FcεRII) — Low-affinity IgE receptor on B cells.
- **IGF1** and **Arg1** (Arginase-1) — Markers and functional effectors of M2 macrophage polarization.
- **SOCS1** and **SOCS3** — Negative feedback regulators of JAK/STAT signaling.

## Biological Functions

### Th2 Differentiation
STAT6 is required for [[Th2]] lineage commitment. STAT6-deficient mice cannot generate Th2 cells, fail to produce [[IgE]], and are resistant to allergic airway inflammation. STAT6 induces [[GATA3]] expression, which then reinforces Th2 identity through an auto-regulatory loop.

### Alternative Macrophage Activation
STAT6 drives M2 macrophage polarization in response to [[IL-4]] and [[IL-13]], inducing genes associated with tissue repair, fibrosis, and anti-helminth defense (e.g., *Arg1*, *Retnla*, *Chil3*).

### IgE Class Switching
STAT6 directly activates germline Cε transcription in B cells, enabling immunoglobulin class switching to [[IgE]]. This is the molecular basis of IgE-mediated allergic responses.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- **Asthma**: STAT6 is required for the development of allergic airway inflammation. Gain-of-function STAT6 variants are associated with severe asthma.
- **Atopic Dermatitis**: STAT6 activation in skin-resident T cells drives the Th2-polarized inflammatory milieu.
- **Allergic Rhinitis**: STAT6-dependent IgE production and eosinophil recruitment underlie nasal allergy.
- **Helminth Infections**: STAT6 is essential for protective anti-helminth immunity.
- **Cancer**: STAT6 activation in tumor-associated macrophages promotes M2 polarization and immunosuppressive tumor microenvironments.
- **Therapeutic Target**: Selective STAT6 inhibitors are in preclinical development for allergic and fibrotic diseases.
