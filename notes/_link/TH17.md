# TH17 (T helper 17 cells)

**TH17 (T helper 17) cells** are a subset of CD4+ T cells defined by production of IL-17A/F. When [[TGFβ]] is present, the SASP factors [[IL-6]] and [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]] promote differentiation of naive T cells into a TH17 phenotype through activation of transcription factors [[STAT3]] and RORγt (RORC).

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

Naive CD4+ T cell commitment to TH17 requires TGFβ signaling (via [[SMAD2]]/[[SMAD3]]) together with IL-6–driven [[STAT3]] activation; [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]] and IL-23 reinforce and stabilize the program. STAT3 and RORγt cooperate to induce the signature cytokines IL-17A, IL-17F, IL-22, and GM-CSF, plus the receptors IL-23R and CCR6. IL-6 signals through IL-6R/[[gp130]]→JAK→STAT3, whereas TGFβ engages TGFBR→SMAD2/3; the balance between TGFβ plus IL-6/IL-1β versus TGFβ alone determines whether cells become regulatory T cells or TH17. STAT3 is the non-redundant master regulator of IL-17 expression.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

- SASP factors (IL-6, IL-1β, TGFβ) supplied by [[Senescent Cells]] can polarize the local adaptive immune response toward TH17.
- TH17 cells amplify SASP-mediated inflammation and contribute to [[Immunosenescence]] and altered vaccine responses with age.
- This axis links stromal/[[Senescence]] to adaptive immunity, with implications for both anti-tumor surveillance and autoimmunity.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance
- TH17 cells and IL-17 drive autoimmune diseases (psoriasis, rheumatoid arthritis, multiple sclerosis).
- Anti-IL-17/IL-23 and anti-IL-6R antibodies are established immunomodulatory therapies.
- Age-related TH17 skewing may contribute to chronic [[Inflammaging]] and tissue pathology.
