# TH17 (T helper 17 cells)

TH17 (T helper 17 cells) are a subset of CD4+ T cells that promote inflammation. When [[TGFβ]] is present, the SASP factors [[IL-6]] and [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]] promote the differentiation of naive T cells to a TH17 phenotype through the activation of transcription factors [[STAT3]] or RORγt.

## Mechanism of Action

- TH17 cells differentiate from naive T cells
- This differentiation is promoted by [[TGFβ]], [[IL-6]], and [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]]
- The process involves activation of [[STAT3]] or RORγt transcription factors
- TH17 cells produce pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-17, IL-22)

## Role in SASP

- SASP factors (IL-6, IL-1β, TGFβ) promote TH17 differentiation
- TH17 cells contribute to SASP-mediated inflammation
- TH17 differentiation links senescence to adaptive immunity

## Clinical Relevance

- TH17 cells are implicated in autoimmune diseases
- TH17 cells are targets for immunomodulatory therapies
- TH17 cells contribute to age-related inflammation
