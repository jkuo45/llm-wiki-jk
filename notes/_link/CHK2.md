# CHK2

CHK2 (Checkpoint Kinase 2, CHEK2) is a [[Serine/Threonine Kinase]] that functions as a [[DNA Damage]] checkpoint protein in the [[Cell Cycle]]. It is activated by [[ATM]] in response to [[Double-Strand Breaks]] (DSBs) through phosphorylation at Thr68. Active CHK2 phosphorylates downstream targets including [[p53]] (at Ser20), [[CDC25A]], [[CDC25C]], and [[BRCA1]], leading to [[Cell Cycle Arrest]] at G1/S, intra-S, or G2/M checkpoints. CHK2 also promotes [[Apoptosis]] through p53 stabilization and [[E2F1]] activation. Germline mutations in CHEK2 (e.g., 1100delC) confer moderate increased risk for [[Breast Cancer]], [[Prostate Cancer]], and [[Colorectal Cancer]].
