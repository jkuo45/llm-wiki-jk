## Overview

**C57BL/6** (commonly "B6") is an inbred laboratory mouse strain originating from Miss Abbie Lathrop's stock and established at the Jackson Laboratory. It is the most frequently used strain in biomedical research and the standard background for transgenic and [[Knockout mouse]] generation.

## Origin and Genetics

C57BL/6 mice are homozygous at essentially all loci due to >20 generations of brother–sister mating, giving high reproducibility. They carry a naturally hypomorphic allele of the *Nnt* (nicotinamide nucleotide transhydrogenase) gene on the C57BL/6J substrains, which can affect glucose metabolism and mitochondrial redox. The strain is relatively resistant to audiogenic seizures and prone to age-related obesity, atherosclerosis on high-fat diet, and retinal degeneration (*Pde6b*).

## Common Research Uses

B6 mice underpin studies of [[Aging]], immunology, behavior, [[Cancer]], and metabolism. They are the reference strain for the Interventions Testing Program (ITP) evaluating longevity drugs such as [[mTOR]] inhibitors. Their sequenced genome serves as the mouse reference assembly.

## Relevance to Longevity and Disease

C57BL/6 is a primary model for age-related phenotypes, caloric restriction, and senolytic compound screening. Because background strongly influences phenotype, most engineered alleles are backcrossed onto C57BL/6 to control confounders.
