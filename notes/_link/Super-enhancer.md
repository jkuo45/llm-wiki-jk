# Super-enhancer

A super-enhancer (SE) is a large cluster of enhancers — often spanning tens of kilobases and containing unusually high density of transcription-factor binding and histone-acetyl marks — that drives exceptionally high, cell-type-specific transcription of genes governing cell identity and stress programs. In senescence, de novo senescence-activated super-enhancers (SA-SEs) form at key locus and are major drivers of the [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]] and the senescence transcription program, as described in the npj Aging review (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2).

## Definition

SEs were defined by Hnisz et al. (2013, PMID 24072981) as clusters of typical enhancers with exceptionally high mediator/coactivator occupancy. They are identified by steep enrichment of H3K27ac, H3K4me1, and acetyl marks, and by binding of master transcription factors. Functionally, an SE behaves as a single regulatory unit with high transcriptional output.

## Formation and Mechanism

Senescence-activated SEs are generated de novo from previously unmarked chromatin. They are enriched in [[H3K4me1]] and multiple histone acetyl marks — [[H3K27ac]], [[H3K18ac]], [[H3K122ac]], and [[H4K5ac]] (Sen et al., 2019, PMID 30753809) — actively transcribe enhancer RNAs (eRNAs), recruit the histone acetyltransferase [[P300]], and directly drive senescence-specific gene expression. The [[Pioneer Factor|pioneer factor]] [[AP-1]] opens the underlying enhancers; the reader [[BRD4]] and [[CEBPα]] bind the SEs and recruit the transcription machinery.

## Role in SASP

SA-SEs sit at the heart of SASP regulation. They form adjacent to key SASP genes and, via [[Enhancer-Promoter Looping|enhancer-promoter looping]] within [[Topologically Associating Domain|TADs]], drive NF-κB-regulated SASP expression. HDAC4, a catalytically inactive class IIa HDAC that acts as an epigenetic reader, is normally recruited to [[H3K27ac]] loci to buffer the SE program; its degradation upon senescence entry unleashes AP-1/p300-driven transcription at selected SEs (Di Giorgio et al., 2021, PMID 33948120). Depletion of AP-1 reverses arrest and SASP; depletion of HDAC4 enhances SASP.

## Mechanistic Details

The acetyl-mark-rich SE landscape is a convergence point for multiple senescence signals: p300 writes the acetyl marks, BRD4 reads H3K27ac, AP-1 opens chromatin, and HDAC4 normally restrains the program. Loss of HDAC4 removes this brake, converting the SE into a potent SASP driver. SEs thereby integrate pioneer-factor, reader, and metabolic (acetyl-CoA) inputs into a single output.

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

BET-family readers (BRD4) and HATs at SEs are tractable [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] targets; BET inhibitors (e.g., apabetalone, molibresib) and BRD4 degraders are in clinical trials and could be repurposed to dampen SASP (Tasdemir et al., 2016, PMID 27193494; see [[KDM4]], [[DOT1L]]).
