# Cortisol

**Cortisol** (hydrocortisone) is the principal glucocorticoid produced by the zona fasciculata of the [[Adrenal gland|adrenal cortex]] under [[ACTH]] control from the HPA axis. It mobilizes glucose, suppresses non-essential functions during stress, and is the hormonal counterpart to the [[Sympathetic Nervous System|sympathetic]] catecholamine response.

## Relation to Stress and Recovery

Acute cortisol release supports the fight-or-flight response, but chronically elevated cortisol (from stacked stressors, sleep loss, or under-recovery) suppresses [[Heart Rate Variability|HRV]], impairs [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis|mitochondrial biogenesis]], and promotes [[Inflammation|inflammation]]. Post-workout carbohydrate intake spikes insulin and lowers cortisol, helping the autonomic nervous system return to parasympathetic dominance so HRV can rebound.
