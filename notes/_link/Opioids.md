# Opioids

[[Opioids]] are analgesic drugs acting primarily at opioid receptors. Certain opioids with serotonergic properties (e.g., [[Tramadol]], meperidine) can contribute to [[Serotonin Syndrome]] when combined with a [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]] such as [[Methylene blue]]. The FDA boxed warning for methylene blue extends to opioid co-administration.
