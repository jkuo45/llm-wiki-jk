# Albuminuria

Albuminuria is the abnormal excretion of the plasma protein albumin into the urine. It is typically a marker of increased glomerular permeability and is a key early sign of [[Diabetic Nephropathy]] and other forms of [[Renal Failure]].

## Clinical Significance

- **Diabetic kidney disease**: Albuminuria is among the earliest detectable manifestations of diabetic nephropathy and tracks with worsening glomerular injury and the risk of progression to end-stage renal disease.
- **Cardiovascular risk**: Albuminuria independently predicts cardiovascular events and mortality, overlapping with [[Atherosclerosis]] risk.

## Modulation by AGE Inhibition

In diabetic clinical trials, [[Aminoguanidine]] (Pimagedine) was reported to significantly reduce albuminuria, delay the onset of end-stage renal disease, and improve the cholesterol profiles of diabetic patients — consistent with a role for [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] and [[Glycation]] in progressive renal injury.
