# mitoRQC

**mitoRQC** (mitochondrial ribosome quality control) is a surveillance pathway that monitors [[Mitochondrial ribosome|mitochondrial ribosomes]] for stalled translation events and targets the incomplete nascent [[Polypeptide|polypeptides]] for degradation.

## Mechanism

- Stalled mitochondrial translation products are recognized by the RQC complex
- [[Vms1]] (yeast) / [[ANKZF1]] (mammals) plays a key role in extracting stalled nascent chains from the ribosome
- [[Ltn1]] (yeast) / [[LISTERIN]] (mammals) mediates [[Ubiquitin|ubiquitylation]] of the stalled polypeptide
- The ubiquitylated products are targeted to the [[Proteasome]] or [[Mitochondrial Protease|mitochondrial proteases]] for degradation
- Prevents accumulation of truncated, potentially toxic mitochondrial translation products
