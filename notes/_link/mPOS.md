# mPOS

**mPOS** (mitochondrial precursor over-accumulation stress) is a cellular stress state in which mitochondrial precursor proteins accumulate in the [[Cytosol]] beyond the capacity of quality control pathways. This cytosolic accumulation triggers [[Proteotoxicity]] and can lead to [[Cell Death]].

## Mechanism

- Approximately 1000 [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] proteins are synthesized on cytosolic [[Ribosomes]] before being imported
- When [[Mitochondrial protein import]] is impaired (due to [[TOM complex|TOM]]/[[TIM complex|TIM]] dysfunction, ΔΨm loss, or saturation), precursors build up in the cytosol
- Accumulated precursors interfere with cytosolic [[Proteostasis]], aggregating and sequestering chaperones
- [[HSF1]] is activated to coordinate a stress response, inducing [[RPN4]] and [[PDR3]]
- If unresolved, mPOS triggers [[Apoptosis]] or [[Necrosis]]

## Related Pathways

- [[mitoTAD]] — Constitutive clearance
- [[mitoCPR]] — Stress-induced clearance
- [[MitoStores]] — Temporary storage of precursors
- [[mitoRQC]] — Quality control at the ribosome
