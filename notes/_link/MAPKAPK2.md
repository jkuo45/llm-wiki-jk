# MAPKAPK2

**Mitogen-activated protein kinase-activated protein kinase 2 (MAPKAPK2)**, also known as MK2, is a serine/threonine kinase that is a direct substrate of [[p38 MAPK]].

## Role in SASP
MK2 is a critical regulator of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] through its control of mRNA stability:
- Activated by p38 MAPK in response to stress and inflammatory signals.
- Phosphorylates and inhibits RNA-binding proteins like [[ZFP36L1]], which normally target SASP mRNAs for degradation.
- This stabilization of SASP factor transcripts (e.g., [[IL-6]], [[IL-8]]) is essential for maintaining the inflammatory secretome of [[Senescent Cells]].

## Therapeutic Targeting
Inhibiting the p38 MAPK-MK2 axis is a promising [[Senomorphic Therapy]] strategy to suppress SASP.
