# 2-Chlorophenothiazine

[[2-Chlorophenothiazine]] (2-CPT) is a derivative of the [[Phenothiazine]] nucleus bearing a chlorine substituent at position 2 and, critically, **no amine side chains** at positions 3 and 7 and a **free 10-nitrogen**. In the methylene blue derivative study it behaved as a direct antioxidant with nanomolar neuroprotective potency, but it lacked the mitochondrial electron-carrier and bioenergetic properties of [[Methylene blue]] and [[Toluidine Blue O]] [^1].

## Structure & Chemistry

2-Chlorophenothiazine retains the free 10-nitrogen and position-5 sulfur of the phenothiazine scaffold but is unsubstituted at 3/7. Its redox potential (E<sub>1/2</sub> ≈ 0.942 V vs NHE) is far more positive than that of [[Methylene blue]]/[[Toluidine Blue O]] (≈ −0.1 V), consistent with its inability to enhance oxygen consumption [^1].

## Mechanism of Action

### Direct free-radical scavenging

Unlike [[Methylene blue]], 2-chlorophenothiazine reduced DCF fluorescence (scavenged oxidants) **independent** of mitochondria and [[NADH]] in the cell-free mitochondria-lysate oxidation assay, at 100 nM and 1 µM (it became pro-oxidative at 10 µM). It therefore acts as a conventional chain-breaking antioxidant rather than a regenerable, mitochondria-dependent one [^1].

### No alternative electron transfer

2-Chlorophenothiazine did **not** increase [[Cytochrome c]] reduction in the [[Respiratory Chain Complex I|Complex I]]→[[Complex III]] assay and did **not** increase cellular oxygen consumption rate (OCR) or decrease ECAR in Seahorse assays [^1].

### No Complex IV upregulation

Western blot and blue-native PAGE showed that 2-chlorophenothiazine had **no effect** on [[Complex IV]] subunit I (Cox1) expression or activity at any concentration tested, in contrast to [[Methylene blue]], which increased Cox1 at 10–100 nM [^1].

## Neuroprotection

Against glutamate insult in HT-22 cells, 2-chlorophenothiazine showed:

- Viability EC50 ≈ **4.57 nM**
- ROS EC50 ≈ **84.14 nM**
- Mitochondrial membrane potential EC50 ≈ **45.09 nM**

It protected against [[Rotenone]], [[Iodoacetic Acid|IAA]], and glutamate neurotoxicity (with efficacy greater than [[Chlorpromazine]] and [[Neutral Red]] in the IAA model), and like the family, gave no protection against direct H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub> from [[Glucose Oxidase]] [^1].

> [!info] Scavenger vs carrier
> 2-Chlorophenothiazine exemplifies the "naked phenothiazine nucleus" group: potent direct ROS scavenger with nanomolar neuroprotection, but without the OCR enhancement, electron shunt, or Complex IV upregulation that define the MB/TB group [^1].
