# Mitochondria

[[Mitochondria]] are double-membrane organelles that produce ATP through [[Oxidative Phosphorylation]], and also govern apoptosis, calcium handling, and redox balance. Mitochondrial dysfunction is a principal driver of age-related disease (neurodegeneration, cardiovascular disease).

## mTOR, Rapamycin, and Mitochondrial Renewal

Inhibition of [[mTOR]] by [[Rapamycin]] improves the NAD⁺/NADH redox balance, supports [[Oxidative Phosphorylation]], and reduces mTOR-hyperactivity "pseudohypoxia" linked to [[HIF-1α]] accumulation. It also promotes mitochondrial biogenesis via [[PGC-1α]] and [[TFAM]], renewing mitochondrial populations and improving fatty-acid metabolism in senescent tissues (myocardium, brown adipose) ([Zerdka et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12766144/)).

> [!tip] Energy efficiency
> Rapamycin shifts cells from glycolytic/pseudohypoxic metabolism toward efficient OXPHOS by relieving HIF-1α accumulation and boosting PGC-1α/TFAM.
