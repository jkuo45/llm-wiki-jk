# NAC

**N-acetylcysteine (NAC)** is a cysteine prodrug and antioxidant that scavenges reactive oxygen species and acts as a selective inhibitor of the **peroxidase** activity of [[PRDX6|peroxiredoxin 6]] (IC50 ≈ 15.5 µM).

## Use as a Comparator in the Apigenin Study

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> NAC markedly reduced PRDX6's H2O2-clearing peroxidase activity (IC50 15.5 µM), whereas apigenin only modestly compromised it (IC50 49.5 µM). This showed apigenin's [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] [[SASP|SASP]] suppression is **not** primarily peroxidase-dependent but instead reflects inhibition of PRDX6's iPLA2 activity.
