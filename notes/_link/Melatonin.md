# Melatonin
**[[Melatonin]]** is a hormone produced by the pineal gland that regulates the sleep-wake cycle.
## Synthesis
[[Melatonin]] is synthesized from [[Serotonin]] through a process that requires the addition of a [[Methyl groups|methyl group]].
## Functions
It primarily signals the body that it is time to sleep and acts as a potent antioxidant.
