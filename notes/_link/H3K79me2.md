# H3K79me2

H3K79me2 is the dimethylation of histone H3 at lysine 79, a methylation placed within the nucleosome core (not the tail) by the methyltransferase DOT1L. Along with the trimethyl form (H3K79me3), H3K79 methylation marks actively transcribed gene bodies and is elevated at senescence-associated secretory phenotype ([[SASP]]) loci, as noted in the npj Aging review (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2).

## Definition

H3K79 lies in the globular core domain of H3, and its methylation is unique among histone marks in being catalyzed by a methyltransferase (DOT1L) that does not require a SET domain. H3K79me2/me3 correlate with active transcription and, like [[H3K36me3]], decorate gene bodies of expressed genes.

## Writer, Reader, Eraser

- **Writer:** DOT1L is the sole H3K79 methyltransferase; its activity is stimulated by elongating RNA polymerase II and by the AF10/ENL complexes.
- **Reader:** The disruptor of telomeric silencing-like (DOT1L) product is recognized by 53BP1 (which binds H4K20me2, a邻近 mark) and other chromatin effectors; H3K79me is also linked to checkpoint responses.
- **Eraser:** No dedicated demethylase is known; turnover is coupled to histone replacement.

## Role in SASP and Senescence

Actively transcribed genes, including SASP loci, show elevated levels of [[H3K36me3]] and H3K79me3 within the gene body (Gates et al., 2017). Functionally, [[DOT1L]] modulates the SASP through epigenetic regulation of [[IL-1α]] (Leon et al., 2021, PMID 33741061): DOT1L controls IL-1α expression, a key upstream cytokine of the SASP. Depletion or inhibition of DOT1L reduces SASP while sparing the growth arrest, mirroring other epigenetic regulators. DOT1L inhibitors (e.g., EPZ-5676) are in clinical trials and could be repurposed as [[Senomorphic|senomorphics]].

## Mechanistic Details

H3K79 methylation at SASP gene bodies marks them as actively transcribed and helps couple transcription elongation to the secretory program. By regulating IL1A, DOT1L sits at the apex of the SASP cytokine cascade. Because H3K79 methylation is elevated with age-related transcription, it is a useful marker of the active senescent transcriptome.

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

DOT1L is a clinical-stage target whose inhibition dampens SASP without abolishing arrest (Leon et al., 2021), making it a leading senomorphic candidate alongside [[KDM4]] and [[BRD4]].
