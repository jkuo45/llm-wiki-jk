# IKK complex

The **IκB kinase (IKK) complex** is the core signaling component of the canonical [[NFKB|NF-κB]] pathway. It consists of two catalytic subunits, [[IKKalpha]] and [[IKKbeta]], and a regulatory scaffold subunit, NEMO (IKKγ).

## Function
Upon activation by various stimuli (e.g., [[TNF-α]], [[IL-1β]], [[DNA Damage]]), the IKK complex phosphorylates [[IkappaB]] inhibitors (such as [[IkappaBalpha]]). This phosphorylation leads to the ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of IκB, releasing the NF-κB dimer (e.g., p65/p50) to translocate to the nucleus and activate target genes.

## Role in Senescence
The IKK complex is a key mediator of [[NFKB|NF-κB]] activation in [[Senescent Cells]], driving the expression of pro-inflammatory [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] factors.
