# Progerin

**[[Progerin]]** is a truncated, farnesylated version of the [[Lamin A]] protein. It is the primary molecular driver of **[[Hutchinson-Gilford Progeria Syndrome]]** (HGPS).

## Molecular Mechanism
Progerin is produced due to a point mutation in the **[[LMNA Gene]]** that activates a cryptic splice site. This results in the loss of 50 amino acids near the C-terminus, including the site where the farnesyl group is normally removed. As a result, progerin remains permanently attached to the inner nuclear membrane.

## Cellular Impact
The accumulation of progerin leads to:
*   **Nuclear Blebbing**: Structural deformation of the nucleus.
*   **[[Genomic Instability]]**: Disruption of chromatin organization and DNA repair.
*   **Accelerated [[Aging]]**: Induction of premature [[Senescence]] and the [[SASP]].
