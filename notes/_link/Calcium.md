## Overview

Calcium (Ca^2+^) is a divalent cation that functions as a universal second messenger in virtually all eukaryotic cells. Cytosolic Ca^2+^ concentrations are tightly regulated through a coordinated system of channels, pumps, and exchangers localized to the plasma membrane, [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]] (ER), and [[Mitochondria]]. Mitochondrial Ca^2+^ uptake, primarily mediated by the [[MCU|mitochondrial calcium uniporter (MCU)]], plays a central role in modulating [[ATP]] production, [[Apoptosis]], and [[Senescence|senescence]].

## Mitochondrial Calcium Handling

- [[Mitochondria]] take up Ca^2+^ from the cytosol via the [[MCU]], driven by the negative [[Mitochondrial Membrane Potential]] (ΔΨm)
- Ca^2+^ efflux occurs through the [[Mitochondrial Permeability Transition Pore|mPTP]] and the Na^+^/Ca^2+^ exchanger (NCLX)
- [[MERCS|Mitochondria-ER contact sites (MERCS)]] provide a microdomain for efficient Ca^2+^ transfer between organelles via [[ITPR2]] and other channels

## Role in Cellular Senescence

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Mitochondrial dysfunction in cellular senescence a bridge to neurodegenerative disease]]
> During [[Oncogene-Induced Senescence|OIS]] and [[Replicative Senescence|RS]], increased Ca^2+^ release via [[ITPR2]] combined with MCU-mediated mitochondrial uptake causes Ca^2+^ accumulation in the mitochondrial matrix, reducing ΔΨm and elevating [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] production. Treating cells under oxidative stress with a Ca^2+^ chelator prevents senescence. The mPTP acts as a Ca^2+^ release valve in senescent cells, with transient "flickering" preventing apoptosis while sustained opening can function as a senolytic mechanism.

## Connections

- [[MCU]] — Primary channel for mitochondrial Ca^2+^ uptake; its activity drives Ca^2+^ accumulation in senescence
- [[Mitochondrial Permeability Transition Pore]] — Activated by matrix Ca^2+^ overload; its opening dissipates ΔΨm and can trigger cell death
- [[ITPR2]] — ER Ca^2+^ release channel that supplies Ca^2+^ for mitochondrial uptake at MERCS
- [[MERCS]] — Physical contact sites between mitochondria and ER that enable efficient Ca^2+^ transfer
- [[Alzheimer's Disease]] — Aβ pathology disrupts Ca^2+^ regulation, promoting mitochondrial Ca^2+^ overload and mPTP activation
- [[Parkinson's Disease]] — α-synuclein forms Ca^2+^-permeable pores, driving mitochondrial Ca^2+^ overload
- [[Huntington's Disease]] — Mutant huntingtin sensitizes IP3R channels, increasing ER Ca^2+^ release and mitochondrial uptake
