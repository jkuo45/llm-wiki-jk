# SREBP-1c

**SREBP-1c** (sterol regulatory element-binding protein 1c) is a basic helix-loop-helix leucine zipper transcription factor that regulates genes involved in [[Lipid Synthesis]], particularly [[Fatty acid]] and [[Triglyceride]] biosynthesis. It is one of three SREBP isoforms (SREBP-1a, SREBP-1c, SREBP-2), where SREBP-1c is the predominant form in [[Liver]] and [[Adipose Tissue]].

## Regulation

SREBP-1c is synthesized as an inactive precursor anchored to the [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]] membrane. [[Insulin]] signaling through [[Akt]]-[[mTORC1]] activates [[SREBP-1c]] transcription and promotes its proteolytic processing. Low sterol levels trigger [[SCAP]]-mediated transport of the SREBP-SCAP complex to the [[Golgi apparatus]], where [[S1P]] and [[S2P]] proteases cleave SREBP-1c to release the active N-terminal domain, which translocates to the [[Nucleus]] and activates target genes.

## Target Genes

SREBP-1c transcriptionally activates:
- [[Acetyl-CoA Carboxylase]] (ACC) — rate-limiting step in fatty acid synthesis
- [[Fatty Acid Synthase]] (FASN) — catalyzes palmitate synthesis
- [[Stearoyl-CoA Desaturase]] (SCD1) — monounsaturated fatty acid production
- [[Elongation of very long chain fatty acids]] (ELOVL6) — fatty acid elongation
- [[ATP citrate lyase]] (ACLY) — provides cytosolic acetyl-CoA

## Clinical Relevance

SREBP-1c hyperactivation contributes to [[Hepatic Steatosis]], [[Insulin Resistance]], and [[Metabolic Syndrome]]. [[mTORC1]]-S6K1 signaling drives SREBP-1c-dependent [[Lipid Synthesis]]. [[Metformin]] and [[AMPK]] suppress SREBP-1c activity via direct phosphorylation. [[SREBP-1c]] also links [[Caloric Restriction]] to altered hepatic lipid metabolism.
