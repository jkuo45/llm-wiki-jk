# MICU2

MICU2 (mitochondrial calcium uptake 2) is a paralog of [[MICU1]] and a regulatory subunit of the [[Mitochondria]]l calcium uniporter (MCU) complex. Like MICU1, MICU2 contains EF-hand Ca²⁺-binding domains, but it primarily reinforces channel closure at resting cytosolic Ca²⁺ levels, sharpening the threshold behavior of mitochondrial Ca²⁺ uptake. MICU2 forms heterodimers with MICU1, and together they ensure that the MCU pore opens only when cytosolic Ca²⁺ rises sufficiently to drive metabolic activation without permitting leaky, toxic Ca²⁺ entry.

By stabilizing the closed state, MICU2 protects the matrix from Ca²⁺ overload that would otherwise promote [Mitochondrial Permeability Transition Pore] opening, [[ROS]] amplification, and [[Apoptosis]]. Genetic disruption of MICU2 shifts the threshold downward, increasing basal Ca²⁺ uptake and mitochondrial vulnerability to stress.

> [!info] MICU2 acts as the dominant "brake" on the uniporter at rest, complementary to MICU1's threshold-setting role.

Through its control of matrix Ca²⁺, MICU2 indirectly shapes mitochondrial redox balance and bioenergetic efficiency, linking calcium signaling to [[Oxidative Stress]] resistance and cellular resilience.
