# Minority MOMP

**Minority MOMP (miMOMP)** refers to sublethal mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization (MOMP) events that occur in a fraction of the mitochondria within a cell, without triggering full-scale [[Apoptosis]].

## Mechanism
miMOMP is mediated by pro-apoptotic [[Bcl-2]] family proteins like [[BAX]] and [[BAK]], but is kept in check by anti-apoptotic mechanisms. This leads to:
- The release of [[mtDNA]] and other mitochondrial DAMPs into the cytosol.
- Activation of the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS-STING]] innate immune sensor.
- Induction of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].

## Role in Senescence
miMOMP is a critical link between [[Apoptosis]] and [[Cellular Senescence]]. It allows damaged cells to survive and signal their state of stress through the SASP, rather than undergoing programmed cell death.
