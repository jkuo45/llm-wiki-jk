# Lamin B1

Lamin B1 is a nuclear lamina protein that is downregulated in senescent cells. Loss of Lamin B1 expression is a marker of cellular senescence, along with multinucleation, irregular nuclear size, and formation of [[Senescence-Associated Heterochromatin Foci|SAHF]].

## Mechanism of Action

- Lamin B1 is a structural component of the nuclear lamina
- It is downregulated during cellular senescence
- Loss of Lamin B1 contributes to nuclear structural changes
- Lamin B1 loss is a marker of senescent cells

## Role in Senescence

- Lamin B1 loss is a hallmark of senescence
- It contributes to nuclear morphology changes in senescent cells
- Lamin B1 is used as a senescence biomarker

## Clinical Relevance

- Lamin B1 is a biomarker for senescence
- Lamin B1 levels decrease with age
- Lamin B1 may be a target for senescence detection
