# NPM1

**NPM1** (nucleophosmin, B23) is a multifunctional nucleolar chaperone involved in ribosome biogenesis, centrosome duplication, and stress responses.

## Role in Senescence Signaling

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> In a proteome-wide interactor screen, [[NPM1]] was one of 19 molecules shared by both [[ATM]] and [[p38 MAPK]]. Co-IP confirmed NPM1 can interact with p-ATM and p38 MAPK in senescent cells — but, unlike [[HSPA8]], its interaction with these kinases was **not** attenuated by [[Apigenin]], marking HSPA8 (not NPM1) as the relevant apigenin-sensitive node for [[SASP|SASP]] suppression.

## Connections

- [[ATM]] / [[p38 MAPK]] — Shared interactors; NPM1 binds both but apigenin-insensitive.
- [[HSPA8]] — Comparator node whose ATM/p38 crosstalk apigenin disrupts.
- [[SASP]] — NPM1 is not the apigenin-sensitive SASP gate.
- [[Apigenin]] — Does not alter NPM1–kinase interactions.
