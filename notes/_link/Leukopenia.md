# Leukopenia

[[Leukopenia]] is a decrease in the total number of circulating white blood cells, increasing infection susceptibility. It is one of the hematological cytopenias linked to chronic [[mTOR]] inhibitor use.

## Rapamycin-Associated Leukopenia

Chronic high-dose [[Rapamycin]] and [[Rapalog|rapalogs]] inhibit cell proliferation via [[mTORC1]], lowering blood cell counts. Mild, reversible leukopenia—alongside [[Anemia]] and [[Thrombocytopenia]]—is a common hematological side effect ([Blagosklonny, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6814615/)). Because rapamycin can also increase infection severity by inhibiting neutrophil function, leukopenia warrants monitoring; dosing in longevity regimens is capped by such cytopenias.

> [!warning] Infection risk
> Leukopenia combined with rapamycin's inhibition of [[Neutrophils]] may raise infection severity; the drug should be discontinued if serious effects occur.
