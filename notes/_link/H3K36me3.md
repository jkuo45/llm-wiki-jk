# H3K36me3

H3K36me3 is the trimethylation of histone H3 at lysine 36, a mark deposited across the bodies of actively transcribed genes and strongly correlated with elongating RNA polymerase II. In senescence, H3K36me3 occupies the perichromatin space of facultative heterochromatin structures such as [[Senescence-Associated Heterochromatin Foci|SAHF]] and is part of the broader activation landscape of [[SASP]] genes, as discussed in the npj Aging review (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2).

## Definition

H3K36me3 is a "gene-body" methyl mark deposited co-transcriptionally by SET2-family methyltransferases (SETD2 in mammals). It recruits chromatin-modifying complexes that help distinguish transcribed from promoter regions and suppresses spurious initiation and cryptic transcription.

## Writer, Reader, Eraser

- **Writer:** SETD2 (and related SET2-family enzymes) deposit H3K36me3 during transcription elongation.
- **Reader:** PWWP- and Tudor-domain proteins (e.g., DNMT3a/b, which couples H3K36me3 to DNA methylation; LEDGF) recognize the mark.
- **Eraser:** KDM4 (JMJD2) and other JmjC demethylases remove H3K36 methylation.

## Role in SASP and Senescence

Actively transcribed genes, including SASP loci, show elevated H3K36me3 within the gene body alongside H3K79 methylation (Gates et al., 2017). SAHF architecture displays concentric layers: interior H3K9me3/H4K20me3, surrounded by H3K27me3, with active marks such as [[H3K4me3]] and H3K36me3 occupying the perichromatin space distinct from the interior (Miron et al., 2020, PMID 32263403). Thus H3K36me3 marks the transcriptionally active surfaces flanking senescent heterochromatin — including SASP genes that remain euchromatic rather than sequestered into SAHF.

## Mechanistic Details

H3K36me3 couples the transcription of SASP genes to the deposition of DNA methylation (via DNMT3a/b recruitment), linking the gene-body methyl code to the DNA-methylation landscape of senescence. Its presence at perichromatin boundaries helps maintain the active/euchromatic identity of SASP loci despite neighboring facultative heterochromatin.

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

SETD2 and KDM4 modulate H3K36 methylation; KDM4 in particular is upregulated in senescence and potentiates SASP, making it a clinical-stage [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] target (see [[KDM4]], [[BRD4]], [[DOT1L]]).
