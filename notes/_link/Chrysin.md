# Chrysin

**Chrysin** (5,7-dihydroxyflavone) is a plant [[Flavones|flavone]] present in honey, propolis, and *Morinda citrifolia* fruit. It shares the flavone scaffold with [[Apigenin]] but lacks the 4′-hydroxyl.

## Anticancer Activity

Chrysin combined with [[Apigenin]] synergistically induced apoptosis and inhibited migration in human breast and liver cancer cells by downregulating [[Matrix Metalloproteinase|MMP-2]] and [[MMP-9]]. Chrysin also modulates [[Aromatase]], [[Estrogen Receptor]] signaling, and exerts anti-inflammatory effects via [[NF-κB]] and [[COX-2]] inhibition.
