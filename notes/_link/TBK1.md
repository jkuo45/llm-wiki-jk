# TBK1

## XIAP Degradation

> [!important] TBK1 Phosphorylates XIAP to Trigger Degradation
> During the innate immune response (e.g., viral infection), [[TBK1]] and [[IKKepsilon]] phosphorylate **[[XIAP]]** at **Ser430** within its RING finger domain. This triggers Lys48-linked autoubiquitination at Lys322 and Lys328, leading to rapid [[Proteasome|proteasomal degradation]]. XIAP degradation liberates [[Caspase-3]], [[Caspase-7]], and [[Caspase-9]] from inhibition, sensitizing the cell to apoptosis. This is the functional opposite of [[Akt]]-mediated phosphorylation at Ser87, which stabilizes XIAP to promote tumor survival.
TANK-binding kinase 1, an enzyme involved in the innate immune response, activated by [[STING]] and regulated by [[TFEB]].
[[TBK1]] (TANK-binding kinase 1) is a kinase involved in innate immunity and cell survival. It phosphorylates [[XIAP]] at Ser430, leading to its autoubiquitination and degradation, particularly in [[Colon Cancer]] cells.
