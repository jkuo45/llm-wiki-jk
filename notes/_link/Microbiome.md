# Microbiome

## Overview
The [[Microbiome]] encompasses the bacteria, fungi, and viruses colonizing the host—principally the [[Gut Microbiome]]—and their functional metabolites. A diverse, balanced community produces [[Short-chain Fatty Acids]] (notably [[Butyrate]]), modulates immunity, and buffers [[Oxidative Stress]], whereas its disruption manifests as [[Dysbiosis]].

## Composition & Ecology
Dominant phyla include [[Firmicutes]] and [[Bacteroidetes]], with health-associated genera such as [[Akkermansia]], [[Bifidobacterium]], and [[Faecalibacterium]]. Diversity metrics ([[Alpha Diversity]], [[Beta Diversity]]) track resilience to perturbation.

## Host Interactions
Microbial metabolites fuel colonocytes, train [[Regulatory T cells]], and restrain [[Inflammation]] and [[NLRP3]] activation. Gut-derived [[Lipopolysaccharide]] translocation promotes [[Inflammaging]] and [[Insulin]] resistance. The microbiota also interfaces with the [[Gut-Brain Axis]], influencing cognition and [[Neurodegeneration]], and contributes to [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]] when imbalanced.

## Redox, Sirtuins & Longevity
A healthy microbiome supports [[Redox Homeostasis]] and may potentiate [[Sirtuins]] activity via NAD+ precursor availability. [[Caloric Restriction]], [[Exercise]], and [[Nicotinamide Riboside]] reshape microbial composition toward longevity phenotypes, while [[Urolithin A]] benefits both microbiota and mitophagy. The [[Gut Microbiome]] is thus a tractable lever for [[Healthspan]].
