# hs-CRP

**High-sensitivity C-reactive protein ([[hs-CRP]])** is a high-sensitivity assay for C-reactive protein, a hepatically produced acute-phase reactant whose circulating concentration rises with systemic [[Inflammation|inflammation]]. Because of its low detection floor, hs-CRP is the preferred marker for low-grade, chronic inflammation.

## Use in the Mitohormesis Protocol

hs-CRP is one of the blood markers recommended for monitoring mitohormetic status. Rising hs-CRP alongside a chronically falling [[Heart Rate Variability|HRV]] indicates the stress has ceased to be hormetic and crossed into systemic inflammation / overtraining. Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) and polyphenol-rich, [[NRF2]]-activating foods help lower baseline inflammation and hs-CRP.
