# IkappaBalpha

**IκBα** (encoded by [[NFKBIA]]) is the prototypical member of the [[IkappaB]] family of [[NFκB]] inhibitors. It is the primary negative regulator of the canonical [[NFκB]] signaling pathway, controlling the activation kinetics and duration of [[Inflammation|inflammatory]] and [[Immune]] responses.

## Mechanism

IκBα contains six [[Ankyrin Repeat]] domains that bind the [[Nuclear Localization Signal]] of NF-κB dimer (p50-[[p65]]), retaining the complex in the [[Cytosol]]. Upon [[TNFα]] or [[Interleukin 1β]] stimulation, [[IKKbeta]] phosphorylates IκBα at Ser32 and Ser36, triggering [[K48-linked Ubiquitination]] by the [[SCF]]-[[β-TrCP]] E3 ligase and rapid [[Proteasomal Degradation]] within minutes. Freed NF-κB translocates to the [[Nucleus]] to activate target genes. [[IkappaBalpha]] itself is a transcriptional target of NF-κB, creating a tight [[Negative Feedback Loop]] in which newly synthesized IκBα enters the nucleus, removes NF-κB from chromatin, and exports it back to the cytoplasm.

## Clinical Relevance

[[NFKBIA]] deletions or mutations are found in [[Hodgkin Lymphoma]] and [[glioblastoma]]. IκBα dysfunction leads to constitutive NF-κB activation and chronic [[Inflammation]]. [[Polymorphisms]] in NFKBIA are associated with [[Inflammatory Bowel Disease]] and [[Sepsis]] susceptibility.
