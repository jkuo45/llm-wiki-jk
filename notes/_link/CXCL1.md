# CXCL1

CXCL1 (C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 1), also known as GROα (growth-regulated oncogene-alpha), is a chemokine that binds to [[CXCR2]] and is secreted by senescent stellate cells in the liver. It mediates paracrine functions in the liver and pancreas as a SASP factor.

## Mechanism of Action

- CXCL1 is secreted by senescent hepatic stellate cells
- It binds to [[CXCR2]] on neighboring cells to mediate paracrine signaling
- CXCL1 is part of a group of C-X-C motif chemokines (including [[CXCL2]] and [[CXCL3]]) that are SASP factors
- CXCL1 contributes to immune cell recruitment and inflammation

## Role in SASP

- CXCL1 is a pro-inflammatory SASP chemokine
- It is regulated by the [[NF-κB]] transcription factor
- CXCL1 contributes to the senescence-associated inflammatory milieu

## Clinical Relevance

- CXCL1 is elevated in various inflammatory conditions
- CXCL1/CXCR2 signaling is a therapeutic target in cancer and inflammatory diseases
