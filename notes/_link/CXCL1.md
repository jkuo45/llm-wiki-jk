# CXCL1

CXCL1 (C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 1), also known as GROα (growth-regulated oncogene alpha) or NAP-3, is an ELR+ C-X-C chemokine that primarily signals through [[CXCR2]] (and at lower affinity through CXCR1). It is a prototypical neutrophil-recruiting chemokine and a conserved component of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]], first characterized in the senescence secretome of human fibroblasts and epithelial cells (Coppé et al., 2008, PMID 18948995; 2010, PMID 20723158).

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

- **Receptor binding**: CXCL1 engages the seven-transmembrane G-protein-coupled receptors [[CXCR2]] (high affinity) and, at lower affinity, CXCR1. Receptor activation couples to Gαi, triggering PLCβ→IP3/DAG→Ca2+ flux, PI3K/Akt, and the [[MAPK|MAPK]]/ERK and [[p38 MAPK]] cascades.
- **Cell sources**: Constitutively low in most tissues but robustly induced in monocytes/[[Macrophages]], endothelial cells, epithelial cells, and fibroblasts upon inflammatory or genotoxic stress.
- **Transcriptional regulation**: Expression is driven by the [[NF-κB]] and [[AP-1]] pathways, the C/EBP family ([[CEBPβ|C/EBPβ]]), and [[p38 MAPK]] signaling. These same pathways are core SASP drivers, explaining why CXCL1 is co-induced with [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]].
- **Downstream cellular effects**: Potent chemoattractant and activator of [[Neutrophils]]; promotes neutrophil degranulation, ROS production, and transendothelial migration, amplifying local inflammation through paracrine loops.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

- CXCL1 is constitutively elevated in senescent human fibroblasts and is part of the core SASP chemokine cluster alongside [[CXCL2]], [[CXCL3]], and IL-8.
- In the liver, activated hepatic stellate cells that undergo stress-induced senescence secrete CXCL1, contributing to the inflammatory infiltrate that both limits and perpetuates fibrosis (Krizhanovsky et al., 2008, PMID 18729207).
- CXCL1 reinforces senescence non-cell-autonomously: by engaging [[CXCR2]] on neighboring cells it helps propagate a paracrine "bystander" senescence response (Acosta et al., 2013, PMID 23746840), making the CXCL1–CXCR2 axis a feed-forward amplifier of the senescent phenotype.
- **Paracrine duality**: acute CXCL1-driven neutrophil recruitment supports immune clearance of damaged cells, whereas chronic CXCL1/CXCR2 signaling in aged or diseased tissue fuels [[Inflammaging]] and tissue dysfunction.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- Elevated CXCL1/CXCR2 signaling is implicated in liver fibrosis, COPD, atherosclerosis, and several cancers (melanoma, breast, prostate), where it promotes neutrophil infiltration and tumor growth.
- CXCR2 antagonists (e.g., navarixin) are under investigation to blunt SASP-driven inflammation and senescence propagation.
- CXCL1 is a candidate circulating biomarker of senescent-cell burden in inflammatory and aging-related disease.
