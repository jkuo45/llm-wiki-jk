# Vasoconstriction

[[Vasoconstriction]] is the contraction of vascular smooth muscle that narrows lumens and raises blood pressure. It is the opposite of [[Vasodilation]]. [[Methylene blue]] induces vasoconstriction in [[Vasoplegic Shock]] by inhibiting the [[Nitric Oxide]]→[[Soluble Guanylyl Cyclase]]→[[Cyclic Guanosine Monophosphate]] relaxation signal.
