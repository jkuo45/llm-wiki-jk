# Chromatin Modifiers

**Chromatin Modifiers** is a collective term for the proteins that establish, read, and maintain chromatin states — including writers, readers, and erasers of histone marks, [[DNA Methylation|DNA methylation]], and higher-order chromatin organization. Prominent members are the [[Sirtuins|sirtuins]] (e.g., [[SIRT1]], [[SIRT6]]), [[HDAC1|class I HDACs]], and [[PARP1]]. They normally reside at specific genomic loci to enforce the gene-expression programs that define cellular identity.

## Role in the RCM Hypothesis

In the [[Relocalization of Chromatin Modifiers (RCM) Hypothesis]], these proteins are recruited away from their native loci to [[Double-Strand Break|DNA repair sites]] when damage occurs. Repeated cycles of relocalization with imperfect resetting erode the [[Epigenetic Landscape|epigenetic landscape]], driving loss of cellular identity and aging phenotypes.
