# FCCP

[[FCCP]] (carbonyl cyanide-4-trifluoromethoxyphenylhydrazone) is a protonophore that collapses the mitochondrial proton gradient, uncoupling oxidative phosphorylation and maximally stimulating [[Mitochondrial Electron Transport Chain]] electron flux and oxygen consumption.

## Use in the methylene blue study

In the Seahorse XF24 cellular bioenergetics assay, FCCP (300 nM) was injected after [[Oligomycin]] to reveal maximal respiration in HT-22 cells treated with [[Methylene blue]] and derivatives [^1]. Under FCCP, the groups treated with MB, [[Toluidine Blue O]], and [[Neutral Red]] showed higher maximal respiration than vehicle control, confirming that these compounds enhance oxidative phosphorylation capacity rather than merely resting OCR [^1].
