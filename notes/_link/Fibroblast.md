# Fibroblast

[[Fibroblast|Fibroblasts]] are mesenchymal cells responsible for synthesizing and remodeling the [[ECM|extracellular matrix (ECM)]]. In [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis|idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF)]], [[Fibroblast|fibroblasts]]—particularly those adjacent to fibroblast foci—become a central effector of fibrotic remodeling, and their acquisition of a [[Senescence|senescent]], apoptosis-resistant, profibrotic phenotype is a defining feature of disease.

## Senescent Fibroblasts in IPF

IPF-derived [[Fibroblast|fibroblasts]] exhibit a senescent phenotype characterized by resistance to [[Apoptosis|apoptosis]], sustained metabolic activity, and increased ECM synthesis. They actively maintain a profibrotic microenvironment by amplifying fibrogenic signaling and responding to the [[SASP|senescence-associated secretory phenotype]] of neighboring senescent [[Alveolar Type II Cells|alveolar type II cells]].

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - cellular_senescence_ipf_diseases-14-00201|Cellular Senescence in IPF (Baurzhan et al., 2026)]]
> Fibroblasts derived from IPF patients exhibit a senescent phenotype (apoptosis resistance, sustained metabolic activity, increased ECM synthesis) and amplify key fibrogenic signaling pathways. Senescent epithelial SASP components—TGF-β and CTGF—stimulate fibroblast proliferation and myofibroblast differentiation.

## Profibrotic Reprogramming

- [[TGFβ|TGF-β]]/[[SMAD2|SMAD2/3]] signaling drives fibroblast-to-myofibroblast differentiation and ECM deposition.
- [[SASP|SASP]] factors ([[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], [[TGFβ|TGF-β]], [[Matrix Metalloproteinase|MMPs]], [[Connective Tissue Growth Factor|CTGF]]) create a self-perpetuating loop with senescent epithelium.
- [[PTEN]] loss → [[PI3K]]/[[Akt|AKT]] signaling supports senescent fibroblast survival and apoptosis resistance.

## Connections

- [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis]] — Senescent fibroblasts are a hallmark of IPF and drive ECM deposition.
- [[SASP]] — SASP from senescent epithelium activates fibroblasts in a paracrine loop.
- [[Alveolar Type II Cells]] — Senescent AT2 cells secrete SASP that reprograms fibroblasts.
- [[TGFβ]] — Central profibrotic signal driving myofibroblast differentiation.
- [[Senolytics]] — Senolytic clearance of senescent fibroblasts reduces fibrosis in models.
