# PQQ

**Pyrroloquinoline quinone ([[PQQ]])** is a small redox-active compound that functions as a vitamin-like cofactor in bacteria and, in mammals, as a bioactive nutrient. It is a potent promoter of [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis|mitochondrial biogenesis]] and a stimulant of [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] function.

## Mechanism

PQQ modulates cellular redox state and activates signaling (including [[PGC-1α]]-associated pathways) that increase mitochondrial number and efficiency. It acts synergistically with [[Ubiquinone|CoQ10/ubiquinol]] — ubiquinol optimizes [[Electron Transport Chain|electron transport chain]] function while PQQ stimulates new mitochondrion formation.
