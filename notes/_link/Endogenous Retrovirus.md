# Endogenous Retrovirus

Endogenous retroviruses (ERVs) are retroviral sequences that infected the germline of ancestors and became permanently integrated into host genomes, propagating vertically as inherited elements. Present across vertebrates (including mice and humans, where the human subset is termed [[Human Endogenous Retrovirus|HERV]]), ERVs are normally kept silent by DNA methylation and repressive histone marks. In senescence and aging, their epigenetic silencing collapses, yielding viral transcripts and proteins that activate innate immunity and contribute to the [[SASP]] and [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]].

## Definition

ERVs resemble proviruses with gag, pol, and env genes but are typically defective and cannot complete replication. They comprise a substantial fraction of mammalian genomes (≈8% in humans as HERVs; larger proportions in some species). Their regulatory long terminal repeats (LTRs) can act as alternative promoters/enhancers for host genes.

## Activation in Senescence

Epigenetic erosion in senescence — global and focal [[DNA Methylation|DNA hypomethylation]], loss of H3K9me3 (via shifts in [[SUV39H1]]/[[KDM4]]) and H3K27me3 (via [[EZH2]]/[[JMJD3]]), and heterochromatin decompaction such as [[Senescence-Associated Distension of Satellites|SADS]] — relieves ERV silencing. The corepressor [[KAP1]] and heterochromatin factor [[HP1alpha]], supported by [[Sirtuin 6|SIRT6]] and [[RB1]], normally package ERV/LINE-1 loci into heterochromatin; their decline in senescence permits ERV transcription.

## Role in Inflammaging / SASP

ERV RNA is reverse-transcribed into cDNA in the cytoplasm, where it is sensed by [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS–STING]] (and RIG-I-like receptors), triggering type I interferon and NF-κB-driven SASP. In humans, the HERV subset releases retrovirus-like particles that can infect neighboring cells, spreading senescence. Thus ERV derepression is a conserved, cross-species engine of senescence-associated inflammation.

## Therapeutic Angles

Blocking ERV (and LINE-1) reverse transcription with [[Nucleoside Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitor|nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors (NRTIs)]] reduces interferon inflammation in vivo and SASP in cells, supporting NRTI repurposing (e.g., emtricitabine, NCT04500847) as [[Senomorphic|senomorphics]] targeting the retrotransposon axis.
