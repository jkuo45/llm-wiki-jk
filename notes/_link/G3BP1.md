# G3BP1

G3BP1 (Ras-GTPase-activating protein SH3-domain-binding protein 1) is a protein that assists [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS]] recognition of and binding to cytoplasmic chromatin fragments (CCFs), which is essential for downstream SASP regulation.

## Mechanism of Action

- G3BP1 assists [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS]] in recognizing cytoplasmic DNA
- It facilitates cGAS binding to cytoplasmic chromatin fragments (CCFs)
- G3BP1 is essential for downstream SASP regulation
- G3BP1 is also involved in stress granule formation

## Role in Senescence

- G3BP1 is required for cGAS–STING activation by cytoplasmic DNA
- It links cytoplasmic DNA sensing to SASP induction
- G3BP1 may be a regulatory point for SASP control

## Clinical Relevance

- G3BP1 is a therapeutic target in viral infections
- G3BP1 inhibitors may modulate SASP
- G3BP1 is implicated in neurodegenerative diseases
