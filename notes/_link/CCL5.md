# CCL5 (RANTES)

CCL5 (C-C motif chemokine ligand 5), also known as RANTES (Regulated on Activation, Normal T Cell Expressed and Secreted), is a chemokine that recruits immune cells and is implicated in cancer progression and reproductive aging as a SASP factor.

## Mechanism of Action

- CCL5 is secreted by senescent cells, particularly therapy-induced senescent (TIS) cancer cells
- It activates MYC–cyclin D signaling to promote cancer cell proliferation
- CCL5 contributes to non-small-cell lung cancer progression when secreted by TIS cancer cells
- In reproductive aging, CCL5 impairs preantral follicle development when secreted by senescent theca and interstitial cells

## Role in SASP

- CCL5 is a SASP chemokine that can promote tumorigenesis
- It is secreted by senescent cells in the tumor microenvironment
- CCL5 contributes to age-related decline in reproductive function

## Clinical Relevance

- CCL5/CCR5 signaling is a therapeutic target in cancer and HIV (maraviroc)
- CCL5 is elevated in age-related inflammatory conditions
- CCL5 may serve as a biomarker for senescence in reproductive tissues
