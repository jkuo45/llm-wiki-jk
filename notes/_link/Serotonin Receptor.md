# Serotonin Receptor

[[Serotonin Receptor]]s (5-HT receptors) are the molecular targets through which [[Serotonin]] exerts its effects. Excessive serotonergic agonism at these receptors — from drug combinations such as a [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]] plus an [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors|SSRI]] — produces [[Serotonin Syndrome]].
