## Overview

MDH2 (malate dehydrogenase 2, mitochondrial) is a key [[TCA cycle]] enzyme that catalyzes the reversible conversion of [[Malate]] to [[Oxaloacetate]] in the mitochondrial matrix, reducing [[NAD+|NAD⁺]] to NADH in the process. Alongside [[MDH1]], it forms the mitochondrial arm of the [[Malate-Aspartate Shuttle]], which transfers reducing equivalents across the inner mitochondrial membrane.
