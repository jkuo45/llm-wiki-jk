# PDE5 Inhibitors

[[PDE5 Inhibitors]] are a class of drugs that block phosphodiesterase type 5, increasing cyclic GMP and promoting vasodilation. Clinically they include sildenafil and tadalafil, used for erectile dysfunction, pulmonary arterial hypertension, and benign prostatic hyperplasia.

## Anti-Aging Relevance

Beyond their primary indications, PDE5 inhibitors suppress inflammation-driven colorectal cancer in mice and are cited as conventional agents that prevent or treat more than one disease—thus qualifying as "somewhat anti-aging" drugs ([Blagosklonny, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6814615/)). They are proposed components of a combined anti-aging formulation with [[Rapamycin]], [[Metformin]], [[Aspirin]], and [[ACE Inhibitors]].

> [!note] Multi-indication rationale
> Aging is the aggregate of age-related diseases; drugs effective against several conditions may extend life and offset one another's side effects when combined with mTOR inhibition.
