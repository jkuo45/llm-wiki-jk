# Mitochondrial Superoxide

[[Mitochondrial Superoxide]] is the upstream [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] produced at [[Complex I]] and [[Complex III]] of the [[Mitochondrial Electron Transport Chain]]. [[Methylene blue]] reduces its generation by shuttling electrons and bypassing damaged complexes, an antioxidant/neuroprotective mechanism.
