# IGFBP3

IGFBP3 (Insulin-like growth factor-binding protein 3) is a protein encoded by a direct [[p53]] target gene. It is part of the p53-dependent SASP profile and contributes to immunosurveillance of senescent cells.

## Mechanism of Action

- IGFBP3 is a direct [[p53]] target gene
- It is part of the p53-dependent SASP profile
- IGFBP3 contributes to immunosurveillance of senescent cells
- IGFBP3 modulates insulin-like growth factor signaling

## Role in SASP

- IGFBP3 is a p53-arm SASP factor
- It contributes to immune surveillance of senescent cells
- IGFBP3 is induced by p53 activation

## Clinical Relevance

- IGFBP3 is a biomarker for p53 activity
- IGFBP3 is implicated in cancer and aging
- IGFBP3 may be a target for modulating SASP
