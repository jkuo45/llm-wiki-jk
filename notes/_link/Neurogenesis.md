# Neurogenesis
**Neurogenesis** is the process by which nervous system cells (neurons) are produced from [[Neural Stem Cell|neural stem cells]] and [[Progenitor Cell|progenitor cells]]. It occurs during embryonic development and persists in specific adult brain regions like the [[Hippocampus]] and [[Subventricular Zone]].
## Regulation
Adult neurogenesis is regulated by [[Exercise]], [[Caloric Restriction]], [[BDNF]], and environmental enrichment. It declines with [[Aging]] due to reduced [[Notch Signaling|Notch]] and [[Wnt signaling|Wnt]] pathway activity.
