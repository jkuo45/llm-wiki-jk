# H3K122ac

H3K122ac is the acetylation of lysine 122, a residue located within the histone H3 core (near the nucleosome dyad) rather than the N-terminal tail. Because K122 lies at the H3–H4 interface and DNA entry/exit points, its acetylation destabilizes the nucleosome and promotes chromatin accessibility. In senescence, H3K122ac is enriched at senescence-activated [[Super-enhancer|super-enhancers]] that drive [[SASP]] expression, as noted in the npj Aging review (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2).

## Definition

Unlike tail acetyl marks (e.g., [[H3K27ac]]), H3K122ac is a core-domain modification. Acetylation at K122 weakens histone–DNA contacts, increasing nucleosome mobility and transcription-factor access — a potent enabler of active enhancers.

## Writer, Reader, Eraser

- **Writer:** p300/CBP and other HATs acetylate K122 at highly active regulatory regions.
- **Reader:** Bromodomain proteins (e.g., [[BRD4]]) recognize the acetyl mark; the altered nucleosome also presents a distinct biophysical state to remodelers.
- **Eraser:** HDACs remove H3K122ac.

## Role in SASP and Senescence

Senescence-activated super-enhancers are enriched in [[H3K4me1]] and multiple acetyl marks — H3K27ac, [[H3K18ac]], H3K122ac, and [[H4K5ac]] (Sen et al., 2019, PMID 30753809). These SEs drive senescence-specific gene expression, including SASP factors. Additionally, loss of the reader [[BRD4]] is associated with emergence of H3K122ac (along with H3K27ac and [[H3K4me3]]) at senescence-specific loci after H3.3 clipping, enhancing chromatin accessibility and SASP transcription (Yang et al., 2023, PMID 36800343). Thus H3K122ac both marks the open SA-SE landscape and rises at senescence loci when BRD4 protection is lost.

## Mechanistic Details

Because K122 acetylation directly destabilizes nucleosomes, H3K122ac is a strong physical correlate of enhancer activity. At SA-SEs it complements the tail acetyl marks to enforce an open, loop-competent chromatin state enabling [[Enhancer-Promoter Looping|enhancer-promoter looping]] within [[Topologically Associating Domain|TADs]].

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

As an SE-associated acetyl mark, H3K122ac is downstream of p300 and upstream of BET readers, both actionable [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] nodes (see [[KDM4]], [[DOT1L]], [[H3K27ac]]).
