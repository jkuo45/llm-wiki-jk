# 3-Hydroxykynurenine

**3-Hydroxykynurenine** (3-HK) is a redox-active intermediate of the [[Kynurenine Pathway]] produced from [[Kynurenine]] by the enzyme kynurenine 3-monooxygenase (KMO). It is further metabolized to [[Quinolinic Acid]] (via 3-hydroxyanthranilic acid).

## Redox Activity

3-HK is highly redox-active, capable of generating [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] through autooxidation. It contributes to [[Oxidative Stress]] in conditions where the KP is overactivated, particularly in the brain and [[Tumor Microenvironment]].

## Pathological Role

Elevated 3-HK is implicated in:
- **[[Neurodegeneration]]:** 3-HK generates ROS and sensitizes neurons to excitotoxic damage
- **[[Cataracts]]:** 3-HK accumulation contributes to lens protein oxidation
- **[[Huntington's disease]]:** Elevated KMO activity and 3-HK levels are observed
- **HIV-associated neurocognitive disorder**
- **[[Diabetes Mellitus]]:** KP dysregulation increases 3-HK levels
