# Hematopoiesis

**Hematopoiesis** is the process by which blood cells are formed from [[Hematopoietic Stem Cell|hematopoietic stem cells]] (HSCs). It occurs primarily in the bone marrow and is regulated by [[notes/_link/Transcription|transcription factors]], [[notes/_link/Epigenome|epigenetic]] modifiers, and the [[Stem Cell Exhaustion|stem cell niche]].
