# Mitochondrial Permeability Transition Pore

The mitochondrial permeability transition pore (MPTP) is a high-conductance channel that, when open, makes the inner [[Mitochondria]]l membrane permeable to solutes up to ~1.5 kDa. Opening is triggered by matrix Ca²⁺ overload together with [[ROS]], depleted adenine nucleotides, and elevated inorganic phosphate. Sustained MPTP opening collapses the proton-motive force, causes osmotic matrix swelling, ruptures the outer membrane, and releases [[Cytochrome c]] and other pro-apoptotic factors, driving [[Apoptosis]] and necrotic cell death.

The pore is a multiprotein assembly whose sensitivity is set by Cyclophilin D (CypD, encoded by PPIF) bound to the F₀F₁-ATP synthase. While its exact molecular composition is debated, the adenine nucleotide translocator, voltage-dependent anion channel, and ATP synthase c-subunit ring have all been implicated. Cyclosporin A inhibits CypD and blocks MPTP, conferring protection in ischemia–reperfusion injury.

> [!warning] MPTP is the terminal effector of calcium- and ROS-driven mitochondrial catastrophe; its inhibition is a major cardioprotective and neuroprotective strategy.

MPTP integrates several redox and calcium nodes: [[MICU1]]/[[MICU2]] gating of mitochondrial Ca²⁺, [[Ryanodine Receptor]] leak, and [[Oxidative Stress]] all converge on pore opening. Because sirtuin activation and [[Autophagy]] preserve mitochondrial integrity, MPTP is a critical endpoint linking redox/longevity pathways to cell survival.

## Connections

  - [[Mitochondria]]: The organelle whose membranes the pore spans and whose swelling it causes.
  - [[Apoptosis]]: The cell-death program executed when MPTP opens and releases intermembrane proteins.
  - [[Cytochrome c]]: Pro-apoptotic factor released following MPTP-driven outer-membrane rupture.
  - [[ROS]]: A core trigger and amplifier of pore opening.
  - [[Oxidative Stress]]: The redox state that promotes sustained MPTP opening.
  - [[MICU1]]: Regulator whose loss permits Ca²⁺ overload that opens the pore.
  - [[Ryanodine Receptor]]: Upstream Ca²⁺ source that can drive pore-opening conditions.
  - [[Autophagy]]: Quality-control pathway that removes damaged mitochondria before they reach MPTP threshold.
