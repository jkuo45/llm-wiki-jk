# Methemoglobin Reductase

[[Methemoglobin Reductase]] (NADH-cytochrome b5 reductase, cytochrome b5 reductase) is the physiologic enzyme that reduces [[Methemoglobin]] (ferric Fe3+) back to functional [[Hemoglobin]] (ferrous Fe2+) in erythrocytes, using [[NADH]] as electron donor.

## Role in Methylene Blue Therapy

In acquired [[Methemoglobinemia]] treatment, [[Methylene blue]] is itself reduced by **NADPH-dependent methemoglobin reductase** (a related flavoprotein) to leucomethylene blue, which then nonenzymatically reduces ferric heme. Thus methylene blue acts as an electron carrier recruiting the reductase system to accelerate MetHb clearance.

> [!info] Why G6PD matters
> The NADPH needed by methemoglobin reductase comes from the [[Pentose Phosphate Pathway]], which is impaired in [[Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase]] deficiency — another reason methylene blue fails/is contraindicated there.
