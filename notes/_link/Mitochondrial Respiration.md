# Mitochondrial Respiration

[[Mitochondrial Respiration]] is the oxygen-consuming process of ATP synthesis by the [[Mitochondrial Electron Transport Chain]]. [[Methylene blue]] enhances respiration when complexes I/III are impaired by providing an alternative electron route.
