# Selective Androgen Receptor Modulators

## Overview

**Selective androgen receptor modulators (SARMs)** are non-steroidal ligands that activate the [[Akt|androgen receptor]] in muscle and bone, producing anabolic effects with fewer virilizing or hepatotoxic liabilities than exogenous testosterone.

## Mechanism of Action & Applications

SARMs bind androgen receptors, recruiting coactivators that drive [[mTORC1|mTORC1]]/translation and counteract [[Muscle Atrophy]] and [[Cachexia|cachexia]]; they are investigated for [[Sarcopenia|sarcopenia]], muscular dystrophy, and wasting syndromes. They are distinct from [[Myostatin]] inhibitors but share anabolic intent.
