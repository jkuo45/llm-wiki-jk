# Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors

[[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors]] ([[SSRI]]s) are antidepressant drugs that block the serotonin transporter, increasing synaptic [[Serotonin]]. Combining them with a [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]] (including [[Methylene blue]]'s MAO-A inhibition) can precipitate [[Serotonin Syndrome]].

## Connections

- [[Serotonin]] — increased synaptic level
- [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]] / [[Methylene blue]] — dangerous combination
- [[Serotonin Syndrome]] — adverse interaction
- [[Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors]] — related class
