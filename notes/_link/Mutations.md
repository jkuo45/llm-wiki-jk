# Mutations

**Mutations** are permanent, heritable alterations to the DNA nucleotide sequence. They arise from replication errors, [[DNA Damage|DNA damage]], and environmental mutagens, and accumulate stochastically over an organism's lifetime.

## Role in Aging Frameworks

In the [[Information Theory of Aging]], mutations represent "hardware" damage — irreversible and distinct from the "software" (epigenetic) information loss that the theory emphasizes as the primary driver of aging. The [[Relocalization of Chromatin Modifiers (RCM) Hypothesis]] and the [[ICE Mouse|ICE mouse]] model deliberately induce [[Double-Strand Break|double-strand breaks]] *without* mutations, isolating epigenetic consequences of repair from mutational load and demonstrating that epigenetic information loss alone can drive aging.
