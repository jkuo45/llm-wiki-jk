# KAP1

KAP1 (KRAB-associated protein 1), also known as TRIM28 or TIF1β, is a ubiquitously expressed transcriptional corepressor and member of the tripartite motif (TRIM) family. In senescence and aging, KAP1 is a critical silencer of retrotransposable elements: the deacetylase [[Sirtuin 6|SIRT6]] mono-ADP-ribosylates KAP1, promoting its interaction with the heterochromatin factor [[HP1alpha]] to package LINE-1 ([[LINE-1]]) elements into repressive heterochromatin.

## Overview

KAP1/TRIM28 contains an N-terminal RBCC/TRIM domain, a central HP1-binding motif, a PHD finger, and a C-terminal bromodomain. It is the universal corepressor for KRAB-zinc-finger transcription factors, recruiting SETDB1 (H3K9 methyltransferase), HDACs, and HP1 to establish H3K9me3-dominated silent chromatin at transposons and endogenous retroviruses.

## Structure & Function

Through its HP1-binding domain and collaboration with SETDB1, KAP1 builds and maintains facultative/repetitive heterochromatin. Its repressive activity is dynamically regulated by post-translational modifications, notably SUMOylation and, in the senescence context, SIRT6-dependent mono-ADP-ribosylation, which enhance its heterochromatinizing function.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

The review places KAP1 on the retrotransposon-derepression axis that drives [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]] and SASP. [[RB1]] and [[Sirtuin 6|SIRT6]] are enriched at the 5′UTR of LINE-1 loci in proliferating cells, where SIRT6 mono-ADP-ribosylates KAP1, promoting KAP1–HP1α interaction and heterochromatinization of L1 (refs. 136, 137). During senescence, RB1 declines and SIRT6 is depleted from L1 loci (especially in aging and after DNA damage), weakening KAP1-mediated repression. LINE-1 elements then become transcriptionally active, their cDNA accumulates in the cytoplasm, and cGAS–STING sensing triggers type I interferon and SASP. KAP1 is thus a key guardian whose failure licenses retrotransposon-driven inflammation in senescence.

## Mechanistic Details

- KAP1–HP1α recruitment via SIRT6 mono-ADP-ribosylation is the molecular switch governing L1 silencing.
- Loss of SIRT6/RB1 in senescence decompacts L1 5′UTRs, paralleling heterochromatin loss at pericentromeres ([[Senescence-Associated Distension of Satellites|SADS]]).
- L1 cDNA → cGAS-STING → SASP is the downstream inflammatory cascade; [[Nucleoside Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitor|NRTIs]] interrupt it.

## Therapeutic Relevance

Because KAP1/SIRT6 silencing of retrotransposons limits SASP, strategies that preserve SIRT6 activity or bolster KAP1 repression may be [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]]. Conversely, NRTIs such as emtricitabine (NCT04500847) blunt L1/HERV reverse-transcription inflammation and are candidate anti-inflammaging agents.
