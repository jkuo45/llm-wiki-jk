## Overview

**RAW264.7** is an immortalized mouse macrophage cell line derived from an ascitic tumor induced by Abelson murine leukemia virus in a male BALB/c mouse. It exhibits adherent, monocyte/macrophage morphology and can be activated toward M1 (pro-inflammatory) or M2 (anti-inflammatory) phenotypes.

## Origin and Genotype

Established in 1978, RAW264.7 carries v-Abl transformant sequences and retains many functions of primary [[Macrophages]], including Fc receptor-mediated phagocytosis, cytokine secretion, and nitric oxide production upon LPS/IFN-γ stimulation.

## Common Research Uses

RAW264.7 is a standard model for [[Inflammation]], Toll-like receptor signaling, oxidative burst, and polarization studies. It is widely used to screen anti-inflammatory and senomorphic compounds and to model macrophage involvement in metabolic and age-related disease.

## Relevance to Aging, Cancer, and Longevity

Macrophages are central to the [[Senescence-Messaging Secretome]] and chronic low-grade inflammation ("inflammaging") that accompanies [[Aging]]. RAW264.7 helps dissect how senolytics and immunomodulators modulate macrophage phenotype, with implications for [[Cancer]] microenvironment remodeling and tissue repair.
