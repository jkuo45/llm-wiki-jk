# Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors

[[Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors]] ([[SNRI]]s) are antidepressants that block reuptake of both [[Serotonin]] and [[Norepinephrine]]. Like [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors]], they are contraindicated with [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]]s (including [[Methylene blue]]) due to [[Serotonin Syndrome]] risk.
