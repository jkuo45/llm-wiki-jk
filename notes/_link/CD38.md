# CD38
CD38 is an ectoenzyme that degrades NAD+ and its precursors like NMN, with its levels and activity increasing during aging and contributing to mitochondrial dysfunction.
## Enzymatic Activities
CD38 catalyzes multiple reactions using [[NAD+]] as substrate:
- **ADP-ribosyl cyclase activity**: Converts NAD⁺ into **cyclic ADP-ribose (cADPR)**, a second messenger that mobilizes intracellular Ca²⁺ from the endoplasmic reticulum.
- **Hydrolase activity**: Hydrolyzes cADPR back to ADP-ribose (ADPR).
- **NAADP synthase activity**: Produces nicotinic acid adenine dinucleotide phosphate (NAADP) from NADP⁺, another Ca²⁺ mobilizing agent.
Because CD38 is a major consumer of [[NAD+]], elevated CD38 activity — particularly during aging and [[Inflammation|chronic inflammation]] — is a leading driver of the age-related decline in NAD⁺ levels.
## Role in NAD+ Metabolism and Aging
CD38 expression increases markedly with age, driven partly by [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]] and infiltration of CD38-high immune cells into tissues. This activity depletes NAD⁺, reducing the activity of [[NAD+]]-dependent enzymes including [[Sirtuins|sirtuins]] (especially [[SIRT3]]) and [[PARP1]].
- **CD38 knockout (KO) mice** or pharmacological inhibition preserves NAD⁺ and its precursor [[Nicotinamide Mononucleotide|NMN]], boosting [[SIRT3]] activity and improving mitochondrial function, exercise capacity, and metabolic health in aged mice.
- The CD38 inhibitor **78c** extended lifespan ~14% in male mice (with adverse reactions noted in female mice), supporting CD38 as a target for healthspan interventions.
- Natural inhibitors include [[Quercetin]] and apigenin, though these are weaker and pleiotropic.
## Immune Function
CD38 plays important roles in the immune system:
- Highly expressed on activated lymphocytes (T cells, B cells, NK cells), neutrophils, monocytes, and dendritic cells.
- Regulates immune cell recruitment, activation, cytokine release, and phagocytosis.
- Mediates **oxytocin release** from hypothalamic neurons, linking it to social behavior; CD38 KO mice show deficits in social memory and parental nurturing.
## Role in Cancer
CD38 is a major therapeutic target in oncology:
- **Highly overexpressed** on malignant plasma cells in [[Multiple Myeloma]] (primary target for anti-CD38 antibodies).
- Also expressed on other hematologic malignancies (certain lymphomas, leukemias) and on immunosuppressive cells in the **tumor microenvironment (TME)**: Tregs, MDSCs (myeloid-derived suppressor cells), TAMs (tumor-associated macrophages), and Bregs.
- In tumors, CD38 can promote cancer cell survival, migration, and proliferation via cADPR-mediated calcium signaling, and immunosuppression via NAD⁺ depletion and adenosine production.

Mechanisms of action include:
- **ADCC** (antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity) via NK cells
- **ADCP** (antibody-dependent cellular phagocytosis)
- **CDC** (complement-dependent cytotoxicity)
- Direct apoptosis (stronger for [[Isatuximab]])
- Immunomodulation: depletion of CD38⁺ Tregs/MDSCs, boosting T/NK anti-tumor activity
### Emerging Strategies
- **Small-molecule enzymatic inhibitors** (e.g., **RBN013209**, **78c**): Block NAD⁺ consumption and cADPR production; explored for immunostimulation and NAD⁺ boosting.
- **CAR-T cell therapy**: CD38-directed CAR-T for MM; CD38 KO in CAR-T to prevent fratricide and exhaustion.
- **Bispecifics, ADCs, nanobodies, radioligand therapy**: Expand specificity and therapeutic index.
- **Combinations**: With IMiDs (immunomodulatory drugs), proteasome inhibitors, checkpoint blockade (e.g., anti-PD-1).
## Risks and Downsides of CD38 Inhibition
| Risk Category | Notes |
|---|---|
| Immune/infection risk | Depletes CD38⁺ immune cells; impairs humoral responses; increased infection rates with mAbs (e.g., pneumonia) |
| Sex-specific effects | In mice treated with 78c, severe adverse reactions in females requiring euthanasia |
| Neurological effects | CD38 KO mice show social memory deficits, altered anxiety, oxytocin signaling disruption |
| Homeostatic disruption | Over-inhibition could perturb fine-tuned NAD⁺ regulation |
| Cancer context complexity | Mixed roles; inhibition aids some TME contexts but CD38 has pro- and anti-tumor facets |
