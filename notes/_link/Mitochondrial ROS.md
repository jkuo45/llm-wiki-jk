# Mitochondrial ROS
**Mitochondrial ROS** (mtROS) are [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] generated primarily at [[Complex I]] and [[Complex III|Complex III]] of the [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] electron transport chain. They are a major source of cellular [[Oxidative Stress]] and play dual roles in [[Apoptosis|apoptotic signaling]] and [[mitohormetic adaptation]].
