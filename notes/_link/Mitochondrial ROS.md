# Mitochondrial ROS

**Mitochondrial ROS** (mtROS) are [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] generated primarily at [[Complex I]] and [[Complex III|Complex III]] of the [[notes/_link/Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] electron transport chain. They are a major source of cellular [[Oxidative Stress]] and play dual roles in [[notes/_link/Apoptosis|apoptotic signaling]] and [[mitohormetic adaptation]].

## Connections

- [[notes/_link/Mitochondria]] — Source organelle
- [[Oxidative Stress]] — mtROS are major contributors
- [[Mitohormesis]] — Low-level mtROS trigger adaptive responses
- [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]] — High mtROS trigger cytochrome c release
