# S100A13

S100A13 is a calcium-binding protein that is involved in the translocation of cleaved [[IL-1α]] from the cell surface to the nucleus. This translocation depends on copper and/or calcium homeostasis and activates transcription of other pro-inflammatory SASP factors.

## Mechanism of Action

- S100A13 is a calcium-binding protein
- It facilitates the translocation of cleaved [[IL-1α]] from the cell surface to the nucleus
- This translocation depends on copper and/or calcium homeostasis
- Nuclear IL-1α activates transcription of pro-inflammatory SASP factors

## Role in SASP

- S100A13 is required for IL-1α-mediated SASP activation
- It links calcium signaling to SASP regulation
- S100A13 may be a target for modulating SASP

## Clinical Relevance

- S100A13 is implicated in cancer and inflammation
- S100A13 may be a therapeutic target
- S100A13 is involved in copper-dependent signaling
