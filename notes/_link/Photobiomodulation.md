# Photobiomodulation

**Photobiomodulation ([[Photobiomodulation|PBM]])** is the use of red and near-infrared (NIR) light (roughly 600–1000 nm) to modulate cellular function. Daily morning sunlight or targeted red/NIR devices deliver photons that are absorbed by [[Complex IV|cytochrome c oxidase (Complex IV)]] in the [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]], enhancing [[ATP]] production and reducing oxidative stress.

## Mechanism

Cytochrome c oxidase absorbs NIR photons, which accelerates electron transfer and the proton gradient, boosting ATP synthesis and transiently modulating [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] signaling in a hormetic direction. This supports recovery and is listed among the environmental mitohormetic exposures alongside cold, heat, and exercise.
