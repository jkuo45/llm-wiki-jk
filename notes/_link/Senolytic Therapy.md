# Senolytic Therapy

Senolytic Therapy refers to treatments that selectively eliminate senescent cells. The most well-known senolytic combination is [[Dasatinib]] plus [[Quercetin]] (D+Q), which targets anti-apoptotic pathways that senescent cells depend on for survival.

## Mechanism of Action

- Senolytics target anti-apoptotic pathways in senescent cells
- Senescent cells are primed for apoptosis but survive through upregulation of anti-apoptotic proteins
- Senolytics trigger apoptosis in senescent cells
- D+Q is the most studied senolytic combination

## Role in Aging

- Senolytic therapy reduces senescence burden
- It improves health span in aged animals
- Senolytics are being tested in clinical trials for age-related diseases

## Clinical Relevance

- D+Q is in clinical trials for diabetic kidney disease, IPF, and other conditions
- SASP biomarkers (IL-6, MMP-9, GM-CSF) are used to evaluate senolytic efficacy
- Senolytic therapy is a promising anti-aging intervention
