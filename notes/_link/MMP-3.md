# MMP-3

MMP-3 (Matrix metalloproteinase-3), also known as stromelysin-1, is a protease that degrades extracellular matrix components. It is a SASP factor involved in tissue remodelling and cancer progression.

## Mechanism of Action

- MMP-3 degrades extracellular matrix proteins
- It is secreted by senescent fibroblasts
- MMP-3 contributes to tissue remodelling and cancer progression
- MMP-3 cooperates with other SASP factors to promote tumorigenesis

## Role in SASP

- MMP-3 is a pro-tumorigenic SASP protease
- It contributes to extracellular matrix degradation
- MMP-3 may be a target for anti-cancer interventions

## Clinical Relevance

- MMP-3 is a biomarker for tissue remodelling
- MMP-3 is implicated in arthritis and cancer
- MMP-3 inhibitors are being developed
