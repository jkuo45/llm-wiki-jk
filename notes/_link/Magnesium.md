# Magnesium

**[[Magnesium]]** is an essential mineral involved in over 300 biochemical reactions in the body.

## Role as a Cofactor
[[Magnesium]] is a critical cofactor for the [[notes/_link/COMT]] enzyme. Adequate magnesium levels are necessary for [[notes/_link/COMT]] to effectively perform the [[Methylation]] reactions required to break down [[Catecholamines]] and [[Estrogen]] metabolites.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/COMT]]: Requires magnesium to function properly.
- [[Methylation]]: The biochemical process supported by magnesium.
- [[Catecholamines]]: Substrates that COMT breaks down using magnesium as a cofactor.
