# Phenelzine

[[Phenelzine]] is a non-selective, irreversible [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]] (MAOI) used as an antidepressant. Its mechanism — raising synaptic [[Serotonin]], [[Dopamine]], and [[Norepinephrine]] by blocking their catabolism — is the same class of action exhibited at low doses by [[Methylene blue]] (a selective MAO-A inhibitor). Both share the [[Serotonin Syndrome]] risk when combined with serotonergic drugs.
