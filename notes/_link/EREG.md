# EREG

**EREG** (epiregulin) is a transmembrane ligand of the EGFR/ErbB family. Like [[Amphiregulin|AREG]], it is secreted by senescent stromal cells as part of the [[SASP|SASP]] and promotes cancer therapy resistance and immunosuppression.

## In the Apigenin Study

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> Senescent stromal cells elevated EREG (with AREG) in conditioned media, conferring chemoresistance; apigenin treatment reduced this. In vivo, mitoxantrone raised circulating EREG (and AREG), and apigenin administration essentially reversed the elevation.
