# CD73
CD73 is an ectoenzyme that can degrade NMN into [[NR]], thereby sustaining NAD+ synthesis.
CD73 (Ecto-5'-nucleotidase) is a cell-surface enzyme that converts AMP to extracellular adenosine, often downregulated during [[Intermittent Fasting]] to modulate the tumor immune environment.
## Function
CD73 (NT5E) is an ecto-5'-nucleotidase that converts extracellular AMP to adenosine, regulating purinergic signaling and [[Immunometabolism]].
## Autophagy Connection
CD73-generated adenosine signaling via [[A2A receptor]] modulates [[Autophagy]] in T cells and cancer cells. CD73 expression correlates with autophagic activity in the tumor microenvironment.
