# Retinoic Acid

**Retinoic acid** (active vitamin A metabolite) is a potent inducer of cellular differentiation. In embryonic stem cell research it is applied to monolayer cultures (without [[Leukemia inhibitory factor|LIF]]) to drive mesenchymal differentiation and test the silencing of pluripotency.

> [!info] In the DGCR8 study
> [[DGCR8]]-deficient [[Embryonic Stem Cells|ES cells]] treated with retinoic acid still failed to fully silence pluripotency markers (Oct4, Rex1, Sox2, Nanog) and retained colony-forming ability — demonstrating that miRNA loss blocks differentiation even under strong inductive pressure.
