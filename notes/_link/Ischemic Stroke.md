# Ischemic Stroke

[[Ischemic Stroke]] is acute brain injury caused by arterial occlusion and resultant hypoxia–ischemia. [[Methylene blue]] is studied investigationaly for neuroprotection in stroke via [[Mitochondrial Electron Transport Chain]] bypass and [[Mitochondrial Superoxide]] reduction.
