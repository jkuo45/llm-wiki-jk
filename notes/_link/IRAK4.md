# IRAK4

**IRAK4** (interleukin-1 receptor-associated kinase 4) is the proximal kinase of MyD88-dependent IL-1R/TLR signaling, recruiting and activating [[IRAK1]] to drive [[NF-κB]] and MAPK activation.

## Role in Apigenin's SASP Suppression

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> Apigenin suppresses the [[SASP|SASP]] partly by inhibiting [[IL-1α]] signaling via [[IRAK1]], IRAK4, [[p38 MAPK]], and [[NF-κB]], reducing breast cancer cell aggressiveness[25].
