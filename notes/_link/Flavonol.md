# Flavonol

**Flavonols** are a subclass of [[Flavonoids|flavonoid]] [[Polyphenols|polyphenols]] built on a 3-hydroxyflavone skeleton (a flavonoid with a hydroxyl at the 3-position of the C ring). They are widely distributed in fruits and vegetables and are noted for [[Antioxidant|antioxidant]], anti-[[Inflammation|inflammatory]], and [[Senolytic|senotherapeutic]] activities.

## Key Members

- **Fisetin** (3,3′,4′,7-tetrahydroxyflavone) — most potent senolytic in a 10-flavonoid screen; extends [[Lifespan|lifespan]] in mice (Yousefzadeh et al., 2018).
- **Quercetin** — the comparator flavonol in the D+Q [[Senolytic|senolytic]] combination.
- **Myricetin, Kaempferol, Rutin (quercetin glycoside), Luteolin** (technically a flavone) — related polyphenols with overlapping biology.

> [!info] Structural relation of fisetin and quercetin
> Fisetin differs from [[Quercetin|quercetin]] by only a single hydroxyl group (at the 5-position), explaining their broadly similar biological activities; fisetin nonetheless outperformed quercetin as a senolytic in the screened panel.
