# Cardiovascular System

The **cardiovascular system** (heart and blood vessels) circulates blood to deliver oxygen and nutrients. Its **hemodynamic** parameters (blood pressure, flow) are one of the seven domains assessed in multi-system [[Biological Aging|biological aging]] scores.

> [!note] Inflammaging relevance
> Chronic [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]] contributes to cardiovascular disease and arterial stiffening with age; interventions that lower background inflammation (e.g., the [[Shingles Vaccine|shingles vaccine]]) may preserve cardiovascular hemodynamic aging.
