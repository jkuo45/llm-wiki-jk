# Myostatin

## Overview

**Myostatin** (GDF-8) is a transforming growth factor-β superfamily cytokine produced predominantly in [[Skeletal Muscle|skeletal muscle]] that restrains muscle growth. Loss-of-function yields markedly increased muscle mass.

## Mechanism of Action & Physiology

Myostatin binds activin type II receptors, activating [[SMAD|SMAD2/3]] to suppress myoblast proliferation/differentiation and antagonize [[mTORC1|mTORC1]]/[[Akt|Akt]] anabolic signaling, thereby promoting [[Muscle Atrophy]] and limiting [[Muscle Hypertrophy|hypertrophy]].

## Therapeutic Relevance

Myostatin inhibitors (antibodies, [[Selective Androgen Receptor Modulators|SARMs]] combinations) are explored for muscle-wasting diseases, [[Sarcopenia|sarcopenia]], and [[Cachexia|cachexia]].
