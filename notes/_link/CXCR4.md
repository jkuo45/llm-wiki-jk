# CXCR4

**CXCR4** (C-X-C chemokine receptor type 4, CD184) is a [[GPCR|G protein-coupled receptor]] that is the primary receptor for [[CXCL12]] (SDF-1). The CXCL12/CXCR4 axis is critical for [[Hematopoietic Stem Cell|hematopoietic stem cell]] homing, [[Angiogenesis]], [[Cancer|metastasis]], and [[Immune System|immune cell]] trafficking.

## Role in Senescence and Vascular Aging

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Senolytic Treatment With Fisetin Reverses Age‐Related Endothelial Dysfunction Partially Mediated by SASP Factor CXCL12|Mahoney et al. 2025]]
> In senescent [[Endothelial Cells|endothelial cells (ECs)]], [[CXCR4]] [[Messenger RNA|mRNA]] levels were **higher** with aging compared to young controls, yet *Cxcl12-Cxcr4* interaction strength was **decreased** — indicating defective cell-cell communication between senescent ECs, non-senescent ECs, [[Fibroblast|fibroblasts]], and mesothelial cells.
>
> This contrasts with [[ACKR3]] signaling (which increased with aging), suggesting a shift from CXCR4-dominant to ACKR3-dominant signaling in senescent ECs. [[Fisetin]] restored both signaling patterns to young-like levels.
>
> The [[CXCL12]]/[[CXCR4]]/[[ACKR3]] axis is a well-known regulator of vascular homeostasis, and the altered receptor balance in senescent ECs may contribute to age-related [[Endothelial Dysfunction|endothelial dysfunction]].

## Connections

- [[CXCL12]]: Sole canonical ligand of CXCR4
- [[ACKR3]]: Alternative CXCL12 receptor; ACKR3 signaling increases while CXCR4 signaling decreases with aging in ECs
- [[Endothelial Cells]]: CXCR4 expression increased in senescent ECs but functional signaling is impaired
- [[Endothelial Dysfunction]]: Altered CXCL12/CXCR4/ACKR3 axis may contribute to age-related vascular impairment
- [[Fisetin]]: Senolytic that restores young-like CXCL12-CXCR4 signaling patterns
