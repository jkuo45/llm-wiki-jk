# TLR4

## Overview

**TLR4 (Toll-like receptor 4)** is a transmembrane [[Toll-like Receptor|toll-like receptor]] that recognizes bacterial lipopolysaccharide (LPS) via the MD-2/CD14 complex. Unlike [[TLR3]], TLR4 engages **both** the MyD88 and [[TRIF]] adaptors.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

- **MyD88-dependent:** rapid [[NFκB|NF-κB]] and [[MAPK|MAPK]] activation → pro-inflammatory cytokines ([[TNF-alpha|TNF-α]], [[IL-6]]).
- **TRIF-dependent:** [[TRAF3]]→[[TBK1]]→[[IRF3]] → type I interferons; also contributes to [[NFκB]]/[[STAT1]] signaling.

## Pathology & Relevance

Excessive TLR4 signaling underlies endotoxic shock, sterile inflammation, and metabolic/[[Inflammation|inflammatory]] disease. Modulating TLR4 is a target for anti-inflammatory therapeutics; [[Urolithin A]]'s anti-inflammatory profile includes related [[TLR3]]/[[TRIF]] axis modulation.

## Connections

- [[TLR4]] → signals via → [[TRIF]] and MyD88
- [[TRIF]] → also used by → [[TLR3]]
- [[Urolithin A]] → modulates → [[TLR3]]/[[TRIF]] axis
