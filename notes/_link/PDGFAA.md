# PDGFAA

PDGFAA (Platelet-derived growth factor AA) is a growth factor secreted by senescent fibroblasts and endothelial cells in response to cutaneous wounds. It induces myofibroblast differentiation and contributes to wound healing.

## Mechanism of Action

- PDGFAA is secreted by senescent fibroblasts and endothelial cells
- It induces myofibroblast differentiation
- PDGFAA contributes to wound healing through vascularization and matrix production
- Removing senescent cells 24h following injury decreased myofibroblast differentiation

## Role in SASP

- PDGFAA is a beneficial SASP factor in wound healing
- It promotes tissue repair through myofibroblast differentiation
- PDGFAA demonstrates the beneficial roles of senescence

## Clinical Relevance

- PDGFAA is a therapeutic target for wound healing
- PDGFAA may be beneficial in tissue repair
- PDGFAA is implicated in fibrosis
