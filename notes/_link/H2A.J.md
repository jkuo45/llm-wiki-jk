# H2A.J

H2A.J is a vertebrate-specific H2A histone variant structurally related to H2A.X, characterized by an extended C-terminal tail. It is markedly induced in senescent cells and at sites of persistent DNA damage, where it fine-tunes chromatin organization to promote an inflammatory gene-expression program. In the review of the senescent epigenetic landscape (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2), H2A.J is highlighted as one of four H2A-family variants with a defined senescence role, alongside [[H2A.X]], [[macroH2A]] and others.

## Overview

H2A.J belongs to the H2A variant family (the most diverse histone subgroup). Its expression is low in proliferating cells but rises strongly in senescence, in aged human skin, and in mouse tissues exposed to persistent DNA damage. It localizes to DNA-damage sites and to the promoters of pro-inflammatory genes.

## Structure and Function

Like H2A.X, H2A.J carries a C-terminal extension; however, it is phosphorylated differently and is associated more with the chronic, unresolved DNA-damage response than with acute break repair. Its deposition/replacement at damaged chromatin alters nucleosome stability and local accessibility.

## Role in Senescence and SASP

H2A.J accumulates in human senescent fibroblasts, in aged human epidermis, and in multiple mouse tissues upon persistent DNA damage (Contrepois et al., 2017, PMID 28368001; Rübe et al., 2021, PMID 33558759). Its senescence functions include:

- **Marking persistent damage:** H2A.J decorates chronic DNA-damage foci and, in radiation-induced senescence, triggers the secretory phenotype (Isermann et al., 2020, PMID 33227801; Abd Al-Razaq et al., 2023, PMID 37093654).
- **Driving SASP:** H2A.J is enriched at [[SASP]] gene promoters, where it facilitates chromatin-accessibility changes that induce SASP expression (Contrepois et al., 2017). It thus helps convert persistent damage signaling into the inflammatory secretome.

## Mechanistic Details

By replacing canonical H2A at damage-proximal and inflammatory promoters, H2A.J remodels nucleosomes so that inflammatory transcription factors and co-activators can engage. This couples the unresolved DNA-damage response to SASP induction in a manner complementary to the γ-H2A.X/[[Cytoplasmic Chromatin Fragments|CCF]]/cGAS–STING axis. Because H2A variants are long-lived, accumulation of H2A.J reflects the durable chromatin memory of damage exposure in aging.

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

H2A.J is a strong candidate biomarker of persistent DNA damage and cellular senescence in aging tissues, and its role at SASP promoters makes it relevant to [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] strategies aimed at uncoupling damage from inflammation (see [[KDM4]], [[BRD4]]).
