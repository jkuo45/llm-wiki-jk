# Phenotype
A **phenotype** is the set of observable characteristics or traits of an organism, such as its morphology, development, biochemical or physiological properties, behavior, and products of behavior.
## Overview
- Determined by: [[Genotype]] and environmental influences.
- In aging: The aging phenotype is characterized by a decline in physiological function and increased vulnerability to disease.
