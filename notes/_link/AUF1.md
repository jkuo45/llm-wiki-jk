# AUF1

**AU-rich element RNA-binding protein 1 (AUF1)**, also known as HNRNPD, is an RNA-binding protein that targets mRNAs containing AU-rich elements (AREs) in their 3' untranslated regions for degradation.

## Role in SASP
AUF1 is a key negative regulator of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]]:
- It normally promotes the decay of transcripts for many pro-inflammatory cytokines and chemokines.
- In [[Senescent Cells]], the [[MAPKAPK2|MK2]] pathway (downstream of [[p38 MAPK]]) phosphorylates AUF1, leading to its inactivation or redistribution.
- This allows for the stabilization and increased production of SASP factors like [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]].

## Significance
The regulation of AUF1 activity is a critical control point for determining the intensity of the SASP.
