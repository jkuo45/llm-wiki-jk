# CXCL3

CXCL3 (C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 3), also known as GROγ (growth-regulated oncogene gamma) or MIP-2β, is an ELR+ C-X-C chemokine that signals through [[CXCR2]] (and weakly through CXCR1). It is the third member of the growth-regulated oncogene (GRO) cluster with [[CXCL1]] and [[CXCL2]], a neutrophil chemoattractant, and a conserved component of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] (Coppé et al., 2008, PMID 18948995).

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

- **Receptor binding**: CXCL3 binds [[CXCR2]] (high affinity) and CXCR1 (low affinity). Receptor engagement activates Gαi → PLCβ/IP3/Ca2+, PI3K/Akt, and the [[p38 MAPK]]/ERK cascades.
- **Cell sources**: Induced in monocytes/[[Macrophages]], endothelial cells, epithelial cells, and fibroblasts by inflammatory cytokines, TLR ligands, and genotoxic stress.
- **Transcriptional regulation**: Promoter activity depends on [[NF-κB]], [[AP-1]], the C/EBP family ([[CEBPβ|C/EBPβ]]), and [[p38 MAPK]] — the same network that governs the broader SASP, yielding co-expression with [[IL-8]] and [[IL-6]].
- **Downstream cellular effects**: Recruits and activates [[Neutrophils]], driving chemotaxis, degranulation, and ROS generation, extending inflammation to neighboring cells via paracrine signaling.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

- CXCL3 is constitutively induced in senescence and completes the core ELR+ CXC SASP cluster alongside [[CXCL1]] and [[CXCL2]].
- In the liver and pancreas, senescent activated stellate cells secrete CXCL3, sustaining the inflammatory infiltrate implicated in fibrogenesis (Krizhanovsky et al., 2008, PMID 18729207).
- By activating [[CXCR2]] on bystander cells, CXCL3 participates in feed-forward reinforcement of senescence and paracrine/bystander senescence propagation (Acosta et al., 2013, PMID 23746840).
- **Paracrine duality**: supports acute neutrophil clearance of damaged cells but, chronically, sustains [[Inflammaging]] and tissue dysfunction.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- CXCL3/CXCR2 signaling is linked to liver and pancreatic fibrosis, neutrophilic inflammation, and tumor-associated neutrophil infiltration.
- CXCR2 antagonists are being evaluated to dampen SASP-driven inflammation and senescence spread.
- CXCL3 is a candidate biomarker of senescent-cell burden in inflammatory and aged tissues.
