# Transcriptomic Aging

**Transcriptomic aging** describes the progressive remodeling of the **[[Transcriptome]]** with age — shifts in RNA abundance and splicing that track biological age and reflect dysregulated inflammatory, stress-response, and [[Senescence|senescence]] pathways.

> [!info] In the shingles-vaccine study
> Vaccinated participants showed **slower transcriptomic aging** alongside slower [[Epigenetic Aging|epigenetic aging]] and lower [[Inflammation|inflammation]], contributing to a better composite [[Biological Aging|biological aging]] score. Slower transcriptomic aging implies dampened expression of inflammaging-associated gene programs.
