# HIIT

**High-intensity interval training ([[HIIT]])** alternates brief, near-maximal efforts with recovery intervals. Performed 1–2 times per week, it is a cornerstone mitohormetic stimulus: the brief maximal efforts generate the perfect acute [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] spike and lactate accumulation to trigger [[PGC-1α]] (the master regulator of [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis|mitochondrial biogenesis]]).

## Hormetic Dosing

HIIT is most effective when HRV is at or above baseline (the "push" day). On recovery days, or when HRV is suppressed, it should be replaced by [[Zone 2 Cardio]], walking, or rest to avoid crossing from hormesis into [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]] and [[Inflammation|inflammation]].

## Post-Workout Nutrition

After HIIT, consuming fast-digesting carbohydrate, protein, and [[Creatine]] within 45–90 min spikes insulin, lowers cortisol, and flips the [[Autonomic Nervous System|autonomic nervous system]] back to parasympathetic dominance, allowing HRV to rebound.
