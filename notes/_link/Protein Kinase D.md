# Protein Kinase D (PKD)

Protein Kinase D (PKD) is a major sensor of oxidative stress that interacts with [[NF-κB]] to modulate SASP gene expression, including [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]].

## Mechanism of Action

- PKD is a sensor of oxidative stress
- It interacts with [[NF-κB]] to modulate SASP gene expression
- PKD regulates [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]] expression
- PKD links oxidative stress to SASP

## Role in SASP

- PKD is a redox-sensitive regulator of SASP
- It connects oxidative stress to SASP composition
- PKD may be a target for modulating SASP through redox pathways

## Clinical Relevance

- PKD is implicated in cancer and cardiovascular disease
- PKD inhibitors are being developed
- PKD may be a target for modulating SASP in aging
