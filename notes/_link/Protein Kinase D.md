# Protein Kinase D (PKD)

Protein Kinase D (PKD) is a family of serine/threonine kinases (PKD1/PRKD1, PKD2/PRKD2, PKD3/PRKD3) that functions as a major sensor of oxidative and genotoxic stress and interacts with [[NFKB|NF-κB]] to modulate SASP gene expression, including [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]].

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

PKD is activated downstream of protein kinase C and G-protein-coupled receptors, but is also directly engaged by reactive oxygen species and genotoxic stress. Oxidative cues promote PKD autophosphorylation (e.g., Ser744/Ser748 in PKD1) and membrane/cytoskeletal translocation. Once active, PKD phosphorylates and activates the IKK complex — phosphorylating [[IKKbeta]] and the [[IKK complex]] regulatory machinery — thereby stimulating [[IkappaBalpha]] degradation and nuclear [[NFKB|NF-κB]] translocation. PKD further modulates NF-κB at the chromatin level (e.g., via phosphorylation of histone H3 at Ser10) and influences AP-1 and CREB transcriptional programs. Its redox sensitivity positions PKD as a translator of cellular redox state into inflammatory gene expression.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

PKD is a redox-sensitive regulator of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]]. In senescent cells, accumulated [[Mitochondrial ROS|mitochondrial ROS]] and oxidative damage activate PKD, which in turn drives [[NFKB|NF-κB]]-dependent transcription of SASP components such as [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]], connecting oxidative stress to SASP composition. Because PKD sits between the redox milieu and NF-κB, it helps determine both the magnitude and the qualitative profile of the senescent secretome. Targeting PKD therefore offers a route to modulate SASP through redox/inflammatory nodes rather than via direct cytotoxic senolysis.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

PKD isoforms are implicated in cancer (proliferation, migration, epithelial–mesenchymal transition), cardiovascular disease, and fibrosis. PKD inhibitors (e.g., CRT0066101, kb-NB142-70) are under study, and the PKD–NF-κB link makes PKD an attractive candidate for senomorphic intervention to limit chronic SASP-driven [[Inflammaging]] and tissue dysfunction in aging.
