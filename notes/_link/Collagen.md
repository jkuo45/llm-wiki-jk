# Collagen

Collagen is the main structural protein in the extracellular matrix of various connective tissues in the body.

## Overview

Collagen provides structural support and elasticity to tissues such as skin, blood vessels, and tendons. During [[Aging]] and [[Hyperglycemia]], collagen undergoes [[Glycation]], leading to the formation of [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGEs) cross-links.

## Effects of Glycation

- **Tissue Stiffness**: Cross-linking between AGEs and collagen impairs mechanical properties and increases stiffness.
- **Vascular Alterations**: Glycation of vascular collagen facilitates plaque formation and basement membrane hyperplasia.
- **Skin Aging**: Altered synthesis and degradation of glycated collagen accelerate skin senescence.
