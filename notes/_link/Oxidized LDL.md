# Oxidized LDL

**Oxidized LDL** (oxLDL) is low-density lipoprotein that has undergone oxidative modification, primarily by [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] and [[notes/_link/Myeloperoxidase]]. It is a key driver of [[notes/_link/Atherosclerosis]] and [[notes/_link/Endothelial Dysfunction]].

## Connections

- [[notes/_link/Atherosclerosis]] — oxLDL uptake by macrophages forms foam cells
- [[notes/_link/Endothelial Dysfunction]] — oxLDL impairs NO bioavailability
- [[CD36 Receptor]] — Major scavenger receptor for oxLDL
- [[Lipid Peroxidation]] — The process that generates oxLDL
