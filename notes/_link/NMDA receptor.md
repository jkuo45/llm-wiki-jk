# NMDA receptor

## Overview

The **[[NMDA receptor]]** (N-methyl-D-aspartate receptor) is a glutamate-gated ion channel mediating excitatory neurotransmission and activity-dependent synaptic plasticity (LTP/LTD). It is highly permeable to [[Calcium|Ca<sup>2+</sup>]]; pathological overactivation drives excitotoxicity, seizures, and ischemic neuronal death. It is a recognized target for antiseizure and neuroprotective strategies.

## Structure and Gating

- Heterotetrameric glutamate receptor with GluN1, GluN2, and GluN3 subunits; requires co-agonism by glutamate and glycine/D-serine and depolarization to relieve Mg<sup>2+</sup> block.
- Couples at the postsynaptic density to scaffolding/effector proteins, including **[[PSD95]]**, which bridges the receptor to [[nNOS]] (neuronal nitric oxide synthase).

## Inhibition by Honokiol

[[Honokiol]] blocks [[NMDA receptor]]-, glutamate-, and K<sup>+</sup>-evoked cationic influx and raises seizure threshold (i.p. 1 and 5 mg/kg; honokiol more potent than [[Magnolol]]). In ischemia-reperfusion, a key neuroprotective mechanism is **disruption of the PSD95–[[nNOS]] interaction** at the NMDA receptor complex, preventing Ca<sup>2+</sup>-dependent, neurotoxic [[Nitric Oxide]] production.

> [!tip] Source: [[Neuro-Modulating Effects of Honokiol A Review]]
> Honokiol protects the brain against ischemia-reperfusion injury by disrupting PSD95–nNOS coupling at the NMDA receptor, blocking NMDA-evoked calcium influx and downstream nitric-oxide-mediated neurotoxicity.

## Physiological Function

- Mediates coincident-detection and Hebbian plasticity; essential for learning, memory, and development.

## Pathology and Clinical Relevance

- Overactivation → excitotoxicity in [[Stroke|stroke]], [[Traumatic Brain Injury]], and [[Epilepsy|epilepsy]].
- Antagonists (e.g., memantine) are used in neurodegeneration; honokiol represents a multitarget natural-product modulator with both antiseizure and neuroprotective profiles.

## Connections

- [[Glutamate]] — Endogenous orthosteric agonist.
- [[Honokiol]] — Inhibits NMDA-evoked influx and uncouples PSD95–nNOS for ischemic neuroprotection.
- [[Magnolol]] — Shared (weaker) NMDA inhibition; contributes to analgesia/antiseizure effects.
- [[PSD95]] — Scaffold linking NMDA receptor to nNOS; disrupted by honokiol.
- [[nNOS]] — Downstream effector whose coupling to the NMDA receptor is blocked by honokiol.
- [[Calcium]] — Pathological Ca<sup>2+</sup> influx mediator of NMDA receptor excitotoxicity.
