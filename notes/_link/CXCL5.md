# CXCL5

CXCL5 (C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 5), also known as ENA-78 (epithelial-derived neutrophil-activating peptide 78), is a chemokine that binds to [[CXCR2]] and is secreted by blastocysts originating from aged oocytes. It is detrimental to pre-implantation embryos used for in vitro fertilization in women of older reproductive age.

## Mechanism of Action

- CXCL5 is secreted by blastocysts from aged oocytes
- It binds to [[CXCR2]] on neighboring cells
- CXCL5 is detrimental to pre-implantation embryo development
- CXCL5 may contribute to age-related decline in fertility

## Role in SASP

- CXCL5 is a SASP factor associated with reproductive aging
- It contributes to the age-related decline in embryo quality
- CXCL5 may link cellular senescence to reproductive dysfunction

## Clinical Relevance

- CXCL5 is a potential biomarker for embryo quality in IVF
- CXCL5 may be a therapeutic target for improving fertility in older women
- CXCL5 is elevated in various inflammatory conditions
