# Magnolol

## Overview

[[Magnolol]] is a naturally occurring **biphenyl-type neolignan** found in the bark of *Magnolia* species (*Magnolia officinalis*, *Magnolia grandiflora*). It is the structural analog and regio-isomer of [[Honokiol]] (the two differ only in the position of one phenolic hydroxyl group) and shares honokiol's lipid solubility and poly-phenolic, aromatic scaffold. Like honokiol, magnolol acts as a positive allosteric modulator of the [[GABA_A receptor]], an [[NMDA receptor]] inhibitor, and an [[NF-kappaB|NF-κB]] suppressor, though it is generally slightly less potent than honokiol in several neuro-modulating assays.

## Chemical Properties and Classification

- **Class:** neolignan (biphenyl lignan); poly-phenol.
- **Solubility:** lipid-soluble.
- **Applications (per comparative table):** antibacterial, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, neuroprotectant.

## Mechanism of Action

### GABA<sub>A</sub> and NMDA modulation

Magnolol, together with [[Honokiol]], increases [[Muscimol|[<sup>3</sup>H]muscimol]] binding-site number ~3-fold by allosterically increasing low-affinity site affinity and is a positive allosteric modulator of both synaptic and extrasynaptic [[GABA_A receptor]]s, with greater efficacy at δ-subunit-containing receptors. It also blocks [[NMDA receptor]]-evoked cation influx and raises seizure threshold (i.p. 1 and 5 mg/kg), though honokiol appeared more potent.

### Anti-inflammatory signaling

Magnolol inhibits [[NF-kappaB|NF-κB]] activation and [[COX-2]] expression in macrophage-like cells, paralleling honokiol's suppression of [[TNF-alpha|TNFα]] and [[Nitric Oxide|NO]].

### Analgesia

In inflammatory and glutamatergic pain models, magnolol reduces formalin-induced paw-licking and c-Fos expression in spinal dorsal horn laminae and blocks glutamate-, [[Substance P]]-, and [[PGE2]]-evoked nociception. For substance P–induced thermal hyperalgesia, magnolol was slightly more effective than honokiol at lower doses, whereas honokiol was more selective for NMDA-driven responses.

## Physiological and Therapeutic Relevance

- **Neuroprotection / cognition:** Oral magnolol (10 mg/kg) with honokiol prevents age-related memory/learning deficits in SAMP8 mice by preserving [[Cholinergic]] neurons and enhancing [[Akt]] activity.
- **Alzheimer's:** Magnolol (with honokiol) significantly decreases β-[[Amyloid beta|amyloid (Aβ)]]-induced neuronal death in PC12 cells; effects attributed to reduced [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]], suppressed intracellular [[Calcium|Ca<sup>2+</sup>]], and inhibited [[Caspase-3|caspase-3]].
- **Smooth muscle:** Inhibits smooth muscle contraction in porcine trachea.
- **Antimicrobial:** Active against periodontopathic microorganisms.
