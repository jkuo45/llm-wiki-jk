## Overview

γH2AX is the phosphorylated form of the [[Histone|histone]] variant H2AX, phosphorylated at serine 139 by [[ATM]], [[ATR]], and [[DNA-PK]] in response to [[DNA Damage|DNA double-strand breaks]]. It serves as a scaffold for the recruitment of DNA repair factors at damage foci and is a widely used biomarker for both DNA damage and [[Senescence|cellular senescence]].

## Connections

- [[DNA Damage]] — γH2AX foci mark sites of double-strand breaks
- [[ATM]]/[[ATR]] — Kinases that phosphorylate H2AX in the DNA damage response
- [[Senescence]] — Persistent γH2AX foci are a hallmark of senescent cells
- [[53BP1]] — Co-localizes with γH2AX at DNA damage foci in senescence
- [[Klotho]] — Ectopic Klotho expression reduces γH2AX foci in renal tubular epithelial cells
