# Src

## Overview
[[Src]] (proto-oncogene tyrosine-protein kinase Src) is the prototypical member of the Src family kinases (SFKs), a group of non-receptor tyrosine kinases that regulate cell proliferation, survival, migration, and immune signaling. Src is the primary kinase responsible for phosphorylating [[Caspase-8]] at Tyr380, inhibiting its apoptotic function and promoting a pro-migratory scaffolding role.

## Structure & Activation
Src contains an N-terminal myristoylation membrane anchor, SH3 and SH2 regulatory domains, a linker region, and a C-terminal catalytic kinase domain. In its inactive conformation, intramolecular interactions between the SH2 domain and a C-terminal phosphotyrosine (Tyr527) keep Src autoinhibited. Activation occurs by dephosphorylation of Tyr527 or by SH3 domain displacement.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **Caspase-8 Phosphorylation**: Src (and related SFKs like Lyn) phosphorylates [[Caspase-8]] at Tyr380 (or Tyr397/Tyr465 depending on isoform). This phosphorylation:
  - Inhibits proteolytic cleavage and maturation of Caspase-8
  - Blocks the extrinsic apoptotic pathway
  - Converts Caspase-8 into a scaffolding protein that recruits [[FAK]], Calpain-2, and [[PI3K]]
  - Promotes cell migration, metastasis, and survival signaling ([[NFKB]], [[mTORC1]])

> [!warning] Src-Driven Apoptotic Resistance
> Src-mediated Caspase-8 phosphorylation represents a gain-of-function switch: the cell loses apoptotic sensitivity while gaining pro-migratory and pro-survival signaling capacity. This is a significant mechanism of tumor progression in colon cancer, glioblastoma, and breast cancer.

- **Proliferation**: Src activates RAS/[[ERK2]] and PI3K/[[Akt]] pathways to promote growth.
- **Migration**: Src phosphorylates [[FAK]] and paxillin to drive focal adhesion turnover and cell motility.
- **Immune Signaling**: Src family kinases (Lck, Fyn) are essential for T cell receptor signaling.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance
- **Cancer**: Src is activated in many cancers (colon, breast, lung, pancreas) through mutations, amplification, or loss of Csk regulation. Activated Src drives tumor progression, metastasis, and drug resistance.
- **Therapeutic Targeting**: Dasatinib and bosutinib are FDA-approved Src/ABL kinase inhibitors used in CML and being investigated in solid tumors.
