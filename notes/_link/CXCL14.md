# CXCL14

CXCL14 (C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 14), also known as BRAK (breast and kidney-expressed chemokine), is a chemokine that is part of an early secretory phenotype supported by [[p21 CIP1|p21]] and [[Retinoblastoma Protein|Rb]] activation. It puts stressed or senescent cells under immunosurveillance.

## Mechanism of Action

- CXCL14 is part of an early secretory phenotype in senescent cells
- Its expression is supported by [[p21 CIP1|p21]] and [[Retinoblastoma Protein|Rb]] activation
- CXCL14 puts stressed or senescent cells under immunosurveillance
- CXCL14 is induced by [[p53]] activation

## Role in SASP

- CXCL14 is an immunomodulatory SASP factor
- It contributes to immune surveillance of senescent cells
- CXCL14 is part of the p53-dependent SASP profile

## Clinical Relevance

- CXCL14 is a potential biomarker for senescence
- CXCL14 may have tumor-suppressive functions through immune surveillance
- CXCL14 is elevated in certain inflammatory conditions
