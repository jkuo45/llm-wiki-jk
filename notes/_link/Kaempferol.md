# Kaempferol

**Kaempferol** (3,5,7,4′-tetrahydroxyflavone) is a [[Flavonols|flavonol]] widely distributed in fruits, vegetables, and tea. Along with [[Quercetin]] and [[Apigenin]], it is one of the five flavonoids most associated with reduced cancer risk in epidemiological studies.

## Biological Activity

Kaempferol inhibits oncogenic proline-directed protein kinase FA (PDPK FA) and exerts anti-proliferative and pro-apoptotic effects in several cancers. It modulates [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]], [[NF-κB]], and [[Estrogen Receptor]] signaling and shows chemopreventive activity in prostate, breast, and ovarian models.
