# Interleukin 11 (IL-11)

**Interleukin-11 (IL-11)** is a gp130-family cytokine that functions as a senescence-induced osteokine and a pro-fibrotic SASP factor. It is transcriptionally upregulated as a consequence of [[SIRT1]] downregulation and vitamin D deficiency, and its signaling to fibroblasts and osteoblasts promotes age-related [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis|pulmonary fibrosis]], cardiac fibrosis, and bone loss.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

IL-11 binds the IL-11 receptor α-chain (IL-11Rα), which complexes with the shared signal-transducing subunit [[gp130]], activating [[JAK]] (primarily JAK1/TYK2) and [[STAT3]] (Ng et al., PMID 33208947). Like other gp130 cytokines, IL-11 also engages [[MAPK]] and [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]] cascades. IL-11 is induced downstream of the senescence program rather than by classic inflammatory stimuli: [[SIRT1]] deacetylase loss relieves repression of IL-11 expression, and vitamin D receptor signaling normally restrains it. Once secreted, IL-11 acts in autocrine/paracrine loops to drive a fibroblast-to-myofibroblast transition, extracellular-matrix deposition, and osteoblast/osteoclast imbalance favoring resorption.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

IL-11 is a distinctive SASP output with tissue-restricted, mostly deleterious consequences:
- In bone, senescent cells secrete IL-11 to activate STAT3 in osteoblasts, suppressing bone formation and stimulating [[Osteoporosis|bone loss]] (the "senescence-associated osteokine" loop).
- In lung and heart, IL-11 amplifies a pro-fibrotic SASP that drives [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis|pulmonary fibrosis]] and cardiac fibrosis.
- Chronic IL-11/STAT3 signaling contributes to [[Inflammaging]] and metabolic decline.

Because IL-11 is low in youth and rises with age, it is a tractable [[Senomorphic Therapy|senomorphic]]/anti-fibrotic target: genetic deletion or antibody blockade of IL-11 extends healthspan and reduces fibrosis in mice (Widjaja et al., PMID 38778135).

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance
- Anti-IL-11 antibodies (e.g., xevkro/loxukimab) are in clinical trials for fibrotic and metabolic disease.
- IL-11 is a candidate circulating biomarker of the senescence burden and of age-related fibrosis.
- IL-11 inhibition mitigates both bone loss and organ fibrosis, separating beneficial from harmful SASP components.
