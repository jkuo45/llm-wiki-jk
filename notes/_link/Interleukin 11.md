# Interleukin 11 (IL-11)

Interleukin 11 (IL-11) is a SASP factor associated with ageing, vitamin D deficiency, and [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis|pulmonary fibrosis]]. It is activated as a consequence of downregulation of [[SIRT1]] and signals to senescent fibroblasts to promote a pro-fibrotic phenotype.

## Mechanism of Action

- IL-11 is activated as a consequence of [[SIRT1]] downregulation
- It signals to senescent fibroblasts
- IL-11 promotes a pro-fibrotic phenotype
- IL-11 is associated with ageing and vitamin D deficiency

## Role in SASP

- IL-11 is a pro-fibrotic SASP factor
- It contributes to age-related fibrosis
- IL-11 is a potential therapeutic target for fibrotic diseases

## Clinical Relevance

- IL-11 is elevated in pulmonary fibrosis
- Anti-IL-11 therapy is being developed for fibrotic diseases
- IL-11 may be a biomarker for age-related fibrosis
