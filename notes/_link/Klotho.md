# Klotho

[[Klotho]] is a transmembrane protein (with a shed, hormone-like extracellular domain) best known as an anti-aging factor; loss-of-function accelerates aging, whereas elevated Klotho extends lifespan in mice. It regulates [[mTOR]]/insulin–IGF signaling and phosphate/vitamin D metabolism.

## Rapamycin and Klotho

Excessive [[mTORC1]] activation drives exhaustion of mesenchymal stem cells and the bone abnormalities of Klotho-deficient (premature-aging) mice. [[Rapamycin]] inhibition of mTORC1 improves mesenchymal stem-cell function in these models, linking mTOR control to Klotho-associated pro-longevity and tissue-regeneration pathways ([Zerdka et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12766144/)).

> [!info] Convergence on longevity
> Both Klotho elevation and mTORC1 inhibition are pro-longevity; rapamycin engages the same stem-cell/homeostasis axis compromised in Klotho deficiency.
