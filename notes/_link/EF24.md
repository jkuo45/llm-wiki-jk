# EF24

[[EF24]] is a synthetic, enone-containing analog of [[Curcumin|curcumin]] engineered for greater chemical stability and cellular uptake. In senescence and fibrosis research it has been profiled as a [[Senomorphic|senescence-modulating]] and [[Polyphenols|polyphenol-derived]] agent that counteracts stress-induced cellular aging.

## Mechanism

EF24 suppresses [[Reactive Oxygen Species|oxidative stress]]–induced epithelial senescence and modulates [[PTEN|PTEN-related signaling pathways]], countering the pro-survival, pro-fibrotic axis that sustains senescent cells.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - cellular_senescence_ipf_diseases-14-00201|Cellular Senescence in IPF (Baurzhan et al., 2026)]]
> [[Curcumin|Curcumin]] and its synthetic analog [[EF24]] have been shown to suppress oxidative stress–induced epithelial senescence and modulate PTEN-related signaling pathways. EF24 was listed among natural/synthetic compounds with senolytic or SASP-modulating properties in preclinical pulmonary fibrosis settings.

## Context in Fibrosis

Within the IPF senescence network, EF24 is grouped with [[Procyanidin C1|procyanidin C1]], [[Resveratrol|resveratrol]], and [[Quercetin|quercetin]] as a polyphenol-class modulator capable of attenuating the [[SASP]] and epithelial senescence that drive [[Fibroblast|fibroblast]] activation and extracellular matrix deposition.
