# Dextromethorphan

[[Dextromethorphan]] (DXM) is an over-the-counter cough suppressant with serotonergic and NMDA-antagonist activity. It is listed among agents that, combined with a [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]] such as [[Methylene blue]], can precipitate [[Serotonin Syndrome]].
