# Anemia

[[Anemia]] is a reduction in red blood cell count and/or hemoglobin concentration, diminishing oxygen-carrying capacity. It is one of the hematological cytopenias that can accompany chronic [[mTOR]] inhibitor therapy.

## Rapamycin-Associated Anemia

Because [[mTORC1]] inhibition slows cell proliferation, chronic high-dose [[Rapamycin]] and [[Rapalog|rapalogs]] reduce blood cell counts. Mild, reversible anemia—together with [[Thrombocytopenia]] and [[Leukopenia]]—is among the most common hematological side effects ([Blagosklonny, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6814615/)). In anti-aging scheduling, dose and frequency are limited by the emergence of these cytopenias; the optimal dose is the highest that does not produce them.

> [!note] Reversibility
> Rapamycin-related cytopenias are typically mild and reverse on dose reduction or discontinuation.
