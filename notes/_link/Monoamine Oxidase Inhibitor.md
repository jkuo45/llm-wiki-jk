# Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor

[[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]]s ([[MAOI]]s) are agents that inhibit [[Monoamine Oxidase]], the enzyme that catabolizes monoamine neurotransmitters. Inhibition raises synaptic [[Serotonin]], [[Dopamine]], and [[Norepinephrine]], producing antidepressant and anxiolytic effects (e.g., [[Phenelzine]]).

## Methylene Blue as a MAO-A Inhibitor

[[Methylene blue]] is a potent, relatively selective **MAO-A inhibitor**. This underlies:

- Its mild mood-stabilizing and anxiolytic effects at low doses (0.5–2 mg).
- Its **serotonin syndrome risk** when combined with other serotonergic drugs (see [[Serotonin Syndrome]]; FDA boxed warning).

> [!warning] Interaction
> Because of MAO-A inhibition, methylene blue must not be combined with [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors]], [[Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors]], [[Tricyclic Antidepressant]]s, or [[Opioids]]. Hold serotonergics ≥72 h after the last dose.
