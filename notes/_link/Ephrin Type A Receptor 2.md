# Ephrin Type A Receptor 2 (EPHA2)

Ephrin Type A Receptor 2 (EPHA2) is a receptor tyrosine kinase that is activated by exosomes from senescent cells to promote cancer cell proliferation. It is a mediator of the pro-tumorigenic effects of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].

## Mechanism of Action

- EPHA2 is a receptor tyrosine kinase
- It is activated by exosomes from senescent cells
- EPHA2 activation promotes cancer cell proliferation
- EPHA2 mediates pro-tumorigenic SASP effects

## Role in SASP

- EPHA2 is a receptor for SASP exosomes
- It mediates cancer promotion by senescent cells
- EPHA2 links cellular senescence to cancer progression

## Clinical Relevance

- EPHA2 is a therapeutic target in cancer
- EPHA2 inhibitors are being developed
- EPHA2 may be a target for reducing SASP-driven cancer promotion
