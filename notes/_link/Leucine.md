# Leucine

## Overview

**Leucine** is an essential branched-chain amino acid and the primary nutritional activator of [[mTORC1|mTORC1]], making it a key anabolic signal for [[Skeletal Muscle|skeletal muscle]]. It is one of the three BCAAs (with isoleucine and valine).

## Mechanism of Action & Physiology

Leucine (and its metabolite α-ketoisocaproate) activates the Rag GTPase complex and upstream regulators to recruit [[mTORC1]] to the lysosome, stimulating translation initiation and muscle protein synthesis—counteracting [[Muscle Atrophy]]. It also modulates insulin sensitivity and energy metabolism.

## Applications

Used clinically and as a supplement to support muscle mass in aging, exercise recovery, and catabolic states. Often paired with [[Creatine]] or [[Urolithin A]] in muscle-health strategies.
