# CD36

CD36 (Cluster of differentiation 36), also known as platelet glycoprotein 4 or fatty acid translocase, is a cell surface protein that is essential for the activation of [[NF-κB]] and its downstream SASP. It may be crucial for mediating external signals from senescence inducers.

## Mechanism of Action

- CD36 is a cell surface receptor that mediates external signals
- It is essential for [[NF-κB]] activation and downstream SASP
- CD36 may mediate signals from senescence inducers
- CD36 is involved in fatty acid uptake and signaling

## Role in SASP

- CD36 is required for NF-κB activation in senescent cells
- It mediates external signals that trigger SASP
- CD36 may be a sensor for lipids that promote SASP

## Clinical Relevance

- CD36 is implicated in atherosclerosis and metabolic diseases
- CD36 is a therapeutic target in cancer and inflammation
- CD36 may be a target for modulating SASP through lipid signaling
