# PEX13

[[PEX13]] encodes an **integral peroxisomal membrane protein (PMP)** that forms a critical component of the peroxisomal receptor docking complex. PEX13, together with [[PEX14]], creates the translocation machinery through which [[PEX5]] (PTS1 receptor) and [[PEX7]] (PTS2 receptor) deliver their cargo into the [[Peroxisome]] matrix. PEX13 acts as a scaffolding and regulatory subunit: it stabilizes [[PEX14]], modulates receptor binding affinity, and participates in the assembly of the import pore. Mutations in PEX13 cause severe peroxisomal biogenesis disorders within the [[Zellweger Spectrum Disorders]].

## Structure & Domains

PEX13 is a ~42 kDa multipass transmembrane protein with the following domain architecture:

- **N-terminal cytosolic domain** — contains a zinc-binding motif (Cys-X₂-Cys-X₁₇-Cys-X₂-Cys) and mediates interaction with [[PEX5]]. This domain also binds ubiquitinated PEX5, linking PEX13 to receptor recycling.
- **Single transmembrane segment** — anchors PEX13 in the peroxisomal membrane. The transmembrane helix is flanked by positively charged residues that ensure correct topology.
- **C-terminal peroxisomal matrix-exposed domain** — contains a SH3-like domain that interacts with [[PEX14]]. This domain also binds the AAA-ATPase [[PEX1]], positioning it for receptor extraction.

PEX13 forms a heteromeric complex with [[PEX14]] at the peroxisomal membrane. The PEX13-PEX14 interaction is constitutive and essential for both proteins' stability — loss of one typically destabilizes the other.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

PEX13 functions within the peroxisomal import machinery as follows:

1. **Complex Assembly** — PEX13 is inserted into the peroxisomal membrane by [[PEX19]] (the PMP chaperone) and [[PEX3]] (the PMP receptor). Once inserted, PEX13 recruits [[PEX14]] to form the core docking complex.
2. **Receptor Docking** — [[PEX5]] binds PEX13's N-terminal domain and PEX14's acidic domain simultaneously, creating a high-affinity ternary complex that positions the cargo for translocation.
3. **Translocation Pore Formation** — PEX13-PEX14 complex oligomerizes to form a protein-conducting channel. PEX5 inserts its C-terminal region into this pore, and cargo is released into the matrix.
4. **Receptor Recycling** — After cargo release, ubiquitinated PEX5 is recognized by PEX13's N-terminal zinc-binding domain, which recruits the RING E3 ligase complex ([[PEX2]]/[[PEX10]]/[[PEX12]]) for monoubiquitination. The AAA-ATPase [[PEX1]]/[[PEX6]] then extracts PEX5 from the membrane.

PEX13 also plays a role in peroxisome membrane dynamics: it interacts with [[PEX11β]] during peroxisome fission and with [[Dynamin-like Protein 1 (DLP1)]] during membrane scission.

## Physiological Function

PEX13 is essential for importing all [[PEX5]]-dependent and most [[PEX7]]-dependent matrix proteins. Its physiological roles include:

- **Fatty acid metabolism** — imports β-oxidation enzymes for very-long-chain and branched-chain [[Fatty acid]] degradation.
- **Plasmalogen synthesis** — imports enzymes required for ether phospholipid biosynthesis, essential for myelin sheath formation.
- **Bile acid synthesis** — imports peroxisomal enzymes that convert C27 bile acid intermediates to mature C24 bile acids.
- **Reactive oxygen species detoxification** — imports catalase, the primary peroxisomal antioxidant enzyme.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

Mutations in *PEX13* cause **PEX13-deficient Zellweger Spectrum Disorder (ZSD)**, typically presenting as:

- **Zellweger syndrome** (severe) — craniofacial dysmorphism, hepatomegaly, renal cysts, severe hypotonia, seizures, chondrodysplasia punctata, death within the first year.
- **Neonatal Adrenoleukodystrophy** (intermediate) — progressive white matter demyelination, adrenal insufficiency, sensorineural hearing loss.
- **Infantile Refsum Disease** (mild) — retinitis pigmentosa, hepatic fibrosis, skeletal abnormalities.

PEX13 mutations account for ~2-5% of ZSD cases. Missense mutations in the SH3-like domain (e.g., p.Ser293Pro) are the most common, partially impairing PEX14 interaction and reducing but not abolishing matrix import. Truncating mutations cause complete loss of function and the most severe phenotypes.

Biallelic PEX13 knockout in mice is embryonic lethal; conditional hepatic knockout reproduces the full spectrum of ZSD hepatic pathology.
