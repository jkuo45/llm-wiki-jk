# CXCR3

CXCR3 (C-X-C chemokine receptor 3) is a G-protein-coupled receptor that binds [[CXCL10]] and [[CXCL11]]. It mediates immune cell recruitment and is implicated in both immune surveillance and cancer progression.

## Mechanism of Action

- CXCR3 binds [[CXCL10]] and [[CXCL11]]
- It is expressed on natural killer cells, T cells, and some cancer cells
- CXCR3 mediates immune cell recruitment to sites of inflammation
- CXCR3 signaling can promote cancer cell migration and metastasis

## Role in SASP

- CXCR3 is a receptor for SASP chemokines (CXCL10, CXCL11)
- It mediates immune cell recruitment to senescent cells
- CXCR3 can have both beneficial (immune surveillance) and detrimental (cancer promotion) effects

## Clinical Relevance

- CXCR3 is a therapeutic target in cancer and autoimmune diseases
- CXCR3 expression correlates with cancer prognosis
- CXCR3 antagonists are being developed for various conditions
