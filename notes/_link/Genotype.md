# Genotype
A **genotype** is the part of the genetic makeup of a cell, and therefore of any individual, which determines one of its characteristics.
## Overview
- Comprises the inherited DNA sequence.
- Interaction with environment: The phenotype is the physical expression of the genotype influenced by environmental factors.
