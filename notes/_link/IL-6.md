# IL-6

**Interleukin-6 (IL-6)** is a pleiotropic pro-inflammatory cytokine and one of the most highly conserved and robustly induced components of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]], with central roles in [[Inflammation]], [[Inflammaging]], and numerous age-related diseases.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

IL-6 signals through two distinct receptor modes. In *classic signaling*, IL-6 first binds membrane-bound IL-6R (CD126), and the complex recruits the signal-transducing subunit [[gp130]] (CD130), which carries all intracellular signaling domains. Because IL-6R is expressed on only a limited set of cells (hepatocytes, neutrophils, some lymphocytes), most cell types respond via *trans-signaling*, in which soluble IL-6R (shed by ADAM10/17) binds IL-6 and the complex activates gp130 on IL-6R-negative cells (Rose-John, PMID 15120865). gp130 engagement activates [[JAK]] family kinases (JAK1, JAK2, TYK2), phosphorylating and activating [[STAT3]] (and [[STAT1]]), as well as the [[MAPK]]/[[ERK2]] and [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]] axes. IL-6 transcription in senescent cells is driven by [[NFKB|NF-κB]] and [[p38 MAPK]], placing IL-6 downstream of the DNA-damage–p38–NF-κB SASP regulatory circuit (Coppé et al., PMID 18708385; Kuilman et al., PMID 20334938). Secreted IL-6 then feeds back to reinforce the senescent arrest and amplify the SASP via STAT3.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

IL-6 is secreted by [[Senescent Cells]] across essentially all models of stress-induced and replicative senescence. It acts in autocrine and paracrine loops to:
- Reinforce growth arrest and sustain the SASP through [[NFKB|NF-κB]]–[[STAT3]] crosstalk.
- Promote [[Paracrine Senescence]] in neighboring epithelial and stromal cells.
- Recruit and polarize immune cells, shaping the tissue microenvironment.

Acute IL-6 supports host defense and tissue repair, but chronic IL-6 signaling underpins [[Inflammaging]] and contributes to [[Cancer]] (e.g., STAT3-driven proliferation), [[Cardiovascular Disease]], and [[Neurodegenerative Disease]].

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance
- Elevated circulating IL-6 is a biomarker of biological age, frailty, and [[Inflammaging]].
- IL-6 trans-signaling is pharmacologically targeted by soluble gp130 (sgp130) and anti-IL-6R antibodies (tocilizumab) in inflammatory disease.
- Blocking IL-6 is a [[Senomorphic Therapy|senomorphic]] strategy to blunt chronic SASP without killing senescent cells.

## Additional Context (Adrenochrome / AGE / Sirtuin)

IL-6 expression is often upregulated by activation of [[NF-κB]] following [[Advanced Glycation End Products|AGE]]-induced stress. [[SIRT1]] deacetylation of [[NF-κB]] downregulates pro-inflammatory cytokines including IL-6; [[SIRT7]] kidney-specific knockout reduces IL-6 and other inflammatory factors.
