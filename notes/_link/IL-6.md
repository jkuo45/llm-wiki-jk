# IL-6

**Interleukin-6 (IL-6)** is a pleiotropic pro-inflammatory cytokine that plays a central role in the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] and [[Inflammation]].

## Role in SASP
IL-6 is one of the most highly conserved and robust markers of SASP. It is produced by [[Senescent Cells]] and acts in both autocrine and paracrine manners to:
- Reinforce [[Senescence]] via [[NFKB|NF-κB]] and [[JAK]]/[[STAT3]] signaling.
- Recruit immune cells and modulate the tissue microenvironment.
- Promote [[Paracrine Senescence]] in neighboring cells.

## Signaling
IL-6 signals through the IL-6 receptor (IL-6R) and [[gp130]], activating the [[JAK]]/[[STAT3]], MAPK, and [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]] pathways.

## Clinical Relevance
Elevated IL-6 levels are associated with [[Aging]], [[Inflammaging]], and various age-related diseases, including [[Cardiovascular Disease]], [[Neurodegenerative Disease]], and [[Cancer]].
