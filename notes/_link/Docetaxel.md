# Docetaxel

**Docetaxel (DTX)** is a taxane chemotherapeutic that stabilizes microtubules and disrupts mitosis. In the apigenin study it served as an alternative chemotherapeutic (to [[Mitoxantrone|MIT]]) to validate apigenin's ability to counteract senescence-associated chemoresistance.

## Relevance to Apigenin Study

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> Replacing MIT with docetaxel largely phenocopied the MIT result: senescent stromal cell-derived conditioned media increased cancer cell apoptosis-resistance and chemoresistance, and both effects were considerably suppressed by [[Apigenin]].
