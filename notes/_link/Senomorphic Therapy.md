# Senomorphic Therapy

**Senomorphic therapies** focus on modulating senescence-associated properties — particularly the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] — without directly leading to cytotoxicity. This contrasts with [[Senolytic Therapy|senolytics]], which selectively kill senescent cells. Senomorphics may provide more beneficial outcomes than senolytics in circumstances where senescent cells contribute to maintaining tissue structure.

## Mechanism

Senomorphic therapies primarily target the pro-inflammatory and pro-tumorigenic SASP factors downstream of [[NF-κB]], [[p38 MAPK]], and [[mTORC1|mTOR]] signalling pathways.

## Key Senomorphic Agents

### Targeting NF-κB and IL-1α

- **Glucocorticoids**: Potent suppressors of chronic inflammation; block pro-inflammatory SASP by impairing IL-1α-mediated NF-κB activity
- **[[Metformin]]**: Inhibits NF-κB signalling in fibroblasts, macrophages, and senescent endothelial cells
- **Olive phenols**: Reduce cGAS–STING–NF-κB pro-inflammatory SASP; preserve lamin B1 expression

### Targeting p53 Stabilization

- **[[Nutlin 3a]]**: MDM2 inhibitor that stabilizes p53; suppresses pro-inflammatory SASP arm

### Targeting p38 MAPK–MK2

- **p38 MAPK inhibitors (SB203580)**: Inhibit SASP from cultured fibroblasts from older donors
- **MK2 inhibitors**: Rescue chemotherapy-induced bone loss in mice

### Targeting mTOR

- **[[Rapamycin]]**: Suppresses SASP by interfering with mTOR and p38 MAPK–IL-1α–NF-κB pathways; also shows STAT3-dependent SASP-suppressing activity in Nrf2-null models
- **Hypoxia-mimetic compounds**: Block mTOR through AMPK activation; suppress pro-inflammatory SASP; improve health span of therapy-induced premature or naturally aged animals

### Targeting cGAS–STING

- **Nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors (NRTIs)**: Lamivudine (3TC) inhibits LINE1 propagation, reducing cGAS–STING–NF-κB SASP

### Targeting JAK–STAT

- **[[Ruxolitinib]]**: JAK1/2 inhibitor; reduces SASP cytokines
- **Tofacitinib**: JAK inhibitor

### Targeting Lipid SASP

- **Zileuton**: ALOX5 inhibitor targeting leukotriene synthesis
- **Colchicine**: Modulates lipid inflammatory mediators

### Natural Compounds

- **[[Resveratrol]]**: Inhibits pro-inflammatory SASP cytokines via SIRT1/NF-κB pathway
- **Apigenin**: Reduces breast cancer aggressiveness by inhibiting NF-κB-dependent SASP factors and CXCL10
- **Avenanthramide C**: Anti-inflammatory SASP modulator
- **Astaxanthin**: Antioxidant with SASP-suppressing properties

### Other Approaches

- **HSP90 inhibitors**: Blunt chemotherapy-induced IL-8 increases, enhancing chemosensitivity
- **EXOC7 targeting**: Compromises pro-tumorigenic SASP in inflammation-driven cancer
- **Simvastatin**: Targets IL-6 secretion
- **Haritaki**: Targets IL-6

## Clinical Applications

Senomorphic therapies are being studied for:

- [[Cancer]] — suppressing pro-tumorigenic SASP to enhance immunotherapy
- Age-related conditions — reducing chronic inflammation
- [[Osteoarthritis]] — modulating SASP-driven joint inflammation
- [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis]] — reducing pro-fibrotic SASP factors

## Lifestyle Interventions with Senomorphic Effects

- **Dietary restriction**: 30% caloric decrease reduces DNA damage, NF-κB, and SASP levels
- **Exercise**: Reduces senescent cell burden and circulating SASP factors
- **Intermittent fasting**: May reduce p21 expression
