# Long COVID

[[Long COVID]] (post-acute sequelae of SARS-CoV-2 infection, PASC) is a chronic multisystem condition following COVID-19, featuring persistent fatigue, cognitive disturbance ("brain fog"), dyspnea, and autonomic dysfunction. [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|Mitochondrial/energy-metabolism impairment]] is among the leading hypothesized mechanisms.

## Methylene Blue Relevance

[[Methylene blue]]'s ability to bypass dysfunctional [[Complex I]]/[[Complex III]] of the [[Mitochondrial Electron Transport Chain]] and maintain [[ATP]] production has led some clinicians to trial it (up to ~10 mg) in Long COVID and [[ME/CFS]] for mitochondrial support, under clinical judgment rather than established guidelines.

> [!note] Evidence status
> Use in Long COVID is exploratory; evidence for doses above 10 mg in wellness contexts is thin and clinician-directed.
