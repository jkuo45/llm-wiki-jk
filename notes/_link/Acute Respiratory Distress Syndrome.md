# Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome

[[Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome]] ([[ARDS]]) is acute inflammatory lung injury causing diffuse alveolar damage and refractory hypoxemia. Methylene blue has been proposed (anti-inflammatory/antioxidant) for COVID-19 and ARDS, but clinical efficacy is unestablished and it is not FDA-approved for this.
