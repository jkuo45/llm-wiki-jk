# uPA

**Urokinase plasminogen activator (uPA)** is a serine protease that is a component of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].

## Role in SASP
uPA is secreted by [[Senescent Cells]] and contributes to:
- ECM degradation and remodeling by converting plasminogen to plasmin.
- Promoting cell migration and invasion.
- Facilitating [[Paracrine Senescence]] and tissue remodeling.

## Regulation
uPA activity is regulated by its inhibitor, [[PAI-1]], which is also a SASP factor.
