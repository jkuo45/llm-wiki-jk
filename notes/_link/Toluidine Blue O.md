# Toluidine Blue O

[[Toluidine Blue O]] (TB, tolonium chloride) is a phenothiazine thiazine dye structurally related to [[Methylene blue]], differing mainly by methyl groups at positions 3 and 7 of the phenothiazine nucleus. In the structure-activity study of methylene blue derivatives, TB was the **most potent** compound tested for neuroprotection and mitochondrial antioxidant action in HT-22 hippocampal neurons [^1].

## Structure & Chemistry

TB retains the free position-10 nitrogen and position-5 sulfur of the phenothiazine scaffold that are required for the methylene blue-like alternative electron-transfer phenotype. Like [[Methylene blue]], TB has a negative redox potential (E<sub>1/2</sub> ≈ −0.012 V vs NHE, the most negative of the series), consistent with its ability to enhance cellular oxygen consumption [^1].

## Mechanism of Action

### Alternative mitochondrial electron transfer

In isolated mitochondrial assays, TB — together with [[Methylene blue]] — was one of only two compounds that significantly increased the rate of [[Cytochrome c]] reduction using [[NADH]] as the electron donor (the [[Respiratory Chain Complex I|Complex I]]→[[Complex III]] shunt), bypassing the need for [[Coenzyme Q10]] [^1]. TB therefore acts as an alternative electron carrier that sustains electron flow when Complex I/III are compromised.

### Cellular bioenergetics

TB increased cellular oxygen consumption rate (OCR) and decreased extracellular acidification rate (ECAR) in Seahorse XF24 assays, mirroring [[Methylene blue]] and indicating enhanced oxidative phosphorylation with suppressed anaerobic glycolysis [^1].

### Antioxidant / neuroprotection

Against glutamate insult in HT-22 cells, TB showed:

- Viability EC50 ≈ **1.75 nM**
- ROS EC50 ≈ **0.11 nM** (most potent of all tested)
- Mitochondrial membrane potential EC50 ≈ **1.54 nM**

TB protected against [[Rotenone]], [[Iodoacetic Acid|IAA]], and glutamate neurotoxicity, and (like MB) provided **no** protection against direct extracellular H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub> from [[Glucose Oxidase]] — confirming it is not a classical chain-breaking antioxidant but acts upstream on mitochondrial electron flow [^1].

### Complex IV

TB, like [[Methylene blue]], increased [[Complex IV]] expression/activity at low nanomolar concentrations [^1].

> [!info] Why TB is the most potent
> The 3,7-dimethyl substitution preserves the free 10-nitrogen and position-5 sulfur while tuning the redox potential to the most negative in the series, which correlates with the strongest OCR enhancement and lowest EC50s for neuroprotection [^1].
