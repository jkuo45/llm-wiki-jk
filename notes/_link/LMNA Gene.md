# LMNA Gene
The **[[LMNA Gene]]** (Lamin A/C) provides instructions for making several proteins called lamins, primarily Lamin A and Lamin C. These proteins are structural components of the nuclear lamina, a meshwork that supports the cell nucleus.
## Pathophysiology
Mutations in the **LMNA gene** are responsible for a group of disorders known as **laminopathies**.
*   **[[Hutchinson-Gilford Progeria Syndrome]] (HGPS)**: Caused by a specific mutation that leads to the production of **[[Progerin]]**.
*   **Dilated Cardiomyopathy**: Some mutations affect heart muscle structure and function.
*   **Emery-Dreifuss Muscular Dystrophy**: Affects skeletal and cardiac muscles.
## Role in [[Aging]]
Even in normal aging, low levels of **[[Progerin]]** have been detected in cells, suggesting that the LMNA pathway may contribute to the standard biological aging process.
