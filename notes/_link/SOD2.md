# SOD2

**Catalytic function:** SOD2 is an enzyme that catalyzes biochemical reactions essential for cellular metabolism and homeostasis.

**Mechanism:** The catalytic activity of SOD2 is typically regulated by substrate availability, post-translational modifications, and allosteric effectors. Its active site contains key residues that coordinate substrate binding and transition state stabilization.

**Physiological role:** SOD2 participates in metabolic pathways that maintain cellular redox balance, energy production, and biosynthetic processes. Its expression and activity are tightly regulated across different tissues.

**Clinical relevance:** Dysregulation of SOD2 activity is implicated in metabolic disorders, oxidative stress-related pathologies, and cancer. Pharmacological modulation of this enzyme represents a therapeutic strategy for diseases involving its pathway.
