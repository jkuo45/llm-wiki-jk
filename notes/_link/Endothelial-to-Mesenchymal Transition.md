# Endothelial-to-Mesenchymal Transition (EndoMT)

**Endothelial-to-Mesenchymal Transition (EndoMT)** is a cellular transdifferentiation process in which [[Endothelial Cells]] (ECs) lose their characteristic endothelial markers and acquire mesenchymal features, including contractile and matrix-producing properties. It is a key mechanism in [[Cardiovascular Disease|cardiovascular pathology]], vascular aging, and fibrosis.

## Role in Senescence and Vascular Aging

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Senolytic Treatment With Fisetin Reverses Age‐Related Endothelial Dysfunction Partially Mediated by SASP Factor CXCL12|Mahoney et al. 2025]]
> The [[SASP|senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP)]] and its key factor [[CXCL12]] promote EndoMT in senescent [[Endothelial Cells|endothelial cells]]:
> - Senescent EC subcluster 10 showed reduced endothelial markers (*[[CDH5]]*, *[[PECAM1]]*) and increased mesenchymal markers (*[[TGFB1]]*, *[[ACTA2]]*)
> - Aged plasma exposure reduced *CDH5* (−72%) and *PECAM1* (−54%), while increasing *TGFB1* (21-fold) and *ACTA2* (7.7-fold)
> - [[Fisetin]] plasma prevented EndoMT by restoring endothelial markers (3.1-fold *CDH5*, 2-fold *PECAM1*) and suppressing mesenchymal markers (−88% *TGFB1*, −89% *ACTA2*)
> - [[CXCL12]] directly drives EndoMT: CXCL12 inhibition with [[LIT-927]] rescued endothelial markers; CXCL12 add-back induced mesenchymal markers
> - This represents a novel mechanism by which cellular senescence drives age-related [[Endothelial Dysfunction|endothelial dysfunction]]

## Connections

- [[Endothelial Cells]]: ECs undergo EndoMT, losing endothelial identity and gaining mesenchymal properties
- [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]]: SASP factors, particularly CXCL12, drive EndoMT in senescent ECs
- [[CXCL12]]: Key SASP chemokine that promotes EndoMT by suppressing endothelial markers and inducing mesenchymal markers
- [[Endothelial Dysfunction]]: EndoMT is a mechanism of age-related endothelial dysfunction
- [[Fisetin]]: Senolytic that prevents SASP/CXCL12-induced EndoMT by normalizing the circulating milieu
- [[LIT-927]]: CXCL12 neutraligand that blocks EndoMT in aged plasma models
- [[TGFB1]]: Mesenchymal marker induced during EndoMT; TGF-β signaling is a canonical driver of the process
- [[ACTA2]]: Smooth muscle actin; mesenchymal marker upregulated during EndoMT
- [[CDH5]]: VE-cadherin; endothelial marker lost during EndoMT
- [[PECAM1]]: CD31; endothelial marker lost during EndoMT
- [[Cardiovascular Disease]]: EndoMT contributes to atherosclerosis, vascular stiffening, and fibrosis
