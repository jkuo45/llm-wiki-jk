## Overview

*ITPR2* encodes the inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate receptor type 2 (IP3R2), an [[IP3R|IP3 receptor]] isoform that mediates [[Calcium]] release from the [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]] (ER) into the cytosol. At [[MERCS|Mitochondria-ER contact sites (MERCS)]], IP3R2 interacts with the [[VDAC]]-[[MCU]] axis to enable direct Ca²⁺ transfer from the ER to mitochondrial matrix, shaping [[Mitochondrial Calcium]] dynamics.

## Connections

- [[Calcium]] — ITPR2 gates ER Ca²⁺ release
- [[MERCS]] — IP3R2 is enriched at MERCS, facilitating ER-to-mitochondria Ca²⁺ transfer
- [[MCU]] — Mitochondrial Ca²⁺ uniporter that receives Ca²⁺ released via IP3R2
- [[IP3R]] — Broader family of IP3 receptors (of which ITPR2 is an isoform)
- [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]] — Cellular compartment where ITPR2 resides
