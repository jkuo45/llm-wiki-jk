# MMP-2

MMP-2 (Matrix metalloproteinase-2), also known as gelatinase A, is a protease that degrades extracellular matrix components. It is one of five SASP factors strongly associated with mortality and is used as a biomarker for senescence.

## Mechanism of Action

- MMP-2 degrades extracellular matrix proteins, particularly collagen
- It is secreted by senescent cells as a SASP factor
- MMP-2 contributes to tissue remodelling and cancer metastasis
- MMP-2 is part of a panel of SASP biomarkers

## Role in SASP

- MMP-2 is a biomarker component of the SASP atlas
- It is one of five SASP factors strongly associated with mortality
- MMP-2 contributes to extracellular matrix degradation

## Clinical Relevance

- MMP-2 is a biomarker for senescence burden
- MMP-2 levels correlate with mortality
- MMP-2 is implicated in cancer metastasis
