# MMP-2

**Matrix metalloproteinase-2 (MMP-2)**, also known as gelatinase A (72-kDa gelatinase), is a zinc-dependent endopeptidase of the matrix metalloproteinase family. It is a secreted component of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] and is one of a small panel of SASP factors whose circulating levels strongly predict all-cause mortality in aged humans.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

MMP-2 is synthesized as a latent pro-enzyme (~72 kDa) whose catalytic domain contains the conserved Zn²⁺-binding HEXXHXXGXXH motif. A distinctive feature is its three fibronectin type II repeats, which allow it to bind and cleave denatured collagens (gelatins) and native [[Collagen]] types I, IV, V, VII and XI, plus laminin, fibronectin, elastin and basement-membrane components. Activation occurs primarily at the cell surface, where membrane type 1-MMP (MT1-MMP/MMP14), organized by TIMP2, converts pro-MMP-2 to the active 62-kDa species; [[TIMP3]] and other tissue inhibitors of metalloproteinases negatively regulate it (PMID 8388427). Unlike many MMPs, MMP-2 transcription is only weakly driven by the canonical SASP transcription factors [[NF-κB]] and [[AP-1]]; its elevation in senescence derives largely from post-translational activation and the high secretory output of senescent cells.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

MMP-2 was among the first matrix-degrading enzymes catalogued in the senescence secretome (Coppé et al., PMID 18708303; PMID 20212498). It cooperates with [[MMP-9]], [[MMP-3]] and [[MMP-12]] to remodel the [[Extracellular Matrix]] around senescent cells, releasing matrix-sequestered growth factors and creating a niche that favors [[Metastasis]], fibrosis and tissue ageing. Critically, MMP-2 belongs to a panel of mortality-associated SASP factors — together with [[GDF15]], [[RAGE]], [[VEGF]] and PARC/[[CCL18]] — that predicts all-cause mortality independently of chronological age (SASP Review 2024). By degrading basement membranes and activating latent [[TGF-β]], MMP-2 amplifies paracrine senescence and fibrogenic signaling.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

Plasma MMP-2 serves as a senescence-burden and [[Cardiovascular Disease]] biomarker, rising in vascular ageing, [[Atherosclerosis]] and heart failure. It promotes cancer invasion and [[Angiogenesis]] through VEGF release and ECM breach. Broad-spectrum synthetic MMP inhibitors (batimastat, marimastat) failed in the clinic because of poor specificity; selective gelatinase modulation is now explored as a senomorphic strategy.
