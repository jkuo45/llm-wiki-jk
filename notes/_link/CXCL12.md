# CXCL12 (SDF-1)

CXCL12 (C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 12), also known as SDF-1 (stromal cell-derived factor 1), is a chemokine that binds to CXCR4 and CXCR7 receptors. It cooperates with matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs) to facilitate cancer cell metastasis as a SASP factor.

## Mechanism of Action

- CXCL12 is secreted by senescent cells in the tumor microenvironment
- It binds to CXCR4 and CXCR7 receptors on cancer cells
- CXCL12 cooperates with MMPs to facilitate cancer cell metastasis
- CXCL12 promotes cancer cell migration and invasion

## Role in SASP

- CXCL12 is a pro-tumorigenic SASP factor
- It contributes to cancer metastasis through cooperation with MMPs
- CXCL12 is part of the SASP that remodels the tumor microenvironment

## Clinical Relevance

- CXCL12/CXCR4 signaling is a therapeutic target in cancer metastasis
- CXCL12 is elevated in various cancers and inflammatory conditions
- CXCL12 may serve as a biomancer for metastatic potential
