# Acetyl-CoA

**Acetyl-coenzyme A (Acetyl-CoA)** is a central metabolic intermediate that serves as the acetyl group donor for [[Histone Acetylation]].

## Role in Epigenetics
- Its availability links metabolism to the epigenetic regulation of gene expression.
