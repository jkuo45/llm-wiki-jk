# Thrombocytopenia

[[Thrombocytopenia]] is a reduction in circulating platelet (thrombocyte) count below the normal reference range, increasing bleeding and bruising risk. It is graded by severity and, when drug-induced, is often reversible upon dose reduction or discontinuation.

## Rapamycin-Associated Thrombocytopenia

Because [[mTORC1]] inhibition slows cell proliferation and function, chronic treatment with [[Rapamycin]] and [[Rapalog|rapalogs]] at high doses reduces blood cell counts. Mild, reversible thrombocytopenia—along with anemia and leukopenia—is among the most common hematological side effects ([Blagosklonny, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6814615/)). A mild reduction in platelet numbers may even be viewed as beneficial, analogous to the intended anti-platelet effect of [[Aspirin]].

> [!note] Monitoring
> In anti-aging scheduling, dose and frequency are limited by the emergence of side effects including thrombocytopenia, anemia, and leukopenia; the tailored optimal dose is the highest dose that does not produce these effects.
