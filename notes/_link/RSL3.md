# RSL3

**RSL3** (Ras-selective lethal 3) is a small-molecule ferroptosis inducer that directly inhibits [[GPX4]] by covalently modifying its catalytic [[Selenocysteine]] residue. It is one of the most widely used tool compounds in ferroptosis research.

## Mechanism of Action

RSL3 contains a chloroacetamide moiety that reacts with the selenol group of the catalytic selenocysteine (Sec46) in GPX4, forming a covalent adduct that irreversibly inactivates the enzyme. This inactivation blocks the reduction of phospholipid hydroperoxides, leading to iron-dependent [[Lipid Peroxidation]] accumulation and [[Ferroptosis|ferroptotic]] cell death. Unlike erastin (which inhibits system Xc⁻ and depletes [[Glutathione]]), RSL3 bypasses upstream regulation and acts directly on GPX4, making it a more specific probe for GPX4-dependent ferroptosis.

## Research Applications

RSL3 is used extensively to:
- Induce ferroptosis in cultured cells (typical IC₅₀ in the low nanomolar to low micromolar range, cell-type dependent)
- Validate GPX4 dependence of ferroptotic phenotypes
- Study resistance mechanisms (e.g., [[FSP1]]/CoQ10 axis, [[DHODH]] pathway)
- Identify genetic modifiers of ferroptosis sensitivity in CRISPR screens
