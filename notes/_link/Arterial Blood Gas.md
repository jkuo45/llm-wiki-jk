# Arterial Blood Gas

[[Arterial Blood Gas]] (ABG) analysis directly measures pH, PaO2, PaCO2, and measured oxygen saturation (SaO2). It is the preferred assessment during [[Methylene blue]] infusion, when [[Pulse Oximetry]] artifactually underestimates saturation; [[Co-oximetry]] further resolves [[Methemoglobin]] fraction.
