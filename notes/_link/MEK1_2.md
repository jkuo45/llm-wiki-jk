# MEK1/2

MEK1 (MAP2K1) and MEK2 (MAP2K2) are dual-specificity kinases that phosphorylate and activate [[ERK1/2]] (MAPK3/MAPK1) in the [[MAPK/ERK pathway]]. They are activated by [[Raf]] kinases ([[BRAF]], [[ARAF]], [[RAF1]]) and integrate signals from [[RAS]] downstream of [[Receptor Tyrosine Kinases]] (RTKs). MEK1/2 are critical mediators of [[Cell Proliferation]], [[Differentiation]], and [[Survival]]. They are clinically important therapeutic targets: [[MEK Inhibitors]] (e.g., [[Trametinib]], [[Cobimetinib]], [[Selumetinib]]) are FDA-approved for [[Melanoma]] with [[BRAF]] V600 mutations and [[Neurofibromatosis Type 1]].
