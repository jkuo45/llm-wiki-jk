# Postherpetic Neuralgia

**Postherpetic neuralgia (PHN)** is persistent neuropathic pain in the dermatome of a resolved **[[Herpes Zoster|herpes zoster (shingles)]]** rash. It is the most common complication of shingles and can last months to years.

> [!note] Prevention
> The [[Shingles Vaccine|shingles vaccine]] lowers the risk of herpes zoster and consequently of postherpetic neuralgia, particularly in older adults where PHN is most frequent and debilitating.
