# Varicella Zoster Virus

**Varicella zoster virus (VZV)**, also called human alphaherpesvirus 3, is the causative agent of **chickenpox** (varicella) on primary infection and establishes lifelong latency in sensory ganglia. Upon waning cellular immunity — especially with aging — it can reactivate as **[[Herpes Zoster|herpes zoster (shingles)]]**.

> [!info] Why it matters for inflammaging
> Latent VZV periodically reactivates subclinically; recurrent antigenic challenge contributes to chronic immune activation. The [[Shingles Vaccine|shingles vaccine]] reduces reactivation and, in a population cohort, was associated with lower [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]] and slower [[Biological Aging|biological aging]] — implying that controlling VZV reactivation calms age-related background inflammation.
