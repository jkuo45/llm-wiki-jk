# Oligomycin

[[Oligomycin]] is a macrolide antibiotic that inhibits the F<sub>O</sub> subunit of mitochondrial ATP synthase ([[Complex IV|Complex V]]), blocking oxidative phosphorylation and causing a characteristic drop in oxygen consumption with a compensatory rise in glycolysis (ECAR).

## Use in the methylene blue study

In the Seahorse XF24 assay of HT-22 cells, oligomycin (1 µg/ml) was injected before [[FCCP]] to suppress ATP-synthase-linked respiration [^1]. Even under oligomycin, treatment with [[Methylene blue]], [[Toluidine Blue O]], and [[Neutral Red]] significantly increased oxygen consumption rate (OCR) and reduced extracellular acidification rate (ECAR) versus vehicle, demonstrating that these compounds sustain electron transport and suppress anaerobic glycolysis independently of ATP synthase [^1]. [[2-Chlorophenothiazine]] and [[Chlorpromazine]] had no effect [^1].
