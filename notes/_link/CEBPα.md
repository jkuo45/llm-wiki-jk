# C/EBPα (CEBPα)

C/EBPα (CCAAT/enhancer-binding protein alpha) is a transcription factor that binds to senescence-activated enhancers to promote the expression of SASP genes in replicative senescence fibroblasts. It is similar to [[CEBPβ|C/EBPβ]] in its role as a SASP regulator.

## Mechanism of Action

- C/EBPα binds to senescence-activated enhancers
- It promotes the expression of SASP genes in replicative senescence
- C/EBPα cooperates with [[NF-κB]] to regulate SASP gene expression
- C/EBPα is similar to [[CEBPβ|C/EBPβ]] in function

## Role in SASP

- C/EBPα is a transcriptional regulator of SASP genes
- It binds to senescence-activated enhancers to promote SASP expression
- C/EBPα contributes to the epigenetic regulation of SASP

## Clinical Relevance

- C/EBPα is a tumor suppressor in certain cancers
- C/EBPα mutations are associated with acute myeloid leukemia
- C/EBPα may be a therapeutic target for modulating SASP
