# Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis
Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) is a progressive neurodegenerative disease that affects nerve cells in the brain and the spinal cord. [[Oxidative Stress]] is heavily implicated, particularly through mutations in the [[Superoxide Dismutase]] (SOD1) gene, which lead to mitochondrial impairment and motor neuron death.
Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) is a progressive [[Neurodegenerative Diseases|neurodegenerative disease]] that affects nerve cells in the brain and the spinal cord. It leads to the loss of muscle control and eventually death.
[[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs) derived from ALS patients have been used to create motor neurons for disease modeling and [[Drug Discovery]]. Research has shown that factors like [[Oct4]] and [[Klf4]] can have neuroprotective effects in ALS models.
