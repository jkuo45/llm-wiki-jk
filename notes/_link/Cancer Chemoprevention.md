# Cancer Chemoprevention

**Cancer chemoprevention** is the strategy of reducing cancer incidence and mortality by administering agents that prevent, reverse, or delay the carcinogenic process. Dietary phytochemicals such as [[Flavonoids]] — particularly [[Apigenin]], [[Quercetin]], and [[Kaempferol]] — are leading candidates.

## Mechanisms

Chemopreventive agents act through antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, modulation of carcinogen metabolism (e.g., [[UGT1A1]], GST phase II enzymes), cell-cycle arrest, apoptosis induction, and epigenetic reprogramming ([[HDAC]] inhibition, DNA-demethylation). [[Apigenin]] exemplifies a multimodal chemopreventive flavone active across >18 human malignancies.
