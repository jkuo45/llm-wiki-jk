# Liver X Receptor

[[Liver X Receptor|Liver X receptors (LXRs)]] are nuclear receptors (LXRα/NR1H3 and LXRβ/NR1H2) that regulate cholesterol, fatty acid, and lipid homeostasis. In the SIRT1 literature, LXR is identified among the diverse non-histone substrates deacetylated/modulated by SIRT1, linking sirtuin activity to cholesterol metabolism.

## SIRT1 Interaction

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - SIRT1 Protein roles at the nexus of health, disease, and therapeutics|SIRT1: Protein Roles at the Nexus (Chaqour, 2026)]]
> SIRT1 modulates over 40 known substrates including the liver X receptor (LXR), deacetylating and regulating its activity in lipid and cholesterol metabolism.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - berrougui2009_resveratrol_as_atheroprotective_compound|Berrougui et al., Atherosclerosis 2009]]
> Resveratrol induces LXR-α expression in human monocyte-derived macrophages, which in turn upregulates ABCA1 and ABCG1 — the key cholesterol efflux transporters. LXR-α links resveratrol's anti-atherogenic effects to cholesterol homeostasis.
