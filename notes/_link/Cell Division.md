# Cell Division

Cell division is the process by which a parent cell divides into two or more daughter cells. In [[Eukaryotic Cell]]s, it comprises [[Mitosis]] (nuclear division) and [[Cytokinesis]] (cytoplasmic division). [[Prokaryote]]s divide by [[Binary Fission]]. Cell division is tightly regulated by the [[Cell Cycle]], with checkpoints ensuring [[DNA Replication]] and [[Chromosome]] segregation accuracy. Key regulators include [[Cyclin]]/[[CDK]] complexes, [[Rb]]/[[E2F]], [[p53]], and [[Checkpoint Kinase]]s. Dysregulated cell division is a hallmark of [[Cancer]]. [[Meiosis]] is a specialized cell division producing [[Gamete]]s.
