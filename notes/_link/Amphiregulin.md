# Amphiregulin

**Amphiregulin (AREG)** is an epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) ligand that is a component of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].

## Role in SASP
Amphiregulin is secreted by [[Senescent Cells]] and acts in an autocrine and paracrine manner to:
- Promote cell proliferation and survival in neighboring cells.
- Contribute to tissue remodeling and repair during acute senescence.
- Potentially promote tumor growth in chronic senescence settings.

## Clinical Relevance
Amphiregulin has been implicated in tissue regeneration but also in the progression of certain cancers where it is overexpressed.
