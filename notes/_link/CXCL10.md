# CXCL10

CXCL10 (C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 10), also known as IP-10 (interferon gamma-induced protein 10), is a chemokine that binds to [[CXCR3]] and is secreted by ROS-induced senescent hepatocytes. It enhances natural killer cell activity and is a key SASP factor involved in immune surveillance.

## Mechanism of Action

- CXCL10 is secreted by ROS-induced senescent hepatocytes
- It binds to [[CXCR3]] on natural killer cells to enhance their activity
- CXCL10 is regulated by the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS–STING]] pathway
- [[EZH2]] represses CXCL10 expression in senescent tumor cells

## Role in SASP

- CXCL10 is an immunomodulatory SASP factor
- It enhances natural killer cell activity, contributing to immune surveillance of senescent cells
- CXCL10 is part of the interferon response associated with senescence

## Clinical Relevance

- CXCL10 is elevated in various inflammatory and autoimmune conditions
- CXCL10/CXCR3 signaling is important for immune cell recruitment
- CXCL10 may serve as a biomarker for senescence in liver diseases
