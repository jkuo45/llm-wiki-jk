# Flavone

**Flavone** is the basic 2-phenylchromen-4-one scaffold of the flavonoid class. Substitution patterns yield diverse bioactive compounds, including **apigenin** (4',5,7-trihydroxyflavone).

## Relevance

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> Apigenin is described as "a flavonoid (4',5,7-trihydroxyflavone)" and a plant-derived flavone. Its flavone scaffold underlies its [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] activity — direct binding of [[PRDX6]] and suppression of the [[SASP|SASP]].
