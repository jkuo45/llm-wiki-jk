# Multiple Myeloma
Multiple myeloma is a cancer that forms in a type of white blood cell called a plasma cell.
**Multiple Myeloma (MM)** is a malignancy of **plasma cells** — terminally differentiated B lymphocytes responsible for antibody secretion — that accumulate in the bone marrow, producing a monoclonal immunoglobulin (M-protein) and causing end-organ damage (bone lesions, renal failure, hypercalcemia, and anemia; **CRAB** criteria).
It is the second most common hematologic malignancy (after non-Hodgkin lymphoma) and remains largely incurable, though survival has improved dramatically with novel agents over the past two decades.
## Pathophysiology
- **Cell of origin**: Post-germinal center plasma cell with oncogenic mutations (e.g., t(4;14), t(14;16), del(17p), 1q gain).
- **Bone marrow microenvironment**: Myeloma cells depend on interactions with stromal cells, osteoclasts, and immunosuppressive cells (Tregs, MDSCs) for survival signals (IL-6, VEGF, RANKL, etc.).
- **CD38 overexpression**: Malignant plasma cells express extremely high levels of **[[CD38]]**, making it the premier therapeutic target in MM.
- **Immunosuppression**: MM patients have profound immune dysfunction — impaired T-cell function, NK cell depletion, and elevated Tregs/MDSCs.
## Diagnostic Criteria
MM requires ≥10% clonal bone marrow plasma cells plus ≥1 myeloma-defining event (MDE):
- CRAB criteria (hyperCalcemia, Renal insufficiency, Anemia, Bone lesions)
- Biomarkers: serum free light chain ratio ≥100, bone marrow plasma cells ≥60%, or >1 focal lesion on MRI
## Treatment
### Anti-CD38 Monoclonal Antibodies (cornerstone)
| Drug | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[Daratumumab]] | [[CD38]] | First anti-CD38 mAb; approved 2015; IgG1κ; ADCC/CDC/ADCP/immunomodulation |
| [[Isatuximab]] | [[CD38]] | Approved 2020; distinct epitope; stronger direct apoptosis |
Both are standard of care across newly diagnosed and relapsed/refractory MM, in combination with:
- **IMiDs**: Lenalidomide, pomalidomide
- **Proteasome inhibitors**: Bortezomib, carfilzomib
- **Steroids**: Dexamethasone
### Other Modalities
- **Autologous stem cell transplant (ASCT)**: Standard for eligible patients
- **CAR-T therapy**: BCMA-directed (e.g., idecabtagene vicleucel, ciltacabtagene autoleucel); CD38-directed CAR-T in development
- **Bispecific antibodies**: Teclistamab (BCMA×CD3), elranatamab
- **Selinexor**: Nuclear export inhibitor
## CD38 and Resistance
A key resistance mechanism to anti-CD38 mAbs is **CD38 downregulation** on myeloma cells over time, driven by trogocytosis (stripping of CD38 from cell surface by NK/macrophage interactions) and transcriptional repression. Compensatory strategies include:
- All-trans retinoic acid (ATRA) to re-induce CD38 expression
- Combining [[Daratumumab]] + [[Isatuximab]] (distinct epitopes) — investigational
