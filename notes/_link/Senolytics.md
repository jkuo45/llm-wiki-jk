# Senolytics
**Senolytics** are a class of small molecules that selectively induce death in [[Senescent Cells]] by targeting senescent cell anti-apoptotic pathways (SCAPs).

## Therapeutic Potential
- Aimed at alleviating age-related diseases by removing the burden of [[Cellular Senescence|Senescent Cells]] and their associated [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].

## Key Senolytics
- **[[Dasatinib]] + [[Quercetin]] (D+Q)**: The first-in-class senolytic combination. Dasatinib targets tyrosine kinases, while quercetin inhibits BCL-2 family proteins and PI3K.
- **[[Navitoclax]] (ABT-263)**: A potent BCL-2/BCL-XL inhibitor that induces apoptosis in senescent cells.
- **Fisetin**: A naturally occurring flavonoid with senolytic activity.
