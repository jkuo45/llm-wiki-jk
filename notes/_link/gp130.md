# gp130

gp130 (glycoprotein 130, also known as [[IL6ST]] or CD130) is a transmembrane glycoprotein that serves as a common signal transducer for the [[IL-6]] family of cytokines. It is a critical component of cytokine receptor complexes that activate [[JAK]]/STAT, [[ERK]], and PI3K signaling pathways.

## Structure

gp130 is a type I transmembrane protein containing:
- Six extracellular domains (D1-D6) including an immunoglobulin-like domain (D2) and a cytokine-binding domain (D3)
- A single transmembrane helix
- An intracellular box1/box2 motif essential for [[Janus Kinase|JAK]] activation

## Role in Humanin Signaling

> [!info] Humanin Receptor Complex
> gp130 is a key component of the trimeric humanin receptor, which consists of [[Ciliary Neurotrophic Factor Receptor]] (CNTFR), [[WSX1]] (IL-27Rα), and gp130 (IL-6Rβ). This complex mediates humanin's cytoprotective effects through [[STAT3]] activation and [[ERK]]1/2 signaling. Humanin promotes hetero-oligomerization of CNTFR, WSX-1, and gp130, activating downstream survival pathways.

## Signaling Pathways

gp130-mediated signaling includes:

1. **[[JAK]]/STAT pathway**: Activation of [[JAK]] kinases (JAK1, JAK2, TYK2) leads to phosphorylation and nuclear translocation of [[STAT3]] and STAT1, promoting expression of anti-apoptotic genes.
2. **[[ERK]]1/2 MAPK pathway**: Activation of the Ras/Raf/MEK/ERK cascade promotes cell proliferation and survival.
3. **PI3K/Akt pathway**: Activation of [[Phosphatidylinositol 3-Kinase]] and [[Akt]] promotes metabolic regulation and cell survival.

## Biological Roles

gp130 is essential for:
- Embryonic development (gp130 knockout mice die perinatally)
- Hematopoiesis and immune responses
- Cardiac function
- Hepatic acute-phase response
- Mediating the pleiotropic effects of [[IL-6]] and related cytokines

## Disease Relevance

Dysregulated gp130 signaling is implicated in:
- [[Inflammation]] and autoimmune diseases
- Cancer (constitutive gp130 activation drives hepatocellular carcinoma)
- [[Atherosclerosis]]: gp130-mediated signaling modulates vascular inflammation

## Connections

- [[IL-6]]: gp130 is the shared signal-transducing receptor for the entire IL-6 cytokine family.
- [[STAT3]]: A primary downstream transcription factor activated by gp130.
- [[JAK]]: Janus kinases associate with gp130 intracellular domain to initiate signaling.
- [[ERK]]: gp130 activates the MAPK/ERK pathway.
- [[Humanin]]: gp130 is a component of the humanin receptor complex.
- [[Ciliary Neurotrophic Factor Receptor]]: Partners with gp130 in the humanin receptor.
- [[WSX1]]: Partners with gp130 in the humanin receptor complex.
