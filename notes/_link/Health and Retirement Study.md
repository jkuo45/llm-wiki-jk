# Health and Retirement Study

The **Health and Retirement Study (HRS)** is a longitudinal, nationally representative panel study of U.S. adults aged 50+ sponsored by the National Institute on Aging. It collects health, economic, and biomarker data, enabling population-level analyses of [[Biological Aging|biological aging]].

> [!info] Used in the shingles-vaccine analysis
> Kim & Crimmins (2026) analyzed >3,800 HRS participants aged ≥70 in 2016 to show that the [[Shingles Vaccine|shingles vaccine]] was associated with slower biological aging and lower [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]].
