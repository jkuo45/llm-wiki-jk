**SLC6A8** is a gene on chromosome Xq28 that encodes the creatine transporter CT1, a member of the [[Solute Carrier 6 (SLC6) Family|solute carrier 6 (SLC6) family]] of sodium- and chloride-dependent symporters. It is the primary transporter responsible for cellular uptake of [[Creatine]] from the extracellular space and across the [[Blood-Brain Barrier|blood-brain barrier]].

## Expression & Function

- SLC6A8 is widely expressed in [[Brain]], [[Muscle]], [[Heart]], [[Kidney]], and at the blood-brain barrier.
- The transporter mediates electrogenic symport of creatine with Na⁺ and Cl⁻, maintaining intracellular creatine against a steep concentration gradient.
- SLC6A8 activity is upregulated in [[Tumor-Infiltrating Immune Cells|tumor-infiltrating immune cells]], where creatine uptake supports [[CD8 T Cell|CD8⁺ T cell]] antitumor immunity \[[^17]\].

## Clinical Significance

### Creatine Transporter Deficiency (CTD)

- X-linked recessive disorder (OMIM #300352) caused by SLC6A8 mutations.
- Accounts for ~2% of [[Intellectual Disabilities|intellectual disability]] in males.
- **Features:** Global developmental delay, [[Epilepsy]], [[Speech Development|speech/language delay]], [[Autism|autistic-like behaviors]], and [[Movement Disorders|movement disorders]].
- **Diagnosis:** Elevated urinary creatine:creatinine ratio; reduced brain creatine on [[Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy|MRS]]; molecular genetic testing.
- **Treatment:** Unlike AGAT/GAMT deficiencies, oral [[Creatine Supplementation|creatine supplementation]] is less effective because the transporter defect blocks cellular uptake. Adjunctive strategies (e.g., [[Cyclocreatine]], [[Arginine]], [[Glycine]]) are under investigation.

### Cancer Immunology

- SLC6A8 is markedly upregulated in [[Tumor Microenvironment|tumor microenvironment]] immune cells.
- Creatine uptake via SLC6A8 is required for optimal CD8⁺ T cell effector function and antitumor immunity \[[^17]\], identifying SLC6A8 as a potential immunometabolic checkpoint.
