# cGAS–STING Pathway

The **cyclic GMP–AMP synthase–stimulator of interferon genes (cGAS–STING)** complex is an innate immune sensor that detects the presence of DNA in the cytosol and activates an inflammatory response. It is a crucial driver of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] and age-associated inflammation.

## Mechanism in Senescence

During [[Cellular Senescence]], the cGAS–STING pathway is activated by cytoplasmic DNA from multiple sources:

### Cytoplasmic DNA Sources

1. **Cytoplasmic Chromatin Fragments (CCFs)**: Downregulation of [[DNASE2]] and [[TREX1]] in senescent cells results in reduced degradation and accumulation of cytoplasmic DNA
2. **Mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA)**: Released from mitochondria through [[BAX]] and [[BAK]] macropores during minority mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization (miMOMP)
3. **LINE1 Retrotransposable Elements**: Elevated during late senescence; cytoplasmic LINE1 cDNA triggers interferon signalling

### Sensing Mechanism

- [[TOP1]] cleavage complexes (TOP1cc) and [[G3BP1]] assist cGAS recognition of and binding to CCFs
- [[Toll-like Receptor 2|TLR2]] activates the SASP via the cGAS–STING–NF-κB cascade during OIS
- cGAS generates cyclic GMP–AMP (cGAMP), which activates [[STING]]
- STING translocates to trigger [[TBK1]]–[[IRF3]] interferon signalling and [[NF-κB]] activation

## Downstream Effects

### SASP Activation
- Drives expression of [[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], [[CXCL2]], [[CXCL10]], [[CCL3]], [[CCL5]], and [[TNFα]]
- Inhibition of cGAS or STING greatly suppresses NF-κB activation in therapy-induced senescence
- Both interferon signalling and NF-κB are part of the cGAS–STING pathway output

### Age-Related Inflammation
- Cytosolic DNA from mitochondria and cGAS–STING drive ageing-associated and SASP-like low-grade inflammation ([[Inflammaging]])
- STING regulation reduced accumulation of inflammatory cells in kidneys of aged mice
- STING inhibition attenuated expression of age-related senescence and immunity genes (Il-1β, Il-6, Tnf, Cxcl9, Cxcl10)

### Immune Modulation
- Drives innate immune responses that can be both beneficial (cancer immunosurveillance) and detrimental (chronic inflammation)
- Contributes to inflammageing and maintenance of the SASP through LINE1 de-repression

## Therapeutic Targeting

- **Nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors (NRTIs)**: Lamivudine (3TC) inhibits LINE1 propagation, reducing cGAS–STING–NF-κB SASP
- **Olive phenols**: Preserve lamin B1 expression, reducing cGAS/STING/NFκB-mediated SASP
- **STING inhibitors**: Reduce age-related inflammation and senescent cell accumulation
- **cGAS inhibitors**: Suppress SASP activation
