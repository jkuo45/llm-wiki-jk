# ASF1a

ASF1a (Anti-Silencing Function 1a) is a conserved histone H3–H4 chaperone that functions as the upstream hand-off factor for replication-independent histone deposition. In senescence, ASF1a is a core member — with [[HIRA]] and [[UBN1]] — of the chaperone complex required for formation of [[Senescence-Associated Heterochromatin Foci|SAHF]], as summarized in the npj Aging review of the senescent epigenetic landscape (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2).

## Overview

ASF1 exists as two paralogs in mammals, ASF1a and ASF1b. ASF1a is the predominant isoform in differentiated and senescent cells. It binds newly synthesized H3–H4 dimers, masking their acidic patches to prevent non-specific aggregation, and delivers them to downstream chaperones (HIRA, CAF-1, or Rtt106/DAXX depending on context).

## Structure and Function

ASF1a adopts a β-sandwich fold that engages the H3–H4 dimer at the H3 β-sheet and the H4 α2 helix. Its interaction with HIRA is essential for replication-independent deposition of the [[H3.3]] variant at active genes and heterochromatin.

## Role in Senescence and SAHF

Dissection of SAHF established that the HIRA/ASF1a/UBN1 pathway is essential for their formation (Zhang et al., 2005, PMID 15680342). Banumathy et al. (2009, PMID 19103796) showed human UBN1 is an ortholog of yeast Hpc2p with an essential role in the HIRA/ASF1a chromatin-remodeling pathway in senescent cells. Depletion of ASF1a blocks the heterochromatic condensation that builds SAHF, mirroring the effect of HIRA loss. SAHF disruption, in turn, correlates with reduced [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]] expression.

## Mechanistic Details

ASF1a buffers the cellular pool of free H3–H4, controlling the flux of H3.3 into nucleosomes. During senescence, when canonical histone biosynthesis declines, ASF1a becomes critical for maintaining chromatin via variant deposition. It bridges the DNA-replication-coupled and -independent deposition systems and connects to DNA-damage and p53/pRb pathways that license SAHF assembly (Ye et al., 2007).

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

Because the HIRA/ASF1a/UBN1 axis modulates SAHF and SASP without abolishing growth arrest, it is a candidate node for [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] intervention (see [[KDM4]], [[BRD4]], [[DOT1L]]). ASF1a's role in variant deposition also intersects with the long-lived [[H3.3]] program that accrues damage with age.
