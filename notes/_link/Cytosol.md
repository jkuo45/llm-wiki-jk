# Cytosol

The **cytosol** is the aqueous intracellular fluid that fills the cytoplasmic compartment of [[Eukaryotic Cells]], excluding the contents of membrane-bound organelles such as the [[Nucleus]], [[Mitochondria]], and [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]]. It comprises approximately 50-60% of total cell volume.

## Composition

The cytosol is a complex aqueous solution (~70% water) containing:
- [[Ions]] — [[K⁺]], [[Na⁺]], [[Ca²⁺]], [[Cl⁻]], [[Mg²⁺]]
- [[Metabolites]] — [[ATP]], [[NADH]], [[NADPH]], [[Amino Acids]], [[Glycolytic intermediates]]
- [[Proteins]] — ~20-40% of cellular protein mass, including [[Enzymes]], [[Structural Proteins]], [[Signaling Proteins]]
- [[RNA]] — mainly [[mRNA]], [[tRNA]], and [[Ribosomal subunits]]
- [[Cytoskeletal Elements]] — [[Actin]], [[Microtubules]], [[Intermediate Filaments]]

## Functions

- **[[Glycolysis]]** — the first stage of glucose catabolism occurs in the cytosol
- **[[Protein Synthesis]]** — [[Translation]] on free [[Ribosomes]] produces cytosolic and nuclear proteins
- **[[Signal Transduction]]** — second messengers ([[cAMP]], [[Ca²⁺]], [[IP3]]) diffuse through the cytosol
- **[[Metabolic Pathways]]** — [[Fatty acid synthesis]], [[Gluconeogenesis]], [[Pentose Phosphate Pathway]]
- **[[Intracellular Transport]]** — diffusion of [[Metabolites]], [[Vesicles]], and [[Organelles]] via [[Cytoskeleton]]

The cytosol is not a static fluid but a crowded, structured environment (macromolecular crowding) where [[Liquid-Liquid Phase Separation]] drives the formation of membrane-less organelles such as [[Stress Granules]] and [[Processing Bodies]].

## Connections
- [[Eukaryotic Cells]] — the cytosol is a defining compartment of eukaryotic cell organization
- [[Glycolysis]] — the primary energy-yielding pathway localized to the cytosol
- [[Cytoskeleton]] — filament network that organizes the cytosol and enables transport
- [[Mitochondria]] — adjacent organelle; cytosol provides substrates for OXPHOS
- [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]] — cytosolic face is the site of ribosome docking and protein synthesis
- [[Metabolites]] — small molecules diffuse through the cytosol
- [[Stress Granules]] — membrane-less organelles assembled via phase separation in the cytosol
