# MMP-12

MMP-12 (Matrix metalloproteinase-12), also known as macrophage metalloelastase, is a protease that degrades extracellular matrix components. It is a SASP factor associated with detrimental effects on senescent dermal fibroblasts during and following tissue damage, particularly noted for its contribution to skin ageing.

## Mechanism of Action

- MMP-12 degrades extracellular matrix proteins
- It is secreted by senescent fibroblasts
- MMP-12 contributes to tissue remodelling and degradation
- MMP-12 is associated with skin ageing

## Role in SASP

- MMP-12 is a pro-ageing SASP protease
- It contributes to extracellular matrix degradation
- MMP-12 may be a target for anti-ageing interventions

## Clinical Relevance

- MMP-12 is a biomarker for skin ageing
- MMP-12 is implicated in COPD and atherosclerosis
- MMP-12 inhibitors are being developed
