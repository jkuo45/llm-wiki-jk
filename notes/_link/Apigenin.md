# Apigenin

**Apigenin** is a naturally occurring flavonoid found in many fruits and vegetables (e.g., parsley, celery, chamomile). It has potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and senomorphic properties.

## Senomorphic Activity
Apigenin suppresses the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] by:
- Inhibiting [[NFKB|NF-κB]] signaling.
- Reducing the expression of pro-inflammatory cytokines like [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]].

## Therapeutic Potential
Apigenin is being studied as a potential [[Senomorphic Therapy]] to mitigate the detrimental effects of chronic SASP in aging and cancer.
