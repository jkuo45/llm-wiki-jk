# HP1alpha

HP1alpha (heterochromatin protein 1 alpha), encoded by CBX5, is a chromodomain-containing chromatin reader that binds the H3K9me3 heterochromatin mark. In senescence, HP1alpha is recruited by the corepressor [[KAP1]] (upon [[Sirtuin 6|SIRT6]] mono-ADP-ribosylation) to package LINE-1 ([[LINE-1]]) retrotransposons into repressive heterochromatin; its failure in aging/senescence permits retrotransposon derepression and SASP-driving inflammation.

## Overview

HP1 proteins (α/β/γ, CBX5/1/3) share an N-terminal chromodomain that specifically recognizes H3K9me3, a hinge, and a chromo-shadow domain mediating protein–protein interactions. HP1alpha is the predominant isoform at constitutive heterochromatin (pericentromeres, telomeres) and is a hallmark of silenced repetitive DNA.

## Structure & Function

The chromodomain "reads" H3K9me3 deposited by SUV39H1/SETDB1, while the chromo-shadow domain dimerizes HP1 and bridges it to KAP1, SUV39H1, and other silencing factors. This nucleation drives heterochromatin spreading and the formation of compact, transcriptionally inert domains.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

The review places HP1α within the retrotransposon-silencing axis. SIRT6 mono-ADP-ribosylates [[KAP1]], promoting KAP1–HP1α interaction and heterochromatinization of LINE-1 5′UTRs (refs. 136, 137). In senescence, declining RB1 and depleted SIRT6 weaken this complex, so HP1α is no longer efficiently recruited to L1 loci. The resulting decompaction permits L1 transcription and reverse transcription; cytoplasmic L1 cDNA activates [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS–STING]], inducing type I interferon and SASP. HP1α loss therefore contributes to the heterochromatin erosion (mirrored by [[Senescence-Associated Distension of Satellites|SADS]]) that licenses retrotransposon-driven inflammaging.

## Mechanistic Details

- HP1α recruitment to L1 is gated by KAP1 ribosylation, tying it to SIRT6 abundance.
- H3K9me3 (deposited by [[SUV39H1]]) is the direct ligand for the HP1α chromodomain, linking the H3K9 axis to retrotransposon control.
- Failure of HP1α silencing is a convergent point of the DNA-methylation loss and heterochromatin decompaction described in the review.

## Therapeutic Relevance

Preserving HP1α-dependent heterochromatin at retrotransposons could be [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]]. NRTIs such as emtricitabine (NCT04500847) interrupt the downstream L1/HERV reverse-transcription inflammation, offering a clinically tractable route to mitigate the consequences of HP1α/SIRT6 silencing failure.
