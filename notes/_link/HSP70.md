# HSP70

HSP70 (Heat Shock Protein 70 kDa) is a family of highly conserved molecular chaperones that assist in [[Protein Folding]], prevent [[Protein Aggregation]], and facilitate [[Proteostasis]]. Major family members include [[HSPA1A]] (HSP72), [[HSPA8]] (HSC70), [[HSPA5]] ([[BiP]]/GRP78, in the ER), and [[HSPA9]] (mortalin, in mitochondria). HSP70 works in conjunction with [[HSP40]] (J-domain proteins) and [[HSP90]] in the chaperone network. It binds exposed hydrophobic patches on unfolded proteins, using [[ATP]] hydrolysis for substrate release. HSP70 is induced by [[Heat Shock]], [[Oxidative Stress]], and [[Proteotoxic Stress]] via [[HSF1]]. It inhibits [[Apoptosis]] by blocking [[Apoptosome]] formation and is implicated in [[Cancer]] and [[Neurodegeneration]].
