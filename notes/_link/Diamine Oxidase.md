# Diamine Oxidase

[[Diamine Oxidase]] ([[DAO]]) is the primary enzyme that breaks down [[Histamine]] from ingested food, expressed mainly in the intestinal mucosa. Adequate DAO activity protects against dietary histamine load.

## Aminoguanidine Inhibition

[[Aminoguanidine]] is a potent inhibitor of DAO. When taken **orally**, it blocks gut DAO, preventing degradation of dietary histamine and causing acute [[Histamine Intolerance]] — headaches, flushing, hives, and tachycardia after histamine-rich foods (aged cheeses, cured meats, fermented foods). IV administration bypasses gut DAO but still affects systemic DAO.

> [!warning] Oral aminoguanidine → histamine intolerance
> Because oral aminoguanidine inhibits intestinal DAO, even normal histamine-containing meals can provoke allergic-like reactions. This is a major route-specific safety concern.
