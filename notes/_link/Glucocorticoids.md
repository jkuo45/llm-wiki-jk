# Glucocorticoids

Glucocorticoids are steroid hormones that have potent anti-inflammatory effects. They block pro-inflammatory [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] by impairing [[IL-1α]]-mediated [[NF-κB]] activity, making them a class of senomorphic therapy.

## Mechanism of Action

- Glucocorticoids bind to glucocorticoid receptors
- They impair [[IL-1α]]-mediated [[NF-κB]] activity
- This blocks pro-inflammatory SASP factor expression
- Glucocorticoids are potent anti-inflammatory agents

## Role in SASP

- Glucocorticoids are senomorphic therapies that modulate SASP
- They specifically block the pro-inflammatory component of SASP
- Glucocorticoids may be beneficial in reducing age-related inflammation

## Clinical Relevance

- Glucocorticoids are widely used anti-inflammatory drugs
- Long-term use has significant side effects
- Glucocorticoids may have senomorphic effects in certain contexts
