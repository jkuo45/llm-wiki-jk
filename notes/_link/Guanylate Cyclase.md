# Guanylate Cyclase

[[Guanylate Cyclase]] (GC) enzymes convert GTP to [[Cyclic Guanosine Monophosphate]] (cGMP). The **soluble** isoform ([[Soluble Guanylyl Cyclase]]) is activated by [[Nitric Oxide]]; the particulate isoform is activated by natriuretic peptides.

> [!note] Methylene blue target
> [[Methylene blue]] inhibits guanylate cyclase (specifically sGC), blocking NO→cGMP signaling — the basis of its vasoconstrictive effect in [[Vasoplegic Shock]].
