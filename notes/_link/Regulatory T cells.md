# Regulatory T cells

[[Regulatory T cells]] (Tregs) are a CD4⁺ T-lymphocyte subset that suppresses excessive immune activation and maintains self-tolerance. They counter chronic inflammation and autoimmunity.

## Rapamycin and Tregs

Inhibition of [[mTORC1]] by [[Rapamycin]] reduces lymphocyte overactivation and pro-inflammatory cytokines while increasing Treg numbers. The immune effect is dose-dependent: lower doses are immunostimulatory and bias toward Treg expansion, whereas higher doses cause broad immunosuppression ([Zerdka et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12766144/)). This biphasic profile underlies rapamycin's use to restore immune balance in aging.

> [!note] Biphasic dosing
> Low-dose mTOR inhibition favors Treg expansion and vaccine potentiation; high-dose inhibition suppresses immunity.
