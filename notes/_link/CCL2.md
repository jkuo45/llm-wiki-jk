# CCL2 (MCP-1)

CCL2 (C-C motif chemokine ligand 2), also known as MCP-1 (monocyte chemoattractant protein-1), is a CC chemokine that binds the receptor [[CCR2]] and is the principal recruiter of monocytes and macrophages to sites of inflammation. It is one of the most conserved and well-characterized components of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] (Coppé et al., 2008, PMID 18948995; 2010, PMID 20723158).

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

- **Receptor binding**: CCL2 signals chiefly through [[CCR2]] (a Gαi-coupled GPCR), with minor activity at CCR4; it activates PI3K/Akt, [[p38 MAPK]]/ERK, and Ca2+ flux to drive chemotaxis and adhesion of monocytes.
- **Cell sources**: Released by senescent fibroblasts, endothelial cells, macrophages, and epithelial cells under stress.
- **Transcriptional regulation**: Transcription is driven by [[NF-κB]], [[AP-1]], [[STAT1]]/STAT3, and the C/EBP family, and is potently induced by the SASP cytokines [[IL-1α]] and [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]] — a feed-forward loop because recruited macrophages further inflame tissue.
- **Downstream cellular effects**: Recruits classical monocytes, macrophages, memory T cells, and dendritic cells; promotes M2-like polarization and tissue remodeling.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

- CCL2 is a core SASP chemokine whose expression is tightly linked to the IL-1α/IL-1β arm of the secretome.
- It mediates immune recruitment to senescent cells that can be beneficial (macrophage-mediated clearance, immune surveillance) or detrimental (chronic monocyte infiltration, tissue damage).
- In senescent mesenchymal stromal cells, the Polycomb protein [[BMI1]] negatively regulates CCL2 through an IL-1α-mediated mechanism, tempering the SASP.
- Urine CCL2 is used as a non-invasive readout of SASP burden in patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, linking the chemokine to fibrotic aging.
- In cancer, CCL2 recruits CCR2+ myeloid-derived suppressor cells, enabling immune evasion.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- Elevated CCL2/CCR2 signaling is found in obesity, atherosclerosis, IPF, and multiple cancers, and correlates with senescence burden in human studies.
- CCR2 antagonists (e.g., CCX872, MK-0812) are being tested to limit SASP-driven inflammation and tumor immunosuppression.
- CCL2 is a candidate circulating/tissue biomarker of inflammaging and senolytic treatment response.
