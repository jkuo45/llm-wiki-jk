# CXCL2

CXCL2 (C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 2), also known as GROβ (growth-regulated oncogene beta) or MIP-2α (human homolog of mouse MIP-2), is an ELR+ C-X-C chemokine that signals through [[CXCR2]] (and weakly through CXCR1). Like [[CXCL1]] and [[CXCL3]], it is an archetypal neutrophil chemoattractant and a conserved member of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] (Coppé et al., 2008, PMID 18948995).

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

- **Receptor binding**: CXCL2 engages [[CXCR2]] (high affinity) and CXCR1 (low affinity) on the neutrophil surface. Ligand–receptor coupling activates Gαi, stimulating PLCβ/IP3/Ca2+, PI3K/Akt, and [[p38 MAPK]]/ERK pathways.
- **Cell sources**: Produced by monocytes/[[Macrophages]], endothelial cells, epithelial cells, and fibroblasts upon TLR, cytokine, or genotoxic stimulation.
- **Transcriptional regulation**: Driven by [[NF-κB]], [[AP-1]], the C/EBP family ([[CEBPβ|C/EBPβ]]), and [[p38 MAPK]]; these overlap with master SASP regulators, so CXCL2 is co-expressed with [[IL-8]] and [[IL-6]].
- **Downstream cellular effects**: Recruits and activates [[Neutrophils]], inducing chemotaxis, degranulation, and ROS release, thereby propagating inflammatory signaling to neighboring cells.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

- CXCL2 is constitutively upregulated in senescent fibroblasts and epithelial cells and forms part of the core ELR+ CXC SASP cluster with [[CXCL1]] and [[CXCL3]].
- In liver and pancreas, senescent activated stellate cells secrete CXCL2, sustaining the inflammatory milieu that both restrains and perpetuates fibrogenesis (Krizhanovsky et al., 2008, PMID 18729207).
- Engagement of [[CXCR2]] on bystander cells feeds forward to reinforce senescence, contributing to paracrine/bystander senescence amplification (Acosta et al., 2013, PMID 23746840).
- **Paracrine duality**: beneficial for acute neutrophil-mediated clearance of damaged cells, but chronically fuels [[Inflammaging]] and age-related tissue dysfunction.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- CXCL2/CXCR2 signaling is implicated in liver and pancreatic fibrosis, neutrophilic inflammation, and tumor growth across multiple cancers.
- CXCR2 antagonism is explored to dampen SASP-driven inflammation and senescence spread.
- CXCL2 is a candidate blood/tissue biomarker of senescent-cell burden.
