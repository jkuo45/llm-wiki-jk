# Pentose Phosphate Pathway

The [[Pentose Phosphate Pathway]] (HMP shunt) is a glucose-metabolism branch producing [[NADP+]]→[[NADPH]] (via glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase) and ribose-5-phosphate. The [[NADPH]] it generates regenerates reduced [[Glutathione]], the key erythrocyte antioxidant.

## Relevance to Methylene Blue

[[Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase]] is the rate-limiting enzyme of this pathway. Deficiency limits NADPH/glutathione, so [[Methylene blue]] (which needs NADPH to reduce methemoglobin) is contraindicated and risks [[Hemolysis]].
