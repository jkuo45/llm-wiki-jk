# GLUT-4

**GLUT-4** (encoded by *SLC2A4*) is the insulin-responsive facilitative [[Glucose]] transporter expressed predominantly in [[Muscle]] (skeletal and cardiac) and [[Adipose Tissue|adipose tissue]]. It is the major mediator of postprandial glucose disposal.

## Mechanism of Regulation

Unlike basal (cell-surface) glucose transporters, GLUT-4 is sequestered in intracellular storage vesicles under resting conditions. In response to:

- **[[Insulin]]** signaling (via IRS/PI3K/Akt), and
- **[[Exercise|Contractile activity]]** (via AMPK and calcium-dependent pathways),

GLUT-4 vesicles translocate to the plasma membrane, where they permit rapid [[Glucose Uptake|glucose uptake]] and [[Muscle Glycogen|glycogen]] synthesis.

## Interaction with Creatine

As described in [[Creatine in Health and Disease]], [[Creatine Supplementation|creatine supplementation]] prevents the decline in GLUT-4 during limb [[Immobilization|immobilization]] and increases GLUT-4 expression by ~40% during rehabilitation after [[Muscle Atrophy|atrophy]]. Co-ingestion of creatine with [[Carbohydrate]] (and [[Protein]]) further enhances creatine uptake and muscle [[Muscle Glycogen|glycogen]] storage. In [[Type 2 Diabetes|type 2 diabetes]], creatine supplementation improved [[Glucose Tolerance|glucose tolerance]] and increased GLUT-4 translocation, with [[AMPK Signaling|AMPK]] signaling implicated in the effect.

## Relevance to Disease

Reduced GLUT-4 translocation/expression underlies [[Insulin Resistance|insulin resistance]] and [[Type 2 Diabetes|type 2 diabetes]]. Strategies that preserve or enhance GLUT-4 (exercise, [[Creatine Supplementation]]) support [[Glucose Management|glucose management]] and [[Glycemic Control|glycemic control]].
