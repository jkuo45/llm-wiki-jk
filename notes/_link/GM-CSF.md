# GM-CSF

GM-CSF (Granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor) is a cytokine that is part of the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] and is measured as a biomarker for senolytic therapy efficacy in clinical studies.

## Mechanism of Action

- GM-CSF is secreted by senescent cells as a SASP factor
- It promotes the production and activation of granulocytes and macrophages
- GM-CSF is measured to evaluate senolytic therapy efficacy
- GM-CSF contributes to immune modulation in senescent tissues

## Role in SASP

- GM-CSF is a biomarker component of the SASP
- It is measured to assess senolytic therapy efficacy in diabetic kidney disease
- GM-CSF contributes to the immune-modulating functions of SASP

## Clinical Relevance

- GM-CSF is a biomarker for senescence burden
- GM-CSF levels are measured in clinical trials of senolytic therapy
- GM-CSF may be a therapeutic target for modulating SASP
