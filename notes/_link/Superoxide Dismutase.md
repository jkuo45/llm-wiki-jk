# Superoxide Dismutase

**Superoxide Dismutase** (SOD) is an enzyme that alternately catalyzes the dismutation of the [[Superoxide anion]] (O₂⁻) radical into ordinary molecular oxygen (O₂) and [[notes/_link/Hydrogen Peroxide]] (H₂O₂).


## Role in Antioxidant Defense
By destroying superoxide anions before they can oxidize [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]], SOD plays a crucial role in preventing the "side pathway" that leads to [[Adrenochrome]] formation.


## Definition
Superoxide dismutase (SOD) is an enzyme that alternately catalyzes the dismutation of the [[Superoxide Radicals]] (O2•−) into ordinary molecular oxygen (O2) and [[notes/_link/Hydrogen Peroxide]] (H2O2).


## Function
It serves as a key component of the antioxidant defense system, protecting cells from ROS-induced damage.
