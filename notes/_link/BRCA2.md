# BRCA2

BRCA2 (Breast Cancer Type 2 Susceptibility Protein) is a [[Tumor Suppressor]] protein critical for [[Homologous Recombination]] repair of [[Double-Strand Breaks]]. It interacts with [[RAD51]] to mediate the loading of RAD51 onto single-stranded [[DNA]] at sites of damage, enabling strand exchange during [[DNA Repair]]. Together with [[BRCA1]] and [[PALB2]], BRCA2 forms a complex essential for homologous recombination. Germline mutations in BRCA2 confer high lifetime risk for [[Breast Cancer]], [[Ovarian Cancer]], [[Pancreatic Cancer]], [[Prostate Cancer]], and [[Melanoma]]. BRCA2-deficient tumors are sensitive to [[PARP Inhibitors]] and [[Platinum-based drugs]] due to synthetic lethality.
