# CXCL11

CXCL11 (C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 11), also known as I-TAC (interferon-inducible T-cell alpha chemoattractant), is a chemokine that binds to [[CXCR3]] and is secreted by therapy-induced senescent (TIS) endothelial cells. It increases breast cancer aggressiveness.

## Mechanism of Action

- CXCL11 is secreted by TIS endothelial cells
- It binds to [[CXCR3]] on cancer cells
- CXCL11 increases breast cancer aggressiveness through promoting tumor progression
- CXCL11 is part of the SASP that can have pro-tumorigenic effects

## Role in SASP

- CXCL11 is a pro-tumorigenic SASP factor
- It contributes to cancer progression in the tumor microenvironment
- CXCL11 is part of the therapy-induced senescence response that can paradoxically promote cancer

## Clinical Relevance

- CXCL11 is a potential therapeutic target in cancer
- CXCL11/CXCR3 signaling may influence cancer immunotherapy outcomes
- CXCL11 is elevated in various inflammatory conditions
