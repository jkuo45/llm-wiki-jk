# Traumatic Brain Injury

[[Traumatic Brain Injury]] ([[TBI]]) is brain damage from mechanical force. [[Methylene blue]] is an investigational neuroprotective agent in TBI, attributed to [[Mitochondrial Electron Transport Chain]] bypass and reduced [[Mitochondrial Superoxide]].
