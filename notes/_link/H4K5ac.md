# H4K5ac

H4K5ac is the acetylation of histone H4 at lysine 5, an early-acetylated residue of the H4 N-terminal tail. It is associated with active chromatin and, in senescence, is one of the acetyl marks enriched at senescence-activated [[Super-enhancer|super-enhancers]] that drive [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]] expression, as reviewed in the npj Aging survey (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2).

## Definition

H4K5 is among the first lysines acetylated during histone deposition and is a hallmark of active promoters/enhancers. Acetylation neutralizes charge and recruits bromodomain readers, marking open, transcriptionally competent chromatin.

## Writer, Reader, Eraser

- **Writer:** HATs of the p300/CBP and MYST families (e.g., TIP60, HBO1) acetylate H4K5; it is often co-acetylated with H4K8/K12/K16.
- **Reader:** BET-family bromodomain proteins such as [[BRD4]] recognize H4 acetyl marks.
- **Eraser:** Class I/IIa HDACs and SIRT deacetylases remove H4K5ac.

## Role in SASP and Senescence

Senescence-activated super-enhancers are enriched in [[H3K4me1]] and multiple acetyl marks — [[H3K27ac]], [[H3K18ac]], [[H3K122ac]], and H4K5ac (Sen et al., 2019, PMID 30753809). These SEs actively transcribe enhancer RNAs, recruit [[P300]], and directly drive senescence-specific (including SASP) gene expression. H4K5ac therefore contributes to the open enhancer state that, via [[Enhancer-Promoter Looping|enhancer-promoter looping]] within [[Topologically Associating Domain|TADs]], activates SASP promoters under the control of the [[AP-1]] pioneer factor and BRD4 reader.

## Mechanistic Details

H4K5ac is part of the coordinate acetyl-mark enrichment defining SA-SEs. Its presence on the H4 tail helps disassemble repressive chromatin and presents an acetyl surface for bromodomain recruitment, reinforcing the feed-forward activation of SASP genes.

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

Because H4K5ac sits within the BET-readable acetyl code at SEs, bromodomain and p300 inhibitors are candidate [[Senomorphic|senomorphics]] (see [[KDM4]], [[DOT1L]], [[H3K27ac]]).
