## Overview

Flavonoids are a diverse class of [[Polyphenol|polyphenolic]] compounds widely distributed in plants, where they serve as pigments, UV protectants, and signaling molecules. Structurally characterized by a 15-carbon skeleton (C6–C3–C6) with two aromatic rings connected by a heterocyclic pyran ring. Subclasses include [[Flavonols]] ([[Quercetin]]), [[Flavones]], [[Flavanones]], [[Flavanols]] ([[Catechins]]), [[Anthocyanins]], and [[Isoflavones]]. Many flavonoids exhibit [[Antioxidant]], [[Anti-Inflammatory]], and [[Senolytic]] activities, making them of interest for [[Aging]] and [[Neurodegenerative Disease]] research.
