# UBN1

UBN1 (Ubinuclein 1) is a metazoan-specific protein and an essential, dedicated subunit of the HIRA histone chaperone complex. Together with [[HIRA]] and [[ASF1a]], UBN1 mediates replication-independent deposition of the [[H3.3]] variant and is required for formation of [[Senescence-Associated Heterochromatin Foci|SAHF]] in senescent cells, as described in the npj Aging review of the senescent epigenetic landscape (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2).

## Overview

UBN1 is the vertebrate ortholog of the yeast Hpc2p protein. It contains a conserved N-terminal domain that binds HIRA and a C-terminal region that contacts histones, conferring specificity to the HIRA complex for H3.3 deposition. UBN1 has a paralog, UBN2, but UBN1 is the isoform functionally linked to senescence chromatin.

## Structure and Function

UBN1 bridges HIRA and the H3–H4 dimer, stabilizing the chaperone–histone intermediate. Its inclusion in the HIRA–UBN1–CABIN1–ASF1a (HUCA) complex is what distinguishes H3.3-specific deposition from other chaperone pathways (e.g., CAF-1 for H3.1).

## Role in Senescence and SAHF

Banumathy et al. (2009, PMID 19103796) demonstrated that human UBN1 is an ortholog of yeast Hpc2p and has an essential role in the HIRA/ASF1a chromatin-remodeling pathway in senescent cells. Depletion of UBN1 phenocopies loss of HIRA/ASF1a: SAHF fail to assemble, and the characteristic heterochromatic reorganization of senescence is impaired. As with the rest of the complex, SAHF disruption correlates with reduced [[SASP]] expression.

## Mechanistic Details

UBN1 confers substrate selectivity, ensuring that the complex deposits H3.3 rather than canonical H3. Because senescent cells rely increasingly on replication-independent deposition (canonical histone biosynthesis falls), UBN1 becomes essential for maintaining the variant-rich, dynamic chromatin state of senescence. UBN1 therefore underpins both the repressive SAHF program and the long-lived [[H3.3]] landscape that accrues age-related damage.

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

As part of the HIRA/ASF1a/UBN1 axis, UBN1 is a candidate node for [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] strategies that tune SAHF/SASP while sparing arrest (see [[KDM4]], [[BRD4]], [[DOT1L]]).
