# HSPA8

**HSPA8** (heat shock cognate protein 70, commonly **HSC70**) is a constitutively expressed member of the [[HSP70|heat shock protein 70 family]]. It functions as a central chaperone — including a key role in [[Chaperone-Mediated Autophagy|chaperone-mediated autophagy]] (CMA), one of the main lysosome-autophagy proteolytic pathways. Deficient protein degradation via CMA compromises cellular proteostasis and activates signaling that culminates in [[Cellular Senescence|cellular senescence]][39].

## Structure & Family Context

HSPA8 is an ~70 kDa ATP-dependent chaperone within the HSP70 family ([[HSP70]]). Unlike the stress-inducible HSP70 (HSPA1A), HSC70/HSPA8 is expressed constitutively and participates in basal protein folding, trafficking, and CMA substrate delivery to the lysosome.

## Role in Senescence and the SASP

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> Proteome-wide interactor profiling identified **HSPA8** as a shared binding partner of both [[ATM]] and [[p38 MAPK]] — two kinases that transduce signals driving [[SASP|SASP]] development. In senescent cells, apigenin specifically disrupts the HSPA8 interaction with p-ATM and with p38 MAPK (but not the interaction of the related protein [[NPM1]]), blocking the cascade that converts the [[Acute Stress-Associated Phenotype|acute stress-associated phenotype (ASAP)]] into the chronic SASP. The selective HSPA8 inhibitor [[VER155008]] phenocopies apigenin, dampening SASP factors and suppressing p38 MAPK and PI3K/Akt/mTOR engagement.

HSPA8 also activates [[NF-κB]] signaling by destabilizing IκBβ in the absence of LPS (or facilitating its nuclear translocation in its presence); this NF-κB-activating activity is synergized by [[TXNDC5]] to exacerbate the inflammatory phenotype of synovial fibroblasts in rheumatoid arthritis[40]. HSPA8 is further required for [[VEGF]]-induced [[Akt]] phosphorylation — its downregulation abolishes Akt phosphorylation[41]. Together these data position HSPA8 as an integrative hub coordinating senescence-maintenance and SASP-promoting signals.

Critically, co-immunoprecipitation/MS demonstrated that **HSPA8 physically interacts with [[PRDX6]]** in senescent cells[67]. Because apigenin binds PRDX6 and inhibits its iPLA2 activity, PRDX6–HSPA8 functional interference is the proposed mechanistic basis of apigenin's senomorphic effect.

## Physiological Function

- **Chaperone-mediated autophagy** — delivers KFERQ-motif client proteins to LAMP-2A at the lysosome; CMA decline is linked to senescence and aging[39].
- **Cytoprotection** — as a constitutive HSC70, supports protein homeostasis under basal and stressed conditions.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Aging / senescence** — HSPA8 is a bridge node linking DDR kinases (ATM) and inflammatory kinases (p38 MAPK); disrupting it suppresses the SASP without killing senescent cells, a senomorphic strategy.
- **Inflammation** — HSPA8–TXNDC5 synergy drives synovial fibroblast inflammation in rheumatoid arthritis *via* NF-κB[40].
- **Angiogenesis / endothelial function** — HSPA8 is essential for VEGF-Akt signaling in endothelial cells[41].
- **Therapeutic targeting** — [[VER155008]] is a tool compound inhibiting HSPA8; [[Apigenin]] achieves SASP suppression indirectly by blocking the PRDX6→HSPA8 axis.
