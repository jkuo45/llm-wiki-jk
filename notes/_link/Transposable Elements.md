# Transposable Elements
**Transposable elements** (TEs), also known as "jumping genes," are DNA sequences that can change their position within a genome.
## Types
- Retrotransposons (e.g., [[LINE-1]], [[Alu element]])
- DNA Transposons
## Role in Aging
- TEs are typically silenced by heterochromatin; loss of silencing during aging leads to their activation and genomic instability.
