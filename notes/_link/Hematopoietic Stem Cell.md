# Hematopoietic Stem Cell

**Hematopoietic Stem Cells** (HSCs) are multipotent precursors with the capacity to both self-renew and differentiate into functional immune cells, red blood cells, and platelets.

## Aging of HSCs
- HSC functions significantly decline with age, contributing to [[Immune Aging]].
- Senescence in HSCs contributes to age-associated pathologies.

## Autophagy and Rejuvenation
- The [[Autophagy]] protein [[Atg7]] is essential for HSC maintenance.
- [[Rapamycin]] treatment has been shown to restore self-renewal and expansion of aged HSCs.
