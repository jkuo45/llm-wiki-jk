# ZFP36L1

ZFP36L1 (Zinc finger protein 36 C3H1-like 1) is an mRNA decay activator protein that is phosphorylated and inhibited by [[MAPKAPK2|MK2]]. This inhibition stabilizes transcripts of many SASP factors, including [[IL-1α]], by preventing their degradation.

## Mechanism of Action

- ZFP36L1 is an mRNA decay activator
- It is phosphorylated and inhibited by [[MAPKAPK2|MK2]]
- This inhibition stabilizes SASP factor transcripts
- ZFP36L1 targets AU-rich element-containing mRNAs

## Role in SASP

- ZFP36L1 is a negative regulator of SASP
- When inhibited by MK2, SASP transcripts are stabilized
- ZFP36L1 links p38 MAPK signaling to SASP mRNA stability

## Clinical Relevance

- ZFP36L1 is implicated in cancer and inflammation
- ZFP36L1 may be a target for modulating SASP
- ZFP36L1 is involved in mRNA regulation
