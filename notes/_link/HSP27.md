# HSP27

**Heat shock protein 27 (HSP27)**, also known as HSPB1, is a small heat shock protein that functions as a molecular chaperone.

## Role in SASP
HSP27 is part of the [[MAPKAPK2|MK2]]-HSP27 axis that regulates the stability of [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] transcripts:
- It is phosphorylated by [[MAPKAPK2|MK2]], which is downstream of [[p38 MAPK]].
- Phosphorylated HSP27 contributes to the stabilization of mRNAs encoding pro-inflammatory SASP factors.
- This mechanism helps maintain the high levels of cytokine and chemokine production in [[Senescent Cells]].

## Function
Beyond its role in SASP, HSP27 is involved in stress resistance, actin cytoskeleton remodeling, and inhibition of [[Apoptosis]].
