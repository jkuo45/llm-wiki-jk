# Cyclic Guanosine Monophosphate

[[Cyclic Guanosine Monophosphate]] ([[cGMP]]) is a cyclic nucleotide second messenger produced by [[Guanylyl Cyclase|guanylyl cyclases]] (notably [[Soluble Guanylyl Cyclase]] activated by [[Nitric Oxide]]). It mediates smooth-muscle relaxation, [[Vasodilation]], and [[Platelet]] inhibition.

## NO–cGMP Vasodilation Cascade

Inflammatory stimulus → [[Inducible Nitric Oxide Synthase]] → NO → [[Soluble Guanylyl Cyclase]] → cGMP → vasodilation/hypotension. [[Methylene blue]] blocks this *downstream* at sGC; [[Aminoguanidine]] blocks it *upstream* at iNOS.

> [!info] Therapeutic interruption
> In [[Vasoplegic Shock]], interrupting the NO→cGMP axis (methylene blue at sGC) restores vascular tone.
