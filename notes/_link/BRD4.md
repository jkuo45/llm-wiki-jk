# BRD4

BRD4 (Bromodomain-containing protein 4) is an epigenetic reader protein that binds acetylated lysine residues on histones via its two tandem bromodomains and is recruited to senescence-activated enhancers in oncogene-induced senescence (OIS). It drives expression of [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]] factors without perturbing the cell-cycle arrest that defines the senescent state.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

BRD4 is a member of the BET (bromodomain and extraterminal) family. Each bromodomain recognizes ε-N-acetylated lysine on histones H3/H4 (notably H3K27ac and H3K14ac), tethering BRD4 to active enhancers and promoters. Once engaged, BRD4 recruits the positive transcription elongation factor b (P-TEFb; [[CDK9]]–Cyclin T) to phosphorylate the RNA Polymerase II C-terminal domain and the negative elongation factor NELF, releasing paused polymerase into productive elongation. In OIS, BRD4 is selectively loaded onto enhancers of SASP genes, where it cooperates with [[NF-κB]] and [[p38 MAPK]] signaling to convert chromatin marks into sustained secretory transcription. Importantly, this enhancer engagement is separable from the pRB/p53 axis enforcing growth arrest — a property that explains why BET inhibitors blunt the SASP while largely preserving senescence.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

Landmark work (Tasdemir et al., 2016, PMID 27693224) showed that BET bromodomain inhibitors such as JQ1 and the clinical candidate apabetalone (RVX-208) suppress the OIS-associated SASP (IL-6, IL-8, MCP-1) without rescinding the DNA-damage-induced arrest. This dissection established BRD4/BET as a selective "SASP-modulating" (senomorphic) rather than senolytic target. BRD4 also amplifies pro-inflammatory feedback: SASP cytokines activate NF-κB, which further acetylates histones at SASP loci, deepening BRD4 occupancy in a feed-forward loop. In tumor contexts, BET inhibition additionally suppresses MYC and BCL2 programs, rationalizing combinations with senescence-inducing chemotherapy.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- BRD4/BET inhibitors (JQ1, apabetalone, PLX2853, molibresib) are in oncology and cardiovascular trials; senomorphic repurposing targets age-related [[Inflammaging]] and chemotherapy-induced senescent phenotypes.
- Selective SASP suppression may blunt the tumor-promoting and fibrogenic paracrine effects of therapy-induced senescence.
- Risk: broad BET blockade can perturb hematopoiesis and cytokine networks, requiring tissue-selective delivery.
