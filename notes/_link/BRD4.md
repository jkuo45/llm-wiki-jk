# BRD4

BRD4 (Bromodomain-containing protein 4) is an epigenetic reader protein that binds to acetylated histones and is recruited to senescence-activated enhancers in oncogene-induced senescence (OIS). It contributes to the expression of SASP factors without interfering with growth arrest.

## Mechanism of Action

- BRD4 binds to acetylated histones at enhancer regions
- It is recruited to senescence-activated enhancers in OIS
- BRD4 contributes to SASP gene expression without affecting growth arrest
- BRD4 is a key regulator of transcriptional elongation

## Role in SASP

- BRD4 is an epigenetic regulator of SASP expression
- It promotes SASP factor expression through enhancer activation
- BRD4 may be a target for selective SASP modulation

## Clinical Relevance

- BRD4 is a therapeutic target in cancer (BET inhibitors)
- BRD4 inhibitors (e.g., JQ1) are being developed for cancer and inflammatory diseases
- BRD4 may be a target for modulating SASP in aging
