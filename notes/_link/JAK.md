# JAK

**Janus kinase (JAK)** is a family of intracellular tyrosine kinases (JAK1, JAK2, JAK3, and TYK2) that transduce cytokine signals from cell surface receptors to the nucleus.

## Role in SASP
JAK, particularly JAK2, is activated by SASP cytokines like [[IL-6]] in an autocrine and paracrine manner. It activates the [[STAT3]] transcription factor, which:
- Amplifies and sustains SASP gene expression.
- Maintains the pro-inflammatory state of [[Senescent Cells]].

## Therapeutic Targeting
JAK inhibitors (e.g., ruxolitinib) are being explored as [[Senomorphic Therapy]] to suppress the detrimental effects of chronic SASP without killing the cells.
