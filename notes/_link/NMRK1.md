# NMRK1
NMRK1 (Nicotinamide riboside kinase 1), also known as NRK1, is an enzyme that directly converts Nicotinamide riboside (NR) to Nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN), bypassing the need for NAMPT in NAD+ salvage biosynthesis.
