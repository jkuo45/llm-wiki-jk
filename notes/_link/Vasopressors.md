# Vasopressors

[[Vasopressors]] are drugs that elevate blood pressure via [[Vasoconstriction]] or increased contractility, used to treat shock. In refractory [[Vasoplegic Shock]], standard vasopressors such as [[Norepinephrine]] may fail, prompting rescue use of [[Methylene blue]].

- [[Vasoplegic Shock]] — indication
