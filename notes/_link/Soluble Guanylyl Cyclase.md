# Soluble Guanylyl Cyclase

[[Soluble Guanylyl Cyclase]] ([[sGC]]) is the intracellular receptor for [[Nitric Oxide]]. NO binding activates sGC to convert GTP to [[Cyclic Guanosine Monophosphate]] (cGMP), which mediates smooth-muscle relaxation and [[Vasodilation]].

## Methylene Blue Inhibition (Downstream)

[[Methylene blue]] inhibits sGC directly, blocking cGMP generation even when NO is already elevated. This interrupts the NO→cGMP vasodilation cascade and underlies methylene blue's vasoconstrictive rescue effect in [[Vasoplegic Shock]]. This is the *downstream* counterpart to [[Aminoguanidine]]'s *upstream* ([[Inducible Nitric Oxide Synthase|iNOS]]) inhibition of the same pathway.

> [!info] NO–cGMP cascade
> Inflammatory stimulus → iNOS → NO → sGC → cGMP → vasodilation/hypotension. Methylene blue blocks at sGC; aminoguanidine blocks at iNOS.
