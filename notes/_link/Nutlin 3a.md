# Nutlin 3a

Nutlin 3a is a small molecule inhibitor of MDM2 that stabilizes [[p53]] and suppresses pro-inflammatory [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]]. It is a senomorphic therapy that modulates SASP without killing senescent cells.

## Mechanism of Action

- Nutlin 3a inhibits MDM2, which normally degrades [[p53]]
- This stabilizes p53 and increases its activity
- p53 activation attenuates the secretory phenotype
- Nutlin 3a suppresses pro-inflammatory SASP factors

## Role in SASP

- Nutlin 3a is a senomorphic therapy
- It suppresses pro-inflammatory SASP through p53 stabilization
- Nutlin 3a may be beneficial in reducing age-related inflammation

## Clinical Relevance

- Nutlin 3a and derivatives are in clinical trials for cancer
- Nutlin 3a may have senomorphic effects in aging
- Nutlin 3a is a tool compound for studying p53 biology
