# Frailty

**Frailty** is a geriatric syndrome of reduced physiological reserve and increased vulnerability to stressors, characterized by weakness, slowness, low activity, exhaustion, and unintended weight loss. It is strongly associated with chronic low-grade [[Inflammation|inflammation]] ([[Inflammaging|inflammaging]]) and adverse outcomes including falls, disability, and mortality.

> [!note] Inflammaging link
> The [[Shingles Vaccine|shingles vaccine]] study frames frailty as one of the age-related conditions tied to persistent inflammaging; interventions that lower background inflammation may mitigate frailty progression.
