# Interleukin 33 (IL-33)

Interleukin 33 (IL-33) is an [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]]-dependent SASP interleukin, the secretion of which from senescent hepatic stellate cells promotes [[Hepatocellular Carcinoma]] development.

## Mechanism of Action

- IL-33 is an [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]]-dependent SASP interleukin
- It is secreted by senescent hepatic stellate cells
- IL-33 promotes [[Hepatocellular Carcinoma]] development
- IL-33 is an alarmin released upon cell damage

## Role in SASP

- IL-33 is a pro-tumorigenic SASP factor
- It contributes to liver cancer development through senescent stellate cells
- IL-33 links cellular senescence to cancer in the liver

## Clinical Relevance

- IL-33 is a therapeutic target in asthma and allergic diseases
- IL-33 may be a target for liver cancer prevention
- IL-33 is elevated in various inflammatory conditions
