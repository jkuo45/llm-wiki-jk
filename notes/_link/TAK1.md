# TAK1

TAK1 (TGFβ-activated kinase 1), also known as MAP3K7, is a mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase kinase (MAP3K) that activates [[p38 MAPK]] and [[mTORC1|mTOR]] signaling to promote the [[Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype|SASP]].

## Mechanism of Action

- TAK1 is activated by [[ATM]] in senescent cells
- It forms a complex with ZSCAN4 to promote SASP
- TAK1 activates [[p38 MAPK]] and [[mTORC1|mTOR]] signaling
- TAK1 is a key kinase linking DDR to SASP

## Role in SASP

- TAK1 is a kinase that promotes SASP
- It activates p38 MAPK and mTOR pathways
- TAK1 links DNA damage response to SASP

## Clinical Relevance

- TAK1 is a therapeutic target in inflammation and cancer
- TAK1 inhibitors are being developed
- TAK1 may be a target for modulating SASP
