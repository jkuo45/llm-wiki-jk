# Toll-like Receptor 2 (TLR2)

Toll-like Receptor 2 (TLR2) is an innate immunity sensor that is upregulated in senescent cells and is essential for the activation of an immunity-regulating SASP during [[Oncogene-Induced Senescence|OIS]] through the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS–STING]] pathway.

## Mechanism of Action

- TLR2 is an innate immunity sensor
- It is upregulated in senescent cells
- TLR2 is essential for activating an immunity-regulating SASP
- TLR2 signals through the cGAS–STING pathway

## Role in SASP

- TLR2 is a receptor that activates SASP
- It links innate immunity to SASP regulation
- TLR2 is essential for immunity-regulating SASP during OIS

## Clinical Relevance

- TLR2 is a therapeutic target in infection and inflammation
- TLR2 agonists are being developed as vaccine adjuvants
- TLR2 may be a target for modulating SASP
