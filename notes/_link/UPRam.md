# UPRam

**UPRam** (unfolded protein response activated by mistargeting of proteins) is a stress pathway triggered when [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] precursor proteins are misdirected to other [[Organelle|organelles]] or fail to be imported, accumulating in the [[Cytosol]].

## Mechanism

- When [[Mitochondrial protein import]] is impaired, mitochondrial-destined precursors may be mistargeted to the [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]] or other organelles
- Mistargeted proteins cause [[Proteotoxicity]] in the mislocalized compartment
- The cell responds by upregulating [[Chaperone|chaperones]] and [[Proteasome|proteasomal]] components to handle the mislocalized proteins
- [[HSF1]] and [[NRF1]] appear to coordinate this response
- In yeast, [[RPN4]] is a key downstream effector
