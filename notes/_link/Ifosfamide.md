# Ifosfamide

[[Ifosfamide]] is an [[Chemotherapy|alkylating antineoplastic]] agent. A well-known toxicity is ifosfamide-induced encephalopathy, which is effectively reversed by [[Methylene blue]] (IV 50 mg every 4 h until resolution).
