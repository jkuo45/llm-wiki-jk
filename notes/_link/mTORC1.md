# mTORC1
Mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1 (mTORC1) is a nutrient-sensitive protein kinase complex that serves as a master regulator of [[cell growth]] and metabolism by promoting [[protein synthesis]], [[Lipid Synthesis]], and [[Ribosome Biogenesis]], while inhibiting catabolic processes such as [[Autophagy|autophagy]].
