## Overview

Denham Harman (1916–2014) was an American biochemist who proposed the [[Free Radical Theory of Aging|free radical theory of aging]] in 1955, postulating that [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] (ROS) produced during [[Aerobic Respiration]] cause cumulative oxidative damage that drives aging. He later refined this into the [[Mitochondrial Free Radical Theory of Aging]] (MFRTA) in 1972, specifically implicating [[Mitochondria]] as the primary source and target of ROS.
