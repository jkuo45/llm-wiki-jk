## Overview

B-type natriuretic peptide (BNP) is a 32-amino-acid peptide hormone secreted predominantly by the ventricular myocardium in response to increased wall stretch and pressure overload. It is synthesized as the prohormone proBNP, cleaved into active BNP and the inactive N-terminal fragment NT-proBNP.

## Biological Role

- **Natriuresis & diuresis:** Promotes sodium and water excretion, reducing intravascular volume.
- **Vasodilation:** Counter-regulates the renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system and sympathetic tone.
- **Cardioprotection:** Opposes fibrotic and hypertrophic remodeling.

## Physiological & Pathological Relevance

Plasma BNP/NT-proBNP is a cornerstone biomarker for the diagnosis and prognosis of heart failure and is elevated in myocardial stress, ischemia, and volume overload. It is conceptually related to [[Atrial natriuretic peptide]] (ANP), with which it shares structure and function. Its measurement informs cardiovascular risk in aging and metabolic disease.
