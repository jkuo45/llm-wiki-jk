## Overview

[[Iba1]] (ionized calcium-binding adaptor molecule 1, gene AIF1) is a 17 kDa EF-hand calcium-binding protein expressed in mononuclear phagocytes. It is the standard immunohistochemical marker for [[Microglia]] in the central nervous system and for tissue [[Macrophages]].

## Biological Role / Mechanism

Iba1 binds [[Actin]] and [[Gelsolin]], promoting actin cross-linking and remodeling essential for phagocytosis, chemotaxis, and process motility of microglia. Its expression increases with microglial activation; morphological changes (ramified → amoeboid) and upregulated Iba1 signal reflect shifts from surveilling to reactive states. Iba1 is frequently paired with [[CD68]] and [[CD206]] to phenotype myeloid activation.

## Physiological / Pathological Relevance

Iba1 immunostaining reveals microgliosis in neurodegeneration, neuroinflammation, and aging. Reactive microglia contribute to neuronal injury but also to debris clearance. In the wiki, Iba1 is used to quantify neuroinflammatory microglial burden when assessing adrenochrome/sirtuin effects on [[Neuron]]al environments and [[Inflammation]].
