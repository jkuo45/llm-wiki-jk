# Oxidative Stress

**Oxidative stress** is an imbalance between the production of reactive oxygen species (ROS) and the cell's ability to detoxify them with antioxidants. In the context of [[Atherosclerosis]], oxidative stress drives [[LDL]] oxidation, impairs [[HDL]] function, and reduces [[Cholesterol Efflux]] from [[Macrophages]].

## Role in Atherosclerosis

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - berrougui2009_resveratrol_as_atheroprotective_compound|Berrougui et al., Atherosclerosis 2009]]
> Oxidative stress is a continuous process in all physiological systems. In vivo oxidative stress impairs the anti-atherogenic properties of HDL — especially its antioxidant activity and capacity to enhance cholesterol efflux and promote reverse cholesterol transport. Fe/ascorbate-induced oxidative stress in macrophages reduces ABCA1, LXR, and PPAR expression but has no effect on SR-BI.

### Effects on Lipoproteins

- **LDL oxidation**: Copper ions or free radicals oxidize LDL polyunsaturated fatty acids, producing [[Conjugated Dienes]] and altering [[Apolipoprotein B|apoB]]
- **HDL oxidation**: Cu-induced oxidation of HDL3 alters phospholipid fluidity, modifies apoprotein structure, and decreases [[PON1]] activity
- **Impaired cholesterol efflux**: Oxidized HDL loses its capacity to mediate cholesterol efflux

### Effects on Macrophages

- Fe/ascorbate stress impairs ABCA1 expression and cholesterol efflux
- Oxidative stress alters the expression of receptors involved in cholesterol flux
- Resveratrol, [[Vitamin E]], and BHT can restore cholesterol efflux under oxidative stress

## Sources in Atherosclerosis

- Transition metal ions (copper, iron)
- Free radicals from γ-radiolysis
- Endothelial cell-induced oxidation
- Fe/ascorbate complexes

## Antioxidant Defense

- **[[Resveratrol]]**: Protects LDL and HDL from oxidation; restores cholesterol efflux under oxidative stress
- **[[Vitamin E]]**: Endogenous antioxidant in lipoproteins; preserved by resveratrol
- **[[PON1]]**: HDL-associated enzyme with antioxidant properties
- **Glutathione system**: GPx and GR activities; preserved by resveratrol
