# ISG15

ISG15 (Interferon-stimulated gene 15) is an interferon pathway factor that is induced by [[p16 INK4a|p16]] overexpression in senescence. It is part of a unique p53-dependent SASP profile that includes interferon pathway factors.

## Mechanism of Action

- ISG15 is an interferon-induced protein
- It is induced by [[p16 INK4a|p16]] overexpression in senescence
- ISG15 is part of the p53-dependent SASP profile
- ISG15 is involved in antiviral immunity

## Role in SASP

- ISG15 is an interferon pathway SASP factor
- It is induced in p16-driven senescence
- ISG15 contributes to the interferon response in senescence

## Clinical Relevance

- ISG15 is a biomarker for interferon activity
- ISG15 is implicated in viral infections and autoimmunity
- ISG15 may be a target for modulating SASP
