# Caspases

**Caspases** are a family of cysteine proteases that play essential roles in [[notes/_link/Apoptosis|programmed cell death (apoptosis)]], [[notes/_link/Inflammation]], and [[Pyroptosis]]. They are synthesized as inactive zymogens (procaspases) and activated by proteolytic cleavage.

## Connections

- [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]] — Executioner caspases (3, 6, 7) drive apoptotic cell death
- [[notes/_link/Inflammation]] — Caspase-1 activates IL-1β and IL-18
- [[Oxidative Stress]] — Can activate caspase-dependent apoptosis
