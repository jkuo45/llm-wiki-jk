# Embryoid Bodies

**Embryoid bodies (EBs)** are three-dimensional aggregates formed when [[Embryonic Stem Cells|embryonic stem cells]] are cultured in suspension without [[Leukemia inhibitory factor|LIF]]. They spontaneously differentiate into heterogeneous cell types representing all three germ layers, serving as an in vitro model of early embryogenesis.

> [!info] In the DGCR8 study
> [[DGCR8]]-deficient EBs failed to form cysts and showed abnormal/absent expression of lineage markers (endoderm, mesoderm, ectoderm), rescued by DGCR8 re-expression — confirming miRNAs are required for normal EB differentiation.
