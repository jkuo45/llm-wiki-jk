# PLGA

**Poly(lactic-co-glycolic acid) (PLGA)** is a biodegradable copolymer of [[PLA|lactic acid]] and glycolic acid, widely used in controlled-release [[Nanoparticles|nanoparticle]] formulations. Fisetin-loaded [[PLGA]]-PEG-COOH nanoparticles (140–200 nm; 70–82% encapsulation) provided pH-responsive release — protecting fisetin in acidic stomach conditions (<15% release) and gradually increasing release in basic intestinal pH. Fisetin-HP-β-CD complexes incorporated into PLGA NPs showed 3.9× higher cytotoxicity against MCF-7 breast cancer cells vs. free fisetin.
