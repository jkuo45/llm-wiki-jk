# CCL18 (PARC)

CCL18 (C-C motif chemokine ligand 18), also known as PARC (pulmonary and activation-regulated chemokine), is a chemokine that is strongly associated with mortality and is part of a panel of SASP factors used as biomarkers for senescence.

## Mechanism of Action

- CCL18 is secreted by senescent cells as a SASP factor
- It is one of five SASP factors (along with [[GDF15]], RAGE, [[VEGF]]A, and [[MMP-2]]) strongly associated with mortality
- CCL18 may contribute to immune modulation in senescent tissues

## Role in SASP

- CCL18 is a biomarker component of the SASP atlas
- It is part of a panel of SASP proteins predictive of mortality
- CCL18 may contribute to age-related immune dysfunction

## Clinical Relevance

- CCL18 is a potential biomarker for senescence burden
- CCL18 levels correlate with mortality in human studies
- CCL18 may be a therapeutic target for age-related conditions
