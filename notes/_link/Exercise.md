# Exercise
**Exercise** is planned, structured physical activity that improves or maintains physical fitness. It is one of the most effective interventions for extending healthspan, reducing [[Oxidative Stress|oxidative stress]], enhancing [[Autophagy|autophagy]], and improving [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] function.
## Health Benefits
- Induces [[Mitohormesis]] — hormetic ROS signaling that upregulates [[NRF2]] and [[Superoxide Dismutase]]
- Stimulates [[Mitophagy]] and mitochondrial biogenesis via [[PGC1-α]]
- Improves [[Insulin Sensitivity]] and [[Cardiovascular Disease|cardiovascular]] health
- Promotes [[Neurogenesis]] via BDNF upregulation
## Sirtuin Connections
- [[Exercise]] increases [[NAD+]] levels and activates [[SIRT1]], [[SIRT3]], and [[SIRT6]] in skeletal muscle, heart, and [[Brain|brain]].
- [[SIRT1]] and [[PGC1-α]] mediate exercise-induced [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis]] and [[Insulin Sensitivity]] improvement.
- [[SIRT3]] is upregulated by [[Exercise]] in muscle and is required for the full metabolic benefits of training, including enhanced [[Oxidative Phosphorylation|OXPHOS]] and reduced [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]].
- [[SIRT6]] supports exercise adaptations through [[DNA Repair|DNA repair]] and metabolic gene regulation.
- [[BDNF]] induction by [[Exercise]] is partially SIRT1-dependent, linking physical activity to [[Neurogenesis|neurogenesis]] and cognitive health.
