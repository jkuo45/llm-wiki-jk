# Zone 2 Cardio

**Zone 2 cardio** is low-to-moderate intensity aerobic training performed at a conversational pace, roughly 60–70% of maximum heart rate, where the body predominantly oxidizes fat. Done 3–4 times per week for 45–60 min, it builds the aerobic base, increases mitochondrial volume, and improves fat-oxidation efficiency.

## Role in the Mitohormesis Protocol

Zone 2 is the recovery-day complement to [[HIIT]]. When [[Heart Rate Variability|HRV]] is below baseline, Zone 2 (or walking) is the preferred activity because it sustains metabolic stimulus without the sympathetic load of high-intensity work. On programmed recovery days, keeping carbohydrates low while doing Zone 2 encourages fat oxidation — a mild, healthy stressor that promotes [[Autophagy|autophagy]] and metabolic flexibility.
