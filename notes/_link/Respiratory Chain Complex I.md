# Respiratory Chain Complex I

**Complex I** (NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase) is the largest enzyme of the [[notes/_link/Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] electron transport chain. It catalyzes the oxidation of [[NADH]] and reduction of ubiquinone, pumping protons across the inner mitochondrial membrane. It is a major source of [[Superoxide Radicals|superoxide]] production.
