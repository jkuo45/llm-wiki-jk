# Ventral Tegmental Area
The **Ventral Tegmental Area** (VTA) is a group of neurons located close to the midline on the floor of the midbrain. It is a key component of the brain's reward and motivation system.
## Dopamine Production
The VTA is one of the two major dopamine-producing areas in the brain. It contains a large population of dopaminergic neurons that project to various brain regions:
- **Mesocortical Pathway:** Projects to the [[Prefrontal Cortex]].
- **Mesolimbic Pathway:** Projects to the [[Nucleus Accumbens]].
## Neuromelanin and Disease Resistance
In contrast to the [[Substantia Nigra Pars Compacta]], dopaminergic neurons in the VTA produce minimal [[Neuromelanin]] over a lifetime.
- **Relative Sparing in Parkinson's:** VTA neurons are largely spared in [[Parkinson's Disease]] compared to the severe loss seen in the SNpc. This supports the hypothesis that neuromelanin accumulation contributes to neuronal vulnerability.
## COMT Expression
Increased [[COMT]] activity in the dopamine neurons of the VTA can influence dopamine release capacity in target regions like the [[Sirtuins]].
