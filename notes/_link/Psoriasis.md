# Psoriasis

**Psoriasis** is a common, chronic, immune-mediated inflammatory disease that primarily affects the skin (and, in ~30% of patients, the joints as psoriatic arthritis).

## Etiology & Pathophysiology

- **Immune dysregulation:** the disease is driven by the **IL-23/Th17 axis**. Activated [[Dendritic cells]] secrete IL-23, expanding [[TH17]] cells that release IL-17 and [[IL-6]]; together with [[TNF-alpha]] this drives keratinocyte hyperproliferation.
- **Keratinocyte turnover:** epidermal transit time is dramatically shortened, producing thickened epidermis (acanthosis) with retained nuclei (parakeratosis).
- **Genetic risk:** strong association with HLA-C*06:02 and polymorphisms in IL-23R and NF-κB pathway genes; triggers include infection, stress, and certain drugs.

## Clinical Presentation & Therapeutic Landscape

> [!info] Presentation and treatment
> The classic form (plaque psoriasis) presents as well-demarcated erythematous plaques with silvery scale over extensor surfaces and scalp. Treatment is tiered: topical corticosteroids/vitamin D analogues, phototherapy, systemic agents (methotrexate, ciclosporin), and highly effective **biologics** targeting [[TNF-alpha]], IL-17, and IL-23. Chronic systemic [[Inflammation]] links psoriasis to increased cardiometabolic risk, and sirtuin/[[NAD+]] biology has been explored for its anti-inflammatory potential in the skin.
