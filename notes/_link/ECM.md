# ECM
The Extracellular Matrix (ECM) is a complex network of extracellular macromolecules, such as collagen, enzymes, and glycoproteins like [[Fibronectin]] and [[Vitronectin]], that provide structural and biochemical support to surrounding cells.
---
### Merged from notes/autophagy/ECM.md
The **ECM** (Extracellular Matrix) is a complex network of proteins and carbohydrates that provides structural and biochemical support to surrounding cells, and its remodeling is regulated by [[Lysosome]] activity during processes like [[C. elegans]] molting.
