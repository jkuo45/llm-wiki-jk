# Autism Spectrum Disorder

Autism spectrum disorder (ASD) is a [[Neurodevelopmental disorder]] characterized by deficits in [[Social Communication]] and the presence of [[Restricted Interests]] and [[Repetitive Behaviors]].

### Related
- [[Pervasive Developmental Disorder]]: A previous category that included ASD and related conditions.
- [[Autistic Disorder]]: One of the former sub-diagnoses now consolidated into ASD.
- [[Asperger’s Disorder]]: Another former sub-diagnosis now part of the ASD spectrum.
- [[Childhood Disintegrative Disorder]]: A rare former sub-diagnosis now under the ASD umbrella.
- [[Pervasive Developmental Disorder Not Otherwise Specified]]: A former diagnostic category now consolidated into ASD.
- [[Rett Syndrome]]: A genetic disorder often considered in differential diagnosis.
- [[Social (Pragmatic) Communication Disorder]]: A diagnosis for social communication deficits without repetitive behaviors.
- [[Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders]]: The authoritative guide for ASD diagnosis.
