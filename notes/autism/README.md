# Autism

This directory contains notes and research regarding [[Autism Spectrum Disorder]] ([[ASD]]), its neurodevelopmental characteristics, genetic and environmental contributors, early identification and screening methods, early intervention strategies, and the full spectrum of presentations across different ages and severity levels. Special focus is placed on early signs in infancy and toddlerhood, reliable screening tools, diagnostic processes, and the importance of timely identification for improved developmental outcomes.
