# Neffy

**Neffy** is a brand of needle-free nasal spray delivery system for [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]].

## Stability Profile
Neffy powder formulations have been shown to possess superior stability under extreme temperatures compared to traditional injectable [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]] solutions, showing less degradation into products like [[Adrenochrome]].
