# Integrated Stress Response

A common adaptive pathway in eukaryotic cells (ISR) triggered by various stressors (ER stress, amino acid starvation, heme deficiency, viral infection). It centers on the phosphorylation of [[EIF2A]], which inhibits global protein synthesis while selectively increasing the translation of specific mRNAs like [[ATF4]].
