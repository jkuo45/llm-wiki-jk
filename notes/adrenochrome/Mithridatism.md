# Mithridatism

Mithridatism is the practice of protecting oneself against a poison by gradually self-administering non-lethal amounts. 

## Historical Context

The term is named after **Mithridates VI**, the King of Pontus, who was said to have developed an immunity to poisons by regularly ingesting small doses of them and a complex potion known as [[Antidotum Mithridaticum]].

## Relation to Hormesis

Mithridatism is a historical precursor to the scientific concept of [[Hormesis]] and [[Mitohormesis]], illustrating the principle that low-dose exposure to a stressor can induce an adaptive, protective response.
