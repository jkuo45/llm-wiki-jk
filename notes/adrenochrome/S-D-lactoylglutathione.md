# S-D-lactoylglutathione

S-D-lactoylglutathione is an intermediate metabolite within the [[Glyoxalase System]] pathway.

## Production and Hydrolysis

It is synthesized by [[GLO1]] from the hemithioacetal adduct formed by the spontaneous reaction of [[Methylglyoxal]] and [[Glutathione]]. It is subsequently hydrolyzed by [[GLO2]] to produce [[D-lactate]] and regenerate [[Glutathione]].
