# Wound Healing

Wound healing is the process by which the skin, or other body tissue, repairs itself after injury. In patients with [[Diabetes Mellitus]], high levels of [[Advanced Glycation End Products|AGEs]] can impair wound healing by promoting [[Inflammation]], reducing [[Nitric Oxide]] activity, and causing vascular and neurological modifications.
