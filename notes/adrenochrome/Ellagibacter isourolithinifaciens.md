# Ellagibacter isourolithinifaciens

**Ellagibacter isourolithinifaciens** is a human intestinal bacterium belonging to the Eggerthellaceae family that is capable of metabolizing [[Ellagic acid|ellagic acid]]. It is specifically characterized by its capacity to produce [[Isourolithin A|Isourolithin A]], an isomer of [[notes/_link/Urolithin A|Urolithin A]], from dietary ellagic acid.
