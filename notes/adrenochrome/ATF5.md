# ATF5

ATF5 (Activating Transcription Factor 5) is a mammalian transcription factor that is functionally analogous to the *C. elegans* [[ATFS-1]] in mediating the [[Mitochondrial Unfolded Protein Response]] (UPRmt).

## Mechanism and Function

Similar to [[ATFS-1]], ATF5 has been shown to partition between the nucleus and the mitochondria depending on the health of the organelle. Under mitochondrial stress, ATF5 localizes to the nucleus, where it induces the expression of mitochondrial chaperones and proteases to restore mitochondrial proteostasis.

## Significance

The role of ATF5 highlights the evolutionary conservation of the UPRmt signaling mechanism between worms and mammals.
