# Urolithins

**Urolithins** are a class of microbially derived metabolites produced in the gut from the metabolism of [[Ellagitannins|ellagitannins]] and [[Ellagic acid|ellagic acid]]. They exhibit various biological activities, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, anti-tumor, and anti-aging properties. The most prominent member of this class is [[notes/_link/Urolithin A|Urolithin A]], which is highly investigated for its role in mitophagy and muscle health.
