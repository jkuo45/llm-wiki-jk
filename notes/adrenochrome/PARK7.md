# PARK7

PARK7 (also known as DJ-1) is a multifunctional protein that plays a critical role in cellular protection against oxidative stress and glycation.

## Function in Glycation Prevention

Historically characterized as a protein deglycase, biochemical evidence indicates that PARK7 primarily functions as a glutathione-independent glyoxalase. It stereospecifically detoxifies free [[Methylglyoxal]] and [[Glyoxal]] directly into [[L-lactate]] and [[Glycolic acid]], respectively, preventing these reactive intermediates from forming irreversible [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGE) crosslinks on [[Cysteine]], [[Lysine]], and [[Arginine]] residues.

Loss-of-function mutations in PARK7 lead to significant tissue-specific accumulation of novel AGEs and are strongly linked to early-onset familial [[Parkinson's Disease]].
