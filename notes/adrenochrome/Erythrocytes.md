# Erythrocytes

**Erythrocytes**, or **Red blood cells** (RBCs), are the most common type of blood cell and the principal means of delivering oxygen (O₂) to the body tissues via blood flow through the circulatory system.

## Vulnerability to Oxidation
Erythrocytes are subject to [[notes/_link/Oxidative Stress]] from the autoxidation of [[Hemoglobin]] and the presence of circulating catecholamine metabolites like [[Adrenochrome]], which can lead to [[Hemolysis]] or [[Methemoglobinemia]].
