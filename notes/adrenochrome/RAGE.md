# RAGE

RAGE (Receptor for Advanced Glycation End Products) is a cell surface molecule that recognizes and binds to [[Advanced Glycation End Products]].

## Overview

RAGE is a member of the immunoglobulin superfamily. The interaction between AGEs and RAGE alters cell and organ functions by activating inflammatory signaling pathways, generating [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] (ROS), and inducing [[Apoptosis]].

## Pathological Role

RAGE signaling is implicated in:
- [[Inflammation]]
- [[notes/adrenochrome/Oxidative Stress]]
- [[Aging]]
- [[Autophagy]]
- Vascular alterations and diabetic vasculopathy.
