# Atrogin-1

**Atrogin-1** (also known as FBXO32 or F-box protein 32) is a muscle-specific E3 ubiquitin ligase that plays a critical role in muscle protein degradation and muscle atrophy. It targets specific myofibrillar and structural proteins for degradation via the [[Ubiquitin-dependent 20S proteasome|ubiquitin-proteasome system]]. Its expression is regulated by transcription factors like [[notes/_link/FOXO|FoxO]] and is downregulated by [[notes/_link/Urolithin A|Urolithin A]] to help preserve muscle mass.
