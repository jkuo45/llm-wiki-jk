# NF-κB

Nuclear factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of activated B cells (NF-κB) is a protein complex that controls transcription of DNA, cytokine production, and cell survival. It plays a key role in regulating the immune response to infection and is frequently activated by the interaction between [[Advanced Glycation End Products|AGEs]] and the receptor [[RAGE]].

## Sirtuin Regulation

[[SIRT1]] deacetylates RelA/p65 (NF-κB subunit) at Ac-Lys310, inhibiting its transactivation potential. SIRT1 also promotes proteasomal degradation of NF-κB p65. [[SIRT6]] deacetylates H3K9 in promoters of NF-κB target genes, suppressing inflammatory responses. [[SIRT7]] inhibits nuclear translocation of NF-κB p65. [[SIRT2]] deacetylates NF-κB p65 to reduce pro-inflammatory cytokines.
