# nuo-6

*nuo-6* (NADH-Ubiquinone Oxidoreductase 6) is a gene in *C. elegans* that encodes a subunit of mitochondrial **Complex I** (NADH dehydrogenase) of the electron transport chain.

## Role in Longevity

Mutations in *nuo-6* result in impaired mitochondrial function and reduced respiration, which, like *isp-1* and *clk-1*, leads to an extension of lifespan. This longevity is dependent on the activation of adaptive stress responses, including [[Mitohormesis]].
