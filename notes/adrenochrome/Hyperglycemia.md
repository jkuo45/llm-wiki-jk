# Hyperglycemia

Hyperglycemia is a medical condition characterized by an abnormally high concentration of glucose in the blood.

## Overview

Hyperglycemia is a primary driver of pathological [[Glycation]]. Under hyperglycemic conditions, the rate of non-enzymatic reaction between sugars and proteins is highly accelerated, leading to the rapid accumulation of [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGEs).

## Consequences

- Acceleration of the [[Aging]] process.
- Pathogenesis of diabetic complications (e.g., neuropathy, nephropathy, retinopathy).
- Increased [[notes/adrenochrome/Oxidative Stress]] due to glucose autoxidation.
