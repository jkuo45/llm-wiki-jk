# Neurodegeneration

Neurodegeneration is the progressive loss of structure or function of neurons, including death of neurons. It is a key feature of diseases like [[Alzheimer's Disease]] and [[Parkinson's Disease]], often linked to [[Oxidative Stress]] and [[Advanced Glycation End Products|AGEs]].
