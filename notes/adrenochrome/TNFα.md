# TNFα

Tumor necrosis factor alpha (TNFα) is a cell signaling protein (cytokine) involved in systemic inflammation and is one of the cytokines that make up the acute phase reaction. Its transcription is often activated by [[NF-κB]] in response to oxidative stress and [[Advanced Glycation End Products|AGE]] accumulation.
