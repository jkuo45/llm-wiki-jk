# HemF-like

HemF-like (coproporphyrinogen III oxidase-like) enzymes are a class of biocatalysts capable of degrading the stubborn advanced glycation end-product crosslink [[Glucosepane]].

## Discovery and Function

Through functional metagenomics, researchers identified HemF-like enzymes from soil bacteria that can utilize synthetic glucosepane as a nutrient source. 

Importantly, the human homolog of HemF, along with homologous bacterial proteins found in the human microbiome (such as *Escherichia* and *Pseudomonas*), catalyzes the cleavage of the glucosepane ring in vitro, releasing [[Citrulline]] as a byproduct.
