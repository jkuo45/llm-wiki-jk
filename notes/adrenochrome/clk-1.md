# clk-1

*clk-1* (Clock-1) is a gene in *C. elegans* that encodes a mitochondrial enzyme (a hydroxylase) involved in the biosynthesis of **Ubiquinone** (Coenzyme Q).

## Role in Longevity

Mutations in *clk-1* lead to a slowed physiological pace (e.g., slower development, slower rhythmic behaviors) and a significant extension of lifespan. This extension is a classic example of longevity conferred by impaired mitochondrial function and is often used to study [[Mitohormesis]].

## Evolutionary Conservation

The mouse homolog, *Mclk1*, also shows that partial deficiency can lead to increased cellular fitness and lifespan extension, suggesting a conserved mechanism of mitohormetic longevity.
