# Isourolithin A

**Isourolithin A** is a microbially derived metabolite and isomer of [[Urolithin A|Urolithin A]] produced by gut bacteria (such as [[Ellagibacter isourolithinifaciens|Ellagibacter isourolithinifaciens]]) from dietary [[Ellagic acid|ellagic acid]].
