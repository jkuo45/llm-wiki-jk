# Lungs

The **Lungs** are a primary site of gas exchange and a major target for oxidative damage and inflammatory responses.

## Role in [[Adrenochrome]] Production
The lungs are a significant site for the oxidative metabolism of [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]] into [[Adrenochrome]], particularly during acute respiratory conditions.
- **Neutrophil Infiltration:** During pneumonia, ARDS (Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome), or COPD exacerbations, [[notes/_link/Neutrophils]] flood the lung tissue and alveolar spaces.
- **Respiratory Burst:** These activated [[notes/_link/Neutrophils]] release massive amounts of [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] (ROS) and [[notes/_link/Myeloperoxidase]], creating a highly oxidative environment.
- **Adrenaline Oxidation:** Local or circulating adrenaline is oxidized to [[Adrenochrome]] within this environment. The presence of adrenochrome can further exacerbate lung injury through [[Redox Cycling]].

## Clinical Significance
The formation of [[Adrenochrome]] in the lungs is a marker of severe oxidative stress and neutrophil-mediated tissue damage. It is often minimal in healthy lungs where few activated neutrophils are present.
