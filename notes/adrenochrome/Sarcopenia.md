# Sarcopenia

Sarcopenia is the progressive and generalized loss of skeletal muscle mass and strength. It is a serious problem among older populations and is associated with increased [[Oxidative Stress]] and [[Inflammation]], potentially exacerbated by the accumulation of [[Advanced Glycation End Products|AGEs]] in muscle tissue.
