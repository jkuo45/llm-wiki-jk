# Pyridoxamine

Pyridoxamine is one of the forms of Vitamin B6.

## Overview

Pyridoxamine is recognized as an inhibitor of [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGEs). It works by interacting with highly reactive carbonyl species, acting as a "carbonyl trap" to prevent them from reacting with proteins and forming AGEs.

## Therapeutic Potential

Pyridoxamine has been investigated for its ability to prevent diabetic complications, such as nephropathy, by reducing AGE accumulation.
