# Superoxide anion

**Superoxide anion** (O₂⁻•) is a highly reactive free radical and a primary type of [[oxidative_stress/Reactive Oxygen Species]] (ROS). It is formed by the one-electron reduction of molecular oxygen.

## Role in [[Adrenochrome]] Formation
Superoxide is a key driver of the [[Oxidation]] of [[Epinephrine]] into [[Adrenochrome]]. It initiates the radical chain reaction required for the cyclization of the adrenaline molecule.

## [[Redox Cycling]]
In the [[Adrenochrome]] redox cycle, an electron is donated to molecular oxygen to regenerate [[Superoxide anion]]. This means that the formation of [[Adrenochrome]] can lead to a self-sustaining loop of superoxide production, exacerbating [[comt/Oxidative stress]].

## Sources
- **Mitochondria:** Leaked electrons from the respiratory chain.
- **[[Neutrophils]]:** Produced in large quantities via NADPH oxidase during the respiratory burst to kill pathogens.
