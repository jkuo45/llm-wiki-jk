# Methylene blue

[[Methylene blue]] is a medication used as the first-line treatment for symptomatic or high-level acquired [[Methemoglobinemia]]. It acts as a cofactor to enhance the NADPH-methemoglobin reductase pathway, reducing [[Methemoglobin]] back to functional [[Hemoglobin]].

## Connections
- [[Methemoglobinemia]]: Treated with IV methylene blue.
- [[G6PD deficiency]]: Methylene blue should be used cautiously or avoided due to the risk of inducing [[Hemolysis]].
- [[notes/_link/Ascorbic Acid]]: An alternative treatment when methylene blue is contraindicated.
