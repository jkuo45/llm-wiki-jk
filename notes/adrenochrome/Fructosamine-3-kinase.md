# Fructosamine-3-kinase

Fructosamine-3-kinase (FN3K) is an intracellular enzyme responsible for initiating protein deglycation, reversing early glycation intermediates before they transition into irreversible [[Advanced Glycation End Products]].

## Mechanism of Action

FN3K phosphorylates fructosamine ([[Amadori products]]) residues on glycated proteins, converting them into [[Fructosamine-3-phosphate]]. This phosphorylated intermediate is highly unstable and spontaneously decomposes, regenerating the original, undamaged amino acid and releasing [[3-deoxyglucosone]], which is subsequently neutralized by detoxifying enzymes.

FN3K is a key focus of anti-aging research, particularly for its potential to deglycate structural proteins like [[Collagen]] when paired with other deglycating agents.
