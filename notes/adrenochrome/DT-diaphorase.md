# DT-diaphorase

**DT-diaphorase** is an alternative name for [[NQO1]] (NAD(P)H:quinone oxidoreductase 1), a flavoprotein that catalyzes the obligatory two-electron reduction of quinones to hydroquinones, thereby bypassing semiquinone radical formation and preventing [[Redox Cycling]].

The name "DT-diaphorase" derives from its historical characterization using the artificial electron acceptor 2,6-dichlorophenolindophenol (DCPIP, "D") with NADH or NADPH as electron donor ("T" for triphosphopyridine nucleotide).

## Protective Function

By reducing [[Adrenochrome]] and other [[Aminochromes]] directly to less reactive hydroquinones, DT-diaphorase limits the generation of [[Superoxide anion]] and protects tissues with high catecholamine turnover from oxidative damage.
