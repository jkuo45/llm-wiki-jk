# Dopaminergic neurons

Nerve cells that produce the neurotransmitter [[Dopamine]].

### Connections
- [[Substantia nigra]]: A brain region rich in dopaminergic neurons.
- [[Neuromelanin]]: Synthesis in these neurons involves [[Aminochromes]] as intermediates.
- [[Dopaminochrome]]: An aminochrome formed from the oxidation of [[Dopamine]].
