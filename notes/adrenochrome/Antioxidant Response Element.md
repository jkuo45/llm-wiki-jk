# Antioxidant Response Element

The **Antioxidant Response Element (ARE)** is a cis-acting regulatory sequence found in the promoter region of many genes encoding antioxidant and phase II detoxifying enzymes, primarily regulated by the transcription factor [[Nrf2]].
