# Alagebrium

Alagebrium (also known as ALT-711) is a synthetic compound developed as a breaker of [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGEs).

## Overview

Alagebrium was designed to destroy preformed AGE-derived protein cross-links. By breaking these cross-links, it can potentially restore the elasticity of tissues like blood vessels and the heart that have been stiffened by glycation.
