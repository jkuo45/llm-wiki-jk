# Cysteine

**Cysteine** is a semi-essential proteinogenic amino acid.

## Antioxidant Role
In some pharmaceutical formulations, cysteine is explored as a sulfite-free alternative antioxidant to stabilize [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]] and prevent its oxidation into [[Adrenochrome]].
