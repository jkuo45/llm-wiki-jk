Description

Aminoguanidine is a one- [carbon](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/element/Carbon) compound whose unique structure renders it capable of acting as a derivative of [hydrazine](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/hydrazine), [guanidine](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/guanidine) or [formamide](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/formamide). It has a role as an EC 1.4.3.4 (monoamine oxidase) inhibitor and an EC 1.14.13.39 ([nitric oxide](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/nitric%20oxide) synthase) inhibitor. It is a member of guanidines and a one- [carbon](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/element/Carbon) compound.

Pimagedine has been developed by Synvista Therapeutics, Inc for the treatment of diabetic kidney disease. It is an advanced glycation end product inhibitor which manages diabetic nephropathy, either alone or in combination with other therapies. It is beneficial in treating patients with diabetic nephropathy.

diamine oxidase & nitric oxide synthase inhibitor; an advanced glycosylation end product inhibitor; used in the treatment of diabetic complications; structure
