# isp-1

*isp-1* (Iron-Sulfur Protein 1) is a gene in *C. elegans* that encodes a subunit of the mitochondrial **Complex III** (ubiquinol-cytochrome c oxidoreductase) of the electron transport chain.

## Role in Longevity

Mutations in *isp-1* reduce mitochondrial respiration and lead to a significant extension of lifespan. This effect is thought to be mediated by the induction of [[Mitohormesis]], potentially involving a transient increase in mitochondrial [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] (mROS) that activates protective pathways.
