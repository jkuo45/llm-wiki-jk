# Substantia nigra

A basal ganglia structure located in the midbrain that plays an important role in reward and movement.

### Connections
- [[Dopaminergic neurons]]: The substantia nigra is characterized by the presence of these neurons.
- [[Neuromelanin]]: Pigment found in the substantia nigra, formed via the oxidation of [[Catecholamines]].
- [[Adrenochrome]]: Related [[Aminochromes]] are intermediates in neuromelanin synthesis here.
