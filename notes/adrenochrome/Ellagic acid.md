# Ellagic acid

**Ellagic acid** is a natural polyphenolic compound found in numerous fruits and vegetables. It is released from the hydrolysis of [[notes/adrenochrome/Ellagitannins|ellagitannins]] during digestion and is subsequently metabolized by intestinal bacteria (such as [[notes/adrenochrome/Gordonibacter urolithinfaciens|Gordonibacter urolithinfaciens]] and [[notes/adrenochrome/Ellagibacter isourolithinifaciens|Ellagibacter isourolithinifaciens]]) to produce [[notes/adrenochrome/Urolithins|urolithins]], including [[notes/_link/Urolithin A|Urolithin A]].
