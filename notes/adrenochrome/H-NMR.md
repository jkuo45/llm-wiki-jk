# H-NMR

**H-NMR** (Proton Nuclear Magnetic Resonance) is an analytical technique used to determine the structure of organic molecules.

## Use
It is a primary method for verifying the molecular structure and purity of synthesized intermediates like [[Leuco-adrenochrome]].
