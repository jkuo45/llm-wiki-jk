# Amadori products

Amadori products are stable intermediate compounds formed during the early stages of [[Glycation]].

## Overview

The formation of Amadori products follows the initial creation of an unstable [[Schiff base]]. Amadori products undergo further irreversible dehydration, rearrangement, and oxidation reactions, leading to the formation of [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGEs).
