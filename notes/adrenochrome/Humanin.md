# Humanin

Humanin is a 24-amino acid peptide encoded by a cryptic open reading frame within the mitochondrial 16S ribosomal RNA gene. It is considered a founding member of the class of [[Mitokines]] or mitochondrially-derived peptides (MDPs).

## Biological Function

Humanin was originally identified as a cytoprotective factor for [[Alzheimer's Disease]]. It circulates in plasma and has broad cytoprotective, anti-apoptotic, and metabolic regulatory effects. It is involved in [[Mitohormesis]] by providing systemic protection against various stresses.
