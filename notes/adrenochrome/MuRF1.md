# MuRF1

**MuRF1** (Muscle RING Finger 1, also known as TRIM63) is a muscle-specific E3 ubiquitin ligase that is upregulated during conditions of muscle wasting and atrophy. It targets sarcomeric proteins for degradation through the [[notes/adrenochrome/Ubiquitin-dependent 20S proteasome|ubiquitin-proteasome system]]. Along with [[notes/adrenochrome/Atrogin-1|Atrogin-1]], its expression is inhibited by [[notes/_link/Urolithin A|Urolithin A]] to mitigate muscle atrophy and promote muscle hypertrophy.
