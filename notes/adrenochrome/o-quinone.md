# o-quinone

An **o-quinone** (ortho-quinone) is a type of organic compound derived from phenols, specifically catechols. In the context of [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]] metabolism, it represents a highly reactive intermediate formed during oxidation.

## Formation in Epinephrine Pathway
Under [[notes/oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress]], the [[Catechol]] ring of [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]] loses electrons to form an o-quinone. This intermediate is unstable and rapidly undergoes internal cyclization to form [[Adrenochrome]].

## Properties
- Highly reactive [[Electrophile]].
- Prone to nucleophilic attack (e.g., [[Michael addition]]).
- Key marker of oxidative decay in catecholamines.
