# Gordonibacter urolithinfaciens

**Gordonibacter urolithinfaciens** is a Gram-positive, anaerobic bacterium isolated from the human gut that belongs to the family Eggerthellaceae. It is capable of metabolizing [[Ellagic acid|ellagic acid]] to produce urolithins, specifically acting as an intermediate producer in the gut microbiome pathway that leads to the generation of [[Urolithin A|Urolithin A]].
