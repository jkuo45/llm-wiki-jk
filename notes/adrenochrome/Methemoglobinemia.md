# Methemoglobinemia

**Methemoglobinemia** is a condition caused by elevated levels of [[Methemoglobin]] in the blood.

## Relationship to Adrenochrome
Intermediates in the epinephrine oxidation pathway, such as [[Adrenochrome]] and potentially [[Leuco-adrenochrome]], can promote the oxidation of [[Hemoglobin]] to [[Methemoglobin]], contributing to oxidative stress in [[Erythrocytes]].
