# NLRP3 Inflammasome

The NLRP3 inflammasome is a multi-protein complex that acts as a sensor for a wide range of endogenous and exogenous danger signals, triggering the inflammatory response.

## Role of Mitochondria

Mitochondria are critical for the activation of the NLRP3 inflammasome. Mitochondrial damage and the release of [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] (mROS) or mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) into the cytosol are key triggers for inflammasome assembly. Activation results in the activation of caspase-1 and the subsequent processing and release of proinflammatory cytokines like IL-1β.

## Significance

The NLRP3 inflammasome represents a key interface where mitochondrial dysfunction leads to systemic [[Inflammation]].
