# indoline-5,6-dione

**Indoline-5,6-dione** is the chemical structure corresponding to [[Adrenochrome]]. It is formed through the internal cyclization of an [[o-quinone]] intermediate derived from [[Epinephrine]].

## Structure
The structure consists of an indoline ring system with two ketone groups at the 5 and 6 positions.

## Significance
It is a "side pathway" product of [[Epinephrine]] oxidation, often serving as a marker for failed [[Homeostasis]] or extreme [[Oxidative Stress]].
