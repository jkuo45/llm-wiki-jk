# Arginine

Arginine (L-arginine) is a basic amino acid involved in protein synthesis, urea cycle metabolism, and nitric oxide production.

## Role in Glycation

Arginine residues on long-lived structural proteins like [[Collagen]] are primary targets for covalent glycation. They react with reducing sugars or reactive dicarbonyls to form complex, irreversible [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGEs), including the prominent crosslinks [[Glucosepane]] and [[Pentosidine]].
