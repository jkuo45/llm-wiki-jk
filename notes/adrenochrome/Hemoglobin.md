# Hemoglobin

[[Hemoglobin]] is a protein tetramer (consisting of 2α and 2β chains) found in [[erythrocytes]] that is responsible for transporting oxygen throughout the body. Each chain contains a heme group with iron in the ferrous (Fe²⁺) state, which binds oxygen.

## Connections
- [[Methemoglobin]]: An oxidized form of hemoglobin (Fe³⁺) that cannot transport oxygen.
- [[Oxyhemoglobin]]: The oxygen-bound form of hemoglobin.
- [[Methemoglobinemia]]: A condition characterized by elevated levels of [[methemoglobin]].
