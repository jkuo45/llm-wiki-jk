# Maillard Reaction

The Maillard Reaction is a non-enzymatic chemical reaction between amino acids and reducing sugars that gives browned food its distinctive flavor.

## Overview

In a biological context, the Maillard reaction is responsible for the endogenous formation of [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGEs). It initiates with the formation of an unstable [[Schiff base]], which rearranges into a more stable [[Amadori products|Amadori product]]. These then undergo further oxidation and cross-linking to form irreversible AGEs.

## Biological Significance

The accumulation of Maillard reaction products is a hallmark of [[Aging]] and is highly accelerated in [[Hyperglycemia]].
