# Fructosamine-3-phosphate

Fructosamine-3-phosphate is an unstable intermediate metabolite generated during the protein deglycation process.

## Formation and Decomposition

It is formed when [[Fructosamine-3-kinase]] phosphorylates fructosamine ([[Amadori products]]) residues on glycated proteins. Due to its chemical instability, it spontaneously decomposes to regenerate the original, undamaged amino acid and release [[3-deoxyglucosone]].
