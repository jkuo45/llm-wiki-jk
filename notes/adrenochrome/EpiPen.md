# EpiPen

An **EpiPen** is a brand of epinephrine auto-injector used for the emergency treatment of [[Anaphylaxis]].

## Stability
The stability of the [[Epinephrine]] within an EpiPen is critical. Formulations are designed to prevent the formation of [[Adrenochrome]] and other degradants for a shelf life of typically 12-24 months.
