# Racemization

**Racemization** is a chemical process in which one enantiomer of a compound is converted into an equal mixture of both enantiomers (a racemic mixture).

## Effect on Epinephrine
Pharmaceutical [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]] is typically the L-isomer. Over time, heat and pH changes can cause racemization to the D-isomer, which is significantly less biologically active, leading to a loss of therapeutic potency.
