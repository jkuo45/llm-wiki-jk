# Carbazochrome

**Carbazochrome** is a stabilized semicarbazone derivative of [[Adrenochrome]]. While adrenochrome itself is too unstable for medical use, carbazochrome is used clinically as a hemostatic agent.

## Medical Use
Carbazochrome (often administered as carbazochrome sodium sulfonate) is used to promote blood clotting and manage hemorrhaging. It works by:
- **Increasing Capillary Resistance:** Strengthening the walls of small blood vessels.
- **Reducing Capillary Permeability:** Preventing the leakage of blood and fluids.

It does not have the psychoactive or "elixir of youth" properties falsely attributed to [[Adrenochrome]] in popular myths.

## Relationship to [[Adrenochrome]]
Carbazochrome is chemically synthesized from [[Adrenochrome]] by reacting it with semicarbazide. This stabilization allows it to be used as a pharmaceutical product, whereas natural adrenochrome rapidly degrades or polymerizes.
