# Respiratory Burst

The Respiratory Burst is the rapid release of [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] (ROS), such as [[Superoxide anion]] and [[Hydrogen peroxide]], from cells like [[Neutrophils]] and monocytes as part of the immune response.
