# Transcriptome

A transcriptome is the complete set of RNA transcripts (including mRNA, rRNA, tRNA, and non-coding RNA) produced by the genome at a specific time or under a specific condition.

## Transcriptomic Analysis

Transcriptomic analysis (e.g., via RNA-seq) is used to measure the expression levels of thousands of genes simultaneously. In the study of [[Mitohormesis]], transcriptomic analysis is essential for identifying the coordinated changes in nuclear gene expression that occur in response to mitochondrial stress.
