# Cardiac arrest

**Cardiac arrest** is a sudden loss of blood flow resulting from the failure of the heart to effectively pump.

## Use of Epinephrine
[[Epinephrine]] is a standard medication used during resuscitation efforts for cardiac arrest to increase coronary and cerebral perfusion pressure.
