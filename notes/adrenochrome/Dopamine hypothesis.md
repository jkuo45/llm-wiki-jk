# Dopamine hypothesis

The **Dopamine hypothesis** of [[Schizophrenia]] is a model that attributes the symptoms of schizophrenia to a disturbed and hyperactive dopaminergic signal transduction.

## Context with Adrenochrome
This hypothesis eventually superseded the [[Adrenochrome Hypothesis]] as the primary biological explanation for schizophrenia, focusing on neurotransmitter receptor activity rather than oxidative metabolites.
