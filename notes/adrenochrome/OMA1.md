# OMA1

A mitochondrial metalloprotease located in the inner mitochondrial membrane. OMA1 is activated by mitochondrial stress (e.g., loss of membrane potential) and is responsible for the cleavage of [[DELE1]] and [[OPA1]], regulating mitochondrial stress signaling and dynamics.
