# Pentosidine

Pentosidine is a well-known biomarker and fluorescent cross-link belonging to the [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGEs) family.

## Overview

Pentosidine is formed by the reaction of ribose, glucose, or other sugars with lysine and arginine residues. It is commonly used to measure the extent of protein glycation and cross-linking in tissues.

## Tissue Accumulation during Aging

Pentosidine levels increase with age in several tissues:
- Heart
- Lung collagen
- Skin
- Patellar tendon
- Vitreous body
- Oocytes
- Intervertebral disk
