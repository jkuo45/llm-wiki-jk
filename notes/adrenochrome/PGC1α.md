# PGC1α

PGC1α (Peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma coactivator 1-alpha) is a transcriptional coactivator that acts as a master regulator of mitochondrial biogenesis and energy metabolism.

## Function

PGC1α coordinates the expression of genes involved in mitochondrial respiration, fat oxidation, and thermogenesis. It is activated by energy-sensing pathways, including [[AMPK]] (via phosphorylation) and [[SIRT1]] (via deacetylation), in response to exercise, caloric restriction, or cold exposure.

## Role in Mitohormesis

PGC1α is a key effector in the [[Mitohormesis]] paradigm, as it not only increases mitochondrial number (biogenesis) but also induces a potent antioxidant defense program to mitigate the ROS produced during increased metabolic activity.
