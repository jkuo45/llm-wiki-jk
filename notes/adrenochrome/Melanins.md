# Melanins

**Melanins** are a broad group of natural pigments found in most organisms.

## Relationship to Adrenochrome
[[Adrenochrome]] is a highly unstable intermediate that readily polymerizes into melanin-like pigments. This is the same process that leads to the formation of [[notes/_link/Neuromelanin]] in certain brain regions.
