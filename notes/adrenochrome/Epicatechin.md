# Epicatechin

Epicatechin is a polyphenolic flavonoid found in various plants, such as green tea and cocoa, known for its antioxidant and therapeutic properties.

## Overview

Epicatechin has been identified as a potent breaker of [[Advanced Glycation End Products]] (AGEs). It can destroy preformed protein cross-links, such as those in glycated [[Serum albumin]]. The nucleophilic carbons (C6 and C8) on its A-ring are thought to attack and break the side chains of AGE structures.

## Beneficial Effects

- **Retinoprotective**: Decreases AGE accumulation in retinal tissues.
- **Antioxidant**: Neutralizes [[Reactive Oxygen Species]].
- **Anti-glycation**: Inhibits the formation of new AGEs.
