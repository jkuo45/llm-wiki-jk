# Inflammation

Inflammation is a part of the complex biological response of body tissues to harmful stimuli, such as pathogens, damaged cells, or irritants. Chronic inflammation is closely linked to the accumulation of [[Advanced Glycation End Products|AGEs]] and the activation of the [[NF-κB]] pathway, contributing to various chronic degenerative diseases.

## Molecular Mediators

Inflammation is orchestrated by a complex network of soluble mediators. **Cytokines** such as [[TNFα]], [[IL-6]], and [[Interleukin 1β]] are primary drivers of the acute-phase response, promoting vasodilation, leukocyte recruitment, and fever. **Chemokines** (e.g., CXCL8/IL-8) establish chemotactic gradients that direct [[notes/_link/Neutrophils]] and macrophages to sites of tissue injury or infection. **Eicosanoids** — prostaglandins, leukotrienes, and thromboxanes derived from arachidonic acid via cyclooxygenase (COX) and lipoxygenase (LOX) pathways — amplify vascular permeability and pain signaling. These mediators act in a coordinated cascade, with temporal and spatial specificity determining the transition from acute to chronic inflammation.

## Acute vs Chronic Inflammation

Acute inflammation is a rapid, self-limiting response lasting hours to days, characterized by neutrophil infiltration, exudation of plasma proteins, and eventual resolution via macrophage-mediated clearance of apoptotic cells. Chronic inflammation persists for weeks to years, driven by sustained activation of [[NF-κB]] and [[NLRP3 Inflammasome]] signaling, with a shift from neutrophilic to lymphocytic and macrophage infiltration. Persistent inflammatory signaling promotes tissue remodeling, fibrosis, and the accumulation of oxidative damage, creating a feed-forward loop with [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] production.

## NF-κB and NLRP3 Inflammasome Signaling

The transcription factor [[NF-κB]] serves as the master regulator of pro-inflammatory gene expression. Activation occurs via canonical (IKKβ-dependent degradation of IκBα) and non-canonical pathways, leading to transcription of cytokines, adhesion molecules, and inducible nitric oxide synthase (iNOS). The [[NLRP3 Inflammasome]] is a multiprotein complex that, upon assembly, activates [[Caspase-1]], which cleaves pro-IL-1β and pro-IL-18 into their active forms and can induce pyroptotic cell death. NLRP3 is uniquely sensitive to oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction — danger signals such as mtDNA release, [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] bursts, and potassium efflux all trigger its activation.

## Connection to Adrenochrome Formation

Inflammation and [[notes/_link/Reactive Oxygen Species]] production create a permissive environment for catecholamine oxidation. During the respiratory burst, [[notes/_link/Neutrophils]] and activated macrophages release [[notes/_link/Myeloperoxidase]] and [[NAD(P)H Oxidase]], generating hypochlorous acid and superoxide that can directly oxidize [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]] to its quinone and onward to [[Adrenochrome]]. Pro-inflammatory cytokines upregulate [[COMT]] and [[MAO]] activity, altering catecholamine flux. Conversely, adrenochrome itself can activate [[NF-κB]] signaling, establishing a positive feedback loop: inflammation promotes adrenochrome formation, and adrenochrome exacerbates inflammatory signaling via redox-dependent mechanisms.

## Therapeutic Anti-Inflammatory Strategies

Pharmacological interventions targeting inflammation include NSAIDs (COX inhibitors), corticosteroids (broad transcriptional suppression), and biologics targeting specific cytokines (e.g., anti-TNFα, anti-IL-6 receptor). More targeted strategies relevant to redox-inflammatory crosstalk include [[NLRP3 Inflammasome]] inhibitors (e.g., MCC950), [[NF-κB]] pathway modulators, and compounds that bolster antioxidant capacity such as [[notes/_link/Glutathione]] precursors ([[GlyNAC]]) and Nrf2 activators (e.g., sulforaphane). Given the link between inflammation and adrenochrome formation, anti-inflammatory interventions may indirectly reduce catecholamine oxidation burden — a hypothesis warranting direct experimental investigation.
