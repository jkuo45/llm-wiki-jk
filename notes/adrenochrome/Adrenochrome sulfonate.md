# Adrenochrome sulfonate

**Adrenochrome sulfonate** is a degradation product formed by the reaction of [[Adrenochrome]] with [[Bisulfite]] antioxidants in the presence of light.

## Significance
It is one of several impurities that must be monitored during the [[Quality Control]] of [[notes/_link/Epinephrine]] formulations to ensure safety and stability.
