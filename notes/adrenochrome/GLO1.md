# GLO1

GLO1 (Glyoxalase 1, or lactoylglutathione lyase) is the rate-limiting enzyme of the cellular [[Glyoxalase System]].

## Mechanism of Action

GLO1 catalyzes the conversion of hemithioacetals—formed spontaneously from the reactive dicarbonyl [[Methylglyoxal]] and the antioxidant [[Glutathione]]—into [[S-D-lactoylglutathione]].

Maintaining or enhancing GLO1 activity is a primary therapeutic target for reducing dicarbonyl stress and preventing the accumulation of [[Advanced Glycation End Products]].
