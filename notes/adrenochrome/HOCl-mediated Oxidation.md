# HOCl-mediated Oxidation

HOCl-mediated Oxidation refers to the chemical process where hypochlorous acid (HOCl), generated by the [[Myeloperoxidase]] system, acts as a powerful electrophile to oxidize substrates such as [[Epinephrine]] into [[Aminochromes]] like [[Adrenochrome]].
