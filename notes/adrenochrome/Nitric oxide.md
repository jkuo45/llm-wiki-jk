# Nitric oxide

[[Nitric oxide]] is a gas used as a medication for inhalation to treat respiratory failure. High levels or prolonged exposure can lead to the formation of [[Methemoglobin]].

## Connections
- [[Methemoglobin]]: Formed as a byproduct of nitric oxide metabolism.
- [[Methemoglobinemia]]: A risk associated with inhaled nitric oxide therapy.
