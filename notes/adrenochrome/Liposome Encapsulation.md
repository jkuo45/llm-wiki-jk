# Liposome Encapsulation

**Liposome encapsulation** is a formulation technique in which an active compound — a small molecule, peptide, nucleic acid, or contrast agent — is sequestered within or anchored to the phospholipid bilayer of a liposome, a spherical vesicle comprising one or more concentric phospholipid membranes surrounding an aqueous core. Encapsulation exploits the amphipathic nature of phospholipids (e.g., phosphatidylcholine) to solubilize both hydrophilic cargo (in the aqueous core) and hydrophobic cargo (within the lipid bilayer).

## Mechanism & Formulation

Liposomes are typically prepared by hydrating dried phospholipids (often with cholesterol to modulate membrane rigidity) and then sizing the vesicles by extrusion, sonication, or microfluidics. Active compounds are co-dissolved or trapped during hydration. Surface functionalization with PEG ("stealth" liposomes) reduces opsonization and extends circulation half-life; ligands such as antibodies or peptides can confer tissue targeting. Encapsulation improves pharmacokinetic behavior by protecting labile molecules from degradation, reducing systemic toxicity, and enabling sustained or localized release.

## Relevance to Adrenochrome & Related Compounds

Many bioactive aminochromes and quinones — including [[Adrenochrome]] and its derivatives — are chemically unstable, redox-active, and poorly soluble, making them strong candidates for encapsulation to control delivery and mitigate oxidative damage. Liposomal formulations can shield encapsulated catecholamine-derived species from rapid autoxidation and protect surrounding tissue from [[Oxidative Stress]]. Encapsulation is also used to deliver antioxidants, enzymes (e.g., [[Cytochrome b5 reductase]] cofactors), or reference standards in controlled experimental systems.

## Clinical & Research Applications

Liposomal drug products are established in the clinic (e.g., liposomal doxorubicin, liposomal amphotericin B), demonstrating reduced cardiotoxicity and improved therapeutic index. In research, liposome encapsulation serves as a platform for targeted delivery of small molecules, RNA, and imaging agents. For reference-standard and impurity work in adrenochrome analytics, encapsulation can stabilize labile analytes during storage and transport.
