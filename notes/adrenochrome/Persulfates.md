# Persulfates

Persulfates (also known as peroxydisulfates) are compounds containing the anion $S_2O_8^{2-}$. They are strong oxidizing agents. Common examples include [[Ammonium Persulfate]], [[Sodium Persulfate]], and [[Potassium Persulfate]].

In the synthesis of [[Adrenochrome]], persulfates are used to oxidize [[Epinephrine]] in an aqueous medium, often in the presence of catalysts like [[Bismuth]] salts or other metal salts.

## Connections
- [[Adrenochrome]]: Used as an oxidizing agent in its preparation.
- [[Epinephrine]]: The substrate for oxidation.
- [[Bismuth]]: Catalyzes the oxidation of adrenaline by persulfates.
