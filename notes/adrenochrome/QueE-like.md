# QueE-like

QueE-like (7-carboxy-7-deazaguanine synthase-like) enzymes are a class of bacterial biocatalysts capable of degrading the stable crosslink [[Glucosepane]].

## Discovery and Application

Identified through metagenomic screening of soil microorganisms, QueE-like enzymes can utilize synthetic glucosepane as a nutrient source. They are being investigated for their potential to cleave glucosepane crosslinks in structural tissues to address tissue stiffening.
