# Glycolic acid

Glycolic acid (or hydroxyacetic acid) is a small organic acid metabolite.

## Production via Dicarbonyl Detoxification

In addition to standard metabolic pathways, glycolic acid is produced inside cells when [[PARK7]] (DJ-1) detoxifies the reactive precursor [[Glyoxal]]. This glutathione-independent glyoxalase reaction neutralizes glyoxal before it can form irreversible [[Advanced Glycation End Products]].
