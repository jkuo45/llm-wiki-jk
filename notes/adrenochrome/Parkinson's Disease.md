# Parkinson's Disease

Parkinson's disease (PD) is a long-term degenerative disorder of the central nervous system that mainly affects the motor system. Like other neurodegenerative diseases, its pathogenesis may involve [[notes/adrenochrome/Oxidative Stress]] and the accumulation of [[Advanced Glycation End Products|AGEs]], which can interfere with protein function and promote neuronal damage.
