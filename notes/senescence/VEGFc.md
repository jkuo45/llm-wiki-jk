# VEGFc (VEGF-C)

**VEGFc** denotes **VEGF-C** (vascular endothelial growth factor C), a member of the [[VEGF]] (vascular endothelial growth factor) family of cysteine-knot growth factors. VEGF-C is a potent **lymphangiogenic** and **angiogenic** factor that signals primarily through [[VEGFR3|VEGFR-3]] (FLT4) on lymphatic endothelium and, at higher concentrations or via proteolytic processing, through [[VEGFR2]] (KDR/FLK1) on blood vascular endothelium. It is a component of the [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]], secreted by senescent cells and implicated in [[Paracrine Senescence]] and tumour-associated vascular remodeling.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

- **Receptors**: VEGF-C binds [[VEGFR3|VEGFR-3]] (its primary lymphatic-endothelial receptor) and, after N- and C-terminal proteolytic maturation, also [[VEGFR2]]. Receptor binding activates VEGFR kinase activity, triggering PI3K/Akt, [[MAPK|MAPK/ERK]], and PLCγ pathways that drive endothelial proliferation, survival, migration, and tube formation.
- **Lymphangiogenesis vs angiogenesis**: Full-length VEGF-C preferentially activates VEGFR-3 and promotes lymphatic vessel sprouting; fully processed VEGF-C additionally engages VEGFR-2 to support blood-vessel angiogenesis.
- **Transcriptional regulation**: In senescence, VEGF-C expression is induced downstream of [[RAS]]/[[RAF]]/[[MEK]] oncogenic signalling and reinforced by the SASP transcriptional program governed by [[IL-1α]] and [[NFKB|NF-κB]].
- **Extracellular processing**: Proteases (e.g., ADAMTS3, CCBE1-assisted) cleave the VEGF-C pro-peptide to generate high-affinity receptor-binding forms, tuning its receptor selectivity.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

> [!info] Source: Acosta et al., 2013 (PMID 23636316)
> Quantitative SILAC proteomics of the OIS secretome identified VEGF family ligands as top SASP hits. VEGF-C (notated VEGFc) was induced as early as 2–3 days after [[RAS]] activation, together with [[CXCL1]], [[IL-8]], [[CCL20]], and [[activin A]]. Among the SASP mediators of paracrine senescence, [[VEGF]] family factors (alongside [[TGFβ]] family ligands, [[CCL2]], and CCL20) were validated by receptor-inhibition: a [[VEGFR2]]/FLT3 inhibitor suppressed paracrine senescence in vitro and, combined with IL-1R, CCR2, and TGFBR1 inhibitors, reduced senescent hepatocyte burden *in vivo* in an Nras[G12V] liver model.

- VEGF-C is a secreted SASP growth factor that, like [[VEGF]]-A, contributes to the pro-angiogenic and paracrine signaling milieu of senescent cells.
- It supports the SASP's dual character: reinforcing the tumour-suppressive senescence arrest microenvironment while simultaneously promoting angiogenesis that can aid tumour growth and immune evasion in the [[Tumor Microenvironment]].
- In [[Paracrine Senescence]], VEGF family signaling is one of the branches (together with TGF-β, CCL2, CCL20) whose receptor blockade partially rescues paracrine arrest.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- **Cancer**: VEGF-C drives tumour lymphangiogenesis and lymphatic metastasis and is a prognostic marker in several cancers; its presence in the SASP links senescence to pro-metastatic vascular remodeling.
- **Lymphatic / vascular disease**: VEGF-C is investigated therapeutically to stimulate lymphatic growth in lymphedema and for therapeutic angiogenesis in ischemia.
- **Senescence-related aging**: As a pro-angiogenic SASP component, VEGF-C contributes to the age-associated vascular changes and the pro-tumorigenic microenvironment generated by accumulating [[Senescent Cells]].
