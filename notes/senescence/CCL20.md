# CCL20

CCL20 (C-C motif chemokine ligand 20), also known as MIP-3α (macrophage inflammatory protein-3 alpha) or LARC (liver activation regulated chemokine), is a CC chemokine that signals exclusively through the receptor [[CCR6]]. It is a component of the [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]] that, together with [[IL-1α]], [[TGFβ]], [[VEGF]], and [[CCL2]], contributes to [[Paracrine Senescence|paracrine growth arrest]] in neighbouring cells.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

- **Receptor binding**: CCL20 is the sole ligand for [[CCR6]], a Gαi-coupled GPCR, driving chemotaxis via PI3K/Akt, [[p38 MAPK]], and Ca2+ flux.
- **Cell sources**: Secreted by senescent fibroblasts and epithelial cells; also produced at mucosal surfaces and inflamed tissues.
- **Transcriptional regulation**: Driven by [[NF-κB]] and induced by the pro-inflammatory SASP cytokines [[IL-1α]] and [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]] and [[TNFα|TNF-α]].
- **Downstream effects**: Recruits CCR6+ cells including immature dendritic cells, effector/memory T cells (notably [[TH17]] cells), and B cells, shaping the tissue immune landscape.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

- In [[Oncogene-Induced Senescence|OIS]] cells, CCL20 is among the SASP components that autocrinally reinforce senescence-associated growth arrest and induce paracrine proliferation arrest by binding their specific receptors on receiving cells.
- The CCL20–CCR6 axis links the SASP to adaptive immunity by recruiting TH17 cells, consistent with the SASP's capacity to skew T-cell differentiation toward the TH17 lineage.
- As with other SASP chemokines, CCL20-mediated immune recruitment can be beneficial (immune surveillance/clearance of senescent cells) or detrimental (chronic inflammation, [[Inflammaging]]).

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- Elevated CCL20/CCR6 signaling is implicated in chronic inflammatory and autoimmune conditions (psoriasis, rheumatoid arthritis, inflammatory bowel disease) and in tumour progression, where it recruits immunosuppressive and pro-tumorigenic immune populations.
- CCL20 contributes to the pro-inflammatory, TH17-skewing microenvironment associated with age-related tissue dysfunction.
