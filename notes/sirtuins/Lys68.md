# Lys68 (MnSOD/SOD2)

**Lys68** (K68) is an acetylatable lysine residue on **MnSOD** ([[SOD2|MnSOD/SOD2]]), the mitochondrial manganese superoxide dismutase. Along with [[Lys122]], it is a principal acetylation site whose modification state governs MnSOD activity.

## Regulation by SIRT3

- **Deacetylation activates MnSOD:** [[SIRT3]] directly deacetylates Lys68 (and [[Lys122]]); deacetylated MnSOD shows dramatically enhanced dismutation of superoxide (O₂⁻) to hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂), reducing mitochondrial oxidative stress.
- **Upstream activation:** This is potentiated by SIRT3 activators such as [[Honokiol]] and [[Dihydromyricetin]], and serves as a canonical indicator of SIRT3 function.
- **Broader impact:** Lower mitochondrial superoxide feeds into redox-sensitive signaling, connecting SIRT3/MnSOD to metabolic and longevity pathways.

## SIRT4 Antagonism & the Mitochondrial Sirtuin Balance

Lys68 (with [[Lys122]]) is the shared downstream node of an antagonistic mitochondrial sirtuin pair:
- **[[SIRT3]]** *activates* MnSOD by deacetylating Lys68/Lys122 → accelerated superoxide clearance → reduced [[ROS]] signaling.
- **[[SIRT4]]** *inhibits* MnSOD via mono-[[ADP-ribosylation]] (distinct from SIRT3's deacetylation), sustaining superoxide and amplifying ROS signaling; SIRT4 overexpression aggravates [[Angiotensin II|angiotensin II]]-induced [[Cardiac Hypertrophy|cardiac hypertrophy]] by suppressing MnSOD.
- The **[[SIRT3-SIRT4 Ratio|SIRT3/SIRT4 ratio]]** acts as a redox rheostat for mitochondrial [[Oxidative Stress|oxidative stress]] and fibrotic susceptibility, defining a **[[Hormetic Window|hormetic window]]** for redox signaling.

## Coupling to Adrenochrome Redox Cycling

[[Adrenochrome]] undergoes catalytic [[Redox Cycling|redox cycling]] that generates superoxide — the exact substrate of SIRT3-activated MnSOD. The two pathways are coupled through shared substrate, enzyme, and downstream effectors ([[PGC-1α]], [[FOXO3a]], [[AMPK]], [[NFKB|NF-κB]]); sub-toxic adrenochrome exposure may upregulate SIRT3 through a retrograde ROS→AMPK→PGC-1α→SIRT3 loop (a "[[Redox Vaccination|redox vaccination]]" effect), increasing MnSOD deacetylation at Lys68/Lys122.

## Biomarker Relevance

MnSOD acetylation at Lys68/Lys122 is used as an indirect surrogate for SIRT3 activity (together with [[MitoSOX]] and urinary 8-OHdG).
