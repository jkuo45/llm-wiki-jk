# Stilbenoid

[[Stilbenoid]]s are a subclass of **polyphenolic phytoalexins** characterized by a **stilbene backbone** — a 1,2-diarylethylene (C₆–CH=CH–C₆) core. They are produced by plants in response to stress/pathogens and are prominent in the [[Sirtuin activating compounds]] family of longevity molecules.

## Structure & Chemistry

- **Core**: trans-stilbene (1,2-diphenylethene); bioactivity depends on hydroxylation pattern and *E/Z* geometry (trans preferred).
- **Key members**: [[Resveratrol]] (3,5,4′-trihydroxy-*trans*-stilbene), pterostilbene (3,5-dimethoxy-4′-hydroxystilbene), piceid (resveratrol glucoside), and combretastatins.
- **Modifications**: Methoxylation (pterostilbene) improves metabolic stability and oral bioavailability versus the parent [[Stilbene]].

## Pharmacological Actions

- **Sirtuin activation**: Resveratrol and analogs are direct [[Sirtuin Activators]] of [[SIRT1]], promoting [[PGC-1α]]-driven mitochondrial biogenesis (PMID 18505964).
- **Antioxidant / anti-inflammatory**: Scavenge radicals and dampen [[NF-κB]] signaling, countering [[Oxidative Stress]].
- **Estrogenic & senolytic activities**: Some stilbenoids display weak estrogen-receptor modulation; certain derivatives show senotherapeutic effects.

> [!tip] Bioavailability matters
> Native resveratrol has poor oral bioavailability; methylated analogs like pterostilbene (candidate note) and formulated/nano-delivered stilbenoids achieve greater systemic exposure.

## Applications

Studied for [[Aging]], cardiovascular protection, [[Cancer]] chemoprevention, and metabolic health, often as calorie-restriction mimetics.
