# CD38 Inhibitor 78c

**CD38 Inhibitor 78c** (also known as compound 78c) is a highly potent, selective, and orally bioactive small-molecule inhibitor of the enzymatic activity of [[CD38]] (specifically its NAD⁺-glycohydrolase / NAD⁺ase activity). 

## Mechanism and Biological Action

CD38 is the primary enzymatic consumer of [[NAD+]] in mammalian tissues during [[Aging|aging]]. As tissues age, chronic low-grade inflammation ([[Inflammaging|inflammaging]]) causes a significant upregulation of CD38 on [[Neutrophils|neutrophils]], [[Macrophage|macrophages]], and other immune cells. This upregulation depletes NAD⁺, starving other NAD⁺-dependent longevity enzymes such as [[Sirtuins]] (especially mitochondrial [[SIRT3]] and nuclear [[SIRT1]]).

By selectively inhibiting CD38, **78c**:
1. **Preserves NAD⁺ levels:** Prevents the age-related degradation of NAD⁺, keeping cellular pools high.
2. **Restores Sirtuin Activity:** Indirectly boosts [[Sirtuins]] (specifically mitochondrial [[SIRT3]] and nuclear [[SIRT1]]) by maintaining co-substrate availability.
3. **Improves Mitochondrial Function:** Alleviates mitochondrial decay and metabolic decline associated with aging.
4. **Synergizes with NAD⁺ Precursors:** Enhances the efficacy of exogenous precursors like [[Nicotinamide Mononucleotide|NMN]] and [[Nicotinamide Riboside|NR]] by preventing their rapid degradation by CD38.

## Preclinical Research and Longevity Benefits

In preclinical rodent studies, administration of 78c to naturally aged mice yielded profound benefits:
- **Lifespan and Healthspan Extension:** Increased exercise capacity, muscle mass, metabolic resilience, and median lifespan (with a particularly clear ~14% lifespan boost in male mice).
- **Glucose Tolerance:** Ameliorated age-related glucose intolerance and insulin resistance.
- **Cardioprotection:** Protected against age-associated cardiac dysfunction and fibrosis.

## Downsides and Caveats

Despite its therapeutic promise, studies using 78c and genetic CD38 knockouts have highlighted potential trade-offs:
- **Sex-Specific Adverse Reactions:** In some long-term studies, aged female mice exhibited severe adverse events leading to early euthanasia, suggesting sex-specific hormonal, metabolic, or immunological interactions.
- **Immune Compromise:** Because CD38 plays a crucial role in immune cell activation and host defense against pathogens, systemic pharmacologic inhibition could potentially raise susceptibility to specific bacterial or viral infections.
