# HDAC1

**HDAC1** (Histone Deacetylase 1) is a class I [[HDAC|histone deacetylase]] that removes acetyl groups from lysine residues on histones and other proteins, compacting chromatin and repressing transcription. It normally occupies specific genomic loci to preserve the gene-expression programs that define cellular identity.

## Role in the RCM Hypothesis

[[HDAC1]] is among the [[Chromatin Modifiers|chromatin modifiers]] — alongside [[SIRT1]], [[SIRT6]], and [[PARP1]] — documented to relocalize to [[Double-Strand Break|DNA damage sites]] during repair. Within the [[Relocalization of Chromatin Modifiers (RCM) Hypothesis]], repeated cycles of HDAC1 recruitment away from its native loci and imperfect return are proposed to erode the [[Epigenetic Landscape|epigenetic landscape]] and de-repress elements such as [[Retrotransposon|retrotransposons]].
