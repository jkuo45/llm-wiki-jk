# p73

**p73** (Tumour Protein p73, encoded by *TP73*) is a structural and functional homologue of the tumor suppressor [[p53]]. Like p53, p73 is a transcription factor that can induce cell cycle arrest or trigger apoptotic cell death in response to DNA damage and other cellular stress signals.

## Regulation by SIRT1

p73 is directly targeted and regulated post-translationally by [[SIRT1]]:

- **Physical Interaction**: p73 physically associates with the nuclear sirtuin [[SIRT1]].
- **Deacetylation**: [[SIRT1]] deacetylates p73. 
- **Inhibition of Transcriptional Activity**: SIRT1-mediated deacetylation of p73 suppresses its transcriptional transactivation activity. This acts to prevent p73-mediated pro-apoptotic target gene expression, thereby inhibiting apoptotic cell death in response to cellular stress.

## Connections

- [[SIRT1]] — physically interacts with and deacetylates p73 to suppress its pro-apoptotic function
