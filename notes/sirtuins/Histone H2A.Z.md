# Histone H2A.Z

**Histone H2A.Z** (encoded by *H2AFZ*) is a highly conserved histone H2A variant that replaces canonical H2A in a subset of nucleosomes. It plays specialized roles in transcriptional regulation, DNA repair, and heterochromatin boundary establishment. H2A.Z is enriched at promoters of actively transcribed genes and at regulatory elements.

## SIRT1 Deacetylation and Proteasomal Degradation

[[SIRT1]] deacetylates H2A.Z at **Lys115** and **Lys121**. Deacetylation of these residues promotes **ubiquitination and proteasome-dependent degradation** of H2A.Z. By controlling H2A.Z turnover, SIRT1 regulates the chromatin landscape and gene expression programs.

## Cardioprotective Role

This SIRT1–H2A.Z axis has been specifically implicated in protection against **cardiac hypertrophy**. Under pathological hypertrophic stress, SIRT1-mediated deacetylation and degradation of H2A.Z prevents aberrant gene activation programs that drive cardiomyocyte enlargement and heart failure. This identifies a novel epigenetic mechanism for cardiac protection regulated by the sirtuin pathway.
