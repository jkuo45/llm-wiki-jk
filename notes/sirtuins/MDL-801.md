# MDL-801

**MDL-801** (and its related compound **MDL-800**) are highly potent, cell-permeable synthetic allosteric activators of **[[SIRT6]]**. They bind the same elongated hydrophobic pocket as [[UBCS039]] but with greater potency, making them valuable research tools for probing SIRT6 biology.

## Mechanism of Action

MDL-801 binds the distal allosteric hydrophobic pocket of SIRT6 (confirmed by crystal structures), stabilizing:
- The catalytically active conformation of the **NAD⁺** co-substrate
- The **acylated/acetylated histone substrate** (H3K9ac, H3K56ac)

This results in enhanced deacetylase and de-fatty-acylase activity, promoting:
- Chromatin compaction
- DNA double-strand break repair
- Suppression of glycolysis (via HIF-1α/SREBP repression)
- Induction of autophagy in tumor cells
