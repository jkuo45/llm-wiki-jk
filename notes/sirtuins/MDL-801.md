# MDL-801 (and MDL-800)

**MDL-801** and its related parent compound **MDL-800** are highly potent, selective, and cell-permeable synthetic allosteric activators of **[[SIRT6]]**. They bind to a structurally conserved, distal allosteric hydrophobic pocket of SIRT6, dramatically enhancing its deacetylase and de-fatty-acylase activity.

---

## 1. Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Bis-benzenesulfonamide-based sirtuin allosteric activators.
- **Target Selectivity**: Highly selective for [[SIRT6]] over other sirtuin isoforms.
- **MDL-800 Potency**: Exhibits an $EC_{50}$ value of approximately $10.3\ \mu\text{M}$ for SIRT6 activation.
- **MDL-801 Potency**: An optimized derivative of MDL-800, displaying significantly higher potency and substrate-binding affinity.

---

## 2. Pharmacodynamics & Pharmacokinetics

### Mechanism of Action
MDL-800 and MDL-801 bind to an elongated hydrophobic channel on the surface of [[SIRT6]], stabilizing the active conformation of the enzyme when bound to its co-substrate **[[NAD+]]** and the acetylated/acylated peptide substrate. This promotes:
- Increased deacetylation rate of sirtuin substrates, specifically histone H3 lysine 9 (H3K9ac) and lysine 56 (H3K56ac).
- Specific chromatin compaction at targeted promoter regions.

### Pathological & Therapeutic Effects
- **Anti-Fibrotic Actions**: MDL-800 acts as a potent inhibitor of myofibroblast differentiation. By activating SIRT6, it deacetylates $\beta$-catenin and selectively downregulates the profibrotic TGF-$\beta$1/Smad signaling pathway, decreasing extracellular matrix (ECM) production.
- **Wound Healing**: MDL-800-mediated SIRT6 activation has been shown to promote angiogenesis and accelerate cutaneous wound healing in mouse models.
- **Oncology & Glycolysis Regulation**: By enhancing SIRT6-dependent histone deacetylation, these compounds act to repress major transcription factors responsible for cancer-associated aerobic glycolysis (e.g., HIF-1α), thereby inhibiting tumor proliferation and stemness.

---

## 3. Applications

- **Fibrosis & Wound Healing Research**: Heavily studied in models of skin, kidney, and lung fibrosis to reverse extracellular matrix accumulation and myofibroblast activity.
- **Cancer Biology**: Used as a chemical tool to study the consequences of SIRT6 activation on glycolysis inhibition, chromatin compaction, and tumor suppression.
