# MDL-800

**MDL-800** is a highly potent, selective, and cell-permeable synthetic allosteric activator of **[[SIRT6]]**. It binds to a structurally conserved, distal allosteric hydrophobic pocket of SIRT6, enhancing its deacetylase and de-fatty-acylase activity. MDL-800 is the parent compound from which the more potent derivative **[[MDL-801]]** was developed.

---

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Bis-benzenesulfonamide-based sirtuin allosteric activator.
- **Target Selectivity**: Highly selective for [[SIRT6]] over other sirtuin isoforms.
- **Potency**: Exhibits an $EC_{50}$ of approximately $10.3\ \mu\text{M}$ for SIRT6 activation.
- **Cell Permeability**: Readily cell-permeable, enabling use in cellular and in vivo models.

---

## Pharmacodynamics & Pharmacokinetics

### Mechanism of Action

MDL-800 binds to an elongated hydrophobic channel on the surface of [[SIRT6]], stabilizing the active conformation when bound to [[NAD+]] and acetylated/acylated peptide substrates. This promotes:
- Increased deacetylation of histone H3 lysine 9 (H3K9ac) and lysine 56 (H3K56ac).
- Chromatin compaction at targeted promoter regions, leading to transcriptional repression of pro-inflammatory and pro-fibrotic genes.

### Pathological & Therapeutic Effects

- **Anti-Fibrotic Actions**: MDL-800 inhibits myofibroblast differentiation by activating SIRT6, deacetylating $\beta$-catenin, and downregulating the pro-fibrotic [[TGF-β/Smad3 pathway]], decreasing extracellular matrix production.
- **Wound Healing**: Promotes angiogenesis and accelerates cutaneous wound healing in mouse models.
- **Oncology**: Represses cancer-associated aerobic glycolysis by suppressing HIF-1α through SIRT6-dependent histone deacetylation, inhibiting tumor proliferation and stemness.

---

## Applications

- **Fibrosis Research**: Studied in models of skin, kidney, and lung fibrosis.
- **Cancer Biology**: Used as a chemical tool to study SIRT6 activation effects on glycolysis inhibition, chromatin compaction, and tumor suppression.
