# Nkx3.2

**Nkx3.2** (also known as **Bapx1**) is a homeobox-containing transcription factor belonging to the NK family of developmental regulators. It plays critical roles in skeletal development, particularly in the formation of the axial skeleton and craniofacial structures, by regulating chondrocyte differentiation and joint formation.

---

## Regulation by SIRT6

**[[SIRT6]]** represses Nkx3.2 transcription by deacetylating [[Histone H3]] at lysine 9 (H3K9ac) within the Nkx3.2 promoter region. This SIRT6-mediated chromatin compaction prevents Nkx3.2 expression, thereby relieving its repression of target genes such as **[[GATA5]]**:

- **Endothelial Protection**: SIRT6 induces [[GATA5]] expression by inhibiting Nkx3.2 transcription, preventing endothelial injury in vascular contexts.

---

## Physiological Function

- **Skeletal Development**: Essential for ventral vertebral column formation, sclerotome development, and maintenance of intervertebral discs.
- **Chondrogenesis**: Promotes chondrocyte differentiation and cartilage formation.
- **Vascular Homeostasis**: Acts as a transcriptional repressor of [[GATA5]] in endothelial cells; its suppression by [[SIRT6]] is protective against endothelial dysfunction.

---

## Connections

- **[[SIRT6]]** — represses Nkx3.2 transcription via H3K9 deacetylation.
- **[[GATA5]]** — downstream target derepressed when SIRT6 silences Nkx3.2.
- **[[Histone H3]]** — chromatin substrate for SIRT6 at the Nkx3.2 locus.
