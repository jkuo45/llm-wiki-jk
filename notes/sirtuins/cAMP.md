# cAMP (Cyclic Adenosine Monophosphate)

**Cyclic Adenosine Monophosphate** (cAMP) is a vital hydrophilic intracellular second messenger that plays a central role in transducing extracellular signals (such as hormones and neurotransmitters) into cellular physiological responses.

In sirtuin biology, cAMP acts as a critical signal transducer within the **AMPK-PDE axis**, mediating the indirect activation of [[SIRT1]] by compounds such as [[Resveratrol]].

## Synthesis and Degradation

The intracellular concentration of cAMP is tightly controlled by the balance between its synthesis and degradation:
1. **Synthesis:** Adenylyl cyclases (ACs) convert adenosine triphosphate ([[ATP|ATP]]) into cAMP in response to G-protein coupled receptor (GPCR) activation.
2. **Degradation:** Phosphodiesterases (PDEs) hydrolyze cAMP to form AMP, terminating the second messenger signal.

## The AMPK-PDE Axis and Sirtuin Activation

Compounds like resveratrol indirectly activate SIRT1 by manipulating cAMP degradation:
- **PDE4 Inhibition:** Resveratrol competitively inhibits cAMP phosphodiesterases (primarily [[PDE4]]), preventing cAMP breakdown.
- **cAMP Accumulation:** This inhibition leads to a rapid elevation of intracellular cAMP levels.
- **Epac1 and Calcium Release:** High cAMP levels activate **Epac1** (Exchange Protein directly Activated by cAMP). Epac1 induces the release of intracellular Calcium ($Ca^{2+}$) from the endoplasmic reticulum into the cytoplasm.
- **CaMKKβ and AMPK Activation:** The calcium surge activates calmodulin-dependent protein kinase kinase beta (CaMKKβ), which phosphorylates and activates [[AMPK|AMPK]].
- **NAMPT and NAD⁺ Generation:** Activated AMPK upregulates [[NAMPT]], the rate-limiting enzyme in the NAD⁺ salvage pathway, thereby boosting cellular [[NAD+]] levels.
- **SIRT1 Hyperactivation:** The increased NAD⁺ fuels SIRT1, resulting in robust deacetylation of target proteins like [[PGC-1α]] and [[FOXO3a]].

## Connections

- **[[PDE4]]**: The enzyme responsible for degrading cAMP, inhibited by resveratrol.
- **[[AMPK|AMPK]]**: Downstream protein kinase activated by cAMP-mediated calcium signaling.
- **[[NAMPT]]**: Regulated downstream of cAMP-AMPK signaling to control NAD⁺ availability.
- **[[SIRT1]]**: Downstream deacetylase whose activity is boosted by cAMP pathway dynamics.
