# TIMP3

**TIMP3** (Tissue Inhibitor of Metalloproteinases 3) is an endogenous secreted inhibitor of matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs) and members of the ADAM and ADAMTS families. Among the four TIMP family members, TIMP3 is unique in its tight association with the extracellular matrix (ECM) and its broad-spectrum inhibitory activity. TIMP3 is a downstream effector of the [[SIRT1]]–[[NAD+]] axis that defends vascular integrity, positioning it at the interface of sirtuin biology and [[Atherosclerosis|atheroprotection]].

## Structure and Inhibitory Mechanism

TIMP3 is a ~24 kDa secreted protein composed of an N-terminal inhibitory domain and a C-terminal ECM-binding domain. The N-terminal domain inserts into the catalytic cleft of target proteases, forming a 1:1 stoichiometric complex that blocks substrate access. Unlike TIMP1, TIMP2, and TIMP4, TIMP3 is not readily diffusible; it remains tethered to the ECM via interactions with heparan sulfate proteoglycans, localizing its inhibitory activity to the matrix microenvironment. This ECM anchoring allows TIMP3 to fine-tune proteolysis precisely where matrix remodeling occurs.

## Function in Vascular Homeostasis

TIMP3 preserves ECM integrity by restraining excessive proteolysis. It inhibits MMP-2, MMP-9, and ADAM17 (TACE), thereby modulating matrix turnover, cytokine shedding, and cell migration. In the vasculature, TIMP3 protects against pathological remodelling and [[Vascular Inflammation|vascular inflammation]]. By inhibiting ADAM17/TACE, TIMP3 reduces ectodomain shedding of TNF-α receptors and EGFR ligands, blunting inflammatory and proliferative signaling. Reduced TIMP3 tips the MMP/TIMP balance toward proteolysis, promoting plaque instability and monocyte infiltration.

## Regulation by SIRT1

[[SIRT1]] positively regulates TIMP3 expression in endothelial cells and vascular smooth muscle cells. SIRT1 activation (by pharmacological agents or NAD+ repletion) upregulates TIMP3 transcription, suppressing MMP activity and attenuating the inflammatory response. Conversely, SIRT1 downregulation in metabolic disease reduces TIMP3 levels, tipping the MMP/TIMP balance toward ECM degradation and vascular dysfunction. The SIRT1–TIMP3 axis thereby defends vascular integrity, limiting monocyte infiltration, plaque instability, and [[Atherosclerosis|atherosclerotic]] lesion progression. SIRT1 may act indirectly via deacetylation of [[FOXO]] and [[PGC-1α]] transcription factors that drive TIMP3 expression, linking the program to [[Caloric Restriction]] and [[Longevity]].

## Clinical Relevance

TIMP3 promoter hypermethylation and reduced expression are observed in atherosclerotic plaques. TIMP3 polymorphisms associate with cardiovascular risk. Therapeutic strategies that boost SIRT1 activity (e.g., [[Resveratrol]], [[NAD+]] precursors) may restore TIMP3 expression and stabilize vulnerable plaques. TIMP3 is thus a mechanistically attractive biomarker and effector of sirtuin-mediated vascular protection in [[Cardiovascular Disease]] and ageing.
