# TIMP3

**TIMP3** (Tissue Inhibitor of Metalloproteinases 3) is an endogenous secreted inhibitor of matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs) and members of the ADAM and ADAMTS families. Among the four TIMP family members, TIMP3 is unique in its tight association with the extracellular matrix (ECM) and its broad-spectrum inhibitory activity.

## Function in Vascular Homeostasis

TIMP3 preserves ECM integrity by restraining excessive proteolysis. It inhibits MMP-2, MMP-9, and ADAM17 (TACE), thereby modulating matrix turnover, cytokine shedding, and cell migration. In the vasculature, TIMP3 protects against pathological remodelling and [[Vascular Inflammation|vascular inflammation]].

## Regulation by SIRT1

[[SIRT1]] positively regulates TIMP3 expression in endothelial cells and vascular smooth muscle cells. SIRT1 activation (by pharmacological agents or NAD+ repletion) upregulates TIMP3 transcription, suppressing MMP activity and attenuating the inflammatory response. Conversely, SIRT1 downregulation in metabolic disease reduces TIMP3 levels, tipping the MMP/TIMP balance toward ECM degradation and vascular dysfunction. The SIRT1–TIMP3 axis thereby defends vascular integrity, limiting monocyte infiltration, plaque instability, and [[Atherosclerosis|atherosclerotic]] lesion progression.

## Clinical Relevance

TIMP3 promoter hypermethylation and reduced expression are observed in atherosclerotic plaques. TIMP3 polymorphisms associate with cardiovascular risk. Therapeutic strategies that boost SIRT1 activity (e.g., [[Resveratrol]], [[NAD+]] precursors) may restore TIMP3 expression and stabilize vulnerable plaques.
