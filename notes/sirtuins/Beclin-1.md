# Beclin-1

[[Beclin-1]] (encoded by *BECN1*) is a 60 kDa coiled-coil protein and the mammalian ortholog of yeast [[Atg]]6/Vps30. It is the central scaffold of the **class III phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K-III) complex**, which generates PI3P to nucleate autophagosomes and endosomes. As a haploinsufficient [[Tumor Suppressor]], reduced *BECN1* dosage is common in human cancers, linking basal autophagy to oncogenesis.

## Structure & Domains

- **N-terminal BATS domain** — senses membrane curvature and localizes Beclin-1 to ER/mitochondria contact sites where autophagosomes initiate.
- **Central coiled-coil / BH3-like domain** — binds pro-survival [[Bcl-2]] family proteins, which inhibit autophagy by sequestering Beclin-1.
- **C-terminal domain** — mediates assembly with the catalytic subunit [[VPS34]] (Vps34), Vps15, and Atg14L (or UVRAG), defining distinct autophagy-versus-endocytic complexes.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

Beclin-1 initiates autophagosome biogenesis downstream of nutrient and stress sensors:

- **mTORC1 / AMPK axis**: [[AMPK]] phosphorylates Beclin-1 (e.g., Ser91/94) under low energy, promoting autophagy; growth-factor [[mTOR]] signaling opposes this.
- **Bcl-2 / Bcl-xL sequestration**: Displacement of Beclin-1 from anti-apoptotic Bcl-2 relieves the autophagy brake.
- **Sirtuin crosstalk**: Beclin-1 function is tuned by acetylation/deacetylation, positioning it within the [[Sirtuins]] network that governs stress-induced autophagy and [[Aging]]. Deacetylation of autophagic machinery and Beclin-1-associated regulators lowers the threshold for autophagic flux during calorie restriction.
- **Selective autophagy**: Beclin-1 cooperates with [[GABARAPL1]] / [[LC3]] and receptor proteins to target organelles for [[Mitophagy]] and quality control.

> [!info] Haploinsufficiency
> Germline *BECN1* monoallelic loss accelerates tumor development in mice and is enriched in breast, ovarian, and prostate cancers, establishing basal autophagy as a barrier to malignancy.

## Physiological Function

Beclin-1 maintains homeostatic [[Autophagy]] across tissues, supporting protein/organelle turnover, development, immunity (antiviral restriction), and lifespan extension in model organisms when autophagy is upregulated.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Cancer**: Monoallelic *BECN1* deletion and epigenetic silencing reduce autophagic tumor suppression.
- **Neurodegeneration**: Impaired Beclin-1–mediated autophagy contributes to aggregate accumulation in proteinopathies.
- **Therapeutic leverage**: Pharmacologic induction of Beclin-1–complex autophagy (e.g., via [[Sirtuin Activators]], [[Resveratrol]]) is explored for healthy aging and cancer.
