# SRT1720

[[SRT1720]] is a synthetic small-molecule [[STACs|STAC]] (sirtuin activating compound) developed by [[Sirtris Pharmaceuticals]] as a potent [[SIRT1]] activator. It belongs to the imidazothiazole class and is structurally distinct from [[Resveratrol|resveratrol]].

## Pharmacodynamics

- [[SRT1720]] activates [[SIRT1]] with an EC₁.₅ of approximately 0.16 μM in a fluorophore-based assay, though the mechanism of direct activation has been debated (some studies suggest the activation is substrate-dependent or assay artifact).
- It improves [[Insulin Sensitivity]], reduces [[Hepatic Steatosis|hepatic steatosis]], and enhances [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis|mitochondrial biogenesis]] in diet-induced obese mouse models.
- [[SRT1720]] extends lifespan in mice fed a [[High-fat Diet|high-fat diet]], but not in chow-fed mice, suggesting context-dependent efficacy.
