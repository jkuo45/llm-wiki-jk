# ICE Mouse

The **ICE Mouse** (**I**nducible **C**hanges in the **E**pigenome) is a mammalian model developed by the [[David Sinclair|Sinclair lab]] to test the [[Relocalization of Chromatin Modifiers (RCM) Hypothesis]]. It uses a controllable endonuclease, [[I-PpoI]], to induce targeted [[Double-Strand Break|double-strand breaks (DSBs)]] *without* introducing [[Mutations|mutations]], thereby isolating the epigenetic consequences of DNA repair from mutational load.

## Design and Findings

- Induction of DSBs, followed by repair, produces accelerated epigenetic aging and physiological decline (metabolic dysfunction, reduced tissue function, cognitive changes).
- The induced changes shift the [[Epigenetic Clock|epigenetic clock]], consistent with RCM.
- Because no mutations are introduced, the phenotype is attributed to loss of epigenetic information rather than hardware damage — supporting the [[Information Theory of Aging]].
