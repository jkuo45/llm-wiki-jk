# ML385

**ML385** is a selective small-molecule inhibitor of [[NRF2|NRF2 (NF-E2-related factor 2)]]. It binds directly to the [[NRF2]] [[Cap'n'collar|Cap'n'Collar (CNC)]] basic-leucine zipper (bZIP) domain, blocking its heterodimerization with [[Small Maf proteins|small Maf proteins]] and preventing transcriptional activation of [[Antioxidant Response Element|antioxidant response element (ARE)]]-containing genes.

## Mechanism

ML385 interferes with NRF2's DNA-binding activity without affecting NRF2 protein stability or [[Keap1]]-mediated degradation. It selectively inhibits the NRF2 transcriptional program, including induction of [[HO-1|heme oxygenase-1 (HO-1)]], [[NQO1]], and [[Glutathione|glutathione]] synthesis genes, without directly affecting [[NFKB|NF-κB]] or [[AP-1]] signaling.

## Use in Sirtuin Research

ML385 is used as a chemical tool to dissect NRF2-dependent vs. NRF2-independent effects of [[Adrenochrome]] and other redox-active compounds:

- Demonstrating whether [[Adrenochrome]] upregulates [[SIRT6]] via NRF2 binding to the [[Antioxidant Response Element|ARE]] in the SIRT6 promoter.
- Testing whether adrenochrome's suppression of [[CD38]] transcription is mediated by NRF2-dependent antagonism of [[NFKB|NF-κB]].
- Differentiating NRF2-dependent and independent mechanisms in [[Hormesis|hormetic]] preconditioning experiments.
