# UBCS039

**UBCS039** is the first synthetic, selective allosteric activator of **[[SIRT6]]** to be characterized. It is a cell-permeable small molecule that binds the unique elongated hydrophobic pocket of SIRT6, stabilizing its active conformation and enhancing both its deacetylase and deacylase activities.

## Mechanism of Action

- **Allosteric binding**: UBCS039 binds to the distal hydrophobic pocket of SIRT6 — the same elongated pocket that normally accommodates long-chain fatty acyl groups (myristoyl, palmitoyl). Crystal structures confirm this binding mode.
- **Conformational stabilization**: Binding stabilizes the catalytically active conformation of both the NAD⁺ co-substrate and the acetylated/acylated histone substrate, facilitating efficient deacetylation.
- **Histone deacetylation**: Results in increased deacetylation of **H3K9ac** and **H3K56ac** on chromatin, promoting chromatin compaction and DNA repair machinery recruitment.

## Physiological Impacts

- **Tumor suppression**: By activating SIRT6's glycolysis-suppressing function (repressing HIF-1α, SREBP), UBCS039 drives tumor cells toward autophagy and away from the Warburg effect. Demonstrated tumor-suppressive activity in several cancer cell lines.
- **Organ protection**: Evaluated in models of **acute liver failure**, where UBCS039 significantly mitigated oxidative stress and inflammatory damage in a SIRT6-dependent manner.
- **Genomic stability**: Enhanced DNA double-strand break repair via H3K9/H3K56 deacetylation.
