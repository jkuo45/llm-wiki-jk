# Sirtuin Activators

[[Sirtuin Activators]] are compounds and interventions that increase the catalytic efficiency or expression of [[Sirtuins]], recapitulating key metabolic benefits of [[Calorie Restriction]] (when noted) without caloric deficit. The best-studied class is the **sirtuin-activating compounds (STACs)** exemplified by [[Resveratrol]].

## Classes & Mechanisms

- **Polyphenol STACs**: Resveratrol and related [[Stilbenoid]]s (e.g., [[Pterostilbene]]) lower the KM of [[SIRT1]] for its peptide/[[NAD+]] substrates, amplifying deacetylation of [[PGC-1α]], [[p53]], and [[FOXO]].
- **Endogenous / lifestyle activators**: Fasting, exercise, and [[NAD+]] precursors ([[NAD+ Salvage Pathway]]) raise the substrate (NAD+) that sirtuins require.
- **Synthetic STACs**: Engineered small molecules (e.g., SRT compounds) with improved potency and selectivity over natural polyphenols.
- **Indirect activators**: [[AMPK]] agonists (e.g., [[Metformin]] when noted) and [[mTOR]] inhibitors raise NAD+/AMPK tone that favors sirtuin action.

> [!info] NAD+ is the gate
> Sirtuin activators cannot function without sufficient [[NAD+]]; combination with NAD+ precursors is a common strategy to maximize deacetylation output (see [[Deacetylation]]).

## Physiological & Longevity Effects

Activators promote [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis]] (via PGC-1α), [[Autophagy]]/[[Mitophagy]], insulin sensitivity, and stress resistance, and are studied for [[Aging]], metabolic syndrome, and neurodegeneration.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Metabolic disease**: Improved glucose handling and lipid oxidation in preclinical models.
- **Healthy aging**: A leading calorie-restriction-mimetic strategy, though human efficacy of resveratrol remains debated.
- Contrast with inhibitors such as [[Sirtinol]] (SIRT1/2 inhibitor) used experimentally to block sirtuin activity.
