# p300 (EP300)

## Overview
**p300** (also known as **EP300** or E1A binding protein p300) is a highly conserved, ubiquitously expressed transcriptional co-activator and lysine acetyltransferase (KAT) / histone acetyltransferase (HAT). Operating closely with its paralog, **CBP** (CREB-binding protein), p300 plays a fundamental role in eukaryotic gene regulation, chromatin remodeling, cell cycle progression, differentiation, and stress responses. It functions as a molecular scaffold that bridges sequence-specific transcription factors to the basal transcription machinery while epigenetically modifying histones and non-histone proteins via lysine acetylation.

---

## Structure & Domains
The p300 protein is a large, multi-domain macromolecule of approximately 2,414 amino acids. Its structural domains are critical for its scaffolding and catalytic functions:
1. **CH1 and CH3 Domains**: Cysteine/histidine-rich regions that mediate interactions with transcriptional regulators such as hypoxia-inducible factor 1α (HIF-1α) and p53.
2. **KIX Domain**: Interacts with the kinase-inducible domain of CREB and other transcriptional activators.
3. **Bromodomain**: Specifically binds to acetylated lysine residues on histones, promoting transcription propagation and chromatin reading.
4. **HAT Catalytic Domain**: Responsible for the transfer of acetyl groups from [[notes/_link/Acetyl-CoA|Acetyl-CoA]] to the ε-amino group of target lysine residues on histones (primarily H3 and H4) and non-histone substrates.
5. **SID (Steroid Receptor Coactivator-1 Interaction Domain)**: Binds nuclear receptors and associated co-activators.

---

## Regulation by SIRT1
The acetyltransferase activity of p300 is tightly regulated by reversible post-translational modifications, particularly acetylation and deacetylation. 
- **Deacetylation Site**: [[SIRT1]] directly physical associates with and deacetylates p300 at specific lysine residues **Lys1020** and **Lys1024**, which reside within its cell cycle regulatory domain 1 (CRD1).
- **Functional Consequence**: Deacetylation of p300 by SIRT1 represses its intrinsic acetyltransferase activity and reduces its co-activator potential. Because p300 is a limiting transcriptional cofactor in the cell, its inhibition by SIRT1 acts as a major hub to shut down anabolic programs, suppress inflammatory gene expression, and modulate cellular differentiation (such as repressing adipogenesis and muscle differentiation).

## Physiological & Pathological Roles

### Autophagy Suppression
p300 serves as a master negative regulator of [[notes/_link/Autophagy|Autophagy]]. Under nutrient-rich conditions, p300 acetylates multiple core autophagy-related (Atg) proteins (such as Atg5, Atg7, Atg8/LC3, and Atg12), which represses their activity and retains LC3 in the nucleus.
- **Spermidine Inhibition**: The natural polyamine [[notes/_link/Spermidine|Spermidine]] is a potent, selective inhibitor of p300. By blocking p300 HAT activity, spermidine induces robust, epigenetic autophagy, which promotes [[notes/_link/Aging|Longevity]] and cardiovascular health.
- **Deacetylation by SIRT1/SIRT2**: Under starvation or [[notes/_link/Caloric Restriction|Caloric Restriction]], intracellular [[notes/sirtuins/NAD+|NAD⁺]] levels rise, activating SIRT1 and SIRT2. These sirtuins deacetylate the Atg machinery and activate autophagic flux, directly opposing the inhibitory acetylation imposed by p300.

### Cell Survival & Apoptosis
p300 acetylates [[notes/_link/p53|p53]] at multiple C-terminal lysines (including Lys373 and Lys382), stabilizing p53 and enhancing its transcriptional activity to drive cell cycle arrest or [[notes/_link/Apoptosis|Apoptosis]]. In contrast, SIRT1 deacetylates these same residues, suppressing p53-dependent apoptosis and promoting cell survival under genotoxic or oxidative stress.

### Hypertrophy & Inflammation
In cardiac myocytes, p300-mediated acetylation of histones and GATA4 promotes transcription of hypertrophy-associated genes, leading to cardiac enlargement. [[SIRT6]] and SIRT1 protect against cardiac hypertrophy and heart failure by restricting p300 activity, leading to hypoacetylation of targets and suppression of pathological cardiac remodeling.

## Connections & Related Concepts
- **[[SIRT1]]** ↔ **p300**: SIRT1 deacetylates and represses p300 at Lys1020/1024, coupling metabolic status directly to chromatin modification.
- **[[notes/_link/Spermidine|Spermidine]]** ↔ **p300**: Spermidine-mediated inhibition of p300 is a critical, translationally validated mechanism for inducing autophagy and extending lifespan.
- **[[notes/_link/p53|p53]]**: Acetylated by p300 (activating apoptosis) and deacetylated by SIRT1 (promoting survival).
