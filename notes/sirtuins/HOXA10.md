# HOXA10

**HOXA10** (Homeobox A10) is a homeodomain-containing transcription factor belonging to the **HOX gene family**, which encodes master regulators of **cell-type determination and positional identity during embryogenesis**. HOXA10 is critical for hematopoiesis, fertility, and reproductive tract development.

## SIRT2 Interaction

[[SIRT2]] interacts with HOXA10, linking sirtuin-mediated deacetylation to homeobox transcription factor regulation. This interaction may modulate HOXA10's transcriptional activity during development and in adult tissue homeostasis, though the specific functional consequences of SIRT2-mediated regulation of HOXA10 remain an active area of investigation.

## Physiological Roles

HOXA10 is essential for:
- **Hematopoiesis** — regulates self-renewal and differentiation of hematopoietic stem cells
- **Fertility** — required for uterine receptivity and implantation during the menstrual cycle
- **Embryogenesis** — establishes body patterning along the anterior-posterior axis
