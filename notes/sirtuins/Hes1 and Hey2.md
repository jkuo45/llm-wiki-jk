# Hes1 and Hey2

**Hes1** (Hairy and Enhancer of Split 1) and **Hey2** (Hairy-related Transcription Factor 2, also known as HRT2 or HERP1) are **basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) transcriptional repressors** that function as downstream effectors of the **Notch signaling pathway**. They play critical roles in cell fate determination, neurogenesis, cardiovascular development, and maintenance of stem/progenitor cell populations.

## SIRT1 Interaction

[[SIRT1]] physically interacts with both Hes1 and Hey2, modulating their ability to repress target promoters. This interaction places SIRT1 as a regulator of Notch-dependent transcriptional programs. By controlling the repressive activity of these bHLH factors, SIRT1 can influence developmental and differentiation processes, including neuronal and cardiac development.
