# Fucoidan

**Fucoidan** is a sulfated polysaccharide extracted from brown seaweeds such as _Fucus vesiculosus_ (Fucoidan-FV). It has been identified as a [[SIRT6]] activator that elevates SIRT6 protein levels and enhances its mono-[[ADP-ribosylation|ADP-ribosyltransferase (mADPr)]] activity[48,49], mimicking the biochemical effects of the centenarian [[SIRT6]] variants (N308K/A313S).

## Mechanism of Action

Fucoidan-FV activates SIRT6 by increasing SIRT6 protein abundance and enhancing its mADPr activity[48]. Unlike most existing SIRT6 activators that primarily target deacetylase activity, fucoidan preferentially stimulates the ADP-ribosyltransferase function of SIRT6 — an activity profile that aligns with centenarian SIRT6 variants.

## Biological Effects

- **LINE1 Repression**: Fucoidan-FV treatment (50–100 µg/mL) reduces [[LINE-1]] ORF1p and ORF2p protein levels in [[Hutchinson-Gilford Progeria Syndrome|HGPS]] patient-derived [[Fibroblasts|fibroblasts]], mimicking the transposable element suppression conferred by centenarian SIRT6 variants.
- **DNA Repair**: Fucoidans enhance [[SIRT6]]-dependent [[DNA Repair]] and act as senotherapeutics[48].
- **Healthspan & Lifespan**: SIRT6 activator fucoidan extends [[Healthspan]] and [[Longevity|lifespan]] in aged wild-type mice[49].
