# Lys122 (MnSOD/SOD2)

**Lys122** (K122) is an acetylatable lysine residue on **MnSOD** (also called [[SOD2|MnSOD/SOD2]]), the mitochondrial manganese superoxide dismutase that converts damaging superoxide (O₂⁻) into hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂). Together with [[Lys68]], it is one of the principal acetylation sites whose modification state controls MnSOD enzymatic activity.

## Regulation by SIRT3

- **Deacetylation activates MnSOD:** [[SIRT3]], the major mitochondrial deacetylase, directly removes the acetyl group from Lys122 (and [[Lys68]]). Deacetylated MnSOD displays dramatically increased dismutation activity, lowering mitochondrial oxidative stress.
- **Upstream activation:** This deacetylation is potentiated by SIRT3-activating small molecules such as [[Honokiol]] and [[Dihydromyricetin]], and is a canonical readout of SIRT3 function.
- **Functional consequence:** Enhanced MnSOD activity converts superoxide to H₂O₂, which can then diffuse out to activate redox-sensitive transcription factors, linking SIRT3/MnSOD to broader mitochondrial and metabolic signaling.

## SIRT4 Antagonism & the Mitochondrial Sirtuin Balance

Lys122 (with [[Lys68]]) is the shared downstream node of an antagonistic mitochondrial sirtuin pair:
- **[[SIRT3]]** *activates* MnSOD by deacetylating Lys122/Lys68 → accelerated superoxide clearance → reduced [[ROS]] signaling.
- **[[SIRT4]]** *inhibits* MnSOD via mono-[[ADP-ribosylation]] (distinct from SIRT3's deacetylation), sustaining superoxide levels and amplifying ROS signaling; SIRT4 overexpression aggravates [[Angiotensin II|angiotensin II]]-induced [[Cardiac Hypertrophy|cardiac hypertrophy]] by suppressing MnSOD.
- The **[[SIRT3-SIRT4 Ratio|SIRT3/SIRT4 ratio]]** thus acts as a redox rheostat for mitochondrial [[Oxidative Stress|oxidative stress]] and fibrotic susceptibility. A "high" ratio rapidly quenches superoxide (protective); a "low" ratio amplifies it (damage). This ratio defines a **[[Hormetic Window|hormetic window]]** for redox signaling — a concept the sirtuin literature frames as a unidirectional protective mechanism, whereas adrenochrome analyses position it as a tunable, oscillatory signal.

## Coupling to Adrenochrome Redox Cycling

[[Adrenochrome]] undergoes catalytic [[Redox Cycling|redox cycling]] that generates superoxide (O₂⁻) — the exact substrate of SIRT3-activated MnSOD. The two pathways are therefore mechanistically coupled through shared substrate, enzyme, and downstream effectors ([[PGC-1α]], [[FOXO3a]], [[AMPK]], [[NFKB|NF-κB]]): adrenochrome-derived superoxide is processed by MnSOD via the identical SIRT3-gated reaction, and sub-toxic adrenochrome exposure may upregulate SIRT3 through a retrograde ROS→AMPK→PGC-1α→SIRT3 feedback loop (a "[[Redox Vaccination|redox vaccination]]" effect), increasing MnSOD deacetylation at Lys122/Lys68.

## Biomarker Relevance

MnSOD acetylation status at Lys122/Lys68 serves as an indirect surrogate marker for SIRT3 activity in research and translational settings (alongside mitochondrial superoxide probes such as [[MitoSOX]] and urinary 8-OHdG).
