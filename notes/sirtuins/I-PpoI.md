# I-PpoI

**I-PpoI** is a homing endonuclease originating from the chloroplast 23S rRNA intron of the green alga *Chlamydomonas*. It recognizes and cleaves a specific 15–20 bp sequence within the rRNA gene repeat, generating a [[Double-Strand Break|double-strand break (DSB)]]. Because its target site is largely restricted to multicopy rRNA repeats, ectopic expression introduces numerous DSBs without altering protein-coding loci.

## Use in the RCM Hypothesis

In the [[ICE Mouse|ICE mouse]] model, inducible [[I-PpoI]] expression triggers targeted DSBs that are repaired without introducing [[Mutations|mutations]]. This allows the [[David Sinclair|Sinclair lab]] to attribute the resulting epigenetic aging and [[Epigenetic Clock|epigenetic clock]] shifts to the [[Relocalization of Chromatin Modifiers (RCM) Hypothesis]] rather than to mutational damage.
