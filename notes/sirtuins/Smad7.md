# Smad7

**Smad7** (SMAD Family Member 7) is an inhibitory SMAD protein that acts as an antagonist of Transforming Growth Factor-beta (TGF-$\beta$) signaling. It binds physically to the TGF-$\beta$ Type I receptor, preventing downstream pathway activation and thereby regulating cell differentiation, tissue fibrosis, and cell death.

## Regulation of Smad7 Stability by SIRT1

In glomerular mesangial cells, Smad7 stability and TGF-$\beta$-induced apoptotic signaling are regulated post-translationally by [[SIRT1]]:

- **Deacetylation**: [[SIRT1]] physically interacts with Smad7 and deacetylates it on two key conserved lysines: **Lys⁶⁰** and **Lys⁷⁰**.
- **Ubiquitination and Degradation**: Deacetylation of these lysines promotes the recruitment of the E3 ubiquitin ligase **Smurf1** (Smad-ubiquitination regulatory factor 1). This leads to the ubiquitin-dependent proteasomal degradation of Smad7.
- **Renal Protection**: By promoting the degradation of Smad7, SIRT1 blocks TGF-$\beta$-dependent apoptotic signaling, thereby protecting glomerular mesangial cells from apoptosis in various kidney diseases.
