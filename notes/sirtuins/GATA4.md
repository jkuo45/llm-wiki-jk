# GATA Binding Protein 4 (GATA4)

**GATA Binding Protein 4 (GATA4)** is a zinc finger transcription factor belonging to the GATA family that recognizes the GATA motif (WGATAR) in gene promoters. It is essential for [[Cardiac Development]], [[Cardiomyocyte]] differentiation, and the hypertrophic response to pressure overload and neurohumoral stimuli.

## SIRT7 Regulation

[[SIRT7]] directly deacetylates GATA4, ameliorating stress-induced [[Cardiac Hypertrophy]]. SIRT7-mediated deacetylation suppresses GATA4 transcriptional activity at hypertrophic gene promoters, including those for [[ANP]], [[BNP]], and β-[[Myosin Heavy Chain]] (β-MHC). This anti-hypertrophic action positions SIRT7 as a cardioprotective sirtuin that counteracts pathological cardiac remodeling.

## Physiological Role

GATA4 regulates target genes involved in [[Embryogenesis]], cardiac morphogenesis, and the adult stress response. It interacts with [[NKX2-5]], [[TBX5]], and [[MEF2C]] in a cardiac transcriptional network. Hypertrophic stimuli (e.g., [[Angiotensin II]], [[Endothelin-1]]) drive GATA4 acetylation and activation, promoting pathological hypertrophy; SIRT7 opposes this through deacetylation.
