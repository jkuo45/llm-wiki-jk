# Coordinated Lysosomal Expression and Regulation

The [[Coordinated Lysosomal Expression and Regulation|CLEAR]] network is a **master transcriptional program** that drives coordinated expression of lysosomal and autophagy genes. It is orchestrated by the MiT/TFE transcription factors — chiefly [[TFEB]] and TFE3 — and ensures that lysosomal capacity scales with autophagic demand for cellular clearance and [[Aging]] resilience.

## Composition & Regulation

- **TFEB / TFE3**: Bind CLEAR elements (GTCACGTGAC) in promoters of lysosomal hydrolases, v-ATPase, and autophagic genes.
- **mTORC1 control**: Nutrient-rich conditions sequester TFEB in the cytoplasm via [[mTOR]]C1 phosphorylation; starvation or [[Rapamycin]] relieves this, nuclearizing TFEB and inducing CLEAR (PMID 20005848).
- **Sirtuin / energetic coupling**: The CLEAR program is coordinated with [[Sirtuins]]–[[NAD+]] and [[AMPK]] signaling and overlaps the mitochondrial program of [[PGC-1α]], jointly governing organelle turnover.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **Lysosomal biogenesis**: CLEAR upregulates [[Lysosome Biogenesis]], cathepsins, and v-ATPase subunits, expanding degradative capacity.
- **Autophagy coordination**: Couples lysosomal expansion to autophagosome flux (see [[Autophagy]], [[Mitophagy]]), preventing substrate backup.
- **Cross-talk with mitochondria**: TFEB/CLEAR and PGC-1α are co-regulated under stress, linking [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis]] with lysosomal clearance — a "mitochondria–lysosome axis" of healthspan.

> [!tip] Therapeutic node
> Pharmacologic TFEB activation (e.g., via mTOR inhibition, [[Caloric Restriction]] when noted, or [[Sirtuin Activators]]) enhances CLEAR and is pursued for proteinopathies and metabolic disease.

## Physiological Function

CLEAR maintains lysosomal homeostasis, recycles macromolecules during fasting, and supports immune, neuronal, and metabolic tissue function.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Aging**: Diminished TFEB/CLEAR activity contributes to lysosomal storage and aggregation diseases.
- **Neurodegeneration & cancer**: CLEAR modulation is investigated for clearance of toxic aggregates and for tumor metabolic vulnerabilities.
