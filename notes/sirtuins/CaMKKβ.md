# Calcium/Calmodulin-Dependent Protein Kinase Kinase 2 (CaMKKβ/CAMKK2)

**Calcium/Calmodulin-Dependent Protein Kinase Kinase 2 (CaMKKβ/CAMKK2)** is a serine/threonine kinase that phosphorylates and activates downstream kinases including [[AMPK]], [[CaMKI]], and [[CaMKIV]]. It is exquisitely sensitive to cytoplasmic [[Ca2+]] signals, functioning as a Ca2+ sensor and signal amplifier in the cytosol.

## Activation Mechanism

Cytoplasmic [[Ca2+]] surge — triggered by [[Epac1]]-mediated release from the [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]] — binds [[Calmodulin]] (CaM), inducing a conformational change that activates CaMKKβ. Activated CaMKKβ phosphorylates [[AMPK]] at Thr172 within its activation loop, potently stimulating AMPK kinase activity. This represents a Ca2+-dependent, [[AMP]]-independent AMPK activation pathway.

## Role in Resveratrol Signaling

CaMKKβ is the critical link in the resveratrol signaling cascade: [[Resveratrol]] inhibits [[PDE4]] → ↑[[cAMP]] → [[Epac1]] → Ca2+ release → CaMKKβ activation → [[AMPK]] phosphorylation → [[NAMPT]] upregulation → ↑[[NAD+]] → [[SIRT1]] activation. This pathway explains how resveratrol activates AMPK and SIRT1 independently of changes in adenine nucleotide ratios.

## Physiological Functions

CaMKKβ is highly expressed in the [[Brain]], where it regulates [[Appetite]], [[Energy Expenditure]], and [[Neuronal Plasticity]]. In peripheral tissues, it mediates Ca2+-dependent metabolic responses to [[Exercise]] and [[Hormesis]]. Dysregulation of CaMKKβ is implicated in [[Obesity]], [[Type 2 Diabetes]], and [[Cancer]].

## Connections
