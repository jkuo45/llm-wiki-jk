# cAMP Response Element Binding Protein (CREB)

**cAMP Response Element Binding Protein (CREB/CREB1)** is a 43 kDa basic leucine zipper (bZIP) transcription factor that binds cAMP response elements (CRE) in the promoters of target genes. It integrates signals from [[cAMP]], [[Ca2+]]/calmodulin, and growth factor pathways to regulate [[Gene Expression]] in metabolism, neuroplasticity, and survival.

## SIRT1 Regulation

[[SIRT1]] directly deacetylates CREB at Lys136. Deacetylation at this site modulates CREB's transcriptional activity, particularly in the context of hepatic [[Lipid Metabolism]]. SIRT1-mediated CREB deacetylation influences gluconeogenic and lipogenic gene programs, linking cellular [[NAD+]] status to energy homeostasis.

## Signaling Pathways

CREB is activated by phosphorylation at Ser133 through multiple kinases including [[PKA]] (downstream of cAMP), [[CaMKIV]] (downstream of Ca2+), and [[RSK]] (downstream of [[ERK]]/MAPK). The [[cAMP]]→[[PKA]]→CREB axis is central to [[Resveratrol]] action: resveratrol inhibits [[PDE4]], elevating cAMP, activating PKA, and driving CREB-dependent transcription including [[BDNF]] expression in neurons.

## Connections
