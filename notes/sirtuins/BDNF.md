# BDNF

[[BDNF]] (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor) is a member of the [[Neurotrophin|neurotrophin]] family of growth factors that supports neuronal survival, differentiation, synaptic plasticity, and [[Neurogenesis|neurogenesis]]. It is expressed throughout the [[Brain|brain]], particularly in the [[Hippocampus|hippocampus]] and [[Cortex|cortex]], and is critical for learning, memory, and mood regulation.

## Sirtuin Connections

- [[SIRT1]] directly regulates [[BDNF]] transcription by deacetylating and repressing [[Hes1 and Hey2|Hes1]] (a transcriptional repressor of BDNF), thereby promoting neuronal survival and [[Neurogenesis|neurogenesis]].
- [[SIRT1]] also activates [[PGC-1α]], which co-activates the [[BDNF]] promoter, linking [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis|mitochondrial biogenesis]] to neurotrophic support.
- [[Exercise]] increases both [[BDNF]] and [[NAD+]] levels, and SIRT1 may mediate exercise-induced BDNF upregulation.
- [[Caloric Restriction]] and [[Intermittent Fasting]] elevate [[BDNF]] through SIRT1/FoxO-dependent pathways, contributing to cognitive resilience.

## Connections

- [[Depression]]: Reduced BDNF levels are implicated in depressive disorders; SIRT1 activation may have antidepressant effects.
- [[Neurodegeneration]]: BDNF loss is a feature of [[Alzheimer's Disease]], [[Parkinson's Disease]], and [[Huntington's Disease]]; SIRT1-mediated BDNF induction is neuroprotective.
- [[CREB]]: A transcription factor critical for BDNF expression.
- [[TRPM2]]: BDNF signaling can modulate TRPM2 channel activity, affecting Ca2+ homeostasis.
