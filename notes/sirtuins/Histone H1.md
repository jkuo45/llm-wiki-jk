# Histone H1

**Histone H1** (Linker Histone H1) is a family of basic nuclear proteins that bind to linker DNA between nucleosomes, facilitating higher-order chromatin structure and compaction. Unlike core histones (H2A, H2B, H3, H4), H1 sits outside the nucleosomal core, stabilizing the 30-nm fiber and limiting DNA accessibility.

## SIRT1 Deacetylation

[[SIRT1]] deacetylates histone H1 at **Lys9** and **Lys26**. Deacetylation of these residues modulates chromatin compaction and gene expression by altering H1's electrostatic interaction with DNA. H1 acetylation generally correlates with a more open, transcriptionally permissive state, while SIRT1-mediated deacetylation promotes chromatin condensation and transcriptional repression.
