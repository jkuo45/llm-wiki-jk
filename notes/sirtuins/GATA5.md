# GATA5

**GATA5** (GATA Binding Protein 5) is a member of the GATA family of zinc-finger transcription factors (GATA1–6) that bind the consensus motif (A/T)GATA(A/G). GATA5 is expressed predominantly in the cardiovascular system, lung, and gastrointestinal tract, where it governs cell-type-specific gene expression during development and adult homeostasis.

## Role in Cardiovascular Development

GATA5 is essential for cardiac morphogenesis and valvulogenesis. It cooperates with [[GATA4]] and [[GATA6]] to regulate genes critical for cardiomyocyte differentiation, septation, and outflow tract development. In zebrafish, gata5 mutations cause cardiac bifida; in humans, GATA5 variants associate with congenital heart defects (atrial septal defects, bicuspid aortic valve).

## Regulation by SIRT6

[[SIRT6]] promotes GATA5 expression at the transcriptional level, thereby protecting the endothelium from injury. SIRT6-dependent upregulation of GATA5 induces a transcriptional programme that suppresses endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition ([[EndMT|EndMT]]), reduces oxidative stress, and preserves vascular barrier integrity. In models of [[Vascular Endothelial Injury|vascular endothelial injury]] (e.g., atherosclerosis, hypertension), SIRT6 loss leads to GATA5 downregulation, exacerbated endothelial dysfunction, and accelerated lesion formation.

## Clinical Relevance

GATA5 is a risk locus for coronary artery disease and bicuspid aortic valve. SIRT6 activation to maintain GATA5 levels represents a strategy to counter endothelial injury and cardiovascular disease progression.
