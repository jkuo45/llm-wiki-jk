# Protein Tyrosine Phosphatase 1B (PTP1B/PTPN1)

**Protein Tyrosine Phosphatase 1B (PTP1B/PTPN1)** is a 50 kDa non-receptor tyrosine phosphatase that dephosphorylates and inactivates the [[Insulin Receptor]] (IR) and [[Insulin Receptor Substrate]] (IRS) proteins, serving as a major negative regulator of [[Insulin Signaling]].

## SIRT1 Regulation

[[SIRT1]] downregulates PTP1B expression and activity, thereby increasing [[Insulin Sensitivity]] and promoting glucose homeostasis. SIRT1-mediated repression of PTP1B occurs through transcriptional mechanisms involving [[FOXO]] factors and [[PGC-1α]], linking [[NAD+]]-dependent deacetylase activity to insulin signaling enhancement.

## Physiological Role

PTP1B is a validated therapeutic target for [[Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus]] and [[Obesity]]. Knockout of PTP1B in mice enhances insulin sensitivity, improves glucose tolerance, and confers resistance to diet-induced obesity. PTP1B also regulates [[Leptin Signaling]] by dephosphorylating JAK2, making it a dual regulator of both insulin and leptin action.
