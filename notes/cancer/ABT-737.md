# ABT-737

**ABT-737** is a small molecule BH3 mimetic developed by Abbott Laboratories. It is a potent inhibitor of several anti-apoptotic proteins of the [[Bcl-2 family]].

## Mechanism
ABT-737 mimics the BH3 domain of pro-apoptotic proteins like [[Bad]]. It binds with high affinity to the hydrophobic grooves of [[Bcl-2]], [[Bcl-xL]], and [[Bcl-w]], but not [[Mcl-1]]. By occupying these grooves, it prevents the anti-apoptotic proteins from sequestering pro-apoptotic effectors like [[Bax]] and [[Bak]].

## Anticancer Activity
- **Cytotoxicity**: Exhibits potent cytotoxicity in lymphoma and [[non-small-cell lung cancer]] cell lines.
- **Regression**: Has been shown to cause regression of established tumors in animal models.
- **Synergy**: Often used in combination with other chemotherapy drugs to sensitize resistant tumor cells.
