# Leukemia

**Leukemia** is a group of blood cancers that usually begin in the bone marrow and result in high numbers of abnormal blood cells.

## Research with Melittin
Both [[Honeybee venom|honeybee venom]] and [[melittin]] have demonstrated cytotoxic potency in leukemia cells. Additionally, bumblebee venom has been shown to induce apoptosis in human chronic myelogenous leukemia cells via inhibition of [[Akt]] phosphorylation.

## Connections
- [[melittin]]: Active against leukemia cells.
- [[Akt]]: A signaling pathway targeted by bee venom components in leukemia.
