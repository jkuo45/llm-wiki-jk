# GlaxoSmithKline

GlaxoSmithKline (GSK) is a British multinational pharmaceutical company headquartered in London, formed in 2000 through the merger of Glaxo Wellcome and SmithKline Beecham. GSK develops and markets medicines, vaccines, and consumer health products across oncology, infectious disease, respiratory, and immunology. Within the cancer knowledge base, GSK is relevant both for its oncology portfolio and for its philanthropic contributions to neglected tropical disease control.

## Oncology Portfolio

GSK's oncology pipeline includes antibody–drug conjugates, [[Biomarker]]-driven targeted therapies, and immunotherapeutics. Notably, GSK markets [[belantamab mafodotin]] (a BCMA-directed ADC) for multiple myeloma and has pursued [[PARP inhibitor]] and [[BCMA]] programs. Its vaccine arm produces HPV ([[Cervarix]]/collaborative) and other cancer-preventive vaccines, contributing to [[Cancer]] prevention globally.

## Philanthropy & Lymphatic Filariasis

GSK is a founding donor to the [[Global Programme to Eliminate Lymphatic Filariasis]] (GPELF), donating [[Albendazole]] free of charge for mass drug administration. Together with [[Merck & Co. Inc]] (donor of [[Ivermectin]]) and the [[World Health Organization]], GSK's albendazole supply has enabled the distribution of billions of doses, advancing the goal of interrupting transmission of *Wuchereria bancrofti* and related filarial parasites. This public–private partnership model illustrates how pharmaceutical manufacturers support global health alongside commercial [[Chemotherapy]] and targeted-drug development.

## Pathological & Clinical Relevance

While GSK is an organization rather than a pathological entity, its products intersect the cancer domain through (1) direct therapeutics (targeted and immune-based [[Medical Treatment]]), (2) prophylactic vaccines that reduce cancer incidence (HPV, hepatitis B), and (3) co-administered anthelmintics (albendazole + ivermectin) that, in cancer research, have drawn interest for their repurposed antiproliferative activities. GSK's research also illuminates targets such as [[BCMA]] and [[PARP]] that are central to modern precision oncology.
