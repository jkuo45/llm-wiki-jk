# Gastric Cancer

Gastric cancer (stomach cancer) is a malignant tumor of the gastric epithelium and remains one of the leading causes of cancer mortality worldwide, particularly in East Asia, Eastern Europe, and parts of Latin America. The vast majority are adenocarcinomas arising from chronic inflammation of the gastric mucosa.

## Etiology & Pathophysiology

The dominant etiological factors are infection with *Helicobacter pylori*, dietary carcinogens (high salt, smoked foods, low fruit/vegetable intake), smoking, and hereditary syndromes (e.g., CDH1 germline mutations causing diffuse [[Hereditary Diffuse Gastric Cancer]]). A metaplasia–dysplasia–carcinoma sequence driven by [[Inflammation]] and oxidative DNA damage underlies intestinal-type tumors. Molecularly, gastric cancer is heterogeneous, encompassing EBV-positive, microsatellite-instable, genomically stable, and chromosomal-instable subtypes (TCGA classification). Key drivers include [[HER2]] amplification, [[MET]] alterations, [[TP53]] loss, and activation of the [[Wnt]]/β-catenin and [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]] pathways.

## Clinical Presentation & Biomarkers

Early disease is often asymptomatic; advanced symptoms include dyspepsia, weight loss, early satiety, and anemia. Diagnosis relies on endoscopy with biopsy and [[Helicobacter pylori]] testing. Biomarkers guiding therapy include HER2 (for [[trastuzumab]]), PD-L1 (for immune checkpoint inhibitors), and MSI status. [[Circulating tumor DNA]] and serum pepsinogen are investigational tools.

## Therapeutic Landscape

Curative resection with lymphadenectomy is the mainstay for localized disease; perioperative [[Chemotherapy]] and chemoradiotherapy improve outcomes. Targeted agents include trastuzumab (HER2+), ramucirumab (anti-[[VEGF]] receptor 2), and pembrolizumab in MSI-high/PD-L1+ tumors. [[Ivermectin]] has demonstrated inhibitory effects on gastric cancer proliferation in preclinical models, where its activity depends on [[YAP1]] expression — cells with high YAP1 are more sensitive. Repurposing ivermectin, possibly with existing targeted drugs, is an active investigational avenue.
