# Gastric Cancer

Gastric cancer (stomach cancer) is one of the most common malignant tumors worldwide.

## Role of Ivermectin
[[Ivermectin]] has demonstrated inhibitory effects on gastric cancer proliferation.

### Mechanisms
- **YAP1 Inhibition:** The inhibitory effect of ivermectin in gastric cancer depends on the expression of [[YAP1]]. Cells with high YAP1 expression are more sensitive to ivermectin treatment.

## Connections
- [[Ivermectin]]: Inhibits proliferation in sensitive gastric cancer cell lines.
- [[YAP1]]: Key protein determining sensitivity to ivermectin in gastric cancer.
