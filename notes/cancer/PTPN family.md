# PTPN family
The **PTPN family** (Protein Tyrosine Phosphatase Non-receptor type) includes members such as [[PTPN2]], [[PTPN5]], and [[PTPN23]] that act as phosphatases regulating apoptotic regulators. They have been identified as being required for the dephosphorylation and inactivation of [[Bak]] at Tyr108 in fibrosarcoma and [[Colon Cancer]] cells.
