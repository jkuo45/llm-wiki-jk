# Puma

**Puma** (p53 upregulated modulator of apoptosis), also known as BBC3, is a potent pro-apoptotic BH3-only member of the [[Bcl-2 family]].

## Mechanism
Puma is a major downstream target of the [[p53]] tumor suppressor protein. Upon DNA damage or other cellular stresses, p53 induces the expression of Puma. Puma then localizes to the mitochondria where it neutralizes anti-apoptotic proteins (like [[Bcl-2]] and [[Bcl-xL]]) and potentially activates [[Bax]] and [[Bak]] to trigger the [[Intrinsic Pathway]].

## Role in Cancer
Deficiency in Puma or the p53 pathway leads to evasion of [[Apoptosis]] and promotes carcinogenesis. Restoring Puma expression is a potential therapeutic strategy.
