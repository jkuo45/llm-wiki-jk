# Prostate Cancer

Prostate cancer is a malignant tumor derived from prostate epithelial cells.

## Role of Ivermectin
[[Ivermectin]] has been explored for its potential to enhance endocrine therapy and overcome drug resistance in prostate cancer.

### Mechanisms
- **Synergy with Enzalutamide:** Ivermectin enhances the activity of the anti-androgen drug [[enzalutamide]] in LNCaP cells.
- **Reversal of Docetaxel Resistance:** Ivermectin reverses the resistance of PC3 cells to [[docetaxel]].
- **HSP27 Inhibition:** Ivermectin inhibits [[HSP27]] phosphorylation, which is associated with drug resistance and poor prognosis.

## Connections
- [[Ivermectin]]: Potential adjunct for prostate cancer treatment.

- [[enzalutamide]]: An anti-androgen drug whose activity is enhanced by ivermectin.
