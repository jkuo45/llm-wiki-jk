# Prostate Cancer

Prostate cancer is a malignant neoplasm derived from the epithelial cells of the prostate gland and is the most common non-cutaneous cancer in men. Its growth is initially driven by androgen receptor (AR) signaling, and disease management hinges on suppressing androgen action. Progression to castration-resistant prostate cancer (CRPC) and [[Metastasis]]—most often to bone—underlies lethal outcomes and reflects several of the [[Hallmarks of Cancer]].

## Etiology & Pathophysiology

Risk factors include age, family history, germline variants in [[BRCA1]]/[[BRCA2]] and other DNA-repair genes, and androgen exposure. On a molecular level, early disease depends on AR transcriptional activity; later stages sustain signaling through AR amplification, splice variants (e.g., AR-V7), intratumoral androgen synthesis, and bypass pathways involving [[PI3K]]/[[AKT]] and [[MAPK]]. Genomic instability and [[Inflammation]] within the tumor microenvironment further promote evolution to CRPC and [[EMT]]-associated invasion.

## Clinical Presentation & Biomarkers

Prostate cancer is often asymptomatic early; presentation may include urinary obstruction or, in metastatic disease, bone pain and fracture. Diagnosis relies on serum [[PSA (Prostate-Specific Antigen)]] measurement and biopsy. Risk stratification uses Gleason grade, stage, and PSA. Emerging biomarkers include AR-V7, circulating tumor cells, and homologous-recombination deficiency status to guide therapy selection.

## Therapeutic Landscape

Standard care includes active surveillance for low-risk disease, radical prostatectomy or radiation for localized disease, and androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) for advanced disease. CRPC is treated with next-generation anti-androgens such as [[Enzalutamide]], abiraterone, taxanes such as [[docetaxel]], and PARP inhibitors in DNA-repair-deficient tumors. Notably, [[Ivermectin]] has been explored preclinically to enhance endocrine therapy and overcome resistance—synergizing with [[Enzalutamide]] in LNCaP cells, reversing [[docetaxel]] resistance in PC3 cells, and inhibiting [[HSP27]] phosphorylation associated with resistance and poor prognosis.
