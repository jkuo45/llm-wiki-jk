# CYP2J2

[[CYP2J2]] (Cytochrome P450 Family 2 Subfamily J Member 2) is an enzyme that in humans is encoded by the [[CYP2J2]] gene. It is primarily expressed in the heart but also found in the liver and other tissues.

## Function
- **Arachidonic Acid Metabolism:** It is a major enzyme responsible for metabolizing [[Arachidonic acid]] to [[epoxyeicosatrienoic acids]] (EETs), which have vasodilatory and anti-inflammatory properties.
- **Drug Metabolism:** CYP2J2 is involved in the metabolism of several drugs, including the benzimidazole anthelmintics [[Albendazole]] and [[Fenbendazole]] in the human liver.
