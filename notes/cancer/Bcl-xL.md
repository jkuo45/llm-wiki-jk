# Bcl-xL

**Bcl-xL** (B-cell lymphoma-extra large) is a potent anti-apoptotic member of the [[Bcl-2 family]]. It is a major regulator of the [[Intrinsic Pathway]] of [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]].

## Role in Cancer
- **Overexpression**: Bcl-xL is frequently overexpressed in various cancers, including [[leukemia]], [[notes/_link/Breast Cancer|breast cancer]], and [[non-small-cell lung cancer]].
- **Resistance**: High levels of Bcl-xL contribute to resistance against chemotherapy and radiotherapy by sequestering pro-apoptotic proteins like [[Bax]] and [[Bak]].
- **Drug Target**: It is a primary target of BH3 mimetics such as [[ABT-737]] and [[ABT-263]].

## Side Effects of Targeting
Inhibition of Bcl-xL (e.g., by [[ABT-263]]) causes thrombocytopenia (low platelet count) because Bcl-xL is essential for the survival of mature platelets.

## Connections
- [[Bcl-2 family]]: A key anti-apoptotic member.
- [[ABT-737]]: A small molecule inhibitor of Bcl-xL.
- [[ABT-263]]: Another inhibitor; causes thrombocytopenia.
