# Phospholipase A2

**Phospholipase A2** (PLA2) is an enzyme that releases fatty acids from the second carbon group of glycerol.

## Role in Venom
- **Honeybee Venom**: Contains PLA2, though the majority of antineoplastic activity is attributed to [[melittin]].
- **Bumblebee Venom**: Contains secretory phospholipase A2 that can induce apoptosis by inhibiting [[Akt]] phosphorylation in certain leukemia cells.

## Connections
- [[Honeybee]]: Its venom contains PLA2.
- [[melittin]]: Often co-occurs with PLA2 in bee venom.
- [[Akt]]: A signaling molecule inhibited by certain secretory PLA2s.
