# SV40

**Simian Virus 40** (SV40) is a polyomavirus that is found in both monkeys and humans.

## Role in Research
- **SV40 Large T Antigen**: A positively charged sequence (K21KKRKV26) from the SV40 large T antigen possesses cell-penetrating capacity.
- **Melittin Study**: This sequence was used to rescue the anticancer activity of a negatively charged [[melittin]] mutant (DEDE-melittin).

## Connections
- [[melittin]]: Engineered with SV40 sequences to study membrane interaction.
