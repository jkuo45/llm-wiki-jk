# Mebendazole

[[Mebendazole]] is a [[Benzimidazole]] medication used to treat a number of parasitic worm infestations, including ascariasis, pinworm disease, hookworm infections, guinea worm infections, hydatid disease, and giardia.

## Anticancer Research
Mebendazole is being widely studied for [[Drug Repositioning]] in [[Cancer]]. It has shown promise in several ways:
- **Microtubule Inhibition:** Like other benzimidazoles, it interferes with [[Microtubule]] formation by binding to [[Tubulin]].
- **Pathway Modulation:** It has been shown to inhibit various kinases and modulate pathways such as [[Hedgehog signaling pathway|Hedgehog]], [[Wnt]], and [[NFκB]].
- **Clinical Trials:** Several clinical trials have investigated its use in patients with [[glioblastoma]] and other solid tumors.
