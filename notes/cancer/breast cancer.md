# Breast Cancer

**Breast cancer** is a type of cancer that develops from breast tissue. It is the most common cancer in women worldwide.

## Subtypes
- **Luminal**: Often ER-positive.
- **[[HER2-enriched breast cancer|HER2-enriched]]**: Overexpresses [[HER2]].
- **[[triple-negative breast cancer|Triple-negative]]**: Lacks ER, PR, and HER2 expression; most aggressive subtype.

## Research with Melittin and Ivermectin
- [[melittin]] and [[Honeybee venom|honeybee venom]] have shown high selectivity and potency against aggressive breast cancer subtypes like TNBC and HER2-enriched.
- [[Ivermectin]] inhibits the proliferation of breast cancer cell lines (e.g., MCF-7, MDA-MB-231) by blocking the [[Akt]]/[[mTOR]] pathway and acting as a [[PAK1]] inhibitor. It also induces [[pyroptosis]] and targets [[Cancer Stem Cells]].

## Connections
- [[triple-negative breast cancer]]: An aggressive subtype sensitive to both melittin and ivermectin.
- [[Ivermectin]]: Induces autophagy and targets CSCs in breast cancer.
- [[melittin]]: Selective anticancer activity via receptor inhibition.
