# PTPN2

**PTPN2** (Protein Tyrosine Phosphatase Non-Receptor Type 2), also known as T-cell protein tyrosine phosphatase (TC-PTP), is a member of the [[PTPN family]] that negatively regulates various signaling pathways, including those involving [[JAK2]] and [[STAT3]].
