# ERK

Extracellular signal-regulated kinases (ERK) are protein kinases in the MAPK pathway that generally promote cell survival and proliferation by inhibiting pro-apoptotic proteins like [[Bim]] and stabilizing anti-apoptotic proteins like [[Mcl-1]].
