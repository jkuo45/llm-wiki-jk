# Cholangiocarcinoma

Cholangiocarcinoma is a malignant tumor that originates in the bile duct, inside or outside the liver.

## Role of Ivermectin
[[Ivermectin]] has been shown to exhibit potent anticancer activity against cholangiocarcinoma.

### Mechanisms
- **Inhibition of Proliferation:** Ivermectin inhibits the proliferation of cholangiocarcinoma cells (e.g., KKU214) in a dose- and time-dependent manner.
- **Cell Cycle Arrest:** Halts the cell cycle in S phase and promotes [[Apoptosis]].
- **Overcoming Resistance:** Gemcitabine-resistant cholangiocarcinoma cells show high sensitivity to ivermectin.

## Connections
- [[Ivermectin]]: Potential therapeutic agent for cholangiocarcinoma, especially in drug-resistant cases.
- [[Apoptosis]]: Induced by ivermectin in bile duct cancer cells.
