# TAT

**TAT** (Transactivator of transcription) is a protein encoded by the *tat* gene in [[HIV-1]].

## Role in Research
- **Cell-Penetrating Peptide**: The TAT sequence is a well-known cell-penetrating peptide.
- **Melittin Study**: Grafting a positively charged TAT sequence to the C-terminus of a [[melittin]] mutant (DEDE-melittin) restored its anticancer activity.

## Connections
- [[HIV-1]]: The source of the TAT protein.
- [[melittin]]: Engineered with TAT to restore membrane binding and activity.
