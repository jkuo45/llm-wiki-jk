# Glioblastoma

**Glioblastoma** (GBM), also called glioblastoma multiforme, is the most aggressive primary malignant tumor of the central nervous system, classified as WHO grade 4 glioma. It arises from astrocytes or glial progenitor cells and is characterized by rapid growth, diffuse infiltration, microvascular proliferation, and necrosis. GBM carries a poor prognosis, with median survival of ~12–15 months despite maximal therapy.

## Etiology & Pathophysiology

GBM is driven by dysregulation of receptor tyrosine kinases (notably [[EGFR]] amplification/mutation, [[PDGFRA]], [[MET]]), loss of [[PTEN]] (activating [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]]/[[mTOR]]), and alterations in [[p53]] and [[RB1]] pathways. The proneural, classical, mesenchymal, and neural transcriptomic subtypes reflect distinct biology. A hallmark is the formation of a aberrant, leaky vasculature via [[VEGF]]-driven [[Angiogenesis]], and an immunosuppressive, hypoxic [[Tumor Microenvironment]] that fosters [[Chemotherapy]] resistance and [[Metastasis]]-like local invasion along white-matter tracts.

## Clinical Presentation & Biomarkers

Symptoms include headaches, seizures, cognitive decline, and focal deficits; diagnosis uses [[MRI]] with contrast and histologic/IDH and MGMT-promoter-methylation profiling. [[MGMT]] methylation predicts response to temozolomide, and EGFRvIII is an investigational vaccine target. Liquid biopsy via [[Circulating tumor DNA]]/ctDNA is emerging.

## Therapeutic Landscape

Standard care is maximal safe resection plus radiotherapy and temozolomide, with tumor-treating fields as adjunct. Bevacizumab (anti-VEGF) treats edema/recurrence. Preclinically, [[Ivermectin]] inhibits proliferation of GBM cells (U87, T98G, U251) in a dose-dependent manner, inducing caspase-dependent [[Apoptosis]] and [[Autophagy]] via the [[Akt]]/[[mTOR]] pathway and suppressing [[Angiogenesis]]. [[melittin]] and [[Honeybee venom|honeybee venom]] also show antitumoral activity in GBM models. These repurposed agents are being studied for sensitization and recurrence control.
