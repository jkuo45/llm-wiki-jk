# JNK2
[[JNK2]] (c-Jun N-terminal kinase 2) is a member of the [[MAPK]] family that phosphorylates [[Bcl-xL]] at Ser62 during cell cycle (G2) arrest, contributing to its inactivation in [[cervical cancer]] cells.
