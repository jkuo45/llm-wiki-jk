# SCF Complex

The SCF complex (Skp1–Cul1–F-box protein, with the RING subunit Rbx1) is a major multisubunit E3 ubiquitin ligase of the [[Ubiquitin-Proteasome System]]. It recognizes substrates primed by phosphorylation and catalyzes their polyubiquitination and proteasomal degradation. By controlling the half-life of short-lived regulators, the SCF complex shapes the [[Cell Cycle]], signal transduction, and apoptosis. Key apoptosis-relevant substrates include [[Mcl-1]] and [[Bim]].

## Structure & Mechanism

The SCF scaffold comprises Skp1 bridged to Cul1, which binds the RING finger protein Rbx1; an F-box protein (e.g., β-TrCP, Fbw7, Skp2) attaches via Skp1 and provides substrate specificity through its substrate-recognition domain. Substrates must first be phosphorylated (often by [[GSK3β]], [[CKI]], or [[Plk]]) at degron motifs, enabling F-box binding. The E2~Ub conjugate is recruited by Rbx1, transferring ubiquitin to the substrate lysine. Degradation of [[Mcl-1]] (an anti-apoptotic Bcl-2 protein) and [[Bim]] (a pro-apoptotic BH3-only protein) illustrates context-dependent tuning of the death threshold.

## Physiological & Pathological Function

Physiologically, SCF complexes enforce cell-cycle checkpoints (degrading cyclins and CDK inhibitors) and terminate signaling. In [[Cancer]], altered F-box expression (e.g., loss of Fbw7 tumor suppressor, overexpression of Skp2) disrupts substrate turnover, stabilizing oncoproteins and degrading tumor suppressors—hallmarks of the [[Hallmarks of Cancer]]. SCF-mediated degradation of [[Mcl-1]] can either sensitize (when Mcl-1 is lost) or, via feedback, affect [[Apoptosis]] commitment depending on competing Bcl-2 family dynamics.

## Clinical & Research Relevance

SCF subunits are biomarkers and therapeutic targets. Small-molecule disruptors of F-box–substrate interactions and immunomodulatory drugs (lenalidomide) that hijack CRL4 (a related Cullin-RING ligase) demonstrate the tractability of targeting ubiquitin ligases. Inducing degradation of [[Mcl-1]] via SCF activation is a strategy to overcome resistance to [[Chemotherapy]] and BH3 mimetics.
