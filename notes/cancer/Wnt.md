# Wnt

The Wnt signaling pathway is a group of signal transduction pathways which begin with proteins that pass signals into a cell through cell surface receptors.

## Role in Cancer
Wnt signaling is crucial for regulating cell growth, differentiation, and development. Dysregulation of the Wnt/β-catenin pathway is a common feature in many cancers, including [[breast cancer]] and [[colorectal cancer]].

## Therapeutic Targeting
- [[Ivermectin]]: Inhibits the Wnt/β-catenin pathway in breast and colorectal cancer cell lines. It blocks the interaction between β-catenin and TCF transcription factors, leading to reduced expression of downstream genes like AXIN2 and LGR5.

## Connections
- [[Ivermectin]]: Acts as a Wnt pathway inhibitor.
- [[PAK1]]: Can facilitate Wnt/β-catenin signaling.
