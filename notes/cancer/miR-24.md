# miR-24

**miR-24** is a microRNA family member (miR-24-1, miR-24-2 loci) with context-dependent roles in development and disease. In cancer, miR-24 is often described as oncogenic because it represses the expression of pro-death regulators—including by modulating [[XIAP]] networks—thereby lowering the [[Apoptosis]] threshold and supporting tumor cell survival and [[Chemotherapy]] resistance. Its precise target set varies by tissue, contributing to its pleiotropic effects.

## Biogenesis & Regulation

miR-24 is transcribed as part of polycistronic clusters (e.g., miR-23b~27b~24-1), processed by Drosha/Dicer into the mature guide strand incorporated into RISC. Its expression is regulated by transcription factors responsive to proliferation and stress signals; in many tumors it is upregulated, where it reinforces survival and proliferative gene programs.

## Mechanism of Action

A key anti-apoptotic action of miR-24 is repression of factors that normally restrain XIAP or promote death. By suppressing these checkpoints, miR-24 elevates effective XIAP activity and reduces caspase engagement, lowering the apoptotic threshold. (Relatedly, [[IKKε]] phosphorylates and degrades XIAP to sensitize cells, illustrating the opposite regulatory direction.) miR-24 also targets additional tumor-relevant transcripts (e.g., p27^Kip1^, MKi67-linked proliferation factors, and death receptors in some contexts), coupling survival to cell-cycle progression.

## Physiological & Pathological Relevance

In malignancies such as breast, colorectal, and hematopoietic cancers, high miR-24 correlates with reduced apoptosis, enhanced proliferation, and poorer response to cytotoxic therapy. Conversely, inhibition of miR-24 (antagomirs) can restore death sensitivity. Its dual nature—pro-survival in cancer but pro-apoptotic in some normal stress contexts—underscores the importance of cellular context.

## Clinical & Research Relevance

miR-24 is investigated both as a prognostic biomarker and as a therapeutic target (antagomirs) to resensitize resistant tumors. Because it converges on the XIAP/apoptosis-threshold axis, combining miR-24 inhibition with XIAP antagonists or death-ligand therapies is a rational strategy under exploration to overcome resistance.
