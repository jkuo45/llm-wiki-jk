# YAP1

YAP1 (Yes-associated protein 1) is a key effector of the [[Hippo signaling pathway]], which regulates organ size and tissue homeostasis.

## Role in Cancer
YAP1 plays an oncogenic role in tumorigenesis. Its overexpression is associated with increased proliferation, survival, and EMT in many cancers, including [[gastric cancer]], [[liver cancer]], and [[lung cancer]].

## Therapeutic Targeting
- [[Ivermectin]]: Acts as a YAP1 inhibitor. It inhibits the proliferation of cancer cells by suppressing YAP1 expression or activity. Sensitivity to ivermectin in certain cancers (like gastric cancer) depends on the baseline level of YAP1 expression.

## Connections
- [[Ivermectin]]: Inhibits YAP1-mediated oncogenic signaling.
- [[Gastric Cancer]]: YAP1 determines sensitivity to ivermectin in this cancer type.
- [[Lung Cancer]]: Ivermectin inhibits lung cancer cell proliferation via YAP1 inhibition.
