# paclitaxel

**Paclitaxel** (also known as Taxol) is a widely used chemotherapy agent belonging to the taxane family. It is used to treat several types of cancer, including [[ovarian cancer]], [[notes/_link/Breast Cancer|breast cancer]], and [[non-small-cell lung cancer]].

## Mechanism of Action
Unlike drugs that disrupt [[Microtubule]] assembly, paclitaxel stabilizes microtubules and prevents their disassembly. This "freezes" the mitotic spindle, leading to cell cycle arrest in the G2/M phase and the subsequent induction of [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]].

## Clinical Trials
Paclitaxel is often used in combination with novel agents in clinical trials, such as the BH3 mimetic [[ABT-263]], to overcome resistance in [[leukemia|chronic lymphocytic leukaemia]].
