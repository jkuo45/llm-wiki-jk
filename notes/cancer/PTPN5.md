# PTPN5

**PTPN5** (Protein Tyrosine Phosphatase Non-Receptor Type 5), also known as STEP (STriatal-Enriched Protein tyrosine phosphatase), is a member of the [[PTPN family]] primarily expressed in the central nervous system that regulates [[MAPK]] signaling and synaptic plasticity.
