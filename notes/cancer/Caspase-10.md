# Caspase-10

**Caspase-10** is an initiator protease that is closely related to [[Caspase-8]]. It plays a significant role in the [[Extrinsic Pathway]] of [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]].

## Mechanism
Like [[Caspase-8]], Caspase-10 is recruited to the **Death-Inducing Signaling Complex** ([[DISC]]) upon the activation of death receptors (e.g., [[Fas]], [[DR4]], [[DR5]]). Within the DISC, it undergoes auto-activation and subsequently activates executioner caspases like [[Caspase-3]].

## Role in Cancer
While Caspase-8 is the primary initiator of the extrinsic pathway, Caspase-10 can also initiate apoptosis and its downregulation has been associated with the development of certain tumors, such as choriocarcinoma.

## Connections
- [[Extrinsic Pathway]]: An initiator caspase for death receptor signaling.
- [[DISC]]: The complex where Caspase-10 is activated.
- [[Caspase-8]]: A functionally similar initiator caspase.
- [[Caspase-3]]: A downstream executioner target.
