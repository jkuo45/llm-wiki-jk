# PIDD

PIDD (p53-induced death domain protein; also known as LRDD) is a multidomain scaffold protein that assembles the [[PIDDosome]] complex, a platform for [[Caspase-2]] activation. It functions at the intersection of [[p53]]-dependent [[Apoptosis]], [[NF-κB]] signaling, and [[DNA damage]] response pathways.

## Overview

PIDD is transcriptionally induced by [[p53]] in response to genotoxic stress and serves as a molecular switch that can direct cells toward either apoptosis or survival signaling. It is the core adaptor of the [[PIDDosome]], a multiprotein complex comprising PIDD, [[RAIDD]] (CRADD), and [[Caspase-2|procaspase-2]].

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

**PIDDosome assembly and caspase-2 activation:** Upon [[DNA damage]], [[ATM]] kinase activates [[Chk2]], which phosphorylates and stabilizes [[p53]]. p53 drives PIDD transcription. PIDD protein then recruits [[RAIDD]] via homotypic death domain (DD) interactions, which in turn recruits [[Caspase-2|procaspase-2]] through [[CARD|CARD domains]]. Proximity-induced dimerization triggers caspase-2 autoproteolysis, releasing active [[Caspase-2]].

**NF-κB signaling:** PIDD can also bind [[NEMO]] (IKKγ) and [[RIP1]], promoting [[NEMO]] sumoylation and nuclear translocation, thereby activating the alternative [[NF-κB]] pathway. This PIDD–NEMO–RIP1 axis is triggered by genotoxic stress independently of the PIDDosome apoptotic function, suggesting a context-dependent fate decision.

**Cell cycle regulation:** Active [[Caspase-2]] can cleave [[MDM2]], relieving p53 suppression, and also process [[BID]] to tBID, triggering mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization ([[MOMP]]) and amplifying the apoptotic cascade. PIDDosome activation also delays cell cycle progression after [[DNA damage]].

## Structure

PIDD is a ~110 kDa protein with three distinct domains:

- **N-terminal LRR region:** Seven [[Leucine-rich repeat|leucine-rich repeats (LRRs)]] that mediate protein–protein interactions and may regulate PIDDosome assembly.
- **Central ZU5 domain:** A conserved domain also found in [[ZO-1]] and [[UNC5]] that contributes to structural integrity and interactions with [[RAIDD]].
- **C-terminal DD (death domain):** Mediates homotypic DD–DD interactions with [[RAIDD]]. Within the DD lies the PIDDosome core domain essential for nucleation of the complex.

## Relevance in Cancer

PIDD sits at a critical node connecting p53-dependent apoptosis to caspase-2 activation, making it a potential therapeutic target. Loss of PIDD expression dampens caspase-2-mediated [[BID]] cleavage, reducing apoptotic capacity in response to genotoxic chemotherapy. Conversely, PIDD overexpression can sensitize tumor cells to [[DNA damage|DNA-damaging agents]]. The dual role of PIDD in both apoptosis and [[NF-κB]]-mediated survival reflects a rheostat function that may influence chemoresistance. Pharmacological modulation of the PIDDosome is under investigation as a strategy to reinstate apoptosis in p53-mutant or p53-deficient cancers.
