# STAT3

**Signal transducer and activator of transcription 3** (STAT3) is a transcription factor which in humans is encoded by the *STAT3* gene.

## Role in Cancer
- **Oncogenic Activity**: Promotes cell survival, proliferation, and immune evasion.
- **Inhibition**: The [[JAK2]]/STAT3 pathway is a target for [[melittin]]-mediated inhibition in certain cancers.

## Connections
- [[JAK2]]: An upstream kinase that activates STAT3.
- [[melittin]]: Shown to modulate STAT3 signaling.
