# BRCA

**BRCA** (BReast CAncer gene) refers to two genes, *BRCA1* and *BRCA2*, that produce tumor suppressor proteins. These proteins help repair damaged DNA.

## Role in Cancer
- **Risk**: Mutations in these genes significantly increase the risk of developing [[notes/_link/Breast Cancer]] and [[ovarian cancer]].
- **B.15 Model**: A murine breast cancer model harboring a BRCA mutation, used to study [[melittin]] efficacy.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/Breast Cancer]]: Strongly associated with BRCA mutations.
- [[ovarian cancer]]: Associated with BRCA mutations.
- [[melittin]]: Evaluated for selectivity against BRCA-mutant tumor cell lines.
