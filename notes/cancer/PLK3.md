# PLK3

**PLK3** (Polo-Like Kinase 3) is a member of the polo-like kinase family involved in cell cycle regulation, DNA damage response, and [[Apoptosis]] by phosphorylating targets like [[Bcl-xL]] and [[notes/_link/p53]].
