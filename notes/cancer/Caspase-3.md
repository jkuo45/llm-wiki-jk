# Caspase-3

**Caspase-3** is a protease enzyme that plays a central role in the execution-phase of cell [[notes/_link/Apoptosis|apoptosis]].

## Regulation
Caspase-3 activity is modulated by [[Phosphorylation|phosphorylation]]:
- **[[PKCδ]]**: Enhances Caspase-3 activity.
- **[[p38 MAPK]]**: Phosphorylates Caspase-3 at Ser150, which suppresses its activity. This process can be reversed by the phosphatase [[PP2A]].

## Role in Cancer Research
- **Apoptosis Marker**: The induction of **cleaved caspase-3** is used as a marker for programmed cell death.
- **Melittin Study**: [[melittin]] treatment has been shown to induce cleaved caspase-3 in [[triple-negative breast cancer]] cells.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/Apoptosis|Apoptosis]]: The process where caspase-3 is a key executioner.
- [[melittin]]: Induces caspase-3 activation in cancer cells.
