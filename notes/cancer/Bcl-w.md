# Bcl-w

**Bcl-w** (Bcl-2-like protein 2), encoded by the *BCL2L2* gene, is an anti-apoptotic member of the [[Bcl-2 family]].

## Role in Apoptosis
Bcl-w functions by binding and sequestering pro-apoptotic members of the Bcl-2 family, such as [[Bax]] and [[Bak]], thereby preventing mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization and the initiation of the [[Intrinsic Pathway]].

## Role in Cancer
- **Overexpression**: Bcl-w is overexpressed in various cancers, including colorectal, gastric, and bladder cancers, where it contributes to tumor cell survival and resistance to apoptosis.
- **Drug Target**: It is a target of broad-spectrum BH3 mimetics like [[ABT-737]] and [[ABT-263]].

## Connections
- [[Bcl-2 family]]: An anti-apoptotic regulatory member.
- [[Bax]] / [[Bak]]: Sequestered by Bcl-w to prevent death.
- [[ABT-737]] / [[ABT-263]]: Small molecule inhibitors of Bcl-w.
