# VEGF

**Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor (VEGF)** — principally the VEGF-A isoform — is a secreted dimeric glycoprotein and the paramount driver of physiological and pathological [[Angiogenesis]]. In cancer, tumor hypoxia and oncogenic signaling induce VEGF expression, which then stimulates formation of the abnormal, leaky vasculature that feeds tumor growth and provides routes for [[Metastasis]].

## Structure and Isoforms

VEGF-A is produced as multiple splice variants (e.g., VEGF121, VEGF165, VEGF189). It is secreted and binds to receptor tyrosine kinases VEGFR-1 (FLT1) and especially VEGFR-2 (KDR/Flk-1) on endothelial cells, as well as to co-receptors such as [[Neuropilin-1]]. Heparan-sulfate proteoglycans and matrix-binding isoforms localize VEGF to the extracellular matrix, creating concentration gradients that guide sprouting.

## Mechanism of Action

Hypoxia stabilizes **HIF-1α**, which transcriptionally activates the *VEGFA* gene. Bound VEGF engages VEGFR-2, triggering PI3K/AKT and RAS/MAPK cascades that promote endothelial proliferation, survival, migration, and increased vascular permeability. VEGF also recruits pro-angiogenic [[Integrins]] (αvβ3, αvβ5) on tips of sprouting vessels and synergizes with molecules such as [[Vitronectin]] in the provisional matrix. The resultant vessels are tortuous and poorly perfused, sustaining a hypoxic, aggressive tumor phenotype.

## Role in Cancer

VEGF-mediated angiogenesis is one of the公认 [[Hallmarks of Cancer]]. High tumor VEGF correlates with progression and poor prognosis across carcinomas. Beyond nourishing the tumor, VEGF fosters an immunosuppressive, metastatic-permissive microenvironment.

## Therapeutic Targeting

Anti-angiogenic agents include the anti-VEGF-A antibody **bevacizumab**, VEGFR [[Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors]] (sunitinib, sorafenib, pazopanib, cabozantinib), and VEGF-trap aflibercept. These normalize or prune tumor vessels, improving chemotherapy delivery or starving the tumor, though resistance (alternative angiogenic pathways, vessel co-option) limits durability. Combination with [[Chemotherapy]] or immunotherapy is standard in several indications.
