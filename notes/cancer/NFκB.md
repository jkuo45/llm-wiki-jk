# NFκB

**Nuclear factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of activated B cells (NFκB)** is a family of REL-domain transcription factors (p50, p52, p65/RelA, RelB, c-Rel) that govern immunity, [[Inflammation]], cell survival, and proliferation. In cancer, aberrant NFκB activation is a hallmark-enabling force that links chronic [[Inflammation]] to tumor development and treatment resistance.

## Structure and Activation

In resting cells, NFκB is sequestered in the cytoplasm by [[IκB]] inhibitors. Stimuli such as TNFα, IL-1, Toll-like receptor ligands, genotoxic stress, and [[ROS]] activate the IKK complex (IKKβ-centered), which phosphorylates IκB leading to its ubiquitination and degradation, freeing NFκB to translocate to the nucleus. The non-canonical pathway (NIK/IKKα) processes p100 to p52 for distinct transcriptional programs.

## Mechanism in Cancer

Once nuclear, NFκB induces genes encoding anti-apoptotic proteins ([[Bcl-2]], [[Bcl-xL]], [[c-FLIP]], [[XIAP]]), cell-cycle drivers (cyclin D1), and pro-inflammatory cytokines ([[TNFα]], IL-6) that sculpt an immunosuppressive, pro-tumor microenvironment. Constitutive NFκB is driven by oncogenic [[EGFR]], [[RAS]], viral proteins (e.g., [[Epstein-Barr virus]] LMP1), and microenvironmental stress, reinforcing the [[Hallmarks of Cancer]] and blunting responses to [[Chemotherapy]] and radiation.

## Physiological and Pathological Roles

Physiologically, NFκB mediates host defense and tissue repair. Pathologically, persistent activation promotes initiation, progression, [[Metastasis]], and angiogenesis, while suppressing anti-tumor immunity. The NFκB–[[miR-29b]] axis exemplifies feedback crosstalk, as NFκB can repress tumor-suppressive miRNAs.

## Clinical and Research Relevance

NFκB is a validated but challenging therapeutic target. Inhibitors of IKK, proteasome-mediated degradation (bortezomib indirectly affects NFκB), and natural products such as [[melittin]] that suppress NFκB signaling in [[non-small-cell lung cancer]] and other models are under study. Combining NFκB inhibition with cytotoxic or immune therapy aims to reverse the survival and resistance phenotype.
