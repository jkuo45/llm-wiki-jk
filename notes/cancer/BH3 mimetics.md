# BH3 mimetics

BH3 mimetics are small molecule inhibitors (e.g., [[ABT-737]], [[ABT-263]], [[ABT-199]]) designed to bind to the hydrophobic grooves of anti-apoptotic [[Bcl-2 family]] proteins, thereby inducing [[apoptosis]].
