# Apitherapy

**Apitherapy** is a branch of alternative medicine that uses products derived from honeybees, including honey, pollen, propolis, royal jelly, and [[Honeybee venom|bee venom]].

## Potential in Oncology
- **Accessibility**: Apitherapy offers cost-effective and easily accessible treatment options, particularly in under-resourced or remote regions.
- **Research**: Investigating the molecular mechanisms of bee venom components like [[melittin]] is key to developing novel therapeutics.

## Connections
- [[Honeybee]]: The source of apitherapy products.
- [[melittin]]: A major bioactive component studied in apitherapy.
