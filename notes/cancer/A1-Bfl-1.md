# A1/Bfl-1

**A1/Bfl-1** (Bcl-2-related protein A1), encoded by the *BCL2A1* gene, is an anti-apoptotic member of the [[Bcl-2 family]].

## Function
A1/Bfl-1 prevents [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]] by neutralizing pro-apoptotic BH3-only proteins and sequestering the effectors [[Bax]] and [[Bak]]. It is particularly important in the survival of leukocytes and is often upregulated in response to inflammatory signals via the [[NFκB]] pathway.

## Role in Cancer
- **Resistance**: High expression of A1/Bfl-1 is associated with resistance to chemotherapy and targeted therapies, especially in B-cell malignancies and [[melanoma]].
- **Therapeutic Challenge**: Most current BH3 mimetics (like [[ABT-737]]) do not effectively target A1/Bfl-1, making it a key mediator of resistance.

## Connections
- [[Bcl-2 family]]: An anti-apoptotic member involved in survival signaling.
- [[NFκB]]: Regulates the expression of A1/Bfl-1.
- [[Bax]] / [[Bak]]: Neutralized by A1/Bfl-1.
- [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]]: Inhibits the intrinsic pathway.
