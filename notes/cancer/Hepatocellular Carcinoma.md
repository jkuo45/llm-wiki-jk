# Hepatocellular Carcinoma

Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) is the most common type of primary liver cancer and the fourth leading cause of cancer death worldwide.

## Role of Ivermectin
[[Ivermectin]] has demonstrated inhibitory effects on the development of HCC.

### Mechanisms
- **YAP1 Inhibition:** Ivermectin inhibits HCC development by blocking [[YAP1]] activity. This has been shown in spontaneous liver cancer mouse models.

## Connections
- [[Ivermectin]]: Potential treatment for HCC.
- [[YAP1]]: Key oncogene inhibited by ivermectin in liver cancer.
