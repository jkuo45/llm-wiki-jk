# CYP2C19

[[CYP2C19]] (Cytochrome P450 Family 2 Subfamily C Member 19) is an enzyme that in humans is encoded by the [[CYP2C19]] gene. It is an important liver enzyme that acts on at least 10% of drugs in current clinical use.

## Function
- **Drug Metabolism:** CYP2C19 metabolizes various drugs, including [[proton-pump inhibitors]], [[anti-epileptics]], and the benzimidazole anthelmintics [[Albendazole]] and [[Fenbendazole]].
- **Genetic Variation:** Genetic polymorphisms in CYP2C19 can lead to significant differences in drug metabolism rates among individuals (e.g., poor vs. rapid metabolizers).
