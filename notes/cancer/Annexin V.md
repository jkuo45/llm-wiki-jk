# Annexin V

**Annexin V** is a cellular protein in the annexin group. In biochemistry, Annexin V is commonly used as a marker for cells undergoing [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]].

## Role in Research
The **Annexin V-FITC Apoptosis Detection Assay** is used to quantify apoptotic, necrotic, or dead cell populations after treatment. In studies of [[melittin]], it helps characterize the kinetics and mechanism of cell death.

## Connections
- [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]]: Annexin V is a marker for the early stages of this process.
- [[melittin]]: Cell death induced by melittin is assessed using Annexin V assays.
