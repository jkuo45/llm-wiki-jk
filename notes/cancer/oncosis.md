# Oncosis

[[Oncosis]] is a term used in pathology to describe a specific type of accidental cell death that results from severe metabolic stress, most notably [[Ischemia]] (lack of blood flow) and subsequent hypoxia. It is characterized by a distinct morphological sequence leading to cell swelling and lysis.

## Pathophysiology

The progression of oncosis involves:
1. **Metabolic Failure**: Depletion of [[ATP]] due to lack of oxygen leads to the failure of ion pumps (e.g., [[Na+/K+-ATPase]]).
2. **Ion Imbalance**: Failure of these pumps causes an influx of sodium and water into the cell, while potassium leaves.
3. **Cell Swelling**: The accumulation of water leads to significant cell swelling (oncotic swelling).
4. **Membrane Permeability**: Increased membrane permeability occurs, often due to damage to the [[Cytoskeleton]] and plasma membrane.
5. **Lysis**: The cell eventually bursts, releasing its contents into the extracellular space.

## Comparison with Apoptosis

| Feature | [[Oncosis]] | [[Apoptosis]] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mechanism** | Passive, energy-independent (necrotic) | Active, energy-dependent (programmed) |
| **Cell Size** | Swollen (oncotic) | Shrunken |
| **Nucleus** | Pyknosis (shrinkage), Karyorrhexis (fragmentation) | Chromatin condensation, Fragmentation |
| **Plasma Membrane** | Increased permeability, blebbing, lysis | Maintained integrity, blebbing |
| **Outcome** | Lysis, inflammation | Formation of apoptotic bodies, no inflammation |

## Clinical Significance

Oncosis is the primary mechanism of cell death in many forms of [[Ischemia-reperfusion Injury|ischemic injury]], such as:
- [[Myocardial Infarction]] (heart attack)
- [[Stroke]] (cerebral ischemia)
- Organ failure due to shock

While often categorized under the broad umbrella of [[Necrosis]], oncosis is distinguished by its specific morphological features and the primary cause of ATP depletion.

## Connections

- [[Apoptosis]] - Contrast this programmed cell death mechanism with oncosis.
- [[Necrosis]] - A broader category of cell death that includes oncosis.
- [[Ischemia]] - The most common cause of oncosis in vivo.
- [[Myocardial Infarction]] - A classic clinical example of oncosis.
- [[ATP depletion]] - The metabolic trigger for the ionic imbalances seen in oncosis.
