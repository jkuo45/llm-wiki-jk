# Lung Cancer

Lung cancer has the highest morbidity and mortality among cancers globally.

## Role of Ivermectin
[[Ivermectin]] has shown promising results in inhibiting the proliferation and metastasis of lung cancer cells.

### Mechanisms
- **YAP1 Inhibition:** Ivermectin significantly inhibits the proliferation of lung cancer cells (e.g., H1299) by inhibiting [[YAP1]] activity.
- **EGFR Targeting:** Combined with [[erlotinib]], ivermectin achieves a synergistic killing effect by regulating [[EGFR]] activity.
- **EMT Inhibition:** Ivermectin reduces the metastasis of lung cancer cells by inhibiting [[EMT]].
- **HSP27 Inhibition:** Ivermectin inhibits [[HSP27]] phosphorylation, enhancing the activity of anti-EGFR drugs.

## Connections
- [[Ivermectin]]: Potential therapeutic agent for lung cancer.
- [[YAP1]]: Key protein targeted by ivermectin in lung cancer.
- [[EGFR]]: Target for combined treatment with ivermectin and erlotinib.
- [[EMT]]: Process inhibited by ivermectin to reduce metastasis.
