# Cisplatin

**Cisplatin** is a chemotherapy medication used to treat a number of cancers, including testicular, ovarian, cervical, head and neck, bladder, and lung cancers. It belongs to the class of platinum-based antineoplastic drugs.

## Use with Melittin
- **Synergy**: Synergistic anticancer effects have been reported between [[melittin]] and cisplatin in cervical and laryngeal malignancies.

## Connections
- [[melittin]]: Acts synergistically with cisplatin.
- [[triple-negative breast cancer]]: Cisplatin is used in the clinic to treat TNBC.
