# erlotinib

**Erlotinib** (also known as Tarceva) is a small molecule tyrosine kinase inhibitor used for the treatment of [[non-small-cell lung cancer]] and [[Pancreatic Cancer]].

## Mechanism of Action
Erlotinib selectively inhibits the epidermal growth factor receptor ([[EGFR]]) tyrosine kinase. By blocking EGFR signaling, it inhibits cell proliferation and promotes [[Apoptosis]] in tumors that are dependent on the EGFR pathway.

## Clinical Trials
Erlotinib is being investigated in combination with apoptotic sensitizers, such as the Bcl-2 inhibitor [[ABT-263]], to enhance clinical outcomes in solid tumors.
