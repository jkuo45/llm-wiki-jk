# Satoshi Ōmura

Satoshi Ōmura is a Japanese microbiologist and Nobel laureate known for the discovery and development of various pharmaceuticals originally occurring in microorganisms. He was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 2015 for his work on avermectins.
