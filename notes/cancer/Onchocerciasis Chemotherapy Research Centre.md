# Onchocerciasis Chemotherapy Research Centre

The **Onchocerciasis Chemotherapy Research Centre (OCRC)**, based in Ghana, is a specialized clinical research institute devoted to developing and evaluating treatments for [[Onchocerciasis]] (river blindness). Operating under the Ghana Health Service and in collaboration with the [[World Health Organization]] TDR programme (Special Programme for Research and Training in Tropical Diseases), it conducts Phase I–IV trials of anti-filarial drugs.

## Research Focus

The OCRC's mandate centers on chemotherapy for onchocerciasis: optimizing [[Ivermectin]] regimens, assessing safety and pharmacokinetics in endemic populations, and crucially pursuing **macrofilaricidal** agents that kill adult *Onchocerca volvulus* worms (ivermectin predominantly clears microfilariae). Candidate macrofilaricides studied include doxycycline (anti-Wolbachia), and newer anthelmintics such as [[Oxfendazole]], building on the benzimidazole class also investigated for [[Cancer]] applications.

## Relevance to Cancer Research

The OCRC generates deep expertise in anthelmintic pharmacology, dose-finding, and repurposing — the same compounds (benzimidazoles, ivermectin) that are now scrutinized for anti-tumor activity via [[Microtubule]] disruption (benzimidazales) or PAK1/[[Akt]] pathway modulation (ivermectin). The clinical infrastructure and safety databases produced by the OCRC therefore inform oncology translational studies.

## Legacy and Collaboration

Through partnerships with TDR, Merck ([[Mectizan]] donation program), and academic networks, the OCRC has advanced community-based trial methodologies and trained researchers, contributing to the global shift from control toward elimination of onchocerciasis.
