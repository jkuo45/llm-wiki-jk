# GLUT1

[[GLUT1]] (Glucose transporter 1) is a uniporter protein that in humans is encoded by the [[SLC2A1]] gene. It facilitates the transport of [[Glucose]] across the plasma membranes of mammalian cells.

## Role in Cancer
- **Overexpression:** GLUT1 is frequently overexpressed in various cancers to meet the high demand for glucose required by the [[Warburg Effect]].
- **Prognostic Marker:** High levels of GLUT1 expression are often associated with poor prognosis and increased tumor aggressiveness, such as in [[non-small-cell lung cancer]].
- **Therapeutic Target:** Inhibiting GLUT1 or down-regulating its expression is a strategy to starve cancer cells. [[Fenbendazole]] has been shown to down-regulate GLUT1 expression, thereby reducing [[Glucose uptake]].
