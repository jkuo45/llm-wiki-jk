# PIDDosome

The **PIDDosome** is a multi-protein scaffold complex built from the p53-induced protein with a death domain ([[PIDD]], PIDD1) and the adapter RIP-associated ICH-1/CED-3 homologous protein with a death domain ([[RAIDD]], also CRADD). It serves as the principal activation platform for [[Caspase-2]], positioning Caspase-2 as an apical initiator that couples genotoxic and mitotic stress to the apoptotic program.

## Structure and Assembly

PIDD is a large death-domain–containing protein induced by [[p53]] and genotoxic stress. Proteolytic processing yields PIDD-CC, the C-terminal caspase-recruitment-domain–containing fragment that oligomerizes with RAIDD. RAIDD's death domain binds PIDD-CC while its CARD recruits and promotes dimerization/activation of procaspase-2. Higher-order assembly of the PIDDosome brings procaspase-2 molecules into proximity, enabling trans-activation.

## Mechanism of Action

The PIDDosome activates Caspase-2 in response to DNA damage, spindle disruption (mitotic catastrophe), and other stressors. Active Caspase-2 can directly cleave [[Bid]] (generating tBid) to engage the mitochondrial pathway and [[MOMP]], and can amplify apoptotic signaling upstream of effector caspases. Thus the PIDDosome functions as a sentinel translating genomic instability — a cardinal feature of [[Cancer]] — into cell death, acting as a tumor-suppressive checkpoint.

## Physiological and Pathological Relevance

Physiologically, the PIDDosome enforces genomic integrity by eliminating cells with irreparable damage. Pathologically, its components are frequently dysregulated or lost in malignancies, disabling the Caspase-2 checkpoint and permitting survival of genomically unstable cells — a contributor to the [[Hallmarks of Cancer]] and to resistance to DNA-damaging [[Chemotherapy]] and radiotherapy. PIDD/RAIDD/Caspase-2 status therefore influences treatment sensitivity.

## Clinical and Research Relevance

The PIDDosome–Caspase-2 axis is studied as a determinant of therapeutic response and as a target for restoring apoptotic competence in resistant tumors. Biomarkers of PIDDosome integrity may help stratify patients for genotoxic regimens.
