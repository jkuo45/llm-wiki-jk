# SLC19A1

## Overview

SLC19A1 (Reduced Folate Carrier 1, RFC1) is a transmembrane transporter that mediates cellular uptake of [[folate]] and reduced folates. It is also a transporter for [[methotrexate]] and, notably, for the cyclic dinucleotide [[cGAMP]] (2'3'-cGAMP).

## Function

- **Folate transport**: Primary importer of [[5-methyltetrahydrofolate]] (5-MTHF), the major circulating folate form.
- **Methotrexate transport**: Key determinant of [[methotrexate]] sensitivity in cancer chemotherapy.
- **cGAMP import**: SLC19A1 imports extracellular [[cGAMP]] into cells, amplifying [[STING]] signaling in a paracrine manner.

## Role in cGAS-STING Signaling

SLC19A1-mediated import of [[cGAMP]] allows transfer of STING activation signals between cells. When [[cGAS]] produces cGAMP in one cell, cGAMP can be exported and then imported by neighboring cells via SLC19A1, propagating the [[type I interferon]] response. This paracrine cGAMP signaling amplifies [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]] in the [[Tumor Microenvironment]].

## Role in Cancer

- **Methotrexate resistance**: Reduced SLC19A1 expression confers resistance to [[methotrexate]] in [[leukemia]] and other cancers.
- **STING amplification**: SLC19A1-mediated cGAMP import amplifies antitumor STING signaling, suggesting that SLC19A1 expression may predict response to [[STING agonists]].
