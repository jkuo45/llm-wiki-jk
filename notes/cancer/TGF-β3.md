# TGF-β3

**Transforming growth factor beta 3** (TGF-β3) is a type of protein, known as a cytokine, which is involved in cell differentiation, embryogenesis, and development.

## Role in Research
- **RGD Motif**: An N-terminal alpha-helical RGD peptide motif (RGD1-melittin) derived from TGF-β3 was engineered to enhance [[melittin]] targeting to cancer cells.

## Connections
- [[melittin]]: Engineered with TGF-β3-derived motifs for enhanced selectivity.
