# Caspase-7

**Caspase-7** is an effector (executioner) protease that plays a critical role in the final stages of [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]].

## Mechanism
Caspase-7 is activated by initiator caspases, such as [[Caspase-8]] (from the [[Extrinsic Pathway]]) and [[Caspase-9]] (from the [[Intrinsic Pathway]]). Once activated, it coordinates with [[caspase-3]] to cleave a wide range of cellular substrates, including structural proteins and DNA repair enzymes, leading to the characteristic morphological changes of cell death.

## Regulation
- **IAPs**: [[XIAP]] and other [[Inhibitor of Apoptosis Proteins]] can directly bind and inhibit active Caspase-7.
- **Smac/DIABLO**: Relieves IAP-mediated inhibition of Caspase-7.

## Connections
- [[Common Pathway]]: A key executioner protease.
- [[caspase-3]]: Its primary partner in the execution phase.
- [[XIAP]]: A potent endogenous inhibitor.
- [[Intrinsic Pathway]] / [[Extrinsic Pathway]]: Converge on Caspase-7 activation.
