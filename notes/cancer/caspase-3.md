# Caspase-3

**Caspase-3** is a protease enzyme that plays a central role in the execution-phase of cell [[apoptosis]].

## Role in Cancer Research
- **Apoptosis Marker**: The induction of **cleaved caspase-3** is used as a marker for programmed cell death.
- **Melittin Study**: [[melittin]] treatment has been shown to induce cleaved caspase-3 in [[triple-negative breast cancer]] cells.

## Connections
- [[apoptosis]]: The process where caspase-3 is a key executioner.
- [[melittin]]: Induces caspase-3 activation in cancer cells.
