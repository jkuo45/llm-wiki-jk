# PTPN23

**PTPN23** (Protein Tyrosine Phosphatase Non-Receptor Type 23), also known as HD-PTP, is a member of the [[PTPN family]] that acts as a tumor suppressor by regulating endosomal trafficking and various signaling pathways.
