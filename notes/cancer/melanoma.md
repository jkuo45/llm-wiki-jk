# Melanoma

**Melanoma** is a type of cancer that develops from the pigment-producing cells known as melanocytes.

## Research with Bee Venom and Ivermectin
- Both [[Honeybee venom|honeybee venom]] and [[melittin]] have demonstrated antitumoral effects in melanoma. Melittin has been shown to inhibit the [[notes/_link/MAPK]] signaling pathway in melanoma cells.
- [[Ivermectin]] inhibits melanoma activity and lung metastasis. It acts as a [[PAK1]] inhibitor and induces [[notes/_link/Autophagy]]-dependent cell death. Ivermectin shows synergistic effects with [[dabrafenib]].

## Connections
- [[melittin]]: Inhibits growth and signaling in melanoma.
- [[Ivermectin]]: Inhibits growth and metastasis via [[PAK1]] and [[notes/_link/Autophagy]].
- [[notes/_link/MAPK]]: A signaling pathway inhibited by both melittin and ivermectin in melanoma.
