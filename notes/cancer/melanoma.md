# Melanoma

**Melanoma** is a type of cancer that develops from the pigment-producing cells known as melanocytes.

## Research with Melittin
Both [[Honeybee venom|honeybee venom]] and [[melittin]] have demonstrated antitumoral effects in melanoma. Melittin has been shown to inhibit the [[MAPK]] signaling pathway in melanoma cells.

## Connections
- [[melittin]]: Inhibits growth and signaling in melanoma.
- [[MAPK]]: A signaling pathway inhibited by melittin in melanoma.
