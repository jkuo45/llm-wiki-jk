# JAK2

**Janus kinase 2** (JAK2) is a non-receptor tyrosine kinase. It belongs to the Janus kinase family and has been implicated in signaling by members of the type II cytokine receptor family.

## Role in Cancer
- **JAK/STAT Pathway**: Involved in cell growth, survival, and differentiation.
- **Inhibition**: [[melittin]] has been shown to inhibit the [[JAK2]]/[[STAT3]] pathway in [[ovarian cancer]].

## Connections
- [[STAT3]]: A downstream target of JAK2.
- [[ovarian cancer]]: A disease where the JAK2/STAT3 pathway is a therapeutic target.
- [[melittin]]: Inhibits JAK2 signaling.
