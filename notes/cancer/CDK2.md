# CDK2
[[CDK2]] (Cyclin-dependent kinase 2) is a kinase that phosphorylates [[Mcl-1]] at Ser64, enhancing its binding to pro-apoptotic proteins like [[Bim]], [[Noxa]], and [[Bak]].
