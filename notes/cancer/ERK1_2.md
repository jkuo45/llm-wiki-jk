# ERK1_2

**ERK1/2** (Extracellular Signal-Regulated Kinase 1 and 2), also known as p44 and p42 MAP kinases, are key components of the [[MAPK Signaling]] pathway that regulate various cellular processes including growth, proliferation, and [[apoptosis]].
