# Oxfendazole

[[Oxfendazole]] is a [[Benzimidazole]] anthelmintic used in veterinary medicine to protect livestock against roundworm, lungworm, and tapeworms. It is also the primary active metabolite of [[Fenbendazole]].

## Pharmacokinetics
In many species, [[Fenbendazole]] is oxidized to [[Oxfendazole]] (fenbendazole sulfoxide) in the liver. These two compounds can interconvert in a redox cycle. Oxfendazole is further metabolized to fenbendazole sulfone.

## Research
- **Cancer:** Like other benzimidazoles, oxfendazole is being investigated for its potential anticancer properties, particularly its ability to inhibit [[Microtubule]] polymerization.
- **Human Use:** Clinical trials have explored the safety and pharmacokinetics of oxfendazole in healthy human volunteers to assess its potential for repurposing.
