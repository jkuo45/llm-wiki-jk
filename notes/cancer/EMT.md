# EMT

Epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) is a biological process by which epithelial cells lose their cell-cell adhesion and apical-basal polarity, and gain mesenchymal properties, such as increased migratory and invasive capabilities.

## Role in Cancer
EMT is a critical step in the [[Metastasis]] of cancer cells. It allows cells to detach from the primary tumor and invade surrounding tissues or enter the bloodstream.

## Inhibition
- [[Ivermectin]]: Can inhibit EMT in various cancers, including [[Lung Cancer]] and [[triple-negative breast cancer]]. It regulates the expression of EMT-related genes, such as E-cadherin.

## Connections
- [[Ivermectin]]: Suppresses EMT to reduce tumor metastasis.
- [[triple-negative breast cancer]]: Subtype where ivermectin regulates EMT genes to restore drug sensitivity.
