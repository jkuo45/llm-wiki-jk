# Bid

**Bid** (BH3 interacting-domain death agonist) is a pro-apoptotic member of the [[Bcl-2 family]] that serves as a critical link between the [[Extrinsic Pathway]] and the [[Intrinsic Pathway]] of [[Apoptosis|apoptosis]].

## Mechanism
Bid is normally an inactive cytosolic protein. It is activated by [[Caspase-8]] (from the extrinsic pathway) which cleaves it into its truncated form, **tBid**.

## Regulation
Bid activation by [[Caspase-8]] is regulated by [[Phosphorylation|phosphorylation]]:
- **[[CK1]] / [[CK2]]**: These kinases phosphorylate Bid to inhibit its cleavage by [[Caspase-8]], thereby blocking its pro-apoptotic function and the subsequent activation of the [[Intrinsic Pathway]].

## Function
tBid translocates to the mitochondria, where it triggers the activation of [[Bax]] and [[Bak]]. This leads to mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization, release of [[Cytochrome c]], and activation of the intrinsic pathway. This mechanism allows the extrinsic signal to be amplified through the mitochondrial loop.
