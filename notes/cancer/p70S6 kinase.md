# p70S6 kinase

**p70S6 kinase (p70S6K / RPS6KB1, S6K1)** is a serine/threonine kinase downstream of the [[mTOR]]C1 complex that couples nutrient and growth-factor signaling to protein synthesis, cell growth, and survival. It is a central node of the [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]]/mTOR pathway frequently hyperactivated in [[Cancer]].

## Structure and Activation

p70S6K contains an N-terminal kinase domain and multiple regulatory phosphorylation sites (Thr389 in the hydrophobic motif, phosphorylated by mTORC1; and Thr229 in the activation loop by PDK1). Full activation requires convergent input from growth factors (via [[Akt]]/PDK1) and nutrients/energy (via mTORC1), embedding it in the cellular growth-control network.

## Mechanism of Action

Once active, p70S6K phosphorylates the 40S ribosomal protein S6, enhancing translation of a subset of mRNAs encoding ribosomal and biosynthetic proteins — directly driving mass accumulation. It also phosphorylates and inactivates the pro-apoptotic Bcl-2 family protein [[Bad]] (at Ser136 and related sites), neutralizing its ability to trigger [[MOMP]] and [[Apoptosis]], thereby promoting cell survival. Through inhibitory feedback to [[IRS1]], sustained p70S6K activation can paradoxically dampen upstream insulin signaling.

## Physiological and Pathological Relevance

Physiologically, p70S6K mediates hypertrophy, lymphocyte activation, and tissue growth. Pathologically, its overactivation supports the [[Hallmarks of Cancer]] — sustained proliferative signaling, resistance to cell death, and metabolic reprogramming. High p70S6K activity correlates with aggressive tumors and, in some contexts, resistance to targeted and cytotoxic [[Chemotherapy]].

## Clinical and Research Relevance

p70S6K is a biomarker of mTOR pathway activity and a therapeutic target. mTOR inhibitors (rapalogues, catalytic inhibitors) and AKT inhibitors indirectly suppress p70S6K; direct S6K inhibitors have been explored. Its Bad-inactivating function makes p70S6K a rational node for combination strategies that restore apoptotic competence.
