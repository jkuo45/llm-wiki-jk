# Oblimersen sodium

**Oblimersen sodium** (also known as Genasense) is an antisense oligonucleotide designed to inhibit the production of [[Bcl-2]] protein.

## Mechanism
Oblimersen is a 18-base phosphorothioate DNA oligomer that is complementary to the first six codons of the human *BCL2* mRNA. By binding to the mRNA, it triggers its degradation by RNase H, thereby reducing the levels of the anti-apoptotic Bcl-2 protein and sensitizing cancer cells to [[notes/_link/Apoptosis]].

## Clinical Use
It was the first agent targeting Bcl-2 to enter clinical trials. It has been investigated for use in:
- [[Chronic lymphocytic leukaemia]] (CLL)
- [[melanoma]]
- Non-Hodgkin lymphoma
