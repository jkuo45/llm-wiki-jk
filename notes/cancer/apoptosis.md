# Apoptosis

**Apoptosis** is a form of programmed cell death that occurs in multicellular organisms. It is a highly regulated process that is essential for development and the elimination of damaged or potentially cancerous cells.

## Role in Cancer Research
- **Markers**: Common markers for apoptosis include [[caspase-3|cleaved caspase-3]] and [[Annexin V]] binding.
- **Induction**: [[melittin]] and [[Honeybee venom|honeybee venom]] have been shown to induce apoptosis in various cancer cell lines, including [[breast cancer]] and [[melanoma]].

## Connections
- [[caspase-3]]: A key executioner protein in the apoptotic pathway.
- [[Annexin V]]: A marker used to detect early stages of apoptosis.
- [[melittin]]: A peptide that induces apoptosis in cancer cells.
