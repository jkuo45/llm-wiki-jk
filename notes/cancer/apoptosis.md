# Apoptosis

**Apoptosis** is a form of programmed cell death that occurs in multicellular organisms. It is a highly regulated process that is essential for development and the elimination of damaged or potentially cancerous cells.

## Role in Cancer Research
- **Markers**: Common markers for apoptosis include [[caspase-3|cleaved caspase-3]] and [[Annexin V]] binding.
- **Induction**: 
    - [[melittin]] and [[Honeybee venom|honeybee venom]] induce apoptosis in various cancer cell lines.
    - [[Ivermectin]] induces apoptosis via the mitochondrial pathway by upregulating [[Bax]] and downregulating [[Bcl-2]], activating [[caspase-3]].

## Connections
- [[caspase-3]]: A key executioner protein in the apoptotic pathway.
- [[Ivermectin]]: Induces apoptosis in many cancer types, including [[HeLa]], [[leukemia]], and [[glioblastoma]].
- [[Autophagy]]: Often exhibits cross-talk with apoptosis during treatment with agents like ivermectin.
