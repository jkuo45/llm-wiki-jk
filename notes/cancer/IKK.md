# IKK

**IKK** (IκB kinase, also known as the IKK complex or IKK signalosome) is a multi-subunit protein kinase complex that serves as the central regulatory node of the [[NFκB]] signaling pathway. The complex is composed of two catalytic subunits, [[IKKα]] (IKK1) and [[IKKβ]] (IKK2), and a regulatory scaffold subunit, IKKγ (also called NEMO). Beyond its canonical role in phosphorylating the inhibitor IκBα to release NFκB, IKK directly phosphorylates pro-apoptotic Bcl-2 family members including [[Puma]] and [[Bad]], thereby modulating their activity and linking inflammatory signaling to cell-survival decisions.

## Structure & Composition

The IKK complex is organized around the IKKγ/NEMO scaffold, which lacks kinase activity but recruits upstream activators such as TRAF family proteins and the TAK1 complex. IKKα and IKKβ are highly homologous serine/threonine kinases each containing an N-terminal kinase domain, a leucine zipper, and a helix-loop-helix domain that mediate dimerization. IKKβ is the principal subunit activated by inflammatory stimuli (TNFα, IL-1), whereas IKKα contributes to alternative (non-canonical) NFκB activation through phosphorylation of the p100 precursor.

## Mechanism of Action

Canonical activation proceeds when upstream receptors engage MYD88- or TRAF-dependent pathways, leading to TAK1-mediated phosphorylation of IKKβ at Ser177/Ser181. Activated IKK phosphorylates IκBα at Ser32/Ser36, triggering K48-linked ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of IκBα, which liberates the p50/RelA NFκB dimer for nuclear translocation. In parallel, IKK can phosphorylate [[Puma]] and [[Bad]], modulating their propensity to activate [[Apoptosis]] and altering the balance between survival (via NFκB target genes) and death. This dual function positions IKK at the crossroads of [[Inflammation]] and tumor cell fate.

## Physiological & Pathological Function

Constitutive IKK/NFκB activation is one of the [[Hallmarks of Cancer]], promoting proliferation, invasion, [[Angiogenesis]], and resistance to [[Chemotherapy]]. Genetic and pharmacological studies show that IKKβ is frequently required for tumor initiation and maintenance in models of [[Colorectal Cancer]] and breast cancer, where it sustains expression of anti-apoptotic proteins ([[BCL-XL]], [[XIAP]], [[c-FLIP]]). IKKα additionally influences proliferation and differentiation through alternative NFκB signaling and through substrates outside the NFκB axis.

## Clinical & Research Relevance

IKKβ and the IKK complex are attractive oncology targets. Small-molecule inhibitors (e.g., BMS-345541, TPCA-1, IKK-16) and NEMO-binding peptides have been explored preclinically to sensitize tumors to apoptosis and overcome therapy resistance. Because NFκB also drives [[Inflammatory]] microenvironments that support tumor progression, IKK inhibition is studied in combination with cytotoxic agents and immune-modulating therapies.
