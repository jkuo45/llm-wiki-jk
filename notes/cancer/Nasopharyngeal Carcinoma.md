# Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma

Nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC) is a malignant tumor derived from the epithelial cells of the nasopharyngeal mucosa. It is closely associated with [[Epstein-Barr virus]] (EBV) infection.

## Role of Ivermectin
[[Ivermectin]] has demonstrated cytotoxic effects on NPC cells in vitro and inhibits tumor growth in vivo.

### Mechanisms
- **PAK1 Inhibition:** Ivermectin reduces [[PAK1]] kinase activity, which in turn inhibits the [[MAPK]] pathway.
- **Selective Toxicity:** Inhibits NPC development at doses that are not toxic to normal cells.

## Connections
- [[Ivermectin]]: Potential therapeutic agent for NPC.
- [[PAK1]]: Key protein targeted by ivermectin in NPC.
- [[MAPK]]: Signaling pathway inhibited by ivermectin in NPC.
- [[Epstein-Barr virus]]: Virus closely linked to NPC.
