# Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma

**Nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC)** is a malignant epithelial tumor arising from the mucosal lining of the nasopharynx. It is epidemiologically distinctive, with the highest incidence in southern China, Southeast Asia, and parts of North Africa, and it is unusual among carcinomas for its strong causal association with [[Epstein-Barr virus]] (EBV) infection, complemented by genetic and dietary (salty/fermented food) risk factors.

## Etiology and Pathophysiology

Virtually all undifferentiated non-keratinizing NPCs harbor EBV, whose latent proteins — notably LMP1, LMP2A, and EBNA1 — activate pro-survival and proliferative signaling ([[NFκB]], [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]], and [[MAPK]] pathways) while impairing apoptotic checkpoints. EBV-encoded small RNAs (EBERs) and [[BART miRNAs]] further remodel the tumor microenvironment. Genetic susceptibility (HLA loci, CYP2E1 variants) and chronic exposure to nitrosamines from preserved foods synergize with viral oncogenesis to drive the [[Hallmarks of Cancer]].

## Clinical Presentation and Biomarkers

NPC classically presents with neck lymph-node metastasis, nasal obstruction, epistaxis, hearing loss, or cranial nerve palsies. Because the nasopharynx is deep-seated, early lesions are often silent, leading to diagnosis at locally advanced stages. Key biomarkers include elevated serum EBV DNA load (a powerful tool for screening, staging, and monitoring treatment response), VCA/[[EBNA1]] IgA serology, and histological confirmation by biopsy.

## Therapeutic Landscape

Radiotherapy is the cornerstone for localized disease, with concurrent chemoradiotherapy (cisplatin-based [[Chemotherapy]]) for advanced stages; NPC is notably radiosensitive. Targeted and immune approaches — including anti-EGFR antibodies and anti-PD-1 checkpoint inhibitors — are increasingly used in recurrent/metastatic disease.

## Role of Ivermectin

Preclinical studies indicate that [[Ivermectin]] exerts cytotoxic effects against NPC cells and inhibits tumor growth in vivo. Proposed mechanisms include inhibition of [[PAK1]] kinase activity, which attenuates the [[MAPK]] proliferative pathway, and a favorable selective toxicity window that spares normal cells at active doses. These findings position ivermectin as a candidate repurposed agent warranting further validation.
