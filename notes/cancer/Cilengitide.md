# Cilengitide

Cilengitide is a cyclic [[RGD peptide]] derivative that acts as a potent antagonist of αvβ3 and αvβ5 [[Integrins]], primarily investigated as an anti-angiogenic agent for the treatment of [[glioblastoma]].
