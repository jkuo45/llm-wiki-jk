# Bioluminescence Resonance Energy Transfer

**Bioluminescence resonance energy transfer** (BRET) is a mechanism of energy transfer between a bioluminescent donor and a fluorescent acceptor.

## Role in Research
- **Molecular Interactions**: BRET is used to monitor the proximity of molecules within a cell (typically within 10 nm).
- **EGFR Study**: BRET experiments were used to determine whether [[melittin]] interfered with the binding of EGF to [[EGFR]].

## Connections
- [[EGFR]]: Its interaction with ligands and melittin was studied using BRET.
- [[melittin]]: Its proximity to receptors was analyzed via BRET assays.
