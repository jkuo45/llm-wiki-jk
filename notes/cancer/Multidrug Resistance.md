# Multidrug Resistance

Multidrug Resistance (MDR) is the phenomenon where tumor cells become resistant to a wide variety of structurally and functionally unrelated chemotherapy drugs. It is a major cause of treatment failure, relapse, and death in cancer patients.

## Mechanisms
- **Efflux Pumps:** Overexpression of ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporters, such as [[P-gp]] (P-glycoprotein), which pump drugs out of the cell.
- **Signaling Pathways:** Activation of pathways like [[EGFR]], [[ERK]], [[notes/_link/Akt]], and [[NF-kappa B]] can upregulate the expression of MDR-associated proteins.

## Reversal of MDR
Developing agents that can reverse MDR is a critical area of oncology research.
- [[Ivermectin]]: Has been shown to reverse drug resistance in various cancers (e.g., vincristine-resistant colorectal cancer, doxorubicin-resistant breast cancer). It works by inhibiting [[P-gp]] and downregulating its expression via the [[EGFR]]/[[ERK]]/[[notes/_link/Akt]]/[[NF-kappa B]] signaling pathway.
