# Triple-negative Breast Cancer

**Triple-negative breast cancer** (TNBC) is an aggressive subtype of [[Breast Cancer]] characterized by the lack of expression of estrogen receptors (ER), progesterone receptors (PR), and human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 ([[HER2]]).

## Characteristics
- **Prognosis**: Associated with poor clinical outcomes and high mortality.
- **Molecular Targets**: Frequently overexpresses [[EGFR]] (approx. 50% of cases), but often shows resistance to standard anti-EGFR therapies.
- **Signaling**: Highly dependent on the [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]]/[[mTOR]] pathway.
