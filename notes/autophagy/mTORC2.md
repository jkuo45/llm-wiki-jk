# mTORC2
mTOR complex 2 (mTORC2) is a rapamycin-insensitive multi-protein complex containing [[mTOR]] that regulates cytoskeletal organization, cell survival, and metabolism, primarily by phosphorylating [[AKT]] at Ser473.
