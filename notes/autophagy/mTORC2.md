# mTORC2
mTOR complex 2 (mTORC2) is a rapamycin-insensitive multi-protein complex containing [[notes/_link/mTOR]] that regulates cytoskeletal organization, cell survival, and metabolism, primarily by phosphorylating [[notes/_link/Akt|AKT]] at Ser473.

## Function

mTORC2 ([[mTOR]]-[[Rictor]]-[[mSIN1]]-[[Protor-1]]-[[mLST8]]) phosphorylates [[AKT]] (S473), [[SGK1]], and [[PKCα]]. It regulates cell survival, metabolism, and cytoskeleton.
