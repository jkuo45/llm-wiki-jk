# 14-3-3 protein

14-3-3 proteins are a family of conserved regulatory molecules that bind to phosphorylated [[TFEB]] (at S211) to retain it in the cytosol, thereby inhibiting its nuclear translocation.

## Family & Structure

14-3-3 proteins are dimeric adapter proteins with conserved phosphoserine/phosphothreonine-binding pockets. The seven mammalian isoforms (β, γ, ε, ζ, η, θ, σ) regulate protein localization and activity.
## Autophagy Regulation

14-3-3 proteins retain [[TFEB]] and [[TFE3]] in the cytosol by binding phosphorylated S211/S321, preventing their nuclear translocation during nutrient-replete conditions.
