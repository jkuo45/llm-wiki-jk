# FIP200

FIP200 (FAK family-interacting protein of 200 kDa), also known as RB1CC1, is a core component of the [[ULK1]] complex essential for the initiation of [[Macroautophagy|autophagy]].

## Function

FIP200 (RB1CC1) is the mammalian ortholog of yeast [[Atg17]]. It scaffolds the ULK1-[[Atg13]]-[[Atg101]] kinase complex for autophagy initiation and is essential for [[Autophagosome]] formation.
