# AP4

**AP4** (Adaptor Protein complex 4) is a vesicle adaptor complex that facilitates the transport of proteins from the trans-Golgi network to the endosomal system and is required for [[Autophagic Lysosome Reformation]] by enriching lysosomal membrane proteins like [[LAMP1]] for tubulation.
