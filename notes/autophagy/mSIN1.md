# mSIN1
Mammalian stress-activated protein kinase interacting protein (mSIN1) is an essential component of [[mTORC2]] that maintains complex integrity and promotes its recruitment to the [[Plasma Membrane]] via its pleckstrin homology (PH) domain.

## Function

mSIN1 (MAPKAP1) is a component of [[mTORC2]] that binds [[Rictor]] and confers substrate specificity toward [[Akt]] and [[SGK1]].
