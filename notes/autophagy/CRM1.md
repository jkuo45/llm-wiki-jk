# CRM1

Chromosomal maintenance 1 (CRM1), also known as Exportin-1, is a nuclear export receptor that recognizes nuclear export signals (NES) on [[TFEB]] to facilitate its redistribution to the cytoplasm.

## Function

CRM1 (exportin 1) mediates nuclear export of proteins with leucine-rich nuclear export signals (NES). It is the major export receptor for autophagy regulators ([[TFEB]], [[TFE3]], [[ZKSCAN3]], [[FOXO]], [[p53]]).
## Therapeutic Targeting

CRM1 inhibitors ([[Leptomycin B]], [[Selinexor]]/KPT-330) cause nuclear accumulation of [[TFEB]] and induce autophagy. Selinexor is FDA-approved for multiple myeloma.
