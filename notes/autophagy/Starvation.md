# Starvation

**Starvation** is a state of severe nutrient deficiency that triggers profound metabolic and cellular adaptations, including the massive activation of [[notes/_link/Autophagy]].

## Cellular Responses
- **[[notes/_link/Autophagy]] Activation**: Starvation inhibits [[mTORC1]], leading to the activation of the [[ULK1]] complex and the translocation of transcription factors like [[TFEB]] (mammals) and [[HLH-30]] (worms) to the nucleus.
- **[[Lipophagy]]**: Induction of [[Lysosomal Lipolysis]] to mobilize stored fats from [[Lipid Droplet]]s.
- **Transcriptional Reprogramming**: Repression of anabolic genes and activation of catabolic genes, coordinated by switches like [[MXL-3]] and [[HLH-30]].

## Survival Mechanisms
- In [[C. elegans]], the ability to survive starvation is dependent on [[HLH-30]] and the activation of [[Lipophagy]].
