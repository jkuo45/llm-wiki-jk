# MXL-3

**MXL-3** (Max-like 3) is a basic-helix–loop–helix ([[bHLH]]) transcription factor in [[C. elegans]] that acts as a key metabolic switch, repressing [[Lysosomal Lipolysis]] and [[Lipophagy]] under nutrient-rich conditions.

## Function and Regulation
- **Nutrient Repressor**: In well-fed animals, MXL-3 localizes to the nuclei of intestinal cells and sensory neurons, where it represses the expression of lysosomal lipase genes ([[LIPL-1]], [[LIPL-2]], [[LIPL-3]], and [[LIPL-5]]).
- **Fasting Response**: Upon [[Starvation]] or [[Fasting]], *mxl-3* mRNA levels drop, and the MXL-3 protein delocalizes from the nuclei, releasing the repression of its target genes.
- **Lifespan Regulation**: Inactivation of *mxl-3* leads to a constitutive fasting-like transcriptional profile and extends [[C. elegans]] [[notes/_link/Longevity|lifespan]] in a process that is additive to [[notes/_link/Caloric Restriction|caloric restriction]].
- **Antagonism with HLH-30**: MXL-3 and [[HLH-30]] share the same binding sites (E-boxes) and share target genes, functioning as a transcriptional circuit to coordinate the response to [[Nutrient Availability]].

## Function

MXL-3 is a C. elegans MYC/Max-like transcription factor that represses [[Lipophagy]] genes. Its nuclear exclusion during fasting derepresses lipophagy.
