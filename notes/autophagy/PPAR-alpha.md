# PPAR-alpha

Peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor alpha, a nuclear receptor protein that functions as a transcription factor regulating the expression of genes involved in [[Metabolism|lipid metabolism]].

## Structure & Function

PPARα is a nuclear receptor (NR1C1) that forms heterodimers with [[RXR]] and binds to peroxisome proliferator response elements (PPREs). It is activated by fatty acids, fibrates (e.g., [[Fenofibrate]]), and synthetic agonists.
## Metabolic Regulation

PPARα is a master regulator of lipid metabolism, controlling [[Beta-Oxidation]], [[Ketogenesis]], and [[Lipoprotein]] metabolism in the liver and skeletal muscle. It also induces [[Autophagy]] and [[Peroxisome]] proliferation.
## Autophagy Connection

PPARα activation promotes hepatic [[Lipophagy]] and [[Autophagic Flux]] during fasting or [[Ketogenic Diet]]. [[PGC1-α]] acts as a PPARα coactivator, linking mitochondrial biogenesis to autophagy regulation.
