# FNIP1

Folliculin-interacting protein 1, which works with [[Folliculin]] and [[FNIP2]] to regulate [[mTORC1]] and the intracellular localization of [[TFE3]].

## Structure & Interaction

FNIP1 contains a folliculin (FLCN) binding domain, a DENN domain, and AMPK phosphorylation sites. It forms a ternary complex with [[Folliculin]] and [[FNIP2]] that functions as a GTPase-activating protein (GAP) for [[Rag proteins|Rag GTPases]] at the lysosomal surface.
## mTORC1 Regulation

The FLCN-FNIP1/2 complex converts RagC/D from its GTP-bound to GDP-bound state, enabling [[mTORC1]] recruitment to the lysosome in response to [[Amino Acid Sensing|amino acid signals]]. FNIP1 deletion impairs mTORC1 activation and causes constitutive nuclear translocation of [[TFE3]] and [[TFEB]].
## Physiological Role

FNIP1 is ubiquitously expressed with enrichment in kidney and muscle. It is essential for [[Folliculin]]-dependent regulation of cell growth, [[Autophagy]] suppression under nutrient-replete conditions, and metabolic adaptation to fasting.
