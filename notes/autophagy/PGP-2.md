# PGP-2

**PGP-2** is an ABC (ATP-binding cassette) transporter in [[C. elegans]] that is essential for the biogenesis of [[Lysosome]]-related organelles ([[LRO]]s), which serve as fat storage sites.

## Function
- **Lysosomal Marker**: PGP-2 is localized to the membrane of LROs and is frequently used as a marker for the lysosomal compartment in [[C. elegans]].
- **Lipid Mobilization**: Co-localizes with lysosomal lipases like [[LIPL-1]] and [[LIPL-3]]. Its absence results in the loss of gut granules and alterations in lipid storage.

## Function

PGP-2 is a C. elegans lysosomal membrane protein used as a marker for lysosomal content and size. It is regulated by [[HLH-30]]/[[TFEB]].
