# Atg15

**Atg15** is an [[Autophagy|autophagy]] protein in yeast (*Saccharomyces cerevisiae*) with predicted triglyceride-lipase activity.

## Function
- **Lipophagy**: Atg15 has been proposed to catabolize lipid-droplet fat stores through lipophagy within the vacuole (yeast equivalent of the [[Lysosome]]).
- **Lipid Degradation**: It is required for the degradation of autophagic bodies and lipids within the vacuolar lumen.
