# FACT complex

The Facilitates Chromatin Transcription (FACT) complex is a histone chaperone that binds to [[TFEB]] and [[TFE3]] to facilitate the expression of lysosomal and antioxidant genes.

## Function

The FACT complex ([[SSRP1]]/[[SUPT16H]]) is a histone chaperone that facilitates transcription elongation by displacing and replacing H2A-H2B dimers during RNA polymerase II passage.
## Autophagy Connection

FACT regulates chromatin accessibility at autophagy gene promoters. FACT inhibition increases [[Autophagy]] by altering the epigenetic landscape.
