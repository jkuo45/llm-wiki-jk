# SLC36A1-4

**SLC36A1-4** (PAT1–PAT4) constitute the SLC36 family of proton-coupled amino acid transporters. Members localize to the [[Lysosome]] membrane (PAT1/SLC36A1, PAT4/SLC36A4) and the plasma membrane (PAT2/SLC36A2, PAT3/SLC36A3), where they export neutral amino acids using the inward proton gradient. By moving amino acids out of the lysosomal lumen into the cytosol, they connect lysosomal catabolism to cellular nutrient sensing and modulate [[mTORC1]] activity, thereby influencing [[Autophagy]] and lysosomal homeostasis.

## Structure & Transport Mechanism

SLC36 proteins are secondary-active symporters that couple the efflux of neutral amino acids (glycine, alanine, proline, and related small neutrals) to proton influx, exploiting the acidic luminal pH maintained by the v-ATPase. The lysosomal isoforms face the lumen and release amino acids to the cytosol upon autophagic degradation of proteins, whereas surface isoforms import amino acids for salvage. Their activity is thus intrinsically tied to organelle acidification and to the flux of degraded material through the autophagic–lysosomal pathway.

## Role in mTORC1 & Autophagy

Lysosomal PAT1 exports the products of autophagic proteolysis into the cytosol, raising cytosolic amino acid pools that—together with the [[Ragulator]]–[[Rag proteins]] arginine-sensing axis—support [[mTORC1]] activation. Conversely, inhibition or depletion of PAT1 reduces amino-acid-dependent mTORC1 signaling and promotes autophagy. PAT1 therefore acts as a downstream effector that translates lysosomal degradation into growth-control signals, closing the loop between catabolism and anabolism.

## Physiological & Pathological Relevance

Dysregulation of SLC36A members alters amino acid homeostasis linked to [[Cancer]] metabolism, renal and intestinal transport, and lysosomal storage phenotypes. Their ability to tune mTORC1 makes them candidates for interventions aimed at restoring autophagic flux in [[Aging]] and [[Neurodegeneration]], where lysosomal amino acid efflux is often compromised.
