# SLC36A1-4

**SLC36A1-4** (PAT1-4) is a family of proton-coupled amino acid transporters that localize to the [[Lysosome]] membrane and are involved in the export of neutral amino acids and the regulation of [[mTORC1]] activity.

## Function

SLC36 family (PAT1-4) are proton-coupled amino acid transporters in lysosomes and plasma membranes. PAT1 regulates [[mTORC1]] by exporting amino acids from lysosomes.
