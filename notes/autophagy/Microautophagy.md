# Microautophagy

**Microautophagy** is a type of [[Autophagy]] characterized by the direct engulfment of cytoplasmic components by the [[Lysosome|lysosomal]] or vacuolar membrane.

## Characteristics
- Unlike [[Macroautophagy]], it does not involve the formation of [[Autophagosome|autophagosomes]].
- It is generally considered a nonselective process, though selective forms exist.
- It enables the direct sequestration of organelles or cytosolic portions for degradation.

## Functions
- Contributes to the maintenance of organelle size and cellular membrane homeostasis.
- Complements other autophagic pathways in responding to metabolic stress.

## Process

Microautophagy involves direct engulfment of cytoplasmic material by the lysosome/vacuole via membrane invagination, independent of [[Autophagosome]] formation.
