# Dietary Restriction

Dietary Restriction (DR) is a nutritional intervention that reduces total food or specific nutrient intake to promote metabolic health, extend lifespan, and activate [[Macroautophagy|autophagy]].

## Definition

Dietary restriction (DR) is a reduction of nutrient intake without malnutrition. It extends lifespan and healthspan across species by activating [[Autophagy]], [[AMPK]], [[SIRT1]], and inhibiting [[mTORC1]].
## Mechanisms

DR induces [[Autophagy]] in multiple tissues, enhancing proteostasis, mitochondrial function, and stem cell maintenance. [[TFEB]] and [[FOXO]] mediate DR effects.
