# Neurodegenerative Disease

**Neurodegenerative Diseases** are a group of disorders characterized by the progressive degeneration of the structure and function of the central nervous system.

## Common Examples
- [[Alzheimer's Disease]] (AD)
- [[Parkinson's Disease]] (PD)
- [[Huntington's Disease]] (HD)
- Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)

## Role of Autophagy
- A hallmark of many neurodegenerative diseases is the accumulation of misfolded proteins and damaged organelles ([[Proteotoxicity]]).
- [[Autophagy]] serves as a critical housekeeping mechanism to scavenge these deleterious proteins and prevent neuronal death.
- Impaired [[Autophagic Flux]] is frequently observed in patients, coinciding with reduced cognitive and motor potential.

## Therapeutic Interventions
- Inducing autophagy via [[Caloric Restriction]], [[Intermittent Fasting]], or [[Autophagy Inducer|pharmacological inducers]] is a major therapeutic strategy.
- IF has been shown to reduce hippocampal atrophy and neuro-anatomic deterioration in AD models.
