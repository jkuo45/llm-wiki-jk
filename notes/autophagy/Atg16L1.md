# Atg16L1

**Atg16L1** (Autophagy Related 16 Like 1) is a critical component of the [[Atg12]]–[[Atg5]]–[[Atg16L1]] complex, which acts as an E3-like enzyme to facilitate the conjugation of [[Atg8]] family proteins (such as [[LC3]]) to the autophagosomal membrane during the elongation phase of [[Autophagy]].
