# Autophagy Inducer

An **Autophagy Inducer** is a substance or intervention that stimulates [[Autophagy]], the cellular process of degrading and recycling damaged organelles and proteins. Inducing autophagy is a promising strategy to counteract [[Aging]], [[Senescence]], and various age-related diseases.

## Key Inducers
- **Drugs**: [[Rapamycin]], [[Metformin]], [[Spermidine]], and [[Rapalog|rapalogs]] like [[Everolimus]].
- **Lifestyle**: [[Dietary Restriction]] (including fasting), [[Exercise]].
- **Natural Compounds**: [[Polyamine|Polyamines]] like spermidine found in certain foods.

## Mechanisms
Autophagy can be induced through several pathways, most notably:
- Inhibition of [[mTORC1]] (e.g., by rapamycin).
- Activation of [[AMPK]] (e.g., by metformin).
- Transcriptional regulation via factors like [[TFEB]].

## Applications
Autophagy inducers are being investigated for their potential to rejuvenate the [[Immune System]] ([[Immune Aging]]), improve metabolic health, and extend healthy lifespan.
