# PEX3

PEX3 is a crucial gene that encodes a peroxisomal membrane protein required for the correct folding and transport of many other peroxins into the [[Peroxisome]] membrane. It acts as an essential building block for the [[Peroxisome Biogenesis Network]]. Mutations in the PEX3 gene lead to misfolded proteins that accumulate and disrupt membrane integrity, severely impairing peroxisome biogenesis. This dysfunction can result in profound neurological problems and developmental delays, characteristic of conditions like Riordan syndrome or variants of [[Zellweger syndrome]].

## Function

PEX3 is a peroxisomal membrane protein that recruits [[PEX19]] for peroxisome biogenesis. It also functions in [[Pexophagy]] recognition.
