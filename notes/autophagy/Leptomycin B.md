# Leptomycin B

Leptomycin B is a specific inhibitor of [[CRM1]] that impairs the nuclear export of [[TFEB]], leading to its accumulation in the nucleus.

## Pharmacology

Leptomycin B is a potent inhibitor of [[CRM1]] (XPO1)-mediated nuclear export. It covalently modifies Cys528 in the CRM1 cargo binding pocket.
## Autophagy Use

Leptomycin B causes nuclear accumulation of [[TFEB]], [[TFE3]], and [[FOXO]] transcription factors, leading to enhanced [[Autophagy]] and lysosomal biogenesis. It is a research tool, too toxic for therapeutic use.
