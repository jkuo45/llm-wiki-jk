# HNRNPD

Heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoprotein D (HNRNPD), also known as AUF1, is an RNA-binding protein that can promote the degradation of [[TFEB]] pre-mRNA following [[METTL3]]-mediated m6A modification.

## Function

HNRNPD (AUF1) is an RNA-binding protein that regulates mRNA stability by binding to AU-rich elements (AREs) in 3' UTRs of target mRNAs.
## Autophagy Connection

HNRNPD stabilizes [[ULK1]] and [[Beclin1]] mRNAs. Its dysregulation contributes to age-related decline in autophagy.
