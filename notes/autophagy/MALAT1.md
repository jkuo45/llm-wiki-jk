# MALAT1

Metastasis Associated Lung Adenocarcinoma Transcript 1, a long non-coding RNA that can fuse with [[TFEB]] in translocation-associated [[Renal Cell Carcinoma]].

## Molecular Function

MALAT1 is a highly conserved nuclear-retained lncRNA that regulates alternative splicing by modulating serine/arginine-rich (SR) splicing factor phosphorylation and localization. It also scaffolds nuclear speckles.
## Autophagy Regulation

MALAT1 modulates [[Autophagy]] through multiple mechanisms: it sponges [[miR-101]] to upregulate [[STUB1]] expression, interacts with [[TFEB]] to influence lysosomal gene transcription, and regulates [[mTORC1]] signaling under hypoxic stress.
## Disease Relevance

MALAT1 is overexpressed in multiple cancers and promotes metastasis. It also regulates [[Inflammation]], [[Vascular Remodeling]], and metabolic gene expression. Antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) targeting MALAT1 is under investigation.
