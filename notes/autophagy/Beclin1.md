# Beclin1

**Beclin1** (also known as **Atg6** and **BECN1**) is a key regulatory protein in the initiation and regulation of [[Autophagy]]. It functions as a molecular scaffold that assembles the class III [[PI3K]] complex, which produces phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PI3P) to nucleate autophagosome formation.

## Structure and Domains

Beclin1 is a 450-amino-acid protein containing several conserved domains:

- **BH3 Domain** (Bcl-2 homology 3): Mediates interaction with anti-apoptotic [[Bcl-2]] family members ([[Bcl-2]], [[Bcl-xL]], [[Mcl-1]]). Binding of Bcl-2 to the BH3 domain sequesters Beclin1 and inhibits autophagy.
- **CCD** (Coiled-Coil Domain): Mediates homodimerisation and interaction with [[UVRAG]] (UV radiation resistance-associated gene).
- **ECD** (Evolutionarily Conserved Domain): Required for interaction with [[Vps34]] and [[Vps15]], forming the core class III PI3K complex.
- **NLS** (Nuclear Localisation Signal): Mediates nuclear-cytoplasmic shuttling of Beclin1.

## The Class III PI3K Complexes

Beclin1 is the central scaffold of three distinct class III [[PI3K]] complexes:

| Complex | Subunits | Function |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Complex I** | Beclin1-[[Vps34]]-Atg14L-[[Vps15]]/p150 | Autophagosome nucleation |
| **Complex II** | Beclin1-Vps34-[[UVRAG]]-Vps15/p150 | Autophagosome maturation and endocytosis |
| **Complex III** | Beclin1-Vps34-UVRAG-[[Rubicon]]-Vps15/p150 | Suppresses autophagosome maturation |
| **Complex IV** | Beclin1-Vps34-UVRAG-[[Bax-interacting factor 1 (Bif-1)]] | Promotes autophagosome formation |

The balance between these complexes determines whether autophagy is initiated, progresses, or terminated.

## Regulation

Beclin1 activity is tightly regulated at multiple levels:

### Bcl-2 Interaction
- [[Bcl-2]] and [[Bcl-xL]] bind Beclin1 via its BH3 domain, inhibiting autophagy. [[DAPK]] (Death-associated protein kinase) phosphorylates Beclin1 at Thr119 within the BH3 domain, disrupting Bcl-2 binding and freeing Beclin1 to induce autophagy.
- [[JNK1]] phosphorylates Bcl-2, causing its dissociation from Beclin1.

### Phosphorylation
- **[[ULK1]]**: Phosphorylates Beclin1 at Ser14, enhancing Vps34 complex activity.
- **[[MAPKAPK2]]** (MK2): Phosphorylates Beclin1 to regulate its stability.
- **[[Akt]]**: Phosphorylates Beclin1 at Ser234/Ser295, inhibiting autophagy.
- **[[AMPK]]**: Phosphorylates Beclin1 to promote autophagy under energy stress.

### Ubiquitination
- **[[TRAF6]]**: E3 ligase that K63-ubiquitinates Beclin1 at multiple sites, promoting autophagy.
- **[[NEDD4]]**: E3 ligase that K11/K48-ubiquitinates Beclin1, targeting it for proteasomal degradation.

## Cancer and Tumour Suppression

Beclin1 is a well-characterised tumour suppressor:

- **Monoallelic Deletion**: The *BECN1* gene on chromosome 17q21 is monoallelically deleted in 40–75% of sporadic ovarian, breast, and prostate cancers.
- **Mouse Models**: *Becn1* heterozygous knockout mice develop spontaneous malignancies (lymphomas, lung and liver cancers) with high penetrance.
- **Mechanism**: Haploinsufficient Beclin1 impairs autophagy-mediated clearance of damaged mitochondria and protein aggregates, leading to genomic instability, chronic inflammation, and tumour progression.
- **[[BRCA1]] Proximity**: BRCA1 is physically adjacent to BECN1 on 17q21, and co-deletion of both tumour suppressors significantly impacts cancer progression in hereditary breast and ovarian cancers.

## Sirtuin Regulation of Beclin1

- **[[SIRT1]]**: Deacetylates Beclin1 at multiple lysine residues, promoting its interaction with Vps34 and enhancing autophagy. SIRT1-mediated Beclin1 deacetylation is required for starvation-induced autophagy.
- **[[SIRT2]]**: Regulates Beclin1 activity through deacetylation of FOXO-dependent transcription of autophagy genes.
- **[[SIRT3]]**: Enhances mitophagy by maintaining mitochondrial NAD⁺ levels and indirectly supporting Beclin1 signalling.
