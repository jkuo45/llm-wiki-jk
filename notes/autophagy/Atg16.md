# Atg16

**Atg16** (Autophagy-related 16) is a critical protein in the [[Autophagy]] pathway, where it forms a complex essential for [[LC3]] lipidation.

## Atg12-Atg5-Atg16 Complex
- Atg16 non-covalently binds to the [[Atg12]]-[[Atg5]] conjugate.
- This complex multimerizes and localizes to the expanding phagophore.
- It functions as an E3-like enzyme, specifying the site of [[LC3-II]] formation and facilitating the conjugation of LC3 to phosphatidylethanolamine on the [[Autophagosome]] membrane.

## Significance
- Essential for the correct assembly and elongation of the autophagosomal membrane.
- Dysregulation is associated with several inflammatory and metabolic conditions.
