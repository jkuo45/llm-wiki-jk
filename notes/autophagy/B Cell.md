# B Cell

B cells are lymphocytes of the humoral [[Adaptive Immune Response]] that produce antibodies, present antigen, and give rise to long-lived plasma and memory cells. Beyond their immune functions, B cells depend on [[Autophagy]] for metabolic homeostasis, survival under stress, proliferation, and the generation of durable immunological memory. Autophagy in B cells intersects with ER stress management (given high rates of immunoglobulin synthesis) and with mitochondrial quality control during differentiation.

## Autophagy in B Cell Biology

During development in the bone marrow and peripheral activation, B cells experience bursts of protein synthesis and proliferative stress. [[Autophagy]] clears misfolded immunoglobulin aggregates and damaged organelles, preventing ER stress–driven apoptosis. In differentiating [[Plasma Cell|plasma cells]], autophagy supports the extraordinary secretory demand and sustains longevity. Memory B cell formation and maintenance also require basal autophagy to preserve metabolic fitness and mitochondrial integrity.

## Selective Forms & Regulation

B cells employ [[Selective Autophagy]] including [[Mitophagy]] to remove depolarized mitochondria and [[Aggrephagy]] to handle protein aggregates. Autophagy is regulated in lymphocytes by [[mTORC1]] (restrained by T cell help and cytokine signals) and by [[AMPK]] during metabolic starvation. Atg5/Atg7 deficiency in B cells causes defective maturation, reduced antibody responses, and impaired memory formation, demonstrating non-redundant roles distinct from those in T cells.

## Physiological & Pathological Relevance

Autophagy defects in B cells contribute to immunodeficiency, defective vaccine responses, and [[Aging]]-associated immune senescence ("immune aging"). Conversely, excessive or dysregulated autophagy can support survival of malignant B cells in [[Cancer]] (e.g., B-cell lymphomas, chronic lymphocytic leukemia) under hypoxia and chemotherapy pressure, making autophagy a candidate therapeutic target. B cell autophagy also modulates autoimmunity and inflammatory cytokine production, linking it to [[Oxidative Stress]] and tissue inflammation.
