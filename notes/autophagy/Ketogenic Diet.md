# Ketogenic Diet

A **Ketogenic Diet** is a high-fat, adequate-protein, low-carbohydrate dietary regimen that forces the body to burn fats rather than carbohydrates.

## Metabolic State
- The diet mimics the metabolic state of fasting by inducing [[Ketogenesis]].
- This results in elevated levels of ketone bodies in the blood, which serve as an alternative energy source.

## Relationship with Autophagy
- Ketogenic diets have been shown to stimulate [[Autophagy]] and increase [[notes/_link/Longevity]] in various animal models.
- The induction of autophagy is thought to contribute to the neuroprotective effects of the diet.

## Therapeutic Applications
- Traditionally used to treat refractory epilepsy in children.
- Investigated for its potential in treating [[notes/_link/Neurodegenerative Diseases|neurodegenerative diseases]] like [[notes/_link/Alzheimer's Disease]] and [[notes/_link/Parkinson's Disease]].
- In [[Cancer]] research, its effects are complex; while it can enhance healing mechanisms, it may also contribute to conflicting outcomes depending on the tumor type and treatment regimen.
