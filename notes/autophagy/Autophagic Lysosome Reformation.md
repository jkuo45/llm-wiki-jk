# Autophagic Lysosome Reformation

**Autophagic Lysosome Reformation** (ALR) is a mechanism by which functional [[Lysosome]]s are regenerated from [[Autolysosome]]s following the degradation of autophagic cargo, a process regulated by [[mTOR]], [[PIKfyve]], and [[PtdIns(4,5)P2]].

## Mechanism

ALR regenerates functional [[Lysosome]]s from degradative [[Autolysosome]]s. Tubules extrude from the autolysosome and bud into protolysosomes via clathrin-mediated membrane sorting.
