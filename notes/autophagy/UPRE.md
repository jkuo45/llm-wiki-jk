# UPRE

The Unfolded Protein Response Element (UPRE) is a DNA sequence found in the promoters of target genes, including [[TFEB]], where transcription factors like [[XBP1]] bind to regulate expression.

## Function

The unfolded protein response element (UPRE) in the promoters of ER chaperone genes is bound by [[XBP1]] and [[ATF6α]] during [[ER Stress]].
