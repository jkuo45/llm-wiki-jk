# MAP4K3

Mitogen-activating protein kinase kinase kinase kinase-3 (MAP4K3), also known as germinal center kinase-like kinase (GLK), is a Ste20-family serine/threonine kinase that integrates amino acid availability with mTORC1 signaling and the regulation of the autophagy-lysosome transcription factor [[TFEB]]. It phosphorylates TFEB at serine 3 (S3), a priming modification that licenses subsequent phosphorylation by [[mTORC1]] and thereby governs TFEB cytoplasmic retention and inactivation.

## Structure & Domains

MAP4K3 contains an N-terminal catalytic domain related to the germinal center kinase (GCK) subfamily and a C-terminal regulatory region with a coiled-coil domain that mediates oligomerization and scaffolding interactions. Like other Ste20 kinases, it lacks the classic kinase + kinase-kinase architecture of canonical MAP3Ks but functions upstream of MAPK cascades (JNK, ERK) and of mTORC1.

## Mechanism of Action

In nutrient-replete cells, MAP4K3 is activated by amino acid signaling and phosphorylates TFEB at S3. This creates the docking site for mTORC1 (acting with its Ragulator-Rag scaffold at the [[Lysosome]]) to phosphorylate additional TFEB residues (notably S211 and S142), promoting TFEB 14-3-3 binding and cytosolic sequestration, suppressing [[Autophagy]] and lysosomal gene expression. Thus MAP4K3 is a priming kinase that couples amino acid sufficiency to TFEB inhibition. Independently, MAP4K3 positively regulates [[mTORC1]] via the [[Rag proteins]] and activates stress MAPKs (JNK, ERK) downstream of nutrient and inflammatory cues, linking catabolic control to proliferation and immune signaling.

## Physiological & Pathological Relevance

By gating TFEB phosphorylation, MAP4K3 sits at the nutrient-sensing hub that decides between growth (mTORC1-on) and catabolism (autophagy-on). Its dysregulation is relevant to [[Cancer]], where MAP4K3/GLK overexpression supports proliferative and survival signaling, and to [[Aging]], where altered amino acid sensing erodes autophagic resilience. Loss of MAP4K3 relieves TFEB suppression and enhances [[Autophagy]], suggesting it as a target to restore lysosomal clearance in [[Neurodegeneration]] and storage diseases. Because MAP4K3 also modulates innate immune responses (e.g., T-cell activation via JNK), it bridges metabolic and inflammatory control of cellular homeostasis.
