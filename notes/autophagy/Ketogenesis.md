# Ketogenesis

**Ketogenesis** is the metabolic process by which the liver produces ketone bodies (such as acetoacetate and β-hydroxybutyrate) from fatty acids.

## Triggering Factors
- Ketogenesis is induced during periods of low glucose availability, such as:
    - [[Caloric Restriction]]
    - [[Intermittent Fasting]]
    - Consumption of a [[Ketogenic Diet]]

## Relationship with Autophagy
- Ketone bodies and the ketogenic state have been shown to stimulate [[Autophagy]] in various tissues, including the brain and heart.
- Ketogenesis provides an alternative energy source for cells during starvation, helping to maintain [[Cellular Homeostasis]].

## Therapeutic Potential
- The promotion of ketogenesis via diet or fasting is investigated for its potential to treat [[Neurodegenerative Disease]] and enhance the tumoricidal properties of treatments in some [[Cancer]] contexts.
