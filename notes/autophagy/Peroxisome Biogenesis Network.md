# Peroxisome Biogenesis Network

The Peroxisome Biogenesis Network (PBN) is a dynamic, interconnected pool of precursor peroxisomes primarily residing in the cytosol and closely interacting with the endoplasmic reticulum (ER). The PBN is critical for the generation of new [[Peroxisome]]s. During [[Selective Autophagy]], damaged peroxisomes are broken down and their recycled fragments, along with new lipids and proteins supplied by the ER, are incorporated into the PBN to form new precursors. This constant turnover and amplification within the PBN ensure a healthy population of peroxisomes. Dysfunction in genes like [[PEX1]] or [[PEX3]] severely impairs the PBN and peroxisome biogenesis.

## Pathway

The PBN involves [[PEX]] genes for peroxisome membrane assembly, matrix protein import, and proliferation. Defects cause Zellweger syndrome.
