# V-ATPase

The **V-ATPase** (Vacuolar-type H+-ATPase) is a multi-subunit proton pump that acidifies the [[Lysosome]] lumen, enabling the activation of acid hydrolases and facilitating nutrient sensing by [[mTORC1]].
