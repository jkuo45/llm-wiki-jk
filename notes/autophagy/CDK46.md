# CDK4/6

Cyclin-dependent kinases 4 and 6 (CDK4/6) are cell cycle regulators that also phosphorylate [[TFEB]] in the nucleus to promote its [[CRM1]]-dependent nuclear export.

## Function

CDK4 and CDK6 are cyclin-dependent kinases that phosphorylate [[Rb]] to drive G1-to-S cell cycle progression. They are targets of therapeutic inhibitors [[Palbociclib]], [[Ribociclib]], [[Abemaciclib]].
## Autophagy Connection

CDK4/6 inhibition induces [[Autophagy]] in cancer cells, representing a resistance mechanism. Dual CDK4/6 and autophagy inhibition is under clinical investigation.
