# RHEB-1

**RHEB-1** is the [[C. elegans]] orthologue of the small GTPase [[Rheb]]. It is a key activator of [[mTOR]] ([[CeTOR]]).

## Function and Regulation
- **mTOR Activation**: RHEB-1 activates CeTOR in response to nutrient signals.
- **Intermittent Fasting**: RHEB-1 is required for the lifespan extension observed in certain intermittent fasting models in [[C. elegans]].
- **Independence from MXL-3**: The longevity phenotype of [[MXL-3]] mutants is not suppressed by the inactivation of *rheb-1*, suggesting they act through different pathways.
