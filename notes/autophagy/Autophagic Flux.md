# Autophagic Flux

Autophagic flux refers to the complete dynamic process of [[Autophagy]], spanning from the formation of the [[Autophagosome]] to its fusion with the [[Lysosome]] and subsequent degradation of cargo.

## Measurement

Autophagic flux is the rate of autophagic degradation through the entire pathway (formation → fusion → degradation). It is measured by LC3-II turnover, p62/SQSTM1 levels, and tandem mCherry-GFP-LC3 probes.
