# XBP1

X-box binding protein 1 (XBP1) is a transcription factor that plays a crucial role in the unfolded protein response (UPR) and regulates hepatic autophagy by activating [[TFEB]].

## Function

XBP1 is a bZIP transcription factor in the unfolded protein response (UPR). Its active form (XBP1s) arises from IRE1-mediated alternative splicing of XBP1 mRNA during [[ER Stress]].
## Autophagy Connection

XBP1s transcriptionally activates autophagy genes including [[Beclin1]] and [[LC3B]]. XBP1 deficiency impairs autophagy and sensitizes cells to ER stress.
