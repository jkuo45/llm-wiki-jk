# MITF

Microphthalmia-associated transcription factor, a member of the [[MiTF TFE family|MiTF/TFE family]] involved in melanocyte development and lysosomal regulation.

## Structure & Family

MITF is a bHLH-LZ transcription factor of the [[MiTF TFE family]]. It recognizes E-box (CANNTG) and M-box motifs. Multiple isoforms (MITF-M, MITF-A, MITF-H) arise from alternative promoters with distinct tissue-specific expression.
## Functions

MITF is the master regulator of [[Melanocytes]] development, differentiation, and survival. It controls genes for melanogenesis ([[TYR]], [[TYRP1]], [[DCT]]), and also regulates [[Osteoclast]] differentiation and [[Mast Cell]] function. MITF transcriptionally activates [[Cathepsin K]] in osteoclasts.
## Autophagy Link

MITF regulates lysosomal biogenesis and [[Autophagy]] via the CLEAR network. It interacts with [[TFEB]] and [[TFE3]] and can compensate for their loss in certain contexts.
