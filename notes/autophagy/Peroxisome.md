# Peroxisome

Peroxisomes are small, membrane-bound organelles found in nearly all eukaryotic cells. They play essential roles in lipid metabolism, particularly the [[Beta-Oxidation]] of very long-chain fatty acids, and the detoxification of harmful substances like hydrogen peroxide via catalase. Peroxisomes are formed through the [[Peroxisome Biogenesis Network]] (PBN) and maintained by [[Selective Autophagy]] (pexophagy) when damaged, a process requiring proteins like [[PERO1]], [[ESCRT]], and [[LC3]]. Mutations in [[PEX Genes]] disrupt peroxisome formation and function, leading to severe metabolic disorders such as [[Zellweger syndrome]] and [[Adrenoleukodystrophy]]. 

## Function

Peroxisomes are organelles for β-oxidation of VLCFAs, ether lipid synthesis, and ROS metabolism. They are degraded by [[Pexophagy]].
