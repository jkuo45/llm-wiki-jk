# Parkin

**Parkin** is an E3 ubiquitin ligase that is recruited to damaged mitochondria by [[Pink1]] to initiate [[Mitophagy]], a process that also involves the activation of [[TFEB]] and [[TFE3]] for lysosome biogenesis.
