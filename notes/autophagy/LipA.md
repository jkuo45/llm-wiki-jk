# LipA

**LipA** is the mouse [[Lysosomal Acid Lipase]], the functional orthologue of human [[Lysosomal Acid Lipase|LAL]] and [[C. elegans]] [[LIPL-1]] and [[LIPL-3]].

## Regulation
- **Fasting Response**: *LipA* mRNA levels are induced in the liver of fasted mice (e.g., C57BL/6J) following overnight food deprivation.
- **Transcriptional Control**: Its induction during fasting is linked to the activity of [[TFEB]].

## Function

LipA (lysosomal acid lipase, LAL) hydrolyzes cholesteryl esters and triglycerides in the [[Lysosome]]. LAL deficiency causes [[Wolman Disease]] and [[CESD]].
