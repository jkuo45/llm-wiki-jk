# BEC-1

**BEC-1** is the [[C. elegans]] orthologue of the mammalian [[Beclin1]] and yeast [[Atg6]] proteins. It is a key regulator of [[Autophagy]] and is part of the Class III [[Vps34|PI3K]] complex.

## Role in Autophagy
- Essential for the nucleation of the [[Autophagosome]].
- **Nutrient Regulation**: Its activity is modulated by various signaling pathways, although the [[MXL-3]] longevity phenotype is reported to be independent of *bec-1* function.

## Function

BEC-1 is the C. elegans ortholog of mammalian [[Beclin1]]. It is required for [[Autophagy]] and longevity extension by dietary restriction.
