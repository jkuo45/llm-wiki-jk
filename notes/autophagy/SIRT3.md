# SIRT3

**SIRT3** (Sirtuin 3) is the primary NAD+-dependent deacetylase localized within the [[Mitochondria]].

## Functions
- SIRT3 is a master regulator of mitochondrial metabolism and antioxidant defenses.
- It is essential for maintaining mitochondrial health and preventing the accumulation of [[oxidative_stress/Reactive Oxygen Species]] (ROS).
- SIRT3 is induced by [[Caloric Restriction]] and [[Intermittent Fasting]].

## Role in Autophagy and Cancer
- SIRT3 acts as a suppressive control checkpoint in response to energy stress.
- In [[Cancer]] cells, SIRT3 is often inactive, contributing to metabolic inflexibility and genomic instability.
- SIRT3 can promote [[Mitophagy]] to clear damaged mitochondria and maintain cellular homeostasis.
