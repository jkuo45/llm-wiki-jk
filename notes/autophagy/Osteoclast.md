# Osteoclast

A type of bone cell that breaks down bone tissue, whose function involves specialized lysosomal secretion regulated by [[TFEB]] and [[MITF]].

## Differentiation & Regulation

Osteoclasts derive from the monocyte/macrophage lineage under the influence of [[RANKL]] and [[M-CSF]] signaling. The transcription factors [[MITF]], [[PU.1]], and [[NFATc1]] are master regulators of osteoclastogenesis. [[TFEB]] and [[TFE3]] control the lysosomal gene expression required for bone resorption.
## Bone Resorption Mechanism

Osteoclasts form a ruffled border membrane that seals against bone matrix, creating an acidic resorption lacuna (pH ~4.5). [[V-ATPase]] pumps protons into this space, while [[Cathepsin K]] and [[MMP-9]] degrade type I collagen and other matrix proteins. Degraded products are endocytosed and transcytosed for release.
## Autophagy Connection

Autophagy supports osteoclast survival during the high metabolic demand of resorption. [[TFEB]] coordinates lysosomal biogenesis and autophagic flux in osteoclasts. Dysregulation of autophagy in osteoclasts is implicated in [[Osteoporosis]] and [[Paget's Disease of Bone]].
