# Mucopolysaccharidosis-IIIA

Also known as Sanfilippo syndrome type A, a [[Lysosomal Storage Diseases|lysosomal storage disease]] characterized by the accumulation of heparan sulfate.

## Etiology

MPS IIIA (Sanfilippo syndrome A) is caused by deficiency of sulfamidase (SGSH), a lysosomal enzyme required for heparan sulfate degradation. Autosomal recessive; incidence ~1:100,000.
## Pathophysiology

Accumulation of heparan sulfate in [[Lysosome|lysosomes]] triggers secondary storage of GM2 gangliosides, cholesterol, and autophagic substrates. [[Autophagic Flux]] is impaired due to lysosomal dysfunction, contributing to severe neurodegeneration.
## Clinical Features

Onset at 2-6 years with developmental delay, behavioral problems, sleep disturbance, coarse facies, hepatosplenomegaly, and progressive dementia. Death typically in the second decade. No approved CNS-penetrant therapy exists.
