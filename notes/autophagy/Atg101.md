# Atg101

Atg101 is a protein that stabilizes [[Atg13]] and is an essential component of the [[ULK1]] complex required for the induction of [[Macroautophagy|autophagy]].

## Function

Atg101 is a small protein that stabilizes [[Atg13]] and facilitates its interaction with [[Atg1]]/ULK1. It is essential for autophagy initiation in higher eukaryotes but absent in yeast.
