# HLH-30

**HLH-30** is the *[[Caenorhabditis elegans]]* orthologue of the mammalian transcription factor [[TFEB]], serving as a master regulator of [[Autophagy]] and [[Lysosome]] biogenesis and playing a key role in lifespan extension and stress resistance.
