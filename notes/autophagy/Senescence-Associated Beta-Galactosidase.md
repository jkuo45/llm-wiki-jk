# Senescence-Associated Beta-Galactosidase

**Senescence-Associated Beta-Galactosidase** (SA β-gal) is a widely used [[notes/_link/Biomarkers|Biomarker]] for [[notes/_link/Senescence]].

## Detection
- SA β-gal activity can be monitored using advanced flow cytometry in human blood samples.
- It reflects lysosomal health and the accumulation of senescent cells.

## Other Biomarkers
- Often monitored alongside other senescence markers such as [[CDKN1A]] (p21) and [[notes/_link/p53|TP53]] (p53).

## Assay

SA-β-gal activity at pH 6.0 is the most widely used [[Senescence]] biomarker. It reflects increased lysosomal content in senescent cells and correlates with [[Autophagy]] status.
