# Birt-Hogg-Dube syndrome

A rare genetic disorder characterized by skin tumors, lung cysts, and an increased risk of [[Renal Cell Carcinoma]], caused by mutations in [[Folliculin]].

## Etiology

BHD syndrome is an autosomal dominant disorder caused by loss-of-function mutations in [[Folliculin]] ([[FLCN]]), a tumor suppressor that regulates [[mTORC1]] signaling in complex with [[FNIP1]] and [[FNIP2]].
## Clinical Features

Characterized by benign skin tumors (fibrofolliculomas), lung cysts with spontaneous [[Pneumothorax]], and increased risk for bilateral renal cell carcinoma (especially chromophobe and hybrid oncocytic types). Onset typically in the third decade.
## Autophagy Connection

FLCN loss causes constitutive nuclear translocation of [[TFE3]] and [[TFEB]], leading to hyperactive lysosomal biogenesis and [[Autophagy]]. This suggests that BHD pathology involves dysregulated nutrient sensing and autophagy.
