# SGK1
Serum- and glucocorticoid-induced protein kinase 1 (SGK1) is a member of the AGC family of kinases that is activated by [[mTORC2]] and shares functional similarities with [[notes/_link/Akt|AKT]] in regulating cell survival and stress responses.
