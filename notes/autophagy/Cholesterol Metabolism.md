# Cholesterol Metabolism

Cholesterol metabolism involves the pathways for cholesterol synthesis, transport, and regulation; enzymes like [[ACAT1]] in these pathways can also regulate [[TFEB]] activity via acetylation.

## Pathways

Cholesterol metabolism involves biosynthesis via the [[Mevalonate Pathway]], uptake via [[LDL Receptor]], esterification by [[ACAT1]], and efflux via [[ABCA1]] and [[ABCG1]].
## Autophagy Connection

[[Autophagy]] delivers cholesterol from lipid droplets to [[Lysosome]]s for hydrolysis by [[Lysosomal Acid Lipase]]. Cholesterol accumulation impairs autophagic flux, while autophagy supports cholesterol efflux.
