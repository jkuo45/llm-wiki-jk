# SetA

SetA is an effector protein from [[Legionella pneumophila]] that glucosylates [[TFEB]] at multiple sites, leading to its nuclear enrichment by interfering with [[14-3-3 protein]] binding.

## Function

SetA is a [[Legionella pneumophila]] effector with glucosyltransferase activity that modifies host [[Rab5]] and [[Rab7]] to subvert endosomal trafficking and block [[Autophagy]] during infection.
