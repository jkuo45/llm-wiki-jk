# PtdIns(3,5)P2

**PtdIns(3,5)P2** (Phosphatidylinositol 3,5-bisphosphate) is a low-abundance phosphoinositide produced by [[PIKfyve]] that regulates [[Lysosome]] dynamics and reformation by activating the calcium channel [[TRPML1]].

## Function

Phosphatidylinositol 3,5-bisphosphate is a low-abundance phosphoinositide produced by [[PIKfyve]] from [[PtdIns3P]]. It regulates endolysosomal trafficking, ion channel activity ([[TRPML1]]), and autophagic flux.
