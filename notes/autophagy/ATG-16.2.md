# ATG-16.2

**ATG-16.2** is a [[C. elegans]] orthologue of the yeast [[Atg16]] and mammalian [[Atg16L1]] proteins. It is essential for the formation of the [[Autophagosome]].

## Function and Regulation
- **Autophagy Mechanism**: Part of the [[Atg12]]-[[Atg5]]-ATG-16.2 complex required for [[LGG-1]]/[[LGG-2]] lipidation.
- **Transcriptional Control**: Its expression is induced during [[Fasting]] and [[Starvation]] in an [[HLH-30]]-dependent manner.
- **Longevity**: Inactivation of *atg-16.2* does not suppress the longevity of [[MXL-3]] mutants, suggesting that the extended lifespan in that specific model is primarily driven by activated [[Lysosomal Lipolysis]] rather than increased macroautophagy.

## Function

ATG-16.2 is the C. elegans ortholog of [[Atg16L1]], functioning in the Atg12-Atg5-Atg16L1 complex for LC3 lipidation.
