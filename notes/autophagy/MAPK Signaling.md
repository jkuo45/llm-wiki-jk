# MAPK Signaling

Mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) signaling pathways communicate signals from cell surface receptors to the nucleus; the [[p38 MAPK]] pathway is a key regulator of [[TFEB]] localization.

## Pathway

The MAPK cascade (RAF → MEK → ERK) transduces growth factor and stress signals. It crosstalks with [[Autophagy]] through [[TFEB]] phosphorylation by [[ERK2]].
