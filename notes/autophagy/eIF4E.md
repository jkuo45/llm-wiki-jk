# eIF4E
Eukaryotic initiation factor 4E (eIF4E) is a key protein in [[cap-dependent translation]] that binds the 5' cap of [[mRNA]]; its activity is regulated by [[mTORC1]] through the phosphorylation of [[4E-BP1]].
