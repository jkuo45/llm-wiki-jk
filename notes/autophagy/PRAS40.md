# PRAS40
Proline-rich AKT substrate 40 kDa (PRAS40) is a component and negative regulator of [[mTORC1]] that inhibits the complex by functioning as a direct inhibitor of substrate binding until it is phosphorylated by [[notes/_link/Akt|AKT]].

## Function

PRAS40 (AKT1S1) is an [[mTORC1]] inhibitor that binds [[Raptor]]. [[AKT]]-mediated phosphorylation of PRAS40 at T246 relieves this inhibition.
