# WHAMM

**WHAMM** (WASP Homolog Associated with Actin, Membranes, and Microtubules) is an actin-nucleation promoting factor that facilitates branched actin network formation on autolysosomes to promote [[Autophagic Lysosome Reformation]] tubulation.
