# Tumor Suppressor

A tumor suppressor is a protein that protects a cell from one step on the path to cancer; an example is [[PDCD4]], which inhibits the translation of the potentially oncogenic [[TFEB]].

## Definition

Tumor suppressors ([[p53]], [[Rb]], [[PTEN]], [[BRCA1]], [[Beclin1]], [[Folliculin]], [[TSC1]]/[[TSC2]]) inhibit cell proliferation and promote [[Autophagy]] as a tumor-suppressive mechanism.
