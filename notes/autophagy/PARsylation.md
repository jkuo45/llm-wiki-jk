# PARsylation

Poly-ADP-ribosylation (PARsylation) is a reversible post-translational modification where poly(ADP-ribose) is added to proteins; it regulates [[TFEB]] nuclear localization via [[Tankyrases]].

## Mechanism

PARsylation is the addition of poly-ADP-ribose chains to target proteins by PARP family enzymes ([[TNKS1]], [[PARP1]], [[PARP2]]). It uses NAD+ as a substrate, linking it to cellular energy status.
## Autophagy Connection

PARsylation regulates [[Autophagy]] through modification of [[ULK1]], [[TFEB]], and [[mTORC1]] components. PARP inhibitors ([[Olaparib]]) induce autophagy in cancer cells.
