# 4E-BP1
Eukaryotic initiation factor 4E (eIF4E)-binding protein 1 (4E-BP1) is a major downstream effector of [[mTORC1]] that, when unphosphorylated, inhibits [[protein synthesis]] by sequestering [[eIF4E]].
