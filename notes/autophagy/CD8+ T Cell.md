# CD8+ T Cell

**CD8+ T Cells** (also known as cytotoxic T lymphocytes) are a subset of [[T Cell|T cells]] that play a critical role in the [[Adaptive Immune Response]] by killing infected or cancerous cells.

## Modulation by Autophagy and Fasting
- [[Autophagy]] is essential for the activation, expansion, and memory formation of CD8+ T cells.
- Reduced calorie intake and [[Intermittent Fasting]] have been shown to make tumor cells more susceptible to apoptosis via the stimulation of CD8+ T cells.
- Under fasting conditions, metabolically reprogrammed CD8+ T cells exhibit improved memory immune responses.

## Function

CD8+ T cells kill infected or malignant cells. [[Autophagy]] supports memory CD8+ T cell formation and metabolic fitness.
