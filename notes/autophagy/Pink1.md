# Pink1

**Pink1** (PTEN-induced kinase 1) is a mitochondrial serine/threonine kinase that senses mitochondrial dysfunction and recruits [[Parkin]] to initiate the selective degradation of mitochondria via [[Mitophagy]].

## Function

PINK1 is a serine/threonine kinase that accumulates on depolarized mitochondria and recruits [[Parkin]] to initiate [[Mitophagy]].
## Regulation

PINK1 is constitutively imported into mitochondria and cleaved by [[PARL]]; loss of membrane potential prevents import, leading to PINK1 stabilization on the outer mitochondrial membrane. PINK1 mutations cause early-onset [[Parkinson's Disease]].
