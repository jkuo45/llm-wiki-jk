# Dopamine Neuron

Dopamine neurons are nerve cells that produce dopamine; their loss is a hallmark of Parkinson's disease, and [[TFEB]]-mediated autophagy has been shown to protect them from [[Alpha-synuclein]] toxicity.

## Function

Dopamine neurons in the [[Substantia Nigra]] pars compacta control movement, motivation, and reward. They are selectively vulnerable in [[Parkinson's Disease]].
## Autophagy Role

Basal [[Autophagy]] is essential for dopamine neuron survival. Impairment of autophagy (via [[Atg7]] or [[TFEB]] deletion in mice) causes dopamine neuron degeneration and protein aggregation.
