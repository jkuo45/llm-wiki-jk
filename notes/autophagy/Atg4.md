# Atg4

Atg4 is a cysteine protease that processes the C-terminus of [[Atg8]] family proteins, a critical step for their conjugation to phosphatidylethanolamine during [[Autophagosome]] formation.

## Function

Atg4 is a cysteine protease that processes [[Atg8]]/LC3 at its C-terminal Gly residue to expose the site for lipid conjugation. It also deconjugates Atg8 from membranes, enabling recycling.
