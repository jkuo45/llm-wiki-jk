# PEX1

PEX1 is a critical gene that encodes a peroxisomal membrane protein (peroxin) essential for the assembly and biogenesis of the [[Peroxisome]]. It plays a central role in the formation of the [[Peroxisome Biogenesis Network]] and the import of other necessary proteins into the peroxisomal membrane. Mutations leading to a loss of PEX1 function cause a complete lack of functional peroxisomes, resulting in severe autosomal recessive peroxisomal disorders such as [[Zellweger syndrome]]. This leads to the toxic accumulation of very long-chain fatty acids due to absent [[Beta-Oxidation]].

## Function

PEX1 is a peroxisomal AAA-ATPase required for receptor recycling during peroxisome biogenesis. Mutations cause Zellweger syndrome.
