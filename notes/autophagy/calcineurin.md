# calcineurin

**calcineurin** is a calcium-dependent protein phosphatase that plays a critical role in [[Lysosome]] biogenesis by dephosphorylating [[TFEB]] and [[TFE3]], thereby promoting their nuclear translocation and activation in response to lysosomal Ca2+ release via [[TRPML1]].

## Function

Calcineurin (PPP3CA) is a Ca2+/calmodulin-dependent phosphatase that dephosphorylates [[TFEB]] at S211 and related sites, triggering its nuclear translocation and activating [[Autophagy]].
