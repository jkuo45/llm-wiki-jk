# Single-cell Omics

**Single-cell omics** refers to a suite of high-throughput technologies that profile the genomic, epigenomic, transcriptomic, or proteomic state of individual cells, circumventing the averaging inherent in bulk measurements. In the study of [[Autophagy]], single-cell approaches have revealed pronounced cell-to-cell heterogeneity in autophagic flux, lysosomal capacity, and nutrient-sensing, clarifying how [[mTORC1]] and [[TFEB]] programs vary between cells within the same tissue during [[Aging]], [[Cancer]], and [[Neurodegeneration]].

## Core Technologies

Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) captures transcriptomes at single-cell resolution, enabling inference of autophagic and lysosomal gene modules (e.g., [[ATG Genes]], LAMP1/2, cathepsins). Single-cell ATAC-seq (scATAC-seq) maps chromatin accessibility, exposing regulatory states of autophagy transcription factors such as TFEB/TFE3 and FOXO. Single-cell proteomics and phospho-proteomics quantify actual protein abundances and post-translational states (e.g., [[LC3B]] lipidation proxies), while spatial transcriptomics preserves tissue context. Combined multiomic profiling links regulatory DNA states to transcripts and proteins in the same cell.

## Application to Autophagy Research

These tools dissect how autophagic activity is distributed across cell types and states—e.g., identifying subpopulations of senescent or metabolically stressed cells with high basal autophagy, or tumor cells that evade autophagic death. Lineage-tracing coupled to scRNA-seq reveals how [[Mitophagy]] and [[Selective Autophagy]] programs shift during differentiation. Perturbation atlases (CRISPR screens + scRNA-seq) map which [[ATG Genes]] are required in specific contexts, informing therapeutic targeting.

## Physiological & Pathological Relevance

Single-cell omics has exposed that autophagic heterogeneity underlies resistant clones in [[Cancer]] and focal vulnerability in [[Neurodegeneration]]. It also enables mapping of immune-autophagy cross-talk at single-cell resolution, relevant to [[T Cell]] function. As cost falls, these methods are becoming standard for stratifying patients by autophagic–lysosomal health and for discovering biomarkers of [[Aging]].
