# ESCRT

The Endosomal Sorting Complexes Required for Transport (ESCRT) is a protein machinery generally involved in membrane remodeling, vesicle budding, and cargo sorting within endosomes. In the context of [[Peroxisome]] quality control, ESCRT plays a crucial role in [[Selective Autophagy]]. When a damaged peroxisome is properly tagged with specific ubiquitin chains and identified by lipid sensors like [[PERO1]], the ESCRT complex is recruited to the organelle. It subsequently interacts with autophagic proteins such as [[LC3]] to initiate the engulfment of the defective peroxisome into an autophagosome for degradation and recycling.
