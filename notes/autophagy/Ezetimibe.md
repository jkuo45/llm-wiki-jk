# Ezetimibe

Ezetimibe is a cholesterol-lowering drug that ameliorates steatohepatitis via [[AMPK]]-mediated activation of [[TFEB]] and subsequent autophagy.

## Pharmacology

Ezetimibe inhibits the [[NPC1L1]] cholesterol transporter at the intestinal brush border, reducing dietary and biliary cholesterol absorption by ~50%. It acts synergistically with [[Statins]] for LDL-C reduction.
## Autophagy Connection

Ezetimibe has been shown to induce hepatic [[Autophagy]] and reduce steatosis in NAFLD models by modulating cholesterol flux. Its autophagy-inducing effects may contribute to its anti-inflammatory and anti-atherogenic benefits.
