# Atg14

Atg14, also known as Atg14L or Barkor, is an essential subunit of the [[Vps34]]-containing class III PI3K complex I that determines its localization to the [[Autophagosome]] isolation membrane.

## Function

Atg14 is a component of the class III [[PI3K]] complex I (PIK3C3-C1) that targets the complex to the phagophore assembly site for [[Autophagosome]] initiation.
