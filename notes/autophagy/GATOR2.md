# GATOR2

GATOR2 is a protein complex that negatively regulates [[GATOR1]], thereby promoting [[mTORC1]] activation in the presence of amino acids.

## Composition

GATOR2 is a large multi-protein complex containing [[MIOS]], [[WDR24]], [[WDR59]], [[SEH1L]], [[SEC13]], and [[Mio]]. It acts as a positive regulator of [[mTORC1]] by inhibiting [[GATOR1]].
## Regulation

GATOR2 is inhibited by [[CASTOR1]]/[[CASTOR2]] (arginine sensing) and [[Sestrin2]] (leucine sensing). Nutrient binding to these sensors releases GATOR2, enabling mTORC1 activation.
