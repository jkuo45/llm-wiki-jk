# KIF5B

**KIF5B** (Kinesin Family Member 5B) is a microtubule-based motor protein that drives the extension of lysosomal tubules along microtubules during [[Autophagic Lysosome Reformation]] by interacting with [[PtdIns(4,5)P2]] on autolysosomal membranes.

## Function

KIF5B (kinesin-1 heavy chain) transports autophagic vesicles along microtubules toward the perinuclear region for fusion with lysosomes.
