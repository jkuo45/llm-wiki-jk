# Vps15

Vps15 is a p150 phosphoinositide 3-kinase regulatory subunit that forms a complex with [[Vps34]] to regulate [[Autophagy]] and endosomal trafficking.

## Function

Vps15 (PIK3R4) is a regulatory subunit of [[Vps34]] (class III PI3K). It recruits Vps34 to membranes and stabilizes the active site for [[PtdIns3P]] production.
## Autophagy Role

The Vps15-[[Vps34]]-[[Atg14]] complex is essential for the formation of the [[Autophagosome]]. PtdIns3P generated by the complex recruits WIPI/Atg18 family proteins to the phagophore.
