# STUB1

STIP1 homology and U-box containing protein 1 (STUB1), also known as CHIP, is an E3 ubiquitin ligase that targets phosphorylated [[TFEB]] for degradation via the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway.

## Function

STUB1 (CHIP) is a chaperone-dependent E3 ubiquitin ligase that ubiquitinates misfolded proteins for [[Proteasome]] degradation. It bridges [[HSP70]]/[[Hsp90]] chaperones to the proteasome.
## Autophagy Connection

STUB1 ubiquitinates [[ULK1]] to regulate its stability and activity. It is also involved in [[Chaperone-Mediated Autophagy]] by targeting substrates to [[LAMP2A]].
