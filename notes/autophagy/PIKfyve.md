# PIKfyve

**PIKfyve** is a phosphoinositide 5-kinase that converts [[PtdIns3P]] to [[PtdIns(3,5)P2]], playing a critical role in [[Lysosome]] biogenesis and reformation by activating [[TRPML1]].
