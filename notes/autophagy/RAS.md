# RAS

The RAS family of oncogenes (including KRAS, HRAS, and NRAS) encodes small GTPases that, when mutated, can upregulate [[Macroautophagy|autophagy]] to support the metabolic needs of established tumors.

## Function

RAS GTPases (HRAS, KRAS, NRAS) are molecular switches that cycle between GDP-bound (inactive) and GTP-bound (active) states. They signal through [[MAPK Signaling]] and [[PI3K-Akt Signaling]].
## Autophagy Connection

Oncogenic RAS induces basal [[Autophagy]] to support metabolic demands of cancer growth. KRAS-mutant cancers are addicted to autophagy for survival.
