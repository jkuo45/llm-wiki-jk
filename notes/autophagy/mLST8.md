# mLST8
Mammalian lethal with Sec13 protein 8 (mLST8), also known as GβL, is a core component of both [[mTORC1]] and [[mTORC2]] that is essential for the stability and kinase activity of the complexes, particularly [[mTORC2]].

## Function

mLST8 (GβL) binds the kinase domain of [[mTOR]] in both [[mTORC1]] and [[mTORC2]].
