# mLST8

Mammalian lethal with Sec13 protein 8 (mLST8), also called GβL (G-protein β-subunit-like protein), is a core, stoichiometric subunit of both [[mTORC1]] and [[mTORC2]]. It binds the kinase domain of [[mTOR]] and is essential for the structural integrity, stability, and catalytic activity of both complexes, with a particularly pronounced requirement for [[mTORC2]] assembly and function.

## Structure & Domain Organization

mLST8 is the mammalian ortholog of Saccharomyces cerevisiae LST8 and a member of the WD40-repeat protein family structurally related to G-protein β subunits (hence GβL). It contains seven WD40 repeats that form a β-propeller fold. The C-terminal tail of mLST8 docks onto the kinase domain of mTOR, positioning the WD40 β-propeller to stabilize the catalytic cleft and promote productive substrate/cofactor engagement. mLST8 lacks catalytic activity itself but is indispensable for complex holoenzyme formation.

## Mechanism of Action

In [[mTORC1]], mLST8 associates with mTOR, [[Raptor]], and [[PRAS40]]; it stabilizes mTOR and supports phosphorylation of certain substrates (e.g., S6K1 and 4E-BP1), though mTORC1 retains partial activity in its absence. In [[mTORC2]], mLST8 is critically required: it partners with mTOR, [[Rictor]], mSIN1, and PROTOR to correctly fold and stabilize the complex. Loss of mLST8 dramatically destabilizes mTORC2, abolishing phosphorylation of AKT (at S473), [[SGK1]], and PKCα, and impairing cytoskeleton organization and growth-factor signaling. Thus mLST8 acts as a conserved scaffold that licenses mTOR kinase activity toward its physiologically distinct substrate sets.

## Physiological & Pathological Relevance

Because mTORC1 and mTORC2 govern growth, metabolism, and survival, mLST8 sits at the apex of nutrient and insulin/IGF-1 signaling. mTORC2-AKT output downstream of mLST8 influences [[Cancer]] proliferation, glucose homeostasis, and [[Aging]]-related metabolic decline. Genetic depletion of mLST8 recapitulates the embryonic-lethal "lethal with Sec13" phenotype of its yeast counterpart, underscoring its non-redundant role. Pharmacologically, the dual mTORC1/mTORC2 inhibitor MLN0128 (sapanisertib) and related catalytic-site drugs act within the mLST8-stabilized kinase domain, making the mLST8-mTOR interface indirectly relevant to targeted cancer and senescence interventions that modulate [[Autophagy]] through relief of mTOR repression.

- [[mTORC2]]: mLST8 is essential for mTORC2 assembly; its loss abolishes AKT S473 phosphorylation.

- [[Raptor]]: mTORC1-specific subunit coexisting with mLST8 in the complex.
