# CLEAR Element

The **CLEAR Element** (Coordinated Lysosomal Expression and Regulation) is a 10-base pair consensus sequence (5′-GTCACGTGAC-3′) found in the promoter regions of many lysosomal and autophagic genes, serving as a binding site for [[TFEB]] and [[TFE3]] to coordinate [[Lysosome]] biogenesis.
