# CeTOR

**CeTOR** (let-363) is the [[C. elegans]] ortholog of mammalian [[mTORC1|mTOR]]. It functions in two complexes analogous to [[mTORC1]] and [[mTORC2]], regulating growth, metabolism, [[Autophagy]], and lifespan.

## Autophagy Regulation
CeTOR inhibition (via RNAi or [[Rapamycin]]) induces [[Autophagy]] in C. elegans. CeTOR controls the nuclear translocation of [[HLH-30]] (TFEB ortholog) and [[DAF-16]] (FOXO ortholog) to coordinate [[Autophagy]] and stress resistance. CeTOR inactivation extends lifespan in a HLH-30- and autophagy-dependent manner.

## Pathway
CeTOR is regulated by [[RHEB-1]] (Rheb ortholog), [[DAF-2]] (insulin/IGF-1 receptor) via [[AGE-1]] (PI3K)/[[AKT-1]]/[[AKT-2]], and by [[Amino Acid Sensing]] through [[SLC-36.1]]. [[Rag proteins|Rag GTPase]] orthologs ([[raga-1]], [[ragc-1]]) recruit CeTOR to lysosomes.
