# TRPML1

**TRPML1** (Mucolipin-1) is a lysosomal calcium channel that regulates the efflux of Ca2+ from the [[Lysosome]] lumen, essential for [[Lysosome]] biogenesis, trafficking, and reformation.

## Function

TRPML1 (MCOLN1) is a Ca2+-permeable non-selective cation channel on late endosomes and [[Lysosome]]s, activated by [[PtdIns(3,5)P2]].
## Autophagy Role

TRPML1-mediated Ca2+ release from lysosomes activates [[TFEB]] via [[calcineurin]]-dependent dephosphorylation. It also regulates autophagosome-lysosome fusion and lysosomal reformation. Loss-of-function causes [[Mucolipidosis IV]].
