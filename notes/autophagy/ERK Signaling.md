# ERK Signaling

Extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK) signaling is a major MAPK pathway that regulates cell growth and differentiation; [[ERK2]] phosphorylates [[TFEB]] at S142 to promote its cytosolic retention.

## Pathway

ERK signaling is a [[MAPK Signaling]] cascade where growth factor-bound RTKs activate RAS → RAF → MEK → ERK phosphorylation. Nuclear ERK phosphorylates [[ELK1]], [[MYC]], and [[FOS]].
## Autophagy Regulation

[[ERK2]] phosphorylates [[TFEB]] at S142, promoting cytosolic retention. ERK signaling also regulates [[ULK1]] and [[Beclin1]] to fine-tune autophagy induction.
