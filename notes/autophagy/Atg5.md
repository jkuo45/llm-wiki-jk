# Atg5

**Atg5** (Autophagy-related 5) is a protein essential for the initiation of [[notes/_link/Autophagy]].

## Role in Immune Cells
- Essential for [[T Cell]] survival and proliferation.
- *Atg5*-deficient T cells are incapable of autophagy initiation, which abrogates the differentiation towards a regulatory [[Foxp3]]+ phenotype induced by [[notes/_link/Spermidine]].
