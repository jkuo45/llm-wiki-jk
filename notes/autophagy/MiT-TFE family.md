# MiT/TFE family

The microphthalmia (MiT/TFE) family of transcription factors includes [[TFEB]], [[TFE3]], [[MITF]], and [[TFEC]], which regulate autophagy and lysosomal genes by binding to [[CLEAR Element]]s.
