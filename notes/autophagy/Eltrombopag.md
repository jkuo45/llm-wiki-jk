# Eltrombopag

Eltrombopag (EO) is an FDA-approved drug that acts as a direct inhibitor of [[TFEB]] by disrupting its interaction with DNA.

## Pharmacology

Eltrombopag is a thrombopoietin receptor ([[MPL]]) agonist used for immune thrombocytopenia (ITP) and aplastic anemia. It is a synthetic small molecule, not a biologic.
## Autophagy Connection

Eltrombopag chelates iron and has been shown to induce [[Autophagy]] and [[Ferritinophagy]] in hematopoietic stem cells, potentially contributing to its therapeutic effect in bone marrow failure syndromes.
