# Sestrin2

Sestrin2 is an amino acid sensor that inhibits [[mTORC1]] activity by interacting with [[GATOR2]] when leucine levels are low.

## Function

Sestrin2 is a stress-inducible leucine sensor and oxidoreductase. It inhibits [[mTORC1]] by binding [[GATOR2]] in the absence of leucine and activates [[AMPK]] via [[LKB1]].
## Autophagy Role

Sestrin2 activates [[Autophagy]] under stress conditions such as hypoxia, genotoxic stress, and metabolic stress. It also functions as an antioxidant by reducing [[Peroxiredoxin]].
