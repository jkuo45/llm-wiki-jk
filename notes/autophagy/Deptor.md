# Deptor
DEP-domain-containing mTOR-interacting protein (Deptor) is an endogenous inhibitor of both [[mTORC1]] and [[mTORC2]] complexes, and its expression is often deregulated in [[Cancer|cancer]].

## Function

Deptor (DEPTOR) is an endogenous inhibitor of both [[mTORC1]] and [[mTORC2]]. It binds the mTOR kinase domain and blocks substrate access. DEPTOR is degraded via [[SCFβ-TrCP]] ubiquitin ligase.
## Regulation

DEPTOR is highly expressed in multiple myeloma and some solid tumors where it suppresses mTOR and activates autophagy. Its expression is regulated by [[mTORC1]] via a negative feedback loop.
