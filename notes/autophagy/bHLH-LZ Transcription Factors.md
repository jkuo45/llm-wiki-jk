# bHLH-LZ Transcription Factors

Basic helix-loop-helix leucine zipper (bHLH-LZ) transcription factors are a superfamily of DNA-binding proteins, including the [[MiT]]/[[TFE]] family, that regulate gene expression by binding to E-box sequences.

## Family

bHLH-LZ factors include the [[MiTF TFE family]] and other regulators of cell fate and metabolism. They dimerize through the HLH-LZ domain and bind E-box sites.
