# Rheb
Ras homolog enriched in brain (Rheb) is a small GTPase that, in its GTP-bound state, directly interacts with and activates [[mTORC1]].

## Function

Rheb is a Ras-family GTPase that directly activates [[mTORC1]] kinase activity when GTP-bound. It is inhibited by [[TSC1]]/[[TSC2]] GAP activity.
