# Lysosomal Localization

Lysosomal localization refers to the recruitment or positioning of proteins on the lysosome membrane, which for [[TFEB]] is required for its phosphorylation by [[mTORC1]] and is regulated by [[GSK3β]].

## Determinants

Lysosomal positioning is regulated by [[Rab7]] effectors: [[FYCO1]] (plus-end transport), [[RILP]]-[[ORP1L]] (minus-end). Perinuclear clustering facilitates [[Autophagy]].
