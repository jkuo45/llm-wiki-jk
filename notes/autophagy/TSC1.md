# TSC1
Tuberous sclerosis complex 1 (TSC1), also known as hamartin, forms a heterodimer with [[TSC2]] to function as a negative regulator of [[mTORC1]] signaling by acting as a GTPase-activating protein (GAP) for [[Rheb]].

## Function

TSC1 (hamartin) forms a complex with [[TSC2]] that acts as a GAP for [[Rheb]], inhibiting [[mTORC1]]. TSC1 mutations cause [[Tuberous Sclerosis Complex]].
