# Amino Acid Sensing

Amino acid sensing is the cellular detection of nutrient availability; high amino acid levels trigger the [[Ragulator]] and [[Rag GTPases]] to recruit [[mTORC1]] to the lysosome, where it inhibits [[TFEB]].
