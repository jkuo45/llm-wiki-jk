# Rab7

**Rab7** is a small Ras-related GTPase that defines late endosomal and lysosomal identity and governs the terminal steps of degradative membrane traffic, including [[Autophagosome]]–[[Lysosome]] fusion. It is activated during endosome maturation as [[Rab5]] hands off control via the Mon1–Ccz1 GEF, and it coordinates tethering, motility, and fusion of autophagic cargo with the degradative compartment. Rab7 dysfunction dissociates autophagosomes from lysosomes, blocking autophagic flux despite normal autophagosome formation.

## Structure & Activation Cycle

Rab7 cycles between GDP- and GTP-bound states on late endosomal membranes. The Mon1–Ccz1 complex acts as its major GEF, while TBC1D15 and TBC1D17 are Rab7-specific GAPs. In the GTP-bound state, Rab7 recruits effector cohorts: the HOPS (homotypic fusion and vacuole protein sorting) tethering complex, the plus-end-directed motor adaptor [[FYCO1]] (via ORP1L and kinesin-1), and the minus-end-directed regulator [[RILP]] together with dynactin, which position and translocate late endosomes/autolysosomes along microtubules.

## Role in Autophagic Flux

Rab7 is indispensable for the fusion of mature autophagosomes with late endosomes and lysosomes to form autolysosomes. Active Rab7 recruits HOPS, which bridges SNARE complexes (e.g., syntaxin 17) and promotes membrane merger. Loss of Rab7 or expression of dominant-negative mutants arrests autophagosomes as undigested vesicles and elevates [[p62]] and LC3-II, classic markers of impaired flux. Rab7 also participates in endo-lysosomal positioning, clustering degradative organelles perinuclearly under nutrient stress to facilitate [[Autophagy]].

## Physiological & Pathological Relevance

Mutations in Rab7 cause Charcot–Marie–Tooth type 2B neuropathy, reflecting its requirement in neuronal degradative traffic. Defective Rab7-mediated fusion contributes to accumulation of protein aggregates in [[Neurodegeneration]] and impairs clearance in [[Aging]]. Conversely, pathogen effectors and oncogenic signals modulate Rab7 to either evade xenophagy or sustain proliferative metabolism in [[Cancer]]. Modulators of Rab7–HOPS interaction are under investigation to restore autophagic clearance.
