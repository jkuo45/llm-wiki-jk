# Rapalog

**Rapalogs** are derivatives of [[Rapamycin]] (also known as sirolimus) that act as allosteric inhibitors of [[notes/_link/mTOR|mechanistic Target of rapamycin]] (mTOR).

## Examples
- [[Everolimus]] (RAD001)
- [[Temsirolimus]]

## Therapeutic Uses
- Widely used in [[Cancer]] therapy.
- Immunosuppressive agents in organ transplantation.
- Investigated as geroprotective treatments for age-associated diseases like [[COVID-19]] and [[Osteoarthritis]].

## Mechanism
- Highly specific [[mTORC1]] inhibitors are desired to avoid adverse effects on glucose tolerance mediated by [[mTORC2]] inhibition.

## Class

Rapalogs are analogs of [[Rapamycin]] ([[Everolimus]], [[Temsirolimus]], [[Ridaforolimus]]) that inhibit [[mTORC1]] and induce [[Autophagy]].
