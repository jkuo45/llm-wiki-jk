# Glutamine Metabolism

Glutamine metabolism is the biochemical utilization of glutamine; in [[Pancreatic Cancer]], [[TFEB]] supports tumor growth by transcriptionally upregulating [[Glutaminase]] to meet metabolic demands.

## Pathways

Glutamine is converted to glutamate by [[Glutaminase]], then to α-ketoglutarate for TCA cycle anaplerosis. It also supports nucleotide and glutathione synthesis.
## Autophagy Connection

Glutamine deprivation induces [[Autophagy]] via [[ULK1]] activation and mTORC1 inhibition. The ammonia released from glutaminolysis directly induces autophagy.
