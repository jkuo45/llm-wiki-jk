# Rab5

**Rab5** is a small GTPase that serves as a master regulator of early endosome dynamics, including vesicle docking and fusion, and is replaced by [[Rab7]] during the process of endosome maturation.

## Function

Rab5 regulates early endosome fusion and motility. It is targeted by [[SetA]] glucosyltransferase during [[Legionella]] infection to subvert autophagy.
