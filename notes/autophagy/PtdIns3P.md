# PtdIns3P

**PtdIns3P** (Phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate) is a phosphoinositide predominantly found on early endosomes and [[Autophagosome]]s, serving as a substrate for [[PIKfyve]] to produce [[PtdIns(3,5)P2]].

## Function

Phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate is generated by [[Vps34]] class III PI3K and is enriched on autophagosomes and endosomes. It recruits WIPI/Atg18 for autophagosome nucleation and expansion.
