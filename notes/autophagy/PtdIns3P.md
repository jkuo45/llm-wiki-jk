# PtdIns3P

**PtdIns3P** (Phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate) is a phosphoinositide predominantly found on early endosomes and [[Autophagosome]]s, serving as a substrate for [[PIKfyve]] to produce [[PtdIns(3,5)P2]].
