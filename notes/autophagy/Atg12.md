# Atg12

**Atg12** (Autophagy-related 12) is a ubiquitin-like protein essential for the initiation and progression of [[Autophagy]].

## Conjugation System
- Atg12 is covalently conjugated to [[Atg5]] in a process requiring [[Atg7]] (E1-like enzyme) and Atg10 (E2-like enzyme).
- The **Atg12-Atg5 complex** then interacts with [[Atg16]] to form a large multimeric complex.
- This Atg12-Atg5-Atg16 complex acts as an E3-like ligase for the lipidation of [[LC3]] ([[Atg8]]), promoting its attachment to the [[Autophagosome]] membrane.

## Function
- The complex is required for the elongation of the phagophore.
- Once the autophagosome is completed, the complex dissociates from the membrane.
