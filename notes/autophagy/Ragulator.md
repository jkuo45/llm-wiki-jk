# Ragulator

The **Ragulator** complex is a pentameric protein complex (LAMTOR1-5) that anchors [[Rag proteins]] to the lysosomal membrane and functions as a guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) for [[Rag proteins|RagA/B]], essential for [[mTORC1]] activation.
