# Glutaminase

Glutaminase is an enzyme that converts glutamine to glutamate; its expression is transcriptionally regulated by [[TFEB]] in [[Pancreatic Cancer]] to support tumor metabolism.

## Function

Glutaminase (GLS) converts glutamine to glutamate, the first step of glutaminolysis. The glutamate feeds into the TCA cycle via α-ketoglutarate.
## Autophagy Connection

Glutaminase-generated ammonia from glutamine deamination induces [[Autophagy]] as a cytoprotective response. GLS inhibition reduces autophagy and sensitizes cancer cells to therapy.
