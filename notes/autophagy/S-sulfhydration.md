# S-sulfhydration

S-sulfhydration is a post-translational modification of cysteine residues by hydrogen sulfide (H2S); [[TFEB]] undergoes S-sulfhydration at C212, which promotes its nuclear translocation.

## Mechanism

S-sulfhydration (persulfidation) is the modification of cysteine thiols to persulfides (-SSH) by hydrogen sulfide (H2S). It enhances the activity of target proteins, including [[TFEB]], [[Keap1]], and [[GAPDH]].
## Autophagy Connection

H2S donors (NaHS, GYY4137) induce [[Autophagy]] via TFEB nuclear translocation and [[AMPK]] activation. This has therapeutic potential in [[Myocardial Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury]] and [[Neurodegeneration]].
