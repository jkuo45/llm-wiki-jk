# LIPL-2

**LIPL-2** is one of several [[C. elegans]] triglyceride lipases upregulated during [[Fasting]]. It is localized to the lumen of the gut and/or vesicles within the intestine and is predicted to have lysosomal localization.

## Regulation
- **Transcriptional Control**: Like [[LIPL-1]] and [[LIPL-3]], *lipl-2* is repressed by [[MXL-3]] under well-fed conditions and induced by [[HLH-30]] during fasting.
- **Nutrient Sensitivity**: Upregulated approximately 12-fold following 6 hours of fasting in fertile adult worms.

## Function

LIPL-2 is a C. elegans lysosomal lipase involved in [[Lipophagy]] and lipid droplet turnover in the intestine.
