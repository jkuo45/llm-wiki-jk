# Immunometabolism

**Immunometabolism** is an emerging field that explores the close association between metabolic pathways and immune cell function.

## Role in Autophagy
- Selective [[Autophagy]] is key in modulating immunity and immune cell dynamics via metabolic reprogramming.
- Autophagy facilitates energy metabolism during effector [[T Cell]] activation.

## Aging and Reprogramming
- [[Immune Aging]] involves transcriptional and metabolic reprogramming in individual cells.
- [[Metformin]] improves memory immune responses by metabolically reprogramming memory CD8+ T cells.
