# Autophagy Inhibitor

An autophagy inhibitor is a compound that blocks autophagic flux; [[Eltrombopag]] is a direct inhibitor of [[TFEB]] that disrupts its interaction with DNA, preventing the induction of autophagy genes.

## Classes

Autophagy inhibitors include lysosomotropic agents ([[Chloroquine]], [[Hydroxychloroquine]], [[Bafilomycin A1]]), PI3K inhibitors ([[3-MA]], [[Wortmannin]], [[LY294002]]), ULK1 inhibitors ([[SBI-0206965]], [[MRT68921]]), and ATG4 inhibitors.
