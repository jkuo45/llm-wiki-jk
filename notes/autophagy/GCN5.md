# GCN5

General control non-repressed protein 5 (GCN5) is an acetyltransferase that inhibits [[TFEB]] activity by acetylating it at K274 and K279, interfering with its dimerization and DNA binding.

## Function

GCN5 (KAT2A) is a histone acetyltransferase (HAT) that acetylates histones H3 (K9, K14, K18) and non-histone targets including [[PGC1-α]] and [[TFEB]].
## Autophagy Connection

GCN5 acetylates and inhibits [[PGC1-α]], suppressing mitochondrial biogenesis. It also acetylates [[TFEB]], affecting its DNA binding. GCN5 activity opposes [[SIRT1]] deacetylation.
