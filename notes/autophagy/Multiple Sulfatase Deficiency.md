# Multiple Sulfatase Deficiency

A rare [[Lysosomal Storage Diseases|lysosomal storage disease]] caused by the lack of all known sulfatase enzymes; treated in models with [[TFEB]].

## Etiology

MSD is caused by mutations in [[SUMF1]], encoding the formylglycine-generating enzyme (FGE) that activates all sulfatases by converting a cysteine residue to Cα-formylglycine in their active sites. This results in combined deficiency of all 17 known human sulfatases.
## Pathophysiology

The dual deficiency of arylsulfatases (causing features of [[Metachromatic Leukodystrophy]]) and sulfamidase (causing MPS-like features) produces a mixed phenotype. Lysosomal storage of sulfatides, glycosaminoglycans, and autophagic substrates leads to progressive neurodegeneration.
## Therapeutic Implications

[[TFEB]] activation has been proposed to augment lysosomal exocytosis and reduce storage burden. Enzyme replacement and gene therapy strategies are preclinical.
