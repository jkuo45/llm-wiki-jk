# BRCA1

**BRCA1** (Breast Cancer Gene 1) is a critical tumour suppressor protein involved in DNA double-strand break repair, cell cycle checkpoint control, chromatin remodelling, and regulation of transcription. Germline mutations in BRCA1 confer high lifetime risks of breast cancer (~60%) and ovarian cancer (~40%).

## Structure and Domains

BRCA1 is a 1,863-amino-acid nuclear phosphoprotein containing several conserved functional domains:

- **RING Finger Domain (N-terminus)**: Interacts with [[BARD1]] to form a heterodimeric E3 ubiquitin ligase complex.
- **Coiled-Coil Domain**: Mediates interaction with [[PALB2]], which bridges BRCA1 to [[BRCA2]] and [[RAD51]].
- **BRCT Domains (C-terminus)**: Tandem BRCA1 C-terminal repeats that bind phospho-proteins such as [[Abraxas]], [[CtIP]], and [[BRIP1]], localising BRCA1 to sites of DNA damage.

## DNA Repair Function

BRCA1 is essential for **homologous recombination (HR)** repair of DNA double-strand breaks:

- **DSB Recognition**: BRCA1 is recruited to double-strand breaks by the [[MRE11-RAD50-NBS1]] (MRN) complex and phosphorylated by [[ATM]] and [[ATR]] kinases.
- **End Resection**: BRCA1 collaborates with [[CtIP]] and the MRN complex to promote 5'-3' end resection, generating single-stranded DNA overhangs required for HR.
- **RAD51 Loading**: BRCA1 facilitates the loading of [[RAD51]] onto single-stranded DNA via interaction with [[BRCA2]] and [[PALB2]], enabling homologous strand invasion and accurate repair.
- **S Phase Checkpoint**: BRCA1 is part of the [[BRCA1-Abraxas-RAP80]] complex that activates the S-phase checkpoint in response to DNA damage.

## Sirtuin Interactions

- **[[SIRT2]]**: Deacetylates BRCA1 and promotes its localisation to DNA damage foci. SIRT2 knockdown impairs homologous recombination repair and sensitises cells to PARP inhibitors, linking sirtuin activity to BRCA1-mediated DNA repair.
- **[[SIRT1]]**: Indirectly regulates BRCA1 through modulation of p53 and FOXO transcription factors, which control BRCA1 expression.

## Autophagy Connection

BRCA1 physically neighbours [[Beclin1]] on chromosome 17q21 and its expression correlates with autophagy regulation:

- **Chromosomal Proximity**: BRCA1 and BECN1 are located within 150 kb on 17q21.31, and co-deletion of this region is common in breast and ovarian cancers.
- **Compensatory Autophagy**: Loss of BRCA1 leads to increased autophagy as a compensatory survival mechanism. BRCA1-deficient tumours exhibit elevated autophagic flux, which may represent a therapeutic vulnerability.
- **PARP Inhibitor Synergy**: The combination of PARP inhibition (e.g., [[Olaparib]]) with autophagy inhibition (e.g., [[Hydroxychloroquine]]) synergistically kills BRCA1-deficient cells.

## Clinical Relevance

- **PARP Inhibitor Sensitivity**: BRCA1-deficient tumours (both germline and somatic) are exquisitely sensitive to [[PARP inhibitors]] such as [[Olaparib]], [[Rucaparib]], [[Niraparib]], and [[Talazoparib]] due to synthetic lethality.
- **Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**: BRCA1 mutations are enriched in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC), and PARP inhibitors are approved for BRCA-mutated TNBC.
- **Chemotherapy Response**: BRCA1-deficient tumours are sensitive to platinum-based chemotherapies ([[Cisplatin]], [[Carboplatin]]) due to defective HR repair.
