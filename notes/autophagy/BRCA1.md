# BRCA1

BRCA1 (Breast Cancer Gene 1) is a critical tumor suppressor gene involved in DNA repair and is frequently mutated in [[notes/_link/Breast Cancer]] and ovarian malignancies.

## Function

BRCA1 is a tumor suppressor essential for homologous recombination repair of DNA double-strand breaks. It functions in complex with [[BARD1]], [[RAD51]], and the [[MRE11-RAD50-NBS1]] complex.
## Autophagy Connection

BRCA1 physically neighbors [[Beclin1]] on chromosome 17q21 and regulates autophagy. Loss of BRCA1 leads to increased autophagy as a compensatory survival mechanism in breast and ovarian cancers.
