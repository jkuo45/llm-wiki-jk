# LIPL-4

**LIPL-4** is a triglyceride lipase in [[C. elegans]] that is expressed in the pharynx and intestine. Unlike other members of the LIPL family, it is not predicted to be lysosomal and its expression is not repressed by [[MXL-3]] under well-fed conditions.

## Regulation
- **Expression**: Expressed in the intestine of starvation-induced dauer larvae and in the pharynx of both well-fed and fasted animals.
- **Fasting Response**: Upregulated approximately 7-fold following 6 hours of fasting in fertile adult worms.
