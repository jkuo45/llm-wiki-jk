# PERO1

PERO1 is a critical lipid sensor protein localized to the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) membrane and associated with the [[Peroxisome Biogenesis Network]]. It acts as a key quality control mechanism during the [[Selective Autophagy]] of damaged peroxisomes. When a [[Peroxisome]] becomes dysfunctional, it often accumulates excessive oleic acid. PERO1's lipid-binding domain directly recognizes and binds to this oleic acid on the peroxisomal membrane. This stabilized interaction subsequently recruits [[ESCRT]] proteins, which initiate the autophagic engulfment and degradation of the faulty organelle.
