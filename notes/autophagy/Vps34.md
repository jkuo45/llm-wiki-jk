# Vps34

**Vps34** (Vacuolar Protein Sorting 34), also known as PIK3C3, is a Class III phosphoinositide 3-kinase ([[notes/_link/PI3K]]) that produces Phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PI3P), a key signaling lipid required for the nucleation and maturation of [[Autophagosome|autophagosomes]] in the [[notes/_link/Autophagy]] pathway.
