# LAMP1

**LAMP1** (Lysosomal-Associated Membrane Protein 1) is a major integral membrane glycoprotein of the [[Lysosome]], essential for maintaining lysosomal integrity and serving as a marker for the organelle.

## Function

LAMP1 is the most abundant protein on the lysosomal membrane, contributing to lysosomal stability and integrity. It is heavily glycosylated to protect the membrane from luminal hydrolases.
## Autophagy Role

LAMP1 is used as a marker for [[Lysosome]]s and [[Autolysosome]]s. It tethers autophagosomes via interactions with [[LC3]] and [[PLEKHM1]] during fusion.
