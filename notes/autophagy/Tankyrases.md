# Tankyrases

Tankyrases (TNKS1 and TNKS2) are poly(ADP-ribose) polymerases (PARPs) that interact with and parsylate [[TFEB]], promoting its nuclear translocation.

## Family & Structure

Tankyrases (TNKS1/[[TNKS1]], TNKS2/[[TNKS2]]) are PARP5 enzymes with ankyrin repeat domains for protein-protein interactions, a sterile alpha motif (SAM) for polymerization, and a PARP catalytic domain.
## Functions

Regulate telomere maintenance (via TRF1), Wnt/β-catenin signaling (via AXIN degradation), GLUT4 trafficking, and [[Autophagy]] (via ULK1/TFEB stabilization). Tankyrase inhibitors are in development for cancer and fibrosis.
