# ischemic injury

Ischemic injury is tissue damage caused by a restriction in blood supply; activation of [[TFEB]] has been shown to be protective in models of cerebral and cardiac ischemia.

## Pathophysiology

Ischemia-reperfusion injury (IRI) involves ATP depletion, oxidative stress, Ca2+ overload, and cell death upon reperfusion. [[Autophagy]] is protective in IRI by clearing damaged mitochondria ([[Mitophagy]]).
## Therapeutic Potential

[[Trehalose]], [[Spermidine]], and [[Melatonin]] activate autophagy and protect against IRI in heart, brain, liver, and kidney preclinical models.
