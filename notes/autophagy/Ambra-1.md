# Ambra-1

Ambra-1 (Autophagy/beclin-1 regulator 1) is a positive regulator of the [[notes/_link/PI3K|PI3K III complex]] that interacts with [[Beclin1]] to promote autophagosome nucleation.

## Function

Ambra-1 is a scaffold protein that bridges [[Beclin1]] to the [[ULK1]] complex, coordinating autophagosome nucleation. It also links autophagy to the [[Cytoskeleton]] via [[Dynein]] and [[Myosin]] interactions.
## Regulation

Ambra-1 is phosphorylated by [[ULK1]] during starvation, releasing it from the dynein complex to enable [[Beclin1]] activation. It also functions in [[Mitophagy]] and neural development.
