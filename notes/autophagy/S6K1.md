# S6K1
The p70 ribosomal S6 kinase 1 (S6K1) is a primary downstream effector of [[mTORC1]] that promotes [[protein synthesis]], [[Ribosome Biogenesis]], and cell growth by phosphorylating substrates like [[ribosomal protein S6]].

## Function

S6K1 (RPS6KB1) phosphorylates ribosomal protein S6, [[eIF4B]], [[PDCD4]], and [[SKAR]]. It is a major effector of [[mTORC1]] in translation and cell growth.
