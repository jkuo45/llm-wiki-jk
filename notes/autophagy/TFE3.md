# TFE3

**TFE3** (Transcription Factor E3) is a member of the MiT/TFE family of basic helix-loop-helix transcription factors that, along with [[TFEB]], regulates the expression of genes involved in [[Lysosome]] biogenesis and [[Autophagy]] by binding to [[CLEAR Element]]s.

## Function

TFE3 is a [[MiTF TFE family]] transcription factor that regulates the CLEAR network for lysosomal biogenesis and autophagy. Regulation parallels [[TFEB]].
