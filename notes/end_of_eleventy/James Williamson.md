# James Williamson

[[James Williamson]] (d. 2019) was a talented web developer and instructor, known for his work on [[Lynda.com]]. He was the creator of the original [[Eleventy]] possum mascot, which was later named Elle. He was a dedicated advocate for web accessibility and design.
