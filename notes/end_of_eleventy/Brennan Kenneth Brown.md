# Brennan Kenneth Brown

[[Brennan Kenneth Brown]] is a web developer and the author of the blog post "[[The End of Eleventy]]". He runs [[Berry House]], an independent web studio that builds static sites for nonprofits and marginalized communities. He is an active member of the [[IndieWeb]] community and a supporter of [[Eleventy]].
