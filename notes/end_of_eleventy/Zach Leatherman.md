# Zach Leatherman

[[Zach Leatherman]] is a web developer and the creator of [[Eleventy]]. He is a vocal advocate for sustainable open-source development and boring technology. 

Initially hired by [[Netlify]] to work on [[Eleventy]] full-time, he joined the [[Font Awesome]] team in September 2024 when the project moved there.
