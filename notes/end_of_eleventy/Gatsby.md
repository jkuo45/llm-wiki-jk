# Gatsby

[[Gatsby]] is a React-based static site generator that introduced the concept of the "content mesh" and utilized GraphQL for data sourcing. Despite raising over $46 million in venture capital, Gatsby Inc. struggled to monetize and was acquired by [[Netlify]] in 2023, after which the project was largely discontinued.
