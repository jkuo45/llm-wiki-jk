# Netlify

[[Netlify]] is a cloud computing company that offers hosting and serverless backend services for web applications and static websites. It played a major role in popularizing the [[JAMstack]] architecture.

Netlify previously employed [[Zach Leatherman]] to work on [[Eleventy]] and has acquired several projects in the SSG space, including [[Gatsby]] and [[Stackbit]].
