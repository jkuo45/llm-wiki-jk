# Build Awesome

[[Build Awesome]] is the 2026 rebranding and commercial evolution of [[Eleventy]], backed by [[Font Awesome]]. It was launched via a successful Kickstarter campaign aiming to raise $40,000 USD to fund the development of an all-in-one site builder for [[Font Awesome]] and Web Awesome.

## Features
- Collaborative visual editing
- Build-in-a-browser functionality
- Premium built-in templates
