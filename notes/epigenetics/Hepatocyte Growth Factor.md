# Hepatocyte Growth Factor

[[Hepatocyte Growth Factor]] ([[HGF]]) is a paracrine cellular growth, motility and morphogenic factor. It is secreted by mesenchymal cells and acts primarily on epithelial cells and endothelial cells, but also on haemopoietic stem cells and [[Muscle Stem Cell|muscle stem cells]]. It is essential for the activation of quiescent muscle stem cells.
