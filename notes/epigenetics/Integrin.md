# Integrin

[[Integrin|Integrins]] are transmembrane receptors that facilitate cell-cell and cell-extracellular matrix ([[ECM]]) adhesion. Upon ligand binding, integrins activate signal transduction pathways that mediate cellular signals such as regulation of the [[Cell Cycle]], organization of the intracellular cytoskeleton, and movement of new receptors to the cell membrane.
