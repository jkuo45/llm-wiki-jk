# Oxidative Stress

[[notes/oxidative_stress/Oxidative Stress|Oxidative Stress]] reflects an imbalance between the systemic manifestation of reactive oxygen species and a biological system's ability to readily detoxify the reactive intermediates or to repair the resulting damage.
