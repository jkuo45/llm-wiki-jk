# Mitophagy

[[Mitophagy]] is the selective degradation of mitochondria by [[notes/_link/Autophagy]]. It often occurs to defective mitochondria following damage or [[notes/_link/Oxidative Stress]]. In quiescent cells, mitophagy helps reduce metabolic activity and [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] production to maintain [[Quiescence]].
