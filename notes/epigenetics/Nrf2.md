# Nrf2
Nrf2 (Nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2) is a transcription factor that serves as a master regulator of the cellular antioxidant response, upregulating endogenous protective enzymes and detoxification systems in response to oxidative stress or xenohormetic compounds.
