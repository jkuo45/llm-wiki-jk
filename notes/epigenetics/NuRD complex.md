# NuRD complex

The Nucleosome Remodeling and Deacetylase (NuRD) complex is a major protein complex that plays a critical role in chromatin remodeling and gene silencing. It combines ATP-dependent nucleosome remodeling with histone deacetylase activities.

In the context of [[Yamanaka Factors]], the NuRD complex interacts with factors like [[Nanog]] and [[CHD4]] to regulate the transition between pluripotency and differentiation. It is involved in the epigenetic changes necessary for successful [[Cellular Reprogramming]].

## Connections
- [[CHD4]]: A key component of the NuRD complex that regulates [[Sox2]] and cancer stem cells.
- [[Nanog]]: Interacts with the NuRD complex to maintain [[Pluripotency]].
- [[Cellular Reprogramming]]: The NuRD complex is part of the machinery that resets the epigenetic state of a cell.
