# SAHF

**Senescence-Associated Heterochromatin Foci (SAHF)** are specialized domains of facultative heterochromatin that form in the nuclei of senescent cells, contributing to the silencing of proliferation-promoting genes.

## Characteristics
- Enrichment of macroH2A and H3K9me3.
- Contributes to the irreversible growth arrest of senescence.
