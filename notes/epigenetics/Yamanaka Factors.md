# Yamanaka Factors

The Yamanaka Factors (also known as [[OSKM]]) are a group of four transcription factors—[[Oct4]], [[Sox2]], [[Klf4]], and [[c-Myc]]—that are used to induce [[Pluripotency]] in differentiated [[Somatic Cells]].

## Discovery
Discovered by [[Shinya Yamanaka]] and Kazutoshi Takahashi in 2006, these factors were identified from a pool of 24 candidate genes highly expressed in [[Embryonic Stem Cells]].

## Variants
Later research identified other combinations of factors (e.g., [[Lin28]], [[Nanog]]) that can also induce pluripotency, but the original OSKM cocktail remains the most widely used.
