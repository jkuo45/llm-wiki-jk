# Fibroblast

A [[Fibroblast]] is a type of biological cell that synthesizes the [[Extracellular Matrix]] and collagen, produces the structural framework (stroma) for animal tissues, and plays a critical role in wound healing. Quiescent fibroblasts remain metabolically active to maintain the [[ECM]].
