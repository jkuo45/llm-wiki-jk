# Drug Discovery

[[Drug Discovery]] is the systematic, multidisciplinary process of identifying, validating, and optimizing new therapeutic candidates, traditionally progressing from target identification through hit discovery, lead optimization, preclinical safety, and clinical trials. The discipline integrates biology, chemistry, pharmacology, and computational modeling.

## Transformation by iPSC Technology

[[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs) have reshaped modern drug discovery by supplying unlimited, patient-specific human cells that differentiate into disease-relevant lineages — cardiomyocytes, hepatocytes, neurons, [[Retinal Pigment Epithelium]], and more. Historically, discovery relied heavily on animal models and immortalized cell lines that often fail to predict human efficacy and toxicity. iPSC platforms enable:
- **[[High-Throughput Screening]]** of large compound libraries on human cells, improving physiological relevance.
- **[[Toxicity Testing]]** for cardiotoxicity, hepatotoxicity, and neurotoxicity earlier and more accurately.
- **[[Drug Repositioning|Drug repurposing]]**: e.g., ropinirole and bosutinib emerged as candidate [[Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis|ALS]] therapies via iPSC screening and advanced to clinical trials.
- **Personalized medicine**: patient-specific iPSCs capture genetic diversity in drug response.

## Integration with Disease Modeling & Gene Editing

Drug discovery is tightly coupled to [[Disease Modeling]]: iPSC-derived "disease-in-a-dish" systems and three-dimensional [[Organoids]] provide the cellular context for screening. Combining iPSCs with [[CRISPR]] editing to build [[Isogenic Pair|isogenic pairs]] isolates the effect of a single pathogenic variant, sharpening target validation and reducing false positives.

## Synergy with Other Modalities

iPSC discovery dovetails with [[Senolytic Drugs]] and [[Rejuvenation]] research, where reprogramming-based models help identify agents that clear senescent cells or mimic [[Partial Reprogramming]]. It also supports [[Regenerative Medicine]], where small molecules are sought to direct differentiation or enhance graft survival, and intersects with [[Cancer]] target identification and resistance studies.

## Clinical & Research Relevance

The principal advantages are reduced animal use, earlier detection of human-specific toxicities, and accelerated timelines. Challenges remain: iPSC-derived cells are often immature/fetal-like, batch variability affects reproducibility, and maturing organoids to adult physiology is an active area of method development.
