# Ribociclib

Ribociclib (formerly LEE011) is an orally bioavailable, selective [[Cyclin-Dependent Kinase]] 4 and 6 (CDK4/6) inhibitor. It is approved, in combination with aromatase inhibitors or fulvestrant, for hormone receptor-positive (HR+), human epidermal growth factor receptor 2-negative (HER2−) advanced or metastatic breast cancer, and is under study in other CDK4/6-dependent tumors.

## Overview

CDK4 and CDK6 partner with cyclin D to phosphorylate the retinoblastoma protein (RB1), releasing E2F transcription factors and driving G1-to-S progression of the [[Cell Cycle]]. In many cancers, cyclin D–CDK4/6 signaling is constitutively active. Ribociclib blocks this kinase activity, enforcing G1 arrest and suppressing proliferation of RB1-proficient tumor cells.

## Mechanism of Action

- **CDK4/6 inhibition**: Ribociclib competitively occupies the ATP-binding pocket of CDK4/6, preventing phosphorylation of [[Retinoblastoma Protein]] (pRB).
- **G1 arrest**: Hypophosphorylated RB1 remains bound to E2F, halting S-phase entry and DNA replication.
- **Senescence and differentiation**: Sustained arrest can precipitate cellular [[Senescence]] or differentiation in susceptible lineages.
- **Combination synergy**: With endocrine therapy, it ablates cyclin D–driven and estrogen-receptor-driven proliferative signals simultaneously.

## Physiological and Pathological Relevance

The cyclin D–CDK4/6–RB axis is a central cell-cycle gatekeeper; its deregulation is among the most common events in human [[Cancer]]. By restoring RB-dependent restraint, ribociclib exploits the dependence of many tumors on continuous CDK4/6 signaling while sparing RB1-deficient cells less predictably.

## Clinical and Research Relevance

Landmark trials (e.g., MONALEESA) showed improved progression-free and overall survival when ribociclib was added to endocrine therapy. Common adverse effects include neutropenia, hepatobiliary enzyme elevation, and QT prolongation, necessitating monitoring. Beyond breast cancer, trials explore its use in lymphoma, melanoma, and glioblastoma. As a [[CDK Inhibitor]], it also serves as a tool to dissect cell-cycle control and senescence induction.
