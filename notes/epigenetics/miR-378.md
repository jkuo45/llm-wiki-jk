# miR-378

## Overview

**miR-378** (miR-378a) is a ~22-nucleotide [[MicroRNA]] that regulates gene expression post-transcriptionally by binding target [[Messenger RNA|mRNA]] 3'-UTRs. It is notable for being co-transcribed with its host gene and for dual roles in mitochondrial energy metabolism and, in aging tissues, in the repression of [[SIRT1]] as part of a pro-inflammatory miRNA network.

## Genomic Locus & Family

- miR-378a is encoded within the **first intron of the PPARGC1B gene ([[PGC-1beta|PGC-1β]])** on chromosome 5q32, tying its expression to metabolic/mitochondrial biogenesis programs.
- Paralogs (miR-378b–i) share a common seed sequence; the miR-378 family is conserved across mammals.

## Validated / Likely Targets

- **Metabolic targets:** ERRγ (ESRRG), GABPA, and CRAT/MED13 — through which miR-378 modulates a metabolic shift and counterbalances PGC-1β activity (Carrer et al., *PNAS* 2012).
- **[[SIRT1]]:** in the context of vascular and cartilage aging, miR-378 is reported among microRNAs that suppress SIRT1 translation via 3'-UTR binding, contributing to reduced deacetylase activity.
- Additional context-dependent targets include apoptosis and angiogenesis regulators (e.g., SUFU, FUS-1, TUSC2).

## Role in Senescence, Aging & Disease

> [!info] Metabolism–inflammation crosstalk
> Because miR-378 sits inside the PGC-1β locus, its dysregulation couples mitochondrial/metabolic decline with the SIRT1–[[NF-kappaB]] inflammatory axis relevant to [[Inflammaging]].

- **Aging vasculature & cartilage:** upregulated alongside [[miR-217]] and [[miR-543]], converging on SIRT1 loss, [[p65|RelA/p65]] hyperacetylation, and sustained inflammatory signaling ([[SASP]]).
- **Cardiac & metabolic disease:** modulates cardiomyocyte survival, hypertrophy, and systemic energy homeostasis; studied in obesity and heart failure.
- **Cancer:** context-dependent oncogenic or tumor-suppressive behavior depending on tissue.
- **[[Epigenetics]] link:** via SIRT1 suppression, miR-378 indirectly influences [[Histone Modification]] states (e.g., H4K16 acetylation) and chromatin-based gene silencing.

## Therapeutic Relevance

miR-378 is a proposed target of **LNA antagomirs** (with [[miR-217]] and [[miR-543]]) to de-repress SIRT1 in aged endothelial and cartilage tissue.
