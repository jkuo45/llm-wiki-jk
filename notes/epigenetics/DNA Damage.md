# DNA Damage

**DNA damage** refers to physical or chemical alterations to DNA that can lead to mutations or cell death if not repaired.

## Sources
- Endogenous (e.g., reactive oxygen species)
- Exogenous (e.g., UV radiation, chemicals)

## Link to Aging
- Accumulation of DNA damage is a key driver of [[Aging]] and [[Cellular Senescence]].
