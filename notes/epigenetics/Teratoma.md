# Teratoma

A teratoma is a type of germ cell tumor that may contain several different types of tissue, such as hair, muscle, teeth, or bone.

In stem cell research, the formation of teratomas is a key indicator of the pluripotency of [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs) or [[Embryonic Stem Cells]] (ESCs). When these cells are injected into immunodeficient mice, they should ideally form teratomas containing tissues from all three germ layers (endoderm, ectoderm, and mesoderm). However, in the context of cell therapy, the risk of teratoma formation from residual undifferentiated cells is a major safety concern.
