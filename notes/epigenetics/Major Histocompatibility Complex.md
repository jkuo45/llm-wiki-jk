# Major Histocompatibility Complex

The [[Major Histocompatibility Complex]] (MHC) is a set of cell surface proteins essential for the acquired [[notes/_link/Immune System|immune system]] to recognize foreign molecules in vertebrates, which in turn determines histocompatibility.
