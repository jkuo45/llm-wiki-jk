# BIG1

Brefeldin A-inhibited guanine nucleotide-exchange protein 1 (BIG1) is a protein responsible for cell migration and neuronal soma growth and formation.

In neurodegenerative research, [[Klf4]] has been shown to bind to the BIG1 promoter, positively regulating it. This interaction mediates neuroinflammation and allows cell migration through the PI3K/Akt/NF-kB signaling pathway, which can improve neuroplasticity.

## Connections
- [[Klf4]]: A Yamanaka factor that regulates BIG1 expression.
- [[Neurodegenerative Diseases]]: BIG1's role in neuroinflammation and plasticity is relevant for treating these diseases.
