# Decitabine

**Decitabine** (5-aza-2'-deoxycytidine; trade name Dacogen) is a deoxyribose nucleoside analog that functions as a potent [[DNA Methylation|DNA methyltransferase]] inhibitor (DNMTi). It is a cornerstone drug for [[Epigenetic Modifiers|epigenetic therapy]] in [[Myelodysplastic Syndrome|myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS)]] and [[Acute Myeloid Leukemia|acute myeloid leukemia (AML)]]. At subcytotoxic concentrations, decitabine is also a potent inducer of [[Stress-Induced Premature Senescence|premature senescence]].

## Mechanism

Decitabine is a prodrug phosphorylated by [[Deoxycytidine kinase]] (dCK) to its triphosphate form (5-aza-dCTP), which is incorporated into DNA during [[DNA Replication|S phase]]. Once incorporated, it forms covalent adducts with [[DNMT1]], the maintenance methyltransferase. The resulting DNMT1-DNA adduct:

1. Traps DNMT1 on DNA, depleting free enzyme (proteasomal degradation).
2. Causes [[DNA Damage Response|DNA damage signaling]] from replication fork stalling at adducted sites.
3. Triggers proteasomal degradation of DNMT1, leading to passive [[DNA Demethylation|demethylation]] of CpG sites over subsequent cell divisions.

## Key Properties

- **IC50 (DNMT1 inhibition):** 0.1–1 µM in cellular assays.
- **Half-life:** ~20 minutes (plasma); rapidly deaminated by [[Cytidine deaminase]] (CDA).
- **DNA incorporation window:** Requires S-phase cells; efficacy is schedule-dependent.
- **Senescence induction:** At 0.5–2 µM in cancer cell lines, decitabine induces [[p16INK4A|p16^INK4a^]] and [[p21|p21^CIP1^]] expression, triggers [[SA-beta-gal]] activity, and activates a [[SASP]] profile within 5–7 days.

## Therapeutic Use

- **Clinical indication:** 15–20 mg/m² IV daily for 5 days per cycle in MDS/AML.
- **Response rate:** 30–50% in MDS; higher in patients with [[TET2]] mutations.
- **Mechanism of action in cancer:** Re-expression of hypermethylated tumor suppressor genes (e.g., [[CDKN2B|p15^INK4b^]], [[CDH1|E-cadherin]], [[MLH1]]).

## Resistance

Resistance arises through:
- Reduced [[dCK]] expression / increased [[CDA]] expression.
- [[DNMT3a]] / [[DNMT3b]] compensation.
- [[ABCB1]] (P-glycoprotein) efflux.
- Selection for cells with pre-existing demethylated genomes.

## Research Applications

Decitabine is the standard compound for studying DNA methylation-dependent gene silencing in vitro. Typical protocol: 1 µM decitabine for 72 h, replacing every 24 h, followed by 72 h recovery. Genome-wide methylation is assessed by [[WGBS]] or [[RRBS]].
