# Heterochromatin

Heterochromatin is a tightly closed and transcriptionally silent subtype of chromatin. With aging, there is a global reduction in heterochromatin along with a reduction in HP1 and H3K9me3 levels. However, in senescent cells, distinct domains called senescence-associated heterochromatin foci (SAHF) form at proliferative genes.
