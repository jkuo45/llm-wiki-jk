# Innate Immunity

[[Innate Immunity]] is the evolutionarily ancient, non-specific first line of host defense against pathogens and foreign genetic material, mediated by physical barriers, phagocytes ([[Macrophage|macrophages]], neutrophils, dendritic cells, [[Natural Killer Cell|NK cells]]), the complement system, and pattern-recognition receptors (TLRs, RLRs, cGAS-STING). In stem cell biology, innate immunity is particularly relevant because the methods used to deliver reprogramming factors frequently provoke it.

## Impact on Cellular Reprogramming

Studies show that viral transduction of the [[Yamanaka Factors]] ([[OSKM]]) into [[Fibroblasts]] induces a rapid innate immune response to both the vector and the exogenous transgenic DNA. This response can lead to:
- Generation of [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] (ROS);
- Oxidative [[DNA Damage]];
- Activation of the tumor-suppressor [[p53]];
- Induction of [[Senescence]] and [[Apoptosis]].

These stress events constitute a major roadblock, significantly lowering the efficiency of [[Cellular Reprogramming]]. The response is largely driven by cytosolic DNA sensing (cGAS-STING) and toll-like receptor signaling, producing type I interferons and inflammatory cytokines.

## Strategies to Overcome the Barrier

Suppressing the innate immune response improves reprogramming yield. Reagents such as [[B18R]] — a vaccinia virus-derived protein that binds and inhibits type I interferons — blunt the antiviral program and enhance iPSC derivation. Alternative integration-free delivery (episomal, mRNA, protein, or small molecules) also reduces innate immune triggering. Notably, the early innate immune burst overlaps with the activation of pluripotency markers such as [[PODXL]] and the [[Mesenchymal-to-Epithelial Transition]].

## Relationship to Adaptive Immunity

Innate immunity primes and instructs [[Adaptive Immunity]] (via [[Lymphocyte|lymphocytes]] and [[Major Histocompatibility Complex]] presentation). Understanding both arms is essential when considering the immunogenicity of iPSC-derived grafts and the clearance of senescent cells in [[Rejuvenation]] strategies.
