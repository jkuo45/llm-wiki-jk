# FL118

FL118 is a small-molecule camptothecin analog and a second-generation [[Topoisomerase I]] (TOP1) inhibitor. Derived from the SN-38 scaffold, it was designed to overcome limitations of earlier camptothecins (e.g., irinotecan, topotecan) by improving potency and selectivity. A distinguishing feature of FL118 is its ability to selectively downregulate the inhibitor of apoptosis protein [[Survivin]], broadening its anticancer effects.

## Overview

Like other camptothecins, FL118 stabilizes the covalent TOP1-DNA cleavage complex, preventing religation of DNA single-strand breaks. Collision of this trapped complex with replicating DNA polymerases converts single-strand breaks into lethal double-strand breaks, triggering [[Apoptosis]] through a [[TP53]]-dependent and -independent network. FL118 exhibits activity across colorectal, ovarian, lung, and other solid tumors.

## Mechanism of Action

- **TOP1 poisoning**: FL118 binds the TOP1-DNA complex at the interface exploited by camptothecins, locking TOP1 onto DNA and blocking religation.
- **Selective survivin suppression**: Unlike classic camptothecins, FL118 markedly reduces transcription of [[Survivin]], lowering the apoptotic threshold of cancer cells.
- **DNA damage and replication stress**: Unrepaired breaks activate ATM/ATR and checkpoint kinases, arresting the [[Cell Cycle]] and engaging death pathways.
- **Synergy**: Downregulation of survivin and other anti-apoptotic factors sensitizes tumors to additional cytotoxic agents.

## Physiological and Pathological Relevance

FL118 targets the heightened replication stress and DNA repair dependence of malignant cells, consistent with the broader strategy of [[Cancer]] therapy via TOP1 inhibition. Its survivin selectivity may spare normal tissues that rely less on survivin, potentially widening the therapeutic index relative to first-generation camptothecins.

## Clinical and Research Relevance

FL118 is primarily an investigational agent in preclinical and early translational studies, valued both as a therapeutic lead and as a chemical probe for TOP1 and survivin biology. Research directions include nanoparticle or antibody-directed delivery to improve tumor selectivity, biomarker discovery (e.g., TOP1 and survivin expression), and rational combinations with PARP or immune-targeted agents.
