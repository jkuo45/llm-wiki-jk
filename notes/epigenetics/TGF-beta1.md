# TGF-beta1

**TGF-beta1** (Transforming growth factor beta 1, [[TGF-beta1|TGF-β1]]) is a polypeptide member of the transforming growth factor beta superfamily of cytokines. It is a secreted protein that performs many cellular functions, including the control of cell growth, cell proliferation, cell differentiation, and apoptosis. It acts as a quiescence-inducing factor in several stem cell niches.

## Structure and Processing

TGF-β1 is synthesised as a 390-amino-acid precursor (pro-TGF-β1) containing an N-terminal latency-associated peptide (LAP) and a C-terminal mature cytokine domain. The precursor homodimerises and is cleaved by furin-like proteases in the Golgi, but the LAP remains non-covalently associated with the mature dimer to form the **small latent complex** (SLC). The SLC is further bound by latent TGF-β-binding proteins (LTBPs) to form the **large latent complex** (LLC), which is secreted and stored in the extracellular matrix. Activation requires release of the mature TGF-β1 from the latent complex through proteolysis, integrin binding, or mechanical force.

## Signalling Pathway

TGF-β1 signals through a heteromeric complex of **type I (TβRI/ALK5)** and **type II (TβRII)** serine/threonine kinase receptors:

1. **Receptor Binding**: TGF-β1 binds TβRII, which recruits and transphosphorylates TβRI.
2. **SMAD Activation**: Activated TβRI phosphorylates [[SMAD2]] and [[SMAD3]] at C-terminal serine residues.
3. **Complex Formation**: Phosphorylated [[SMAD2]]/[[SMAD3]] form a complex with [[SMAD4]] (co-SMAD) and translocate to the nucleus.
4. **Transcriptional Regulation**: The SMAD complex regulates target gene expression in concert with co-activators (e.g., [[P300]], [[CBP]]) and co-repressors (e.g., [[Ski]], [[SnoN]]).
5. **Negative Feedback**: Inhibitory [[Smad7]] is induced by TGF-β signalling and blocks TβRI-mediated SMAD2/3 phosphorylation.

## Physiological Functions

- **Embryonic Development**: TGF-β1 is essential for mesoderm formation, left-right axis determination, and cardiovascular development. TGF-β1 knockout mice die in utero from yolk sac vascular defects.
- **Immune Regulation**: TGF-β1 is a master anti-inflammatory cytokine. It promotes [[Regulatory T cell]] (Treg) differentiation and suppresses effector T cell and macrophage activation. Global TGF-β1 knockout mice die postnatally from lethal multi-organ inflammation.
- **Wound Healing**: TGF-β1 is released by platelets and macrophages at wound sites, promoting fibroblast proliferation, myofibroblast differentiation, and extracellular matrix deposition.
- **Stem Cell Niche Maintenance**: TGF-β1 induces quiescence in haematopoietic and neural stem cells.

## Role in Fibrosis

TGF-β1 is the primary driver of pathological fibrosis across multiple organ systems:

- **Mechanism**: TGF-β1 activates SMAD3-dependent transcription of pro-fibrotic genes including [[COL1A1]], [[COL3A1]], [[FN1]] (fibronectin), [[ACTA2]] (α-SMA), and [[TIMP1]]. It also promotes epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT) and endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EndMT).
- **Disease Contexts**: TGF-β1 drives fibrosis in [[Pulmonary Fibrosis]], [[Liver Cirrhosis]], [[Chronic Kidney Disease]], [[Cardiac Fibrosis]], and [[Systemic Sclerosis]].

## Role in Cancer

TGF-β1 exhibits a paradoxical dual role in oncology:

- **Tumour Suppressor**: In normal epithelial cells and early-stage cancers, TGF-β1 induces cell cycle arrest (via CDKN1A/p21 and CDKN2B/p15 upregulation) and apoptosis.
- **Tumour Promoter**: In advanced cancers, TGF-β1 promotes EMT, invasion, metastasis, immune evasion, and cancer stem cell maintenance. Mutations in TGFBR2 or SMAD4 are common in colorectal and pancreatic cancers, disabling the tumour-suppressive arm of TGF-β signalling.

## Sirtuin Regulation of TGF-β1 Signalling

Multiple sirtuins converge on the TGF-β1 pathway:

- **[[SIRT1]]**: Deacetylates and inhibits [[Smad7]] (the negative regulator of TGF-β signalling), promoting SMAD7 ubiquitination and degradation. SIRT1 also deacetylates the TβRI receptor to suppress its kinase activity.
- **[[SIRT6]]**: Suppresses TGF-β1 signalling by deacetylating H3K9 at the promoters of TGF-β target genes and by blocking SMAD3 transcriptional activity. SIRT6 activators ([[MDL-801]]) reduce fibrosis through TGF-β pathway suppression.
- **[[SIRT7]]**: Deacetylates [[SMAD3]] and suppresses its pro-fibrotic transcriptional programme.
