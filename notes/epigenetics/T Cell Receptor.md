# T Cell Receptor

The [[T Cell Receptor]] ([[TCR]]) is a protein complex found on the surface of [[T Lymphocyte|T cells]] (or T cells) that is responsible for recognizing fragments of antigen as peptides bound to major histocompatibility complex (MHC) molecules.
