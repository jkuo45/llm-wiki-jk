# Neural Stem Cell

[[Neural Stem Cell|Neural stem cells]] ([[NSC|NSCs]]) are self-renewing, multipotent progenitors that generate the neurons, astrocytes, and oligodendrocytes of the nervous system. During embryonic development they first give rise to radial glial [[Progenitor Cell|progenitor cells]], which populate the CNS; subsets persist into adulthood within specialized niches — the subventricular zone (SVZ) of the lateral ventricles and the subgranular zone (SGZ) of the hippocampal dentate gyrus — where they sustain lifelong [[Neurogenesis]].

## Structure & Niche

Adult NSCs are typically astrocyte-like (GFAP+) cells expressing Sox2, Nestin, and [[Notch signaling]] components. They reside in a vascular and extracellular-matrix-rich niche where signals from endothelial cells, microglia, and [[Extracellular Matrix]] regulate their fate. Quiescent NSCs ([[Quiescence]]) can be activated to proliferate and produce transiently amplifying progenitors that differentiate into neurons or glia.

## Mechanism of Action

NSC maintenance balances self-renewal and differentiation through antagonistic pathways: [[Wnt signaling]] and [[Notch signaling]] promote stemness, whereas pro-neurogenic cues (BDNF, [[FGF]], [[Shh]]) drive neuronal commitment. Epigenetic regulators — including the [[NuRD complex]], Polycomb, and DNA methyltransferases — establish and maintain the NSC transcriptional program, linking NSC biology to [[Epigenetic Remodeling]] and [[Pluripotency]]-associated networks ([[Sox2]], [[Oct4]]-like activity). Cell-cycle control via [[Retinoblastoma Protein]] and [[E2F]] governs the decision to divide or exit to differentiate.

## Physiological Function

NSCs support adult hippocampal neurogenesis (critical for learning, memory, and [[Cognition]]), olfactory bulb neuron turnover, and compensatory repair after injury. Their activity declines with [[Aging]], contributing to cognitive aging and reduced regenerative capacity.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

NSC dysfunction or loss is implicated in [[Neurodegeneration]] ([[Frontotemporal Dementia]], [[Alzheimer's Disease]], Huntington's), psychiatric disease, and age-related cognitive decline. Patient-derived [[Induced Neural Stem Cells]] — generated by reprogramming [[Mesenchymal Stromal Cells]] or fibroblasts with [[Yamanaka Factors]] and converted to NSCs — provide isogenic models of CNS disease and a platform for drug screening. NSC transplantation is being explored for stroke, spinal cord injury, and [[Rejuvenation]] of aging circuits. Understanding NSC [[Quiescence]] exit is central to harnessing endogenous repair and to controlling [[Tumorigenicity]] in stem-cell therapies.
