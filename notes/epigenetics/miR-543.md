# miR-543

## Overview

**miR-543** is a ~22-nucleotide [[MicroRNA]] that post-transcriptionally silences target genes by binding their 3'-UTRs. In the context of aging tissue it is grouped with [[miR-217]] and [[miR-378]] as a senescence-associated miRNA that represses [[SIRT1]], reinforcing chronic inflammatory signaling.

## Genomic Locus & Family

- In humans, miR-543 maps to the **imprinted DLK1–DIO3 locus on chromosome 14q32**, one of the largest mammalian miRNA megaclusters, whose expression is controlled by parent-of-origin [[DNA Methylation]] and is itself an [[Epigenetics|epigenetic]] read-out.
- Its embedding in an imprinted, methylation-regulated cluster makes miR-543 both an effector and a sensor of the epigenome.

## Validated / Likely Targets

- **[[SIRT1]]:** reported among miRNAs binding the SIRT1 3'-UTR to suppress translation in vascular and cartilage aging, reducing deacetylase activity and enabling [[p65|RelA/p65]] hyperacetylation.
- Other documented targets across tissues include **PTEN**, **KRAS**, **Smad7/TWIST1**, and **YAP1**, giving miR-543 context-dependent tumor-suppressive or oncogenic roles.

## Role in Senescence, Aging & Disease

> [!important] Imprinted-cluster miRNA
> Because miR-543 resides in the methylation-controlled DLK1–DIO3 cluster, its dysregulation connects [[Epigenetic Drift]] directly to the SIRT1–[[NF-kappaB]] inflammatory axis.

- **Vascular & cartilage aging:** upregulated with [[miR-217]] and [[miR-378]], converging on SIRT1 loss and a self-sustaining inflammatory loop ([[SASP]], [[Inflammaging]]).
- **Senescence:** contributes to the senescent phenotype through SIRT1 suppression and altered [[Histone Modification]] balance.
- **Cancer & fibrosis:** modulates proliferation, EMT, and apoptosis depending on tissue and target.

## Therapeutic Relevance

Targeted with **LNA antagomirs** (alongside [[miR-217]] and [[miR-378]]) to restore SIRT1 expression in aged endothelial and cartilage cells.

## Connections

- [[SIRT1]]: Repressed by miR-543 via 3'-UTR binding.
- [[miR-217]]: Co-upregulated senescence-associated miRNA targeting SIRT1.
- [[miR-378]]: Co-upregulated miRNA in the same SIRT1/NF-κB network.
- [[DNA Methylation]]: Governs expression of the imprinted DLK1–DIO3 cluster hosting miR-543.
- [[SASP]]: Amplified downstream of SIRT1 loss.
