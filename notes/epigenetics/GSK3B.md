# GSK3B

[[GSK3B]] (Glycogen Synthase Kinase-3 Beta) is a constitutively active serine/threonine kinase that integrates signals from the [[Wnt Signaling]], insulin/PI3K-AKT, and Hedgehog pathways. Encoded by the *GSK3B* gene on chromosome 3, it is a hub regulator of cell fate, metabolism, proliferation, and neurobiology. Its activity is normally restrained by AKT-mediated phosphorylation at Ser9 and stimulated by [[Phosphorylation]] at Tyr216.

## Structure and Regulation

GSK3B consists of an N-terminal catalytic kinase domain and a C-terminal regulatory tail. Uniquely among kinases, GSK3B requires a "priming" phosphate on a downstream substrate residue (created by another kinase) before it can phosphorylate the substrate N-terminally—a substrate-templating mechanism underpinning its broad target range. In the absence of Wnt ligand, GSK3B resides in the β-catenin destruction complex (with APC, Axin, and CK1), phosphorylating β-catenin for degradation. Wnt pathway activation inhibits GSK3B, stabilizing β-catenin.

## Role in Cellular Reprogramming and Pluripotency

[[GSK3B]] acts as an inhibitor of the pluripotent state. In [[Cellular Reprogramming]], its chemical inhibition (e.g., with CHIR99021) markedly enhances the efficiency of [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] generation and sustains [[Pluripotency]] in [[Embryonic Stem Cells]]. One mechanism is the prevention of the [[Epithelial-to-Mesenchymal Transition]] (EMT), preserving the epithelial morphology required for pluripotency. GSK3B inhibition also stabilizes key substrates—[[c-Myc]] and [[MYCN]]—blocking their proteasomal degradation so they can drive pluripotency genes such as [[Nanog]] and *DNMT3a*. The module [[Oct4]]–[[FRAT2]]–[[GSK3B]]–[[MYCN]] illustrates how OSKM expression suppresses GSK3B activity, amplifying the stemness signal. GSK3B is a highly connected node in the OSKM interaction network.

## Physiological and Pathological Relevance

Beyond reprogramming, GSK3B regulates glycogen metabolism (its namesake), neuronal polarity, and apoptosis. Its dysregulation is implicated in [[Cancer]] (where it can be tumor-suppressive or oncogenic depending on context), type 2 diabetes, and neurodegenerative disease; GSK3B inhibitors are investigated as neuroprotective and mood-stabilizing agents.

## Connections

- [[Wnt Signaling]]: GSK3B is the central kinase of the Wnt destruction complex; its inhibition activates canonical Wnt.
- [[c-Myc]] / [[MYCN]]: GSK3B substrates whose stabilization upon inhibition promotes pluripotency.
- [[Epithelial-to-Mesenchymal Transition]]: GSK3B inhibition prevents EMT, favoring the epithelial state.
- [[Nanog]]: GSK3B inhibition promotes Nanog activation, a core pluripotency regulator.
- [[FRAT2]]: An endogenous GSK3B-binding protein that antagonizes its activity in the Oct4 module.
