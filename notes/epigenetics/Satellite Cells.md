# Satellite Cells

Satellite cells (SCs), also known as muscle stem cells, are small mononuclear cells found in mature muscle. They are situated between the sarcolemma and the basement membrane of a muscle fiber.

Satellite cells are responsible for the growth and repair of skeletal muscle. In research involving [[Yamanaka Factors]], it has been found that short-term expression of these factors in myofibers can modify the stem cell niche, thereby accelerating the regenerative capacity of satellite cells and helping to reverse muscle aging.
