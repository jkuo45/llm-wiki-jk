# Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells

[[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs) are a type of [[Pluripotency|pluripotent]] stem cell generated directly from differentiated adult [[Somatic Cells]] by ectopic expression of defined transcription factors. The technology was pioneered by [[Shinya Yamanaka]] and colleagues in 2006, who demonstrated that four factors — [[Oct4]], [[Sox2]], [[Klf4]], and [[c-Myc]], collectively the [[Yamanaka Factors]] (or [[OSKM]] cassette) — could revert fibroblasts to an embryonic-stem-cell-like state. Kazutoshi Yamanaka shared the 2012 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for this discovery. iPSCs self-renew indefinitely and can differentiate into all three germ layers (ectoderm, mesoderm, endoderm), paralleling [[Embryonic Stem Cells]] without the ethical constraints of embryo derivation.

## Mechanism of Reprogramming

Reprogramming is a gradual, stochastic transition requiring sustained transgene expression (typically 1–2 weeks). The factors act as a pioneer-like network: [[Oct4]] and [[Sox2]] form a核心 heterodimer occupying both active and silent chromatin, recruiting [[Nanog]] and chromatin remodelers such as the [[NuRD complex]]. [[Klf4]] and [[c-Myc]] reinforce proliferation and open chromatin. Mesenchymal-to-epithelial transition (MET), global DNA demethylation, [[Histone]] mark resetting (loss of H3K9me3/H3K27me3 repressive marks, gain of H3K4me3/H3K27ac active marks), and X-chromosome reactivation ultimately establish the pluripotent ground state. Partial reprogramming (e.g., to [[Induced Neural Stem Cells]] or [[Mesenchymal Stromal Cells]]-derived intermediates) is used to study [[Cellular Reprogramming]] without full pluripotency. [[MYCN]] and [[Wnt signaling]] augment efficiency by stabilizing pluripotency-associated factors.

## Physiological & Developmental Significance

iPSCs recapitulate the naive and primed pluripotent states and provide an essentially unlimited source of patient-matched cells. They bridge basic [[Stem Cell Research]] with translational medicine, enabling the study of human lineage specification that is otherwise inaccessible.

## Clinical & Research Relevance

iPSCs have transformed [[Disease Modeling]] for genetic disorders — including [[Frontotemporal Dementia]], [[Olivopontocerebellar Atrophy]], and [[Neurodegeneration]] — by generating isogenic neuronal models from patient fibroblasts. They power [[Drug Discovery]] (toxicity screening, phenotype-based assays), gene-correction strategies, and [[Regenerative Medicine]] applications such as retinal pigment epithelium grafts, cardiomyocyte patches, and dopaminergic neurons in Parkinson's trials. Pluripotency is conventionally validated by the [[Teratoma]] assay (tumor formation in immunodeficient mice containing all three germ layers), a gold-standard readout of differentiation potential. Key limitations are incomplete reprogramming, genomic integration from integrating vectors (mitigated by Sendai viral, episomal, and mRNA/[[CRISPR]]-based methods), and residual [[Tumorigenicity]] risk from undifferentiated cells. iPSCs also serve as a platform for [[Rejuvenation]] research, where transient [[Reprogramming]] can reset age-associated epigenetic clocks without erasing cell identity.
