# Extracellular Matrix

The [[Extracellular Matrix]] ([[ECM]]) is a three-dimensional network consisting of extracellular macromolecules and minerals, such as collagen, enzymes, and glycoproteins, that provide structural and biochemical support to surrounding cells. It plays a key role in providing niche signals for [[Quiescence]].
