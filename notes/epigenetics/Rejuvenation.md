# Rejuvenation

Rejuvenation refers to the process of reversing the aging process in cells, tissues, or organisms. In stem cell research, this is often achieved through [[Cellular Reprogramming]] using [[Yamanaka Factors]].

Partial reprogramming is a technique where the expression of [[OSKM]] factors is induced for a short period, allowing cells to regain youthful characteristics without losing their somatic identity or turning into [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]], thereby avoiding the risk of teratoma formation.

## Connections
- [[Yamanaka Factors]]: The primary tools used for epigenetic rejuvenation.
- [[Senescence]]: The biological aging process that rejuvenation aims to reverse.
- [[OSKM]]: The core set of transcription factors used in rejuvenation studies.
- [[Partial Reprogramming]]: A method to achieve rejuvenation while maintaining cell identity.
