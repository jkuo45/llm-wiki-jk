# Nucleosome Remodeling

**Nucleosome remodeling** is the process of altering the structure, composition, and positioning of nucleosomes to regulate access to genomic DNA.

## Overview
- Driven by: ATP-dependent remodeling complexes (e.g., [[SWI/SNF]])
- Role in aging: Progressive loss of remodeling efficiency can lead to genomic instability and aberrant gene expression.
