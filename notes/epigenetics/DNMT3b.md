# DNMT3b

**[[DNMT3b]]** (DNA Methyltransferase 3 beta) is a "de novo" methyltransferase that works alongside [[DNMT3a]] to establish new [[DNA Methylation]] patterns. It plays a critical role in setting up the epigenetic landscape during early development and is essential for silencing repetitive elements.

### 1. Key Functions
DNMT3b is particularly important for the methylation of pericentromeric satellite repeats. Mutations in the DNMT3b gene are the cause of ICF syndrome (Immunodeficiency, Centromere instability, and Facial anomalies), highlighting its role in maintaining [[Genomic Instability|genomic integrity]].

### 2. Role in [[notes/_link/Aging]] and Disease
As with other DNMTs, the expression of DNMT3b can be altered during [[notes/_link/Aging]]:
*   **[[Cancer]]**: Overexpression of DNMT3b is frequently observed in various cancers, where it contributes to the hypermethylation and silencing of tumor suppressor genes.
*   **Developmental Remnants:** While primarily active during development, its continued or misdirected activity in aging can lead to the accumulation of abnormal methylation marks.

### 3. Comparison with [[DNMT3a]]
While both are de novo methyltransferases, DNMT3b has a higher preference for certain types of repetitive DNA, whereas [[DNMT3a]] is more involved in the methylation of specific gene bodies and intergenic regions.
