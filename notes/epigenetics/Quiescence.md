# Quiescence

[[Quiescence]] is a state of reversible proliferative arrest in which cells exit the [[Cell Cycle]] but retain the capacity to reenter it upon receiving appropriate stimuli. It is a critical state for stem cell maintenance, tissue repair, and organismal longevity.
