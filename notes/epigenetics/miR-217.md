# miR-217

## Overview

**miR-217** is a ~22-nucleotide non-coding [[MicroRNA]] belonging to the miR-217/miR-216 cluster. It functions as a post-transcriptional regulator of gene expression by base-pairing with the 3'-untranslated region (3'-UTR) of target [[Messenger RNA|mRNAs]], leading to translational repression and/or transcript degradation. miR-217 is best characterized as a pro-senescence, pro-inflammatory miRNA that accumulates in aging vascular endothelium and articular cartilage.

## Genomic Locus & Family

- In humans, miR-217 is encoded within the **miR-217–miR-216a–miR-216b cluster** on chromosome 2p16.1, embedded in an intron of a host non-coding transcript.
- The mature guide strand is highly conserved across vertebrates, and its seed sequence directs target recognition.

## Validated / Likely Targets

- **[[SIRT1]]** — the canonical, experimentally validated target. miR-217 binds the SIRT1 3'-UTR and suppresses translation, reducing NAD+-dependent deacetylase activity (Menghini et al., *Circulation* 2009; PMID: 19188522).
- Downstream consequence: loss of SIRT1 leads to hyperacetylation of **[[p65|RelA/p65]]** at Lys310, stabilizing active [[NF-kappaB]] and driving chronic inflammation.
- Additional reported targets include components of the [[PTEN]]/PI3K–AKT axis and forkhead ([[FOXO]]) signaling in various tissues.

## Role in Senescence, Aging & Disease

> [!important] SIRT1–NF-κB axis
> By silencing SIRT1, miR-217 removes a key brake on inflammatory signaling, linking miRNA dysregulation directly to [[Inflammaging]] and the [[SASP]].

- **[[Endothelial senescence]]:** miR-217 accumulates with replicative age in human endothelial cells, impairing angiogenesis and eNOS function via SIRT1 loss.
- **Vascular aging & atherosclerosis:** overexpression promotes a senescent, pro-inflammatory endothelial phenotype.
- **Osteoarthritis:** upregulated in aging articular cartilage, contributing to chondrocyte senescence and matrix degradation.
- **[[Epigenetics]] link:** because SIRT1 deacetylates histones (H3K9, H4K16) and non-histone substrates, miR-217-mediated SIRT1 loss shifts the chromatin landscape toward a de-repressed, inflammatory state ([[Histone Modification]]).

## Therapeutic Relevance

miR-217 is a candidate target for **LNA antagomirs** delivered in lipid nanoparticles to restore SIRT1 activity in aged/inflamed vascular and cartilage tissue.
