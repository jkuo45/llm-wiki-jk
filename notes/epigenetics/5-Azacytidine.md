# 5-Azacytidine

**5-Azacytidine** (5-aza-CR; trade name Vidaza) is a ribose nucleoside analog of cytidine that functions as a dual [[DNA Methylation|DNA methyltransferase (DNMT)]] inhibitor and [[RNA]] incorporation agent. It is the prototypical [[Epigenetic Modifiers|epidrug]] and first-line therapy for [[Myelodysplastic Syndrome|myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS)]]. It is also a potent inducer of [[Stress-Induced Premature Senescence|premature senescence]] in vitro.

## Mechanism

5-Azacytidine is phosphorylated by [[Uridine-cytidine kinase]] to its triphosphate form and incorporated into both [[DNA]] (during replication) and [[RNA]] (during transcription). When incorporated into DNA:

- Covalently traps [[DNMT1]], depleting it via proteasomal degradation.
- Causes passive demethylation over subsequent cell divisions.
- Triggers a [[DNA Damage Response]] from replication fork stalling at DNMT1-DNA adducts.

RNA incorporation disrupts RNA metabolism: it inhibits [[tRNA]] methylation (affecting translation), impairs [[rRNA]] processing, and alters [[mRNA]] splicing.

## Senescence Induction

5-Azacytidine (at 0.5–5 µM in vitro) induces senescence in both normal and cancer cells through:

- Re-expression of hypermethylated tumor suppressor genes ([[CDKN2B|p15^INK4b^]], [[CDH1]]).
- Activation of [[p53]]-[[p21]] axis through replication stress signaling.
- Upregulation of [[p16INK4A]] upon demethylation of the [[CDKN2A]] promoter.
- [[SASP]] elaboration ([[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], [[MMP3]]) within 5–7 days.

## Clinical Use

| Regimen | Dose | Indication |
|---|---|---|
| FDA-approved MDS regimen | 75 mg/m² SC/IV daily × 7 days, q28d | Higher-risk MDS, CMML-2 |
| Low-dose (research) | 0.3–0.5 µM continuous | Epigenetic reprogramming in vitro |

Response rates in MDS are 40–50%, with better outcomes in patients with [[TET2]] mutations. The mechanism in MDS involves reactivation of silenced tumor suppressors and induction of terminal differentiation or senescence in leukemic cells.

## Limitations

- **Chemical instability:** Rapid hydrolysis in aqueous solution (half-life ~4 h at 37°C, pH 7.4); must be freshly prepared.
- **Cytidine deaminase sensitivity:** Rapidly inactivated (t½ ~20 min in plasma).
- **DNA vs. RNA effects:** RNA incorporation contributes to cytotoxicity; [[Decitabine]] (deoxyribose analog) is more selective for DNA.
- **Resistance:** Occurs through dCK downregulation, CDA upregulation, and compensatory methylation by [[DNMT3a]]/[[DNMT3b]].

## Research Notes

- **Stock solution:** 10 mM in DMSO (stored at −80°C for < 1 month).
- **Working concentration:** 0.5–5 µM in culture.
- **Replenishment:** Replace with fresh drug every 24 h due to instability.
- **Positive control:** [[Decitabine]] at equivalent concentrations for comparative studies.
