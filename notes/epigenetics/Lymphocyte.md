# Lymphocyte

A [[Lymphocyte]] is a class of white blood cell central to the adaptive [[Immune System]] of jawed vertebrates. Lymphocytes comprise three principal lineages: [[T Lymphocyte|T cells]] (thymus-derived), [[B Cell|B cells]] (bone-marrow-derived antiblast producers), and [[Natural Killer Cell|natural killer (NK) cells]]. They are distinguished morphologically by a large, round nucleus occupying most of the cell and sparse cytoplasm.

## Development & Subtypes

B cells mature in the bone marrow, where V(D)J recombination assembles diverse [[T Cell Receptor|B cell receptors (BCRs)]]; T cells mature in the thymus after their [[T Cell Receptor|T cell receptors (TCRs)]] are selected for self-MHC restriction and central tolerance. Functional T-cell subsets include CD4⁺ helper T cells (Th1/Th2/Th17/Treg), CD8⁺ cytotoxic T cells, and innate-like γδ T cells. Mature lymphocytes recirculate between blood, lymph, and secondary lymphoid organs (lymph nodes, spleen, mucosa-associated tissue).

## State of Quiescence & Activation

Naïve lymphocytes are largely in a state of [[Quiescence]] — metabolically restrained yet poised for rapid expansion. Upon antigen recognition (TCR/MHC or BCR engagement) plus co-stimulation, they exit quiescence, undergo clonal expansion, and differentiate into effector and memory cells. This controlled proliferation is essential for immune memory but is also tightly regulated to prevent autoimmunity and [[Lymphoma|lymphoid malignancies]].

## Interactions with Innate Immunity

Although lymphocytes define adaptive immunity, they cooperate intimately with [[Innate Immunity]]: dendritic cells and macrophages present antigen via the [[Major Histocompatibility Complex]] (MHC), and innate cytokines shape T-cell polarization. NK cells bridge the two arms, killing stressed or MHC-deficient cells without prior sensitization.

## Links to Epigenetics & Aging

Lymphocyte fate, subset specification, and memory formation are governed by dynamic epigenetic remodeling (DNA methylation, enhancer accessibility), making them a tractable model for studying [[Epigenetic Clock]] aging and [[Immunosenescence]]. Reprogramming and iPSC derivation from lymphocytes is also common, given their accessibility from blood.
