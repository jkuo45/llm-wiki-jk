# Lymphocyte

A [[Lymphocyte]] is a type of white blood cell in the [[Immune System]] of jawed vertebrates. Lymphocytes include [[Natural Killer Cell|natural killer cells]], [[T Lymphocyte|T cells]], and [[B Cell|B cells]]. Many lymphocytes reside in a state of [[Quiescence]] until activated by antigens.
