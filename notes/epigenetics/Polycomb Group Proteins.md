# Polycomb Group Proteins

**Polycomb group proteins (PcG)** are a family of proteins that maintain gene silencing through epigenetic modifications.

## Overview
- Form complexes like PRC1 and PRC2.
- Essential for developmental regulation and maintaining cell identity.

## Link to Aging
- Changes in PcG-mediated silencing are observed during aging and in [[notes/_link/Cancer]].
