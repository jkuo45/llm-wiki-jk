# Polycomb Group Proteins

**Polycomb group (PcG) proteins** are evolutionarily conserved chromatin regulators that maintain transcriptional repression of developmental genes, cell cycle regulators, and senescence-associated loci through deposition of repressive [[Histone Modification|histone marks]]. First discovered in *[[Drosophila melanogaster]]* as regulators of [[Hox gene]] silencing, PcG proteins form two principal multi-subunit complexes: [[PRC1]] and [[PRC2]].

## Complex Composition and Function

### PRC2 (Polycomb Repressive Complex 2)
PRC2 catalyzes the tri-methylation of [[H3K27me3|histone H3 lysine 27]] (H3K27me3), a hallmark of facultative [[Heterochromatin]]. Core components:
- [[EZH2]] or [[EZH1]]: Catalytic subunit with [[SET domain]] methyltransferase activity.
- [[SUZ12]]: Essential for complex integrity and nucleosome binding.
- [[EED]]: Binds H3K27me3 to propagate the mark through positive feedback.
- [[RBAP48]]/[[RBAP46]]: Histone chaperone subunits.

### PRC1 (Polycomb Repressive Complex 1)
PRC1 ubiquitinates [[H2AK119ub1|H2A at lysine 119]], compacting chromatin and blocking [[RNA Polymerase II]] elongation. Canonical PRC1 contains [[RING1B]]/[[RING1A]] (catalytic RING E3 ligase) and [[CBX]] proteins (bind H3K27me3). Variant PRC1 complexes (containing [[RYBP]]/[[YAF2]] instead of CBX) deposit H2AK119ub1 independently of PRC2.

## Regulation of Senescence

PcG proteins are central to the senescence gene expression program:

- **CDKN2A repression:** In young cells, PRC2 maintains [[H3K27me3]] at the [[CDKN2A]] locus, silencing [[p16INK4A]] and p14^ARF^. During senescence, PRC2 eviction from CDKN2A derepresses p16^INK4a^.
- **Senescence gene silencing:** PRC1/2 are recruited to [[E2F]] target genes during senescence, contributing to [[SAHF]]-associated repression.
- **SASP regulation:** PRC1 represses [[IL-6]] and [[IL-8]] in non-senescent cells; PRC1 loss in senescence contributes to SASP activation.

## Aging and Disease

- **PcG decline with age:** EZH2 levels decrease in aged hematopoietic stem cells and neural stem cells, contributing to derepression of lineage-inappropriate genes and stem cell exhaustion.
- **Cancer:** EZH2 is overexpressed or hyperactivated (by gain-of-function mutations) in multiple cancers, including [[Breast Cancer|breast]], [[Prostate Cancer|prostate]], [[Lymphoma|lymphomas]] (e.g., [[Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma|DLBCL]]). [[Tazemetostat]] (EZH2 inhibitor) is FDA-approved for epithelioid sarcoma and follicular lymphoma.
- **Epigenetic therapy:** PRC2 inhibitors represent a major class of [[Epigenetic Modifiers]]. Combining EZH2 inhibitors with [[Senolytic Drugs]] is being explored to prevent senescence escape in cancer.
