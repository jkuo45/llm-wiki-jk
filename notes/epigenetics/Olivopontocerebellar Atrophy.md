# Olivopontocerebellar Atrophy

Olivopontocerebellar Atrophy (OPCA) is the degeneration of neurons in the cerebellum, pons, and inferior olives. It is a form of [[Neurodegenerative Diseases|neurodegeneration]] that often presents with progressive ataxia and other motor impairments.

In the context of [[Yamanaka Factors]] research, [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs) have been developed from the fibroblasts of OPCA patients to obtain patient-specific neural cells. These cells can be used for disease modeling and potential future cell replacement therapies.
