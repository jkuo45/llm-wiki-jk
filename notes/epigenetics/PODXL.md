# PODXL

[[PODXL]] (Podocalyxin-like) is a cell surface glycoprotein originally identified in kidney podocytes but later found to be a highly specific marker for undifferentiated [[Embryonic Stem Cells]] (ESCs) and [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs).

## Role in Reprogramming
During the early stages of [[Cellular Reprogramming]] (24-72 hours post-induction of [[OSKM]]), PODXL is one of the pluripotency-associated surface antigens that is rapidly "switched on."
- It can be used for fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS) to enrich for cells that have successfully initiated the reprogramming program.
- Pre-selection of PODXL-positive cells is a strategy to improve the efficiency and speed of deriving iPSC lines.

## Connections
- [[Embryonic Stem Cells]]: PODXL is a canonical surface marker for the undifferentiated ESC state.
- [[Cellular Reprogramming]]: Its early expression makes it a valuable tool for monitoring and enriching reprogramming cells.
- [[OSKM]]: The factors that drive the expression of PODXL in somatic cells.
