# Muscle Stem Cell

[[Muscle Stem Cell|Muscle stem cells]], also known as [[Satellite Cells|satellite cells]], are small mononuclear progenitor cells with virtually no cytoplasm found in mature muscle. They are situated between the sarcolemma and the endomysium. They are typically in a state of [[Quiescence]] and are activated upon muscle injury.
