# Entities

- [[Wheat Berries]]: The whole wheat kernel, consisting of bran, germ, and endosperm.
- [[Milled Wheat]]: Wheat ground into various types of flour.
- [[Enriched Flour]]: Flour with added nutrients.
- [[Bran]]: The protective outer layer of the wheat kernel.
- [[Germ]]: The nutrient-dense embryo of the wheat kernel.
- [[Endosperm]]: The starchy energy source of the wheat kernel.
- [[Semolina]]: Coarse particles of endosperm from durum wheat.
