# Bran

[[Bran]] is the hard, protective outer layer of the [[Wheat Berries]] (and other cereal grains). It is a byproduct of the milling process when producing [[Refined Flour]].

## Characteristics
- **Nutritional Value**: High in dietary fiber, essential fatty acids, vitamins (primarily B-vitamins), and minerals.
- **Processing**: Separated from the [[Endosperm]] and [[Germ]] during the milling of [[Milled Wheat]].

## Culinary Uses
- **Fiber Supplement**: Often added to cereals, muffins, and breads to increase fiber content.
- **Animal Feed**: A common component in livestock nutrition.
