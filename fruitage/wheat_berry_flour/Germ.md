# Germ

The [[Germ]] is the embryo of the [[Wheat Berries]], the part of the seed that sprouts into a new plant. It is the most nutrient-dense part of the grain.

## Characteristics
- **Nutritional Value**: Rich in Vitamin E, folate, phosphorus, thiamin, zinc, and magnesium. It also contains healthy fats.
- **Shelf Life**: Because of its high oil content, the germ can go rancid quickly, which is why it is removed to produce [[Refined Flour]].

## Culinary Uses
- **Health Food**: Eaten on its own as a toasted topping for yogurt or cereal.
- **Baking**: Added to [[Whole-wheat flour]] products for flavor and nutrition.
