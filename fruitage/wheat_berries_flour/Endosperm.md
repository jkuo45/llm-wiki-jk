# Endosperm

The [[Endosperm]] is the largest part of the [[Wheat Berries]], making up about 83% of the kernel. It provides the energy source for the developing embryo ([[Germ]]).

## Characteristics
- **Nutritional Value**: Primarily composed of starch and protein. It is the source of white flour.
- **Processing**: Isolated during the milling of [[Milled Wheat]] to create [[Refined Flour]] and [[Semolina]].

## Culinary Uses
- **Flour Production**: The base for almost all white flours used in baking and pasta making.
