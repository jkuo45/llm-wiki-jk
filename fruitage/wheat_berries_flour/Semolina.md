# Semolina

[[Semolina]] is the coarse, purified wheat middlings (large particles of [[Endosperm]]) of [[Durum]] wheat mainly used in making pasta and couscous.

## Characteristics
- **Texture**: Gritty and coarse compared to regular [[Flour]].
- **High Gluten**: [[Durum]] semolina is high in gluten, which provides the elasticity needed for pasta.

## Culinary Uses
- **Pasta**: The primary ingredient for Italian pasta.
- **Puddings**: Used in sweet desserts and puddings in various cultures.
- **Couscous**: The base for this North African staple.
