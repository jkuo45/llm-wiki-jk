# Zero-shot Prompting

[[Zero-shot Prompting]] is a technique where a [[Large Language Model]] ([[LLM]]) is given a task without any prior examples or demonstrations. The model relies entirely on its pre-trained knowledge and the specific instructions provided in the prompt.

## Key Characteristics
- No examples are provided in the prompt.
- Relies on the model's ability to follow [[instructions]].
- Performance is heavily dependent on the clarity and specificity of the instruction.

## Comparison
- Contrast with [[Few-Shot Prompting]], which provides examples.
- Often used as a baseline to evaluate a model's inherent capabilities.
