## Current 12 Domains

**01. Low-Dose Radiation** — Badge: `contested`
- Natural background variation is within evolutionary exposure; no deliberate dosing.
- E.g. Avoid unnecessary CT scans; natural background (2-10 mSv/yr) is not harmful.
- **Avoid:** Chronic high-dose LDR; LNT zero-tolerance framing.
- Sensors `ATM`, `p53`, `NF-kB`; effectors DNA repair (BRCA1, GADD45), CD69+ T cells, NRF2/ARE enzymes.

**02. ROS Signaling / Exercise** — Badge: `strong`
- Exercise ROS drives mitochondrial biogenesis via AMPK-PGC-1alpha.
- E.g. 150 min/wk moderate aerobic + 1-2 HIIT; no vitamin C/E around workouts.
- **Avoid:** High-dose antioxidant co-supplementation; chronic maximal ROS scavenging.
- Sensors `AMPK`, `NRF2`, `PGC-1alpha`; effectors mitochondrial biogenesis, SOD/catalase/GPx, TFEB-lysosomal biogenesis.

**03. Inflammation / EIMD** — Badge: `strong`
- Acute inflammation drives repair; repeated bouts build hormetic memory.
- E.g. Eccentric work (downhill, slow negatives); reserve NSAIDs for injury, not DOMS; let mild fever run.
- **Avoid:** Post-exercise NSAIDs; blunting acute fever in mild infection.
- Sensors `NF-kB`, `TNF-alpha`, `IL-6`; effectors satellite cells, muscle regeneration, repeated-bout adaptation.

**04. Intermittent Hypoxia** — Badge: `emerging`
- Brief low-O2 pulses trigger EPO/VEGF/BDNF via HIF-1alpha.
- E.g. 5 min hypoxia / 5 min normoxia cycles, 60-120 min, 3-5x/wk for 2-4 wks; or train in hypoxia.
- **Avoid:** Chronic severe hypoxia; uncontrolled sleep apnea.
- Sensors `HIF-1alpha`, `AMPK`, `SIRT1`; effectors EPO, VEGF, BDNF, BAT activation.

**05. Caloric Stress / Fasting** — Badge: `strong`
- Energy deficit triggers autophagy; the glucose-to-BHB switch is the signal.
- E.g. 12-14h overnight fast building to 16:8; periodic 24-36h; protein on feed days.
- **Avoid:** Chronic severe deprivation; isocaloric TRF without deficit.
- Sensors `AMPK`, `mTORC1 inhibition`, `SIRT1`, `TFEB`; effectors autophagy/mitophagy, BDNF-neurogenesis, lysosomal biogenesis.

**06. Immune / Old Friends** — Badge: `strong`
- Microbial exposure trains Tregs and immune tolerance.
- E.g. Gardening, pets, outdoor play; kimchi/kefir/sauerkraut; diverse fiber; targeted hygiene.
- **Avoid:** Routine antibiotic overuse; complete microbial avoidance; over-sanitization.
- Sensors `TLR`, `TGF-beta`, `IL-25`, `TSLP`; effectors Treg differentiation, tolerogenic DCs, Th1/Th17 to Th2/Treg skew.

**07. Heat / Cold Exposure** — Badge: `strong`
- Sauna 4-7x/wk halves CVD mortality; cold builds BAT, RBM3, dopamine.
- E.g. Sauna 15 min at 80C 1-5x/wk; cold 1-3 min at 10-15C; Soberg 11 min cold + 57 min heat/wk, end on cold.
- **Avoid:** Heat stroke; hypothermia/cardiac events; permanent thermal comfort.
- Sensors `HSF1/HSP70/HSP90`, `AMPK`, `UCP1`, `SIRT1/3`; effectors proteostasis, BAT, RBM3, norepinephrine, endothelial training.

**08. Psychological Stress Inoculation** — Badge: `emerging`
- Manageable challenges grow prefrontal cortex; chronic overload shrinks hippocampus.
- E.g. Graduated cold, physical, speaking, cognitive challenges; SIT 8-15 sessions; recover between.
- **Avoid:** Chronic overwhelming stress; trauma without recovery; total adversity avoidance.
- Sensors `HPA-cortisol`, `norepinephrine`, `BDNF`; effectors PFC thickening, dendritic branching, synaptogenesis, cognitive reserve.

**09. Xenohormesis / Phytochemicals** — Badge: `strong`
- Plant mild-toxins switch on 10-100x endogenous antioxidants via NRF2.
- E.g. Broccoli sprouts, turmeric + pepper, berries, green tea; rotate sources.
- **Avoid:** High-dose single-antioxidant supplements; antioxidant framing that ignores hormesis.
- Sensors `NRF2`, `HSF1`, `AMPK`, `SIRT1`; effectors SOD/catalase/GPx/HO-1, phase II detox, proteostasis.

**10. Low-Dose Alcohol (J-Curve)** — Badge: `contested`
- J-curve is contested; WHO 2023: no safe level; do not start for health.
- E.g. If abstinent, stay abstinent; if drinking, max 1-2/day with full risk weighting.
- **Avoid:** Heavy drinking; using J-curve to justify binges; ignoring Mendelian confounders.
- Sensors `AMPK`, `eNOS`, `HDL-C`; effectors HDL up, fibrinogen/platelets down, insulin sensitivity, eNOS.

**11. Gut Microbial Colonization** — Badge: `strong`
- Fiber + fermented foods drive SCFA-autophagy and barrier integrity.
- E.g. 30+ plant species/wk; kefir/kimchi/miso daily; inulin/FOS (garlic, onion); probiotics with antibiotics.
- **Avoid:** Broad-spectrum antibiotics; low-fiber diet; chronic PPI.
- Sensors `GPR41/43`, `TLR`, `vagus gut-brain`; effectors SCFA (butyrate), Tregs, barrier integrity, BDNF.

**12. Mechanical Stress on Bone** — Badge: `emerging`
- Loading is the primary bone signal; calcium/D insufficient without it.
- E.g. Walking, stairs, squats/deadlifts, jumps/running 2-4x/wk; progressive overload.
- **Avoid:** Bed-rest disuse; sudden overload; wear-and-tear framing.
- Sensors `osteocyte mechanotransduction`, `irisin/IL-6/IGF-1`; effectors osteoblast activation, BMD, tendon strengthening.

## Candidate Domains 13-19

### P0 — Add first (strong RCT base, distinct sensors)

**13. Light / Photobiomodulation** — Badge: `strong`

- Morning sunlight resets sleep/mood; red light helps skin/joint pain.
- E.g. 5-15 min outdoor light before 10am without sunglasses; work near window; dim warm lights after sunset; red/NIR panel 5-10 min 1-3x/wk.
- **Avoid:** Bright screens 11pm-2am; all-day indoor darkness; >20 min red-light sessions.
- Sensors `OPN4/melanopsin`, `CLOCK/BMAL1`; effectors mitochondrial biogenesis, BDNF, circadian reset. Dose 1-10 J/cm2 stim / >20 inhib. Vocab: photobiomodulation, LLLT, red/NIR, heliotherapy (~80% invisible).

**14. Chemical / Pharmaceutical hormesis** — Badge: `strong`

- Bitter plant foods mildly stress cells so your own defenses turn on; prescription versions are doctor-only.
- E.g. Broccoli sprouts, turmeric + black pepper, green tea, bitter greens; do NOT self-dose metformin/rapamycin.
- **Avoid:** Megadose antioxidant pills around exercise; home pesticide/metal exposure is NOT hormesis.
- Sensors `AMPK/mTORC1`; effectors autophagy/mitophagy, PGC-1alpha, GDF15. Vocab: Arndt-Schulz, biphasic dose-response, metformin/rapamycin pleiotropy (~65% invisible).

**15. Ischemic Pre/Post/Remote Conditioning** — Badge: `strong`

- Brief controlled blood-flow cutoff before surgery protects heart/brain; reduces infarct 30-60%.
- E.g. Clinic cuff protocol 5 min x 3-4 cycles pre-procedure; lifestyle proxy is interval exercise only — not a substitute.
- **Avoid:** DIY tourniquets; prolonged occlusion.
- Sensors `adenosine/PKC`, `HIF-1alpha`, `mitoK-ATP`; effectors EPO/VEGF. Windows early 1-3h, delayed 12-72h. Vocab: IPC, RIPC, postconditioning, ischemic tolerance (~75% invisible).

### P1 — Add next (fills mechanistic gaps)

**16. Proteotoxic / ER hormesis (UPRmt/UPRer)** — Badge: `emerging`

- Heat + hard exercise teach cells to refold damaged proteins; protects aging brain/muscle.
- E.g. Sauna 15-20 min at 80C 3-5x/wk; hard sets near-failure + protein; post-exercise sauna; protect sleep for repair phase.
- **Avoid:** Chronic sleep deprivation; extreme heat stroke.
- Sensors `HSF1/ATF4/XBP1`; effectors HSP70/HSP90, ERAD, mitophagy. Vocab: UPRmt, proteostasis priming, ER hormesis (~70% invisible).

**17. Dehydration / Osmotic stress** — Badge: `emerging`

- One sweaty session then full rehydration trains water-handling and heat/cold tolerance.
- E.g. Sauna sweat + water + electrolytes; heat-acclimation walks; full rehydration after.
- **Avoid:** Chronic dehydration; unsafe if kidney disease, elderly on diuretics.
- Sensors `osmosensing MAPK`, `NRF2`; effectors aquaporins, HSP/cryoprotectants, cross-tolerance. Vocab: desiccation hardening, osmopriming, POS (~85% invisible).

### P2 — Contested / comparative-physiology (flag badge)

**18. Gravity / Vibration / Crowding** — Badge: `emerging`

- Bones need impact, not just calcium; brief jumps carry groceries-up-stairs signals.
- E.g. Jump rope 2-5 min, stair hops, farmer's carry, gardening digging; vibration plate 10 min 2-3x/wk if joints limit impact.
- **Avoid:** Sudden maximal jumps without progression; bed rest.
- Sensors `integrin/YAP-TAZ`, `AMPK`; effectors bone/muscle remodeling, fecundity/longevity +30-60%. Vocab: hypergravity conditioning, whole-body vibration, crowding hormesis (~85% invisible).

**19. Hyperoxia / Ozone / Acoustic** — Badge: `contested`

- Clinic oxygen/ozone is unproven for home use; sound enrichment builds attention reserve.
- E.g. Choir, music practice, nature sound walks; earplugs at concerts; HBOT/ozone clinic-only.
- **Avoid:** Home ozone generators; chronic loud noise; unsupervised HBOT.
- Sensors `NRF2/HIF-1alpha`, `BDNF`; effectors antioxidant rebound, cognitive reserve. Vocab: hyperoxic preconditioning, HBOT, ozone therapy, sound enrichment (~80-90% invisible).
