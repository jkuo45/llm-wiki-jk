# Selenium attenuation of Cd/Pb-induced necroptosis via MAPK/NF-kB — Web Research
Date: 11_Sep_2026 12:00 PM PDT
Source framing: Niu et al. 2026, "Regulatory complexity and therapeutic targeting of the necroptosis network" (PMC13171333) — Inflammation crosstalk section states: "Environmental toxins such as cadmium and lead have been shown to promote necroptosis via NF-κB dependent mechanisms, whereas protective agents like selenium can attenuate cell death by inhibiting the MAPK/NF-κB axis (93, 94)." Source note: `raw/_document_ - Regulatory complexity and therapeutic targeting of the necroptosis network.md`. Update below grounds that single sentence in primary studies plus selenoprotein mechanism.

## Source claim in context

Niu review embeds the Cd/Pb-Se sentence in a feed-forward loop model: NF-kB → TNF/cytokines → necroptosis sensitization → DAMP release → further NF-kB. The claim is accurate for cited preclinical models but is a two-reference compression of a largely avian literature. No human Cd/Pb-necroptosis-Se trial data was found.

## Toxin arm — Cd/Pb drive RIPK1/RIPK3/MLKL

Convergent signature across chicken liver/spleen/kidney/heart studies:

- Oxidative trigger: ↓ SOD/GPx/CAT, ↑ MDA/NO
- MAPK activation: ↑ ERK/JNK/p38 mRNA plus ↑ p-ERK/p-JNK/p-p38 protein
- NF-kB arm: ↑ NF-kB, TNF-α, COX-2, iNOS
- Necrosome shift: ↑ RIPK1/RIPK3/MLKL, ↓ Caspase-8
- Co-markers tissue-specific: HSP27/40/60/70/90 in spleen; PPARα/Adiponectin/AdipoR1/R2/AMPKα1 suppression in heart; Bax/Caspase-3 co-induction in kidney

Vault anchor: [[Necroptosis]] is RIPK1 → RIPK3 → [[MLKL]] when Complex I [[NF-κB]] survival fails and [[Caspase-8]] is blocked; active Caspase-8 cleaves RIPK1/RIPK3 to suppress necroptosis.

## Primary evidence grounding the sentence

### Cd liver injury rescued by Se-yeast
Cai et al., Ecotox Environ Saf 2020.111329 — 120-day-old layers, 120d Cd diet.
Cd raised MLKL/RIP1/RIP3/ERK/JNK/p38 and lowered Caspase-8 at mRNA and protein; Se-yeast co-treatment restored SOD/GPx/CAT, lowered MDA, and normalized MAPK plus necrosome markers.

### Pb spleen necroptosis rescued by selenite
Zhang et al., Ecotox Environ Saf 2020.111049 — Pb acetate 150 mg/kg + Na2SeO3 2 mg/kg.
Pb increased spleen Pb burden, RIP1/RIP3/MLKL, ERK/JNK/p38/NF-kB/TNF-α, HSPs; decreased Caspase-8 and antioxidant enzymes. Se reduced Pb accumulation, restored antioxidant function, blocked MAPK/NF-kB and HSP activation.

### Pb kidney apoptosis plus necroptosis via p38/JNK/ERK
Ecotox Environ Saf 2022.113176 — Pb upregulated MAPK genes and pro-apoptotic plus necroptosis genes; Se attenuated p-P38/p-JNK/p-ERK, with p-JNK most responsive, and restored Bcl-2/Bax/Caspase-3 balance alongside RIPK1/RIPK3/MLKL/Caspase-8.

### Cd heart injury via adiponectin-necrotic route
Cai et al., RSC Adv 2017 C7RA07952D — Cd raised JNK/p-JNK/TNFα/RIPK1/MLKL and suppressed PPARα/Adiponectin/AdipoR1/R2/AMPKα1; Se reversed both arms.

### Se form comparison in Cd cardiotoxicity
Sci Total Environ 2021 — Nano-Se > Se-yeast > sodium selenite for reversing Cd accumulation, ion imbalance (K+/Na+, Mg2+/Ca2+), histopathology, and iNOS via NF-kB/IkB. Single head-to-head study.

### Parallel Pb pyroptosis model
Fish Shellfish Immunol 2023.109101 — Pb 500 μM in grass carp kidney cells: ROS → IRAK1/TAK1/IKK → NLRP3/GSDMD/IL-1β/IL-18; Se 20 nM lowered ROS and pyroptosis/inflammation markers. Supports MAPK/NF-kB-adjacent protection beyond necroptosis.

## Why selenium protects — selenoprotein logic

Selenium is not a direct scavenger; it is the catalytic Sec residue in ~25 selenoproteins.

- [[Glutathione Peroxidase]] family: Sec⁻ reduces H2O2/ROOH → Sec-SeOH → regenerated by 2 [[Glutathione]]; Se availability rate-limits activity. [[GPX4]] uniquely reduces membrane phospholipid/cholesterol/cardiolipin hydroperoxides, the ferroptosis brake.
- [[Thioredoxin reductase]] TXNRD1 cytosolic / TXNRD2 mitochondrial: NADPH → FAD → Sec → reduced [[Thioredoxin]] → reduced peroxiredoxins; reduced Trx inhibits [[ASK1]]; oxidized Trx releases it.
- Clinical anchor: [[Keshan disease]] — Se-poor region → cardiac GPx failure → necrosis/fibrosis; supplementation prevented it.

Protective layers in Cd/Pb context:

- Redox sink: restores GPx/TXNRD tone, lowers peroxide load driving ASK1-MAPK
- Signaling brake: less ROS → less IKK/IkB phosphorylation → less NF-kB; at supra-nutritional Se, selenometabolites oxidize critical Cys on NF-kB/AP-1/JNK/PKC/caspase-3 → reduced DNA binding and kinase activity; macrophage work shows Se adequacy lowers NF-kB/TNF-α/COX-2/PGE2 and raises pro-resolution 15d-PGJ2
- Toxicokinetic antagonism: reduced tissue Cd/Pb accumulation, restored ion homeostasis, plausibly via Se-metal complexation and preserved transport

Net effect: Caspase-8 preserved → RIPK1/RIPK3 cleavage → MLKL not phosphorylated → necrosome aborted.

## Dose form and safety window

- RDA 55 μg/d adult; GPx/TXNRD saturate at adequacy — benefit beyond adequacy is pharmacologic thiol-oxidizing and eicosanoid-shifting action, not more GPx
- Inorganic selenite/selenate: cheap, narrower window
- Organic Se-yeast/selenomethionine: better retained, used in liver rescue study
- Nano-Se: most potent in one chicken comparison, investigational
- Excess → selenosis; high-dose Se is pro-oxidant and can trigger apoptosis/DNA damage — the antioxidant paradox also noted for GPx1 overexpression causing insulin resistance via overquenching H2O2 signaling

## Limitations and open questions

- Near-exclusive chicken spleen/liver/kidney/heart plus one fish cell line; mammalian/human translation unverified
- HSP induction is correlative in Pb-spleen paper, not proven driver
- Adiponectin-PPARα-AMPK route is heart-specific Cd finding
- Doses such as 2 mg/kg diet Na2SeO3 in chickens do not map directly to human UL 400 μg/d
- Which selenoprotein mediates the MAPK/NF-kB effect in each tissue remains unresolved; SELENOP transport and ER selenoproteins underexplored in this literature
