# Task Output — Four-Compound Functional Food Strategy for Aging (Rzeski 2026)

**Date:** 17_Sep_2026 08:00 AM PDT
**Source doc:** `raw/_document_ - Natural Bioactive Compounds Targeting Key Hallmarks of Aging Functional Food Potential of Spermidine, Fisetin, Berberine, and Urolithin A.md` (Rzeski & Rzeska, *Nutrients* 2026, 18(15), 2511; narrative review, literature through 2025)

## Summary

Review frames [[Spermidine]], [[Fisetin]], [[Berberine]], [[Urolithin A]] as mechanistically distinct but complementary functional-food ingredients: general [[Autophagy]] + [[Senolysis|senolysis]] + metabolic regulation + [[Mitophagy]]. No prior review unites all four in one food-formulation framework. Direct co-administration evidence is essentially absent (one registered UA+fisetin sleep/aging trial NCT06990256); rationale is mechanistic complementarity, not proven synergy.

## Key Pathways (per compound)

| Compound (food origin) | Primary axis | Key molecular nodes |
|---|---|---|
| [[Spermidine]] (wheat germ, fermented foods, soy, mushrooms) | General autophagy / caloric-restriction mimetic | [[EP300]] inhibition → deacetylation-driven autophagy; [[EIF5A]] hypusination → [[TFEB]]/Atg3, mitochondrial FAO; [[SIRT1]]/[[AMPK]] support; [[mTOR]] suppression; fasting-mediated autophagy requires spermidine |
| [[Fisetin]] (strawberries, apples) | Senolytic + neuroprotective | PI3K/[[AKT]] + [[Bcl-2]]/[[Bcl-xL]] inhibition → senescent-cell apoptosis; [[TFEB]]/[[Nrf2]] → tau clearance; p25/inflammatory-pathway modulation; intermittent dosing works (hit-and-run senolysis) |
| [[Berberine]] (*Berberis* spp.) | Metabolic / gut-liver axis | [[AMPK]] activation (+[[SIRT1]]/[[PGC-1alpha]] in muscle); [[SIRT1]]→[[CPT1A]] in liver; PI3K/AKT + [[NF-kB]] suppression, [[Nrf2]] activation; high intraluminal exposure → microbiota reshape (↑ *Akkermansia*, *Bifidobacterium*/*Lactobacillus*, SCFA; FXR activation); <1% oral bioavailability |
| [[Urolithin A]] (postbiotic from ellagitannins: pomegranate, berries, walnuts; metabotype-dependent) | Selective mitophagy | [[PINK1]]/[[Parkin]] axis (phospho-Parkin Ser65, UBE2N/R2, Parkin-ubiquitin-proteasome signature at 500 mg); OXPHOS up + mtDNA/DNA ratio at 1000 mg; SIRT3/FOXO3 support; ↓ cGAS-[[STING]] / [[NLRP3]] inflammaging signals |

Convergence: all four touch [[AMPK]]–[[mTOR]]–[[SIRT1]] and damp [[Inflammation]] ([[NLRP3]], CRP, SASP); spermidine = broad autophagy, UA = targeted mitochondrial QC, fisetin = senescent-cell removal, berberine = systemic metabolic/microbiome reset.

## Organ Systems

- **Cardiovascular:** spermidine (cardioprotection, ↓ all-cause mortality — Kiechl 2018 prospective cohort; POLYCAD trial ongoing); berberine (lipids, glycemia; BUDCA/HTD1801 ↓ liver fat, HbA1c); UA (preclinical cardioprotection, human CV biomarkers).
- **Brain / cognition:** spermidine (hypusination preserves mitochondrial + cognitive function; SCD trials mixed); fisetin (AD-mouse cognition, tau clearance, dementia risk factors); berberine (AMPK/SIRT1/PGC-1α rescues cognition in aging rats); UA (diabetes-cognitive-impairment models).
- **Muscle / physical function:** fisetin (intermittent ↓ muscle senescence, improves function — Murray 2025); berberine (muscle AMPK/SIRT1/PGC-1α); UA (strongest human data: +10–12% leg strength, endurance/VO2, 6-min walk at 500–1000 mg × 4 mo; mitophagy signature in biopsies).
- **Liver / metabolic:** spermidine (EIF5A → FAO, anti-NASH); berberine (strongest clinical set: T2D meta-analyses, PREMOTE microbiome study, phase 2 BUDCA in NASH+T2D ↓ liver fat/HbA1c/enzymes/weight).
- **Gut / immune:** berberine (microbiota modulator despite low absorption); UA (metabotype A/B/0 gating — only producers benefit from precursors; direct UA bypasses this); UA 2025 RCT ↓ age-related immune decline; spermidine (antitumor immunity via MTP activation).
- **Skin / joint / sepsis:** fisetin STOP-Sepsis trial; UA+fisetin sleep/aging trial (NCT06990256).

## Combined Food Formulations (review rationale + gaps)

- **Proposed logic:** daily low-dose autophagy/mitophagy/metabolic base (spermidine-rich wheat-germ/fermented matrix + direct UA + berberine salt) + pulsed fisetin senolytic (intermittent, not daily) — mirrors pharmacology (chronic maintenance vs hit-and-run clearance).
- **Concrete anchors from evidence:** spermidine food doses linked to ↓ mortality; UA 500–1000 mg/d (Mitopure-type) with biopsy proof; berberine 500 mg 2–3×/d with meals (GI tolerability) or BUDCA/HTD1801 / dihydroberberine for bioavailability; fisetin needs hybrid-hydrogel/nano formulation (poor bioavailability) + intermittent schedule.
- **What is missing:** no published co-administration PK/PD, no synergy or interaction matrix, no combined clinical endpoint; needed: factorial trials, SASP/mitophagy/autophagy biomarker panels, metabotype stratification for UA, COMT-genotype awareness for fisetin (substrate-inhibitor → [[Geraldol]]).
- **Safety/regulatory flags:** spermidine trihydrochloride tox package clean; berberine under EFSA safety re-evaluation, EU novel-food BUDCA procedure terminated 2025, GI/diarrhea + CYP interactions; UA US GRAS (GRN 791) but EU novel-food still ongoing; fisetin formulation-dependent PK.

## Bottom Line

Four non-redundant levers, one plausible stack: spermidine (bulk autophagy) + UA (mitochondrial QC) + berberine (metabolic/microbiome) + pulsed fisetin (senescent-cell clearance). Combination is rational but untested — next step is co-administration studies, not more single-compound reviews.
