## Ingestion methods (recorded in the vault)

Oral is the route recorded for every compound reviewed. The only non-oral route recorded is [[Urolithin A]] topical: 1% cream, three registered trials (NCT05079607, NCT05300984, NCT05300542) — periorbital wrinkle depth −3.4% vs +4.0% placebo (p = 0.0437), UVB erythema reduction 13% (p = 0.02), 0.5% formulation null; taken with a fat-containing meal to enhance absorption. No component of the reviewed set is documented for intravenous delivery.

Recorded ingestion details:

- [[Spermidine]] — wheat germ ~337 mg/kg dry weight, soybeans ~207 mg/kg, wheat-germ extract 0.9–3.3 mg/day (SmartAge); average diet 8–12 mg/day, declining with age.
- [[Fisetin]] — 100–1000 mg/day; intermittent senolytic paradigm (1 wk on / 2 off / 1 on); galactomannan complex raises bioavailability >25-fold.
- [[Berberine]] — 900–1500 mg/day divided; systemic effects require enhanced forms (dihydroberberine, phospholipid complex).
- [[Urolithin A]] — oral: 500–1000 mg/day, Tmax ~4–6 h, t½ ~20 h; direct supplementation bypasses metabotype B/0 non-conversion; ellagitannin foods deliver only to metabotype A (~25–55%).
- [[Resveratrol]] — Baur et al. (2006) mice: 22.4 mg/kg/day; human data not detected in CSF (Nesci & Algieri 2026).
- [[Apigenin]] — parsley ≈45,035 µg/g, chamomile 3–5 mg/g, celery seeds ≈787 µg/g; rapid glucuronidation by UGT1A1; nano-formulations under investigation.
- [[Curcumin]] — oral bioavailability < 1%; Meriva (phospholipid complex), theracurmin (nanoparticle), liposomal curcumin; piperine co-administration (glucuronidation inhibition).
- [[Cyanidin-3-glucoside]] — elderberry 350–450 mg/100 g; ~300 mg therapeutic dose from 70–85 g of fruit.
- [[Carnosine]] — co-administered with [[Spermidine]] and aminoguanidine in the GOPS protocol.

No specific ingestion-method details are recorded for [[EGCG]], [[Luteolin]], [[Rutin]], [[Oleacein]], [[Hesperidin]], [[Methylfolate]], [[Folinic acid]], [[Catechols]], [[Green tea]], [[Kaempferol]], [[Chrysin]], [[Catechin]], [[Proanthocyanidins]], [[Sulforaphane]], [[Piperine]], [[Butyrate]], [[Genistein]].

## Observations

- Evidence tiers per cluster: Berberine has the strongest human clinical base among the four stack compounds (46-RCT meta-analysis, metabolic); Urolithin A has the most registered trials (ATLAS RCT, Phase I/II to 2000 mg single, NCT06990256 combo); Fisetin has preclinical + early human (STOP-Sepsis, NCT05758246) and the Rzeski note records "no phase III; not established in humans"; Resveratrol has human data limited to Baur et al. (2006) mouse/metabolic studies plus yeast/nematode lifespan data.
- Cross-link consistency: the COMT cluster is densely linked via inter-note link sections and the comt/README hub; the sirtuin/longevity cluster is denser via the _link/ resolver notes.
- Note depth is uneven: Curcumin's Limitations section records PAINS concern and no double-blind RCT demonstrating disease-modification in age-related conditions; Carnosine is a stub without Documents/Connections depth; Apigenin's note has the most structure (mechanism, ~18-cancer review, PRDX6).
- Design honesty in the stack narrative: the four compounds target non-overlapping hallmarks, the combination is "mechanistically grounded but prospectively unvalidated", and co-administration PK (Berberine–Fisetin, both CYP3A4-linked) is flagged as an open question.
