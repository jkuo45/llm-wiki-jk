# ADHD

**Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder** is a common neurodevelopmental
disorder of childhood onset, diagnosed on persistent (≥6 months) and
developmental-inappropriate inattention, hyperactivity, or impulsivity across
settings. It is not a disorder of attention *capacity* but of
**self-regulation of attention** — the ability to sustain and direct focus
appropriately to context — which is why the deficits show up most in tasks
requiring inhibition rather than in simple alertness tests.

> [!info] Why the vault treats it as a catecholamine disorder
> The monoamine pharmacology of ADHD is the most robust pharmacological result
> in the field: stimulants and non-stimulants that raise synaptic [[Dopamine]]
> and [[Norepinephrine]] in [[Prefrontal Cortex]] are effective, whereas agents
> acting elsewhere are not. That places ADHD squarely in the [[COMT]] topic —
> not because COMT is the disease gene, but because synaptic catecholamine tone
> in PFC is where COMT is one of the two rate-limiting determinants.

## Pathophysiology

**Dopamine.** The core circuit model is inverted cortico-striatal signalling: a
failure to upregulate task-relevant cortical activity with a corresponding
downregulation of task-irrelevant activity, mediated by catecholamines.
PhPET imaging has shown reduced striatal dopamine synthesis capacity in
medicated-naive adults with persistent symptoms, and increased dopamine
transporter density in some patients — both patterns interpreted as reflecting
elevated tonic dopamine, which would blunt phasic responses to reward and
salience.

**Norepinephrine.** Noradrenergic signalling in PFC underpins the regulation of
arousal and of signal-to-noise gain. This is why [[Norepinephrine transporter]]
blockade is therapeutic: inhibiting NET raises extracellular noradrenaline in
PFC and improves the inhibitory control deficits.

> [!important] COMT is a modifier, not a cause
> The PFC depends on a fine-tuned balance between dopamine release and clearance
> by [[COMT]] — too little release impairs cognition, too much impairs it
  again. That inverted-U shape is why the [[Val158Met]] polymorphism, which
> gives roughly 40% lower enzymatic activity for the Met allele, was a leading
  candidate: Met carriers have higher synaptic dopamine at the same release,
  and Met is over-represented among ADHD cases with the Val158Met genotype
  moderating task-dependent executive behaviour.
>
> The finding has not held up as an effect large enough to be clinically useful.
> Meta-analyses of Val158Met in ADHD are inconsistent, and pharmacogenetic
> prediction of methylphenidate response from COMT genotype has not replicated
> reliably. COMT genotype should not be used to select drug therapy.

**Genetics.** ADHD is highly heritable, with heritability estimates of roughly
0.7–0.8 in twin studies, but is highly polygenic. Dopamine transporter
([[Dopamine Transporter]]), serotonin transporter, and COMT loci have all been
associated at small effect sizes; none is sufficient. Environmental contributions
include prematurity, low birth weight, and lead exposure.

## Treatment

| Modality | Notes |
|---|---|
| Methylphenidate and amphetamines | First-line; raise catecholamines in PFC and striatum; effective acutely and long-term, with cardiovascular monitoring |
| Atomoxetine | Selective NET inhibitor; non-controlled; useful when stimulants are contraindicated |
| Bupropion | Noradrenergic/dopaminergic; off-label |
| Guanfacine, clonidine | α2 agonists; useful as adjuncts |
| [[Modafinil]] | Studied but not approved for ADHD; [[COMT]] genotype is a candidate predictor of response |
| Psychosocial | Behavioural parent training, classroom accommodations, organizational skills — effective and under-used relative to medication |

> [!warning] Treatment of the core disorder reduces comorbidity, it does not cure
> In randomized trials, stimulant treatment reduces risk of substance-use
> disorder and criminality. Untreated childhood ADHD carries elevated lifetime
  risk of substance misuse, occupational failure, and cardiovascular morbidity,
  largely as a consequence of comorbid conduct disorder rather than inattention
  itself.

## Beyond ADHD

The same PFC dopaminergic logic appears in [[Schizophrenia]] (dopamine excess,
hypofrontality) and in age-related cognitive decline (see [[Aging]]), but the
pharmacology is not interchangeable: schizophrenic patients often respond poorly
to stimulants, who relapse on them, or who experience their symptoms worsened.

## Connections
- [[COMT]] — the PFC relationship between dopamine release and COMT-mediated
  clearance is inverted-U, and the [[Val158Met]] polymorphism shifts the position
  of that curve. This is the mechanistic reason the disorder is discussed in a
  COMT context at all — and the reason genotype-based dosing claims failed.
- [[Val158Met]] — the Met allele lowers enzymatic activity ~40% and raises
  synaptic dopamine at equivalent release. Over-representation of Met in ADHD
  cohorts is real but small and not clinically predictive.
- [[Prefrontal Cortex]] — the anatomical locus of the disorder's core deficits:
  inhibitory control, working memory, and cognitive flexibility. PFC dysfunction
  is also the shared mechanism with [[Schizophrenia]] and age-related decline.
- [[Norepinephrine transporter]] — NET blockade raises PFC noradrenaline and is
  the mechanism of atomoxetine, and also part of how NET and COMT jointly set
  local catecholamine concentrations.
- [[Dopamine Transporter]] — the second catecholamine clearance route. Increased
  striatal DAT density is among the few replicated imaging findings in ADHD and
  is the direct target of methylphenidate.
- Methylphenidate — the archetypal psychostimulant in this context; blocks
  DAT and NET to raise synaptic catecholamines. Effects are region-specific: the
  orbitofrontal cortex benefits while the striatum's signal is blunted.
- [[Modafinil]] — studied in ADHD but not approved; the COMT-genotype predictor
  hypothesis is worth noting precisely because it did not survive testing.
- [[Stimulus-response learning]] — the shift from model-based to habitual
  control seen in ADHD is a behavioural readout of the same dopaminergic
  dysregulation.
- [[Schizophrenia]] — shares the PFC-dopamine axis with inverted pathology: ADHD
  as under-activated cortical control, schizophrenia as over-activated mesolimbic
  signalling with hypofrontality.
- [[Working Memory]] — one of the most consistently impaired cognitive domains
  in ADHD and a target of both pharmacological and cognitive-training approaches.
