# TNFR1 complex I

**TNFR1 complex I** is the short-lived membrane complex assembled within seconds of TNFα–[[TNFR1]]
engagement: [[TNFR1]]–[[TRADD]]–[[RIPK1]]–[[TRAF2]]/5–[[cIAPs]]. It is strictly pro-survival —
ubiquitinated [[RIPK1]] scaffolds IKK/NF-κB and MAPK activation and transcribes *CFLAR*
([[c-FLIP]]) — and it is the obligate precursor whose dissolution (via [[CYLD]]) births the
death-competent [[TNFR1 complex II]].

## Structure & Components

- **Receptor–adaptor core:** trimerized [[TNFR1]] death domains recruit [[TRADD]], which brings
  [[RIPK1]] via DD–DD interaction.
- **Ubiquitin writers:** [[TRAF2]]/5 recruit [[cIAPs]] ([[BIRC2]]/BIRC3), which decorate [[RIPK1]]
  with K63 and (via LUBAC) linear M1 chains — the physical scaffold for NEMO/IKK and TAK1
  recruitment.
- **Lifespan:** minutes; persistence requires continuous ubiquitin writing to outpace [[CYLD]]
  erasure.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **Survival output.** Ubiquitin-scaffolded [[NEMO]]/[[IKK complex]] activation → NF-κB-dependent
  transcription of *CFLAR*, *BCL2L1*, *XIAP* and cytokines — pre-loading the
  [[Caspase-8-c-FLIP Rheostat]] brake before complex II ever forms.
- **MAPK arm.** TAK1–MKK–JNK/p38 signaling tunes proliferation and stress responses in parallel.
- **Handoff.** [[CYLD]] deubiquitination of [[RIPK1]] dissolves the scaffold; TRADD–[[RIPK1]]
  internalize as [[TNFR1 complex II]]. Block deubiquitination and TNF is purely trophic; block
  ubiquitination ([[Smac mimetics]]) and complex I collapses straight into [[Ripoptosome]]-like
  death signaling.

## Physiological Function

Complex I lets a single cytokine be both growth factor and death ligand: gene activation first,
death checkpoint second. Barrier epithelia and innate immune cells depend on robust complex-I
output to survive their own TNF during infection.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- Anti-TNF biologics silence complex I wholesale (infection risk); selective IKK/NEMO or
  [[RIPK1]]-kinase inhibitors aim to preserve its survival output while blocking death arms.
- *CFLAR* induction failure at complex I (transcriptional blockade, HDAC inhibition) converts
  otherwise survivable TNF exposure into [[TNFR1 complex II]]-mediated [[Apoptosis]] — a
  chemosensitization principle.
