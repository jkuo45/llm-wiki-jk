# Liproxstatin-1

**Liproxstatin-1** (Lip-1) is a spiroquinoxalinamine small molecule that inhibits [[Ferroptosis]] by suppressing [[Lipid Peroxidation]]. It is structurally distinct from [[Ferrostatin-1]] but mechanistically related, acting as a lipophilic radical-trapping antioxidant.

## Mechanism

Lip-1 traps lipid peroxyl radicals (LOO•) in cellular membranes, terminating the autocatalytic lipid peroxidation chain reaction. It is highly potent (low nanomolar IC₅₀ in most cell types) and has demonstrated in vivo efficacy in mouse models of ferroptosis-driven pathologies.

## Research Applications

- Validating ferroptosis in cell culture alongside Fer-1 (rescue by either compound supports ferroptosis involvement)
- In vivo studies requiring metabolic stability and pharmacokinetic properties superior to Fer-1
- [[Ischemia-reperfusion Injury]] models (kidney, liver, heart) where Lip-1 has shown organ-protective effects
