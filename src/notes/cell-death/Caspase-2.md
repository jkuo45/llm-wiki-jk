# Caspase-2
[[Caspase-2]] is an initiator caspase that is regulated by [[Phosphorylation|phosphorylation]] at Ser308 by [[CDK1]]/[[CYCLIN B1]] and at Ser164 by [[CaMKII]] ([[PP1]]), and at Ser157 by [[CK2]].

## Metabolic regulation by SIRT1–14-3-3ζ

Caspase-2 is also suppressed through a **metabolic, sirtuin-dependent** route (Andersen et al., *Mol Cell* 2011 — [[_document_ - A Biotin Switch-Based Proteomics Approach Identifies 14-3-3zeta as a Target of Sirt1 in the Metabolic Regulation of Caspase-2]]):

- **14-3-3ζ** (deacetylated by [[SIRT1]] at Lys49) binds caspase-2 in a phosphorylation-dependent manner, protecting **Ser135** (phosphorylated by [[CaMKII]]) from dephosphorylation.
- **High pentose-phosphate-pathway flux** (G6P/NADPH) → SIRT1 activity ↑ → 14-3-3ζ deacetylated → caspase-2 suppressed.
- **Falling PPP flux** → 14-3-3ζ acetylated → dissociates from caspase-2 → caspase-2 activation and cell death.
- In breast tumor cell lines (primarily **cytoplasmic** SIRT1), SIRT1 inhibition rapidly dissociates caspase-2 from 14-3-3ζ and markedly enhances **paclitaxel** sensitivity.
- This places caspase-2 as the metabolic arm of the [[Sirtuin-Caspase Crosstalk]].
