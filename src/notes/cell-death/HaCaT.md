# HaCaT

**HaCaT** is a spontaneously immortalized human keratinocyte cell line derived from adult skin. The acronym expands to human, adult, low-calcium, high-temperature — the culture conditions it is maintained in. It is the standard human keratinocyte model in cell biology and cancer research, and in the cell-death context it is the transformed-but-non‑death‑prone control line: the defining study shows TLR3-driven [[Apoptosis]]/[[Necroptosis]] sensitivity and spontaneous [[Ripoptosome]] assembly in HaCaT, absent in primary keratinocytes.

## Culture & Context

- Standard epithelial cell line from adult skin keratinocytes; used as the HaCaT model for TLR3/poly(I:C) death sensitivity and ripoptosome formation across the cell-death documents.
- Cell-type specificity of death assays (Feoktistova et al. 2011, doc line 61): RIPK1-dependent TLR3 death in HaCaT, but not in fibroblasts or endothelial cells; TRIF–[[RIPK3]] can bypass RIPK1 dependence.
- The FLIP_S-licensed necroptosis route observed in HaCaT is RIP1-kinase dependent (Nec-1) and [[RIPK3]]-dependent (doc Results 5C–5D).
