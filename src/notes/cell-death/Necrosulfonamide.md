# Necrosulfonamide

**Necrosulfonamide** (NSA) is a small-molecule inhibitor of [[MLKL]] that blocks its membrane translocation and pore formation, arresting [[Necroptosis]] at the terminal executor step. Critical caveat: NSA also blocks [[Gasdermin D]] pore formation, so NSA rescue alone cannot separate necroptosis from [[Pyroptosis]] — pair it with [[Necrostatin-1]]/GSK872 (necroptosis-specific) versus caspase-1/NLRP3 inhibitors (pyroptosis-specific) in the death-dissection panel.

In the comparison-table context, necrosulfonamide completes the necroptosis Blocked-by triad (RIPK1 → RIPK3 → MLKL).
