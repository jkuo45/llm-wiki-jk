# TAM Kinases

**TAM kinases** (Tyro3, Axl, Mer/MERTK) are phosphatidylserine-sensing receptor tyrosine kinases. During phagocytosis, TAM kinases are activated by exposed phosphatidylserine and can directly phosphorylate [[MLKL]], regulating necroptosis and the inflammatory clearance of dead cells.

## Structure & Domains

Extracellular Gla/laminin-G domains binding Gas6/Protein S–phosphatidylserine complexes; intracellular tyrosine kinase domain.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **MLKL phosphorylation.** Activated TAM kinases directly phosphorylate [[MLKL]], modulating its oligomerization, membrane translocation, and lytic activity during efferocytosis.

## Physiological Function

Coordinate silent clearance of apoptotic/necroptotic corpses; TAM loss converts tolerogenic clearance into inflammation.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Inflammation-related diseases:** dysregulated TAM–MLKL signaling impairs corpse clearance and sustains inflammation.
