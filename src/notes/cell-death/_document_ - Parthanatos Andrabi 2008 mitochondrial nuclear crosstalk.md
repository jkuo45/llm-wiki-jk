# Mitochondrial and Nuclear Cross Talk in Cell Death: Parthanatos (Andrabi/Dawson 2008)

Naming review: [[Parthanatos]] (PAR + Thanatos) — [[PARP1]]-dependent death executed by toxic [[PAR]] accumulation and mitochondrial [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] nuclear translocation; caspase-independent, distinct from [[Apoptosis]], [[Necrosis]], and [[Autophagy]].

> [!note]
> Core model — overactivated [[PARP1]] makes unregulated [[PAR]]; [[PAR]] translocates nucleus → cytosol, colocalizes with [[Mitochondria]], and triggers [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] release; nuclear [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] drives large-scale DNA fragmentation.

## [[PARP1]] and cell death

- [[PARP1]] (founding PARP; >90% cellular PARP activity) uses [[NAD+]] to PARylate acceptors (hetero-modification) or itself (auto-modification); mild stress = genome guardian/repair, massive damage (stroke, TBI, [[Ischemia-reperfusion Injury|ischemia-reperfusion]], excitotoxicity) = death.
- Pharmacologic/genetic [[PARP1]] blockade protects across stroke, trauma, cardiac/splenic/skeletal/retinal [[Ischemia-reperfusion Injury|I/R]], arthritis, liver injury, [[Alzheimer's Disease|Alzheimer's]], MPTP [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's]], spinal cord injury, diabetes.
- Biochemical signature: rapid [[PARP1]] activation, early [[PAR]] accumulation, mitochondrial depolarization, early [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] translocation, [[NAD+]]/[[ATP]] loss, late caspase activation (epiphenomenal; inhibitors ineffective). Morphology: shrunken condensed nuclei, membrane disintegration, PI positivity within hours.

## [[PAR]] is the death signal

- Energy-depletion ("suicide") hypothesis is insufficient: [[NAD+]] repletion confounds (nicotinamide feedback inhibits [[PARP1]]; pleiotropic targets), and [[PARP1]] activation kills without obligatory energy collapse.
- Cytosolic [[PAR]] (minutes after [[NMDA receptor|NMDA]]; mitochondrial colocalization) is the cascade trigger; anti-[[PAR]] antibodies or [[PARG]] degradation protect, exogenous [[PAR]] kills dose-/complexity-dependently (long branched > short simple).
- Six+ PAR-synthesizing PARPs may make structurally distinct polymers with different life/death roles; cytosolic PARP contributions remain open, but nuclear [[PARP1]] deletion fully prevents parthanatos.

## [[PARG]] and parthanatos

- Single [[PARG]] gene (human 102/99 kDa cytosolic splice forms abundant; 111 kDa nuclear rare) with endo-/exo-glycosidase activity; [[ARH3]] (39 kDa) has [[PARG]]-like activity but does not compensate KO lethality.
- [[PARG]] KO: embryonic day 3.5 lethality (mouse), larval lethality + adult neurodegeneration at 29°C (Drosophila) from [[PAR]] accumulation; trophoblasts survive only with PARP inhibitor, die on withdrawal.
- Cytosolic [[PARG]] overexpression protects neurons/[[HeLa Cells|HeLa]] against [[PAR]]/[[NMDA receptor|NMDA]]/MNNG; predigested [[PAR]] is nontoxic; [[PARG]] transgenics resist excitotoxicity and stroke.

## Mechanisms: [[Mitochondria]] and [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] release

- [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] (mitochondrial flavoprotein, redox/Complex-I role) translocates to nucleus → large-scale fragmentation; [[PARP1]]-KO cells fail to release [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]]; [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] knockdown/neutralization protects.
- Harlequin mice (~80% [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] reduction): resistant to parthanatos ([[NMDA receptor|NMDA]], purified [[PAR]]), smaller excitotoxic/stroke lesions; re-expression restores susceptibility.
- Open mechanistic candidates: mitochondrial permeability transition pore (ANT/[[VDAC]]/[[Cyclophilin D]]; cyclosporin A data confounded), [[BAX]] translocation (KO resistant but possibly delayed; [[Bcl-2]] overexpression only delays), calpain truncation of [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] (knockdown/calpastatin protect but delay-vs-rescue unresolved). [[PAR]] binds many proteins covalently/non-covalently; the direct [[PAR]]→[[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]]-release adaptors were still unidentified.

## Summary

Nuclear/mitochondrial crosstalk with [[PAR]] as initiator and [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] as executioner; manipulating [[PARG]] and mapping [[PAR]]-binding proteins are the proposed therapeutic handles.
