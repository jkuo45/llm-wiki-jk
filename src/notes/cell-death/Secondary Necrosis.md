# Secondary Necrosis

**Secondary Necrosis** is the loss of plasma-membrane integrity in apoptotic cells (or their [[Apoptotic Bodies]]) that were not cleared by efferocytosis — the standard outcome in cell culture lacking phagocytes. The apoptotic program completes (caspases, DNA laddering) and *then* the membrane fails, spilling DAMPs ([[HMGB1]], ATP, nucleic acids) and recruiting the same TLR/RAGE/[[NLRP3]] sterile-inflammation machinery as primary [[Necrosis]].

It is the key in-vitro/in-vivo divergence: cultured "apoptosis" readouts past the clearance window are frequently secondary necrosis, which is why efferocytosis-competent models and early Annexin V+/PI− gating matter. In the comparison-table context, it is the necrosis Crosstalk entry — the bridge by which failed apoptosis becomes inflammatory.
