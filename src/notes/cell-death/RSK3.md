# RSK3

**RSK3** (p90 ribosomal protein S6 kinase 3; gene *RPS6KA2*) directly acts as an upstream regulator of [[RIPK3]] phosphorylation, promoting activation of the necroptosis pathway.

## Structure & Domains

Bipartite kinase architecture: N-terminal effector kinase domain and C-terminal ERK-activated regulatory kinase domain, as in the [[RSK]] family.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **RIPK3 phosphorylation.** RSK3 phosphorylates [[RIPK3]], licensing its autophosphorylation and recruitment of [[MLKL]] into the necrosome.

## Physiological Function

Couples MAPK/ERK stress signaling to necroptotic clearance of damaged cells.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Ischemic retinal injury:** RSK3-driven RIPK3 activation promotes necroptosis of retinal cells.
