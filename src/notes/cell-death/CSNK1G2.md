# CSNK1G2

**CSNK1G2** (casein kinase 1G2) directly binds to [[RIPK3]] to inhibit its kinase activity and downstream necroptosis signaling.

## Structure & Domains

Conserved casein kinase 1 catalytic domain with membrane-targeting lipidation motifs.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **RIPK3 inhibition.** CSNK1G2 binding suppresses [[RIPK3]] kinase activity, blocking [[MLKL]] recruitment and necrosome firing.

## Physiological Function

Protects aging-sensitive tissues (notably male reproductive tract) from spurious necroptosis.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Male reproductive aging:** loss of CSNK1G2 restraint permits RIPK3-driven necroptosis and tissue decline.
