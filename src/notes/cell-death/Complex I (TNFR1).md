# Complex I (TNFR1)

**Complex I (TNFR1)** is the membrane-proximal TNF-receptor signaling complex assembled within seconds of [[TNFα]] binding: TRADD, [[RIPK1]], TRAF2, LUBAC, cIAP1/cIAP2, and NEMO/IKK, with polyubiquitinated RIPK1 recruiting TAK1 and the IKK complex to degrade IκB and activate [[NF-κB]] survival/inflammatory transcription. It is the "first choice" (live) of the three-way TNFR1 decision. Distinct from mitochondrial respiratory [[Complex I]] (NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase) — same numeral, unrelated machine.

CYLD-mediated RIPK1 deubiquitination dissolves Complex I and licenses cytosolic [[Complex II (TNFR1)]] assembly (apoptosis) or, when caspase-8 is blocked, necrosome formation ([[Necroptosis]]). cIAP/LUBAC activity therefore gates the entire survival-vs-death branch point.
