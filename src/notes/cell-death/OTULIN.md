# OTULIN

**OTULIN** is a linear (M1) linkage-specific deubiquitinase that regulates [[RIPK1]] ubiquitination during necroptosis. During necroptosis, phosphorylation of OTULIN at Tyr-56 regulates [[RIPK1]] ubiquitination and drives cell death.

## Structure & Domains

OTU catalytic domain with exclusive M1-linkage specificity; N-terminal PUB-interacting motif coupling it to the LUBAC complex.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **RIPK1 ubiquitin editing.** OTULIN trims linear ubiquitin chains on [[RIPK1]] within TNFR1 [[TNFR1 complex I|complex I]], opposing LUBAC and gating the complex I → [[TNFR1 complex II|complex II]] / necrosome transition.
- **Tyr-56 switch.** Phosphorylation at Tyr-56 during necroptosis alters OTULIN activity toward [[RIPK1]], promoting death signaling.

## Physiological Function

Fine-tunes TNF-induced survival vs death decisions; restrains spontaneous inflammation from unchecked linear ubiquitination.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Inflammation:** OTULIN dysfunction causes hyperinflammatory syndromes; Tyr-56 phosphorylation drives necroptotic inflammation.
