# Caspase-10

**Caspase-10** is an initiator protease of the cysteine-aspartic acid protease (caspase) family, most closely related to [[Caspase-8]]. It plays a significant role in the [[Extrinsic Pathway]] of [[Apoptosis]] by transducing death-receptor engagement into executioner caspase activation. Germline and somatic alterations in *CASP10* have been linked to defective apoptosis and increased tumor susceptibility.

## Structure & Activation

Like [[Caspase-8]], caspase-10 is synthesized as a zymogen with tandem death-effector domains (DEDs) in its prodomain that mediate recruitment to death-inducing complexes via FADD (Fas-associated death domain protein). Upon ligation of death receptors — [[Fas]] (CD95), [[DR4]] (TRAIL-R1), and [[DR5]] (TRAIL-R2) — procaspase-10 is concentrated at the **Death-Inducing Signaling Complex** ([[DISC]]), where induced proximity drives self-cleavage and generation of the large (p17–p18) and small (p12) subunits that assemble into the active heterotetramer.

## Mechanism of Action

At the [[DISC]], active caspase-10 cleaves and activates executioner caspases, particularly [[Caspase-3]] and [[Caspase-7]], which then dismantle the cell through proteolysis of structural and repair proteins. Caspase-10 can also process the pro-apoptotic BH3-only protein Bid (tBid), linking the extrinsic pathway to mitochondrial amplification via [[Caspase-9]]. Although [[Caspase-8]] is the dominant initiator in most cells, caspase-10 provides redundancy and, in certain lineages (e.g., lymphoid cells), is the predominant DISC protease.

## Physiological & Pathological Function

Because death-receptor apoptosis is a key immune-surveillance mechanism that eliminates transformed and virus-infected cells, impaired caspase-10 function undermines tumor suppression. *CASP10* polymorphisms (e.g., the CASP-10 variant associated with reduced enzymatic activity) correlate with heightened risk of several cancers, including non-Hodgkin lymphoma and gastric carcinoma. Caspase-10 deficiency also predisposes to autoimmune lymphoproliferative syndromes, reflecting failed lymphocyte deletion. In choriocarcinoma and some other tumors, caspase-10 downregulation attenuates TRAIL-induced killing, fostering immune evasion.

## Clinical & Research Relevance

Therapeutic TRAIL receptor agonists and DR4/DR5 antibodies aim to engage the caspase-10/caspase-8 axis in cancer cells, but resistance via DISC defects limits efficacy. Restoring caspase-10 expression or circumventing the extrinsic block with [[BH3 mimetics]] that unleash intrinsic apoptosis is an active strategy. Biomarker studies correlate *CASP10* methylation or mutation with poor response to death-receptor-targeted agents, informing patient selection.
