# diABZI

**diABZI** (diamidobenzimidazole; commonly "diABZI compound 3") is a synthetic, non-cyclic-dinucleotide [[STING]] agonist. Two amidobenzimidazole units are linked into a symmetric dimeric ligand that exploits the dimeric structure of STING, binding its cyclic-dinucleotide-binding domain with high affinity. Unlike CDN agonists, it is systemically bioavailable; agonist binding triggers STING translocation from the [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]] to the Golgi, [[TBK1]] recruitment, [[IRF3]] phosphorylation and type-I interferon / pro-inflammatory transcription. It is widely used as a tool compound to activate STING independently of [[cGAS]] and cytosolic DNA.

## Use in the Ye 2026 study

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Aging Cell - 2026 - Ye - NAD  Metabolic Reprogramming Drives CD8  T Cells Senescence and Exacerbates Ulcerative Colitis|Ye et al., 2026, Aging Cell]]
> To validate that [[cGAS-STING Pathway]] activation drives NAD+-depletion-induced senescence, [[CD8 T cells|CD8+ T cells]] were treated with the STING agonist diABZI or the inhibitor [[C178]]. Immunoblotting confirmed that [[FK866]] activated the pathway (upregulated [[cGAS]] and [[IRF3]], increased phosphorylated STING). Direct STING hyperactivation by diABZI recapitulated the senescence phenotype, inducing [[p16]] and [[p21 CIP1|p21]] expression. Flow cytometry showed diABZI promoted IFN-γ and TNF-α production.

diABZI supplies the gain-of-function half of the pharmacological pair with [[C178]]: agonist sufficiency plus inhibitor rescue, together with [[STING]]-knockout resistance, establishes the cGAS-STING pathway as necessary and sufficient for the [[Senescent CD8+ T Cells|senescent CD8+ T-cell]] program in [[Ulcerative Colitis]].
