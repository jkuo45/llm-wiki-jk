# Infliximab

**Infliximab** (Remicade) is a chimeric human-murine IgG1 monoclonal antibody that binds soluble and transmembrane [[TNF-alpha]] with high affinity, blocking its interaction with TNFR1/TNFR2. It is a cornerstone biologic for moderate-to-severe [[Inflammatory Bowel Disease]] and other immune-mediated inflammatory diseases. As an IgG1 it can also engage Fc-mediated effector functions and complement-dependent cytotoxicity against TNF-expressing cells.

## Predictive biomarker in ulcerative colitis

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Aging Cell - 2026 - Ye - NAD  Metabolic Reprogramming Drives CD8  T Cells Senescence and Exacerbates Ulcerative Colitis|Ye et al., 2026, Aging Cell]]
> In the GSE16879 cohort, UC patients with high pretreatment scores for both [[NAD+ Metabolic Reprogramming|NAD+ metabolism]] and [[Senescent CD8+ T Cells|senescent CD8+ T-cell infiltration]] show a significantly lower response rate to infliximab; responders have lower baseline scores than non-responders, and post-treatment score reductions are more pronounced in responders. High-scoring patients show upregulated TNFA-signalling-via-[[NF-κB]] and interferon-alpha-response pathways.

The finding positions the NAD+-senescence axis as a candidate pretreatment biomarker for primary non-response to anti-TNF-α therapy, complementing the validation of the same signature for [[Vedolizumab]] in the independent GSE73661 cohort. The authors propose that high [[Senescent CD8+ T Cells|senescent CD8+ T-cell]] burden identifies refractory patients who might instead benefit from senescence-targeted intervention.
