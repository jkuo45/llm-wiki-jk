# SASP Detrimental and Beneficial Functions (Table)

> [!abstract] Overview
> The [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]] is a double-edged sword. The same secretome that enforces tumor suppression and drives tissue repair during acute injury also propagates [[Paracrine Senescence|senescence]], fuels [[Inflammation|chronic inflammation]], and promotes [[Cancer|tumorigenesis]] when sustained. These tables map specific SASP factors to their detrimental and beneficial functions.

## Detrimental Functions of SASP

| Function | Most Relevant SASP Factors | Mechanisms | Examples | Refs. |
|----------|----------------------------|------------|----------|-------|
| Propagation of senescence in normal tissue | [[IL-6]], [[TGFβ]], [[Chemokines\|CCLs]], [[Chemokines\|CXCLs]] | Senescent cells induced by ageing stresses or therapies secrete SASP factors that can induce senescence in the surrounding cells, which propagates the detrimental effects | SASP factors (in small extracellular vesicles) propagate senescence in ageing persons; soluble SASP factors secreted by senescent [[Adipose Tissue\|bone marrow adipocytes]] spread senescence in bones | 25,128 |
| Propagation of cell death, excessive matrix production and fibrosis in normal tissue | [[IL-1α]], [[IL-1β]], [[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], [[CCL2]], [[VCAM1]], [[MMP-2\|MMP2]], [[TGFβ]] | SASP factors, including [[Matrix Metalloproteinase\|MMPs]], can induce cell death, be pro-fibrotic and remodel the tissue matrix, thereby contributing to tissue dysfunction and [[Fibrosis\|fibrosis]] | SASP factors, such as IL-6, CCL2 and MMPs, secreted by senescent [[Fibroblasts\|fibroblasts]] in the lung of animals with [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis\|idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis]] contribute to the fibrotic process | 237 |
| Tumorigenesis | [[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], [[CCL5]], [[CXCL5]], [[CXCL12]], [[VEGF]], [[HGF]], [[E-cadherin]] | In the [[Tumor Microenvironment\|tumour microenvironment]], SASP factors promote different stages of tumorigenesis, including cell proliferation, migration, [[Metastasis\|metastasis]] and [[Angiogenesis\|angiogenesis]] | IL-6, IL-8 and HGF promote cancer cell proliferation; IL-6, VEGF and CXCL5 promote angiogenesis; IL-6, IL-8 and CXCL12 contribute to migration and metastasis | 5,238 |
| Cancer immune evasion | [[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], [[CCL2]] | Under specific conditions, certain SASP factors can recruit [[Myeloid-Derived Suppressor Cells\|myeloid suppressor cells]] to inhibit the killing of cancer cells by functional immune cells | IL-6 and IL-8 activate [[HLA-E]] to blunt the activities of [[Natural Killer Cells\|NK cells]] and [[CD8 T cells\|CD8+ T cells]]; IL-6 attracts CD11b+;Gr-1+ myeloid cells to suppress T cells; CCL2 attracts CCR2+ myeloid cells to suppress NK cells | 205,239,240 |
| Chronic inflammation | [[IL-1α]], [[IL-1β]], [[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], [[CXCL1]], [[CCL2]] | In therapy-treated or aged animals/patients, systemic senescent cells secrete low-grade SASP factors to maintain a chronic inflammatory status | High serum IL-6 levels contribute to disabilities in aged individuals | 241 |
| Ageing-related pathologies | [[IL-1α]], [[IL-1β]], [[IL-6]], [[IL-8]], [[CXCL1]], [[CCL2]], [[TNFα\|TNF]], [[MMP-13\|MMP13]] | Senescent cells gradually accumulate during the ageing process, and the SASP factors secreted in multiple organs contribute to different age-related pathologies | Age-associated dysfunctions, such as [[Cardiovascular Disease\|cardiovascular diseases]] or [[Osteoarthritis\|osteoarthritis]], are severely affected by senescent cells and their SASP | 153,178,242,243 |

> [!warning] Chronic SASP drives ageing pathology
> Persistent, low-grade secretion of [[IL-6]], [[IL-1α]], [[IL-1β]], [[IL-8]], [[CXCL1]], [[CCL2]], and [[TNFα|TNF]] sustains [[Inflammaging|systemic inflammaging]], linking senescent-cell burden to [[Cardiovascular Disease|cardiovascular disease]], [[Osteoarthritis]], and frailty.

## Beneficial Functions of SASP

| Function | Most Relevant SASP Factors | Mechanisms | Examples | Refs. |
|----------|----------------------------|------------|----------|-------|
| Propagation of senescence in pre-malignant lesions or tumours | [[IL-1α]], [[TGFβ]], [[VEGF]], [[CCL2]], [[CCL20]] | In pre-malignant settings, [[Oncogene-Induced Senescence\|OIS]] senescent cells secret SASP factors that induce senescence in surrounding tumour cells to suppress tumorigenesis | SASP factors secreted by senescent cells in serrated sessile adenoma spread senescence in the lesion | 155 |
| Induction of cell death in tumours | [[IL-6]], [[TNFα\|TNF]] | SASP factors secreted by the senescent (cancer) cells can induce [[Apoptosis\|apoptosis]] or other types of cell death in surrounding cancer cells | IL-6 can trigger apoptosis in T cell lymphoma and inhibits tumour growth; TNF can induce [[Reactive Oxygen Species\|ROS]]-mediated cell death in cancer cells | 200,201 |
| Immunosurveillance of cancer cells | [[CCL2]], [[CXCL1]], [[CXCL14]], [[IGFBP3]] | SASP factors secreted by senescent (cancer) cells into the tumour microenvironment attract immune cells such as T cells and NK cells for clearance of tumour cells | CCL2 can recruit [[Macrophages\|macrophages]] in liver cancer models; CXCL14 and IGFBP3 can recruit macrophages and lymphocytes to liver tumours | 71,75,173 |
| Tissue regeneration | [[PDGFAA]], [[CCN1]], [[IL-1α]], [[IL-6]], [[CCL2]] | SASP factors expressed by senescent cells in the early response to tissue damage promote repair by recruiting immune cells and stimulating the proliferation and differentiation of stromal cells | PDGFAA promotes optimal cutaneous [[Wound Healing\|wound healing]] | 160,165 |
| Embryogenesis | [[BMP\|BMPs]], [[Matrix Metalloproteinase\|MMPs]], [[IGFBP5]] | Cellular senescence is induced during development and SASP factors contribute to patterning and growth | SASP factors, such as TGFβ and IGFBP5, contribute to neural tube and forelimb development | 157,158 |

> [!tip] Acute SASP is pro-regenerative
> During development and acute injury, transient SASP factors — [[PDGFAA]], [[CCN1]], [[BMP|BMPs]], [[IGFBP5]] — promote [[Wound Healing|tissue repair]], embryonic patterning, and stromal-cell differentiation, underscoring the biphasic (acute-beneficial vs. chronic-detrimental) nature of senescence (see [[Paracrine Reprogramming]]).

> [!important] Therapeutic corollary
> Because the SASP is context-dependent, indiscriminate elimination of senescent cells can be harmful during acute repair and pre-malignant tumor suppression. This motivates carefully timed [[Senolytic Therapy|senolytic]] and SASP-modulating [[Senomorphic Therapy|senomorphic]] strategies.
