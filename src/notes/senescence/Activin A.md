# Activin A

**Activin A** is a homodimer of inhibin beta-A (INHBA) subunits and a member of the [[TGFβ]] superfamily. It signals through the type I receptor [[ALK4]] (activin receptor-like kinase 4) and type II receptor activin RIIA/RIIB, phosphorylating receptor-regulated SMADs [[SMAD2]] and [[SMAD3]], which complex with [[SMAD4]] to regulate transcription. Activin A is a component of the [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]] and a mediator of [[Paracrine Senescence]].

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

- **Receptor complex**: Activin A binds activin type II receptors (ACTRIIA/ACTRIIB), which recruit and phosphorylate the type I receptor [[ALK4]]. ALK4 then phosphorylates [[SMAD2]] and [[SMAD3]].
- **SMAD signaling**: Phosphorylated SMAD2/3 complex with [[SMAD4]] and translocate to the nucleus to regulate target gene transcription, including cell cycle inhibitors ([[CDKN2B|p15^INK4b]], [[p21 CIP1|p21^CIP1]]) and pro-apoptotic factors.
- **Non-SMAD pathways**: Activin A can also engage [[MAPK]], [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]], and p38 MAPK pathways depending on cellular context.
- **Antagonists**: Follistatin binds and neutralizes activin A extracellularly; inhibins compete for type II receptor binding.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

> [!info] Source: Acosta et al., 2013 (PMID 23636316)
> Activin A (a homodimer of inhibin A) was identified as a TGF-β family ligand acutely upregulated during [[Oncogene-Induced Senescence|OIS]]. It contributes to [[Paracrine Senescence]] alongside TGF-β1, BMP2, and GDF15. Combination of blocking antibodies targeting TGF-β1, activin A, and BMP2 partially rescued paracrine arrest, demonstrating that multiple TGF-β family branches cooperate to mediate paracrine senescence. Activin A signals through [[SMAD2]]/[[SMAD3]] phosphorylation, placing it upstream of [[CDKN2B|p15^INK4b]] and [[p21 CIP1|p21^CIP1]] induction in receiving cells.

- Activin A is among the SASP components whose expression is driven by [[IL-1α]] and [[NFKB|NF-κB]] during senescence.
- It autocrinally reinforces senescence-associated growth arrest and paracrinely induces proliferation arrest in neighbouring cells.
- The activin A–ALK4–SMAD2/3 axis overlaps with but is distinct from the TGF-β1–ALK5 axis; both converge on p15^INK4b/p21^CIP1 induction.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- Elevated activin A is associated with chronic inflammatory conditions, fibrosis, and cancer.
- In the tumour microenvironment, activin A can have context-dependent pro- or anti-tumorigenic effects.
- Activin A contributes to the pro-inflammatory milieu of age-related tissue dysfunction through its role in the SASP.
