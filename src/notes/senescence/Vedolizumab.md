# Vedolizumab

**Vedolizumab** (Entyvio) is a humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody that binds the α4β7 integrin heterodimer on circulating lymphocytes. By blocking the α4β7–MAdCAM-1 interaction it selectively inhibits lymphocyte trafficking into gastrointestinal mucosa, giving it a gut-selective mechanism distinct from systemic immunosuppression. It is approved for moderate-to-severe [[Ulcerative Colitis]] and [[Inflammatory Bowel Disease|Crohn's disease]].

## Predictive biomarker in ulcerative colitis

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Aging Cell - 2026 - Ye - NAD  Metabolic Reprogramming Drives CD8  T Cells Senescence and Exacerbates Ulcerative Colitis|Ye et al., 2026, Aging Cell]]
> To test the robustness and broad applicability of the NAD+-senescence biomarker, the authors validated it in the independent UC cohort GSE73661 treated with a mechanistically distinct biologic, the α4β7 integrin antagonist vedolizumab. Concordantly, high pretreatment [[NAD+ Metabolic Reprogramming|NAD+ metabolism]] and [[Senescent CD8+ T Cells|senescent CD8+ T-cell]] infiltration scores were associated with therapeutic non-response following vedolizumab administration.

Replication across two biologics with unrelated mechanisms — [[Infliximab|anti-TNF-α]] and α4β7 blockade — supports the interpretation that the NAD+-senescence signature reflects a disease-intrinsic refractory state rather than drug-specific pharmacokinetics. The scores also correlate with endoscopic disease activity indices (Mayo scores) in UC patients.
