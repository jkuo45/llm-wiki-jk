# Ulcerative Colitis

**Ulcerative colitis (UC)** is a predominant form of [[Inflammatory Bowel Disease|inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)]] characterized by unchecked inflammation of the colonic mucosa, with pathogenic [[T Cell|T cells]] contributing substantially to tissue injury (Ye et al., 2026). Classical therapeutic agents predominantly target T-cell hyperactivation or recruitment, yet high rates of primary non-response to biologics remain.

## A senescence-driven pathogenic axis

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Aging Cell - 2026 - Ye - NAD  Metabolic Reprogramming Drives CD8  T Cells Senescence and Exacerbates Ulcerative Colitis|Ye et al., 2026, Aging Cell]]
> Single-cell RNA-seq of colonic mucosa (GSE182270, validated in GSE214695) showed elevated senescence-module scores specifically in T cells from UC patients, whereas B cells and myeloid cells showed no such increase. In a chronic 3% [[Dextran Sulfate Sodium|DSS]]-induced colitis model, colonic T cells show increased SA-β-gal activity, γ-H2A.X/p21 positivity, CD27−KLRG1+ phenotype and shortened [[Telomere|telomeres]].

[[NAD+ Metabolic Reprogramming|NAD+ metabolic dysregulation]] within the inflamed mucosa depletes [[NAD+]] and drives CD8+ T-cell senescence via [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]] and [[cGAS-STING Pathway]] activation. Adoptive transfer of NAD+-depleted [[Senescent CD8+ T Cells|senescent CD8+ T cells]] accelerates colitis in vivo, while [[Senolytic Therapy|senolytic therapy]] ([[Dasatinib]] + [[Quercetin]]) attenuates disease by clearing these cells.

## Clinical relevance

In the GSE16879 and GSE73661 cohorts, high pretreatment NAD+-metabolism and senescent-CD8+-T-cell infiltration scores correlate with endoscopic/Mayo disease activity and predict primary non-response to [[Infliximab]] (anti-TNF-α) and [[Vedolizumab]] (anti-α4β7 integrin). This positions the senescent CD8+ T-cell signature as a candidate prognostic biomarker rather than a purely correlative severity metric.
