# DVE-1

**DVE-1** is a *C. elegans* homeobox-containing transcription factor that functions as a downstream effector of the [[Mitochondrial Unfolded Protein Response]] (UPRmt). When mitochondrial proteotoxic stress is sensed in the cytosol (via the transcription factor ATFS-1, which fails to import into stressed mitochondria and instead enters the nucleus), DVE-1 is recruited to target promoters together with the small acidic protein [[UBL-5]].

The DVE-1–UBL-5 complex binds chromatin at UPRmt-responsive genes, facilitating expression of mitochondrial chaperones (e.g., hsp-60), proteases, and components that restore proteostasis. Genetic studies show that *dve-1* or *ubl-5* loss blunts the UPRmt transcriptional program and suppresses the longevity and stress-resistance phenotypes normally conferred by mitochondrial respiratory-chain perturbations (such as *cco-1* or *isp-1* knockdown).

> [!info] Conserved logic
> Although DVE-1 is nematode-specific, its partnership with UBL-5 reflects a broadly conserved principle: stress-activated transcription factors recruit chromatin-modifying cofactors to reprogram nuclear gene expression in response to mitochondrial dysfunction.

DVE-1 thus sits at the effector end of the mitohormetic cascade linking calibrated mitochondrial stress to adaptive gene expression and [[Lifespan]] extension.
