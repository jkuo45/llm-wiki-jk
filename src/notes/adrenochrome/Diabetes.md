# Diabetes

**Diabetes mellitus** encompasses metabolic disorders — chiefly type 1 (autoimmune β-cell destruction) and type 2 (insulin resistance with progressive β-cell failure) — defined by chronic [[Hyperglycemia]]. Persistently elevated glucose drives non-enzymatic glycation of proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids, accelerating formation and tissue accumulation of [[Advanced Glycation End Products|AGEs]].

The pathophysiology is self-reinforcing: hyperglycemia promotes mitochondrial [[Superoxide anion]] overproduction (the "unifying mechanism" of diabetic complications), which in turn increases dicarbonyl flux ([[Methylglyoxal]], glyoxal) and AGE formation. AGEs cross-link matrix proteins (e.g., [[Collagen]], crystallins) and engage the receptor for AGE ([[RAGE]]), sustaining [[Inflammation]] and [[Oxidative Stress]]. These processes underlie microvascular (retinopathy, nephropathy, neuropathy) and macrovascular (accelerated atherosclerosis) complications.

> [!info] Longevity intersection
> Diabetes accelerates vascular [[Arterial Stiffening]], cognitive decline, and frailty, making AGE-breakers, glycation inhibitors, and [[GlyNAC]]/redox support recurring themes in longevity and geroscience.

Therapeutic pillars include glucose lowering, [[Insulin Resistance]] management (metformin, GLP-1 agonists), and reduction of AGE load via diet (limiting dietary AGEs) and pharmacological approaches.
