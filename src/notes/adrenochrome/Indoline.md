# Indoline

**Indoline** is a heterocyclic organic compound consisting of a six-membered benzene ring fused to a five-membered nitrogen-containing ring. It is the partially hydrogenated version of indole.

## Role in [[Aminochromes]]
The indoline structure forms the core of [[Aminochromes]], including [[Adrenochrome]] and [[Dopaminochrome]]. During the [[Oxidation]] of [[Catecholamines]], the catechol ring is converted to a [[Quinone]], and the side-chain nitrogen cyclizes to form the five-membered indoline ring.

## Structural Features in Adrenochrome
In [[Adrenochrome]], the indoline core is substituted with:
- A hydroxyl group at position 3.
- An N-methyl group on the nitrogen atom.
- Two carbonyl groups (at positions 5 and 6), making the fused benzene ring a quinone.

## Connections
