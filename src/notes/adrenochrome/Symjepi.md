# Symjepi

Symjepi is a brand-name pre-filled syringe delivering a fixed dose of [[Epinephrine]] for the emergency self-treatment of [[Anaphylaxis]] in adults and older children. It is a compact, single-use alternative to the pen-style auto-injector ([[EpiPen]]) and to the nasal spray ([[Neffy]]), emphasizing manual, needle-based intramuscular delivery into the anterolateral thigh.

Formulation stability is central to its design. Epinephrine is a catecholamine highly susceptible to [[Autoxidation]]: in aqueous solution, especially exposed to oxygen, light, and trace metals, it undergoes radical-coupled oxidation to adrenochromes and polymeric pigments, with loss of potency and accumulation of potentially adverse aminochromes such as [[Adrenochrome]]. Symjepi's container and formulation are engineered to preserve the reduced catechol, with commercial and extemporaneous preparations typically carrying a 1–2 year room-temperature (≤25 °C) shelf life. Preserving potency means minimizing the very oxidation chemistry that generates adrenochrome.

The redox relevance is twofold: clinically, a stable epinephrine product ensures efficacy during allergic emergencies; mechanistically, the autoxidation pathway degraded in storage is identical to the physiologically relevant catecholamine oxidation that occurs under [[Oxidative Stress]] in vivo. Symjepi therefore sits at the interface of formulation science and aminochrome biology, where keeping epinephrine reduced is synonymous with keeping adrenochrome formation low.

> [!tip] Stability imperative
> Epinephrine products must minimize light/oxygen/metal exposure to prevent aminochrome formation and potency loss.
