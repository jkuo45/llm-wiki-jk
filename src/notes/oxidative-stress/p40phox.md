# p40phox

**p40phox** (NCF4, neutrophil cytosolic factor 4) is the fourth and smallest of the three cytosolic subunits of the phagocyte [[NADPH Oxidase]]. Its contribution is subtler than that of [[p47phox]] or [[p67phox]]: it supports assembly and appears to provide autoregulation of the assembled complex rather than directly driving it.

## Structure and Mechanism

p40phox is defined by two modular interaction domains:

- **PX domain** (Phox homology) — binds phosphatidylinositol lipids and the C-terminal SH3 domain of p47phox, which is what couples p40phox to the organizer and thereby to the membrane complex.
- **SH3 domain** — binds a proline-rich sequence in the C-terminal region of p67phox, forming the reciprocal interaction that completes the p40–p67–p47 network.

p40phox also carries a C-terminal sequence that interacts with the autoinhibitory domain of NOX2, so it contributes both to assembly and to setting the activation threshold of the assembled oxidase.

> [!info] Cooperative, non-redundant roles
> Each cytosolic subunit has a distinct, non-substitutable role: p47phox organizes and is phosphorylated during priming, p67phox activates by contacting both p47phox and gp91phox, and p40phox stabilizes the complex and contributes autoregulatory feedback. Assays that measure oxidase activation use defined combinations of recombinant components, and omitting any one of them changes the measured rate.

## Clinical Significance

*NCF4* mutations cause **autosomal recessive chronic granulomatous disease**. Compared with the more common X-linked *CYBB* form and the *CYBA*, *NCF1*, and *NCF2* autosomal forms, **NCF4/p40phox deficiency is a mild and atypical CGD**, generally without invasive bacterial or fungal infection. Diagnosis still uses the dihydrorhodamine assay, though serum-opsonized *E. coli* is recommended as the stimulus for detecting the more subtle residual-burst defects of this genotype (Yu et al., *Clin Rev Allergy Immunol* 2021; PMID 32524254).

> [!warning] Mouse models do not reproduce the human phenotype
> This discrepancy matters for translational research: *Ncf4*-null mice develop severe spontaneous granulomatous inflammation and substantially reduced lifespan, whereas human p40phox deficiency is comparatively mild. The discrepancy between species has been a persistent source of error in interpreting NOX-complex genetics and in designing NOX-targeted interventions.

CGD phenotypes also extend beyond infection: neutrophils from CGD patients are deficient in neutrophil extracellular trap formation, autophagy, and apoptosis, and hyperactivation of NF-κB and the inflammasome produces sustained pro-inflammatory cytokine output, granuloma formation, and inflammatory-bowel-disease-like colitis. Residual NADPH oxidase activity is significantly associated with disease severity and survival.

## Connections

- [[NOX2]] — the catalytic subunit whose assembly and activation threshold p40phox helps set.
- [[p67phox]] — binds p40phox reciprocally through its own SH3 domain and proline-rich region, completing the cytosolic assembly network.
- [[p47phox]] — the organizer subunit whose C-terminal SH3 domain binds p40phox's PX domain.
- [[p22phox]] — the membrane subunit of cytochrome b558 with which the cytosolic subunits assemble.
- [[NADPH Oxidase]] — the enzyme complex of which p40phox is a required cytosolic component.
- [[Chronic Granulomatous Disease]] — *NCF4* mutations cause its mildest and most atypical autosomal form.
- [[Respiratory Burst]] — the phagocyte oxidative burst whose assembly p40phox supports and whose failure defines CGD.
- [[Neutrophils]] — the cell in which NOX2 assembly and the CGD phenotype were characterized.
- [[Macrophage]] — uses the same subunit set for microbial killing and inflammatory ROS.
- [[Inflammasome]] — NOX-derived ROS regulate inflammasome activation, and CGD phagocytes show inflammasome hyperactivation.
- [[Autophagy]] — CGD neutrophils show defective autophagy alongside defective NETosis and apoptosis.
- [[Apoptosis]] — CGD phagocytes are deficient in apoptosis, contributing to persistent inflammation and granuloma formation.
- [[Superoxide Radicals]] — the immediate product of the oxidase that p40phox helps assemble.
- [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] — phagocyte ROS output from the NOX2 complex is a central effector of innate immunity.
- [[Inflammation]] — loss of regulated ROS production in CGD paradoxically produces chronic hyperinflammation.
- [[Chronic Granulomatous Disease|interferon-gamma]] — interferon-γ prophylaxis substantially improves overall survival in CGD, including in NCF4 deficiency.
