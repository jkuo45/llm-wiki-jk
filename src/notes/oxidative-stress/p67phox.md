# p67phox

**p67phox** (NCF2, neutrophil cytosolic factor 2) is the **activator subunit** of the phagocyte [[NADPH Oxidase]]. It does not contribute catalytic chemistry; it works as a scaffold and allosteric activator that licenses assembly of the complex on the membrane.

## Mechanism of Action

In a resting phagocyte, the oxidase is disassembled: the two membrane subunits ([[NOX2]]/gp91phox and [[p22phox]]) are present but catalytically inactive, and the three cytosolic subunits ([[p47phox]], [[p40phox]], and p67phox) sit free in the cytoplasm. Stimulation by receptor engagement drives a signal-transduction sequence in which the cytosolic components translocate to the membrane and associate with cytochrome b558.

p67phox's distinctive contribution is that it carries the **activation domain** — a highly basic region with a helix-turn-helix motif that binds the proline-rich region of p47phox, plus a second site that engages the N-terminus of gp91phox (NOX2). This dual docking is what relieves the autoinhibitory N-terminal domain of NOX2 and allows the conformational change that starts electron flow.

> [!info] Asymmetry between priming and activation
> p47phox is phosphorylated during the priming phase and serves as the organizer; p67phox binds p47phox and couples organizer to catalytic subunit. Because p67phox membrane translocation and its phosphorylation state both track oxidase activation, phospho-p67phox content at the membrane is a commonly used readout of NOX activity in tissue — for example, elevated membrane-associated p67phox and phospho-p47phox mark increased renal oxidase activity in diabetic nephropathy, and both are normalized by melatonin treatment in Zucker diabetic fatty rats (Winiarska et al., *J Pineal Res* 2016; PMID 26514550).

## Clinical Significance

Loss-of-function mutations in *NCF2* cause **autosomal recessive chronic granulomatous disease** — one of four autosomal forms, alongside *CYBA* (p22phox), *NCF1* (p47phox), and *NCF4* (p40phox), in contrast to the X-linked *CYBB* form (Chiriaco et al., *Pediatr Allergy Immunol* 2016; PMID 26693837). The defining defect is failure of the [[Respiratory Burst]], with recurrent bacterial and fungal infections and dysregulated granulomatous inflammation. Diagnosis uses the dihydrorhodamine flow cytometry assay.

> [!warning] Human versus mouse phenotype mismatch
> Mouse *NCF2*-deficient models reproduce CGD more faithfully than *NCF4* models do: p40phox deficiency in humans is a mild, atypical CGD without invasive infection, whereas *Ncf4*-null mice show severe spontaneous granulomatous inflammation and markedly reduced lifespan. This asymmetry has repeatedly misled therapeutic work on the NOX complex and is why human genotype–phenotype data remain the reference.

## Evolution and Non-Phagocytic Expression

Population resequencing of *CYBB*, *CYBA*, *NCF2*, and *NCF4* in over 100 ethnically diverse individuals found differentiated haplotype structure in *NCF2* in Asian populations, consistent with pathogen-driven selective pressure on an innate immune pathway (Tarazona-Santos et al., *Mol Biol Evol* 2013; PMID 23821607). The same evolutionary analysis highlighted the extracellular portion of gp91phox as adaptively selected, suggesting a role in host–pathogen interaction beyond ROS generation.

## Connections

- [[NOX2]] — the catalytic subunit that p67phox activates by engaging its N-terminal proline-rich region and relieving NOX2 autoinhibition.
- [[p22phox]] — the membrane partner whose cytochrome b558 heterodimer p67phox helps assemble into a functional oxidase.
- [[p47phox]] — the organizer subunit; p67phox binds p47phox through its activation domain and translocates with it.
- [[p40phox]] — the fourth cytosolic subunit, co-assembling with p67phox on the activated complex.
- [[NADPH Oxidase]] — the enzyme family of which p67phox is the activator, required across NOX1–4.
- [[Chronic Granulomatous Disease]] — *NCF2* mutations cause its autosomal recessive form; residual oxidase activity tracks severity and survival.
- [[Respiratory Burst]] — the phagocyte oxidative burst that fails in CGD and that p67phox helps initiate.
- [[Neutrophils]] — the phagocyte in which p67phox translocation and assembly were first defined.
- [[Macrophage]] — shares the same NOX2 assembly machinery for microbial killing.
- [[Superoxide Radicals]] — the product whose generation p67phox licensing enables.
- [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] — NOX2-derived superoxide is a major ROS source in phagocytes, microglia, endothelium, and cardiomyocytes.
- [[Nitric Oxide]] — superoxide from an assembled oxidase consumes NO, driving the superoxide/NO balance that governs vascular tone.
- [[Kidney Diseases]] — renal NOX4 and p47phox/p67phox activation drives diabetic nephropathy and albuminuria.
- [[Melatonin]] — its nephroprotective action in diabetic rats operates partly by preventing membrane translocation of p47phox and p67phox.
- [[Inflammation]] — NOX-derived ROS drive pro-inflammatory signaling and inflammasome activation.
- [[Vascular Stiffening]] — endothelial and vascular-wall oxidase activity contributes to arterial stiffening and hypertensive remodeling.
