# Silver compounds

## Overview

Silver compounds are derivatives of metallic [[Silver]], produced for antimicrobial, electronic, and synthetic applications. [[Silver(I) Oxide]] is used to prepare other silver compounds, serving as a versatile silver precursor in synthetic chemistry.

## Preparation & Types

Because silver(I) oxide is a stable, readily handled oxidant and silver source, it can be converted into soluble [[Silver salts]], coordination complexes, and metal [[Silver nanoparticles]], including the silver oxide nanoparticle dispersions used in antimicrobial and electronic applications.

> [!info]
| Compound type | Applications |
|---|---|
| Silver(I) oxide | Precursor, batteries |
| Soluble silver salts | Antimicrobials |
| Coordination complexes | Synthesis |
| Silver nanoparticles | Antimicrobial, electronics |

## Applications & Toxicology

Silver compounds appear in consumer and industrial products, with reported functional uses including antimicrobial activity, biocidal action, batteries, and personal care formulations. Their bioactivity stems from the release of [[Silver ion|silver ions]], which inhibit enzymes and promote oxidative stress, and chronic exposure can lead to [[Argyria]].
