# PtdIns4P

**Phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate (PtdIns4P)** is, together with [[PtdIns(4,5)P2]], one of the two most abundant phosphoinositides in the cell. It is enriched in the [[Golgi apparatus]] and the trans-Golgi network, but distinct sub-pools also exist at the plasma membrane, lysosomes, [[Endocytosis|endosomes]] and [[Autophagosome|phagophores]] — compartment specificity being enforced by distinct kinase and phosphatase isoforms rather than by the lipid itself.

## Biosynthesis & Turnover

The dominant synthetic route is phosphorylation of phosphatidylinositol on the 4-OH position by **phosphatidylinositol 4-kinases**: PI4KIIIβ at the Golgi, PI4KIIIα at the plasma membrane, and the type II isoforms PI4KIIα/PI4KIIβ (gene *PI4K2A*, *PI4K2B*) at the TGN, lysosomes and vesicles. Minor routes run in reverse: dephosphorylation of [[PtdIns(4,5)P2]] at the 5-OH by synaptojanin/OCRL-family 5-phosphatases, or of PI(3,4)P2 by [[PTEN]].

The dominant catabolic route is the ER-localised phosphatase **Sac1**, whose spatial restriction is what creates the PI4P concentration gradient between ER and Golgi/plasma membrane. That gradient, not the absolute pool size, is the currency of lipid exchange.

> [!info] PI4P phosphorylation is the committed step to PIP2 signalling
> [[PIP5KI]] (type I PIP 5-kinases) converts PtdIns4P to [[PtdIns(4,5)P2]], which is the substrate for [[Phospholipase C]]-mediated production of IP3 and DAG and for [[PI3K]]-generated PIP3. Loss of the PtdIns4P pool therefore suppresses receptor-driven signalling at the point of origin.

## Membrane Roles

- **Golgi/TGN:** recruits GGA adaptors, AP-1/clathrin machinery, and four-phosphate adaptors (PLEKHA3/8); ARF-GTP drives COPI/COPII coat recruitment. PI4P depletion stalls anterograde trafficking to the plasma membrane and almost abolishes TGN-to-multivesicular-body traffic.
- **Oxysterol-binding protein family:** ORP1 (long isoform [[ORP1L]]), ORP2, ORP3 and OSBP function as **PI4P-countertransporters** — exchanging PI4P from an apposed membrane for cholesterol or phosphatidylcholine from the ER, which is how cholesterol is delivered to the Golgi, secretory vesicles and lysosomes.
- **Lysosomal repair:** on lysosomal membrane damage, PI4K2A rapidly generates PI4P at the lesion; the ORP1L PH domain binds it, ORP1L is recruited, and VAPA/B–ORP1L contacts deliver cholesterol to reseal the membrane (*EMBO J* 2022).
- **Pathogen hijacking:** [[Legionella]] effectors (MavQ, LepB, SidF) synthesise PI4P de novo on the phagosome to convert it into a Rab7-positive, PtdIns4P-rich compartment and block phagosome–lysosome fusion.
- **Autophagy:** a PI4P pool exists at autophagosomes, and pharmacological or genetic inhibition of PI4K blocks autophagic flux at the autophagosome-formation stage (Gomez *et al.* 2022; Baba *et al.* 2020 review).

## Clinical Relevance

PI4P metabolism is an emerging cancer target: PI4KIIIβ inhibition perturbs Golgi trafficking, and *PIK3CA* — the kinase acting downstream at PIP3 — is one of the most frequently mutated oncogenes in the vault's cancer notes. Elevated PI4P-dependent cholesterol delivery is also implicated in myc-driven lysosomal biogenesis and in antiviral innate immunity, since the same host lipid machinery is what viruses and bacteria hijack.

## Connections

- [[PtdIns(4,5)P2]] — PtdIns4P is the direct precursor of PIP2, PIP5KI-catalysed, and the two together constitute the bulk cellular phosphoinositide pool.
- [[ORP1L]] — Its PH domain binds PtdIns4P to tether the cholesterol-transfer protein to TGN and late endosomal membranes; PI4P binding is the targeting step for its countertransport activity.
- [[Autophagosome]] — A PI4P pool at the phagophore/isolation membrane is required for autophagosome formation, and PI4K inhibition blocks autophagic flux.
- [[PIP5KI]] — The type I PI4P 5-kinase that consumes PtdIns4P to make PIP2, linking this lipid to receptor signalling.
- [[Golgi apparatus]] — The main site of PI4P synthesis (via PI4KIIIβ) and the compartment whose identity and coat recruitment depend on the local PI4P pool.
- [[PTEN]] — A 3-phosphatase that generates PI4P from PI(3,4)P2, one of the minor routes to this lipid.
- [[Legionella]] — Bacterial effectors synthesise PI4P on the phagosome to block maturation, making this note the pathogen-facing side of phosphoinositide compartmentalisation.
