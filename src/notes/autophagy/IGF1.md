# IGF1

**IGF1** (Insulin-like Growth Factor 1) is a hormone that plays a central role in regulating cell growth, proliferation, and lifespan.

## Impact on Autophagy
- IGF1 is a potent inhibitor of [[Autophagy]].
- It binds to the [[IGF1R]], activating the [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]] signaling axis.
- This pathway stimulates [[mTORC1]] and inhibits [[FoxO1|FOXO]] transcription factors, both of which serve to suppress autophagic activity.

## Longevity and Aging
- Low levels of IGF1 (as seen in [[Caloric Restriction]] or [[Intermittent Fasting]]) are associated with increased [[Longevity]] and the induction of beneficial autophagy.
- In contrast, high systemic IGF1 and insulin concentrations are often observed in various [[Cancer]] types and are linked to poor therapeutic efficacy.

## Function

IGF1 is a growth factor that signals through IGF1R to activate [[PI3K-Akt Signaling]] and suppress [[Autophagy]]. Reduced IGF1 signaling extends lifespan across species.
