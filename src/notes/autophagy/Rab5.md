# Rab5

**Rab5** is a small Ras-related GTPase that serves as the master regulator of early endosome biogenesis, homotypic fusion, motility, and the onset of endosome maturation. Together with its effectors, Rab5 establishes the early endosomal identity that ultimately transitions to late endosomal/lysosomal fate under the control of [[Rab7]]. Because endocytic and autophagic pathways converge at the [[Lysosome]], Rab5 also interfaces with [[Autophagy]] by governing the upstream membrane compartments that supply autophagic and endolysosomal traffic.

## Structure & Activation Cycle

Rab5 belongs to the Rab family of ~70 human GTPases that function as molecular switches tethered to membranes via prenyl groups. In its GTP-bound state, Rab5 engages effectors such as early endosome antigen 1 (EEA1) (via FYVE domains recognizing PtdIns3P), the class C Vps (HOPS) tethering complex, and motor adaptors that drive endosome motility along microtubules. Guanine nucleotide exchange factors (Rabex-5) activate Rab5, while RabGAPs (e.g., TBC1D2) inactivate it, establishing spatially restricted domains of active Rab5 on endosomal membranes.

## Role in Membrane Traffic & Autophagy

Rab5 promotes homotypic early endosome fusion and recruits the phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase [[Vps34]] to generate PtdIns3P, a lipid also essential for phagophore formation. During endosome maturation, Rab5 actively recruits Rab7 through the Rab7 GEF Mon1–Ccz1 complex and through SAND-1/MON1, driving a Rab5-to-Rab7 conversion that defines the early-to-late endosome transition. This handoff is critical for proper autophagosome–[[Lysosome]] fusion and for delivering autophagic cargo to degradative compartments. Aberrant Rab5 persistence delays maturation and impairs autophagic flux.

## Pathological & Host-Pathogen Relevance

Rab5 is a frequent target of microbial subversion. The [[Legionella]] effector [[SetA]] glucosylates Rab5 (and Rab22), immobilizing endosomes and blocking autophagosome–lysosome fusion to create a replication-permissive vacuole. Similar strategies are employed by other vacuolar pathogens to evade xenophagy. Overactive Rab5 signaling is also implicated in oncogenic growth and receptor-tyrosine-kinase-driven [[Cancer]] proliferation, linking endocytic trafficking to proliferative signaling.
