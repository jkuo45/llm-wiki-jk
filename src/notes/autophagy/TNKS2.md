# TNKS2

**TNKS2** (tankyrase 2, PARP5B) is one of two tankyrase isozymes (with [[TNKS1]]) that catalyze poly-ADP-ribosylation ([[PARsylation]]) of substrate proteins. Tankyrases are atypical poly(ADP-ribose) polymerases distinguished by an N-terminal ankyrin repeat domain (ARD) for substrate recognition, a sterile alpha motif (SAM) mediating oligomerization, and a C-terminal PARP catalytic domain. In [[Autophagy]] and lysosomal biology, TNKS2 PARsylates [[TFEB]], modulating its stability and nuclear-cytoplasmic shuttling, thereby influencing lysosomal biogenesis and autophagic flux.

## Structure & Catalytic Mechanism

Each tankyrase assembles into multimers via SAM-domain head-to-tail interactions, forming a catalytic scaffold. The ARD binds substrates such as axin, TRF1, and TFEB. The PARP domain transfers ADP-ribose units from NAD+ onto glutamate/aspartate/lysine residues, building branched PAR chains that typically tag substrates for recognition and often proteasomal degradation or altered localization. TNKS2 and TNKS1 are largely functionally redundant, though tissue expression differs.

## Regulation of TFEB & Autophagy

PARsylation of TFEB by TNKS2 promotes its destabilization and restricts its transcriptional program for lysosomal and autophagic genes. Inhibition of tankyrases (e.g., with small-molecule inhibitors such as XAV939 or JW74) stabilizes TFEB, enhancing expression of autophagy–lysosome components and boosting clearance of aggregated proteins—relevant to [[Tau Pathology]] and [[Neurodegeneration]]. The tankyrase–TFEB link thus couples ADP-ribosylation signaling to catabolic flux.

## Physiological & Pathological Relevance

Beyond autophagy, tankyrases regulate telomere length (via TRF1 PARsylation), Wnt/β-catenin signaling (via axin), and mitotic spindle function; their dysregulation is implicated in [[Cancer]] and metabolic disease. Targeting TNKS2/TNKS1 to stabilize TFEB is being explored to restore autophagic clearance in aging and proteinopathies, though cross-talk with Wnt pathways requires careful therapeutic indexing.
