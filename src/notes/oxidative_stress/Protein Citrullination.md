# Protein Citrullination

## Overview

Protein citrullination (deimination) is a post-translational modification in which peptidylarginine deiminase (PAD) enzymes convert arginine residues to citrulline, removing a positive charge and altering protein structure and function.

## Oxidative Stress Connection

[[Oxidative Stress]] promotes protein citrullination by increasing intracellular [[Calcium]], which activates PAD enzymes. The directionality is oxidant → calcium influx → PAD activation → citrullination.

> [!info]
> PAD enzymes are also regulated by other factors (pH, NO), so ROS is a contributor rather than the only trigger of citrullination.

## Clinical Relevance

Citrullinated proteins become autoantigens in [[Rheumatoid Arthritis]]: citrullinated peptides are recognized by anti-citrullinated protein antibodies (ACPA), a hallmark of the disease. This links oxidative stress to autoimmunity, with smoking (a source of oxidants) being a major environmental risk factor for ACPA-positive rheumatoid arthritis.
