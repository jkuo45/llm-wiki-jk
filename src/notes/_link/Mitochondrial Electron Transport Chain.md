# Mitochondrial Electron Transport Chain

The [[Mitochondrial Electron Transport Chain]] (ETC, respiratory chain) is the ensemble of complexes in the [[Inner Mitochondrial Membrane]] that passes electrons from donors ([[NADH]], [[FADH2]]) through [[Complex I]]–[[Complex IV]] to [[Oxygen]], pumping protons to build the membrane potential that drives [[ATP]] synthesis via [[ATP Synthase]].

## Methylene Blue Bypass

[[Methylene blue]] can bypass dysfunctional [[Complex I]]/[[Complex III]], shuttling electrons from [[NADH]] directly to [[Cytochrome c]], sustaining [[Mitochondrial Respiration]] and reducing [[Mitochondrial Superoxide]] — the mechanistic basis of its [[Hormetic Window|hormetic]] neuroprotective and mitochondrial-support effects.
