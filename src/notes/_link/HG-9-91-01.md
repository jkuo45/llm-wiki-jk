# HG-9-91-01

**HG-9-91-01** is a potent small-molecule inhibitor of the salt-inducible kinase (SIK) family (SIK1/2/3; also active against related AMPK-family kinases). It suppresses [[Necroptosis]] by disrupting the interaction between [[RIPK3]] and [[MLKL]], blocking the terminal phosphorylation step of the necrosome.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Regulatory complexity and therapeutic targeting of the necroptosis network|Niu et al. 2026]]
> The review cites HG-9-91-01 as a pharmacological example that the RIPK3/MLKL interaction is targetable, illustrating that the necroptosis execution step can be interrupted independently of RIPK1.
