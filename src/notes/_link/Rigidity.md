# Rigidity

**Rigidity** — velocity-independent resistance to passive movement (lead-pipe; cogwheel when tremor superimposes) — is a cardinal [[Parkinson's Disease]] motor sign alongside bradykinesia and rest tremor. It reflects basal-ganglia dopamine depletion disrupting direct/indirect pathway balance at striatal [[Synapse|synapses]], and responds to dopaminergic therapy and DBS.
