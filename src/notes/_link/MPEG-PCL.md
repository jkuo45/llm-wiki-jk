# MPEG-PCL

**Monomethyl poly(ethylene glycol)-poly(ε-caprolactone) (MPEG-PCL)** is an amphiphilic block copolymer that self-assembles into [[Nanoparticles|polymeric micelles]] (22 ± 3 nm) with high encapsulation efficiency (98.53%). Fisetin-loaded MPEG-PCL micelles showed enhanced cytotoxicity against CT26 colon cancer and SKOV3 ovarian cancer cells, prolonged survival (+17 days vs. free fisetin), and 1.3× stronger tumor growth inhibition in murine models.
