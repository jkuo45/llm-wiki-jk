# Platelet-Derived Growth Factor

**Platelet-Derived Growth Factor (PDGF)** is a dimeric growth factor (PDGF-A/-B/-C/-D subunits) that signals through receptor tyrosine kinases (PDGFR-α/β) to promote cell proliferation, migration, and survival. In the vasculature, PDGF is a key activator of vascular smooth muscle cells (VSMCs).

## Role in Mitochondrial Dynamics

In the context of [[Atherosclerosis|atherosclerosis]], PDGF activation of VSMCs is associated with decreased [[MFN2]] levels and increased [[Mitochondrial Fission|mitochondrial fragmentation]]. Inhibition of fission with [[Mdivi-1|Mdivi-1]] counteracts PDGF-induced fragmentation and reduces VSMC proliferation, indicating that PDGF shifts the mitochondrial network toward fission to support pathological vascular remodeling.
