### Overview
Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP) is the universal energy currency of the cell, coupling exergonic and endergonic reactions through phosphate-bond hydrolysis. It is produced mainly by [[Oxidative Phosphorylation]] in mitochondria, with glycolysis contributing under anaerobic conditions.

### Production and Mitochondrial Dynamics
The bulk of cellular ATP is generated at the [[Inner Mitochondrial Membrane]] by the OXPHOS machinery. The balance between [[Mitochondrial Fusion|fusion]] and [[Mitochondrial Fission|fission]] directly influences ATP supply: fragmented mitochondria can be selectively removed by [[Mitophagy]], and excessive fusion can paradoxically reduce ATP availability through increased mitophagic turnover.

### Homeostatic Relevance
Maintaining [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] through balanced dynamics is therefore essential for meeting cellular energy demand. Defects in ATP production underpin the bioenergetic failure seen in many mitochondrial diseases and neurodegenerative disorders.
