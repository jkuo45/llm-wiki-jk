# Glycine

**Chemical properties and classification:** **Glycine** is the simplest stable amino acid, lacking a chiral side chain (just a hydrogen). It is proteinogenic yet structurally flexible, frequently found in turns and collagen triple helices.

**Biosynthetic and metabolic roles:** Glycine is a critical precursor for the synthesis of [[Glutathione]] (with cysteine and glutamate), [[Creatine]] (with arginine and S-adenosylmethionine), [[Heme|heme]], purines, and [[Collagen]]. It participates in one-carbon metabolism via the glycine cleavage system and serves as an inhibitory neurotransmitter in the spinal cord and brainstem.

**Applications and longevity relevance:** In longevity protocols such as [[GlyNAC]] (glycine + N-acetylcysteine), glycine replenishes age-related [[Glutathione]] deficiency, lowering [[Oxidative Stress]] and supporting [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] function. Glycine supplementation has been associated with improved redox status, reduced inflammation, and better muscle and sleep health in older adults. Its safety profile at gram doses is favorable, making it a practical geroprotective nutrient.
