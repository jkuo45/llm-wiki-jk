# NETosis

**NETosis** is the regulated death (suicidal form) or secretory (vital form) program in which neutrophils extrude neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs): webs of decondensed chromatin decorated with histones, neutrophil elastase, and [[Myeloperoxidase]] (MPO). The canonical axis is NADPH-oxidase ROS → [[PAD4]]-mediated histone citrullination (cit-H3) → chromatin decondensation → nuclear envelope breakdown → NET release. Suicidal NETosis ruptures the cell over ~2–4 h; vital NETosis preserves the membrane and releases NETs within minutes.

## Mechanism

Pathogens, LPS, PMA, and immune complexes trigger ROS production, activating peptidylarginine deiminase 4 ([[PAD4]]), which citrullinates histones and unravels chromatin. The swollen, decondensed nucleus fills the cell, the envelope disintegrates, chromatin mixes with granule proteins (elastase, MPO), and the DNA–histone web fountains out to trap pathogens.

> [!warning] PAD4/NOX-independent routes exist
> NE-mediated, mitochondrial-ROS, and ionomycin-driven NET release proceed without PAD4 or NADPH oxidase. The machinery is therefore "largely" rather than strictly PAD4/NOX-dependent.

## Three NETosis pathways

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Burgener & Schroder 2020 resolve NETosis into three pathways that differ in trigger, machinery, and cell fate. [[Suicidal NETosis]] is NADPH-oxidase/ROS → [[Myeloperoxidase]] → [[Neutrophil Elastase]] → [[Gasdermin D]]-driven and lytic. [[Noncanonical NETosis]] is triggered by cytosolic [[Gram-Negative Bacteria]] sensed by the [[Noncanonical Inflammasome]] ([[Caspase-4]]/[[Caspase-11]] → GSDMD), is lytic, and is independent of NE, MPO, and PAD4. [[Vital NETosis]] is receptor/Ca²⁺/[[PAD4]]-driven, NADPH-oxidase-independent, and releases NETs by nuclear blebbing without plasma-membrane permeabilization, leaving the neutrophil viable and motile. Suicidal and noncanonical NETosis converge on [[Gasdermin D]] as a single executioner (Chen et al. 2018; Sollberger et al. 2018).

| Pathway | Trigger | Core machinery | Cell fate |
| ------- | ------- | -------------- | --------- |
| [[Suicidal NETosis]] | PMA, extracellular microbes | NADPH oxidase, ROS, MPO, NE, GSDMD | Lytic death |
| [[Noncanonical NETosis]] | Cytosolic Gram-negative bacteria / LPS | Caspase-4/11, GSDMD, NLRP3 | Lytic death |
| [[Vital NETosis]] | Bacteria, TLR4-activated platelets, complement | TLR2/TLR4/CR3, Ca²⁺, PAD4 | Viable, motile |

## Detection and blockade

Detection: extracellular DNA (Sytox Green), citrullinated histone H3 (cit-H3), MPO–DNA complexes. Blockade: [[PAD4]] inhibitors (Cl-amidine), neutrophil elastase / MPO inhibition, DNase digestion of NETs.

## Connections

- [[PAD4]] — the largely required histone-citrullinating enzyme; essential in vital NETosis, dispensable in suicidal and noncanonical NETosis.
- [[Myeloperoxidase]] — granule cargo and NET marker with DNA; gates NE release from the azurosome in suicidal NETosis.
- [[Neutrophil Elastase]] — clips histones and cleaves GSDMD in suicidal NETosis; dispensable in noncanonical NETosis.
- [[Gasdermin D]] — shared executioner of suicidal and noncanonical NETosis; GSDMD-p30 permeabilizes granule, nuclear, and plasma membranes.
- [[Suicidal NETosis]] / [[Noncanonical NETosis]] / [[Vital NETosis]] — the three mechanistically distinct pathways of this program.
- [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps]] — the extruded DNA–protein web produced by all three pathways.
- [[Noncanonical Inflammasome]] — LPS-sensing cassette specifying the noncanonical route.
- [[Caspase-11]] — nuclear histone-degrading substitute for NE in noncanonical NETosis.
- [[NADPH Oxidase]] — ROS source required for suicidal but dispensable for vital NETosis.
- [[NLRP3]] / [[Pyroptosis]] — NETs license NLRP3 activation in macrophages.
- [[Sepsis]] — major pathological context; NET burden tracks immunothrombosis.
- [[Protein Citrullination]] — the general modification cit-H3 belongs to.
- Neutrophil biology and [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] signaling.
