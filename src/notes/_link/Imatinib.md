# Imatinib

[[Imatinib]] is a prototype tyrosine-kinase inhibitor targeting BCR-ABL, [[c-KIT]], and [[PDGFR]], revolutionizing treatment of chronic myeloid leukemia and KIT-mutant gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GIST).

> [!info] Source: [[c-KIT]] (cross-link)
> Referenced as the classic KIT inhibitor in contrast to multi-targeted agents such as [[Pazopanib]].
