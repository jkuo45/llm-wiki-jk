# WI-38

**WI-38** is a diploid human fetal lung [[Fibroblast|fibroblast]] cell line derived by Leonard Hayflick in 1962 from a first-trimester female fetus. It has a finite replicative lifespan and undergoes [[Replicative Senescence]] after approximately 50 population doublings, making it a classical model system for cellular aging research.

## Role in Ferroptosis Research

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex|Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile… (Soriano-Castell et al., 2026)]]
> Using WI-38 cells driven to [[Replicative Senescence]] by serial passage (senescence at PDL55–60), Soriano-Castell et al. discovered that [[Acid ceramidase]] is overexpressed 5- to 20-fold in senescent cells, driving a pro-[[Ferroptosis|ferroptotic]] membrane lipid profile via the [[Lands cycle]].
