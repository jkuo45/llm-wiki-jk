# P-gp

**Overview:** P-gp is a membrane transport protein that facilitates the movement of molecules across biological membranes. It mediates substrate translocation through conformational changes driven by electrochemical gradients or ATP hydrolysis.

**Substrate specificity:** P-gp transports specific ions, metabolites, or xenobiotics across cellular membranes. Its selectivity is determined by structural features of the substrate-binding pocket.

**Tissue distribution:** Expression of P-gp varies across tissues, with highest levels in barrier and excretory organs where transport activity is critical for homeostasis.

**Clinical relevance:** Genetic variants of P-gp influence drug disposition and response. Inhibition or dysregulation of this transporter contributes to drug resistance and metabolic diseases.
