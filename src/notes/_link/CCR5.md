# CCR5

CCR5 (C-C chemokine receptor type 5, CD195) is a Gαi-coupled GPCR that is the primary receptor for [[CCL5]] (RANTES), also binding CCL3 and CCL4. It is expressed on T cells, monocytes, NK cells, and — critically in aging — on [[Hematopoietic Stem Cell|hematopoietic stem cells]] and granulocyte–monocyte progenitors (Gabandé-Rodríguez et al., 2026, *Nat Aging*, doi:10.1038/s43587-026-01209-9).

## Structure & Domains

- Seven-transmembrane GPCR; co-receptor for HIV-1 entry with [[CD4]]. The CCR5-Delta32 allele (homozygous loss-of-function) confers HIV resistance and, in population data, better outcomes after stroke and in multiple sclerosis (Joy et al., 2019; Kantor et al., 2003).

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- Ligand engagement activates PI3K/Akt, [[p38 MAPK]]/ERK, and Ca2+ signaling for chemotaxis, adhesion, and differentiation.
- **Hematopoietic axis**: In aged mice, HSCs and GMPs upregulate CCR5; CCR5 signaling from BM-infiltrating [[Cytotoxic CD4+ T Cells|CD4+ CTL-derived]] [[CCL5]] drives myeloid-biased lineage output (expanded LS−K, CMP, GMP, Ly6C^hi monocyte, and neutrophil fractions) without affecting HSC number, CLPs, or CD8+ cells — a selective myeloid bias.

## Physiological Function & Pathology

- Age-associated CCR5 signaling on progenitors is a causal link between [[Immunosenescence|T cell aging]] and myeloid skewing; hematopoietic progenitor *Ccr5* ablation mitigates T cell-induced neutrophil expansion.

## Therapeutic Landscape

- **[[Maraviroc]]**: FDA-approved CCR5 antagonist (HIV-1 therapy). One month of maraviroc in 23-month-old mice normalized myelopoiesis, reduced circulating and tissue-infiltrating [[Neutrophils]], normalized the neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio, lowered inflammaging cytokines, and improved glucose, ALT/AST, grip strength, and rotarod performance.
- CCR5 blockade is also explored in cancer (TIS-driven promotion), inflammatory disease, and stroke recovery.
