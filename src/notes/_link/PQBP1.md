# PQBP1

## Overview

Polyglutamine binding protein 1 (PQBP1) is an intranuclear, polyglutamine-rich [[RNA-Binding Proteins|RNA-binding protein]] involved in RNA processing and splicing. Beyond its canonical nuclear functions, PQBP1 has emerged as a critical adaptor that links pathological [[Tau|tau]] to the innate immune sensor [[cGAS]], activating the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS-STING]] pathway in a manner that bypasses the classic requirement for cytosolic DNA.

## Structure and Function

PQBP1 contains a polyglutamine tract and an RNA-recognition motif consistent with its roles in transcription and pre-mRNA splicing. It localizes predominantly to the nucleus and participates in RNA metabolism. Like [[G3BP1]], PQBP1 is an IDR-mediated interactor of cGAS, enriched among cGAS-interacting RNA-binding proteins identified in interactome studies. Its scaffolding role positions it at the interface of RNA metabolism and innate immune signaling.

## Mechanism

In tauopathy, monomeric tau binds PQBP1, and the resulting tau–PQBP1 complex engages cGAS through direct protein-protein interaction, activating cGAS-STING and driving brain inflammation (Jin et al., Nat Commun 2021, PMID 34782623). Crucially, this activation bypasses the canonical DNA requirement: the complex stimulates cGAS independently of cytosolic dsDNA. This establishes PQBP1 as a noncanonical adaptor that converts a [[Proteinopathy|proteinopathy]] signal into innate immune activation. The tau–PQBP1–cGAS axis contributes to [[Neuroinflammation]] and neurodegeneration in tau-driven disease, and represents an important exception to the otherwise DNA-centric model of cGAS activation.

## Pathology

By coupling tau accumulation to cGAS-STING, PQBP1-mediated activation promotes microglial inflammatory states, neuronal injury, and cognitive decline in [[Tauopathy|tauopathies]]. Inhibition of cGAS-STING suppresses the downstream [[Type I Interferon|IFN-I]] and [[Interferon-Stimulated Genes|ISG]] programs triggered by this axis, underscoring PQBP1 as a potential therapeutic node in tau-related neurodegeneration.
