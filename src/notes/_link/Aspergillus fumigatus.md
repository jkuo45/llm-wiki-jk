# Aspergillus fumigatus

Aspergillus fumigatus is a filamentous fungus well characterised to induce [[NETosis]] (Bruns et al. 2010). As with [[Candida albicans]] hyphae, large fungal structures that exceed the capacity for [[Phagocytosis]] favour [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps|NET]] deployment. Invasive aspergillosis is a leading cause of death in [[Chronic Granulomatous Disease]] patients, and gene therapy restoring [[NADPH Oxidase]] function reinstated NETs able to inhibit Aspergillus nidulans growth (Bianchi et al. 2009).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> ROS-dependent NETs may be critical for host defense against Aspergilli species (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
