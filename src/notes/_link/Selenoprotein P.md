# Selenoprotein P

**Overview:** **[[Selenoprotein|Selenoprotein P]]** is the major **[[Selenium]]** transport protein in plasma, delivering selenium from the liver to peripheral tissues. It is unusual among selenoproteins in carrying multiple **[[Selenocysteine]]** residues — 10 in humans (17 in mice).

## Functions

- **Selenium distribution / homeostasis** — the principal vehicle moving dietary selenium into the body's selenoproteome.
- **Extracellular antioxidant defense** — its Sec residues protect endothelial and neuronal cells from **[[Reactive Oxygen Species]]**.
- **Tissue supply** — critical for selenium delivery to brain and testis.

## Significance

SELENOP is a key node linking dietary **[[Selenium]]** intake to the [[Selenoprotein]] family; its own expression is selenoprotein-dependent, creating a feedback loop that prioritizes essential selenoproteins under low selenium.
