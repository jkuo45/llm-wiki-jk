# Embryogenesis

**Embryogenesis** is the process by which a single fertilised cell becomes a recognisable multi-organised embryo, through coordinated cell division, patterning, and morphogenesis. In humans it occupies roughly the first eight weeks after fertilisation; from week 9 the structure is termed the fetus and continues to grow and mature rather than to form.

> [!info] Why this note is in a longevity vault
> Embryogenesis is where most of the mechanistic vocabulary of this vault is written. Germ-layer specification uses the same signalling axes — [[Wnt signaling|Wnt]], [[Notch]], [[TGF-beta Signaling|TGF-β]], [[Retinoic Acid]] — that reappear in adult tissue homeostasis, repair, and [[Stem Cells|regeneration]]. The epigenetic machinery (imprinting, [[Methylation]], chromatin remodelling) is set up here and maintained for life. The [[Hypusination|polyamine–hypusine]] pathway, via [[DHPS]] and [[DOHH]], is embryonic-lethal-null in mice — embryogenesis is a place where this vault's metabolic chemistry stops being optional.

## Major stages

Cleavage → blastocyst → implantation → gastrulation → neurulation → organogenesis, in that order. Order is meaningful here, so the sequence above is deliberate.

- **Fertilisation and cleavage.** The zygote undergoes rapid mitoses without growth, producing smaller cells ([[Meiosis|blastomeres]]) and eventually a solid morula. The [[X-Chromosome Inactivation|dosage compensation]] decision and the maternal-to-zygotic genome transition occur here.
- **Blastulation.** A fluid-filled cavity (blastocoel) forms, giving the [[Blastocyst]] with its inner cell mass and trophoblast.
- **Implantation.** The blastocyst embeds in the endometrium; the trophoblast contributes to the placenta, the embryoblast to the embryo proper.
- **Gastrulation.** The first major cell movement. A bilaminar disc (epiblast and hypoblast) becomes a trilaminar germinal disc: epiblast cells migrate through the primitive streak to form intraembryonic mesoderm; the hypoblast is replaced to give endoderm; the remaining epiblast becomes ectoderm. **Gastrulation is when the three germ layers are fixed** — ectoderm gives skin and nervous system, endoderm gives gut epithelium and associated organs, mesoderm gives everything else. Whether the blastopore becomes mouth (protostomes) or anus (deuterostomes) is the deepest division in animal phylogeny. Cells that pass through the primitive streak can become any tissue, which is why they are also the experimental starting point for induced pluripotent stem cells.
- **Neurulation.** The notochord induces overlying ectoderm to thicken into the neural plate, which invaginates, folds and fuses to form the neural tube — the precursor of the central nervous system. Neural crest cells delaminate from the plate borders and migrate to form, among much else, the peripheral nervous system, melanocytes, and much of the craniofkeleton. Failure of neural tube closure produces spina bifida and anencephaly, and is preventable by periconceptional folate.
- **Organogenesis.** Weeks 3–8 (most organs begin), with continued growth and functional maturation thereafter. In the human, weeks 3–4 are gastrulation and neurulation, weeks 5–8 organogenesis.

## Molecular control

- **Homeotic transcription factors.** [[HOXA10]] and the wider homeobox and [[SOX gene family|SOX]] gene families give each segment and each tissue its address. Hox genes specify positional identity along the anterior–posterior axis; their misexpression causes segment-identity transformations and multiple congenital malformations.
- **Signalling axes.** [[Wnt signaling|Wnt]], [[Notch]], FGF, Hedgehog and [[TGF-beta Signaling|TGF-β]] act as morphogens, forming gradients that cells read positionally. This is the same toolkit used in adult [[Wound Healing]] and regeneration, which is why embryonic development and repair are so often mechanistically parallel.
- **Pluripotency factors.** [[Oct4]], [[Nanog]], [[Sox2]] — the [[Yamanaka Factors]] — maintain the pluripotent state in the inner cell mass and, when introduced into somatic cells, reprogram them ([[Reprogramming]]). Their expression is a direct readout of pluripotency.
- **Epigenetic control.** DNA methylation, imprinting, and histone modification lock in cell fate once it is specified, so that a neuron does not re-express muscle genes for the next 80 years. This is the mechanistic origin of the "epigenetic clock" concept and of [[Transcription Factor|transcription factor]]-based fate stability.

## Clinical relevance

> [!info] Two large populations of human conceptions
> Roughly half of all human conceptions are lost before implantation; a further substantial fraction are lost in the first trimester, most of them chromosomally abnormal. The majority of first-trimester loss is therefore a consequence of errors in the cell division and chromosomal segregation of [[Embryogenesis]], not of maternal factors.

- **Teratogens act in windows, not generally.** The classic sensitivity windows: organogenesis weeks 3–8, the CNS weeks 3–16, the heart weeks 3.5–6.5. Thalidomide and isotretinoin are the textbook demonstrations of window-specific teratogenicity. Anything disturbing embryonic development is much less consequential before implantation (all-or-none) or after week 8 (growth and functional defects).
- **Chromosomal and single-gene errors** manifest here: [[Trisomy]] 21, 18 and 13; trisomy tolerance windows differ by chromosome and by placental mosaicism. Single-gene disorders frequently trace to this window, which is why their aetiology is termed *inborn* or *congenital*.
- **Developmental origins of adult disease.** The developmental origins of health and disease hypothesis links first-trimester exposures (undernutrition, [[Diabetes]], maternal stress) to adult cardiometabolic disease, on the same epigenetic-plasticity logic described above.
- **Stem cell and regenerative medicine.** Because developmental signalling gradients are re-deployable, and because induced pluripotent cells are made from epiblast-like states, most of the mechanism in [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] and [[Regenerative Medicine]] comes from this field.

## Documents

- [[HOXA10]] — a homeotic transcription factor whose misexpression in Müllerian duct precursors causes hand-foot-genital syndrome; the clearest single-gene illustration of Hox positional specification.
- [[Gastrulation]] — the stage that fixes the three germ layers and establishes the body axes; the point of no return in vertebrate development.
- [[Blastocyst]] — the pre-implantation structure containing the inner cell mass, and the source of embryonic stem cell lines.
- [[Meiosis]] — the reduction division whose errors, combined with early mitotic errors, generate most aneuploid embryos.
- [[Oct4]] — the core pluripotency transcription factor of the inner cell mass; the functional definition of pluripotency.
- [[Stem Cells]] — the mechanistic continuation of embryonic patterning into adult tissue maintenance and repair.

## Connections

- [[HOXA10]] — homeobox genes give spatial identity along the embryonic axes. A HOXA10 mutation producing a phenotype localised to specific body regions is direct evidence that this positional code is real and clinically consequential.
- [[Gastrulation]] — the defining morphogenetic event of embryogenesis and the moment germ layers become irreversibly specified. All vertebrate tissues downstream are described in terms of their germ layer of origin.
- [[Blastocyst]] — the structure whose inner cell mass is pluripotent; everything from here to [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] and whole-organoid work derives from this population.
- [[Oct4]] — a member of the [[Yamanaka Factors]] and the master regulator of the pluripotent state, linking embryonic identity, cellular reprogramming, and the regenerative medicine field.
- [[Wnt signaling|Wnt]] — an embryonic patterning morphogen that is also a core adult stem-cell and [[Cancer|tumorigenesis]] pathway. Its dual role is the clearest evidence that development and disease share signalling logic.
- [[Retinoic Acid]] — a vitamin A derivative that patterns the hindbrain and, via retinoic acid response elements, is one of the best-understood morphogen gradients in the embryo.
- [[Methylation]] — the epigenetic machinery set up during embryogenesis and maintained for life, connecting this note to the epigenetic clocks in the aging notes of this vault.
- [[Meiosis]] and [[Trisomy]] — the cell division events whose errors generate most spontaneous abortions and aneuploidy, making embryogenesis the main determinant of early pregnancy outcome.
- [[DHPS]] and [[DOHH]] — knockout of either, or of [[eIF5A]], is embryonic lethal in mice. The polyamine–hypusine pathway is therefore a developmental requirement, not merely a growth-support pathway.
- [[Neurogenesis]] — neural tube formation and subsequent neurogenesis are the developmental faces of the same processes that fail in [[Neurodegeneration]]; developmental and degenerative neurobiology share a large part of their mechanism set.
- [[Stem Cells]] — embryonic patterning establishes progenitor compartments whose maintenance rules are the same ones perturbed in [[Aging]] and [[Senescence]], giving developmental biology direct explanatory power in this vault's central topic.
