# DDX3Y

[[DDX3Y]] (DEAD-box helicase 3 Y-linked, DBY) is the Y-chromosome homolog of [[DDX3X]]. Functions overlap in translation/splicing/Wnt but are non-identical; DDX3Y partially compensates for DDX3X loss in males.

## Function

- ATP-dependent RNA unwinding; translation initiation and stress-granule biology shared with DDX3X.
- Male germ-cell expression; mosaic chrY loss removes backup.

## Sex Differences

- Male tumors with [[DDX3X]] LOF show trend toward chrY loss (10.2% vs 5.8%, P=0.077); female X-X compensation stronger (36% vs 8.2%, P=0.0022; Dunford et al., *Nature Genetics* 2017).
- DDX3Y not fully equivalent — females retain net EXITS advantage (see [[EXITS]]).
