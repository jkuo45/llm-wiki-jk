# Neuroblastoma

**Neuroblastoma** is an embryonal malignancy arising from neural crest cells of the sympathetic nervous system. It is the most common extracranial solid tumor in children and exhibits remarkable biological heterogeneity, ranging from spontaneous regression to aggressive, treatment-resistant disease.

## Pathophysiology

Neuroblastoma cells have high metabolic demands. Mitochondrial dynamics, including [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|fission and fusion]], influence tumor cell survival, metabolic adaptation, and therapy resistance. Excessive mitochondrial fission has been observed in aggressive neuroblastoma cell lines.

## Clinical Features

Presentations vary by primary site (adrenal medulla, paraspinal ganglia), stage, and biological features. High-risk disease requires intensive multimodal therapy. MYCN amplification is a key prognostic marker.
