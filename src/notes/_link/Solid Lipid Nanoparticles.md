# Solid Lipid Nanoparticles

**Solid lipid nanoparticles (SLNs)** are colloidal carriers (typically <100–1000 nm) prepared by high-pressure homogenization of physiological lipids, emulsifiers, and water, with a lipophilic matrix that remains solid at room and body temperature. Compared with [[Liposomes|liposomes]] and polymeric [[Nanoparticles|nanoparticles]], SLNs offer controlled/sustained release, protection of labile actives, avoidance of organic solvents, and improved [[Bioavailability|bioavailability]] for lipophilic drugs.

## Fisetin SLNs

Kulbacka et al. co-loaded fisetin with the photosensitizer IR-780 (5:1) into SLNs (Phospholipon 90 G / cetyl palmitate / Tween 80) by solvent diffusion — hydrodynamic diameter 133.8 nm, 41.19% fisetin entrapment. In LoVo colon adenocarcinoma cells (electropermeabilized), fisetin-IR-780 SLNs reduced viability ~50% at 24 h (sustained to 72 h), induced cytoskeletal reorganization, and increased p53 and [[MnSOD]] — a multifunctional theranostic platform for colon cancer.
