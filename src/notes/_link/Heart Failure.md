# Heart Failure
**Heart failure** is a complex clinical syndrome where the heart is unable to pump sufficiently to maintain blood flow. It is a common end-stage of [[Cardiovascular Disease]] and [[Hypertension]].

## Mitochondrial Dynamics in Heart Failure

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - The role of mitochondrial dynamics in disease]]
> In HF patients, mitochondrial fragmentation, vacuolar degeneration with decreased mitochondrial size, and crista destruction are detected, along with increased expression of [[DRP1]], [[FIS1]], and [[BNIP3]]. [[MFN1]], [[MFN2]], and [[OPA1]] are downregulated, correlating with increased mitochondrial fragmentation. DRP1 is essential for cardiac embryonic development — DRP1 deletion results in lethal heart defects. Mild activation of [[PINK1]]–[[Parkin]] mitophagy prevents HF development and alleviates myocardial infarction, but excessive activation is detrimental. Therapeutic approaches include [[Mdivi-1]] (DRP1 inhibitor), Sacubitril/Valsartan, and [[Metformin]] (via [[AMPK]]/[[PGC-1α]] pathway).

The high energy requirement of the heart makes it particularly vulnerable to [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial dynamics]] imbalance. Excessive mitochondrial fission and insufficient fusion are consistently associated with cardiac pathology across hypertrophic, dilated, and ischemic cardiomyopathies.
