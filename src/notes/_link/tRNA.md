# tRNA

**Overview:** **tRNA** molecules are adaptor RNAs that deliver amino acids to the ribosome in **[[Translation]]**. The **[[Selenocysteine]]**-specific isoacceptor **tRNA[Ser]Sec** is unique: it is first charged with **[[Serine]]** then converted on the tRNA to Sec by **[[PSTK]]** and **[[SepSecS]]**.

## Special features

- tRNA[Ser]Sec bears the **Um34** modification at position 34 (installed by **[[FTSJ1]]**), required for efficient recoding of the **[[UGA codon]]** as Sec.
- It is also isopentenylated and interacts with the SECIS machinery via **[[SECISBP2]]** and **[[eEFSec]]**.

## Significance

tRNA[Ser]Sec is the physical carrier of the 21st amino acid; its modifications determine **[[Selenoprotein]]** output and, consequently, cellular redox resilience.
