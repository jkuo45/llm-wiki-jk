# Acute Lung Injury

**Acute lung injury (ALI)** and its more severe form, **acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS)**, are acute inflammatory conditions of the lung characterized by diffuse alveolar damage, increased pulmonary vascular permeability, and hypoxemic respiratory failure.

## Pathophysiology

ALI/ARDS involves epithelial and endothelial injury, neutrophilic inflammation, and disruption of the alveolar-capillary barrier. Mitochondrial dysfunction in lung epithelial cells and immune cells, including excessive [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial fission]] and impaired [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]], contributes to cell death and inflammatory amplification.

## Clinical Features

Acute onset of bilateral pulmonary infiltrates, severe hypoxemia, and impaired lung compliance. Causes include pneumonia, sepsis, aspiration, and trauma. Management is supportive with mechanical ventilation and treatment of the underlying cause.
