# Sodium-Potassium ATPase

**Sodium-Potassium ATPase** (Na+/K+-ATPase) is the plasma-membrane antiporter that exports 3 Na+ for every 2 K+ imported at the cost of one [[ATP]], maintaining resting membrane potential and cell volume. When ATP collapses during [[Ischemia]] or mitochondrial poisoning, the pump stalls: intracellular sodium and water accumulate ([[Oncosis]]), and reverse-mode Na+/Ca2+ exchange floods the cytosol with calcium.

In the comparison-table context, pump failure is the first domino of the necrosis core-machinery chain (pump → [[Ca2+ overload]] → [[Calpain]] → mPTP/LMP). Cardiac glycosides (ouabain, digoxin) inhibit the pump pharmacologically, which is why they narrow the therapeutic window in ischemic myocardium.
