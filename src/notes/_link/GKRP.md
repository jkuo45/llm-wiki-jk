# GKRP

**GKRP (glucokinase regulatory protein, gene GCKR)** is an inhibitory binding partner of glucokinase in the liver. It sequesters glucokinase in the nucleus, lowering its activity, and releases it in response to glucose and fructose-6-phosphate, thereby regulating hepatic glucose uptake and disposal.

## Sirtuin Regulation

- **[[SIRT2]]** promotes glucose-dependent hepatic glucose uptake by deacetylating GKRP at K126.

## Physiological Function

By gating glucokinase availability, GKRP sets the threshold for hepatic glucose phosphorylation and clearance. Its acetylation state modulates this gating, with SIRT2-mediated deacetylation enhancing glucose uptake.

## Clinical Relevance

GKRP is a druggable node in hepatic glucose metabolism and type 2 diabetes. SIRT2 regulation of GKRP adds to the sirtuin control of glucose homeostasis.
