# Dimethyl Sulfoxide

[[Dimethyl Sulfoxide]] ([[DMSO]]) is a polar aprotic solvent widely used as a pharmaceutical vehicle and penetration enhancer. Beyond solubilization, DMSO inhibits several cytochrome P450 subtypes (2C9, 2C19, 2E1, 3A4) in a concentration-dependent manner (51, 52).

## Role in Fenbendazole Repurposing

Because [[CYP2C19]] and [[CYP3A4]] metabolize [[Fenbendazole]], DMSO-mediated inhibition of these enzymes prolongs fenbendazole systemic exposure, enhancing cytotoxicity and apoptosis even in [[Paclitaxel]]-resistant cells (53). DMSO and the DNTC mixture (DMSO, NMP, Tween-80, Cremophor) are highlighted as promising vehicles to overcome fenbendazole's low water solubility (0.3 μ g/ml) (11, 12).

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> Fen-DMSO and Fen-DNTC are more cytotoxic than controls and than paclitaxel in resistant prostate cancer models; DMSO inhibition of 2C19/3A4 is the proposed bioavailability mechanism (53).
