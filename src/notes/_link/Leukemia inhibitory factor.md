# Leukemia inhibitory factor

**Leukemia inhibitory factor (LIF)** is an IL-6-family cytokine that maintains [[Embryonic Stem Cells|embryonic stem cell]] pluripotency and self-renewal via STAT3 signaling. In differentiation assays it is withheld (or cells are plated without feeders) to permit lineage commitment.

> [!info] In the DGCR8 study
> [[DGCR8]]-deficient ES cells continued to express alkaline phosphatase and failed to flatten/differentiate even in the absence of LIF, whereas wild-type colonies differentiated — showing miRNAs are required to exit the self-renewal program regardless of LIF.
