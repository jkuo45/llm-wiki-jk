# Escherichia coli

Escherichia coli is a Gram-negative bacterium reported to induce [[NETosis]] (Grinberg et al. 2008). Confocal intravital microscopy imaged [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps|NETs]] capturing circulating E. coli in the hepatic sinusoids (McDonald et al. 2012). NETs can directly kill E. coli, and [[GSDMD-p30]] is reported to target bacterial membranes to kill extracellular E. coli (Ding et al. 2016; Liu et al. 2016).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> [[Neutrophil Elastase]] kills bacteria by cleaving outer-membrane proteins and is crucial in host defense against Gram-negative bacteria such as E. coli or [[Klebsiella pneumoniae]] (Belaaouaj et al. 1998; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
