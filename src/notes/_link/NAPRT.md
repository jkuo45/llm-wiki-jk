# NAPRT

## Overview

[[NAPRT]] (Nicotinic Acid Phosphoribosyltransferase) is a rate-limiting enzyme in the [[Preiss-Handler Pathway]] of [[NAD+ Biosynthesis|NAD+ biosynthesis]]. It catalyzes the conversion of nicotinic acid (NA) to nicotinic acid mononucleotide (NaMN), a key step in the utilization of [[Niacin|vitamin B3 (niacin)]] for NAD+ production.

## Structure & Function

- **Gene:** NAPRT, located on chromosome 8q24.3
- **Protein:** ~539 amino acid phosphoribosyltransferase
- **Reaction:** Nicotinic acid + PRPP → Nicotinic acid mononucleotide (NaMN) + PPi
- **Cofactor:** Requires Mg<sup>2+</sup> and phosphoribosyl pyrophosphate (PRPP)
- **Tissue expression:** Highly expressed in liver, kidney, and small intestine

## Mechanism of Action

### Preiss-Handler Pathway

NAPRT functions in the Preiss-Handler pathway:

1. **NAPRT:** Nicotinic acid → NaMN (nicotinic acid mononucleotide)
2. **NMNAT:** NaMN → NaAD (nicotinic acid adenine dinucleotide)
3. **NAD+ synthase:** NaAD → [[NAD+]]

This pathway utilizes dietary [[Niacin]] (nicotinic acid) as a precursor for NAD+ biosynthesis.

### Regulation

- **Feedback inhibition:** NAPRT is inhibited by NAD+ and NADH
- **Substrate availability:** Activity depends on nicotinic acid and PRPP levels
- **Tissue-specific expression:** Highest in tissues with high NAD+ turnover

## Physiological Function

- **NAD+ biosynthesis:** Primary route for dietary niacin utilization
- **Niacin metabolism:** Converts vitamin B3 to NAD+ precursor
- **Tissue NAD+ maintenance:** Supports NAD+ pools in liver, kidney, and intestine

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Niacin metabolism:** NAPRT polymorphisms may affect niacin utilization efficiency
- **Cancer:** NAPRT expression varies across tumor types; may influence NAD+ biosynthesis capacity
- **Metabolic disease:** Impaired NAPRT function could limit NAD+ availability for sirtuins
