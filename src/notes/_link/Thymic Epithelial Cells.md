# Thymic Epithelial Cells

**Thymic epithelial cells (TECs)** are specialized stromal cells in the [[Thymus|thymus]] that form a three-dimensional network essential for [[Thymopoiesis|thymopoiesis]]. They exist in two functionally distinct populations:

## Subtypes

### Cortical TECs (cTECs)
- Located in the thymic cortex
- Support early thymocyte development and TCR gene rearrangement
- Present self-peptide/MHC complexes for positive selection
- Express cytokines and chemokines that guide thymocyte migration

### Medullary TECs (mTECs)
- Located in the thymic medulla
- Express tissue-restricted antigens via the AIRE transcription factor
- Mediate negative selection of auto-reactive thymocytes
- Essential for establishing central self-tolerance

## Role in Thymopoiesis

TECs provide critical signals at every stage of [[T Lymphocyte|T cell]] development:

- **Cytokine production** — TECs produce [[IL-7]], [[IL-6]], IL-1, IL-3, [[TGFβ|TGF-β]], oncostatin M, and [[Leukemia inhibitory factor|leukemia inhibitory factor (LIF)]]
- **Notch signaling** — cTECs express DLL4 ligand, essential for T cell lineage commitment
- **Antigen presentation** — MHC class I and II expression for positive and negative selection
- **Chemokine gradients** — Guide thymocyte migration through cortex and medulla

## Age-Related Changes

With [[Aging|aging]], TEC numbers and function decline, contributing to [[Thymic Involution|thymic involution]]:

- Reduced TEC proliferation and differentiation
- Decreased cytokine production (particularly [[IL-7]])
- Disrupted thymic architecture
- Impaired thymocyte-stromal cross-talk

> [!important] Rejuvenation targets
> TECs are the primary target of thymic rejuvenation therapies. [[Keratinocyte Growth Factor|KGF]] stimulates TEC proliferation and differentiation, [[IL-22]] promotes TEC survival and regeneration after injury, and [[Sex Steroid Ablation]] restores TEC-mediated thymic architecture.
