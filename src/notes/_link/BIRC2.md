# BIRC2

***BIRC2*** (baculoviral IAP repeat-containing 2; 11q22.2) encodes **cIAP1**, the workhorse subunit of
the [[cIAPs]] pair. cIAP1's RING domain ubiquitinates [[RIPK1]] (and itself) to sustain TNFR1
[[TNFR1 complex I|complex I]] survival signaling; it is the primary casualty of [[Smac mimetics]],
whose binding triggers its minutes-scale auto-destruction and thereby licenses [[Ripoptosome]]
assembly.

## Structure & Domains

Three BIR domains (BIR2 binds Smac-mimetic IBM motifs), a CARD auto-regulatory region, and a
C-terminal RING conferring E3 ligase activity toward [[RIPK1]], TRAF2-associated complex members,
and — upon antagonist binding — itself (K48-linked self-ubiquitination → [[Proteasome]]).

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **Complex-I ubiquitination.** With TRAF2, cIAP1 attaches K63/linear chains to [[RIPK1]],
  building the IKK/NF-κB scaffold and inducing *CFLAR* ([[c-FLIP]]) — the transcriptional arm of the
  [[Caspase-8-c-FLIP Rheostat]].
- **Antagonist-induced suicide.** [[Smac mimetics]] binding BIR2/BIR3 dimerizes RINGs and flips
  ligase activity onto cIAP1 itself; cellular cIAP1 half-life collapses from hours to minutes.
- **Genetics.** 11q22 *BIRC2/BIRC3* amplification marks apoptosis-resistant carcinomas and myeloma;
  germline lesions are rare, consistent with embryonic/developmental restraint on the locus.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Drug target:** cIAP1 degradation is the proximal pharmacodynamic readout in every Smac-mimetic
  trial; rapid loss with persistent tumor survival signals downstream rheostat blockade
  ([[c-FLIP]] high / [[RIPK3]] absent) rather than drug failure.
- **Resistance:** compensatory cIAP2 upregulation and [[RIPK3]] silencing blunt mimetic efficacy.
