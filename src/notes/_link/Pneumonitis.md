# Pneumonitis

[[Pneumonitis]] is inflammation of the lung tissue (often interstitial). In the context of [[mTOR]] inhibitors, it presents as a noninfectious interstitial pneumonitis—a rare but potentially severe adverse event requiring discontinuation of the offending drug.

## Rapamycin-Associated Pneumonitis

A rare side effect of [[Rapamycin]] is noninfectious interstitial [[Pneumonitis]] ([Blagosklonny, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6814615/); [Lee et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38310895/)). By inhibiting neutrophil function, rapamycin may also increase the severity of bacterial infections, further complicating pulmonary risk. Because pneumonitis is not reliably reversible with continued dosing, it is listed among the side effects that mandate stopping therapy.

> [!danger] Discontinue on onset
> Unlike mild thrombocytopenia or mucositis, interstitial pneumonitis is a rare but serious event; rapamycin should be discontinued if it occurs.
