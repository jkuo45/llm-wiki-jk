# Integrated Stress Response

A common adaptive pathway in eukaryotic cells (ISR) triggered by various stressors (ER stress, amino acid starvation, heme deficiency, viral infection, mitochondrial dysfunction). It centers on the phosphorylation of [[EIF2A]] by one of four kinases — [[HRI]] (mitochondrial/[[DELE1]]), [[PERK]] (ER), [[GCN2]] (amino acid), [[PKR]] (viral) — which inhibits global protein synthesis while selectively increasing the translation of specific mRNAs like [[ATF4]].
