# Filaggrin

**Filaggrin** (filament-aggregating protein) is a calcium-binding protein expressed in the granular layer of the epidermis. It aggregates keratin filaments into tight bundles as [[Keratinocyte|keratinocytes]] terminally differentiate into corneocytes, flattening the cells and contributing to the physical barrier. Its proteolytic products form a substantial part of the skin's natural moisturizing factor.

## Role in Barrier Function

- **Terminal differentiation.** Filaggrin expression marks the final stages of epidermal differentiation, alongside keratin 10; reduced filaggrin indicates impaired barrier maturation.
- **Barrier and hydration.** Loss-of-function FLG variants cause ichthyosis vulgaris and predispose to atopic dermatitis through barrier dysfunction and altered hydration.
- **Aging readout.** Aged epidermis shows reduced differentiation-marker expression; interventions that raise filaggrin are interpreted as improving barrier function.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Topical application of urolithin A activates mitophagy and enhances mitochondrial function to promote skin longevity|Topical urolithin A skin study]]
> In a full-thickness skin model reconstructed from an aged donor, 1 μM [[Urolithin A]] for 25 days increased filaggrin expression together with [[Ki67]] and keratin 10, and human biopsy RNA-seq showed upregulation of epidermis-development gene sets after 8 weeks of 1% topical urolithin A.
