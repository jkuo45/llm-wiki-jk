# IgE

**IgE** is the antibody isotype mediating allergy and anti-parasite immunity. [[Th2]] cytokines IL-4 and [[IL-13]] drive B-cell class switching to IgE via [[STAT6]] and GATA3; IgE arms mast cells and basophils through FcεRI, triggering histamine release on allergen crosslinking.

## Documents

- [[Th2]] — Th2-polarized CD4+ T cells produce the IL-4/IL-13 signals driving IgE switching.
- [[STAT6]] — transcription factor transducing IL-4/IL-13 receptor signals toward IgE production.
- [[IL-13]] — promotes IgE class switching in B cells alongside M2 macrophage polarization.
