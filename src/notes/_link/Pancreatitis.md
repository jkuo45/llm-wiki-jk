# Pancreatitis

**Pancreatitis** is inflammation of the pancreas. Acute pancreatitis ranges from mild edema to severe necrotizing disease with acinar-cell damage and systemic inflammation; chronic pancreatitis is characterized by persistent inflammation that can drive fibrosis and epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition.

## Sirtuin Axis

- **Acute pancreatitis**: [[SIRT1]] is protective — low expression is associated with disease; resveratrol protects against acute necrotizing pancreatitis via SIRT1-mediated p53/HSF1 deacetylation.
- **Chronic pancreatitis**: SIRT1 is significantly upregulated; absence of SIRT1 expression inhibition by miR-278 leads to inflammation-induced EMT.

## Clinical Relevance

SIRT1 plays a context-dependent dual role — protective in the acute phase via deacetylation of stress regulators, but pro-fibrotic/pro-EMT in the chronic phase. Sirtuin modulation timing is therefore critical.
