# Isoleucine

**Isoleucine** is one of the three [[Branched-Chain Amino Acids]] and an [[Essential Amino Acids|essential amino acid]] that must be obtained from the diet. It has recently emerged as a powerful regulator of metabolism and aging: dietary isoleucine correlates with body mass index in humans, and increased circulating isoleucine is associated with increased mortality risk. In contrast, isoleucine restriction consistently improves metabolic health and extends lifespan.

## Overview

Isoleucine is a ketogenic and glucogenic amino acid. Its catabolism (via transamination and branched-chain α-ketoacid dehydrogenase) yields acetyl-CoA and succinyl-CoA, which enter the [[TCA cycle]]. It is metabolized alongside [[Leucine]] and [[Valine]] by shared BCAA catabolic enzymes, but exerts distinct physiological effects.

## Metabolism and Signaling

Isoleucine, like the other BCAAs, is catabolized primarily in skeletal muscle. It contributes to the [[mTORC1]]-activating pool of amino acids, although it is a weaker mTORC1 activator than [[Leucine]]. In Western diet-fed mice, increasing dietary isoleucine promotes weight gain, fat accretion, glucose intolerance, and insulin resistance — effects that parallel the human epidemiology linking circulating isoleucine to obesity and metabolic disease.

## Isoleucine Restriction

Dietary isoleucine restriction (~66% reduction) consistently improves metabolic health across dietary contexts and age groups:

- Extends lifespan of both *Drosophila* and mice (~33% in male UM-HET3 mice)
- Reduces [[Frailty]] and increases [[Healthspan]]
- Rejuvenates aged mice at the molecular level
- Induces substantial [[Epigenome|epigenetic]] changes
- Reduces [[Cellular Senescence]] and [[SASP]]
- Improves [[Alzheimer's Disease]] outcomes in male mice

> [!important] Isoleucine Is an Exceptionally Powerful Modifier
> Among the individual amino acids, isoleucine is the most powerful single modifier of the hallmarks of [[Protein Restriction]] — recapitulating improvements in metabolic health, reduced senescence, reduced frailty, and lifespan extension.
