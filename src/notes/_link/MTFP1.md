# MTFP1

## Overview

MTFP1 (mitochondrial fission process 1, also known as MTP18) is an inner mitochondrial membrane protein that plays an essential role in mitochondrial division. Unlike the outer membrane fission machinery ([[DRP1]], [[MFF]], [[MID49]], [[MID51]]), MTFP1 operates at the inner membrane level to coordinate the final stages of mitochondrial constriction and division.

## Mechanism of Action

MTFP1 is required for the completion of mitochondrial fission after DRP1-mediated outer membrane constriction. While initially considered a dispensable IMM protein for mitochondrial division, its role in cardiac tissue has proven essential. MTFP1 likely coordinates inner membrane remodeling with outer membrane fission machinery to ensure complete organelle division.

## Physiological Function

MTFP1 is particularly critical for cardiac structure and function:

- Essential for maintaining mitochondrial network integrity in cardiomyocytes
- Required for proper mitochondrial distribution during cardiac development
- Contributes to energy metabolism in the heart through mitochondrial quality control

## Pathology

Mutations in MTFP1 cause severe cardiac disease:

- **Fatal dilated cardiomyopathy** — severe mutations lead to neonatal or early childhood death
- **Adult-onset dilated cardiomyopathy** — milder mutations present with progressive heart failure in adulthood

These findings highlight the non-redundant role of MTFP1 in cardiac mitochondrial dynamics and the heart's dependence on proper mitochondrial fission for function.
