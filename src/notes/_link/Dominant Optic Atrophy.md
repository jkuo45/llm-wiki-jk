### Overview
Dominant Optic Atrophy (DOA) is the most common inherited optic neuropathy, caused by mutations in [[OPA1]] (mostly missense). It produces progressive bilateral vision loss due to retinal ganglion cell degeneration.

### Mechanism
OPA1 mutations impair [[Inner Mitochondrial Membrane]] fusion and cause mitochondrial dysfunction, disrupting cristae architecture and energy production in neurons with high bioenergetic demand. The resulting failure of [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] selectively damages optic nerve axons.

### Severe Form
A severe "DOA plus" phenotype includes additional neurological features such as deafness and ataxia. DOA can overlap with [[Charcot-Marie-Tooth Disease Type 2A]], reflecting shared mitochondrial dynamics pathways. OPA1 processing also depends on [[YME1L]].
