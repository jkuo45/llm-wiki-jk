# 1-Methylnicotinamide

NNMT methylates nicotinamide to 1-methylnicotinamide, withdrawing nicotinamide from the NAMPT-dependent salvage pathway and thereby limiting NAD+ biosynthesis and indirectly modulating sirtuin activity (Szczęsny-Małysiak et al., 2026).
