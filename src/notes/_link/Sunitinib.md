# Sunitinib

[[Sunitinib]] is a multi-targeted receptor tyrosine-kinase inhibitor of [[VEGFR]], [[PDGFR]], and [[c-KIT]], approved for renal cell carcinoma, GIST, and pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors.

> [!info] Source: [[VEGFR]] (cross-link)
> Cited as a representative VEGFR-targeted antiangiogenic TKI alongside [[Pazopanib]].
