# Echinacea

**Asteraceae root extracts of *Echinacea pallida*, *Echinacea angustifolia* and *Echinacea purpurea*.**

## Cytotoxic activity (Tier 4: in vitro)

- Chicca et al. 2007 (PMID 17052874; *J Ethnopharmacol* 110:148–153): root hexanic extracts of the three species reduced viability of human pancreatic cancer (MIA PaCa-2) and colon cancer (COLO320) cells; *E. pallida* most active — IC50 46.41 µg/mL (MIA PaCa-2) and 10.55 µg/mL (COLO320); the extract induces apoptosis (caspase 3/7 up, nuclear DNA fragmentation).

## Interference with doxorubicin (Tier 4: in vitro)

- Huntimer et al. 2006 (PMID 17193302; *Chem. Biodivers.* 3:695–703): *E. angustifolia* root extract fractions — cynarine increased HeLa cell growth by 48–125% at 0.05–0.5 µM doxorubicin (p<0.01); chicoric acid increased MCF-7 growth by 10–15% at 0.5 µM doxorubicin (p<0.01).
- Miracle et al. 2020 (reported as cited; not verified in this extraction).

## Recorded observation

- Presence of Echinacea compounds (cynarin/chicoric acid) increased cancer cell growth in doxorubicin co-treatment; direct cytotoxic activity on cancer cells is confirmed for *E. pallida* root hexanic extract (IC50s above).
