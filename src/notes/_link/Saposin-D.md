# Saposin-D

**Saposin-D** (SapD) is a small lysosomal accessory protein belonging to the saposin family of sphingolipid activator proteins. It facilitates the hydrolysis of [[Ceramide]] by [[Acid ceramidase]] by extracting ceramide from the lysosomal membrane and presenting it to the enzyme's active site.

## Structure & Function

Saposin-D adopts a globular closed conformation or an open V-shaped form capable of lipid interactions. In its open configuration, SapD binds and solubilizes [[Ceramide]] from membranes, delivering it to [[Acid ceramidase]] for cleavage into [[Sphingosine]] and free fatty acids.

## Role in Sphingolipid Metabolism

> [!info] Source: Gebai et al., 2018, *Nat Commun* (PMID: 29692406)
> The crystal structure of [[Acid ceramidase]] revealed a hydrophobic surface surrounding the substrate-binding channel that serves as a membrane-attachment site where the enzyme accepts substrates facilitated by Saposin-D.
