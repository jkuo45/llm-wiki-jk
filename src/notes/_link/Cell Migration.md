# Cell Migration

**Cell migration** proceeds by leading-edge [[Actin]] polymerization, integrin adhesion ([[FAK Signaling]]), [[Myosin]] contraction, and trailing-edge release, steered by gradients of [[Hepatocyte Growth Factor|HGF]], [[FGF]], and chemokines. It underlies development, wound healing, immune recruitment — and cancer invasion when hijacked.

## Documents

- [[FAK Signaling]] — focal-adhesion kinase coordinates adhesion turnover during directed migration.
- [[Hepatocyte Growth Factor]] — scatter factor guiding epithelial migration via MET signaling.
