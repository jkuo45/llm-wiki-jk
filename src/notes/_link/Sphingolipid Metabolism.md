# Sphingolipid Metabolism

**Sphingolipid metabolism** is the ensemble of pathways that synthesize and degrade sphingolipids—[[Ceramide|ceramide]], [[Sphingomyelin|sphingomyelin]], [[Sphingosine|sphingosine]], and [[Sphingosine-1-phosphate|S1P]]. The [[Ceramide|ceramide]] ↔ [[Sphingosine|sphingosine]]/S1P **rheostat** governs cell fate: ceramide accumulation is generally pro-stress/pro-death, whereas sphingosine/S1P signaling is pro-survival.

## Pathways & Enzymes

- **Ceramide synthesis:** *de novo* ceramide synthase and [[Sphingomyelin|sphingomyelin]] hydrolysis via [[Acid Sphingomyelinase|sphingomyelinase (SMase)]].
- **Ceramide breakdown:** [[Acid ceramidase]] (ASAH1) cleaves ceramide into [[Sphingosine|sphingosine]] + free fatty acid; sphingosine is phosphorylated to [[Sphingosine-1-phosphate|S1P]].
- **Membrane crosstalk:** Free fatty acids liberated by [[Acid ceramidase|ACase]] feed membrane [[Phospholipid|phospholipid]] [[PUFA|PUFA]] enrichment (Lands cycle), creating a pro-[[Ferroptosis|ferroptotic]] profile—especially in [[Senescent Cells|senescent cells]] where ACase is over-expressed 5- to 20-fold.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex|Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile… (Soriano-Castell et al., 2026)]]
> The study repositions [[Sphingolipid Metabolism|sphingolipid metabolism]] as a upstream regulator of [[Ferroptosis]]: ACase over-expression in replicative senescence raises membrane PL-[[PUFA|PUFA]] content and ferroptosis sensitivity **independently of [[GPX4]]/[[Glutathione|GSH]] and [[Iron|iron]]**, by shrinking the ceramide pool and releasing free fatty acids for phospholipid PUFA remodeling.

## Connections

- [[Ceramide]] — Central hub; its flux sets the rheostat and (via ACase) membrane PUFA levels.
- [[Acid ceramidase]] — Key breakdown enzyme; newly identified ferroptosis regulator in senescence.
- [[Sphingosine]] / [[Sphingosine-1-phosphate]] — Pro-survival products of ceramide catabolism.
- [[Sphingomyelin]] — Ceramide consumer/reservoir; modestly altered by the ACase axis.
- [[Phospholipid]] / [[PUFA]] — Glycerophospholipids whose PUFA content is shaped by sphingolipid-derived free fatty acids.
- [[Ferroptosis]] — Downstream death modality governed by the ACase→ceramide→PUFA limb.
- [[GPX4]] / [[Glutathione]] / [[Iron]] — Classic ferroptosis regulators; the ACase axis acts independently of them.
- [[Senescent Cells]] — Where ACase over-expression manifests as pro-ferroptotic lipid remodeling.
- [[ACSL4]] — Convergent (distinct) enhancer of membrane PUFA-PLs and ferroptosis.
