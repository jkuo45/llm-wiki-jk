# cit-H3

**cit-H3** (citrullinated histone H3) is histone H3 deiminated on arginine residues by [[PAD4]] during [[NETosis]]. Loss of positive charge unravels chromatin for NET extrusion, and cit-H3 immunostaining — combined with extracellular DNA ([[Sytox Green]]) and MPO–DNA complexes — forms the diagnostic triad distinguishing NETs from generic necrotic DNA release.
