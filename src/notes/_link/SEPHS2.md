# SEPHS2

**Overview:** **SEPHS2** activates **[[Selenium]]** for incorporation into **[[Selenocysteine]]**. It converts selenide (derived from dietary **[[Selenium]]**) and ATP into **monoselenophosphate**.

## Mechanism

- Monoselenophosphate is the selenium donor in the **[[SepSecS]]**-catalyzed conversion of O-phosphoseryl-tRNA[Ser]Sec to Sec-tRNA[Ser]Sec.
- Thus SEPHS2 sits at the entry point of selenium into the amino acid, upstream of **[[PSTK]]** and SepSecS.

## Significance

SEPHS2 expression/output gates the whole **[[Selenoprotein]]** family; through this enzyme, selenium availability tunes selenoproteome capacity — a link relevant to aging and redox decline (see [[How Cysteine and Selenocysteine Guard Cells Against Aging and Oxidative Damage]]).
