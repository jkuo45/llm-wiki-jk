# Piceatannol

**Piceatannol** (3,3',4,5'-tetrahydroxystilbene) is a natural stilbenoid [[Polyphenols|polyphenol]] found in grapes, red wine, blueberries, and passion fruit. It is the hydroxylated analogue of [[Resveratrol|resveratrol]] and shares many of its biological activities.

## Sirtuin Activation

Piceatannol is a broad-spectrum [[STACs|sirtuin-activating compound (STAC)]]. According to the sirtuin literature summarized by Zhao et al. (2020), piceatannol activates [[SIRT1]], [[SIRT3]], and [[SIRT5]], making it one of the few natural compounds reported to stimulate several mammalian sirtuins simultaneously.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Sirtuins and their Biological Relevance in Aging and Age-Related Diseases]]
> Table 6 of Zhao et al. lists piceatannol among the activators of SIRT1, SIRT3, and SIRT5, positioning it as a pan-sirtuin natural activator alongside resveratrol.

Like [[Resveratrol|resveratrol]], piceatannol is studied for [[Anti-inflammatory|anti-inflammatory]], antioxidant, and [[Neuroprotection|neuroprotective]] properties and is investigated as a [[Caloric Restriction|caloric-restriction-mimetic]] agent.
