# Methionine

**Methionine** is one of the nine [[Essential Amino Acids|essential amino acids]] and the only sulfur-containing essential amino acid in mammals. It is the precursor of [[S-adenosylmethionine]] (SAM), the universal [[Methyl Donor|methyl donor]], placing it at the interface of diet, one-carbon metabolism, and the [[Epigenome]]. Dietary [[Methionine Restriction]] extends lifespan of many species and recapitulates many of the benefits of [[Protein Restriction]].

## Overview

Methionine is an endogenous metabolite involved in cellular energy metabolism, redox reactions, and biosynthetic pathways. Its cellular concentration reflects metabolic flux and energy status, and it participates as a substrate and intermediate in one-carbon metabolism, the methionine cycle, and transsulfuration. Intracellular levels are maintained through biosynthesis (in lower organisms), catabolism, and transport mechanisms, and its concentration serves as a metabolic signal regulating enzyme activity and gene expression.

## Role in One-Carbon Metabolism and the Epigenome

Methionine is adenylated to form [[S-adenosylmethionine|S-adenosyl-L-methionine]], the universal methyl donor used by DNA and [[Histone Methylation|histone]] methyltransferases. Dietary methionine deprivation therefore reduces methyl-donor availability, altering the [[Epigenome]]:

- Cells preserve heterochromatic stability through upregulation of [[H3K9me3|H3K9 mono-methylation]] in the liver during methionine depletion
- Methionine availability couples nutrient status to [[DNA Methylation]] and histone methylation programs
- In humans, strict vegan diets reduce blood methionine and improve glucose homeostasis

## Methionine Restriction

[[Methionine Restriction]] (MR) extends lifespan of many species and, when initiated early or late in life, reduces body weight, increases lean mass, improves mitochondrial function and lipid metabolism, reduces [[Frailty]], and extends lifespan of rodents. The metabolic response is partially dependent on [[FGF21]] and also inhibits [[mTORC1]]. Most MR studies require [[Cysteine]] depletion — since cysteine can be synthesized from methionine via transsulfuration — for the metabolic benefits to be observed.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - The-hallmarks-of-protein-and-amino-acid-restriction|The Hallmarks of Protein and Amino Acid Restriction]]
> Among the individual essential amino acids, methionine is one of the primary drivers of protein restriction benefits, alongside the [[Branched-Chain Amino Acids]]. In humans, sulfur amino acid restriction increased FGF21, reduced leptin, and reduced body weight.

## Clinical Significance

Altered methionine levels are observed in metabolic disorders, mitochondrial diseases, and nutritional deficiencies. Human studies link high circulating sulfur amino acids to cardiovascular and metabolic disease risk, while sulfur amino acid restriction improves biomarkers of metabolic health.
