# T-bet

**T-bet** (TBX21) is the lineage-defining transcription factor for Th1 cells: [[IL-12]]→STAT4 induces T-bet, which drives IFNγ production while suppressing [[Th2]] (GATA3) and Th17 fates. It also programs NK cytotoxicity and CD8 effector function; dysregulation skews allergy versus intracellular-pathogen defense.
