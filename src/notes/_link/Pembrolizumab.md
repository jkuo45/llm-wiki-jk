# Pembrolizumab

[[Pembrolizumab]] is a humanized IgG4 monoclonal antibody that blocks the [[PD-1]] receptor, releasing inhibitory signaling on T cells and restoring anti-tumor immunity. It is approved across many malignancies including [[non-small-cell lung cancer]] (NSCLC), melanoma, and others.

## Clinical Context

Pembrolizumab is a cornerstone of immune-oncology. In the fenbendazole literature it appears in a self-administration case report.

> [!warning] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> An 80-year-old female with advanced NSCLC on pembrolizumab monotherapy for nine months who added 1 g/day oral fenbendazole developed severe drug-induced liver injury (Naranjo score consistent); liver function recovered after fenbendazole discontinuation (56).
