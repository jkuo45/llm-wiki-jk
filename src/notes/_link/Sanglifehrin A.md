# Sanglifehrin A

## Overview

Sanglifehrin A (SfA) is a macrolide that binds cyclophilins including [[Cyclophilin D]] and potently inhibits the [[Mitochondrial Permeability Transition]], like [[Cyclosporine A]] but through a structurally distinct scaffold.

## Use as a Dissection Tool

Because SfA does not inhibit calcineurin, it blocks MPT-dependent necrosis without CsA's immunosuppressive confound. A death phenotype sensitive to both SfA and CsA but resistant to [[Necrostatin-1]] points to MPT-driven necrosis rather than [[RIPK3]]–[[MLKL]] [[Necroptosis]].
