# Homer1

Homer1 (Homer homolog 1, also known as [[Vesl-1]]) is a postsynaptic scaffold protein at excitatory synapses that links [[Glutamate|glutamate]] receptors to downstream signaling pathways.

## Role in PINK1-Mediated Synaptic Function

[[PINK1]] knockout [[Neuron|neurons]] exhibit reduced Homer1 expression, which correlates with a lower density of mature dendritic spines and excitatory synapses, as well as impaired [[Long-term potentiation|long-term potentiation]]. PINK1 maintains synaptic integrity by phosphorylating [[DRP1|Drp1]] at the S616 site, promoting the [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial fission]] necessary for their recruitment into dendritic spines. The reduction in Homer1 expression is a consequence of defective mitochondrial dynamics at synapses.
