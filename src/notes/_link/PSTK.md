# PSTK

**Overview:** In eukaryotes and archaea, **[[Selenocysteine]]** is synthesized *on its tRNA* rather than free in solution. After seryl-tRNA synthetase loads **[[Serine]]** onto tRNA[Ser]Sec, **PSTK** phosphorylates the serine side chain to **O-phosphoseryl-tRNA[Ser]Sec**.

## Mechanism

- PSTK uses ATP to add a phosphate, generating the phosphoseryl intermediate that **[[SepSecS]]** then converts to Sec using monoselenophosphate (from **[[SEPHS2]]**).
- This places PSTK immediately upstream of the selenium-incorporation step and upstream of the **[[UGA codon]]** recoding event.

## Significance

PSTK is essential for Sec biosynthesis; defects collapse the **[[Selenoprotein]]** pool and phenocopy other blocks in the Sec pathway.
