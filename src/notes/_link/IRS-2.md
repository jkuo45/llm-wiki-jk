# IRS-2

SIRT1 deacetylates insulin receptor substrate-2 (IRS-2), enabling its efficient tyrosine phosphorylation and downstream PI3K–Akt activation, which improves insulin signaling in peripheral tissues and counteracts insulin resistance in T2DM (Szczęsny-Małysiak et al., 2026).
