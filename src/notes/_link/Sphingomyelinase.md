# Sphingomyelinase

**Sphingomyelinases** (SMases) are the enzymes that cleave [[Sphingomyelin]] to release [[Ceramide]] and phosphocholine. They are the rate-controlling step of sphingomyelin catabolism and the point at which a structural membrane lipid becomes a signalling lipid — which is why they sit upstream of ceramide-driven death programs such as [[Eryptosis]] and [[Apoptosis]].

## Acid and neutral forms

| Form | Gene | pH optimum | Main sites | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acid sphingomyelinase (ASM, secretory and lysosomal forms) | *SMPD1* | 4.5–5.5 | [[Lysosome]], secretory vesicles | Zn²⁺-dependent; see [[Acid Sphingomyelinase]] |
| Neutral sphingomyelinase 2 (nSMase2) | *SMPD2* | ~7.0 | Golgi, endoplasmic reticulum | Mg²⁺-dependent; inhibited by GW4869 |
| Neutral sphingomyelinase 3 (nSMase3) | *SMPD3* | ~7.0 | Golgi, mitochondria | Mg²⁺-independent; implicated in mitochondrial ceramide and neurotoxicity |

Erythrocytes are unusual: having no ER and no de novo sphingolipid synthesis, their ceramide can only come from **hydrolysis of plasma-membrane sphingomyelin** by sphingomyelinases brought in from outside or released from leukocytes.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **Ceramide as the effector.** Sphingomyelin hydrolysis shifts the membrane into ceramide-rich microdomains, which reorganize signalling proteins and sensitize the cell to Ca2+.
- **Extrinsic death arm.** In red cells, sphingomyelin hydrolysis drives [[Fas]] oligomerization in [[Lipid Rafts|lipid rafts]], [[FADD]]/DISC assembly and [[Caspase-8]]/[[Caspase-3]] activation — "lipid-driven eryptosis" (ceramide → rafts → DISC → PS exposure).
- **Cigarette smoke.** Cigarette smoke extract acts on erythrocytes through a **neutral** sphingomyelinase: nSMase activity generates ceramide, caspase-8 then initiates the cascade, and p38 MAPK phosphorylation triggers Fas/FADD DISC assembly with caspase-8/-3 activation. Because this route does not raise cytosolic Ca2+, it is a Ca2+-independent eryptosis trigger.
- **Inflammation and infection.** Secreted SMases during sepsis, sepsis-related COVID-19, hyperosmotic shock, *Clostridium perfringens* epsilon toxin, platelet-activating factor and leukocyte acidic SMase secretion all converge on ceramide generation.
- **Pharmacology.** GW4869 inhibits neutral SMases and blocks ceramide formation and PS exposure in cigarette-smoke-extract-treated erythrocytes; D609 inhibits acidic SMase.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- Loss of *SMPD1* causes [[Niemann-Pick Disease]] type A/B and broader acid sphingomyelinase deficiency (see [[Acid Sphingomyelinase]]).
- Excess or mislocalised SMase activity contributes to ceramide-driven death in sepsis, CKD/uremia, malaria, cigarette smoking and oxidant injury, and to insulin resistance and neurotoxicity via neutral SMases.
- SMase activity in plasma is used as a biomarker of sepsis severity and is a pharmacologic target for cytoprotection.
