# Alveolar Macrophages

**Alveolar macrophages** are tissue-resident macrophages lining the lung alveolus. They clear particulates and pathogens and are key sources of inflammatory mediators in lung disease.

> [!info] In the DROSHA–AIM2 IPF study
> Both [[Drosha]] and [[AIM2]] protein were elevated in **alveolar macrophages** of [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis|IPF]] patients and bleomycin-injured mouse lung. DROSHA deficiency suppressed AIM2 inflammasome-dependent [[Caspase-1]] activation and [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]]/[[IL-18]] release in primary alveolar macrophages and [[Bone marrow-derived macrophages|BMDMs]], linking Microprocessor activity to lung inflammation.
