# Methionine Restriction

**Methionine restriction (MR)** is a dietary intervention that reduces intake of the [[Essential Amino Acids|essential]] sulfur-containing amino acid [[Methionine]]. MR extends lifespan of many species and, when initiated early or late in life, reduces body weight, increases lean mass, improves mitochondrial function, lipid metabolism, and [[Frailty]], and extends lifespan of rodents. The metabolic response is partially dependent on [[FGF21]] and also inhibits [[mTORC1]].

## Overview

Methionine is the precursor of [[S-adenosylmethionine]] (SAM), the universal [[Methyl Donor|methyl donor]]. Dietary methionine deprivation may impact physiology through changes in [[Histone Methylation]] due to decreased availability of methyl donors. Cells can preserve heterochromatic stability through upregulation of [[H3K9me3|H3K9 mono-methylation]] in the liver, and most MR studies require cysteine depletion (cysteine can be synthesized from methionine) for the metabolic benefits to be observed.

## Mechanisms

- **[[FGF21]] dependence**: The metabolic response to MR is partially dependent on FGF21, consistent with MR acting through the same endocrine axis as [[Protein Restriction]]
- **[[mTORC1]] inhibition**: MR inhibits mTORC1 signaling, coupling amino acid scarcity to reduced anabolic signaling
- **Methyl-donor effects**: Methionine is the precursor of SAM, the universal methyl donor; MR reduces methyl-donor availability, altering [[Histone Methylation]] and DNA methylation landscapes
- **Mitochondrial effects**: MR improves mitochondrial function and lipid metabolism

## Lifespan and Healthspan Evidence

MR extends lifespan in rodents across multiple studies:

- ~10% increase in female CB6F1 mice (Miller et al., 65–77% restriction)
- 7% increase in male CB6F1 mice (Sun et al., 93% restriction)
- 30–42% increase in male Fisher 344 rats (Orentreich et al.; Richie et al., 80% restriction)

In humans, sulfur amino acid restriction increased FGF21, reduced leptin, and reduced body weight; strict vegan diets reduce blood methionine and improve glucose homeostasis.

> [!tip] MR Recapitulates PR Benefits
> Among the individual essential amino acids, methionine restriction is one of the most effective at recapitulating the benefits of protein restriction, alongside [[Isoleucine]] and [[Valine]].
