# RNF168

**RNF168** is a ~580-residue RING finger E3 ubiquitin ligase (3q29) that amplifies the
ubiquitin signal at DNA double-strand breaks. It does not initiate the signal — it is
recruited second, by the ubiquitin that the first ligase, RNF8, has already laid
down — and it converts a small, local monoubiquitination event into a broad
K63-linked chromatin domain large enough to recruit the entire downstream repair
machinery.

## Domains

RNF168 is a protein of modular pieces, and each piece does one job:

- **N-terminal RING finger** (residues ~14–61) confers E3 activity. It works with the
  E2 enzymes UbcH5c (K48/K63-capable) and UBC13 (K63-specific).
- **Two MIU domains** (motif interacting with ubiquitin) bind ubiquitin and mediate
  recruitment to damaged chromatin, providing partial redundancy in how RILP-family
  style recruitment happens.
- **UDM1** (residues ~110–183), a high-affinity ubiquitin-binding module that has
  emerged as the key determinant of site specificity and of the signal amplification
  loop.

Structural work — 2024 cryo-EM of the H2A-K15-ubiquitinated nucleosome core particle
in complex with RNF168 (PDB 8TXV, 8TXW) plus NMR — showed that a canonical
ubiquitin-binding domain in full-length RNF168 engages both ubiquitin *and* the
nucleosome surface. Binding the substrate as well as the modifier is what explains how
RNF168 achieves site-specific, processive accumulation of ubiquitin conjugates rather
than diffuse modification.

## The RNF8-RNF168 cascade

> [!info] Mechanism
> The canonical sequence at a DSB is: [[ATM]] phosphorylates histone H2AX to
> [[γ-H2AX]]; [[MDC1]] binds γ-H2AX through its BRCT domains and amplifies the
> signal by recruiting more of the MRN complex; MDC1 then recruits RNF8, which
> writes initial ubiquitin on H2A/H2AX; that ubiquitin is the docking signal that
> recruits RNF168; RNF168 monoubiquitinates K13/K15 and both ligases together extend
> K63-linked chains; and the resulting ubiquitinated chromatin is the binding surface
> for [[53BP1]], [[BRCA1]] and the Fanconi anaemia complex. Each step is a
> concentration-amplification step, which is why a single break produces a visible
> focus rather than a diffuse signal.

The division of labour between the two ligases has been a matter of some revision.
RNF8 is not the only priming ligase reported — nucleotide-excision-repair-induced H2A
ubiquitination is RNF8-dependent but proceeds through distinct mechanisms, suggesting
a universal DSB-signalling framework with pathway-specific routes in. RNF168's own
site assignment was established by Mattiroli and colleagues, who identified
K13–K15 as the target and showed that mutation there does not impair the chain-forming
capacity of either ligase in vitro or in vivo.

## Downstream function

The ubiquitin domain RNF168 creates is read by two mutually antagonistic proteins that
determine repair pathway choice:

- **53BP1** reads H2AK15ub through its ubiquitin-dependent recruitment motif,
  cooperating with its Tudor-domain recognition of H4K20me2, and commits the break to
  [[Non-homologous End Joining]] by blocking DNA end resection.
- **BRCA1** is recruited to the same ubiquitin domain and favours
  homologous recombination in S/G2.

The balance is cell-cycle dependent: ATM/ATR phosphorylation of 53BP1 and BRCA1
counteracting activities tilt it toward resection in S/G2, whereas 53BP1 dominance in
G1 secures NHEJ. Loss of RNF168 therefore has a *double* phenotype — defective repair
from absent ubiquitin, and loss of 53BP1 recruitment, which in a BRCA1-deficient
background is not merely a defect but a route to PARP inhibitor resistance.

## Other functions and regulation

- **Interstrand cross-link repair.** RNF168 ubiquitinates FANCD2 and recruits the Fanconi
  anaemia complex, so it is required for the repair of cross-linked DNA, not only for
  free ends.
- **SUMOylation.** RNF168 is sumoylated by PIAS4 in response to DSBs, which promotes its
  recruitment to chromatin and is itself a checkpoint-regulated event.
- **PCNA binding.** A 2024 study separated RNF168's PCNA-binding function from its
  ubiquitin-dependent DSB function, indicating post-replication roles beyond the
  canonical damage response.
- **Patient genetics.** Biallelic RNF168 mutations cause a RIDDLE syndrome
  (radial ray defects, immunodeficiency, dysmorphism, learning difficulties) with a
  radiosensitive immunodeficiency and defective γ-H2AX and 53BP1 focus formation; this
  phenotype is rescued by exogenous RNF168 expression, which is direct human evidence
  that RNF168 sits upstream of 53BP1.

## Connections

- [[53BP1]] — RNF168 generates the H2AK15ub mark that 53BP1's UDR motif reads, and
  RNF168 loss abolishes 53BP1 focus formation; this is the direct output of the
  ligase's activity.
- [[γ-H2AX]] — the upstream phospho-mark whose binding by MDC1 positions the RNF8 and
  RNF168 recruitment that makes the whole ubiquitin cascade possible.
- [[MDC1]] — the mediator that both amplifies γ-H2AX signalling and physically
  recruits RNF8, whose ubiquitin product is what RNF168 in turn reads; MDC1 is the
  scaffold that makes the cascade sequential.
- [[Ubiquitination]] — RNF168 is a RING E3 acting with UbcH5c and UBC13, and its
  K63 chains are the archetypal example of ubiquitin functioning as a signalling
  scaffold read by WD40 and ubiquitin-binding-domain proteins rather than as a
  degradation tag.
- [[E3 Ubiquitin Ligase]] — the protein class RNF168 belongs to, here distinguished
  from the substrate-degradation ligases by the non-degradative fate of its chains.
- [[DNA Repair]] — RNF168 is required for both DSB repair and interstrand cross-link
  repair; its deficiency causes a radiosensitive human immunodeficiency syndrome.
- [[DNA Damage]] — the trigger; RNF168 is recruited specifically to damaged, γ-H2AX-
  marked chromatin rather than acting constitutively.
- [[BRCA1]] — recruited to the same ubiquitin domain as 53BP1, and the reason RNF168
  is a determinant of repair pathway choice; loss of RNF168 removes 53BP1 and with it
  the NHEJ commitment.
- [[Non-homologous End Joining]] — the pathway 53BP1 commits the cell to, and
  therefore the pathway whose failure follows from absent RNF168.
- [[Homologous Recombination]] — the competing pathway, favoured by BRCA1 in S/G2;
  RNF168's absence tilts the balance because the 53BP1 anti-resection function is
  lost with it.
- [[PARP inhibitors]] — mechanistically downstream in the clinic: 53BP1 loss restores
  end resection in BRCA1-deficient tumours, which is one route to PARP inhibitor
  resistance.
- [[SIRT6]] — the sirtuin whose H3K9 deacetylation at break-flanking chromatin enables
  53BP1 recruitment and NHEJ, placing chromatin deacetylation upstream of the same
  repair decision RNF168's ubiquitin domain informs.
- [[Cell Cycle]] — pathway choice is cell-cycle dependent, and the 53BP1/BRCA1 balance
  RNF168 sets the substrate for is itself phosphorylation-controlled.
