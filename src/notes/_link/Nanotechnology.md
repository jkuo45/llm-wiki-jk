# Nanotechnology

**Nanotechnology** in the pharmaceutical context involves engineering materials and carriers at the nanometer scale (1–1000 nm) to overcome the poor [[Bioavailability|bioavailability]], limited solubility, and rapid metabolism that limit the therapeutic use of many bioactive compounds such as [[Fisetin]].

Key nanoplatforms include polymeric [[Nanoparticles|nanoparticles]], [[Liposomes|liposomes]], solid lipid [[Nanoparticles|nanoparticles]], [[Nanoemulsions|nanoemulsions]] (including [[SNEDDS]]), [[Nanocrystals|nanocrystals]], and [[Cyclodextrins|cyclodextrin]] inclusion complexes.
