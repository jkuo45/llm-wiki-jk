# TBARS

**TBARS** (Thiobarbituric Acid Reactive Substances) is a colorimetric assay that quantifies [[Malondialdehyde]] (MDA) as a surrogate biomarker of [[Lipid Peroxidation]]. It is one of the oldest and most widely used methods for assessing oxidative damage in biological samples.

## Principle

The assay is based on the reaction of thiobarbituric acid (TBA) with MDA and other aldehydes under acidic, high-temperature conditions to form a pink chromophore (TBA₂-MDA adduct) with peak absorbance at 532 nm. The signal is read spectrophotometrically or fluorometrically (excitation 532 nm, emission 553 nm).

## Limitations

The TBARS assay is sensitive but not specific for MDA — TBA reacts with many aldehydes (4-HNE, acrolein, glyoxal), carbohydrates, and amino acids, leading to overestimation of lipid peroxidation. More specific methods (HPLC, LC-MS/MS for MDA or [[F2-Isoprostanes]]) are preferred for rigorous quantification, but TBARS remains useful for rapid, low-cost screening.

## Applications

- Screening for lipid peroxidation in plasma, tissue homogenates, and cell lysates
- Measuring oxidative stress in animal models of [[Ischemia-reperfusion Injury]], [[Neurodegenerative Diseases]], and [[Cardiotoxicity]]
- Validating ferroptosis in cell culture (alongside C11-BODIPY and LC-MS lipidomics)
