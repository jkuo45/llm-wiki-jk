# MMP-3

**Matrix metalloproteinase-3 (MMP-3)**, also known as stromelysin-1, is a zinc-dependent endopeptidase secreted as a component of the [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]]. It is a broad-spectrum ECM-degrading enzyme that promotes tissue remodelling, [[Epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition|EMT]] and cancer progression.

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

MMP-3 is a secreted zinc metalloproteinase whose catalytic domain bears the conserved HEXXHXXGXXH zinc-binding motif. Its substrate range is unusually broad: it degrades [[Collagen]] types III, IV, V, IX and X, fibronectin, laminin, proteoglycans and [[Elastin]], and—importantly—it proteolytically activates latent pro-MMPs (pro-MMP-1, -7, -8, -9, -13), positioning MMP-3 as an upstream "trigger" of collagenolysis cascades (PMID 10545505). Its expression is transcriptionally driven by the SASP transcription factors [[NF-κB]] and [[AP-1]] and restrained by tissue inhibitors of metalloproteinases ([[TIMP3]]). A common functional polymorphism (rs3025058, 5A/6A) modulates MMP-3 promoter activity and associates with arthritis and cancer risk.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

Senescent [[Fibroblasts]] and epithelial cells secrete MMP-3 as part of the pro-inflammatory, matrix-remodelling SASP. By activating collagenases and releasing matrix-bound growth factors, MMP-3 reshapes the [[Extracellular Matrix]] and potentiates paracrine senescence. A landmark finding is that MMP-3 can itself induce [[Epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition|EMT]] in mammary epithelial cells by cleaving E-cadherin and triggering mesenchymal reprogramming (Sternlicht et al., PMID 10545505), a mechanism by which senescent-cell-derived MMP-3 fosters a pre-neoplastic, invasive phenotype in neighboring epithelium. Through these actions MMP-3 contributes to the detrimental, pro-tumorigenic arm of chronic senescence in the [[Tumor Microenvironment]].

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

MMP-3 is elevated in [[Rheumatoid Arthritis]], osteoarthritis and invasive cancers, where it marks active ECM turnover and poor prognosis. It is implicated in arterial and skin ageing. Early broad MMP inhibitors failed clinically because of toxicity and lack of selectivity; current efforts target MMP-3's activation cascade and downstream [[TGFβ]] signaling rather than catalytic blockade.
