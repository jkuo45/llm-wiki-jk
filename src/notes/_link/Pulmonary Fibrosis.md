# Pulmonary Fibrosis

**Pulmonary fibrosis** is a chronic, progressive interstitial lung disease characterized by excessive deposition of extracellular matrix and scar tissue in the lungs, leading to impaired gas exchange and respiratory failure. The most common form is idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF), where the cause is unknown.

## Pathophysiology

Pulmonary fibrosis involves dysregulated wound healing following repetitive alveolar epithelial injury. Key processes include epithelial cell apoptosis, fibroblast proliferation and differentiation into myofibroblasts, and excessive collagen deposition. Mitochondrial dysfunction, including excessive [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial fission]] and impaired [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]], contributes to alveolar epithelial cell death and fibrotic remodeling.

## Clinical Features

Progressive dyspnea, dry cough, and restrictive ventilatory defects. Median survival for IPF is 3–5 years from diagnosis. Current treatments include antifibrotic agents (pirfenidone, nintedanib) and lung transplantation.

## Sirtuin Involvement (Wu et al., 2022)

Lung fibrosis (LF) accounts for ~45% of global deaths attributed to fibrosis. Sirtuins predominantly oppose fibrotic remodeling:

- **[[SIRT1]]**: Loss is involved in pathogenesis; activation promotes alveolar epithelial type 2 self-renewal/differentiation in IPF patients. Expression is controversial (increased in some IPF studies). Activation/overexpression attenuates lung fibrosis via canonical TGF-β1/p300 signaling.
- **[[SIRT3]]**: Deficiency within the aging lung promotes a fibrotic response via [[TGF-β1]]; deficiency promotes lung fibrosis via alveolar epithelial cell mitochondrial DNA damage/apoptosis; overexpression ameliorates asbestos-induced pulmonary fibrosis.
- **[[SIRT6]]**: Inhibits TGF-β-induced cellular senescence; inactivates TGF-β1/Smad2 signaling; prevents myofibroblast differentiation (TGF-β1/Smad2 + [[NF-κB]]); inhibits EMT during IPF (TGF-β1/Smad3).
- **[[SIRT7]]**: Most decreased in lung fibrosis fibroblasts; its decline exerts a profibrotic effect via Smad3 changes.
