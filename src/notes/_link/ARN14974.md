# ARN14974

**ARN14974** (also known as Compound 17a) is a potent benzoxazolone carboxamide inhibitor of intracellular [[Acid ceramidase]] (ACase, ASAH1) with an IC50 of 79 nM against recombinant human ACase. It was developed by Pizzirani et al. (2015) as a systemically active, brain-penetrant tool compound for studying ACase function *in vivo*.

## Pharmacological Properties

- **IC50:** 79 nM (human ACase)
- **Administration:** Systemically active (i.p. and i.v.)
- **Tissue distribution:** Reduces ACase activity in brain, liver, heart, lungs, and kidney
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Cmax 1768 ng/mL, t½ 458 min (i.p. 10 mg/kg in mice)
- **Tolerability:** Well tolerated at tested doses

## Role in Ferroptosis Research

> [!info] Source: Pizzirani et al., 2015, *Angew Chem Int Ed Engl*; Soriano-Castell et al., 2026
> ARN14974 belongs to the same chemical series as [[ARN14794]], which was specifically used by Soriano-Castell et al. (2026) to demonstrate that [[Acid ceramidase]] inhibition protects [[Senescent Cells|senescent cells]] from [[Ferroptosis]].
