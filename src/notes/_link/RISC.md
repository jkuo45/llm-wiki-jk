# RISC

The **RNA-induced silencing complex (RISC)** is the effector machinery of small-RNA silencing. It is centered on an **[[Argonaute]]** protein loaded with a guide [[MicroRNA|miRNA]] or siRNA, which directs sequence-specific translational repression or endonucleolytic cleavage of target mRNAs.

## Assembly and function

1. [[Dicer]] produces a miRNA/siRNA duplex from pre-miRNA (or long dsRNA).
2. One strand (the guide) is loaded onto [[Argonaute]]; the passenger strand is discarded.
3. RISC scans the transcriptome; partially complementary targets are translationally repressed/deadenylated (canonical miRNA mode), fully complementary targets are cleaved (siRNA/slicer mode, e.g., Ago2).

> [!note] Position in the biogenesis pipeline
> RISC is the cytoplasmic terminus of the [[MicroRNA]] pathway: nucleus ([[Microprocessor complex|Microprocessor]] → pre-miRNA) → [[Exportin-5]] export → [[Dicer]] → RISC → gene silencing.
