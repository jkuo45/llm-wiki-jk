# IKKalpha

**IKKα** (IKK1; gene *CHUK*, conserved helix-loop-helix ubiquitous kinase) is one of the two catalytic subunits of the [[IKK]] complex — the central regulatory node of [[NF-κB]] signaling. Together with its sister kinase [[IKKbeta]] and the regulatory scaffold [[NEMO]] (IKKγ), it forms the signalosome that responds to virtually every inflammatory and stress stimulus.

## Mechanism

- **Canonical NF-κB**: within the IKKα/IKKβ/NEMO heterocomplex, IKKα contributes to phosphorylation of the inhibitor IκBα, triggering its ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation and freeing NF-κB (RelA/p50) to enter the nucleus. Upstream stimuli include [[TNFα]], [[Interleukin 1β]], LPS, and DNA damage.
- **Non-canonical NF-κB**: released from NEMO, IKKα forms **homodimers** that — activated by NIK downstream of CD40/BAFF/LTβR — phosphorylate p100, processing it into p52:RelB. This arm controls lymphoid organogenesis, B-cell survival, and development.
- **Beyond NF-κB**: IKKα has nuclear roles independent of IκB — histone H3 Ser10 phosphorylation during mitosis and transcriptional regulation at specific promoters; the IKK complex also phosphorylates pro-apoptotic Bcl-2 family members ([[Puma]], [[Bad]]), linking inflammatory signaling to cell-survival decisions.

## Disease relevance

Genetic ablation and kinase-dead studies show IKKα's roles are strongly developmental and tissue-specific: it is required for limb and skeletal patterning and epidermal differentiation, and its non-canonical arm is implicated in B-cell malignancies. As a druggable kinase it sits upstream of the same inflammatory programs [[SIRT1]] restrains by deacetylating RelA/p65.

## Connections

- [[IKKbeta]] — the sister catalytic subunit; dominant canonical IκB kinase
- [[IKK]] — the multi-subunit complex both catalytic subunits compose
- [[IKKε]] — the related non-canonical IKK family kinase (TBK1 partner)
- [[NEMO]] — the regulatory scaffold that assembles the heterocomplex
- [[NF-κB]] — the transcription factor both canonical and non-canonical arms serve
- [[TNFα]] — the upstream death/survival ligand context
- [[Puma]] / [[Bad]] — pro-apoptotic substrates phosphorylated by the IKK complex
- [[SIRT1]] — restrains the same NF-κB program by deacetylating RelA/p65
