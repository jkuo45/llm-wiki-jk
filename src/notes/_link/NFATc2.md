# NFATc2

**NFATc2 (nuclear factor of activated T-cells 2, NFAT1)** is a calcium/calcineurin-responsive transcription factor that regulates immune responses, and in the heart, pathological growth and gene programs associated with hypertrophy.

## Sirtuin Regulation

- **[[SIRT2]]** deacetylates NFATc2, attenuating agonist-induced cardiac hypertrophy.
- Loss of SIRT2 reduces [[AMPK]], promoting aging-related and angiotensin II-induced hypertrophy.

## Physiological Function

NFATc2 integrates calcium/calcineurin signaling into transcriptional responses. Its deacetylation by SIRT2 dampens pro-hypertrophic transcriptional activity in cardiomyocytes.

## Clinical Relevance

NFAT signaling is a hallmark of pathological cardiac remodeling. SIRT2-mediated NFATc2 deacetylation is a protective node against cardiac hypertrophy.
