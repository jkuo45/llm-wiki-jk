# Epilepsy

**Epilepsy** is a chronic neurological disorder defined by recurrent, unprovoked seizures resulting from abnormal, excessive, or synchronous neuronal activity. Excitotoxicity, mitochondrial dysfunction, and disrupted circadian rhythms contribute to seizure generation and neuronal damage.

## Sirtuin Axis

- **[[SIRT1]]**: Therapeutic target to rescue circadian rhythm genes; the SIRT1 agonist CAY10602 is neuroprotective.
- **[[SIRT3]]**: Protects against kainic acid excitotoxicity via miR-134-5p inhibition.
- **[[SIRT5]]**: Deficiency increases mortality and seizure severity, indicating a neuroprotective role.

## Clinical Relevance

Sirtuin activators may limit excitotoxic neuronal loss and normalize circadian signaling in epilepsy. SIRT1/SIRT3/SIRT5 constitute a neuroprotective axis against seizure-related damage.
