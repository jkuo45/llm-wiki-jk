# Flubendazole

[[Flubendazole]] is a benzimidazole anthelmintic structurally related to [[Fenbendazole]], [[Mebendazole]], and [[Albendazole]]. Spagnuolo et al. reported that flubendazole inhibits microtubule function through a mechanism distinct from vinca alkaloids and displays preclinical activity in leukemia and myeloma models.

## Relevance to Fenbendazole

> [!info] Source: [[Fenbendazole as a Potential Anticancer Drug]]
> Flubendazole is cited as a related benzimidazole for which synergism with microtubule-targeting agents has been reported, in contrast to fenbendazole which showed only additive toxicity with docetaxel in EMT6 cells (Duan et al. 2013).
