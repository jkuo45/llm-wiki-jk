# EP300

**[[EP300]]** (E1A binding protein p300, commonly **p300**) is a [[Histone Acetyltransferase]] and transcriptional coactivator encoded by the *EP300* gene. Paralog of [[CBP]] (CREBBP); the two are often jointly referenced as p300/CBP.

## Autophagy Brake (Spermidine Target)

[[EP300]] normally keeps cytoplasmic [[Autophagy]]-related proteins (ATG5, ATG7, ATG12, [[LC3]]) acetylated and inactive. [[Spermidine]] inhibits [[EP300]] (Pietrocola 2015), driving ATG hypoacetylation and mTOR-independent autophagic flux — the primary geroprotective mechanism in the Rzeski four-compound framework ([[_document_ - Natural Bioactive Compounds Spermidine Fisetin Berberine Urolithin A]]).

## Broader Roles

- Transcriptional coactivation via histone acetylation (H3K9ac and others — cf. [[H3K9ac]]); recruited by [[p53]], [[NF-kB]], nuclear receptors.
- Acetylates non-histone substrates including [[p53]], tuning apoptosis/senescence decisions.
- Functional opposition to deacetylases such as [[SIRT1]] at shared promoters (e.g. [[AR]], [[BRCA1]], [[EZH2]] loci).
