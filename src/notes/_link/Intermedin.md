# Intermedin

**Intermedin** (adrenomedullin 2; AM2) is a calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) superfamily member that signals through the calcitonin receptor-like receptor (CLR) with receptor activity-modifying proteins (RAMPs). It is expressed in the cardiovascular system, kidney, gastrointestinal tract, and central nervous system, and exerts vasodilatory, cardioprotective, and renal-protective effects. Intermedin is processed from preprointermedin into multiple bioactive forms, with amidated peptides being the major circulating species.

## Intermedin and the AMPK/SIRT3 axis

Intermedin enhances mitochondrial bioenergetic capacity via [[AMPK]]/[[SIRT3]]-mediated [[Cristae|cristae]] remodeling in [[Vascular Calcification|vascular calcification (VC)]] models[314]. Because VC — a hallmark of advanced chronic kidney disease — is driven by mitochondrial dysfunction and osteogenic transdifferentiation of vascular smooth muscle cells, intermedin's SIRT3-dependent improvement of cristae architecture and oxidative phosphorylation capacity opposes the calcific phenotype, positioning it as a candidate therapeutic peptide for VC.

## Clinical relevance

Peptide hormones such as intermedin offer an alternative to small-molecule SIRT3 activators: systemic or targeted delivery could engage the AMPK/SIRT3 axis to restore vascular mitochondrial function in uremic calcific vasculopathy.
