# HEK 293

**HEK 293** is a human embryonic kidney cell line commonly used for transfection, recombinant protein expression, and protein-protein interaction studies (e.g., co-immunoprecipitation).

> [!info] In the DGCR8 study
> HEK 293 cells were used for epitope-tagged [[Drosha]]/[[DGCR8]] co-immunoprecipitation, showing an RNA-independent interaction, and for in vitro pri-miR-27a cleavage assays confirming the functional Microprocessor. DGCR8/Drosha knockdown here phenocopied the [[HeLa Cells|HeLa]] results (reduced pre-/mature miRNA, pri-miRNA accumulation).
