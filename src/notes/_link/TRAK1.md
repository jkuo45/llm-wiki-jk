# TRAK1

TRAK1 (Trafficking kinesin-binding protein 1, also known as [[OIP106]]) is a mitochondrial transport adaptor protein that forms a complex with [[OPTN|Optineurin (OPTN)]] and [[KIF5B]] for proper anterograde axonal mitochondrial transport.

## Role in Neurodegeneration

The OPTN/[[TRAK1]]/[[KIF5B]] complex is critical for axonal mitochondrial distribution. Overexpression of OPTN/[[TRAK1]]/[[KIF5B]] not only prevents neurodegeneration induced by OPTN truncation but also protects against damage from high intraocular pressure while promoting optic nerve regeneration. OPTN directly interacts with TRAK1 and KIF5B in a C-terminal-dependent manner to stabilize them for proper axonal transport.
