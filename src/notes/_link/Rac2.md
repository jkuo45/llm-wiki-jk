# Rac2

Rac2 is a hematopoietic small GTPase of the [[Rac GTPase|Rac]] family required for [[NADPH Oxidase]] function in [[Neutrophils]]. PMA- and microbe-induced NET release is suppressed in Rac2-deficient neutrophils, placing Rac2 upstream of the [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] burst that drives [[Suicidal NETosis]] (Lim et al. 2011; Gavillet et al. 2018).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Rac2 deficiency phenocopies [[Chronic Granulomatous Disease]] neutrophils, which lack NADPH oxidase function and fail to produce ROS-dependent NETs (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
