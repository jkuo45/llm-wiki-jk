# Intestinal Stem Cells (ISCs)

## Overview

**Intestinal stem cells** (ISCs) are LGR5+ stem cells located at the base of intestinal crypts that drive continuous epithelial renewal of the intestinal lining. They are among the most rapidly dividing stem cell populations in the body and are particularly vulnerable to [[Mitochondrial DNA|mtDNA]] mutations and [[NAD+]] depletion.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Mitochondrial Drivers Stem Cell Aging Inflammaging Bautista 2026]]
> A high burden of mtDNA mutations reduces LGR5+ cell function and organoid-forming capacity, mediated by ATF5-driven [[Mitochondrial Unfolded Protein Response|UPR^mt^]] activation, and is reversible with [[NMN]] supplementation.

## Role in Aging

### mtDNA Mutations and ISC Function

- High burden of [[Mitochondrial DNA|mtDNA]] mutations reduces LGR5+ cell function and organoid-forming capacity
- Phenotype is mediated by [[ATF4|ATF5]]-driven [[Mitochondrial Unfolded Protein Response|UPR^mt^]] activation
- Reversible with [[Nicotinamide Mononucleotide|NMN]] supplementation, which restores ISC regenerative potential

### Mitochondrial Age and Stem Cell Heterogeneity

> [!tip] Mitochondrial Age as a Fate Determinant
> Mitochondrial age itself — rather than mtDNA mutation load — can define functionally distinct stem cell subpopulations. Selective retention/inheritance of older mitochondria elevates α-ketoglutarate and promotes TET-dependent epigenetic remodeling and niche regeneration via Paneth cell formation.

- **ISC^mito-O^** subpopulation: selectively inherits aged mitochondria with enhanced α-ketoglutarate production
- Promotes TET-dependent epigenetic remodeling and tissue regeneration
- This demonstrates that mitochondrial heterogeneity partitions stem cell states and regenerative trajectories

### NAD+ Depletion

- [[NAD+]] depletion compromises ISC function
- [[Nicotinamide Riboside|NR]] and [[NMN]] supplementation can rejuvenate aged gut adult stem cells
