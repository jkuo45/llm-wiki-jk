# Translocase of the Inner Mitochondrial Membrane

The translocase of the inner mitochondrial membrane (TIM complex) translocates imported precursor proteins across or into the [[Mitochondria|inner mitochondrial membrane]] and delivers them to the [[Mitochondrial Matrix]]. Working downstream of the [[Translocase of the Outer Mitochondrial Membrane]], TIM completes the import of nuclear-encoded mitochondrial proteins bearing [[Mitochondrial Targeting Sequence|mitochondrial targeting sequences]].

According to [[PINK1]], PINK1 passes through the TIM complex during import. Successful translocation enables processing by [[Mitochondrial Processing Peptidase]] and [[PARL]], leading to PINK1 degradation and suppression of [[Mitophagy]]. Loss of membrane potential arrests import at this stage, stabilizing PINK1 on the outer membrane to activate [[Parkin]]-mediated mitophagy.

The TIM complex is thus an essential component of the mitochondrial import machinery that gates PINK1 signaling.
