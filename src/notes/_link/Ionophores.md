# Ionophores

Ionophores such as A23187, nigericin and [[Ionomycin]] raise cytosolic calcium in [[Neutrophils]]. They trigger [[Suicidal NETosis]] through a poorly characterised mechanism independent of [[Neutrophil Elastase]], [[Myeloperoxidase]] and [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] (Neeli and Radic 2013; Kenny et al. 2017), and activate [[PAD4]]-dependent histone citrullination that is absent with PMA stimulation.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> A cytosolic calcium spike induced by ionophores activates [[PAD4]] in neutrophils; PMA does not raise cytosolic calcium sufficiently to do so (Neeli et al. 2008; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
