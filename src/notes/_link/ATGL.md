# ATGL

**ATGL** (adipose triglyceride lipase, encoded by *PNPLA2*) is the first and rate-limiting enzyme of catabolic lipolysis, hydrolyzing triacylglycerol in lipid droplets to diacylglycerol. Its activity determines the rate of free fatty acid release from adipose tissue and therefore systemic lipid mobilization.

## Regulation by Sirtuins

- [[SIRT1]] promotes lipolysis in white adipocytes partly through FOXO1-dependent induction of ATGL expression, enhancing triglyceride hydrolysis.
- [[SIRT6]] promotes adipose lipolysis and prevents ectopic lipid accumulation through FOXO1-dependent induction of ATGL, protecting against lipotoxic insulin resistance.
- Together with [[HSL]] (hormone-sensitive lipase) and [[MGL]] (monoacylglycerol lipase), ATGL governs the stepwise mobilization of stored fat used for energy and thermogenesis.
