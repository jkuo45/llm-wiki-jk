# GSK840

**GSK840** (GSK'840) is a small-molecule ATP-competitive inhibitor of [[RIPK3]] kinase activity, developed alongside [[GSK872]] as a pharmacological tool to block [[Necroptosis]]. It is used preclinically to test RIPK3 dependence of a death phenotype, either alone or in combination with [[Necrostatin-1]] ([[RIPK1]]) and [[Necrosulfonamide]] ([[MLKL]]) inhibitors to dissect the necrosome rung by rung.

> [!warning] Kinase-dead rerouting caveat
> As with all RIPK3 kinase inhibitors, kinase-dead RIPK3 can still act as an adaptor that reroutes signaling to [[Apoptosis]]; GSK840 rescue therefore needs death-mode controls rather than being taken as definitive proof of necroptosis.
