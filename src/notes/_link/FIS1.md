## Overview

FIS1 (mitochondrial fission 1 protein) is an outer [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] membrane protein that serves as a receptor for [[DRP1]] (dynamin-related protein 1), recruiting it to the mitochondrial surface to drive [[Mitochondrial Fission|mitochondrial fission]]. It is a key component of the mitochondrial fission machinery and plays important roles in [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]], [[Apoptosis]], and [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial dynamics]].

## Role in Cellular Senescence

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Mitochondrial dysfunction in cellular senescence a bridge to neurodegenerative disease]]
> In senescent cells, FIS1 expression is downregulated, contributing to an elongated and interconnected mitochondrial morphology that impairs mitophagy. Knockdown of FIS1 leads to a senescent morphology with increased SA-β-gal activity, while reintroducing FIS1 reduces SA-β-gal activity. The effect on senescence is mediated through OPA1, as OPA1 knockdown reverses the senescent phenotype in FIS1-deficient cells.

## Connections

- [[DRP1]] — FIS1 recruits DRP1 to mitochondria to execute fission; both are downregulated in senescence
- [[OPA1]] — Mitochondrial fusion protein that opposes FIS1/DRP1-mediated fission; OPA1 knockdown rescues senescence in FIS1-depleted cells
- [[Mitophagy]] — Mitochondrial fission via FIS1 is a prerequisite for mitophagy; reduced FIS1 impairs mitophagy
- [[MFN1]] — Mitofusin 1 promotes fusion, functionally opposing FIS1 action in mitochondrial dynamics
- [[Alzheimer's Disease]] — FIS1 expression changes in favor of fission in AD brain, unlike the elongation seen in senescence
- [[Mitochondrial Fission]] — FIS1 is a core OMM adaptor for DRP1-mediated fission
- [[MFF]] — Fellow DRP1 recruiter; works with FIS1, MID49, MID51
- [[MID49]] / [[MID51]] — Additional DRP1-recruiting adaptors
