# DPPH

**DPPH** (1,1-diphenyl-2-picryl-hydrazyl) is a stable free radical used in the [[DPPH]] assay to measure the free radical scavenging activity of [[Antioxidant|antioxidants]]. The assay measures the hydrogen-donating ability of antioxidants by monitoring the decrease in absorbance at 518 nm as DPPH• is reduced to DPPH-H.

## DPPH Assay

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - berrougui2009_resveratrol_as_atheroprotective_compound|Berrougui et al., Atherosclerosis 2009]]
> The DPPH assay measures the hydrogen-donating ability of antioxidants over a relatively short period. Resveratrol (0–50 µM) significantly reduced DPPH free radical content in a concentration-dependent manner (r² = 0.96, p = 0.0032). At identical concentrations, resveratrol and vitamin E exhibited equivalent DPPH scavenging activity, with over 50% of activity observed in the first 30 minutes.

- DPPH is dissolved in ethanol at 0.1 mM
- Absorbance is measured at 518 nm
- The decrease in absorbance reflects the reduction of DPPH• to DPPH-H
- Results are expressed as percentage of remaining DPPH or antioxidant activity (AA%)

## Interpretation

- Lower absorbance = higher antioxidant activity
- IC50 and IC20 values indicate the concentration needed for 50% and 20% inhibition
- Both [[Resveratrol]] and [[Vitamin E]] showed rapid scavenging kinetics
