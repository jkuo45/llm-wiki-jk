# SAT1

**SAT1** (spermidine/spermine N1-acetyltransferase 1), also called **SSAT**, is the rate-limiting enzyme of polyamine catabolism. It is a transcriptional target of the tumor suppressor [[p53]] and a positive regulator of [[Ferroptosis]]. SAT1 induction promotes lipid peroxidation and iron-dependent cell death, acting in concert with the lipoxygenase [[ALOX15]] (ALOX-15). It provides a p53-controlled route to ferroptosis that is independent of the [[System Xc-]]–[[GPX4]] axis.

## Structure & Biochemistry

SAT1 is a cytosolic acetyltransferase that transfers acetyl groups from acetyl-CoA to spermidine and spermine, generating N1-acetylspermidine/spermine, which are then exported and oxidized by polyamine oxidase—producing H₂O₂ and downstream ROS. SAT1 is tightly regulated at the level of mRNA stability and protein degradation, making it a rapid-response node to stress signals.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathway

Genotoxic and oxidative stress activates [[p53]], which transcriptionally induces SAT1. Elevated SAT1 remodels the polyamine pool and increases generation of [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]], sensitizing membrane phospholipids to peroxidation. SAT1-driven ferroptosis is closely tied to [[ALOX15]] expression: ALOX15 oxygenates polyunsaturated phospholipid substrates, and SAT1 amplifies the oxidative load. This p53–SAT1–ALOX15 axis complements the better-known p53–SLC7A11 (system Xc-) suppression route to ferroptosis (PMID: 25799957).

> [!info] Two arms of p53 ferroptosis
> p53 promotes ferroptosis through (1) repression of [[SLC7A11]]/system Xc-, and (2) activation of the SAT1–ALOX15 lipid-peroxidation axis. Both converge on [[Lipid Peroxidation]] but originate from distinct metabolic programs.

## Physiological Function

Polyamine catabolism is essential for cellular homeostasis, proliferation, and response to hypertrophic stress (SAT1 is prominent in cardiac remodeling). Its ferroptotic role links amino-acid/nitrogen metabolism to iron-dependent cell death.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

SAT1 expression correlates with sensitivity to ferroptosis inducers and with p53-driven tumor suppression. Modulating SAT1 offers a strategy to sensitize resistant cancers to ferroptosis without solely relying on cystine-deprivation. Its partnership with [[ALOX15]] suggests lipoxygenase inhibitors might counteract this axis.
