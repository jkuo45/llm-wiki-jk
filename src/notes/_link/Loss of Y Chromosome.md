# Loss of Y Chromosome

Mosaic loss of the Y chromosome ([[Loss of Y Chromosome|mLOY]]) in hematopoietic cells is the most common somatic mutation in men — detectable in 40% of 70-year-olds and 57% of 93-year-olds — and the strongest male-specific systems counterpart to the estrogen hub: pathogenic gain with age rather than protective loss at menopause (the LOY cliff mirrors the estrogen cliff).

## Prevalence & Risk Factors

- Age-exponential rise from ~60y; smoking dose-response (reversible after quit); inherited genomic-instability predisposition; co-occurrence with clonal hematopoiesis (Thompson Nature 2019; Nat Rev Genet 2025).
- LOY blood >17%: HR 1.41 all-cause, 1.49 cardiovascular death, 2.65 fatal MI with profibrotic plasma OPG/MMP-12/GDF-15/HB-EGF/resistin (LURIC 2025); LOY-blood HR 1.91 mortality, 5.5y vs 11y survival (Forsberg Nat Genet 2014).

## Mechanism

- Y-deficient cardiac macrophages overactivate TGFβ1/galectin-3 signaling → fibroblast proliferation, matrix excess, cardiac/pulmonary/renal fibrosis; TGFβ1 blockade rescues; Uty disruption phenocopies (Sano Science 2022; Nat Cardiovasc Res 2024).
- ~500 autosomal genes dysregulated in LOY leukocytes, cell-type-specific (Dumanski 2021); loss of [[UTY]]/KDM5D epigenetic regulation → immune evasion.
- T-cell exhaustion with PD-1 sensitivity enables bladder-cancer growth (Abdel-Hafiz Nature 2023); impaired senescent-cell clearance → renal disease (Sci Transl Med 2025).

## Disease Links

- Cardiovascular/fibrosis/HF mortality, atherosclerotic events, aortic stenosis, CKD, Alzheimer's disease, solid + hematologic cancers, macular degeneration, diabetes.
- Male LOY-AD, LOY-CKD, LOY-coronary associations replicate across UK Biobank, ULSAM, PIVUS, LURIC cohorts.

## Male-Counterpart Framing

- Breadth rivals E2→ER (heart, kidney, lung, brain, cancer, immunity) with opposite valence: E2 protective hub lost at menopause; LOY pathogenic clone gained with age.
- Compounds single-X vulnerability: males losing [[UTY]] backup plus [[KDM6A]] LOF lose more than females with X-X compensation (see [[EXITS]], [[UTY]], [[DDX3X]]).
- Interacts with [[Testosterone]]→[[Androgen Receptor|AR]] axis via shared inflammatory/mTOR terrain; [[Inflammation]] and smoking amplify LOY cardiovascular harm.
