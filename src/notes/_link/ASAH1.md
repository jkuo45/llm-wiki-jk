# ASAH1

**ASAH1** (N-acylsphingosine amidohydrolase 1) is the gene encoding [[Acid ceramidase]] (ACase, aCDase), located on chromosome 8p22. It spans approximately 30 kb across 14 exons.

## Function

The ASAH1 gene product, [[Acid ceramidase]], is a lysosomal hydrolase that catalyzes the cleavage of [[Ceramide]] into [[Sphingosine]] and a free fatty acid, controlling the ceramide ↔ [[Sphingosine-1-phosphate]] rheostat.

## Clinical Significance

- Loss-of-function mutations cause [[Farber disease]] (severe, <5% activity) and [[Spinal muscular atrophy with progressive myoclonic epilepsy|SMA-PME]] (milder, ~5–30% activity)
- Over-expression in [[Glioblastoma]], [[Melanoma]], [[Prostate Cancer]], and other malignancies confers poor prognosis
- [[Acid ceramidase|ACase]] is over-expressed 5- to 20-fold in [[Replicative Senescence|replicatively senescent]] cells, driving a pro-[[Ferroptosis|ferroptotic]] lipid profile
