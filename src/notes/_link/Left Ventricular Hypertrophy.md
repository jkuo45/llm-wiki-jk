### Overview
Left Ventricular Hypertrophy (LVH) is the thickening or enlargement of the left ventricular wall, often in response to pressure overload. It is associated with altered mitochondrial dynamics: increased fission protein [[DRP1]] and reduced fusion protein [[MFN2]].

### Mechanism and Therapy
The shift toward excessive fission disrupts [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] and contributes to cardiac dysfunction. Inhibition of fission with [[Mdivi-1]] ameliorates LVH by modulating [[Mitophagy]] and angiogenesis, restoring a healthier balance of dynamics.

### Functional Significance
These findings position mitochondrial dynamics as both a biomarker and therapeutic target in cardiomyopathies, linking the fission/fusion machinery to cardiac remodeling.
