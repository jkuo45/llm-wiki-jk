# Lovastatin

Lovastatin is a statin, a competitive inhibitor of HMG-CoA reductase and a cholesterol-lowering drug. Beyond its canonical lipid-lowering action (related to the broader class of [[Statins]]), Lovastatin has been identified in the vault as an [[SHP2]] agonist.

According to [[Parkin]], Lovastatin promotes [[Parkin]] activation through its agonism of SHP2. SHP2 dephosphorylates Parkin at Tyr143, unlocking Parkin's E3 ubiquitin ligase activity and thereby facilitating [[Mitophagy]]. This positions Lovastatin as a potential pharmacological tool to engage the PINK1/Parkin mitophagy pathway via SHP2-dependent dephosphorylation.

The finding expands the therapeutic relevance of statins beyond cardiovascular indications, suggesting a role in modulating mitochondrial quality control through phosphatase signaling.
