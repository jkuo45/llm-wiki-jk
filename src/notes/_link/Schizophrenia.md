# Schizophrenia
Schizophrenia is a concept related to [[Adrenochrome]] and catecholamine metabolism.
Schizophrenia is a serious mental disorder in which people interpret reality abnormally. It may result in some combination of hallucinations, delusions, and extremely disordered thinking and behavior that impairs daily functioning.
In stem cell research, [[Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells]] (iPSCs) have been generated from the peripheral blood mononuclear cells of schizophrenia patients. Studies have found that these patient-derived iPSCs exhibit higher and more severe inflammatory responses compared to normal iPSCs, suggesting a role for the immune system and neuroinflammation in the etiology of the disorder.
**[[Schizophrenia]]** is a chronic and severe mental disorder that affects how a person thinks, feels, and behaves.
## COMT and Schizophrenia
The [[COMT]] [[Val158Met]] [[SNP]] has been extensively studied in relation to [[Schizophrenia]]. The **Fast [[COMT]] (Val/Val)** genotype is sometimes associated with a slightly increased risk of schizophrenia in certain populations and may predict a poorer response to certain antipsychotic medications. This is thought to be related to lower [[Dopamine]] levels in the [[Prefrontal Cortex]].
