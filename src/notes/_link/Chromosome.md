# Chromosome

A **chromosome** is a single long DNA molecule wrapped around histones into [[Chromatin]] and condensed for segregation. Humans have 46 chromosomes in somatic cells. The [[Centromere]] assembles the kinetochore for spindle attachment during [[Mitosis]] and [[Meiosis]].

## Segregation and Aneuploidy

[[Cell Division]] checkpoints verify [[DNA Replication]] and chromosome alignment before anaphase. Mis-segregation causes aneuploidy — e.g. [[Trisomy]] 21 ([[Down Syndrome]]) from meiotic nondisjunction.

## Documents

- [[Cell Division]] — checkpoints ensure DNA replication and chromosome segregation accuracy via Cyclin/CDK, Rb/E2F, and p53 control.
- [[Centromere]] — specialized chromosome region where the kinetochore assembles for spindle microtubule attachment.
- [[Trisomy]] — extra-chromosome state; meiotic errors producing conditions such as Down Syndrome.
