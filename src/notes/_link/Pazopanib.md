# Pazopanib

[[Pazopanib]] is an orally administered multi-targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitor blocking [[VEGFR]]-1/2/3, [[PDGFR]], and [[c-KIT]], producing antiangiogenic and antitumor effects. It is approved for renal cell carcinoma and soft-tissue sarcoma.

## Context in Fenbendazole Literature

Pazopanib appears in patient case reports summarized in the fenbendazole review as part of combination contexts where fenbendazole self-administration was reported alongside standard therapy.
