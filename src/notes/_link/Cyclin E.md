# Cyclin E

**Cyclin E** (CCNE1/CCNE2) binds [[CDK2]] to drive the [[G1 Phase]]-to-S transition: Cyclin E-CDK2 phosphorylates [[Retinoblastoma Protein]], completes E2F release, and fires replication origins. It is degraded after S-phase entry by SCF-Fbw7 ubiquitination; impaired turnover causes replication stress and genomic instability.

## Regulation and Disease

- Inhibited by [[CDKN1B]] (p27) and [[p21 CIP1|p21]]; induced downstream of Cyclin D-[[CDK4 6]] signaling.
- CCNE1 amplification occurs in ovarian, endometrial, and triple-negative breast cancers and confers CDK2 dependence.
