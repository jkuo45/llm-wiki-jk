# Podophyllotoxin

[[Podophyllotoxin]] is a lignan isolated from *Podophyllum peltatum* that binds [[Tubulin]] at the colchicine site and inhibits microtubule polymerization. Its semisynthetic derivative etoposide (a topoisomerase II inhibitor) is a widely used chemotherapy drug.

## Relevance to Fenbendazole

> [!info] Source: [[Fenbendazole as a Potential Anticancer Drug]]
> Podophyllotoxin is listed among agents whose tubulin-microtubule effects could theoretically synergize with or be antagonized by fenbendazole (Duan et al. 2013).
