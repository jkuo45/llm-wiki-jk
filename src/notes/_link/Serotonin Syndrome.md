# Serotonin Syndrome

[[Serotonin Syndrome]] is a potentially fatal drug-induced condition resulting from excessive [[Serotonin]] activity at central and peripheral [[Serotonin Receptor|serotonin receptors]]. It classically presents as a triad of **altered mental status**, **autonomic instability**, and **neuromuscular abnormalities**.

## Triggers and Mechanisms

Serotonin syndrome arises from drug combinations or overdoses that raise synaptic serotonin — most dangerously the combination of a [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor]] with a [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors|SSRI]], [[Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors|SNRI]], [[Tricyclic Antidepressant]], certain [[Opioids]], or [[Dextromethorphan]].

> [!danger] Methylene blue boxed warning
> [[Methylene blue]] is a potent MAO-A inhibitor. Its FDA labeling carries a **boxed warning** that it may cause serious or fatal serotonin syndrome when combined with serotonergic drugs (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs) and opioids. Concomitant use should be avoided entirely; patients should hold serotonergics for ≥72 h after the last methylene blue dose.

## Clinical Features

- **Altered mental status:** agitation, hallucinations, delirium, coma
- **Autonomic instability:** tachycardia, labile blood pressure, diaphoresis, flushing, hyperthermia
- **Neuromuscular:** tremor, rigidity, [[Myoclonus]], hyperreflexia, incoordination
- **Other:** seizures, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea

## Management

Discontinue the offending agent(s), provide supportive care (sedation, cooling, autonomic control), and escalate to intensive care for severe cases. Cyproheptadine may be used as a serotonin antagonist in refractory cases.
