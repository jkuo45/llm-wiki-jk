# IRAK1

**IRAK1** (interleukin-1 receptor-associated kinase 1) is a serine/threonine kinase downstream of IL-1R and TLR receptors, propagating MyD88-dependent signaling to [[NF-κB]] and MAPKs.

## Role in Apigenin's SASP Suppression

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> Apigenin suppresses the [[SASP|SASP]] partly by inhibiting [[IL-1α]] signaling via [[IRAK1]], [[IRAK4]], [[p38 MAPK]], and [[NF-κB]], reducing breast cancer cell aggressiveness[25]; and in BJ fibroblasts by inhibiting NF-κB p65 through IRAK1/IκBα signaling[33].
