# 3-Methyladenine

**3-Methyladenine** (3-MA) inhibits class-III PI3K ([[Vps34]]) in the [[Beclin1]] nucleation complex, blocking phagophore formation and hence canonical autophagy initiation. It is the standard early-stage autophagy-blockade tool, paired with late-stage chloroquine (lysosomal deacidification) for flux assays. Caveats: prolonged exposure also hits class-I PI3K (paradoxical pro-autophagic signaling), and it cannot distinguish canonical from ATG7-dependent alternative autophagy — relevant given the male-canonical vs female-alternative dimorphism (see [[Autophagy]]).

In the comparison-table context, 3-MA/chloroquine is the autophagy Blocked-by entry (with [[Rapamycin]] as the inducer counterpart).
