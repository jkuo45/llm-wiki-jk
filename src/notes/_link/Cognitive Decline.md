# Cognitive Decline

**Cognitive decline** is the age-associated reduction in memory, processing speed, and executive function. It is linked to [[Neurodegeneration]], chronic [[Inflammation|inflammation]], and [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]], and shares risk with [[Frailty]] and cardiovascular disease.

> [!note] Inflammaging link
> Persisting low-grade inflammation (inflammaging) is implicated in cognitive decline. The [[Shingles Vaccine|shingles vaccine]] study cites cognitive decline among outcomes tied to background inflammation that vaccination may help mitigate; other adult vaccines have been linked to lower dementia risk.
