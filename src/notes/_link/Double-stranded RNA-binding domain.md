# Double-stranded RNA-binding domain

The **double-stranded RNA-binding domain (dsRBD / dsRBM)** is a conserved ~65–70 amino-acid protein module that binds double-stranded RNA (dsRNA) in a sequence-independent, structure-specific manner. It positions RNA substrates for processing enzymes.

## Roles in small-RNA biogenesis

- **[[DGCR8]]** contains two tandem dsRBDs that recognize the pri-miRNA hairpin apex and position [[Drosha]] at the basal cleavage site (the [[Microprocessor complex|Microprocessor]]).
- **[[Dicer]]** contains dsRBDs that measure the distance from the hairpin terminus to define the ~22-nt product length.
- Plant **HYL1** (tandem dsRBD) plays an analogous nuclear role to DGCR8 in miRNA biogenesis.

> [!info] Binding geometry
> Structural studies show two dsRBDs can bind 11–16 bp stretches of dsRNA, allowing simultaneous DGCR8 + Drosha association with a single miRNA stem-loop.
