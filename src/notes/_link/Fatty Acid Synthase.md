# Fatty Acid Synthase

**Fatty Acid Synthase** ([[Fatty Acid Synthase|FASN]]) is a cytosolic enzymatic complex that catalyzes the NADPH-dependent synthesis of palmitate from acetyl-CoA and malonyl-CoA. It is overexpressed in breast, prostate, and other cancers, supporting membrane biosynthesis and proliferation.

## Inhibition by Apigenin

Plant flavonoids including [[Apigenin]], [[Quercetin]], and [[Kaempferol]] inhibit FASN activity, and this inhibition correlates with their ability to induce cancer-cell apoptosis. Apigenin thus couples metabolic suppression to its broader anticancer signaling effects.
