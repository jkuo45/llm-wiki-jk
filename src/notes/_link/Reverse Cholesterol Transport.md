# Reverse Cholesterol Transport

**Reverse cholesterol transport (RCT)** is the process by which excess [[Cholesterol]] is transported from peripheral tissues (especially arterial [[Macrophages]]) to the liver for excretion or recycling. It is considered the primary anti-atherogenic function of [[HDL]].

## Overview

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - berrougui2009_resveratrol_as_atheroprotective_compound|Berrougui et al., Atherosclerosis 2009]]
> RCT involves the movement of cholesterol from peripheral tissues to the liver, beginning with the transfer of free cholesterol and phospholipids from peripheral tissue cells to lipid-poor or lipid-free apolipoprotein A-1 (apoA-1) and HDL3. This process is initiated by cholesterol efflux.

RCT was first proposed by Glomset (1968) and involves several steps:

1. **[[Cholesterol Efflux]]** — Free cholesterol is exported from peripheral cells (especially [[Macrophages]]) to lipid-poor [[Apolipoprotein A1|apoA-1]] and [[HDL]] via [[ABCA1]], [[ABCG1]], and [[SR-BI]]
2. **HDL maturation** — Lipid-poor apoA-1 acquires cholesterol and phospholipids, forming mature HDL particles
3. **Cholesterol esterification** — LCAT esterifies free cholesterol on HDL, trapping it in the core
4. **Hepatic uptake** — HDL delivers cholesterol to the liver via receptor-mediated processes
5. **Biliary excretion** — Cholesterol is excreted in bile

## Key Mediators

- **[[ABCA1]]** — Initiates RCT by lipidating lipid-poor apoA-1
- **[[ABCG1]]** — Transfers cholesterol from foam cells to mature HDL
- **[[SR-BI]]** — Bidirectional cholesterol transporter
- **[[HDL]]** — Central lipoprotein carrying cholesterol to the liver
- **[[Apolipoprotein A1]]** — Primary protein acceptor for cholesterol efflux
- **[[PON1]]** — Antioxidant enzyme carried on HDL

## Regulation

- **[[Liver X Receptor|LXR]]**: Transcriptionally activates ABCA1 and ABCG1
- **[[Resveratrol]]**: Induces LXR-α expression; upregulates ABCA1; enhances cholesterol efflux
- **[[Oxidative Stress]]**: Impairs RCT by damaging HDL function and reducing ABCA1 expression

## Clinical Relevance

- Impaired RCT contributes to [[Foam Cells]] formation and [[Atherosclerosis]]
- [[HDL]] levels correlate inversely with cardiovascular disease risk
- Therapeutic strategies aim to enhance RCT via ABCA1/ABCG1 upregulation or [[PON1]] delivery
