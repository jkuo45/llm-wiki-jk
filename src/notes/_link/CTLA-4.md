# CTLA-4

**CTLA-4** (CD152) is an inhibitory receptor upregulated after T-cell activation that outcompetes CD28 for [[CD86]]/CD80 binding, delivering off-signals that terminate responses. Germline haploinsufficiency causes autoimmunity; blockade with ipilimumab (anti-CTLA-4) is approved melanoma/NSCLC [[Immunotherapy]], often combined with PD-1 inhibition.
