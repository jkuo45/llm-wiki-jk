# Pycnogenol

**Pycnogenol** is a proprietary, standardized bark extract from the French maritime pine (*Pinus pinaster* subsp. *atlantica*) grown in southwest France. Its defined composition is ~65–75% [[Proanthocyanidins]] (predominantly A-type oligomeric proanthocyanidins, OPCs), with smaller amounts of monomeric [[Catechin]]/epicatechin, phenolic acids (caffeic, ferulic), and taxifolin. It is among the most clinically studied proanthocyanidin-rich supplements.

## Composition & Classification

- Primarily **A-type [[Proanthocyanidins]]** — the doubly-linked (C4→C8 plus C2→O→C7 ether) oligomers also characteristic of cranberry PACs, which are more stable and metabolically distinctive than B-type PACs.
- Sits under the [[Polyphenols]] / [[Flavonoids]] umbrella as a concentrated, proanthocyanidin-dominant botanical extract; chemically related to but more complex than isolated [[Flavan-3-ols]].

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **Antioxidant:** Potent direct radical scavenger and metal chelator; upregulates endogenous [[Antioxidant]] defenses via the [[NRF2]]/KEAP1 pathway and spares [[Vitamin C|vitamin C]]/[[Glutathione]].
- **Vascular / endothelial:** Enhances eNOS activity and NO bioavailability, improving microcirculation, capillary resistance, and venous tone; reduces edema and markers of [[Endothelial Dysfunction]].
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Inhibits [[NF-κB]] signaling and adhesion-molecule expression.
- **SIRT1 / metabolic:** As a proanthocyanidin-rich preparation, Pycnogenol engages the natural-polyphenol STAC axis that activates [[SIRT1]] (K-type allosteric) and [[AMPK]], contributing to its metabolic and skin-aging effects.

## Physiological Function

- **Microvascular & venous health:** Clinically used for chronic venous insufficiency, edema, and retinal/capillary microcirculation support.
- **Skin & connective tissue:** Stimulates collagen/elastin and photoprotection; common in dermatological anti-aging formulations, consistent with broader [[Proanthocyanidins]] activity.
- **Exercise & cognition:** Some trials report improved endurance, recovery, and attention/working-memory metrics.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Aging & age-related disease:** Counteracts age-associated [[Oxidative Stress]], [[Inflammation]], and vascular dysfunction; studied in [[Cardiovascular Disease]], [[Diabetes Mellitus]] (glycemic and endothelial endpoints), and menopausal/dermatological aging.
- **Therapeutic positioning:** Positioned as a natural [[STACs]]/[[Antioxidant]] supplement for vascular, metabolic, and skin healthy-aging; benefits are limited by the same low systemic bioavailability and gut-metabolite conversion that qualify other polyphenol STACs.
