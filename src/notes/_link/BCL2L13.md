# BCL2L13

BCL2L13 (Bcl-2-like protein 13, also known as Bcl-Rambo) is the functional homolog of the yeast mitophagy receptor [[Atg32]] in mammals. It operates independently of [[Parkin]] and is located on the outer mitochondrial membrane (OMM).

## Structure and Mechanism

BCL2L13 contains two key domains: a [[LIR Motif|LIR motif]] (W-x-x-I at positions 270–273) for binding [[LC3]], and a BH domain. It can simultaneously induce [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial fragmentation]] and recruit [[Autophagosome|autophagosomes]], making it a dual-function mitophagy receptor. Unlike other receptors that require prior mitochondrial damage or ubiquitination, BCL2L13 can actively fragment [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]] and initiate mitophagy independently.

## Role in Mitophagy

BCL2L13-mediated [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]] provides an alternative pathway for mitochondrial clearance when the canonical [[PINK1]]/[[Parkin]] pathway is compromised. This is particularly relevant in [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's disease]] where Parkin function is impaired.
