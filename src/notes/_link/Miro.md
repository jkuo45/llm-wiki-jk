### Overview
Miro is an [[Outer Mitochondrial Membrane]] protein that, together with the adaptor [[Milton]], forms the complex linking [[MFN2]] to [[Kinesin|kinesin motors]] for axonal mitochondrial trafficking.

### Mechanism
Miro serves as the mitochondrial receptor for the Milton–kinesin transport machinery, anchoring mitochondria to microtubule motors. This complex enables the distribution of mitochondria to distal neuronal compartments, supporting local ATP supply and [[Mitochondrial Integrity]].

### Disease Relevance
Mutations in [[MFN2]] causing [[Charcot-Marie-Tooth Disease Type 2A]] disrupt the Miro/Milton/kinesin linkage, leading to axonal mitochondrial depletion and neurodegeneration.
