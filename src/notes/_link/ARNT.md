# ARNT

ARNT (Aryl hydrocarbon receptor nuclear translocator), also known as HIF1B or HIF-1β, is the obligate heterodimerization partner of [[HIF1A|HIF-1α]]. Together they form the active [[HIF1A|HIF-1]] transcription factor complex that mediates the cellular response to [[Hypoxia]].

## Structure & Domains

ARNT contains a basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) domain and two PER-ARNT-SIM (PAS) domains (PAS-A and PAS-B) that mediate heterodimerization with [[HIF1A]]. The C-terminal transactivation domain (TAD) recruits transcriptional coactivators such as [[p300]]/[[CBP]].

## Mechanism of Action

Under [[Hypoxia]], [[HIF1A]] is stabilized (escape from [[VHL]]-mediated proteasomal degradation), translocates to the nucleus, and dimerizes with ARNT:

1. [[HIF1A|HIF-1α]] and ARNT form a heterodimer via their PAS domains
2. The dimer binds to Hypoxia Response Elements (HREs) containing the consensus sequence 5'-RCGTG-3' in target gene promoters
3. Transcriptional coactivators ([[p300]]/[[CBP]]) are recruited
4. Target genes are transactivated, including [[VEGF]], [[EPO]], [[BNIP3]], [[BNIP3L]], [[GLUT1]], and glycolytic enzymes

ARNT is constitutively expressed and not oxygen-regulated, providing a stable dimerization platform for the oxygen-sensitive [[HIF1A]] subunit. This arrangement allows rapid transcriptional responses to fluctuating oxygen levels.

## Related Pathways

- **[[HIF1A]]**: Oxygen-sensitive partner whose stabilization triggers dimerization
- **[[Hypoxia]]**: Physiological signal activating the HIF-1 complex
- **[[BNIP3]]** / **[[BNIP3L]]**: Mitophagy receptor genes transactivated by HIF-1
- **[[VHL]]**: E3 ubiquitin ligase that targets HIF-1α for degradation under normoxia
- **[[Aryl Hydrocarbon Receptor]] (AhR)**: ARNT also dimerizes with AhR to mediate xenobiotic responses
