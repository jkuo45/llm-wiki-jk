# ARN14794

**ARN14794** is a benzoxazolone carboxamide small-molecule inhibitor of [[Acid ceramidase]] (ACase, ASAH1) with an IC50 of approximately 100 nM against human ACase. It belongs to the same chemical series as [[ARN14974]].

## Role in Ferroptosis Research

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex|Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile… (Soriano-Castell et al., 2026)]]
> ARN14794 was used to pharmacologically inhibit [[Acid ceramidase]] in replicatively senescent [[WI-38]] fibroblasts and HT22 neuronal cells. ACase inhibition with ARN14794 protected both proliferative and senescent cells from [[RSL3]]-induced [[Ferroptosis]] by reducing membrane PL-[[PUFA]] content and [[Lipid Peroxidation]].
