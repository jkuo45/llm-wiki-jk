### Overview
Milton is an adaptor protein that connects the mitochondrial fusion factor [[MFN2]] to the microtubule motor [[Kinesin|kinesin]] via the OMM protein [[Miro]], thereby enabling mitochondrial trafficking along axons.

### Mechanism
Milton bridges Miro (on the mitochondrion) to kinesin (on microtubules), forming the transport complex that moves mitochondria to sites of high energy demand. This linkage is essential for neuronal health and [[Mitochondrial Integrity]].

### Disease Relevance
Disruption of the MFN2–Miro–Milton–kinesin axis, as in [[Charcot-Marie-Tooth Disease Type 2A]], impairs axonal mitochondrial transport and underlies the peripheral neuropathy.
