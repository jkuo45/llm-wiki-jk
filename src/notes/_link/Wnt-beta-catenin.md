# Wnt-beta-catenin

Canonical **Wnt/β-catenin** signaling stabilizes [[β-Catenin]] (Wnt ligand → Frizzled/LRP → destruction-complex inhibition → nuclear TCF/LEF transcription), sustaining stemness, proliferation, and fate decisions. It is restrained by [[APC]] and amplified by [[FRAT2]]; hyperactivation initiates colorectal cancer and sustains cancer stem cells.
