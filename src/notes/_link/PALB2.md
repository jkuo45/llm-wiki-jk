# PALB2

**PALB2** bridges BRCA1 and [[BRCA2]] to load RAD51 onto resected double-strand breaks during homologous recombination. Biallelic loss causes Fanconi anemia (FANCN); monoallelic pathogenic variants confer high risk of [[Breast Cancer]], [[Pancreatic Cancer]], and ovarian cancer, with PARP-inhibitor sensitivity akin to BRCA deficiency.
