# Intervertebral Disc Degeneration

**Intervertebral disc degeneration (IDD)** is the age- and load-related deterioration of the intervertebral disc, characterized by loss of nucleus pulposus cells, extracellular-matrix breakdown, oxidative stress, inflammation, and cellular senescence. It is a leading cause of back pain and disability.

## Sirtuin Axis

- **[[SIRT1]]**: Reduced in degenerative nucleus pulposus tissues, decreasing with disease severity; inhibits NF-κB transcriptional activity and suppresses c-Fos/c-Jun phosphorylation.
- **[[SIRT2]]**: Reverses IL-1β action by inhibiting the p53/p21 pathway, suppressing oxidative stress and senescence.
- **[[SIRT3]]**: Maintains nucleus pulposus cell homeostasis via mitochondrial oxidative-stress regulation.
- **[[SIRT6]]**: Inhibits inflammatory response and senescence via NF-κB inhibition.

## Clinical Relevance

The sirtuin network (SIRT1/2/3/6) opposes the senescence, oxidative stress, and inflammation that drive disc degeneration, making sirtuin activation a candidate disease-modifying strategy for IDD.
