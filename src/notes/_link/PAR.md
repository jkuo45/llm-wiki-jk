# PAR

**PAR** (poly(ADP-ribose)) is the branched, nucleic-acid-like polymer synthesized by [[PARP1]] from [[NAD+]] at sites of DNA damage. At physiological levels it is a repair scaffold; at 10–500x overproduction it becomes the parthanatos death signal itself: PAR peaks nuclear/cytosolic ~15 min and mitochondrial ~30 min, exits the nucleus carried by PARylated histone H1.2 (gated by the [[Iduna]] E3 ligase), inhibits [[Hexokinase-1]] (bioenergetic hit), and binds the PAR-binding motif on [[AIF]]-D3 (Arg588/Lys589/Arg592) to trigger AIF release (death hit).

Purified PAR delivered by lipid carrier kills dose- and length-dependently, blocked by PAR glycohydrolase ([[PARG]]) but not by z-VAD — the formal proof that the polymer, not the enzyme, is the commitment signal. PAR is erased chiefly by [[PARG]]/[[ARH3]]; anti-PAR antibodies and PARG overexpression are parthanatos-blocking interventions.
