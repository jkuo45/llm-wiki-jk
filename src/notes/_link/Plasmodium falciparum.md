# Plasmodium falciparum

Plasmodium falciparum is the apicomplexan parasite causing malaria and is reported among the pathogens that induce [[NETosis]] (Baker et al. 2008). It appears alongside [[Leishmania]] spp. and [[Toxoplasma gondii]] as a parasitic NET trigger, supporting NET extrusion as a conserved defense strategy across microbes.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Diverse extracellular and intracellular microbes induce neutrophils to extrude [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps]] by suicidal, noncanonical and vital NETosis (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
