# Systemic Inflammatory Response Syndrome

## Overview

Systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS) is a clinical syndrome of systemic inflammation (fever or hypothermia, tachycardia, tachypnea, leukocytosis) triggered with or without infection.

## Link to Necroptosis

Sterile SIRS frequently follows massive [[Necroptosis]]: [[MLKL]]-ruptured cells spill [[Damage-Associated Molecular Patterns]] (HMGB1, ATP, mitochondrial DNA) that activate [[Toll-like Receptor]] and inflammasome cascades systemically. [[Cerulein-Induced Pancreatitis]] is a canonical example where pancreatic necroptosis escalates to SIRS.
