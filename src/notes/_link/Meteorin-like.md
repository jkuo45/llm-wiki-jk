# Meteorin-like

**Meteorin-like (Metrnl)** is a secreted cytokine-like protein homologous to the neurotrophic factor meteorin, produced by skeletal muscle (as an exercise-induced myokine), adipose tissue, and other tissues. Metrnl stimulates energy expenditure, promotes browning of white adipose tissue, and exerts anti-inflammatory and tissue-protective effects. It signals through receptor complexes that engage [[AMPK]] and [[PGC-1α]] pathways, positioning it as a systemic mediator of the beneficial adaptations to physical activity.

## PGC-1α/SIRT3 axis in diabetic nephropathy

Mechanistic investigations revealed that meteorin-like protein activates the [[PGC-1α]]/[[SIRT3]] axis, which stimulates [[AMPK]] at Thr172 and increases [[UCP1]]-dependent proton leak, thereby preserving mitochondrial ultrastructural integrity and regulating energy metabolism, particularly in diabetic nephropathy[207]. The proposed cascade is: Metrnl → PGC-1α transcription → SIRT3 expression → AMPK activation → UCP1-mediated mild uncoupling → improved mitochondrial quality and redox balance in the diabetic kidney.

## Clinical relevance

Metrnl levels are reduced in obesity and metabolic disease and improve with exercise. Pharmacological delivery or induction of Metrnl represents a strategy to recapitulate exercise-like mitochondrial benefits via the PGC-1α/SIRT3 axis in diabetic kidney disease.
