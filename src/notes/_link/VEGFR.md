# VEGFR

The [[VEGFR]] family (VEGFR-1/2/3) are receptor tyrosine kinases activated by VEGF ligands; VEGFR-2 is the main driver of tumor [[Angiogenesis]]. They are targeted by antiangiogenic tyrosine-kinase inhibitors such as [[Pazopanib]] and [[Sunitinib]].

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> Pazopanib is cited as a VEGFR/PDGFR-targeted agent in fenbendazole patient case-report contexts (54-57).
