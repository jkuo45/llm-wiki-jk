# ICAM-1

## Overview

ICAM-1 (**CD54**) is the adhesion molecule that converts a rolling leukocyte
into a firmly adherent one. It is a cell-surface receptor, not an enzyme,
and it is the principal ligand for the **β2 integrins** — **LFA-1**
(ITGAL/ITGB2) on leukocytes and **Mac-1** (ITGAM/ITGB2) on monocytes and
neutrophils. Through these interactions ICAM-1 is the physical basis of
leukocyte recruitment to sites of injury, and of the transendothelial
migration that lets leukocytes enter tissue at all.

ICAM-1 has a second career as an **infectious entry receptor**: it is the
cellular receptor for major-group rhinoviruses and for Coxsackievirus A21,
a fact discovered by cDNA cloning of the major-group rhinovirus receptor
(1989), which showed it to be nearly identical to ICAM-1.

## Structure and domains

ICAM-1 (UniProt P05362) is a type I single-pass transmembrane glycoprotein,
28–532 residues after signal-peptide cleavage (signal peptide ~1–27; mature
chain 28–532; transmembrane 481–503).

- **Five immunoglobulin-like (Ig) domains** — Ig1 (41–103), Ig2 (128–193),
  Ig3 (230–297), Ig4 (325–378), Ig5 (412–464) — with a domain arrangement
  homologous to the neural cell adhesion molecule (NCAM). Notably ICAM-1
  contains **no RGD sequence**, so it does not engage classical
  fibronectin-type integrin ligands.
- **Domain 1** carries the **LFA-1 (ITGB2) binding site**, with the key
  contact residues in the ~152–154 motif. LFA-1 engagement requires
  ICAM-1 in an extended/open conformation, which is achieved by
  **inside-out activation** of the integrin (a conformational change in
  LFA-1) and by mechanical/tensegrity forces.
- **Mac-1 (ITGAM/ITGB2)** binds a distinct site on domain 2 (and additional
  contacts), typically binding ICAM-1 in a more clustered/fibrillar
  conformation than LFA-1 does.
- **Dimerisation.** ICAM-1 forms homodimers on the cell surface, and homodimer
  formation is functionally important for strong adhesive avidity.
- **Cytoplasmic tail** (C-terminal, ~30 residues) links to the actin
  cytoskeleton; in endothelial cells the tail couples to actin through
  ezrin/moesin/α-actinin.

## Mechanism of action

1. **Induction.** Basal ICAM-1 is expressed on vascular endothelial cells,
   thymic epithelial cells, other epithelia, fibroblasts, tissue macrophages,
   mitogen-stimulated T-lymphocyte blasts, and germinal-centre dendritic cells
   in tonsils, lymph nodes, and Peyer's patches; it is present in low amounts
   on resting peripheral blood leukocytes.
2. **Inflammatory induction.** [[TNF-alpha]], [[IL-1β]], and other
   inflammatory cytokines strongly upregulate ICAM-1 on endothelium. In
   atria it is upregulated by rapid pacing resembling atrial fibrillation.
3. **Firm adhesion.** LFA-1 engagement produces firm adhesion, "calibrated" to
   the strength of antigen stimulation — a system for tuning T-cell killing to
   how strongly the target is stimulating the T cell.
4. **Transendothelial migration.** During diapedesis, ICAM-1 engagement promotes
   assembly of endothelial **apical cups** through ARHGEF26/SGEF and RHOG
   activation, and drives adherens junction remodelling.
5. **Immunological synapse.** On the T-cell side ICAM-1 interacts with CD81,
   CD247, and CD9 at immunological synapses between antigen-presenting cells
   and T cells.
6. **Non-immune function.** ICAM-1 promotes epithelial cell aggregation through
   interaction with [[MUC1]].

> [!info] ICAM-1 is a *regulated* adhesion molecule
> The defining feature of ICAM-1 is that adhesion strength is not set by
> whether the molecule is present, but by (a) how much is present, (b) its
> clustering, and (c) the activation state of the β2 integrin on the other
> cell. That is why ICAM-1 upregulation, not ICAM-1 presence, is what turns
> endothelial inflammation into leukocyte recruitment.

## Pathophysiology and clinical relevance

> [!important] A hub of leukocyte-driven disease
> - **Atherosclerosis and cardiovascular disease.** ICAM-1 upregulation on
>   endothelium is one of the earliest and best-replicated steps in
>   atherogenesis, preceding visible plaque. Elevated ICAM-1 (and soluble
>   ICAM-1) is associated with coronary artery disease and stroke risk.
> - **Metabolic memory.** Prior hyperglycaemic or oxidative exposure leaves
>   *persistent* ICAM-1/NF-κB activation — a "trained" inflammatory state —
>   which is why vascular complications lag behind glycaemic control.
> - **Rheumatoid arthritis, [[Psoriasis]], [[Multiple Sclerosis]],
>   [[Inflammatory Bowel Disease]]** and other chronic inflammatory diseases:
>   ICAM-1 is a therapeutic target, though blockade alone has been
>   disappointing, plausibly because β2 integrin and ICAM-1 form one of
>   several redundant adhesion pathways.
> - **Cerebral malaria.** ICAM-1 mediates sequestration of infected
>   erythrocytes and leukocytes in cerebral microvessels, and ICAM-1
>   susceptibility polymorphisms (particularly ICAM-1 exon 6, Glu469Lys)
>   were implicated in geographic variation in cerebral malaria prevalence.
> - **Allergic disease.** ICAM-1 upregulation on airway epithelium and
>   endothelium contributes to airway inflammation and hyperresponsiveness,
>   including in [[Asthma]].
> - **Viral infection.** Because it is the rhinovirus receptor, ICAM-1
>   expression level modulates the severity of common cold. A soluble form of
>   ICAM-1 is a potent, specific inhibitor of rhinovirus infection — proof of
>   principle for receptor-targeted antiviral design. ICAM-1 is degraded by
>   the KSHV/HHV-8 viral E3 ubiquitin ligase MIR2, presumably to prevent
>   lysis of infected cells by cytotoxic T cells and NK cells.
> - **Circulating biomarker.** Soluble ICAM-1 is measurable in plasma and is
>   used as an inflammatory/ endothelial-activation marker in cardiovascular,
>   inflammatory, and oncological biomarker panels.

## Connections

- [[Cell Adhesion]] — ICAM-1 is a canonical adhesion molecule, and its adhesion function is the whole of its immunological purpose: converting transient rolling into firm arrest.
- [[Integrin]] — ICAM-1 is the ligand of the β2 integrins LFA-1 (ITGAL/ITGB2) and Mac-1 (ITGAM/ITGB2); ICAM-1 is the non-RGD counterexample to classical integrin ligand biology.
- [[T Cell]] — LFA-1–ICAM-1 adhesion calibrates T-cell killing to antigen stimulation strength, and ICAM-1 participates in the immunological synapse via CD81, CD247, and CD9.
- [[Atherosclerosis]] — ICAM-1 upregulation is one of the earliest endothelial events in atherogenesis and a principal target of the Nrf2/ARE- and SIRT1-mediated protective effects of [[Resveratrol]].
- [[NRF2]] — Nrf2/ARE signalling suppresses ICAM-1 expression, which is the documented mechanism by which resveratrol reduces monocyte adhesion in experimental atherosclerosis.
- [[SIRT1]] — SIRT1 and SIRT6 lower ICAM-1 expression by deacetylating and inhibiting NF-κB; SIRT1 deficiency raises ICAM-1 and microvascular leukocyte adhesion.
- [[NF-κB]] — oxidative stress activates NF-κB, which induces ICAM-1 and feeds forward into further ROS production; sirtuins break this loop by deacetylating RelA/p65.
- [[Resveratrol]] — lowers ICAM-1 via both Nrf2/ARE and SIRT1/NF-κB routes in endothelial and inflammatory models.
- [[Inflammation]] — ICAM-1 is one of the canonical endothelial activation markers; soluble ICAM-1 is a standard circulating inflammation readout.
- [[TNF-alpha]] and [[IL-1β]] — the principal cytokines that induce ICAM-1 on endothelium, establishing the inflammatory induction axis.
- [[Focal Adhesion]] — although ICAM-1 is an immunological rather than a focal-adhesion molecule, it shares the actin-coupling logic of cell adhesion receptors; its cytoplasmic tail links to the actin cytoskeleton.
