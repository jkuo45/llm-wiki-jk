# RNA Interference

**RNA interference (RNAi)** is a conserved post-transcriptional gene-silencing pathway in which double-stranded RNA is processed into small interfering RNAs (siRNAs) or [[MicroRNA|microRNAs]] that guide the **[[RISC]]** (centered on **[[Argonaute]]**) to repress complementary target mRNAs.

## Core machinery

- **[[Dicer]]** (RNase III) cleaves long dsRNA or pre-miRNA into ~21–23 nt small RNAs.
- **[[Argonaute]]** loads the guide strand and executes repression/cleavage.
- In *Drosophila*, **Dicer-2** specializes in the siRNA branch and **Dicer-1** in the miRNA branch.

> [!note] Experimental use
> RNAi (e.g., dsRNA or siRNA knockdown) is the standard reverse-genetics tool used to deplete [[DGCR8]], [[Drosha]], and other biogenesis factors and reveal their requirement for [[MicroRNA]] maturation.
