# H1.2

**H1.2** (histone H1.2, encoded by *HIST1H1C*/*H1-2*) is a replication-independent linker [[Histone]] variant. In [[Parthanatos]] it acts as the [[PAR]] carrier that exports the polymer from the nucleus, a key step in transmitting the death signal to the cytosol and mitochondria.

## Overview

- Linker histones bind nucleosomal DNA and compact [[Chromatin]]; the H1 family comprises multiple variants, including the replication-dependent/independent H1.2.
- H1.2 is PARylated by [[PARP1]] on DNA damage, and the PARylated protein can shuttle [[PAR]] from the nucleus to the cytoplasm.
- Pharmacological or genetic interference with H1.2 PARylation blocks PAR export and reduces parthanatos.

## Role in parthanatos

- Nuclear [[PARP1]] hyperactivation produces [[PAR]] that is carried out of the nucleus on PARylated H1.2; cytosolic/mitochondrial PAR then inhibits [[Hexokinase-1]] and binds [[Apoptosis-Inducing Factor|AIF]] at its PAR-binding motif to trigger release.
- The PAR-dependent E3 ligase [[Iduna]] ubiquitinates PARylated H1.2, promoting its degradation and blocking PAR export — an endogenous brake on the pathway.
- H1.2 also has pro-apoptotic behaviour: on DNA damage it can translocate to mitochondria and promote [[Cytochrome c]] release, providing a second intersection with cell-death signalling.
- How PAR cargo is protected from [[PARG]] and delivered across the nuclear envelope remains an open question (no dedicated PAR exporter has been identified).

> [!info]
> Source: [[PAR]] and [[Parthanatos]]
> H1.2 is described as the PARylated carrier gated by Iduna; [[_document_ - Parthanatos Moura 2024 molecular mechanisms more questions than answers|Moura et al. 2024]] treats free-PAR generation, protection, and nuclear export as unresolved.
