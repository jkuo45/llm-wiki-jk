# ADAM10

[[ADAM10]] (A Disintegrin And Metalloproteinase 10) is a transmembrane metalloprotease that serves as the principal [[APP|amyloid precursor protein]] α-secretase. Cleavage of APP within the Aβ sequence by ADAM10 releases a soluble ectodomain (sAPPα) and precludes generation of neurotoxic [[Amyloid Beta|β-amyloid]] peptides, positioning ADAM10 at a protective branch of APP processing relative to the [[BACE1|β-secretase]] pathway.

## SIRT1 axis in Alzheimer's disease

In a mouse model of [[Alzheimer's Disease|Alzheimer's disease (AD)]], [[SIRT1]]-mediated deacetylation of the retinoic acid receptor RARβ activates transcription of *ADAM10*. The resulting rise in α-secretase activity directs APP away from β-secretase processing, attenuating β-amyloid production; conversely, brain-specific *Sirt1* knockout increases β-amyloid aggregates and worsens AD phenotypes ([[_document_ - Sirtuins Guardians of Mammalian Healthspan|Sirtuins: Guardians of Mammalian Healthspan, Giblin et al. 2014]], refs 59–61).

> [!info] Anti-amyloid gatekeeper
> SIRT1 → RARβ → ADAM10 shifts [[APP]] processing toward the non-amyloidogenic α-secretase pathway, reducing β-amyloid plaque load in AD mice.
