# mitoTAD

**mitoTAD** (mitochondrial protein translocation-associated degradation) is a [[Proteasome|proteasomal]] quality control pathway that surveils the [[Mitochondrial protein import]] machinery. When a mitochondrial precursor protein becomes stalled during import through the [[TOM complex|TOM]]/[[TIM complex|TIM]] translocons, mitoTAD mediates its extraction and degradation.

## Mechanism

- In yeast, stalled precursors are recognized by [[Ubx2]], which recruits [[Cdc48]] (the yeast [[VCP]]/p97 homolog)
- Cdc48 extracts the stalled precursor from the translocon
- The extracted protein is [[Ubiquitin|ubiquitylated]] and degraded by the [[Proteasome]]
- Works in concert with the [[Ubiquitin-Proteasome System]]
