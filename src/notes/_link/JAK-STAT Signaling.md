### Overview

JAK-STAT signaling is a conserved cytokine-receptor-coupled pathway in which Janus kinases (JAKs) phosphorylate signal transducer and activator of transcription (STAT) proteins, most prominently STAT3. In the context of cellular senescence, the JAK-STAT3 axis is a major driver of a pro-inflammatory [[SASP]] (senescence-associated secretory phenotype). It integrates cues from SASP cytokines such as [[IL-6]] and engages interacting pathways including [[p38 MAPK]] and [[mTOR]] to amplify inflammatory factor expression.

### Mechanism / Structure

The canonical flow is receptor-bound JAK1/2 activation → STAT3 tyrosine phosphorylation → STAT3 dimerization and nuclear translocation → transcription of SASP genes. Key features in senescence:

- **Feed-forward inflammation** — STAT3 activation promotes secretion of IL-6 and IL-8, which can further reinforce JAK-STAT signaling in an autocrine/paracrine loop characteristic of chronic senescence.
- **Crosstalk** — the axis intersects with [[p38 MAPK]] and [[mTOR]], consolidating stress and nutrient signals into a sustained inflammatory output.
- **Systemic impact** — STAT3 activation contributes to age-related frailty and to the immune-modulatory and tumor-promoting facets of the secretome.

> [!info] JAK-STAT3 as a senomorphic node
> Unlike senolytics, which kill senescent cells, senomorphics dampen their harmful secretory output. The JAK-STAT3 axis is a validated senomorphic target: JAK1/2 inhibitors reduce SASP cytokines IL-6 and IL-8, interrupting the inflammatory feed-forward loop without eliminating the cells.

### Role in Senescence

[[SIRT6]] inhibits the JAK2/STAT3 pathway, suppressing growth and survival signaling — for example, in gastric cancer contexts — placing the axis downstream of a longevity-associated deacetylase. The pathway is especially important for the self-reinforcing, chronic inflammation that distinguishes persistent senescence from a transient arrest, linking local tissue inflammation to systemic aging phenotypes.

### Clinical & Research Significance

JAK-STAT signaling is a translational focal point for [[Senomorphic]] strategies. [[Ruxolitinib]], a clinically approved JAK1/2 inhibitor, reduces SASP cytokines IL-6 and IL-8 in senescent fibroblasts, validating the axis and supporting its use as a senomorphic. Its established clinical track record strengthens the case for repurposing JAK inhibitors to blunt senescence-associated inflammation in aging and disease.
