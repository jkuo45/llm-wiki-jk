# Sphingosine

**Sphingosine** is the long-chain amino alcohol that forms the structural backbone of all [[Sphingolipid Metabolism|sphingolipids]]. It is generated when [[Acid ceramidase]] cleaves the *N*-acyl linkage of [[Ceramide|ceramide]], releasing free sphingosine and a fatty acid. Sphingosine is rapidly phosphorylated by sphingosine kinases to [[Sphingosine-1-phosphate|S1P]], a potent signaling lipid.

## Function & Signaling

- **Ceramide/sphingosine/S1P rheostat:** High [[Ceramide|ceramide]] and sphingosine are generally growth-inhibitory and pro-death, whereas [[Sphingosine-1-phosphate|S1P]] is pro-survival, pro-proliferative, and anti-[[Apoptosis|apoptotic]].
- **Senescence link:** Over-expression of [[Acid ceramidase]] in [[Senescent Cells|senescent cells]] raises sphingosine/S1P levels, which promotes senescent-cell survival—yet the same ACase activity inadvertently creates a **pro-[[Ferroptosis|ferroptotic]] lipid profile** by releasing free fatty acids from [[Ceramide|ceramide]] catabolism.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex|Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile… (Soriano-Castell et al., 2026)]]
> ACase breaks [[Ceramide|ceramide]] into [[Sphingosine|sphingosine]] + free fatty acid. The resulting free fatty acids feed membrane [[Phospholipid|phospholipid]] PUFA enrichment, sensitizing cells to [[Ferroptosis]] — a death modality the sphingosine/S1P arm would otherwise protect against.

## Connections

- [[Ceramide]] — Immediate precursor; cleaved by ACase to yield sphingosine.
- [[Acid ceramidase]] — The enzyme that produces sphingosine from ceramide.
- [[Sphingosine-1-phosphate]] — Phosphorylated derivative; pro-survival signaling lipid.
- [[Sphingomyelin]] — Sphingomyelin is synthesized from ceramide; part of the same network.
- [[Ferroptosis]] — Sphingosine-producing ACase activity indirectly sensitizes cells via lipid remodeling.
- [[Senescent Cells]] — ACase-driven sphingosine/S1P supports senescent-cell survival while increasing ferroptotic vulnerability.
- [[Apoptosis]] — Sphingosine/S1P generally oppose apoptosis.
