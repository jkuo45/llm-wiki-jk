# MacroD2

**MacroD2** (macrodomain-containing protein 2, gene `MACROD2`) is a MacroD-type macrodomain [[ADP-ribosylation]] eraser distributed in cytosol and nucleus. It hydrolyzes mono-ADP-ribose ester-linked to aspartate/glutamate (e.g. PARP10/PARP1 mono-marks), deacetylates [[O-acetyl-ADP-ribose]], and restores activity of MAR-inhibited kinases such as GSK3beta.

> [!info] MacroD2 in one sentence
> Cytosolic/nuclear MacroD2 is the best-characterized Asp/Glu-deMARylase, reversing inhibitory kinase MARylation and ATM-linked damage signaling; it cannot degrade [[PAR]] polymers, acting downstream of [[PARG]].

## Overview

- ~47 kDa, 17 exons; N-terminal region (7-47), macrodomain (78-228).
- Broad embryonic expression (liver, brain, lung, thymus, heart, kidney); high neuronal expression embryonic through adult.
- Paralog of [[MacroD1]] (mitochondrial); closest functional twin with shared substrate-assisted chemistry.
- Inactive toward poly-[[PAR]]; strictly mono-ADP-ribosylhydrolase plus OAADPr deacetylase.

## Structure & Catalytic mechanism

Canonical macrodomain fold; 1.5-A MacroD2-ADP-ribose complex shows distal ribose locked by Ile189/Tyr190 stacking, Asn92/Asn102/Asp102 hydrogen-bond network, and a catalytic water cleft between loop 1/2. H2-18O labeling proves attack at ribose C1'' (1''-OH-linked Glu ester). N92A/D102A retains PARP10-deMARylation, so Asp102/His106 play indirect roles; current model is substrate-assisted: ADP-ribose alpha-phosphate activates the positioned water for nucleophilic attack on C1''. Inhibited by ADP-HPD and free ADP-ribose; no Mg/Ca/Mn dependence.

## Physiological function

- **Kinase switch:** reverses ARTD10/PARP10-mediated inhibitory MARylation of GSK3beta, restoring kinase activity in vitro and in cells; paradigm for MAR-regulated WNT signaling.
- **DNA-damage response:** phosphorylated by ATM after double-strand breaks; reverses MARylation on damage factors; recruited to lesions at higher damage loads (delayed kinetics vs early PAR writers).
- **Compartment coverage:** with [[MacroD1]], covers all [[Sirtuins]] OAADPr-producing compartments (MacroD2 cytosol/nucleus, MacroD1 mitochondria).

## Pathology & Clinical relevance

- `MACROD2` deletions/translocations associated with autism spectrum traits, intellectual disability, and Kabuki-syndrome-overlapping neurodevelopmental phenotypes; GWAS links to schizophrenia and brain connectivity.
- Frequently deleted in colorectal and other cancers (20q11 amplicon context); candidate haploinsufficient tumor suppressor, though causality still debated.
- No selective MacroD2 inhibitors in clinic; viral-nsp3 macrodomain programs borrow MacroD2 chemistry (see [[SARS-CoV-2]]).
