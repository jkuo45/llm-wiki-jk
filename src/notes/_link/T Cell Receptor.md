# T Cell Receptor

The **[[T Cell Receptor]]** ([[TCR]]) is a disulfide-linked αβ (or γδ) heterodimeric membrane protein complex on [[T Lymphocyte|T cells]] that recognizes antigenic peptides presented by [[Major Histocompatibility Complex]] (MHC) molecules, initiating [[Adaptive Immunity]]. It is the cardinal specificity determinant of cellular immunity. TCR gene rearrangement during [[Thymopoiesis|thymopoiesis]] generates the diverse receptor repertoire essential for adaptive immune function.

## Structure and Complex

- **Heterodimer:** Most commonly αβ (alpha-beta) chains; γδ (gamma-delta) in a minority of T cells
- **Associated molecules:** CD3 complex (CD3γ, δ, ε, ζζ) for signal transduction, whose immunoreceptor tyrosine-based activation motifs (ITAMs) transduce signals
- **Variable regions:** V(D)J recombination generates unique antigen-binding sites
- **Co-receptors:** CD4 or CD8 bind MHC class II or I, respectively, stabilizing the interaction
- **Diversity:** Estimated >10¹⁵ possible TCR specificities

## Gene Rearrangement

### In the thymus
- TCR β-chain rearrangement occurs in CD4⁻CD8⁻ (double negative) thymocytes in the cortex
- TCR α-chain rearrangement occurs in CD4⁺CD8⁺ (double positive) thymocytes
- Rearrangement involves V(D)J recombination, generating [[sjTREC]] as a byproduct
- Successful rearrangement is required for positive selection

### Age-related decline
- [[Thymic Involution|Thymic involution]] reduces TCR gene rearrangement capacity
- Decreased output of diverse TCR repertoires with age
- Restricted peripheral TCR diversity contributes to immune senescence

## Antigen Recognition and Signaling

The TCR recognizes peptide–MHC complexes via its complementarity-determining regions. Engagement triggers Lck phosphorylation of CD3 ITAMs, recruitment/activation of ZAP-70, and downstream cascades (NF-AT, AP-1, NF-κB) that drive proliferation, cytokine production, and effector differentiation. Recognition of self-peptide–MHC in the thymus eliminates or tolerizes autoreactive clones, underpinning self-[[Immunity]] without autoimmunity.

## Selection in the Thymus

### Positive selection (cortex)
- Thymocytes that recognize self-peptide/MHC with adequate affinity survive
- Mediated by cortical [[Thymic Epithelial Cells|TECs]]
- Determines CD4 vs CD8 lineage commitment

### Negative selection (medulla)
- Thymocytes highly reactive to self-antigens are eliminated
- Mediated by medullary TECs expressing tissue-restricted antigens (AIRE-dependent)
- Establishes central self-tolerance

## Age-Related Changes

- Reduced TCR gene rearrangement capacity with [[Thymic Involution|thymic involution]]
- Restricted peripheral TCR repertoire diversity
- Increased oligoclonal expansion of memory T cells
- Decreased naïve T cell output

## Biological and Clinical Relevance

TCR specificity defines helper, cytotoxic, regulatory, and memory T-cell functions and is essential for clearing intracellular pathogens and tumors. TCR-engineered T cells (TCR-T therapy) target intracellular tumor antigens; soluble TCR mimics and TCR sequencing are used in diagnostics and immunomonitoring. Defective TCR signaling causes immunodeficiency, while aberrant selection contributes to autoimmunity and to escaping [[Cancer]] immune surveillance.
