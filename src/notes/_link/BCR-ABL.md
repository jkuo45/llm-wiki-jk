# BCR-ABL

**BCR-ABL** is the constitutively active tyrosine kinase produced by the t(9;22) Philadelphia translocation fusing BCR to [[c-ABL]]. It drives chronic myeloid leukemia via RAS/MAPK, PI3K/AKT, and STAT5 survival signaling. Imatinib inhibition of BCR-ABL pioneered targeted [[Cancer]] therapy; T315I gatekeeper mutation necessitates ponatinib or asciminib.
