# Candida albicans

Candida albicans is an opportunistic fungal pathogen. Its filamentous hyphae are too large for [[Phagocytosis]], so [[Neutrophils]] release [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps]] instead; about eight neutrophils engage one 100-um hyphal filament, with extracellular [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] deployment and strong [[IL-1β]] secretion (Branzk et al. 2014; Warnatsch et al. 2017). Beta-glucan of C. albicans immobilised by [[Fibronectin]] is recognised by [[Complement Receptor 3]], inducing robust [[Vital NETosis]] (Byrd et al. 2013).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Neutrophils from MPO-deficient patients cannot form NETs on exposure to C. albicans, whereas partial MPO deficiency permits NET formation (Metzler et al. 2011; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
