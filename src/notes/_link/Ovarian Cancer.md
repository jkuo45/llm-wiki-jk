# Ovarian Cancer

**Ovarian cancer** encompasses a heterogeneous group of malignancies arising from the ovary, fallopian tube, or peritoneum. Epithelial ovarian cancer is the most common histological type and the leading cause of gynecological cancer mortality.

## Pathophysiology

Ovarian cancer cells exhibit metabolic reprogramming and rely on mitochondrial function for energy production and biosynthesis. [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|Mitochondrial fission and fusion]] are dysregulated in ovarian cancer, contributing to chemoresistance and metastatic potential.

## Clinical Features

Often presents at advanced stage with vague symptoms (bloating, pelvic pain, ascites). Treatment involves cytoreductive surgery and platinum-based chemotherapy. PARP inhibitors are effective in BRCA-mutated tumors.
