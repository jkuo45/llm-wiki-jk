# G6PD

Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase (G6PD) is the rate-limiting enzyme of the [[Pentose Phosphate Pathway]], catalyzing the oxidation of glucose-6-phosphate to 6-phosphoglucono-δ-lactone while reducing NADP+ to [[NADPH]]. This reaction supplies reducing equivalents that defend against oxidative stress and support biosynthesis.

Beyond its canonical metabolic role, G6PD has been identified in the vault as a regulator of [[PINK1]] protein stability. According to [[PINK1]], G6PD stabilizes the PINK1 protein and modulates [[Mitophagy]] through a mechanism that is independent of the pentose phosphate pathway's NADPH-generating activity. This suggests a moonlighting, scaffolding, or protective function distinct from its enzymatic role in carbohydrate metabolism.

The dual involvement of G6PD in both redox homeostasis and mitophagy control positions it at the intersection of metabolic state and mitochondrial quality control. Loss of G6PD activity could therefore compromise both antioxidant defense and the PINK1/Parkin mitophagy axis, potentially sensitizing cells to mitochondrial damage.
