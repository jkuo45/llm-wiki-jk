# Imiquimod

**Imiquimod** is a small-molecule imidazoquinoline that acts as an agonist of
**Toll-like receptor 7** (TLR7). It was discovered at 3M Pharmaceuticals and
first FDA-approved in 1997; it is now generic. Applied topically, it does not
kill the target cells directly — it recruits the local innate immune system to
do so.

## Mechanism

> [!info] Mechanism
> TLR7 is an endosomal receptor for ssRNA, and imiquimod is a mimetic of the
> uridine/guanosine-rich region of viral RNA. Receptor engagement drives
> [[MyD88]]-dependent activation of [[IRF3]] and [[IRF7]], producing a strong
> local [[Interferon|interferon-alpha]] response. Keratinocytes,
> [[Dendritic cells]] (Langerhans cells in skin), and [[B Cells]] are all
> activated. Langerhans cells mature and migrate to draining lymph nodes,
> converting a topical treatment into a genuine adaptive antitumour response.
>
> A second, mechanistically distinct action is the **upregulation of the opioid
> growth factor receptor (OGFr)**. Antagonising OGFr — with siRNA or with
> naltrexone — abolishes imiquimod's antitumour activity, indicating OGFr is
> required, not incidental.

Downstream cytokine output includes [[TNFα]], [[IL-6]], [[IL-1β]], IL-8/CXCL8,
and TNF-related apoptosis-inducing ligand. The recruitment of
[[Natural Killer Cells]] and [[Macrophages]] adds cytotoxic capacity.

> [!warning] Clinical caveat
> A large fraction of the local erythema, erosion, crusting, and burning that
> patients complain of is the pharmacological effect, not a hypersensitivity
> reaction — the lesion is inflamed because the drug is working. Grade 3–4
> local reactions occur in a substantial minority. Systemic flu-like symptoms
> are likewise cytokine spillover. Conversely, imiquimod is **contraindicated
> in organ-transplant recipients** on immunosuppressants, because its immune
  stimulation can precipitate graft rejection.

## Indications and evidence base

- **5% cream**: external genital and perianal warts (condylomata acuminata);
  superficial basal cell carcinoma; actinic keratoses of the face or scalp when
  cryotherapy is contraindicated or impractical.
- **3.75% cream**: actinic keratosis of the full face or balding scalp.

The wart indication rests on clearing HPV-infected keratinocytes through
induced apoptosis and immune recruitment, not on direct antiviral activity —
imiquimod has no direct effect on HPV replication. Clearance rates for
genital warts are in the 30–70% range depending on endpoint and regimen, and
recurrence after clearance is common, reflecting reactivation of latent HPV in
the basal epithelium rather than treatment failure.

Systemic imiquimod (oral, for recurrent genital warts) was tested and failed to
meet efficacy endpoints; topical use is the established route.

## Applications beyond dermatology

> [!info] Emerging uses
> Intralesional and topical imiquimod has been trialled in cervical, vulvar,
> and anal intraepithelial neoplasia and in basal cell carcinoma, where it is
> attractive because it is non-surgical and avoids the morbidity of excision.
> It has also been studied as an adjuvant to therapeutic cancer vaccination,
> where the local IFN-α burst is the point rather than a side effect. These
> indications remain off-label and the trials are small; evidence is strongest
> for the on-label dermatological uses.

## Connections

- [[PAMP]] — Imiquimod is a synthetic PAMP mimetic: it is a drug shaped to
  fool a receptor whose normal job is detecting viral nucleic acid. It is one
  of the clearest pharmacological demonstrations that TLR7 signalling can be
  hijacked into therapeutic [[Innate Immunity]]. The stub's inbound document
  link is retained in the Documents section.

- [[Toll-like Receptor]] — Imiquimod's efficacy and toxicity both trace to TLR7
  agonism. It is one of the few clinically approved TLR agonists, and a
  reference point for the whole innate-immunity-modulating drug class.

- [[IRAK4]] — TLR7 signals obligatorily through MyD88 and IRAK4 to reach
  IRF3/IRF7. IRAK4 inhibitors are a systemic version of what topical imiquimod
  does locally, and the safety comparison between the two is an active question.

- [[Interferon]] — Type I interferon is the dominant effector cytokine of
  imiquimod's response and is what produces the antiviral and antitumour
  effect. The drug was conceived as a interferon inducer before its receptor
  was identified.

- [[Apoptosis]] — Keratinocyte apoptosis is the proximate lesion-level outcome
  in warts. The mechanism is not direct cytotoxicity but interferon- and
  TNF-driven death signalling in virus-infected cells.

- [[Human Papillomavirus]] — Genital warts are HPV (types 6 and 11
  predominantly) driven. Imiquimod clears the lesions via immunity, not by
  inhibiting viral replication, which is why recurrence is frequent.

- [[Skin]] — Local Langerhans-cell activation and migration to lymph nodes is
  the step that converts a topical drug into a systemic immunological memory
  response; without it, imiquimod would be an irritant.
