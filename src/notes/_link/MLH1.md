# MLH1

**MLH1** (MutL homolog 1) pairs with PMS2 to excise base mismatches and insertion/deletion loops left by [[DNA Polymerase]] during replication. Germline MLH1 mutation causes Lynch syndrome (hereditary nonpolyposis colorectal cancer); somatic MLH1 promoter methylation drives sporadic microsatellite-unstable colorectal and endometrial cancers, which respond to checkpoint blockade.
