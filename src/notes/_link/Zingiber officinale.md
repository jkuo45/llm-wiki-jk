# Zingiber officinale

**Zingiber officinale** is the rhizome of ginger. Its activity is driven by the non-volatile phenolic alkanones **6-gingerol**, **6-shogaol**, and **6-paradol**; the pungent volatile fraction is distinct from this bioactive phenolic fraction.

## Chemical Composition

- **6-gingerol** — major rhizome phenolic alkanone; the standardising analyte for extracts.
- **6-shogaol** — dehydrated derivative of 6-gingerol, formed by heating or prolonged storage.
- **6-paradol** — desmethoxy analog of 6-gingerol with its own bioactivity profile.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways (In Vitro)

- **TRAIL sensitisation via NF-κB/cIAP1**: 6-gingerol enhances TRAIL-induced viability reduction of gastric cancer cells by inhibiting TRAIL-induced NF-κB activation while down-regulating cIAP1 (Ishiguro, Biochem Biophys Res Commun 2007, PMID 17706603).
- **Microtubule damage and mitotic arrest**: 6-shogaol alone reduces viability of gastric cancer cells by microtubule damage and mitotic arrest (Ishiguro 2007).
- **Autophagy flux and p53/ROS sensitisation**: 6-shogaol attenuates autophagy flux in Huh7 cells and sensitises to TRAIL via p53 and ROS production (Nazim & Park, Int J Mol Med 2019).
- **IC50 (Pei 2021)**: 6-shogaol — HeLa 25.68 µM, SiHa 37.52 µM.
- These data are cell-line specific; no human trials have quantified the TRAIL effects of 6-gingerol or 6-shogaol. Han 2015 additionally found that 6-gingerol did not enhance TRAIL-induced apoptosis or down-regulate cFLIP(L) in renal carcinoma Caki cells — consistent with the TRAIL sensitisation being a 6-shogaol effect in that study set.

## Clinical Notes

- **Feverfew parthenolide trial (Curry 2004)**: Phase I trial of feverfew parthenolide; long-outcome reported as tolerated, with no detectable plasma parthenolide.
- **ACT001 (dimethylamino-micheliolide, DMAMCL)** — synthetic parthenolide-family analogue (verified: fumarate salt form of micheliolide, guaianolide sesquiterpene lactone, CAS 1403357-81-2, PubChemLite CID 52939461; orphan drug, FDA and EU).
  - Phase 1 GBM (Lickliter 2021, J Clin Oncol; 1 complete remission ongoing 27 months from starting ACT001; 3 stable disease lasting ≥ 6 months). Pediatric advanced brain/solid tumor trial, activity in diffuse midline glioma (Ziegler 2021 Neuro-Oncology; PMC11183933).
  - Verified mechanisms (search): PAI-1/PI3K/AKT inhibition (Cell Death Dis 2019; CISMeF); IKKβ/NF-κB inhibition, MnSOD down, ROS generation (J Mol Med 2020); STAT3 inhibition, PD-L1 reduction (Theranostics 2020; NSCLC 2025); G2/M arrest.
  - Crosses the BBB (CISMeF definition; Theranostics 2020). Well tolerated.

## Evidence Tiers & Gaps

- Tier 4 (in vitro): Ishiguro 2007 gastric cancer cells (6-gingerol NF-κB/cIAP1; 6-shogaol microtubule/mitotic arrest); Huh7 (6-shogaol autophagy/p53/ROS); Caki cells (6-shogaol, Han 2015); HeLa/SiHa IC50 (Pei 2021).
- Minimal clinical data: Curry 2004 tolerance outcome; ACT001 phase 1 GBM (Lickliter 2021; Ziegler 2021) reported tolerability and limited efficacy outcomes.
