# H3K18ac

H3K18ac is the acetylation of histone H3 at lysine 18, a mark associated with active enhancers, promoters, and p300-target regions. In senescence, H3K18ac is one of the acetyl marks enriched at senescence-activated [[Super-enhancer|super-enhancers]] that drive [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]] gene expression, as reviewed in the npj Aging survey (Dasgupta et al., 2024; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-024-00172-2).

## Definition

H3K18ac neutralizes the lysine positive charge, loosening chromatin and providing a binding surface for bromodomain readers. It is often co-deposited with other enhancer acetyl marks and is a readout of p300/CBP activity at active regulatory elements.

## Writer, Reader, Eraser

- **Writer:** p300/CBP histone acetyltransferases install H3K18ac at active enhancers and SEs.
- **Reader:** Bromodomain-containing proteins, including [[BRD4]] and other BET-family members, recognize acetylated lysines.
- **Eraser:** Class I/IIa HDACs remove the mark.

## Role in SASP and Senescence

Senescence-activated super-enhancers are enriched in [[H3K4me1]] and multiple histone acetyl marks — [[H3K27ac]], H3K18ac, [[H3K122ac]], and [[H4K5ac]] (Sen et al., 2019, PMID 30753809). These SEs actively transcribe enhancer RNAs, recruit [[P300]], and directly drive senescence-specific gene expression, including key SASP factors. H3K18ac therefore contributes to the open, transcriptionally competent state of SASP enhancers and cooperates with the [[AP-1]] pioneer factor and BRD4 reader to sustain SASP output.

## Mechanistic Details

H3K18ac is part of the acetyl-mark "code" that defines SA-SEs. Because SEs operate via [[Enhancer-Promoter Looping|enhancer-promoter looping]] within [[Topologically Associating Domain|TADs]], the H3K18ac-rich enhancer landscape is the physical substrate that recruits the transcription machinery to SASP promoters. Loss of the buffering HDAC4 (a reader recruited to acetylated loci) upon senescence unleashes the acetyl-rich program.

## Clinical and Therapeutic Relevance

BET/bromodomain inhibitors that recognize acetyl marks (including H3K18ac) and p300 inhibitors are candidate [[Senomorphic|senomorphics]] that could dampen SASP (see [[KDM4]], [[DOT1L]], [[H3K27ac]]).
