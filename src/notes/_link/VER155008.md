# VER155008

**VER155008** is a selective inhibitor of [[HSPA8]] (HSC70), a constitutively expressed member of the [[HSP70|heat shock protein 70 family]] that bridges [[ATM]] and [[p38 MAPK]] signaling in senescent cells.

## Senomorphic Activity

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> In senescent PSC27 cells, VER155008 concentration-dependently dampens canonical [[SASP|SASP]] factors and suppresses the cascade of p38 MAPK activation and PI3K/Akt/mTOR engagement triggered by genotoxic stress — phenocopying [[Apigenin]]'s senomorphic effect. Both agents also reverse IκBα degradation and NF-κB (p65/p50) nuclear translocation.

VER155008 is therefore a pharmacological tool confirming that disrupting HSPA8 function is sufficient to suppress the SASP, independent of senolysis.
