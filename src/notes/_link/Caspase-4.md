# Caspase-4

**Caspase-4** is a human inflammatory (non-canonical) caspase and the functional ortholog of mouse [[Caspase-11]], and a paralog of human [[Caspase-5]]. It is a cytosolic sensor of Gram-negative bacterial lipopolysaccharide (LPS): direct binding of LPS to the caspase-4 CARD induces oligomerization, auto-cleavage, and activation. Once active, caspase-4 cleaves [[Gasdermin D]], releasing its N-terminal pore-forming domain to assemble plasma-membrane pores that drive [[Pyroptosis]] and the release of inflammatory mediators. Caspase-4 also cooperates with [[Caspase-1]] to license maturation of IL-1β/IL-18, and contributes to [[IL-1α]] processing relevant to the [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]].

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

- **LPS sensing**: Caspase-4 binds cytosolic LPS via its CARD, causing oligomerization and activation (the human counterpart of the mouse caspase-11 LPS-sensing axis; Shi et al., Nature 2014/2015; PMID 26375003, 25686114).
- **Gasdermin D cleavage**: Active caspase-4 cleaves [[Gasdermin D]] at its linker, freeing the N-terminal pore-forming domain that oligomerizes in the plasma membrane to form lytic pyroptotic pores.
- **IL-1α processing**: Caspase-4 cleaves [[IL-1α]], an alarmin that can transit from the cell surface to the nucleus in a copper/calcium-dependent manner, shaping SASP composition.
- **Caspase-1 cooperation**: Gasdermin-D pores and K+ efflux license canonical [[Caspase-1]] activation, amplifying IL-1β/IL-18 maturation.

## Role in Senescence

- Caspase-4 (with its human paralog [[Caspase-5]] and mouse ortholog [[Caspase-11]]) is essential for certain IL-1α activation events within the SASP, linking non-canonical inflammasome signaling to SASP regulation.
- Through gasdermin-D pores it can enforce an inflammatory, lytic cell state that overlaps functionally with senescence-associated inflammation and [[Inflammaging]].

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- Caspase-4 is a central mediator of pyroptosis in response to cytosolic LPS and a candidate target in autoinflammatory and septic syndromes.
- Inhibitors of caspase-4/gasdermin-D may modulate the SASP and attenuate age-related inflammation.
