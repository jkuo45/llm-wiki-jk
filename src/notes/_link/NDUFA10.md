# NDUFA10

NDUFA10 is a subunit of [[Mitochondrial Complex I]] (NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase), the first and largest complex of the [[Oxidative Phosphorylation]] electron transport chain. Complex I catalyzes electron transfer from NADH to ubiquinone, coupled to proton pumping across the [[Mitochondria|inner mitochondrial membrane]] to build the electrochemical gradient that drives ATP synthesis.

According to [[PINK1]], PINK1 phosphorylates NDUFA10, a Complex I subunit, thereby regulating the bioenergetic efficiency of the respiratory chain. This identifies NDUFA10 as a downstream effector through which PINK1 can couple mitochondrial quality-control signaling to cellular energy metabolism. By modulating Complex I activity, PINK1 may fine-tune redox state and ATP production in response to mitochondrial stress, beyond its canonical role in recruiting [[Parkin]] for [[Mitophagy]].
