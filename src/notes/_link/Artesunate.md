# Artesunate

**Artesunate (ART)** is a water-soluble semisynthetic derivative of artemisinin used worldwide as an antimalarial. Preclinically it shows broad anti-cancer, antiviral, and anti-fibrotic activity, with accumulating evidence that a key mechanism is the induction of ROS-dependent [[Ferroptosis]] in tumor and injured cells.

## Chemistry & Mechanism of Action

Artemisinins contain an endoperoxide bridge that, in the presence of Fe²⁺, generates carbon-centered radicals that alkylate proteins (notably in malaria parasites). In mammalian cells, ART elevates [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] and promotes iron-dependent lipid peroxidation. In ferroptosis models, ART triggers GSH depletion, GPX4 inactivation, and accumulation of lipid peroxides; death is rescued by [[Ferrostatin-1]], [[Liproxstatin-1]], [[Vitamin E]], and iron chelators such as [[Deferoxamine]].

## Ferroptosis Relevance by Disease

- **Pancreatic cancer:** Eling et al. found ART specifically induced ROS production and activated ferroptosis in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma cells; combinations (e.g., with piperlongumine, Cotylenin A, sulfasalazine) amplified ferroptotic killing (review "Ferroptosis and tumors / Pancreatic cancer").
- **Ovarian cancer:** High ART concentrations cause ROS-dependent DNA damage, G2/M arrest, and ferroptosis-associated cell death.
- **Head and neck cancer:** ART induced ferroptosis with GSH depletion and ROS accumulation; NRF2-ARE pathway inhibition abolished resistance to ART (review "Head and neck cancer").
- **Liver fibrosis / neurodegeneration:** ART reduces fibrotic scarring (hepatocyte ferroptosis morphology) and, via ferroptosis inhibition rescue, has neuroprotective implications.

> [!info] ART as a ferroptosis inducer
> ART's endoperoxide requires iron to generate radicals—the same iron dependency that defines ferroptosis—making it a natural ferroptosis-inducing scaffold for drug development.

## Clinical Relevance

ART is safe and approved for malaria; repurposing it for ferroptosis-driven oncology ([[Pancreatic Cancer]], [[Ovarian Cancer]], [[Head and neck cancer]], [[Melanoma]]) and fibrosis is under active study. Its ferroptotic action can be potentiated by raising labile iron or combining with GPX4 inhibitors.
