# Luteoloside

[[Luteoloside]] is a flavonoid glycoside (luteolin-linked) within the broader [[Polyphenols|polyphenol]] family. It has been profiled as an [[Autophagy|autophagy]]-restoring, [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]]-reducing agent with antifibrotic and [[Senomorphic|senescence-modulating]] activity.

## Mechanism

Luteoloside exhibits antifibrotic activity by restoring autophagic flux, reducing [[Reactive Oxygen Species|oxidative stress]], and suppressing [[SASP|SASP-mediated fibroblast activation]].

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - cellular_senescence_ipf_diseases-14-00201|Cellular Senescence in IPF (Baurzhan et al., 2026)]]
> Other polyphenols, including [[Hesperetin|hesperetin]], [[Luteoloside|luteoloside]], and [[Procyanidin C1|procyanidin C1]], exhibit antifibrotic activity by restoring autophagic flux, reducing oxidative stress, and suppressing SASP-mediated fibroblast activation.

## Context in Fibrosis

Luteoloside is grouped with [[Hesperetin]] and [[Procyanidin C1]] among [[Polyphenols|polyphenol]] senotherapeutics that oppose the [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]]/[[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]]/[[SASP]] axis in IPF.
