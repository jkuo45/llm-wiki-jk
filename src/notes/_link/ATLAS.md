# ATLAS

The **ATLAS** trial (Singh et al., 2022, *Cell Reports Medicine*, NCT03464500) is a randomized controlled trial of [[Urolithin A]] supplementation in 88 middle-aged adults over 4 months.

## Study Design

- **Participants**: 88 middle-aged adults.
- **Intervention**: [[Urolithin A]] 500 or 1000 mg/day vs placebo, 4 months.
- **Outcomes**: ~12% hamstring-strength increase (500 mg/day), improved aerobic endurance (peak VO2) and 6-minute walk distance, reduced plasma acylcarnitines and C-reactive protein; muscle biopsies showed [[Parkin]]-mediated ubiquitin-proteasome enrichment (500 mg) and OXPHOS upregulation (1000 mg).

## Significance

Core functional-evidence anchor for [[Urolithin A]] as the selective-[[Mitophagy]] arm: first demonstration of strength, endurance, and anti-inflammatory benefits with muscle-biopsy target engagement ([[PINK1]]/[[Parkin]] pathway) following oral dosing.
