### Overview
Mdivi-1 (Mitochondrial Division Inhibitor 1) is a selective inhibitor of the fission GTPase [[DRP1]]. By blocking [[Mitochondrial Fission|fission]], it modulates mitochondrial dynamics and downstream pathological processes.

### Therapeutic Effects

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - The role of mitochondrial dynamics in disease]]
> Mdivi-1 attenuates cardiac dysfunction in diverse mouse cardiomyopathy models and shows therapeutic promise in neurodegeneration models (PD, AD). However, treatment of MI in pigs with Mdivi-1 did not reduce MI size or preserve cardiac function. Mdivi-1 has pleiotropic/off-target effects including cleaving L-OPA1 and altering OXPHOS complex protein expression, ultimately increasing superoxide production.

Mdivi-1 reduces PDGF-induced vascular smooth muscle proliferation and [[Atherosclerosis|Atherosclerosis]]. It ameliorates pressure-overload [[Left Ventricular Hypertrophy]] by modulating [[Mitophagy]] and angiogenesis. It also attenuates hypoxia-induced cancer cell migration and metastasis.

### Mechanism
Through DRP1 inhibition, Mdivi-1 preserves [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] and prevents excessive fragmentation linked to disease. It remains a widely used pharmacological probe for dissecting fission-dependent phenotypes.
