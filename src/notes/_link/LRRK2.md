# LRRK2

LRRK2 (Leucine-rich repeat kinase 2, encoded by [[PARK8]]) is a large multi-domain kinase that regulates [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]], [[Lysosome|lysosomal]] function, and vesicle sorting. Mutations in LRRK2, particularly the G2019S mutation, are among the most common genetic causes of familial [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's disease]].

## Role in Mitophagy

Pathogenic LRRK2 activity impairs [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]]. The kinase inhibitor [[GSK3357679A]] inhibits pathogenic LRRK2 activity and has been shown to restore mitophagy defects in LRRK2 G2019S mice, demonstrating for the first time that targeting LRRK2 can rescue mitophagy damage in vivo.
