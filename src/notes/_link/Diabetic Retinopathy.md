# Diabetic Retinopathy

**Diabetic retinopathy (DR)** is a microvascular complication of diabetes mellitus affecting the retinal capillaries. Hyperglycemia drives capillary pericyte and endothelial cell apoptosis, formation of degenerative capillaries, retinal inflammation, and pathological neovascularization, ultimately causing visual impairment and blindness.

## Sirtuin Axis

- **[[SIRT1]]**: Overexpression prevents capillary cell apoptosis and degenerative capillary formation, reduces diabetes-induced retinal inflammation, and improves visual function.

## Clinical Relevance

DR is the leading cause of blindness in working-age adults. SIRT1 activation offers a retinal-protective strategy targeting apoptosis and inflammation of the retinal vasculature.
