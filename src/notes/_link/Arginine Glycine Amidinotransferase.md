# Arginine Glycine Amidinotransferase

**Arginine:glycine amidinotransferase** (AGAT, GATM) catalyzes the first committed step of endogenous [[Creatine]] synthesis — arginine + glycine → guanidinoacetate (GAA) — mainly in kidney, completed by GAMT methylation in liver. AGAT deficiency causes cerebral creatine deficiency with intellectual disability, treatable by creatine supplementation.

## Documents

- [[Creatine Supplementation]] — endogenous synthesis via AGAT→GAMT supplies roughly half the body creatine pool.
- [[Creatine Synthesis Deficiency]] — AGAT deficiency as the first of three creatine-deficiency disorders.
