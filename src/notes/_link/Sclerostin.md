# Sclerostin

**Sclerostin (SOST)** is a glycoprotein secreted by osteocytes that binds LRP5/6 to inhibit canonical [[Wnt signaling]] and bone formation. Its activity is central to the fine control of bone mass.

## Sirtuin Regulation

- **[[SIRT1]]** deacetylates sclerostin to activate Wnt/β-catenin signaling and increase bone anabolism.
- SIRT1 knockout mice have a low bone-mass phenotype, and SIRT1 overexpression increases osteoblast osteogenesis via FoxO3a.

## Physiological Function

By antagonizing Wnt/β-catenin signaling, sclerostin restrains osteoblast differentiation and bone formation. SIRT1-mediated deacetylation dampens this brake, promoting bone accrual.

## Clinical Relevance

Sclerostin is a validated therapeutic target in osteoporosis (anti-sclerostin antibodies). SIRT1's regulation of sclerostin acetylation adds an epigenetic dimension to bone-mass control.
