# Phospholipase A2

**Phospholipase A2 (PLA2)** enzymes hydrolyze the sn-2 acyl bond of phospholipids, releasing [[Arachidonic Acid|arachidonic acid]] and lysophospholipids that feed inflammatory eicosanoid signaling. The family includes secreted (sPLA2), cytosolic (cPLA2), and the calcium-independent (iPLA2) forms.

## PRDX6 iPLA2 and the Apigenin Mechanism

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> [[PRDX6|peroxiredoxin 6]] uniquely carries an **iPLA2 (acidic calcium-independent PLA2)** activity. Apigenin suppresses this PRDX6 iPLA2 activity (not its peroxidase activity), which is the basis of its [[Senomorphic|senomorphic]] [[SASP|SASP]] suppression; the selective iPLA2 inhibitor [[MJ33]] phenocopies the effect.
