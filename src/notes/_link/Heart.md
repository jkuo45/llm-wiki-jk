# Heart

The **heart** propels circulation via atrial/ventricular [[Cardiomyocytes]] under autonomic and hormonal control. Atrial stretch releases atrial natriuretic peptide (cGMP signaling) to unload volume; cardiac mitochondria and SIRT3 set a moderate hormetic window, with narrow preconditioning dosing in [[Brain]]-comparable low-regeneration tissue.
