# SLC7A11

**SLC7A11** (solute carrier family 7 member 11), commonly known as **xCT**, encodes the catalytic light chain of the [[System Xc-]] cystine/glutamate antiporter. As the rate-limiting component for cystine import, SLC7A11 expression level is one of the strongest cellular predictors of sensitivity or resistance to [[Ferroptosis]]. High xCT confers abundant [[Glutathione]] (GSH) and therefore robust [[GPX4]]-mediated defense against [[Lipid Peroxidation]]; low or absent xCT forces cystine starvation and ferroptotic death.

## Structure & Domains

SLC7A11 is a 12-pass transmembrane protein of the SLC7 family. It contains the substrate-binding pocket that recognizes cystine and glutamate and is covalently linked via a conserved disulfide bond to the heavy chain [[SLC3A2]] (4F2hc/CD98). The SLC7A11–SLC3A2 heterodimer is the functional unit; SLC7A11 alone is retained in the ER and cannot reach the plasma membrane. Post-translational regulation (ubiquitylation, glycosylation) modulates its stability and turnover.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathway

xCT mediates the electrochemically coupled exchange of extracellular cystine for intracellular [[Glutamate]] (1:1). Imported cystine is reduced to cysteine, the limiting substrate for GSH biosynthesis. By sustaining the GSH pool, xCT directly feeds [[GPX4]] activity, the principal eliminator of phospholipid hydroperoxides. When xCT is transcriptionally repressed by [[p53]] (PMID: 25799957) or pharmacologically inhibited by [[Erastin]]/sulfasalazine, cystine import collapses, GSH falls, GPX4 fails, and [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]]-driven lipid peroxidation executes ferroptosis.

> [!info] xCT as a ferroptosis rheostat
> xCT abundance is itself regulated by the [[Keap1]]–[[NRF2]] axis (NRF2 activates SLC7A11 transcription) and by [[p62]]-mediated NRF2 stabilization, positioning xCT at the crossroads of oxidative-stress signaling and cell-death commitment.

## Physiological Function

xCT is expressed in tissues with high antioxidant demand (intestine, kidney, macrophages, neurons, activated lymphocytes). It supports cysteine homeostasis, redox buffering, and glutathione-dependent detoxification. In immunity it shapes the cytokine/glutamate milieu; in the CNS it modulates excitotoxicity.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

xCT is frequently overexpressed in cancers (e.g., [[Hepatocellular Carcinoma]], [[Breast Cancer]], [[Renal Cell Carcinoma]]) where it enforces ferroptosis resistance and therapy tolerance. It is a predictive biomarker for response to system Xc- inhibitors and to [[GPX4]]-targeted therapy. The [[MUC1]]-C/xCT/CD44v complex sustains GSH in triple-negative breast cancer. Loss of xCT sensitizes tumors but can also protect normal cells in some injury models, underscoring context-dependent roles.

> [!important] Druggability
> Sulfasalazine (an approved drug) and erastin analogs inhibit xCT. Combining xCT blockade with [[GPX4]] inhibition or [[Iron]] loading is a rational ferroptosis-inducing combination in oncology.
