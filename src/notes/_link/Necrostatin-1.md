# Necrostatin-1

**Necrostatin-1** (Nec-1) is a selective small-molecule inhibitor of [[RIPK1|receptor-interacting protein kinase 1]] (RIPK1), a key signaling node in the [[Necroptosis|necroptosis]] pathway. It is the most widely used tool compound for distinguishing necroptosis from other forms of regulated cell death.

## Mechanism of Action

Nec-1 allosterically inhibits RIPK1 kinase activity, preventing the formation of the necrosome (RIPK1–RIPK3–MLKL complex) that executes necroptotic cell death. Nec-1 does not affect apoptosis (caspase-dependent) or ferroptosis (lipid peroxidation-dependent), making it a specific pharmacological probe for necroptosis.

## Research Applications

- Cell death mechanism dissection in combination with [[Z-VAD-FMK]] (caspase inhibitor) and [[Ferrostatin-1]] (ferroptosis inhibitor)
- In vivo protection in models of [[Ischemia-reperfusion Injury]], [[Myocardial infarction]], [[Stroke]], and [[Neurodegenerative Diseases]]
- Validating RIPK1-dependent signaling in [[Inflammation]] and [[Cytokine]] release

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Necroptosis a regulated inflammatory mode of cell death|Dhuriya & Sharma 2018]]
> Nec-1 acts by blocking [[RIPK1]] phosphorylation and translocation, disrupting downstream necrosome signaling; with zVAD-fmk it is complementary across neurodegeneration models (AD: reduced Aβ/tau, improved cognition in APP/PS1; PD: survival in 6-OHDA PC12; HD: ST14A survival). After TBI it also engages Akt/mTOR anti-apoptotic/autophagy effects, and in SOD1-G93A ALS (as 7-Cl-O-Nec-1) it reduces axonal degeneration and motor dysfunction.
