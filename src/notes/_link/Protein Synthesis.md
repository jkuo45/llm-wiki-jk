# Protein Synthesis

**Protein synthesis** (translation) decodes mRNA on ribosomes at ~4 ATP equivalents per peptide bond, powered by [[ATP]] and amino-acid supply ([[Essential Amino Acids]]). Growth signals ([[mTORC1]]) license ribosome biogenesis and initiation; stress kinases ([[GCN2]], PERK) brake synthesis while permitting [[ATF4]] translation.
