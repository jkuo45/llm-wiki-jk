# Thymic Involution

**Thymic involution** is the progressive age-related shrinkage and functional decline of the [[Thymus|thymus]], resulting in reduced [[Thymopoiesis|thymopoiesis]], decreased naïve [[T Lymphocyte|T cell]] output, and impaired immune function. It is one of the most conspicuous and consistent features of mammalian aging and is a major contributor to [[Immune Aging|immune aging]].

## Timeline

- **Onset:** Begins in the first year of life
- **Puberty:** Accelerated decline coinciding with increased sex steroid production
- **Middle age (35–45):** ~3% annual decline in thymic epithelial space
- **Post-middle age:** ~1% annual decline throughout remaining life
- **Age 70:** Thymic epithelial space constitutes <10% of total thymic tissue

## Structural Changes

### Thymic epithelial space (TES)
- Progressive loss of cortical and medullary lymphoid tissue
- Decreased [[Thymic Epithelial Cells|thymic epithelial cell]] numbers and function
- Disruption of thymic architecture
- Reduced cytokine production (particularly [[IL-7]])

### Perivascular space (PVS)
- Expansion of [[Adipose Tissue|adipocytes]], peripheral lymphocytes, and stroma
- Progressive replacement of functional TES
- Altered TES:PVS ratio

## Functional Consequences

- Reduced TCR gene rearrangement capacity
- Decreased output of naïve T cells to the periphery
- Restricted [[T Cell Receptor|TCR]] repertoire diversity
- Measurable decline in [[sjTREC]] levels (three-log decline across 80-year lifespan)
- Increased susceptibility to infection, [[Autoimmune Disease|autoimmunity]], and cancer
- Impaired immune reconstitution after [[Chemotherapy|chemotherapy]], radiation, or [[HIV|HIV-1]] infection

### Impact on Immune System
- Results in a decrease in peripheral naïve adaptive immune cells
- Contributes to [[Immune Aging]] and impaired immune reactions to new antigens
- Perturbs memory responses in the [[Adaptive Immune Response]]

## Evolutionary Hypotheses

- **Energy reallocation** (Shanley et al.) — Thymic involution evolved to redirect energy from T cell production to reproduction once a diverse TCR repertoire is established
- **Peripheral selection** (Dowling & Hodgkin) — Involution permits peripheral homeostatic proliferation of a well-adapted limited clone repertoire

## Mouse Models

In BALB/c mice (aged 6–90 weeks):
- Thymus weight and cellularity decrease significantly with age
- Decrease detectable as early as 12 weeks of age
- After 35 weeks, thymus weight stabilizes at ~45% of 6-week size
- sjTREC per milligram of thymus tissue decreases significantly with age

## Relationship with Autophagy

- Strategies to rejuvenate the aged [[Immune System]] often focus on counteracting the effects of thymic involution through [[Autophagy Inducer|autophagy induction]].
- Autophagy decline contributes to thymic aging, linking the two processes in age-related immune decline.

## Therapeutic Reversal

Multiple strategies aim to reverse or attenuate thymic involution:

- **[[Sex Steroid Ablation]]** — Surgical castration or [[LHRH]] analogues restore thymic architecture
- **[[IL-7]]** — Promotes thymocyte survival and differentiation
- **[[Keratinocyte Growth Factor|KGF]]** — Stimulates TEC proliferation and differentiation
- **[[IL-22]]** — Drives TEC regeneration after injury
- **[[Human Growth Hormone|Growth hormone]]** — Enhances hematopoiesis and thymic function
- **[[Ghrelin]]** — Increases early thymic progenitors and recent thymic emigrants
