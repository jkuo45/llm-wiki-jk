# FKBP38

## Overview

FKBP38 (FK506 binding protein 38, also known as FKBP8) is an outer mitochondrial membrane protein that can induce [[Mitophagy]] independently of the canonical [[Parkin]] pathway. It represents an alternative, receptor-mediated mechanism for mitochondrial clearance that does not require ubiquitination.

## Mechanism of Action

FKBP38 mediates mitophagy through a unique mechanism:

- **LIR motif aggregation** — FKBP38 induces mitophagy through aggregation of [[LC3]] (specifically LC3A) via its [[LIR Motif]], directly recruiting autophagosomal membranes to mitochondria
- **Parkin independence** — Unlike the PINK1/Parkin pathway, FKBP38-mediated mitophagy does not require ubiquitination or Parkin recruitment
- **Direct receptor function** — FKBP38 acts as a mitophagy receptor, directly tethering mitochondria to autophagosomes

## Physiological Function

FKBP38 provides an alternative route for mitochondrial clearance when:

- The canonical PINK1/Parkin pathway is compromised
- Cells require Parkin-independent quality control
- Specific mitochondrial subsets need targeted removal

## Relationship to Other Mitophagy Pathways

FKBP38 represents one of several Parkin-independent mitophagy pathways, alongside:

- [[BNIP3L]]/NIX-mediated mitophagy
- NIX/BNIP3 receptor-mediated pathways
- Lipid-mediated mitophagy mechanisms
