# GABPβ1

SIRT7 deacetylates GABPβ1, facilitating GABP complex formation and transcriptional activation of nuclear-encoded mitochondrial genes; SIRT7 deficiency causes systemic mitochondrial dysfunction, including lactate accumulation and cardiac abnormalities (Szczęsny-Małysiak et al., 2026).
