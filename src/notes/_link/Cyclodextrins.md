# Cyclodextrins

**Cyclodextrins (CDs)** are cyclic oligosaccharides of six, seven, or eight α-D-glucopyranose units (α-, β-, γ-CD respectively), linked by α-1,4-glycosidic bonds into a truncated cone with a hydrophobic interior and hydrophilic exterior. They form inclusion complexes with lipophilic guests, improving aqueous solubility and [[Bioavailability|bioavailability]]. Most CDs are GRAS (generally recognized as safe).

## Fisetin Complexes

Fisetin was complexed with natural α/β/γ-CD and derivatives (HP-β-CD, HP-γ-CD, SBEβ-CD, SHPβ-CD, DIMEB, RAMEB, QABCD). Reported solubility enhancement ranged 1.6- to 161.9-fold; γ-CD and HP-β-CD gave the best solubility. Stability constants spanned 0.628–2309.13 M<sup>−1</sup> (strongest with SHPβ-CD).

> [!warning] Solubility up, bioactivity mixed
> Although CD inclusion raised fisetin solubility markedly, cytotoxic/[[Antioxidant|antioxidant]] activity was inconsistent — sometimes enhanced (β/γ-CD on MCF-7/Hela), sometimes diminished (RAMEB). CDs were the weakest carrier for anticancer potency among systems reviewed.
