# BDH2

## Overview

BDH2 (3-hydroxybutyrate dehydrogenase 2) is an enzyme that localizes to [[Mitochondria-Lysosome Contact Site|mitochondria–lysosome contact sites]] and synthesizes 2,5-dihydroxybenzoic acid (2,5-DHBA), an iron-binding siderophore. BDH2-generated 2,5-DHBA carries lysosomal Fe²⁺ into mitochondria. In melanoma cells, this route determines the iron available for mitochondrial metabolism and shapes cellular vulnerability to [[Ferroptosis]] across melanoma cell states.

## Mechanism

Lysosomes and mitochondria both store iron, and M-L contacts provide a route for direct transfer. At the interface, BDH2 converts its substrate to 2,5-DHBA, which chelates Fe²⁺ released from the lysosome and delivers it to mitochondria. A parallel route transmits lysosomal iron to mitochondria through [[TRPML1]] and VDAC2/VDAC3. Under basal conditions iron is efficiently shuttled between the organelles; during stress, mitochondrial dysfunction can increase oxidative stress and reduce the efficiency of Fe²⁺ import through either route, sensitizing cells to lipid peroxidation.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Mitochondria-lysosome crosstalk in health and disease]]
> Under basal conditions iron is efficiently shuttled between mitochondria and lysosomes. Stress-induced mitochondrial dysfunction can sensitize cells to [[Ferroptosis]] by increasing oxidative stress and by decreasing the efficiency of Fe²⁺ import from lysosomes, via [[TRPML1]]–VDAC2/3 or via BDH2.

## Pathophysiology

In the heart, mitochondrial outer-membrane remodeling, OPA1 processing, and lysosomal iron transfer form a potential axis: deregulated autophagy, stalled mitophagy, and impaired OPA1 processing accompany mitochondrial cardiomyopathy, while OPA1 promotes ferroptosis through its GTPase activity in mouse and human models. The review notes that lysosomal iron dynamics may link these observations, but the functional connection is proposed rather than demonstrated. In melanoma, BDH2-dependent iron transfer is documented as a determinant of ferroptosis vulnerability across cell states.

## Unresolved Questions

Whether the BDH2 and TRPML1–VDAC2/3 routes operate in the same cell types, and their relative contribution under basal versus stressed conditions, is not resolved. The link between OPA1 activity and lysosomal iron dynamics is presented as a possibility rather than an established mechanism.
