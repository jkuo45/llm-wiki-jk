# p22phox

**p22phox** (CYBA) is the obligate membrane partner of NOX1–4 [[NADPH Oxidase]] enzymes, stabilizing the catalytic subunit for superoxide generation. Loss-of-function mutations cause autosomal chronic granulomatous disease with defective microbial killing; polymorphisms modulate vascular [[Oxidative Stress]] and [[Atherosclerosis]] risk.

- [[NOX2]] — phagocyte oxidase requiring p22phox for membrane stability and ROS production.
- [[NOX4]] — constitutively active oxidase heterodimerized with p22phox.
