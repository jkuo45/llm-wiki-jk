# Human Endogenous Retrovirus

Human endogenous retroviruses (HERVs) are ancient retroviral sequences integrated into the primate germline over millions of years, now fixed in the human genome and constituting roughly 8% of human genetic material. In senescence, age-related [[DNA Methylation|DNA demethylation]] and heterochromatin decompaction reactivate HERV proviruses, producing retrovirus-like particles (RVLPs) whose RNA is reverse-transcribed and sensed by [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS–STING]] to upregulate SASP factors — and whose released particles can infect neighboring cells, spreading senescence in a paracrine manner.

## Overview

HERVs are proviral relics containing gag, pol, and env sequences, typically fragmented and replication-defective. They retain promoters and regulatory elements that, when derepressed, drive transcription of viral proteins and RVLPs. Specific HERV families (e.g., HERV-K, HERV-W) are most transcriptionally active in pathology and aging.

## Activation in Senescence

The review describes that in senescent cells, DNA demethylation and heterochromatin decompaction activate HERV provirus transcription, leading to RVLP protein synthesis (refs. 126, 127, 139). HERV expression is elevated in aged human tissues and in the serum of elderly individuals. HERV RNA is reverse-transcribed by viral reverse transcriptase in the cytoplasm of senescent cells, triggering cGAS–STING-dependent upregulation of SASP factors — paralleling the mechanism by which LINE-1 ([[LINE-1]]) cDNA activates innate immunity.

## Role in Inflammaging / SASP

HERV-derived cDNA engages cGAS–STING to induce type I interferon and SASP, contributing to [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]] across tissues. Critically, HERV viral particles released from senescent cells — or present in sera of older individuals — can infect healthy cells via their envelope protein, stimulating innate immune responses and inducing a senescence phenotype in those cells in a paracrine fashion, thereby amplifying SASP expression community-wide.

## Therapeutic Angles

Inhibition of HERV (and LINE-1) reverse transcriptase by [[Nucleoside Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitor|nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors (NRTIs)]] alleviates interferon-mediated inflammation in mouse tissues and SASP in cells (refs. 135, 136, 139). Repurposing HIV drugs such as emtricitabine (NCT04500847 for Alzheimer's disease) is proposed to blunt HERV/L1-associated inflammaging.
