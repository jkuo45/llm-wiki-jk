# Anakinra

**Anakinra** is a recombinant form of the human [[Interleukin 1 Receptor Antagonist|IL-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1Ra)]] that competitively blocks the binding of both [[IL-1α]] and [[IL-1β]] to the [[IL-1 Receptor|IL-1R1]] receptor, inhibiting IL-1-mediated inflammatory signaling. It is marketed under the brand name **Kineret**.

## Mechanism of Action

Anakinra binds the [[IL-1 Receptor|IL-1R1]] with high affinity but does not recruit the accessory protein [[IL-1RAcP]], making it a competitive antagonist that prevents activation of downstream [[MyD88]]-dependent signaling. By occupying the receptor without triggering signal transduction, anakinra blocks [[NF-κB|NF-κB]] and [[MAPK]] activation, reducing transcription of pro-inflammatory genes including *IL1B*, [[TNFα]], [[IL-6]], and [[COX-2]].

## Pharmacokinetics

- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection
- **Half-life**: 4–6 hours
- **Dosing**: Typically 100 mg once daily
- **Metabolism**: Rapidly degraded by proteolysis; not hepatically metabolized

## Clinical Indications

- **[[Rheumatoid Arthritis]]** — approved for moderate-to-severe RA refractory to DMARDs
- **[[Gout]]** — acute flares unresponsive to colchicine or NSAIDs
- **Cryopyrin-Associated Periodic Syndromes (CAPS)** — including [[Familial Cold Autoinflammatory Syndrome]] and Muckle-Wells syndrome
- **Neonatal-Onset Multisystem Inflammatory Disease (NOMID)**
- **Systemic Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis (sJIA)**
- **[[Atherosclerosis]]** — studied in the [[CANTOS Trial]] context as a proof-of-concept for IL-1 blockade

## Role in Inflammaging and Senescence

By blocking [[IL-1β]] signaling, anakinra reduces [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]] output from [[Senescent Cells]], decreasing the paracrine inflammatory burden that drives [[Inflammaging]]. This makes it a prototype [[Senomorphic Therapy|senomorphic]] agent.

## Adverse Effects

- Increased susceptibility to serious infections (especially bacterial)
- Injection site reactions (most common)
- Neutropenia (rare)
- Hypersensitivity reactions
