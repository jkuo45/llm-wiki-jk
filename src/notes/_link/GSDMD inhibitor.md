# GSDMD inhibitor

GSDMD inhibitors are tool compounds that block [[Gasdermin D]] pore formation. In neutrophils, a GSDMD inhibitor blocked PMA-induced [[Suicidal NETosis]] without affecting [[NADPH Oxidase]], [[Neutrophil Elastase]] or [[Myeloperoxidase]] function, isolating membrane permeabilisation as the required terminal step (Sollberger et al. 2018).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Because suicidal and [[Noncanonical NETosis]] converge on [[Gasdermin D]], Burgener & Schroder 2020 propose GSDMD inhibitors as candidate therapeutics for [[Thrombosis]], [[Rheumatoid Arthritis]], [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]] and [[Autoimmune Vasculitis]]; this therapeutic application remains untested in humans.
