# Inflammatory Diseases

**Inflammatory diseases** — [[Rheumatoid Arthritis]], [[Inflammatory Bowel Disease]], psoriasis, [[Atherosclerosis]] — arise when [[NF-κB]]-driven cytokine circuits ([[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]], TNF-α, IL-6) fail to resolve. [[IKKbeta]] inhibition blocks pro-inflammatory cytokine production; micronutrients such as [[Selenium]] modulate disease-associated oxidative inflammation.

## Documents

- [[IKKbeta]] — therapeutic target whose inhibition blocks NF-κB-driven cytokine production in arthritis, IBD, and psoriasis.
- [[Selenium]] — selenium status modulating oxidative inflammation in inflammatory-disease contexts.
