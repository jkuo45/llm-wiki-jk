# AKR1C1

**AKR1C1** (aldo-keto reductase family 1 member C1) is a cytosolic enzyme that catalyzes NADPH-dependent reduction of steroids, prostaglandins, and xenobiotics. It functions as a 20α-hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase and is implicated in steroid hormone metabolism and drug detoxification.

## Sirtuin Regulation & Cancer Role

- **[[SIRT2]]** suppresses NSCLC migration by deacetylating AKR1C1 and inhibiting STAT3.

## Physiological Function

AKR1C1 modulates steroid signaling and cellular detoxification. Its activity is regulated by lysine acetylation, with deacetylation by SIRT2 affecting downstream signaling in cancer cells.

## Clinical Relevance

AKR1C1 is a marker of aggressive, hormone-driven tumors and a therapeutic target. SIRT2-mediated deacetylation links this enzyme to the sirtuin axis of tumor suppression in lung cancer.
