# MBOAT2

**MBOAT2** (membrane-bound O-acyltransferase domain-containing 2) is the androgen-receptor-driven counterpart of [[MBOAT1]]: a lyso-PL acyltransferase enriching PE-MUFA over PE-PUFA to suppress [[Ferroptosis]] independently of [[GPX4]] and [[FSP1]]. Androgen blockade downregulates MBOAT2, sensitizing AR+ prostate cancers to ferroptosis induction (*Cell* 2023, PMCID PMC10330611).

Together MBOAT1 (ER-gated) and MBOAT2 (AR-gated) form the hormone-gated ferroptosis remodeling axis — the strongest vault-backed sex-dimorphic ferroptosis node and the mechanistic basis of the table's E2→MBOAT1 cell.
