# Trimethylamine N-oxide

[[Trimethylamine N-oxide|Trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO)]] is a metabolite produced from dietary choline/phosphatidylcholine and L-carnitine by gut microbiota (trimethylamine) and host hepatic [[Flavonoids|flavin]] monooxygenases (FMO3). It is a recognized promoter of [[Atherosclerosis|atherosclerosis]] and cardiovascular risk.

## SIRT1 Interaction

A JCCS study (Yin et al.) identified a mechanistic link between [[SIRT1]], gut microbiota-derived [[Trimethylamine N-oxide|TMAO]], and the inflammatory response in vascular smooth muscle cells. [[SIRT1]] may attenuate atherosclerotic plaque formation and slow disease progression by dampening inflammation and modulating SM22α expression in response to TMAO.
