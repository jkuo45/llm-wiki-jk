# HIF2α

**HIF2α** (hypoxia-inducible factor 2 alpha) is the oxygen-regulated subunit of the HIF-2 transcription factor, a paralog of [[HIF-1α]] with distinct target-gene repertoire.

## Regulation by SIRT1

- Under hypoxia, [[SIRT1]] activates HIF2α through its deacetylation and initiates hypoxic stress responses (Dioum et al., 2009).
- This is the opposite arm of SIRT1 deacetylating [[HIF-1α]] at Lys647, which inhibits it — splitting the two HIF paralogs at the same enzyme.
- As hypoxia progresses, [[NAD+]] falls and SIRT1 is inactivated, prompting a proposed HIF2α-to-HIF1α activation switch that coordinates metabolism, vascular formation, and hypoxic stress responses (Lim et al., 2010).
