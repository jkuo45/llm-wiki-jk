### Overview
Charcot-Marie-Tooth Disease Type 2A (CMT2A) is an autosomal dominant axonal peripheral neuropathy characterized by distal limb sensory and motor loss. It is the most common axonal form of CMT.

### Genetics and Mechanism
CMT2A is caused by heterozygous mutations in [[MFN2]], many of which are missense (e.g., T105M, R94W). These mutations impair axonal mitochondrial transport by disrupting the [[Miro]]/[[Milton]]/[[Kinesin]] transport complex, leading to mitochondrial depletion in distal axons and neurodegeneration.

### Overlap with Other Disorders
CMT2A can overlap clinically and genetically with [[Dominant Optic Atrophy]], another mitochondrial dynamics disorder, reflecting shared pathways of axonal and neuronal mitochondrial maintenance and [[Mitochondrial Integrity]].
