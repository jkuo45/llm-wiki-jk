# Pirfenidone

**Pirfenidone** is a synthetic anti-[[Inflammation|inflammatory]] and antifibrotic drug approved for [[Pulmonary Fibrosis|idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis]]. It inhibits [[TGFβ|TGF-β]] signaling and reduces [[Oxidative Stress|oxidative stress]].

In the fisetin senolytic screen (Yousefzadeh et al., 2018), pirfenidone was included in the 10-compound flavonoid panel at 5 μM and showed no significant reduction in [[Senescence|senescent cell]] burden in *Ercc1*<sup>−/−</sup> [[Fibroblasts|MEFs]].
