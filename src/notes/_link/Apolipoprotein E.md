# Apolipoprotein E

**Apolipoprotein E (ApoE)** is a major plasma apolipoprotein that mediates cholesterol transport and clearance via LDL receptor family members. The *APOE* gene has isoforms (ε2, ε3, ε4) with differential cardiovascular and neurodegenerative risk.

## Role in Mitochondrial Dynamics Research

[[Apolipoprotein E|ApoE]] knockout (and diabetic) mice are a widely used model in cardiovascular research. In [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial dynamics]] studies, inhibition of [[DRP1|Drp1]] (or fission) reduced endothelial dysfunction and [[Atherosclerosis|atherosclerosis]] in ApoE-knockout diabetic mice, demonstrating that excessive [[Mitochondrial Fission|fission]] contributes to lesion progression in this model.
