# Ionomycin

Ionomycin is a calcium ionophore that raises cytosolic calcium in [[Neutrophils]], activating [[PAD4]] and histone citrullination. A selective PAD4 inhibitor abrogated [[Vital NETosis]] induced by ionomycin or [[Staphylococcus aureus]] (Lewis et al. 2015), establishing ionomycin as a standard [[Ionophores|ionophore]] stimulus for the vital pathway.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Ionophores, bacterial products or elevated extracellular pH trigger mitochondrial [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]], calcium influx and calcium-activated PAD4 enzymatic function in neutrophils (Douda et al. 2015; Naffah de Souza et al. 2017; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
