# BCMA

**BCMA** (B-cell maturation antigen; gene *TNFRSF17*, CD269, at chromosome 16p13.13) is a transmembrane member of the TNF receptor superfamily that is expressed almost exclusively on late-stage B cells and [[Multiple Myeloma|plasma cells]]. It is the receptor for [[BAFF]] (TNFSF13B, also called TALL-1) and, more weakly, for APRIL — a second TNFSF13B product that binds the same receptor via heparan sulfate proteoglycans.

## Structure and ligand binding

BCMA is a type I transmembrane protein with an extracellular cysteine-rich domain, a single transmembrane segment and a short cytoplasmic tail. The extracellular domain contains a single N-terminal cysteine-rich (CRD) module that binds trimeric BAFF; the crystal structure of the soluble BAFF–BCMA complex (PDB 1OQD) showed that, unlike most TNF receptor family members, BCMA uses only one CRD for high-affinity binding, which makes it unusually sensitive to excess soluble BAFF as a decoy.

## Signaling

Ligand binding recruits the adaptor proteins TRAF1, TRAF2 and TRAF3 to the cytoplasmic tail, leading to activation of canonical and non-canonical [[NF-κB]], [[ERK]]/MAPK, [[p38 MAPK]], [[JNK]] and [[ELK1]]. The net output is survival and proliferation of the receptor-bearing B cell, not apoptosis or differentiation.

> [!info] Functional logic
> BCMA sits alongside two other BAFF receptors, BAFF-R and TACI (TNFRSF13B), and also acts as a decoy receptor that sheds a soluble form. The shed ectodomain — serum BCMA (sBCMA) — is measurable in plasma at low ng/mL concentrations in healthy people and is elevated in multiple myeloma and in primary antibody deficiencies, which is why it is used as a disease-activity biomarker.

## Clinical significance

BCMA is expressed on malignant plasma cells in multiple myeloma and on malignant B cells in some [[Lymphoma|lymphomas]]. Because normal tissue expression is confined to plasma cells, it is a well-spaced therapeutic target — loss of BCMA does not cause an obvious normal phenotype.

- **CAR T cells.** The first proof-of-concept came from Carpenter et al. (2013) and Ali et al. (2016), the latter producing remissions in myeloma patients. Two autologous BCMA CAR-T products are approved: idecabtagene vicleucel (Abecma; US 2021) and ciltacabtagene autoleucel (Carvykti; US 2022, EU 2022). Allogeneic off-the-shelf variants (e.g. ALLO-715) with a safety switch and γ-secretase-inhibitor co-administration to limit differentiation-related persistence are in trial.
- **Antibody-drug conjugates.** [[GlaxoSmithKline]]'s belantamab mafodotin (Blenrep; anti-BCMA–mcMMAF) gained accelerated US approval in August 2020, was withdrawn in 2023 after the confirmatory DREAMM-3 phase III missed its primary endpoint versus pomalidomide–dexamethasone, was reauthorized in the EU in July 2025 and reapproved in the US in October 2025 on the strength of DREAMM-7. Its signature toxicity is keratopathy, severe enough to require a REMS in the US.
- **Bispecific antibodies and naked antibodies** (T-cell engagers such as teclistamab, elranatamab and talquetamab; elotuzumab) extend the BCMA-directed modality beyond CAR-T.

> [!warning] Clinical caveat
> Antigen escape is a real mechanism of relapse after BCMA-directed therapy: a small fraction of myeloma clones down-regulate or mutate *TNFRSF17*, and some patients relapse with amplified plasma-cell numbers that are simply BCMA-negative. Gamma-secretase inhibition (e.g. nirogacestat) is used with some BCMA CAR-T constructs on the reasoning that it blocks NOTCH cleavage and preserves a less-differentiated, more persistent T-cell state.

## Connections

- [[BAFF]] — BAFF (TNFSF13B/TALL-1) is BCMA's principal ligand; binding recruits TRAF1/2/3 and activates NF-κB, ERK and p38 to sustain B-cell and plasma-cell survival. Because the same ligand signals through BAFF-R and TACI, the relative receptor expression is what determines whether a given cell responds to BAFF.
- [[Multiple Myeloma]] — BCMA is nearly uniformly expressed by the malignant plasma cells of myeloma, and its expression level tracks disease burden through soluble BCMA. It is the single most exploited antigen in myeloma therapy today.
- [[NF-κB]] — Canonical and non-canonical NF-κB activation downstream of TRAF recruitment is the dominant BCMA output and the reason BCMA signalling is pro-survival rather than pro-apoptotic.
- [[Cell Signaling]] — BCMA is a textbook case of a type I cytokine receptor whose ligand-binding module and short tail are enough to launch a multi-branch kinase cascade.
- [[BAFF]] links forward to [[B Cell|B cells]], [[TACI]] and [[APRIL]], which are the other two receptors of the same ligand axis and are the natural context for BCMA's role in humoral immunity.
