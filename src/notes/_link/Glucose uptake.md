# Glucose uptake

[[Glucose uptake]] is the transport of extracellular glucose into cells, predominantly mediated by the [[GLUT1]] family of facilitative glucose transporters and regulated by insulin/GLUT4 in peripheral tissues. Cancer cells sharply increase glucose uptake to fuel [[Glycolysis]] and biomass production (the [[Warburg Effect]]).

## Relevance to Fenbendazole

Fenbendazole reduces glucose uptake in cancer cells, lowering lactate production and reversing drug resistance (4). It activates [[p53]], driving mitochondrial p53-p21 signaling that suppresses GLUT transporter expression, and additionally impedes [[Hexokinase 2]] (4, 7). These actions collectively starve tumor cells.

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> "Fenbendazole has been found to inhibit glucose uptake, resulting in reduced lactate levels (4)... Through p53 activation, fenbendazole is believed to impede hexokinase II (HKII) (4, 7)."
