### Overview
Protein Kinase A (PKA) is a cAMP-dependent serine/threonine kinase that negatively regulates mitochondrial fission. It [[Phosphorylation|phosphorylates]] the fission GTPase [[DRP1]] at Ser637.

### Mechanism
Phosphorylation of DRP1 at Ser637 by PKA retains the protein in the [[Cytosol|cytosol]], preventing its recruitment to the [[Outer Mitochondrial Membrane]] and thereby inhibiting [[Mitochondrial Fission|fission]]. This spares mitochondria from autophagic degradation during starvation and certain forms of cell death.

### Functional Significance
By opposing fission, PKA helps preserve [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] and mitochondrial content under energy stress. Its action contrasts with pro-fission kinases such as [[ERK]] (Ser616) and CDK1 (Ser585).
