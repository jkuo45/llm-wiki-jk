# iFSP1

**iFSP1** is the first reported **FSP1 inhibitor**, identified from a ~10,000-compound screen. It is **human-specific** (hFSP1), binding a pocket centered on residue **F360** with an IC₅₀ of approximately **103 nM**.

## Mechanism & ferroptosis relevance

iFSP1 blocks FSP1's NAD(P)H-dependent reduction of ubiquinone to ubiquinol, removing the GPX4-independent radical trap. Critically, **iFSP1 alone is often insufficient** to kill cells with intact GPX4, but it **strongly synergizes with GPX4 inhibitors** (and system Xc− inhibitors) to trigger ferroptosis — consistent with the two-pillar defense model.

> [!info] Therapeutic rationale
> Because many therapy-resistant cancers rely on FSP1 for ferroptosis resistance, iFSP1 (or next-generation FSP1 inhibitors) is being explored to sensitize tumors — e.g., hepatocellular carcinoma — to ferroptosis-based therapy, often in combination rather than as monotherapy.

## Physiological & clinical relevance

- **Research tool:** Probes the FSP1–CoQ10–NAD(P)H axis and its cooperation with GPX4.
- **Oncology:** Demonstrated ferroptosis induction and immunomodulatory effects in HCC models.
