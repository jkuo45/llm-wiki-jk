# Z-VAD-FMK

**Z-VAD-FMK** is a synthetic, cell-permeable pan-caspase inhibitor that irreversibly binds to the catalytic site of multiple caspases. It is the standard pharmacological tool for determining whether cell death is [[Apoptosis|caspase-dependent apoptosis]].

## Mechanism of Action

Z-VAD-FMK contains a fluoromethylketone (FMK) moiety that reacts with the active-site cysteine of effector caspases (caspase-3, -6, -7) and initiator caspases (caspase-8, -9), forming a covalent thioether adduct that irreversibly inactivates the enzyme. The carbobenzoxy (Z) group enhances cell permeability, and the O-methyl ester protects aspartate side chains from premature hydrolysis.

## Research Applications

- Cell death mechanism dissection: failure of Z-VAD-FMK to rescue indicates non-apoptotic death (necrosis, necroptosis, ferroptosis, pyroptosis)
- Used in combination with [[Necrostatin-1]] (necroptosis inhibitor) and [[Ferrostatin-1]] (ferroptosis inhibitor) for comprehensive cell death panels
- Validating caspase dependence in developmental biology, immune cell killing, and drug toxicity studies
