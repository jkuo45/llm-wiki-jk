# eEFSec

**Overview:** **[[eEFSec]]** (EEFSEC) is the dedicated elongation factor for **[[Selenocysteine]]** insertion — the eukaryotic functional analog of bacterial SelB. It forms a ternary complex with GTP and tRNA[Ser]Sec (charged with Sec) and delivers Sec to the ribosomal A site when the ribosome encounters a recoded **[[UGA codon]]**.

## Mechanism

- Recruitment depends on **[[SECISBP2]]**, which binds the SECIS element and contacts eEFSec, tethering the delivery complex to the translating ribosome.
- eL30 additionally anchors the SECIS ribonucleoprotein complex to the large ribosomal subunit, freeing SECISBP2 for another round of insertion.
- eEFSec acts on the Sec-charged tRNA produced by the [[PSTK]]/[[SepSecS]] biosynthesis pathway and modified by [[FTSJ1]] (Um34).

> [!info]
> Source: [[Selenocysteine tRNA methylation promotes oxidative stress resistance in melanoma metastasis]]
> The SECIS–SECISBP2–eEFSec–tRNA[Ser]Sec complex delivers selenocysteine; Um34 tuning of tRNA[Ser]Sec determines recoding efficiency for a subset of selenoproteins.

## Significance

eEFSec is essential for synthesis of the entire **[[Selenoprotein]]** family; its function is complemented by the Um34 modification of tRNA[Ser]Sec, which tunes recoding efficiency for antioxidant selenoproteins.
