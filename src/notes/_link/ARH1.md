# ARH1

**ARH1** (ADP-ribosylarginine hydrolase 1, gene `ADPRH`) is the founding ARH-family [[ADP-ribosylation]] eraser. It is a ~39 kDa Mg2+-dependent hydrolase that cleaves alpha-anomeric ADP-ribose-arginine bonds, reversing arginine MARylation written by cholera-toxin-like ARTs (e.g. mammalian ARTC1), plus alpha-[[NAD+]] and [[O-acetyl-ADP-ribose]] in vitro.

> [!info] ARH1 in one sentence
> ARH1 is the Arg-specific counterpart to [[ARH3]] (Ser) and [[MacroD1]]/[[MacroD2]]/[[TARG1]] (Asp/Glu): it restores Arg residues for a new MARylation cycle and shows <1% of ARH3 PAR activity.

## Overview

- 362 aa (mouse) / human ortholog; 41% similarity (~20% identity) to [[ARH3]]; binuclear Mg2+ center, stereospecific for alpha-ADPr-Arg made from beta-[[NAD+]].
- Family: ARH1/ARH2/ARH3; ARH2 (ADPRHL1) catalytically inactive.
- Widespread tissue expression; intracellular compartment while ART1 acts extracellularly, implying translocation or vesicle-mediated substrate access.
- Structure: ARH fold distinct from macrodomain; ADP-ribose complex (PDB 6G28) shows cation/Mg2+ coordination.

## Structure & Catalytic mechanism

ARH-fold hydrolase with vicinal-aspartate Mg2+ coordination (homologous to ARH3 Asp77/78, Asp314/316 architecture). Cleaves the N-glycosidic Arg-ADPr bond and O-linked alpha-NAD+/OAADPr bonds; no activity on Cys/Asn/diphthamide linkages. Only trace activity on [[PAR]] glycosidic bonds (<1% of [[ARH3]]). Product ADP-ribose competes for the active site.

## Physiological function

- **Arg-MAR cycle with ART1:** opposes ARTC1-mediated Arg-MARylation; cholera-toxin ADP-ribosylarginine is the discovery substrate; `Arh1`-KO mice hypersensitive to cholera toxin.
- **Membrane repair:** with ART1, regulates TRIM72 (MG53) ADP-ribosylation cycle in heart/skeletal muscle — MARylated TRIM72 recruited to injury sites, ARH1 de-MARylation promotes oligomerization and vesicle recruitment for repair (JCI Insight 2017).
- **Secondary processing:** Arg-ADPr can mature to phosphoribosyl-Arg or ornithine; ARH1 full removal regenerates free Arg.

## Pathology & Clinical relevance

- **Tumor suppressor:** heterozygous and KO mice develop spontaneous tumors (lymphomas, carcinomas); human `ADPRH` locus alterations reported in cancers — consistent with unrepaired Arg-MAR dysregulation.
- **Cardiomyopathy:** `Arh1`-null hearts show fibrosis, blunted dobutamine response, and increased ischemia/reperfusion susceptibility; TRIM72 R207K/R260K non-MARylatable mutants impair repair-complex assembly.
- Inhibitors of mono-MARylation (vitamin K1, novobiocin) block TRIM72 oligomerization, phenocopying ARH1-cycle disruption; selective ARH1 modulators not yet clinical.
