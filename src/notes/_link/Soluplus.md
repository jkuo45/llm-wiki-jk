# Soluplus

[[Soluplus]] is a amphiphilic graft copolymer (polyvinyl caprolactam–polyvinyl acetate–polyethylene glycol) used to formulate low-toxicity polymeric micelles that encapsulate poorly soluble drugs for parenteral delivery.

## Role in Fenbendazole Repurposing

Fenbendazole-loaded Soluplus micelles showed lower total clearance and volume of distribution in vitro pharmacokinetic studies, supporting improved delivery (60).

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> "Fenbendazole was encapsulated in low-toxicity Soluplus[®] micelles for injection... lower total clearance and volume of distribution" (60).
