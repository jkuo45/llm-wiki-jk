# WNT16B

**WNT16B** is a Wnt family ligand that is prominently induced and secreted by senescent/therapy-damaged stromal cells as part of the [[SASP|SASP]], where it promotes paracrine therapy resistance in adjacent cancer cells.

## In the Apigenin Study

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> WNT16B was among the canonical SASP factors (with [[IL-6]], [[IL-8|CXCL8]], [[IL-1α]]/[[IL-1β]], [[MMP1|MMP1/3]]) whose expression declined dose-dependently upon apigenin treatment of senescent cells. Prior work established WNT16B as a stromal SASP factor conferring prostate cancer therapy resistance[26].
