# DRP1

**DRP1** (Dynamin-Related Protein 1; gene *DNM1L*) is a cytosolic dynamin-like GTPase that executes [[Mitochondrial Fission|mitochondrial fission]] in mammals. Recruited to the [[Outer Mitochondrial Membrane|outer mitochondrial membrane]] by adaptor proteins, it oligomerizes into a ring, hydrolyzes GTP, and severs the organelle. Balanced against [[MFN1|MFN1]]/[[MFN2|MFN2]]/[[OPA1|OPA1]]-mediated [[Mitochondrial Fusion|fusion]], DRP1 controls mitochondrial morphology, [[Mitophagy]], and [[Apoptosis]], and is implicated in [[Parkinson's Disease]], [[Atherosclerosis]], [[Left Ventricular Hypertrophy]], and [[Cancer]].

## Structure and Domains

DRP1 contains an N-terminal GTPase domain, a middle domain, a variable (B-insert) domain, and a C-terminal GTPase-effector domain (GED). It is recruited from the [[Cytosol|cytosol]] to mitochondria by OMM adaptors [[MFF]], [[MID49]], [[MID51]], and [[FIS1]], forming a ring-like structure that constricts the organelle. After DRP1, [[DNM2|Dynamin 2 (DNM2)]] assembles at the constriction site to complete division (though DRP1 alone can constrict and sever, making DNM2 dispensable).

## Initiation by ER and Actin

Fission is initiated where [[Endoplasmic Reticulum|ER]] tubules contact mitochondria, reducing diameter to ~150 nm. The ER-bound formin [[INF2|Inverted Formin 2 (INF2)]] and mitochondria-anchored Spire1C drive actin assembly for constriction. [[Inner Mitochondrial Membrane|IMM]] constriction is [[Calcium|Ca2+]]-dependent, and [[Cardiolipin]] binds DRP1 to stimulate its oligomerization and GTPase activity.

## Regulation

- **[[CDK1]]/[[Cyclin B]]** phosphorylates DRP1 at Ser585 during mitosis, promoting fission.
- **[[ERK|ERK2 (MAPK1)]]** phosphorylates DRP1 at Ser616, activating fission — a central node in [[MAPK|MAPK]]-driven tumor growth.
- **[[Protein Kinase A]]** phosphorylates DRP1 at Ser637, retaining it cytosolic and inhibiting fission during starvation.
- **[[Calcineurin]]** dephosphorylates Ser637, enabling mitochondrial translocation and fission.
- **SUMOylation** by MAPL stabilizes ER–mitochondrial contacts and promotes cytochrome c release in apoptosis.
- **S-nitrosylation** (by [[Nitric Oxide|nitric oxide]]) and **O-GlcNAcylation** increase fission activity.
- **[[Ubiquitination]] by [[Parkin]]** targets DRP1 for proteasomal degradation.

## Pathological Significance

- **[[Parkinson's Disease]]** — excess fission via OPA1 cleavage and DRP1 hyperphosphorylation; DRP1 inhibition reduces fragmentation and ROS in sporadic PD.
- **[[Atherosclerosis]]** — DRP1 inhibition (or [[Mdivi-1]]) reduces endothelial dysfunction and lesion progression.
- **[[Left Ventricular Hypertrophy]]** — DRP1 is upregulated; Mdivi-1 ameliorates dysfunction.
- **[[Cancer]]** — ERK/DRP1 Ser616 signaling and hypoxia-driven DRP1 promote tumorigenesis and [[Metastasis|metastasis]]; [[Mdivi-1]] attenuates migration.
