# SREBP2

[[SREBP2]] (sterol regulatory element-binding protein 2; gene *SREBF2*) is a membrane-bound basic helix-loop-helix leucine zipper transcription factor that controls the cholesterol arm of lipid homeostasis, activating genes of cholesterol biosynthesis and LDL-receptor-mediated uptake. It is activated by SREBP-cleavage-activating protein (SCAP) when sterol levels are low and cleaved by S1P/S2P proteases to release the active N-terminal domain.

## SIRT6 regulation

[[SIRT6]] negatively regulates the lipogenic transcription factors [[SREBP-1c|SREBP1]] and SREBP2 via multiple mechanisms, restraining de novo lipogenesis and cholesterol synthesis. Loss of hepatic SIRT6 accordingly produces liver steatosis, reduced fatty acid oxidation, and elevated expression of lipogenic genes ([[_document_ - Sirtuins Guardians of Mammalian Healthspan|Sirtuins: Guardians of Mammalian Healthspan, Giblin et al. 2014]], refs 94–96).

> [!info] Lipid-homeostasis brake
> SIRT6 suppression of [[SREBP-1c|SREBP1]]/[[SREBP2]] couples sirtuin activity to control of lipogenesis and cholesterol metabolism.
