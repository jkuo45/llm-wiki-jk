# VLA-4

**VLA-4** (very late antigen-4) is the [[Integrin|integrin]] heterodimer **α4β1**, also
known by its CD nomenclature as **CD49d/CD29**. It sits on essentially all leukocytes except
[[Neutrophil|neutrophils]] and is the dominant firm-adhesion receptor for T lymphocytes
crossing activated endothelium. It is also the pharmacological target of
[[Natalizumab (Tysabri)|natalizumab]].

## Ligands

- **VCAM-1** (CD106) on inflamed vascular endothelium — the critical pairing for transendothelial
  migration. Notably, VCAM-1 binding occurs at a site on α4 distinct from the fibronectin
  binding site, which is why a single antibody can block one and spare the other.
- **Fibronectin**, via the alternatively spliced CS-1 (connecting segment-1) domain —
  supports migration *within* tissue after extravasation, and provides co-stimulatory signals
  for CD3-dependent T-cell activation.
- **Osteopontin** — a third ligand with a partly distinct site, on the N-terminal domain
  near Asn89/90.

The α4 subunit also pairs with β7 to form **α4β7 (LPAM-1)**, which binds **MAdCAM-1** on gut
endothelium rather than VCAM-1. Because natalizumab blocks the shared α4 subunit, it
inhibits both receptors at once — the basis of its efficacy in both multiple sclerosis and
Crohn disease.

## Mechanism of leukocyte extravasation

Extravasation proceeds in ordered steps, and VLA-4 governs the third:

1. **Rolling** — mediated by [[E-selectin]] and [[P-selectin]]; weak, reversible.
2. **Firm adhesion** — chemokines presented on the endothelium, binding CCR2 and other
   GPCRs, trigger *inside-out* integrin activation, raising VLA-4 affinity. VLA-4 then binds
   VCAM-1 and arrests the cell. Unlike LFA-1/ICAM-1, VLA-4 carries organ tropism: brain-tropic
   effector T cells upregulate α4β1, gut-tropic cells upregulate α4β7.
3. **Transmigration** — VLA-4/VCAM-1 signals back into the endothelium via Rac-1, destabilising
   endothelial junctions and opening a paracellular gap.

VLA-4 also carries an anti-apoptotic role in T lymphocytes, so antibody blockade does not
merely block adhesion — it renders lymphocytes more apoptosis-prone.

## Pharmacology

**Natalizumab** is a humanised IgG4κ monoclonal antibody against the α4 subunit. Because it
binds an epitope adjacent to but *outside* the ligand-binding groove, antagonism is
**non-competitive**: the Fab does not occlude VCAM-1 binding, and the complex requires a
change in the relative orientation of VCAM-1's Ig-like domains 1 and 2.

The foundational experiment was Yednock et al. (*Nature* 1992): an anti-α4β1 antibody
prevented experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis by blocking leukocyte infiltration into
brain. That result produced the drug.

> [!warning] Clinical caveat: PML
> Blocking α4 removes a surveillance mechanism. Natalizumab monotherapy carries a boxed
> warning for progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy from reactivation of the
> John Cunningham virus, and is associated with anti-JCV antibody positivity and natalizumab
> exposure duration. It also carries a warning for increased mortality when added to
> bortezomib plus dexamethasone in multiple myeloma. Because it is an IgG4 with reduced
> Fc receptor binding and impaired complement fixation, its efficacy is thought to be
> adhesion-blocking rather than Fc-mediated depletion, which is why monotherapy is preferred.

Non-antibody approaches under or in development include the small-molecule α4 antagonists
firategrast and BIO5192, and ATL1102, an antisense oligonucleotide against the α4 chain.

## Connections

- [[Natalizumab (Tysabri)]] — the clinical proof that VLA-4 blockade works; its efficacy and
  PML risk both follow directly from VLA-4's role.
- [[VCAM-1]] — the endothelial counter-receptor whose engagement by VLA-4 produces firm
  adhesion and drives the transmigration signalling.
- [[Integrin]] — VLA-4 is a member of this adhesion-receptor family, using inside-out and
  outside-in signalling like all integrins.
- [[Fibronectin]] — the matrix ligand that supports post-extravasation migration along CS-1.
- [[Multiple Sclerosis]] — the disease for which VLA-4 blockade is approved; brain-tropic
  T cells depend on α4β1 to cross the blood-brain barrier.
- [[Blood-Brain Barrier]] — the barrier VLA-4-positive T cells breach in MS; VCAM-1 is
  expressed by pericytes and astrocytes as well as endothelium, which additionally permits
  trans-barrier migration.
- [[Atherosclerosis]] — monocyte VLA-4/VCAM-1 engagement initiates NF-κB activation in both
  cell types, driving IL-1β from monocytes and IL-6 from endothelium in atheroma.
- [[Inflammation]] — VLA-4 is the general-purpose firm-adhesion step of leukocyte recruitment
  in most acute and chronic inflammatory diseases.
