# PEX10

**PEX10** is a RING-finger ubiquitin ligase forming the importomer RING complex with [[PEX2]] and [[PEX12]], ubiquitinating the [[PEX5]] receptor for recycling after peroxisomal cargo import. Biallelic PEX10 variants cause [[Zellweger Spectrum Disorders]].
