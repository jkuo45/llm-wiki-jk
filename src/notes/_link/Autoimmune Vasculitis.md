# Autoimmune Vasculitis

Autoimmune vasculitis is a group of autoimmune diseases characterised by vessel-wall inflammation. [[Vital NETosis]] is often observed during autoimmune vasculitis, as well as [[Thrombosis]], [[Rheumatoid Arthritis]] and [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]] (Clark et al. 2007; Kessenbrock et al. 2009; Pilsczek et al. 2010; Yipp et al. 2012; Martinod et al. 2016).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Because suicidal and noncanonical NETosis converge on [[Gasdermin D]], Burgener & Schroder 2020 suggest [[GSDMD inhibitor|GSDMD inhibitors]] may have therapeutic potential in NET-associated diseases including autoimmune vasculitis; this therapeutic application remains untested in humans.
