# dNTP

**dNTPs** (deoxyribonucleoside triphosphates) are the four nucleotide building blocks of [[DNA]]: **dATP**, **dGTP**, **dCTP**, and **dTTP**. They differ from ribonucleoside triphosphates only by the missing 2′-hydroxyl on the deoxyribose sugar, yet this single difference is what makes DNA chemically stable. dNTPs are synthesized *de novo* by [[Ribonucleotide Reductase]] (RNR) and consumed stoichiometrically by DNA polymerases during [[DNA Replication]] and [[DNA Repair]]; their supply is rate-limiting, tightly regulated, and a prime anticancer drug target.

## Biosynthesis

- **De novo pathway**: the cellular ribonucleotide pool (NDPs) is reduced at C2′ by [[Ribonucleotide Reductase]] to yield dNDPs, then phosphorylated to dNTPs by nucleoside diphosphate kinase. RNR is the rate-limiting, allosterically committed step (ATP activates; dATP inhibits; effector nucleotides balance the four substrates).
- **Salvage pathway**: deoxynucleosides rescued by kinases (e.g., deoxycytidine kinase, thymidine kinase) replenish pools independently of RNR.
- **Degradation**: SAMHD1 is a dNTP triphosphohydrolase that destroys dNTPs in non-cycling cells — depleting the pools that retroviruses such as HIV need for reverse transcription.

## Pool Homeostasis & Genome Stability

- Cytosolic dNTP pools are **small (low µM) yet precisely balanced**; the four pools modulate each other through RNR allostery so supply matches genome base composition.
- **Imbalance** → nucleotide misincorporation → elevated mutation rates (the "mutator phenotype" seen when RNR activity is deregulated in cancer).
- **Depletion** → replication fork stalling and collapse → [[DNA Replication Stress]] → [[ATR]]/[[ATM]] checkpoint activation → [[Senescence]] or [[Apoptosis]].
- **Therapeutic exploitation**: dNTP-starving agents ([[Hydroxyurea]], [[Gemcitabine]], [[Triapine]], [[Clofarabine]]) selectively disable proliferating cancer cells; dNTP imbalance also increases incorporation of nucleoside analogs, self-potentiating their toxicity.

## Connections

- [[Ribonucleotide Reductase]] — the biosynthetic source and rate-limiting step of de novo dNTP supply.
- [[DNA]] — the macromolecule dNTPs assemble.
- [[DNA Replication]] — the S-phase process with maximal dNTP demand.
- [[DNA Repair]] — repair synthesis consumes dNTPs, especially via the p53R2 route.
- [[DNA Replication Stress]] — the consequence of dNTP shortage.
- [[ATR]] — the primary checkpoint kinase sensing dNTP-limited replication stress.
- [[S Phase]] — the cell-cycle window of RNR-driven dNTP production.
- [[Hydroxyurea]] — quenches the RNR tyrosyl radical, starving cells of dNTPs.
- [[Gemcitabine]] — nucleoside analog that inactivates RNR and depletes dNTP pools (dCTP in particular).
- [[Triapine]] — investigational iron-chelating RNR inhibitor that collapses dNTP supply.
- [[Clofarabine]] — purine analog whose diphosphate inhibits RNR, lowering dNTP pools.
