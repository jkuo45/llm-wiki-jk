### Overview
Fzo (fuzzy onion) is a Drosophila melanogaster mitochondrial outer-membrane GTPase that is the ancestral ortholog of the mammalian mitofusins [[MFN1]] and [[MFN2]]. It was among the first fusion proteins identified genetically through its "fuzzy onion"-like mitochondrial aggregation phenotype in mutant flies.

### Function
Like the mitofusins, Fzo localizes to the [[Outer Mitochondrial Membrane]] and mediates mitochondrial fusion through tethering and membrane merger. Its HR2 domain engages in trans interactions analogous to those of MFN1/MFN2 in the [[Mitochondrial Intermembrane Space]].

### Evolutionary Conservation
The conservation of Fzo from flies to the mammalian mitofusins underscores the ancient, conserved nature of mitochondrial fusion machinery, traceable to the origin of mitochondria via [[Endosymbiosis]].
