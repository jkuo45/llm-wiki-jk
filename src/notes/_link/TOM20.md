# TOM20

TOM20 (Translocase of Outer Membrane 20, also known as [[TOMM20]]) is a mitochondrial import receptor on the outer mitochondrial membrane (OMM) that functions as part of the [[TOM complex|TOM (Translocase of the Outer Membrane) complex]].

## Role in Parkinson's Disease

In [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's disease]], toxic [[Alpha-synuclein|α-synuclein (α-Syn)]] binds to the TOM20 receptor. This binding blocks protein import into [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]] by 50%–60%, triggering [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] production and the accumulation of damaged mitochondria. By interfering with mitochondrial protein import, α-Syn-mediated TOM20 inhibition contributes to [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]] and impaired [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]].
