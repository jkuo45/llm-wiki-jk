# Withaferin A

**Withaferin A** is a steroidal lactone (withanolide) isolated from *Withania somnifera* (ashwagandha), a cornerstone herb of Ayurvedic medicine. It is the most extensively studied withanolide, with reported activities spanning anti-inflammatory, anti-angiogenic, anti-tumor, and pro-apoptotic effects, often mediated through covalent modification of cysteine residues on target proteins (e.g., annexin A2, vimentin, IKKβ) and modulation of stress-responsive transcription factors such as [[NRF2]] and [[NF-κB]].

## SIRT3-dependent anti-fibrotic activity

In hepatic fibrosis models, withaferin A has been implicated in mediating anti-fibrotic effects through [[SIRT3]], and this effect is abolished in SIRT3-knockout mice — reinforcing the role of SIRT3 as an indispensable molecular effector of pharmacological anti-fibrotic agents[134],[126]. The finding places withaferin A in the class of natural compounds whose therapeutic benefit is transduced, at least in part, by SIRT3-dependent mitochondrial and redox programs, consistent with SIRT3's established role as a central suppressor of fibrogenesis across hepatic, pulmonary, cardiac, and renal models.

## Applications

- Experimental liver fibrosis models: SIRT3-dependent suppression of fibrotic progression.
- Broader research context: anti-inflammatory and anti-tumorigenic activities investigated in multiple preclinical disease models.
