# Coronary Artery Disease

**Coronary artery disease (CAD)** is the narrowing or occlusion of the coronary arteries, most commonly due to atherosclerosis, resulting in reduced myocardial perfusion, angina, myocardial infarction, and heart failure. It is the leading global cause of death.

## Sirtuin Axis

- **[[SIRT1]]**: Genetic SIRT1 polymorphisms are associated with CAD risk; expression is reduced in CAD patients and linked to acetylated p53 in CAD monocytes. The lncRNA C2dat1 promotes SIRT1 to suppress miR-34a, driving vascular smooth muscle cell proliferation and migration.
- **[[SIRT3]]** and **[[SIRT6]]**: Genetic polymorphisms associated with CAD risk (mechanism not yet elucelated).

## Clinical Relevance

CAD risk is modulated by the sirtuin network through vascular cell protection, inflammation control, and DNA-damage response. SIRT1 restoration is a candidate strategy for attenuating vascular remodeling and plaque progression.
