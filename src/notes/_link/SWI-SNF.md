# SWI-SNF

**SWI/SNF** (mammalian BAF) complexes use [[BRG1]]/BRM ATPases for [[Nucleosome Remodeling]], opening promoters and enhancers to regulate [[Gene Expression]], differentiation, and DNA repair. Subunits are mutated in ~20% of cancers (SMARCB1 in rhabdoid tumors, ARID1A in ovarian clear-cell carcinoma) and in Coffin-Siris neurodevelopmental syndrome.
