# Selenoprotein

**Overview:** **[[Selenoprotein|Selenoproteins]]** are proteins that incorporate **[[Selenocysteine]]** (Sec) as a (usually catalytic) residue. In humans ~25 selenoproteins are expressed; nearly all are **redox-active enzymes** whose Sec selenol makes them exceptionally efficient catalysts. Sec is inserted by **UGA recoding** directed by the SECIS element and [[SECISBP2]].

## Major families

- **Glutathione peroxidases ([[Glutathione Peroxidase|GPx1–4, GPx6]])** — reduce hydroperoxides using [[Glutathione]]; [[Glutathione Peroxidase 4|GPx4]] blocks [[Ferroptosis]].
- **Thioredoxin reductases ([[Thioredoxin reductase|TrxR1–3]])** — maintain the [[Thioredoxin]] system.
- **Iodothyronine deiodinases (DIO1–3)** — activate/inactivate thyroid hormone ([[Triiodothyronine]]).
- **Selenoprotein P** — [[Selenium]] transport and extracellular antioxidant defense.
- **Methionine sulfoxide reductases, selenoprotein N/H, and others** — protein repair, ER stress, redox signaling.

## Regulation and decline

- Selenoprotein expression is tuned by [[Selenium]] availability and by the [[FTSJ1]]/Um34 status of tRNA[Ser]Sec; loss of Um34 reduces translation of a subset of oxidative-stress selenoproteins (Nease et al., 2024).
- With **[[Aging]]**, selenoprotein expression and [[Glutathione]] regeneration decline alongside rising [[Reactive Oxygen Species]], contributing to redox imbalance and neurodegeneration (Pace et al., 2025).

> [!important]
> Source: [[Selenocysteine tRNA methylation promotes oxidative stress resistance in melanoma metastasis]]
> A subset of selenoproteins involved in oxidative stress requires Um34-modified tRNA[Ser]Sec for efficient translation; their loss sensitizes cells to oxidative stress and suppresses metastasis.
