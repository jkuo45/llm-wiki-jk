# Akkermansia muciniphila

**Akkermansia muciniphila** is the principal gut-dwelling species of the genus [[Akkermansia]] (phylum [[Verrucomicrobia]]). It colonizes the intestinal mucus layer, degrading host mucin glycoproteins, and is consistently associated with gut barrier integrity, metabolic homeostasis, and [[Longevity]].

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Natural Bioactive Compounds Spermidine Fisetin Berberine Urolithin A]]
> Berberine's low oral bioavailability (1–5%) yields high intraluminal concentrations that selectively reshape the microbiome, increasing beneficial taxa including Akkermansia muciniphila while reducing conditionally pathogenic taxa linked to metabolic endotoxemia (PREMOTE trial linkage).
