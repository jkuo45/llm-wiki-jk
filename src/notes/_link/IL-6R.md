# IL-6R

**Interleukin-6 receptor (IL-6R; CD126)** is the ligand-binding subunit of the receptor complex for [[IL-6]]. IL-6R itself has no intrinsic enzymatic activity: it must associate with the shared signal-transducing subunit **gp130** (IL6ST) to form a functional receptor, after which gp130-associated JAK kinases activate [[JAK-STAT Signaling|JAK–STAT]] and, secondarily, MAPK and PI3K–AKT signalling. IL-6R is the receptor arm of the autocrine [[IL-6]] loop that reinforces the [[SASP|senescence-associated secretory phenotype]], making the receptor–JAK–STAT axis a senomorphic target.

## Structure & Domains

IL-6R is a type I cytokine receptor built from three extracellular domains: an N-terminal immunoglobulin-like domain (D1), followed by two fibronectin type III domains (D2 and D3) that together form the cytokine-binding module and contain the conserved WSXWS motif. A single transmembrane helix is followed by a short cytoplasmic tail that lacks kinase activity. The receptor is a member of the gp130-utilising family, which includes the receptors for IL-11, LIF, OSM, CNTF and others.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

Signalling is initiated by a two-step assembly. IL-6 first binds IL-6R through site I; the binary complex then recruits gp130 through site II, producing a hexameric 2:2:2 (IL-6 : IL-6R : gp130) signalling complex. gp130 dimerisation brings associated JAK1, JAK2 and TYK2 into proximity, enabling transphosphorylation and activation. JAKs phosphorylate tyrosine motifs in the gp130 cytoplasmic tail, creating docking sites for STAT3 (and STAT1) via their SH2 domains. Recruited STATs are phosphorylated, dimerise and translocate to the nucleus to activate target genes including *SOCS3*, which provides negative feedback, and *PTPRD*-class phosphatases that terminate signalling. The same phosphotyrosines recruit SHP2, coupling the receptor to RAS–MAPK, and PI3K, activating AKT.

Two modes of signalling are distinguished by the source of IL-6R. In **classical (cis) signalling**, membrane-bound IL-6R — restricted largely to hepatocytes and a subset of leukocytes — presents IL-6 to gp130 on the same cell. In **trans-signalling**, soluble IL-6R (generated by ADAM17-mediated shedding or by alternative splicing) binds IL-6 in the extracellular space and enables cells that lack membrane IL-6R to respond, greatly broadening the set of IL-6-responsive cells. Soluble gp130 acts as a natural inhibitor that selectively blocks trans-signalling while sparing classical signalling.

## Physiological Function

Classical IL-6 signalling drives the acute-phase response in the liver and supports aspects of B-cell and T-cell biology. Trans-signalling is the principal route by which IL-6 acts on stromal, epithelial and endothelial cells, and is the mode most closely associated with chronic inflammatory pathology. During infection, IL-6R–STAT3 signalling coordinates fever, acute-phase protein synthesis and immune-cell recruitment.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

IL-6R is a validated drug target. The anti-IL-6R antibody tocilizumab (and sarilumab) blocks receptor-mediated signalling and is approved for rheumatoid arthritis, systemic juvenile idiopathic arthritis, giant cell arteritis and cytokine release syndrome, including CAR-T-associated CRS. Because IL-6R blockade suppresses inflammatory output without killing cells, it is the pharmacological archetype for cytokine-pathway-directed modulation of chronic inflammation.

## Role in Senescence & SASP

IL-6 is among the most prominent SASP cytokines, and IL-6R is the entry point for the autocrine loop that sustains it: secreted IL-6 acts on IL-6R in the same or neighbouring cells, activating JAK–STAT3, which promotes SASP gene expression and reinforces the [[Senescence|senescent]] state. This loop is why JAK inhibitors such as ruxolitinib act as senomorphics — suppressing the secretome without clearing senescent cells — and why IL-6 and IL-6R blockade is under investigation as a strategy to dampen [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]]. In the SASP-induction scheme of Birch and Gil, IL-6R sits with [[IL-1R]] on the receptor tier, feeding JAK–STAT.
