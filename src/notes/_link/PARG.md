# PARG

**PARG** (poly(ADP-ribose) glycohydrolase) is the primary [[PAR]]-degrading enzyme, using endo- and exo-glycosidase activity to hydrolyze glycosidic bonds between ADP-ribose units. Single mammalian gene encoding nuclear 110–111 kDa, cytosolic 102/99 kDa (human), and 59–60 kDa isoforms; exon-1-deleted cytosolic constructs are the experimental parthanatos-blocking tool. A 2026 preprint adds a 53-kDa splice isoform (PARG53) and revises PARG55/60 annotation (bioRxiv 2026.05.12.724507).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Parthanatos Moura 2024 molecular mechanisms more questions than answers|Moura et al. 2024]]
> The review flags the PARG paradox as an explicit open question: "There is extensive but conflicting evidence as to the role of PARG in parthanatos, with several studies suggesting that PARG can either prevent or promote PARP1-dependent cell death." Protection by PARG overexpression/knockdown (Andrabi 2006 neurons; Yu 2006 NMDA-AIF) contrasts with protection by PARG inhibition (Lu 2003 brain ischaemia) or PARG silencing (Blenn 2006, H2O2 but not MNNG). Endo-activity generates the free-[[PAR]] executioner; high exo-activity destroys it — a dose-balanced dual function.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Parthanatos Andrabi 2006 PAR polymer death signal|Andrabi et al. 2006 PNAS]]
> Cytosolic wild-type [[PARG]] adenovirus (E756/E757 catalytic site) lowers cytosolic [[PAR]] and halves [[NMDA receptor|NMDA]]/MNNG death; dead mutant does not. [[PARG]] transgenics −62% hemisphere infarct (120-min MCAO); heterozygotes +56% (90-min MCAO); heterozygous neurons make ~2x [[PAR]] with ~20% excess death; siRNA knockdown prolongs [[PAR]] and adds ~30% MNNG death.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Parthanatos Andrabi 2008 mitochondrial nuclear crosstalk|Andrabi/Dawson 2008]]
> [[PARG]] KO is embryonic-day-3.5 lethal (mouse) and larval-lethal (Drosophila) from [[PAR]] accumulation; trophoblasts survive only with PARP inhibitor. Predigested [[PAR]] is nontoxic; transgenics resist excitotoxicity/stroke. [[ARH3]] does not compensate.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Parthanatos David 2009 messenger of death|David et al. 2009]]
> Exon 2–3 deletion (59 kDa intact) is viable with elevated [[PAR]]/infarcts and compensatory 65 kDa mitochondrial form; exon 4 null is fully lethal. [[PAR]] half-life is set jointly by [[PARG]] and ARH3 lyase.

> [!important] 2026 update — PARG required, NAD/ATP uncoupled
> Hoch-lab RPE1/MNNG preprint (bioRxiv 2026.05.12.724507): complete PARG inhibition prevents parthanatos and ATP depletion but not NAD+ depletion; low residual activity suffices for death; ARH3 KO has no effect in this system. PAR formation *and* hydrolysis are both required. Phenotypic CRISPR-vs-inhibitor gap traced to a newly described PARG53 splice variant (prior PARG55/60 annotation incorrect) — grade inhibition and genotype isoforms in all future PARG manipulations.
