# PUFA

**Polyunsaturated fatty acids ([[PUFA|PUFAs]])** are fatty acids containing two or more *cis* double bonds. Key members include [[Arachidonic acid|arachidonic acid (AA, 20:4)]], adrenic acid (AdA, 22:4), dihomo-γ-linolenic acid (DGLA, 20:3), [[Docosahexaenoic acid|docosahexaenoic acid (DHA, 22:6)]], eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA, 20:5), and linoleic acid (LA, 18:2). Their bis-allylic methylene groups make them exquisitely prone to [[Lipid Peroxidation|lipid peroxidation]].

## Role in Ferroptosis

When esterified at the *sn-2* position of membrane [[Phospholipid|phospholipids]] (PL-PUFAs), PUFAs become the direct substrates for iron-dependent peroxidation—the execution step of [[Ferroptosis]]. The abundance and species of membrane PL-PUFAs (shaped by enzymes such as [[ACSL4]] and, newly, [[Acid ceramidase]]) are major determinants of cellular ferroptosis sensitivity.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex|Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile… (Soriano-Castell et al., 2026)]]
> [[Acid ceramidase]] over-expression in replicatively [[Senescent Cells|senescent]] fibroblasts increased membrane PL-[[PUFA|PUFA]] content (notably all [[Arachidonic acid|arachidonic acid]]-containing PLs), creating a pro-ferroptotic profile. ACase knockdown lowered PL-PUFAs and protected against [[RSL3]]-induced [[Ferroptosis]] independently of [[GPX4]]/[[Glutathione|GSH]] and [[Iron|iron]]—because less PUFA substrate means less [[Lipid Peroxidation|LPO]].

## Connections

- [[Phospholipid]] — PUFAs occupy the sn-2 position of PLs, forming the ferroptosis substrate pool.
- [[Lipid Peroxidation]] — The oxidative process acting on PUFA double bonds.
- [[Ferroptosis]] — PL-PUFA abundance sets ferroptosis sensitivity.
- [[ACSL4]] — Activates PUFAs for phospholipid incorporation; canonical ferroptosis enhancer.
- [[Acid ceramidase]] — Independently raises PL-PUFA content via ceramide-derived free fatty acids.
- [[Arachidonic acid]] / [[Docosahexaenoic acid]] — Specific PUFA species enriched in the senescent pro-ferroptotic profile.
- [[GPX4]] / [[Glutathione]] / [[Iron]] — Classic ferroptosis regulators; ACase acts independently of them by lowering PUFA substrate.
- [[RSL3]] — GPX4 inhibitor used to reveal PUFA-dependent sensitivity.
- [[Senescent Cells]] — Senescent cells display elevated PL-PUFAs and ferroptosis sensitivity.
