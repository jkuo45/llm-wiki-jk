# Azurosome

The azurosome is the complex formed in [[Azurophil Granule|azurophil granules]] in which [[Neutrophil Elastase]] clusters with [[Myeloperoxidase]], azurocidin, [[Cathepsin G]], defensin-1, lysozyme and [[Lactotransferrin]]. It keeps NE sequestered in resting [[Neutrophils]]. On [[NADPH Oxidase]] activation, [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] activate MPO and dissociate MPO and NE from the azurosome, enabling MPO-dependent NE release into the cytosol.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Dissociation of the azurosome is the gating step of the NE/MPO/ROS-driven [[Suicidal NETosis]] model; MPO-deficient neutrophils fail to form NETs against Candida albicans whereas partial MPO deficiency permits NET formation (Metzler et al. 2011; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
