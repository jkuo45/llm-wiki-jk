# Olaparib

**Olaparib** inhibits PARP1/PARP2, trapping PARP on DNA breaks; in homologous-recombination-deficient tumors (BRCA1/2, [[PALB2]], ATM loss) unrepaired breaks cause synthetic lethality. Approved for BRCA-mutated breast, ovarian, pancreatic, and prostate cancers; ATM-deficient tumors show heightened vulnerability to PARP plus ATR inhibition.
