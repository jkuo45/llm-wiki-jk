# OMA1-DELE1-HRI axis

The OMA1-DELE1-HRI axis is a mitochondrial stress-signaling pathway that couples organelle dysfunction to the cytosolic integrated stress response. The mitochondrial metalloprotease [[OMA1]] cleaves the ER-anchored protein [[DELE1]], releasing its cytosolic tail. The freed DELE1 tail then engages and activates the kinase [[HRI]] (heme-regulated inhibitor), which phosphorylates eIF2α to induce the [[Integrated Stress Response]] (including ATF4 and CHOP transcription).

According to [[OMA1]], OMA1 releases DELE1's tail to engage HRI, thereby transmitting mitochondrial perturbation into a translational and transcriptional stress program. This axis acts as a backup to the OMA1-dependent cleavage of OPA1 and complements other mitochondrial stress outputs, allowing cells to adapt to or eliminate damaged mitochondria.

The pathway illustrates how [[Mitochondria]] communicate distress to the cytosol independently of the canonical PINK1/Parkin mitophagy route.
