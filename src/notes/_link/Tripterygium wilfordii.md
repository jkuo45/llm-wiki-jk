# Tripterygium wilfordii

**Tripterygium wilfordii** (雷公藤) is a Traditional Chinese Medicine plant; the entire aboveground parts and the root bark enter formulations, with root bark as the canonical TCM part. The major phytochemicals are the diterpenoid triepoxides [[Triptolide]] and triptonide, and the pentacyclic triterpenes celastrol and pristomerin ([[Celastrol]]).

## Mechanisms of Action (via [[Triptolide]])

- [[Triptolide]] covalently binds the XPB subunit of TFIIH, blocking RNA Polymerase II transcription initiation; RNA Polymerase II RPB1 is hyperphosphorylated and degraded downstream.
- [[Autophagy]], [[Senescence]], and [[Apoptosis]] induction; [[Chemoresistance]] reversal; [[Cancer Stem Cells]] inhibition; [[NF-κB]] inhibition.

## Toxicity

A post-hoc synthesis across 3,639 trials (2,437 patients) (He et al. 2019, Front Pharmacol 7) found hematotoxicity in 5.66% and leukopenia in 5.74% of reports; reproductive toxicity, hepatotoxicity, and nephrotoxicity also occur, and the therapeutic window of root formulations is narrow.

## Clinical Data

- **Minnelide (Phase 1, GI)**: 45 allocated, 42 dosed; ORR 4%; DCR 54%; neutropenia 38% (Propper 2024, Oncologist 13; Oncologist 2024). Minnelide is a water-soluble phosphate prodrug of [[Triptolide]].
- **Minnelide (Phase 2, ASCP, NCT04896073)**: 16 evaluable, no ORR; median OS 4.91 months (Zhao 2024).
- **Refractory pancreatic cancer (NCT03117920)**: completed, no results posted (per 2026 review).
- **Registered trials (per Molecules 2026 registry)**: NCT03760523 — AML, terminated with DLT; NCT03347994 — AML, withdrawn; ongoing trials in NSCLC and AML.
- **Minnelide + paclitaxel (advanced gastric cancer, NCT05566834)**: partial response in 50% of paclitaxel-naive patients (2026 review).
