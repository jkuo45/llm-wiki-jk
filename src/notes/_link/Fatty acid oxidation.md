# Fatty acid oxidation

**Definition:** Fatty acid oxidation (β-oxidation) is mitochondrial/peroxisomal lipid catabolism promoted by [[AMPK]] alongside mitochondrial biogenesis to generate ATP.

**Mechanism:** The process of Fatty acid oxidation is executed by specific protein complexes, organelles, and signaling cascades. Key regulatory nodes control its initiation, progression, and termination.

**Physiological importance:** Fatty acid oxidation is essential for development, tissue maintenance, stress adaptation, and energy homeostasis. Dysregulation of this process contributes to aging and disease.

**Regulation and dysregulation:** Fatty acid oxidation is controlled by nutrient sensors, stress pathways, and hormonal signals. Impairment of this process is linked to metabolic diseases, neurodegeneration, and cancer.
