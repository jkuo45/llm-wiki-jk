# TFAM

[[TFAM]] (mitochondrial transcription factor A) is a nuclear-encoded, mitochondrial-targeted protein that binds and packages mitochondrial DNA, regulating its replication and transcription. It is a key determinant of mitochondrial biogenesis and respiratory-chain capacity.

## Rapamycin and TFAM

Inhibition of the [[mTOR]] pathway by [[Rapamycin]] promotes mitochondrial biogenesis and raises expression of [[TFAM]] together with [[PGC-1α]], renewing mitochondrial populations and improving fatty-acid metabolism in senescent tissues such as myocardium and brown adipose tissue ([Zerdka et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12766144/)). Rapamycin also boosts TFAM via cap-independent translation and ATF4/H₂S-mediated stress resistance, supporting longevity.

> [!tip] Mitochondrial renewal
> TFAM and PGC-1α are the major effectors by which mTOR inhibition rebuilds mitochondrial networks and improves cellular energy efficiency.
