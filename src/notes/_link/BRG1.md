# BRG1

**BRG1** (SMARCA4) is a mutually exclusive ATPase of [[SWI-SNF]] (BAF) remodeling complexes that slide and evict nucleosomes to open enhancers for differentiation programs. SMARCA4 loss defines small-cell carcinoma of the ovary (hypercalcemic type) and subsets of NSCLC, sensitizing to EZH2 and CDK4/6 inhibition.
