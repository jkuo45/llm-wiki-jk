# HeLa Cells

**HeLa cells** are an immortalized human epithelial cell line derived from cervical carcinoma, the first continuously cultured human cell line. Variants such as HeLa S3 are routinely used in RNAi, Northern, and reporter assays.

> [!info] In the DGCR8 study
> Knockdown of [[DGCR8]] or [[Drosha]] in HeLa S3 (and HEK 293) reduced pre-miR-16 and mature miR-16, with pri-miRNA accumulation, confirming DGCR8 requirement for [[MicroRNA]] maturation in human cells. A HeLa EGFP reporter confirmed impaired miR-21 silencing upon DGCR8/Drosha/Ago2 knockdown.
