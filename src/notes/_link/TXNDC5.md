# TXNDC5

**TXNDC5** (thioredoxin domain-containing protein 5) is an ER-localized thioredoxin-family chaperone implicated in protein folding and redox regulation.

## Interaction with HSPA8 and Inflammation

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> TXNDC5 synergizes with [[HSPA8]] (HSC70) to exacerbate the inflammatory phenotype of synovial fibroblasts in [[Rheumatoid Arthritis|rheumatoid arthritis]] via [[NF-κB]] signaling[40]. Because HSPA8 is the bridge node apigenin disrupts to suppress the [[SASP|SASP]], TXNDC5–HSPA8 cooperativity is relevant to senescence-associated inflammatory signaling.
