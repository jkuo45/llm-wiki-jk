# TUNEL

**TUNEL** (terminal deoxynucleotidyl transferase dUTP nick-end labeling) enzymatically tags free 3′-OH DNA ends with labeled dUTP, visualizing the internucleosomal fragmentation of [[Apoptosis]] in tissue sections and fixed cells. It is the canonical histological counterpart to the DNA ladder on gels and cleaved [[Caspase-3]] immunostaining.

> [!warning] Not apoptosis-specific
> TUNEL also labels the large-scale fragmentation of [[Parthanatos]], late [[Necroptosis]], and other programs with abundant DNA ends. Score it alongside Annexin V/PI status, caspase activity, and morphology — never alone.
