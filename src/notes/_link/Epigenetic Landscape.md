# Epigenetic Landscape

The **Epigenetic Landscape** denotes the coordinated ensemble of chromatin states, [[Histone modification|histone modifications]], and [[DNA Methylation|DNA methylation]] patterns that establish and maintain cellular identity and gene-expression potential. Coined from Waddington's metaphor of cells descending a rugged landscape into differentiated states, it captures how stable epigenetic configurations define cell fate.

## Erosion in Aging and RCM

The [[Relocalization of Chromatin Modifiers (RCM) Hypothesis]] proposes that repeated recruitment of [[Chromatin Modifiers|chromatin modifiers]] to [[Double-Strand Break|DNA repair sites]] causes gradual erosion of the epigenetic landscape. Imperfect resetting after each repair event leads to loss of cellular identity — cells "forget" their type — and dysregulated gene expression, manifesting as aging phenotypes.
