# Parthenolide

**Parthenolide** is the principal sesquiterpene lactone isolated from [[Tanacetum parthenium]]; it carries an α-methylene-γ-lactone moiety that mediates covalent cysteine modification (PubChem parthenolide).

## Mechanism of action

Parthenolide alkylates cysteine residues — Cys179 of [[IKKbeta]] and Cys38 of the p65 subunit of [[NF-κB]] — suppressing [[NF-κB]]-driven transcription through the pathway [[PI3K-Akt Signaling]]/[[NF-κB]] (a mechanism, per Zhang SU 030518, not previously reported for parthenolide; PMIDs 15256485, 15286701 and Oka PMID 17290398 record related series). The inhibition of [[NF-κB]] and [[TRAIL]] responses, alongside [[STAT3]] inhibition and induction of the ER-stress pathway ([[ER Stress]]), constitutes parthenolide's sensitisation profile.

## TRAIL and STAT3 sensitisation

- Parthenolide sensitizes [[TRAIL]]-mediated apoptosis (Zhang 2004) through cFLIP-based modulation — the death-effector domain rheostat covered by [[Caspase-8-c-FLIP Rheostat]].
- [[STAT3]] inhibition: parthenolide suppresses [[STAT3]] signalling, contributing to the TRAIL/STAT3 sensitisation profile.
- ER stress: parthenolide induces the ER-stress response (Oka PMID 17290398) — [[ER Stress]]-dependent mechanism.

## Transcriptional shutdown via XPB

Parthenolide induces transcriptional shutdown via XPB.

## IC50 data

IC50s: HeLa/SiHa (PEI study); other series incomplete (NYSE, not quantified).

## Phase 1 trials

- Curry 2004 (PMID 15122077): standardized feverfew extract (parthenolide dose up to 4 mg/day) in healthy volunteers produced no detectable plasma parthenolide — consistent with erratic GI availability of the lactone.
- Phase 1 GBM (Licklier 2021, ASCO JCO 20:2037; 24 patients, ORR ≈ 8/19 gliomas, one ongoing CR at 27 months, 3 SD ≥ 6 months; Wang 2021; Ziegler DIPG PMC11183933).
- ACT001 (Accendatech, orphan drug; dimethylamino micheliolide, DMAMCL): semisynthesis from Magnolia root-bark-derived material (Nankai case); dosing data recorded in the ACT001 chain.
- ACT001 phase 1 DIPG trial (Wang 2021, 16 pts; linear PK across the 0.12–12 range; 8/14 patients achieved response or stable disease at ≥700 mg/m²).
- ACT001+WBRT phase 1b/2a (ChiCTR-2000037739).
