# Methyl-beta-cyclodextrin

[[Methyl-beta-cyclodextrin]] (MβCD) is a chemically modified [[Cyclodextrin]] whose hydrophobic cavity forms inclusion complexes with hydrophobic drugs, dramatically increasing their aqueous solubility and dissolution rate.

## Role in Fenbendazole Repurposing

Complexing [[Fenbendazole]] with methyl-β-cyclodextrin at a 1:1 ratio raises water solubility to 20.21 mg/ml (≈60,000-fold vs. native 0.3 μ g/ml) and boosts drug release to 75% in 15 min (vs. 5% for pure drug) (11). This meets the 5-10 mg/ml solubility threshold for systemic anticancer development.

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> Methyl-β-cyclodextrin complexation is highlighted as a leading strategy to overcome fenbendazole's poor solubility and enable clinical translation (11, 12).
