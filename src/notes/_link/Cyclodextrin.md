# Cyclodextrin

[[Cyclodextrin]]s are cyclic oligosaccharides (α, β, γ from 6, 7, 8 glucopyranose units) with a hydrophobic inner cavity and hydrophilic outer surface. They encapsulate hydrophobic drugs to improve aqueous solubility and bioavailability; chemical derivatives such as [[Methyl-beta-cyclodextrin]] are common pharmaceutical solubilizers.

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> Fenbendazole complexed 1:1 with methyl-β-cyclodextrin reached 20.21 mg/ml water solubility and 75% release in 15 min, meeting thresholds for systemic anticancer use (11).
