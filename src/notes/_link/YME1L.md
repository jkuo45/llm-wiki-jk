### Overview
YME1L is an ATP-dependent metalloprotease of the [[Inner Mitochondrial Membrane]]. It constitutively cleaves the fusion GTPase [[OPA1]] at the S2/S3 sites to generate the short-form S-OPA1.

### Mechanism
S-OPA1, which faces the [[Mitochondrial Intermembrane Space]], cooperates with long-form L-OPA1 to mediate inner-membrane fusion. YME1L processing is therefore essential for producing the isoform balance required for [[Mitochondrial Fusion|fusion]] and cristae maintenance.

### Functional Significance
Loss of YME1L shifts OPA1 toward long forms and disrupts fusion, compromising [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] and linking protease activity directly to optic neuropathies such as [[Dominant Optic Atrophy]].
