# Neutral Red

[[Neutral Red]] (NR, toluylene red) is a phenazine dye and a methylene blue-related compound in which the position-5 **sulfur** of the phenothiazine scaffold is replaced by **nitrogen**, converting the ring system to a phenazine. It retains 3,7-amine side chains. In the methylene blue derivative study it occupied a unique fourth group: it enhanced cellular oxygen consumption yet lacked the alternative mitochondrial electron-transfer function and showed the weakest neuroprotection of the series [^1].

## Structure & Chemistry

Replacing the thiazine sulfur with nitrogen removes the structural feature required for methylene blue-like electron-carrier activity. Neutral Red has a biphasic redox potential (E<sub>1/2</sub> ≈ 0.450 V and −0.269 V vs NHE); the negative component places it with [[Methylene blue]]/[[Toluidine Blue O]] among OCR-enhancing compounds, but its phenazine core prevents electron shuttling [^1].

## Mechanism of Action

### Oxygen consumption without electron shuttling

Neutral Red increased cellular oxygen consumption rate (OCR) in Seahorse assays (like [[Methylene blue]] and [[Toluidine Blue O]]) and initially spiked ECAR, but it did **not** increase [[Cytochrome c]] reduction in the [[Respiratory Chain Complex I|Complex I]]→[[Complex III]] assay — i.e., it is not an alternative electron carrier between Complex I and cytochrome c [^1].

### Weak, context-dependent antioxidant action

In the cell-free mitochondria-lysate oxidation assay, NR mildly decreased DCF fluorescence at 1 µM (with mitochondria/[[NADH]]) but increased it at 10 µM; in the absence of mitochondria/NADH it increased DCF fluorescence up to 15-fold [^1].

## Neuroprotection

Against glutamate insult in HT-22 cells, Neutral Red was the weakest protector:

- Viability EC50 ≈ **5836 nM**
- ROS EC50 ≈ **15760 nM**
- Mitochondrial membrane potential EC50 ≈ **8958 nM**

It protected in the [[Rotenone]] and [[Iodoacetic Acid|IAA]] models but with reduced efficacy versus [[Methylene blue]] and the naked [[Phenothiazine]] nucleus, and (like all study compounds) gave no protection against direct H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub> from [[Glucose Oxidase]] [^1].

> [!important] Sulfur-to-nitrogen swap dismantles the carrier
> The position-5 sulfur of the phenothiazine ring is as essential as the free 10-nitrogen for MB-like protection. Swapping S5→N (Neutral Red) preserves OCR enhancement but destroys electron-carrier function and collapses neuroprotective potency versus [[Methylene blue]]/[[Toluidine Blue O]] [^1].
