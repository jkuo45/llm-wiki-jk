# CUT&Tag

**CUT&Tag** (Cleavage Under Targets and Tagmentation) is a method for genome-wide mapping of [[Histone Modifications]], [[Transcription Factor]] binding, and [[Chromatin]]-associated proteins using an antibody-targeted [[Tn5 Transposase]] fusion protein (pA-Tn5 or pAG-Tn5).

## Principle

A primary [[Antibody]] against the target [[Epigenetic Mark]] or protein binds to permeabilized cells or nuclei. A secondary antibody conjugated to [[Protein A]]-Tn5 fusion is added, and [[Ca²⁺]]-dependent activation of Tn5 cleaves and tags DNA adjacent to the antibody binding site. Tagmented DNA fragments are sequenced to generate high-resolution binding profiles.

## Advantages

CUT&Tag requires fewer cells (100–10,000) than [[ChIP-seq]], has lower background, higher signal-to-noise ratio, and shorter protocol time. It is compatible with Single-cell [[CUT&Tag]] for cell-type-specific profiling.

## Applications

- [[Histone Modification]] mapping ([[H3K27ac]], [[H3K4me3]], [[H3K27me3]])
- [[Transcription Factor]] binding profiling
- [[Epigenomics]] of rare cell populations
- Integration with [[ATAC-seq]] for multi-omic chromatin analysis
