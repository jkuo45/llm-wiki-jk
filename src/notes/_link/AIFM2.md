# AIFM2

**AIFM2** (apoptosis-inducing factor mitochondria-associated 2) is the human gene encoding **FSP1** (ferroptosis suppressor protein 1). It was first characterized as a pro-apoptotic, mitochondrially associated protein in the apoptosis-inducing factor family, but was renamed **FSP1** after 2019 studies showed its dominant cellular role is suppressing **ferroptosis** at the plasma membrane — mechanistically distinct from the apoptosis regulation of AIFM1.

## Why the rename matters

The 2019 back-to-back discoveries (Doll et al., *Nature*; Bersuker et al., *Cell*) identified *AIFM2* in complementary screens for GPX4-independent ferroptosis resistance and renamed it to avoid confusion with the apoptosis-inducing factor family. The protein is a **flavoprotein NAD(P)H:ubiquinone oxidoreductase** that regenerates ubiquinol, a lipophilic radical-trapping antioxidant.

> Use [[AIFM2]] when referring to the gene symbol or its apoptosis-era history; use [[FSP1]] for the ferroptosis-suppressor function and mechanism. The canonical entity note is [[FSP1]].
