# Shigella flexneri

Shigella flexneri is a Gram-negative bacterium reported among NET-inducing bacteria (Brinkmann et al. 2004). [[PAD4]] deficiency in S. flexneri-infected [[Neutrophils]] rendered the cells unable to citrullinate [[Histone|histones]], undergo [[Vital NETosis]], or mediate bacterial killing (Li et al. 2010), making this infection model a key demonstration of PAD4 dependence in the vital pathway.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> NE targets virulence factors of colonic enterobacteria such as Shigella or Yersinia (Belaaouaj et al. 1998; Weinrauch et al. 2002; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
