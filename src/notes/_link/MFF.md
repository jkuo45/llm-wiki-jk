### Overview
MFF (Mitochondrial Fission Factor, gene at human chr 2q36.3, also C2orf33) is an [[Outer Mitochondrial Membrane]] adaptor protein that recruits the fission GTPase [[DRP1]] to mitochondria to initiate [[Mitochondrial Fission|fission]].

### Mechanism
MFF acts together with the related adaptors [[MID49]], [[MID51]], and [[FIS1]] to anchor DRP1 oligomers at constriction sites. Once recruited, DRP1 assembles a helical scaffold that constricts and severs the mitochondrial membranes.

### Functional Significance
MFF is a principal node governing fission frequency and is essential for maintaining [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] through quality control. Its activity is counterbalanced by fusion mediators such as the mitofusins [[MFN1]] and [[MFN2]].
