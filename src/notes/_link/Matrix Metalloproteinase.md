# Matrix Metalloproteinase

**Matrix metalloproteinases** ([[Matrix Metalloproteinase|MMPs]]) are zinc-dependent proteases that remodel the extracellular matrix. [[MMP-2]] and [[MMP-9]] (gelatinases) are prominently upregulated in cancer, promoting invasion, metastasis, and angiogenesis.

## Modulation by Apigenin

Apigenin downregulates MMP-2 and MMP-9 expression/activity across multiple cancers (breast, prostate, neuroblastoma, colorectal), contributing to reduced migration and invasion. In triple-negative breast cancer, apigenin combined with [[Chrysin]] inhibited MMP-2/9 leading to apoptosis; in prostate (TRAMP) models it reduced MMP-2/9 with metastasis suppression.
