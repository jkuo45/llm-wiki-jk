# Myricetin

**Myricetin** is a [[Flavonol|flavonol]] [[Flavonoids|flavonoid]] found in many fruits, vegetables, and medicinal herbs, with reported [[Antioxidant|antioxidant]], anti-[[Inflammation|inflammatory]], and anticancer activities.

In the fisetin senolytic screening panel (Yousefzadeh et al., 2018), myricetin was tested at 5 μM in *Ercc1*<sup>−/−</sup> [[Fibroblasts|MEFs]] alongside [[Fisetin]], [[Quercetin]], and other flavonoids. It showed weak senotherapeutic activity compared to [[Fisetin]].
