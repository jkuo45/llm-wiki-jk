### Overview
MID49 (mitochondrial dynamics protein 49, gene at human chr 17p11, also MIEF2) is an [[Outer Mitochondrial Membrane]] adaptor that recruits the fission GTPase [[DRP1]] to mitochondria to drive [[Mitochondrial Fission|fission]].

### Mechanism
MID49 functions alongside [[MFF]], [[MID51]], and [[FIS1]] to tether DRP1 at division sites. Overexpression of MID49 can recruit DRP1 and promote fission, while its loss shifts the balance toward fusion.

### Functional Significance
By regulating DRP1 recruitment, MID49 contributes to the homeostatic equilibrium that preserves [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] and controls organelle number and distribution.
