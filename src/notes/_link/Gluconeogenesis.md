# Gluconeogenesis

**Definition:** Gluconeogenesis is a fundamental biological process that maintains cellular and organismal homeostasis. It involves coordinated molecular events regulated by signaling pathways and environmental cues.

**Mechanism:** The process of Gluconeogenesis is executed by specific protein complexes, organelles, and signaling cascades. Key regulatory nodes control its initiation, progression, and termination.

**Physiological importance:** Gluconeogenesis is essential for development, tissue maintenance, stress adaptation, and energy homeostasis. Dysregulation of this process contributes to aging and disease.

**Regulation and dysregulation:** Gluconeogenesis is controlled by nutrient sensors, stress pathways, and hormonal signals. Impairment of this process is linked to metabolic diseases, neurodegeneration, and cancer.
