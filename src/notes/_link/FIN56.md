# FIN56

**FIN56** is a **Class III ferroptosis inducer (FIN)** that kills cells by simultaneously disabling **both** pillars of ferroptosis defense. It engages the **mevalonate pathway** at two levels:

1. **Squalene synthase activation** — FIN56 binds to and activates **squalene synthase**, pushing mevalonate flux toward sterol/cholesterol biosynthesis and away from **CoQ10/ubiquinone** synthesis. The resulting ubiquinone depletion **converges on FSP1**, collapsing its radical trap (see [[Mevalonate pathway]] and [[FSP1]]).
2. **GPX4 degradation** — independently, FIN56 promotes GPX4 degradation via **acetyl-CoA carboxylases (ACCs)**, crippling the GSH-dependent axis.

## Ferroptosis relevance

FIN56 is a textbook example of the **MVA→ubiquinone→FSP1 convergence**: by starving FSP1 of its CoQ10 substrate it sensitizes cells to ferroptosis, and its dual mechanism makes it a potent experimental probe of the two-pillar defense model.

> [!info] Convergence in action
> FIN56 illustrates precisely the passage's claim that "loss of ubiquinone convergence on FSP1" predicts ferroptosis sensitivity: no ubiquinone → no FSP1 regenerated ubiquinol → lipid peroxidation runs unchecked.

## Physiological & clinical relevance

- Primarily a **research tool** to dissect ferroptosis pathways and sensitize resistant cancer cells.
- Demonstrates that co-targeting the MVA/CoQ10 and GPX4 nodes is a rational ferroptosis-inducing strategy.
