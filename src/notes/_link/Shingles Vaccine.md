# Shingles Vaccine

The **shingles vaccine** prevents **[[Herpes Zoster|herpes zoster (shingles)]]** and its complication [[Postherpetic Neuralgia|postherpetic neuralgia]]. Current formulations include a recombinant subunit vaccine (VZV glycoprotein E + AS01B adjuvant) and an older live-attenuated vaccine; it is typically recommended for adults ≥50–60.

## Evidence for slowed biological aging

A U.S. population-based cohort study (Health and Retirement Study, >3,800 adults ≥70; Kim & Crimmins, *J Gerontol A*, 2026, doi:10.1093/gerona/glag008) found that, after adjustment for health and demographics, vaccinated individuals showed:

- **Slower [[Biological Aging|biological aging]]** overall
- **Lower [[Inflammation|inflammation]]** and slower [[Epigenetic Aging|epigenetic]] and [[Transcriptomic Aging|transcriptomic aging]]
- Effects persisting ≥4 years post-vaccination

> [!important] Inflammaging mechanism
> By preventing reactivation of latent [[Varicella Zoster Virus]], the vaccine may reduce chronic background inflammation — the persistent low-grade "[[Inflammaging|inflammaging]]" linked to heart disease, frailty, and cognitive decline. This positions vaccination as a modulator of the age-related inflammatory state.
