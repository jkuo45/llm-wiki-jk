## Overview

MDH1 (malate dehydrogenase 1, cytosolic) catalyzes the reversible conversion of [[Malate]] to [[Oxaloacetate]] in the cytosol, coupled with reduction of [[NAD+|NAD⁺]] to NADH. It is a key component of the [[Malate-Aspartate Shuttle]], which transfers reducing equivalents from cytosolic [[NADH]] into the [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] matrix for [[Oxidative Phosphorylation]].
