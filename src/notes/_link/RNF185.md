# RNF185

RNF185 (RING finger protein 185) is a mitochondrial [[Ubiquitin|ubiquitin]] E3 ligase that regulates [[Autophagy|autophagy]] through interaction with [[BNIP1]]. It functions as part of the non-Parkin E3 ligase network that provides backup [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]] capacity.

## Mechanism

RNF185 ubiquitinates its substrate [[BNIP1]] with K63-linked [[Ubiquitin|ubiquitin]] chains. These K63-linked chains serve as a recognition signal for [[p62]]/SQSTM1, which then recruits the autophagy machinery to clear the targeted [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]].

## Role in Mitochondrial Quality Control

RNF185 is part of the broader network of E3 ubiquitin ligases (alongside [[MUL1]], [[AMBRA1]]/[[HUWE1]]) that can maintain [[Mitochondrial Quality Control|mitochondrial quality control]] when the primary [[PINK1]]/[[Parkin]] pathway is impaired. This redundancy ensures that cells can still clear damaged mitochondria even when the canonical pathway is compromised.
