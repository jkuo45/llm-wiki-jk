# SHP2

Src homology 2 domain-containing phosphatase 2 (SHP2), encoded by the gene [[PTPN11]], is a protein tyrosine phosphatase with two SH2 domains and a catalytic PTP domain. It transduces signals from receptor tyrosine kinases and cytokine receptors and is a known oncogene when mutated.

In the context of [[Parkin]], SHP2 dephosphorylates Parkin at Tyr143, relieving autoinhibition and unlocking Parkin's E3 ubiquitin ligase activity. This activation is required for Parkin-mediated [[Mitophagy]]. The statin [[Lovastatin]] acts as an SHP2 agonist, promoting this dephosphorylation and thereby enhancing Parkin activation.

SHP2 thus provides a phosphorylation-dependent regulatory node that controls the PINK1/Parkin mitophagy axis through [[Tyrosine Phosphorylation]] state.
