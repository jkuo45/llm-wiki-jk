# Flippase

**Flippases** (P4-type ATPases such as ATP11C/CDC50A complexes) actively pump phosphatidylserine (PS) and phosphatidylethanolamine from the outer to the inner membrane leaflet, maintaining the resting asymmetry that keeps viable cells Annexin V–negative. Regulated death flips this balance: Ca2+ influx (in [[Eryptosis]]) or caspase cleavage of ATP11C (in [[Apoptosis]]) inactivates flippases while [[Scramblase]] activates — collapsing asymmetry and externalizing the PS eat-me signal.
