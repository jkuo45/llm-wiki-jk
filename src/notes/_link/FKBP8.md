# FKBP8

FKBP8 (FK506-binding protein 8, also known as [[FKBP38]]) is a [[Parkin]]-independent [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]] receptor located on the outer mitochondrial membrane (OMM). It possesses a unique "escape mechanism" that distinguishes it from other mitophagy receptors.

## Mechanism and Unique Features

Unlike most OMM proteins that are ubiquitinated and degraded by the [[Proteasome|proteasome]] during [[Autophagy|autophagy]], FKBP8 avoids this process. It strongly recruits [[LC3|LC3A]] via its N-terminal [[LIR Motif|LIR motif]] (F-E-V-I at positions 22–25) and directly mediates Parkin-independent mitophagy. FKBP8 preferentially binds to the LC3A isoform, a selectivity that may allow differential regulation of mitophagy under specific cellular conditions.

## Role in Neurodegeneration

FKBP8 provides an alternative pathway for [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] clearance when the canonical [[PINK1]]/[[Parkin]] pathway is impaired. This is particularly relevant in diseases like [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's disease]] where Parkin function is compromised. The escape mechanism may help maintain mitochondrial quality control without depleting essential OMM proteins.
