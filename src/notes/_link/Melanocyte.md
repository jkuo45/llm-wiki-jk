# Melanocyte

**Overview:** **Melanocytes** are neural-crest-derived cells that produce melanin in the epidermis, hair follicles, and uvea. They are the cell of origin of **[[Melanoma]]**.

## Relevance to redox and metastasis

- Malignant transformation of melanocytes yields melanoma, whose distant **[[Metastatic colonization]]** is limited by **[[Oxidative Stress]]**.
- Metastatic cells exploit **[[Selenocysteine]]** translation (**[[FTSJ1]]**/Um34) to sustain antioxidant **[[Selenoprotein|selenoproteins]]** and survive the oxidative stress of the metastatic niche (see [[Selenocysteine tRNA methylation promotes oxidative stress resistance in melanoma metastasis]]).
