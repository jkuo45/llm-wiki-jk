# MFN1

**MFN1** (Mitofusin 1) is a dynamin-related GTPase resident in the [[Outer Mitochondrial Membrane|outer mitochondrial membrane (OMM)]] that drives [[Mitochondrial Fusion|mitochondrial fusion]] by tethering and fusing adjacent mitochondria. With [[MFN2|MFN2]], it is the mammalian homolog of the drosophila protein [[Fzo|Fzo]]. Balanced activity of MFN1 against [[DRP1|DRP1]]-mediated [[Mitochondrial Fission|fission]] governs mitochondrial network morphology, with consequences for [[Cancer|cancer]], [[Neurodegenerative Disease|neurodegeneration]], and [[Cardiovascular Disease|cardiovascular disease]].

## Structure and Domains

MFN1 contains an N-terminal GTPase domain, two helical-repeat regions (HR1 and HR2), and a membrane-anchoring region. Updated topology places both MFN1 and [[MFN2|MFN2]] with a single transmembrane domain, such that the N-terminal GTPase and HR1 face the [[Cytosol|cytosol]] while the C-terminal HR2 domain extends into the [[Mitochondrial Intermembrane Space|intermembrane space (IMS)]].

## Mechanism of Outer-Membrane Fusion

Tethering of two OMMs is achieved through oligomerization of the GTPase domains of the mitofusins. GTP binding and hydrolysis drive a conformational change that brings the two mitochondria into docking and fusion. A redox-dependent alternative mechanism was identified: elevated oxidized [[Glutathione|glutathione]] oxidizes two cysteine residues in the HR2 domains (in the IMS), forming disulfide bonds that drive MFN oligomerization; addition of GSH reverses the oligomers, showing [[Redox Signaling|redox signaling]] directly regulates OMM fusion.

## Regulation

- **[[ERK|ERK]]** phosphorylates MFN1 in the HR1 domain, inhibiting [[Mitochondrial Fusion|fusion]] and promoting [[Apoptosis]].
- **[[HDAC6|HDAC6]]** (histone deacetylase 6) deacetylates MFN1, activating it and promoting fusion under [[Glucose|glucose]] deprivation.
- MFN1 interacts with [[MFN2|MFN2]] and, via the [[Miro]]/[[Milton]] complex, with [[Kinesin|kinesin]] for axonal transport (primarily an MFN2 function).

## Pathological Significance

Reduced MFN1 expression (and MFN1-dependent alterations in [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial dynamics]]) drives [[Cancer|hepatocellular carcinoma]] metastasis through glucose metabolic reprogramming and promotes triple-negative [[Breast Cancer|breast cancer]] survival, linking fusion deficits to tumor progression.
