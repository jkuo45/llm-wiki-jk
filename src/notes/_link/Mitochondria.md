# Mitochondria

[[Mitochondria]] are double-membrane organelles that produce ATP through [[Oxidative Phosphorylation]], and also govern apoptosis, calcium handling, and redox balance. Mitochondrial dysfunction is a principal driver of age-related disease (neurodegeneration, cardiovascular disease).

## mTOR, Rapamycin, and Mitochondrial Renewal

Inhibition of [[mTOR]] by [[Rapamycin]] improves the NAD⁺/NADH redox balance, supports [[Oxidative Phosphorylation]], and reduces mTOR-hyperactivity "pseudohypoxia" linked to [[HIF-1α]] accumulation. It also promotes mitochondrial biogenesis via [[PGC-1α]] and [[TFAM]], renewing mitochondrial populations and improving fatty-acid metabolism in senescent tissues (myocardium, brown adipose) ([Zerdka et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12766144/)).

> [!tip] Energy efficiency
> Rapamycin shifts cells from glycolytic/pseudohypoxic metabolism toward efficient OXPHOS by relieving HIF-1α accumulation and boosting PGC-1α/TFAM.

## Mitochondria as Master Regulators of Aging

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Mitochondrial Drivers Stem Cell Aging Inflammaging Bautista 2026]]
> Mitochondria are not passive bystanders in the aging process but active regulators of cellular fate, metabolic plasticity, and tissue regeneration. Their dysfunction lies at the heart of multiple aging hallmarks, including [[Stem Cell Exhaustion|stem cell exhaustion]], [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]], and bioenergetic collapse.

### Mitochondrial Control of Stem Cell Fate

- Mitochondria govern [[Stem Cells|stem cell]] fate through energy production, [[Mitochondrial ROS|ROS]] signaling, [[Mitochondrial DNA|mtDNA]] integrity, metabolite-mediated epigenetic regulation, and dynamic segregation during cell division
- Quiescent stem cells rely on glycolysis; transition to activation involves a metabolic shift toward [[Oxidative Phosphorylation|OXPHOS]]
- **Asymmetric inheritance**: selective retention of aged mitochondria in [[Intestinal Stem Cell|intestinal stem cells]] promotes TET-dependent epigenetic remodeling via α-ketoglutarate
- Mitochondrial membrane potential (Δψm) can stratify chronologically old [[Hematopoietic Stem Cell|HSCs]] into functionally distinct states

### Mitochondrial Quality Control

- [[Mitochondrial Quality Control]] encompasses fission ([[DRP1]]), fusion ([[MFN1]], [[MFN2]], [[OPA1]]), [[Mitophagy]] ([[PINK1]]–[[Parkin]]), and [[Mitochondrial Unfolded Protein Response|UPR^mt^]]
- Aging disrupts this balance, often favoring fission and impairing mitophagy
- Failed quality control permits persistence of dysfunctional mitochondria and mtDNA/mtDAMP escape, activating [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS–STING]] and [[NF-κB]]

### Mitochondria-Driven Inflammaging

- Cytosolic [[Mitochondrial DNA|mtDNA]] and other mtDAMPs activate innate immune sensors, reinforcing [[SASP|senescence-associated secretory phenotype]] and chronic inflammatory tone
- [[Mitochondria-Associated Membranes|MERCs]] between mitochondria and the [[Endoplasmic Reticulum|ER]] are remodeled in aging, weakening Ca²⁺ signaling and stress adaptation
