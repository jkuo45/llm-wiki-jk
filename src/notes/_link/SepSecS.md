# SepSecS

**Overview:** **SepSecS** is the enzyme that installs the selenium atom of **[[Selenocysteine]]** onto its tRNA. It replaces the phosphoseryl group of O-phosphoseryl-tRNA[Ser]Sec (made by **[[PSTK]]**) with Sec, using **monoselenophosphate** as the selenium donor.

## Mechanism

- Monoselenophosphate is generated from selenide and ATP by **[[SEPHS2]]** (selenophosphate synthetase 2).
- The product, **Sec-tRNA[Ser]Sec**, is then delivered by **[[eEFSec]]** to the ribosome for insertion at recoded **[[UGA codon|UGA codons]]**.

## Significance

SepSecS is the final, selenium-incorporating step of Sec biosynthesis; its loss abolishes **[[Selenoprotein]]** synthesis and is lethal in mice.
