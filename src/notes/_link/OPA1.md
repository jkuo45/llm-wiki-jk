# OPA1

**OPA1** (Optic Atrophy Protein 1) is a dynamin-related GTPase anchored to the [[Inner Mitochondrial Membrane|inner mitochondrial membrane (IMM)]] that mediates [[Mitochondrial Fusion|inner-membrane fusion]] in cooperation with the lipid [[Cardiolipin]]. It is the mammalian ortholog of the yeast protein [[Mgm1|Mgm1]]. Mutations in *OPA1* are the leading cause of [[Dominant Optic Atrophy|dominant optic atrophy (DOA)]], the most common inherited optic neuropathy.

## Structure and Isoforms

OPA1 is produced as eight splice isoforms differing in three small N-terminal exons (4, 4b, 5b). All carry an N-terminal [[Mitochondrial Targeting Sequence|mitochondrial targeting sequence (MTS)]] that is cleaved by the matrix processing protease on import into the [[Mitochondrial Matrix|matrix]]. OPA1 is proteolytically processed at up to three sites (S1, S2, S3):
- **S1** (present in all isoforms) is cleaved by **[[OMA1]]**, yielding the short form **S-OPA1**; an intact S1 yields the long, membrane-anchored **L-OPA1**.
- **S2/S3** are cleaved constitutively by **[[YME1L]]**, also yielding S-OPA1.

## Mechanism of Inner-Membrane Fusion

Efficient IMM fusion requires L-OPA1 and S-OPA1 acting together (though some studies report L-OPA1 alone suffices). [[Cardiolipin|Cardiolipin]] binds L-OPA1 on liposomes and is absolutely required for fusion — removing cardiolipin abolishes membrane merging even with L-OPA1 on both leaflets — and GTP hydrolysis by OPA1 is essential.

## Regulation

- **[[SIRT3]]** (NAD-dependent sirtuin-3) deacetylates OPA1 at Lys926 and Lys931 in its GTPase effector domain, raising GTPase activity and enhancing [[Mitochondrial Fusion|fusion]] during stress.
- Proteolytic processing by [[OMA1]] and [[YME1L]] dynamically tunes the L/S-OPA1 ratio in response to membrane potential and stress.

## Pathological Significance

- **[[Dominant Optic Atrophy]]** — mostly missense *OPA1* mutations impair IMM fusion and mitochondrial function; DOA-plus includes deafness/ataxia. Disease alleles show defects in cardiolipin-stimulated GTP hydrolysis and membrane tubulation.
- **[[Parkinson's Disease]]** — increased cleavage of the OPA1 long isoform contributes to fission excess in sporadic PD.
- **[[Cancer]]** — OPA1-dependent fusion supports liver tumor cell metabolism and growth.
