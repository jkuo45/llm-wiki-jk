# p47phox

**p47phox** (NCF1) is the cytosolic organizer of the NOX2 [[NADPH Oxidase]]: upon phosphorylation it translocates with p67phox/p40phox to membrane cytochrome b558 ([[p22phox]]/gp91phox), triggering the respiratory burst. NCF1 mutations are the commonest autosomal-recessive cause of [[Chronic Granulomatous Disease]].
