# HSL

HSL acts with ATGL and monoacylglycerol lipase in the sequential breakdown of stored triacylglycerol; sirtuins (SIRT1, SIRT6) promote lipolysis partly via ATGL induction, with HSL providing the downstream diacylglycerol step.
