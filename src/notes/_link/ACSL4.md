# ACSL4

**ACSL4 (acyl-CoA synthetase long-chain family member 4)** is a lipid-activating enzyme that preferentially charges long-chain and polyunsaturated fatty acids ([[PUFA|PUFAs]] such as [[Arachidonic acid|arachidonic acid]]) to their acyl-CoA thioesters, enabling their re-esterification into membrane [[Phospholipid|phospholipids]] (notably via the Lands cycle). It is a well-established **positive regulator of [[Ferroptosis]]**.

## Structure & Function

ACSL4 localizes to the outer [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] membrane and [[Endoplasmic Reticulum|ER]] and is selective for arachidonoyl- and adrenoyl-CoA synthesis. By supplying PUFA-CoA substrates for [[Phospholipid|phospholipid]] remodeling (Lands cycle, with [[Phospholipase A2|PLA2]]), ACSL4 enriches the *sn-2* position of phosphatidylethanolamines and phosphatidylcholines with oxidizable PUFAs—the direct substrates of [[Lipid Peroxidation|lipid peroxidation]].

## Role in Ferroptosis & Senescence

- ACSL4 over-expression raises membrane PL-[[PUFA|PUFA]] content and ferroptosis sensitivity; its knockdown protects cells from [[RSL3]] and [[GPX4]] inhibition.
- ACSL4 is itself **up-regulated during [[Senescence|senescence]]**, contributing to the pro-ferroptotic state of [[Senescent Cells|senescent cells]].

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex|Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile… (Soriano-Castell et al., 2026)]]
> In replicatively senescent WI-38 fibroblasts, [[Acid ceramidase]] (ACase) knockdown *lowered* PL-[[PUFA|PUFA]] content and ferroptosis sensitivity **without altering ACSL4 protein levels**. This demonstrates that ACase acts through a distinct, ACSL4-independent mechanism to shape the pro-ferroptotic lipid profile, even though both enzymes converge on membrane PUFA-PL abundance.

## Connections

- [[Ferroptosis]] — ACSL4 is a canonical positive regulator; its activity sets membrane PUFA-PL substrate levels.
- [[Acid ceramidase]] — Converges on PUFA-PL abundance but via an independent (ACSL4-unchanged) mechanism in senescence.
- [[PUFA]] / [[Arachidonic acid]] — Preferred ACSL4 substrates; their CoA thioesters feed phospholipid remodeling.
- [[Phospholipid]] — The membrane compartment whose PUFA content ACSL4 enriches.
- [[Lipid Peroxidation]] — The downstream execution step enabled by ACSL4-driven PUFA incorporation.
- [[GPX4]] — The peroxidase whose inhibition (or ACSL4-driven substrate excess) triggers ferroptosis.
- [[RSL3]] — GPX4 inhibitor used to reveal ACSL4-dependent ferroptosis sensitivity.
- [[Senescent Cells]] — ACSL4 is up-regulated in senescence, contributing to ferroptotic vulnerability.
- [[Phospholipase A2]] — PLA2 releases sn-2 fatty acids, the partner step in the Lands cycle that ACSL4 supplies.
