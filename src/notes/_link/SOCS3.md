# SOCS3

**SOCS3** (Suppressor of Cytokine Signaling 3) is a member of the SOCS (suppressor of cytokine signaling) protein family that functions as a critical negative-feedback regulator of [[JAK]]/[[STAT1|STAT]] signaling and cytokine responses.

## Structure

SOCS3 contains a central SH2 domain that binds phosphorylated tyrosine residues on target receptors and a C-terminal SOCS box that recruits the ubiquitin-transferase machinery (Elongin B/C, Cullin5, Rbx2) for proteasomal degradation of associated proteins.

## Role in IDO1 Regulation

SOCS3 mediates post-translational regulation of [[IDO1]]:
- [[IDO1]] phosphorylation at ITIM (immunoreceptor tyrosine-based inhibitory motif) residues recruits SOCS3
- SOCS3 targets IDO1 for ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation
- This constitutes a negative feedback loop that limits IDO1 protein stability and catalytic activity

## Broader Functions

- Potent inhibitor of [[Interferon-gamma]] and IL-6 signaling
- Induced by [[JAK]]/[[STAT1|STAT3]] signaling as a classic negative-feedback regulator
- Implicated in [[Insulin Resistance]] (inhibits insulin receptor signaling)
- Key regulator of [[Inflammation]] and immune homeostasis
