# PEX6

**PEX6** (peroxin 6; formerly *peroxisomal assembly factor 2 / PAF2* or *PXAAA1*) is an ATPase associated with diverse cellular activities ([[ATP|AAA+ ATPase]]) embedded in the peroxisomal membrane, and a **perxin** — one of the proteins required for [[Peroxisome]] biogenesis and maintenance. The human gene lies at 6p21.1 and encodes a ~980-residue protein (OMIM 601498).

## Mechanism of Action

Peroxisomal matrix enzymes are imported post-translationally by two soluble receptors, PEX5 (PTS1) and PEX7 (PTS2), which dock at the membrane docking complex assembled by [[PEX13]] and [[PEX14]]. Cargo translocates through the pore, and the now-unloaded, mono-ubiquitinated PEX5 is then removed from the membrane by the **PEX1–PEX6 exportomer**, tethered to the membrane by PEX26.

> [!info] How PEX6 works mechanically
> PEX6 and PEX1 each contain **two tandem AAA+ ATPase domains** (D1 and D2) and assemble into a **heterohexameric double ring in which the two proteins alternate** around the ring (Ding *et al.*, *PNAS* 2015). Ring stacking gives one ring a substrate-unfolding/threading role and the other an assembly or substrate-selection role, as in p97/ER-associated protein degradation. ATP hydrolysis drives processive extraction of poly-ubiquitinated PEX5, its deubiquitination and its return to the cytosol for another import round.

A second, mechanistically distinct role is **protection against pexophagy**: loss of PEX6 (or PEX1/PEX26) causes a fall in peroxisome number, attributed primarily to excessive peroxisomal degradation rather than failed import (Law *et al.*, *Autophagy* 2017).

## Physiological Function

PEX6 supplies the only energy-consuming step in peroxisome matrix protein import, and peroxisomes are the organelle responsible for very-long-chain fatty acid [[Beta-Oxidation]] and ether-phospholipid synthesis (with plasmalogen production), [[Cholesterol]]/bile-acid precursor side chains, and [[Fatty acid oxidation]] of very-long-chain species. In the vault's cargo network PEX6 is the recycling partner of [[PEX5]] and the energy source behind continued [[PEX1]]/[[PEX26]]-anchored import.

## Pathology

Loss-of-function *PEX6* variants cause the **peroxisome biogenesis disorder (PBD) spectrum** — Zellweger syndrome, neonatal adrenoleukodystrophy, infantile Refsum disease, and the milder Heimler syndrome (PMID 26387595).

> [!warning] The two genes dominate the spectrum
> *PEX1* and *PEX6* together account for roughly 80% of PBD cases — about 60% for *PEX1* and about 10% for *PEX6* (Geisbrecht *et al.*, *PNAS* 1998). Most *PEX6* mutations are truncating/deletion alleles producing the severe neonatal phenotype, whereas hypomorphic missense alleles underlie the milder, sometimes hearing-and-vision-predominant Heimler syndrome, which has been called an **Usher syndrome mimic**. Loss of PEX6 also produces excess very-long-chain fatty acids and reduced plasmalogens — the same plasma signature used to screen newborns for the PBD spectrum.

## Connections

- [[PEX1]] — Its obligate heterodimeric partner in the AAA+ exportomer; the two genes dominate the Zellweger spectrum, and loss of the PEX1–PEX6 interaction is itself the commonest cause of PBD.
- [[PEX5]] — The PTS1 import receptor that PEX1/PEX6 extract from the membrane after mono-ubiquitination by the PEX2/PEX10/PEX12 ligase complex; this recycling step is PEX6's core function.
- [[Peroxisomes]] — The organelle whose biogenesis PEX6 sustains; its metabolic output (β-oxidation, plasmalogens) is what fails in PBD.
- [[Pexophagy]] — Selective autophagic removal of peroxisomes, which PEX6 normally suppresses — peroxisome loss in PEX6-deficient cells is driven by excessive pexophagy.
- [[Zellweger syndrome]] — The severe end of the PBD spectrum and the classic clinical phenotype of *PEX6* null alleles.
- [[Neonatal Adrenoleukodystrophy]] — The intermediate, neurologically progressive PBD presentation caused by residual PEX6 activity.
- [[ATP]] — PEX6 is a member of the AAA+ ATPase family, the energy source for the translocation step; the tandem-domain architecture resembles p97/ERAD.
