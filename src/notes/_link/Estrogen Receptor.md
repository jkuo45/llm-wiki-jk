# Estrogen Receptor

The **estrogen receptors** ([[Estrogen Receptor|ERα]] encoded by *ESR1*, and [[Estrogen Receptor|ERβ]] encoded by *ESR2*) are ligand-activated [[Nuclear Receptor|nuclear receptors]] that regulate gene transcription in reproductive and other tissues.

## Role in Cancer

ERα is the dominant driver of luminal [[Breast Cancer]]; its expression guides endocrine therapy. Flavonoids such as [[Apigenin]] and [[Genistein]] show biphasic estrogenic/anti-estrogenic activity via ER-binding-dependent and -independent mechanisms, and apigenin exerts anti-estrogenic effects in MCF-7 breast cancer cells. ER status also influences apigenin's growth-inhibitory effects in endometrial cancer.
