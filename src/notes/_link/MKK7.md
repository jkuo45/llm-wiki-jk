# MKK7

**MKK7** (MAP kinase kinase 7, gene *MAP2K7*) is a dual-specificity MAP2K that phosphorylates and activates [[JNK]]. It sits immediately below the MAP3K tier ([[ASK1]], MEKK1, MLK3) in the stress-activated arm of the [[MAPK]] cascade, and it is the kinase that [[c-FLIP|cFLIP_L]] binds directly to inhibit.

## Structure & Activation

Cytosolic dual-specificity kinase with a kinase domain, a regulatory domain containing the S/T-X-S activation loop, and a C-terminal docking region. Activated by phosphorylation of its activation loop (Ser218/Thr222 in the human MKK7a isoform) by upstream MAP3Ks in response to ROS, calcium, receptor signals and stress.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **JNK activation.** MKK7 phosphorylates [[JNK]] on Thr and Tyr within the TPY motif; MKK4 acts in parallel with the same output kinase. Together with MKK3/MKK6 for [[p38 MAPK]], MKK7 defines the two effector branches of the stress-activated MAPK cascade.
- **cFLIP_L inhibition.** cFLIP_L binds MKK7 and inhibits JNK signaling. Because [[JNK]] is in turn required for Itch-mediated cFLIP_L degradation, the two form a closed loop: cFLIP_L restrains JNK, JNK1 activates [[Itch]], Itch removes cFLIP_L.
- **ASK1 upstream.** [[ASK1]] phosphorylates MKK4 and MKK7 upon activation, which is the principal route from ROS and receptor-mediated stress to JNK.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - FLIP the Switch Regulation of Apoptosis and Necroptosis by cFLIP|Tsuchiya et al. 2015]]
> cFLIP_L binds MAP kinase kinase 7 (MKK7) and inhibits the JNK pathway, and the downregulation of cFLIP induces JNK activation and the accumulation of [[Reactive Oxygen Species|ROS]] in tumor cells. The authors frame the mutual regulation between cFLIP and the JNK–Itch pathway as critically important in determining cell destiny, since JNK1-dependent [[Itch]] activity polyubiquitylates and degrades cFLIP_L during TNF-α-dependent cell death.

## Physiological Function

MKK7 is the conduit between upstream stress sensing and JNK-dependent transcription and apoptosis. Its coupling to the cFLIP rheostat means that the concentration of a caspase-8 inhibitor sets the tone of the JNK branch, and vice versa.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Tumor cell death.** cFLIP downregulation releases MKK7–JNK signaling and raises ROS in tumor cells, biasing the stress axis toward apoptosis.
- **Inflammatory transcription.** MKK7 sustains the AP-1 arm of stress-activated transcription alongside the NF-κB arm.
