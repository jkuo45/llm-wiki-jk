# Exportin-5

**Exportin-5 (XPO5)** is a RanGTP-dependent [[Nuclear Export|nuclear export]] receptor that transports precursor [[MicroRNA|miRNA]] (pre-miRNA) hairpins from the nucleus to the cytoplasm, where they are matured by **[[Dicer]]** and loaded onto **[[Argonaute]]** in the [[RISC]].

## Mechanism

- Binds the ~60–70 nt pre-miRNA hairpin (with its 2-nt 3′ overhang generated by the [[Microprocessor complex|Microprocessor]]) in a RanGTP-dependent manner.
- Shuttles the pre-miRNA through the nuclear pore; release in the cytoplasm delivers substrate to Dicer.

> [!note] Step in the miRNA pipeline
> [[DGCR8]] + [[Drosha]] (Microprocessor) → pre-miRNA → **Exportin-5** → cytoplasm → [[Dicer]] → [[RISC]].
