# FGF

**Fibroblast growth factors** (22 members) signal through FGFR tyrosine kinases to drive proliferation, [[Cell Migration]], and tissue repair. Canonical FGFs act locally in paracrine fashion, while the FGF19 subfamily ([[FGF21]]) functions as endocrine hormones regulating metabolism and mitochondrial stress responses.
