# Thioredoxin-2

**Thioredoxin-2 (Trx2)**, encoded by the *TXN2* gene, is the **mitochondrial** isoform of the thioredoxin system — the organellar counterpart to the cytosolic [[Thioredoxin-1]] (Trx1). It contains an N-terminal mitochondrial targeting sequence that is cleaved on import, yielding a mature ~12 kDa protein that carries the characteristic CxxC redox-active site through which it maintains the mitochondrial thiol-redox balance, supports [[Oxidative Phosphorylation]] and mitochondrial antioxidant defenses, and regulates the mitochondrial branch of stress signaling.

## Structure & Localization

- Trx2 shares the canonical thioredoxin fold and a Cys-Gly-Pro-Cys (CxxC) catalytic motif with [[Thioredoxin-1]], but is distinguished by a mitochondrial presequence directing it to the matrix.
- It is recycled to the active reduced form by the mitochondrial isoform of [[Thioredoxin reductase|thioredoxin reductase]] (TXNRD2) using NADPH, in a membrane-associated system that also regenerates [[Peroxiredoxin|peroxiredoxin]] 3 ([[Peroxiredoxin 3|Prx3]]) — the major mitochondrial H₂O₂-removal enzyme.
- Trx2 is essential for life: *Txn2* knockout mice are embryonic lethal, reflecting the irreplaceable role of mitochondrial redox homeostasis in development.

## Redox Function in Mitochondria

Within mitochondria, Trx2 reduces oxidized cysteine residues on matrix proteins and, together with Prx3 and glutaredoxin-2, disposes of respiratory ROS:

- **Peroxiredoxin 3 regeneration**: Trx2 donates electrons to reduce oxidized [[Peroxiredoxin 3|Prx3]], sustaining mitochondrial H₂O₂ clearance.
- **Respiratory support**: Trx2 helps maintain the reduced/active state of respiratory chain components and matrix enzymes, supporting complex activity and membrane potential under stress.
- **Sensitive to peroxynitrite**: As noted in the oxidative-stress literature, oxidation of Trx2 by [[Peroxynitrite]] (along with [[Mitochondrial Permeability Transition Pore]] components) contributes to mitochondrial swelling and [[Apoptosis]].

## ASK1 Regulation & Apoptosis

Unlike cytosolic Trx1, Trx2 specifically antagonizes the **mitochondria-located** pool of the stress kinase [[ASK1]]:

- In basal conditions, Trx2 keeps mitochondrial ASK1 inactive.
- On oxidative/mitochondrial stress, Trx2 is oxidized and dissociates, de-repressing ASK1 to drive [[JNK]]-independent, mitochondrial apoptotic signaling.
- This Trx2–ASK1 axis couples mitochondrial redox status to the intrinsic apoptosis pathway, complementing Trx1's regulation of the cytoplasmic ASK1 pool.

## Clinical & Pathological Relevance

- **Ischemia–reperfusion and cardiac injury**: Mitochondrial oxidative/nitrative damage and Trx2 depletion contribute to cardiac dysfunction; preserving Trx2/Prx3 redox capacity is a goal of cardioprotective strategies.
- **Mitochondrial disease and metabolic stress**: Trx2 redox defects impair respiration and elevate mitochondrial ROS, linked to metabolic and age-related dysfunction.
- **Cancer**: Some tumors up-regulate mitochondrial antioxidant systems (Trx2/Prx3) to resist oxidative and therapy-induced damage.
