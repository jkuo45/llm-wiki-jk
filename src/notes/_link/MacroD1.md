# MacroD1

**MacroD1** (macrodomain-containing protein 1, gene `MACROD1`, alias LRP16) is a MacroD-type macrodomain [[ADP-ribosylation]] eraser, predominantly localized to the mitochondrial matrix with nuclear splice forms. It hydrolyzes mono-ADP-ribose ester-linked to aspartate/glutamate, deacetylates 2'-/3'-O-acetyl-ADP-ribose ([[O-acetyl-ADP-ribose]]), and clears 5'/3'-phosphorylated DNA-ADP-ribose adducts in vitro.

> [!info] MacroD1 in one sentence
> Mitochondrial MacroD1 reverses acidic-residue MARylation and sirtuin-generated OAADPr, opposing writers such as PARP10/PARP7; inactive toward [[PAR]] polymers, which are left to [[PARG]]/[[ARH3]].

## Overview

- ~35 kDa, 11 exons; N-terminal mitochondrial-targeting sequence (1-85), N-terminal region (91-136), macrodomain (151-322).
- High mRNA in skeletal muscle; protein enriched in [[Mitochondria]].
- Paralog of [[MacroD2]] (similar fold, distinct localization); founding member of MacroD-like subgroup with E. coli YmdB and archaeal AF1521.
- OAADPr deacetylase kinetics: Km ~100-400 uM, competitively inhibited by ADP-ribose (Ki ~145 uM).

## Structure & Catalytic mechanism

Three-layered alpha-beta-alpha sandwich with central six-stranded beta-sheet. ADP-ribose binding closes the beta5-alpha10 loop (Phe272 flip) over the distal ribose. Conserved Gly182 anchors the 1''-OH; Asn174/Asp184 coordinate the 2''-OH for OAADPr chemistry but are dispensable for MAR hydrolysis (N174A/D184A retains PARP10-deMARylation), supporting a substrate-assisted mechanism: the ADP-ribose alpha-phosphate activates a conserved water (water12) for nucleophilic attack on ribose C1'', cleaving the Asp/Glu ester. No metal requirement; inhibited by ADP-HPD and free ADP-ribose.

## Physiological function

- **MAR reversal:** erases PARP10/PARP7-mediated Asp/Glu-MARylation on kinases and signaling proteins (e.g. GSK3beta paradigm for MacroD2); counteracts [[PARP1]] mono-modification after [[PARG]] pre-trimming.
- **OAADPr homeostasis:** with [[MacroD2]], co-covers all compartments where [[Sirtuins]] generate OAADPr; links [[NAD+]] deacetylation flux to acetate/ADP-ribose recycling.
- **Nuclear roles:** binds/activates estrogen and androgen receptors, NF-kB signaling subunit RELA; disputed direct DNA-repair recruitment (unlike [[MacroD2]]-ATM axis).

## Pathology & Clinical relevance

- Expression/fusions implicated in leukemia and breast, gastric, liver, lung, colorectal cancers; proposed negative regulator of insulin signaling via IRS1 down-regulation.
- `Macrod1`-KO mice viable/fertile with female-specific motor-coordination defect; rhabdomyosarcoma loss causes mitochondrial fragmentation.
- No human monogenic disease established; paralog [[MacroD2]] carries stronger neurodevelopmental signal.
