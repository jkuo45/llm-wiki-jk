# Dendritic cells

[[Dendritic cells]] (DCs) are professional antigen-presenting cells that capture antigen, migrate to lymph nodes, and prime naive T cells, bridging innate and adaptive immunity.

## Rapamycin and Dendritic cells

Low-dose inhibition of [[mTORC1]] by [[Rapamycin]] improves immune responses to infections and vaccinations by increasing [[CD8 T cells|CD8⁺ memory lymphocyte]] count and dendritic cell activity. The effect is dose-dependent—immunostimulatory at low dose, immunosuppressive at high dose ([Zerdka et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12766144/)).

> [!note] Dose matters
> As with Tregs and CD8⁺ cells, dendritic cell potentiation by mTOR inhibition is a low-dose phenomenon.
