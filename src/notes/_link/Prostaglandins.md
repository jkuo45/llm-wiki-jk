# Prostaglandins

**Prostaglandins (PGs)** are 20-carbon lipid autacoids derived from [[Arachidonic acid]] through the [[Cyclooxygenases|cyclooxygenase]] pathway. They act locally rather than as circulating hormones, and mediate inflammation, pain, fever, vascular tone, and platelet aggregation.

## Biosynthesis

Free [[Arachidonic acid]] is released from membrane phospholipids by cytosolic phospholipase A2 (cPLA2), then oxidized in two steps by COX-1 or COX-2:

1. **[[Prostaglandin G₂]] (PGG₂)** — the bis-dioxygenation product bearing a C15 hydroperoxide
2. **[[Prostaglandin H₂]] (PGH₂)** — the reduced endoperoxide and common precursor

PGH₂ is converted by terminal synthases into the principal prostaglandins and related prostanoids:

| Product | Synthase | Principal actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PGE₂ | PGE synthase | pain, fever, vasodilation, inflammation, mucosal protection |
| PGD₂ | PGD synthase (mast cells) | allergy, sleep, bronchoconstriction |
| PGF₂α | PGF synthase | uterine contraction, luteolysis |
| PGI₂ ([[Prostacyclin]]) | prostacyclin synthase | vasodilation, inhibition of platelet aggregation |
| [[Thromboxane A₂]] | thromboxane synthase | vasoconstriction, platelet aggregation |

COX-1 is constitutive and maintains gastric mucosa, renal perfusion, and platelet function; COX-2 is inducible at inflammatory sites and in vascular endothelium. Aspirin irreversibly acetylates COX-1 Ser530, and at low dose selectively suppresses platelet [[Thromboxane A₂]] while sparing endothelial [[Prostacyclin]].

## Relationship to Lipid Peroxidation

The COX pathway is enzymatic and stereospecific, unlike the free-radical route that produces [[F2-Isoprostanes]]. However, the COX peroxidase cycle requires a hydroperoxide activator and generates high-valent iron-oxo intermediates that can leak and oxidize nearby [[Polyunsaturated fatty acids|PUFAs]] — coupling prostanoid synthesis to local [[Lipid Peroxidation]]. Under [[Glutathione]] depletion this coupling strengthens, which is why inflamed tissue shows both elevated prostaglandins and elevated oxidative damage markers.

> [!info] Reading the two pathways together
> Prostaglandin flux reports *inflammatory enzymatic* activity; [[F2-Isoprostanes]] report *radical* oxidative damage. Measuring both separates the two, and a rise in prostaglandins with stable F2-IsoPs points to inflammation rather than oxidative stress.

## Physiological and Clinical Significance

- **Inflammation and pain**: PGE₂ is the dominant prostaglandin of acute inflammation, sensitizing nociceptors to bradykinin and histamine.
- **Gastric protection**: COX-1-derived PGE₂ and PGI₂ stimulate mucus and bicarbonate secretion; NSAID gastropathy follows their suppression.
- **Renal**: prostaglandins maintain glomerular filtration and renal blood flow, especially under volume depletion.
- **Vascular**: the PGI₂/TXA₂ balance determines thrombotic tendency; endothelial dysfunction shifts it toward [[Thromboxane A₂]].
- **Pharmacology**: NSAIDs inhibit the cyclooxygenase oxygenase site; selective COX-2 inhibitors reduce inflammation but raise cardiovascular risk by tipping the balance toward thrombosis.
- **Cancer**: COX-2 overexpression and elevated PGE₂ characterize colorectal, breast, and lung tumors; PGE₂ promotes angiogenesis, immune evasion, and cell survival.

## Connections

- [[Prostaglandin G₂]]: First committed intermediate of the cascade.
- [[Cyclooxygenases]]: COX-1 and COX-2 catalyze PGG₂/PGH₂ formation.
- [[Prostacyclin]]: Vasodilator, anti-aggregatory prostanoid from PGH₂.
- [[Thromboxane A₂]]: Vasoconstrictor, pro-aggregatory prostanoid from PGH₂.
- [[Lipid Peroxidation]]: COX peroxidase cycle can leak radicals into surrounding lipids.
- [[F2-Isoprostanes]]: Non-enzymatic counterpart used to distinguish radical from enzymatic oxidation.
- [[Hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acids]]: Parallel lipoxygenase branch of arachidonic acid metabolism.
- [[Polyunsaturated fatty acids]]: Membrane substrate pool feeding prostanoid synthesis.
- [[Inflammation]]: Prostaglandins are the dominant lipid mediators of acute inflammation.
- [[Atherosclerosis]]: Prostanoid imbalance drives vascular inflammation and thrombosis.
- [[Glutathione]]: Depletion strengthens COX-coupled lipid peroxidation.
