### Overview
Mgm1 (mitochondrial genome maintenance 1) is a dynamin-like GTPase in Saccharomyces cerevisiae that is the ortholog of mammalian [[OPA1]] and is essential for mitochondrial fusion. Loss of Mgm1 leads to fragmented, dysfunctional mitochondria and loss of mitochondrial DNA.

### Function
Mgm1 resides at the [[Inner Mitochondrial Membrane]] and, like OPA1, exists in long and short forms generated by proteolytic processing. These forms cooperate to mediate inner-membrane fusion and maintain cristae architecture.

### Evolutionary Conservation
The Mgm1–OPA1 axis is conserved from yeast to humans, reflecting the fundamental requirement for inner-membrane fusion in [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] and the ancient origin of mitochondria via [[Endosymbiosis]]. Mutations in human [[OPA1]] cause [[Dominant Optic Atrophy]].
