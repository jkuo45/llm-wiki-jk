# FTSJ1

**Overview:** [[FTSJ1]] (FtsJ homolog 1) is a **tRNA 2′-*O*-methyltransferase** that catalyzes the methylation of the ribose at position 34 of tRNA[Ser]Sec, producing the **Um34** (2′-*O*-methyluridine-34) modification. It was independently linked to X-linked intellectual disability before its role in selenocysteine biology was uncovered.

## Mechanism and role in selenocysteine incorporation

- Um34 distinguishes the two tRNA[Ser]Sec **isodecoders**; only the methylated form supports efficient insertion of [[Selenocysteine]] at recoded [[UGA codon|UGA stop codons]].
- **[[FTSJ1]] is the Um34 writer:** loss of FTSJ1 activity removes Um34, causing **ribosomal stalling** during selenoprotein translation and **decreased UGA recoding**, selectively impairing translation of antioxidant selenoproteins.

> [!important]
> Source: [[Selenocysteine tRNA methylation promotes oxidative stress resistance in melanoma metastasis]]
> FTSJ1-mediated Um34 methylation of tRNA[Ser]Sec is required for efficient selenocysteine insertion. FTSJ1-deficient cells are hypersensitive to oxidative stress and show reduced melanoma metastatic colonization; Um34 modification is necessary and sufficient for metastasis in vivo, marking FTSJ1 as a potential metastasis-selective therapeutic target.

## Phenotypic links

- **X-linked intellectual disability** — germline *FTSJ1* loss-of-function mutations disrupt codon-specific translation efficiency in the brain (Nagayoshi et al., *Sci. Adv.* 2021; Guy et al., *Hum. Mutat.* 2015).
- **Cancer metastasis** — tumor cells exploit FTSJ1/Um34 to sustain antioxidant selenoprotein translation under the high oxidative stress of the metastatic niche (Nease et al., 2024).
