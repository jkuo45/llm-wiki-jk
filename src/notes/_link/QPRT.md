# QPRT

## Overview

[[QPRT]] (Quinolinate Phosphoribosyltransferase) is an enzyme in the de novo [[NAD+ Biosynthesis|NAD+ biosynthesis]] pathway. It catalyzes the conversion of [[Quinolinic Acid]] to nicotinic acid mononucleotide (NaMN), linking [[Tryptophan]] metabolism via the [[Kynurenine Pathway]] to NAD+ production.

## Structure & Function

- **Gene:** QPRT, located on chromosome 16p11.2
- **Protein:** ~297 amino acid phosphoribosyltransferase
- **Reaction:** Quinolinic acid + PRPP → Nicotinic acid mononucleotide (NaMN) + CO₂ + PPi
- **Cofactor:** Requires Mg<sup>2+</sup> and phosphoribosyl pyrophosphate (PRPP)
- **Tissue expression:** Highly expressed in liver, kidney, and brain

## Mechanism of Action

### De Novo NAD+ Biosynthesis

QPRT functions at the junction of the kynurenine pathway and NAD+ biosynthesis:

1. **[[Tryptophan]]** → N-Formylkynurenine (via IDO/TDO)
2. **N-Formylkynurenine** → Kynurenine → 3-Hydroxykynurenine → [[Quinolinic Acid]]
3. **QPRT:** Quinolinic acid → NaMN (nicotinic acid mononucleotide)
4. **[[NMNAT]]:** NaMN → NaAD → [[NAD+]]

This pathway represents the de novo route for NAD+ synthesis from the essential amino acid tryptophan.

### Regulation

- **Substrate availability:** Activity depends on quinolinic acid levels from kynurenine pathway
- **Tissue specificity:** High expression in liver and kidney reflects their role in NAD+ metabolism
- **Brain expression:** Important for maintaining neuronal NAD+ pools

## Physiological Function

- **NAD+ biosynthesis:** Converts kynurenine pathway intermediate to NAD+ precursor
- **Tryptophan catabolism:** Links amino acid metabolism to NAD+ production
- **Neuroprotection:** Prevents accumulation of neurotoxic quinolinic acid

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Neurotoxicity:** Quinolinic acid is an NMDA receptor agonist; accumulation is neurotoxic
- **Neurodegenerative disease:** Kynurenine pathway dysregulation in Alzheimer's, Huntington's, and ALS
- **Inflammation:** [[IFN-γ]] and inflammatory signals upregulate IDO, increasing quinolinic acid production
- **Metabolic disease:** Impaired QPRT function may limit de novo NAD+ biosynthesis
