# Tamoxifen

**Tamoxifen** is a selective estrogen receptor modulator (SERM) that antagonizes estrogen signaling in breast tissue, a cornerstone of adjuvant and metastatic therapy for estrogen receptor-positive (ER+) breast cancer.

## Sirtuin Axis

- **[[SIRT1]]** causes tamoxifen resistance in ER+ breast cancer by upregulating multidrug resistance protein 2 (MRP2) via FoxO1 deacetylation; SIRT1 inhibition augments paclitaxel/doxorubicin cytotoxicity.
- **[[SIRT4]]** enhances tamoxifen sensitivity via STAT3 inhibition.
- **[[SIRT3]]** overexpression reduces tamoxifen sensitivity (MTR-3 line).

## Pharmacodynamics

Tamoxifen binds ERα to block estrogen-driven proliferation. Sirtuins influence response by modulating drug efflux (SIRT1/MRP2), STAT3 signaling (SIRT4), and tumor metabolism (SIRT3).

## Clinical Relevance

Sirtuin status may predict or modulate tamoxifen response, suggesting sirtuin-targeted combinations (e.g., SIRT1 inhibition) to overcome endocrine therapy resistance in ER+ breast cancer.
