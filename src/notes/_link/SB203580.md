# SB203580

**SB203580** is a selective inhibitor of **p38 MAPK** (MAPK14), blocking its phosphorylation/activation. In the apigenin study it was used (10 μM) as a pharmacological tool to verify p38 MAPK's contribution to [[SASP|SASP]] expression in senescent cells.

## Relevance

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> SB203580 was applied to senescent cells alongside the [[PRDX6]]-PLA2 inhibitor [[MJ33]] to probe SASP mechanisms. Apigenin itself blocks the crosstalk between [[HSPA8]] and p38 MAPK, consistent with p38 being a downstream node in the SASP program.

## Connections

- [[p38 MAPK]] — Direct inhibition target.
- [[SASP]] — Suppressed when p38 is inhibited.
- [[Apigenin]] — Achieves related SASP suppression via HSPA8/p38 crosstalk blockade.
