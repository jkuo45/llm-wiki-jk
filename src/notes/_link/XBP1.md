# XBP1

SIRT6 alleviates endoplasmic reticulum stress by regulating XBP1, complementing its H3K9 deacetylation at glycolytic promoters to limit inflammatory and metabolic dysfunction in the liver (Szczęsny-Małysiak et al., 2026).
