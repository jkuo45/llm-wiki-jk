# UCP1

**Uncoupling protein 1 ([[UCP1]], thermogenin)** is a mitochondrial inner-membrane carrier expressed almost exclusively in [[Brown Adipose Tissue|brown adipose tissue]]. It transports protons from the intermembrane space back into the matrix, short-circuiting the proton gradient established by the [[Electron Transport Chain|electron transport chain]] so that energy is released as heat instead of being captured as [[ATP]].

## Function and Activation

Cold exposure activates BAT and induces UCP1, increasing mitochondrial density and non-shivering thermogenesis. This controlled cold stress is a classic mitohormetic stimulus that, with recovery, enhances mitochondrial quality and metabolic flexibility.
