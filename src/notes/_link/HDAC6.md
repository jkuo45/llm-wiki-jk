### Overview
HDAC6 (Histone Deacetylase 6) is a cytoplasmically localized deacetylase that regulates mitochondrial dynamics. Under glucose deprivation, HDAC6 deacetylates the fusion protein [[MFN1]], activating it and thereby promoting [[Mitochondrial Fusion|fusion]].

### Mechanism
Deacetylation of MFN1 by HDAC6 enhances its fusogenic activity, shifting the balance away from fission toward fusion. This adaptation helps maintain [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] and bioenergetic resilience when nutrient supply is limited.

### Functional Significance
HDAC6 couples metabolic state to organelle morphology, illustrating how post-translational modifications of fusion factors integrate stress signals. Its opposing partners include the pro-fission axis of [[DRP1]] and its adaptors.
