# J147

**J147** is a curcumin-derived, disease-modifying small molecule developed by Pamela Maher's lab. It is a potent **anti-[[Ferroptosis|ferroptotic]]** and anti-[[Lipid Peroxidation|lipid peroxidation]] agent, and has completed **Phase 1 clinical trials for [[Alzheimer's Disease]]**.

## Mechanism & Relevance

J147 was identified through a phenotypic, [[Ferroptosis|ferroptosis]]-based screening platform as a compound that specifically prevents [[Lipid Peroxidation|lipid peroxidation]] and [[Ferroptosis]] cell death. It improves cognitive function in aged Alzheimer's-disease mice and protects neurons from oxidative and metabolic stress.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex|Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile… (Soriano-Castell et al., 2026)]]
> In the WI-38 replicative-senescence study, J147 was used as a positive control that prevents [[RSL3]]-induced [[Ferroptosis]] and [[Lipid Peroxidation|LPO]], confirming that the elevated [[Lipid Peroxidation|lipid peroxidation]]/ferroptotic sensitivity of senescent cells is genuinely ferroptotic. Co-treatment with J147 rescued RSL3-killed senescent cells.
