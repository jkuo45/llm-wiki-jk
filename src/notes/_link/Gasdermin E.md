# Gasdermin E

**Gasdermin E** (**GSDME**, historically DFNA5) is a member of the gasdermin pore-forming family and the molecular switch that lets [[Apoptosis]] degrade into [[Pyroptosis]]. Unlike [[Gasdermin D]] — cleaved by inflammatory caspases — GSDME is cleaved by the **executioner caspase-3**, which is why a "quiet," non-inflammatory death can become lytic and inflammatory purely on the basis of GSDME abundance.

## Mechanism

- **Caspase-3 cleavage**: activated caspase-3 cuts GSDME, releasing the N-terminal pore-forming domain. High GSDME expression → oligomeric membrane pores → pyroptotic swelling and rupture on top of the apoptotic program.
- **Expression-level switch**: in **GSDME-high** cancer cells, chemotherapy-activated caspase-3 converts the death program to pyroptosis; in **GSDME-low** contexts, GSDME instead acts as an upstream activator of caspase-3, reinforcing classical apoptosis.
- **Bidirectional crosstalk**: caspase-3/GSDME-dependent pyroptosis can follow caspase-1-driven apoptosis in gasdermin-D-deficient cells (clearing apoptotic bodies the lytic way), and PINK1-mediated mitophagy suppresses caspase-3/GSDME-dependent pyroptosis in postoperative cognitive dysfunction.

> [!important]
> GSDME and [[Gasdermin D]] are regulated in opposite directions by apoptotic caspases: caspase-3/-7 **activate** GSDME (full pyroptosis) but **inactivate** GSDMD (non-canonical D87 cleavage, pyroptosis brake). The gasdermin substrate available — not the caspase activated — determines the death modality.
