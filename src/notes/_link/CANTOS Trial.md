# CANTOS Trial

The **CANTOS trial** randomized post-MI patients with elevated hs-CRP to [[Canakinumab]] (anti-IL-1β) or placebo, showing reduced cardiovascular events without lipid lowering — first clinical proof that targeting [[Inflammation]] prevents [[Atherosclerosis]] complications. Exploratory analyses reported lower lung-cancer incidence, fueling interest in IL-1 blockade for [[Inflammaging]] and senescence-associated disease.
