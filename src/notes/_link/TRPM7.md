# TRPM7

**TRPM7** (transient receptor potential melastatin 7) is a non-selective cation channel permeable to Mg2+ and Ca2+, unusual in that its C-terminus carries a functional alpha-kinase domain. It regulates cellular Mg2+ homeostasis, proliferation, migration, and stress responses, and has been linked to cell death in ischemia, oxidative stress, and inflammatory injury.

## Role in necroptosis

- The canonical [[MLKL]] execution route is oligomerization, membrane translocation, and pore formation. However, MLKL may also compromise membrane integrity indirectly, including through activation of ion channels such as TRPM7 — a route that contributes to necroptotic and necroptosis-like death.
- TRPM7 can also depolarize the membrane and alter Ca2+/Mg2+ handling during oxidative stress, potentially feeding ion-overload death pathways parallel to MLKL pores.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Regulatory complexity and therapeutic targeting of the necroptosis network|Niu et al. 2026]]
> The review notes that the terminal execution phase of necroptosis is more heterogeneous than previously appreciated, with MLKL-independent mechanisms and indirect membrane compromise via ion channels such as TRPM7.
