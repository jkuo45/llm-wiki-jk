# SN011

**SN011** is a small-molecule antagonist of the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS–STING]] pathway that binds the cyclic-dinucleotide (CDN) binding pocket of [[STING|STING]] and blocks STING activation. It is a selective tool compound used to study STING-dependent inflammation and [[Interferon|type I interferon]] responses.

## Use in Senescence Research

In senescent cells, [[mtDNA|mitochondrial DNA]] released into the cytosol can activate cGAS–STING signaling. SN011 is used to test whether STING activity contributes to the [[SASP|senescence-associated secretory phenotype]] independently of the mitochondrial [[Acetyl-CoA]]–[[H3K27ac]] axis.

> [!important] Source: [[_document_ - Mitochondrial metabolism and epigenetic crosstalk drive SASP|Mitochondrial metabolism and epigenetic crosstalk drive SASP]]
> Treating senescent cells with SN011 did not block H3K27ac loss or SASP suppression induced by [[SLC25A1]] inhibition, indicating that the mitochondrial citrate–acetyl-CoA SASP axis operates independently of mtDNA–cGAS–STING signaling.
