# Catechin

**Catechin** is a flavan-3-ol [[Flavonoids|flavonoid]] abundant in green tea, cocoa, and various fruits. It exhibits [[Antioxidant|antioxidant]], anti-[[Inflammation|inflammatory]], and cardiovascular protective activities.

In the fisetin senolytic screening panel (Yousefzadeh et al., 2018), catechin was one of the 10 flavonoids tested at 5 μM but showed no significant reduction in SA-β-gal<sup>+</sup> cells, indicating it lacks senotherapeutic activity at that dose.
