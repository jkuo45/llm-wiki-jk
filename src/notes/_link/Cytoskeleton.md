# Cytoskeleton

The **cytoskeleton** — [[Actin]] microfilaments, microtubules, and intermediate filaments — organizes cell shape, division, and motility. Integrin-FAK adhesions ([[FAK Signaling]], [[Focal Adhesion]]) couple extracellular matrix to actin stress fibers, transducing stiffness into [[Cell Migration]] and proliferation signals.

## Documents

- [[FAK Signaling]] — focal-adhesion kinase links integrins to cytoskeletal remodeling in migration contexts.
- [[Focal Adhesion]] — adhesion complexes anchoring the actin cytoskeleton to matrix.
