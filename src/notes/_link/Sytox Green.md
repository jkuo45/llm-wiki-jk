# Sytox Green

**Sytox Green** is a high-affinity, membrane-impermeant DNA dye that is non-fluorescent until bound to nucleic acids. Because it cannot cross intact membranes, live-cell Sytox positivity reports either membrane rupture or extracellular DNA — making it the standard real-time readout for [[NETosis]] (extruded NET DNA), alongside endpoint cit-H3 and MPO–DNA staining.
