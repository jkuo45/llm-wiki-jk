# Salicylic acid

[[Salicylic acid]] is a beta-hydroxybenzoic acid with keratolytic, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic uses (e.g., acne, warts). In the fenbendazole literature it is explored as a crystal-engineering vehicle.

## Role in Fenbendazole Repurposing

Fenbendazole-salicylic acid cocrystals achieved 100% drug release within 1 hour and 1.052 mg/ml solubility, attributed to carboxylic–carboxylic or carboxylic–amino hydrogen bonding (12).

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> "Salicylic acid would also be an excellent vehicle to improve the drug release rate of fenbendazole... achieving a 100% drug release rate in under 1 hour and a 1.052 mg/ml solubility" (12).
