# Murine embryonic fibroblast

**Murine embryonic fibroblasts (MEFs)** are primary fibroblasts derived from mouse embryos. They are commonly used as feeder layers that secrete factors (including LIF) supporting [[Embryonic Stem Cells|embryonic stem cell]] culture.

> [!info] In the DGCR8 study
> Seven miRNAs that appeared unchanged in *Dgcr8* knockout ES cells were shown to originate from MEF feeder contamination (high in MEFs, decreasing after passage on gelatin without feeders) — confirming DGCR8 is broadly required for authentic ES-cell miRNA processing.
