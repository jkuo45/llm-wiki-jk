# Deiodinase

**Overview:** The three **[[Selenoprotein|selenoenzymes]]** **DIO1, DIO2, and DIO3** regulate thyroid hormone signaling by deiodinating thyroid hormone metabolites. **DIO2** converts thyroxine (T4) to the active **[[Triiodothyronine]]** (T3); **DIO3** inactivates T4/T3 to reverse T3; **DIO1** contributes to both activation and inactivation.

## Mechanism

- Each contains an active-site **[[Selenocysteine]]** whose selenol is essential for the reductase chemistry; loss of Sec abolishes activity.
- They thereby act as the **gatekeepers of thyroid hormone action** at the tissue level, setting local T3 concentrations independently of systemic thyroid status.

## Clinical relevance

- **[[SECISBP2]]** deficiency impairs deiodinase expression/activity, causing abnormal thyroid hormone metabolism despite normal circulating thyroid hormones — illustrating that the Sec insertion machinery gates endocrine selenoprotein function.
