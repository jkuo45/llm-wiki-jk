# MBOAT1

**MBOAT1** (membrane-bound O-acyltransferase domain-containing 1) is a lyso-phospholipid acyltransferase that suppresses [[Ferroptosis]] [[GPX4]]- and [[FSP1]]-independently: it enriches membranes with monounsaturated-fatty-acid phosphatidylethanolamines (PE-MUFA) at the expense of peroxidizable PE-PUFA, shrinking the lipid-peroxidation substrate pool. MBOAT1 is a direct estrogen-receptor transcriptional target (estradiol up, tamoxifen/fulvestrant down), which sensitizes ER+ breast cancers to ferroptosis induction combined with hormonal blockade (*Cell* 2023, PMCID PMC10330611).

In the comparison-table context, the ER→MBOAT1 axis is the mechanistic core of the ferroptosis sex-dimorphism cell (with AR→[[MBOAT2]] as the male counterpart).
