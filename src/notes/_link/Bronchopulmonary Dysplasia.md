# Bronchopulmonary Dysplasia

**Bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD)** is a chronic lung disease that predominantly affects premature infants who have received mechanical ventilation and supplemental oxygen. It is characterized by impaired alveolar and pulmonary vascular development.

## Pathophysiology

BPD results from injury to the immature lung during a critical window of alveolar and vascular development. Oxidative stress, inflammation, and mitochondrial dysfunction in lung epithelial and endothelial cells contribute to arrested lung development. Dysregulated [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial fission and fusion]] and impaired [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]] have been implicated in lung cell injury in BPD.

## Clinical Features

Chronic oxygen dependence, tachypnea, and respiratory distress in premature infants. Long-term consequences include impaired lung function and neurodevelopmental delays.
