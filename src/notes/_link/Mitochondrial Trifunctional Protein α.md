# Mitochondrial Trifunctional Protein α

SIRT4 represses PPARα signaling and destabilizes mitochondrial trifunctional protein α (MTPα) in hepatocytes, shifting metabolism away from mitochondrial fatty-acid utilization toward anabolic lipid conservation (Szczęsny-Małysiak et al., 2026).
