# 2,5-DHBA

## Overview

2,5-DHBA (2,5-dihydroxybenzoic acid, gentisic acid) is an iron-binding siderophore synthesized by [[BDH2]]. At [[Mitochondria-Lysosome Contact Site|mitochondria–lysosome contacts]], BDH2-derived 2,5-DHBA chelates Fe²⁺ released from the [[Lysosome]] and delivers it to [[Mitochondria]]. The route is documented in melanoma cell models (Tier 3) and shapes vulnerability to [[Ferroptosis]].

> [!info]
> Iron moves without bulk transfer: the siderophore carries the metal across a 10–30 nm contact gap while both organelles remain intact.

## Mechanism

BDH2 converts its substrate to 2,5-DHBA at the contact interface. The catechol-like scaffold binds Fe²⁺ with sufficient affinity to shuttle the ion from the lysosomal lumen to the mitochondrial matrix. A parallel iron route uses [[TRPML1]] with VDAC2/VDAC3. Under basal conditions iron is efficiently exchanged; mitochondrial dysfunction lowers import efficiency and increases oxidative stress, sensitizing cells to lipid peroxidation.

## Unresolved Questions

The relative contribution of the 2,5-DHBA and TRPML1–VDAC routes, and their operation in non-melanoma cell types, is not resolved. Whether the same chemistry operates in chronic neurodegenerative models remains untested.
