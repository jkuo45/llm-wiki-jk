# p43-FLIP

**p43-FLIP** is the 43 kDa N-terminal fragment generated when [[Caspase-8]] cleaves [[c-FLIP|FLIP_L]]
at Asp376 (Asp377 in mouse) during the first, interdimer processing step at the [[DISC]]. It comprises
both death-effector domains plus the large pseudo-caspase subunit, stays [[DISC]]-bound in
heterodimers with partially processed procaspase-8, and is both a marker and a mediator of the
[[Caspase-8-c-FLIP Rheostat]]'s restrained state.

## Structure & Generation

- **Cleavage site:** Asp376 in human FLIP_L, cut in *trans* by neighboring [[Caspase-8]] homodimers or
  [[Caspase-8]]/FLIP_L heterodimers during interdimer processing (Micheau 2002). The companion C-terminal
  p12 fragment remains non-covalently associated and is required for heterodimer activity.
- **What it retains:** DED1–DED2 (FADD/DISC anchoring) + p20-like pseudo-caspase domain; what it lacks:
  any catalytic cysteine — it cannot cut procaspase-8's DED2–p18 linker (Asp210/216/223), which is why
  the heterodimer never completes intradimer processing.
- **Sibling fragment:** cytoplasmic [[Caspase-8]]/[[c-FLIP]] heterodimers can additionally cut FLIP_L
  (and FLIP_S) at Asp198/Asp196 to yield **p22-FLIP**, a DED1–DED2 fragment that binds NEMO/IKKγ and
  potently activates NF-κB independently of [[FADD]].

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **Heterodimer glue.** p43-FLIP heterodimerizes with procaspase-8 *more avidly* than full-length
  FLIP_L (Yu et al.), stabilizing the membrane-tethered, partially active complex that cleaves local
  substrates ([[RIPK1]], adjacent homodimers) while releasing no apoptotic signal.
- **NF-κB scaffold.** p43-FLIP recruits [[RIPK1]], TRAF2/TRAF6 and Raf-1 at the [[DISC]] and in lipid
  rafts, driving NF-κB and ERK activation that underwrites FLIP_L-dependent T-cell proliferation and
  survival signaling (the p43 → [[RIPK1]]-recruitment step is rate-limiting for this arm).
- **Kinetic readout.** In quantitative models (Neumann et al.), the rate of p43-FLIP generation is the
  critical parameter of the life/death decision — fast p43 accumulation marks heterodimer-dominated,
  survival-favoring DISCs.

## Physiological Function

p43-FLIP is the molecular memory of a restrained [[DISC]]: its presence reports that the rheostat
fired in its intermediate (heterodimer) mode. In T cells, TCR-induced [[Caspase-8]] activity generates
p43-FLIP to license NF-κB-dependent proliferation without [[Apoptosis]].

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Cancer:** constitutive p43-FLIP at the [[DISC]] marks TRAIL-resistant tumors; displacing FLIP_L
  from the [[DISC]] (e.g. DR5-binding-domain peptides, Jin et al.) collapses the heterodimer pool and
  restores ligand-induced [[Apoptosis]].
- **Biomarker use:** p43:p55 (cleaved:full-length FLIP_L) ratio in [[DISC]] immunoprecipitates reads
  out rheostat setting more faithfully than total [[c-FLIP]] levels.
