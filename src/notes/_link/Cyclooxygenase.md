Cyclooxygenases (COX) catalyse the first committed steps of prostanoid synthesis,
converting arachidonic acid to prostaglandin H2 via cyclooxygenase and peroxidase
activity on a single heme-containing homodimer. COX-1 is constitutively expressed
and supports constitutive prostaglandin synthesis, including gastric cytoprotection
and platelet aggregation; COX-2 is largely inducible and drives inflammatory
prostaglandin production, which is why NSAIDs are both analgesic and ulcerogenic.
