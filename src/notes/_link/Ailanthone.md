# Ailanthone

**Pentacyclic diterpene lactone, C20 quassinoid family, isolated from *Ailanthus altissima* bark.**

## Cytotoxicity vs MCF-7 cells (Tier 4: in vitro)

- IC50: 0.5/1.0/2.0/4.0/8.0 µg/mL at 24/48/72 h (verified; PMID 29552229).

## Mechanism

- G0/G1 cell-cycle arrest (verified).
- p53/p21 modulation recorded (user note; not verified here).
