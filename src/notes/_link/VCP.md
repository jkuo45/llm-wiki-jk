# VCP

VCP (Valosin-containing protein, also known as [[p97]]/[[Cdc48]]) is an [[ATP|ATPase]] that accumulates in [[Huntington's Disease|Huntington's disease]], binds to mutant [[Huntingtin|Huntingtin (mHtt)]], and induces excessive [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]], leading to neuronal death.

## Role in Huntington's Disease

In [[Huntington's Disease|HD]], the accumulation of VCP, which binds to mHtt, has been shown to induce excessive mitophagy, contributing to striatal cell death. This highlights the complex dual role of mitophagy in HD, where both insufficient clearance (due to impaired autophagosome formation) and excessive mitophagy (via VCP and [[BNIP3L|NIX]]) contribute to pathology.
