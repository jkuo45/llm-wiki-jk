# PARL

Presenilin-associated rhomboid-like protease (PARL) is a mitochondrial rhomboid intramembrane serine protease resident in the [[Mitochondria|inner mitochondrial membrane]]. Rhomboid proteases cleave substrate transmembrane domains within the lipid bilayer, and PARL participates in the regulated processing of several mitochondrial proteins.

In the context of [[PINK1]] biology, PARL acts together with the [[Mitochondrial Processing Peptidase]] to cleave PINK1's C-terminal transmembrane domain during routine import into mitochondria. Under healthy conditions, PINK1 is imported across the outer membrane via the [[Translocase of the Outer Mitochondrial Membrane]] and into the matrix, where it is processed and degraded, preventing accumulation on the mitochondrial surface. When mitochondrial membrane potential collapses, import is halted; PINK1 is no longer cleaved by PARL and instead accumulates and stabilizes on the [[Mitochondria|outer mitochondrial membrane]], where it initiates the mitophagy cascade by recruiting and phosphorylating [[Parkin]].

Thus PARL is a key determinant of the import-and-degrade versus stabilize-and-signal decision that gates PINK1-dependent mitophagy.
