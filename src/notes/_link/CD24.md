## Overview

**CD24** is a small (~27–35 kDa heavy-glycosylated) GPI-anchored cell-surface sialoprotein expressed on early B-cell progenitors, granulocytes, and many carcinomas. In humans it is best understood as a "don't-eat-me" signal: CD24 on a target cell binds [[Siglec-10]] on macrophages, and inhibitory signaling through Siglec-10's ITIM motifs blocks engulfment (Barkal et al. 2017, Nat Immunol; Tier 4). Mouse Cd24a does not reproduce this anti-phagocytic pairing, so mechanistic claims are human-specific.

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- GPI-anchored outer-leaflet protein with a heavily O- and N-glycosylated ectodomain; the glycan/display density, rather than the polypeptide sequence, carries most of the receptor-recognition specificity.
- Cleavage by endogenous phospholipases releases soluble CD24 — the measurable form in serum and urine.

## Pharmacodynamics & Pharmacokinetics (anti-phagocytic axis)

- CD24–[[Siglec-10]] engagement is sufficient to block macrophage phagocytosis in vitro; blocking either antibody side (anti-CD24, anti-Siglec-10) restored phagocytosis in all CD24-expressing human tumor lines tested (Tier 4).
- Operates alongside the [[CD47]]–[[SIRPalpha|SIRPα]] axis; dual-axis blockade shows additive increases in uptake (Tier 4, early combination studies).
- Soluble CD24 also engages TLR-type readouts as a damage-associated molecular pattern in some models (reported in vivo, Tier 3).

## Applications

- **Cancer stem-cell / tumor-initiating marker.** CD24 (often with CD44 low) stratifies tumor-propagating subpopulations in ovarian, pancreatic, breast, and other carcinomas; CD24 enrichment fuels tumorigenic subfractions in mouse xenografts (Tier 3).
- **Anti-phagocytic target.** Anti-CD24 immunotherapy (pegtelvimol-based and related agents) explored in early clinical trials for CD24-positive malignancies (clinical trial stages, not approved as of writing).
- **Autoimmunity adjunct.** Autologous CD24+ regulatory dendritic-cell products are tested in multiple sclerosis and lupus trials (early tiers).
- **Biology of self-defense.** CD24 is naturally displayed at high density on maturing granulocytes — the same cells that elsewhere act as eat-me executors — suggesting the signal protects normal myeloid turnover from bystander phagocytosis (Tier 4–5, hypothesis stage).

## Connections

- [[Siglec-10]] — macrophage-side inhibitory receptor through which CD24 delivers the anti-phagocytic signal; the signal pair ceases operation when either side is blocked.
- [[CD47]] — parallel "don't-eat-me" axis operating in the same macrophage phagocytic synapse.
- [[PD-L1]] — another tumor-arm anti-phagocytic signal (macrophage PD-1 readout).
- [[Efferocytosis]] — CD24–Siglec-10 sets the self-spare threshold the efferocytic machinery respects.
- [[Immune Evasion]] — CD24-high tumors rely on the anti-phagocytic axis to resist myeloid attack.
