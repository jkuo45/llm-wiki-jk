# Lipid Metabolism

[[Lipid Metabolism|Lipid metabolism]] encompasses the synthesis, storage, transport, and oxidation of fatty acids, cholesterol, and complex lipids. In the sirtuin literature it is a major output of [[SIRT1]] deacetylase activity.

## SIRT1 Regulation

SIRT1 deacetylates multiple metabolic transcription factors to govern lipid handling:
- [[PGC-1α]] — drives fatty acid oxidation and mitochondrial lipid catabolism.
- [[Liver X Receptor|LXR]] — regulates cholesterol and lipid homeostasis.
- [[FOXO|FOXO factors]] and [[CREB]] — modulate lipogenic/gluconeogenic programs.

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - SIRT1 Protein roles at the nexus of health, disease, and therapeutics|SIRT1: Protein Roles at the Nexus (Chaqour, 2026)]]
> SIRT1 modulates over 40 substrates including PGC-1α, LXR, FOXO, and CREB, coordinating lipid and cholesterol metabolism; SIRT1 deficiency is linked to metabolic and lipid disorders.
