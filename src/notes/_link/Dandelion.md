# Dandelion (Taraxacum officinale)

**Dandelion** is a temperate weed of the Asteraceae family, traditionally used for its diuretic and hepatic properties. The data in this note concern the **root**, analyzed as defatted extract.

## Root Chemistry

- Triterpenes: taraxasterol, ψ-taraxasterol, α-amyrin, β-amyrin, lupeol.
- Phenolic acids: caffeic acid, chlorogenic acid.
- Flavonoids: luteolin, quercetin, apigenin (existing notes: [[Luteolin]], [[Quercetin]], [[Apigenin]]).
- Sesquiterpene lactones: taraxacin (taraxacins).
- Polysaccharides.
- Root fructans/inulin: 16.1 g per 100 g dry weight in the defatted root ([[Inulin]] — the dominant root fructan source, ~63% of total root fructans).

## Mechanism Map (In Vitro Unless Noted)

- Extrinsic [[Apoptosis]]: extrinsic FADD/caspase-8 apoptosis, with cFLIP suppression.
- Mitochondrial ROS: promotes mitochondrial ROS generation in isolated mitochondria (Tier 4: isolated-mitochondria assay).
- Pro-death autophagy: activates autophagy toward cell death ([[Autophagy]]).
- Signaling suppression: inhibits PI3K/Akt and suppresses NF-κB ([[NF-κB]]) and STAT3 ([[STAT3]]).
- Glycerophospholipid interference: interferes with CHKA/phosphocholine in glycerophospholipid metabolism.
- EC50s: low-micromolar EC50s against cancer cell lines (exact values not specified in the source data).

## Preclinical Model Results

- Ovadje 2016 (Oncotarget): EC50 2.0/3.5 mg/mL; >95% cell death at 48 h; >90% tumor retardation at 40 mg/kg/day for 75 days (rodent models).
- Nguyen 2019 (prostate cancer): 4 mg/mL + mitoxantrone synergy; 40 mg/kg/day.

## Human Record

- One published case report of CMML, n=1 (Rahmat & Damon 2018).
- A Phase 1 trial (approved 2012, opened 2015) enrolled 30 patients and was never published — no results available.

## Safety Profile

- Estrogenic activity reported.
- CYP3A4 and CYP1A2 inhibition; ≈50% reduction of dasatinib bioavailability in rats.
- Hepatotoxicity: a case report exists.
- Digital necrosis from dandelion tea.
- Asteraceae cross-reactivity.

## Evidence Quality

- Mechanism and chemical data are Tier 4 (in vitro; isolated mitochondria).
- Preclinical results are Tier 3: rodent models (Ovadje 2016; Nguyen 2019 prostate cancer).
- Human data are Tier 5: one published case report (CMML, n=1); the Phase 1 trial was never published, so no results are available.
