# SLC25A1

**SLC25A1** (also known as the mitochondrial citrate carrier, CIC) is a mitochondrial inner-membrane transporter of the SLC25 family that exports [[Citrate]] from the mitochondrial matrix to the cytosol in exchange for malate (or phosphoenolpyruvate). Cytosolic citrate is then converted to [[Acetyl-CoA]] by [[ACLY|ATP-citrate lyase]], making SLC25A1 the first committed step in the transport of mitochondrial carbon into the cytosolic acetyl-CoA pool used for lipogenesis and [[Histone Acetylation]].

## Overview

SLC25A1 is the primary route by which TCA-cycle-derived citrate leaves the mitochondrion. In the cytosol, citrate serves two major fates: (1) cleavage by ACLY to acetyl-CoA + oxaloacetate, and (2) allosteric activation of [[Acetyl-CoA Carboxylase]] for de novo lipogenesis. Because nuclear [[Histone Acetyltransferase|acetyltransferases]] draw on the cytosolic acetyl-CoA pool, SLC25A1 activity links mitochondrial metabolism to the [[Epigenome]].

## Role in Cellular Senescence

In senescent cells, SLC25A1 expression is significantly increased at both mRNA and protein levels across multiple senescence contexts (irradiation-induced, doxorubicin-induced, replicative). This upregulation sustains the supply of cytosolic acetyl-CoA needed for [[H3K27ac|histone H3K27 acetylation]] at [[SASP]] gene loci.

> [!important] SLC25A1 Inhibition Selectively Suppresses the SASP
> Genetic ablation of SLC25A1 or pharmacological inhibition with [[CTPI2]] markedly reduces SASP gene expression and secretion without altering cell cycle arrest, p16/p21 expression, or general mitochondrial function. Mechanistically, SLC25A1 inhibition reduces cytosolic (but not mitochondrial) acetyl-CoA, decreasing H3K27ac and H3K9ac at SASP loci while leaving OXPHOS, ATP production, coupling efficiency, mitochondrial ROS, and mitochondrial morphology largely unchanged.

## Therapeutic Relevance

In aged mice, treatment with [[CTPI2]] (50 mg kg^-1^, 3×/week for 3 months) improves [[Frailty]] and neuromuscular function, reduces systemic inflammation and SASP across tissues (muscle, liver, heart), and remodels chromatin accessibility at SASP loci — without clearing senescent cells or inducing broad metabolic reprogramming. In human tissues (GTEx), *SLC25A1* expression positively correlates with a mitochondrial-dependent SASP gene signature and with p16/p21 across muscle, heart, hippocampus, and kidney.
