# Lens Crystallins

**Lens crystallins** (α/β/γ) are effectively permanent proteins maintaining lens transparency; with negligible turnover they accumulate racemization, deamidation, and AGE adducts ([[Glycation]], [[Maillard Reaction]]) over decades. α-crystallin moonlights as a small heat-shock chaperone; its failure precipitates cataract, a readout of whole-body long-lived-protein aging.
