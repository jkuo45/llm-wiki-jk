# TAX1BP1

## Overview

TAX1BP1 (tax1-binding protein 1, also CALCOCO3) is one of several autophagy receptors that connect [[Ubiquitin|ubiquitinated]] cargo to the forming autophagosome. In [[PINK1]]–[[Parkin]] [[Mitophagy]], it binds ubiquitin chains on mitochondrial outer-membrane substrates and engages [[LC3]]/[[GABARAP]] through LC3-interacting regions (LIR motifs). Its activity overlaps with [[Optineurin]], [[NDP52]], [[NBR1]] and [[p62]].

> [!info]
> Receptor redundancy is substantial: loss of a single receptor often fails to block mitophagy, so receptor occupancy is not a straightforward proxy for degradative flux.

## Mechanism

Loss of membrane potential prevents PINK1 import, stabilizing it on the outer membrane. PINK1 phosphorylates ubiquitin and Parkin at Ser65; activated Parkin extends ubiquitin chains on substrates including [[MFN1]], [[MFN2]], [[VDAC]] and [[TOM20]]. TAX1BP1 reads this signal through its ubiquitin-binding domain and recruits autophagic membranes via LIR-dependent LC3/GABARAP binding. The sealed mitophagosome fuses through [[STX17]]–[[SNAP29]]–[[VAMP8]] with [[Rab7]] and [[HOPS Complex]] participation.

## Unresolved Questions

Whether TAX1BP1 marks a distinct cargo subpopulation, and how its contribution changes in chronic disease, is not established in the available cell and animal models.
