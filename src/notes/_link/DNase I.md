# DNase I

DNase I digests the extracellular [[DNA]] scaffold of [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps]]. Applying DNase I at a primary infection site dismantled NETs and promoted bacterial dissemination in wild-type mice but not in Casp11- or Gsdmd-deficient mice, showing that NETs themselves mediate host defense (Chen et al. 2018). Several microbes encode nucleases that degrade NETs as an evasion strategy.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Group A [[Streptococcus pyogenes]] expresses nucleases to degrade NETs (Sumby et al. 2005; Walker et al. 2007), and NET composition is analysed by DNase-1-released proteomics (Urban et al. 2009; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
