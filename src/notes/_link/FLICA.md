# FLICA

**FLICA** (fluorescent labeled inhibitors of caspases) probes are cell-permeant, fluorescently tagged caspase inhibitors that covalently bind the active site of cleaved caspases, reporting enzymatic activity rather than mere protein presence. Caspase-1 FLICA (FAM-YVAD-FMK) is the standard live-cell readout of inflammasome-driven [[Caspase-1]] activation in [[Pyroptosis]], paired with [[Gasdermin D]]-N staining and [[IL-1β]]/LDH release.
