# SLC25A51

**SLC25A51** (solute carrier family 25 member A51, also known as MCART1) is the mammalian mitochondrial inner-membrane importer for oxidized [[NAD+]], supplying the matrix pool that sustains the [[TCA Cycle]], [[Oxidative Phosphorylation]], and NAD+-dependent enzymes such as [[SIRT3]] and [[SIRT5]].

## Overview

Mitochondria cannot synthesize NAD+ de novo and the inner membrane is impermeable to pyridine nucleotides, so matrix NAD+ must be imported from the cytosol. Three independent 2020 studies converged on SLC25A51 as the long-sought mammalian transporter:

- Co-essentiality mining with [[Electron Transport Chain]] components identified MCART1-null cells with collapsed respiration and complex I activity (Kory et al., *Sci Adv* 2020).
- Intact-NAD+ uptake assays in isolated mitochondria showed loss of uptake on deletion and gain of uptake on overexpression, including rescue by the near-identical paralog [[SLC25A52]] and complementation of yeast NDT1/NDT2 mutants (Luongo et al., *Nature* 2020).
- Epistasis and metabolomics showed SLC25A51 controls mitochondrial NAD(H) and is the functional ortholog of the yeast mitochondrial NAD+ transporter (Girardi et al., *Nat Commun* 2020).

Loss of SLC25A51 lowers mitochondrial but not whole-cell NAD+, impairs respiration, and reduces TCA-cycle flux — a compartment-specific lesion.

## Structure & Transport Mechanism

SLC25A51 is a six-transmembrane-helix SLC25 family carrier in the mitochondrial inner membrane.

> [!info]
> SLC25A51 mediates direct uptake of intact NAD+ into the matrix, driven in part by membrane potential. Isolated mitochondria from knockout cells fail to take up exogenous NAD+, while overexpression increases matrix NAD+ and uptake rate. SLC25A52 can substitute when overexpressed but is barely expressed in most tissues, leaving SLC25A51 as the dominant physiological route.

Matrix NAD+ imported by SLC25A51 feeds complex I (NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase), matrix dehydrogenases ([[IDH2]], [[Glutamate Dehydrogenase (GDH)]], pyruvate dehydrogenase complex), and NAD+-consuming enzymes ([[SIRT3]], [[SIRT5]], mitochondrial [[PARP1]]).

## Physiological Function

- **Respiration and redox:** maintains matrix NAD+/NADH for TCA flux, complex I activity, oxygen consumption, and ATP output. Knockout cells show depressed oxygen consumption and compensatory glycolytic shift.
- **Sirtuin gating:** local NAD+ supply sets the activity envelope of [[SIRT3]] (deacetylation of [[IDH2]], [[ACADL]]) and [[SIRT5]] (desuccinylation/demalonylation). SLC25A51 knockdown raises acetylation of SIRT3 targets without changing SIRT3 protein.
- **Liver metabolism:** hepatic knockdown lowers mitochondrial NAD+, suppresses fatty-acid oxidation, and produces steatosis with hypertriglyceridemia, phenocopying partial SIRT3 loss.

## Regulation

> [!tip]
> SLC25A51 is a fasting-induced, circadian gene and a direct [[BMAL1]] target (Fu et al., *Metabolism* 2022). Hepatic expression rises with fasting, oscillates diurnally, and is disrupted in liver-specific *Bmal1* knockouts. Reduced expression lowers mitochondrial NAD+ and SIRT3 activity and reduces oxygen consumption in intact hepatocytes.

Upstream context:

- **Fasting / [[Caloric Restriction]]:** induction parallels [[PGC-1α]]–[[SIRT3]] activation and the switch to fatty-acid oxidation.
- **Circadian:** [[BMAL1]]/CLOCK drive rhythmic import; [[NAMPT]]-driven NAD+ biosynthesis oscillations and age-related [[CD38]] induction together define the spatiotemporal NAD+ available to mitochondria.
- **Essentiality:** SLC25A51 scores as essential in human cell-line screens, consistent with a non-redundant housekeeping role; tissue paralog compensation by SLC25A52 is limited.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Metabolic disease:** impaired import links mitochondrial NAD+ depletion to hepatic steatosis, insulin resistance, and age-related respiratory decline. Hepatocyte knockdown is sufficient for fatty liver in mice.
- **Cancer:** overexpression of SLC25A51 promotes hepatocellular carcinoma progression by driving aerobic glycolysis through [[SIRT5]] activation (Bai et al., *Free Radic Biol Med* 2022) — the same NAD+ supply that protects normal liver can be co-opted for tumor metabolic plasticity.
- **Cardiac aging:** mitochondrial NAD+ gradient maintenance via SLC25A51 is under investigation as a determinant of age-related cardiac energetics and [[Ischemia-reperfusion Injury]] tolerance.
- **Therapeutic angle:** unlike bulk NAD+ precursors ([[Nicotinamide Riboside]], NMN), SLC25A51 controls the *mitochondrial* fraction specifically; modulating import offers a compartment-targeted alternative to systemic NAD+ repletion, with [[Parthanatos]] and ferroptosis programs as downstream death-mode contexts.
