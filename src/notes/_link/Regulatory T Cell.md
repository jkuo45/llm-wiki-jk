# Regulatory T Cell

**Regulatory T cells** (Tregs, FoxP3+) suppress effector immunity to maintain self-tolerance. They are induced by [[TGF-beta1|TGF-β]], IDO1-driven tryptophan depletion via [[GCN2]], and [[Aryl Hydrocarbon Receptor]] ligands such as kynurenine-pathway metabolites. Tumors and senescent niches recruit Tregs for immune evasion.

## Documents

- [[TGF-beta1]] — canonical inducer of FoxP3+ regulatory T cell differentiation.
- [[GCN2]] — translates IDO1-mediated tryptophan depletion into Treg differentiation and T-cell anergy.
- [[IDO1]] — kynurenine-pathway enzyme whose metabolites drive regulatory T cell induction via AhR.
