# Vascular Inflammation

**Vascular inflammation** is the ordered process by which circulating leukocytes —
predominantly [[Macrophage|monocytes and macrophages]], plus T lymphocytes — leave the
circulation, enter the vessel wall, and become activated there. It is not a single disease
but a shared mechanism: the same cascade underlies [[Atherosclerosis]], aortic aneurysm and
dissection, venous graft failure, [[Pulmonary Hypertension]], [[Stroke]] after
reperfusion, and the vasculitides.

## The canonical cascade

The process has three sequential stages, each under distinct transcriptional control:

1. **Adherence** — endothelial cells increase surface [[VCAM-1]], [[ICAM-1]],
   [[E-selectin]] and [[P-selectin]]. Leukocyte integrins bind them.
2. **Chemotaxis** — endothelial and mural cells secrete MCP-1 (CCL2), [[CCR2]] ligands, and
   GM-CSF; degraded [[Elastin]] fragments also act as chemoattractants.
3. **Activation** — infiltrating monocytes differentiate into macrophages and polarise; local
   [[Interleukin-6]] signalling through STAT3 drives activation markers (F4/80,
   CD14^lo/CD11b^lo) and confers resistance to ROS-induced stress.

Leukocytes enter by two routes: "inside-out" through activated endothelium at the
intima, and "outside-in" through the adventitia via the vasa vasorum.

## NF-κB as the signal integrator

All of the above adhesion molecules and chemokines are NF-κB-dependent. NF-κB is activated
in the vessel wall by a wide range of stimuli — [[Angiotensin II]], oxidised
[[Low-Density Lipoprotein|LDL]], CD40–CD40L, advanced glycation end-products, [[TNF-alpha]],
IL-1β — which is what makes it the point of integration rather than a pathway-specific
switch. Two linked modules operate: the canonical IKK–IκBα module and an
ROS-PKAc-RelA Ser276 module that operates independently of IκBα degradation.

A subtlety worth recording: endothelial NF-κB signalling is *pre-positioned* at
atherosclerosis-prone sites before any lesion exists. Regions of the aorta susceptible to
plaque show constitutively higher levels of NF-κB system components and more NF-κB
activation in response to lipopolysaccharide or high-fat feeding. Deleting the tandem NF-κB
sites in the VCAM-1 promoter lowers endothelial VCAM-1 expression in acute inflammation but
leaves intimal-cell expression in established lesions untouched — the requirement for NF-κB
depends on cell type and on whether inflammation is acute or chronic.

## IL-6 as the activation-stage effector

The [[Interleukin-6]] pathway is the less obvious arm. IL-6 knockout mice show a
significantly reduced rate of aortic dissection, and aortic monocytes from IL-6-null animals
fail to phosphorylate STAT3. IL-6 and MCP-1 also cross-regulate: MCP-1 expression is
reduced in IL-6-null mice and IL-6 expression reduced in MCP-1-null mice, forming a local
amplification loop in the aortic adventitia together with fibroblast interaction.

## Hemodynamic modulation

Inflammation is spatially patterned, not uniform. Human aortic endothelial cells exposed to a
shear gradient plus TNF-α show that VCAM-1 and E-selectin induction *peaks at low shear
(2–4 dyne/cm²)* and is suppressed below untreated levels above 8 dyne/cm², while ICAM-1
and NF-κB nuclear translocation rise with shear up to about 9 dyne/cm². Monocyte recruitment
tracks the low-shear VCAM-1/E-selectin maximum. The endothelium can detect a shear gradient
of ~0.25 dyne/cm² over about ten cells. This is the physical basis of focality in
atherosclerosis.

## Matrix consequences and the TIMP3 brake

Infiltrated macrophages release ROS and [[Matrix Metalloproteinase|proteases]] that degrade
[[Extracellular Matrix]], contributing to plaque cap thinning and aneurysm wall weakening.
[[TIMP3]] restrains this: as an ECM-anchored inhibitor of MMP-2, MMP-9 and ADAM17, it
limits matrix turnover and — by inhibiting ADAM17/TACE — reduces ectodomain shedding of
TNF receptors and EGFR ligands, blunting inflammatory signalling. TIMP3 promoter
hypermethylation and reduced expression are observed in atherosclerotic plaques, and
[[SIRT1]] normally upregulates TIMP3 to hold the line.

## Connections

- [[TIMP3]] — the ECM-anchored MMP/ADAM inhibitor that constrains vascular inflammation;
  its loss is one route to plaque instability.
- [[VCAM-1]] — the endothelial adhesion molecule whose NF-κB-dependent induction is the
  commitment step for monocyte and T-cell entry; engaged by [[VLA-4]].
- [[ICAM-1]] — the companion adhesion molecule, engaged by LFA-1, binding LFA-1
  preferentially in early atherogenesis.
- [[SIRT1]] — NAD+-dependent deacetylase that raises TIMP3 expression and restrains the
  matrix/inflammatory arm of vascular inflammation.
- [[Angiotensin II]] — a canonical NF-κB activator in the vessel wall and a driver of
  aneurysm and atheroma in mouse models.
- [[Atherosclerosis]] — the disease whose defining histology is this process.
- [[TNF-alpha]] — an upstream inducer of VCAM-1 and ICAM-1; autocrine and paracrine, it
  sustains endothelial activation.
- [[Macrophage]] — the dominant recruited cell and the source of both ROS and matrix
  proteases once activated.
- [[Extracellular Matrix]] — the substrate whose breakdown by macrophage proteases makes
  inflammation structurally consequential.
- [[Inflammation]] — the parent process; vascular inflammation is the specific instance
  confined to and dictated by the vessel wall.
- [[Endothelial Dysfunction]] — the state that permits this cascade to begin, and which
  endothelial inflammation in turn deepens.
