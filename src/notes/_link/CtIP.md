# CtIP

[[CtIP]] (CtBP-interacting protein; gene *RBBP8*) is a nuclear protein required for 5'–3' end resection of DNA double-strand breaks, a rate-limiting step that commits cells to [[Double-Strand Break|homologous recombination]] repair. Through this role CtIP supports [[DNA Repair]], genome stability, and suppression of [[Genomic Instability|chromosomal instability]].

## SIRT6 regulation

[[SIRT6]] deacetylates [[CtIP]] as one of its specific protein substrates (alongside [[H3K9ac|H3K9Ac]], [[H3K56ac|H3K56Ac]], and [[GCN5]]), contributing to SIRT6's roles in [[Genomic Instability|genomic stability]], metabolic homeostasis, and inflammation ([[_document_ - Sirtuins Guardians of Mammalian Healthspan|Sirtuins: Guardians of Mammalian Healthspan, Giblin et al. 2014]], refs 41–44).
