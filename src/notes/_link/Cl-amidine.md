# Cl-amidine

**Cl-amidine** is a covalent pan-PAD inhibitor (chloro-acetamidine warhead) that blocks citrullination by [[PAD4]] and related deiminases. In [[NETosis]] assays it suppresses histone citrullination ([[cit-H3]]) and NET extrusion, serving — with DNase digestion and NE/MPO inhibition — as the standard blockade panel proving PAD-dependent NET formation.
