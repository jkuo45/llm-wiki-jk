# Threonine

**Threonine** is an essential amino acid required for [[Protein Synthesis]], gut mucin barrier integrity, and glycine/acetyl-CoA production via threonine dehydrogenase. Glycation chemistry links threonine residues to [[Methylglyoxal]]-derived adducts in aging tissues.

## Documents

- [[Essential Amino Acids]] — threonine listed among the nine amino acids humans cannot synthesize de novo.
- [[Methylglyoxal]] — dicarbonyl chemistry modifying threonine (and arginine/lysine) residues in glycated proteins.
