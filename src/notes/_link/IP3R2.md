# IP3R2

IP3R2 (Inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate receptor type 2, also known as [[ITPR2]]) is an [[Endoplasmic Reticulum|endoplasmic reticulum (ER)]]-resident [[Calcium|calcium]] channel that interacts with [[FUNDC1]] at [[MERCS|Mitochondria-Associated ER Membranes (MAMs)]] to regulate calcium signaling.

## Role in MAMs

[[FUNDC1]] is localized to [[MERCS|MAMs]] and interacts with the IP3R2 receptor on the [[Endoplasmic Reticulum|endoplasmic reticulum]]. The presence of FUNDC1 helps maintain the structural stability of MAMs. When FUNDC1 is absent, the MAM structure is disrupted, leading to impaired [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial fission]] and abnormal calcium signaling. Given that MAMs are often over-strengthened or dysfunctional in [[Alzheimer's Disease|Alzheimer's disease]], the FUNDC1-IP3R2 interaction provides a mechanism for understanding calcium homeostasis imbalance in [[Neurodegenerative Diseases|neurodegenerative diseases]].
