# GSK-428

**GSK-428** is a quinazoline-scaffold **[[SARM1]] inhibitor** with orthosteric, high-potency activity (nanomolar IC₅₀) against the TIR-domain NADase active site.

## Mechanism

GSK-428 binds orthosterically within the [[SARM1]] TIR dimer NADase pocket, blocking [[NAD+]] cleavage and preventing the axonal NAD⁺ catastrophe that drives Wallerian degeneration. It belongs to the next-generation non-covalent inhibitor class alongside [[DSRM-3716]].

## Therapeutic Context

- High selectivity and potency profile relative to the repurposed covalent inhibitor [[Disulfiram]].
- Indicated for axon-degeneration disorders including chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy and traumatic brain injury ([[Traumatic Brain Injury]]).
- Shares the conserved **adduct-forming uncompetitive inhibitor** strategy with the [[CD38]] inhibitor 78c (Bratkowski et al. 2022, _Neuron_).
