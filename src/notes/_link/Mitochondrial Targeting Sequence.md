### Overview
The Mitochondrial Targeting Sequence (MTS) is an N-terminal amphipathic helix found on [[OPA1]] isoforms that directs the protein to the [[Inner Mitochondrial Membrane]]. It is a classic signal peptide recognized by the mitochondrial import machinery.

### Import and Processing
During import into the [[Mitochondrial Matrix]], the MTS is removed by the matrix processing protease. Cleavage yields the mature OPA1 isoforms that subsequently undergo further proteolytic processing by [[YME1L]] and other rhomboid-like proteases to generate long (L-) and short (S-) forms.

### Functional Significance
The MTS is essential for localizing OPA1 to the inner membrane, where it mediates inner-membrane fusion. Proper import and processing of OPA1 are prerequisites for maintaining cristae structure and [[Mitochondrial Integrity]]. Mutations affecting OPA1 processing or localization contribute to [[Dominant Optic Atrophy]].
