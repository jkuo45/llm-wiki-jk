# Canakinumab

**Canakinumab** (brand name **Ilaris**) is a human monoclonal anti-[[IL-1β]] antibody that selectively neutralizes IL-1β without affecting IL-1α or other IL-1 family members. It demonstrated the first clinical proof that targeting [[Inflammation]] can reduce cardiovascular events independently of lipid lowering in the landmark [[CANTOS Trial]].

## Mechanism of Action

Canakinumab binds free human IL-1β with high affinity and specificity, preventing it from engaging the [[IL-1 Receptor|IL-1R1]]/IL-1RAcP receptor complex. Unlike [[Anakinra]] (which blocks both IL-1α and IL-1β), canakinumab is selective for IL-1β alone. By neutralizing extracellular IL-1β, it inhibits downstream [[MyD88]]-dependent signaling, reducing [[NF-κB|NF-κB]] activation and transcription of pro-inflammatory mediators including [[IL-6]], [[TNFα]], and [[COX-2]].

## Pharmacokinetics

- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection
- **Half-life**: ~26 days (long-acting)
- **Dosing**: 150 mg every 8 weeks (varies by indication)
- **Metabolism**: Proteolytic degradation; no CYP-mediated metabolism

## Clinical Indications

- **Cryopyrin-Associated Periodic Syndromes (CAPS)** — including Muckle-Wells syndrome, familial cold autoinflammatory syndrome, and NOMID
- **Systemic Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis (sJIA)** — aged 2 years and older
- **Adult-onset Still's Disease**
- **Gout** — acute flares refractory to other treatments
- **[[Atherosclerosis]] / Cardiovascular Risk Reduction** — studied in the [[CANTOS Trial]]

## The CANTOS Trial

The [[CANTOS Trial]] (Canakinumab Anti-inflammatory Thrombosis Outcomes Study) was a landmark randomized, double-blind trial involving 10,061 patients with prior myocardial infarction and elevated high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP ≥ 2 mg/L). Canakinumab 150 mg every 3 months reduced:

- **Primary endpoint**: Nonfatal MI, nonfatal stroke, or cardiovascular death (HR 0.85, p=0.021)
- **hs-CRP**: Sustained reduction of ~37%
- **IL-6**: Reduced by ~28%
- LDL cholesterol was **not** reduced, proving the benefit was inflammation-specific

This was the first clinical demonstration that reducing [[Inflammation]] alone — independent of lipid lowering — can prevent cardiovascular events, establishing IL-1β as a central nexus linking [[Oxidative Stress]], [[Autoinflammation]], and [[Atherosclerosis]].

## Role in Inflammaging

By neutralizing IL-1β, canakinumab attenuates the chronic inflammatory signaling that drives [[Inflammaging]]. The CANTOS Trial demonstrated that reducing IL-1β-mediated inflammation decreases cardiovascular events, providing a direct link between inflammaging and clinical outcomes.

## Adverse Effects

- Dose-dependent increase in fatal infections (especially pneumonia and sepsis)
- Neutropenia and thrombocytopenia
- Hepatotoxicity (elevated transaminases)
- Macrophage activation syndrome (rare)
