# ALS

**Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS)** is a fatal neurodegenerative disease characterized by progressive loss of upper and lower motor neurons, leading to paralysis and respiratory failure.

## Mechanism

SARM1 is upregulated in spinal motor neurons in ALS. In the SOD1^G93A mouse model, SARM1 knockout modestly extends survival, implicating [[SARM1]]-mediated [[NAD+]] depletion and [[Axon Degeneration]] in motor neuron vulnerability. The shared NAD⁺ consumer network ([[CD38]], [[PARP1]], sirtuins) modulates the metabolic stress background.

## Therapeutic

SARM1 inhibitors ([[Disulfiram]], [[DSRM-3716]], [[GSK-428]], [[NB-4746]]) are hypothesized to protect motor neuron axons, though clinical validation in ALS is pending.

## Sirtuin Involvement (Wu et al., 2022)

- **[[SIRT1]]**: Increased in SOD1^G93A ALS mice.
- **[[SIRT3]]**: Increased in ALS spinal cord.
- **[[SIRT6]]**: SIRT6 activity abrogates ALS-linked mutant SOD1 astrocyte neurotoxicity.
