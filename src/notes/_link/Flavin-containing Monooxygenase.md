# Flavin-containing Monooxygenase

The [[Flavin-containing Monooxygenase]] (FMO) family are flavin-dependent monooxygenases that catalyze the NADPH- and O2-dependent oxidation of soft nucleophiles (notably sulfur, nitrogen, and phosphorus centers). FMO3 is the dominant hepatic form in adults.

## Role in Fenbendazole Metabolism

FMO, together with [[CYP3A4]], mediates the rapid hepatic conversion of [[Fenbendazole]] to its sulfoxide derivative [[Oxfendazole]] (fenbendazole sulfoxide) (38, 39). This first-pass sulfoxidation is the gateway to fenbendazole's active metabolite.

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> Fenbendazole is rapidly metabolized by FMO and CYP3A4 to oxfendazole; CYP2J2 and CYP2C19 additionally form hydroxyfenbendazole (40).
