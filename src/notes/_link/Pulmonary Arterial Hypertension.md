# Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension

**Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH)** is a progressive vascular disease characterized by remodeling of the small pulmonary arteries, leading to increased pulmonary vascular resistance, elevated pulmonary artery pressure, and ultimately right ventricular failure.

## Pathophysiology

PAH involves proliferation and apoptosis resistance of pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells (PASMCs) and endothelial dysfunction. Mitochondrial dysfunction, including excessive [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|mitochondrial fission]] and impaired [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]], contributes to PASMC proliferation and vascular remodeling. [[DRP1]]-mediated fission has been implicated in PAH pathogenesis.

## Clinical Features

Progressive dyspnea, exercise intolerance, and right heart failure. Treatment includes prostacyclin analogs, endothelin receptor antagonists, PDE5 inhibitors, and soluble guanylate cyclase stimulators.
