# Glioblastoma

**Glioblastoma** (glioblastoma multiforme, GBM) is the most common and aggressive primary malignant brain tumor in adults (WHO grade IV). It is characterized by rapid growth, extensive vascularization, and resistance to therapy.

## Pathophysiology

Glioblastoma cells exhibit high metabolic demands and rely heavily on [[Oxidative Phosphorylation|oxidative phosphorylation]] and glycolysis. Mitochondrial dynamics, including [[Mitochondrial Dynamics|fission and fusion]], are frequently dysregulated, contributing to tumor metabolic plasticity, treatment resistance, and invasion.

## Clinical Features

Rapid neurological decline, headaches, seizures, and focal deficits. Standard treatment involves maximal safe resection, concurrent temozolomide and radiation (Stupp protocol), and tumor-treating fields. Median survival is 14–16 months.
