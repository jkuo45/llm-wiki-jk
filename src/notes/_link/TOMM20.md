# TOMM20

**TOMM20** (translocase of the outer mitochondrial membrane 20) is a receptor subunit of the TOM complex that recognizes N-terminal presequences of nuclear-encoded proteins and mediates their import across the outer mitochondrial membrane. Because its abundance scales with mitochondrial content, TOMM20 immunostaining is widely used as a surrogate marker of mitochondrial mass and [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis]].

## Use as a Biomarker

- Increased TOMM20 signal indicates greater mitochondrial content, but it does not by itself establish increased [[Mitophagy|mitophagic]] flux or respiratory capacity — functional assays such as [[Oxygen Consumption Rate|OCR]] are required to distinguish these.
- In aging research, reduced TOMM20 is interpreted as loss of mitochondrial mass; interventions that raise it are described as promoting mitochondrial biogenesis.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Topical application of urolithin A activates mitophagy and enhances mitochondrial function to promote skin longevity|Topical urolithin A skin study]]
> In a full-thickness human skin model reconstructed from an aged donor, 1 μM urolithin A for 25 days significantly increased TOMM20 immunostaining in the epidermal layer. The authors interpret this as increased epidermal mitochondrial biogenesis accompanying [[Urolithin A]]-induced [[Mitophagy]].
