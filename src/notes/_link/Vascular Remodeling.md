# Vascular Remodeling

**Vascular remodeling** is the change in vessel-wall *structure* — layer geometry, cell
number, matrix composition — that determines lumen diameter. It is distinct from
[[Vascular Inflammation]] (which is the inflammatory signalling) and from
[[Endothelial Dysfunction]] (which is the functional deficit): remodeling is the structural
consequence, and is what actually narrows a vessel.

Two broad outcomes exist. **Adaptive (outward) remodeling** enlarges the vessel to preserve
lumen despite wall growth; **constrictive remodeling** shrinks the outer elastic lamina
perimeter and narrows the lumen. Which occurs depends on matrix turnover and on the balance
between collagen scaffold and its proteolysis — and the literature is genuinely divided on
the mechanism, as below.

## Intimal hyperplasia and restenosis

The best-characterised form follows mechanical injury from balloon angioplasty and stenting.

Endothelial denudation removes the antithrombotic surface and triggers
[[Platelet]] adhesion, cytokine and growth factor release, and leukocyte recruitment.
[[Platelet]]-derived growth factor, bFGF, [[TGF-beta]] and thromboxane A2 drive vascular
smooth muscle cells out of the quiescent contractile phenotype:

> [!info] Contractile-to-synthetic switch
> Contractile markers (ACTA2, SM-MHC, SM22α) fall, driven by decommissioning of serum
> response factor and myocardin; ECM proteins (collagen I/III, fibronectin) and MMPs-1, -2,
> -3, -7, -9, -14 rise. Cells take a rhomboid, fibroblast-like morphology with abundant ER
> and Golgi. The basement membrane that maintained quiescence via β1 integrins and heparan
> sulfate proteoglycans is degraded, releasing the cell from quiescence.

Synthetic cells then migrate from media to intima and proliferate, forming a neointima of
smooth-muscle-like cells plus matrix. Proliferation is biphasic: early medial migration,
later intimal thickening.

> [!warning] What drug-eluting stents actually trade
> Sirolimus- and paclitaxel-eluting stents cut restenosis below 10% by inhibiting neointimal
> proliferation — but they do so by *also* delaying endothelial reendothelialisation, which
> is why late in-stent thrombosis became a concern. Restenosis after plain balloon
> angioplasty was 40–60% pre-stent, 17–41% with bare metal stents. This is a genuine
> trade-off, not a solved problem.

## Adventitial remodeling

A substantial body of work argues that chronic arterial remodeling is largely an
**adventitial** process, with myofibroblasts as the essential players. Their phenotypic
conversion from fibroblasts is the hallmark; they produce and modify adventitial matrix,
secrete inflammatory mediators, and alter tensile force. Integrin-mediated anchoring of
myofibroblasts to [[Collagen]] has been proposed to create a constrictive band, with
contraction narrowing the lumen — plausible but not established.

## The MMP/collagen controversy

Collagen is 20–30% of vascular protein and its content after intervention has been reported
to *increase* with both constrictive and adaptive remodeling, or to change with neither.
Likewise for MMP activity: MMP-2 is constitutively expressed in the media and MMP-9 appears
only after injury, and the direction of effect is inconsistent. Batimastat and marimastat
administration promoted *adaptive* remodeling in rat, pig and rabbit balloon-injury models;
MMP-9 overexpression also reduced lumen loss; but MMP-9 knockout showed increased
adventitial collagen with *unchanged* remodeling. Collagen cross-linking by
transglutaminases adds a further layer of opposing evidence.

> [!tip] Honest summary
> "MMP activity determines the direction of remodeling" is an appealing story with poor
> support. The ECM's role is real and its *abundance*, *organisation*, and
> *cross-linking* are all distinct variables. Claims that collagen constrains or that
> collagen scaffolds the wall are each supported by half the literature.

## Hemodynamic feedback

Remodeling changes flow, which changes remodeling. Post-intervention wall shear stress
drives smooth muscle proliferation, ECM production, and endothelial activation through
mechanotransduction. Patient-specific multiscale modelling of superficial femoral artery
angioplasty reproduces this: rapid cellular migration into balloon-lacerated regions,
exacerbated proliferation and matrix production, lumen reduction peaking around one month,
then stabilisation as inflammation resolves and hemodynamics settle.

## Non-MALAT1 triggers

Chronic hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction drives adventitial fibroblast-to-myofibroblast
conversion and matrix deposition, producing [[Pulmonary Hypertension]]. Loss of the
endothelial gasotransmitters [[Nitric Oxide]] and hydrogen sulfide promotes vasoconstriction,
platelet aggregation and oxidative stress; cystathionine γ-lyase knockout mice show
markedly increased intimal hyperplasia. Vein grafts used as bypass conduits remodel
intimately and fail. So does the aging aorta, where diffuse intimal thickening accumulates.

## Long non-coding RNAs

[[MALAT1]] is reported to regulate [[Vascular Remodeling]] alongside [[Inflammation]] and
metabolic gene expression. The specific MALAT1→remodeling axis is asserted in review
literature and in the sirtuins review this vault ingested, but the mechanistic chain has not
been traced to primary work here; treat the specific claim as thinly supported.

## Connections

- [[MALAT1]] — the lncRNA linked to remodeling and inflammation in the vault's sirtuins
  source; mechanistic detail unverified here.
- [[Vascular Inflammation]] — the signalling that initiates remodeling; the two are
  causally chained after injury.
- [[Matrix Metalloproteinase]] — the protease class whose activity is proposed to set the
  direction of remodeling, with conflicting experimental support.
- [[Extracellular Matrix]] — collagen, elastin and proteoglycans are the material being
  remodeled, and their cross-linking state appears decisive.
- [[TIMP3]] — restrains MMP and ADAM17 activity, and therefore the proteolytic half of
  remodeling.
- [[Endothelial Dysfunction]] — the initiating lesion; loss of endothelium permits the
  growth-factor and inflammatory environment remodeling requires.
- [[Angiotensin II]] — a circulating mitogen acting on smooth muscle cells after
  endothelial denudation.
- [[Pulmonary Hypertension]] — chronic hypoxic remodeling of the pulmonary vasculature, the
  clearest non-interventional example.
- [[TGF-beta]] — drives myofibroblast conversion and ECM synthesis; non-canonical TGF-β
  signalling via MAPK inhibits SMAD and promotes fibrosis in this context.
- [[Nitric Oxide]] — endothelial vasodilator whose loss favours constriction, platelet
  activation and smooth muscle proliferation.
- [[Platelet]] — the upstream source of PDGF and thromboxane after denudation.
- [[Collagen]] — the principal load-bearing matrix protein and the variable most often
  invoked, most often inconsistently.
- [[Angiogenesis]] — shares the matrix-remodelling and hypoxia-sensing machinery with
  neovascularisation of ischaemic tissue and of atherosclerotic plaques.
