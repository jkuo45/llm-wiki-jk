# SmgGDS

**SmgGDS** (RAP1GDS1) is a guanine-nucleotide exchange regulator for small GTPases that functions, in the female heart, as an estradiol-gated ferroptosis brake: estradiol induces SmgGDS, which restrains ferritinophagy-mediated [[Ferroptosis]] in isoproterenol takotsubo-like injury via AMPK/mTOR signaling. Ovariectomy lowers SmgGDS to male levels and abolishes protection; estradiol replacement restores it; SmgGDS loss abolishes protection (2023, PMCID PMC10719533).

In the comparison-table context, SmgGDS is the cardiac node of the ferroptosis sex-dimorphism story (with renal [[NRF2]] tone and MBOAT1/2 remodeling as the kidney and lipid nodes).
