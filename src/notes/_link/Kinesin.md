### Overview
Kinesin is a family of microtubule-based motor proteins that transport cargo, including mitochondria, along axons. Mitochondrial trafficking depends on the linkage of [[MFN2]] to kinesin via the adaptors [[Miro]] and [[Milton]].

### Mechanism of Transport
The OMM protein Miro, together with Milton, forms a complex that bridges MFN2 to kinesin motors, enabling processive movement of mitochondria to distal neuronal compartments. This ensures local ATP delivery and [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] in axons.

### Disease Relevance
Disruption of this transport axis is central to [[Charcot-Marie-Tooth Disease Type 2A]], where [[MFN2]] mutations impair the Miro/Milton/kinesin linkage and cause axonal mitochondrial depletion.
