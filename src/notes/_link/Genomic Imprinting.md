# Genomic Imprinting

**Genomic imprinting** silences one parental allele via germline [[DNA Methylation|methylation]] at imprinting control regions, maintained by [[DNMT1]] through mitosis. Classic clusters include IGF2/H19 (Beckwith-Wiedemann on loss of imprinting) and 15q11-q13 (Prader-Willi/Angelman). The DLK1-DIO3 locus expresses [[miR-543]] and other noncoding RNAs exclusively from one parental allele.

## Documents

- [[miR-543]] — imprinted microRNA of the DLK1-DIO3 locus regulated by parental origin.
- [[Histone Deacetylase]] — histone deacetylation cooperates with DNA methylation to maintain imprinted silencing.
- [[DNMT1]] — maintenance methyltransferase preserving imprints through cell division.
