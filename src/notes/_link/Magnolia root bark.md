# Magnolia Root Bark

**Root bark of *Magnolia biondii* and *Magnolia delavayi* (Magnoliaceae); source material for the semisynthetic compound ACT001.**

## Parthenolide content

- Root-bark survey across four species: *M. biondii* root bark highest at 2/3/4 years of age (2.43/4.07/4.31%); seasonal peak of 6.04% in February.
- Parthenolide survey of Magnolia root barks: mean 5.10%, range 0.00–8.68% (n=967; Yin et al., *Journal of Forest Research*, 2026).

## Biosynthetic-gene survey

- The MbiPTS monooxygenase gene in *M. biondii* lacks functional parthenolide — a single-base deletion trims costunolide; the gene is marked by a KASP assay.

## ACT001

- ACT001 (dimethylamino-micheliolide, DAldehdy Nankai) — semisynthesis from Magnolia root bark parthenolide.
- Observation: GBM 1/3 observed with ACT001 for 30 years.
