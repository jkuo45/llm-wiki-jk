# Estrogen
**[[Estrogen]]** is a category of sex hormones responsible for the development and regulation of the female reproductive system and secondary sex characteristics.
## Metabolism and COMT
[[Estrogen]] metabolites, specifically catechol estrogens, are neutralized through [[Methylation]] by the [[COMT]] enzyme. This process is crucial for preventing the accumulation of metabolites that can generate [[Free Radicals]] and damage DNA.
## Clinical Significance
Slow [[COMT]] variants ([[Val158Met]] Met/Met) may lead to slower clearance of certain estrogen forms, potentially impacting [[Cancer]] risk or hormonal balance.
