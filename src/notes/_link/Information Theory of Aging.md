# Information Theory of Aging

The **Information Theory of Aging** is a conceptual framework advanced by [[David Sinclair]] positing that aging arises primarily from the loss of epigenetic and nuclear-architectural information — a "software" problem — rather than from irreversible "hardware" damage such as [[Mutations|mutations]]. Because epigenetic information is, in principle, rewritable, the theory implies that aging may be reversible at the information level.

## Relationship to RCM

The [[Relocalization of Chromatin Modifiers (RCM) Hypothesis]] is a mechanistic instantiation of this theory: repeated recruitment of [[Chromatin Modifiers|chromatin modifiers]] to [[DNA Damage|DNA repair]] sites progressively degrades the [[Epigenetic Landscape|epigenetic landscape]], eroding cellular identity.

## Supporting Lines of Evidence

- The [[ICE Mouse|ICE mouse]] model induces [[Double-Strand Break|DSBs]] without mutations yet produces epigenetic aging.
- [[Partial Reprogramming|Partial reprogramming]] with [[Yamanaka Factors|Yamanaka factors]] can reset epigenetic marks and restore youthful phenotypes.
- [[Epigenetic Clock|Epigenetic clocks]] track age-associated drift that can be advanced or reversed experimentally.
