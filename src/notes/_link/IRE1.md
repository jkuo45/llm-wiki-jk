# IRE1

**IRE1** (ERN1) is one of three UPR sensors alongside PERK and ATF6: unfolded proteins trigger its kinase/RNase activity to splice [[XBP1]] mRNA, producing the XBP1s transcription factor that expands ER folding capacity. Persistent IRE1 signaling flips toward [[Apoptosis]] via JNK; the PERK/ATF6/IRE1 triad couples [[ER Stress]] to [[Autophagy]] induction.
