# Hepatitis B Virus

**Hepatitis B virus (HBV)** is a hepatotropic DNA virus that establishes chronic infection in over 250 million people worldwide and causes more than 800,000 deaths annually from cirrhosis and hepatocellular carcinoma. Viral persistence relies on the stable nuclear cccDNA minichromosome.

## Sirtuin Axis

- **[[SIRT3]]** and **[[SIRT6]]**: Downregulated in HBV-positive patients; both inhibit HBV replication via H3K9 deacetylation on viral cccDNA.
- **[[SIRT4]]**: Downregulated in HBV-positive patients; HBV X protein suppresses SIRT4 expression.
- **[[SIRT1]]**, **[[SIRT2]]**, **[[SIRT5]]**, **[[SIRT7]]**: Slightly elevated mRNA in HBV-infected hepatocytes, contributing to global histone hypoacetylation as an HBV-induced pathomechanism. Resveratrol (SIRT1 agonist) activates HBV transcription, while sirtinol and EX-527 (SIRT1 inhibitors) are anti-HBV.
- **[[SIRT7]]**: Dual — restricts HBV transcription/replication via cccDNA H3 desuccinylation.

## Clinical Relevance

Sirtuin-mediated cccDNA silencing is a promising epigenetic axis for functional HBV cure, complementing antiviral therapy by suppressing the viral minichromosome.
