# Chronic Inflammation

**Chronic inflammation** is the sustained persistence of an inflammatory response — weeks to years — that fails to resolve and therefore drives progressive tissue damage rather than restoration. It is distinct from [[Inflammation]] in duration and outcome, not in mechanism: the same signalling modules (pattern-recognition receptors, [[NF-κB]], [[STAT3]], inflammasomes) simply never reach their termination signals.

> [!info] Why it is a longevity problem
> Chronic inflammation is one of the twelve [[Hallmarks of Aging]], and its age-associated form is often called [[Inflammaging]] — a low-grade, largely sterile background inflammatory tone that rises with biological age even in the absence of infection.

## Acute-to-Chronic Switch

Acute inflammation begins neutrophil-predominant and is normally terminated within days by neutrophil apoptosis, [[Macrophage Polarization]] shift from M1 toward M2, and the release of pro-resolving mediators including [[Resolvins]], [[IL-10]], and lipid mediators derived from [[Arachidonic acid]]. Chronic inflammation is histologically defined instead by a **mononuclear infiltrate** — [[Monocytes]], [[Macrophages]], [[Lymphocytes]] — indicating that the monocyte-recruitment step (driven chiefly by chemokines such as [[IL-8]] and MCP-1/[[CCL2]]) has become the rate-limiting stage.

> [!important] Failure of resolution, not excess of initiation, is the key lesion
> Neutrophils die within hours by [[Apoptosis]]; the switch to chronicity reflects a shift in which [[Macrophages]] and [[T Lymphocyte|T cells]] persist, plus impaired efferocytosis. This is why chronic inflammation is histologically mononuclear even when the initiating insult is an acute infection.

## Molecular Circuit

The core axis is [[TNFα]]/[[IL-1β]] receptors and [[Toll-like Receptor|TLRs]] → [[IKK complex]] → [[IκBα]] degradation → [[NF-κB]] nuclear translocation, driving the [[Pro-inflammatory Cytokines]] [[TNFα]], [[IL-1β]], [[IL-6]], [[IL-12]], [[COX-2]], and [[iNOS]]. NF-κB also primes the [[NLRP3 Inflammasome]] by inducing NLRP3, pro-IL-1β and pro-IL-18 transcription.

[[IL-6]] is the pivotal amplifier: it signals through [[IL-6R]]/gp130 to activate [[STAT3]], which sustains NF-κB activity — a positive feedback loop converting a transient insult into self-perpetuating inflammation. [[Interleukin 11]] has a similar profibrotic amplifier role, which is why it is an emerging anti-fibrotic target.

A second loop runs through [[Inflammasome|Inflammasomes]] and [[Interferon]] pathways: cytosolic DNA and RNA sensed by [[cGAS]]/[[STING]] and [[RIG-I]]/[[MDA5]] drive [[IRF3]]/[[IRF7]] type I interferon signalling, which is chronically triggered by senescence- and damage-derived nucleic acids.

## Chronic Sources

- **Persistent infection** — chronic viral, bacterial, or parasitic infection ([[Helminth|Helminths]] are a classic example).
- **Autoimmunity and sterile injury** — [[Rheumatoid Arthritis]], [[Inflammatory Bowel Disease]], [[Multiple Sclerosis]], [[Psoriasis]].
- **Metabolic and body-composition drivers** — [[Obesity]], [[Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus]], [[Metabolic Syndrome]], [[NAFLD]], and alcohol-associated liver injury; adipose tissue acts as a cytokine source.
- **Cell-intrinsic damage signalling** — [[SASP|Senescence-associated secretory phenotype]] factors from [[Senescent Cells]], [[Mitochondrial ROS]] and [[DAMP|DAMPs]] from damaged tissue.
- **Ageing itself** — [[Immunosenescence]], reduced [[Autophagy]]-mediated clearance of damaged material, and [[Epigenetic Aging]]-linked inflammatory gene derepression.

## Consequences

Persistent inflammatory signalling promotes [[Atherosclerosis]] and [[Cardiovascular Disease]], [[Osteoarthritis]] and [[Fibrosis]] (via [[Matrix Metalloproteinases]] and [[TGF-beta1]]), [[Neurodegenerative Diseases|neurodegeneration]] through microglial ([[Microglia]]) and [[Neuroinflammation|neuroinflammatory]] activation, sarcopenia in [[Skeletal Muscle]] and [[Skeletal Muscle|muscle]], and — with [[Tumor Microenvironment|tumour-microenvironment]] cross-talk — initiation and progression of [[Cancer]].

## Connections

- [[Inflammation]] — Chronic inflammation is the unresolved, tissue-damaging form of the same host defence programme; resolution pathways that fail here are described in the parent note.
- [[Inflammaging]] — The age-associated, largely sterile chronic inflammatory state; chronic inflammation is the process, inflammaging its demographic expression.
- [[NF-κB]] — The dominant transcription factor for inflammatory gene programmes and the convergence point for TNF, IL-1 and TLR signalling; its chronic activation is the mechanistic core of the condition.
- [[SASP]] — Senescent cells supply a self-reinforcing cytokine source (IL-6, IL-8, TNF) that sustains chronic inflammation in ageing tissues.
- [[NLRP3 Inflammasome]] — Supplies IL-1β maturation and bridges sterile damage sensing to chronic inflammatory amplification; it also feeds back on NF-κB priming.
- [[IL-6]] — Central amplifier through IL-6R/STAT3, and the reason IL-6 blockade (e.g. tocilizumab-class agents) is effective in chronic inflammatory disease.
