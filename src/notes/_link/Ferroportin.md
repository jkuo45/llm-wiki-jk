# Ferroportin

**Ferroportin (FPN)**, encoded by **SLC40A1**, is the only known cellular iron exporter in mammals. By moving Fe²⁺ out of cells (notably macrophages, hepatocytes, and duodenal enterocytes), ferroportin controls the size of the intracellular labile iron pool (LIP). Because ferroptosis requires redox-active iron to drive the [[Fenton Reaction]], ferroportin activity is inversely related to ferroptosis susceptibility—higher export means less iron available for lipid peroxidation.

## Structure & Biochemistry

Ferroportin is a multipass transmembrane exporter that translocates Fe²⁺ across the plasma membrane, where [[Ceruloplasmin]] oxidizes the exported iron to Fe³⁺ for binding by [[Transferrin]]. Its stability is regulated by hepcidin, which binds ferroportin and triggers its internalization and degradation.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathway

When intracellular iron is in excess, ferroportin oxidizes Fe²⁺ to Fe³⁺ and exports it, lowering the LIP and curtailing [[Fenton Reaction]]-driven [[Lipid Peroxidation]]. Reduced ferroportin expression (e.g., hepcidin up-regulation, or in tumors with iron retention) raises cytosolic Fe²⁺, fueling ferroptosis. In the iron-metabolism module of ferroptosis (review), ferroportin is the terminal efflux step that balances the transferrin→TFR1→[[STEAP3]]→[[DMT1]] import cascade (review "Role of iron metabolism in ferroptosis").

> [!info] Iron export as a brake on ferroptosis
> Tissues/cancers that down-regulate ferroportin (or up-regulate iron import) accumulate labile iron and become " iron-loaded," priming them for ferroptosis when antioxidant defenses fail—seen in [[Ovarian Cancer]].

## Physiological Function

Ferroportin is central to systemic iron homeostasis, dietary iron export, and recycling from senescent red cells by macrophages. Its hepcidin control couples iron traffic to inflammation.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

Ferroportin mutations cause hemochromatosis type 4. In cancer, low ferroportin (high iron retention) predicts ferroptosis sensitivity; modulating hepcidin/ferroportin could tune therapeutic ferroptosis. Ferroportin also counteracts iron-overload toxicity in [[Ischemia-reperfusion Injury]] and neurodegeneration.
