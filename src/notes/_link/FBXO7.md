# FBXO7

FBXO7 (F-box protein 7, encoded by the [[PARK15]] gene) is a scaffolding protein that interacts with both [[PINK1]] and [[Parkin]], markedly accelerating [[Parkin]] translocation from the cytosol to damaged [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]]. It is a key component of the [[PINK1]]/[[Parkin]]-mediated [[Mitophagy]] pathway and belongs to the F-box protein family, which typically functions as substrate-recognition subunits of [[Ubiquitin|SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box)]] E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes.

## Function in Mitophagy

FBXO7 promotes the clustering of damaged mitochondria and facilitates efficient downstream clearance. This process is crucial for amplifying the initial [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]] signal — when [[Mitochondrial Membrane Potential|mitochondrial membrane potential (ΔΨm)]] is lost, [[PINK1]] stabilizes on the outer mitochondrial membrane (OMM) and recruits [[Parkin]]. FBXO7 enhances this recruitment by acting as a molecular bridge between PINK1 and Parkin, ensuring rapid and robust Parkin translocation.

## Role in Parkinson's Disease

Mutations in [[PARK15]] (FBXO7) cause autosomal recessive early-onset [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's disease]], linking FBXO7 dysfunction directly to [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]] and impaired mitophagy. Loss of FBXO7 function reduces Parkin recruitment efficiency, leading to accumulation of damaged mitochondria and increased susceptibility to [[Oxidative Stress|oxidative stress]] in [[Dopaminergic Neurons|dopaminergic neurons]].
