# Androgen Receptor

The **androgen receptor** ([[Androgen Receptor|AR]]), encoded by *AR*, is a ligand-activated [[Nuclear Receptor|nuclear receptor]] essential for prostate biology and a principal driver of [[Prostate Cancer]].

## Role in Cancer & Modulation by Apigenin

In prostate cancer, apigenin reduces AR protein expression and secreted PSA, inducing G1 arrest with cyclin D1/D2/E and [[CDK2]]/[[CDK4]]/[[CDK6]] downregulation and p21/p27 induction. Flavonoids can act as partial AR agonists at low concentrations. AR signaling interacts with [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]] and [[IGF1]] axes modulated by apigenin.

## Sex Differences — Male-Axis Counterpart Hub

AR is the transcriptional core of the male-axis counterpart to ER: narrower than the 9-arm estrogen hub, anabolic/immunosuppressive, pro-cancer in male tissues.

- **AR→mTOR/Akt:** T→AR cooperates with nongenomic PI3K→Akt→[[mTORC1]]→S6K1 to drive hypertrophy; AR blockade abolishes whole-cell growth even when sarcomeric protein synthesis persists (Basualto-Alarcón 2013; White 2013). In cancer, androgen–AR drives [[mTORC1]] in male contexts with reciprocal AR↔PI3K/AKT/mTOR feedback (see [[Prostate Cancer]]).
- **AR→USP18→NF-κB immunosuppression:** androgen signaling upregulates USP18, blocking NF-κB in T cells and suppressing antitumor immunity; AR activity inversely tracks immune infiltration and checkpoint response across lineages (Zhang Cancer Cell 2023; Guan Nature 2022; Hu 2024 bioRxiv).
- **SIRT–AR:** [[SIRT1]] deacetylates AR-K630, repressing DHT-driven growth (FHL2 co-activation context); [[SIRT7]] depletion suppresses AR signaling and androgen-induced autophagy in LNCaP/22Rv1 (Ding 2020). See [[Testosterone]].
- **AR/ER mirror:** AR target MBOAT2 vs ER target MBOAT1 gate ferroptosis sensitivity in AR+ prostate / ER+ breast cancers (Cell 2023 PMC10330611).
