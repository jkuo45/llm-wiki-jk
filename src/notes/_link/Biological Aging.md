# Biological Aging

**Biological aging** describes how well the body's systems function over time, independent of chronological (calendar) age. Two 70-year-olds can have very different biological profiles — one resembling a younger person, another showing accelerated aging.

## Measurement domains

A multi-domain assessment (as used in the Health and Retirement Study shingles-vaccine analysis) integrates:

- [[Inflammation|Inflammation]]
- [[Innate Immune System|Innate immunity]]
- [[Adaptive Immune System|Adaptive immunity]]
- [[Cardiovascular System|Cardiovascular hemodynamics]]
- [[Neurodegeneration]]
- [[Epigenetic Aging|Epigenetic aging]] (DNA methylation clocks)
- [[Transcriptomic Aging|Transcriptomic aging]] (gene-expression changes)

> [!info] Inflammaging and interventions
> Chronic low-grade [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]] is a core component of biological aging and is linked to heart disease, frailty, and [[Cognitive Decline|cognitive decline]]. The [[Shingles Vaccine|shingles vaccine]] was associated with slower biological aging and lower inflammation in a population cohort, illustrating that modulating chronic inflammation can shift biological age.
