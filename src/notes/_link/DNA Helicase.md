# DNA Helicase

[[DNA Helicase|DNA helicases]] are motor enzymes that unwind double-stranded DNA using ATP hydrolysis, essential for [[DNA Replication]], repair, and transcription. Some bis-benzimidazole compounds bind the minor groove of DNA at specific sequences and inhibit DNA helicase activity, thereby blocking cell proliferation.

## Relevance to Fenbendazole

> [!info] Source: [[Fenbendazole as a Potential Anticancer Drug]]
> Several series of substituted bis-benzimidazoles bind in the minor groove of DNA at specific sequences, inhibiting DNA helicase activity and cell proliferation; some undergo bioreductive activation in hypoxic conditions, producing selective cytotoxicity toward hypoxic cells (Duan et al. 2013).
