# MID51

## Overview

MID51 (mitochondrial dynamics protein 51, also known as MIEF1) is an outer mitochondrial membrane protein that functions as a [[DRP1]] adaptor during [[Mitochondrial Fission]]. While structurally and functionally similar to [[MID49]], MID51 possesses distinct regulatory properties, including the ability to modulate DRP1 GTPase activity.

## Mechanism of Action

MID51 recruits DRP1 to the mitochondrial surface and participates in fission complex assembly. Its unique regulatory features include:

- **ADP cofactor requirement** — MID51 requires ADP as a cofactor for its function, linking its activity to cellular metabolic state
- **Inhibition of MFF-stimulated DRP1 GTPase activity** — MID51 can suppress the enhancement of DRP1's GTPase activity that is normally stimulated by [[MFF]], providing an additional layer of regulation over fission rate

This positions MID51 as both a facilitator and modulator of DRP1-mediated fission, allowing fine-tuned control based on metabolic conditions.

## Physiological Function

Like [[MID49]], MID51 contributes to [[Mitochondrial Integrity]] maintenance by regulating fission. Its ADP dependence suggests a role in coupling mitochondrial dynamics to cellular energy status.
