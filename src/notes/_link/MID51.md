### Overview
MID51 (mitochondrial dynamics protein 51, gene at human chr 22q13, also MIEF1) is an [[Outer Mitochondrial Membrane]] adaptor that recruits the fission GTPase [[DRP1]] to mitochondria to drive [[Mitochondrial Fission|fission]].

### Mechanism
MID51 acts with [[MFF]], [[MID49]], and [[FIS1]] to anchor DRP1 oligomers at constriction sites. It is among the primary receptors that translate fission signals into DRP1 assembly on the mitochondrial surface.

### Functional Significance
MID51 helps set the fission rate and is integral to the balance of dynamics that maintains [[Mitochondrial Integrity]]. Its activity is opposed by fusion factors such as [[OPA1]] and the mitofusins.
