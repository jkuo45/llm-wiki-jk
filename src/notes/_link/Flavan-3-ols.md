# Flavan-3-ols

**Flavan-3-ols** (commonly called **flavanols**) are a subclass of [[Flavonoids]] characterized by a 2-phenyl-3,4-dihydro-2H-chromen-3-ol (flavan) skeleton with the hydroxyl at C3. Unlike most flavonoids they are non-glycosylated and lack a C2=C3 double bond in the C-ring, giving them a saturated heterocycle. They exist as monomers and, through polymerization, as [[Proanthocyanidins]] (condensed tannins).

## Structure & Classification

- **Monomers:** (+)-[[Catechin]], (−)-epicatechin, (+)-gallocatechin, (−)-epigallocatechin, and their gallate esters — most notably (−)-epigallocatechin-3-gallate ([[EGCG]], the dominant green-tea catechin).
- **Stereochemistry:** C2/C3 trans (catechins) vs. cis (epicatechins); galloylation (e.g., EGCG) adds a gallic-acid ester that markedly increases antioxidant potency.
- **Polymerization:** Monomers link via B-type (C4→C8 / C4→C6) and A-type (additional C2→O→C7 ether) bonds to form oligomeric/polymeric [[Proanthocyanidins]] — the condensed-tannin end of the flavan-3-ol axis.
- Dietary sources: tea (especially green/white), cocoa, apples, grapes, berries; unlike [[Flavones]]/[[Flavonols]] (quercetin, kaempferol, fisetin), flavan-3-ols are not typically glycosylated in planta.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **Antioxidant:** Potent radical scavengers via catechol/galloyl H-donation and metal chelation; activate the [[NRF2]]/KEAP1 cytoprotective program. EGCG is among the most potent natural antioxidants (galloylation-driven).
- **SIRT1 / metabolic crosstalk:** Monomeric [[Catechin]] and polymeric [[Proanthocyanidins]] belong to the natural-polyphenol STAC family that activates [[SIRT1]] via the shared K-type allosteric mechanism (lowering substrate K~m~), and engage [[AMPK]] and inhibit [[NF-κB]] inflammatory signaling.
- **Endothelial & vascular:** Enhance eNOS/NO bioavailability and improve endothelial function, capillary resistance, and blood-pressure regulation.

## Physiological Function

- **Cardiovascular protection:** Flavan-3-ol–rich diets (cocoa, tea) improve flow-mediated dilation and lower cardiovascular risk, overlapping with [[Polyphenols]] and [[Resveratrol]] benefits.
- **Metabolic:** Support insulin sensitivity and glucose handling; modulate [[Glycation]] and [[Advanced Glycation End Products]].
- **Neuroprotection:** Cross the [[Blood-Brain Barrier]] and attenuate oxidative/inflammatory neuronal injury ([[Alzheimer's Disease]], [[Parkinson's Disease]] models).

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- As [[Antioxidant|antioxidants]] and natural [[STACs]], flavan-3-ols are investigated for healthy-aging, cardiovascular, metabolic, and dermatological applications; low systemic bioavailability and extensive gut-microbiome conversion to simpler phenolic acids temper direct target engagement.
