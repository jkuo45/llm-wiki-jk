# Ceramide

**Ceramide** is the central hub of [[Sphingolipid Metabolism|sphingolipid metabolism]], consisting of a [[Sphingosine|sphingosine]] backbone *N*-acylated to a fatty acid. It sits at the branch point between synthesis ([[Sphingomyelin|sphingomyelin]] hydrolysis via [[Acid Sphingomyelinase|SMase]] and *de novo* ceramide synthase) and breakdown (cleavage by [[Acid ceramidase|acid ceramidase, ASAH1]] into [[Sphingosine|sphingosine]] + free fatty acid).

## Role in Membrane Lipid Homeostasis

Ceramide synthesis **consumes free saturated (SFA) and monounsaturated (MUFA) fatty acids**, diverting them away from [[Phospholipid|phospholipid]] PUFA production. Conversely, **catabolism of ceramide by [[Acid ceramidase]] releases those free fatty acids**, which—via the Lands cycle—can be exchanged into the *sn-2* position of membrane [[Phospholipid|phospholipids]], enriching them with [[PUFA|PUFAs]] (the substrates of [[Lipid Peroxidation|lipid peroxidation]]).

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex|Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile… (Soriano-Castell et al., 2026)]]
> In replicatively [[Senescent Cells|senescent]] WI-38 fibroblasts, [[Acid ceramidase]] is over-expressed 5- to 20-fold. Despite this, senescent cells retain *higher* [[Ceramide|ceramide]] levels than proliferative cells; ACase knockdown raises ceramide further while lowering membrane PL-[[PUFA|PUFA]] content and protecting against [[Ferroptosis]]. This positions ceramide flux—not just abundance—as a determinant of the pro-ferroptotic lipid profile.

## Signaling & Pathology

- **Stress signaling:** Ceramide accumulation is a conserved stress signal promoting [[Apoptosis|apoptosis]], [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]], and inflammation.
- **Senescence & cancer:** Ceramide/sphingosine rheostat shifts (e.g., ACase-driven lowering of ceramide, raising [[Sphingosine|sphingosine]]/S1P) are implicated in [[Senescent Cells|senescent-cell survival]] and in [[Melanoma]] / [[Prostate Cancer]] biology where ACase is over-expressed.

## Connections

- [[Acid ceramidase]] — Enzyme that cleaves ceramide into sphingosine + free fatty acid; its activity shapes ferroptosis sensitivity.
- [[Sphingosine]] / [[Sphingomyelin]] — Direct metabolic neighbors in the sphingolipid network.
- [[Phospholipid]] — Ceramide flux indirectly sets membrane PUFA-PL abundance via free FA release.
- [[PUFA]] — The ferroptosis substrate whose membrane incorporation is promoted by ceramide breakdown.
- [[Ferroptosis]] — Ceramide catabolism (via ACase) creates the pro-ferroptotic lipid profile.
- [[Lipid Peroxidation]] — Execution step whose substrate pool is governed by ceramide/ACase dynamics.
- [[Senescent Cells]] — Senescent cells show elevated ceramide and ACase, sensitizing them to ferroptosis.
- [[Apoptosis]] / [[Melanoma]] / [[Prostate Cancer]] — Contexts where the ceramide rheostat is pathologically relevant.
