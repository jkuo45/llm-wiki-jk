# Bone marrow-derived macrophages

**Bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs)** are macrophages differentiated in vitro from bone marrow hematopoietic progenitors (typically with M-CSF). They are a standard model for studying [[Inflammation|inflammatory]] signaling, including the [[AIM2]] and NLRP3 inflammasomes.

> [!info] In the DROSHA–AIM2 IPF study
> DROSHA deficiency suppressed AIM2 inflammasome-dependent [[Caspase-1]] activation and [[Interleukin 1β|IL-1β]]/[[IL-18]] secretion in BMDMs; miRNA transduction increased [[ASC]] speck formation, required for AIM2 activation — paralleling findings in primary [[Alveolar Macrophages|alveolar macrophages]].
