# CDK4 6

**CDK4** and **CDK6** are closely related [[Cyclin-Dependent Kinases]] that regulate the [[G1 Phase|G1]]-to-[[S Phase|S]] phase transition of the [[Cell Cycle]]. They form active complexes with [[Cyclin D]] (D1, D2, D3) to phosphorylate and inactivate the [[Retinoblastoma Protein]] (pRb), releasing [[E2F]] transcription factors to drive S-phase gene expression.

## Regulation

CDK4/6 activity is controlled by:
- [[Cyclin D]] binding — induced by [[Mitogen]] signaling via [[RAS]]-[[MEK]]-[[ERK]] pathway
- [[INK4 Family]] inhibitors — [[p16INK4A]], [[p15INK4b]], [[p18INK4c]], [[p19INK4d]]
- [[CIP/KIP Family]] inhibitors — [[p21Cip1]], [[p27Kip1]]; paradoxical positive regulators of CDK4/6-Cyclin D assembly
- [[Phosphorylation]] by [[CDK-Activating Kinase]] (CAK) at Thr172

## Clinical Relevance

CDK4/6 is overactive in many cancers via [[Cyclin D amplification]] (breast cancer, mantle cell lymphoma), [[p16INK4A]] loss, and [[pRb]] inactivation. [[CDK4]]/[[CDK6]] inhibitors ([[Palbociclib]], [[Ribociclib]], [[Abemaciclib]]) are FDA-approved for HR+/HER2− breast cancer and cause [[Cell Cycle Arrest]] in [[G1 Phase]].
