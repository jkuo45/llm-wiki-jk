# Inulin

**Inulin** is a non-digestible fructan oligosaccharide (a β(2→1) linked fructose polymer) classified as a prebiotic carbohydrate. Beyond its dietary benefits ([[Bioavailability|bioavailability]], safety, low cost), enzymatically synthesized inulin forms [[Nanoparticles|nanoparticles]] that can encapsulate and protect hydrophobic bioactive compounds.

## Fisetin–Inulin Nanoparticles

Charoenwongpaiboon et al. encapsulated fisetin in inulin nanoparticles (enzymatic synthesis from sucrose via *Lactobacillus reuteri* inulosucrase; 95.9–238.5 nm depending on temperature). The complex:

- Increased fisetin solubility 3.6-fold vs. free compound.
- Showed higher DPPH [[Antioxidant|antioxidant]] activity than free fisetin.
- Improved thermal stability — retained greater DPPH scavenging after 120 min at 70 °C (glycerol/water-induced lipid fluidization protects the cargo).
