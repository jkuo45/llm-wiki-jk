# Phospholipid

**Phospholipids (PLs)** are the principal structural lipids of biological membranes, composed of a glycerol backbone, two fatty acyl chains, a phosphate, and a head group (e.g., phosphatidylcholine **PC**, phosphatidylethanolamine **PE**, phosphatidylserine **PS**, phosphatidylglycerol **PG**, phosphatidylinositol **PI**). Their **polyunsaturated fatty acid ([[PUFA|PUFA]]) content at the *sn-2* position** is the critical determinant of [[Lipid Peroxidation|lipid peroxidation]] susceptibility and thus of [[Ferroptosis]].

## PUFA-PLs as Ferroptosis Substrates

- The execution of [[Ferroptosis]] depends on membrane PL-[[PUFA|PUFAs]] (PL-PUFAs), which are the substrates oxidized during [[Lipid Peroxidation|LPO]].
- Remodeling occurs through the **Kennedy pathway** (initial SFA/MUFA incorporation) and the **Lands cycle** ([[Phospholipase A2|PLA2]]-mediated sn-2 removal followed by [[ACSL4]]-dependent PUFA re-acylation).
- Cardiolipin (PG-derived, mitochondrial) PUFA peroxidation is a marker of mitochondrial [[Lipid Peroxidation|LPO]].

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex|Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile… (Soriano-Castell et al., 2026)]]
> In replicatively [[Senescent Cells|senescent]] WI-38 fibroblasts, over-expression of [[Acid ceramidase]] drove a pro-ferroptotic profile: increased sn-2 PUFA-containing PLs (all [[Arachidonic acid|arachidonic acid]]-containing PLs plus PC-LA, PC-DHA, PE-DHA, PG-DHA, etc.). ACase knockdown *decreased* these PL-PUFAs (significantly for PC-AA, PG-DHA, PG-DPA) and protected against [[RSL3]]-induced [[Ferroptosis]]—independent of [[GPX4]]/[[Glutathione|GSH]] and [[Iron|iron]].

## Connections

- [[PUFA]] — The oxidizable acyl chains whose membrane incorporation sets ferroptosis sensitivity.
- [[ACSL4]] — Drives PUFA incorporation into PLs; canonical ferroptosis enhancer.
- [[Acid ceramidase]] — Independently of ACSL4, ACase raises PL-PUFA content via ceramide-derived free fatty acids.
- [[Ceramide]] — Its flux indirectly governs free FA availability for PL remodeling.
- [[Lipid Peroxidation]] — The oxidative execution step acting on PL-PUFAs.
- [[Ferroptosis]] — PL-PUFA abundance is the rate-limiting substrate pool for ferroptotic death.
- [[GPX4]] / [[Glutathione]] / [[Iron]] — Classic ferroptosis regulators; ACase acts upstream/independently of them.
- [[RSL3]] — GPX4 inhibitor used to reveal PL-PUFA-dependent sensitivity.
- [[Senescent Cells]] — Senescent cells display elevated PL-PUFAs and ferroptosis sensitivity.
- [[Cardiolipin]] — Mitochondrial PG-derived PL; its PUFA peroxidation marks mitochondrial LPO.
