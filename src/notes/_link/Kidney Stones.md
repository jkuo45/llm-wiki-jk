# Kidney Stones

**Kidney stones (nephrolithiasis)** are crystalline deposits that form within the kidney, most commonly composed of calcium oxalate. Stone formation involves crystal nucleation, crystal-cell adhesion, and tubular injury, and it is a risk factor for chronic kidney disease.

## Sirtuin Axis

- **[[SIRT3]]**: Downregulated in patients with kidney stones; protective via the Nrf2/heme oxygenase-1 pathway.
- **[[SIRT1]]**: Suppressing SIRT1 promotes calcium oxalate crystal-cell adhesion and exacerbates injury.

## Clinical Relevance

Sirtuin-dependent antioxidant defense (SIRT3–Nrf2/HO-1) protects tubular epithelium from crystal-induced injury, and SIRT1 preservation limits crystal-cell adhesion. These are candidate targets for preventing recurrent stone disease.
