# Epigenetic Aging

**Epigenetic aging** refers to the accumulation of age-related changes in chromatin states — principally **[[DNA Methylation]]** drift and histone modifications — that can be integrated into epigenetic clocks (e.g., Horvath, Hannum, PhenoAge, DunedinPACE) estimating biological age.

> [!info] In the shingles-vaccine study
> Vaccinated participants showed **slower epigenetic aging** (and slower [[Transcriptomic Aging|transcriptomic aging]]) than unvaccinated, with effects persisting ≥4 years — a domain of the composite [[Biological Aging|biological aging]] score. This aligns with the broader view that epigenetic derepression of retroelements (e.g., [[LINE-1]]) and heterochromatin loss fuels [[Inflammaging|inflammaging]].
