# Adrenal Cortex

The **adrenal cortex** is the outer, glandular portion of the [[Adrenal gland]], sitting between the fibrous capsule and the chromaffin tissue of the [[Adrenal medulla]]. It is a steroid-secreting tissue: all of its products — mineralocorticoids, glucocorticoids and C19 androgen precursors — are derived from cholesterol, and the distinct output of each zone is set by which cytochrome P450 enzymes that zone expresses.

> [!info] The Standard Model of zonation
> Adrenocortical cells arise in the outer part of the gland and migrate centripetally, changing phenotype as they pass from the outer zone inward, and undergo apoptosis in the innermost zone. The zonation is maintained by a gradient of [[Angiotensin II]] and extracellular [[Potassium]] signalling from the capsular vasculature versus ACTH signalling centrally, but the boundary between zones is leaky in practice — the "Standard Model" is a useful approximation rather than a hard rule (Frontiers in Neuroscience, 2016).

## Zonal Architecture

| Zone | Principal product | Defining enzyme | Main regulator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Zona glomerulosa** (outermost, ~15% of cortical thickness) | [[Aldosterone]] | CYP11B2 (aldosterone synthase) | [[Angiotensin II]], extracellular K⁺; inhibited by [[Angiotensin II Type 1 Receptor]] feedback and [[Dopamine]]-like dopaminergic input |
| **Zona fasciculata** (widest layer) | [[Cortisol]] (glucocorticoids) | CYP11B1 (11β-hydroxylase) | ACTH |
| **Zona reticularis** (innermost) | Adrenal androgens (DHEA) | CYP17 (17α-hydroxylase) | ACTH |

The functional distinction is enzymatic, not merely positional. Fasciculata and reticularis cells express the full glucocorticoid synthesis set — CYP11A1, CYP21A2, 3β-HSD and StAR — whereas glomerulosa cells are *selectively* spared from CYP17 expression, which is what diverts the C21 (cortisol) pathway into the C19 (aldosterone) pathway.

## Steroidogenesis Pathway

Steroid synthesis proceeds from cholesterol through a fixed sequence of mitochondrial and microsomal enzymes:

- **StAR-mediated cholesterol transport** into the inner mitochondrial membrane is the rate-limiting, rate-controlling step; its expression is what ACTH ultimately raises.
- **CYP11A1** (P450scc) converts cholesterol → pregnenolone.
- **3β-HSD** routes pregnenolone/progesterone; **CYP17A1** adds a 17α-hydroxyl in fasciculata and reticularis.
- **CYP11B1** gives 11β-hydroxylation (cortisol); **CYP11B2**, expressed only in glomerulosa, performs the same 11β-hydroxylation then adds an 18-oxidation to give aldosterone.

> [!important] Why 11β-hydroxylation decides the hormone
> Glomerulosa cells express CYP11B2 rather than CYP11B1 despite carrying the same ACTH receptor repertoire. The divergence is maintained locally; loss of the zona-specific transcription programme blurs the border and is one route to mixed hormone output in adrenal pathology.

Most adrenal cholesterol is supplied by the circulation, but the cortex also synthesises a substantial fraction locally.

## Regulation and Clinical Relevance

ACTH from the pituitary drives fasciculata and reticularis output; the renin–angiotensin system and plasma potassium drive the glomerulosa. Negative feedback operates at two levels: cortisol suppresses CRH/ACTH release, and angiophorin II–driven aldosterone suppresses renin release.

- **Hypertension** — chronic aldosterone excess (primary aldosteronism) produces sodium retention, potassium wasting and increased vascular and myocardial fibrosis beyond what blood pressure alone explains.
- **Adrenal insufficiency** — loss of cortical function causes glucocorticoid and mineralocorticoid deficiency, with hypotension, hyponatraemia, hyperkalaemia and hypoglycaemia risk.
- **Congenital adrenal hyperplasia** — deficiencies in CYP21A2, CYP11B1 or 3β-HSD shunt precursors toward androgens, producing virilisation and salt-wasting crises.
- **Cushing syndrome** — autonomous cortisol production or exogenous glucocorticoid excess; chronic excess drives muscle catabolism, insulin resistance, osteoporosis and immune suppression.

> [!warning] Adrenal insufficiency is an emergency when acute
> Crisis presents as refractory hypotension, vomiting and altered mental status, and requires hydrocortisone plus fluids, not mineralocorticoid manipulation. Chronic management is glucocorticoid replacement with dose adjustment around illness and surgery, since the HPA axis cannot mount an endogenous response.

- [[Adrenal gland]] — the adrenal cortex is the outer glandular region of the gland whose medulla it anatomically surrounds; the vault's adrenal note describes both compartments.
- [[Aldosterone]] — produced exclusively by the zona glomerulosa, so the hormone note is downstream of this one.
- [[Cortisol]] — the zona fasciculata product whose synthesis depends on the same StAR-limited cholesterol pathway.
- [[Angiotensin II]] — the dominant humoral stimulus for glomerulosa aldosterone synthesis and the main driver of the cortex's zonation signal.
- [[Cholesterol]] — the substrate for every adrenocortical steroid; circulating and locally synthesised cholesterol both feed the pathway.
- [[Mitochondria]] — the first and rate-limiting steps of steroidogenesis (StAR transport and CYP11A1 catalysis) are intramitochondrial, which is why steroidogenic cells are mitochondria-rich.
