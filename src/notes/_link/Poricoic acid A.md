# Poricoic acid A

**Poricoic acid A** is a lanostane-type triterpenoid isolated from the traditional medicinal fungus *Poria cocos* (Fuling), long used in East Asian medicine. Poria-derived triterpenoids exhibit anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and anti-tumor activities. Poricoic acid A is among the most studied of these compounds for kidney disease, with particular interest in its anti-fibrotic actions.

## Anti-fibrotic mechanism via SIRT3

*Poria cocos*-derived poricoic acid A upregulates [[SIRT3]] and promotes [[β-Catenin|β-catenin]] K49 deacetylation, effectively attenuating renal [[Fibroblasts|fibroblast]] activation and renal interstitial [[Fibrosis|fibrosis]] *in vivo* and *in vitro*[296]. β-catenin acetylation state governs its transcriptional activity in fibrogenic gene programs; deacetylation at K49 by the SIRT3-dependent axis dampens profibrotic signaling, while SIRT3 upregulation reinforces mitochondrial redox protection in tubular and interstitial cells.

## Applications

- Research use: in vivo and in vitro renal fibrosis models.
- Clinical context: candidate disease-modifying agent for tubulointerstitial fibrosis in chronic kidney disease.
