# Thymidylate Synthase

[[Thymidylate Synthase]] (TS) catalyzes the methylation of deoxyuridine monophosphate (dUMP) to deoxythymidine monophosphate (dTMP) using 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate as the methyl donor. It is the principal target of [[5-Fluorouracil]], whose active metabolite FdUMP forms a lethal ternary complex with TS and folate, depleting thymidine and triggering [[DNA Repair]] stress and [[Apoptosis]].

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> Fenbendazole overcomes 5-FU-resistant colorectal cells via glycolysis interference rather than TS inhibition, highlighting TS as the canonical 5-FU target (5).
