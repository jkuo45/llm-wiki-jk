# IKK complex

The **IκB kinase (IKK) complex** is the core signaling component of the canonical [[NF-κB|NF-κB]] pathway. It consists of two catalytic subunits, [[IKKalpha]] (IKKα) and [[IKKbeta]] (IKKβ), and a regulatory scaffold subunit, NEMO (IKKγ, encoded by IKBKG).

## Mechanism of Action & Signaling

In resting cells, NF-κB dimers (p50/[[p65]]) are sequestered in the cytoplasm by [[IkappaBalpha]] and related inhibitors. Upon stimulation by [[TNF-α]], [[IL-1β]], pathogen-associated molecular patterns (via TLRs), or genotoxic stress, upstream kinases — including [[TAK1]] together with TAB proteins — activate the IKK complex. IKKβ is the principal kinase that phosphorylates [[IkappaBalpha]] on Ser32 and Ser36. This creates a phosphodegron recognized by the [[SCF]]^β-TrCP E3 ligase, leading to K48-linked ubiquitination and rapid proteasomal degradation of IκB. Freed NF-κB translocates to the nucleus and activates target genes. NEMO is essential for signal-dependent assembly and activation of the complex; IKKα contributes mainly to non-canonical NF-κB (p100 processing) and to chromatin/gene repressor phosphorylation.

## Role in Senescence / SASP

The IKK complex is a key mediator of [[NF-κB|NF-κB]] activation in [[Senescent Cells]] and a master driver of the [[SASP|Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype]]. NF-κB is necessary and, in cooperation with C/EBPβ, sufficient for SASP transcription (Chien et al., 2011, PMID 21876164). In senescence, persistent DDR and cytokine/receptor signals keep IKKβ active, sustaining IκBα turnover and nuclear NF-κB, which transcribes IL-6, IL-8, chemokines, and matrix metalloproteinases. Because the IKK–NF-κB module intersects with JAK/STAT and p38–MK2 axes, it integrates transcriptional and post-transcriptional layers of the SASP. Inhibiting IKKβ (or upstream [[TAK1]]) suppresses SASP secretion, marking the complex as a central senomorphic node.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

Dysregulated IKK/NF-κB signaling underlies chronic inflammatory and malignant disease, and IKKβ/NEMO are longstanding anti-inflammatory targets. Within geroscience, dampening IKK-driven NF-κB is a leading strategy to blunt SASP-mediated [[Inflammaging]], fibrosis, and metastasis-promoting tumor microenvironments, though systemic NF-κB inhibition risks immunosuppression.
