# Securin

**Securin** (PTTG1) binds and inhibits separase, the protease that cleaves cohesin to separate sister chromatids. At anaphase onset, the [[Anaphase Promoting Complex-Cyclosome]] with [[CDC20]] ubiquitinates securin for destruction, freeing separase and triggering segregation. Non-degradable securin blocks anaphase; overexpression is common in pituitary, thyroid, and colorectal tumors.
