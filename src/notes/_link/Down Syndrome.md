# Down Syndrome

**Down Syndrome** (trisomy 21) results from meiotic nondisjunction producing a third [[Chromosome]] 21. The extra *APP* copy drives lifelong Aβ overproduction with Alzheimer pathology by age 40; premature [[Senescence]], immune dysregulation, and [[Epigenetic Aging]] acceleration make it a natural model of Aβ-driven neurodegeneration and segmental progeria.
