# SRCAP

**SRCAP** (Snf2-related CREBBP activator protein) is an ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler of the SNF2 family, encoded on human chromosome 16p11.2. It has two apparently separable functions that give the protein its dual name: it is the catalytic subunit of the **SRCAP complex**, the vertebrate ortholog of the yeast **Swr1** complex that loads the [[Histone H2A.Z]] variant into nucleosomes; and it is a **transcriptional coactivator** that works through the histone acetyltransferase [[CREBBP]] (CBP).

> [!info] Mechanism
> SRCAP contains a SNF2-family ATPase domain that hydrolyzes ATP to evict and reposition nucleosomal H2A–H2B dimers. Swapping canonical H2A for H2A.Z is the defining reaction; the exchange requires the H2A.Z chaperone ANP32E and is antagonized by ATP-dependent H2A.Z eviction by the INO80 complex. A human SRCAP complex purified in vitro was shown to directly remodel chromatin by incorporating H2A.Z into nucleosomes, and genetic work established that SRCAP is required for H2A.Z deposition at promoters.

## The SRCAP complex

The SRCAP complex is a multi-subunit assembly. Unlike the closely related **INO80** complex, the SRCAP complex does **not** act as a functional E3 ubiquitin ligase for the H2A.Z chaperone ANP32E, because its RuvBL-associated module is not positioned to interact with the chaperine. The human SRCAP and INO80 complexes share a conserved YL1 (DPA2/DPB3) protein as a common subunit, and both also contain RuvBL-like AAA+ ATPases. SRCAP is expressed broadly across tissues, with notable expression in testis, endometrium, granulocytes, and colonic epithelium.

## Transcriptional coactivator function

Independently of chromatin remodeling, SRCAP was originally cloned for binding CBP and was shown to synergize with **CARM1 (PRMT4)** and **GRIP-1** in steroid-receptor-mediated transcription. Its Drosophila homolog, **DOM** (Domino), functions in the [[Notch]] signaling pathway, indicating the SRCAP/Notch link is an ancient one. Two viral interactions are also documented: hepatitis C virus **NS5A** modulates transcription through SRCAP, and adenovirus DNA-binding protein inhibits SRCAP-mediated transcription.

> [!warning] Clinical caveat
> The two SRCAP functions are separable in principle but not cleanly in vivo, and the chromatin-remodeling and coactivator literatures rarely cite each other. A claim that SRCAP "activates transcription" may refer to either route. An OMIM entry links SRCAP to Floating-Harbor syndrome, but the human disease evidence for SRCAP haploinsufficiency is considerably thinner than the well-established H2A.Z-deposition biochemistry; treat the clinical link as provisional.

## Connections

- [[Histone H2A.Z]] — SRCAP is the H2A.Z-specific exchange factor; loss of SRCAP reduces H2A.Z occupancy at promoters, and the INO80 complex is the counter-acting H2A.Z evictor.
- [[Chromatin Remodeling]] — SRCAP belongs to the SNF2 ATPase family and catalyzes nucleosome disassembly and reassembly, placing it among the principal ATP-dependent remodelers.
- [[Histone Acetylation]] — SRCAP's second function is to work with CBP/CREBBP as a transcriptional coactivator, so H2A.Z deposition and histone acetylation are two routes by which SRCAP touches transcription.
- [[Notch]] — the Drosophila homolog DOM acts in the Notch pathway, and human SRCAP has been linked to Notch target expression, making SRCAP a candidate effector of Notch-dependent transcription.
- [[Autophagy]] — chromatin accessibility at autophagy gene promoters is a recurring mechanism in the TFEB/FACT literature in this vault, and SRCAP is a promoter-targeting remodeler, so it is a plausible node in that axis. The direct SRCAP–autophagy relationship is not established and is listed as a candidate rather than a fact.
- [[Chromatin]] — the substrate on which all SRCAP activity is exerted; nucleosome composition is the mechanism through which SRCAP changes gene output.
