# SSRP1

**SSRP1** (structure specific recognition protein 1) is the **80 kDa subunit of the [[FACT complex]]**, a histone chaperone that allows RNA polymerase II and the replication machinery to traverse nucleosomes. It is a human **high mobility group (HMG)-box** protein, distinct from the small chromatin-binding HMG1/HMG2 (HMGB1/HMGB2) family but structurally related to them.

> [!info] Mechanism
> FACT is a heterodimer of SSRP1 and [[SUPT16H]]. It acts catalytically, not as a scaffold: SSRP1's HMG box binds bent/distorted DNA and the nucleosomal acidic patch, while SPT16 provides the second dimer contact. Together they transiently **disassemble the H2A–H2B dimer** on the entry side of a nucleosome, allowing the polymerase through, and **reassemble it on the exit side** behind the polymerase. This reversible, "hand-in-hand" chaperone action is what permits transcription of chromatin templates in vitro, which naked-DNA transcription with general factors alone cannot do.

## Transcription and replication

FACT was purified by Reinberg and colleagues in 1998 as a factor that allows transcript elongation through nucleosomes, and the human SPT16/SSRP1 composition was resolved in 1999. FACT relieves the DSIF/NELF-mediated pause that otherwise blocks productive elongation, and it works cooperatively with H2B monoubiquitination to set elongation kinetics. FACT also functions in **DNA replication**, coordinating with the MCM helicase at replication origins, and participates in **DNA repair** and the response to DNA damage. Chromatin-dependent transcription in vitro requires both FACT and the ISWI-containing **RSF** complex.

## DNA-damage and cisplatin recognition

SSRP1's HMG box is the structural-recognition element for cisplatin-damaged DNA. The original cDNA clones were isolated precisely because the protein bound selectively to DNA distorted by cisplatin adducts, and the FACT–SSRP1–cisplatin interaction was later confirmed directly. This underpins the proposal that FACT–SSRP1 engagement with cisplatin- and UV-modified DNA contributes to the drug's cytotoxic mechanism. SSRP1 also assembles into a **CK2–hSpt16–SSRP1 complex** that phosphorylates **p53 at Ser392** in response to UV, linking FACT to the p53 damage response.

## Other transcriptional roles

Beyond FACT, SSRP1 acts as a coactivator of several transcription factors, including full-length **p63** (but not the ΔN-p63 isoform), PU.1, and serum response factor, and it was independently identified as an embryonic β-like globin gene activator. [[FACT complex]] inhibition is also a known tool for increasing [[Autophagy]] by altering the epigenetic landscape at autophagy gene promoters.

> [!warning] Clinical caveat
> Autoantibodies against SSRP1 have been reported at high prevalence in sera of patients with [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]] and not in other rheumatic diseases. This is a useful disease marker but does not establish causality, and SSRP1 autoantibody positivity should not be read as diagnostic on its own.

## Connections

- [[SUPT16H]] — SUPT16H is the obligate heterodimer partner of SSRP1 in FACT. Neither subunit is functional alone; the HMG box of SSRP1 and the N-terminal domain of SPT16 form the two halves of the H2A–H2B handling machinery.
- [[FACT complex]] — SSRP1 is one of exactly two subunits of FACT, and FACT is its defining biochemical context; every function described here except the coactivator and cisplatin-recognition roles is FACT-mediated.
- [[Nucleosome]] — FACT's substrate. Disassembly and reassembly of the H2A–H2B dimer within the nucleosome is the physical step that lets the polymerase pass.
- [[Cisplatin]] — cisplatin-distorted DNA is the substrate for which SSRP1's HMG box was originally selected, and FACT–SSRP1 binding to cisplatin-adducted DNA is proposed to be part of the drug's anticancer mechanism.
- [[p53]] — SSRP1 participates in the CK2–hSpt16–SSRP1 kinase complex that phosphorylates p53 at Ser392 after UV damage.
- [[Autophagy]] — FACT inhibition, which necessarily removes SSRP1's H2A–H2B chaperone activity, increases autophagy by changing the epigenetic landscape; this is the connection the vault's FACT note records.
- [[Chromatin Remodeling]] — FACT occupies a distinct category from ATP-dependent remodelers like SRCAP: it is a histone chaperone that transiently disassembles and reassembles dimers rather than sliding or ejecting whole nucleosomes.
