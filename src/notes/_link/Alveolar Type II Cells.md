# Alveolar Type II Cells

[[Alveolar Type II Cells|Alveolar type II (AT2) cells]] are the surfactant-producing epithelial cells of the distal lung alveolus and serve as the facultative progenitor population that regenerates the flattened [[Epithelial cells|alveolar type I (AT1) epithelium]] after injury. In [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis|idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF)]], AT2 cells are increasingly recognized as the cell-of-origin and primary sensor of senescence-driven fibrotic remodeling.

## Overview

AT2 cells reside along the alveolar wall, synthesize and secrete pulmonary surfactant via lamellar bodies, and participate in ion/water transport to maintain alveolar airspace stability. They are metabolically active, mitochondria-rich, and uniquely equipped with progenitor capacity—properties that also make them exquisitely sensitive to stress.

## Vulnerability to Senescence in IPF

A 2026 narrative review on [[Senescence|cellular senescence]] in IPF identifies [[Alveolar Type II Cells|AT2 cells]] as the most senescence-vulnerable epithelial population in the diseased lung:

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - cellular_senescence_ipf_diseases-14-00201|Cellular Senescence in IPF (Baurzhan et al., 2026)]]
> In IPF-affected lungs, AT2 cells demonstrate elevated expression of [[p16INK4A|p16^INK4a]] and [[p21 CIP1|p21^CIP1]] alongside [[DNA Damage|DNA damage response]] activation, consistent with stress-induced premature senescence. Premature epithelial senescence can occur independently of chronological aging (triggered by environmental exposure, oxidative injury, smoking, [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]], viral infection, and repeated microinjury) and compromises epithelial repair, facilitating fibrotic remodeling.

Critically shortened [[Telomere|telomeres]] in AT2 cells—driven by [[Telomere Attrition|telomere attrition]] and [[Telomerase|telomerase]] mutations—trigger sustained [[p53]] activation and replicative senescence, reducing regenerative capacity after injury. [[Senescent Cells|Senescent AT2 cells]] then secrete a [[SASP|senescence-associated secretory phenotype]] that amplifies [[Fibroblast|fibroblast]] activation, closing a self-reinforcing fibrotic loop.

> [!important] Temporal priority
> Murine and human evidence indicates that accumulation of senescent [[Alveolar Type II Cells|AT2 cells]] *precedes* fibrotic remodeling, reframing senescence as an upstream driver rather than a downstream marker of IPF.
