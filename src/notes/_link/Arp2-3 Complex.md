# Arp2-3 Complex

The **Arp2/3 complex** is a ~220 kDa, seven-subunit assembly that nucleates new actin filaments as branches off the sides of existing ("mother") filaments. Isolated in 1994 from *Acanthamoeba* as a profilin-binding partner, it takes its name from the two subunits that proved to be actin-like — actin-related proteins 2 and 3. It is the only well-characterised branched-actin nucleator and is conserved from yeast to humans.

## Subunits and Architecture

One copy each of **Arp2** (*ACTR2*) and **Arp3** (*ACTR3*) plus five scaffolding subunits **ARPC1–ARPC5** (historically p41/p40, p34, p21, p20, p16). ARPC1 is a WD40 beta-propeller; ARPC2 and ARPC4 form the structural core as a tight heterodimer, and both are required for assembly.

> [!info] The pseudo-dimer trick
> Arp2 and Arp3 are each folded like a monomer of G-actin, and their exposed surfaces mimic the **barbed end** of an actin filament. Bringing the two "pseudo-barbed-ends" together therefore supplies the template for a filament nucleus, bypassing the kinetically unfavourable dimerisation step of spontaneous polymerisation.

In the basal state the two Arps are splayed apart and twisted, so no pseudo-dimer exists — the complex is constitutively inactive when purified.

## Activation and Branched Nucleation

Activation is a two-step, spatially segregated process:

1. **Nucleation-promoting factor (NPF) binding.** WASP-family proteins (WASP, N-WASP, WAVE/Scar) and [[Cortactin]] dock on Arp2–ARPC1 and on Arp3 through their Central-Acidic (CA) region, while their WH2 domains deliver profilin-actin to the pseudo-barbed ends. This drives a ~19 degree rotation into the filament-like **short-pitch conformation**.
2. **Mother-filament engagement.** The complex must also bind the side of a pre-existing filament, preferably near a growing barbed end where ATP-rich subunits sit. Only then does a daughter filament emerge at a remarkably constant **~70 degree** angle.

> [!important] Branching angle as a mechanistic signature
> The 70 degree angle was among the first striking observations of Arp2/3 function and remains its experimental hallmark. Cryo-EM of branch junctions later confirmed that five subunits contact the mother filament and that the whole complex rotates perpendicular to the mother axis — a geometry that explains its mechanical stability.

Cysteine-crosslinking experiments separated the two steps: forcing the short-pitch conformation suffices for the complex to nucleate and generate forces at a membrane, but only mother-filament engagement yields a *sustained* branched network.

## Cellular Roles

Branched actin built by Arp2/3 drives lamellipodial protrusion, [[Cell Migration]], endocytic [[Clathrin]]-coated pit invagination, vesicle trafficking, and the actin rocket that propels *Listeria monocytogenes* (via its surface protein ActA, which mimics an NPF).

> [!warning] Not all polymerisation is Arp2/3
> Unbranched filaments used for cytokinesis, contractile rings and stress fibres are nucleated by **formin** proteins instead. Treating "actin nucleation" as synonymous with Arp2/3 is a common error.

In cancer, sustained Arp2/3 activity at invadopodia drives matrix breach and invasion, which makes the activation step — not the complex itself — the more tractable drug target.

## Connections
- [[Cortactin]] — Its conserved DDW motif in the N-terminal acidic domain binds Arp3, and unlike WASP-family factors cortactin stays bound at the branch, stabilising it against dissociation.
- [[Actin Cytoskeleton]] — Arp2/3 is the nucleator that converts monomeric actin into the branched networks underlying protrusion and migration.
- [[Cell Migration]] — Lamellipodial protrusion at the leading edge requires continuous branching, so the complex sits downstream of Rac-family GTPase activation.
- [[WHAMM]] — A WASP-family nucleation-promoting factor, so it activates Arp2/3 in the same biochemical sense as N-WASP, but at ER- and membrane-associated structures rather than the cortex.
- [[Clathrin]] — Clathrin-coated pit and vesicle formation in mammals is nucleated by Arp2/3 with adaptors AP2 and AP4, linking the complex to membrane trafficking as well as motility.
- [[Endocytosis]] — Arp2/3 supplies the actin machinery for clathrin-dependent internalisation, including the force needed to invaginate a membrane under tension.
