# Zscan4

**Zscan4** (zinc finger and SCAN domain containing 4) is a transcription factor implicated in the feedforward regulatory mechanisms that potentiate the [[SASP|SASP]] during senescence.

## Relevance to the ASAP→SASP Transition

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> The SASP is potentiated by dual feedforward mechanisms involving **Zscan4** and [[TAK1]][27, 46]. Apigenin blocks the downstream [[Acute Stress-Associated Phenotype|ASAP]]→SASP transition (via the [[PRDX6]]→[[HSPA8]] axis), intercepting this feedforward cascade.

## Connections

- [[SASP]] — Zscan4 potentiates SASP via feedforward.
- [[TAK1]] — Co-driver of SASP feedforward.
- [[Acute Stress-Associated Phenotype]] — ASAP→SASP transition Zscan4 helps drive.
- [[Apigenin]] — Intercepts the transition downstream of Zscan4/TAK1.
