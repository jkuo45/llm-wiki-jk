# Rab8

Rab8 (encoded by [[RAB8A]]) is a small [[GTP|GTPase]] that interacts with the [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]] receptor [[OPTN|Optineurin]] and is required for retrograde transport of [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]]-containing [[Autophagosome|autophagosomes]] along microtubules to the cell body.

## Role in Huntington's Disease

In [[Huntington's Disease|Huntington's disease]], the interaction between [[OPTN|Optineurin]] and Rab8 is compromised by mutant [[Huntingtin|huntingtin (mHtt)]]. This impairs the efficient retrograde transport of mitochondria-containing autophagosomes along microtubules to the cell body, where [[Lysosome|lysosomes]] are primarily located, thereby preventing the autophagic process from proceeding normally.
