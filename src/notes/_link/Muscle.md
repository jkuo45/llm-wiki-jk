# Muscle

**Muscle** converts ATP into force via actin-[[Myosin]] crossbridges: skeletal muscle for locomotion, [[Heart|cardiac]] for perfusion, smooth muscle for vascular tone. As the largest glucose-disposal and [[Creatine]]-storage tissue, muscle dominates whole-body [[Metabolism]] and defines hormetic exercise dosing.
