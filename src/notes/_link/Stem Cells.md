# Stem Cells

[[Stem Cells]] are undifferentiated cells capable of self-renewal and lineage-specific differentiation; they maintain tissue homeostasis and regeneration. Their function declines with age, contributing to loss of regenerative capacity.

## mTORC1, Rapamycin, and Stem-Cell Homeostasis

Excessive [[mTORC1]] activation impairs proliferation and differentiation of stem cells, promoting their aging and apoptosis. [[Rapamycin]] restores functionality by activating [[Autophagy]], reducing [[Inflammation]] and oxidative stress, and inducing growth factors ([[IGF-1]], [[VEGF]]). In models it improves myogenic/chondrogenic differentiation, mesenchymal-cell function in [[Klotho]]-deficient mice, and survival of transplanted stem cells after myocardial infarction ([Zerdka et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12766144/)).

> [!info] Regenerative axis
> Rapamycin preserves stem-cell pools mainly through autophagy plus anti-inflammatory and growth-factor signals—a basis for tissue-regeneration therapies.
