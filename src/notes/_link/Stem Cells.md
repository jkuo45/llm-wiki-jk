# Stem Cells

[[Stem Cells]] are undifferentiated cells capable of self-renewal and lineage-specific differentiation; they maintain tissue homeostasis and regeneration. Their function declines with age, contributing to loss of regenerative capacity.

## mTORC1, Rapamycin, and Stem-Cell Homeostasis

Excessive [[mTORC1]] activation impairs proliferation and differentiation of stem cells, promoting their aging and apoptosis. [[Rapamycin]] restores functionality by activating [[Autophagy]], reducing [[Inflammation]] and oxidative stress, and inducing growth factors ([[IGF1]], [[VEGF]]). In models it improves myogenic/chondrogenic differentiation, mesenchymal-cell function in [[Klotho]]-deficient mice, and survival of transplanted stem cells after myocardial infarction ([Zerdka et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12766144/)).

> [!info] Regenerative axis
> Rapamycin preserves stem-cell pools mainly through autophagy plus anti-inflammatory and growth-factor signals—a basis for tissue-regeneration therapies.

## Mitochondrial Control of Stem Cell Fate

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Mitochondrial Drivers Stem Cell Aging Inflammaging Bautista 2026]]
> [[Mitochondria]] play a decisive role in stem cell fate determination by acting as metabolic rheostats that integrate bioenergetic, redox, and epigenetic cues. Their influence extends across the spectrum of stem cell states, from deep quiescence to active proliferation and eventual senescence.

### Metabolic Programming

- Quiescent stem cells typically rely on glycolysis; transition to activation involves a metabolic shift toward [[Oxidative Phosphorylation|OXPHOS]]
- Mitochondrial membrane potential (Δψm) can stratify chronologically old [[Hematopoietic Stem Cell|HSCs]] into functionally distinct states
- _In vivo_ pharmacological potentiation of Δψm in aged HSCs improves engraftment and reverses age-associated myeloid-biased output

### Asymmetric Inheritance

- Selective inheritance of aged mitochondria in [[Intestinal Stem Cell|intestinal stem cells]] gives rise to ISC^mito-O^ subpopulation with enhanced α-ketoglutarate production, promoting TET-dependent epigenetic remodeling
- In HSCs, asymmetric segregation of mitochondria predicts daughter cell differentiation and long-term function

### NAD+ and Stem Cell Rejuvenation

- [[NAD+]] depletion leads to senescence, loss of quiescence, and diminished regenerative capacity in aged [[Muscle Stem Cell|MuSCs]], neural stem cells, and melanocyte stem cells
- Restoring NAD+ with [[Nicotinamide Riboside|NR]] reactivated mitochondrial metabolism and delayed stem cell senescence

### Mitochondrial Quality Control

- [[Mitophagy]] failure in stem cells leads to loss of quiescence, reduced self-renewal, and premature functional exhaustion
- [[Mitochondrial Unfolded Protein Response|UPR^mt^]] activation serves as an adaptive checkpoint; chronic dysregulation converts to terminal senescence
- [[Mitochondria-Associated Membranes|MERCs]] support mitochondrial remodeling during fate transitions and asymmetric division
