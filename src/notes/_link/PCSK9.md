# PCSK9

SIRT6 deficiency accelerates atherosclerosis through dysregulation of PCSK9 and LDL metabolism, indicating SIRT6 normally constrains PCSK9-driven LDL accumulation and vascular disease (Szczęsny-Małysiak et al., 2026).
