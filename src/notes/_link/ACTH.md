# ACTH

**Adrenocorticotropic hormone ([[ACTH]], corticotropin)** is a peptide hormone of the anterior pituitary released under hypothalamic CRH stimulation. It binds the adrenal cortex to drive [[Cortisol]] synthesis and release, forming the effector arm of the HPA-axis stress response.

## Relation to Mitohormesis

ACTH–cortisol axis activation is the endocrine counterpart of the [[Sympathetic Nervous System|sympathetic]] response. Acute activation supports the fight-or-flight state, but chronic activation (stacked stressors, sleep loss) sustains cortisol, suppresses [[Heart Rate Variability|HRV]], and impairs [[Mitochondrial Biogenesis|mitochondrial biogenesis]] — turning hormesis into [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]].
