# Myosin

**Myosin** motors walk along [[Actin]] filaments: sarcomeric myosin II drives [[Muscle]] contraction in [[Cardiomyocytes]]; non-muscle myosin II powers cytokinesis, adhesion turnover, and [[Cell Migration]]. ATPase cycling couples ATP hydrolysis to the power stroke; myosin light-chain phosphorylation tunes contractility in smooth muscle and endothelium.
