# DAXX

[[DAXX]] (Death Domain Associated Protein) is the obligate histone-chaperone partner of [[ATRX]]. The ATRX–DAXX complex deposits [[H3.3]] at telomeres, pericentric heterochromatin, and endogenous retroviruses.

## Function

- H3.3-specific chaperone; targets ATRX remodeling to heterochromatic repeats.
- Loss phenocopies ATRX loss for ALT activation in some contexts.

## Sex Differences

- No direct sex-biased mutation signal; sex relevance is via [[ATRX]] (EXITS, female-brain escape) and [[Telomere]] ALT pathway (see [[EXITS]]).

## Connections

- [[ATRX]] — obligate remodeling partner
- [[H3.3]] — chaperoned histone variant
- [[Histone Variant]] — H3.3 deposition axis
- [[Telomere]] — telomeric chromatin maintenance; ALT on loss
- [[Telomere Attrition]] — ALT paradox
