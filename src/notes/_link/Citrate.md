# Citrate

**Citrate** is a tricarboxylic acid produced in the [[TCA cycle|TCA cycle]] by condensation of [[Acetyl-CoA]] with oxaloacetate (catalyzed by citrate synthase) and oxidized back to oxaloacetate over subsequent cycle turns. It is a central hub linking mitochondrial oxidative metabolism to biosynthetic and regulatory processes in the cytosol.

## Roles in Metabolism

- **TCA intermediate**: citrate is the entry point of acetyl-CoA into the cycle.
- **Lipid synthesis**: mitochondrial citrate exported to the cytosol is cleaved by [[ACLY]] into acetyl-CoA and oxaloacetate, supplying the acetyl-CoA for [[Lipid Synthesis|de novo lipogenesis]] and for [[Histone Acetylation|histone acetylation]].
- **Allosteric regulation**: cytosolic citrate inhibits phosphofructokinase-1, favoring glycolysis suppression, and activates acetyl-CoA carboxylase.
- **Iron chelation**: citrate chelates iron and other divalent cations, contributing to plasma anticoagulant and metal-ion buffering properties.

## Role in Senescence and the SASP

In senescent cells, the mitochondrial [[Pyruvate]]–citrate–acetyl-CoA axis is upregulated. The mitochondrial citrate carrier [[SLC25A1]] exports citrate to the cytosol, where [[ACLY]] regenerates the [[Acetyl-CoA]] pool used for [[H3K27ac|H3K27ac]] deposition at SASP loci. Genetic or pharmacological ([[CTPI2]], [[UK5099]]) disruption of this axis depletes cytosolic acetyl-CoA and selectively suppresses the [[SASP|senescence-associated secretory phenotype]] without affecting cell viability. Exogenous citrate supplementation is sufficient to drive histone acetylation and pro-inflammatory gene expression.

> [!important] Source: [[_document_ - Mitochondrial metabolism and epigenetic crosstalk drive SASP|Mitochondrial metabolism and epigenetic crosstalk drive SASP]]
> Citrate export, not just TCA oxidation, is the critical metabolic step: [[SLC25A1]] inhibition reduces citrate, isocitrate and cis-aconitate levels and selectively depletes the cytosolic acetyl-CoA pool. In vivo, SLC25A1 inhibition improves healthspan and reduces frailty in aged mice.
