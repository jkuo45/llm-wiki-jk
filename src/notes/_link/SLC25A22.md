# SLC25A22

**SLC25A22** (solute carrier family 25 member A22, also GC1) is a **mitochondrial inner-membrane transporter** that exchanges **glutamate** across the mitochondrial inner membrane — importing glutamate into the matrix where it feeds [[Glutathione]] (GSH) synthesis and amino-acid metabolism. By sustaining mitochondrial glutamate flux, SLC25A22 supports glutathione production and monounsaturated fatty-acid (MUFA) synthesis, making it an endogenous suppressor of [[Ferroptosis]].

## Function & Anti-Ferroptotic Role

- **Transport:** exchanges cytosolic glutamate for matrix aspartate, delivering glutamate for mitochondrial [[Glutathione]] synthesis and the malate–aspartate shuttle.
- **GSH supply:** mitochondrial glutamate is a limiting precursor for glutathione biosynthesis — a core reducing buffer against [[Lipid Peroxidation]].
- **MUFA bias via AMPK:** SLC25A22-driven glutamate flux activates [[AMPK]], which upregulates stearoyl-CoA desaturase and shifts lipids toward monounsaturated fatty acids (MUFAs); MUFAs replace oxidation-prone PUFAs in membranes, depriving ferroptosis of its lipid targets.
- **Anti-ferroptotic (PDAC):** in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma models, SLC25A22 blocks lipid peroxidation and cell death through iron-independent mechanisms.

## Regulation by SIRT3: Deacetylation–Ubiquitination Crosstalk

> [!info] Source: Wei et al., *Antioxidants* 2025;14(4):403 (doi:10.3390/antiox14040403); reviewed in *Front. Organ Systems* 2026 (SIRT3 at the crossroads of ferroptosis)
> **SIRT3 binds and deacetylates SLC25A22 at lysine K83.** Deacetylation suppresses the transporter's ubiquitination, preventing its 26S-proteasomal degradation and extending its half-life. In [[Lung Cancer|lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD)]], SIRT3 and SLC25A22 are positively correlated and both elevated in tumor vs. adjacent tissue; both rise further during ferroptosis induction, reflecting an adaptive survival response.
> - Targeting SIRT3 increases SLC25A22 acetylation → increased ubiquitination → proteasomal degradation, sensitizing LUAD cells to [[RSL3]]-induced ferroptosis *in vivo* and *in vitro*.
> - This is a **non-enzymatic substrate** mode of SIRT3 action: deacetylation does not change transporter kinetics but governs protein turnover.

This establishes the **SIRT3–SLC25A22 axis** as a tumor-cell survival defense: SIRT3 protects LUAD (and glioblastoma, via SLC7A11 and mitophagy) from ferroptotic death by stabilizing the glutamate/GSH-supply transporter.

## Integrated Role with the SIRT3 Redox Network

SLC25A22 is one node in a broader SIRT3 ferroptosis-defense network:

- **Enzymatic arm:** SIRT3 deacetylates/activates [[IDH2]], [[MnSOD]] (MnSOD), [[MTHFD2]], PRDX3, and catalase to regenerate NADPH/GSH and scavenge ROS.
- **GPX4 support:** SIRT3's NADPH/GSH output sustains [[GPX4]] activity; SIRT3 stabilizes SLC25A22 to supply mitochondrial glutamate for GSH.
- **Iron control:** SIRT3 suppresses mitochondrial ROS to keep IRP1 in its aconitase form, restricting [[Transferrin receptor 1|TfR1]]-mediated iron import and the labile iron pool (LIP) that fuels [[Fenton Reaction|Fenton]] chemistry.
- **Contrast (context-dependence):** the same SIRT3 that protects LUAD from ferroptosis can *sensitize* glioblastoma to ferroptosis by promoting mitophagy — showing again that SIRT3's net redox/pro-survival effect is disease- and context-dependent.

## Clinical Relevance

- **Ferroptosis-sensitization strategy:** inhibiting the SIRT3–SLC25A22 axis (via SIRT3 knockdown/inhibition) primes LUAD and other metabolically constrained tumors for ferroptosis-inducing therapy.
- **Correlation/biomarker:** elevated SIRT3/SLC25A22 correlates with poor LUAD prognosis; the axis is a candidate biomarker for stratifying tumors by ferroptosis sensitivity.
- **Therapeutic window:** because SIRT3 is dispensable in many normal cells but exploited by OXPHOS/ferroptosis-prone tumors, the SIRT3-SLC25A22 axis offers a tumor-selective vulnerability.
