# SMURF2

**SMURF2** (SMAD Ubiquitin Regulatory Factor 2) is a HECT-domain E3 ubiquitin ligase best known as a negative regulator of [[TGF-β]]/BMP signaling — it ubiquitinates SMADs and their adaptor Smad7-bound complexes, shaping the strength and duration of the pathway. Its relevance to the sirtuin network comes from a second substrate: **[[SIRT1]]**.

## SIRT1 ubiquitination (cancer context)

Yu et al. (Oncogene 2020) showed SMURF2 ubiquitinates [[SIRT1]] and promotes its proteasomal degradation, suppressing proliferation and tumorigenesis in colorectal cancer — a tumor-suppressive E3–substrate pair. This was one of the two SIRT1 E3 ligases reported before DNA-damage-conditioned studies (see below); the other is **MDM2** (p53 pathway context).

> [!info]
> Three SIRT1 E3 ligases now partition by context: **SMURF2** (colorectal cancer, tumor-suppressive), **MDM2** (p53-pathway crosstalk), and **[[KAP1|TRIM28]]** (severe DNA damage, ATM-dependent — the axis that cooperates with caspase cleavage of SIRT1).

## Connections
