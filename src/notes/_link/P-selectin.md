# P-selectin

P-selectin (CD62P) is an adhesion molecule exposed on activated [[Platelets]] and endothelial cells. In whole blood, neutrophil exposure to bacterial [[Lipopolysaccharide|LPS]] triggers [[Vital NETosis]] that requires [[TLR4]] expression on platelets and platelet-neutrophil interaction via P-selectin (Pieterse et al. 2016).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> P-selectin-mediated platelet-neutrophil contact is a receptor-level trigger of vital NETosis, contrasting with the ROS/NE/GSDMD-driven [[Suicidal NETosis]] axis (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
