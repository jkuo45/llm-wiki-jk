# Enterocloster

**Enterocloster** is a genus of anaerobic gut bacteria. Select species contribute to the multi-step conversion of dietary [[Ellagic acid]] toward [[Urolithin A]], alongside the principal converters [[Gordonibacter urolithinfaciens]] and [[Ellagibacter isourolithinifaciens]].

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Natural Bioactive Compounds Spermidine Fisetin Berberine Urolithin A]]
> Urolithin A biosynthesis is carried out principally by Gordonibacter urolithinfaciens and Ellagibacter isourolithinifaciens, with contributions from select Enterocloster species; converter status defines metabotypes A, B, and 0.
