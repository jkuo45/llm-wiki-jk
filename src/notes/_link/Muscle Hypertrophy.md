# Muscle Hypertrophy

## Overview

**Muscle hypertrophy** is the expansion of [[Skeletal Muscle|skeletal muscle]] size via increased myofibrillar protein accretion, principally triggered by resistance exercise and anabolic stimuli. It is the physiological counterpoint to [[Muscle Atrophy]].

## Mechanism of Action & Physiology

Hypertrophy requires a positive net protein balance: mechanical tension and metabolic stress activate [[mTORC1]] (via [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]] and [[Leucine|leucine]] sensing), boosting translation, while suppressing degradation through the [[Ubiquitin-Proteasome System]] and [[FOXO]]–[[Atrogin-1]]/[[MuRF1]] axis. Satellite cell proliferation adds myonuclei to support growth.

## Applications

Resistance training, adequate protein/[[Leucine]], and [[Creatine]] supplementation are first-line. [[Urolithin A]] may augment hypertrophy by enhancing [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] function, ATP supply, and recovery, supporting longer, more intense training.
