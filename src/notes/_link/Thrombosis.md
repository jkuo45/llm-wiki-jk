# Thrombosis

Thrombosis is the formation of a blood clot within a vessel. [[Vital NETosis]] is often observed during thrombosis and autoimmune disease, and NETs carrying [[Histone|histones]], [[Neutrophil Elastase]] and [[Myeloperoxidase]] are implicated in immunothrombosis (Fuchs et al. 2010; Martinod et al. 2016). [[Platelets]] activated via [[TLR4]] and [[P-selectin]] trigger vital NETosis in whole blood (Pieterse et al. 2016).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> NETs are a proposed target in NET-associated disease, with [[GSDMD inhibitor|GSDMD inhibitors]] suggested as candidate therapeutics; whether distinct NETosis pathways differ in immunomodulatory properties remains untested (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
