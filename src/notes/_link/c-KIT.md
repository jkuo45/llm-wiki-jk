# c-KIT

[[c-KIT]] (KIT, CD117) is a receptor tyrosine kinase activated by stem cell factor; gain-of-function mutations drive gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GIST) and other malignancies. It is co-inhibited by multi-targeted TKIs such as [[Pazopanib]] and [[Imatinib]].

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> Pazopanib is described as a VEGFR/PDGFR/c-KIT–targeted agent in fenbendazole case-report contexts (54-57).
