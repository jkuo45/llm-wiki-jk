# Glaucoma

**Glaucoma** is a group of optic neuropathies defined by progressive degeneration of retinal ganglion cell (RGC) axons and their targets in the lateral geniculate nucleus, leading to irreversible vision loss.

## Mechanism

Retinal ganglion cell degeneration in glaucoma involves [[SARM1]]-dependent [[NAD+]] depletion and [[Axon Degeneration]] of the optic nerve. The shared NAD⁺ consumer network ([[CD38]], [[PARP1]]) modulates retinal metabolic stress.

## Therapeutic

SARM1 inhibitors ([[Disulfiram]], [[DSRM-3716]], [[GSK-428]], [[NB-4746]]) are hypothesized to protect RGC axons, though clinical validation in glaucoma is pending.
