# PREMOTE

The **PREMOTE** trial is a multi-center randomized controlled trial in approximately 400 newly diagnosed [[Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus]] patients evaluating [[Berberine]] (and probiotics) effects on glycemia and the gut microbiome.

## Study Design

- **Participants**: ~400 newly diagnosed [[Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus]] patients, multi-center.
- **Intervention**: [[Berberine]] treatment with gut microbiota and microbial metabolite profiling.
- **Outcome**: HbA1c reduction of approximately 1% at 12 weeks with significant, measurable microbiota composition and metabolite changes.

## Significance

First human trial directly linking [[Berberine]]'s clinical metabolic improvement to microbiome-mediated mechanisms — the keystone evidence for [[Berberine]]'s high-intraluminal-exposure model (enriching [[Akkermansia muciniphila]], [[Bifidobacterium]], SCFA-producing [[Lactobacillus]]) operating independently of systemic absorption.
