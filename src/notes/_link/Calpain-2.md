# Calpain-2

**Calpain-2** (m-calpain; gene *CAPN2* + regulatory *CAPNS1*) is a millimolar-Ca²⁺-dependent cytosolic
cysteine protease. In the rheostat context it is the motility effector of the pro-survival
[[Caspase-8]] state: tyrosine-phosphorylated [[Caspase-8]] (pY-Casp8) scaffolds [[FAK]]–Calpain-2–PI3K
complexes that turn over focal adhesions and drive invasion — the same phosphorylation that switches
[[Caspase-8]] from killer to scaffold.

## Structure & Regulation

Heterodimer of an 80 kDa catalytic subunit (protease core PC1/PC2 + Ca²⁺-binding penta-EF-hand
domains) and a 28 kDa regulatory subunit; activated by millimolar Ca²⁺ transients (often at
adhesion sites) and restrained by endogenous calpastatin.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **pY-Casp8 scaffold effector.** Src-family-phosphorylated [[Caspase-8]] recruits [[FAK]] and
  Calpain-2; localized Calpain-2 cleaves talin, vinculin and FAK itself, accelerating focal-adhesion
  disassembly/reassembly cycles that power mesenchymal migration.
- **Survival crosstalk.** Adhesion turnover feeds PI3K/AKT and ERK signaling, reinforcing the
  anti-apoptotic state that preserves the scaffold — a feed-forward migration–survival loop in
  colon cancer and glioblastoma models.
- **Apoptotic counter-role.** Under Ca²⁺ overload, Calpain-2 can instead cleave [[BAX]]/Bid-family
  members and Atg5, tipping toward death — context (adhesion-localized vs cytosolic flood) decides.

## Physiological Function

Wound-healing migration, myoblast fusion, and activity-dependent synaptic remodeling all use
Calpain-2 adhesion turnover; knockout is embryonic lethal (placental failure).

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Metastasis:** CAPN2 overexpression tracks invasion depth and poor prognosis in colorectal,
  glioblastoma and breast cancer — downstream of the pY-[[Caspase-8]] switch, so Src inhibitors
  (restoring caspase-8 killer function) simultaneously collapse Calpain-2 motility signaling.
- **Therapy angle:** calpain inhibitors (calpeptin, BDA-410) are preclinical anti-invasive agents;
  specificity over Calpain-1 remains the hurdle.
