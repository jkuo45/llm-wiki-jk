# Flavones

[[Flavonoids]] are divided into subclasses; **flavones** are characterized by a 2-phenylchromen-4-one backbone *without* a 3-hydroxyl group (unlike flavonols). Major dietary flavones include [[Apigenin]] (parsley, celery, chamomile), [[Luteolin]], and [[Chrysin]]. They share the generic C6-C3-C6 polyphenolic structure and are especially abundant in parsley and celery.

## Biological Activities

Flavones exert antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anticancer effects. [[Apigenin]] inhibits breast cancer proliferation through the [[Akt]]/[[FOXO3a]] pathway and induces proteasomal degradation of [[HER2|HER2/neu]]; [[Chrysin]] synergizes with apigenin to induce apoptosis and inhibit migration in breast and liver cancers. Flavones generally act as weaker direct antioxidants than flavonols (lack of 3-OH) but retain strong kinase-modulating and epigenetic activities.
