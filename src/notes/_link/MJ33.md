# MJ33

**MJ33** is a selective inhibitor of the **iPLA2** (acidic calcium-independent [[Phospholipase A2|phospholipase A2]]) activity of [[PRDX6|peroxiredoxin 6 (PRDX6)]], the direct molecular target of the senomorphic [[Apigenin]].

## Senomorphic Activity

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - repurposing_apigen_senomorphic.09.09.611999v1.full|Repurposing apigenin for senomorphic effect in antiaging pipelines]]
> MJ33 concentration-dependently lowers expression of canonical [[SASP|SASP]] factors in senescent cells and, at the proteome level, significantly inhibits the PI3K/Akt signaling pathway — substantiating that suppressing PRDX6's iPLA2 activity is sufficient for a senomorphic effect. MJ33 thus validates apigenin's mechanism (PRDX6 iPLA2 inhibition → SASP suppression).
