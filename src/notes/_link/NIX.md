# NIX

**NIX** (also known as [[BNIP3L]] or NIP3L) is a Bcl-2 homology 3 (BH3)-only protein and outer mitochondrial membrane (OMM) receptor that mediates mitophagy. It is a homologue of [[BNIP3]] and shares the ability to interact directly with [[LC3]] through its LIR (LC3-interacting region) motif, targeting damaged mitochondria for autophagic degradation.

## Structure & Function

NIX is an OMM-anchored protein containing a BH3 domain and a transmembrane domain. It functions as a mitophagy receptor by directly binding LC3/GABARAP family proteins, bypassing the [[PINK1]]–[[Parkin]] pathway. NIX-mediated mitophagy is particularly important during hypoxia and reticulocyte maturation, where it facilitates mitochondrial clearance.

## Role in Mitophagy

NIX and [[BNIP3]] mediate Parkin-independent mitophagy through hypoxia and the stabilization of [[HIF-1α]]. Under hypoxic conditions, HIF-1α transcriptionally upregulates NIX expression, promoting mitochondrial clearance. NIX also interacts with [[FUNDC1]] and other OMM receptors to coordinate mitophagy.
