# CMS121

**CMS121** is a [[Fatty Acid Synthase|fatty-acid-synthase (FASN)]] inhibitor developed by Pamela Maher's lab, alongside its sister compound [[J147]]. It protects against excess [[Lipid Peroxidation|lipid peroxidation]] and inflammation and alleviates cognitive loss in transgenic mouse models of [[Alzheimer's Disease]], and has reached **Phase 1 clinical trials for AD**.

## Relevance

CMS121 was identified through the same phenotypic, [[Ferroptosis|ferroptosis]]-based screening platform that yielded [[J147]]. By inhibiting FASN it reduces pathological lipid accumulation and oxidative stress in neurons.
