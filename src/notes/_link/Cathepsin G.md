# Cathepsin G

Cathepsin G is one of the three neutrophil serine proteases, with [[Neutrophil Elastase]] and [[Proteinase 3]], stored in [[Azurophil Granule|azurophil granules]] and delivered to the [[Phagosome]] during [[Phagocytosis]]. It is a constituent of [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps]] and part of the [[Azurosome]] complex in resting cells.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> The granule proteases NE, CatG and PR-3 are reported to remain enzymatically active on NETs even when exposed to endogenous protease inhibitors, although activity-probe studies report inactive NET-bound serine proteases in some models (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
