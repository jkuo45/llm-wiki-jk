# Um34

**Overview:** **Um34** is a 2′-*O*-methylated uridine at nucleotide 34 of tRNA[Ser]Sec. It distinguishes the two tRNA[Ser]Sec isodecoders and is essential for high-fidelity **[[Selenocysteine]]** insertion.

## Mechanism

- **[[FTSJ1]]** is the Um34 methyltransferase. Loss of Um34 causes ribosomal stalling and reduced **[[UGA codon]]** recoding, selectively impairing antioxidant **[[Selenoprotein|selenoproteins]]**.

> [!important]
> Source: [[Selenocysteine tRNA methylation promotes oxidative stress resistance in melanoma metastasis]]
> Um34 is necessary and sufficient for [[Melanoma]] metastatic colonization in vivo; FTSJ1/Um34 is positioned as a metastasis-selective therapeutic target. Historically ALKBH8 was also implicated in this modification in some contexts.

## Significance

Um34 is a post-transcriptional control point that tunes the selenoproteome's antioxidant capacity under oxidative stress.
