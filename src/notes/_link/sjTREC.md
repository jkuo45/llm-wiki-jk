# sjTREC

**Single joint T cell receptor excision circle (sjTREC)** is a circular DNA molecule generated during [[T Cell Receptor|TCR]] α-chain gene rearrangement in the [[Thymus|thymus]]. It serves as a molecular marker for recent thymic emigrants and active [[Thymopoiesis|thymopoiesis]].

## Biology

### Formation
- Generated during TCRα gene rearrangement when the TCRδ locus is excised
- The excised DNA forms a stable episomal circle (signal joint TREC)
- Does not replicate with cell division → diluted with each subsequent division
- Found exclusively in T cells that developed in the thymus

### Properties
- Stable and not degraded during normal T cell lifespan
- Does not replicate during mitosis → diluted with peripheral proliferation
- Quantitative marker: sjTREC per million peripheral blood mononuclear cells reflects thymic output

## Measurement

- **Quantitative PCR** — Standard method for sjTREC quantification in peripheral blood
- **Units:** sjTREC per microgram of thymic tissue or per million PBMCs
- **Normalization:** Often normalized to CD3⁺ T cell count

## Age-Related Decline

sjTREC levels decline dramatically with age, reflecting [[Thymic Involution|thymic involution]]:

- **Humans:** Steady three-log decline in circulating sjTREC across an 80-year lifespan
- **Mice:** Significant decrease in sjTREC per milligram of thymus tissue with increasing age (Sempowski et al., 2002)
- **Correlation:** Low sjTREC levels correlate with reduced naïve T cell output and restricted TCR repertoire

> [!info] Clinical utility
> sjTREC quantification is used clinically to assess thymic function in patients undergoing immune reconstitution after [[Chemotherapy|chemotherapy]], [[HIV|HIV-1]] infection, or [[Hematopoietic Stem Cell|hematopoietic stem cell]] transplantation.

## Applications in Rejuvenation Studies

sjTREC measurement has been used to evaluate the efficacy of thymic rejuvenation strategies:

- [[Ghrelin]] — Administration to aged mice increased recent thymic emigrants (measured by sjTREC)
- [[Human Growth Hormone|Growth hormone]] — Clinical trials use sjTREC as an endpoint for thymic reconstitution
- [[Sex Steroid Ablation]] — sjTREC levels increase after castration, indicating restored thymic output
