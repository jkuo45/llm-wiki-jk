# Q-VD-OPh

**Q-VD-OPh** is a cell-permeant, irreversible pan-caspase inhibitor with lower toxicity than z-VAD-FMK, widely used to test caspase dependence in vivo. In murine ischemia models it protects females only — consistent with the XX preference for caspase-dependent [[Apoptosis]] over [[Parthanatos]] — and rescues PARP-1-deficient female cells shunted into cytochrome-c/caspase death.
