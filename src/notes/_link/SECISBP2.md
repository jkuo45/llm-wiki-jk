# SECISBP2

**Overview:** **[[SECISBP2]]** (SECIS-binding protein 2, **SBP2**) is an RNA-binding protein and an essential component of the [[Selenocysteine]] insertion machinery. It specifically recognizes the **SECIS element** — a conserved stem-loop in the 3′UTR of eukaryotic selenoprotein mRNAs — and nucleates the complex that recodes the [[UGA codon]] as Sec.

## Mechanism

- Upon ribosome encounter of an in-frame UGA, [[SECISBP2]] binds the SECIS element and contacts the 40S ribosomal protein RPS27A/eS31, helping prevent premature termination.
- It recruits the Sec-specific elongation factor **eEFSec** (EEFSEC) together with tRNA[Ser]Sec and GTP, delivering Sec to the ribosomal A site.
- SECISBP2 also contacts eL30, which tethers the SECIS ribonucleoprotein complex to the large subunit and frees SECISBP2 for another round of insertion.
- Differential binding affinity of SECISBP2 across SECIS elements contributes to the **tissue-specific hierarchy** of selenoprotein expression.

> [!info]
> Source: [[Selenocysteine tRNA methylation promotes oxidative stress resistance in melanoma metastasis]]
> Efficient UGA recoding of a subset of selenoproteins additionally requires the Um34 modification of tRNA[Ser]Sec installed by [[FTSJ1]], acting downstream of the SECISBP2/eEFSec recruitment step.

## Clinical relevance

- **SECISBP2 loss-of-function** mutations impair selenoprotein synthesis (reduced GPx, TrxR, deiodinase activity) and cause abnormal thyroid hormone metabolism and multisystem disease, demonstrating that the insertion machinery — not just [[Selenium]] supply — gates the selenoproteome.
