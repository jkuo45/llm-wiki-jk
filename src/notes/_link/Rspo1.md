# Rspo1

**Rspo1 (R-spondin 1)** is a secreted protein that potentiates canonical [[Wnt signaling]] by stabilizing the LGR5 receptor complex, thereby maintaining intestinal epithelial stem-cell function and tissue regeneration.

## Sirtuin Regulation

- **[[SIRT6]]** preserves Rspo1 expression to confer intestinal epithelium injury resistance in inflammatory bowel disease; SIRT6 deletion promotes inflammation via NF-κB.

## Physiological Function

Rspo1 sustains the crypt stem-cell niche and intestinal barrier integrity, enabling rapid epithelial repair after injury.

## Clinical Relevance

Maintaining Rspo1 via SIRT6 is a candidate strategy to protect the intestinal epithelium from inflammatory damage in IBD.
