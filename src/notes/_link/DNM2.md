### Overview
DNM2 (Dynamin 2, gene symbol DNM2 at human chr 19p13.2, also DYN2) is a dynamin-like GTPase related to [[DRP1]]. It is recruited to the DRP1-constricted mitochondrion to assemble and complete the final membrane scission event of [[Mitochondrial Fission|fission]].

### Role in Fission
While DRP1 initiates constriction at the [[Outer Mitochondrial Membrane]], DNM2 contributes to the maturation and severing of the division furrow. However, studies suggest that DRP1 alone can constrict and sever mitochondria, rendering DNM2 dispensable for fission under many conditions.

### Functional Significance
DNM2 illustrates the redundancy and layered control of mitochondrial division, complementing DRP1 and its adaptors [[MFF]], [[MID49]], [[MID51]], and [[FIS1]] in maintaining [[Mitochondrial Integrity]].
