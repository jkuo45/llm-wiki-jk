# BubR1

**BubR1** (encoded by *BUB1B*) is a conserved serine/threonine kinase that functions as a core component of the **spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC)**, ensuring accurate chromosome segregation during mitosis. Reduced BubR1 levels cause aneuploidy and a premature-aging (progeroid) phenotype in mice.

## SIRT2 and the BubR1 Progeria Model

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Sirtuins and their Biological Relevance in Aging and Age-Related Diseases]]
> Zhao et al. (2020) note that *SIRT2* has been found to be a key modulator of ageing, and it extends lifespan in the *BubR1* mice model — a hypomorphic BubR1 progeroid strain characterized by shortened lifespan, atrophy, and age-related pathology.

This places [[SIRT2]] among the sirtuins with demonstrated *in vivo* pro-longevity effects in a mammalian progeria model, complementing the better-characterized roles of [[SIRT1]], [[SIRT6]], and [[SIRT3]] in aging.

## Biological Roles

- Spindle assembly checkpoint enforcement during mitosis
- Maintenance of chromosomal stability
- Regulation of aneuploidy and age-related cellular decline
