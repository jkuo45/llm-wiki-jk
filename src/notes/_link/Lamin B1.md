# Lamin B1

Lamin B1 (encoded by **LMNB1**) is a type B intermediate-filament protein of the nuclear lamina — the meshwork underlying the inner nuclear membrane. Its downregulation is one of the most conserved, cell-autonomous markers of [[Senescence]], occurring across replicative, oncogene-induced, and therapy-induced senescence in both human and mouse cells (Freund et al., 2012, PMID 22300562; Shah et al., 2013).

## Mechanism of Action

- **Structural lamina component**: Lamin B1 polymerizes with [[Lamin A]] to form the scaffold anchoring nuclear pores and peripheral chromatin (lamina-associated domains, LADs).
- **Transcriptional downregulation**: Senescence triggers LMNB1 repression (partly via [[p53]]/[[RB]] and Polycomb-mediated silencing), without commensurate loss of Lamin A.
- **Nuclear envelope remodeling**: Loss of Lamin B1 distorts the lamina, causing nuclear envelope blebbing, loss of peripheral heterochromatin anchoring, and altered nuclear shape.
- **Chromatin reorganization**: Detachment of LADs from the periphery permits a global "shuffling" of the genome, facilitating both repression ([[Senescence-Associated Heterochromatin Foci|SAHF]]) and the "leaky" transcription seen in senescence.

## Role in Senescence

- **Hallmark marker**: Lamin B1 loss is among the most reliable single-protein senescence biomarkers; often quantified by absent/punctate LMNB1 immunostaining.
- **Nuclear morphology**: Contributes to the enlarged, irregular, often multinucleated nuclei of senescent cells.
- **Sa-β-gal proxy & SAHF link**: Co-occurs with [[Senescence-Associated Heterochromatin Foci|SAHF]] formation and loss of peripheral heterochromatin; Lamin B1 loss is upstream of large-scale chromatin rearrangements.
- **cGAS-STING nexus**: Envelope defects may permit cytosolic DNA accumulation, feeding the [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS-STING]]–SASP axis; olive phenols that preserve Lamin B1 blunt this SASP.

## Detection and Use as a Biomarker

LMNB1 negativity combined with other markers ([[p16 INK4a|p16]], [[p21 CIP1|p21]], SA-β-gal, [[γ-H2AX]]) is widely used to enumerate senescent cells in tissue sections and single-cell assays. Because loss is cell-autonomous, it is valuable for *in vivo* senescent-cell burden quantification.

## Clinical / Pathological Relevance

- **Aging**: Lamin B1 levels decline in multiple aged tissues, correlating with senescent burden and [[Inflammaging]].
- **Laminopathies**: *LMNB1* duplication causes autosomal-dominant leukodystrophy; while distinct from senescence, it highlights the importance of lamin dosage for nuclear integrity.
- **Cancer & fibrosis**: Lamin B1 loss marks therapy-induced senescence in tumours and contributes to the SASP in [[Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis]] and atherosclerotic plaques.
- **Therapeutic implication**: Preserving Lamin B1 (e.g., olive phenols) is an emerging [[Senomorphic Therapy|senomorphic]] strategy to limit cGAS-STING–driven SASP.
