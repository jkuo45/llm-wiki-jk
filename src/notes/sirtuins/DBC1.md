# DBC1

**DBC1** (**D**eleted in **B**ladder **C**ancer **1**, also known as **CCAR2**) is a tumor suppressor identified by its deletion in bladder cancer. It is a negative regulator of [[SIRT1]]: DBC1 binds the N-terminus of SIRT1 and inhibits its NAD+-dependent deacetylase activity (Kim et al., 2008; Zhao et al., 2008).

## Mechanism

- Binding to the SIRT1 N-terminus blocks deacetylation of substrates, restraining SIRT1-driven survival programs (including p53 deacetylation).
- The tumor suppressors DBC1 and [[HIC1]] act on opposite levels: DBC1 inhibits SIRT1 enzyme activity, while [[HIC1]] represses SIRT1 gene transcription.
- Because DBC1 and [[HIC1]] are silenced in certain cancers, their loss indirectly promotes tumorigenesis — at least in part — by leaving SIRT1 unrestrained.
