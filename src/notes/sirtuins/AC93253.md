# AC93253

[[AC93253]] is a small-molecule sirtuin inhibitor reported to target [[SIRT2]] at micromolar concentrations. It was identified among several structurally diverse sirtuin inhibitors (together with [[Tenovins|tenovins]], [[AGK2]], 1,4-dihydropyridines, and bisnaphthalimidopropyl derivatives) and is notable for its anticancer activity in leukemia models.

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Small-molecule SIRT2 inhibitor.
- **Target**: [[SIRT2]].
- **Potency**: Active at micromolar concentrations.

## Mechanism & Effects

SIRT2 inhibition with AC93253 produces antileukemia effects that are accompanied by [[Akt|Akt acetylation]], de-phosphorylation, and consequent inhibition. Because SIRT2 deacetylates Akt and other cytoplasmic regulators, its blockade shifts Akt toward an inactive state. This connects sirtuin inhibition to PI3K/Akt signaling suppression in cancer cells.

## Applications

- Research tool to dissect SIRT2-dependent effects in leukemia and other cancers.
- Illustrates the rationale for SIRT2 inhibition as an anticancer strategy, in contrast to the caution that SIRT2 inhibition may be counterproductive when combined with microtubule inhibitors (which relies on chronic mitotic arrest).
