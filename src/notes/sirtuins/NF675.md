# NF675

[[NF675]] is a small-molecule sirtuin inhibitor obtained through optimization of the [[Suramin|suramin]] scaffold. It is the most potent and selective compound in this series, showing a 20-fold selectivity ratio for [[SIRT1]] over [[SIRT2]] (compound **13** in Fig. **2** of the review).

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Suramin-derived sirtuin inhibitor.
- **Target/Selectivity**: [[SIRT1]]-selective, 20-fold over [[SIRT2]].
- **Potency**: Potent against SIRT1; drug-likeness is modest due to high molecular weight and anionic character.

## Mechanism & Effects

Suramin occupies the nicotinamide ribose pocket (B-pocket), the nicotinamide pocket (C-pocket), and part of the substrate-binding site of sirtuins (established by co-crystallization with human [[SIRT5]]). NF675 presumably shares this binding mode while improving SIRT1 selectivity. Its anionic, high-molecular-weight nature limits therapeutic application, though it remains a valuable selectivity probe.

## Applications

- Research tool as a SIRT1-selective inhibitor.
- Demonstrates scaffold-optimization of suramin toward greater isoform selectivity.
