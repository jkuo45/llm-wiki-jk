# Amurensin G

[[Amurensin G]] is a resveratrol tetramer (a stilbenoid oligomer) isolated from the wild grape *Vitis amurensis*. It is reported as a micromolar sirtuin inhibitor with anticancer activity, grouped with other natural-product sirtuin modulators in the review's SIRTUIN INHIBITORS section.

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Stilbenoid oligomer (resveratrol tetramer); natural product.
- **Potency**: Micromolar sirtuin inhibition.
- **Known Activity**: Anticancer effects in several cancer cell lines.

## Pharmacodynamics & Pharmacokinetics

- Inhibits sirtuin deacetylase activity at micromolar concentrations; the specific isoform selectivity profile is not fully established in the literature.
- Its anticancer activity has been attributed at least partly to sirtuin inhibition, though additional on- and off-target effects are plausible.

## Applications

- Research tool in sirtuin inhibition and natural-product anticancer studies.
