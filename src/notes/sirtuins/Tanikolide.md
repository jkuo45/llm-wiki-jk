# Tanikolide

[[Tanikolide]] is a natural product (a pentadecanolide-type lactone) reported among a series of naturally occurring compounds that inhibit sirtuins at micromolar concentrations (see the review's SIRTUIN INHIBITORS section, alongside [[Aristoforin]] and [[Amurensin G]]).

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Pentadecanolide lactone; natural product.
- **Potency**: Micromolar sirtuin inhibition.
- **Origin**: Marine or plant natural product chemistry.

## Pharmacodynamics & Pharmacokinetics

- Inhibits sirtuin deacetylase activity at micromolar concentrations.
- Structural selectivity/mechanism is less characterized than for synthetic sirtuin inhibitors.

## Applications

- Research tool in natural-product sirtuin inhibition studies.
