# Butein

[[Butein]] is a chalcone polyphenol found in several medicinal plants (e.g., *Rhus verniciflua*, dahlia, and Butea). It belongs to the family of natural polyphenolic sirtuin-activating compounds ([[STACs|STACs]]) that were the first small molecules reported to activate [[SIRT1]] (compound **4** in Fig. **3** of the review), together with [[Resveratrol|resveratrol]], [[Piceatannol|piceatannol]], [[Quercetin|quercetin]], and [[Myricetin|myricetin]].

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Chalcone polyphenol.
- **Bioactivities**: Antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anticancer properties have been reported.
- **Sirtuin Activity**: Reported activator of [[SIRT1]].

## Pharmacodynamics & Pharmacokinetics

- As a polyphenolic STAC, butein's SIRT1 activation shares the same controversy as resveratrol: activation was often only demonstrable with fluorescently tagged substrates, and *in vivo* effects may be indirect (e.g., via the [[cAMP]]-[[Epac1]]-[[AMPK]] pathway).
- Its pleiotropic antioxidant and kinase-modulating effects complicate attribution of biological outcomes to SIRT1 activation alone.

## Applications

- Research tool in sirtuin/aging studies and natural product pharmacology.
- Broad polyphenol health benefits are under investigation.
