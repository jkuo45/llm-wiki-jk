# GW5074

[[GW5074]] is an indolinone compound originally developed as a potent selective inhibitor of c-Raf kinase. After screening a series of known [[Kinase|kinase]] inhibitors against [[SIRT2]] (based on the shared adenosine-binding site between sirtuins and protein kinases), it was shown to possess SIRT1/SIRT2 inhibitory properties in the low micromolar range (compound **10** in Fig. **2** of the review). Bisindolylmaleimides and indolinones thus emerged as two distinct sirtuin-relevant scaffolds from this screen.

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Indolinone; ATP-competitive kinase inhibitor repurposed against sirtuins.
- **Original Target**: c-Raf kinase.
- **Sirtuin Targets**: [[SIRT1]] and [[SIRT2]], low micromolar.

## Mechanism & Effects

Shares an adenosine binding site between protein kinases and the sirtuin NAD⁺ pocket, allowing scaffold cross-engagement. As with [[Ro31-8220]], interpretation of GW5074 effects in cells is complicated by its primary kinase-inhibitory activity.

## Applications

- Pharmacological tool to probe sirtuin inhibition; caution warranted due to off-target Raf/kinase activity.
- Contributes to the dual kinase/sirtuin inhibitor scaffold rationale.
