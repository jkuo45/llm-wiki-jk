# 2-APQC

## Overview

2-Acetylphenolquinone congener (2-APQC) is a small-molecule pharmacological agonist of [[SIRT3]] identified by Fu *et al.* as a novel SIRT3 activator. In integrated preclinical models spanning cellular and organismal levels, 2-APQC significantly alleviated isoproterenol-induced pathological [[Cardiac Hypertrophy|cardiac hypertrophy]] and fibrotic progression, whereas its efficacy was completely lost in SIRT3-knockout models — establishing causal dependence on SIRT3.

## Mechanism of Action

- **Target validation:** SIRT3 inhibition via [[Nicotinamide|NAM]] markedly attenuated the anti-hypertrophic effects of 2-APQC, confirming SIRT3 as its primary molecular target.
- **Signaling:** SIRT3 activation by 2-APQC works in tandem with suppression of the [[Akt|AKT]]/[[mTOR|mTOR]]/[[p70S6 kinase|p70S6K]] and [[JNK|JNK]]/[[TGFβ|TGF-β]]/[[SMAD3|Smad3]] pathways to attenuate pathological cardiac remodeling.
- **Effects:** Targets hypertrophic responses and maladaptive fibrotic deposition, preserving [[Mitochondria|mitochondrial]] homeostasis and cardiac function.

## Clinical Relevance

- Represents a prototype of a selective, target-validated SIRT3 agonist with translational potential for cardiac disease — highlighted alongside melatonin and pseudo-natural macrocyclic sulfonamides as emerging SIRT3-directed therapeutics.
