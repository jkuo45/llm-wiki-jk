# SRT2104

**SRT2104** (SRT-2104) is a highly potent, selective, and orally bioavailable synthetic small-molecule allosteric activator of **[[SIRT1]]**, commonly classified as a **Sirtuin Activating Compound (STAC)**. It has a significantly greater substrate-binding affinity for SIRT1 ($EC_{1.5} = 0.43\ \mu\text{M}$) compared to the natural polyphenol [[Resveratrol]].

---

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Synthetic allosteric sirtuin activating compound (STAC).
- **Target Selectivity**: Highly selective for [[SIRT1]] over [[SIRT2]] and [[SIRT3]].
- **Potency**: $EC_{1.5}$ of approximately $0.43\ \mu\text{M}$ for SIRT1 activation.

---

## Pharmacodynamics & Pharmacokinetics

### Mechanism of Action
SRT2104 binds to an allosteric site located in the N-terminal domain of [[SIRT1]], promoting a conformational change that stabilizes the binding of both the **[[NAD+]]** co-substrate and the acetylated peptide substrate. This lowers the Michaelis constant ($K_m$) for the acetylated substrate, thereby enhancing SIRT1's deacetylase rate.

### Physiological & Metabolic Effects
- **Cardiovascular Protection**: SIRT1 activation by SRT2104 in aging or diabetic models decreases cardiovascular risks. It mitigates pro-inflammatory and pro-apoptotic events in monocytes by reducing the acetylation levels of [[p53]] and the RelA/p65 subunit of [[NF-κB|NF-κB]].
- **Redox Regulation**: Enhances antioxidant defense signaling (e.g., upregulating expression of manganese superoxide dismutase and catalase) to counter oxidative damage under stressful states.
- **Lipid Homeostasis**: Clinical and pre-clinical studies demonstrate that SRT2104 administration improves the serum lipid profile, showing significant reductions in total cholesterol, low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C), and triglycerides.
- **Anti-Inflammatory Action**: SRT2104 attenuates lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced cytokine release (specifically IL-6 and IL-8) and suppresses acute-phase protein responses (such as C-reactive protein).

---

## Applications & Clinical Trials

SRT2104 has been evaluated in multiple Phase I and Phase II randomized controlled trials (RCTs):
- **Stable Plaque-Psoriasis**: Phase IIa studies showed substantial clinical and histological improvements in psoriatic lesions after 84 days of oral SRT2104 treatment.
- **Endotoxemia and Coagulation**: Shown to inhibit endotoxin-induced coagulation activation (reflected by lower plasma levels of prothrombin fragment $F_{1+2}$) and vascular endothelial activation.
- **Metabolic Disorders**: Evaluated in type 2 diabetes mellitus and healthy elderly volunteers, demonstrating safe metabolic profiling.
