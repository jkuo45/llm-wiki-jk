# SIRT1 BAC Mice

The **SIRT1 BAC mice** overexpress [[SIRT1]] from a bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC) construct, providing a [[SIRT1]] gain-of-function model.

## Phenotype

- SIRT1 BAC (gain-of-function) mice show increased energy efficiency and are protected against high-fat-diet-induced [[Diabetes|type 2 diabetes]] (Banks et al., 2008; Pfluger et al., 2008).
- Complementing the beta-cell-restricted BESTO model, this whole-body gain-of-function demonstrates that elevated SIRT1 dosage is sufficient to prevent diet-induced metabolic disease.
