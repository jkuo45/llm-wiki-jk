# K122

**K122** is the shorthand notation for **Lysine 122** of mitochondrial [[MnSOD]] (SOD2), an acetylation site deacetylated and activated by [[SIRT3]] (and opposed by [[SIRT4]] mono-ADP-ribosylation). It is the same residue described in full at [[Lys122]].

## Connections

  - [[Lys122]]: Canonical note for this residue.
  - [[MnSOD]]: The enzyme bearing K122.
  - [[SIRT3]]: The deacetylase that activates MnSOD at this site.
  - [[SIRT4]]: Opposing sirtuin that inhibits MnSOD at the same axis.
