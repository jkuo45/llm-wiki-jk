# GABARAPL1

[[GABARAPL1]] (GABA type A receptor-associated protein-like 1; also GEC1) is a member of the **ATG8 family** of ubiquitin-like proteins. Together with [[LC3]] and [[GABARAP]], it is conjugated to phosphatidylethanolamine on nascent phagophores, where it drives autophagosome elongation, cargo recruitment, and fusion with lysosomes.

## Structure & Domains

- **Ubiquitin-fold core** — shared with all ATG8 proteins, exposing the C-terminal glycine (Gly120) for lipidation.
- **N-terminal helix** — distinguishes GABARAPL1/GABARAP from LC3 isoforms and biases interaction with specific receptors and the [[Beclin-1]]–VPS34 complex.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **Lipidation (PE conjugation)**: Processed by the E1 [[Atg]]7, E2 Atg3, and E3 Atg12–Atg5–Atg16L1 complex, then anchored to phagophore membranes.
- **Selective autophagy**: GABARAPL1 binds autophagy receptors (e.g., p62/SQSTM1, NDP52, optineurin) to deliver ubiquitinated cargo for degradation, including damaged mitochondria in [[Mitophagy]].
- **Autophagosome maturation**: Cooperates with [[LC3]] and the class III PI3K [[VPS34]] complex anchored by [[Beclin-1]]; GABARAPL1 isoforms preferentially facilitate later fusion steps.
- **Sirtuin intersection**: As a core autophagy effector, GABARAPL1 operates downstream of the [[Sirtuins]]–[[AMPK]] axis that licenses autophagic flux during fasting and stress.

> [!info] Isoform specialization
> LC3 subfamily primarily marks early phagophores, whereas GABARAP/GABARAPL1 subfamily members are enriched at later stages (closure and lysosomal fusion), making GABARAPL1 a marker of maturing autophagosomes.

## Physiological Function

GABARAPL1 sustains basal and induced [[Autophagy]] for protein/organelle turnover, development, and cellular quality control, and is transcriptionally induced under starvation.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- Dysregulated ATG8 lipidation impairs clearance of aggregates and organelles, contributing to [[Aging]] and neurodegeneration.
- GABARAPL1 is investigated as a biomarker of autophagic flux and a node in tumor-suppressive versus pro-survival autophagy.
