## Overview

Pan-sirtuin activators are compounds or interventions that elevate the activity of multiple members of the [[Sirtuin]] family simultaneously. In contrast to direct sirtuin-activating compounds ([[STACs]]) that bind a single sirtuin allosterically, most physiological "pan" activation is indirect: it works by raising the intracellular concentration of [[NAD+]], the obligate nicotinamide-adenine-dinucleotide co-substrate required by all seven mammalian sirtuins ([[SIRT1]]–[[SIRT7]]).

## Mechanism / Structure

- The catalytic reaction of every sirtuin consumes one NAD+ per deacetylation event, releasing [[Nicotinamide]] as a product. Because NAD+ availability is rate-limiting, raising the NAD+/NAM ratio directly boosts sirtuin deacetylase flux.
- [[Nicotinamide Mononucleotide|NMN]] and [[Nicotinamide Riboside|NR]] are direct NAD+ precursors. Once converted intracellularly to NAD+, they serve as universal indirect pan-sirtuin activators (directionality: NMN/NR → NAD+ → sirtuin activation). They counteract the age-related NAD+ decline that attenuates sirtuin function.
- [[Caloric Restriction]] is the canonical endogenous pan-sirtuin activator: CR raises the NAD+/nicotinamide ratio, relieving nicotinamide feedback inhibition and boosting [[Sirtuin]] activity. In yeast, the CR lifespan benefit requires [[SIR2]] (the founding sirtuin), establishing the genetic template later extrapolated to mammalian [[SIRT1]].

> [!info] Direct vs. Indirect
> Direct STACs (e.g., certain small molecules in the [[Sirtuin Activators]] literature) bind a specific sirtuin and enhance catalysis allosterically — often with narrow isoform selectivity. Pan-sirtuin activators that work via NAD+ precursors or CR activate the whole family non-selectively, because they act on the shared co-substrate rather than on an individual enzyme.

## Role in Longevity & Metabolism

- Restoring NAD+ with NMN or NR reconstitutes sirtuin-dependent maintenance programs: mitochondrial biogenesis, oxidative-stress resistance, genomic stability, and metabolic homeostasis.
- The systemic, family-wide engagement distinguishes pan-activators from isoform-specific tool compounds; it is the dominant nutraceutical strategy (NMN, NR) to address age-related sirtuin attenuation.

> [!tip] Therapeutic distinction
> Because pan-sirtuin activation is co-substrate driven, it is bounded by the cell's capacity to synthesize and use NAD+ and is generally better tolerated than supra-physiological direct activation. It nonetheless lacks the isoform precision of selective agents.

## Clinical & Research Significance

Pan-sirtuin activation via NAD+ repletion is among the most translationally advanced longevity strategies, with multiple clinical trials of NMN and NR underway. Limitations include incomplete tissue-delivery data, the possibility that raising NAD+ also feeds competing consumers (e.g., CD38, PARPs), and uncertainty about whether systemic activation mirrors the circumscribed, stress-specific sirtuin induction seen with [[Caloric Restriction]].
