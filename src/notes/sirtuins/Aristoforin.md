# Aristoforin

[[Aristoforin]] is a natural product reported among a series of compounds with various structural cores that inhibit sirtuins at micromolar concentrations. In the cancer-review context it is grouped with [[Amurensin G]], polyphenols, and [[Tanikolide]] as a natural-product sirtuin inhibitor (see the review's SIRTUIN INHIBITORS section).

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Natural product sirtuin inhibitor.
- **Potency**: Micromolar sirtuin inhibition.
- **Origin**: Plant-derived secondary metabolite.

## Pharmacodynamics & Pharmacokinetics

- Inhibits sirtuin deacetylase activity at micromolar concentrations.
- As with many natural-product sirtuin modulators, target selectivity and mechanism are less characterized than for synthetic inhibitors; effects may extend to additional cellular targets.

## Applications

- Research tool in sirtuin inhibition studies and natural product drug discovery.
