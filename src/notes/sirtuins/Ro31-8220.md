# Ro31-8220

[[Ro31-8220]] is a bisindolylmaleimide compound classically used as a cell-permeable protein kinase C (PKC) inhibitor. It was identified as an interesting sirtuin-inhibitor scaffold after the rationale that sirtuins and protein [[Kinase|kinases]] share an adenosine-binding site prompted screening of known kinase inhibitors against [[SIRT2]]. Ro31-8220 showed SIRT1/SIRT2 inhibitory properties in the low micromolar range (compound **9** in Fig. **2** of the review).

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Bisindolylmaleimide; ATP/adenosine-site directed kinase inhibitor repurposed against sirtuins.
- **Original Target**: Protein kinase C (PKC).
- **Sirtuin Targets**: [[SIRT1]] and [[SIRT2]], low micromolar.

## Mechanism & Effects

The adenosine-binding pocket shared by protein kinases and the sirtuin NAD⁺ binding site allowed bisindolylmaleimide and indolinone scaffolds to engage sirtuins. This cross-inhibition means Ro31-8220 must be interpreted carefully in experiments because its pleiotropic kinase inhibition confounds attribution of effects to sirtuin blockade.

## Applications

- Pharmacological tool to probe sirtuin inhibition; caution warranted due to off-target kinase activity.
- Provides a rationale for dual kinase/sirtuin inhibitor design.
