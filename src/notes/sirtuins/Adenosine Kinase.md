# Adenosine Kinase

## Overview

Adenosine kinase (ADK) is an enzyme classically known for phosphorylating adenosine to AMP, a key step in the purine salvage pathway. A recently discovered second function repositions it within [[NAD+ Biosynthesis]]: ADK acts as an **NRH kinase**, phosphorylating the reduced nucleoside [[NRH]] (dihydronicotinamide riboside) to generate [[NMNH]]. This provides an [[NMRK1]]-independent route for the NRH-induced rise in cellular NAD+, complementing the canonical [[NAD+ Salvage Pathway]].

## Mechanism & Pathways

NRH is the reduced form of [[Nicotinamide Riboside]] (NR). It is more stable than NR, which degrades to [[Nicotinamide]] in the blood. In cultured cells NRH elevates NAD+ 5–10-fold above baseline, more potently and rapidly than NR itself, and it also raises NAD+ in mice. Endogenous NRH has been detected in the liver of mouse models, establishing NRH as a natural precursor of NAD+. The ADK-catalyzed conversion of NRH to NMNH feeds the salvage machinery, ultimately replenishing NAD+ through the combined action of NMNAT and downstream adenylation steps.

## Physiological Function

This pathway expands the recognized flexibility of NAD+ metabolism: rather than a single canonical route, multiple reduced and amidated precursors ([[NMN]], NR, [[Nicotinamide]], NRH, NMNH) can feed the NAD+ pool. Because NRH is orally bioavailable and comparatively stable, it is of interest for strategies aimed at countering the [[NAD+]] decline associated with [[Aging]] and age-related disease.

> [!info] Mechanism
> ADK-dependent NRH phosphorylation → NMNH → NAD+, a parallel salvage route that bypasses [[NAMPT]] and the NMRK-dependent activation of NR, explaining NRH's superior potency.

## Applications

Understanding ADK's dual role has implications for precursor design: inhibitors or tissue-specific expression of ADK may modulate how efficiently reduced NAD+ precursors are converted in different tissues, analogous to the variable-step logic described for [[Purine Nucleoside Phosphorylase]].

## Connections

  - [[NRH]]: Substrate for ADK; its phosphorylation to NMNH raises NAD+ more potently than NR.
  - [[NMNH]]: Product of ADK's NRH kinase reaction and a reduced NAD+ precursor.
  - [[NAD+]]: End-product of the ADK-dependent reduced-precursor route.
  - [[NAD+ Biosynthesis]]: ADK adds a non-canonical entry point to NAD+ biosynthesis.
  - [[Nicotinamide Riboside]]: Oxidized counterpart that degrades in blood and is less potent than NRH.
  - [[NMRK1]]: Independent kinase pathway that ADK's route bypasses.
