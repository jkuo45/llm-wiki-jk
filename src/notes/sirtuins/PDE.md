# PDE

[[PDE]] (phosphodiesterase) denotes the large superfamily of metallophosphohydrolases that degrade the second messengers **cyclic AMP ([[cAMP]])** and **cyclic GMP (cGMP)** to their inactive 5′-nucleoside monophosphates. By terminating cyclic-nucleotide signals, PDEs set the amplitude, duration, and subcellular localization of PKA/PKG activation and therefore influence metabolism, cognition, cardiovascular tone, and inflammation.

## Structure & Families

- Eleven families (PDE1–PDE11) with distinct cyclic-nucleotide selectivity, kinetics, and regulatory domains.
- **PDE3/PDE4** are prominent in cardiac, adipocyte, and immune cells; **PDE5** degrades cGMP in vasculature.
- Catalytic domains bind a conserved Zn²⁺/Mg²⁺ motif; regulatory domains confer cAMP/cGMP feedback and phosphorylation control.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- **cAMP/cGMP hydrolysis**: PDEs close the loop on [[cAMP]]→[[CREB]] and cGMP→PKG signaling that govern gene expression and bioenergetics.
- **Intersection with longevity pathways**: Cyclic-nucleotide tone modulates [[AMPK]] and [[Sirtuins]] outputs — e.g., cAMP/CREB and PGC-1α networks overlap with SIRT1-dependent mitochondrial programs, and PDE inhibition can shift cells toward oxidative metabolism.
- **Resveratrol context**: Some pleiotropic effects of [[Resveratrol]] and related [[Sirtuin Activators]] involve cyclic-nucleotide cross-talk, placing PDE among modulators of the sirtuin-responsive phenotype.

> [!info] Therapeutic class
> PDE inhibitors (e.g., PDE3 in heart failure, PDE4 in COPD, PDE5 in erectile dysfunction/pulmonary hypertension) are established drugs; their metabolic and neuroprotective actions are under active longevity investigation.

## Physiological Function

PDEs tune autonomic, metabolic, and inflammatory responses, and constrain excessive cyclic-nucleotide signaling that would otherwise drive hypertrophy, lipolysis, or excitotoxicity.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- **Metabolic/aging**: Dysregulated PDE activity contributes to insulin resistance and declined cAMP resilience with age.
- **Cardiovascular & CNS**: PDE modulation affects [[Aging]]-related vascular and cognitive phenotypes.
