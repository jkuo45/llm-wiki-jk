# JGB1741

[[JGB1741]] is a small-molecule sirtuin inhibitor developed on the [[Sirtinol|sirtinol]] structure and identified via sirtuin fluorimetric activity assays as a [[SIRT1]] inhibitor. It belongs to the growing class of sirtinol-derived modulators that also includes [[Salermide]] and [[Cambinol]].

## Chemical Properties & Classification

- **Class**: Sirtinol-derived SIRT1 inhibitor.
- **Target**: [[SIRT1]].
- **Identification**: Found through sirtuin fluorimetric activity (Fluor de Lys-style) screening of compounds.

## Mechanism & Effects

As a sirtinol analogue, JGB1741 is expected to inhibit SIRT1 deacetylase activity, leading to hyperacetylation of SIRT1 substrates such as [[p53]]. This generally promotes p53 stabilization and apoptosis in cancer cells, consistent with the reported anticancer activity of the sirtinol family of inhibitors.

## Applications

- Research tool to study SIRT1 inhibition and p53-dependent effects.
- Contributes to the structure-activity landscape of sirtinol analogues.
