# Eustress

[[Eustress]] is beneficial low-dose stress: 25 mM NaCl stimulates sunflower microgreen yield, nutrition, and antioxidants, while 75-100 mM causes distress. It is the plant-biology word for [[Hormesis]].

## Evidence (sunflower microgreens, 10-day hydroponics, 0-100 mM NaCl)

- Growth at 25 mM: shoot fresh +58.7%, dry +55.4%, shoot length +48.3%, root +25.7%, leaf area +81.4%, SLA +19.7%; 50 mM weaker stimulation; 75/100 mM suppression.
- Redox: proline +5.7% with unchanged catalase, lipid peroxidation, H2O2 at 25 mM; marked oxidative stress above.
- Nutrition: carbohydrates +27.2%, proteins +27.6%, Ca/Mg/Mn/Zn +11.9-14.3%, linoleic acid +5.0%.
- Antioxidants: total activity +38.1%, DPPH +6.0%, phenols +8.8%, ascorbate +37.5%, carotenoids +11.5%; reversed at high salt.
