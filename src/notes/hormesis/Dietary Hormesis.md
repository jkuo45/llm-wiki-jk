# Dietary Hormesis

[[Dietary Hormesis]] is the safest everyday form of [[Hormesis]]: cumulative adaptive responses to complex arrays of [[Hormetin|hormetins]] consumed within whole foods, distinct from single-compound effects tested in isolation.

> [!info]
> Food matrix (fiber, nutrients, co-constituents) modulates hormetin bioavailability and bioactivity, so whole-diet effects differ from summed isolate effects.

## Mechanisms

- [[Nrf2]]/ARE antioxidant defense: sulforaphane and quercetin modify Keap1 cysteines, freeing [[Nrf2]] to induce SOD, GPx, GST and 500+ cytoprotective genes.
- [[Mitohormesis]]: resveratrol activates sirtuins and PGC-1alpha, driving mitochondrial biogenesis.
- Epigenetic reprogramming: sulforaphane inhibits HDACs; EGCG inhibits DNMTs; caloric restriction alters histone/DNA marks.
- Anti-inflammatory: curcumin suppresses NF-kB (low bioavailability; piperine co-administration used); capsaicin activates TRP channels, improving thermogenesis and insulin sensitivity.
- Microbiota-host co-metabolism: flavonoids become phenolic acids; SCFAs like butyrate strengthen barrier via occludin; seaweed components shift Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes.

> [!warning]
> High-dose isolates flip to pro-oxidant or toxic: green-tea catechin liver injury, capsaicin GI distress, polyphenol nutrient-interference. Whole foods keep doses inside the [[Hormetic Window]].
