# Exercise Hormesis

[[Exercise Hormesis]]: transient exercise ROS, calcium, AMP/ATP, and lactate pulses activate NF-kB, SIRT1, PGC-1alpha, AMPK, and [[Nrf2]], producing mitochondrial biogenesis, antioxidant defense, autophagy/mitophagy, BDNF neuroplasticity, and gut-brain remodeling.

> [!important]
> Antioxidant paradox: excessive supplements suppress ROS signaling and blunt mitohormesis, biogenesis, and autophagy. Benefit depends on antioxidant type, dose, timing, modality, and disease stage.

## Pathways

- [[Nrf2]]-BDNF axis (conceptual network, not linear cascade): [[Nrf2]] preserves BDNF via redox/inflammation control; BDNF signals via PI3K/Akt and ERK/CREB; exercise raises proBDNF/BDNF/humanin in NDEVs (APOE4-dependent).
- Autophagy: mTOR suppression → ULK1 → Beclin-1 → LC3-I to LC3-II flux; clears Abeta/tau and damaged mitochondria.
- Mitochondrial plasticity: PGC-1alpha biogenesis; AMPK/SIRT1/FNDC5/BDNF plus MG53 repair axis; HIIT strongest for SOD/CAT and plaque reduction.
- Modalities differ: aerobic, HIIT, resistance, and mind-body each vary ROS magnitude; individualized prescription required.
