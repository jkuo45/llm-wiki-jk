# Trained Immunity

[[Trained Immunity]] (TRIM) is persistent innate memory: after returning to homeostasis, monocytes/macrophages mount enhanced (trained) or blunted (tolerant) responses to rechallenge. It is the immunological face of [[Hormesis]] with a dose-dependent dichotomy.

> [!info]
> Low/acute DAMP dose activates mTORC1/HIF-1alpha glycolysis, H3K4me3/H3K27ac priming, and heightened cytokines. High/chronic dose activates AMPK/SIRT1/autophagy, FAO, and repressive marks, producing tolerance.

## Mechanisms

- Epigenetics: promoter H3K4me3 plus enhancer H3K4me1/H3K27ac; latent enhancers; lncRNA UMLILO; miR-9-5p vs miR-146a; G9a inhibition augments training.
- Metabolism: PI3K/AKT/mTOR/HIF-1alpha Warburg shift; fumarate-KDM5 inhibition; mevalonate/IGF1R feedback (statins block); fatty-acid synthesis in aldosterone training; tolerance uses OXPHOS/FAO.
- DAMP specifics: oxLDL via CD36/TLR4-mTOR; HMGB1 dose/context-dependent (RAGE-AMPK/SIRT1 vs mTOR); heme via SYK, mTOR-independent; MSU/urate via mTOR/NLRP3; mtDNA/TLR9-STING toward autophagy-tolerance; S100A4 at beta-glucan strength; hyperglycemia via Runx1 central training.
