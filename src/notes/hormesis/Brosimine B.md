# Brosimine B

[[Brosimine B]] (4,7-dihydroxy-8-(3,3-dimethylallyl)flavan; 2(S)-7,4'-dihydroxy-8-prenylflavan, C20H22O3) from Brosimum acutifolium bark is a flavonoid [[Hormetin]] with a fitted inverted-U: peak viability ~10.2 uM, sigma 6.5 uM, R2 0.984.

## Chemical Properties and Classification

Prenylated flavan from Moraceae (Murure); related to moracin P and mulberrofuran Q; prior anticancer reports (glioblastoma, CML).

## Pharmacodynamics and Pharmacokinetics

- Viability: 145% of control at 10 uM (24 h, normoxia); 1/5 uM inactive; 25-100 uM cytotoxic.
- OGD protection: rescues viability at 3 h (107%) and partly 6 h, not 24 h.
- ROS: suppresses superoxide only at 3 h OGD (peak ROS 453% at 6 h otherwise).
- Catalase: restores activity to 124% at 3 h, 141% vs OGD at 6 h; no effect at 24 h.
- Authors propose intrinsic antioxidant plus endogenous-defense upregulation; needs 5-25 uM dose refinement and positive-control comparison.
