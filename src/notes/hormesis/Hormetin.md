# Hormetin

A [[Hormetin]] is an individual hormesis-inducing compound (resveratrol, curcumin, sulforaphane, quercetin, [[Brosimine B]], capsaicin, butyrate) showing low-dose stimulation and high-dose inhibition.

## Chemical Properties and Classification

Plant polyphenols, isothiocyanates (sulforaphane), TRP agonists (capsaicin), fermentation metabolites (SCFAs, nattokinase peptides), cooking products (Maillard products, caramel), prenylated flavans ([[Brosimine B]], C20H22O3).

## Pharmacodynamics and Pharmacokinetics

Activate [[Nrf2]]/ARE, sirtuin/PGC-1alpha ([[Mitohormesis]]), HDAC/DNMT modulation; food matrix and microbiota govern bioavailability. [[Brosimine B]] peak 10.2 uM, sigma 6.5 uM (R2 0.984); cytotoxic above 10 uM. High-dose polyphenols turn pro-oxidant.

## Applications

Whole-food dosing keeps hormetins in the [[Hormetic Window]]; concentrated supplements risk overshoot (hepatotoxicity, GI distress, drug interactions).
