# Cetuximab

**Cetuximab** (Erbitux, C225) is a chimeric human–murine IgG1 monoclonal antibody that binds the extracellular domain of the epidermal growth factor receptor ([[EGFR]]) with high affinity, blocking ligand binding and receptor dimerization.

## Mechanism of Action

EGFR is a receptor tyrosine kinase of the HER family. Ligand binding activates three downstream cascades — [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]], [[RAS]]/[[RAF]]/[[MEK]]/[[ERK]], and PLCγ/PKC — that together drive proliferation, survival, and metastatic potential (Brand et al., *Cancer Biol Ther* 2011; PMID 21293176). Cetuximab intercepts this at the first step:

1. **Ligand blockade and receptor internalization** — antibody binding prevents dimerization and promotes receptor degradation, reducing surface EGFR.
2. **Antiproliferative signaling** — interruption of RAS/RAF and PI3K/AKT signaling blunts proliferation and survival cues.
3. **Immune effector function** — the IgG1 Fc domain recruits natural killer cells and macrophages for antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, and can prime antigen presentation.
4. **Combination radiosensitization** — cetuximab with radiotherapy improves locoregional control in head and neck cancer, reflecting combined inhibition of DNA-damage repair and proliferative signaling.

> [!info] Why cetuximab outperforms gefitinib
> Cetuximab binds the receptor ectodomain and therefore works on tumors with activating extracellular-domain mutations or receptor amplification, not only kinase-domain mutants. The same property also makes it dependent on an intact RAS–MAPK downstream axis.

## Biomarker-Defined Efficacy

Cetuximab is effective in metastatic colorectal cancer only in **RAS wild-type** tumors, where it prolongs survival by roughly 8.2 months. In RAS-mutant tumors the downstream pathway is constitutively active and antibody blockade of the receptor adds nothing (Zhou et al., *J Exp Clin Cancer Res* 2021; PMID 34663410). This has become the textbook example of a targeted agent whose efficacy depends on downstream pathway genotype rather than target expression.

In head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, EGFR overexpression is a driver of pathogenesis and cetuximab is approved in late-stage disease, though anti-PD-1 checkpoint inhibitors have largely displaced it in frontline settings.

## Mechanisms of Resistance

- **Intrinsic/tumor-intrinsic** — EGFR ligand overexpression, EGFR amplification or mutation, activating mutations in RAS/RAF/PI3K, bypass signaling through ERBB2, MET, or IGF-1R, metabolic remodeling, microsatellite instability, and altered [[Autophagy]].
- **Extrinsic/microenvironmental** — immune-cell phenotype, cancer-associated fibroblasts, and VEGF-driven angiogenesis all blunt antibody activity; anti-VEGFR combination is one reversal strategy.

## Connections

- [[EGFR]] — the direct target; cetuximab blocks ligand binding and receptor dimerization at the ectodomain rather than the kinase domain.
- [[Colorectal Cancer]] — the main approved indication alongside head and neck cancer, with efficacy gated on RAS wild-type status.
- [[Head and Neck Cancer]] — approved in combination with radiotherapy and platinum chemotherapy; now largely superseded by checkpoint inhibitors.
- [[KRAS]] — the decisive biomarker: mutant KRAS uncouples signaling from EGFR and abolishes cetuximab benefit.
- [[RAS]] — downstream pathway activation through RAS/RAF/MEK/ERK is the shared mediator of intrinsic resistance.
- [[PI3K]] — the parallel survival arm blocked along with the proliferative arm by receptor-level inhibition.
- [[Apoptosis]] — loss of AKT survival signaling, and enhancement of immune-mediated killing, push tumor cells toward apoptosis.
- [[Autophagy]] — altered autophagy contributes to intrinsic resistance and is one axis targeted by reversal strategies.
- [[Drug Resistance]] — resistance to cetuximab arises from tumor genotype and from the surrounding microenvironment.
- [[Targeted Therapy]] — cetuximab exemplifies both the success and the biomarker constraint of antibody-based receptor targeting.
- [[Immunotherapy]] — its IgG1 effector function and its combination with checkpoint inhibitors place it at the interface of targeted and immune oncology.
- [[Metastasis]] — the metastatic colorectal setting is where its survival benefit was quantified.
