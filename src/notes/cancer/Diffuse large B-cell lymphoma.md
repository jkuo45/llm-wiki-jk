# Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma

[[Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma]] ([[DLBCL]]) is the most common subtype of aggressive non-Hodgkin lymphoma, originating from germinal-center or activated B cells. Standard first-line therapy is [[R-CHOP]] (rituximab plus cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine, prednisone).

## Context in Fenbendazole Literature

A Stage IVa DLBCL case report is frequently cited in the fenbendazole self-administration literature.

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> An 83-year-old male with Stage IVa DLBCL self-administered 1 g/day fenbendazole (adjusted 1-6 tablets/day by symptoms), with repeated PET/CT over 12 months showing improved lymphadenopathy and no new lesions; physicians speculated regression might be due to fenbendazole, though attribution remained uncertain (54).
