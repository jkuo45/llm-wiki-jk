# DR5

**DR5** (Death Receptor 5), also known as TRAIL-R2 or TNFRSF10B, is a cell surface receptor that initiates the [[Extrinsic Pathway]] of [[Apoptosis]] when activated by [[TRAIL]].

## Mechanism
Similar to [[DR4]], the binding of [[TRAIL]] to DR5 leads to receptor trimerization and the formation of the **Death-Inducing Signaling Complex** (DISC), involving [[FADD]] and pro-[[Caspase-8]]. This leads to the activation of the initiator [[Caspase-8]] and subsequent executioner caspases.

## Role in Cancer
- **Drug Target**: DR5 is a major target for cancer therapy, including recombinant TRAIL and agonistic antibodies (e.g., lexatumumab).
- **Regulation**: Its expression can be upregulated by cellular stress and DNA-damaging agents, often via [[p53]].
