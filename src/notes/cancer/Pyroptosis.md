# Pyroptosis

Pyroptosis is a type of inflammatory cell death induced by inflammasomes. It is characterized by cell swelling and rupturing, leading to the release of proinflammatory cytokines.

## Mechanism
Pyroptosis is initiated by the assembly of inflammasomes, which typically contain a pattern recognition receptor (PRR), [[ASC]], and pro-caspase-1. This leads to the activation of [[Caspase-1]], which:
- Cleaves pro-IL-1β and pro-IL-18 into their mature forms.
- Cleaves [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] (Gasdermin D), which forms pores in the plasma membrane, executing pyroptosis.

## Role in Cancer
Pyroptosis can inhibit tumor growth by inducing cell death and stimulating an inflammatory response in the tumor microenvironment.

## Inducers
- [[Ivermectin]]: Has been observed to induce characteristic pyroptosis phenomena in breast cancer cells, possibly via the P2X4/P2X7/[[NLRP3]] pathway.

## Sex differences

Sex restrains the [[NLRP3]] inflammasome–pyroptosis axis in females. In ovariectomized mouse and rat models, 17β-estradiol suppresses NLRP3, [[ASC]], cleaved caspase-1 and mature IL-1β across lung (OVA airway inflammation; Liu et al., *Biosci Rep* 2019, BSR20181117) and hippocampus (global cerebral ischemia; Thakkar et al., *Oxid Med Cell Longev* 2016, PMID 27843532), acting via ERβ and the ER coregulator PELP1 with upstream dampening of P2X7R/TXNIP priming. Ovariectomy alone activates hippocampal NLRP3/caspase-1/IL-1β/IL-18, reversed by estradiol, ERβ agonist (not ERα agonist), or caspase-1 inhibition with VX-765 (Xu et al., *Brain Behav Immun* 2016, PMID 26928197). In sepsis, intact females show estradiol–GPER-1–PPARδ suppression of NLRP3 with preserved cardiac function (*Metabolism* 2024). Counterpoint: after trauma-hemorrhage, [[Gasdermin D]]-dependent systemic release scores higher in biological females (mouse + human multiomic cohort; Sun et al., *Sci Transl Med* 2025, doi:10.1126/scitranslmed.ado2622) — the executor step can be female-biased even when NLRP3 priming is estrogen-restrained. Male-biased NLRP3 responses in pure-LPS shock are not universal (Thompson et al. 2025, PMC12554040: females worse on primary challenge, reversed on rechallenge), so sepsis claims stay model-qualified.
