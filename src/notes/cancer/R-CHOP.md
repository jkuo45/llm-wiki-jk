# R-CHOP

[[R-CHOP]] is the standard first-line regimen for [[Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma]] (DLBCL), combining the anti-CD20 antibody rituximab with cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, [[Vincristine]], and prednisone.

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> The DLBCL case summarized in the review (Stage IVa, fenbendazole self-administration) is framed against standard DLBCL therapy such as R-CHOP (54).
