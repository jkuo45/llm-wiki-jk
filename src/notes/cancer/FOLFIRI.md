# FOLFIRI

## Overview

**FOLFIRI** is a combination [[Chemotherapy]] regimen consisting of three agents:
- **5-Fluorouracil (5-FU)**: an antimetabolite that inhibits thymidylate synthase
- **Leucovorin** (folinic acid): a folate derivative that enhances 5-FU activity
- **Irinotecan**: a topoisomerase I inhibitor

FOLFIRI is primarily used in the treatment of [[Cancer|colorectal cancer]] and other gastrointestinal malignancies.

## Role in NAD+ Metabolism and Muscle Wasting

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Chemotherapy NAD+ Loss Pöllänen 2026]]
> FOLFIRI treatment in mice demonstrates time-dependent effects on [[NAD+]] metabolism and [[Skeletal Muscle|skeletal muscle]] mass.

### Short-Term (2-Week) Treatment

- Does not affect body weight or muscle mass
- Does not alter NAD+ metabolites or their ratios
- Does not alter expression of muscle NAD+ biosynthetic enzymes
- However, [[NRK2|Nrk2]] downregulation is detectable as early as 24 hours after treatment, preceding any measurable muscle loss

### Long-Term (5-Week) Treatment

- Reduces final body weight and muscle mass (minor effects)
- Elicits a near-significant decrease of −18% (p=0.069) in muscle [[NAD+]] levels
- Reduces muscle [[NADPH|NADP+]]
- Selectively downregulates muscle [[NRK2|Nrk2]] (−84%, p<0.001) with no significant changes in other NAD+ biosynthetic enzymes
- In liver: modest NAD+ loss (−20%, p=0.013) with reduced [[NRK2|Nrk1]] expression

### Comparison with Other Regimens

| Regimen | Duration | Muscle Mass Loss | Muscle NAD+ Loss | Nrk2 Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FOLFIRI | 2 weeks | None | None | −84%* |
| FOLFIRI | 5 weeks | Minor | −18% (p=0.069) | −84% |
| [[Cisplatin]] | 2 weeks | Severe | −14% (p=0.047) | −93% |
| [[FOLFOX]] | 5 weeks | Mild | None | −92% |

*24h timepoint showed early Nrk2 downregulation preceding muscle loss
