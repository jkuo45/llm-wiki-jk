# EMT6

[[EMT6]] is a cloned, cell culture-adapted mouse mammary tumor cell line originally derived from a BALB/c mouse. It is used both as monolayer cultures in vitro and as intradermal solid tumors in BALB/cRw mice in vivo, and has been extensively characterized for responses to radiation and anticancer drugs.

## Relevance to Fenbendazole

> [!warning] Source: [[Fenbendazole as a Potential Anticancer Drug]]
> Fenbendazole was cytotoxic to EMT6 cells in vitro at high concentrations (1-3 μM) with 24-h incubations, with modest preferential toxicity under severe hypoxia. However, fenbendazole (50 mg/kg/day i.p. or 150 ppm diet) did not alter EMT6 tumor growth, radiation response, or metastatic pattern in BALB/cRw mice (Duan et al. 2013).
