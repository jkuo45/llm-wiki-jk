# Propolis

## Overview

Propolis (bee glue) is a resinous substance produced by [[honeybee|honeybees]] ([[Apis mellifera]]) by mixing plant exudates, beeswax, and salivary enzymes. It is used in the hive as a sealant and antimicrobial barrier. In traditional medicine, propolis has been used for its [[antimicrobial]], [[anti-inflammatory]], [[antioxidant]], and [[anti-cancer]] properties.

## Chemical Composition

- **Flavonoids**: [[Chrysin]], [[Galangin]], [[Pinocembrin]], [[Quercetin]], [[Apigenin]].
- **Phenolic acids**: [[Caffeic acid]], [[Ferulic acid]], [[Caffeic Acid Phenethyl Ester]] (CAPE).
- **Volatile compounds**: [[Terpenes]], [[benzyl alcohol]] derivatives.
- Composition varies by geographic source and plant origin (e.g., [[Brazilian green propolis]], [[European poplar propolis]]).

## Anti-Cancer Activity

- [[Caffeic Acid Phenethyl Ester]] (CAPE) induces [[Apoptosis]] and inhibits [[NF-κB]].
- Propolis extracts inhibit proliferation in [[Breast Cancer]], [[Prostate Cancer]], [[Colorectal Cancer]], [[Melanoma]], and [[Glioblastoma]] cell lines.
- Mechanisms include [[cell cycle arrest]], [[apoptosis]] induction, [[antioxidant]] activity, and modulation of [[PI3K]]/[[Akt]] and [[Wnt]]/[[β-catenin]] signaling.

## Anti-Inflammatory Activity

- CAPE is a potent inhibitor of [[NF-κB]] and [[COX-2]].
- Propolis reduces [[IL-6]], [[TNF-α]], and [[IL-1β]] production in activated [[macrophages]].
