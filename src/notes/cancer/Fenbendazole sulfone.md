# Fenbendazole sulfone

[[Fenbendazole sulfone]] is a downstream oxidized metabolite of [[Fenbendazole]], formed by presystemic and systemic metabolism (41, 42). Although fenbendazole sulfone predominates in plasma after administration, the sulfoxide [[Oxfendazole]] is the primary metabolite responsible for fenbendazole's biological (anthelmintic/anticancer) activity (44).

## Metabolic Context

Fenbendazole is converted to oxfendazole (sulfoxide) mainly by [[Flavin-containing Monooxygenase|FMO]] and [[CYP3A4]], and to hydroxyfenbendazole by [[CYP2J2]]/[[CYP2C19]]; further oxidation yields fenbendazole sulfone (43).
