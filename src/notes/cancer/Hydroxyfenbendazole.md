# Hydroxyfenbendazole

[[Hydroxyfenbendazole]] is a hydroxylated metabolite of [[Fenbendazole]], produced by [[CYP2J2]] and [[CYP2C19]] (40). After intravenous administration in cattle, over 50% of the fenbendazole dose was recovered as hydroxyfenbendazole in excreta (46).

## Metabolic Context

It represents one of several phase-I metabolites alongside the sulfoxide [[Oxfendazole]] and [[Fenbendazole sulfone]].

> [!info] Source: [[Oral Fenbendazole for Cancer Therapy in Humans and Animals]]
> "CYP2J2 and CYP2C19 enzymes metabolize fenbendazole into hydroxyfenbendazole (40)." and "When administered intravenously, over 50% of the fenbendazole dose was excreted as hydroxyfenbendazole (46)."
