# H4K16ac

H4K16ac is the acetylation of lysine 16 on histone H4 — the residue occupying the N-terminal tail that protrudes from the nucleosome at the dyad entry/exit — and is one of the most functionally distinctive marks in chromatin biology. Unlike most acetylation marks, which act chiefly by recruiting bromodomain readers, H4K16ac has a direct physical effect on chromatin architecture: the bulkied acetyl group prevents the H4 N-tail of one nucleosome from docking into the acidic groove of the neighbouring nucleosome's H2A/H2B, blocking the fibre–fibre interaction that compacts chromatin.

> [!info] The mark that is also a structural brake
> This gives H4K16ac a dual role. As an enzyme-substrate it is a docking platform for bromodomain proteins; as a physicochemical feature it actively opposes chromatin compaction. Its removal is therefore a switch, not merely a signal — the cell can condense chromosomes by taking it away.

## Enzyme Machinery

| Role | Enzyme | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Writer | KAT8 (MOF), with its partner KAT7/HBO1 in the MOZ/MORF family; TIP60/KAT5 with EP400 | KAT8 is the essential writer at active promoters and the primary source of global H4K16ac |
| Eraser | HDAC1/2 in the NuRD complex, HDAC1/2 in Sin3A complexes, HDAC8 (which is selective for H4K16) | Class I/IIa HDACs require the acetyl-lysine as substrate; HDAC8 is unusual in being efficient at H4K16 |
| Reader | Bromodomains of BRD2/BRD3/BRD4, BPTF (NURF), TAF1, and the NuRD-associated protein CHD4-associated readers | Recognition is typically preference-selective for the di-acetylated H4K16/K23 pair on the same tail |

The NAD<sup>+</sup>-dependent deacetylase [[SIRT2]] is a specific and mechanistically important eraser of H4K16ac. SirT2's substrate preference for H4K16ac during mitosis is what allows chromosome condensation to proceed; constitutively active SIRT2 mutants cause decondensation and genomic instability, while loss of SIRT2 impairs compaction. [[SIRT1]] also deacetylates H4K16ac at the promoters of SASP genes, which is one route by which sirtuin activity restrains the senescent inflammatory programme.

## Genomic Distribution and Function

H4K16ac is strongly enriched at active promoters and enhancers, particularly over transcription start sites of highly expressed genes, and its genome-wide distribution tracks transcriptional activity more tightly than total H3 acetylation. Additional notable roles:

- **Mitotic chromosome condensation**: deacetylation during M phase licenses chromatin compaction.
- **DNA repair**: H4K16ac and the related H4K5/K8 acetylation are required to recruit repair machinery at broken ends; loss of KAT8 or of H4K16ac impairs repair factor assembly.
- **Chromatin remodelling and the [[Epigenetic Clock]]**: age-related drift in H4K16ac at specific loci is a documented component of epigenetic age prediction.
- **Inflammaging and senescence**: SIRT1-mediated reduction of H3K9 and H4K16 acetylation at the IL-6 and IL-8 promoters restrains [[SASP]] transcription; conversely, loss of H4K16ac contributes to the permissive chromatin state of the senescence-associated secretory phenotype.
- **Sperm chromatin**: retained H4K16ac in spermatozoa is a marker of protamine deficiency and has been linked to reduced embryo competence.

## Disease Relevance

H4K16ac is over-represented at oncogene and super-enhancer loci in many tumours, and the KAT8–NSD2 axis is recurrently altered in cancer, with the H4K16ac writer machinery both amplified and mutated. Reducing H4K16ac via HDAC inhibition re-compacts chromatin and can silence aberrant transcription programs, which is the rationale for combining HDAC inhibitors with [[BET inhibitors|bromodomain inhibitors]]. Reported abnormalities also include reduced H4K16ac in ageing tissues and in models of neurodegeneration.

> [!warning] Reader-assay caveat
> Most H4K16ac measurements come from pan-acetylation (H4ac) or H4K12ac antibodies that cross-react. Claims about H4K16ac specifically should rest on mass spectrometry or well-validated K16-specific reagents; some widely used "H4K16ac" datasets are H4K12ac measurements mislabelled.
