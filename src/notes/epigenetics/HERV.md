## Overview

Human endogenous retroviruses (HERVs) are ancient retroviral sequences fixed in the human genome, comprising a large fraction of our repetitive DNA. Normally silenced by [[DNA Methylation]] and [[Heterochromatin]], HERVs become derepressed during aging and cellular [[Senescence]], with important consequences for inflammation.

## Mechanism of Activation

Age-related loss of DNA methylation and heterochromatin permits HERV transcription. The resulting HERV RNA is reverse transcribed into cDNA that is sensed by [[cGAS]], activating [[STING]]-dependent signaling and upregulating [[Type I Interferon|type I interferons]] and SASP factors (the senescence-associated secretory phenotype, [[SASP]]).

> [!important]
> HERV-driven innate immune signaling contributes to [[Inflammaging]] and the paracrine spread of senescence. Suppressing HERV expression or reverse transcription reduces this senescence-driving inflammatory signal, identifying HERVs as a druggable senescence axis.

## Regulation & Therapeutics

Because HERV cDNA synthesis depends on reverse transcriptase, [[NRTI|nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors]] can blunt HERV-dependent inflammation. In senescent cells, blocking HERV expression or reverse transcription lowers SASP output, an emerging senomorphic strategy. HERV activation is also linked to age-related diseases including cancer and autoimmune conditions, though causality remains under investigation.

## Research Status

The role of HERVs in aging is an active area. While derepression of retroelements — including [[LINE-1]] — is established during senescence, the specific contribution of HERVs versus other repeats, and whether HERV-encoded proteins themselves have functions, are open questions.

## Connections

- [[cGAS]]: Cytosolic DNA sensor that recognizes HERV-derived cDNA.
- [[STING]]: Downstream signaling hub triggering type I interferon and SASP.
- [[SASP]]: The senescence secretome amplified by HERV-driven signaling.
- [[Senescence]]: Cellular state in which HERV derepression occurs.
- [[Inflammaging]]: Chronic low-grade inflammation fed by HERV activation.
- [[Type I Interferon]]: Cytokines upregulated by HERV–cGAS-STING signaling.
- [[LINE-1]]: Related retroelement also derepressed in aging and senescence.
- [[NRTI]]: Drug class that blocks reverse transcription of HERV RNA.
