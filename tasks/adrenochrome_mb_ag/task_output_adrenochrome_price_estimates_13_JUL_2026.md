- **MB + AG annual, low end:** ~$770–$1,350 (wholesale MB vials + raw‑powder AG)
- **MB + AG annual, high end:** ~$10,400–$15,500 (retail MB vials + raw‑powder AG)

- **NAD+ IV** and **rapamycin/temsirolimus IV** are *outside* the 10‑days/quarter MB+AG schedule and are priced per‑session / per‑vial above.

## Safety Notes

- All four agents are **prescription / license‑restricted**. MB and AG IV require a licensed provider and sterile compounding.
- **Methylene Blue IV** carries severe serotonin‑syndrome risk with SSRIs/SNRIs; acute hemolysis in G6PD deficiency; tissue necrosis if extravasated.
- **Aminoguanidine IV** has **no established human dose**; high doses inhibit constitutive NOS (eNOS/nNOS), risking transient hypertension. Oral form blocks gut DAO → histamine intolerance.
- Therapeutic use should be supervised by a qualified healthcare provider.
