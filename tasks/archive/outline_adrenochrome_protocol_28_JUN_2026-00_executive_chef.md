## Overview

### Recommended Eating Window
**16:8 Time-Restricted Eating (TRE)**
- **Eating window:** 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (training days) / 11:00 AM – 6:00 PM (rest days → 17:7)
- **Rationale:** 16–17 hours of fasting is sufficient to meaningfully engage AMPK, suppress mTORC1, upregulate PINK1/Parkin-mediated mitophagy, and allow lysosomal clearance (SRAC). Post-workout morning protein consumption is timed inside the window; evening carbohydrate cutoff supports overnight autophagy deepening.

### Daily Macro Targets (for ~75–80 kg active individual)
| Macro | Training Day | Rest Day |
|---|---|---|
| **Calories** | ~2,400–2,600 kcal | ~1,900–2,100 kcal |
| **Protein** | ~165–175 g (2.2 g/kg) | ~140 g (1.8 g/kg) |
| **Carbohydrates** | ~280–320 g | ~180–200 g |
| **Fat** | ~70–85 g | ~65–75 g |

> **Note:** Slight caloric surplus on training days, mild deficit on rest days — net weekly maintenance/slight recomposition deficit. Adjust by ±200 kcal based on body response.

Aminoguanidine is not available as a standard supplement in most jurisdictions. Consult your physician for these two compounds separately from this dietary protocol.

### "Carnosine Power Bowl"
*Pan-seared chicken breast over wheat germ brown rice with avocado, broccoli & nutritional yeast*

**Ingredients:**
- 200 g chicken breast (high carnosine, high tryptophan → NAD+ de novo)
- ¾ cup brown rice + 2 tbsp toasted wheat germ (spermidine ~5 mg)
- ½ avocado (NMN ~1 mg, monounsaturated fat)
- 1 cup steamed broccoli (sulforaphane — mitohormetic stressor)

- Seasoning: garlic, lemon, black pepper, olive oil drizzle

**Recipe:**
1. Season chicken with garlic powder, salt, cracked black pepper. Sear in cast iron 4 min/side, finish 375°F oven 8 min. Rest 3 min, slice.
2. Cook brown rice; stir in toasted wheat germ off heat.
3. Steam broccoli 4–5 min — slightly al dente to preserve sulforaphane.
4. Assemble bowl: rice base, broccoli, sliced chicken, fan of avocado. Dust nutritional yeast, squeeze lemon, drizzle olive oil.

**Macros:** ~680 kcal · 58 g protein · 65 g carbs · 22 g fat
**Protocol Hit:** MRR (tryptophan→NAD+, niacin, avocado NMN), GOPS (carnosine, sulforaphane), SRAC (GlyNAC supplement from AM)

### "Spermidine Shiitake Beef Stir-Fry"
*Lean beef with dried shiitake mushrooms, green peas, garlic, and edamame over miso broth noodles*

**Ingredients:**
- 180 g lean beef sirloin, thin-sliced (carnosine, creatine ~0.8 g, NAD+ precursors)
- 30 g dried shiitake, rehydrated (spermidine ~2.7 mg)
- 1 cup green peas (spermidine ~2 mg)
- ½ cup shelled edamame (NMN ~0.55 mg, isoflavones)
- 100 g soba noodles (whole buckwheat preferred)
- 2 cloves garlic (cysteine → glutathione, sulfur compounds)
- 1 tbsp low-sodium miso (fermented, probiotic — supports Gordonibacter for urolithin A conversion)
- Seasoning: ginger, sesame oil, tamari, green onion

**Recipe:**
1. Rehydrate shiitake 20 min in warm water; reserve liquid for broth. Slice caps.
2. High-heat wok: sear beef strips 2–3 min until lightly caramelized. Remove.
3. Same wok: sauté garlic + ginger 30 sec. Add shiitake, peas, edamame — stir-fry 3 min.
4. Return beef. Add tamari, splash mushroom soaking liquid. Toss.
5. Whisk miso into ½ cup warm water; drizzle over assembled noodles. Top with stir-fry, sesame oil, green onion.

**Macros:** ~720 kcal · 58 g protein · 75 g carbs · 18 g fat
**Protocol Hit:** GOPS (carnosine, spermidine ~4.7 mg cumulative), MRR (edamame NMN, miso probiotic for urolithin A bioconversion)

*High-carnosine pork, spermidine-rich parmesan, sulforaphane broccoli, glycine-dense bone broth*

**Ingredients:**

### "Chicken Liver & Mushroom Ragù over Polenta"
*Spermidine from liver + mushrooms, carnosine from chicken, tryptophan → NAD+ de novo*

**Ingredients:**
- 150 g chicken livers (spermidine ~7 mg; highest organ meat; iron, B12, folate)
- 100 g cremini/shiitake mushrooms (spermidine ~2 mg)
- ½ cup polenta / coarse cornmeal (corn provides spermidine ~3 mg/100 g)
- 1 cup bone broth (glycine)
- Shallot, garlic, fresh thyme, sage, splash red wine, olive oil
- 1 tbsp aged parmesan to finish

**Recipe:**
1. Trim chicken livers, pat dry, season. Sear in hot pan with olive oil 2 min/side — keep slightly pink inside (overcooking destroys nutrients). Remove.
2. Sauté shallot, garlic in same pan. Add mushrooms, cook 5 min until golden. Deglaze with red wine. Add bone broth, thyme. Simmer 5 min.
3. Return livers, heat through 1–2 min. Rough chop to desired texture.
4. Prepare polenta: whisk cornmeal into boiling salted water + bone broth; stir 15 min. Finish with parmesan.
5. Serve ragù over polenta. Drizzle olive oil. Fresh sage crispy-fried in butter optional.

**Macros:** ~580 kcal · 42 g protein · 48 g carbs · 20 g fat
**Protocol Hit:** GOPS (spermidine ~12 mg cumulative day, parmesan), MRR (tryptophan→NAD+ from liver, bone broth glycine), SRAC (fermented/probiotic microbiome maintenance)

### "Slow-Cooked Oxtail Broth Ramen with Shiitake, Egg & Green Peas"
*Glycine-maximal bone broth as the star — GlyNAC precursor dinner*

**Ingredients:**
- 300 g oxtail or beef shank (collagen connective tissue → glycine ~5 g after cooking, carnosine)
- 1.5 L water (slow cook 4–6 hrs or pressure cook 90 min)
- 100 g shiitake mushrooms (spermidine ~2 mg)
- 1 cup green peas
- 2 soft-boiled eggs (marinated in tamari/mirin)
- 1 serving ramen noodles or soba
- Garlic, ginger, star anise, tamari, green onion, nori, sesame

**Recipe:**
1. Brown oxtail in oven 400°F 20 min for deep umami. Transfer to pot with water, garlic, ginger, star anise. Slow cook 4–6 hours (or Instant Pot 90 min). Skim fat/foam. Strain broth.
2. Season broth with tamari, splash mirin. Rich, dark, collagen-rich.
3. Cook noodles. Sauté shiitake.
4. Marinate soft-boiled eggs (halved) in tamari + mirin 30 min minimum.
5. Assemble bowl: noodles, ladle rich broth, oxtail meat (pulled from bone), shiitake, peas, egg halves, nori, green onion, sesame oil.

**Macros:** ~680 kcal · 58 g protein · 62 g carbs · 18 g fat
**Protocol Hit:** SRAC (GlyNAC — glycine ~5+ g from collagen broth), GOPS (spermidine from shiitake, carnosine from beef), MRR (fermented tamari microbiome support)

## Shopping & Prep Tips

### Weekly Prep (Sunday or Monday, ~2 hrs)
1. **Bone broth:** Make a large batch of oxtail/shank broth Sunday. Freeze in 1-cup portions. Used across multiple meals.
2. **Grains:** Cook brown rice, farro, and soba in batches. Refrigerate up to 5 days.
3. **Wheat germ:** Purchase raw wheat germ (refrigerate after opening — goes rancid quickly). Toast small batches in dry pan.
4. **Dried shiitake:** Rehydrate only what you need per meal — keeps indefinitely dry.
5. **Pomegranate juice:** Buy 100% pure, no sugar added. Can use frozen pomegranate arils and press.
6. **Natto:** Available frozen at Asian grocery stores. Thaw overnight in fridge. Never heat above 50°C.
7. **Marinated eggs:** Make a batch of 6 tamari-marinated eggs at start of week (Day 7 ramen, Day 2 niçoise).

## Rationale — The Science Behind the Plate

### MRR: Mitohormetic Redox Relay
**NAD+ Precursor Strategy:** Tuna (19 mg niacin/100 g), chicken breast (11 mg/100 g), nutritional yeast (~30 mg/tbsp), and edamame (NMN 1.1 mg/100 g) collectively support the de novo and Preiss-Handler NAD+ synthesis pathways. However, as the protocol notes, supplemental NMN/NR (500 mg/day fasted AM) is **required** — food sources alone are marginal in aging adults.

**Sulforaphane (broccoli, cruciferous):** An isothiocyanate that activates Nrf2 transcription factor, upregulating antioxidant response element (ARE) genes. This is a textbook mitohormetic agent — a mild pro-oxidant that induces a disproportionate antioxidant and mitochondrial biogenesis response. Key: **chop broccoli 40 min before cooking** to allow myrosinase-glucoraphanin interaction, then steam briefly (not boil).

**Urolithin A Strategy:** Pomegranate juice + walnuts + berries provide ellagitannins. Only ~30–40% of people efficiently convert these to Urolithin A via gut *Gordonibacter* spp. The miso, yogurt, natto, and sourdough inclusions support microbiome diversity. Mitopure® supplement (500 mg/day) bypasses this bottleneck as the protocol recommends.

### GOPS: Glycation & AGE Suppression
**Spermidine Protocol:** Target ≥10–15 mg/day dietary spermidine (matching longevity study ranges). Wheat germ (2.4 mg/tbsp) + natto (10 mg/50 g serving) + shiitake (2 mg) + parmesan (2–4 mg) + green peas (2 mg) + corn (1.5 mg) allows accumulation of 15–20 mg on peak days. This inhibits EP300 acetyltransferase → epigenetic autophagy induction operating independently of mTOR.

**Carnosine Sources:** Pork tenderloin is the highest food source (2,100 mg/100 g), followed by chicken breast (2,000 mg/100 g) and beef (1,500 mg/100 g). Rotated daily to maintain consistent carnosine intake without repetition. **β-Alanine supplementation (3.2 g/day)** is more effective than food alone for muscle carnosine loading — carnosine is rapidly hydrolyzed by serum carnosinase before tissue uptake.

### SRAC: Senescent Cell Clearance
**Fisetin Strategy:** 200 g strawberries delivers ~32 mg fisetin — the highest natural concentration. For daily cellular maintenance, this is meaningful. For actual senolytic "clearing" pulses, the protocol prescribes 1,000–1,500 mg supplemental fisetin 2 consecutive days/month with quercetin co-administration. The dietary strawberry strategy is the continuous low-dose maintenance between pulses.

**GlyNAC Food Strategy:** Bone broth provides glycine (2–5 g/cup). Collagen peptides add another 3 g/10 g scoop. Eggs and garlic provide cysteine (NAC precursor). Combined with supplemental NAC (1,200 mg/day) and glycine (5 g/day) taken fasted AM, this comprehensively restores glutathione precursor availability — critical for lysosomal function in SRAC.
