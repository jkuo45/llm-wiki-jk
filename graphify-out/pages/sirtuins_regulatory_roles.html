SIRT-by-SIRT Regulatory Roles — Where Each Sirtuin Is Protective vs Harmful

The seven mammalian sirtuins (SIRT1–SIRT7) each carry a distinct, system-specific disease footprint: SIRT3 and SIRT6 are near-universal protectors, SIRT4 is tumor-suppressive but cardiotoxic, SIRT2 mostly counter-regulatory and harmful in brain/lung, while SIRT1 and SIRT7 are genuinely dual. This page inverts the Wu et al. 2022 review into sirtuin-first regulatory maps — a stance spectrum, an organ-system matrix, and a graph subgraph — built from the task output synthesis.

The seven members at a glance

Localization and catalytic activity predict disease stance. Three nuclear/chromatin deacetylases, one cytosolic, three mitochondrial acyl-modifying enzymes — each with a different disease fingerprint.

SIRT1

the context-dependent master regulatorNucleus ↔ cytosol
Deacetylase

Protective / beneficial

  • Cardiac fibrosis, heart failure (SERCA2a K492), atherosclerosis, MI/R, hypertension
  • COPD, lung fibrosis, asthma, sepsis-induced ALI
  • Liver IRI, acute pancreatitis, renal AKI & fibrosis, kidney stones
  • AD, PD, HD, stroke, epilepsy, cognitive decline, SCI
  • DKD, DR, DCM, obesity, OA, osteoporosis, IDD, muscle atrophy

Harmful / deleterious

  • Cardiac hypertrophy at high dose (12.5×) — dual, dose-dependent
  • Alcoholic hepatitis, cholestatic disease, endotoxemic liver injury
  • Chronic pancreatitis (EMT), intestinal IBD (Treg-dependent)
  • HBV transcription (resveratrol activates virus)
  • Endometrial & cervical cancer, T-ALL (CDK2→p27), chemoresistance (tamoxifen, cisplatin)
  • CRC dose-dependent dual: hetero-KO → c-Myc/glutamine, homo-KO → apoptosis
Net stance: DUAL. The only sirtuin implicated in all seven organ-system disease categories plus aging — protective lean in CV/respiratory/renal/neuro, harmful lean in several cancers and liver conditions. The most-studied sirtuin; richest modulator toolkit; most RCTs (resveratrol, SRT1720, SRT2104).

SIRT2

the counter-regulatory sirtuinCytosol (nucleus G2/M)
Deacetylase

Protective / beneficial

  • Cardiac hypertrophy (NFATc2), fibrosis (LKB1/AMPK), atherosclerosis (plaque reduction)
  • NAFLD (HNF4α), IBD gut homeostasis (Wnt/β-catenin)
  • OA, IDD, skeletal-muscle atrophy, neuropathic pain
  • Cervical cancer (favorable survival), ovarian cancer (cisplatin sensitivity)
  • Breast (BRCA1–BARD1 HR), CRC anti-metastasis (IDH1)

Harmful / deleterious

  • AD (β-secretase 1, tau, H4K16ac), PD (α-syn toxicity), HD, ischemic stroke (serum biomarker)
  • Asthma — aggravates Th2 / macrophage inflammation
  • Liver IRI (MKP-1), AKI (MAPK), obesity (HIF-1α→SIRT2–PGC-1α)
  • Basal-like breast (Slug), lung tumor (PGM K100 → NADPH), endometrial (MEK–ERK), T-ALL (LMO2)
Net stance: HARMFUL-LEAN. Often opposes the protective SIRT1/3/6 axis. Repeatedly harmful in neurodegenerative disease (AD, PD, HD, stroke) and asthma; protective in OA, IDD, kidney fibrosis, neuropathic pain, and several cancers where it is a tumor suppressor (BC, OC, CC). Well-studied but mostly cell/animal; no approved clinical modulators.

SIRT3

the mitochondrial protectorMitochondria
Deacetylase

Protective / beneficial

  • Cardiac hypertrophy, fibrosis, MI/R, HF, hypertension, vascular calcification
  • COPD, lung fibrosis, asthma, hyperoxia ALI, COVID-19 (serum biomarker)
  • NAFLD, liver IRI, HBV (cccDNA H3K9), IBD, intestinal IRI
  • AD, PD, stroke, epilepsy, cognitive deficits
  • DM, DCM, obesity, AKI, kidney fibrosis/stones, IDD, muscle atrophy

Harmful / dual

  • Lung radio/chemoresistance (ATM–Chk2) — low-ROS flip side
  • Cervical cancer invasion (ACC1 lipogenesis), CRC drug resistance (SOD2/PGC-1α)
  • Breast tamoxifen insensitivity; glioma higher expression → worse prognosis (Ku70–Bax)
  • OA dual: protects via SOD2, promotes apoptosis when overexpressed
Net stance: NEAR-UNIVERSALLY PROTECTIVE. Best-characterized mitochondrial sirtuin; the primary mitochondrial quality-control hub. The guardian phenotype prevents tumor initiation but keeps established tumors alive in therapy (radioresistance, chemoresistance). Only dual in OA and FLD/alcoholic liver disease.

SIRT4

the tumor-suppressive metabolizerMitochondria
ADP-ribosyl transferase

Protective / beneficial

  • Breast, HCC, colorectal, gastric — tumor suppression via glutamine-metabolism repression
  • Enhances tamoxifen sensitivity (STAT3 inhibition) and 5-FU sensitivity
  • Atherosclerosis (PI3K/Akt/NF-κB), MI/R (mitochondrial preservation)
  • DM — protective against progression (though metabolically thrifty)

Harmful / deleterious

  • Adverse cardiac — aggravates Ang II cardiac hypertrophy (MnSOD inhibition) & fibrosis
  • NAFLD dual: SIRT4/Smad4 protective but MTPα destabilization harmful
  • Ovarian cancer: high expression ↔ poor survival (only tumor exception)
  • Reduced amino-acid-stimulated insulin secretion (GDH)
Net stance: TUMOR-SUPPRESSIVE, CARDIAC-ADVERSE. ADP-ribosyl transferase with weak/no deacetylase activity; literature concentrated on glutamine metabolism and tumor suppression (BC/HCC/CRC/GC). The only sirtuin with clear adverse cardiac effects. Understudied; no specific modulators.

SIRT5

the metabolic double-edged swordMitochondria
Desuccinylase / deglutarylase / demalonylase

Protective / beneficial

  • Cardiac hypertrophy (age-related), fibrosis, MI/R (succinylation modulation)
  • NAFLD (mitochondrial FAO), IBD (KO susceptible to colitis)
  • Epilepsy (deficiency increases seizure severity/mortality)
  • AKI (mito vs peroxisomal FAO balance), pancreatic cancer (favorable prognosis)

Harmful / deleterious

  • DM — negatively regulates PDX1 → promotes progression
  • CRC (deglutarylation-dependent glutaminolysis, LDHB autophagy), HCC (OXPHOS→glycolysis)
  • Ovarian cancer cisplatin resistance (Nrf2/HO-1)
  • Stroke — BBB breakdown via occludin degradation
Net stance: MIXED. Non-deacetylase acyl-modifier (almost no classical deacetylase activity); resists nicotinamide inhibition — stays active where SIRT1–3 are suppressed. Protective in fatty liver, epilepsy, AKI, pancreas; pro-disease in DM, CRC, HCC via glutamine/glucose reprogramming.

SIRT6

the genome-stability guardianNucleus / chromatin
Deacetylase

Protective / beneficial

  • Most cancers: gastric, CRC, ovarian, endometrial, glioma, HCC; lung anti-Warburg (HIF-1α corepressor)
  • Cardiac hypertrophy, fibrosis, HF (TERT/TRF1), atherosclerosis, CAD, MI/R, hypertension
  • COPD, lung fibrosis, asthma, ARDS, NAFLD, HBV, cirrhosis, IBD, intestinal IRI
  • AD (diagnostic potential), ALS (SOD1-astrocyte), SCI, DM, metabolic syndrome
  • AKI, kidney fibrosis, aging-kidney, vascular calcification, OA, osteoporosis, IDD

Harmful / dual

  • Lung — snail-dependent KLF4 transrepression → EMT/metastasis
  • Obesity — STAT3 deacetylation / POMC lowering (hypothalamus)
  • AML — DNA-repair deficiency synergizes with NAMPT (context)
  • The same DDR/austerity program that prevents initiation keeps tumors alive in therapy
Net stance: MOST CONSISTENTLY PROTECTIVE. Deacetylase for genome stability (DNA repair, telomere, BER), chromatin silencing (H3K9/H3K56), and metabolic regulation (SREBP, ChREBP). Direct activators emerging (UBCS039, MDL-800). Dual only in lung cancer and obesity.

SIRT7

the under-studied wildcardNucleus / nucleolus
Deacetylase

Protective / beneficial

  • Cardiac hypertrophy (GATA4 deacetylation), atherosclerosis (VSMC Wnt/β-catenin)
  • Lung fibrosis (decline is profibrotic)
  • HBV — cccDNA H3 desuccinylation restricts viral transcription (dual)
  • IBD (KO susceptible to colitis), leukemia treatment-response biomarker

Harmful / deleterious

  • Breast, HCC, CRC, endometrial, glioma — RNA Pol I / ribosome-biogenesis-driven growth
  • NAFLD / alcoholic hepatitis — high expression harmful
  • Asthma airway remodeling; cardiac fibrosis (Smad2/ERK, inconsistent)
  • AKI — SIRT7-KO mice are protected, so it promotes tubular damage
Net stance: HARMFUL-LEAN / INCONSISTENT. Least studied sirtuin; research infancy (rRNA transcription, ribosome biogenesis, GATA4 deacetylation, cccDNA desuccinylation). Roles frequently conflicting (cardiac fibrosis, HBV). No specific modulators; virtually no pharmacology.

Read the stance

Green +/++ = protective / therapeutic target. Red −/−− = harmful / knockdown is protective. ⇄ = the same sirtuin helps and harms within the system (dose-, tissue-, or stage-dependent). · = not characterized in the review's synthesis.

Pattern

SIRT3 and SIRT6 columns are almost entirely green; SIRT2 and SIRT7 are red-leaning; SIRT1 is amber nearly everywhere. SIRT4 is green in cancer but red in cardiovascular — the only system where its stance inverts.

Caveat

"Protective" here means benefit in the disease model, not a blanket health claim. A protective sirtuin in normal tissue (e.g., SIRT3 low-ROS, SIRT6 DDR) can be a liability inside established tumors during therapy.

SIRT by SIRT — regulatory roles

Per-sirtuin regulatory roles from the review: protective face vs harmful face, with the context that decides which dominates.

SIRT1

context-dependent master regulator — dual in nearly every system Nucleus ↔ cytosol

Protective face

  • Cardiac fibrosis (resveratrol→TGF-β/Smad3), HF (SERCA2a K492), atherosclerosis (eNOS), MI/R (FoxO1/MnSOD), hypertension
  • COPD, lung fibrosis (AT2 self-renewal), asthma, sepsis-induced ALI
  • Liver IRI (MFN2), acute pancreatitis (p53/HSF1), NAFLD hepatic (ChREBP/SREBP-1c)
  • AD (senescence, tau O-GlcNAc, Beclin-1), PD (α-syn autophagy), HD (CREB–CRTC1), stroke, epilepsy, cognitive, SCI
  • DKD, DR, DCM, obesity, OA (Wnt/β-catenin), osteoporosis (FoxO3a), IDD, atrophy

Harmful face

  • Cardiac hypertrophy dose-dependent: low 2.5×/7.5× protective, 12.5× pathogenic
  • Alcoholic hepatitis (myeloid, apoptosis-defect), cholestatic & endotoxemic liver injury
  • Chronic pancreatitis (inflammation→EMT), intestinal IBD (Treg-dependent harm)
  • HBV (resveratrol activates transcription; sirtinol/EX-527 anti-HBV)
  • Breast (tamoxifen resistance, metadherin), cervical (AIM2), endometrial, T-ALL (CDK2→p27), CRC dual (c-Myc/glutamine)
  • Pancreatic/ovarian chemoresistance; cisplatin resistance (VEGF-A) in NSCLC
Context: dose (CRC, cardiac), tumor type (suppressor in glioma/HCC, promoter in gastric/endometrial/leukemia), and stage. Most-studied sirtuin with the deepest literature and richest modulator toolkit; most RCTs (resveratrol, SRT1720, SRT2104).

SIRT2

counter-regulatory — harmful in brain & lung, protective in joints & kidney Cytosol

Protective face

  • Cardiac hypertrophy (NFATc2), cardiac fibrosis (LKB1→AMPK), atherosclerosis (macrophage polarization)
  • NAFLD (HNF4α), IBD (Wnt/β-catenin gut homeostasis)
  • OA (blocks p65 acetylation), IDD (p53/p21), muscle atrophy (autophagic flux), neuropathic pain
  • Cancer suppressor: breast (BRCA1–BARD1 HR), CRC (IDH1, anti-metastasis), ovarian (cisplatin sensitivity), cervical (favorable survival)

Harmful face

  • AD (β-secretase 1 via RTN4B, tau, nuclear H4K16ac), PD (α-syn tubulin deacetylation), HD, ischemic stroke (serum SIRT2 ↑ = risk marker)
  • Asthma — genetic exaggeration worsens, pharmacologic ablation attenuates
  • Liver IRI (MKP-1→MAPK), AKI (MAPK), obesity (HIF-1α→SIRT2–PGC-1α axis)
  • Cancer promoter: basal-like breast (Slug), lung (PGM K100→NADPH), endometrial (stemness, MEK–ERK), T-ALL (LMO2)
Context: cytosolic sirtuin that often opposes the protective SIRT1/3/6 axis. In leukemia it splits: NAMPT/SIRT2 activation reduces AML proliferation, but SIRT2 inhibition suppresses T-ALL. Focus of SIRT2 inhibitor development (AK-7, SirReal2, AGK2, tenovins) for PD/HD.

SIRT3

mitochondrial guardian — near-universally protective Mitochondria

Protective face

  • Cardiac hypertrophy (FoxO1 autophagy), fibrosis (TGF-β/Smad3), MI/R, HF, hypertension (SOD2), vascular calcification
  • COPD, lung fibrosis (mitochondrial ROS), asthma, hyperoxia ALI (MnSOD), COVID-19 (serum prognostic)
  • NAFLD (LCAD/trifunctional protein), liver IRI, HBV (cccDNA H3K9), IBD, intestinal IRI (peroxiredoxin 3)
  • AD, PD (α-syn mitochondrial dysfunction), stroke (HIF-1α/VEGF), epilepsy, cognitive deficits
  • DM, DCM, obesity, AKI (central role), kidney fibrosis/stones (Nrf2/HO-1), IDD, muscle atrophy

Harmful / dual face

  • Lung radioresistance (ATM–Chk2 DNA repair) — suppresses ROS so well it protects tumors
  • Cervical cancer invasion (ACC1→lipogenesis), CRC drug resistance (SOD2/PGC-1α)
  • Breast tamoxifen insensitivity; glioma higher expression → worse prognosis (Ku70–Bax)
  • OA dual: mitochondrial protection vs overexpression-driven apoptosis; NAFLD dual (alcoholic injury)
Core tension: the low-ROS/intact-OXPHOS "guardian" phenotype prevents cancer initiation but keeps established tumors alive by preventing stress-induced death. Elevated in node-positive breast cancer and OSCC. Activators are largely indirect (resveratrol, honokiol, Dihydromyricetin).

SIRT4

tumor-suppressive metabolizer — with a cardiac cost Mitochondria

Protective face

  • Breast, HCC, colorectal, gastric — tumor suppression via glutamine-metabolism repression (GDH / mitochondrial–nuclear crosstalk)
  • Enhances tamoxifen sensitivity (STAT3 inhibition), 5-FU sensitivity (cell-cycle inhibition)
  • Atherosclerosis (PI3K/Akt/NF-κB), MI/R (mitochondrial preservation)
  • DM — protective against progression (though metabolically thrifty)

Harmful face

  • Cardiac hypertrophy — aggravates Ang II-induced disease (MnSOD inhibition)
  • Cardiac fibrosis — global KO confers resistance to Ang II
  • NAFLD dual: SIRT4/Smad4 axis protective, but MTPα destabilization harmful
  • Ovarian cancer: high expression ↔ poor survival (exception to tumor-suppressor pattern)
Bottom line: the only sirtuin with clear adverse cardiac effects; loss is uniformly prognostic for poor survival in HCC, gastric, CRC, bladder, breast cancer. ADP-ribosyl transferase, weak/no deacetylase activity; no specific modulators.

SIRT5

metabolic double-edged sword Mitochondria

Protective face

  • Cardiac hypertrophy (prevents age-related), fibrosis (KO → increased fibrosis), MI/R (succinylation modulation)
  • NAFLD (mitochondrial medium-chain FAO), IBD (KO susceptible to colitis)
  • Epilepsy — deficiency increases mortality & seizure severity
  • AKI (mito vs peroxisomal FAO balance), pancreatic cancer (favorable prognosis; GOT1 loss → proliferation)

Harmful face

  • DM — negatively regulates PDX1 transcription, promotes progression; inhibition restores β-cell proliferation/insulin
  • CRC (deglutarylation-dependent glutaminolysis; LDHB desuccinylation → hyperactive autophagy)
  • HCC (OXPHOS→glycolysis reprogramming, growth/metastasis), ovarian (cisplatin resistance via Nrf2/HO-1)
  • Stroke — increases BBB permeability via occludin degradation
Note: almost no classical deacetylase activity (desuccinylase/demalonylase/deglutarylase) and resists nicotinamide inhibition. Pro-disease in DM/CRC/HCC, protective in fatty liver/epilepsy/AKI. Few substrates validated in vivo; no clinical modulators.

SIRT6

genome-stability guardian — consistently protective Nucleus / chromatin

Protective face

  • Cancer suppressor: gastric (JAK2/STAT3, ferroptosis), CRC, ovarian, endometrial (survivin), glioma, HCC (hnRNP A1), pancreatic (KLF10); lung anti-Warburg (HIF-1α corepressor)
  • Cardiac hypertrophy (FoxO3 autophagy), fibrosis (TGF-β/Smad3), HF (TERT/TRF1), atherosclerosis, CAD, MI/R, hypertension (Nkx3.2–GATA5)
  • COPD, lung fibrosis (TGF-β1/Smad2), asthma (EMT regulation), ARDS (macrophage polarization)
  • NAFLD (ChREBP/SREBP-1c), HBV (cccDNA H3K9), cirrhosis, IBD (Rspo1), intestinal IRI
  • AD (diagnostic), ALS (SOD1-astrocyte), SCI, DM (PGC-1α, FoxO1), AKI, kidney fibrosis (MDL-800), vascular calcification, OA, osteoporosis, IDD

Harmful / dual face

  • Lung — snail-dependent KLF4 transrepression → EMT/metastasis (dual)
  • Obesity — overexpression exacerbates diet-induced obesity (STAT3 acetylation, POMC lowering)
  • AML — DNA-repair deficiencies synergize with NAMPT targeting (combination context)
  • The DDR/austerity program that prevents initiation keeps established tumors alive in therapy
Bottom line: the most unambiguously anti-cancer, pro-genome-stability sirtuin. Longevity/centenarian genetics interest (N308K/A313S). Direct activators emerging (UBCS039, MDL-800, MDL-801). Dual only in lung cancer and obesity.

SIRT7

under-studied wildcard — inconsistent roles Nucleus → nucleolus

Protective face

  • Cardiac hypertrophy (GATA4 deacetylation), atherosclerosis (VSMC Wnt/β-catenin)
  • Lung fibrosis — declined most in LF fibroblasts; decline has profibrotic effect (Smad3)
  • HBV — restricts transcription/replication via cccDNA H3 desuccinylation (dual with pathomechanism role)
  • IBD (KO susceptible to colitis), leukemia treatment-response biomarker (rises with response, falls on relapse)

Harmful face

  • Breast (depletion inhibits via p38/MAPK), HCC (ribosome biogenesis, poor survival), CRC (E-cadherin→EMT invasion), endometrial (invasiveness), glioma (ERK/STAT3)
  • NAFLD / alcoholic hepatitis — high expression harmful
  • Asthma airway remodeling (TGF-β1 smooth-muscle proliferation/migration)
  • Cardiac fibrosis (Smad2/ERK, inconsistent — KO also reported fibrotic)
  • AKI — SIRT7-KO mice protected; promotes tubular damage/kidney inflammation
Biomarker note: expression rises with positive leukemia treatment response and falls on relapse — monitoring utility rather than a fixed tumorigenic role. Nuclear/nucleolar functions (rRNA transcription, ribosome biogenesis, GATA4, cccDNA desuccinylation) suggest distinct biology awaiting exploration. Undrugged target.

Cross-cutting patterns & the bottom line

1 · Compartment predicts stance. Mitochondrial sirtuins (SIRT3/4/5) gate metabolic state — mostly protective with metabolic caveats; nuclear/chromatin sirtuins (SIRT6/7) gate genome stability and transcription — SIRT6 protective, SIRT7 oncogenic-lean.
2 · "Protective" ≠ safe in cancer. SIRT3's low-ROS guardian phenotype and SIRT6's DNA-repair austerity program both prevent initiation but sustain established tumors (radioresistance, chemoresistance, therapy-induced survival).
3 · SIRT1 is the dose-and-context paradox. Low/moderate SIRT1 protects (cardiac 2.5–7.5×, CRC hetero-KO c-Myc/glutamine), high/chronically active SIRT1 flips to pathology (cardiac 12.5×, chronic p53 suppression, chemoresistance).
4 · SIRT2 and SIRT7 are the harm-leaning outliers. SIRT2 opposes the SIRT1/3/6 axis (harmful in AD/PD/HD/stroke/asthma, protective in joints/kidney and several cancers); SIRT7 is ribosome-biogenesis-driven and mostly oncogenic, with protective roles confined to HBV/leukemia biomarker contexts.
5 · The glutamine and NAD+ hubs concentrate the leverage. SIRT4 (glutamine brake, tumor suppressor) and SIRT5 (glutaminolysis driver, pro-disease in CRC/HCC/DM) sit at the same anaplerotic node with opposite signs — and SIRT5's nicotinamide resistance keeps it active where SIRT1–3 are inhibited.
Caveat. Stance labels are synthesis-level, not absolute: within any single disease the same sirtuin can show opposing findings across studies, models, and stages (e.g., SIRT3 in OA, SIRT7 in cardiac fibrosis, SIRT1 in CRC). "Protective" vs "harmful" here means the dominant direction reported in the Wu et al. 2022 review — treat every cell as a context-dependent distribution, not a fixed sign. That is exactly why sirtuin-targeted therapy must be tissue-, stage-, and isoform-aware, and why NAD+ availability is emerging as the shared lever across all seven.
