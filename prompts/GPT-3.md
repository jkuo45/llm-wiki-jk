# GPT-3

GPT-3 (Generative Pre-trained Transformer 3) is a large language model developed by OpenAI, known for its ability to perform a wide variety of tasks through few-shot and zero-shot prompting. It consists of 175 billion parameters and served as a major breakthrough in the field of LLMs.
