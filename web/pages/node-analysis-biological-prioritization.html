Node-level Network Analysis for Biological Prioritization

Each entity in a knowledge graph carries a metric fingerprint — in- and out-degree, PageRank, betweenness, clustering, k-core, community — that can be read as a biological role. This page shows how those readings are computed in NetworkX, what they meant on real nodes in this vault, and which practices keep the numbers trustworthy.

01 · Framing

The per-node fingerprint is a role, not a score

Centrality is often treated as a ranking: higher is more important, pick the top ten. That is the wrong object for a directed, relation-typed biomedical graph. A source enzyme that spreads a vulnerability into many lipid nodes looks unimportant under PageRank and decisive under out-degree. A secretory program that sits on shortest paths between mitochondria, epigenetics, and inflammation looks ordinary by clustering and extraordinary by betweenness. The fingerprint is the pattern across metrics, read against the biology already in the vault.

In this corpus the fingerprint is attached in enrich_graph_metrics() and stored on every node of graph.json: degree, in_degree, out_degree, pagerank, betweenness_centrality, clustering_coefficient, k_core_number, community_size, community_name. Two pipeline choices make the numbers usable as biology rather than as co-occurrence artefacts.

  • Type-hub denylisting. Abstract categories (chemical, protein, enzyme, gene, and the rest of DENYLIST) are pruned before centrality. Otherwise a generic “protein” blob dominates every ranking and specific entities (ACSL4, GPX4, ferroptosis) disappear.
  • Directed graph, undirected metrics. In- and out-degree and PageRank run on the DiGraph. Betweenness, clustering, and k-core run on the undirected projection — the usual NetworkX pattern when direction is incomplete or when the question is module membership rather than causal flow.

Interpretation trap

Acid ceramidase vs Aging · graph bd1894acf7b72406

PageRank and out-degree answer opposite questions. PageRank rewards being pointed at by other important nodes. Out-degree marks a node that points outward. Acid ceramidase is a spreader (out-degree 17, PageRank 0.00078). Aging is an attractor (in-degree 35, PageRank 0.0076). Using either metric alone would invert the biological reading.

Literature context, not vault metrics

Degree–essentiality (Jeong et al., 2001), bottleneck proteins (Yu et al., 2007), and k-shell enrichment for essential proteins (Wuchty & Almaas, 2005) are the classical PPI justifications for reading these metrics as biology. This vault is a literature-derived knowledge graph, not a physical interactome: the same mathematics applies, but edges are typed relations with curator confidence, not binding events. Treat ranks as hypotheses to check against the source notes.

04 · Beyond the static fingerprint

Relation-aware methods already in the repo

scripts/04_node_analysis.py runs on the same graph.json and answers questions the per-node vector cannot: how two entities connect, whether they share a neighbourhood, whether a missing edge is plausible, and how far apart two pathways really are once low-confidence routes are down-weighted. 

uv run --with networkx --with scipy python3 scripts/04_node_analysis.py \
    --sources sirt1 sirt3 --targets adrenochrome

nx.all_shortest_paths · edge relation / confidence

Shortest-path multiplicity

Not only whether A reaches B, but the labelled chain (Acid ceramidase —promotes→ Lipid Peroxidation —drives→ Ferroptosisgraph bd1894acf7b72406) and the first-hop bridges shared across equally short paths.

Multiplicity is engineering redundancy read off the graph. When many equally short, relationally distinct chains connect a driver to an effector, the program is built to reroute: a block at any single hop is absorbed by parallel compensation rather than propagating failure, the signature of a resilient, evolvable module and a warning against one-node reasoning. Multiplicity of one is the converse — a genuine, rate-limiting choke point whose causative edge is the intervention target. Where only a few bridges link otherwise distant processes, read cross-talk: two modules coupled through scarce intermediaries, not a shared pathway.

Example: ask whether SIRT1 reaches NF-κB by one route or several — if three equally short chains (NAD+ competition, direct RelA deacetylation, a chromatin relay) connect them, boosting SIRT1 will be partially absorbed by parallel wiring rather than cleanly silencing NF-κB.

neighbour-set Jaccard

Neighbourhood similarity

Entities that share neighbours are mechanistic analogs or off-target twins. Low Jaccard with a high-degree partner is a distinct neighbourhood, not a restatement of the same module.

Neighbourhood overlap is a functional-signature prior: entities sharing interaction neighbourhoods are typically co-complexed, co-regulated, or paralogous, and therefore redundant under perturbation. High Jaccard thus flags redundancy and de-risks a target — interrupting one component is likely to register in its twin. The low-Jaccard case against a high-degree partner is where the insight hides: it marks genuinely distinct biology, the evidence that separates true functional twins from off-target hits.

Example: PARP1 and PARP2 share NAD+ turnover, PARP inhibitors, and DNA-repair partners — a high Jaccard therefore warns that a supposed PARP1-selective inhibitor is likely to nudge PARP2, a de-risking check before prioritisation.

nx.adamic_adar_index

Link prediction

Shared neighbours down-weighted by log-degree. Surfaces plausible missing edges as a hypothesis queue, not as facts. Requires manual literature check before a triple is written back.

Link prediction is a probabilistic expectation about relationships that exist in biology but aren't yet recorded in the graph. Two entities connected only through rare, otherwise-quiet intermediaries carry the strongest signal for a real but under-curated interaction — a shared neighbour is informative precisely because it is not a promiscuous hub. Use the queue as a curation mandate, not a finding: the mechanism that makes a pair plausible is the same one that manufactures false positives, so every call earns a literature check before it becomes a triple.

Example: if SIRT1 and an unconnected kinase share only a single rare scaffold with no curated edge, that pairing warrants a targeted literature search before being written off as unrelated.

scipy.linalg.eigh on the Laplacian

Spectral cut (Fiedler)

Algebraic connectivity λ2 and the Fiedler vector split the graph into two coherent subsystems. The script uses dense eigh because eigsh(which="SM") returns the trivial zero mode on a singular Laplacian and makes the Fiedler vector useless.

The Fiedler cut severs the network at its weakest functional seam, separating the two least-coupled subsystems — catabolic from anabolic programs, stress responses from housekeeping metabolism. Algebraic connectivity (λ2) quantifies how cleanly the graph resists that separation: values approaching zero expose an almost-fragmented network; larger values describe tightly interwoven wiring. Nodes stranded along the cut are the integrative bridges — regulators and metabolite shuttles both partitions depend on, and the most systemically consequential targets.

Example: on a senescence corpus the seam tends to fall between the lipid-peroxidation/secretory program and housekeeping metabolism, stranding bridges like Acid ceramidase squarely on the cut — the first place to look for cross-program regulators.

Laplacian pseudoinverse

Effective resistance

A metric-aware distance that down-weights long, low-confidence routes. Z-scored against a 300-node random sample. Self-resistance is a sanity check and should be ~0. Truncated SVD is not a valid commute-time proxy here; the script builds the pseudoinverse from thresholded eigenvalues.

Effective resistance is a robustness-weighted distance. Two nodes are electrically "close" not when one favourable path exists but when many redundant routes operate in parallel — the currency of signalling resilience. A target reached through abundant parallel wiring is correspondingly hard to disable, whereas a driver reached only by sparse, low-confidence connections is a fragile and therefore druggable link. Because the metric penalises long speculative chains, it answers a genuinely biological question: how far apart are two pathways once redundancy is taken seriously?

Example: Ferroptosis can sit electrically close to Inflammation despite no single dominant edge — many parallel routes mean low resistance; a driver reachable by only one thin, low-confidence chain is the fragile, druggable exception.

nx.pagerank(..., personalization=)

Personalized PageRank

Seed a node of interest and rank the rest of the graph by walk-mass reaching it (or from it, depending on orientation). This is the correct global-to-local move when the fingerprint has already named a backbone node.

Personalized PageRank recovers context-specific importance. Global centrality simply names hubs; a walk seeded on a process redistributes probability mass along its own wiring, ranking the entities that process actually drives — its downstream effectors and recurrent co-targets rather than the well-studied generalists. Intersecting the top ranks across several seeds is where convergence becomes convincing: the effectors shared by related processes are the robust, mechanism-relevant biology, distinct from the idiosyncratic neighbours of any single node.

Example: seeding PPR on Senescent cells and intersecting with seeds on Ferroptosis and SASP surfaces the shared effectors — lipid peroxidation, IL-6 — that generalist ranking would bury.

05 · Worked examples

Successful readings already in the graph

The five cases below are not illustrations of the algorithms. They are cases where the fingerprint recovered a role that the underlying documents already support — which is the validation a professional reader should demand before using the same reading on an unfamiliar node.

Acid ceramidase · graph bd1894acf7b72406

Acid ceramidase — the out-degree spreader

Degree 20, out-degree 17 / in-degree 3, PageRank 0.00078, betweenness 0.0083, k-core 4, community “Acid ceramidase” (size 18). The asymmetry marks a source: it pushes effects onto lipid and ferroptosis nodes rather than being acted upon. In the source document, elevated acid ceramidase in senescent cells reshapes membranes toward a PUFA-rich, pro-ferroptotic profile; knockdown or ARN14794 protects cells. Source: Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile and exacerbates sensitivity to ferroptosis in WI-38 replicative senescent cells (Soriano-Castell, Goujon, Dar, Currais & Maher, Cell Death and Disease, 2026; DOI 10.1038/s41419-026-09108-y). Note: src/notes/_link/_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex.md. The graph also carries a “Senolytic Drug Target” node pointing at it. High out-degree, mid betweenness, and named-community-anchor is the signature of a spreading senolytic or senomorphic candidate.

SASP · graph bd1894acf7b72406

SASP — convergent and divergent secretory hub

Degree 101, in-degree 56 / out-degree 45, PageRank 0.0074, betweenness 0.060, k-core 6. Highest betweenness in the surveyed set: SASP sits on shortest paths between mitochondria, epigenetics, inflammation, and senescence. It is a sink for upstream triggers and a source for paracrine cytokines (IL-6, IL-8) that, in the acid-ceramidase document, induce the enzyme in bystander cells. High in, high out, top betweenness: a master program. Perturbing it reverberates network-wide — which is both the therapeutic appeal and the on-target risk.

Aging · graph bd1894acf7b72406

Aging — PageRank and the core backbone

Degree 48, in-degree 35, out-degree 13, PageRank 0.0076 (highest of the sample), betweenness 0.046, k-core 6. PageRank is high because Aging is connected to other important nodes, not merely to many nodes. k-core 6 places it in the densest mutually reinforcing shell with SASP and Senescent cells. Top PageRank plus inner-core membership is the “common soil” reading: downstream disease communities attach here.

GPX4 · graph bd1894acf7b72406

GPX4, inducible NOS, Cataract — clustering = 1.0

A clustering coefficient of 1.0 means every neighbour of the node is connected to every other neighbour: a redox complex, an NO cascade, an AGE-cross-linking cluster. GPX4 also sits at k-core 2, consistent with a small clique rather than the global backbone. Contrast Acid ceramidase at clustering ≈ 0.04: a connector. The two patterns are complementary druggability strategies — disable the module, or cut the bridge.

k-core 6 backbone · graph bd1894acf7b72406

The k-core 6 backbone

Nodes at k-core 6 — Aging, SASP, Senescent cells — form the resilient inner machinery of the senescence/aging axis. Everything else hangs off it. k-core decomposition is the fastest “essential versus incidental” ranking in the fingerprint, and the right filter for deciding which nodes merit a deep entity note.

06 · From reading to triage

A rule layer on top of the fingerprint

Once the metrics are trusted, a small classifier turns them into roles that a target-ID workflow can sort on. These rules are proposed, not yet emitted into nodes.json. The hits listed on each card are the worked examples that would fire today.

Spreader

out_degree ≫ in_degree

Control point that broadcasts into many effectors. Senolytic / senomorphic candidate when the community is a damage program.

Master regulator

high out_degree ∧ high pagerank

Both a source and an endorsed attractor. None of the three fully worked nodes match both clauses at once — a useful negative control.

Bottleneck

betweenness in the top decile

Systemic leverage, and systemic side-effect risk. SASP is the archetype in this graph.

Module member

clustering > 0.5

Participant in a cohesive mechanism. Strategy: disrupt the complex, not the bridge.

Core backbone

k_core ≥ 5

Inner shell of the senescence / aging axis. First-class entity notes; seeds for Personalized PageRank sweeps.

Periphery

k_core == 1

Contextual leaves. Keep as supporting evidence; do not spend a deep-dive note first.

Composite senolytic score — a checkable next step

Acid ceramidase · graph bd1894acf7b72406

Combine out-degree (spreading control), betweenness (leverage), community-bridge status (neighbours in a different Leiden community), and low-to-mid clustering (module disruptability versus bridge-cutting). Rank, write the top N to a task output, and require that Acid ceramidase surface near the top. If it does not, the score is wrong, not the biology.

07 · NetworkX

NetworkX

NetworkX is a Python package for the creation, manipulation, and study of the structure, dynamics, and functions of complex networks. It provides tools for the study of the structure and dynamics of social, biological, and infrastructure networks, and a standard programming interface and graph implementation that is suitable for many applications.

This briefing leans on the biological half of that remit. A knowledge graph of biomedical entities and the typed relations between them — an enzyme remodelling a membrane, a cytokine driving inflammation, a senescent cell broadcasting a secretory program — is precisely the kind of complex network NetworkX was built to describe. Centrality, shortest paths, community detection, and core decomposition are first-class operations here, not bolt-ons, which is why the same calls that map a social network also triage a senescence axis.

Why a graph library, not a dataframe

The questions that matter are topological: which node sits on the most shortest paths, which cluster is internally cohesive, which shell of the network is resilient. NetworkX answers them directly on the graph object, so the fingerprint developed in the next sections is computed rather than hand-derived.

NetworkX is the right library for graphs of this size (thousands of nodes, not full STRING). The failure mode is not performance. It is an unstated representation choice: directed versus undirected, weight as strength versus weight as distance, whole graph versus giant component, topology versus annotation bias. The list below is the working standard for this vault and for PPI / GRN analysis more generally.

  1. General practice

    State the graph you actually computed on

    Keep a DiGraph for in/out-degree and PageRank. Project with G.to_undirected() for clustering, betweenness, and k-core. Multi-node analytics in 04_node_analysis.py further restrict to the giant component after dropping self-loops. Path metrics on a disconnected graph are otherwise undefined or silently harmonic-flavoured. 

    G_und = G.to_undirected()
cc = max(nx.connected_components(G_und), key=len)
U = G_und.subgraph(cc).copy()

  2. This vault

    Prune type hubs before any centrality

    Study bias in PPI (TP53 accumulates interactions because it is studied) has a knowledge-graph analogue: ontology classes collect edges from every instance. The DENYLIST in the rebuild script is the correct first filter. Do not interpret a high-degree “protein” node as a target.

  3. General practice

    Do not pass a confidence score as a betweenness distance

    PageRank wants a strength: high confidence should carry more walk mass (weight="weight"). Betweenness and other shortest-path metrics want a distance: high confidence should be a short hop. Invert first. The current rebuild stores weight = confidence_score after centrality, so the stored fingerprint is unweighted topology. That is a documented limitation, not a secret. 

    for u, v, d in G.edges(data=True):
    d["weight"] = d.get("confidence_score", 0.7)
    d["distance"] = 1.0 / max(d["weight"], 1e-9)

pr = nx.pagerank(G, alpha=0.85, weight="weight")
bt = nx.betweenness_centrality(U, weight="distance", normalized=True)

  4. General practice

    Never rank on one metric

    Degree predicts essentiality in classical yeast PPI (Jeong et al., 2001) but misses bottlenecks whose degree is ordinary (Yu et al., 2007). The worked examples in this vault exist because the fingerprint disagrees with itself in informative ways. Combine: high out-degree plus mid betweenness (spreader); high in and out plus top betweenness (nexus); top PageRank plus inner k-core (backbone).

  5. General practice

    Test against a degree-preserving null

    High-degree nodes score high on almost everything. A configuration-model ensemble (nx.configuration_model / directed variants, 100–1,000 draws) gives z-scores or empirical p-values. Effective-resistance in 04_node_analysis.py already z-scores commute distance against a random-node sample of 300; the same habit should apply to betweenness and clustering before a node is called a bottleneck.

  6. General practice

    Approximate betweenness; do not skip it

    Brandes is O(nm). Fine on this vault. On a filtered human interactome, pass k= (sampled sources) and a seed. Prefer that approximation to dropping the metric. Restricting to the k-core or the giant component is the other legitimate speedup. 

    bt = nx.betweenness_centrality(U, k=100, seed=1, normalized=True)

  7. This vault

    Read clustering and k-core as complementary, not redundant

    Clustering is local transitivity. k-core is a nested global peel. GPX4 can be a perfect triangle (C = 1, k = 2) and still sit outside the senescence backbone (k = 6). Inner cores often coincide with complexes in PPI; here they coincide with the aging/SASP program. Report both the core-number distribution and the size of the main core.

  8. General practice

    Seed PageRank when the question is local

    Global PageRank answers “important in the whole graph.” Target prioritization from a process of interest is Personalized PageRank: personalization={seed: 1.0}, alpha 0.85. Sweeping PPR from each k-core-6 node and intersecting the top ranks is the operational next step proposed below.

  9. General practice

    Pin versions, seeds, and the edge list

    PPI and knowledge graphs change with database version and confidence threshold. Record NetworkX version, the denylist, the giant-component rule, RANDOM_SEED = 1, and the source-file provenance on edges. Export GraphML / GEXF if a reviewer needs Cytoscape; do not rely on NetworkX drawing for publication figures of this scale.

What this vault already does well

Directed in/out split; undirected projection for module metrics; type-hub denylist; Leiden communities with stable labels; giant-component restriction and a random-node null for commute distance; Personalized PageRank with a documented alpha. The main gap versus PPI best practice is passing weights into PageRank and inverted distances into betweenness, plus a configuration-model envelope around the static fingerprint itself.
