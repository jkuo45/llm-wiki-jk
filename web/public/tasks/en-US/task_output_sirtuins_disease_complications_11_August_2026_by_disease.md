# Sirtuins in Health and Disease — Complication-by-Complication Excerpt Compilation

> Excerpts from `_document_ - sirtuins in health and disease s41392-022-01257-8.md` (Wu et al. 2022), organized around complications/diseases and the SIRTs implicated in each.
> Date: 11_August_2026
> Method: Manual extraction of the "Regulatory role of SIRTs in human diseases" sections (cancer, CVD, respiratory, digestive, nervous, endocrine, urogenital, motor system, aging).

## Overview

Seven mammalian sirtuins (SIRT1–7) are expressed aberrantly in almost all disease states, acting as **tumor suppressors and/or oncogenes**, and as **protective or harmful factors**, depending on genetic context, tissue, tumor type, and disease stage. Each disease below is annotated with: (a) the SIRT expression pattern reported, (b) prognostic/biomarker associations, and (c) the mechanistic role of specific SIRTs.

## Cancer

General context: SIRTs are aberrantly expressed in almost all cancer types via mechanisms involving cancer metabolism, genome stability, and the tumor microenvironment.[3] Their functions are characterized as tumor suppressor and/or oncogene depending on genetic context and tumor type/stage.[392] SIRTs also regulate the response of tumors to chemotherapy.[393]

### [[Breast Cancer|Breast cancer (BC)]]
- BC is the most common malignancy worldwide and the fifth leading cause of cancer-related deaths.[394]
- **Expression**: SIRT2 and SIRT4 downregulated;[395][396] SIRT1 and SIRT7 upregulated in BC tissues vs. adjacent/normal tissue.[357][397]
- **Prognosis**: Increased SIRT2/SIRT4 ↔ longer overall survival;[395][396] increased SIRT1/SIRT7 ↔ poor prognosis.[398][399]
- **Role**: Generally tumor suppressors but may act as promoters.
  - SIRT1: regulates [[EMT]]-associated programming → BC invasion/metastasis in triple-negative BC;[401] yet suppresses BC metastasis by reducing EMT in nude mice.[402] Loss of SIRT1 destabilizes EMT inducer PRRX1, disinhibits KLF4, activates ALDH1 → cancer stem cells → metastatic reversion.[403] SIRT1 represses ERα-mediated proliferative response to estrogens;[405] upregulates DNA polymerase δ1 (p125) promoting MCF-7 proliferation/migration;[397] promotes metadherin-driven progression/chemoresistance.[406]
  - SIRT2: binary. Tumor suppressor via promoting BRCA1–BARD1 heterodimerization → homologous recombination;[407] promoter via deacetylating Slug → stabilizes it → basal-like BC tumorigenesis/invasion.[409]
  - SIRT3: disrupts ERα–p53 interaction → decreased proliferation, colony formation, migration.[410]
  - SIRT4: tumor-suppressive by negatively regulating SIRT1 via repressing glutamine metabolism (mitochondrial–nuclear crosstalk).[411]
  - SIRT7: depletion inhibits tumor growth via activating p38/MAPK signaling.[357]
- **Drug resistance**: SIRT1 causes tamoxifen resistance in ER-α+ BC via multidrug resistance protein 2 upregulation (FoxO1 deacetylation);[412] SIRT1 inhibition augments paclitaxel/doxorubicin cytotoxicity;[412] SIRT3 overexpression reduces tamoxifen sensitivity (MTR-3 line);[357][413] SIRT4 enhances tamoxifen sensitivity via STAT3 inhibition.

### [[Lung Cancer|Lung cancer (LC)]]
- LC is the leading cause of cancer-related deaths and second most diagnosed cancer; NSCLC most common.[394]
- **Expression/biomarkers**: Significant SIRT expression differences between NSCLC and non-tumor tissue → diagnostic biomarkers.[414][–][416] Serum SIRT3 distinguishes LC patients from healthy (AUC 0.918, cutoff 3.12, sensitivity 86.4%, specificity 94%).[416]
- **Prognosis**: High SIRT1–3 expression ↔ poor survival in NSCLC.[414][415]
- **Role**: Conflicting.
  - SIRT1: tumor suppressor (suppressed by SNHG10, protects against osteopontin-induced NF-κB p65 acetylation/EMT);[420][421] but promotes lung adenocarcinoma growth via circ_0001946 → Wnt/β-catenin.[422]
  - SIRT2: suppresses NSCLC migration by deacetylating AKR1C1, inhibiting STAT3;[379] but deacetylates phosphoglycerate mutase K100 → NADPH → accelerated tumor growth.[423]
  - SIRT6: anticarcinogenic via CHD4 coordination → chromatin relaxation/DNA repair;[424] pro-carcinogenic via snail-dependent transrepression of KLF4 → EMT/metastasis.[425]
- **Chemo/radioresistance**: SIRT1 promotes cisplatin resistance via VEGF-A;[426] SIRT2 upregulation increases cisplatin sensitivity;[253][427] SIRT3 reduces cisplatin resistance via FoxO3/CDT1 axis;[253][427] SIRT3 promotes DNA repair/radioresistance via ATM–Chk2;[428] SIRT6 inhibits glycolysis → enhances radiosensitivity.[428]

### [[Hepatocellular Carcinoma|Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)]]
- HCC is the sixth most common cancer and third-leading cause of cancer-related mortality.[394]
- **Expression**: Non-mitochondrial SIRT1, SIRT2, SIRT6 higher;[429][–][431] mitochondrial SIRT3–5 lower in HCC vs. normal liver.[432][–][434]
- **Prognosis**: High SIRT1/SIRT7 ↔ poor survival; low SIRT4 ↔ decreased survival.[434][–][436]
- **Role**:
  - SIRT4: tumor suppressor via inhibiting glutamine metabolism.[434]
  - SIRT5: dual — suppresses immune evasion/bile-acid-metabolism-driven HCC;[438] but promotes growth/metastasis via OXPHOS→glycolysis reprogramming.[439]
  - SIRT1/SIRT6: deacetylate hnRNP A1 to suppress glycolysis/growth;[440] SIRT6 (stabilized by USP48) attenuates glycolysis/metabolic reprogramming.[441]
  - SIRT4: upregulates p16/p21, suppresses CyclinB1/Cdc2 and Cdc25c → apoptosis.[442]
- **Therapy**: SIRT3 modulation via CDK4/6 inhibition may enhance sorafenib combination therapy;[443] SIRT3 downregulates GST-π1 → enhances chemo/sorafenib-induced apoptosis;[252] SIRT6 depletion downregulates multidrug resistance protein 1 → enhanced chemosensitivity.[444]

### [[Colorectal Cancer|Colorectal cancer (CRC)]]
- CRC ranks third in incidence worldwide and second most common cause of cancer deaths.[394]
- **Expression**: SIRT1/SIRT7 increased;[389][445] SIRT2/SIRT4/SIRT6 decreased in CRC tissue.[446][–][448]
- **Prognosis**: SIRT5 overexpression ↔ poor prognosis; SIRT6 ↔ improved survival.[448][449]
- **Role**:
  - SIRT1: dose-dependent dual role via glutamine metabolism/apoptosis — heterozygous deletion induces c-Myc → glutamine metabolism → proliferation/autophagy; homozygous deletion triggers apoptosis, reduces cancer formation.[450]
  - SIRT2: IDH1 deacetylation inhibits liver metastasis.[381]
  - SIRT4: upregulates E-cadherin, suppresses proliferation/migration/invasion via glutamine metabolism inhibition.[140]
  - SIRT5: enhances glutaminolysis (deglutarylation-dependent) → colorectal carcinogenesis;[449] deacetylates lactate dehydrogenase B → hyperactive autophagy → tumorigenesis.[310]
- **Drug resistance**: SIRT3 overexpression improves drug resistance via SOD2/PGC-1α;[451] SIRT4 increases sensitivity to 5-fluorouracil by inhibiting cell cycle;[447] FoxQ1-mediated SIRT1 upregulation → β-catenin nuclear translocation → radioresistance.[452]

### [[Gastric Cancer|Gastric cancer (GC)]]
- Fifth most frequently diagnosed cancer (5.6% incidence) and fourth most common cause of cancer death (7.7%).[394]
- **Expression**: SIRT1 upregulated; SIRT4/SIRT6 downregulated — low expression negatively correlates with tumor size/pathological grade → poor prognosis.[383][453]
- **Role**: SIRT1 depletion promotes progression via STAT3/MMP-13;[331] SIRT4 inhibits proliferation/migration/invasion via EMT; SIRT6 inhibits JAK2/STAT3 pathway → suppresses growth.
- **Drug resistance**: SIRT6 silencing overcomes sorafenib resistance by promoting ferroptosis.[454]

### [[Pancreatic Cancer|Pancreatic cancer (PC)]]
- PC ranks as the seventh leading cause of cancer-related deaths worldwide.[394]
- **Role**: SIRT5 expression directly correlated with favorable prognosis — loss promotes GOT1 acetylation → proliferation via glutamine/glutathione metabolism;[455] KLF10-upregulated SIRT6 influences glycolysis, EMT, distant metastasis.[456]
- **Drug resistance**: SIRT1 facilitates chemoresistance by regulating adaptive response to chemotherapy-induced stress.[457]

### [[Ovarian Cancer|Ovarian cancer (OC)]]
- One of the most aggressive female malignancies with poor prognosis.[394]
- **Expression**: SIRT1–3 and SIRT6 significantly decreased; SIRT5 significantly increased.[458][–][460]
- **Prognosis**: High SIRT2/SIRT5–7 ↔ favorable survival; high SIRT1/SIRT4 ↔ poor survival.[458]
- **Role**: SIRT1 suppresses HMGB1 expression/acetylation → inhibits migration, invasion, angiogenesis;[461] MHY2245 (SIRT1 inhibitor) blocks pyruvate kinase M2/mTOR → antitumor;[462] SIRT3 overexpression suppresses metastasis via Twist-downregulation EMT inhibition;[463] SIRT2 overexpression enhances cisplatin sensitivity;[465] SIRT5 promotes cisplatin resistance via Nrf2/HO-1 ROS-dependent DNA-damage suppression.[460]

### [[Endometrial Cancer|Endometrial cancer (EC)]]
- Most common gynecological cancer in high-income countries; incidence rising.[466]
- **Role**: SIRT1 elevated — promotes autophagy/proliferation by reducing LC3 acetylation;[272] SIRT2 increased in most EC cell lines — promotes proliferation, inhibits apoptosis;[467] SIRT6 tumor suppressor — represses survivin → inhibits AN3CA/KLE proliferation;[468] SIRT1 promotes growth and cisplatin resistance;[469] SIRT2 promotes stemness and MEK/ERK signaling while repressing chemosensitivity.[470]

### [[Cervical Cancer|Cervical cancer (CC)]]
- One of the most severe and prevalent female malignancies.[394]
- **Expression/prognosis**: SIRT2 decreased in CC tissue; negatively correlated with tumor size and lymph node metastasis → favorable survival.[471]
- **Role**: SIRT1 overexpressed in HPV-infected CC cells, correlated with poor outcomes — nullifies AIM2 inflammasome-mediated immunity;[472] SIRT3 promotes invasion/metastasis by reprogramming fatty acid synthesis (ACC1 upregulation, de novo lipogenesis).[473]

### [[Glioma|Glioma]]
- Most common and malignant primary CNS tumor, especially glioblastoma.[474]
- **Expression**: SIRT1/SIRT7 upregulated; SIRT3/SIRT6 downregulated vs. normal/adjacent brain tissue.[475][–][478]
- **Prognosis**: Higher SIRT1 or SIRT3 ↔ worse prognosis; SIRT5 downregulation ↔ shorter survival in glioblastoma.[475][477][479]
- **Role**: SIRT1 tumor suppressor (PAK1 K420 deacetylation hinders autophagy/glioblastoma growth);[480] SIRT6 suppresses growth via apoptosis, oxidative-stress inhibition, JAK2/STAT3 inhibition;[478] SIRT3 promotes viability by stabilizing Ku70–Bax interaction;[477] SIRT7 promotes proliferation/invasion via ERK and STAT3.[476]
- **Resistance**: SIRT1 inhibition increases temozolomide sensitivity via ROS generation.[475]

### [[Leukemia|Leukemia]]
- Malignant clonal disease of hematopoietic stem cells.[482]
- **Role**: SIRT1 promotes T-ALL progression via CDK2 deacetylation → p27 phosphorylation/degradation;[483] SIRT2 overexpressed in primary AML blasts; SIRT2 activation by NAMPT reduces proliferation, induces apoptosis via Akt/GSK-3β/β-catenin;[335] SIRT2 inhibition suppresses T-ALL growth/engraftment via diminished LMO2 deacetylation;[484] SIRT1 knockdown sensitizes AML to tyrosine kinase inhibitor treatment;[485] SIRT6 DNA-repair deficiencies synergize with NAMPT targeting in AML;[486] SIRT7 as biomarker for treatment response in myeloid stem cell disorders.[487]

## Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs)

General context: CVDs are the major cause of mortality worldwide.[489][490] SIRT1, SIRT3, SIRT6 play protective roles in cardiac fibrosis, heart failure, atherosclerosis, and MI/R injury.[493][494]

### [[Cardiac Hypertrophy|Cardiac hypertrophy]]
- **SIRT1**: Inconsistent — overexpression alleviates Ang II-induced hypertrophy (reduced apoptosis, promoted autophagy);[498][499] but also exacerbates via Akt/PDK1 membrane localization.[501] Dose-dependent: low (2.5×) or moderate (7.5×) overexpression attenuated hypertrophy; high (12.5×) increased it.[502]
- **SIRT3**: Protective — reduced in Ang II-induced hypertrophy;[505] overexpression protects, silencing exacerbates;[505] promotes autophagy via FoxO1 deacetylation;[507] FoxO3a-dependent antioxidant defense;[164] reduces PARP-1 acetylation/activity.[508]
- **SIRT6**: Protective — inhibits isoproterenol-induced hypertrophy via autophagy (FoxO3 nuclear retention via Akt attenuation);[314] decreases p300 → NF-κB p65 acetylation;[510] blocks IGF–Akt signaling via c-Jun;[511] STAT3 suppression.[512]
- **SIRT2/SIRT5/SIRT7**: Protective — SIRT2 deacetylates NFATc2;[513] SIRT5 prevents age-related hypertrophy;[515] SIRT7 deacetylates GATA4.[516]
- **SIRT4**: Adverse — aggravates Ang II-induced hypertrophy by inhibiting MnSOD activity.[517]

### [[Cardiac Fibrosis|Cardiac fibrosis]]
- **SIRT1/SIRT3**: Resveratrol activation attenuates fibrosis via TGF-β/Smad3 pathway inhibition.[519][520]
- **SIRT6**: Systemic KO induces fibrosis via TGF-β/Smad3 activation;[521] prevents Ang II-mediated fibrosis via AMPK–ACE2 signaling.[522]
- **SIRT2**: Overexpression protects (promotes AMPK via LKB1 deacetylation).[514]
- **SIRT5**: KO increases fibrosis vs. age-matched WT.[515]
- **SIRT4**: Contributes to fibrosis — global KO confers resistance to Ang II infusion.[517]
- **SIRT7**: Inconsistent — enhanced expression/phosphorylation promotes fibrosis via Smad2/ERK;[523] but SIRT7 KO also reported to result in fibrosis.[265]

### [[Heart Failure|Heart failure]]
- Affects ~64.3 million people worldwide.[391][525]
- **SIRT1**: Decreased in advanced HF hearts;[528][529] attenuates oxidative stress/apoptosis via MnSOD/thioredoxin1/Bcl-xL upregulation, p53 acetylation decrease, FoxO1 nuclear translocation;[528] reduces cardiomyocyte apoptosis via NF-κB p65/miR-155/BDNF;[532] restores SERCA2a activity via K492 deacetylation.[533]
- **SIRT6**: Decreased in chronic HF;[527] overexpression increases survival of TAC-induced HF mice via telomerase upregulation (TERT, TRF1).[540]
- **SIRT2/SIRT4/SIRT5/SIRT7**: Limited studies.[541][542]

### [[Atherosclerosis|Atherosclerosis]]
- Chronic inflammatory disease involving LDL, endothelial dysfunction, immune infiltration, plaque formation.[494][543]
- **SIRT1**: Protective — endothelial overexpression protects in ApoE KO mice (eNOS activation → inhibited endothelial apoptosis);[545] SRT1720 activation ameliorates endothelial dysfunction (COX-2, reduced oxidative stress/inflammation);[546] VSMC DNA-damage protection via NBS1;[544] reduces Lox-1-mediated foam cell formation via NF-κB suppression.[548]
- **SIRT2**: Decreases plaque formation in LDL receptor-deficient mice via macrophage polarization.[552]
- **SIRT3**: Associated with endothelial apoptosis in atherosclerosis rats;[553] SIRT3/SOD2 signaling (circ_0003423) protects HUVECs from oxLDL-induced dysfunction.[554]
- **SIRT4**: Suppresses PI3K/Akt/NF-κB → relieves oxLDL-induced HUVEC injury.[555]
- **SIRT6**: Protects against endothelial dysfunction, VSMC senescence, atherosclerosis;[201][556][557] reduces oxLDL uptake in RAW macrophages via scavenger receptor 1 downregulation.[558]
- **SIRT7**: Regulates VSMC proliferation/migration via Wnt/β-catenin.[559]

### [[Coronary Artery Disease|Coronary artery disease (CAD)]]
- **SIRT1**: Protective — genetic SIRT1 polymorphisms associated with CAD risk;[563] expression reduced in CAD patients;[564] downregulation linked to acetylated p53 in CAD monocytes;[565] lncRNA C2dat1 promotes SIRT1 → suppresses miR-34a → VSMC proliferation/migration.[567]
- **SIRT3/SIRT6**: Genetic polymorphisms associated with CAD risk (mechanism unelucidated).[563]

### [[Myocardial Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury|Myocardial ischemia/reperfusion (MI/R) injury]]
- **SIRT1**: Protective — cardiac-specific KO increases infarct size;[572] overexpression upregulates FoxO1/MnSOD antioxidant pathways, downregulates caspase-3/Bax pro-apoptotic pathways;[572] miR-132 inhibition → PGC-1α/Nrf2;[573] lncRNA Oip5-as1 sponges miR-29a via SIRT1/AMPK/PGC-1α.[574]
- **SIRT6**: Protective — FoxO3α antioxidant defense;[575] attenuates CHMP2B accumulation.[576]
- **SIRT3**: Deficiency exacerbates MI/R injury.[577]
- **SIRT4**: Downregulated after MI/R; overexpression decreases infarct size (mitochondrial preservation, reduced apoptosis).[578]
- **SIRT5**: Loss increases infarct size via succinylation modulation.[579]
- **SIRT2/SIRT7**: Few studies.[490]

### [[Hypertension|Hypertension]]
- ≥140/90 mmHg, ~1.39 billion affected (2010).[581]
- **SIRT1**: VSMC overexpression attenuates Ang II-induced hypertension;[582] activation attenuates Klotho-deficiency arterial stiffness via AMPKα/eNOS;[584] NAMPT-mediated overexpression alleviates Ang II ROS production.[585]
- **SIRT3**: Overexpression attenuates Ang II and DOCA-salt-induced hypertension;[583] diminished SIRT3/redox inactivation → SOD2 inactivation → hypertension pathogenesis.[586]
- **Complications**: Decreased urinary SIRT1 as non-invasive biomarker of early renal damage;[587] SIRT3 alleviates hypertensive renal injury by suppressing EMT;[588] endothelial SIRT6 deletion → enhanced BP, cardiorenal injury via Nkx3.2–GATA5.[589]

## Respiratory system diseases

### [[COPD|COPD]]
- Fourth leading cause of death worldwide.[593][–][595]
- **SIRT1**: Substantially decreased in COPD/emphysema lungs and CS-exposed rodent lungs;[597] anti-inflammatory/anti-apoptotic/antioxidant (NF-κB regulation);[598][599] deacetylates FoxO3 and p53 (senescence/apoptosis);[597][599] SRT1720 inhibits LPS-induced cytokine release from COPD PBMCs.[600]
- **SIRT6**: Reduced expression → COPD via insufficient autophagy (cellular senescence) during CS exposure;[601] overexpression weakens autophagy via IGF–Akt–mTOR.[601]
- **SIRT3**: Inhibits airway epithelial mitochondrial oxidative stress → attenuates COPD progression.[603]

### [[Pulmonary Fibrosis|Lung fibrosis (LF)]]
- ~45% of global deaths attributed to fibrosis.[607]
- **SIRT1**: Loss involved in pathogenesis; activation promotes alveolar epithelial type 2 self-renewal/differentiation in IPF patients;[609] expression controversial (increased in some IPF studies);[610] activation/overexpression attenuates LF via canonical TGF-β1/p300 signaling.[611]
- **SIRT3**: Deficiency within aging lung promotes fibrotic response via TGF-β1;[612] deficiency promotes LF via AEC mitochondrial DNA damage/apoptosis;[614] overexpression ameliorates asbestos-induced pulmonary fibrosis.[615]
- **SIRT6**: Inhibits TGF-β-induced cellular senescence;[616] inactivates TGF-β1/Smad2 signaling;[617] prevents myofibroblast differentiation (TGF-β1/Smad2 + NF-κB);[618] inhibits EMT during IPF (TGF-β1/Smad3).[619]
- **SIRT7**: Decreased most in LF fibroblasts; decline has profibrotic effect via Smad3 changes.[620]

### [[Asthma|Asthma]]
- ~300 million affected worldwide.[621][622]
- **SIRT1**: Decreased in severe asthma;[625] anti-inflammatory (IL-6 regulation via Akt);[626][627] inhibits IL-9-producing CD4+ T-cell differentiation via mTOR–HIF-1α/glycolysis;[628] miR-138-5p/SIRT1 axis blocks NF-κB in asthmatic mice.[621]
- **SIRT2**: Enhances allergic asthmatic inflammation; pharmacologic ablation attenuates, genetic overexpression exaggerates phenotype;[629] aggravates via Th2 responses and macrophage polarization.[630]
- **SIRT3**: Upregulation reduces bronchial epithelial apoptosis and airway inflammation.[631]
- **SIRT6**: Upregulated in asthmatic bronchial epithelial cells; ameliorates airway remodeling via EMT regulation.[633][634]
- **SIRT7**: Upregulated; promotes airway remodeling via TGF-β1-induced airway smooth muscle proliferation/migration.[633][635]

### [[Acute Lung Injury|Lung injury (ALI)]]
- **SIRT1**: Protective — KO mice highly susceptible to sepsis-induced lung injury (NF-κB);[641] resveratrol reduces ALI in LPS sepsis model;[637] but EX-527 (SIRT1 inhibitor) suppresses mTOR → alleviates endotoxemia ALI → SIRT1 may be detrimental in some conditions.[642]
- **SIRT3**: Promotes MnSOD expression → protective in hyperoxia-induced ALI;[643] SIRT3-deficient mice develop more severe ALI (NLRP3 inflammasome activation in macrophages).[63]
- **SIRT6**: Regulates macrophage polarization → alleviates sepsis-induced ARDS (autophagy-dependent and -independent).[644]
- **External factors**: HDAC inhibition by SIRT1-silencing RNA attenuates NAMPT in ventilator-induced lung injury;[645] resveratrol reduces paraquat-induced lung injury via SIRT1/Nrf2.[646]

### [[COVID-19|COVID-19]]
- **SIRT1**: Unbalanced p53/SIRT1 axis impacts lymphocyte homeostasis; persistently activated p53 due to low SIRT1;[649] increased SIRT1 may alleviate pathogenesis.
- **SIRT3**: Serum levels associated with clinical outcome/prognosis; markedly lower in severe vs. mild/moderate patients.[650]

## Digestive system diseases

### [[Non-alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease|Fatty liver diseases (FLDs) — including NAFLD]]
- NAFLD most common liver disease (25% worldwide prevalence).[658][659]
- **Expression**: Decreased SIRT1–3, SIRT5, SIRT6 in NAFLD/fibrosis patients.[660][–][662]
- **SIRT1**: Protective — deacetylates ChREBP and SREBP-1c → maintains hepatic lipid homeostasis;[5][136][663][664] antagonizes liver fibrosis by blocking hepatic stellate cell activation;[655][665] but harmful in alcoholic hepatitis (myeloid SIRT1/SIRT7 prevent p-FoxO3, cause apoptosis defect);[224] intestinal SIRT1 harmful by intensifying hepatic ferroptosis/inflammation via gut microbiota imbalance.[678]
- **SIRT6**: Hepatocyte-specific KO → elevated fibrosis/oxidative stress in NASH mice;[158][669] deacetylates ChREBP/SREBP-1c.[663][664]
- **SIRT2**: Prevents NAFLD by deacetylating hepatocyte nuclear factor 4α;[670] deleterious in hepatic fibrosis via SIRT2/ERK/c-Myc axis.[675]
- **SIRT3**: Improves mitochondrial function via deacetylation of mitochondrial trifunctional proteins and LCAD;[671][672] but liver-specific knockdown alleviated alcoholic feeding-induced injury (improved autophagy).[676][679]
- **SIRT5**: Systematic KO → impaired medium-chain fatty acid oxidation → periportal macrovascular steatosis.[673]
- **SIRT4**: Upregulation inhibits HFD-induced lipid accumulation/inflammation/fibrogenesis via SIRT4/Smad4 axis;[662][674] but harmful in NAFLD by deacetylating/destabilizing mitochondrial trifunctional protein-α.[661]
- **SIRT7**: High expression in alcoholic hepatitis/NAFLD → harmful.[661][675][676]

### [[Liver Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury|Liver ischemia/reperfusion injury (IRI)]]
- **SIRT1**: Decreased after IRI;[280] high levels → superior survival in human liver transplants;[681] suppresses mitochondrial dysfunction via MFN2;[280] overexpression alleviates autophagy depletion/inflammation.[281][682][683]
- **SIRT3**: Protective — systematic Takeda G protein-coupled receptor 5 KO suppresses SIRT3 → proinflammatory macrophages → exacerbated liver injury.[684]
- **SIRT2**: Detrimental — deacetylates MAPK phosphatase-1 → activates MAPK → augments inflammatory response/cell death.[685]

### [[Hepatitis B Virus|HBV infection]]
- Affects over 250 million chronic carriers, >800,000 deaths annually.[686]
- **SIRT3/SIRT4/SIRT6**: Downregulated in HBV-positive patients;[442][687][688] SIRT3/SIRT6 inhibit HBV replication via H3K9 deacetylation on viral cccDNA;[687][688] HBV X protein transfection suppresses SIRT4.[442]
- **SIRT1/SIRT2/SIRT5/SIRT7**: Slightly elevated mRNA in HBV-infected hepatocytes → global histone hypoacetylation → HBV-induced pathomechanism;[689] resveratrol (SIRT1 agonist) activates HBV transcription, while sirtinol and EX-527 (SIRT1 inhibitors) exhibit anti-HBV activity;[690] SIRT7 restricts HBV transcription/replication via cccDNA H3 desuccinylation.[691]

### Other liver diseases
- **Acute liver failure**: SIRT1 protective via HIF-1α deacetylation → reduced ROS;[692] quercetin/SRT1720 beneficial.[696]
- **HCV**: Elevated SIRT1 anti-aging effect on senescent CD4+ T cells;[697] HCV core protein downregulates SIRT1 → sinusoidal dysfunction;[698] HCV core 1b-induced steatosis alleviated in liver-specific SIRT1 KO mice (PPAR-γ2 downregulation).[699]
- **Autoimmune hepatitis**: SIRT1-null mice develop autoimmune-like disease;[700] resveratrol protects against ConA-induced autoimmune hepatitis via p66shc repression.[701]
- **Cirrhosis**: SIRT1/SIRT6 decreased in cirrhotic livers;[665][669] SIRT3 enhanced by curcumin → protective.[703]
- **Drug-induced liver injury**: miR-128-3p suppression of SIRT1 aggravates doxorubicin-induced injury;[693] quercetin/SIRT1 attenuates isoniazid-induced injury (NLRP3/apoptosis);[704] SIRT6 activation alleviates acetaminophen hepatotoxicity (glutathione normalization).[705]
- **Endotoxemic/cholestatic injury**: Hepatocyte SIRT1 may be detrimental (p65 deacetylation → compromised NF-κB → endotoxemic susceptibility);[706] SIRT1-overexpressing myeloid cells contribute to cholestatic liver injury/fibrosis.[709]

### [[Pancreatitis|Pancreatic diseases]]
- **Acute pancreatitis (AP)**: SIRT1 low expression, protective;[711] resveratrol protects against acute necrotizing pancreatitis via SIRT1-mediated p53/HSF1 deacetylation.[711][712]
- **Chronic pancreatitis**: SIRT1 significantly upregulated; absence of SIRT1 expression inhibition by miR-278 → inflammation-induced EMT.[713]

### [[Inflammatory Bowel Disease|Inflammatory bowel diseases (IBDs)]]
- Affect 6.8 million people worldwide.[714]
- **SIRT2/SIRT6**: Downregulated in IBD patients; deletion promotes inflammatory responses via NF-κB activation;[715][–][717] SIRT2 inhibits Wnt/β-catenin to maintain gut homeostasis;[333] SIRT6 preserves Rspo1 expression → intestinal epithelium injury resistance.[715]
- **SIRT3/SIRT5/SIRT7**: Systematic KO mice susceptible to colitis.[718][–][720]
- **SIRT1**: Dual role — downregulated in IBD patients;[721] deficiency induces paneth/goblet cell activation, increased NF-κB, proinflammatory genes;[722] but deletion may be protective via Foxp3+ Treg induction → attenuated autoimmune colitis.[724][725]

### [[Intestinal Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury|Intestinal ischemia/reperfusion injury]]
- **SIRT1**: Suppresses epithelial ROS accumulation/apoptosis (miR-34a-5p knockdown);[229] resveratrol protects via SIRT1–NF-κB–iNOS–NO pathway.[728]
- **SIRT3**: Alleviates mitochondrial oxidative damage/apoptosis via peroxiredoxin 3 deacetylation.[727]
- **SIRT6**: Downregulation by miR-351-5p aggravates injury via oxidative stress/inflammation/apoptosis.[726]

### Other intestinal diseases
- **Hirschsprung-associated enterocolitis**: SIRT1 downregulated; suppressed via miR-132/miR-212 or exosomal miR-18a-5p → NF-κB/NLRP3/caspase-1 pyroptosis.[729][731]
- **Necrotizing enterocolitis**: SIRT1 activation decreases proinflammatory cytokines/oxidative stress, increases anti-inflammatory pathway;[730][732] SIRT1 regulates HIF-1α to alleviate inflammatory response and barrier dysfunction.[730]

## Nervous system diseases

### [[Alzheimer's Disease|Alzheimer's disease (AD)]]
- Most common neurodegenerative disorder.[735][736]
- **Aβ**: SIRT1 overexpression reduces Aβ-induced senescence/mitochondrial dysfunction;[740] SIRT3 protects against Aβ pathology/excitotoxicity;[743] SIRT2 harmful — suppression alleviates Aβ pathology/cognitive deficits (β-secretase 1 via reticulon 4B deacetylation).[744]
- **Tau**: SIRT1 reduces O-GlcNAcylation of tau via CREB;[749] SIRT2 affects tau phosphorylation and autophagic flux;[750] SIRT3 plays a role in tau acetylation → potential therapy target.[751]
- **SIRT2**: Nuclear SIRT2 hyperactivated in AD — FoxO1 recruits SIRT2 to Fzd1/Fzd7 promoters → H4K16ac reduction;[752] SIRT2 inhibition attractive target.
- **Autophagy**: SIRT1 enhances Beclin-1 deacetylation → beneficial;[270] SIRT2 loss (AK1 inhibitor or KO) recovers microtubule stabilization and improves autophagy.[299]
- **SIRT3**: Dysfunction → mitochondrial/neuronal damage; protective in hippocampal neurons;[755] intermittent food deprivation ameliorates deficits in SIRT3-dependent manner;[756] SIRT3 mirrors Aβ spatiotemporal deposition and is upregulated in AD temporal neocortex.[758]
- **Clinical**: Inverse relationship between serum/saliva SIRT1, SIRT3, SIRT6 levels and AD — noninvasive diagnostic potential.[759][760]

### [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's disease (PD)]]
- Most common movement disorder of older adults.
- **Genetics**: rs2015 SNP may influence SIRT2 expression via miR8061 binding → PD risk (Chinese Han case-control).[761] (Epidemiological data conflicting.)[762]
- **SIRT1**: Ameliorated LC3 deacetylation-mediated autophagic degradation of α-synuclein; improved motor defects in PD mice.[763]
- **SIRT3**: Pharmacologically increased SIRT3 counteracts α-syn-induced mitochondrial dysfunction (reduced oligomers, normalized bioenergetics);[764] age-dependent mitochondrial oxidative stress in substantia nigra associated with decreased SIRT3 protective function.[766]
- **SIRT2**: Mediates exacerbation of α-synuclein toxicity;[298] NAD+ metabolism altered in sporadic PD cells → SIRT2 activation → decreased acetylated α-tubulin;[297] SIRT2 deletion protective.[682]

### [[Huntington's Disease|Huntington's disease (HD)]]
- **SIRT1**: Brain-specific KO exacerbates pathology; overexpression improves survival/neuropathology/BDNF expression in HD mice — rescues CREB-regulated transcription coactivator 1–CREB interaction → BDNF transcription.[767]
- **SIRT2**: Inhibition achieves neuroprotection in cellular/invertebrate models — sterol biosynthesis manipulation mimics SIRT2 inhibition → diminishes mutant huntingtin toxicity.[769]

### [[Stroke|Brain injury (IRI, stroke)]]
- **SIRT1**: Neuroprotective via oxidative/inflammatory/autophagy/apoptotic pathways;[214][772][773] NAMPT promotes neuronal survival in SIRT1-dependent manner during cerebral ischemia;[774] deacetylates RNA-binding protein quaking 6 → activates PGC-1α via PPAR-γ → inhibits neuronal apoptosis.[690]
- **SIRT3**: Prosurvival factor protecting from excitotoxic injury; protective in ischemic stroke via HIF-1α/VEGF in astrocytes;[775][776] downregulated in cerebral IRI; enhancing SIRT3 + Wnt/β-catenin activation = therapeutic targets.[777]
- **SIRT5**: Mediates IR-induced brain damage by increasing blood-brain barrier permeability via occludin degradation.[778]
- **SIRT2**: Detrimental — inhibition neuroprotective in ischemic stroke (downregulation of Akt/FoxO3a and MAPK);[248] serum SIRT2 increased in acute ischemic stroke (AIS) patients → marker of risk/prognosis.[779]

### Other nervous system diseases
- **Multiple sclerosis / ALS**: SIRT1 increased in SOD1-G93A mice;[784] SIRT3 increased in ALS spinal cord;[785] SIRT6 activity abrogates ALS-linked mutant SOD1 astrocyte neurotoxicity.[786]
- **Epilepsy**: SIRT1 therapeutic target to rescue circadian rhythm genes;[788] SIRT5 deficiency increases mortality/seizure severity;[789] SIRT1 agonist CAY10602 neuroprotective;[233] SIRT3 protects from kainic acid excitotoxicity (miR-134-5p inhibition).[790]
- **Cognitive deficits**: SIRT1 protective against hippocampal atrophy/aging-related cognitive impairment;[794] surgery-induced hippocampal SIRT1 downregulation → cognitive impairment;[795] fluoride exposure → SIRT3 inhibition → mitochondrial dysfunction/cognitive impairment;[796] honokiol upregulates SIRT3 → protective.[797]
- **Spinal cord injury (SCI)**: SIRT1 neuroprotective via suppressing microglial activation/proinflammatory cytokines;[798][799] AMPK/SIRT1 autophagy regulation restrains spinal cord neuron damage;[286] SIRT6 upregulation alleviates inflammation/oxidative stress/apoptosis.[800]
- **Neuroinflammation/neuropathic pain**: SIRT1/SIRT2 neuroprotective;[61] SIRT1 activation attenuates Mn-induced oxidative stress/neuroinflammation;[804] SIRT2 overexpression alleviates neuropathic pain;[805] AK-7 (SIRT2 inhibitor) exacerbates traumatic brain injury (NF-κB p65 nuclear translocation);[88] microglial SIRT2 protective in amnesic deficits;[806] SIRT3 regulates mitochondrial oxidative stress response/neuroinflammation (Mst1–JNK–SRV2 pathway).[808]

## Endocrine system diseases

### [[Diabetes Mellitus|Diabetes mellitus (DM)]]
- >425 million affected; prevalence expected to increase ≥50% by 2045.[818]
- **Expression**: SIRT1–3 reduced in DM patients;[825][–][827] SIRT5/SIRT6 elevated.[350][828]
- **SIRT1/SIRT2**: Dual function. SIRT1 overexpression improves insulin sensitivity;[829][830] but hepatic SIRT1 knockdown prevented fasting hyperglycemia (decreased hepatic glucose production, increased hepatic insulin responsiveness).[832] SIRT2 promotes glucose-dependent hepatic glucose uptake (GKRP K126 deacetylation);[833] SIRT2 downregulation ameliorates reduced Akt activity and increases insulin-stimulated glucose uptake in insulin-resistant neuro-2a cells.[834]
- **SIRT3/SIRT4/SIRT6**: Protective. SIRT3 KO impairs insulin-stimulated muscle glucose uptake;[812] SIRT4 overexpression → dyslipidemia/lipogenesis/decreased fatty acid oxidation (deactivates AMPK, inhibits insulin secretion);[835] SIRT6 induces PGC-1α acetylation and suppresses hepatic glucose production;[836] SIRT6 cooperates with p53 to deacetylate FoxO1 → suppresses gluconeogenic genes.[837]
- **SIRT5**: Promotes DM progression — inhibition facilitates pancreatic β-cell proliferation/insulin secretion;[350] negatively regulates PDX1 transcription via deacetylase activity → aggravates DM.[838][839]

### [[Diabetic Kidney Disease|Diabetic kidney disease (DKD)]]
- Dominant cause of end-stage kidney disease.[844]
- **SIRT1**: Protective — high expression protects kidney/slows DKD progression;[814][815] deacetylates p53;[815] activates FoxO3a and Nrf2;[815][847] resveratrol promotes resistance to diabetic renal fibrosis via Nrf2.[847]

### [[Diabetic Neuropathy|Diabetic neuropathy (DN)]]
- Most prevalent diabetic complication; ≥50% of diabetics develop DN over time.[840]
- **SIRT1**: Modulates neuronal viability/differentiation/synaptic plasticity;[849][850] alleviates cognitive decline,[843] neuropathic pain,[851] peripheral neuropathy.[852] Decreased hippocampal SIRT1 in diabetic rats → reduced dendritic length/spine density, decreased TORC1/p-CREB/BDNF.[843]

### [[Diabetic Retinopathy|Diabetic retinopathy (DR)]] and [[Diabetic Cardiomyopathy|diabetic cardiomyopathy (DCM)]]
- **DR**: SIRT1 overexpression prevents capillary cell apoptosis/degenerative capillary formation,[854] reduces DM-induced retinal inflammation, improves visual function.[129]
- **DCM**: SIRT1 activation inhibits ROS-induced oxidative stress/fibrosis;[856] SIRT3 regulates fibrosis/inflammation/apoptosis/oxidative stress in diabetic myocardium,[149] attenuates DCM via p53-acetylation reduction and PFKFB3 upregulation;[842] SIRT3 deficiency aggravates hyperglycemic mitochondrial damage, ROS, necroptosis, NLRP3 activation.[857]

### [[Obesity|Obesity]]
- >603.7 million adults obese.[858]
- **SIRT1/SIRT3**: Suppress obesity by inhibiting adipogenesis and stimulating energy expenditure.[862][–][864] SIRT1 3'-UTR binds miR-146b → adipogenesis via SIRT1 downregulation;[862] SIRT3 activates macroautophagy (AMPK–ULK1) → smaller lipid droplets;[864] SIRT3 induces perilipin-1–Hsc71–LAMP2 complex → chaperone-mediated autophagy → lipid droplet instability.[864]
- **SIRT2/SIRT6**: Promote obesity. HIF-1α negatively regulates SIRT2–PGC-1α axis → fatty acid catabolism negated;[867] SIRT6 overexpression exacerbates diet-induced obesity by decreasing STAT3 acetylation and lowering proopiomelanocortin in hypothalamus.[868]

### Other metabolic disorders
- **SIRT1/SIRT6**: Downregulated in lipid metabolism-related diseases;[869][870] SIRT1 downregulated in metabolic syndrome.[178] SIRT1 modest overexpression protects from hepatic steatosis (MnSOD/Nrf1 induction, PGC-1α, NF-κB downregulation);[869] SIRT6 overexpression improves hypercholesterolemia (FoxO3 recruitment to SREBP2 promoter → H3K9/H3K56 deacetylation).[870] SIRT1 post-transcriptional stabilization by HuR represses inflammation/hyperglycemia to counter metabolic syndrome.[817]

## Urogenital system diseases

### [[Acute Kidney Injury|Acute kidney injury (AKI)]]
- **SIRT3–SIRT5**: Protective (mitochondrial). SIRT3 deficiency exacerbates sepsis-induced histopathological/mitochondrial damage, apoptosis (Bax/caspase-3 up, Bcl-2 down);[254] SIRT3 deletion aggravates fatty acid oxidation dysfunction;[788] honokiol activation increases ATP, reduces ROS/lipid peroxidation;[151] SIRT3 overexpression attenuates ischemia-reperfusion mitochondrial damage.[885]
- **SIRT1**: Protective but inconsistent mechanisms (JNK signaling, DUSP16 deacetylation);[887] SIRT1/p53 up-regulated modulator of apoptosis/FoxO3a deacetylation improves tubulointerstitial fibrosis after AKI;[873] NAD+/SIRT1/GSK-3β/Nrf2 axis protects against AKI.[890]
- **SIRT2**: Systematic KO ameliorates cisplatin-induced renal injury; transgenic mice aggravated;[891] SIRT5/SIRT6 protective (fatty acid oxidation balance; LPS-induced apoptosis inhibition/autophagy promotion);[892][312] SIRT7-deficient mice protected against AKI — promotes tubular damage/kidney inflammation.[893]

### [[Chronic Kidney Disease|Chronic kidney disease (CKD)]]
- ~10% of adults affected worldwide; 14th leading cause of death.[894][–][896]
- **Kidney fibrosis**: SIRT1 overexpression abolishes TGF-β1-induced apoptosis/fibrosis (CTGF suppression);[903] SRT1720 attenuates UUO-induced fibrosis;[904] SIRT2 knockdown/chemical inhibition attenuates TGF-β1-induced fibroblast activation;[905] SIRT3 KO susceptible to severe fibrosis (PDH E1α hyperacetylation at K385);[906] proximal tubule-specific SIRT6 KO aggravates UUO fibrosis — MDL-800 mitigates.[806] But SIRT1/SIRT2 downregulation may also inhibit fibroblast activation (EGFR/PDGFR-β signaling) → therapeutic potential for CKD.[911]
- **Kidney stones**: Downregulated SIRT3/SIRT1; SIRT3 protective via Nrf2/heme oxygenase-1 pathway;[912] suppressing SIRT1 promotes calcium oxalate crystal-cell adhesion/exacerbates injury.[913]
- **Aging-induced kidney injury**: SIRT1-induced HIF-1α deacetylation protects tubulointerstitial damage;[914] podocyte SIRT1 reduction → aggravated glomerulosclerosis/albuminuria;[915] SIRT6-deficient mice exhibit kidney hypertrophy/glomerular enlargement/proteinuria.[916]
- **Vascular calcification (VC)**: Soluble epoxide hydrolase destabilizes SIRT3 → accelerates VSMC calcification;[875] SIRT6 markedly downregulated in CKD/VC patients — suppresses osteogenic transdifferentiation of VSMCs via runt-related transcription factor 2;[917] MSC exosomes inhibit high-phosphate aortic calcification via SIRT6–HMGB1 deacetylation.[918]

### Genital system diseases
- **Male infertility**: Resveratrol/SIRT1 protective in erectile function and nicotine-induced reproductive damage;[919][920] dysregulation of SIRT1 and mitochondrial SIRT3–5 associated with human male infertility.[921][922]
- **PCOS**: Higher SIRT1 levels than healthy controls;[923] SIRT1/AMPK axis in autophagy activation;[924] SIRT3 deficiency in granulosa cells potentially induces impaired oocytes.[925]
- **Endometriosis**: SIRT1 overexpressed in eutopic endometrium, participates in pathogenesis;[926] resveratrol therapeutic potential;[601] miRNA-34a potential biomarker.[927]

## Motor system diseases

### [[Osteoarthritis|Osteoarthritis (OA)]]
- Most common joint disease (degenerative).[930]
- **SIRT1**: Best studied — reduces apoptosis and ECM degradation via Wnt/β-catenin;[932] circ0001103/miR-375 axis regulates SIRT1 → attenuates IL-1β-induced apoptosis/ECM degradation;[933] reverses homocysteine-induced changes via SIRT1/PGC-1α/PPAR-γ cascade;[934][935] inhibits EGFR ubiquitination → improves cartilage oxidative-stress resistance;[936] inhibits chondrocyte senescence via Wnt/β-catenin.[937]
- **SIRT2**: Protective — inhibits ECM degradation by preventing p65 acetylation.[89]
- **SIRT6**: Inhibits chondrocyte senescence by negatively regulating NF-κB-mediated inflammatory response.[81]
- **SIRT3**: Dual role — inhibits degeneration by maintaining mitochondrial homeostasis (restores SOD2 acetylation-dependent activity);[938][939] but overexpression promotes OA chondrocyte apoptosis/reduced proliferation → OA progression.[940]

### [[Osteoporosis|Osteoporosis]]
- Systemic skeletal disease of low bone mass/bone fragility.[941]
- **SIRT1**: KO mice have low bone mass phenotype;[944] overexpression increases osteoblast osteogenesis via FoxO3a deacetylation/oxidative-stress inhibition;[946] deacetylates sclerostin → activates Wnt/β-catenin → increased bone anabolism;[947] resveratrol activation restores alkaline phosphatase/osteocalcin via NF-κB inhibition.[949][950]
- **SIRT6**: Inhibits age-related bone loss by stabilizing ER alpha in preosteoblastic cells.[948]

### [[Intervertebral Disc Degeneration|Intervertebral disc degeneration (IDD)]]
- **SIRT1**: Reduced in degenerative nucleus pulposus tissues, decreasing with disease severity;[952] inhibits NF-κB transcriptional activity;[58] suppresses activin subunit c-Fos/c-Jun phosphorylation.[953]
- **SIRT2**: Reverses IL-1β action by inhibiting p53/p21 pathway → inhibited oxidative stress/senescence;[954]
- **SIRT3**: Maintains nucleus pulposus cell homeostasis via mitochondrial oxidative-stress regulation;[955][956]
- **SIRT6**: Inhibits inflammatory response/senescence via NF-κB inhibition.[957]

### [[Skeletal Muscle Atrophy|Skeletal muscle atrophy]]
- **SIRT1**: Inhibits drug-induced mitochondrial dysfunction → alleviates atrophy via PGC-1α signaling.[959]
- **SIRT2**: Inhibits autophagic flux → maintains protein metabolism homeostasis in skeletal muscle.[960]
- **SIRT3**: Deficiency enhances Ang II-induced fiber type transformation and mitochondrial metabolic reprogramming → exacerbates atrophy.[961]

## Aging

- **SIRT1**: La Ribonucleoprotein 7 activation dampens p53 and NF-κB (p65) transcriptional activity → ameliorates cellular senescence;[966] chromatin redistribution on DNA damage promotes DNA repair, genomic stability, suppresses age-dependent transcriptional changes.[967]
- **SIRT6**: Inhibition shortens VSMC lifespan/induces senescence (telomeric H3K9 hyperacetylation, 53BP1 binding); overexpression preserves telomere integrity;[201] deacetylates Polβ → DNA-damage resistance → prevents progeroid pathologies.[35]
- **SIRT3**: Deficiency → lamina-associated domain detachment, increased chromatin accessibility, aberrant repetitive-sequence transcription → senescence phenotypes of human MSCs.[968]

## Cross-cutting observations

1. **SIRT1** is the most extensively studied sirtuin and shows **opposing roles** across and within diseases (tumor suppressor vs. promoter in BC/LC/HCC/CRC; protective vs. harmful in hypertrophy, FLD, IBD, AKI, kidney fibrosis, DM, lung injury).
2. **SIRT2** often opposes the SIRT1/SIRT3/SIRT6 protective axis — harmful in AD, PD, HD, asthma, liver IRI, and mostly protective in OA, IDD, kidney fibrosis, neuropathic pain.
3. **SIRT3** is the primary mitochondrial sirtuin with near-universal protective roles in AKI, MI/R injury, DCM, LF, DKD, and metabolic diseases (with dual roles only in OA and FLD).
4. **SIRT4** is predominantly tumor-suppressive (BC, HCC, CRC, GC) and protective in metabolism (DM, DKD), but **adverse** in cardiac hypertrophy/fibrosis.
5. **SIRT6** is consistently protective across CVD, respiratory, digestive, kidney, and most cancers (except context-dependent roles in LC/HCC).
6. **SIRT7** is the least studied; roles are frequently inconsistent (dual in HBV, cardiac fibrosis, CRC).
7. **SIRT5** is mixed — protective in hepatic steatosis, epilepsy, AKI; pro-disease in DM, CRC, HCC (dual).
