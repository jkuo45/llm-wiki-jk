### New web findings folded into the schedule

- **Tocotrienols ≫ tocopherols against ferroptosis, head-to-head.** Yang/Ito et al., *Sci Rep* 2026 (doi:10.1038/s41598-025-34673-1; PMID 41501350): across RSL3, erastin, BSO, and genetic *Gpx4* deletion, tocotrienols prevented ferroptosis at ~10× lower concentrations (EC50 0.12 µM α-tocotrienol vs 2.0 µM α-tocopherol), tracking membrane integration.
- **Vitamin A is a direct anti-ferroptotic nutrient.** Jakaria et al. 2023 (PMID 37236031): retinol, atRAL, atRA inhibit ferroptosis with potency **superior to α-tocopherol** (retinol EC50 0.4–4.8 µM vs 7.4–36.1 µM). Studer et al., *Nat Commun* 2024 (doi:10.1038/s41467-024-51996-1): retinol/retinal act as radical-trapping antioxidants, while ATRA upregulates [[GPX4]], [[FSP1]], GCH1, ACSL3 via RAR/RXR — suppression of ferroptosis is *required* for neuronal development.
- **Red palm oil composition (food-form dose anchor).** RPO carries carotenoids 600–750 ppm (≈41% β-carotene, 41% α-carotene) and vitamin E 717–863 ppm of which ~70% is tocotrienols (α-T3 29%, γ-T3 41%, δ-T3 10%; α-tocopherol 19%), plus CoQ10 18–25 ppm, squalene, phytosterols (Nagendran et al., J Am Coll Nutr 2000; MPOB data). One tablespoon (13 g) → ~8.7 mg carotenoids (~400–500 µg RAE) and ~10–11 mg vitamin E.
- **Sub-saturating vitamin A dose.** Meta-analysis of RPO RCTs (Zhang et al., *Food Nutr Res* 2018, PMC5748732): ≤8 g/day RPO already raises serum retinol; >8 g gives no further increase — a self-limiting carotenoid-conversion ceiling.
- **Reference intakes.** DRI: vitamin A RDA 900 µg RAE (M) / 700 µg RAE (W), UL 3,000 µg RAE *preformed* (carotenoids have no UL); vitamin E RDA 15 mg α-tocopherol, UL 1,000 mg supplemental (NCBI Bookshelf NBK222318/NBK234920).

## Mechanistic Basis: Why These Three Together

[[Ferroptosis]] = iron-dependent phospholipid [[Lipid Peroxidation]] running unchecked past [[GPX4]]–[[Glutathione]]. The exogenous (dietary) brake tier is the radical-trapping antioxidant network in the membrane:

- **Vitamin E** (8 vitamers): chromanol phenolic-H donation terminates LOO• chains; tocotrienols integrate deeper and outperform tocopherols.
- **Vitamin A** (retinol/retinal): polyene chain traps lipid radicals directly (potency ≥ α-tocopherol); ATRA additionally upregulates GPX4/FSP1.
- **Carotenoids** (β/α-carotene, lycopene in RPO): quench [[Singlet Oxygen]] and serve as regulated, non-toxic provitamin A (conversion feedback-limited).
- **[[Coenzyme Q10]]** (small but real in RPO): substrate of the FSP1 ubiquinol axis.

Red palm oil is the single food that co-delivers all four on an oleic/palmitic lipid vehicle — which is also the absorption requirement. It is the Tier-1 dietary source in the [[Queen Palm]] ranking for exactly this reason.

### Timing and interaction rules

- **Keep antioxidant *supplements* ≥3–4 h away from training, sauna, or fasting windows.** This preserves the exercise/fasting ROS signal that drives [[Mitohormesis]] (Ristow 2009; see [[Antioxidant Supplementation Paradox]] and [[Exercise Hormesis]]). Food-level intake at meals is not the concern — the 400 IU bolus is.
- **Always take with dietary fat.** Fat-soluble absorption via mixed micelles; a fat-free meal can cut uptake to near zero.
- **Prefer carotenoids over preformed retinol supplements.** β-Carotene conversion to retinol is feedback-regulated (no UL); preformed retinol accumulates and excess retinoid is itself pro-oxidant/mitochondrially toxic (see the fission task output: Trolox rescues retinol-induced mitochondrial membrane damage).
- **Iron context matters for ferroptosis.** The chemistry that ferroptosis runs on is Fe²⁺-driven ([[Fenton Reaction]]). If ferritin is high or hemochromatosis is present, that is a bigger lever than any vitamin dose; discuss iron status before optimizing the antioxidant tier.

### What to avoid

- **400 IU vitamin E capsules** — 12–18× RDA in one bolus; SELECT +17% prostate cancer; displaces γ/δ-tocopherol and tocotrienols via α-TTP; blunts redox adaptation.
- **β-Carotene *supplements* in current/former smokers** (ATBC, CARET lung-cancer signal) — this does not apply to food-form carotenoids in RPO.
- **High-heat frying in RPO / repeated re-use** — destroys carotenoids and tocochromanols (refining already strips 50–80%; don't finish the job in the pan).
- **Preformed retinol >3,000 µg RAE/day** — and [[COMT]] gate: slow COMT (rs4680 A/A) may benefit from vitamin E, fast COMT (G/G) trended toward harm in the 10-year follow-up — food-form dosing keeps both genotypes in the safe band.

## Monitoring

- **Skin palms slightly yellow-orange (carotenodermia)** — visible proof of delivery, harmless, self-resolving if RPO is trimmed.
- **Annual or symptom-driven labs**: serum retinol only if clinical concern; ferritin/CRP for the iron side of ferroptosis risk; plasma α-tocopherol is lipoprotein-confounded and a poor status marker (see [[Alpha-tocopherol]] PK section).
- **Reassess after 8–12 weeks**: the membrane tocopherol/carotenoid pool turns over slowly; earlier readouts mostly reflect the last meals.
