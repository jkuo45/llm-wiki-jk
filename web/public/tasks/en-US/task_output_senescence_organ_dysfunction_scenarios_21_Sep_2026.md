# Multiple Scenarios Explaining Senescence-Mediated Organ Dysfunction

Compiled 21_Sep_2026 12:00 AM UTC. Seed quote (Birch and Gil 2020, *Genes & Development* 34:1565, Fig. 3):

> Removal of senescent cells increases healthy life span in murine models (Baker et al. 2011, 2016), but how senescent cells contribute to aging and disease is difficult to discern. This problem is exacerbated as only a small percentage of cells become senescent, even at very old age (Biran et al. 2017). Multiple scenarios explaining senescence-mediated organ dysfunction likely exist (Fig. 3). […]

Fig. 3 groups these into SASP-independent (top) and SASP-dependent (bottom) effects. The paragraph lists five scenarios. Each is traced below: vault baseline → 2024–2026 update.

## Short Answer

The 2020 five-scenario model still holds, but 2024–2026 reframes it: a small senescent burden acts as a network hub via [[Paracrine Senescence]], extracellular vesicles, and systemic [[SASP]] / [[Inflammaging]], with tissue-specific execution. Donor-transfer models (skin, bone marrow, whole organ) now replicate Xu et al. 2018 with distal brain effects. Stem-depletion is confirmed but narrowed (geriatric [[Muscle Stem Cells|MuSCs]], [[FAPs]], [[Neural Progenitor Cells|NPCs]] plus niche suppression). Fibrosis / ECM remodeling and plaque build-up are now framed as maladaptive-repair states. Human senolytics show target engagement but no controlled functional win yet.

## Scenario 1 — Stem / Progenitor Pool Depletion (cell-intrinsic)

Original: senescence of [[Neural Progenitor Cells|neural progenitors]] (Molofsky et al. 2006) → neurogenesis decline; [[Muscle Stem Cells|muscle stem cell]] senescence (Garcia-Prat et al. 2016) → regenerative failure.

2024–2026 update:

- Skeletal muscle is now the best-mapped case. Reviews (Front Cell Dev Biol Nov 2025; Endocrinol Metab Mar 2026; Rejuvenation Res Jun 2025; Stem Cell Res Ther Oct 2025) agree: aged [[Muscle Stem Cells|MuSCs]] accumulate SA-β-gal / p16 / p21, lose autophagy/mitophagy and OXPHOS, fail activation after injury. Refinement: senescence is clearest in geriatric (>28 mo) MuSCs, not merely old (Sousa-Victor 2014, reaffirmed 2025). [[FAPs]], macrophages, endothelial cells, and even post-mitotic myofibers are parallel senescent pools; p16+ [[FAPs]] fibrogenically convert and secrete WISP1-loss / CCL5 / IL-6 signals.
- Human confirmation: Li et al. 2025 snRNA+ATAC on aging human muscle defines heterogeneous senescent MuSCs and their niche-remodeling SASP factors.
- p16-3MR model: transient senescence after cardiotoxin injury even in young muscle (day 3 → cleared day 7); aged muscle shows persistence. Clearing RFP+ cells (ganciclovir), D+Q, or CD36-neutralizing antibody restores MuSC function and healing.
- ABT263 (Navitoclax) rejuvenates aged HSCs and MuSCs in vitro / after transplant (Chang 2016 lineage, reviewed Matteini 2024 FEBS Lett).
- Neural: senescent [[Neural Progenitor Cells|NPCs]] show reduced proliferation; astroglial IL-6 overexpression cuts dentate-gyrus neurogenesis ~63%; LPS/microglial IL-1β/TNF-α/IL-6 suppresses new-neuron formation ~85%, rescued by minocycline (Ekdahl 2003, re-cited J Transl Med Nov 2025). D+Q improves memory/cognition in aged and AD models; brain-organoid senolytics alleviate aging/COVID neuropathology.
- Open: whether MuSC loss drives sarcopenia per se (Fry 2015 vs. Zhu 2024 melatonin rescue) — function loss matters more than number.

## Scenario 2 — Paracrine / Systemic Spread from Few Cells → Inflammaging

Original: <1% senescent preadipocytes injected → widespread dysfunction (Xu et al. 2018); SASP → chronic low-grade [[Inflammaging]] (Franceschi and Campisi 2014); ~40% SASP factors age-associated in plasma (Basisty et al. 2020); clearance lowers inflammatory factors (Baker 2011, 2016).

2024–2026 update:

- Direct replications with distal effects:
  - Franco et al., *Aging Cell* Oct 2024: dermal senescent fibroblasts in young mice → proximal + distal senescence, frailty, musculoskeletal impairment, hippocampal senescence markers + cognitive decline.
  - Iske et al., *Am J Transplant* 2024: old-heart → young-recipient isograft → p16/p21 in lymph nodes, liver, fat, muscle at 30 d + RotaRod + spatial-memory deficits; systemic eotaxin, IP-10, Gro-α, MCP-1, MIP, IL-13, VEGF and mt-DNA elevated; mt-DNA injection phenocopies; donor D+Q pretreatment lowers mt-DNA and rescues function.
  - Hou / Nature Aging Sep 2024: aged bone-marrow macrophages propagate senescence via extracellular vesicles (miRNA → PPARα axis) → liver/muscle/fat/brain senescence, insulin resistance, bone loss, microglial synapse engulfment; D+Q-pretreated BMMs or fenofibrate (PPARα agonist) rescue; 7,986-person cohort links fenofibrate to lower chronic-disease risk.
- Framework papers: Trends Endocrinol Metab Mar 2026 (interorgan communication opinion — SASP + EVs + impaired immunosurveillance); Liu et al., *Commun Biol* Sep 2026 (SASP–inflammaging vicious cycle, duality / cycle / tissue-specificity); Biogerontology Jul 2026 (senescent cells as principal but not sole inflammaging source; network-hub model, Nelson 2012 / da Silva 2019).
- Therapeutic read-through: Baker lifespan extension now paired with "unleashing endogenous regeneration" hypothesis (J Transl Med Nov 2025) — senolytics de-repress progenitor activity rather than only removing inflammation.

## Scenario 3 — Specific Local SASP Components

Original: hemostatic SERPINs / [[PAI-1]] → chemotherapy thrombosis (Wiley et al. 2019); [[TGFβ]]-driven [[Paracrine Senescence]] → acetaminophen liver-regeneration block (Bird et al. 2018).

2024–2026 update:

- Coagulation arm persists but is now one module among many: cardiovascular review (Front Aging Jan 2026) lists endothelial/VSMC SASP (IL-6, TNF-α, adhesion molecules, proteases, PAI-1) → leukocyte recruitment, thrombotic microenvironment, wall degeneration; MMPs degrade fibrous cap.
- Liver duality clarified: transient HSC SASP degrades scar and restores homeostasis; persistent hepatocyte/stellate TGF-β + IL-6 SASP locks tissue into fibrosis and blocks regeneration (Cells 2025; Comm Biol 2026). Same TGF-β → paracrine-senescence logic as Bird 2018, now with zone-specific hepatocyte SASP heterogeneity.
- Orthopedic parallel (Front Aging May 2026): MMP-13 / ADAMTS-4/5 degrade type-II collagen/proteoglycans; CCL5/IL-6 skew BMSC → osteoclast over osteoblast; IL-6 downregulates P2X7 mechanosensing.
- Implication: senomorphics must be arm-specific; hitting only IL-6/IL-8 leaves hemostatic/profibrotic modules intact.

## Scenario 4 — Microenvironment Remodeling / Homeostasis Disruption → Fibrosis

Original: ECM secretion, immune recruitment, fate-switching; fibrosis example (Schafer et al. 2017).

2024–2026 update:

- Fibrosis is now the canonical maladaptive-repair outcome across lung (IPF), kidney (DKD/CKD), liver (NAFLD→NASH→cirrhosis), muscle: senescent epithelial / stellate / fibroblast pools secrete TGF-β + MMPs → fibroblast activation, collagen deposition, architecture loss, repair suppression (Biogerontology 2026; Cells Apr 2025; Front Aging 2026 orthopedic review).
- Immune loop detailed: SASP recruits macrophages/T cells for clearance acutely; aged immunosenescence (NKG2D downregulation, DC dysfunction, Th1 IFN-γ boosting chondrocyte SASP, ILC3 IL-17/GM-CSF loop) converts this to a self-amplifying inflammation–senescence–immune-dysfunction cycle.
- Small-EV spread: Cx43-enriched sEVs from OA chondrocytes induce senescence via NF-κB/ERK1/2; CRLF1/CLCF1–JAK/STAT3 amplifies disc senescence; RG-7112 / o-vanillin reduce burden in disc models.
- Regeneration vs. fibrosis rule: high-regenerative organs (liver, muscle) benefit from transient SASP (progenitor proliferation + immune clearance) but fibrose when persistent; low-regenerative organs (lung) fibrose earlier.

## Scenario 5 — Physical Build-up (Atherosclerotic Plaque Example)

Original: senescent foamy macrophages in plaques (Childs et al. 2016).

2024–2026 update:

- Extended to full vascular-aging program (Front Aging Jan 2026; Biogerontology 2026): senescent endothelial + VSMC + perivascular-adipocyte SASP → endothelial dysfunction, arterial stiffness, plaque progression/instability; MMPs + IL-6/IL-8/MCP-1 recruit immune cells and amplify local inflammation.
- Post-MI senescence worsens remodeling; senolytics/senomorphics proposed as plaque-stabilizing adjuncts, but human cardiovascular endpoints remain preclinical.
- Concept generalizes: build-up matters where clearance fails and space/function is constrained (plaque, joint, fibrotic niche).

## Therapeutic Corollary — Baker 2011/2016 to 2025–2026 Trials

- Murine: intermittent D+Q → +36% median post-treatment lifespan, mortality hazard 0.65 (Xu 2018, re-cited throughout 2024–2026).
- Human (as of Sep 2026 synthesis): no senolytic approved for aging. Five small D+Q pilots (n=5–14: IPF 2019 +21.5 m 6-min walk; DKD 2019 p16/p21↓ in fat, IL-1α/IL-6/MMP-9/12↓; IPF RCT 2023 n=12 no functional difference; SToMP-AD 2023 n=5 CSF penetrance; STAMINA 2025 n=12 MoCA +1.0 NS, +2.0 in lowest-baseline subgroup, TNF-α Δ inversely correlates with MoCA r=-0.65). Fisetin knee-OA RCT n=75 (2024) negative; AFFIRM / AFFIRM-LITE pending to 2027; foselutoclax ASPIRE n=52 (2025) missed primary non-inferiority; SEN-SURVIVORS (childhood-cancer frailty, D+Q vs. fisetin) active to 2027; osteoporosis DQ/NR trial to 2026.
- Doctrine shift: hit-and-run senolytics for burden + context-specific senomorphics (JAK, mTOR, p38/MK2, cGAS–STING, NAMPT, SLC25A1/CTPI2, NRTIs/LINE1) timed to spare acute repair; hybrid "modulate-then-clear" / "induce–prime–purge" in oncology.

## What Remains Open

1. Threshold: dose/time/composition flip from beneficial to detrimental still undefined (Liu 2026).
2. Which senescent states in which tissue at which stage matter — biomarker panels separating SASP from generic inflammation still missing.
3. Non-inflammatory SASP arms (profibrotic, hemostatic, ECM) under-targeted.
4. Recipient rules: why same SASP → joint vs. vascular vs. neurodegenerative outcome (receptor landscape + niche stiffness + sex).
5. Translation: sex-dimorphic responses, off-target toxicity, premature-clearance harm to repair.
