# Male Distributed Topology vs Centralized Estrogen Hub

## Summary

**Central finding: male biology is a distributed topology —
[[Testosterone]]→[[Androgen Receptor|AR]] + PARP-1/AIF +
single-X vulnerability + [[Loss of Y Chromosome|LOY]] —
vs the female centralized E2→ER hub.**

`src/tasks/task_output_gender_specific_attributes_vault_topics_02_SEP_2026.md:Part IX`
defines estrogen (E2) as a 9-arm master mediator:
E2→ER → SIRT1, SIRT3, SOD2, Bcl-2, NF-kB/IkBa, STING/HDAC3, PARP-1,
TERT, p53 — with menopausal withdrawal causing a systems-level
"estrogen cliff."

No single male factor spans that breadth — and that is the point.
Web + vault review establishes **five distributed male nodes**,
each scientifically backed, jointly covering the same territory
with different architecture (active + loss-based, anabolic +
pathogenic):

- **Testosterone→AR** — anabolic/immune node; bidirectional,
  gradual decline (LOH), no cliff.
- **Mosaic loss-of-Y (mLOY)** — systems-accelerator node;
  pro-fibrotic/immunosuppressive, causal in mice.
- **Single-X vulnerability (EXITS/UTY)** — structural node;
  no biallelic backup, compounded by LOY.
- **PARP-1/AIF necrotic program** — executioner node;
  male-dominant death mode and NAD+ consumer.
- **SIRT6→IGF-1 / mTORC1 / canonical autophagy** — growth/longevity node;
  male-leaning output wiring.

Together AR (drive) + PARP-1/AIF (execution) + single-X (structure) +
LOY (age-accelerator) form the distributed male system — no center,
no cliff, gradual + clonal erosion vs female centralized protection
with sharp withdrawal.

## Reference: Estrogen Master Mediator (02_SEP_2026 Part IX)

```
ESTROGEN (E2)
  ├──→ ERα/β → SIRT1 transcription ↑
  ├──→ ERα/β → SIRT3 expression & mitochondrial targeting ↑
  ├──→ ERα/β → SOD2 protein ↑
  ├──→ ERα/β → Bcl-2 ↑ (neuroprotection)
  ├──→ ERα/β → IκBα ↑ → NF-κB suppression
  ├──→ ERα/β → HDAC3 → STING ↓
  ├──→ ERα → PARP-1 stabilization
  ├──→ TERT promoter → telomerase ↑
  └──→ p53 dual activation/sequestration
```

At menopause all nine arms derepress simultaneously.
The male system has no equivalent center — erosion is gradual (LOH),
clonal (LOY), structural (single-X), and event-driven (PARP-1/AIF).

## Testosterone→AR — Anabolic/Immune Node

### Vault status

- [[Testosterone]]: Leydig/LH axis, muscle/bone/erythrogenesis,
  aromatization to [[Estrogen]], SIRT1-associated redox resilience,
  mitohormesis optimum; andropause gradual.
- [[Prostate Cancer]]: "AR is the master driver" — androgen→AR→[[mTORC1]]
  in males (Gökduman 2023 unverified), AR↔PI3K/AKT/mTOR reciprocal
  feedback, higher T raises HCC/melanoma incidence.
- [[Telomere]]: "Testosterone is unfavorable" — lacks antioxidant
  property, may ↑ oxidative telomere damage (Gardner 2014).
- [[p53]]: testosterone ↓p-p53 only in C2C12 oxidative model
  (Pronsato Steroids 2017) — do not generalize.
- [[Ferroptosis]]: MBOAT2 is direct AR target (vs MBOAT1 ER target,
  Cell 2023 PMC10330611); testosterone dominant susceptibility factor
  in renal ischemia (Park JBC 2004, pre-ferroptosis era).
- SIRT1↔AR: SIRT1 deacetylates AR-K630, represses DHT-driven growth;
  FHL2 enhances SIRT1→FoxO1 in prostate (Michan/Sinclair review).
  SIRT7→AR→autophagy in LNCaP/22Rv1 (Ding 2020).

### Web verification — anti-inflammatory parallel to E2

Biogerontology 2023 review (PMC9810526):

- T ↓ TNF-α, IL-6, IFN-γ, IL-2, IL-1β; ↑ IL-10.
- Inhibits NF-κB/p65 (Wang 2021), ↓ TLR4 on macrophages
  (orchiectomy ↑ TLR4 + endotoxin shock, Rettew 2008),
  ↓ ILC2 proliferation (Cephus 2017), AR→IL-10 in CD4+ T cells
  (Liva/Voskuhl 2001).
- Activates PI3K→Akt→mTOR, mTOR level testosterone-concentration
  dependent (Basu 2013; White 2013).

### Web verification — anabolic mTOR mechanism

- Basualto-Alarcón Med Sci Sports Exerc 2013: 100 nM T →
  p-ERK 5 min, p-Akt 15 min, p-S6K1 60 min 3.5×; LY294002/Akt-VIII/
  rapamycin abolish, PD98059 does not; bicalutamide abolishes CSA gain.
  Nongenomic PI3K/Akt + genomic AR integrated.
- White Mol Cell Endocrinol 2013: castration 42d ↓ grip, GAS mass,
  IGF-1 mRNA, myofibrillar synthesis, p-Akt/p-GSK3β/p-PRAS40/p-FoxO3a;
  ↑ MuRF1/atrogin-1/REDD1; nandrolone reverses. Low T activates mTOR
  Akt-independently; high T required for Akt.
- Wu BBRC 2010: L6 hypertrophy Erk + mTOR dependent.

### Critical asymmetry — inflammaging→↓T, not T→protection

- UK Biobank men n=18,347: IL-6 + GDF-15 mediate age T-decline;
  GDF-15 strongest mediator; high-T-for-inflammation = ↑ mortality
  (Muehlenbein 2026 preprint doi:10.64898/2026.06.04.730222).
- SAMP8 Leydig: ROS↑ → p38→COX2 + NF-κB→COX2 → StAR/P450scc↓;
  SB203580 + Bay 11-7082 synergistically restore T (Sci Rep 2019).
- Front Endocrinol 2024 LOH review: NF-κB impairs GnRH transcription,
  IL-6 impairs pituitary stem repair, M2→M1 testicular macrophage shift,
  SASP + mitochondrial dysfunction + impaired autophagy → LOH.
- TRT helps hypogonadal wasting/sarcopenia/osteopenia but long-term
  safety contentious; concern in chronic inflammation.

**Node role:** AR is the male system's drive node — anabolic/immune hub
(muscle, bone, erythropoiesis, NF-κB, mTOR, T-cell suppression).
Distributed by design: narrower than E2 per-node (no SIRT3/SOD2/TERT/STING
equivalent), bidirectional with inflammation, gradual LOH vs cliff,
pro-cancer in prostate/liver/melanoma — the male counterpart role,
not a failed copy.

## Mosaic Loss-of-Y — Systems-Accelerator Node

No female counterpart; loss-based, not hormone-based.

- Prevalence: 40% of 70y, 57% of 93y men; most common somatic mutation
  in men (Thompson Nature 2019; Nat Rev Genet 2025).
- Mortality: LOY-blood HR 1.91 all-cause, 5.5y vs 11y survival
  (Forsberg Nat Genet 2014); LURIC coronary n=1698 LOY>17% HR 1.41
  all-cause, 1.49 CV death, 2.65 fatal MI + profibrotic plasma
  OPG/MMP-12/GDF-15/HB-EGF/resistin (2025).
- Causality: BM-LOY mice → ↓ lifespan, cardiac/pulmonary/renal fibrosis,
  HF death; LOY macrophages TGFβ1/galectin-3 → fibroblast activation;
  TGFβ1 blockade rescues (Sano Science 2022 abn3100); Uty disruption
  phenocopies (Nat Cardiovasc Res 2024).
- Immunity/cancer/AD: ~500 autosomal genes dysregulated (Dumanski 2021);
  T-cell exhaustion + PD-1 sensitivity, bladder-cancer growth via immune
  evasion (Abdel-Hafiz Nature 2023); LOY-AD, CKD, aortic stenosis,
  impaired senescent-cell clearance → renal disease (Sci Transl Med 2025).
- Risk factors: age + smoking (dose-response, reversible after quit),
  genomic instability/CHIP co-occurrence.

**Node role:** LOY is the male system's age-accelerator — broadest
single node (heart, kidney, lung, brain, cancer, immunity) with opposite
valence to E2: pathogenic clone gained with age rather than protective
hub lost at menopause. The **LOY cliff** (exponential from 60y) is the
structural mirror of the estrogen cliff.

### Single-X Vulnerability — Structural Node

Compounding LOY: six X-linked tumor suppressors (ATRX, CNKSR2, DDX3X,
KDM5C, KDM6A, MAGEC3) carry female biallelic protection with zero
autosomal/PAR equivalent (Dunford Nature Genetics 2017); males rely on
weaker X–Y paralog backup ([[UTY]] for KDM6A, DDX3Y for DDX3X) that LOY
itself removes. Structure, not signaling — the distributed topology's
hard-coded asymmetry.

## PARP-1/AIF — Executioner Node

From 02_SEP_2026 Part II + stroke literature:

- McCullough JCBFM 2005; Liu Stroke 2011 (PMC3066270):
  PARP-1 KO males infarct 19.7% vs 38.4% WT (protected);
  PARP-1 KO OVX females 52.2% vs 36.0% WT (exacerbated);
  Q-VD-OPh caspase inhibitor protects females only.
- Mechanism: male nNOS→PARP→NAD+ depletion→AIF nuclear→50-kbp
  fragmentation (parthanatos); female cytochrome-C→caspase-9→caspase-3.
  PARP-1 deletion abolishes AIF translocation both sexes yet
  ↑ cytochrome-C/caspase-9/-3 only in females.
- PJ34 delayed inhibition ↓ iNOS/MMP9, improves mNSS mainly in males
  (Front Cell Neurosci 2020).

**Node role:** the male system's executioner — dominant death pathway
plus NAD+ consumer (links to [[NAD+]] male decline). Downstream by
position, load-bearing by function. Therapeutic corollary stands:
PARP inhibitors male-leaning, caspase inhibitors female-leaning.

## SIRT6→IGF-1 / mTOR / Autophagy — Growth/Longevity Node

- Kanfi Nature 2012: Sirt6-Tg males median +14.5%/+9.9%, max +15.8%/
  +13.1%; females n.s.; mechanism ↓ serum IGF-1 + ↑ IGFBP1 +
  ↓ p-AKT/p-FOXO to female levels; 50% of male-Tg liver genes overlap
  female-WT signature.
- Roichman Nat Commun 2021: SIRT6 extends both sexes, male-stronger,
  via hepatic NAD+/gluconeogenesis, ↓ IGF-1 both sexes.
- Human: Finnish rs117385980 male longevity; centenarian SIRT6 variant
  genome stability (Simon EMBO J 2022).
- mTORC1: male fasted p70S6K1↑ / female fed↑ (needs full-text check);
  female cardiac mTOR ERβ-specified (Gürgen Hypertension 2013).
- Autophagy: males basal Beclin-1/LC3↑ (Campesi Life Sci 2013 heart),
  canonical Beclin-1→LC3-II→p62↓ post-stroke; females ATG7
  Beclin-1-independent (Patrizz Cells 2021); Du JBC 2009 male neurons
  autophagy-prone/starvation death.

**Node role:** the male system's growth/longevity wiring — real male
bias in IGF-1/mTOR/autophagy output. No E2→SIRT3/SOD2-style induction;
instead the male-leaning set-point of the shared anabolic axis.
