# Feoktistova et al., Molecular Cell 2011 — Results and Discussion Review

## Summary

In the absence of cellular inhibitor of apoptosis proteins (cIAPs), a 2 MDa intracellular platform termed the Ripoptosome forms spontaneously — it contains receptor-interacting protein kinase 1 (RIPK1), Fas-associated death domain (FADD), Caspase-8, Caspase-10 and cellular FLICE-like inhibitory protein (cFLIP) isoforms — and activates apoptosis or RIPK3-dependent necroptosis. Caspase-8 activity within the complex disassembles it by cleaving scaffold proteins; the long isoform (cFLIPL) blocks formation and restrains the activity, while the short isoform (cFLIPS) promotes assembly and suppresses the activity, so when cIAPs are absent, cFLIPS cells show necroptosis.

## Results

### 1. TLR3-induced cell death is blocked by cIAPs and proceeds in a Caspase- and RIP1-kinase-dependent manner independent from TNF signaling

Toll-like receptor 3 (TLR3) ligand (poly(I:C)) induced cell death in HaCaT, MET-1 and A5RT3 cells; pretreatment with an IAP antagonist (compound A, a SMAC mimetic/SMAC mimetic) or TWEAK (TNF-like weak inducer of apoptosis) sensitized HaCaT and MET-1 but not A5RT3. Caspase-8 and RIPK1 knockdown respectively reduced and eliminated TLR3-induced death in the absence of cIAPs; the combination of Z-VAD (Z-VAD-FMK, a pan-caspase inhibitor) and Nec-1 (Necrostatin-1, a RIPK1 kinase inhibitor) fully rescued, with cell death morphology consistent with simultaneous apoptosis and necroptosis. High mobility group box 1 (HMGB1) release, a Caspase-8-independent marker, was unchanged by Z-VAD, reduced by Nec-1, and fully suppressed by the Z-VAD + Nec-1 combination. Death was independent of autocrine tumor necrosis factor receptor 1 (TNFR1) signaling: TNFR1 knockdown and blocking FAS-associated death domain (FADD)-containing death receptor ligands (TNFR2-Fc, TRAILR2-Fc, CD95-Fc) did not alter TLR3 death. Caspase-8 immunoprecipitation showed that cIAPs depletion increased Caspase-8–RIPK1 association upon TLR3 stimulation; the complex contained FADD, the cFLIP cleavage fragment p43, Caspase-10 p47/p43 and TIR-domain-containing adaptor-inducing interferon-β (TRIF), but not in TNFR1 complex II. Primary human keratinocytes (PKs) were sensitized to TLR3 death by cIAP/X-linked inhibitor of apoptosis (XIAP) depletion, independent of TNFR1.

### 2. cIAPs block spontaneous Ripoptosome formation; RIPK1 is required

Cell death required Toll-like receptor 3 (TLR3) expression and TRIF. Knockdown of Caspase-8 reduced TLR3 death in the absence of cIAPs (rescued by Nec-1 but not Z-VAD); knockdown of RIPK3 produced no protection, while Z-VAD protected independent of Nec-1 — the two effectors being Caspase-8 (apoptosis) and RIPK3 (necroptosis); combined RIPK3 and Caspase-8 knockdown fully protected. Knockdown of RIPK1 fully protected; Ripoptosome formation (Caspase-8 IP) required RIPK1. IAP antagonist alone produced Caspase-8–RIPK1–FADD interaction insufficient for death; additional poly(I:C) stimulation recruited TRIF into the complex.

### 3. cFLIP is a critical regulator of the Ripoptosome

A5RT3 cells (no endogenous cFLIP): knockdown of cFLIP sensitized to TLR3 death. In A5RT3, cFLIP knockdown produced spontaneous Ripoptosome formation in the absence of cIAPs, amplified by TLR3 stimulation, with Caspase-10 accumulating in the complex.

### 4. cFLIP isoforms differentially regulate poly(I:C)-induced death by regulation of the Ripoptosome

In HaCaT (low endogenous cFLIP): cFLIPL protected from TLR3 death under all conditions, while cFLIPS cells died spontaneously upon cIAPs depletion — a RIPK1-kinase-dependent, Z-VAD-insensitive death. Ripoptosome IP: cFLIPL suppressed RIPK1/FADD association after IAP antagonist/TLR3; cFLIPS increased RIPK1 association (total RIPK1 levels slightly elevated). HeLa cells showed the same pattern: cFLIPS was protective from IAP antagonist, cFLIPL inhibitory. In PK, spontaneous Ripoptosome formation was not detectable without cIAPs function; high endogenous cFLIPL levels in PK versus HaCaT argue that spontaneous formation is cell-type specific, occurring mainly in transformed cells in a cFLIP-isoform-dependent manner.

### 5. Promotion of necroptosis by cFLIPS in the absence of cIAPs is independent of TNFR1 signaling and occurs in a 2 MDa complex

HaCaT-cFLIPS: IAP antagonist alone induced spontaneous Ripoptosome formation (no TRIF association). TNFR1 knockdown, autocrine TNFR1 blockade, and Caspase-8 IP in TNFR1 knockdown cells all showed unchanged spontaneous Ripoptosome formation and RIPK1 association; TNFR1 complex I/II analysis excluded Caspase-8 recruitment through the receptor — the complex cannot be called TNFR1 complex II. Nec-1 or RIPK3 knockdown fully blocked IAP antagonist-induced death; Ripoptosome formation preceded death detection. Gel filtration (Superose 6): RIPK1, FADD and Caspase-8 were mainly monomeric; a 2 MDa complex was detected in the column range of fractions 4–6; after cIAPs degradation, Caspase-8/RIPK1/cFLIP association appeared only in the 2 MDa fractions — cIAPs block assembly of the 2 MDa platform.

### 6. cFLIPS but not cFLIPL blocks Caspase-8 activity within the Ripoptosome

HaCaT-cFLIPS Ripoptosome contained uncleaved RIPK1 (even with Z-VAD-FMK present); control cells contained cleaved RIPK1 (p39), indicative of Caspase-8 activity within the complex, and Caspase-8 activity disassembles the complex by cleaving scaffold proteins. Flag-IP of the complex: in cFLIPS cells, RIPK1, FADD and (after TLR3) TRIF co-precipitated; in cFLIPL cells, less complex formation and mostly cleaved Caspase-8 — Caspase-8/cFLIPL heterodimers retaining catalytic activity at a level sufficient for selected substrate cleavage (including RIPK1) but insufficient for full Caspase-8 activation. Proteasome inhibitor MG132 (N-acetyl-leucyl-leucyl-norleucinal) alone induced spontaneous Ripoptosome formation despite cIAP presence; MG132 + IAP antagonist suppressed it. Model: cIAPs prevent Ripoptosome formation by ubiquitinating a "modified" RIPK1 pool targeted to proteasomal degradation; proteasome blockade alone triggers spontaneous formation.

## Discussion

### The 2 MDa Ripoptosome as the regulatory platform

The 2 MDa complex integrates multiple upstream stress signals converging at RIPK1; on cIAP loss it forms spontaneously and is necessary but not sufficient for cell death. The complex contains FADD, Caspase-8, Caspase-10 and cFLIP isoforms; the 2011 nomenclature has since been absorbed into the TNFR1 complex II framework — current usage often describes the same components as a TNF-independent complex-II variant or as a TRIF-recruited necrosome — but the paper's model is retained: RIPK1 is the core scaffold, and the platform links TLR3 (via TRIF) to the death effectors.

### Regulation by cIAPs

cIAPs block spontaneous assembly of the 2 MDa platform; the MG132 data argue they prevent assembly by ubiquitinating and degrading "modified" RIPK1; IAP antagonists themselves trigger cIAP autoubiquitination and degradation. The MG132-suppression of the MG132+IAP-antagonist complex formation points to a lysosomal pathway as a second cIAP control mechanism (speculative, based on MG132's incomplete effect).

### Caspase-8 activity as the death-quality rheostat

Caspase-8 activity within the Ripoptosome determines the death quality: Caspase-8/cFLIPL heterodimers cleave scaffold proteins (RIPK1) to disassemble the complex; cFLIPL–Caspase-8 heterodimers retain activity sufficient for RIPK1 cleavage but insufficient for full activation. In the absence of cIAPs, cFLIPS prevents this activity, so the accumulated RIPK1 activates RIPK3-dependent necroptosis — the stoichiometry of Caspase-8, cFLIPL and cFLIPS sets whether apoptosis or necroptosis occurs. Where Caspase-8 and RIPK3 are both absent, cells are fully protected. The paper explicitly notes the unresolved question of how the stoichiometric ratio of cFLIPL and cFLIPS impacts the outcome.

### Cell-type specificity and in vivo relevance

Spontaneous assembly was observed mainly in transformed cells (HaCaT-cFLIPS); PKs express high cFLIPL and show no spontaneous assembly upon IAP antagonist treatment. In vivo, double knockout of Caspase-8 and FADD is embryonic lethal and is rescued by RIPK3/RIPK1 loss — consistent with necroptosis routing when the complex cannot be disassembled.

### Translational proposals

The paper proposes combining TLR3 agonists with IAP antagonists (SMAC mimetics). It also flags cFLIP as a critical regulator and a potential target.

### Points left open by the paper

Stoichiometry of Caspase-8/cFLIPL/cFLIPS within the complex; the specific ubiquitin chains cIAPs attach to RIPK1 and whether the ubiquitination occurs on the same "modified" pool that assembles the complex; whether spontaneous, TLR3-independent assembly occurs in primary cells (the data argue it is cell-type specific); the RIPK3–MLKL necrosome downstream of this platform as a drug target distinct from RIPK1 kinase inhibition; and whether the TRIF-recruited necrosome reported in current reviews corresponds to this same 2 MDa platform.
