# Acetylcholine

**Acetylcholine (ACh)** is a diffusible quaternary-ammonium neurotransmitter and paracrine signal that mediates all cholinergic transmission at neuromuscular junctions, autonomic ganglia, and a large fraction of CNS synapses, and that also acts as a non-neuronal signalling molecule in immune, endothelial and metabolic tissues.

## Overview

ACh is synthesized in the cytoplasm by choline acetyltransferase (ChAT) from [[Choline]] and acetyl-CoA, loaded into secretory vesicles by the vesicular acetylcholine transporter (VAChT), and released by Ca-dependent exocytosis. It is terminated within the synaptic cleft by acetylcholinesterase (AChE) and the enzyme butyrylcholinesterase. Because ACh does not cross the blood–brain barrier well, peripheral and central pools are largely compartmentalized; however, vagally derived ACh reaches the brain via the [[Vagus Nerve]] and the [[Gut-Brain Axis]].

## Structure & Properties

ACh is a small, permanently charged quaternary ammonium molecule (C7H14N2O2, MW 130.19) with a positively charged trimethylammonium head and a short acetate ester tail. It is water-soluble and cannot cross the blood-brain barrier, which is why peripheral and central cholinergic pools are segregated. At the neuromuscular junction its very short diffusion distance terminates it within milliseconds, and at synapses its action is terminated by acetylcholinesterase rather than by diffusion away.

## Receptors & Pathways

ACh signals through two families of receptors:

- **Muscarinic (GPCR) M1–M5:** M1, M3, M5 couple to Gq (PLC, IP3/DAG, Ca2+ and PKC), M2 couples to Gi (inhibiting adenylyl cyclase, opening K+ channels), M4 couples to Gi. Muscarinic signalling in macrophages and microglia is predominantly M1/M3-driven and pro-inflammatory in the periphery, but the α7 nicotinic arm is anti-inflammatory.
- **Nicotinic (ligand-gated ion channel):** homopentameric pentamers made of α and β subunits (α7, α3β4, α4β2, muscle-type α1) that flux Na+/Ca2+ directly and depolarize.

## Physiological Function

- **Neuromuscular transmission:** nicotinic α1 receptors at the motor endplate convert a nerve impulse into muscle contraction.
- **Autonomic control:** vagal preganglionic fibres release ACh onto nicotinic receptors in the intrinsic cardiac ganglia and postganglionic parasympathetic fibres onto muscarinic M2 receptors in the heart, slowing rate and contractility.
- **Cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway:** ACh released by vagal afferents and by ChAT-positive T cells acts on the α7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor (α7nAChR) on [[Macrophage|macrophages]] and [[Microglia|microglia]], engaging the JAK2/STAT3 axis and inhibiting NF-κB nuclear translocation and inflammasome activation. This is the mechanistic basis of the "cholinergic anti-inflammatory reflex", and explains the anti-inflammatory efficacy of vagus-nerve stimulation and of α7 agonists such as GTS-21 in experimental [[Inflammation]] and [[Sepsis]].
- **Metabolism and longevity crosstalk:** ACh signalling intersects with [[AMPK]], [[SIRT1]] and the [[NAD+]] salvage pathway. α7nAChR activation in skeletal muscle and liver promotes mitochondrial biogenesis and fatty-acid oxidation via the [[AMPK-PGC-1α Pathway]], while sirtuin activity modulates acetyl-CoA availability for ACh synthesis. The cholinergic phenotype is also linked to [[Gut Dysbiosis]] and [[Gut Microbiome]] composition, partly through vagal tone and partly through microbial modulation of host ACh pools.
- **Endothelium:** endothelial ACh release via eNOS-derived caveolar vesicles drives flow-dependent vasodilation and prevents atherosclerosis.

## Pathology & Clinical Relevance

- Cholinesterase inhibition (organophosphates, carbamates) causes a cholinergic toxidrome: miosis, bronchorrhea, bradycardia and seizures; atropine plus pralidoxime is standard.
- Alzheimer's disease features loss of cholinergic neurons in the basal forebrain; donepezil, rivastigmine and galantamine are acetylcholinesterase inhibitors.
- Loss of neuronal α7 nicotinic signalling and reduced vagal tone contribute to inflammation and ageing; vagal stimulation and α7 agonists are being explored for inflammatory and metabolic indications.
- In the [[Gut-Brain Axis]], ACh tone shapes microbiota composition and gut motility, and microbial ACh production (e.g. by Bacillus species) is an endocrine-like signal.
