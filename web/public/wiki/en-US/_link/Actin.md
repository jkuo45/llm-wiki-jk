# Actin

**Actin** polymerizes into microfilaments that shape cells, power motility, and anchor junctions: endothelial [[CDH5]] links to actin via the catenin complex to control permeability; Iba1 crosslinks actin in [[Microglia]] for phagocytosis and chemotaxis. Actin-[[Myosin]] contraction drives [[Muscle]] force and cytokinesis; cleavage by [[Caspase-3]] dismantles the cytoskeleton in [[Apoptosis]].

## Documents

- [[CDH5]] — connects to the actin cytoskeleton via p120/β/α-catenin to establish adherens junctions.
- [[Cytosol]] — lists actin among cytoskeletal elements alongside microtubules and intermediate filaments.
- [[Iba1]] — binds actin and gelsolin, promoting crosslinking for microglial phagocytosis and motility.
