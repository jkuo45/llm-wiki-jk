# PAD4

**PAD4** (peptidylarginine deiminase 4, gene *PADI4*) is a calcium-dependent enzyme that converts protein arginine residues to citrulline. In [[NETosis]], ROS-triggered calcium flux activates PAD4, which hypercitrullinates histones (notably H3 → cit-H3), neutralizing their charge, unraveling chromatin, and licensing nuclear decondensation and NET extrusion.

## Mechanism

PAD4 requires millimolar-range Ca2+ for full activity, coupling calcium signaling to epigenetic-scale chromatin remodeling. Histone citrullination decondenses the lobed neutrophil nucleus until it fills the cell; envelope breakdown and mixing with granule proteins follow. PAD4-independent routes (elastase-mediated, mitochondrial ROS, ionomycin) exist, so PAD4 is the dominant but not exclusive path.

> [!tip] cit-H3 as a marker
> Citrullinated histone H3 (cit-H3), together with extracellular DNA and MPO–DNA complexes, is the standard immunodetection signature of PAD4-dependent NETosis.

## Inhibition

Cl-amidine (pan-PAD) and PAD4-selective inhibitors suppress histone citrullination and NET release; DNase digests already-extruded NETs downstream. PAD4 is a therapeutic target in [[Sepsis]], thrombosis, autoimmunity (rheumatoid arthritis, lupus — citrullinated autoantigens), and cancer-associated immunothrombosis.

> [!important]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> PAD4 requirement is pathway-specific. It is often dispensable for [[Suicidal NETosis]] because [[Neutrophil Elastase]]-dependent histone cleavage provides redundancy, and dispensable for [[Noncanonical NETosis]] where [[Caspase-11]] degrades histones. It is essential for chromatin decondensation in [[Vital NETosis]]: PAD4 deficiency in *Shigella flexneri*-infected neutrophils abolished histone citrullination, vital NETosis, and bacterial killing, and a selective PAD4 inhibitor abrogated ionomycin- or *S. aureus*-induced vital NETosis (Li et al. 2010; Lewis et al. 2015). PMA does not raise cytosolic Ca²⁺ sufficiently to activate PAD4; ionophores do (Neeli and Radic 2013). NADPH oxidase function is dispensable for vital NETosis.
