# Baicalin

**Baicalin** is the flavone-7-O-glucuronide of Baicalein, the dominant hydrophilic flavone glycoside of [[Scutellgling baalensis]] root.

## Mechanisms of Interest

- **p53-dependent G0/G1 arrest in MCF-7 breast cancer cells** — Wang et al., *J Asian Nat Prod Res* 2008, 10:1129–1135 (cited in the Current Issues in Molecular Biology 2025 systematic review of Baicalin/Baialein in breast cancer): "arresting cells in the G0/G1 phase and upregulating p53 and the bax gene".
- **STAT3 inhibition** — reported across cancer-cell contexts, including the [[Scutellgling baalensis]] HCC review (Ma et al., Ann Med 55(2):2247004).
- **PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway** — inhibition observed across cancer lines (e.g., Dou et al., Oncotarget 9:20089).
- **Autophagy** — BAi induces autophagy across cancer lines (Ma et al., Ann Med 55(2):2247004 — "BAe, BAi, WoG induce autophagy ... by Pi3K/Akt/mTOR, Hippo, and NF-κB pathways").
- **Wnt/β-catenin** — decreases β-catenin and target genes (e.g., Jia et al., DLD-1 cells; Dou et al., Oncotarget 9:20089).
- **QR-mediated clearance** — induction of NAD(P)H:quinone reductase via AP-1/NF-κB transactivation: Park HJ et al., *Eur J Cancer Prev* 1998, 7(6):465–471 (PMID 9926295); Park HJ et al., *Eur J Cancer Prev* 2004, 13(6):521–528 (PMID 15548947).
