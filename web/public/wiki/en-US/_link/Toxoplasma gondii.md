# Toxoplasma gondii

Toxoplasma gondii is an apicomplexan parasite reported among NET-inducing pathogens (Abi Abdallah and Denkers 2012; Abi Abdallah et al. 2012). Most NET studies agree that NETs trap extracellular microbes such as [[Candida albicans]] or T. gondii (Urban et al. 2006).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> NET extrusion is reported as a conserved immune defense strategy in response to bacteria, fungi and parasites including [[Leishmania]], [[Plasmodium falciparum]] and T. gondii (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
