# Amiodarone

**Amiodarone** is a class III (plus I/II/IV properties) antiarrhythmic blocking potassium, sodium, and calcium channels plus β-adrenergic receptors, used for refractory ventricular arrhythmias and [[Cardiac arrest]] resuscitation protocols. Its iodine load and lipophilicity cause thyroid dysfunction, pulmonary fibrosis, corneal deposits, and hepatotoxicity with chronic use.
