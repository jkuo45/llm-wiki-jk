# Sivelestat

Sivelestat (ONO-5046) is a selective small-molecule inhibitor of [[Neutrophil Elastase]] used as a tool compound to probe elastase-dependent processes. It is not cited in Burgener & Schroder 2020, but NE inhibition is one of the standard NETosis blockade approaches; NE is required for histone clipping, F-[[Actin]] degradation and [[Gasdermin D]] cleavage in [[Suicidal NETosis]].

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> NE/MPO inhibition is listed among the standard NETosis blockade approaches, alongside [[PAD4]] inhibition with [[Cl-amidine]] and [[DNase I]] digestion of already-extruded NETs (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
