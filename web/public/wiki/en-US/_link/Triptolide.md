# Triptolide

**Triptolide** is a diterpenoid triepoxide (three fused epoxides) produced by [[Tripterygium wilfordii]]; it is the primary bioactive of the leigongteng root bark (雷公藤).

## Mechanism of Action

- Covalent binding of its α,β-unsaturated ketone moiety to the XPB subunit of TFIIH; RNA Polymerase II RPB1 is hyperphosphorylated and degraded, blocking transcription initiation.
- Transcriptional inhibition drives [[Apoptosis]], [[Autophagy]], and [[Senescence]]; reverses [[Chemoresistance]]; inhibits [[Cancer Stem Cells]]; blocks [[NF-κB]].

## Toxicity

Narrow therapeutic window; hematotoxicity (5.66% of AE reports), leukopenia (5.74%), reproductive toxicity, hepatotoxicity, and nephrotoxicity (synthesis across 3,639 trials, 2,437 patients; He et al. 2019, Front Pharmacol 7).
