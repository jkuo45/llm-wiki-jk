# Bordetella pertussis

Bordetella pertussis is a Gram-negative respiratory pathogen. It encodes an adenylate cyclase toxin (ACT) that produces intracellular cAMP to suppress the [[ERK2|ERK]]-dependent oxidative burst in [[Neutrophils]] and hence [[NETosis]] (Eby et al. 2014).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> ACT is an example of microbial NET evasion by blocking upstream signaling, complementary to nuclease-mediated NET degradation and resistance to NET-bound antimicrobial proteins (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
