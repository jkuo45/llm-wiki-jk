# Streptococcus pyogenes

Streptococcus pyogenes (group A Streptococcus) is a Gram-positive bacterium reported to induce [[NETosis]] (Sumby et al. 2005). It evades NET-dependent host defense by expressing nucleases that degrade NETs (Sumby et al. 2005; Walker et al. 2007) and by suppressing [[Myeloperoxidase]] release via the M1T1 serotype streptococcal collagen-like protein 1 (Scl-1) (Döhrmann et al. 2014).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Some microbes suppress NETs by blocking upstream signaling, others encode DNases to degrade the NET, and others resist NET-bound antimicrobial proteins (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
