# ADM

**Adrenomedullin** (ADM) is a vasodilatory, endothelial-protective peptide elevated in [[Heart]] failure, sepsis, and aging. DNAm surrogates of ADM are GrimAge-clock components alongside [[Cystatin C]], B2M, GDF15, and PAI-1, translating ADM levels into an accelerated-aging signal.
