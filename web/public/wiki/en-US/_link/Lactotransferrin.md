# Lactotransferrin

Lactotransferrin (lactoferrin, LTF) is an iron-binding granule protein of [[Neutrophils]] stored in [[Azurophil Granule|azurophil granules]] and reported within [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps]]. It is a constituent of the [[Azurosome]] complex with [[Neutrophil Elastase]], [[Myeloperoxidase]], azurocidin, [[Cathepsin G]], defensin-1 and lysozyme in resting cells.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Lactotransferrin is listed among the key antimicrobial effector proteins of NETs, alongside histones, granule proteases and myeloperoxidase (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
