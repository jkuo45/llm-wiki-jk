# Pseudomonas aeruginosa

Pseudomonas aeruginosa is a Gram-negative bacterium. Proteomics identified 80 proteins within NETs induced by nonmucoid and mucoid strains of P. aeruginosa, compared with 24 proteins within [[PMA]]-induced NETs, indicating that NET composition is influenced by the eliciting stimulus (Dwyer et al. 2014; Burgener & Schroder 2020). [[Calprotectin]] and other S100 proteins are reported in P. aeruginosa-induced NETs.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Azurocidin, lysozyme C and alpha-defensins were independently reported within NETs induced by P. aeruginosa (Dwyer et al. 2014).
