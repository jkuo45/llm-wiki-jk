# Taraxasterol

**Taraxasterol** is a pentacyclic triterpene of the taraxasterane family, the primary sterol skeleton of dandelion root. It is distinct from the amyrin triterpenes (α-/β-amyrin, oleanane family) and from the sesquiterpene lactones (taraxacin). Concentration in commercial dandelion root is 0.5–2 % w/w.

## Chemistry

- Pentacyclic triterpenoid with five six-membered rings.
- Occurs as taraxasterol itself and related hydroxylated forms (taraxerol).
- Identified in root by UPLC-DAD-MS (Wang et al., 2022; Ovadje et al., 2016).

## Mechanism map (in vitro, unless noted)

- **Metabolic vulnerability**: suppresses choline kinase alpha (CHKA) in TNBC, lowering phosphocholine, phosphatidylcholine and phosphatidylethanolamine, and reduces the PC/SM membrane-fluidity ratio (Wang et al., 2022, Front Pharmacol).
- **Autophagy**: ψ-taraxasterol activates pro-death autophagy by blocking [[mTOR]]/4EBP1 and engaging [[Beclin1]]; cell death in MCF-7 by LC3-I → LC3-II conversion.
- **Class partner**: taraxacin drives the extrinsic, FADD-dependent death program when delivered as part of the root extract.

## Preclinical evidence

- TNBC (Wang et al., Front Pharmacol 2022): the dandelion extract IC50s were ≈111 µg/mL (MDA-MB-231) and ≈108 µg/mL (MDA-MB-468) in 24 h; reduced tumor volume/weight in the MDA-MB-231 xenograft and 4T1 models at 50/100 mg/kg IP daily for 15–20 days, with no body-weight difference. Quantitative proteomics and lipidomics showed CHKA down-regulation and the PC/SM ratio drop attributed to the active compounds; network analysis nominated picrasinoside F and [[Luteolin]] as the strongest compound-target matches.
- Cervical: ψ-taraxasterol at 1 µM blocks cell cycle at G0/G1 via downregulation of cyclin D1 and PCNA, upregulation of p21 in HGC-27/NCI-N87.
- No human trials have been registered; the biosynthesis-in-cell-culture optimisations of Sharma & Zafar (2016) are lab-scale only.
