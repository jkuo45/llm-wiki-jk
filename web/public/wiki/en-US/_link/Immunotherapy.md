# Immunotherapy

**Immunotherapy** treats cancer by activating anti-tumor immunity: [[Checkpoint Inhibitor|checkpoint blockade]] ([[CTLA-4]], PD-1/PD-L1), CAR-T and TIL cell therapies, bispecific engagers, and therapeutic vaccines. It is combined with [[Chemotherapy]], radiotherapy, and targeted agents; FAK inhibition is explored to sensitize stiff, immune-excluded tumors.

## Documents

- [[Chemotherapy]] — routinely combined with immunotherapy; chemotherapy-driven NAD+ loss modulates treatment context.
- [[FAK]] — FAK inhibitors in clinical evaluation, often combined with immunotherapy to overcome stromal resistance.
