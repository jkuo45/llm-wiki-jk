# Brucea Javanica

**Fruit of *Brucea javanica* (Simarrobaceae); the oil emulsion (BJOE) is used as adjuvant therapy in China.**

## Chemistry

- ≥200 secondary metabolites; core quassinoids: bruceine A–J, brusatol, bruceantin, dehydrobrusatol, yadanzioside, etc.

## Adjuvant clinical evidence

- Lung-cancer meta: BJOE combined with chemotherapy — RR 1.36 (95% CI 1.23–1.51), n=1399, survival improvement reported (Mei et al., 2021; Nutrients 192594 — as cited).

## Clinical data gaps

- No strong clinical data on isolated compounds; BJOE in combination with chemo improved treatment completion; additional clinical data exist.
