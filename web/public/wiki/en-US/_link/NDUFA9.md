# NDUFA9

**NDUFA9** (NADH dehydrogenase [ubiquinone] 1 alpha subcomplex 9) is an accessory subunit of mitochondrial [[Complex I]] (responsible for NADH oxidation at the entry point of the [[Electron Transport Chain|electron transport chain]]).

## Regulation by SIRT3

- [[SIRT3]] deacetylates NDUFA9, a [[Complex I]] component involved in regulating cellular [[ATP]] levels (Ahn et al., 2008).
- Directionally, SIRT3 sits upstream of [[Complex I]] function and [[ATP]] output; this places mitochondrial sirtuin activity directly on respiratory-chain flux.
