# Ailanthus Altissima

**Stink tree (Ailanthus altissima, Simarrobaceae); root bark contains the quassinoid ailanthone.**

## Ailanthone in MCF-7 cells (Tier 4: in vitro)

- Ailanthone induces apoptosis in MCF-7 cells; flow cytometry shows G0/G1 arrest; caspase-3 and Bax upregulated, Bcl-2 downregulated (PMCID PMC5840722, PMID 29552229; Oncology Letter 2018).

## Repression of base-excision repair via p23 (Tier 4: in vitro)

- Gastric-cancer study: ailanthone downregulates P23 and decreases XRCC1 activity; P23 inhibition decreases XRCC1 expression — regulating XRCC1 via P23; the P23–XRCC1 interaction with HSP90 is inhibited by ailanthone (PMID 34345209; Int. J. Biol. Sci. 17:2811–2825, 2021).
- The same compound induces DNA damage and apoptosis in gastric cancer cells; the XRCC1 base-excision-repair protein is decreased via P23 downregulation (recorded effect).
