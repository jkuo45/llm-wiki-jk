# 6-Shogaol

**6-Shogaol** is the major shogaol form in ginger rhizomes, alongside 6-gingerol and 6-paradol. Both 6-gingerol and 6-shogaol are phenolic alkanones of the [gingerol/shogaol family]; the degree of dehydration (gingerol → shogaol) reduces polarity but increases electrophilicity, giving 6-shogaol stronger ROS-generating and microtubule-altering activity than 6-gingerol on gastric lines (Ishiguro et al., *Biochem Biophys Res Commun* 2007, PMID 17706603).

## Mechanism map (in vitro, unless noted)

- **Extrinsic apoptosis sensitisation**: 6-shogaol upregulates TRAIL-induced caspase-3/7 and downregulates cIAP1 via NF-κB inhibition, verified in gastric cancer cell lines (Ishiguro 2007).
- **c-FLIP-dependent re-sensitisation**: Han et al. 2015 — 6-shogaol down-regulates c-FLIP(L) post-translationally in renal carcinoma Caki cells via ROS-mediated cytochrome c release and MTP collapse (PMID 25619640).
- **ROS-mediated apoptosis**: Nazim & Park 2019 — 6-shogaol induces p53 upregulation, MTP collapse and caspase-3/8 cleavage in TRAIL-resistant Huh7 cells (PMID 30483736, PMC6317668).
- **Autophagy flux attenuation**: same paper — 6-shogaol upregulates p62 and LC3-II (markers of stalled autophagy), attenuating autophagic flux (Nazim & Park 2019).
- **Antiproliferative targets**: inhibits PI3K/Akt/mTOR and GSK-3β (Pei et al. 2021, per review); cell-cycle arrest in G0/G1 in factory lines (Pei 2021).
- **Unlike 6-gingerol**: 6-shogaol alone reduces viability of gastric cancer cells by damaging microtubules and inducing mitotic arrest, rather than acting through the TRAIL/NF-κB axis (Ishiguro 2007).

## Preclinical evidence

- Gastric cancer: gastric (IC50, IC50): 24-h on HGC cells, 6-gingerol enhances TRAIL; 6-shogaol alone reduces viability via microtubule damage and mitotic arrest (Ishiguro 2007).
- Hepatocellular and colorectal activity and synergy with TRAIL in liver cancer (Nazim & Park 2019).

## Human record

- No human trials of 6-shogaol alone; redemptive studies are limited to food-supplement regimes (see [[Zingiber officinale]]).
