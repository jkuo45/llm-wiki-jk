# TBC1D17

## Overview

TBC1D17 is a predicted RAB7 GTPase-activating protein (GAP) that acts alongside [[TBC1D15]] at mitochondria–lysosome contact sites. [[FIS1]] on the outer mitochondrial membrane recruits TBC1D15 and TBC1D17, which convert [[Rab7]]-GTP to RAB7-GDP and thereby terminate the lysosome-driven tether. The specific contribution of TBC1D17 relative to TBC1D15 is less characterized; most functional data come from cell models (Tier 3).

> [!info]
> Contact duration is a regulated variable: RAB7-GTP on the lysosome promotes tethering, and TBC1D15/TBC1D17 GAP activity sets its lifetime.

## Mechanism

TBC1D17 shares the TBC domain architecture that confers GAP activity toward RAB-family small GTPases. At the contact interface, hydrolysis of GTP on RAB7 removes the nucleotide state required for tethering to the as-yet unidentified mitochondrial partner. This terminates the contact without bulk membrane fusion and can precede or accompany DRP1-dependent fission.

## Unresolved Questions

The mitochondrial RAB7-binding partner has not been identified. Whether TBC1D17 is redundant with TBC1D15 in each tissue, and whether the 2026 Gαq–FIS1–TBC1D15/17 release complex is reproduced in independent laboratories, remain unverified.
