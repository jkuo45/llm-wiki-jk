# Arctium lappa

**Arctium lappa** (burdock) is an Asteraceae plant; the root matrix enters formulations, while seed preparations carry most lignans. Root chemistry includes the lignan arctigenin (docks [[TGFBR1]]), chlorogenic and caffeic acids, lupeol, ursolic acid, [[Quercetin]], and polyphenols.

## Chemistry and Evidence (Tier 4 In Vitro)

- **Colorectal cancer, ethanol root extract (Hassan 2025, Biomed Res Ther 11:960)**: IC50 ≈ 11.80 µg/mL in colorectal cancer cells vs ≈ 1485 µg/mL in normal fibroblasts; DNA fragmentation observed, MMP-2/9 ↓, [[BAX]] ↑ 22.4×, Bcl-2 −13.3×, caspase-3 ↑ 4.07×, p53 ↑ 2.69×, IL-6/IL-1β/TNF-α ↓, and arctigenin docks [[TGFBR1]]. No human data.
- **Jurkat T cells (Don 2019, Biomed Pharmacother 110:918–929)**: ethyl acetate root extract IC50 ≈ 102.2 µg/mL.
- **Refractory 3T3-type cells (Don 2019)**: root extract activity reported against refractory cells; arctigenin activity vs triptolide-class agents remains preclinical and not directly comparative.
