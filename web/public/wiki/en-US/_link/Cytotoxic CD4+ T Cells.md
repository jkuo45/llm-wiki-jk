# Cytotoxic CD4+ T Cells

Cytotoxic CD4+ T cells (CD4 CTLs) are effector [[CD4+ T Cells]] that acquire cytotoxic machinery — EOMES, T-bet, perforin, granzymes ([[Granzyme B]], GzmK, GzmA) — classically under chronic antigenic stimulation (chronic infection, cancer) and increasingly recognized as an age-associated T cell population (Elyahu et al., 2019; Hashimoto et al., 2019 — expanded in supercentenarians).

## Phenotype & Differentiation

- In aged mouse [[Bone Marrow]], CD4 CTLs are CD38^hi^CD44^lo^CCL5+ (>25% of BM CD4+ cells at 24 months versus ~1.6% in young mice), express PD-1, EOMES, T-bet, perforin, GzmA, [[Granzyme B]], and NK-associated transcripts (*Nkg7, Gzmk, Klrb1f, Runx3*).
- Differentiation in an aged environment requires [[T Cell Receptor|TCR]] signaling: OT-II cells (antigen-free TCR) transferred into old hosts fail to become CCL5+CD4+ CTLs, while cytokine exposure alone is insufficient. Chronic anti-CD3/CD28 stimulation (CS) in vitro reproduces the phenotype including functional cytotoxicity against antigen-loaded B cells.

## Mechanism of Action & Pathways

- CD4 CTLs exhibit depolarized mitochondria (low TMRM/MitoTracker Green ratio), elevated mitochondrial ROS, and cytosolic DNA foci; chronic TCR stimulation → mitochondrial stress → [[cGAS-STING Pathway|cGAS–STING]] activation → [[CCL5]] production. STING is required for CCL5 induction but dispensable for EOMES/cytotoxic differentiation.
- CD38, a [[NAD+]]ase, is overexpressed and may feed the mitochondrial stress loop; T-cell-specific [[TFAM]] deletion in mice recapitulates CD4 CTL accumulation and myeloid skewing by 8 months.

## Physiological Function & Pathology

- Beneficial: eliminate senescent cells in skin (Hasegawa et al., 2023) and may control tumor growth; persist in supercentenarians.
- Deleterious at scale: massive accumulation in aged [[Bone Marrow]] drives myeloid-biased [[Hematopoiesis]] — CCL5 signaling to [[CCR5]] on [[Hematopoietic Stem Cell|HSCs]] and GMPs expands myeloid output, raising the neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio, a mortality predictor across age-associated diseases.

## Therapeutic Landscape

- [[Maraviroc]] (CCR5 antagonist) blocks the downstream myelopoiesis axis without targeting the cells themselves; open strategies include modulating CD4 CTL function, limiting BM recruitment, and improving their mitochondrial fitness.
