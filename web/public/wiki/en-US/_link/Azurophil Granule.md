# Azurophil Granule

Azurophil granules are the primary granules of [[Neutrophils]], storing [[Neutrophil Elastase]], [[Myeloperoxidase]], [[Cathepsin G]], [[Proteinase 3]], azurocidin, lysozyme, [[Lactotransferrin]] and alpha-defensins. Their contents are delivered to the [[Phagosome]] during [[Phagocytosis]], externalised by degranulation, and form the granule cargo of [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps]]. In resting cells these proteins are organised into the [[Azurosome]] complex.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> NADPH-oxidase-derived [[Reactive Oxygen Species]] trigger [[Myeloperoxidase]] activation and azurosome dissociation within azurophil granules, releasing [[Neutrophil Elastase]] into the cytosol to initiate [[Suicidal NETosis]] (Metzler et al. 2011, 2014; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
