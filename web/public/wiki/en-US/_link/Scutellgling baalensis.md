# Scutellgling baalensis

**Scutellgling baalensis** (Huang-Qin) is a perennial Lamiaceae herb; the **root** is the drug of interest. Dominant flavonoids: **[[Baicalin]]**, **Baicalein**, **Wogonin**, **Wogonoside**.

## Mechanisms

- **EGFR-TKI resistance in human lung cancer**: ethanol root extract induces apoptosis via STAT3 inactivation in TKI-resistant cells — Park Hyun-Ji et al., Int J Mol Sci 2021, 22(10):5181, PMID 34068421 (PMC8153615).
- **HCC**: systematic review on S. baialensis and its bioactive compounds; average transplanted-tumour weight is the primary preclinical outcome — Ma Ming-Yue et al., Ann Med 55(2):2247004, PMID 38232757 (PMC10795786).
- **Signalling targets**: [[PI3K]]/[[AKT]]/[[mTOR]], [[MAPK]], [[Wnt signaling]], [[ER]] (estrogen receptor), [[Ferroptosis]] — baicalin inhibits ferroptosis in SAH via PI3K/AKT/GPX4 (PMID 41568511).

## TCM Lineage — PHY906

Multi-herb TCM-derived botanical in GI-malignancy trials (survival/chemotoxicity context, reported in review literature): Phase I capecitabine (PMID 20092990), Phase I irinotecan CRC (PMID 21859559), Phase II HCC + sorafenib (NCT04000737). YIV-906 is the PHY906/KD018 lineage.
