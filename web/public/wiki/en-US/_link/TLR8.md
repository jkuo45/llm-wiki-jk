# TLR8

## Overview

TLR8 (toll-like receptor 8) is an endosomal pattern-recognition receptor that binds single-stranded RNA. In the mitochondria–lysosome network, mitochondrial RNA released into the lysosomal lumen engages TLR8 and, with [[TLR9]]-dependent sensing of poorly methylated [[Mitochondrial DNA]], activates [[MyD88]]–[[NF-κB]] signalling and inflammatory cytokine production. The mechanism is established in cell-culture models (Tier 3).

> [!info]
> Cargo can act as information: lysosomal processing of mitochondrial nucleic acids converts a degradation product into a cell-wide inflammatory signal.

## Mechanism

TLR8 resides in endolysosomal membranes where acidic proteolysis generates ligands from ingested material. Ligand binding recruits the adaptor MyD88, leading to NF-κB activation and transcription of pro-inflammatory cytokines. The pathway links lysosomal cargo handling to [[Inflammation]] rather than to organelle degradation per se.

## Unresolved Questions

The contribution of TLR8 relative to [[TLR7]] in a given cell type, and whether the pathway operates in chronic mitochondrial disease in vivo, remain untested in the available sources.
