# POMC

**POMC** (**Pro-O**pio**M**elanocortin) is a precursor polypeptide that is proteolytically cleaved (via PC1/3 and PC2) into biologically active peptides, including adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH), alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (alpha-MSH), and beta-endorphin.

## Hypothalamic role

- Arcuate [[Hypothalamus|hypothalamic]] POMC neurons are a central node of energy homeostasis and feeding behavior.
- [[SIRT1]] expression is induced in POMC neurons during dietary restriction (caloric restriction and fasting), where SIRT1 regulates feeding behaviors (Cakir et al., 2009; Ramadori et al., 2010; Satoh et al., 2010).
- SIRT1 deacetylase activity in POMC neurons is required for homeostatic defenses against diet-induced obesity (Ramadori et al., 2010).
- Brain-specific SIRT1 knockout mice lose the caloric-restriction-induced increase in adaptive physical activity, which brain SIRT1 overexpression enhances (Cohen et al., 2009).
