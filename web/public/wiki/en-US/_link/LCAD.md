# LCAD

**LCAD** (long-chain acyl-CoA dehydrogenase) is a mitochondrial flavoenzyme of fatty-acid beta-oxidation that dehydrogenates long-chain acyl-CoA species feeding electrons into the [[Electron Transport Chain|electron transport chain]].

## Regulation by SIRT3

- [[SIRT3]] deacetylates LCAD and upregulates its enzymatic activity during fasting (Hirschey et al., 2010).
- SIRT3-knockout mice accumulate higher levels of fatty-acid oxidation intermediates and have lower [[ATP]] levels during fasting, establishing LCAD activation as a required fasting-adaptation arm of SIRT3.
