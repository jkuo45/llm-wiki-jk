# Klebsiella pneumoniae

Klebsiella pneumoniae is a Gram-negative bacterium. Murine [[Neutrophil Elastase]] deficiency prevents [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps|NET]] formation during in vivo challenge with K. pneumoniae, establishing NE as critical for [[Suicidal NETosis]] (Papayannopoulos et al. 2010). NE is also crucial in host defense against Gram-negative bacteria such as [[Escherichia coli]] or K. pneumoniae (Belaaouaj et al. 1998).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> NETs limit microbial dissemination and NE-rich NETs contribute direct antibacterial activity (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
