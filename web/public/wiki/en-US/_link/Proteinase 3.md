# Proteinase 3

Proteinase 3 (PRTN3) is one of the three neutrophil serine proteases, with [[Neutrophil Elastase]] and [[Cathepsin G]], stored in [[Azurophil Granule|azurophil granules]] and carried on [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps]]. It participates in the [[Azurosome]] complex and in microbial killing after degranulation or phagocytosis.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Alpha-defensins, lysozymes and the three neutrophil serine proteases NE, CatG and PR-3 are listed as the key granule proteins involved in microbial killing (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
