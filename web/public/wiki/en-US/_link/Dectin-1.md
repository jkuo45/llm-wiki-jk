# Dectin-1

Dectin-1 is the C-type lectin receptor (CLEC7A) that mediates [[Phagocytosis]] of fungi by recognising beta-glucan. Neutrophils deficient in dectin-1 cannot correctly select an antimicrobial response and show uncontrolled [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps|NET]] release, identifying the receptor as part of the microbe size-sensing machinery described by Branzk et al. 2014.

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> Small microbes that are phagocytosed sequester granule proteases in the [[Phagosome]] and are poor NET stimulants; large microbes such as [[Candida albicans]] hyphae instead trigger cytosolic NE release and [[NETosis]] (Branzk et al. 2014; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
