# Complement Receptor 3

Complement receptor 3 (CR3, CD11b/CD18, Mac-1) is a beta-2 integrin on [[Neutrophils]] and a receptor of the [[Complement System]]. When beta-glucan of [[Candida albicans]] is immobilised by [[Fibronectin]], CR3 recognition induces robust [[Vital NETosis|NET formation]] (Byrd et al. 2013).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> CR3 is one of several surface receptors, with [[TLR2]] and [[TLR4]], that specify receptor-mediated rapid [[Vital NETosis]], which proceeds through [[Calcium Signaling|calcium influx]] and [[PAD4]] rather than [[NADPH Oxidase]] (Burgener & Schroder 2020).
