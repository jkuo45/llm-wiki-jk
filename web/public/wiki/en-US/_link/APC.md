# APC

**APC** (adenomatous polyposis coli) scaffolds the Axin/GSK3β destruction complex that phosphorylates [[β-Catenin]] for degradation, restraining [[Wnt signaling]]. Truncating APC mutations initiate most colorectal cancers via constitutive β-catenin activity; germline mutation causes familial adenomatous polyposis. [[FRAT2]] antagonizes the destruction complex to amplify Wnt signaling.
