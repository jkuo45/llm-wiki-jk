# Citrobacter rodentium

Citrobacter rodentium is a Gram-negative bacterium that accesses the cytosol of [[Neutrophils]]. Human and murine neutrophils infected with C. rodentium or Delta-sifA [[Salmonella enterica]] died and extruded [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps|NETs]] with nuclear delobulation, DNA extrusion, DNA-MPO colocalisation and histone citrullination (Chen et al. 2018).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> NET extrusion in these infections required [[Caspase-4]]/[[Caspase-11]] and [[Gasdermin D]] but proceeded independently of [[Neutrophil Elastase]] or [[Myeloperoxidase]], defining [[Noncanonical NETosis]] (Chen et al. 2018).
