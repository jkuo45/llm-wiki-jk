# Calprotectin

Calprotectin is the S100A8/S100A9 heterodimer abundant in [[Neutrophils]] and a cytoplasmic constituent of [[Neutrophil Extracellular Traps]]. Immunodepletion of calprotectin abolishes the in vitro antifungal activity of NET proteins, and approximately one third of cellular S100A9 is released NET-bound rather than as a soluble protein (Urban et al. 2009).

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - Neutrophil Extracellular Traps in Host Defense]]
> S100 calcium-binding proteins are listed among the cytoplasmic and cytoskeletal proteins reported within NETs; proteomics identified 24 proteins in [[PMA]]-induced NETs and 80 in [[Pseudomonas aeruginosa]]-induced NETs (Urban et al. 2009; Dwyer et al. 2014; Burgener & Schroder 2020).
