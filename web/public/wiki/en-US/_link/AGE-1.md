# AGE-1

**AGE-1** is the *C. elegans* class-I PI3K catalytic subunit acting downstream of the DAF-2 insulin/IGF-1 receptor: reduced AGE-1 signaling derepresses DAF-16/FOXO, extending [[Lifespan]] and stress resistance. The age-1(hx546) mutant was among the first single-gene longevity mutants, anchoring the conserved nutrient-sensing longevity axis ([[CeTOR]], mTOR, AMPK).
