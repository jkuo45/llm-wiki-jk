# Cichorium Intybus

**Chicory root; studied in a DMH-induced rat colon-cancer model (Tier 4: in vitro).**

## Composition

- Inulin: 14.9–18.3% of fresh root weight.

## Prebiotic effect (Tier 4: in vitro)

- DMH-induced aberrant crypt foci (ACF) inhibition: 51.2% (Qamar et al., *Nutrients*, 2016; PMCID PMC4997378).

## Gut FX microbiome changes

- Gut Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes (FX) shifts observed in the DMH model (Vanhoutvin et al., *Gut*, 2017).
