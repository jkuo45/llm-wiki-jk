# Tithonia Diversifolia

**Tithonia diversifolia (Asteraceae), an invasive shrub native to Central America; traditional external use for haematomas and wounds in Central America.**

## Sesquiterpene lactones (Tier 4: in vitro)

- The main sesquiterpene lactones of central America: diversifolin, diversifolin methyl ether, and tirotundin.
- Anti-inflammatory activity in human Jurkat cells: the Sls do not inhibit cyclooxygenase-1 or phospholipase A2 (arachidonic acid pathway); they inhibit NF-κB activation via alkylation of cysteine residues in the DNA-binding domain of NF-κB (Rüngeler et al., *Planta Medica*, 1998; PMID 9810261).

## Cytotoxicity of flowers (Tier 4: in vitro)

- Tagitinin C IC50 against KB, MCF-7, HepG2 and A549: 0.28/1.18/1.38/1.15 µM (Vietnam Journal of Science and Technology, 2026).

## Traditional use

- Traditional cancer use in Central America.
