# BESTO Mice

The **BESTO mice** (**BE**ta-cell-specific [[SIRT1]] **T**ransgenic **O**verexpressors) are transgenic mice that specifically overexpress [[SIRT1]] in pancreatic beta cells.

## Phenotype

- BESTO mice have improved [[Glucose]] tolerance when fed a high-fat diet (Moynihan et al., 2005).
- Mechanistically, beta-cell SIRT1 represses uncoupling protein 2 ([[UCP2]]), preserving mitochondrial membrane potential and [[ATP]] synthesis needed for glucose-stimulated [[Insulin]] secretion.
- The model links sirtuin activity directly to beta-cell function and [[Diabetes]] susceptibility.
