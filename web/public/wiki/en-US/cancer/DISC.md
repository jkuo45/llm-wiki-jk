# DISC

The **Death-Inducing Signaling Complex (DISC)** is a multi-protein assembly that forms during the initiation of the [[Extrinsic Pathway]] of [[Apoptosis]].

## Assembly
The DISC forms on the intracellular tails of death receptors (e.g., [[Fas]], [[DR4]], [[DR5]], [[TNFR1]]) following ligand binding.
1.  **Receptor Trimerization**: Ligand binding causes receptors to cluster.
2.  **Adaptor Recruitment**: The adaptor protein [[FADD]] (and sometimes [[TRADD]]) is recruited to the receptor's death domain.
3.  **Caspase Recruitment**: FADD then recruits pro-[[Caspase-8]] (or Caspase-10) molecules through [[Death Effector Domain|death effector domain]] interactions.

## Function
The primary function of the DISC is to facilitate the proximity-induced auto-activation of [[Caspase-8]]. Once activated, caspase-8 is released into the cytoplasm to activate executioner caspases like [[Caspase-3]].

> [!info]
> Source: [[_document_ - FLIP the Switch Regulation of Apoptosis and Necroptosis by cFLIP|Tsuchiya et al. 2015]]
> The DISC is a stoichiometrically imbalanced, [[c-FLIP]]-titrated platform rather than a fixed 1:1 assembly. Quantitative mass spectrometry finds several-fold more procaspase-8 than [[FADD]] with very little c-FLIP (Dickens; Schleich), while a competing model caps (procaspase-8 + c-FLIP):FADD near 2:1 (Majkut). Two-step kinetic models (Kallenberger; Majkut) separate intradimer prodomain cleavage within one DISC from interdimer enzymatic-domain cleavage driven by DISC clustering; only the latter releases fully processed caspase-8. [[c-FLIP|FLIP_L]] heterodimers perform the interdimer step without intradimer processing and stay membrane-bound (yielding [[p43-FLIP]]), whereas [[c-FLIP|FLIP_S/R]] cap DED-chain elongation and terminate activation. The N-terminal procaspase-8 prodomain left behind after complete processing structurally resembles FLIP_S and provides a negative-feedback brake.

## Connections
- [[Extrinsic Pathway]]: The central activation platform for death receptor-mediated apoptosis.
- [[FADD]]: The core structural adaptor of the DISC.
- [[Caspase-8]]: The enzyme activated within the DISC.
- [[c-FLIP]]: Isoform ratio (FLIP_L vs FLIP_S/R) sets whether the DISC releases apoptotic caspase-8 or stays in a restrained, survival-favoring state.
- [[p43-FLIP]]: Fragment marking c-FLIP_L heterodimer firing at the DISC.
- [[Ripoptosome]]: Receptor-independent cytosolic counterpart that mirrors DISC DED-chain assembly.
- [[Intrinsic Pathway]]: Linked to the DISC via [[Bid]] cleavage.
- [[Death Effector Domain|DED]]: The dimerization module through which procaspase-8, c-FLIP and FADD assemble the complex.
- [[vFLIP]]: Viral DED mimics that occupy the same interface and block DISC output.
