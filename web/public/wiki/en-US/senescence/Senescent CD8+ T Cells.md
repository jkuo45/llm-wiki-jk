# Senescent CD8+ T Cells

**Senescent CD8+ T cells** are [[CD8 T cells|CD8+ T cells]] that have entered a state of irreversible cell-cycle arrest with a pro-inflammatory [[SASP|senescence-associated secretory phenotype]], distinct from [[T Cell Exhaustion]]. Ye et al. (2026) identify them as the primary pathogenic T-cell compartment in [[Ulcerative Colitis]].

## Phenotype

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Aging Cell - 2026 - Ye - NAD  Metabolic Reprogramming Drives CD8  T Cells Senescence and Exacerbates Ulcerative Colitis|Ye et al., 2026, Aging Cell]]
> High-senescence-score (HSS) CD8+ T cells from UC mucosa upregulate pro-inflammatory mediators (IFNG, TNF, CCL3, CCL4) and senescence genes ([[CDKN2A|CDKN2A]]/[[p16]], CDKN1A/[[p21 CIP1|p21]], [[KLRG1]]), while downregulating cytotoxic molecules (GZMB, PRF1) and co-stimulatory [[CD27]]/CD28. They lack differential expression of canonical exhaustion checkpoints ([[PD-1|PDCD1]], [[CTLA-4]], [[LAG-3]], HAVCR2).

The population is marked by SA-β-gal activity, γ-[[H2A.X|H2A.X]] and [[p16]]/[[p21 CIP1|p21]] positivity, shortened [[Telomere|telomeres]], a CD27−KLRG1+ surface phenotype, impaired proliferative capacity, and reduced [[Perforin|perforin]] production.

## Induction and pathogenic role

[[NAD+ Metabolic Reprogramming|NAD+ depletion]] — via [[FK866]]-mediated [[NAMPT]] inhibition, [[SIRT1]] agonism ([[SRT1720]]), or siRNA NAMPT knockdown — is sufficient to induce this senescent state; [[NMN]] supplementation largely abrogates it. Mechanistically, NAD+ exhaustion suppresses [[Oxidative Phosphorylation|OXPHOS]], damages mitochondria, and releases [[mtDNA]] into the cytosol through [[VDAC|VDAC1]] oligomerization, activating [[cGAS-STING Pathway]]–[[IRF3]] signaling. [[STING]]-knockout CD8+ T cells are resistant to FK866-induced senescence.

Adoptive transfer of NAD+-depleted senescent CD8+ T cells into DSS-colitic mice accelerates weight loss and disease activity, expands colonic CD8+ T cells and IFN-γ-producing cells, and increases KLRG1+CD27− senescent CD8+ T cells. [[Senolytic Therapy|Senolytic therapy]] ([[Dasatinib]] + [[Quercetin]]) reduces CD27− CD8+ T cells and their IFN-γ/TNF-α output; CD8 depletion yields no additive benefit, indicating senolysis acts primarily through clearance of this subset.

## Clinical relevance

Baseline infiltration scores of senescent CD8+ T cells, together with NAD+ metabolism scores, correlate with endoscopic/Mayo disease activity and predict primary non-response to [[Infliximab]] and [[Vedolizumab]] in UC cohorts.
