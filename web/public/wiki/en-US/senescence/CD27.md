# CD27

**CD27** (TNFRSF7) is a type-I transmembrane glycoprotein of the tumour-necrosis-factor receptor superfamily, constitutively expressed on naive and early-memory [[T Cell|T cells]]. Engagement by its ligand CD70 provides co-stimulatory signalling that supports T-cell expansion, survival and memory formation. Progressive antigen-driven differentiation downregulates CD27, so loss of CD27 is a standard marker of late-differentiated, senescent T cells.

## Role in CD8+ T-cell senescence

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Aging Cell - 2026 - Ye - NAD  Metabolic Reprogramming Drives CD8  T Cells Senescence and Exacerbates Ulcerative Colitis|Ye et al., 2026, Aging Cell]]
> Colitic mice show increased CD27-[[KLRG1]]+ CD4+ and CD8+ T cells in the intestine. D+Q senolytic treatment significantly decreases the frequency of CD27- CD8+ T cells while CD27- CD4+ and CD27- CD4+Foxp3+ cells are unchanged, and the CD27- CD8+ fraction — but not the CD27- CD4+ fraction — correlates with body-weight and colon-weight disease metrics. FK866-induced senescent CD8+ T cells downregulate CD27 and CD28.

CD27 loss, paired with [[KLRG1]] gain, is the operational surface signature of [[Senescent CD8+ T Cells|senescent CD8+ T cells]] in [[Ulcerative Colitis]]. In vivo depletion of CD8+ T cells produces no additive benefit over [[Senolytic Therapy|senolytic therapy]], indicating that clearance of the CD27- senescent CD8+ compartment mediates the therapeutic effect.
