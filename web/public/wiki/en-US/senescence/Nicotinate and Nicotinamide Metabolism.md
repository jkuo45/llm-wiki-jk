# Nicotinate and Nicotinamide Metabolism

**Nicotinate and nicotinamide metabolism** is the conserved pathway (KEGG *Nicotinate and nicotinamide metabolism*) that supplies and recycles [[NAD+]]. It comprises de novo synthesis from [[Tryptophan]] via the kynurenine pathway, the Preiss-Handler pathway from [[Niacin|nicotinic acid]], and the [[NAD+ Salvage Pathway|salvage pathway]] in which [[NAMPT]] converts nicotinamide to [[NMN]] and NMNAT enzymes produce NAD+. NAD+-consuming enzymes — [[CD38]], [[SIRT1]] sirtuins and [[PARP1]] PARPs — cleave NAD+ to nicotinamide and [[ADP-ribose]], closing the loop.

## Enrichment in ulcerative colitis

> [!info] Source: [[_document_ - Aging Cell - 2026 - Ye - NAD  Metabolic Reprogramming Drives CD8  T Cells Senescence and Exacerbates Ulcerative Colitis|Ye et al., 2026, Aging Cell]]
> Untargeted metabolomics of murine intestinal mucosa showed the nicotinate and nicotinamide metabolism pathway significantly enriched in the colitis group, with reduced NAD+ biosynthetic precursors (tryptophan, nicotinic acid) and accumulated catabolites (nicotinamide, ADP-ribose), plus directly quantified lower NAD+. Expression of Nampt, Sirt1, Cd38 and Parp1 was upregulated. GSEA corroborated pathway enrichment in human UC mucosa (GSE87466); single-cell and bulk profiling showed the pathway profoundly upregulated in high-senescence-score CD8+ T cells, with reduced NAMPT and elevated CD38/SIRT1/PARP1.

Enrichment of this pathway is the molecular signature of [[NAD+ Metabolic Reprogramming]]: impaired biosynthesis plus heightened consumption produces a hyperconsumptive state that exhausts the NAD+ pool and drives [[Senescent CD8+ T Cells|CD8+ T-cell senescence]] through [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]] and [[cGAS-STING Pathway]] activation in [[Ulcerative Colitis]].
