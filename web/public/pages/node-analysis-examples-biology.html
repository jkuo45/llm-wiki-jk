Node-level Network Analysis for Biological Knowledge Graphs

Each entity in a knowledge graph carries a metric fingerprint — in- and out-degree, PageRank, betweenness, clustering, k-core, community — that can be read as a biological role. This page is a reusable reference for the analysis pipeline, the metrics and their NetworkX calls, and the relation-aware methods that turn a node's fingerprint into an actionable reading. The same workflow applies to any biological node: read its fingerprint off graph.json, compare it against peers, and check it with the methods below.

01 · Framing

The per-node fingerprint is a role, not a score

Centrality is often treated as a ranking: higher is more important, pick the top ten. That is the wrong object for a directed, relation-typed biomedical graph. A source enzyme that spreads a vulnerability into many lipid nodes looks unimportant under PageRank and decisive under out-degree. A secretory program that sits on shortest paths between mitochondria, epigenetics, and inflammation looks ordinary by clustering and extraordinary by betweenness. The fingerprint is the pattern across metrics, read against the biology already in the implementation. In this corpus the fingerprint is attached in enrich_graph_metrics() and stored on every node of graph.json: degree, in_degree, out_degree, pagerank, betweenness_centrality, clustering_coefficient, k_core_number, community_size, community_name.

Two pipeline choices make the numbers usable as biology rather than as co-occurrence artifacts.

  • Type-hub denylisting. Abstract categories (chemical, protein, enzyme, gene, and the rest of DENYLIST) are pruned before centrality. Otherwise a generic “protein” blob dominates every ranking and specific entities (ACSL4, GPX4, ferroptosis) disappear.
  • Directed graph, undirected metrics. In- and out-degree and PageRank run on the DiGraph. Betweenness, clustering, and k-core run on the undirected projection — the usual NetworkX pattern when direction is incomplete or when the question is module membership rather than causal flow.

02 · Metric atlas

What each number is asking the graph

Scope of the fingerprint — metrics versus relation-aware methods

Each card below names its NetworkX call, the biological question it poses, and the caveat that most often misleads a reading. The values are the definitions used in this implementation, not generic textbook paraphrases.

Every metric is a single scalar stored on one node: a static, per-node fingerprint of that entity's role in isolation — a source, a bottleneck, a backbone, a module, or a leaf. The pattern across these numbers characterizes what kind of node you are looking at. What a scalar cannot express is the relationship between two named entities: how they connect along labelled, confidence-weighted edges, whether they share a neighbourhood, whether a missing edge is plausible, or how far apart two pathways sit once low-confidence routes are down-weighted. Those questions are the job of the relation-aware methods in scripts/04_node_analysis.py, which operate pairwise and multi-node on the same graph.json. Read the fingerprint as the characterization layer; read the relation-aware methods as the how and why.

Literature context, not implementation metrics

Degree–essentiality (Jeong et al., 2001), bottleneck proteins (Yu et al., 2007), and k-shell enrichment for essential proteins (Wuchty & Almaas, 2005) are the classical PPI justifications for reading these metrics as biology. This implementation is a literature-derived knowledge graph, not a physical interactome: the same mathematics applies, but edges are typed relations with curator confidence, not binding events. Treat ranks as hypotheses to check against the source notes.

Interpretation trap

Acid ceramidase vs Aging · graph 867a5ae5fdb8a46c

PageRank and out-degree answer opposite questions. PageRank rewards being pointed at by other important nodes. Out-degree marks a node that points outward. Acid ceramidase is a spreader (out-degree 17, PageRank 0.000758). Aging is an attractor (in-degree 35, PageRank 0.00739). Using either metric alone would invert the biological reading.

03 · Beyond the static fingerprint

Relation-aware methods in this repository/implementation.

scripts/04_node_analysis.py runs on the same graph.json and answers questions the per-node vector cannot: how two entities connect, whether they share a neighbourhood, whether a missing edge is plausible, and how far apart two pathways really are once low-confidence routes are down-weighted. 

uv run --with networkx --with scipy python3 scripts/04_node_analysis.py \
    --sources sirt1 sirt3 --targets adrenochrome

nx.all_shortest_paths · edge relation / confidence

Shortest-path multiplicity

Not only whether A reaches B, but the labelled chain (Acid ceramidase —promotes→ Lipid Peroxidation —drives→ Ferroptosis) and the first-hop bridges shared across equally short paths.

Count of equally short, relationally distinct chains between a driver and an effector, read off the undirected graph. High multiplicity is the network signature of a module built to reroute: a block at any single hop is absorbed by parallel bypass rather than propagating failure. SIRT1 reaches NF-κB by three chains (NAD+ competition, direct RelA deacetylation, a chromatin relay), so boosting SIRT1 only partly dampens NF-κB through redundant wiring.

Caveat. Multiplicity of one is a genuine, rate-limiting choke point whose single causative edge is the precise intervention target. Read a connection of only a few scarce bridges as cross-talk — two modules coupled through rare intermediaries, not a shared linear pathway.

Example: ask whether SIRT1 reaches NF-κB by one route or several — if three equally short chains (NAD+ competition, direct RelA deacetylation, a chromatin relay) connect them, boosting SIRT1 will be partially absorbed by parallel wiring rather than cleanly silencing NF-κB.

neighbour-set Jaccard

Neighbourhood similarity

Entities that share neighbours are mechanistic analogs or off-target twins. Low Jaccard with a high-degree partner is a distinct neighbourhood, not a restatement of the same module.

Fraction of shared neighbours (Jaccard) between two entities, on the undirected projection. High overlap is the network signature of entities that are echoes of one another — co-complexed, co-regulated, or paralogous — so a hit to one is heard in the other. PARP1 and PARP2 share NAD+ turnover, PARP inhibitors, and DNA-repair partners, so a supposed PARP1-selective inhibitor is likely to nudge PARP2.

Caveat. A low Jaccard against a high-degree partner is not a weak copy of the same biology but a genuinely distinct address — the distinction between a true functional twin and an off-target confound. Separation reads as mechanistic independence: two name-adjacent nodes that share no actual interactors argue for their own mechanism.

Example: PARP1 and PARP2 share NAD+ turnover, PARP inhibitors, and DNA-repair partners — a high Jaccard therefore warns that a supposed PARP1-selective inhibitor is likely to nudge PARP2, a de-risking check before committing to it.

nx.adamic_adar_index

Link prediction

Shared neighbours down-weighted by log-degree. Surfaces plausible missing edges as a hypothesis queue, not as facts. Requires manual literature check before a triple is written back.

Score of plausible missing edges from shared neighbours down-weighted by log-degree (Adamic–Adar), on the undirected projection. High score is the network signature of a real, under-recorded interaction: a quiet shared neighbour is a strong hint because a promiscuous hub would make the signal meaningless. SIRT1 and an unconnected kinase that share only one rare scaffold warrant a targeted literature search before being written off as unrelated.

Caveat. The same sparsity that earns a prediction also manufactures false twins — under-curation produces both the missing edge and the artifact. Treat every call as a to-read list, never a finding: a primary-literature check is required before a triple is written back.

Example: if SIRT1 and an unconnected kinase share only a single rare scaffold with no curated edge, that pairing warrants a targeted literature search before being written off as unrelated.

scipy.linalg.eigh on the Laplacian

Spectral cut (Fiedler)

The script uses dense eigh because eigsh(which="SM") returns the trivial zero mode on a singular Laplacian and makes the Fiedler vector useless.

Algebraic connectivity λ2 and the Fiedler vector split the graph into two coherent subsystems, computed on the Laplacian of the undirected projection. The cut is the network signature of the weakest functional seam: it cleaves catabolic from anabolic programs, or stress responses from housekeeping metabolism. On the senescence corpus the seam falls between the lipid-peroxidation/secretory program and housekeeping metabolism, stranding bridges like Acid ceramidase squarely on the cut.

Caveat. λ2 near zero exposes a near-fragmented architecture prone to dissociation; larger values denote a tightly interwoven state. Nodes deep in one half are captive to that program alone — local specialists, not cross-program levers.

Example: on a senescence corpus the seam tends to fall between the lipid-peroxidation/secretory program and housekeeping metabolism, stranding bridges like Acid ceramidase squarely on the cut — the first place to look for cross-program regulators.

Laplacian pseudoinverse

Effective resistance

Self-resistance is a sanity check and should be ~0. Truncated SVD is not a valid commute-time proxy here; the script builds the pseudoinverse from thresholded eigenvalues.

Metric-aware distance from the Laplacian pseudoinverse that down-weights long, low-confidence routes, z-scored against a random-node null sample. Low resistance is the network signature of abundant parallel wiring: two processes sit electrically close because many redundant routes run alongside, not because one edge connects them. Ferroptosis sits electrically close to Inflammation despite no single dominant edge, so many parallel routes mean low resistance; a driver reachable by only one thin, low-confidence chain is the fragile, druggable exception.

Caveat. As with betweenness, pass an inverted confidence (1/confidence or −log) as distance, never the raw score.

Example: Ferroptosis can sit electrically close to Inflammation despite no single dominant edge — many parallel routes mean low resistance; a driver reachable by only one thin, low-confidence chain is the fragile, druggable exception.

nx.pagerank(..., personalization=)

Personalized PageRank

Seed a node of interest and rank the rest of the graph by walk-mass flowing from that seeded node. This is the correct global-to-local move when the fingerprint has already named a backbone node.

Walk-mass ranking from a seeded node (personalization=), on the undirected graph. High local rank is the network signature of effectors a process actually drives, surfaced by redistributing probability down that node's own wiring rather than the famous global hubs. Seeding Senescent cells and intersecting with Ferroptosis and SASP surfaces the shared effectors — lipid peroxidation, IL-6 — that generalist ranking would bury.

Caveat. A lone seed's top ranks reflect as much the choice of origin as the underlying network, so over-read one walk at your peril. Seed several independent nodes and intersect the top ranks; effectors that recur across seeds are the robust, mechanism-true biology.

Example: seeding PPR on Senescent cells and intersecting with seeds on Ferroptosis and SASP surfaces the shared effectors — lipid peroxidation, IL-6 — that generalist ranking would bury.

04 · Node roles

The Interpretation Layer

Node roles are the interpretation layer over the fingerprint. A compact rule set reads each node's metric pattern — degree asymmetry, betweenness, k-core depth, clustering — and assigns one or more roles: spreader, bottleneck, core backbone, module member, master regulator, and periphery. A role is a claim about function, not merely a ranking, which is why roles overlap rather than partition the graph: a single node can be both a spreader and a master regulator, so the role shares do not sum to 100%. The same role map serves any downstream question — target triage, hypothesis generation, comparative node studies, literature-curation checks — and the table below lists the taxonomy with its counts while the cards beneath it show the rules that fire and the worked examples that instantiate each role.

05 · Worked examples

Reference node characterizations

The five cases below are not illustrations of the algorithms. They are cases where the fingerprint recovered a role that the underlying documents already support — which is the validation a professional reader should demand before using the same reading on an unfamiliar node.

Acid ceramidase — the out-degree spreader

Degree 20, out-degree 17 / in-degree 3, PageRank 0.000758, betweenness 0.00798, k-core 4, community “Acid ceramidase” (size 17). The asymmetry marks a source: it pushes effects onto lipid and ferroptosis nodes rather than being acted upon. In the source document, elevated acid ceramidase in senescent cells reshapes membranes toward a PUFA-rich, pro-ferroptotic profile; knockdown or ARN14794 protects cells. Source: Acid ceramidase modulates the lipid profile and exacerbates sensitivity to ferroptosis in WI-38 replicative senescent cells (Soriano-Castell, Goujon, Dar, Currais & Maher, Cell Death and Disease, 2026; DOI 10.1038/s41419-026-09108-y). Note: src/notes/_link/_document_ - Acid_ceramidase_modulates_the_lipid_profile_and_ex.md. The graph also carries a “Senolytic Drug Target” node pointing at it. High out-degree, mid betweenness, and named-community-anchor is the signature of a spreading senolytic or senomorphic candidate.

SASP — convergent and divergent secretory hub

Degree 101, in-degree 56 / out-degree 45, PageRank 0.00712, betweenness 0.0555, k-core 6. Highest betweenness in the surveyed set: SASP sits on shortest paths between mitochondria, epigenetics, inflammation, and senescence. It is a sink for upstream triggers and a source for paracrine cytokines (IL-6, IL-8) that, in the acid-ceramidase document, induce the enzyme in bystander cells. High in, high out, top betweenness: a master program. Perturbing it reverberates network-wide — which is both the therapeutic appeal and the on-target risk.

Aging — PageRank and the core backbone

Degree 48, in-degree 35, out-degree 13, PageRank 0.00739 (highest of the sample), betweenness 0.0421, k-core 6. PageRank is high because Aging is connected to other important nodes, not merely to many nodes. k-core 6 places it in the densest mutually reinforcing shell with SASP and Senescent cells. Top PageRank plus inner-core membership is the “common soil” reading: downstream disease communities attach here.

GPX4, inducible NOS, Cataract — clustering = 1.0

A clustering coefficient of 1.0 means every neighbour of the node is connected to every other neighbour: a redox complex, an NO cascade, an AGE-cross-linking cluster. GPX4 also sits at k-core 2, consistent with a small clique rather than the global backbone. Contrast Acid ceramidase at clustering ≈ 0.04: a connector. The two patterns are complementary druggability strategies — disable the module, or cut the bridge.

The k-core 6 backbone

Nodes at k-core 6 — Aging, SASP, Senescent cells — form the resilient inner machinery of the senescence/aging axis. Everything else hangs off it. k-core decomposition is the fastest “essential versus incidental” ranking in the fingerprint, and the right filter for deciding which nodes merit a deep entity note.

06 · NetworkX

NetworkX

NetworkX is a Python package for the creation, manipulation, and study of the structure, dynamics, and functions of complex networks. It provides tools for the study of the structure and dynamics of social, biological, and infrastructure networks, and a standard programming interface and graph implementation that is suitable for many applications.

This reference leans on the biological half of that remit. A knowledge graph of biomedical entities and the typed relations between them — an enzyme remodelling a membrane, a cytokine driving inflammation, a senescent cell broadcasting a secretory program — is precisely the kind of complex network NetworkX was built to describe. Centrality, shortest paths, community detection, and core decomposition are first-class operations here, not bolt-ons, which is why the same calls that map a social network also characterize a senescence axis.

  1. General practice

    State the graph you actually computed on

    Keep a DiGraph for in/out-degree and PageRank. Project with G.to_undirected() for clustering, betweenness, and k-core. Multi-node analytics in 04_node_analysis.py further restrict to the giant component after dropping self-loops. Path metrics on a disconnected graph are otherwise undefined or silently harmonic-flavoured. 

    G_und = G.to_undirected()
cc = max(nx.connected_components(G_und), key=len)
U = G_und.subgraph(cc).copy()

  2. This implementation

    Prune type hubs before any centrality

    Study bias in PPI (TP53 accumulates interactions because it is studied) has a knowledge-graph analogue: ontology classes collect edges from every instance. The DENYLIST in the rebuild script is the correct first filter. Do not interpret a high-degree “protein” node as a target.

  3. General practice

    Do not pass a confidence score as a betweenness distance

    PageRank wants a strength: high confidence should carry more walk mass (weight="weight"). Betweenness and other shortest-path metrics want a distance: high confidence should be a short hop. Invert first. The current rebuild stores weight = confidence_score after centrality, so the stored fingerprint is unweighted topology. That is a documented limitation, not a secret. 

    for u, v, d in G.edges(data=True):
    d["weight"] = d.get("confidence_score", 0.7)
    d["distance"] = 1.0 / max(d["weight"], 1e-9)

pr = nx.pagerank(G, alpha=0.85, weight="weight")
bt = nx.betweenness_centrality(U, weight="distance", normalized=True)

  4. General practice

    Never rank on one metric

    Degree predicts essentiality in classical yeast PPI (Jeong et al., 2001) but misses bottlenecks whose degree is ordinary (Yu et al., 2007). The worked examples in this implementation exist because the fingerprint disagrees with itself in informative ways. Combine: high out-degree plus mid betweenness (spreader); high in and out plus top betweenness (nexus); top PageRank plus inner k-core (backbone).

  5. General practice

    Test against a degree-preserving null

    High-degree nodes score high on almost everything. A configuration-model ensemble (nx.configuration_model / directed variants, 100–1,000 draws) gives z-scores or empirical p-values. Effective-resistance in 04_node_analysis.py already z-scores commute distance against a random-node null sample; the same habit should apply to betweenness and clustering before a node is called a bottleneck.

  6. General practice

    Approximate betweenness; do not skip it

    Brandes is O(nm). Fine on this implementation. On a filtered human interactome, pass k= (sampled sources) and a seed. Prefer that approximation to dropping the metric. Restricting to the k-core or the giant component is the other legitimate speedup. 

    bt = nx.betweenness_centrality(U, k=100, seed=1, normalized=True)

  7. This implementation

    Read clustering and k-core as complementary, not redundant

    Clustering is local transitivity. k-core is a nested global peel. GPX4 can be a perfect triangle (C = 1, k = 2) and still sit outside the senescence backbone (k = 6). Inner cores often coincide with complexes in PPI; here they coincide with the aging/SASP program. Report both the core-number distribution and the size of the main core.

  8. General practice

    Seed PageRank when the question is local

    Global PageRank answers “important in the whole graph.” Target importance from a process of interest is Personalized PageRank: personalization={seed: 1.0}, alpha 0.85. Sweeping PPR from each k-core-6 node and intersecting the top ranks is the operational next step proposed below.

  9. General practice

    Pin versions, seeds, and the edge list

    PPI and knowledge graphs change with database version and confidence threshold. Record NetworkX version, the denylist, the giant-component rule, RANDOM_SEED = 1, and the source-file provenance on edges. Export GraphML / GEXF if a reviewer needs Cytoscape; do not rely on NetworkX drawing for publication figures of this scale.
