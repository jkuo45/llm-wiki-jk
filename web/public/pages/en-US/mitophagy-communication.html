Crosstalk mechanism

Mitophagy is a network,
not a bin.

A mitochondrion can be engulfed, sampled, or coupled to a lysosome without losing its identity. Explore four communication routes — and the moment an acute adaptation becomes a chronic failure.

Tier 3 · cell & animal models Published · 17 Nov 2026 Verified · 27 Nov 2026

Bin versus network

Two models of the same observation

The disposal picture

One organelle is cargo; the other is the bin

Classical [[Mitophagy|mitophagy]] is drawn as a one-way pipe. Loss of membrane potential blocks [[PINK1|PINK1 (PTEN-induced kinase 1)]] import; PINK1 phosphorylates [[Ubiquitin|ubiquitin]] and [[Parkin]] at Ser65, outer-membrane proteins are ubiquitinated, receptors such as [[Optineurin]], [[NDP52]] and [[p62|p62/SQSTM1]] connect them to [[LC3]]/[[GABARAP]], and [[STX17]]–[[SNAP29]]–[[VAMP8]] with [[Rab7|RAB7]] fuses the [[Autophagosome|mitophagosome]] to a [[Lysosome|lysosome]]. The output is always hydrolysis and the cargo loses its identity.

  • One route: tag → engulf → deliver.
  • One direction: cargo → lysosome.
  • One outcome: the organelle is erased.

The communication picture

The regulated unit is the relationship

The same [[Mitochondria|mitochondrion]]–[[Lysosome|lysosome]] pair is coupled through four parallel routes. Material and signals move in both directions, most exchanges are non-destructive, and the response is set by stimulus duration and tissue context.

  • Four routes: degradation (A), selective [[Mitochondrial-Derived Vesicles|mitochondrial-derived vesicles (MDVs)]] (B), contact exchange (C), transcriptional signaling (D).
  • Two directions: lysosome → mitochondrion Ca²⁺ via [[TRPML1]]–[[VDAC]]–[[MCU|mitochondrial calcium uniporter (MCU)]], Fe²⁺ via [[BDH2]]/[[2,5-DHBA|2,5-dihydroxybenzoic acid (2,5-DHBA)]], amino acids and cholesterol via [[STARD3]]/[[NPC1]]; mitochondrion → lysosome electron-transport-chain protons.
  • Non-destructive sampling: vesicles carry 70–150 nm selected cargo, and [[MFF|mitochondrial fission factor (MFF)]]-bearing vesicles recruit [[DRP1|dynamin-related protein 1 (DRP1)]] to damaged lysosomes for budding-type fission. Neither organelle is erased.
  • Cargo becomes information: lysosomal processing of [[Mitochondrial DNA|mitochondrial DNA]] and mitochondrial RNA engages [[TLR9]]/[[TLR8]] and [[MyD88]]–[[NF-κB|NF-κB (nuclear factor kappa B)]].
  • Duration sets the sign: acute stress activates [[AMPK|AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK)]]–[[TFEB|transcription factor EB (TFEB)]] and TRPML1–[[Calcineurin|calcineurin]]; persistent dysfunction suppresses those programs and lowers [[PtdIns(3,5)P2|phosphatidylinositol 3,5-bisphosphate (PtdIns(3,5)P2)]] and hydrolysis.

Degradative mitophagy is Route A — one branch, not the whole system. The bin framing collapses the entire relationship into its single most destructive output, so it predicts neither the non-destructive routes nor the observation that restoring communication ([[TRPML1]] agonism, lysosomal-pH correction, balanced [[AMPK]]–[[mTORC1|mTOR complex 1 (mTORC1)]]) rescues mitochondrial phenotypes in defined models. Two molecular details remain unresolved: the mitochondrial RAB7 partner and the proton-conducting channel at the contact.

    Route dossiers

    Molecular anchors, measured quantities, assay caveats,
    and the observation that would falsify each route.

    Route A · Label, engulf, deliver Tier 3

    Molecular anchors

    • [[PINK1|PINK1 (PTEN-induced kinase 1)]] accumulates on the outer mitochondrial membrane when import is blocked by loss of membrane potential; it phosphorylates [[Ubiquitin|ubiquitin]] at Ser65 and [[Parkin]] at Ser65 of its Ubl domain.
    • [[Parkin]] (465 aa, RING-between-RING E3) extends ubiquitin chains on outer-membrane substrates including [[MFN1]]/[[MFN2]], [[VDAC|VDAC1]] and [[TOM20]].
    • Receptors [[Optineurin|OPTN]], [[NDP52]]/CALCOCO2, [[TAX1BP1]], [[NBR1]], [[p62|p62/SQSTM1]] bind ubiquitin and [[LC3]]/[[GABARAP]] through LIR motifs.
    • Fusion uses the [[STX17]]–[[SNAP29]]–[[VAMP8]] SNARE complex with [[HOPS Complex|HOPS]] and [[Rab7|RAB7]].

    Measured quantities

    • Mitophagosome diameter 0.5–1.5 µm; lysosome 0.1–0.5 µm.
    • Mitochondrial diameter 0.5–1 µm; length 1–2 µm in typical somatic cells.
    • [[Mitochondrial DNA|mtDNA]] copy number 10²–10⁴ per cell, so one engulfed organelle carries tens to hundreds of genomes.

    Assay caveat

    LC3-II immunoblot and GFP-LC3 puncta counts cannot separate increased delivery from blocked degradation. Flux requires a tandem mCherry-GFP-LC3 reporter plus a lysosomal inhibitor (bafilomycin A1) or a pulse-chase mito-Keima readout.

    What would falsify it

    A depolarized mitochondrion cleared without detectable ubiquitin recruitment, or mitophagosome–lysosome fusion that proceeds with [[STX17]] and [[Rab7|RAB7]] both depleted.

    Primary sources

    Narendra 2008 · Matsuda 2010 · Koyano 2014 · Lazarou 2015 · Itakura 2012

    Route B · Selective vesicles, then remodelling Tier 3

    Molecular anchors

    • [[Mitochondrial-Derived Vesicles|MDVs]] bud from the outer membrane or intermembrane space without dissolving the organelle; several populations form in ATG5/ATG7-null cells.
    • Oxidized-cargo MDVs require [[PINK1]]/[[Parkin]] and [[STX17]]; the vesicle carries the oxidized protein, not the whole organelle.
    • An [[MFF|MFF (mitochondrial fission factor)]]-bearing population docks damaged lysosomes through [[Miro|MIRO2]]–[[ITM2C]] and delivers MFF, which recruits [[DRP1|DRP1 (dynamin-related protein 1)]] for budding-type lysosomal fission.

    Measured quantities

    • Vesicle diameter 70–150 nm — roughly one quarter to one eighth of a lysosome.
    • Lysosomal fission daughters 100–300 nm.
    • MFF is a ~38 kDa outer-membrane adaptor; DRP1 is a ~80 kDa mechanochemical GTPase.

    Assay caveat

    MDV fractions overlap in density with exosomes and other small extracellular vesicles. Protease protection plus a matrix marker (and a non-mitochondrial vesicle control) is required before a mitochondrial origin is claimed.

    What would falsify it

    A single genetic lesion that abolishes every MDV population while leaving mitochondrial function and lysosomal fission intact.

    Primary sources

    Soubannier 2012 · McLelland 2014 · Luo 2026 (Nat Cell Biol)

    Route C · Contact-site exchange Tier 3

    Molecular anchors

    • Lysosomal [[Rab7|RAB7-GTP]] promotes tethering. [[FIS1]] recruits [[TBC1D15]] (and [[TBC1D17]]), whose GAP domain hydrolyses RAB7-GTP and terminates the contact.
    • [[HOPS Complex|HOPS]] contributes a bridging function at the interface.
    • A Gαq–FIS1–TBC1D15/17 release complex is reported in a November 2026 preprint — not peer reviewed, and not yet independently replicated.

    Cargo, with direction

    • Ca²⁺: lysosome → mitochondrion via [[TRPML1]] → [[VDAC|VDAC1]] → [[MCU|MCU (mitochondrial calcium uniporter)]].
    • Fe²⁺: [[BDH2]]-derived [[2,5-DHBA|2,5-DHBA (2,5-dihydroxybenzoic acid)]] carries [[Iron]] into the mitochondrial matrix.
    • Amino acids: [[Parkin]]–[[Rab7|RAB7]] interface. Cholesterol: [[STARD3]]/[[NPC1]], context-dependent.
    • H⁺: electron-transport-chain-derived protons reach the lysosomal lumen; the conducting channel is unidentified and VDAC1 is only a candidate.

    Measured quantities

    Contact gap 10–30 nm; lysosomal pH 4.5–5.0. Direct lysosome→mitochondria Ca²⁺ transfer was not detectable in one 2026 triple-negative breast cancer model under Ca²⁺-free conditions.

    What would falsify it

    Identification of the mitochondrial RAB7 partner, or lysosomal pH rescue that persists after the mitochondrial proton source is removed.

    Primary sources

    Yamano 2014 · Yamano 2018 · Tian 2026 (Cell Reports) · Liu 2026 (Molecular Cell)

    Route D · Duration sets the sign Tier 3–4

    Molecular anchors

    • [[mTORC1|mTORC1 (mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1)]] phosphorylates [[TFEB]] at S211, creating a [[14-3-3]] site that holds TFEB in the cytoplasm.
    • [[AMPK|AMPK (AMP-activated protein kinase)]] phosphorylates TFEB at S466/S467 (mouse numbering) and promotes nuclear import.
    • [[TRPML1]]–Ca²⁺–[[Calcineurin|calcineurin]] dephosphorylates TFEB; [[PIKfyve]] synthesises [[PtdIns(3,5)P2]] from PtdIns3P.

    Measured quantities

    • [[Glucocerebrosidase|GBA1]]-mutant dopaminergic neurons: lysosomal pH 4.89–5.06 versus 4.74–4.77 in controls; basal oxygen consumption rate ~40% lower.
    • Both phenotypes were rescued by [[Rapamycin|rapamycin]] or acidic nanoparticles through [[mTORC1]]-dependent [[V-ATPase]] assembly (Sheshadri 2026).

    Assay caveat

    Lysosomal pH probes (LysoSensor, Oregon Green dextran) are sensitive to probe loading and photobleaching. A pH claim needs a calibration curve and a V-ATPase-inhibitor control in the same experiment.

    What would falsify it

    A chronic mitochondrial-disease model in which [[mTORC1]] inhibition worsens lysosomal hydrolysis, or a tissue where chronic stress raises rather than lowers [[PtdIns(3,5)P2]].

    Primary sources

    Settembre 2012 · Medina 2015 · Sheshadri 2026 (Transl Neurodegener)

    The six claims to carry away

    Evidence grades refer to the review's synthesis.
    No cell animation is clinical evidence.

    The network is the unit of regulation

    [[Mitochondria]] and [[Lysosome|lysosomes]] coordinate through [[Mitochondria-Lysosome Contact Site|contacts]], vesicles, diffusible signals, and signaling cascades. Disease often disrupts the relationship rather than one organelle alone.

    Review synthesis · Tier 2–3

    Cargo can be information

    Poorly methylated [[Mitochondrial DNA|mtDNA]] engages [[TLR9]] and mitochondrial RNA engages [[TLR7]]/[[TLR8]]. [[MyD88|MYD88]]–[[NF-κB]] signaling links lysosomal cargo processing to inflammatory cytokine production.

    Cell-culture mechanism · Tier 3

    Vesicles remodel, not only deliver

    [[Mitochondrial-Derived Vesicles|MDVs]] selectively package mitochondrial cargo. [[MFF]]-bearing vesicles target damaged lysosomes through [[Miro|MIRO2]]–[[ITM2C]], where MFF recruits [[DRP1]] for budding-type lysosomal fission.

    Primary cell-based study · Tier 3

    Contacts exchange without fusion

    [[TRPML1]]–[[VDAC|VDAC1]]–[[MCU]] Ca²⁺, [[BDH2]]/[[2,5-DHBA|2,5-DHBA (2,5-dihydroxybenzoic acid)]] iron, amino acids, cholesterol, and electron-transport-chain-derived protons can move across a contact without bulk membrane fusion.

    Multiple cell models · Tier 3

    Duration flips the sign

    Acute respiratory-chain stress activates [[AMPK]]–[[TFEB]] and [[TRPML1]]-dependent adaptation. Persistent dysfunction suppresses the same programs and lowers lysosomal [[PtdIns(3,5)P2]] and hydrolysis.

    Cellular + animal models · Tier 3–4

    Restore communication, not one organelle

    [[TRPML1]] agonism, lysosomal-pH correction, and balanced [[AMPK]]–[[mTORC1]] signaling rescue mitochondrial phenotypes in defined models. Effects remain tissue- and disease-dependent.
