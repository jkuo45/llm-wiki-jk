Male Distributed Topology vs the Centralized Estrogen Hub

Female biology runs a centralized hub: estradiol (E2) → estrogen receptor (ER) fans out to nine protective arms — and menopause withdraws all nine at once, the “estrogen cliff.” Male biology has no equivalent center. In its place: five distributed nodes — T→AR (drive: testosterone → androgen receptor) + mLOY (accelerator) + single-X (structure) + PARP-1/AIF (execution) + SIRT6/IGF-1/mTOR (growth) — eroding gradually, clonally, structurally, and episodically. No center, no cliff.

Model

One hub vs no hub

The female system in this synthesis is a star topology: one mediator (E2→ER) with nine spokes. Remove the center and every spoke derepresses simultaneously. The male system is a mesh with an empty middle: five nodes that jointly cover the same territory — sirtuins, redox, apoptosis, senescence, autophagy, NAD⁺, NF-κB, telomeres, cancer — through different architecture: active and loss-based, anabolic and pathogenic. Failure is local and asynchronous, never systemic and simultaneous.

Fig. 1 · The two architectures side by side

How to read it. Left: every protective arm routes through one hub, so one withdrawal event collapses all of them. Right: the five male nodes link to each other (shared territory — NF-κB, mTOR, NAD⁺, autophagy), but nothing routes through the middle; each node fails on its own schedule (§4). That is the whole model in one picture.

No single male factor spans the hub's breadth — and that is the point. Testosterone→AR is narrower per-node (no SIRT3/SOD2/TERT/STING equivalent), bidirectional with inflammation, pro-cancer in prostate/liver/melanoma, and declines gradually (LOH) rather than cliff-wise.

The centralized hub: E2→ER and its nine arms

The companion gender-attributes audit defines estrogen as a 9-arm master mediator. Each arm below is one spoke of Fig. 1 (left). The architectural consequence matters more than any single arm: because all nine share one upstream switch, menopausal withdrawal derepresses all nine simultaneously — a systems-level event with no male equivalent.

Fig. 2 · The nine estrogen arms and what each one holds up

Why group the arms: the male mesh covers the same three territories with different wiring — SIRT6 (not SIRT1/3 induction) for the sirtuin territory, AR→NF-κB suppression for the inflammation territory, and single-X/LOY/PARP-1 for the genome territory. Same map, different roads.

At menopause, E2 withdrawal simultaneously:

  • Removes SIRT1/3 transcriptional support → mitochondrial dysfunction
  • Removes SOD2 upregulation → oxidative stress increase
  • Removes NF-κB suppression → inflammaging acceleration
  • Removes STING suppression → cGAS-STING activation
  • Removes PARP-1 stabilization → NAD⁺ depletion accelerates
  • Removes telomerase activation → telomere attrition speeds up
  • Removes Bcl-2 neuroprotection → neuronal vulnerability increases

The male system

Five distributed nodes, five failure schedules

Each node below is one circle of Fig. 1 (right). Read the role line first (what it does in the system), then the temporal line (when it fails) — the two schedules never coincide, which is exactly why there is no male cliff.

1 · Testosterone → AR — the drive node

Anabolic / immune · bidirectional · gradual LOH
3.5× p-S6K1
100 nM T → p-ERK 5 min → p-Akt 15 min → p-S6K1 60 min; LY294002 / Akt-VIII / rapamycin abolish (Basualto-Alarcón 2013)
  • Anti-inflammatory parallel to E2: T ↓ TNF-α, IL-6, IFN-γ, IL-2, IL-1β and ↑ IL-10; inhibits NF-κB/p65, ↓ macrophage TLR4, ↓ ILC2 proliferation, AR→IL-10 in CD4⁺ T cells (Biogerontology 2023 review, PMC9810526).
  • Anabolic mTOR: castration ↓ grip, muscle mass, IGF-1 mRNA, p-Akt/p-GSK3β/p-PRAS40/p-FoxO3a and ↑ MuRF1/atrogin-1/REDD1; nandrolone reverses (White 2013). Low T activates mTOR Akt-independently; high T required for Akt.
  • Critical asymmetry — inflammaging → ↓T, not T → protection: UK Biobank men (n=18,347): IL-6 + GDF-15 mediate age-related T decline, GDF-15 strongest; SAMP8 Leydig ROS↑ → p38→COX2 + NF-κB→COX2 → StAR/P450scc↓, rescued by SB203580 + Bay 11-7082.
  • Ferroptosis mirror: MBOAT2 is a direct AR target vs MBOAT1 as ER target (Cell 2023); testosterone is the dominant susceptibility factor in renal ischemia (Park 2004).
  • Limits: “testosterone is unfavorable” for telomeres — lacks antioxidant property, may ↑ oxidative telomere damage (Gardner 2014); pro-cancer in prostate/liver/melanoma.

2 · Mosaic loss-of-Y — the accelerator node

Pathogenic gain · opposite valence to E2 · exponential 60y+ (the LOY cliff)
40% → 57%
prevalence at 70y → 93y; most common somatic mutation in men (Thompson Nature 2019)
  • Mortality: LOY-blood HR 1.91 all-cause, 5.5y vs 11y survival (Forsberg Nat Genet 2014); LURIC coronary (n=1,698) LOY>17%: HR 1.41 all-cause, 1.49 CV death, 2.65 fatal MI + profibrotic plasma (OPG/MMP-12/GDF-15/HB-EGF/resistin).
  • Causality (not correlation): BM-LOY mice → ↓ lifespan, cardiac/pulmonary/renal fibrosis, HF death; LOY macrophages signal TGFβ1/galectin-3 → fibroblast activation; TGFβ1 blockade rescues (Sano Science 2022); Uty disruption phenocopies.
  • Immunity/cancer/AD: ~500 autosomal genes dysregulated; T-cell exhaustion + PD-1 sensitivity; bladder-cancer growth via immune evasion (Abdel-Hafiz Nature 2023); LOY–AD, CKD, aortic stenosis, impaired senescent-cell clearance → renal disease.
  • No female counterpart: loss-based, not hormone-based. Risk factors: age + smoking (dose-response, reversible after quitting), genomic instability/CHIP co-occurrence.

3 · Single-X vulnerability — the structural node

Hard-coded asymmetry · no backup · lifelong, compounded by LOY
6 EXITS genes
ATRX · CNKSR2 · DDX3X · KDM5C · KDM6A · MAGEC3 — female biallelic protection, zero autosomal/PAR equivalent (Dunford Nat Genet 2017)
  • Males rely on weaker X–Y paralog backup (UTY for KDM6A, DDX3Y for DDX3X) — and LOY itself removes that backup.
  • Structure, not signaling: this node never “declines,” it simply never had redundancy. The distributed topology’s hard-coded asymmetry.
  • Vault: [[UTY]] / [[EXITS]] present.

4 · PARP-1 / AIF — the executioner node

Dominant death mode + NAD⁺ consumer · acute, event-driven
19.7% vs 38.4%
infarct in PARP-1 KO vs WT males (protected); KO OVX females 52.2% vs 36.0% WT (exacerbated — Liu Stroke 2011)
  • Mechanism: male nNOS → PARP → NAD⁺ depletion → AIF nuclear → 50-kbp fragmentation (parthanatos); female cytochrome-C → caspase-9 → caspase-3. PARP-1 deletion abolishes AIF translocation in both sexes yet ↑ cytochrome-C/caspase-9/-3 only in females.
  • Q-VD-OPh caspase inhibitor protects females only; PJ34 delayed PARP inhibition improves mNSS mainly in males.
  • Therapeutic corollary: PARP inhibitors male-leaning, caspase inhibitors female-leaning. Downstream by position, load-bearing by function — and the NAD⁺ link ties this node to male NAD⁺ decline.

5 · SIRT6 → IGF-1 / mTOR / autophagy — the growth node

Male-leaning set-point of the shared anabolic axis · lifelong
+14.5% median
Sirt6-Tg males median +14.5%/+9.9%, max +15.8%/+13.1%; females n.s. — via ↓ serum IGF-1 + ↑ IGFBP1 + ↓ p-AKT/p-FOXO to female levels (Kanfi Nature 2012)
  • 50% of male-Tg liver genes overlap the female-WT signature — SIRT6 partially “feminizes” male hepatic signaling.
  • Roichman 2021: SIRT6 extends both sexes, male-stronger, via hepatic NAD⁺/gluconeogenesis, ↓ IGF-1 both sexes. Human: Finnish rs117385980 male longevity; centenarian SIRT6 variant (genome stability).
  • Autophagy wiring: males basal Beclin-1/LC3↑, canonical Beclin-1→LC3-II→p62↓ post-stroke; females ATG7 Beclin-1-independent; male neurons autophagy-prone under starvation (Du 2009). No E2→SIRT3/SOD2-style induction — a set-point difference, not a switch.

Joint coverage — the five nodes span the hub’s territory

Same map · different roads
  • Sirtuins/redox: hub induces SIRT1/SIRT3/SOD2; mesh tunes SIRT6→IGF-1 and SIRT1↔AR (K630 deacetylation) / SIRT7→AR→autophagy — modulation, not induction.
  • Inflammation: hub brakes via IκBα→NF-κB↓ and HDAC3→STING↓; mesh brakes via AR→NF-κB/USP18 T-cell suppression — and loses the brake when inflammaging suppresses T (bidirectional).
  • Genome: hub stabilizes PARP-1/TERT/p53; mesh gambles — single-X removes backup, LOY removes the paralog rescue, PARP-1/AIF converts damage into the male death program.

Dynamics

Two clocks: one cliff vs gradual + clonal erosion

Architecture becomes destiny over time. The hub produces a step function: protection is flat, then menopause withdraws all nine arms at once. The mesh produces three superimposed curves that never synchronize: testosterone slides gradually (LOH), LOY clones expand exponentially from ~60y (the structural mirror of the estrogen cliff), and single-X/PARP-1 are lifelong background conditions discharged by acute events.

Fig. 3 · Protection over age: estrogen step-down vs male three-curve erosion (schematic, anchored to cited prevalences)

The LOY cliff is the structural mirror of the estrogen cliff — exponential from 60y, not stepwise at 51y — but with opposite valence: a pathogenic clone gained with age rather than a protective hub lost at menopause. Note the curves never synchronize: that asynchrony is the absence of a male cliff. Key: ♀ = female trajectory (oxblood); ♂ = male trajectories (teal/red/slate).

Temporal summary. E2→ER: cliff at menopause (~51y). T→AR: gradual LOH from ~40s, bidirectional with inflammaging (GDF-15 strongest mediator). mLOY: exponential 60y+ (40% @70y, 57% @93y). Single-X: lifelong, compounded when LOY deletes the Y-paralog backup. PARP-1/AIF: acute, event-driven (stroke/trauma). SIRT6/IGF-1: lifelong set-point.

Execution

The death-mode switch: same insult, different program

The executioner node is where the topology difference becomes a therapeutic decision. After ischemic injury, male neurons default to PARP-1 → NAD⁺ depletion → AIF nuclear translocation → 50-kbp fragmentation (parthanatos), while female neurons default to cytochrome-C → caspase-9 → caspase-3. The knockout experiment proves the switch is load-bearing: deleting PARP-1 protects males and harms OVX females, shunting them harder into the caspase route.

Fig. 4 · PARP-1 KO infarct volumes by sex (McCullough 2005; Liu Stroke 2011) + pathway schematic

How to read it. The bars are the proof that the two programs are genuinely sex-divergent (same deletion, opposite outcome). The cascades show why: PARP-1 sits upstream of the male death route but parallel to the female one — deleting it removes the male weapon while forcing females onto their backup weapon. Therapeutic corollary: PARP inhibitors male-leaning, caspase inhibitors female-leaning.
