Across ischemia–reperfusion models, female cells default to caspase-dependent apoptosis; male cells to PARP1–AIF parthanatos; the lytic and iron arms add context-specific exceptions. Scope: the cell-autonomous XX/XY split is established in murine ischemia–reperfusion models (MCAO stroke, neonatal HI, renal IRI) and XX/XY cultures; one human correlate — post-MI autopsy shows a ~10× higher apoptotic index in men (Biondi-Zoccai 2005). Direct human validation of the PARP1/AIF arm is still lacking.
