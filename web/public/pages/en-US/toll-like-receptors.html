TLR architecture and ligands

TLRs are type-I transmembrane glycoproteins of the Innate Immune System. Each has an N-terminal horseshoe-shaped leucine-rich repeat (LRR) ectodomain (19-25 LRRs, ~24 aa each, LxxLxLxxN; flanked by LRR-NT/LRR-CT caps), a single-pass α-helical transmembrane segment, and a C-terminal Toll/IL-1 receptor (TIR) domain (~200 aa) that recruits adaptors. Ligand-induced dimerization (homo- or hetero-; TLR1/TLR2, TLR2/TLR6, TLR4) or conformational change positions TIR domains for TIR-TIR homotypic adaptor binding. (vault: Toll-like Receptor; web Kawai & Akira lineage 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.immuni.2024.03.004)

Human receptor count. Humans express ten functional TLRs, TLR1-TLR10. TLR11 is a non-functional pseudogene in humans; mouse has TLR11-TLR13 in addition (mouse 12 members; mouse TLR8 ligand recognition differs from human — see Species caveat). TLR1-TLR10 localization splits into cell-surface (TLR1, TLR2, TLR4, TLR5, TLR6, TLR10) and endosomal (TLR3, TLR7, TLR8, TLR9; endosomal TLRs require acidification; UNC93B1 traffics endosomal TLRs; proteolytic cleavage licenses signaling; endosomal TLR7/9 traffic to lysosome-related organelles for IRF7 type-I-IFN output).

Ligand-table caveat. Reported DAMP associations are not settled receptor–ligand pairs: direct binding of proteinaceous DAMPs such as HMGB1 and heat-shock proteins to TLR2/TLR4 remains under critical scrutiny (2026 review; DOI 10.1007/s44466-026-00040-6). Nucleic-acid ligands (CpG DNA, ssRNA/dsRNA, hypomethylated mtDNA/mtRNA) have direct structural and mutational support.

Signaling: MyD88 vs TRIF

Four TIR adaptors: MyD88, MAL/TIRAP, TRIF, TRAM. Two cascades (vault: Toll-like Receptor, Myddosome, MyD88, TRIF; web Fitzgerald & Kagan 2020; 2024-2025 reviews DOI 10.1186/s12929-025-01151-9, 10.1002/mco2.70308):

  • MyD88-dependent — all TLRs except TLR3. TIR→(MAL/TIRAP for TLR2/4)→MyD88 self-assembly (clusters at resting state; short isoform MyD88-S inhibits)→myddosome (MyD88·IRAK4·IRAK1/2; supramolecular organizing center)→TRAF6 K63-polyubiquitination→TAK1→IKK→NF-κB; TAK1→MAPK (p38/ERK/JNK)→AP-1. Output: pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6), chemokines, NLRP3 priming. Endosomal TLR7/8/9: myddosome first at early endosome (NF-κB) then AP-3 transport to lysosome-related organelles + TRAF3 → IRF7/IRF3 → type I interferon (esp. plasmacytoid dendritic cells; web 2025 DOI 10.1186/s12929-025-01151-9).
  • TRIF-dependent — TLR3, and TLR4 endosomal. TLR3→TRIF directly (no TRAM); TLR4→TRAM→TRIF after endocytosis (CD14/Rab11a). TRIF→TRAF3→TBK1/IKKε→IRF3→IFN-β; TRIF→TRAF6→NF-κB.
  • TLR4 compartmentalized signaling. Plasma membrane: MAL+MyD88→early NF-κB. Endosome: TRAM+TRIF→IRF3→IFN-β (vault: Toll-like Receptor). Correction: endocytosis and endosomal TLR4 signaling are distinct and independent outcomes in human cells; the "sequential two-phase" model is a simplification, not a universal coupling (EMBO Rep 2025, DOI 10.1038/s44319-025-00444-2).

TLR3–TRIF links to cell death (vault cell-death + cancer + adrenochrome; web 2025 DOI 10.1186/s12929-025-01151-9): TRIF has a RHIM; TLR3–TRIF recruits RIPK1→caspase-8 (FADD-independent; atypical apoptosis; vault FADD, Caspase-8); TLR3/4–TRIF recruits RIPK3→necrosome→necroptosis (without RIPK1 in this route; vault Necroptosis; ZBP1 distinct).

Negative regulation. A20 (TNFAIP3; edits K63/linear ubiquitin; NF-κB brake; vault oxidative-stress), miR-146a (TRAF6/IRAK1 targeting; induced by NF-κB; web), MyD88-S (blocks IRAK4 recruitment; web), SIGIRR/TIR8 (vault MyD88; inhibits MyD88-dependent TLR/IL-1R), SARM1 (TIR adaptor; inhibits TRIF; vault _link/SARM1; note SARM1 also has separate mitophagy/necroptosis roles in cell-death — distinct mechanism), SOCS1/SOCS3 (downstream IRF7 degradation; vault senescence + web), A20-inducing, IRAK-M, OTULIN/CYLD ubiquitin editing (vault cell-death; web), DNase II / DNase1L3 / TREX1 / DNase2a clearing DNA (anti-cGAS; TLR9 needs digested DNA; web 2023-2025), RNase T2 + PLD dual role (generate TLR7 ligands; negatively regulate TLR3 by dsRNA degradation; web DOI 10.1093/jb/mvaf011), PLD3/PLD4 (inhibit TLR7/9 + cGAS; also positively generate TLR7 ligands; web), TFAM two LIR motifs → LC3B nucleoid-phagy clearing mtDNA (web 2024), Retromer → lysosomal mtDNA turnover (web DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adr6415), OCRL (autophagosome-lysosome fusion / TLR9 regulation; vault autophagy doc).

Post-translational regulation (web-verified 2024-2025). Reversible acetylation of TIR domains in the TLR4-MAL-MyD88 signalosome on lysine residues regulates assembly and M1 macrophage polarization in sepsis; proposed therapeutic target (EMBO J 2024, DOI 10.1038/s44318-024-00237-8). SIRT1 deacetylates NF-κB p65 K310 (and other NF-κB sites; reduces transcription; vault sirtuins doc + web 2023-2024); SIRT1→NLRP3↓ (web); see Sirtuins. Phosphorylation/ubiquitination layers additionally modulate myddosome kinetics (IRAK1 hyperphosphorylation→degradation; TRAF6 ~24 h proteasomal downregulation; vault MyD88).

Endosomal lysosomal NA processing determines ligand availability (web 2024-2025). Lysosomal DNase II + RNase T2 + PLD + PLD3/4 generate ssDNA/ssRNA ligands; SLC29A3 nucleoside export; lysosomal NA/metabolite accumulation ("lysosomal NA stress") can activate or suppress TLR3/7/8/9 + cGAS-STING depending on which intermediate accumulates (DOI 10.1093/jb/mvaf011); TLR9 needs DNase-II-processed DNA (undigested DNA accumulation in DNase-II-deficient cells does not productively activate TLR9 while driving cGAS-STING — human DNase-II-deficiency lethal anaemia is STING- not TLR9-driven; web).

Species caveat. Human TLR8 responds to ssRNA; mouse TLR8 is functionally inactive (5-aa motif; Nunes et al 2024 EMBO Rep cited in vault autophagy doc; web). TLR7/8 species differences confound mouse-to-human translation. mtRNA→TLR8 additionally requires RNase 6 processing and is inhibited by RNA 2'-O-methylation (web 2024; vault autophagy doc). Human TLR9 also binds on erythrocytes in addition to ER/endosome routes (vault Erythrocytes + web 2025).

Senescence and SASP

(vault topic: src/notes/senescence/; Cellular Senescence, SASP, Senescence, Inflammaging)

  • TLR2 is upregulated in senescent cells and essential for immunity-regulating SASP (Tier 3/4; vault SASP; web Science Advances 2019 DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aaw0254).
  • cGAS–STING → TLR2 + NF-κB + SAA positive feedback. Senescence-associated DAMPs — cytoplasmic chromatin fragments (CCFs), mtDNA, mobilized retroelements, RIG-I-sensed cytosolic RNA, HMGB1, SAA1/SAA2 — engage cGAS→cGAMP→STING→NF-κB; STING then drives TLR2 transcription + A-SAA; TLR2 recognizes A-SAA → NF-κB → proinflammatory SASP (vault SASP, cGAS-STING Pathway, HMGB1; web 2019 + 2025; "gut microbiota-derived LTA on senescent hepatic stellate cells → TLR2→COX-2→PGE2" immunosuppressive output variant, web 2022/2025 cancer-senescence review). TLR2 knockdown/knockout reduces p16/p21 + SASP; TLR2 overexpression → cell-cycle arrest + senescence markers (Tier 3/4; web).
  • TLR7 chronic activation → senescence + SASP-like output (vault TLR7; web 2025 nucleic-acid-TLR review). TLR9 + STING synergy for IFN-γ in senescence-adjacent contexts (web 2023).
  • Gut-derived TLR2 ligands in aging liver (2026). Circulating TLR2 ligands were elevated in elderly humans and aged mice; hepatic Tlr2 increased in aging mice; Tlr2 deletion or the TLR2 inhibitor ortho-vanillin reduced hepatic senescence/fibrosis markers in mice; LTA raised p16/p21 mRNA in human PBMCs in vitro (DOI 10.1186/s11658-026-00881-4 PMID 41772418; Tier 3 + Tier 4 + human observational; sample size not quantified in retrieved text).
  • Inflammasome. Senescence DAMPs + TLR/NF-κB prime NLRP3 (Signal 1; upregulate NLRP3 + pro-IL-1β + pro-IL-18; license via deubiquitination; vault Inflammasome, NLRP3 Inflammasome, adrenochrome NLRP3 Inflammasome, sirtuins doc); Signal 2 (ATP→P2X7, crystals, mtDNA, mtROS, oxLDL…) → caspase-1 → IL-1β/IL-18 + pyroptosis. MyD88 required for SASP (vault senescence MyD88; OIS Myddosome note). Senescent cells activate neighboring macrophages (vault _document_ - Senecent cell activate neighboring macrophages.md); SASP-driven clearance vs cancer-promoting SASP duality (vault; web 2025 Genes & Dev).
  • Update (web 2025). cGAS-STING in aging cells can activate SASP/IFN without canonical cGAMP→STING phosphorylation→PNC trafficking ("noncanonical cGAS–STING"; STING at ER/nuclear envelope bound to chromatin; reduced canonical response in aged/senescent/progeria cells; pharmacological inhibition ameliorates progeria; PNAS 2025 DOI 10.1073/pnas.2424666122). Relationship to TLR2 is not equivalent: canonical STING→TLR2-SAA loop is separate; noncanonical cGAS-STING does not collapse into TLR2.
  • Senolytic/senomorphic context (vault). TLR-related nodes sit adjacent to senolytics/senomorphics; no TLR-directed senolytic is established in vault.

Autophagy and mitophagy

(vault topic: src/notes/autophagy/; Autophagy, Mitophagy, TFEB; cell-death autophagy links)

  • Mitophagy is information transfer, not only degradation (vault Mitophagy; autophagy/Mitochondria-Lysosome Crosstalk.md; web 2024-2025). Damaged mitochondria → PINK1/Parkin → autophagosome→lysosome; poorly methylated mtDNA (bacterial-like CpG) → lysosomal TLR9; mtRNA → TLR7/8; MyD88→NF-κB→TNF-α/IL-1/IL-6.
  • Mitochondrial-derived vesicles (MDVs; ~70-100 nm) bud independently of core fission/autophagy; can deliver mtDNA/mtRNA to lysosomes without depolarization; retromer/fumarate routes (web DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adr6415; vault).
  • Basal autophagy clears TLR ligands + supports lysosomal homeostasis; autophagosome→lysosome fusion triggers TLR9 under control of OCRL (vault autophagy doc; web 2016 Nat Cell Biol; 2025 lysosomal-NA-stress review).
  • Defective autophagy prolongs/activates TLR signaling. LC3B−/− B cells: delayed endosome→lysosome maturation → prolonged TLR9-NF-κB + reduced signal termination (Tier 3; web 2024 Int Immunol). OPA1 loss incomplete mitophagy → mtDNA→TLR9 NF-κB myopathy (Tier 3; web). IRGM1 loss accelerates mitochondrial lysosomal delivery → TLR7 instead of TLR9 mtRNA-driven IFN-I (Tier 3; web). ATG7 knockout / bafilomycin ↑ cytosolic mtDNA; TFAM-LIR2 nucleoid-phagy prevents mtDNA→cGAS-STING and mtDNA→TLR9 leakage (Tier 4; web 2024). Autophagy also limits inflammasome (vault autophagy doc + senescence Inflammasome).
  • TFEB/TFE3 (master lysosomal/autophagy transcription factors). TLR ligands + live bacteria → TFE3 nuclear translocation; mTORC1-independent; TFEB+TFE3 redundant; drives autophagy/lysosomal biogenesis + immune genes; depletion reduces TLR-induced cytokines/chemokines (Tier 3/4; vault autophagy/_document_ - TFEB AND TFE3… + TFEB; web Pastore 2016). TLR8→TFEB→autophagy required for HIV replication; then Nef→Beclin→mTOR→TFEB retention downregulates autophagy (Tier 3; vault + web). MyD88-dependent TLR signaling regulates mitophagy-promoting proteins + anti-apoptotic proteins (web 2024 MyD88 review).
  • Autophagy-regulated TLR signal termination + PANoptosis (see Cell death).

Sirtuins

(vault topic: src/notes/sirtuins/; SIRT1; NAD+ metabolism; also _link/NAD+)

  • TLR2/SIRT1/NF-κB. SIRT1 counteracts IL-1β-mediated pro-inflammatory stress through TLR2/SIRT1/NF-κB; SIRT1 anti-inflammatory against IL-1β-driven pro-inflammatory stress; SIRT1-deficiency ↑ inflammation in rodents; resveratrol ↓ adhesion molecules ↑SIRT1 + improved survival in rodents; TLR4→NF-κB→NLRP3 priming is inhibited by SIRT1 (vault sirtuins IL-1R.md + _document_ - sirtuins in health and disease s41392-022-01257-8.md; Resveratrol; Tier 2/3; web).
  • Mechanism. SIRT1 deacetylates NF-κB p65 K310 → reduced transcription of pro-inflammatory genes; also AP-1, HIF-1α; histone deacetylation at inflammatory promoters (vault + web 2023-2024 + 2025). SIRT1 activates NRF2 antioxidant signaling while repressing NF-κB (web 2023). SIRT1→NLRP3 attenuation (web 2023). NAD+ → SIRT1: boosting NAD+ blunts TLR4-induced type I IFN in control + SLE monocytes (Tier 3 + Tier 2-adjacent; JCI 2022; web; vault sirtuins doc).
  • Update (web 2024). PNPLA7 (patatin-like phospholipase) downregulated by LPS/TLR4 → ↓SIRT1 → ↑p65 acetylation → M1 polarization; PNPLA7 modulates TLR4 downstream without affecting basal macrophage activity (Tier 4 + Tier 3; DOI 10.3390/ijms231414983).
  • Update (web 2024). Reversible TLR4 TIR-domain acetylation is distinct from SIRT1's p65 deacetylation; both are post-translational layers on TLR4→NF-κB output; whether SIRT1 directly deacetylates TLR4/MAL/TIRAP TIR domains is unverified.
  • Other sirtuins. SIRT6 anti-inflammatory (vault sirtuins doc); SIRT3 mitochondrial anti-inflammatory links via mitophagy/ROS/NLRP3 restraint (vault; not a verified direct TLR target); no vault-verified direct TLR binding for SIRT3/SIRT6.

Oxidative stress

(vault topic: src/notes/oxidative-stress/; Oxidative Stress, Reactive Oxygen Species, Mitohormesis)

  • Bidirectional ROS↔TLR. TLR activation → NOX2/NADPH Oxidase assembly on phagosomes → superoxide→H2O2→hypochlorous acid bactericidal burst (up to ~2-4 nmol O2•−/min per 10^6 cells reported in vault note; vault oxidative-stress/Immunity.md, Phagocytes.md); oxidative stress conversely activates TLRs; ROS-DAMPs; mtDNA/mtROS→TLR9/NLRP3. TLR signaling requires TRAF6 K63-ubiquitin; redox-sensitive cysteines in TIR domains are a documented regulatory layer (general knowledge; TLR4 TIR acetylation 2024 separate; no vault REDOX-map).
  • CD36. CD36 co-receptor for TLR2/6; oxLDL + amyloid-β sterile inflammation; CD36 promoter NF-κB/PPARγ redox-regulated; CD36 itself induced by oxLDL/TNF-α/IL-1β/TGF-β (vault oxidative-stress/CD36 Receptor.md; _link/CD36; web trained-immunity DAMPs).
  • A20. A20 dual-function ubiquitin editor terminates TLR/TNF/IL-1R/NOD output; prevents chronic inflammation (vault oxidative-stress/A20.md; web MyD88 review).
  • Mitochondrial damage → TLR. Mitochondrial dysfunction → cytosolic mtDNA + mtRNA + mtROS → TLR7/8/9 + cGAS-STING + NLRP3; mitophagy restrains; hypomethylated mtDNA CpG; TFAM; TREX1; DNase2 (vault; web 2024-2025).
  • Adrenochrome/neuromelanin angle (this vault). Oxidative catecholamine pathways produce DAMPs feeding TLR/NLRP3 arms; see Adrenochrome, Neuromelanin, COMT. No vault-verified direct "adrenochrome→TLR4" ligand claim; that link is inferred Tier 5 from generic oxidative-DAMP→TLR4 evidence.

Hormesis and trained immunity

(vault topic: src/notes/hormesis/; Hormesis, Trained Immunity, Mitohormesis; _link/Inflammaging)

  • TLR ligands are hormetic triggers. Trained Immunity (innate immune memory): after homeostasis, rechallenge → enhanced (trained) or blunted (tolerant) responses. TLR signaling drives epigenetic + metabolic reprogramming (vault; web-verified 2025 eLife review "Toll-like receptors in the trained immunity era" DOI 10.7554/eLife.106443; Tier 2/3 + Tier 4).
  • Dose/duration dichotomy (vault Trained Immunity + web). Low/acute DAMP/PAMP → trained: mTORC1/HIF-1α, PI3K/AKT, Warburg glycolysis, fatty-acid synthesis; H3K4me3 + H3K4me1/H3K27ac, latent enhancers, fumarate→KDM5 inhibition, mevalonate/IGF1R (statins block), miR-9-5p vs miR-146a, G9a inhibition augments; SIRT1-linked. High/chronic → tolerance: AMPK/SIRT1/Autophagy, OXPHOS/fatty-acid oxidation, repressive marks; endotoxin tolerance; vault + web.
  • DAMP specifics (vault Trained Immunity; web-verified 2025 eLife). oxLDL via CD36→TLR4-mTOR; HMGB1 context-dependent (RAGE-AMPK/SIRT1 vs mTOR; dose); heme via SYK, mTOR-independent; MSU/urate via mTOR/NLRP3; mtDNA/TLR9-STING toward autophagy-tolerance; S100A4; hyperglycemia Runx1; SCFAs/flavonoids; Nrf2 crosstalk with HIF-1α + NF-κB.
  • TLR agonists as trained-immunity inducers + vaccine adjuvants. CpG/TLR9, imiquimod/TLR7, BCG TLR2/4, MPL/TLR4, β-glucan Dectin-1+TLR adjunct; cancer + infectious disease (vault + web 2025 eLife + Tier 1/2 therapeutic; see Cancer).
  • Chronic/low-grade → inflammaging. Chronic TLR + DAMP + SASP + mtDNA + microbiota → Inflammaging; reduced type-I-IFN in aged pDCs (IRF7↓; vault senescence; web 2025 immunosenescence review).
  • Hormesis linkage (vault). Trained immunity = immunological face of Hormesis; Hormetic Window; preconditioning; exercise/dietary hormesis; mitohormesis ROS signaling adjacent.

Epigenetics

(vault topic: src/notes/epigenetics/; Epigenetic Alterations, epigenetics; epigenetics/Innate Immunity.md)

  • TLR-driven epigenetic memory = trained immunity core (see above; vault hormesis + web 2025 eLife; Tier 2/3/4). TLR-induced chromatin remodeling + metabolite-driven epigenetic modifier regulation.
  • Innate immunity in reprogramming. Cellular reprogramming roadblocks: cytosolic DNA sensing (cGAS-STING) + TLR signaling → type I IFN + inflammatory cytokines; innate immune activation lowers efficiency (vault epigenetics/Innate Immunity.md; Tier 4 + Tier 3).
  • Sirtuin deacetylation layer. SIRT1 p65 K310 + histone deacetylation at inflammatory promoters; NAD+-dependent epigenetic regulation of inflammation; SASP epigenetic regulation (vault senescence + sirtuins; web 2023-2024).
  • TLR7 escape. Xist-RNP female-biased autoimmunity alongside TLR7 escape + estrogen (vault epigenetics/XIST.md; Tier 2/3; Chang et al Cell 2024 cited in vault).
  • Senescence-associated epigenetic changes interact with TLR sensing (SASP regulation; DNA methylation/retroelement mobilization feeding TLR9/cGAS; vault senescence + web 2024-2025).

Cell death

(vault topic: src/notes/cell-death/; adrenochrome RIPK1/NLRP3; Necroptosis, Apoptosis, Pyroptosis, Ferroptosis, Caspase-8, RIPK1, RIPK3, FADD, Cytoplasmic Chromatin Fragments)

  • TLR3→TRIF→caspase-8 atypical apoptosis: RIPK1, no FADD (vault cell-death FADD, Death Effector Domain, adrenochrome RIPK1; web 2025).
  • TLR3/4→TRIF→RIPK3 necroptosis RHIM; necrosome; without RIPK1 in TRIF route; distinct ZBP1/DAI route (vault Necroptosis; web 2025).
  • PANoptosis. Integrated apoptosis+pyroptosis+necroptosis via PANoptosome; TLR/TNF/IFN/ZBP1/NLRP3 triggers in vault cell-death + web; not TLR-specific.
  • Pyroptosis. TLR-primed NLRP3→caspase-1→gasdermin D; SIRT1 inhibits (vault + web; SIRT1/p65/NLRP3 2023-2024). TLR + mtDNA/ROS Signal 2.
  • Necrosis/DAMP loop. Necrotic death releases DAMPs (HMGB1, mtDNA, mtRNA, ATP, uric acid, S100, heat-shock proteins, CCFs, retroelement DNA) → TLR (+cGAS-STING + NLRP3 + RAGE) → inflammation; efferocytosis/phagocytic clearance + NETosis (vault cell-death + cancer DAMP; web 2024-2025).
  • Autophagic cell death vs autophagy-regulated inflammation. Autophagy can clear TLR ligands + terminate TLR9; autophagic cell death distinct from protective autophagy; TLR-myddosome itself autophagy-regulated (vault Autophagy-cell-death links + web).
  • Eryptosis/erythrocyte. Erythrocyte TLR9 + cell-free DNA + phosphatidylserine-exposing cells intersect DAMP clearance in adrenochrome/neuromelanin-adjacent cell-death contexts (vault Eryptosis, Erythrocytes; web 2025).

Cancer

(vault topic: src/notes/cancer/; Cancer, PAMP, DAMP, HMGB1, Tumor Microenvironment; senescence-cancer links)

  • Dual-edged. TLRs both anti-tumor and pro-tumor; context/cell-type/NF-κB balance; chronic vs acute (vault cancer; web 2025 MedComm DOI 10.1002/mco2.70308; Tier 1-3 + Tier 4).
  • PAMP→tumorigenesis. Chronic infection TLR activation → inflammation → tumorigenesis (H. pylori→gastric, HPV→cervical, HBV/HCV→HCC; vault PAMP; Tier 2). Gut microbiota LPS/LTA → TLR2/4 → SASP/HCC (vault; web).
  • TLR2→SASP. Senescent hepatic stellate cells TLR2→NF-κB→SASP; obesity-LTA; PGE2 immunosuppression; HCC (vault Hepatocellular Carcinoma; web 2017/2022/2025; Tier 2/3).
  • DAMP→TLR. Reported HMGB1→TLR4+RAGE and heat-shock-protein→TLR links are contested as direct ligand pairs; necrosis→DAMP and senescence→SASP→TLR2 are supported at the pathway level (vault DAMP; cancer senescence; web 2026).
  • TLR agonist immunotherapy (Tier 1/2 where noted; vault + web 2024-2025). Imiquimod TLR7 approved basal cell carcinoma + actinic keratosis; CpG-ODN TLR9 intratumoral + vaccine adjuvant (CpG 1018 Heplisav-B); BCG TLR2/4; MPL TLR4 (HPV/HepB vaccines); combos with checkpoint inhibitors; nanodelivery; caution: pro-tumor inflammation contexts.
  • TLR antagonism. Anti-inflammatory/anti-tumor in chronic-inflammation-driven cancer; TLR4 blockade sepsis-translation lessons (eritoran phase III in 1961 adults with sepsis did not reduce mortality; vault + 2026 review; Tier 1); no TLR antagonist is licensed for clinical use as of 2026 (DOI 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1761361).

Adrenochrome

(vault topic: src/notes/adrenochrome/; Adrenochrome, adrenochrome_inflammaging, IL-1β, TRAF6, RIPK1)

  • Oxidative catecholamine → inflammaging substrate. Adrenochrome (oxidized adrenaline) forms under oxidative stress; vault frames adrenochrome/neuromelanin + inflammaging; no standalone clinical TLR claim.
  • TLR-linked vault facts within adrenochrome notes. TLR agonists/TNFα/IL-1β prime NLRP3 (Signal 1; NF-κB; NLRP3+pro-IL-1β+pro-IL-18; BRCC3 deubiquitination; vault adrenochrome/NLRP3 Inflammasome.md; senescence Inflammasome; Tier 4). Signal 2 unchanged. TRAF6 at MyD88-IRAK/RIG-I/MAVS/CD40/RANK/TNFR convergence; adrenochrome-adjacent inflammatory DAMPs would enter at TLR priming stage (inference). RIPK1 scaffold; TLR3 stimulation relieves cIAP inhibition → ripoptosome; TLR3→TRIF→caspase-8; necroptosis crosstalk (vault adrenochrome/RIPK1.md; cell-death; Tier 4). IL-1β maturation needs pro-IL-1β (TLR/NOD priming) + NLRP3 + caspase-1 (vault adrenochrome/Interleukin 1β.md). Erythrocyte TLR9 + cell-free DNA (vault adrenochrome/Erythrocytes.md).
  • Direct adrenochrome→TLR ligand binding: unverified (Tier 5 inference only via oxidative-DAMP→TLR4/2 + NLRP3 priming).
  • COMT link (this vault comt/). Adrenochrome formation ↔ catecholamine oxidation; COMT clearance reduces substrate; no vault-verified direct COMT→TLR edge; inferred reduction of oxidative-DAMP TLR load.

Neuromelanin

(vault topic: src/notes/neuromelanin/; Neuromelanin, neuromelanin/Microgliosis.md)

  • Extracellular neuromelanin released from dying dopaminergic neurons is a potent microglial activator via scavenger receptors + TLRs (vault Microgliosis.md; Tier 3/4; TLR4/2 specificity not vault-pinned; Tier 5 for exact receptor assignment).
  • Microgliosis/inflammaging in neurodegeneration; adrenochrome/neuromelanin catecholamine-oxidation theme; TLR-adjacent NLRP3 priming; no vault-verified neuromelanin→specific-TLR-subtype claim.

COMT

(vault topic: src/notes/comt/; COMT, Dopamine; adrenochrome links)

  • Catechol-O-methyltransferase O-methylates catecholamines (dopamine/norepinephrine/epinephrine) → reduces reactive catechol availability for oxidation to adrenochrome/quinones → reduces oxidative-DAMP formation upstream of TLR/cGAS/NLRP3 arms.
  • Vault COMT topic has no direct TLR edge in its non-document notes; linkage is inferred Tier 5 via oxidative-stress→DAMP→TLR. COMT genotype/pharmacology effects on TLR signaling: unverified.

Web-verified updates (2024-2026)

Retrieved 2026-11-27 via websearch; PMID/DOI as printed by source; not independently re-verified. These supplement, not replace, vault content. General-knowledge items elsewhere are marked.

  1. TLR4 signalosome TIR-domain acetylation. LPS induces reversible acetylation of TIR domains in TLR4-Mal-MyD88; regulates M1 polarization; therapeutic target (EMBO J 2024; DOI 10.1038/s44318-024-00237-8; Tier 4 + Tier 3).
  2. SIRT1 p65 K310 + NLRP3 + NAD+. SIRT1 deacetylates p65 K310; reduces pro-inflammatory output; SIRT1→NLRP3↓; NAD+→SIRT1→TLR4 type-I-IFN↓ in SLE + control monocytes; PNPLA7→SIRT1→p65 axis (Tier 2-4; 2022-2024; sources: DOI 10.1186/s12964-023-01177-2; DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0046364 (SIRT1 activators, source-cited); DOI 10.3390/ijms21113858 PMID 32485811; JCI 2022 132:e139828; DOI 10.3390/ijms232314983 PMID 36499308; DOI 10.1007/s00011-024-01890-9).
  3. Endosomal lysosomal NA processing. RNase T2 + PLD generate TLR7 ligands (Immunity 2024; DOI ref in source); RNase T2 also negatively regulates TLR3; PLD3/PLD4 inhibit TLR7/9 + cGAS while positively generating TLR7 ligand; DNase II → TLR9 vs cGAS-STING; SLC29A3; lysosomal NA stress (J Biochem 2025 PMID 40037613; DOI 10.1093/jb/mvaf011; Tier 3-4 + human genetic).
  4. Mitophagy→TLR trafficking. TFAM LIR1/LIR2 nucleoid-phagy prevents mtDNA→cGAS-STING + TLR9 (2024); Retromer lysosomal mtDNA turnover (Science Advances 2025 DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adr6415); OPA1; IRGM1→TLR7; LC3B prolongs TLR9; IRF7↓ in aged pDCs (Tier 3-4 + human observational).
  5. TLR-driven trained immunity. eLife 2025 review DOI 10.7554/eLife.106443: epigenetic+metabolic reprogramming; trained vs tolerant; TLR agonist adjuvants; chronic inflammation. (Tier 1-3 + Tier 4.)
  6. Noncanonical cGAS-STING in senescence/aging. Reduced canonical cGAMP/STING-PNC in aged/senescent/progeria; STING at ER/NE bound to chromatin; cGAS+STING-dependent SASP/IFN; pharmacological inhibition ameliorates progeria (PNAS 2025 DOI 10.1073/pnas.2424666122; Tier 3 + Tier 4). Does not erase TLR2 loop.
  7. TLR2-SAA feedback. cGAS-STING→NF-κB→TLR2+SAA1/SAA2→TLR2→SASP; Science Advances 2019 + 2025 re-analyses (Tier 3-4).
  8. MyD88 regulation. MyD88 resting clusters; myddosome; MyD88-S; A20; miR-146a; NF-κB-dependent; protein destabilization mitigates protection against macrophage apoptosis; MyD88 regulates NLRP3 + mitophagy proteins + anti-apoptotic proteins (Tier 4 + Tier 3; 2024; DOI 10.1186/s12964-024-01930-1).
  9. TLR3–TRIF cell-death routes. TLR3→TRIF→RIPK1→caspase-8 FADD-independent; TLR3/4→TRIF→RIPK3 necroptosis; TLR3-TRIF-RIPK1-TRAF6 (2025; Tier 4).
  10. Cancer immunotherapy. TLR dual-role review 2025; imiquimod TLR7 Tier 1 approval; CpG TLR9 adjuvant Tier 1; BCG/MPL; anti-TLR antagonism research; eritoran Tier 1 negative sepsis (not necessarily cancer).
  11. Species. Human TLR8 active ssRNA; mouse TLR8 inactive; RNase 6 + 2'-O-methylation; TLR8 ligand motif (2024 EMBO Rep; vault).
  12. Vault-internal discrepancy (not web). _link/Toll-like Receptor.md states "Humans possess 10 functional TLRs (TLR1–TLR10)" then lists TLR11 in endosomal table; human TLR11 is pseudogene/non-functional; mouse TLR11-13 exist. Flag for vault update.
  13. 2026 syntheses. TLR4 review: no licensed TLR antagonist; eritoran phase III n=1961 no mortality benefit (Front Immunol 2026, DOI 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1761361). Broad innate-immunity review: conserved LRR–TM–TIR architecture; TLR2/1 vs TLR2/6 ligand discrimination; TLR10 functionally active with elusive ligand (STTT 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41392-026-02920-0). TLR review: multilayered regulation (PTMs, epigenetic reprogramming, metabolic rewiring, signaling condensates, alternative splicing); contested proteinaceous DAMPs (Immunity & Inflammation 2026, DOI 10.1007/s44466-026-00040-6 PMID 42052154).
  14. 2026 TLR2 in aging liver. Gut-derived LTA/TLR2 axis drives hepatic senescence and fibrosis; TLR2 deletion and ortho-vanillin reduced markers in mice (DOI 10.1186/s11658-026-00881-4 PMID 41772418).
  15. 2026 endosomal pharmacology. ENDOtollins inhibit the Munc13-4–STX7 interaction, reduce endolysosomal flux and TLR3/7/9 responses in primary dendritic cells, and lower CpG-induced systemic inflammation in mice (Nat Chem Biol 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41589-026-02181-6; Tier 3/4).
  16. 2026 TLR agonism/antagonism. Intratumoral TLR7 + TLR4 co-agonists enhanced antitumor responses in CT26 mice without formal synergy analysis (Front Immunol 2026, DOI 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1911644; Tier 3). TLR7/8 antagonist 621 raised IL-10, Tregs and M2 macrophages and mitigated DSS colitis in mice (ACS Pharmacol Transl Sci 2026, DOI 10.1021/acsptsci.5c00604; Tier 3/4). BMS-986256 clinical advancement reported in a 2026 conference abstract — unverified full report.

Research gaps

Evidence-preserving questions; not medical recommendations. Distinguishing "unknown" from "vault does not cover."

Molecular mechanism gaps

  • Proteinaceous DAMP specificity. Direct TLR binding by HMGB1 and heat-shock proteins is contested; whether effects are receptor-mediated or downstream of nucleic-acid/DAMP complexes remains unresolved (DOI 10.1007/s44466-026-00040-6).
  • TLR10. Ligand(s), adaptor preference, signaling output, physiological role unknown; anti-inflammatory vs pro-inflammatory reports unresolved (vault + Tier 4/5).
  • TLR1 function relative to TLR1/TLR2 vs any distinct ligand; sparse in vault + literature.
  • Canonical vs noncanonical cGAS-STING interaction with TLR2/TLR7/TLR9 in senescence. Exact partitioning; whether noncanonical cGAS-STING and TLR2-SAA are parallel or sequential; tissue/cell-type specificity; progeria relevance (web 2025; vault).
  • Degradative vs signaling lysosomes. What directs endolysosomes to remain degradative vs become TLR signaling organelles; drivers of TLR7 vs TLR9 choice in the same vesicle (web 2024); relationship to lysosomal NA stress outputs.
  • mtDNA vs mtRNA partitioning. What routes mtDNA→TLR9 vs mtRNA→TLR7/8 vs cytosolic cGAS/RIG-I/NLRP3; preference mechanisms; role of TFAM, TREX1, DNase II, DNase2a, PLD3/4, RNase T2, RNase 6, methylation (web 2024-2025; vault).
  • Basal vs stress mitophagy tradeoff. When basal autophagy reduces TLR ligands vs when stress mitophagy delivers self-ligands to TLRs; net inflammation sign; senescence vs trained-immunity contexts (vault; web 2024-2025).
  • Hormetic window quantification. Exact dose/duration inflection for TLR trained vs tolerant vs inflammaging; cell-type; acute vs chronic; endogenous vs exogenous ligand mix (vault Trained Immunity + web 2025 eLife; schematic only here).
  • Post-translational regulation map. Full kinase/acetylation/ubiquitin phosphorylation map of TLR/TIR/adaptor; whether SIRT1 directly deacetylates TLR4/TIRAP/MyD88 vs only p65; relation to TLR4 TIR acetylation (web 2024; vault).
  • Endosomal TLR species translation. Human vs mouse TLR8 + TLR7 differences; TLR10 human vs mouse; mouse models mis-estimating human endosomal TLR output (vault + web 2024).
  • TLR-IFN vs TLR-NF-κB in aged cells. Immunosenescence reduced type-I-IFN (IRF7↓) + persistent NF-κB/inflammaging; cell-type; relation to TLR9/TLR7 in senescence (vault + web 2025).

Senescence / SASP gaps

  • TLR2 vs cGAS-STING relative contribution to SASP initiation across inducers (OIS vs DNA damage vs replicative vs mitochondrial); whether TLR2 always downstream or parallel; in vivo human validation of senolytic/senomorphic TLR2 targeting is absent (vault; web 2024-2025; Tier 3-4 + research).
  • TLR2→PGE2 immunosuppression vs pro-inflammatory SASP balance; gut-LTA senescent cells in cancer (web; vault).
  • TLR7 chronic senescence mechanism depth; SASP composition shifts.
  • Anti-inflammatory SASP (IL-10, GDF15 etc.) interaction with TLR arms (vault + web 2025).

Autophagy gaps

  • OCRL / UNC93B1 / retromer / AP-3 / TFAM / IRGM1 quantitative contributions to TLR signal amplitude vs duration; tissue context (web 2023-2025; vault).
  • TFEB/TFE3 TLR-mTORC1-independent activation mechanism; full target overlap immune + lysosomal; pathogen counter-measures (vault + web).
  • MDV biogenesis routes fumarate vs ROS vs stress; lysosomal vs endosomal vs extracellular vesicle outputs (web 2025; vault).
  • Autophagy as TLR terminator vs amplifier cell-type + chronicity; autophagic cell death separate from TLR regulation.

Sirtuins / NAD+ gaps

  • Direct SIRT1 targets in TLR signalosome beyond p65; NAD+ compartmentation; SIRT1 vs NF-κB antagonism kinetics; NAD+→TLR4-IFN clinical relevance; resveratrol mechanism specificity (vault + web 2022-2024).
  • SIRT3/SIRT6 direct TLR edges vs indirect ROS/NLRP3; vault-unverified.

Oxidative-stress / hormesis gaps

  • Redox regulation of TLR TIR cysteines + interaction with TIR acetylation; Nrf2/NF-κB crosstalk kinetics; NOX2 vs mtROS source separation; hormetic ROS window vs TLR hormetic window co-regulation (vault + web; Tier 4/5).
  • CD36 routing nuances oxLDL vs amyloid-β vs LTA; TLR2/6 vs scavenger duality.

Cell-death gaps

  • TLR→cell-death vs TLR→inflammation decision logic (c-FLIP, cIAP, RIPK1 phosphorylation, ubiquitin, caspase-8 availability; known in cell-death vault but not TLR-specifically mapped); PANoptosis TLR contribution; ZBP1 vs TRIF parallelism.
  • Pyroptosis TLR→NLRP3→gasdermin D vs other gasdermins; SIRT1 quantitative.

Cancer gaps

  • Pro- vs anti-tumor TLR resolution per receptor/cell/context; chronic vs acute; TLR2→SASP dual; in-tumor agonist vs antagonist; human clinical durable responses beyond imiquimod/CpG/BCG; combination with checkpoint inhibitors durable endpoints.
  • TLR4 cancer duality; eritoran-style antagonism in cancer vs sepsis lessons.
