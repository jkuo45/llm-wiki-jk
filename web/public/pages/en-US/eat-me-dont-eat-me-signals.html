Eat-me and don't-eat-me signals — the phagocytic balance

Phagocytosis of a dying, stressed, or malignant cell is gated by the relative display of pro-phagocytic eat-me ligands and anti-phagocytic don't-eat-me ligands on the target-cell surface. Eat-me signals converge on loss of plasma-membrane lipid asymmetry and exposure of [[Phosphatidylserine|phosphatidylserine (PS)]], surface [[Calreticulin|calreticulin]], and opsonins; don't-eat-me signals engage inhibitory ITIM- or lectin-bearing receptors on the phagocyte. This page illustrates the ratio rule with an interactive three.js model, charts the signal balance across cell states, maps the signal–receptor wiring, and inventories each ligand with its receptor, execution machinery, and evidence tier. Evidence tiers: Tier 1 human RCT / approved · Tier 2 human trial / association · Tier 3 rodent / in vivo · Tier 4 in vitro / ex vivo · Tier 5 hypothesis. Signal biochemistry is Tier 4 unless a clinical axis is named.

Two opposing signal classes

A phagocyte integrates activating and inhibitory inputs at the phagocytic synapse. Eat-me ligands are displayed on dying, stressed, infected, or opsonized cells; don't-eat-me ligands are constitutively displayed on viable cells and are up-regulated on tumor cells in reported models. A widely used comparison is surface calreticulin versus CD47: calreticulin licenses uptake while CD47–SIRPα signalling blocks it (Tier 4, cellular assays).

Eat-me — pro-phagocytic

Signals exposed on dying, stressed, or infected cells that tag the cell for engulfment. Most recognize a common endpoint: loss of plasma-membrane lipid asymmetry with PS on the outer leaflet. Uptake of PS-positive material skews macrophages toward HO-1-producing, anti-inflammatory states.

PS · calreticulin · cardiolipin · oxPL · desialylated glycans · ubiquitin · C1q · CXCL14 · SLAMF7

Don't-eat-me — anti-phagocytic

Anti-phagocytic ceilings. Most act through inhibitory ITIM-bearing or homophilic receptors on the phagocyte, recruiting SHP-1/2 and blocking myosin-II-dependent engulfment. Cancer and fibrosis overexpress them.

CD47 · CD24 · PD-L1 · sialic acid · β2M/MHC-I · CD31
The balance rule

Efferocytosis is triggered by the ratio of eat-me to don't-eat-me display, not by any one ligand. A viable cell maintains PS on the inner leaflet through flippase activity while displaying CD47, sialic acid, and MHC-I; death or stress inactivates flippase and activates scramblase, externalizing PS with reported reduction in CD47, so the ratio shifts toward engulfment (Tier 4).

The balance rule across cell states

The ratio is reported to vary with cell state: viable cells are brake-dominant; apoptotic, eryptotic, and aged cells become eat-dominant; tumors raise the inhibitory ceiling; immunogenic-cell-death (ICD) therapy raises the eat-me side and removes the brake. The chart is a qualitative schematic — the source inventory reports no quantified tissue-level ratio (see Open problems).

Membrane asymmetry loss and PS-mediated engulfment

Healthy cells retain PS on the inner leaflet through ATP-dependent flippase activity. Apoptotic and eryptotic execution inactivates flippase and activates scramblase — caspase cleavage of ATP11C with XKR8 activation in apoptosis, Ca²⁺-driven TMEM16F/ANO6 activation in eryptosis — externalizing PS. PS is then read directly by TIM-family, BAI1, and stabilin receptors or through bridging molecules (Gas6/Protein S to MerTK-type TAM receptors; MFGE8 to integrins). Inhibitory ligands engage a parallel ITIM/SHP-1/2 branch.

Two-lane mechanism. Upper (green): loss of lipid asymmetry → PS exposure → receptor recognition → non-inflammatory engulfment. Lower (red): inhibitory ligands → ITIM-bearing receptors → SHP-1/2 and myosin-II block. The phagocyte integrates both lanes; the ratio sets the outcome (Tier 4).
  1. Viable cell. Flippase keeps PS inward; CD47, sialic acid, and MHC-I dominate the outer surface → no sustained phagocytosis.
  2. Death or stress. Flippase off, scramblase on (Ca²⁺-driven in eryptosis; caspase-driven in apoptosis) → PS on the outer leaflet; CD47 loss with age is reported → ratio shifts → engulfment, non-inflammatory when PS-mediated.
  3. Disease effects. Cancer overexpresses brakes (CD47, CD24, PD-L1); fibrosis and atherosclerosis impair eat-me recognition downstream; excess eryptosis saturates clearance and delivers PS-positive procoagulant cells.
  4. ICD therapy. Surface calreticulin appears after anthracyclines, photodynamic therapy, and some radiotherapy. DC uptake of PS-positive corpses is reported as tolerogenic in vitro unless paired with calreticulin and danger signals.

Eat-me signals — inventory

Signals exposed on dying, stressed, or infected cells that tag the cell for engulfment. A complementary class of find-me signals recruits phagocytes without itself triggering engulfment (see Find-me).

SignalExposed on / contextRecognized byNotes (evidence tier)
Phosphatidylserine (PS) Multiple contexts: outer leaflet of apoptotic, eryptotic, activated-platelet, and tumor-cell membranes; apoptotic bodies. Direct PS receptors (TIM-4, BAI1, stabilin-2) and bridging molecules (Gas6/Protein S → MerTK-type TAM receptors; MFGE8 → integrins). Widely reported mammalian eat-me signal Tier 4. Execution requires scramblase activation + flippase inactivation (TMEM16F/ANO6 in eryptosis; caspase-cleaved ATP11C and XKR8 in apoptosis). PS-positive uptake skews macrophages toward HO-1-producing anti-inflammatory states Tier 4–Tier 3.
Calreticulin (surface-exposed) Cell surface after anthracycline chemotherapy, photodynamic therapy, some radiotherapy; reported as the dominant eat-me signal in immunogenic cell death models. LRP1/CD91 on phagocytes; removal of the opposing CD47 brake licenses uptake. Surface CRT is recognized by LRP1/CD91 (Gardai et al. 2005); CRT is also a reported C1q-binding co-receptor, and secreted or cleaved CRT can act as an opsonin. CRT is required for uptake while tumor antigens from the same death trigger the adaptive response Tier 4.
SLAMF7 (CS1 / CD319) Hematopoietic tumor cells (myeloma, some lymphoma), plasma cells, NK cells; also macrophages. Homotypic SLAMF7–SLAMF7 binding with Mac-1 (CD11b/CD18) on macrophages → FcRγ/DAP12, Syk/Btk. Pro-phagocytic eat-me signal, not a brake: SLAMF7 is required for macrophage phagocytosis of hematopoietic tumor cells during CD47–SIRPα blockade (Chen et al. 2017, Nature 544:493–497; PMID 28424516) Tier 4. The requirement is disputed (He et al. 2019, Nat Commun); the SLAM-family brakes are SLAMF3/SLAMF4. Elotuzumab is approved in myeloma combinations Tier 1 via NK-cell ADCC and macrophage ADCP, not by neutralizing an inhibitory pair.
Cardiolipin (externalized) Outer surface of stressed mitochondria and of dying cells; cardiolipin peroxidation is cytochrome-c-linked. CD36 on macrophages for extracellular/exposed cardiolipin; LC3 for intracellular mitophagic clearance. Externalized cardiolipin is a CD36-dependent eat-me signal (Balasubramanian et al. 2015, Sci Signal; PMID 26396268); translocation to the outer mitochondrial membrane recruits LC3 for mitophagy (Chu et al. 2013, Nat Cell Biol; PMID 24036476) Tier 4.
Oxidized phospholipids / oxLDL epitopes Oxidatively stressed cells; atheromatous debris. Scavenger receptors (CD36, SR-A, LOX-1). Reported contributor to unstable-plaque foam-cell uptake; overlaps with PS via oxPS determinants Tier 4.
Desialylated glycans Aged erythrocytes — the classical senescent eat-me cue; neuraminidase treatment experimentally drives PS exposure. Naturally occurring IgG autoantibodies (reported against AE1/band 3) → Fc-receptor bridging. Distinct from the PS route: not eryptosis. Desialylation bridges the two clearance programs, since desialylation and PS exposure co-occur Tier 4.
Ubiquitin chains on damaged organelles Parkin-tagged mitochondria (PINK1/Parkin pathway); mitophagic receptors. Autophagy receptors p62, NBR1, NDP52, OPTN. The eat-me logic of mitophagy mirrors efferocytosis at the organelle scale Tier 4; PINK1/Parkin failure is linked to familial Parkinson's disease.
ECM-anchored CXCL14 Early senescence secretome, before NF-κB/IL-6 SASP matures. Macrophage, DC, NK, T-cell recruiters (unidentified receptor). An eat-me chemoattractant rather than a surface tag; retained on endothelial glycosaminoglycans Tier 4.
Complement C1q (bridging opsonin) Apoptotic cells; classical pathway sensor. C1q bridging to surface calreticulin / CD91 (LRP1) on phagocytes. CRT (cC1qR) is a reported C1q-binding co-receptor with CD91/LRP1; the assignment is debated because CRT has no transmembrane domain (Park & Kim 2017). Listed for completeness Tier 4.

Don't-eat-me signals — inventory

Anti-phagocytic ceilings. Most act through inhibitory ITIM-bearing or homophilic receptors on the phagocyte; cancer and fibrosis overexpress them.

SignalExpressed onRecognized byNotes (evidence tier)
CD47 Nearly all nucleated cells + erythrocytes — a widely used brake example. SIRPα on macrophages/DCs → ITIM, SHP-1/2, myosin-II accumulation blocks engulfment. Ubiquitously expressed; levels rise with age in some tissues (arteries, lung, photoaged skin). Loss on aged erythrocytes removes the brake and accelerates clearance Tier 4, Tier 3 in RBC models. Anti-CD47 agents (monoclonal antibodies, SIRPα decoys, bispecifics) remain investigational with no approval: Gilead terminated magrolimab in 2024 after ENHANCE-3 showed futility and increased deaths (FDA full hold in AML/MDS), and the remaining solid-tumor trials were removed from the pipeline; other agents had further setbacks in 2024–25 Tier 2.
CD24 Human tumors (murine Cd24a differs functionally), developing hematopoietic cells. Siglec-10 on macrophages → inhibitory SHP-1. Reported as an additional don't-eat-me signal alongside CD47: CD24–Siglec-10 blockade augments phagocytosis of CD24-expressing human tumors (Barkal et al. 2019) Tier 4.
PD-L1 (CD274) Tumor cells, inflamed tissues. PD-1 on macrophages, at the phagocytic synapse. PD-L1 on tumor cells engaging PD-1 on macrophages suppresses phagocytosis; blocking the pair licenses tumor-cell uptake — the same axis that gates T-cell checkpoints (Gordon et al. 2017, Nature 545:495–499; PMID 28514441) Tier 4. PD-1/PD-L1 blockade is approved in oncology Tier 1 for T-cell checkpoint blockade, not specifically for the macrophage arm.
Sialic acid / α2,8-polysialic acid Healthy viable cells (immune, neural lineages). Siglecs (inhibitory I-type lectins, incl. Siglec-10, Siglec-7; α2,8-polysialic acid is recognized by Siglec-11). Removal by sialidase converts the cell to an eat-me state; age-related desialylation of erythrocytes links to the eat-me table Tier 4.
β2-microglobulin (+ MHC-I display) Nucleated cells; tumor cells up-regulate the axis. LILRB1 (ILT2) on macrophages; LILRB2 / PirB in mouse CNS. Surface MHC-I/β2M sets an anti-phagocytic threshold via LILRB1; disruption of either MHC-I or LILRB1 potentiated tumor-cell phagocytosis in vitro and in mouse models (Barkal et al. 2018, Nat Immunol 19:76–84; PMID 29180808) Tier 3. PILRβ is an activating CD99 receptor and KIRs are NK-cell receptors, not this brake.
CD31 (PECAM-1) Endothelium, platelets, leukocytes. CD31–CD31 homophilic engagement on live leukocytes; competence lost on apoptotic cells. A live-cell don't-eat-me badge: homophilic CD31 engagement blocks uptake only while the target cell can signal through it (reported in vitro) Tier 4.

Find-me signals — recruitment, not engulfment

A complementary class of soluble or matrix-bound cues recruits phagocytes to the site without itself triggering engulfment. PS is listed among find-me cues in some reviews, but the standard soluble set is nucleotides, lysophospholipids, sphingosine-1-phosphate, and chemokines Tier 4.

Evidence tiers and clinical translation

Ligand biochemistry is uniformly Tier 4 (cellular assays). Only a few axes have reached human intervention: PD-1/PD-L1 checkpoint blockade and anti-SLAMF7 elotuzumab are approved in oncology (Tier 1). The anti-CD47 class remains in earlier-phase trials with no approval; magrolimab was terminated in 2024 after phase 3 futility and safety findings. Blocking a don't-eat-me brake in fibrosis or atherosclerosis is mechanistically plausible but not human-validated; existing anti-CD47 clinical data are oncology-only.

Highest tier reached per axis. Tiers are ordinal, not effect sizes: Tier 5 = hypothesis, Tier 4 = in vitro / ex vivo, Tier 3 = rodent / in vivo, Tier 2 = human trial / association, Tier 1 = human RCT / approved intervention. Bar length encodes ordinal strength, not magnitude of effect.
Interpretive limit

Which cells in a given tissue read which pairing (CD47 vs CD24 vs C1q–CRT) is context-dependent and not fully mapped in vivo Tier 5 across tissues. Quantifying a tissue's CD47 : sialic acid : PS ratio at single-cell resolution is an unresolved technical gap.

Failure modes: brake overexpression and impaired clearance

Cancer — brake overexpression

Tumors overexpress CD47, CD24, and PD-L1. Don't-eat-me blockade is a cancer-immunotherapy strategy (Khalaji et al. 2023); anti-CD47 antibodies, SIRPα decoys, and bispecifics remain investigational, and magrolimab was terminated in 2024 after phase 3 futility and safety findings.

Fibrosis and atherosclerosis — impaired recognition

Efferocytosis is impaired downstream of eat-me recognition, producing necrotic-core debris and persistent inflammation (atherosclerosis efferocytosis data, Tier 4–Tier 3).

Excess eryptosis — clearance saturation

In CKD, sepsis, and hemoglobinopathies, excess eryptosis saturates clearance and delivers PS-positive procoagulant cells. See the eryptosis evidence synthesis.

ICD therapy — increasing eat-me display

Surface calreticulin appears after anthracyclines, photodynamic therapy, and some radiotherapy. DC uptake of PS-positive corpses is reported as tolerogenic in vitro unless paired with CRT and danger signals, so the eat-me signal also biases the adaptive response.

Open problems

  • Pairing map. Which cells in a given tissue read which pairing (CD47 vs CD24 vs C1q–CRT) is context-dependent and not fully mapped in vivo Tier 5 across tissues.
  • Brake blockade outside oncology. Blocking a don't-eat-me brake in fibrosis or atherosclerosis is mechanistically plausible but not human-validated; existing anti-CD47 clinical data are oncology-only Tier 5.
  • Ratio measurement. Quantifying a tissue's CD47 : sialic acid : PS ratio at single-cell resolution is an unresolved technical gap (not re-derived from the cited sources).
