Caspase-8 — initiator

FasL/TRAIL nucleate the FADD DISC; c-FLIP is the rheostat brake. Caspase-8 cleaves BID toward mitochondria and RIPK1/3 to shut necroptosis off.

Caspase-9 — amplifier

mitochondria · apoptosome

p53 → PUMA/NOXA + BAX → Bax/BAK activation → MOMP → cytochrome c + Apaf-1 apoptosome → Caspase-9. Bcl-2/Bcl-xL guard; XIAP brakes executioners until Smac/DIABLO release.

Caspase-3 — executor

cytosol → membrane + nucleus

Cleaves ICAD (DNA ladder), ROCK1 (blebs), flips PS. Caspase-3 cuts GSDMD at D87 to inactivate it while activating GSDME — the anti-GSDMD / pro-GSDME switch. Bodies stay sealed.
