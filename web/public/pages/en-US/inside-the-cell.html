Organelles

Molecules, ribosomes and membrane proteins are enlarged for visibility — see the scale table below for true sizes.

How to read the scale. The cell is Ø 20 µm and the bar at bottom-right is live — it recomputes every frame from camera distance. Mitochondria, ER tubules, Golgi, lysosomes, centrioles and microtubules are true 1:1. Ribosomes (Ø 25 nm) are shown ×4, membrane proteins ≈ ×3, and tour molecules are enlarged dots — at true scale all three would be subpixel, which is why the render enlarges them.
