Model design and scope

Both models initialize from an identical geometry: an icosahedral actin cortex, inner and outer phospholipid leaflets, a nuclear envelope enclosing three chromatin domains, and mitochondria with folded cristae. The divergent variable is bioenergetics. Apoptosis proceeds with ATP available to caspase cascades, cortical contraction, and lipid remodeling; necrosis follows ATP depletion, transport failure, and osmotic lysis.

Secondary necrosis

Apoptotic bodies that escape engulfment — e.g., high cell density, in vitro culture, or impaired macrophage function — lose membrane integrity and release DAMPs, converging on a necrotic endpoint. The two trajectories are displayed on independent timescales to keep this distinction explicit.

Geometry is original to this page (geodesic lattice, Fibonacci bilayer, folded cristae, dual-model layout), distinct from the single-program models elsewhere on the site.
