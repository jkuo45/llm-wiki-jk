RIPK1 — decision integrator

cytosol · phosphate + ubiquitin codes

DAPK1 phosphorylates Ser321 to restrain; ROS drives autophosphorylation; PTPN6 removes tyrosine phosphates. TRIM21 ubiquitinates to activate; CYLD deubiquitinates to license necrosome entry; OTULIN-pY56 counterbalances. Caspase-8 cleavage is the brake (off here, so death is licensed).

pro (→ death) anti (→ survival) P · Ub carrier

RIPK3 — amplifier

cytosol · RHIM + kinase inputs → MLKL + mitochondria

RHIM recruitment by RIPK1 / ZBP1 / TRIF triggers autophosphorylation; p90-RSK3 phosphorylates directly; viral SFTSV-NSs bypasses RHIM; CK1G2 binds to inhibit. Output splits to MLKL and mitochondrial branches — CaMKII → mPTP, PGAM5 → Drp1 fission, PDC-E3-T135 metabolic reprogramming.

pro activation brake (CK1G2) mito · metabolic output

MLKL — executor

cytosol → membrane · transcription + phosphorylation + pores

BRD4 drives MLKL mRNA supply; HSP90 shields the protein from the proteasome; RIPK3 phosphorylates Thr357/Ser358 (mouse RIPK1 can also; TAM kinases add p-Tyr376 during phagocytosis to drive oligomerization). Oligomers bind PIPs/cardiolipin and punch membrane pores → ion influx, swelling, DAMP release.

licensed p-MLKL supply · shield pore · DAMP
