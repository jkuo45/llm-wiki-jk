Reading a Discovery Arrival: Graph Metrics and Typed Edges When New Entities Enter the Graph

A new discovery does not enter a knowledge graph all at once. It arrives as an orphan, accretes seed edges, gets absorbed by a neighboring field, starts bridging fields, and — if productive — nucleates its own community. This page traces that lifecycle metric-by-metric through two replays on this vault's graphs — ferroptosis (named 2012, now a 141-edge hub) and COVID/vaccines (2019–2021) — and then shows how the graph's 3,895 typed, directed edges turn co-mention into implication: connections nobody has stated yet, computed rather than guessed.

Date: 30 August 2026

Data sources: graphify-out/graph.json (triples: typed, directed, confidence-scored) · wiki-out/wiki-graph.json (wiki: co-mention links_to) · web/public/data/edges.json (combined: 37,318 edges = 3,895 typed-directed + 33,423 co-mention)

Methods: staged year-by-year replay simulations (node emptied, edges added in literature-timeline batches, metrics recomputed per batch; Louvain fixed seed; 400-pivot betweenness) · 04_node_analysis.py · 04_role_query.py · external literature (Cell, Nature, NEJM, Lancet, PNAS, eLife)

Audience: biologists and data analysts. Informational — not medical advice.

Framing

What should a metric "see" when a discovery arrives?

The motivating question: suppose new entities arrive — a new mechanism (ferroptosis, named in one Cell paper in 2012), a new disease-plus-intervention cluster (COVID-19 and vaccines, 2019–2021). What happens to the calculations at introduction, as the entities mature, and as implications accrete? And — because edges in this graph carry relation type, direction, confidence, and provenance — can the metrics surface connections that nobody has explicitly stated yet?

The short answers, developed below: (1) each metric is a different instrument pointed at the same maturation curve — betweenness is the maturation signal, k-core the depth signal, Adamic-Adar the foresight signal (highest at birth, decaying as the node fills in), PageRank the rear-view mirror. (2) Topology proposes, semantics dispose: undirected metrics tell you where implications will land; the typed edges tell you what they are — cause, cure, or mere association. (3) Graph output is a triage instrument with a calibrated base rate (top-ranked candidates hit ~10–30% vs 0.8% unguided), never an oracle.

Why replay? You cannot observe a discovery's metrics retrospectively — today's graph already contains the mature node. So the runs used counterfactual replay: the ferroptosis node was emptied, its edges re-added in literature-timeline batches (2012, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2024), and every metric recomputed per batch. The result is a metric trajectory, not a snapshot.

Lifecycle

The five-phase discovery lifecycle

Bibliometrics of real concepts show a recurring curve: a lag phase (ferroptosis: 1 paper in 2012, dozens/year through 2016; PROTACs: named 2001, dormant until ~2014), an inflection (one bridging paper or trial), explosive edge growth (ferroptosis >1,000 papers/year by 2021, exponential fit R² = 0.95), then saturation and fragmentation. Each graph metric fires at a different phase.

Two rules the replays support:

Rule 1 — Foreseeability decays with maturity. Adamic-Adar against the thin 2012 ferroptosis node predicted 80% of its eventual edge set (precision@10 = 0.8), falling to 0.6 by the 2024 stage and 0.0 when fully attached — the "easy" attachments get drawn first. Triage a discovery's neighborhood early.

Rule 2 — Topology proposes, semantics dispose. Undirected calculations (AA, PPR, k-core) say where implications will land; the relation type, direction, confidence, and provenance on typed edges say what they are.

Read as an instrument panel:

  • Betweenness ≈ 1,000× growth (0.01 → 9.86 ×10⁻³), largest leap (×11) at the 2020 stage — bridging = the node now carries implications for other fields.
  • k-core 8 → 19: graduation into the wiki graph's maximum core tier. The role classifier agrees — Ferroptosis = Sink + Master regulator + Bottleneck + Core backbone.
  • AA foreseeability was highest at birth (0.8) and decayed as the node filled in. The top-25 misses at the 2012 snapshot are exactly the post-2012 literature: FSP1 (+7 yr), ALOX15/ALOX5, ASAH1/Acid Ceramidase (+12 yr), Artesunate, CD44, STEAP3. The miss list is the research agenda.
  • PPR stayed local until maturity — flow follows degree, so "where does influence reach" is answerable only after implication edges exist. Flow metrics measure accumulated implications; local-similarity metrics measure future ones.
  • Mirror experiment (delete the matured node): 13 satellite nodes drop out of the triples giant component; PageRank shifts onto the defense belt (NADPH −1,159, Statins −977 on triples; Vitamin K −315, HMG-CoA reductase −279 on wiki). A discovery's implications are visible as the satellite belt that would dangle without it.

Three readings worth internalizing:

  1. One seed edge opens or closes an entire mechanistic corridor. Granting the thin 2020 node a single clinical observation — Lymphopenia — put CD38 at predicted-rank #6 and NAD+ at #7 (Adamic-Adar, out of 2,995 candidates); without that seed both fall out of the top-25. CD38-NADase-driven NAD+ depletion in severe COVID was then confirmed by the literature (Heer et al., JBC 2020; Physiol Rev 2021). Record which seed relation types were granted — they gate the corridor.
  2. The graph predicted edges that science later created and the wiki still has not drawn. The prediction was validated against the literature, not today's edge list. Graph and literature are two different ground truths; the gap between them is a to-do list.
  3. Replayed vs actual wiring quantifies what's missing. The 2021-mechanism replay (CD38/NAD+/PARP wired directly) reaches k-core 15 / betweenness 0.69 vs the actual graph's k-core 11 / 0.34 with indirect wiring via [[Lymphopenia]]/[[Viral Replication]]. In the triples layer SARS-CoV-2 has 2 edges and is not even in the giant component (graphify path "SARS-CoV-2" "NAD+" → no path), while the combined layer carries sars_cov_2 --[disrupts]--> NAD+_metabolism. Same corpus, three layers, three verdicts — always check which layer a metric ran on.

The stratification lesson (vaccines). The wiki has exactly one vaccine node (shingles_vaccine) — no mRNA/LNP/PEG/IgG4/myocarditis/ACE2/spike nodes; a future vaccine discovery would arrive as an orphan. And the highest-quality 2024–26 evidence is conditioned: myocarditis ~150/10⁶ second doses in males 12–15 with a steep age gradient; IgG4 class-switching is dose-count-dependent and plateaus after dose 3. A single averaged vaccine→myocarditis edge washes out exactly the heterogeneity that is the clinical truth — the graph needs edge attributes (dose × age × sex × evidence tier) or hyperedges (myocarditis meta, Epidemiol Rev 2025; IgG4, Sci Immunol 2023).

Typed edges

Edges have semantics and direction: four mechanisms for unstated connections

The combined layer carries 3,895 typed, directed edges among 37,318 total — 1,033 distinct relation labels, the most common being inhibits (220), activates (170), deacetylates (111), promotes (104), reduces (79), induces (65), causes (64), suppresses (56), regulates (43) — each with confidence_score and sources. This is what turns the graph from an index into an inference engine.

4.1 · Attribute transfer — a new edge moves an entire attribute cloud

When statins --[sensitize_to]--> ferroptosis was extracted, statins did not merely gain one neighbor: they inherited ferroptosis's attribute cloud — iron overload, lipid peroxidation, PUFA membranes, GPX4/CoQ10 defense, NCOA4 ferritinophagy — none of which the statin note mentions. Any new discovery in that cloud (say, 7-DHC in 2024) is instantly one hop from statins. This is the general form of "we didn't know X was connected to cancer": a new edge to a hub silently pre-wires X to everything the hub already knows. COVID demonstrates it in-repo: the single typed edge sars_cov_2 --[disrupts]--> NAD+_metabolism pulls the virus into the CD38/PARP/sirtuin/inflammaging attribute space with no further edges.

4.2 · Typed-path composition — predicting connections that exist in neither layer

Relation types compose along paths, generating new typed hypotheses. Real chain from the repo's typed edges:

This is the metapath idea behind Hetionet's DWPC features and ROBOKOP's template queries: relation-typed paths, not undirected similarity, are the feature engine of drug repurposing. Adamic-Adar (undirected, untyped) cannot express "inhibits-an-activator-of"; typed path enumeration can — a missing pass in the current 04_link_prediction.py.

4.3 · Direction separates levers from consequences

With direction, in-edges and out-edges answer different questions. For ferroptosis: the pro-ferroptotic in-edges (ACSL4, NCOA4, p53, Iron, Lipid Peroxidation — and, inferred, COVID-19 via §4.2) are the druggable upstream levers; the anti- in-edges (GPX4, FSP1, SIRT3, Glutathione, Mitohormesis) are the defense system — whose inhibition is a tumor strategy and whose support is a healthy-aging strategy. The same node, two opposite therapeutic programs, disambiguated only by edge sign and direction. Directed PageRank/PPR (the wiki layer stores in/out degree per node: Ferroptosis in 105 / out 83) approximates "what does this drive" vs "what drives this."

4.4 · Signed triads — inferring the sign of unknown edges

Typed signs enable balance-style inference: if A inhibits B and B inhibits C, A likely relieves pressure on C ("enemy of my enemy"). Repo instance: statins --[sensitize_to]--> ferroptosis (+) and mitohormesis --[protects_against]--> ferroptosis (−) ⇒ predict a statin↔mitohormesis antagonism edge (CoQ10 depletion undermining the hormetic defense) — no such edge exists in the graph, no paper states it directly, and it is checkable. This is classic signed link prediction (Leskovec et al. 2010) transplanted to biology, and it is a natural generator of Assumptions-Lab conflicts: when a curated stance resolves a conflict, the correction is materialized into the graph itself (via 07_sync_assumptions.py → 03_rebuild_from_triples.py), and every downstream metric — including the replay metrics above — recomputes against the corrected topology.

4.5 · Caveats that keep typed inference honest

  • Extraction noise: typed edges come from LLM extraction; confidence-0.5 edges (e.g. covid_19 --[ameliorates]--> NMN, a case-series artifact) propagate through paths just like solid ones — weight paths by minimum edge confidence.
  • Composition is not deduction: two true edges do not guarantee a true composed edge (context, dose, tissue drop out). Calibrated base rate: ~10–30% precision of top-ranked candidates vs 0.8% unguided (§ Calibration).
  • Layer discipline: classic centrality scripts run on undirected projections — direction is collapsed in those calculations. The wiki-only layer is genuinely polarity-blind (links_to); the combined layer is not. State the layer with every number.

External case studies

Four real discovery arcs, read through the same lens

Discovery arcs from the wider literature and the metric lesson each contributes
CaseArcMetric lesson
Baricitinib BenevolentAI's Feb-2020 query was typed-path composition operationalized: [endocytosis module]—inhibited-by—[approved drug]—inhibits—[cytokine module]. Baricitinib was the only approved compound bridging both typed modules at achievable exposure: 16 dual-inhibitors → 7 survived the PK filter → 1 winner. Hypothesis published Lancet, Feb 4 2020; FDA EUA Nov 19 2020; COV-BARRIER 38% mortality reduction. ~9.5 months from graph hypothesis to authorization. Typed composition works as a product. Note what the graph did not contain: pharmacokinetics — the decisive filter was an attribute outside the graph, applied by humans.
GLP-1 agonists 2005–2015: endocrine leaves. Then one community edge per outcomes trial: cardiology (LEADER 2016, MACE −13%), obesity (STEP 2021), cardiology without diabetes (SELECT 2023, MACE −20%), nephrology (FLOW 2024, −24%), sleep medicine (first OSA drug, Dec 2024), hepatology (MASH resolution, FDA indication Aug 2025), addiction (semaglutide cut heavy-drinking days >70% in AUD, Lancet 2026). By 2026 semaglutide is one of the highest-degree, highest-bridging drug nodes in medicine. Degree explosion across five communities. But the most fragile edge was analogical (obesity→Alzheimer's): it snapped when tested — EVOKE/EVOKE+ negative (Nov 2025); exenatide failed Phase 3 in Parkinson's the same way. Analogical edges are a graph's most profitable and most breakable links — flag them in §4.2's composition rule.
SGLT2 inhibitors Empagliflozin was a glucose-excretion leaf when EMPA-REG (2015) delivered −38% CV death; heart failure (DAPA-HF 2019, ~half non-diabetics), HFpEF (2021), kidney (EMPA-KIDNEY 2022, −28%) followed. Topologically the node had no edge to myocardium at all. The newest edge — dementia — repeats the pattern: observational ORs 0.45–0.62, pooled RCT null (23 RCTs, n≈160k, 2025). Direction is not predictable from network distance. Observational co-occurrence edges overweight confounded links; RCT-grade edges arrive years later and can erase them — an argument for evidence-tier attributes on edges.
PROTACs Named 2001; ~18 years dormant (degree ≈ 10, zero clinical edges). First-in-human 2019, proof-of-concept 2022, Phase 3 by 2026 — and the mechanism introduced a new relation type ("degrades") that rewired chemistry↔ubiquitin↔oncology into its own community. Low degree is a state, not a verdict. During dormancy the metrics said "peripheral, k-core 1"; the correct reading was "unrealized bridge" — indistinguishable from "dead end" by topology alone. Node provenance (who works on it, what tools exist) must be read alongside degree.

Scorecard

So — do the metrics (plus typed edges) aid understanding of implications?

Yes, as triage instruments, gap detectors, and typed-inference engines — no, as oracles.

What still requires attributes, not topology: effect size, dose/tissue/age context, evidence tier, pharmacokinetics (the baricitinib filter), and polarity when only the wiki layer is used. The combined graph's typed subset already carries relation + direction + confidence + provenance — the substrate is in place; the calculations that exploit it (typed-path passes, signed-triad mining) are the missing pieces.

Playbook

Operational checklist for this vault

  1. On every new note/entity: run thin-node AA attachment + PPR field assignment immediately (foreseeability decays); log the top-25 as the review queue; record which seed relation types were granted — they gate the mechanistic corridor (the Lymphopenia effect).
  2. Add a typed-path pass (04_ candidate): enumerate 2-hop compositions over the combined graph's 3,895 typed edges (min-confidence-weighted, analogical flagged), rank by composed confidence × AA support — the "unknown connection" engine (X→cancer class) that would have ranked COVID→ferroptosis two years early.
  3. Mine signed triads for Assumptions-Lab conflicts (enemy-of-enemy and double-positive triangles); register in assumptions.json and resolve stances — resolved conflicts are materialized into the graph (selections → directional edge adds/removals; excludedSources for noisy documents). Run order for any triples/assumption change: 03_normalize_triples_schema.py → 07_sync_assumptions.py → 03_rebuild_from_triples.py → 05_build_combined.py / 05_rebuild_from_wiki.py → 07_sync_to_db.py + 07_sync_content.py.
  4. Track betweenness/k-core trajectory per entity across rebuilds (extend graph-diff.json) — the maturation signal that flagged ferroptosis-2020.
  5. Close the literature-vs-graph gaps the runs exposed: direct SARS-CoV-2↔CD38/NAD+/PARP edges (k-core 11→15, betweenness ×2), SARS-CoV-2↔ferroptosis (Nat Commun 2024 — currently only a 2-hop typed implication), Metformin↔Long-COVID-prevention (COVID-OUT), negative-RCT annotation on COVID-19↔NMN.
  6. Carry attributes before adding vaccine/stratified nodes (dose×age×sex, evidence tier) — averaging erases the finding.
  7. Weight every predicted implication by the calibration priors: top-K graph output is a ~10–30%-precision hypothesis stream vs 0.8% unguided — valuable, never oracular.
