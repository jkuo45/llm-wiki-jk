Biological Processes in the Combined Knowledge Graph (wiki and triples)

The vault now runs its graph viewer on a combined dataset — the union of the triples graph (typed relations extracted from _triples.json) and the wiki graph (Obsidian [[wikilinks]] across entity notes). Merging the two is not cosmetic: recomputing centrality on the union topology re-wires the whole picture. Fragmented triples fragments get stitched into one dense network (max k-core 6 → 20), a wave of wiki-only biology (cGAS–STING, cell cycle, NMN / NAD+, mitophagy, p53/TP53) enters the inner core, and every established hub gains 2–5× PageRank. This page walks the biology the combined graph is uniquely positioned to show, process by process.

01 · Composition

One dense network, assembled from two sparse views

The triples graph is a small, typed, fragmented spine: 2,629 nodes and only 3,832 edges. The wiki graph is a large, untyped, dense authoring network: 2,994 entity notes and 34,850 links_to edges. Their union is the combined graph — 4,084 nodes and 36,982 edges — and it is what the viewer shows by default. Node ids are canonicalised with the same norm() (Unicode-normalised; NF-κB = nf_kappab in all three), so the two sources join cleanly at the entity level.

Union node composition — shared (both graphs): 1,539 · wiki-only: 1,455 · triples-only: 1,090.

Union edge provenance — present in both graphs: 1,700 (4.6%) · wiki-only: 33,150 (89.7%) · triples-only: 2,132 (5.8%). The typed triples spine is a minority of the union; the wiki edges dominate the topology.

The processes the wiki alone contributes

  • Innate immune DNA sensing

    cGAS–STING — type I interferon axis

    cGAS (96), STING (83), Type I Interferon (66), Macrophage (75), with the shared hubs NF-κB, Inflammation, SASP and the wiki-only Adaptive Immune System · AIM2 · 5xFAD set in the wiki graph's “cGAS-STING Pathway” community (wiki-out/GRAPH_REPORT.md). This axis is exactly the kind of biology the triples extraction missed: cytosolic DNA → cGAMP → STING → IRF3/NF-κB → type I IFN / SASP. It is the mechanistic bridge between mitochondrial DNA escape, senescence, and neuroinflammation.

  • Tumor-suppressor control

    p53 (TP53) and cell-cycle checkpoints

    After consolidation, p53 is a shared core node — union degree 211, k-core 20, Sink · Bottleneck · Core backbone — sitting in the union's innermost shell. It brings Cell Cycle (83), Replicative Senescence (65), Epigenetics and aging (64), and Histone Variant (60) into a single reachable component with SASP and the senescence machinery. The wiki graph also shows the gap: CDK4 / CDK6 are still isolated (≤1 connection) in the wiki report — a concrete curation gap for cell-cycle-arrest biology downstream of p53.

  • NAD+ metabolism

    NMN and the NAD+ salvage axis

    NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide) (71, k-core 19) enters the core, connecting to the shared NAD+ / SIRT1 / SIRT3 hubs and, in the wiki graph, the NAD+ community (77 nodes: 1-Methylnicotinamide, BMAL1, Axon Degeneration…). The union therefore links NMN's pharmacology to circadian (BMAL1), sirtuin, and axonal-degeneration contexts in one hop region.

  • Mitochondrial quality control

    PINK1/PARK2, mPTP, and mitophagy machinery

    Wiki-only PARK2, Mitochondrial Permeability Transition Pore (57), Autophagosome, and Atg1 join the shared Mitochondria / DRP1 / Mitophagy communities, wiring the Parkinson's (α-synuclein, 6-OHDA) and neurodegeneration context into fission/fusion and mitophagy. 04_node_analysis-style PPR seeded on Cancer ranks mitochondria and neurodegeneration among its nearest neighbours — the mitochondrial-aging axis is one of the union's central seams.

05 · Biology processes

Worked biological examples in the combined dataset

Each example below picks one biology process and reads it off the combined graph — noting which nodes each graph contributes (gold = wiki-only addition, slate = shared hub). These are readings of the union topology; treat every claim as a hypothesis checkable against the notes.

cGAS–STING → type I IFN → senescence / SASP

Combined-graph evidence: cGAS (k18) · STING (k16) · Type I Interferon (k16) — all wiki-only, all inside the union core — cascade into the shared NF-κB (368, k20) / Inflammation (351, k20) / SASP (547, k20) hubs. The wiki graph's own community view confirms a 96-node “cGAS-STING Pathway” community that includes Alzheimer's (5xFAD, APOE3 Christchurch), supporting the neuroinflammation interpretation. Why this matters: cytosolic mtDNA escape (a mitohormesis / mitophagy failure) and senescence-associated DNA damage both feed this axis; the union makes the mitochondrial → cGAS → SASP path explicit as one reachable chain.

Ferroptosis and the lipid-peroxidation module

The wiki graph's Ferroptosis community (91 nodes) and Oxidative Stress (104 nodes) flank the corpus's top hub Oxidative Stress (607, k20). The module's mechanics are all present in the union: GPX4 (clustering 1.0 — a tight redox module member), ACSL4 and ALOX15 (PUFA peroxidation), 4-Hydroxynonenal, and the spreader Acid ceramidase (out ≫ in; senescent-cell membrane remodeling toward a pro-ferroptotic PUFA profile). Combination reading: the union couples the enzymatic engine (ACSL4/ALOX15/GPX4, triples-typed) with the redox-sensing context (cGAS-STING, Nrf2/ARE) via wiki links — a single module where the two graphs each carried half.

NAD+ → SIRT1 → mitohormesis / autophagy

SIRT1 (579, k20, Spreader·Master) is the union's second-most-connected node and its single highest betweenness (0.080). Around it the union assembles: NAD+ (shared), NMN (wiki-only, k19), Nicotinamide Riboside, AMPK, mTORC1, and the wiki graph's Mitohormesis (45 nodes: ATF4, ATF5, cco-1, CHOP) and DRP1 (43 nodes: fission/fusion) communities. Personalized PageRank from oxidative_stress ranks NAD and SIRT3 as nearest neighbours — the redox-SIRT axis is a coherent neighborhood in the union. The biological point: NAD+ precursor pharmacology (NMN/NR) now sits in the same connected component as mitochondria, mitohormesis, and autophagy, with the C. elegans hormesis community (DAF-2 → HLH-30) only a few hops away.

p53 (TP53), cell-cycle arrest, and the senescence checkpoint

Once the TP53/p53 duplication is consolidated, p53 is a shared union core node (union degree 211, k-core 20, Sink · Bottleneck · Core backbone): on the triples side it was a mid-degree suppressor, and the wiki links lift it into the innermost shell, a betweenness-bottleneck connecting Cell Cycle (83), Replicative Senescence (65), Histone Variant (60), and the SASP core. The wiki report flags the flanking gap: CDK4/CDK6 are isolated (≤1 connection), i.e. the p16/p21-CDK-RB axis that executes p53-dependent arrest is under-curated — a concrete next target for note creation (the Senomorphic Therapy community, 51 nodes with BRD4 / BET inhibitors, already awaits it).

Parkinson's disease and mitochondrial quality control

The wiki graph's largest community is Parkinson's Disease (202 nodes: α-synuclein, A53T, 6-OHDA, 5,6-indolequinone) and it is the union's 12th hub (295, k20). The wiki-only additions PARK2, mPTP (57), and Autophagosome bridge it to the shared Mitophagy / DRP1 (fission) / Mitochondria communities. Pairing with the link-prediction queue (Parkinson's ↔ ROS, Alzheimer's ↔ Parkinson's), the combined graph's reading is: dopaminergic vulnerability = failed mitophagy + redox stress + neuroinflammation (cGAS-STING), all reachable in a few union hops.

Caveats that matter for interpretation

  • Wiki edges are untyped. 89.6% of union edges are links_to (direction = note authorship, confidence 1.0). Degree, k-core, and connectivity are robust; directed role semantics (spreader / sink) on those edges are authoring artifacts — read them only on the typed triples spine.
  • Stored fingerprints are inherited per-source. The combined nodes.json carries triples-side metrics on shared nodes and wiki-side values on wiki-only nodes; the numbers in this page are recomputed on the union (Methods).
  • Thresholds recalibrated. Role counts above are percentiles of the union, not 1:1 comparable to the triples page table.

A practical consequence: on the union, use role labels to find structure (core/backbone, bottlenecks, local modules). When the question is causal — “what does this enzyme do to that pathway?” — restrict to edges with sources ∋ triples (the typed spine: 2,132 triples-only + 1,700 shared-endpoint edges ≈ the full 3,832 typed edges), which recovers typed semantics on the denser topology.

07 · Methods & sources

How the numbers were produced

  1. This analysis

    Recompute on the union

    The union DiGraph is built from web/data/nodes.json + edges.json (canonical combined dataset from scripts/05_build_combined.py). Metrics: nx.pagerank(G, alpha=0.85, max_iter=200); undirected projection → betweenness_centrality, clustering, core_number, connected_components; roles via scripts/_node_roles_lib.py. Triples baseline reads graphify-out/graph.json. Full details and tables: the task output document. (Ad-hoc analysis, not a tracked script.)

  2. Companion artifacts

    What each source supplies

    wiki-out/GRAPH_REPORT.md — wiki-graph god nodes, 68 communities, surprising connections, isolated-node gaps. wiki-out/GRAPH_DIFF.md — triples-vs-wiki node and edge deltas (wiki-only / triples-only lists). web/data/wiki-link-prediction.json — Adamic-Adar missing-edge candidates on the wiki graph. web/data/node_roles.json — canonical combined role artifact; note its web-node fields omit in/out degree, so Spreader/Sink are under-counted there (a known artifact; the table above recomputes from full fingerprints).

  3. Graph to cite

    Always name the graph

    All analysis documents predating 27 Aug 2026 (including node-analysis-examples-biology.html) were computed on the triples graph. Metrics, roles, and backbone claims are graph-specific: k-core 6 (triples) vs 20 (combined); SASP vs SIRT1 as top betweenness. State which dataset a number came from.
