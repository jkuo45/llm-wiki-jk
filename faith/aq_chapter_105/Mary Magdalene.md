# Mary Magdalene

A prominent and devoted follower of [[Jesus]] from [[Galilee]]. She is recognized as one of the "women who possessed much wealth" who provided the financial means and support for the missionary tours of [[Jesus]], [[The Twelve]], and the [[Masters from the foreign lands]].

## Key Events
- **Deliverance:** She was healed of seven "homeless spirits of the air" by [[Jesus]] using the [[Omnific Word]] (Chapter 105).
- **Resurrection Witness:** Following the crucifixion, she was one of the first to visit the tomb and the first to announce the resurrection of the [[Son of Man]] to the other disciples (Chapter 173).

## Connections
- [[Jesus]] ↔ [[Mary Magdalene]]: Jesus healed her of seven spirits; she remained a loyal follower and patron.
- [[Susanna]], [[Johanna]], [[Rachel]] ↔ [[Mary Magdalene]]: Fellow wealthy women from [[Galilee]] and beyond who supported the ministry.
