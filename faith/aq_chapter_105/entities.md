# Entities in Chapter 105

## People
- [[Jesus]]: The central figure, teacher, and healer who works through the [[Omnific Word]].
- [[The Twelve]]: The primary disciples accompanying [[Jesus]].
- [[Masters from the foreign lands]]: Teachers from other nations traveling with [[Jesus]].
- [[Mary Magdalene]]: A woman from [[Galilee]] who was healed of seven spirits by [[Jesus]].
- [[Susanna]]: A wealthy woman who owned vast estates at [[Caesarea Philippi]].
- [[Johanna]]: The wife of [[Chuza]] and a follower of [[Jesus]].
- [[Chuza]]: An official in [[Herod]]'s court and husband of [[Johanna]].
- [[Herod]]: The ruler whose court included [[Chuza]].
- [[Rachel]]: A follower from the coast of [[Tyre]].
- [[Son of Man]]: A title used by [[Jesus]] to refer to himself or the messianic figure.

## Places
- [[Galilee]]: The primary region of [[Jesus]]' ministry in this chapter.
- [[Caesarea Philippi]]: The location of [[Susanna]]'s estates.
- [[Tyre]]: A coastal city from which [[Rachel]] originated.
- [[Jordan]]: The river and surrounding region beyond which followers came.
- [[Sea of Galilee]]: The body of water central to the region's geography.
- [[Israel]]: Addressed collectively by [[Jesus]] during his teaching.

## Concepts and Divine Entities
- [[Omnific Word]]: The powerful word spoken by [[Jesus]] to drive out spirits and heal.
- [[Gospel of Christ]]: The core message preached by [[Jesus]] and [[The Twelve]].
- [[Holy Breath]]: A divine feminine entity; sinning against her by disregarding her light is described as an unpardonable act for the current age.
- [[Beelzebul]]: A demonic figure associated with hypocrisy and spiritual blight.
- [[Tree of Life]]: A metaphor used by [[Jesus]] to describe the relationship between words (leaves) and deeds (fruit).
- [[Bread of Life]]: Spiritual sustenance brought by messengers of God.

## Connections
- [[Jesus]] ↔ [[Mary Magdalene]]: Jesus healed her of seven spirits using the [[Omnific Word]].
- [[Johanna]] ↔ [[Chuza]]: Johanna is the wife of Chuza, an official in [[Herod]]'s court.
- [[Susanna]] ↔ [[Caesarea Philippi]]: Susanna is a wealthy landowner with estates in this city.
- [[Holy Breath]] ↔ [[Jesus]]: Jesus teaches that while sins against the [[Son of Man]] may be pardoned, sins against the Holy Breath are far more grave.
