# Righteousness (Yi)

**Righteousness** (*yì*) is one of the cardinal virtues in [[Confucianism]], particularly emphasized by [[Mencius]]. It is the disposition to disdain or regard as shameful any dishonorable behavior or demeaning treatment.

## Origins and Manifestation
Righteousness is one of the [[Four Sprouts (Confucianism)|Four Sprouts]], manifesting in:
- **The Feeling of Disdain**: The innate sense of shame that prevents a person from accepting a bribe or being addressed disrespectfully.
- **Incorruptibility**: A righteous person would rather die than do something shameful.

## Comparison with Benevolence
While [[Benevolence (Ren)|Benevolence]] is the virtue of love and compassion, righteousness is often associated with duty, honor, and the correct distinctions between right and wrong.
