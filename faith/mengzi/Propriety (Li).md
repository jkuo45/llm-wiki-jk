# Propriety (Li)

**Propriety** (*lĭ*), also translated as **Ritual Propriety**, is the virtue of following social mores, etiquette, and ceremonies that maintain social order in [[Confucianism]].

## Origins and Manifestation
Propriety is one of the [[Four Sprouts (Confucianism)|Four Sprouts]] identified by [[Mencius]], manifesting in:
- **The Feeling of Respect**: A natural sense of deference toward elders and authority figures.
- **Ritual (Li)**: The practice of traditional rites and ceremonies.

## Flexibility in Mencius
Unlike some rigid interpretations of ritual, Mencius argued that propriety must be applied with wisdom. For example, he stated that one should violate the ritual prohibition on physical contact between unmarried men and women if it is necessary to save a drowning woman.
