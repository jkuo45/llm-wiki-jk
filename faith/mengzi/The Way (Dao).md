# The Way (Dao)

**The Way** (*dào*) refers to the right way to live, to organize society, and to govern. It is a central concept across all schools of Chinese philosophy.

## Confucian Interpretation
In [[Confucianism]], the Way is the path of the ancient sage-kings, characterized by [[Benevolence (Ren)]], [[Righteousness (Yi)]], and [[Ritual (Li)]]. [[Mencius]] sought to articulate a version of the Way that would rescue people from the chaos of the [[Warring States Period]].

## Competition of Ways
During the "Hundred Schools of Thought," different masters proposed competing accounts of the Way, including [[Mozi]]'s utilitarianism and [[Yang Zhu]]'s egoism, both of which Mencius vigorously opposed.
