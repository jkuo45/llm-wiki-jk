# Four Books

The **Four Books** (Sishu) are Chinese classic texts that [[Zhu Xi]] selected as the foundational curriculum for [[Confucianism]] during the Song Dynasty.

## The Collection
The Four Books consist of:
1. *Great Learning* (Daxue)
2. *Doctrine of the Mean* (Zhongyong)
3. *Analects* (Lunyu) - The sayings of [[Confucius]].
4. *[[Mengzi (Text)|Mengzi]]* - The dialogues of [[Mencius]].

## Significance
The Four Books replaced the Five Classics as the basis for the imperial examination system. This elevation significantly increased the influence of [[Mencius]]'s thought in late imperial China.
