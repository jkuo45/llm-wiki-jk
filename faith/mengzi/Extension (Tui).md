# Extension (Tui)

**Extension** (*tuī*) is the mental and ethical process described by [[Mencius]] for developing innate virtuous tendencies (the [[Four Sprouts (Confucianism)|Four Sprouts]]) into fully formed virtues.

## The Process
Extension involves recognizing the similarity between a situation where one naturally feels a virtuous emotion and a situation where one does not, and then "extending" that emotion to the new situation.
- **Example**: In a famous dialogue (1A7), Mencius encourages a king to extend the compassion he felt for an ox being led to slaughter to his own suffering subjects.

## Role of Reflection
Successful extension requires **[[Reflection (Si)]]**, which allows a person to see the moral parallels between different cases and motivates them to act consistently with their innate goodness.
