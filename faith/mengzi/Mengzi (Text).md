# Mengzi (Text)

The ***Mengzi*** (or *Mencius*) is a collection of dialogues, debates, and sayings of the philosopher [[Mencius]]. It is one of the [[Four Books]] of [[Confucianism]].

## Composition and Structure
The text was likely compiled by Mencius's disciples. It is divided into seven "books," each with two parts (A and B). Unlike the *Analects* of [[Confucius]], which consists of brief fragments, the *Mengzi* contains long, structured arguments and vivid metaphors.

## Key Commentaries
- **Zhao Qi** (2nd century CE): Edited the text and provided the earliest extant commentary.
- **[[Zhu Xi]]** (12th century CE): Included it in the [[Four Books]] and provided a commentary that became the standard for imperial examinations.
