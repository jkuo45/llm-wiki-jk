# Wisdom (Zhi)

**Wisdom** (*zhì*) is one of the cardinal virtues in [[Confucianism]]. In the thought of [[Mencius]], it is the capacity to distinguish between right and wrong and to apply other virtues appropriately.

## Origins and Manifestation
Wisdom is one of the [[Four Sprouts (Confucianism)|Four Sprouts]], manifesting in:
- **The Feeling of Approval and Disapproval**: The innate ability to judge the moral quality of actions and characters.
- **Timeliness**: The ability to perform the right action out of the appropriate motivation in a particular situation.

## Role in Cultivation
Wisdom serves as a guiding virtue, helping a person understand and commit to [[Benevolence (Ren)]] and [[Righteousness (Yi)]]. It is what allows a person to know when to follow or override [[Propriety (Li)|Ritual Propriety]].
