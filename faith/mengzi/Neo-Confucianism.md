# Neo-Confucianism

**Neo-Confucianism** is a moral, ethical, and metaphysical Chinese philosophy influenced by [[Confucianism]], which originated with Han Yu and Li Ao in the Tang Dynasty and became prominent during the Song and Ming dynasties.

## Key Figures
- **[[Zhu Xi]]**: The most influential Neo-Confucian, who organized the [[Four Books]] and provided definitive commentaries on the [[Mengzi (Text)|Mengzi]].
- **[[Wang Yangming]]**: Known for his doctrine of the "unity of knowing and acting," which he derived from his interpretation of [[Mencius]].

## Relation to Mencius
Neo-Confucianism elevated [[Mencius]] to the status of "Second Sage" and emphasized his doctrine that [[Human Nature (Confucianism)|human nature is good]]. The *[[Mengzi (Text)|Mengzi]]* became a central text in the Neo-Confucian curriculum.
