# Zisi

**Zisi** (c. 481–402 BCE), also known as Kong Ji, was the grandson of **[[Confucius]]** and a significant philosopher in early **[[Confucianism]]**.

## Relation to Mencius
Mencius is traditionally said to have studied under Zisi, or more likely, under one of Zisi's disciples. Zisi is thus the link between Confucius and **[[Mencius]]**, representing the lineage of the "Second Sage."

## Philosophical Contributions
Zisi is traditionally credited with the *Doctrine of the Mean* (Zhongyong), which later became one of the **[[Four Books]]**. His work emphasizes the inner life of the individual and the harmony between human nature and **[[Heaven (Tian)]]**.
