# Benevolence (Ren)

**Benevolence** (*rén*) is the most central virtue in [[Confucianism]]. In [[Mencius]]'s philosophy, it is an innate ethical disposition that originates from the feeling of compassion.

## Origins and Manifestation
Mencius identifies benevolence as one of the [[Four Sprouts (Confucianism)|Four Sprouts]], manifesting in:
- **The Feeling of Compassion**: A spontaneous reaction to the suffering of others, such as seeing a child about to fall into a well.
- **Affection for Kin**: The root of benevolence is found in familial love, particularly [[Filial Piety (Xiao)]].
- **Extension**: A fully benevolent person "extends" their natural compassion from their family to all humanity.

## Political Application
A benevolent ruler ensures the well-being of their subjects, providing for their physical needs to enable their own moral cultivation.
