# Impartial Caring

**Impartial Caring** (*jiǎn'ài*), often translated as "universal love," is the central doctrine of [[Mozi]] and the [[Mohism|Mohist]] school.

## Definition
The doctrine advocates that one should care for all people equally, without the preferences for family or friends that are central to [[Confucianism]]. Mozi argued that social chaos was caused by partiality and that "impartiality is the Way of the sage-kings."

## Mencius's Critique
[[Mencius]] vigorously opposed impartial caring, arguing that it was "to have no father" and thus subverted the natural order of human affection. Mencius instead advocated for **[[Differentiated Caring]]**, which starts with [[Filial Piety (Xiao)]] and is extended outward.
