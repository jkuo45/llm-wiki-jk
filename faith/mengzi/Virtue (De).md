# Virtue (De)

**Virtue** (*dé*) in ancient Chinese thought originally referred to a kind of political charisma or power that a ruler accumulated through good character and acts of kindness.

## Evolution of the Concept
During the [[Zhou Dynasty]], *de* evolved from a royal prerogative to a moral quality that any person could possess. In [[Confucianism]], it refers to the ethical power of a virtuous person to inspire others without the use of coercion.

## In Mencius
[[Mencius]] emphasized that a ruler with *de* would naturally attract subjects and unify the realm, as people would follow them willingly rather than out of fear of punishment.
