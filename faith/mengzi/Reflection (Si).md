# Reflection (Si)

**Reflection** (*sī*) is the mental activity that [[Mencius]] identifies as crucial for ethical development and the "extension" of innate virtuous dispositions.

## Function of the Heart
Mencius distinguishes the "heart/mind" (*xin*) from the sensory organs (eyes and ears). While the sensory organs respond automatically to external stimuli (desires), the function of the heart is to reflect.
- "If it reflects, then it will get it. If it does not reflect, then it will not get it." (6A15)

## Role in Cultivation
Reflection allows an individual to focus on their "greater part" (ethical motivations) rather than being misled by their "petty part" (sensual desires). It is the mechanism by which one achieves **[[Extension (Tui)]]** and develops the [[Four Sprouts (Confucianism)|Four Sprouts]] into virtues.
