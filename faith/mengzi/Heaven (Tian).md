# Heaven (Tian)

In the time of [[Mencius]], **Heaven** (*tiān*) was regarded as a higher power responsible for the general course of history and the moral order of the universe.

## Relation to Government
Heaven was believed to grant the [[Mandate of Heaven]] to virtuous rulers. Mencius argued that Heaven’s will is manifested through the acceptance of the people: "Heaven sees as my people see; Heaven hears as my people hear."

## Relation to Human Nature
Mencius believed that [[Human Nature (Confucianism)|human nature]] is what Heaven has given us. By developing our innate tendencies toward virtue (the [[Four Sprouts (Confucianism)|Four Sprouts]]), we are serving and knowing Heaven.
