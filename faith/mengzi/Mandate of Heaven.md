# Mandate of Heaven

The **Mandate of Heaven** (*tiānmìng*) is a traditional Chinese philosophical concept used to justify the rule of the Kings or Emperors of China.

## Core Concept
According to this doctrine, **[[Heaven (Tian)]]** blesses the rule of a just and virtuous leader but withdraws its mandate from a despotic or corrupt ruler. The loss of the mandate is often signaled by natural disasters, famines, and rebellions.

## In Mencius
**[[Mencius]]** used the Mandate of Heaven to argue that rulers have a responsibility to their people. He famously claimed that the people are more important than the ruler, and that the people’s acceptance of a ruler is the primary sign that they possess the Mandate.
