# Filial Piety (Xiao)

**Filial Piety** (*xiào*) is the virtue of respect and care for one's parents and ancestors, considered the root of all other virtues in [[Confucianism]].

## Role in Moral Development
[[Confucius]] and his followers, including [[Mencius]], believed that the family is the primary environment for nurturing virtue. Mencius argued that [[Benevolence (Ren)]] and [[Righteousness (Yi)]] are achieved through the "extension" of the love and respect first manifested toward one's parents.

## Extension
Mencius's doctrine of extension starts with filial piety: the natural love one feels for parents is the "sprout" that, when expanded, becomes universal benevolence.
