[[Zacharias]] is cited by [[Jesus]] in Chapter 108 as the final holy man in a lineage of martyrs starting from [[Abel]]. [[Jesus]] declares that the blood of all these holy men, including [[Zacharias]], stains the hands of the ungodly generation of leaders who continue to persecute those sent by [[God]].
