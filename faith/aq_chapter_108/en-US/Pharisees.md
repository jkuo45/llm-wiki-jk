In Chapter 108, the [[Pharisees]] are rebuked by [[Jesus]] for their hypocrisy. They are criticized for meticulously cleaning the outside of their bodies and titheing herbs like mint and rue, while neglecting judgment and the love of [[God]]. [[Jesus]] calls them "tinselled gentry" and "ungodly," accusing them of seeking honor while harboring wickedness, extortion, and deceit in their hearts.
