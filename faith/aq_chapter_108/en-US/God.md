In Chapter 108, [[God]] is described by [[Jesus]] as the creator of both the outside and the inside of the body, challenging the Pharisaic focus on external cleanliness. [[Jesus]] also emphasizes that those who hear, receive, and live the word of [[God]] are truly blest, and He rebukes the leaders for neglecting the love of [[God]] and persecuting His sent prophets and apostles.
