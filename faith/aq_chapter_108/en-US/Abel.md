[[Abel]] is mentioned by [[Jesus]] in Chapter 108 as the first of the holy men whose blood stains the hands of the ungodly generation. His name marks the beginning of a lineage of martyrs, ending with [[Zacharias]], whose blood the hypocritical leaders were held accountable for.
