In Chapter 108, [[Jesus]] rebukes the multitudes for their selfishness and greed, likening them to wild beasts and vipers. He shares the parable of the unclean spirit to warn against snatching blessings from others. Later, at a feast, He exposes the hypocrisy of the [[Pharisees]], condemning their focus on external rituals while their hearts are full of wickedness. He also denounces the masters of the law for persecuting the holy men sent by [[God]].
