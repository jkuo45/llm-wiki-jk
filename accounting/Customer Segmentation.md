# Customer Segmentation

Customer Segmentation is the process of dividing a customer base into groups of individuals that are similar in specific ways relevant to marketing, such as age, gender, interests, and spending habits. This is often achieved using [[Machine Learning]] clustering algorithms within [[Customer Data Analytics]].
