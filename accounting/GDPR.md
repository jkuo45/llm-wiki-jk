# GDPR

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is a legal framework that sets guidelines for the collection and processing of personal information from individuals who live in the European Union (EU). Ensuring [[Compliance]] with GDPR is a critical aspect of [[Customer Data Analytics]].
