# Predictive Analytics

Predictive analytics is the use of data, statistical algorithms, and [[Machine Learning]] techniques to identify the likelihood of future outcomes based on historical data. It is crucial for sales forecasting, budget planning, and risk management in [[Customer Data Analytics]].
