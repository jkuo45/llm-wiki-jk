# Business Intelligence (BI)

Business Intelligence (BI) comprises the strategies and technologies used by enterprises for the data analysis of business information. It provides historical, current, and predictive views of business operations, often leveraging [[Customer Data Analytics]] and [[Predictive Analytics]].
