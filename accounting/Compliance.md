# Compliance

Compliance in accounting refers to the process of ensuring that financial practices and reporting adhere to laws, regulations, and standards such as [[GDPR]] or [[CCPA]]. [[AI Accounting Agents]] can help monitor changes in legislation to maintain compliance.
